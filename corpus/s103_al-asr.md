# Surah 103: TIME (al-’Asr)
Source: The Clear Quran, English translation by Talal Itani (ClearQuran edition A; 'Allah' rendering)
Basmala: In the name of Allah, the Gracious, the Merciful

[103:1] By Time.
[103:2] The human being is in loss.
[103:3] Except those who believe, and do good works, and encourage truth, and recommend patience.
