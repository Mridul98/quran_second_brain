# Surah 112: MONOTHEISM (al-Ikhlas)
Source: The Clear Quran, English translation by Talal Itani (ClearQuran edition A; 'Allah' rendering)
Basmala: In the name of Allah, the Gracious, the Merciful

[112:1] Say, “He is Allah, the One.
[112:2] Allah, the Absolute.
[112:3] He begets not, nor was He begotten.
[112:4] And there is nothing comparable to Him.”
