# Surah 91: THE SUN (ash-Shams)
Source: The Clear Quran, English translation by Talal Itani (ClearQuran edition A; 'Allah' rendering)
Basmala: In the name of Allah, the Gracious, the Merciful

[91:1] By the sun and its radiance.
[91:2] And the moon as it follows it.
[91:3] And the day as it reveals it.
[91:4] And the night as it conceals it.
[91:5] And the sky and He who built it.
[91:6] And the earth and He who spread it.
[91:7] And the soul and He who proportioned it.
[91:8] And inspired it with its wickedness and its righteousness.
[91:9] Successful is he who purifies it.
[91:10] Failing is he who corrupts it.
[91:11] Thamood denied in its pride.
[91:12] When it followed its most wicked.
[91:13] The messenger of Allah said to them, “This is the she-camel of Allah, so let her drink.”
[91:14] But they called him a liar, and hamstrung her. So their Lord crushed them for their sin, and leveled it.
[91:15] And He does not fear its sequel.
