## 3. Structural Arc
* **The Book speaks; some refuse to hear (1–8):** letters → mercy-names → detailed Arabic → good news/warnings (verbatim-twin formula of `[2:119]`) → the three excuses (screen, deafness, barrier) → the messenger's bare creed → the sorting: alms-withholding disbelievers vs "a reward uninterrupted."
* **The cosmos answered before you did (9–18):** creation interrogates ("And you attribute equals to Him?"); earth in two days, provisions in four, sky-as-smoke summoned into seven universes with laws; then the precedent of the two arrogant peoples — screaming wind, thunderbolt, blindness preferred over guidance, and "We saved those who believed and were righteous."
* **The courtroom of the body (19–25):** the enemies herded; their own hearing/sight/skins testify — the excuses of 41:5 turned into witnesses; the skins cite Allah "Who made all things speak"; bad thought about the Lord = the ruin-verdict; patience useless; assigned companions and the Word proved true.
* **The counter-recitation and the straight (26–32):** the disbelievers' noise-strategy against the recitation; the intense agony; Allah's enemies' permanent home; the wish to trample the seducers — flipped to the straight-speakers at whose falling the angels descend, fear gone, grief gone, desires granted: "As Hospitality from an All-Forgiving, Merciful One."
* **Speech, bowing, stamina, and the Book's invincibility (33–44):** who is better in speech than the caller to Allah; evil repelled with good (a perseverance-ethic); refuge from the Devil's sting; the lights that must not be bowed to, while the angels praise untiring; the still earth stirred — resurrection evidence; none hidden from the Seeing; the Reminder rejected — "it is an invincible Book"; the language complaint answered: guidance and healing vs "heaviness in their ears," called "from a distant place."
* **Doubt's end, evidence's verdict (45–54):** Moses's disputed Book as precedent; every soul its own account, no divine injustice; the Hour's knowledge reserved to Him — fruit-sheaths, conceptions, "Where are My associates?"; the invoked things forsake; man's despair-then-boast weather ("This is mine"); the Have-you-considered challenge; the signature verse — proofs on the horizons and in themselves until it is clear; the Lord witness over everything, comprehending everything.
