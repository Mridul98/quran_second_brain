# The Day the skins testify: Allah, Who made all things speak (41:19–25)
**UID:** 202610142004
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 41:19–25 (Ruku 3 of 6 in Fussilat)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[41:19]` – `[41:25]` (7 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The herding of Allah's enemies into the Fire turns into a courtroom where the prosecution is the defendants' own hardware — hearing, sight, skin — and the skins give the surah its kalam apex: "Allah, Who made all things speak, made us speak"; the verdict then lands on *thought about God* (23), patience-as-refuge (24), and the glamorizing companions over whom the Word proved true (25).
* **Placement:** Ruku 3 of 6; the eschatological center of the first half. It answers ruku 1's three excuses (41:5: screened hearts, deaf ears, barrier) organ-for-organ, and recalls ruku 2's answered cosmos (41:11) in the skins' willing confession (41:21). Its ending "They were losers" (25) rhymes 41:23's "the losers," binding the scene; the witness-motif it opens closes at 41:47 ("none of us is a witness") and 41:53 ("your Lord is witness over everything").

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[41:19]`** "The Day when Allah’s enemies are herded into the Fire, forcibly."
  * **Gloss:** A Day-narrative opening (the surah's third *yawm*-scene after 41:14-18's precedent). "Herded… forcibly" — the *wurud* verb used of the humiliated at `[25:34]` ("herded into Hell on their faces"), `[8:36]` ("herded into Hell") and `[19:86]` ("herd the sinners into hell, like animals to water").
* **`[41:20]`** "Until, when they have reached it, their hearing, and their sight, and their skins will testify against them regarding what they used to do."
  * **Gloss:** The organs testify: hearing and sight — exactly the two that pleaded disability at 41:5 ("in our ears is deafness") — plus the skin, the boundary of selfhood. Twin scenes: `[24:24]` (tongues, hands, feet testify) and `[36:65]` (mouths sealed, hands speak, feet testify).
* **`[41:21]`** "And they will say to their skins, “Why did you testify against us?” They will say, “Allah, Who made all things speak, made us speak. It is He who created you the first time, and to Him you are returned.”""
  * **Gloss:** The task's quoted scene, verbatim in this edition. The skins' answer is a creed in two clauses: God as the giver of speech to *all things* (kalam apex — the same power that answered the summoned sky, 41:11) and first-creation-plus-return (twin of `[40:3]` "To Him is the ultimate return"). Accusation against the flesh becomes confession of the Creator.
* **`[41:22]`** "You were unable to hide yourselves from your hearing, and your sight, and your skins, to prevent them from testifying against you, and you imagined that Allah was unaware of much of what you do."
  * **Gloss:** The address shifts to second person — the real crime named: not stealth but the *theory of divine ignorance*. Cf. `[11:5]`: "They wrap their chests to hide from Him. But even as they cover themselves with their clothes, He knows what they conceal and what they reveal."
* **`[41:23]`** "It is that thought of yours about your Lord that led you to ruin—so you became of the losers."
  * **Gloss:** The *zann* verdict: a wrong opinion about God is itself the destructive act (cf. `[48:12]` "you harbored evil thoughts"; `[57:14]` "doubted, and became deluded by wishful thinking"). Theology has outcomes; "the losers" is 41:25's closing word and 2:166's ledger-term.
* **`[41:24]`** "If they endure patiently, the Fire will be their residence; and if they make up excuses, they will not be pardoned."
  * **Gloss:** Patience past its deadline: endurance now only changes the address, not the status — the mirror image of the corpus's patience-victories (`[40:55]` class; cf. the damned agreeing at `[14:21]` "whether we mourn, or are patient; there is no asylum for us"). Excuses are speech without purchase (Source: agent observation on the ruku's speech-economy).
* **`[41:25]`** "We had assigned companions for them, who glamorized to them what was in front of them, and what was behind them. And the Word proved true against them in communities of jinn and humans that have passed away before them. They were losers."
  * **Gloss:** The verdict's backstory: *qarīn*s assigned (cf. `[43:36]` "We assign for him a devil, to be his companion"; `[6:129]` "We make some of the wrongdoers befriend one another"), glamorizing front and back — the same totality phrase as the messengers' address at 41:14, run in reverse. "The Word proved true" (*waqaʿa al-qawl*) — the sentence executed like the twin at `[40:6]` "the sentence of your Lord became realized" and `[20:129]` "a word that issued from your Lord"; jinn-and-human communities (cf. the submitting jinn of `[72:14]`) show the courtroom spans both species.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`shahida` (testify)**: the forensic verb of the Day.
  * **Rendering:** organs "will testify against them" (41:20, 22); the same verb for tongues/hands/feet at `[24:24]`, feet at `[36:65]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** the arc completes later in this surah — `[41:47]` "none of us is a witness" and `[41:53]` "your Lord is witness over everything": created witnesses end in the uncreated Witness (Source: agent observation on structure).
* **`anṭaq / nāṭiq` (made speak)**: "Allah, Who made all things speak (anṭaq), made us speak."
  * **Rendering:** corpus-rarest phrasing for speech-gift as eschatological evidence; cf. `[36:65]` "their hands will speak to Us."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[41:11]` — the sky and earth already "spoke" ("We come willingly"); this edition lets creation answer twice, before and at the Day — kalam as the surah's hidden grammar (Source: agent observation).
* **`qarīn` (companion)**: the assigned glamorizer.
  * **Rendering:** "We had assigned companions for them, who glamorized…" (41:25).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[43:36]` the devil-assigned-to-the-blind "to be his companion"; `[14:22]` Satan's closing speech ("I called you, and you answered me") gives the companions' Day-of-repudiation lines; the 41:29 demand to "show us those who led us astray" is the same cast (ruku 4).
* **`waqaʿa al-qawl` (the Word proved true)**: the divine sentence becoming fact.
  * **Rendering:** "the Word proved true against them" (41:25) — capitalized Word, this edition's revelation-register.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[40:6]` "the sentence of your Lord became realized" (adjacent surah, same idiom, different rendering); `[20:129]` "a word that issued from your Lord… there is an appointed term" — promise-grammar standing where covenant-language is absent in these Meccan surahs.
