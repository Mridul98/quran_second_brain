# The Hour's knowledge reserved, the human weathervane, and proofs on the horizons (41:45–54)
**UID:** 202610142007
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 41:45–54 (Ruku 6 of 6 in Fussilat)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[41:45]` – `[41:54]` (10 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The closing ruku widens to the last things: this Quran's dispute is the pattern of Moses's Book (45); judgment is individual and God non-unjust (46); the Hour's knowledge — and every conception, every sprouting — reserved to Him alone (47), while the invoked allies vanish (48); man's despair-then-boast weather exposes the gratitude failure (49–51); and the surah signs off with its epistemic promise: the proofs will be shown "on the horizons, and in their very souls, until it becomes clear to them that it is the truth" (53) — for the One who is witness over all and comprehends all (53–54).
* **Placement:** Ruku 6 of 6; the final bracket. 41:54's total comprehension annuls 41:22's fantasy of divine unawareness; 41:47's "none of us is a witness" completes the witness-arc opened by the testifying skins; 41:50's "I do not think that the Hour is coming" is the lay verdict set against 41:47's reserved one.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[41:45]`** "We gave Moses the Book, but disputes arose concerning it. Were it not for a prior decree from your Lord, judgment would have been pronounced between them. But they are in perplexing doubt concerning it."
  * **Gloss:** The precedent: even Moses's Book met dispute — reception, not revelation, is the battlefield (cf. `[3:19]` "differed only after knowledge came"). Verbatim-twin formula: `[10:19]` "Were it not for a prior decree from your Lord, the matters over which they had disputed would have been settled"; near-twins `[42:14]` ("a predetermined decision… grave doubt") and `[20:129]` ("a word that issued from your Lord").
* **`[41:46]`** "Whoever acts righteously does so for himself; and whoever works evil does so against himself. Your Lord is not unjust to the servants."
  * **Gloss:** Individual ownership of deeds; the non-injustice clause is near-verbatim divine self-declaration: `[50:29]` "I am not unjust to the servants" (cf. `[3:182]` "Allah is not unjust towards the creatures"). Answers 41:27's full-value recompense: severity without wrongdoing.
* **`[41:47]`** "To Him is referred the knowledge of the Hour. No fruit emerges from its sheath, and no female conceives or delivers, except with His knowledge. And on the Day when He calls out to them, “Where are My associates?” They will say, “We admit to you, none of us is a witness.”"
  * **Gloss:** Ledger-item confirmed: the Hour-knowledge reservation IS here, at 41:47 — "To Him is referred the knowledge of the Hour" (near-verbatim twin `[31:34]` "With Allah rests the knowledge of the Hour"); it is NOT 43:61, which reads "He is a portent of the Hour." The micro-omniscience pair (sheath, womb) has its twin at `[35:11]` ("No female conceives, or delivers, except with His knowledge"); the associates-interrogation echoes verbatim-in-kind `[28:62]` ("Where are My associates whom you used to claim") and `[18:52]`; and the answer — "none of us is a witness" — is the Day stripped of all testimony except His (arc from 41:20–22; cf. `[25:2]` "no partner in His kingship").
* **`[41:48]`** "What they used to pray to before will forsake them, and they will realize that they have no escape."
  * **Gloss:** The invoked ones go silent (cf. `[46:5]` "who invokes, besides Allah, those who will not answer him"; `[17:56]` "they have no power to relieve your adversity"; `[25:3]` the gods "create nothing, but are themselves created"). "No escape" rhymes 41:24's no-pardon and `[14:21]` "there is no asylum for us."
* **`[41:49]`** "The human being never tires of praying for good things; but when adversity afflicts him, he despairs and loses hope."
  * **Gloss:** The weather-vane anthropology begins: du'a without pause when well, despair when touched — twins `[17:11]` ("prays for evil as he prays for good… very hasty") and `[11:9]` ("he becomes despairing and ungrateful"; cf. `[70:20–21]` fretful/ungenerous).
* **`[41:50]`** "And when We let him taste a mercy from Us, after the adversity that had afflicted him, he will say, “This is mine, and I do not think that the Hour is coming; and even if I am returned to my Lord, I will have the very best with Him.” We will inform those who disbelieve of what they did, and We will make them taste an awful punishment."
  * **Gloss:** The mercy claimed as property — "This is mine" — and the Hour discounted, and even return spun into entitlement: three failures of the gratitude audit (cf. `[11:10]` "Troubles have gone away from me… excited and proud"). His private "I do not think" collides head-on with 41:47's reserved knowledge (Source: agent observation). The kalam echo: he too makes a "word" about his Lord — 41:23's ruinous thought in everyday dress.
* **`[41:51]`** "When We provide comfort for the human being, he withdraws and distances himself; but when adversity befalls him, he starts lengthy prayers."
  * **Gloss:** The reversal of posture: at ease, turning away; in pain, long du'a — prayer as weathervane, not relationship (the same man, twin-verse to 49; cf. `[11:10]`-pair again). The edition renders the "lengthy/broad" invocation-word as "lengthy prayers" (Source: agent Arabic identification — verify).
* **`[41:52]`** "Say, “Have you considered? If it is from Allah and you reject it—who is further astray than he who is cutoff and alienated?”"
  * **Gloss:** The surah's own "have-you-considered" — without the creation-challenge: the fuller taunt ("show me which portion of the earth they have created") is `[46:4]`, the invoke-fragment is `[46:5]` — ledger-correction kept at the index. "Cutoff and alienated" renders the far-split state (*shiqāq mutabāʿid* — "alienation far-gone"); the distant-root *b-'-d* links it verbatim-wise to 41:44's "called from a distant place"; "further astray" kin of `[7:179]` (cattle… "In fact, they are further astray") — corpus-unique phrasing for the state itself here (rg-verified).
* **`[41:53]`** "We will show them Our proofs on the horizons, and in their very souls, until it becomes clear to them that it is the truth. Is it not sufficient that your Lord is witness over everything?"
  * **Gloss:** The signature epistemic verse — ledger: rendered "Our proofs" (*āyāt*), not "signs" in this edition, though the surah's earlier āyah-lines ("And of His signs are…" 41:37, 41:39) prove the referent. The two laboratories, cosmos and self, are the twin of `[51:20–21]` ("on earth are signs for the convinced. And within yourselves. Do you not see?"); the verdict-verb is clarity, arriving "until it becomes clear" — the same evidence-horizon as the guidance note's road, here promised as slow revelation-in-experience (honest reading: Source: agent inference — verify). The second half crowns the witness-arc: `[4:166]` "Allah is a sufficient witness," `[5:117]` "You are Witness over everything," `[6:19]`.
* **`[41:54]`** "Surely they are in doubt about the encounter with their Lord. Surely He comprehends everything."
  * **Gloss:** The last two beats: doubt about the *liqāʾ* — "the encounter with their Lord" (cf. `[10:45]` "Those who denied the meeting with Allah will be the losers") — and the surah's final word: *aḥāṭa* — "He comprehends everything" (twins `[65:12]` "Allah Encompasses everything in knowledge," `[72:28]` "has tallied everything by number"). Where 41:22 imagined much hidden, the book closes: nothing is.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **ʿilm al-sāʿa (the knowledge of the Hour)**: reserved data.
  * **Rendering:** "To Him is referred the knowledge of the Hour" (41:47) — the referral verb (*yuraddu*) makes it a returning deposit.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[31:34]` "With Allah rests the knowledge of the Hour" (near-verbatim); `[43:61]` "He is a portent of the Hour" (the task's rival locum — different construction); 41:50's "I do not think that the Hour is coming" is the lay counter-claim.
* **āfāq/anfus (the horizons and the selves)**: the two evidence-fields.
  * **Rendering:** "on the horizons, and in their very souls" (41:53) — plural "horizons" corpus-unique (rg-verified; singular at `[53:7]`, `[81:23]`).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[51:20–21]` — earth-signs and self-signs, the shorter twin; the surah has been stocking the fields since 41:37 (lights) and 41:39 (land).
* **shahīd / witness**: the arc's end-term.
  * **Rendering:** "your Lord is witness over everything" (41:53); the defeated admit "none of us is a witness" (41:47).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[4:166]` "a sufficient witness"; `[5:117]` "You are Witness over everything"; `[6:19]` "Allah is Witness between you and me" — organs testified (41:20); partners default (41:47); only He remains sufficient (41:53).
* **aḥāṭa (comprehends/encompasses)**: total cognitive grasp.
  * **Rendering:** "Surely He comprehends everything" (41:54).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[65:12]` "Encompasses everything in knowledge"; `[72:28]` "encompasses… tallied everything by number"; the mercy-knowledge encompassing of `[40:7]` ("You have encompassed everything in mercy and knowledge") — same verb, the Ha-Meem family's closing note.
