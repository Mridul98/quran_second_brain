# Ha, Meem: the detailed Arabic Scripture and the screened hearts (41:1–8)
**UID:** 202610142002
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 41:1–8 (Ruku 1 of 6 in Fussilat)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[41:1]` – `[41:8]` (8 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The letters Ha, Meem hand the Book over to its senders-of-mercy and four properties — revealed, detailed, Arabic, addressed to knowing people — and the ruku instantly stages both reactions to it: the total refusal of the screened hearts (5) versus the messenger's bare human creed (6), sorted at the close into alms-withholders and workers, "a reward uninterrupted."
* **Placement:** Ruku 1 of 6; the surah's self-declaration. Every later ruku answers one of its terms: the cosmos that DID listen (41:11), the limbs that testify (41:20–22), the command to listen when recited (41:26 ↔ 7:204), the "heaviness in their ears" verdict (41:44). The mercy-names of 41:2 bracket the ruku-block at 41:32 and the name-pair at 41:43.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[41:1]`** "Ha, Meem."
  * **Gloss:** Muqatta'at letters, transliterated and untranslated, numbered as ayah 1 — head of the Ha-Meem family, all seven openings (40–46) rg-verified. The edition's refusal to gloss them is itself a stance (see Fleeting link).
* **`[41:2]`** "A revelation from the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful."
  * **Gloss:** The Book-claim the letters front: descent ("a revelation") from the two mercy-names. Ledger: the task's "bestowal … from the Most Merciful, the Compassionate" is not this edition's wording; the pair here is "the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful."
* **`[41:3]`** "A Scripture whose Verses are detailed, a Quran in Arabic for people who know."
  * **Gloss:** The surah's name-verse: *fussilat* — verses made distinct, spelled out; language and audience follow ("in Arabic," "for people who know" — cf. `[39:9]` "Are those who know and those who do not know equal?"). The kalam claim is epistemic from the start: this speech is meant to be understood.
* **`[41:4]`** "Bringing good news, and giving warnings. But most of them turn away, so they do not listen."
  * **Gloss:** The Scripture's two functions — and the first failure: "they do not listen." Verbatim formula-twin of `[2:119]` ("bringing good news, and giving warnings"); the refusal here becomes the organized strategy at `[41:26]` and the verdict at `[41:44]`.
* **`[41:5]`** "And they say, “Our hearts are screened from what you call us to, and in our ears is deafness, and between us and you is a barrier. So do what you want, and so will we.”"
  * **Gloss:** The three-level excuse — heart screened, ear deaf, a barrier between; then the shrug of parity, "So do what you want, and so will we." Echoes `[2:7]` (seal on hearts and hearing, a veil over vision) and `[17:45]` ("We place between you and those who do not believe in the Hereafter an invisible barrier" at the recitation). The very organs pleading disability will be conscripted as witnesses in ruku 3.
* **`[41:6]`** "Say, “I am only a human like you; it is inspired in me that your God is One God. So be upright towards Him, and seek forgiveness from Him.” And woe to the idolaters."
  * **Gloss:** The answer is not spectacle but species-equality plus one inspired sentence: your God is One God (twins `[2:163]`, `[37:4]`; near-verbatim twin `[18:110]`). Two commands follow — be upright (s-t-q-m: answered inside the surah at `[41:30]` "and then go straight"; cf. `[11:112]`) and seek forgiveness (`[11:3]`, `[71:10]`). The "woe to the idolaters" interjection is the prophetic warning-register itself (cf. `[14:2]` "woe to the disbelievers," `[2:79]` twofold woe).
* **`[41:7]`** "Those who do not pay the alms; and regarding the Hereafter, they are disbelievers."
  * **Gloss:** The idolaters' two markers: withheld zakat and denial of the Hereafter — the same paired defaults the Fire-dwellers confess at `[74:43–46]` ("we were not of those who prayed… nor did we feed the destitute… we used to deny the Day of Judgment"). Note: zakat here, not infaq-vs-riba economics (series ledger).
* **`[41:8]`** "As for those who believe and do righteous deeds—for them is a reward uninterrupted."
  * **Gloss:** The sorting closes: the ubiquitous formula "believe and do righteous deeds" (`[2:25]`'s glad-tidings twin) meets "a reward uninterrupted" — the mimn (unbroken bounty) rendering, corpus-unique in this form (rg-verified).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`Ha, Meem`**: transliterated letter-names, non-semantic.
  * **Rendering:** "Ha, Meem." exactly, as ayah 1; family signature of surahs 40–46 (rg-verified at all seven openings; `[42:2]` adds "Ayn, Seen, Qaf").
  * **Elsewhere:** `[46:2]` — the same letters fronted with the Book-claim structure ("The sending down of the Scripture is from Allah, the Honorable, the Wise"), the strongest evidence the letters work as a family header (twin of 41:2's send-off).
* **`fussilat / detailed`** (f-ṣ-l): made distinct, elaborated, spelled out — the surah's title-verb.
  * **Rendering:** "whose Verses are detailed" (41:3); same root re-rendered "made clear" in the complaint at 41:44 ("If only its verses were made clear") — the name-verse quoted back by the objectors.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[11:1]` "whose Verses were perfected, then elaborated"; `[12:111]` "a detailed explanation of all things"; `[10:37]` "an elaboration of the Book" — f-ṣ-l is a revelation-property across the corpus, always about verse-distinctness, never vague inspiration.
* **`bishāra wa nidhār`**: good news and warnings — the Scripture's dual function.
  * **Rendering:** "Bringing good news, and giving warnings." (41:4).
  * **Elsewhere:** verbatim twin `[2:119]`; the glad-tidings verb then governs 41:8's reward-line and `[10:64]` "For them is good news in this life, and in the Hereafter."
* **`zakāt` (the alms)**: the purifying due.
  * **Rendering:** "do not pay the alms" (41:7); the set phrase elsewhere is "give alms" (`[2:110]`) — with drift to "charity" at `[19:31]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** the withholding pairs with Hereafter-denial exactly as the Saqar confession-pair at `[74:43–46]`, sharpening 41:7 from catalog to indictment.
