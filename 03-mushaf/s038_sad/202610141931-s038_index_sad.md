## 4. Structural Arc
* **Oath and refusal (1–14):** letter → Book-oath → arrogance/defiance → the wonder at a warner "from among them" → the notables' plan → the ownership reductio (treasuries? sovereignty? "then let them ascend the ropes") → the defeated army → the roll-call (Noah, Aad, Pharaoh of the Stakes, Thamood, Lot, the Woods).
* **David (15–26):** the scream no one recovers from; the mockers' demand to hasten the writ before the Day of Account; "Be patient… and mention Our servant David" — the doxology (mountains, birds, kingdom, decisive speech), the two disputants, the ewes, the self-judgment, the kneeling, the forgiveness, the vicegerent-commission to judge with justice and not follow desire.
* **Cosmos and Solomon (27–40):** creation not created "in vain"; the pious/shameless equation refused; the blessed Book as the pondering ground; Solomon — "an excellent servant," the horses, the body on his throne, the unrepeatable kingdom, wind and demons "without account," nearness and beautiful resort.
* **Job and the patriarchs (41–48):** the afflicted cry answered with cool water and doubled family; "We found him patient"; Abraham, Isaac, Jacob "endowed with ability and vision… the remembrance of the Home"; Ishmael, Elisha, Ezekiel — "the chosen, the outstanding."
* **The two returns (49–64):** "This is a reminder. The devout will have a good place of return" — Eden's opened doors, reclining, modest-gaze attendants — pivoting twice on "All This": the transgressors' "miserable return," roasting, "boiling and bitter cold," no-welcome crowds, the double-torment demand, the missing mockees' poem, "the feuding of the people of the Fire."
* **The great message (65–88):** the warner's shahada ("the One, the Conqueror"), the message of great importance they turn away from, the dispute of the Highest Assembly the Prophet is not given to know — then the Assembly's subject enacted: clay, spirit, the prostration command, Satan's fire-vs-clay argument, the curse, the respite "Until the Day of the Time Appointed," "By Your majesty, I will seduce them all — Except for your loyal servants among them," the filling of Hell, and the closing: no wage, no pretense, a reminder — "And you will know its message after a while."
