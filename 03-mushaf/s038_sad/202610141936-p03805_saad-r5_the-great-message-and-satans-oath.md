# The great message: Adam's clay, Satan's oath, and the fee-free reminder (38:65–88)
**UID:** 202610141936
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 38:65–88 (Ruku 5 of 5 in Saad)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[38:65]` – `[38:88]` (24 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah's closing docket: the warner states his fee-less credentials and the shahada ("the One, the Conqueror," 65–66), names the "message of great importance" the audience is exhaling (67–68), and disclaims knowledge of the Highest Assembly's dispute (69–70) — then the Assembly's subject is staged in full: clay, divine breath, the prostration command, Satan's fire-versus-clay argument, the curse and the respite, the oath "By Your majesty, I will seduce them all — Except for your loyal servants among them" (82–83), and God's counter-declaration to fill Hell (84–85), before the last three lines re-assert: no wage, no pretense, a reminder — "And you will know its message after a while" (86–88).
* **Placement:** Ruku 5 of 5; the surah's bracket. 38:65–66 answer 38:10's ownership question (sovereignty named), 38:69's upper "Assembly" mirrors 38:6's lower notables, and 38:88's deferred knowing answers 38:8's "not yet tasted My punishment."

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[38:65]`–`[38:66]`** "Say, "I am only a warner, and there is no god except Allah—the One, the Conqueror. / The Lord of the heavens and the earth, and everything between them; the Mighty, the Forgiver.""
  * **Gloss:** Office and creed in one breath: "only a warner" (cf. `[7:188]`, `[13:7]`) plus the shahada — "the One, the Conqueror" (al-Wahid al-Qahhar; cf. `[13:16]`/`[40:16]` "the One, the Irresistible"); the Lordship clause re-states 38:10's question as an answer, pairing "the Mighty, the Forgiver" before the respite scene proves both.
* **`[38:67]`–`[38:68]`** "Say, "It is a message of great importance. / From which you are turning away."
  * **Gloss:** The naba' adhīm — the same news as `[78:2]` "About the Great Event"; their posture (i'rad, "turning away") is the surah's recurring refusal verb — the quotation runs unclosed through 38:69–70.
* **`[38:69]`–`[38:70]`** "I have no knowledge of the Highest Assembly as they dispute. / It is only revealed to me that I am a clear warner.""
  * **Gloss:** The unseen-bracket: the Prophet is not given the argument of the mala' al-a'la; kalam discipline — revelation's extent is credential only. The surah's two malas rhyme: the notables "announced" below (38:6) and the Assembly disputes above (38:69) (Source: agent observation — verify).
* **`[38:71]`–`[38:72]`** "Your Lord said to the angels, "I am creating a human being from clay. / When I have formed him, and breathed into him of My spirit, fall prostrate before him."
  * **Gloss:** What the Assembly disputes about, now narrated: near-verbatim twin `[15:28]` "I am creating a human being from clay, from molded mud" and `[15:29]`'s breath-and-prostrate command; cf. `[32:9]` "breathed into him of His Spirit" (documented at [p03201](../s032_as-sajdah/202610141832-p03201_as-sajdah-r1_the-book-the-clay-and-the-spirit.md)). (Quote opened at 38:71, never closed — extraction artifact.)
* **`[38:73]`–`[38:74]`** "So the angels fell prostrate, all of them. / Except for Satan. He was too proud, and one of the faithless."
  * **Gloss:** Total obedience and the one exception: this edition names him "Satan" (as `[2:34]`, `[18:50]`; the name Iblis is absent from s038), and the exception is twice described — "too proud" (the arrogance of 38:2 personified) and "one of the faithless" (`[2:34]` renders the same clause "one of the disbelievers").
* **`[38:75]`** "He said, "O Satan, what prevented you from prostrating before what I created with My Own hands? Are you too proud, or were you one of the exalted?""
  * **Gloss:** The divine hands claim — the corpus's only creation "with My Own hands" (cf. `[5:64]` "His hands are outstretched"); the question offers two readings of the refusal (pride vs. claiming rank), and the answer takes none of them.
* **`[38:76]`** "He said, "I am better than he; You created me from fire, and You created him from clay.""
  * **Gloss:** The argument is elemental — verbatim logic of `[7:12]` (where this edition has "from mud," vs. "from clay" here): comparison of substances instead of obedience to the speaker.
* **`[38:77]`–`[38:78]`** "He said, "Then get out of here! You are an outcast! / And My curse will be upon you until the Day of Judgment.""
  * **Gloss:** Eviction and curse, term-limited "until the Day of Judgment" — the very Day the mockers tried to skip (38:16).
* **`[38:79]`–`[38:81]`** "He said, "Lord, defer me until the Day they are resurrected." / He said, "You are one of those deferred. / Until the Day of the Time Appointed.""
  * **Gloss:** The respite granted on God's terms — the same mawqūt formula as `[15:38]` "Until the Day of the time appointed"; Satan asks for resurrection, is answered with an appointed time he will not see arrive.
* **`[38:82]`–`[38:83]`** "He said, "By Your majesty, I will seduce them all. / Except for your loyal servants among them.""
  * **Gloss:** The oath and the exception-list: the izza ("majesty") sworn by is the same noun Pharaoh's people swear by at `[26:44]` "By the majesty of Pharaoh"; the exception clause re-appears verbatim-family at `[15:40]` "Except for Your sincere servants among them," `[37:74]` "Except for the sincere servants of Allah," `[12:24]` "one of Our loyal servants" — the whole surah's anthropology folds into mukhlās: those God has exempted from seduction (Source: agent observation on renderings).
* **`[38:84]`–`[38:85]`** "He said, "The truth is, and I say the truth. / I will fill Hell with you, and with every one of them who follows you.""
  * **Gloss:** God's counter-declaration — truth twice stated, then the filling of Hell: the verbatim-family of `[32:13]` and `[11:119]` "I will fill Hell with jinn and humans, altogether" (the as-Sajdah twin documented at [p03202](../s032_as-sajdah/202610141833-p03202_as-sajdah-r2_heads-bowed-and-sides-from-beds.md)).
* **`[38:86]`–`[38:87]`** "Say, "I ask of you no wage for this, and I am not a pretender. / It is but a reminder to mankind."
  * **Gloss:** The credentials close the bracket: zero fee and zero imposture — the twin line at `[6:90]` "Say, 'I ask of you no compensation for it; it is just a reminder for all mankind'" and `[12:104]`; "pretender" (the corpus's only instance, rg-verified) denies the mercenary-prophet charge of 38:4's "lying magician."
* **`[38:88]`** "And you will know its message after a while.""
  * **Gloss:** The surah ends on deferred knowledge, not on the punishment-line the task proposed (correction in the index): "after a while" (ruwayda — cf. `[37:174, 178]` "for a while"; `[2:36]` "enjoyment for a while") — the same grammar as 38:8's "they have not yet tasted My punishment."

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`the One, the Conqueror` (38:65)**: al-Wahid al-Qahhar.
  * **Rendering:** "the One, the Conqueror" — "Conqueror" is used only here and at `[6:61]` "He is the Conqueror over His servants."
  * **Elsewhere:** the same divine name is "the Irresistible" at `[13:16]`, `[14:48]`, `[40:16]` — 40:16 stages the Day's sovereignty-claim ("To whom does the sovereignty belong today?") that 38:66 answers with Lordship.
* **`Highest Assembly` (38:69)**: al-mala' al-a'la.
  * **Rendering:** "the Highest Assembly as they dispute" — subject left unspecified in the corpus.
  * **Elsewhere:** the lower mala' is "the notables among them" at `[38:6]`; the Day counterpart is `[42:7]` "the Day of Assembly" — the surah's own above/below rhyme is the best internal gloss (Source: agent observation — verify).
* **`majesty` / `loyal` (38:82–83)**: the oath by izza and the exempt servants (mukhlasun).
  * **Rendering:** "By Your majesty" (divine) vs. "By the majesty of Pharaoh" (`[26:44]`, created); "your loyal servants among them."
  * **Elsewhere:** the same exception lemma renders as "sincere servants" at `[15:40]` and `[37:74]`, "Our loyal servants" at `[12:24]` — a translation-family, not two categories.
* **`after a while` (38:88)**: ruwayda — deferred knowledge.
  * **Rendering:** "And you will know its message after a while."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[37:174]` "So disregard them for a while," `[37:178]` "So avoid them for a while," `[2:36]` "enjoyment for a while" — the same respite-grammar the surah has been narrating since the Day's mockers asked to hurry it (38:16).

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Meccan (Source: classical lists — verify); the closing credential-triad (no wage, no pretense, reminder) is the early-Meccan prophetic self-definition, twin to `[6:90]` and `[52:29]` "you are neither a soothsayer, nor a madman."
* **Asbab / narrative context:** what the Highest Assembly disputes about is unstated in the corpus; classically read as the angels' dispute over Adam (or over the revelation to a human) — Source: classical tafsir (Ibn Kathir / at-Tabari on 38:69) — verify. The "My Own hands" claim (38:75) is the corpus's only direct divine-hands creation statement (cf. `[5:64]`); the interpretive history of that language (hands = power, honor, or affix) is beyond this translation — Source: classical tafsir — verify.
