# Purify the Soul, or Bury It: Thamud's Hamstringing (91:1–15)
**UID:** 202610142312
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 91:1–15 (Ruku 1 of 1 in ash-Shams)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[91:1]` – `[91:15]` (15 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The cosmos is sworn upward — sun, moon, day, night, sky-and-Builder, earth-Spreader — until the oath lands on the soul "and He who proportioned it," the one creature given both "its wickedness and its righteousness" (91:7–8); from there the surah is a ledger of two verbs (purifies / corrupts, 91:9–10) with one exhibit: Thamud, which followed its most wicked, mocked the she-camel warning, hamstrung her, and was crushed-for-sin and leveled, while their Lord "does not fear its sequel" (91:11–15).
* **Placement:** Ruku 1 of 1; the batch's terminus. 86 swore the soul into being under a Protector; 89 addressed it home; 90 gave it a road; 91 gives it a verdict-word and shows a city that read the verdict backwards.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[91:1]`–`[91:4]`** "By the sun and its radiance. / And the moon as it follows it. / And the day as it reveals it. / And the night as it conceals it."
  * **Gloss:** Four celestial-clock oaths: emission (radiance — the *ḍuḥā*-word that becomes `[93:1]` "By the morning light," rg-verified), succession (moon "follows it"), manifestation (day "reveals it"), concealment (night "conceals it") — the trio "follows/reveals/conceals it" is corpus-unique to 91 (rg-verified), and the object "it" is left deliberately open (sun? all things? — classical glosses differ; *Source: transmitted — verify*). The reveal/conceal pair answers the batch's hidden/exposed axis (86:4–9).
* **`[91:2]`** "And the moon as it follows it."
  * **Gloss:** The second oath-term: lunar succession — the moon defined by following (talāhā), a dependency-relation built into the cosmos's timekeeping.
* **`[91:3]`** "And the day as it reveals it."
  * **Gloss:** The third oath-term: daylight as manifestation (jalāhā) — the same root-family as "clarity" (jaliyy), making revelation a solar act.
* **`[91:5]`–`[91:7]`** "And the sky and He who built it. / And the earth and He who spread it. / And the soul and He who proportioned it."
  * **Gloss:** Each oath is now doubled by its Maker: the built sky ("who built it" is corpus-unique as this phrase, rg-verified), the spread earth (the spreader-word shared with `[50:7]`, `[79:30]`, `[88:20]` — rg-verified), and the *proportioned* soul — *sawwā*/"proportioned" joins the soul-craft dossier `[75:38]` "He created and proportioned," `[32:9]` "Then He proportioned him, and breathed into him of His Spirit," `[82:7]` (rg-verified). Cosmos → earth → soul: the escalation is the argument.
* **`[91:6]`** "And the earth and He who spread it."
  * **Gloss:** The sixth oath-term: earth as spread surface (daḥāhā) — the spread-word shared with `[50:7]`, `[79:30]`, `[88:20]` (rg-verified), linking the oath to the batch's creation-survey vocabulary.
* **`[91:8]`** "And inspired it with its wickedness and its righteousness."
  * **Gloss:** The hinge: God *ilhām*-inspires the soul with both polarities — the fajr/taqwa pair rendered "its wickedness and its righteousness" is corpus-unique (rg-verified); inspiration elsewhere in the corpus goes outward (to Moses' mother `[28:7]`, to the bee `[16:68]`, to the earth itself on the Day `[99:5]`) — here it is inward endowment. This is the verse k-sabr's "taqwa names the steering" map needs and currently lacks.
* **`[91:9]`–`[91:10]`** "Successful is he who purifies it. / Failing is he who corrupts it."
  * **Gloss:** The verdict-couplet: *zakka* / *dassā* — "purifies" shared verbatim in formula with `[87:14]` "Successful is he who purifies himself" (sibling-surah twin, rg-verified), while "corrupts" (burying, defiling) is corpus-unique (rg-verified): the soul can be invested up or written down.
* **`[91:11]`–`[91:12]`** "Thamood denied in its pride. / When it followed its most wicked."
  * **Gloss:** The exhibit opens mid-sentence — a people ("it," grammatically the tribe) denying in pride and tailing "its most wicked" (*ashqāhā* — the wretched-superlative kin of `[87:11]` "the wretched," rg-verified drift); the she-camel dossier locates the crime-scene: `[7:73]` (sign + "do her no harm"), `[26:155]` (the shared water-turn), `[54:27]` ("so watch them and be patient" — a divine patience-command in the same story; classical reading addresses it to the Prophet — *Source: transmitted — verify*; a k-sabr site the batch keeps supplying).
* **`[91:13]`** "The messenger of Allah said to them, 'This is the she-camel of Allah, so let her drink.'"
  * **Gloss:** The warning in direct speech; "so let her drink" (*ruwayy*) is corpus-unique (rg-verified); the unnamed "messenger of Allah" is classically Saleh — `[7:73]` and `[11:64]` name him (rg-verified): the corpus lets 91 keep him anonymous and titles the camel "of Allah" twice — the creature as owned evidence.
* **`[91:14]`** "But they called him a liar, and hamstrung her. So their Lord crushed them for their sin, and leveled it."
  * **Gloss:** The doubling of the crime (lying to the messenger, killing his sign) answers the warning's two clauses; "hamstrung" is the dossier's fixed verb (`[7:77]`, `[11:65]` — rg-verified). The punishment mirrors the crime's geometry: crushing then leveling — "crushed" here joins the destruction-cluster of `[21:11]` ("How many a guilty town have We crushed"), the Thamud-adjacent `[54:31]` ("a single Scream, and they became like crushed hay"), `[89:21]` (sibling), and `[69:14]` (rg-verified English echoes; Arabic roots differ — agent inference, verify), and "leveled" ties to `[89:21]`, `[84:3]`, `[4:42]` (rg-verified). The crime-word: "for their sin" — the seized-for-sins cluster `[6:6]`, `[3:11]`, `[8:52]`, `[40:21]` (rg-verified).
* **`[91:15]`** "And He does not fear its sequel."
  * **Gloss:** The audacious period: the corpus's only line with God as the subject of "does not fear" (rg-verified — other negations of fear are commands to humans, e.g., `[20:46]` "Do not fear", or human claims, e.g., Abraham's `[6:80]` "I do not fear what you associate with Him", or denials about people, `[74:53]` "they do not fear the Hereafter"). "Sequel" (*uqba*) belongs to the outcome-word cluster of `[13:35]`, `[28:37]`, `[6:135]` ("the sequel of the abode"), where it is always *humans'* end — 91:15 alone attaches consequence to God only to negate it: what He does, He does not have to survive (Source: observable grammar of this edition's renderings).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`its radiance` (ḍuḥā)**: the sun's fore-light.
  * **Rendering:** "By the sun and its radiance" (91:1).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[93:1]` "By the morning light" (same word, next-but-one sibling surah, rg-verified) — the batch's two ḍuḥā oaths bracket the soul-question.
* **`inspired it` (ilhām)**: inward implanting.
  * **Rendering:** "And inspired it with its wickedness and its righteousness" (91:8).
  * **Elsewhere:** outward ilhām sites: `[28:7]` (Moses' mother), `[16:68]` (the bee), `[99:5]` (the earth reporting) (rg-verified) — 91:8 is the soul's own installation of the moral pair.
* **`purifies / corrupts` (zakka / dassā)**: the two verbs of soul-capital.
  * **Rendering:** 91:9–10; "corrupts" (also "buries/defiles") corpus-unique (rg-verified).
  * **Elsewhere:** formula-twin `[87:14]`; reward-twin `[20:76]` "the reward for him who purifies himself" (rg-verified).
* **`its most wicked` (ashqāhā)**: the wretched-superlative.
  * **Rendering:** "When it followed its most wicked" (91:12).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[87:11]` "the wretched will avoid it" — same root, batch-sibling; the shaqā' axis runs 87→91 (rg-verified).
* **`sequel` (uqba)**: after-effect, ultimate end.
  * **Rendering:** "And He does not fear its sequel" (91:15).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[13:35]`, `[28:37]`, `[6:135]` — the "sequel of the abode" for humans (rg-verified).
