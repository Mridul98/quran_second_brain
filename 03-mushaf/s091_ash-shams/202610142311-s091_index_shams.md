## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Meccan — agreed in the classical type-lists; the soul-polarity oath-chain and the Thamud case-file are the Meccan anthropological note at its tightest. *Source: classical type-lists — verify.* Revelation order: **transmitted nuzul tables — verify** (corpus states no nuzul; unnumbered Basmala heads the surah).
* **Size:** 15 ayahs, 1 ruku (ruku_map global 533 — verified). Terminal door of the 86–91 night-oath run: the batch that opened on the Piercing Star closes on the sun's radiance, and the soul 86:4 guarded, 89:27 addressed, and 90:4 created is here finally given its verdict-formula: purify or corrupt (91:9–10).
* **Title:** ash-Shams — "THE SUN" tracks `[91:1]` "By the sun and its radiance"; the *ḍuḥā*-word returns as the next sibling surah's oath, "By the morning light" `[93:1]` (rg-verified).
* **Central theme:** One soul, two verbs. Seven oath-clauses escalate cosmos → earth → soul, and 91:8 hands the soul its grammar: "And inspired it with its wickedness and its righteousness" (corpus-unique pairing, rg-verified). Success/failure then hangs entirely on what the person does with that soul (91:9–10), and the surah drops its exhibit: Thamud "denied in its pride," followed "its most wicked," heard the she-camel-warning — "This is the she-camel of Allah, so let her drink" — called the messenger a liar, hamstrung her; "So their Lord crushed them for their sin, and leveled it. And He does not fear its sequel" (91:11–15).
* **Reading hint:** the batch's moral-anatomy arc completes here: 90:10 showed a man two roads; 91:8 installs two inclinations; 91:9–10 make the road-choice identical with the soul-choice. Then 91:15's closing audacity — God fears no consequences — is the exact inverse of the 90:5 delusion ("Does he think that no one has power over him?"): the weak never fear outcomes; the All-Powerful is the only One who could and doesn't.

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Oath-chain (91:1–7):** sun/radiance, following moon, revealing day, concealing night, sky-and-Builder, earth-and-Spreader, soul-and-Proportioner — the first four doubled by their attendant phenomena, the last three by their Maker; classically counted as ten oaths (*Source: transmitted counting — verify*).
* **The inspiration clause (91:8):** the soul equipped with *fujūr* and *taqwā* — rendered "its wickedness and its righteousness," a corpus-unique pairing (rg-verified).
* **The verdict couplet (91:9–10):** "Successful is he who purifies it. / Failing is he who corrupts it." — the same success-formula as `[87:14]` with the self shifted to the soul (rg-verified).
* **The exhibit (91:11–14):** Thamud's denial-in-pride, the pursuit of "its most wicked" (shaqā'-kin of `[87:11]`, rg-verified), the she-camel warning, the lying-and-hamstringing, the crushing "for their sin," the leveling.
* **The audacious close (91:15):** "And He does not fear its sequel" — the corpus's only line with God as the subject of "does not fear" (rg-verified; other fear-negations are commands to humans — e.g., `[20:46]`, `[15:53]` — or human claims, e.g., Abraham's `[6:80]` "I do not fear what you associate with Him", and `[74:53]` "they do not fear the Hereafter"). (*Source: structural observation on corpus renderings.*)
