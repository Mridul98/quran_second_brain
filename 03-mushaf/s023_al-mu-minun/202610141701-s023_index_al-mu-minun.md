## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Meccan — per the classical surah lists; nuzul slot 23 (a few enumerations give 24), placing it in the middle-early Meccan cluster alongside al-Anbiya (21), whose parallel verses this surah repeatedly doubles. *Source: classical lists — verify.*
* **Size:** 118 ayahs, 6 ruku (`data/ruku_map.json` globals 295–300: 22+10+18+27+15+26 = 118 ✓). Mushaf position 23, between al-Hajj (22) and an-Nur (24). **Anomaly:** the an-Nur index (`202610141711-s024_index_an-nur.md`) calls this surah “az-Zumar (23)” twice — az-Zumar is 39; flagged here, left untouched per task rules.
* **Central theme:** The surah's project is *proof of the return*. It opens with the verdict (“Successful are the believers,” `[23:1]`), cashes it as a nine-line quality list (2–11), argues resurrection from the reader's own biography — clay→seed→clot→lump→bones→flesh→“another creature” (12–16) — then rehearses salvation history as a repeatable cycle (Noah→an unnamed generation→Moses→Mary's son, 23–50), cross-examines the audience until they concede earth, seven heavens and dominion to Allah (84–89), and closes at the scales where the opening verdict is re-issued and inverted (`[23:102]` ↔ `[23:1]`; `[23:117]` “The disbelievers will not succeed”).
* **Task-theme checks, verified against the corpus (correcting the task's assertions where needed):**
  * Believers' success-list 23:1–11 ✓ — verdict (1), qualities (2–9), inheritance (10–11).
  * Human creation stages 23:12–16 ✓ — and the ladder deliberately ends in death and resurrection (15–16).
  * Noah→Moses→Maryam cycles: the cycle begins at **23:23**, not 23:26 (23:26 is Noah's distress-prayer mid-cycle), and between Noah and Moses sits an *unnamed* generation-and-messenger episode (31–44) closed by the Blast; full arc 23:23–50, split across ruku 2–3.
  * “Seven heavens in layers”: **locate exactly** — 23:17 has “seven **pathways**” (a unique rendering of *sab‘a ṭara’iq*; classical readers take it as the seven heavens — *Source: consensus of classical tafsir — verify*); the words “seven heavens” themselves occur only at 23:86; the explicit “layers” language is elsewhere — 67:3 and 71:15, not in s023.
  * Resurrection-doubt refrain: located at **23:35–37** (first cycle, spoken against a messenger) and reprised at **23:82–83** (“dust and bones… legends of the ancients,” near-verbatim twin of 27:68).
  * “Who is Lord of the seven heavens… they will say Allah” ✓ at 23:86–87 — but it is the **middle** of a triple interrogation (84–89: earth, seven heavens + Throne, dominion of all things), not the close. The formula “They will say, ‘To Allah.’” is unique to this surah in the corpus (rg-verified).
  * Closing doxology: the task's “Glory be to Allah, Lord of the Throne” **does not close the surah as worded**. 23:116 reads “So Exalted is Allah, the Ruler, the Real… the Lord of the Noble Throne”; “Glory be to Allah, far beyond what they describe” is at 23:91; the phrase “glory be to Allah, Lord of the Throne” is 21:22. The surah's actual last line is a prayer: 23:118, echoing the mocked servants' own prayer at 23:109.

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Verdict → biography → inventory (1–22):** success is defined by qualities, then argued from the reader's own making; “Most Blessed is Allah, the Best of Creators” (14) is the hinge doxology.
* **The cycle runs twice (23–50):** Noah is rescued; the second (unnamed) generation is annihilated; Moses/Aaron and Mary's son flash past as further proofs — the pattern, not the chronology, is the point.
* **Address to the messengers, indictment of the sects (51–63):** “This nation of yours is one nation” (52) fractures immediately (53); wealth-and-children are re-read as trial, not endorsement (55–56).
* **Two rosters (57–62 vs 63–77):** the awe-struck who race to goodness; then the decadent who groan too late, mock the Word, and would rather it bent to their desires (66–71).
* **Court session (78–92):** senses audited (78), the refrain reprised (82–83), three questions asked, three times conceded — “To Allah” (84–89); the son-claim refuted (91).
* **The far side and the close (93–118):** four prayers teach the prophet's posture; then death, barrier, Horn, scales, the Fire's dialogue, the years-that-were-a-day, and the Throne doxology (116–118) — the surah ends where it began, on success and its absence.
