# One community torn into sects — the quaking hearts, the indulged, and the unpored Word (23:51–77)
**UID:** 202610141705
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 23:51–77 (Ruku 4 of 6 in al-Mu’minun)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[23:51]` – `[23:77]` (27 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Addressed first to the messengers — eat good, do right, your community is one — the sermon pivots to the fracture humanity actually produced (sects, each party pleased with what it has), then sets two rosters side by side: the awe-struck who give while their hearts quake and *race* toward goodness, and the indulged who read wealth-and-children as divine endorsement and never pondered the Word; the ruku closes by exposing their final objection — they want the truth to conform to their desires.
* **Placement:** Ruku 4 of 6; the surah's pivot from narrative (ruku 2–3) to direct address. It doubles al-Anbiya's 21:92 ("This community of yours is one community… so worship Me") — the same sermon in the surah's own Meccan twin — and its mockers (66–67) will be judged at the surah's end (110).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[23:51]`** "O messengers, eat of the good things, and act with integrity. I am aware of what you do."
  * **Gloss:** Plural address to the whole prophetic line just surveyed — piety starts at the table ("eat of the good things," the ṭayyibāt idiom of `[2:172]`).
* **`[23:52]`** "This nation of yours is one nation, and I am your Lord, so fear Me." **`[23:53]`** "But they tore themselves into sects; each party happy with what they have."
  * **Gloss:** One umma under one Lord — then the fracture; near-verbatim twin at `[21:92]` (closing imperative differs in this translation: "fear Me" vs "worship Me" — root difference needs print verification — *Source: agent inference — verify*); "each party happy with what they have" repeats `[30:32]`, with `[6:159]` as the severance clause.
* **`[23:54]`** "So leave them in their bewilderment until a time." **`[23:55]`** "Do they assume that, in furnishing them with wealth and children." **`[23:56]`** "We race to give them the good things? In fact, they have no idea."
  * **Gloss:** "Bewilderment" is unique to 23:54 in the corpus (rg-verified); the 55–56 sentence is split mid-clause across two ayahs [sic — book typography]; provision is *not* approval — the gradual-drawal reading (classically *istidrāj*) is stated at `[7:182]` ("We will gradually lead them from where they do not know") and `[68:44]`.
* **`[23:57]`** "Those who, from awe of their Lord, are fearful." **`[23:58]`** "And those who believe in their Lord’s Verses." **`[23:59]`** "And those who associate no partners with their Lord."
  * **Gloss:** Roster one, three lines: awe (wajl), belief in the Verses (the k-kalam object), and clean tawhid — each clause ends on "their Lord," a trained possessive.
* **`[23:60]`** "And those who give what they give, while their hearts quake, knowing that to their Lord they will return." **`[23:61]`** "It is they who race towards goodness. It is they who will reach it first."
  * **Gloss:** Giving audited by dread, not receipt — the intent-check the k-infraq note prices at 2:267/3:92; "race towards goodness" is verbatim the imperative of `[2:148]`, and cf. `[21:90]` "vie in doing righteous deeds."
* **`[23:62]`** "We never burden any soul beyond its capacity. And with Us is a record that tells the truth, and they will not be wronged."
  * **Gloss:** The capacity-law — identical formula at `[2:286]`, `[6:152]`, `[7:42]` — paired here with "a record that tells the truth" (unique wording; cf. `[35:11]`'s capitalized "Record").
* **`[23:63]`** "But their hearts are puzzled because of this; and they have deeds that do not conform to this, which they continue to perpetrate." **`[23:64]`** "Until, when We seize the decadent among them with torment, they begin to groan." **`[23:65]`** "Do not groan today. You will receive no help from Us."
  * **Gloss:** The counter-roster: hearts "puzzled" (unique word), deeds that don't match the claim — a mouth–deed split in Meccan key (the k-nifaq portrait's structure, though these are open mockers — *Source: agent inference — verify*); "groan" resonates with `[16:53]` ("it is to Him that you groan" — in trouble only).
* **`[23:66]`** "My Verses were recited to you, but you turned back on your heels." **`[23:67]`** "Arrogant towards it—talked nonsense about it—disregarded it."
  * **Gloss:** The recitation as the crime's trigger (k-kalam's addressed book); "turned back on your heels" is the apostasy idiom of `[2:143]`; "talked nonsense" answers the believers' "avoid nonsense" at 23:3 — same English word, opposite sides.
* **`[23:68]`** "Have they not pondered the Word? Or has there come to them what came not to their forefathers of old?" **`[23:69]`** "Or is it that they did not recognize their messenger, so they are denying him?" **`[23:70]`** "Or do they say, “He is possessed?” In fact, he brought them the truth, but most of them hate the truth."
  * **Gloss:** Four diagnostic questions: failure to ponder ("the Word" — the Quran calling itself *kalima*; cf. `[4:82]`, `[47:24]` "ponder the Quran", `[38:29]`), failure to recognize, and the "possessed" charge imported verbatim from the Noah script (23:25); verse 70's verdict: misology, not evidence.
* **`[23:71]`** "If the truth conformed to their desires, the heavens, the earth, and everyone in them would have gone to ruin. In fact, We have given them their message, but they keep avoiding their message."
  * **Gloss:** The truth is world-bearing — subjunctive cosmology as rebuttal; cf. `[21:22]`'s many-gods thought experiment and `[45:23]` "him who has taken his desire for his god."
* **`[23:72]`** "Or are you asking them for a payment? The revenue from your Lord is better, and He is the Best of providers." **`[23:73]`** "You are inviting them to a straight path." **`[23:74]`** "But those who do not believe in the Hereafter are swerving from the path."
  * **Gloss:** The fee-question (the same defense in 38:86's "no wage" wording); "revenue" is a commercial rendering of *rizq* here; the straight path — *ṣirāṭ* — the k-guidance road, swerved off by denial of the Hereafter (23:33's symptom returns).
* **`[23:75]`** "Even if We had mercy on them, and relieved their problems, they would still blindly persist in their defiance." **`[23:76]`** "We have already gripped them with suffering, but they did not surrender to their Lord, nor did they humble themselves." **`[23:77]`** "Until, when We have opened before them a gate of intense agony, at once they will despair."
  * **Gloss:** Mercy would not work — the pathology is incurable by relief; suffering already administered (the subtraction-test grammar, cf. `[2:155]`) failed to bend them; the "gate of intense agony" is not identified in the line (classically read as death or the Hereafter — *Source: agent inference — verify*); despair is their own arrival, not Allah's surprise.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`umma`** ("one nation," 23:52): humanity-under-messengers as one band — then *sects* (23:53).
  * **Rendering:** "nation… nation" for *umma*; "the sects" (*shiya‘*) same word at `[6:159]`, `[30:32]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[21:92]` twin verse; `[2:219]`-class *umma* uses drift to "community" (observable).
* **`wajl` / `khāshi’` ("awe," "quake," 57, 60):**
  * **Rendering:** "are fearful" / "hearts quake" — the quake-word is unique here (rg); cf. `[21:90]` "call on Us in love and awe, and… humble themselves."
  * **Elsewhere:** 23:109–111's mocked group is the future tense of this roster — the two lists are the same people under different times of day.
* **`ṣirāṭ` ("straight path," 73–74):**
  * **Rendering:** "a straight path… swerving from the path" — path kept singular through both clauses.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[1:6]` the petition; `[6:153]` "My path, straight"; the k-guidance note's road grammar.
* **`sāba` ("race," 56, 61):**
  * **Rendering:** one verb, two audiences — Allah "race[s] to give" the indulged good things (56, ironic), and the awed "race towards goodness" (61).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:148]` "Therefore, race towards goodness" — verbatim; `[21:90]` "vie in doing righteous deeds" — same image in al-Anbiya.
