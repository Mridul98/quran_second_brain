# The senses, the triple interrogation, and the refused son (23:78–92)
**UID:** 202610141706
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 23:78–92 (Ruku 5 of 6 in al-Mu’minun)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[23:78]` – `[23:92]` (15 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah turns prosecutor: it audits the audience's own equipment (hearing, eyesight, feelings — granted, and thanked "little"), lets them re-speak the dust-and-bones denial, then cross-examines them three times — the earth, the seven heavens and the Splendid Throne, the dominion of all things — and three times they answer "To Allah"; the ruku ends on the only rival-claim left standing and the only one that ruins cosmology: a son.
* **Placement:** Ruku 5 of 6; the argument's courtroom center. The task's "closing Who is Lord of the seven heavens" is located here (86–87) — mid-surah, inside a triple sequence (84–89), not the close (that is 116–118, ruku 6).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[23:78]`** "It is He who produced for you the hearing, and the eyesight, and the feelings. But little gratitude you show."
  * **Gloss:** The sense-faculty inventory ("af’ida" rendered "the feelings" — this edition prints "the brains" for the same triplet at `[16:78]`/`[32:9]` — observable term-choice), capped by the shukr verdict: *qalīlan* — "little."
* **`[23:79]`** "And it is He who multiplied you on earth, and to Him you will be gathered." **`[23:80]`** "And it is He who gives life and brings death, and to Him is the alternation of night and day. Do you not understand?"
  * **Gloss:** Population, gathering, life-and-death, the day-night switch (the `[2:164]` sign-idiom) — four sovereign verbs before the questions.
* **`[23:81]`** "But they say the like of what the ancients said." **`[23:82]`** "They say, “After we have died, and become dust and bones, will we be resurrected?" **`[23:83]`** "We were promised this before—we and our ancestors—these are nothing but legends of the ancients.”"
  * **Gloss:** **The resurrection-doubt refrain's second site**, now in the Quraysh's own present tense; 23:83 is a near-verbatim twin of `[27:68]`, and "legends of the ancients" recurs at `[16:24]`, `[83:13]`.
* **`[23:84]`** "Say, “To whom does the earth belong, and everyone in it, if you happen to know?”" **`[23:85]`** "They will say, “To Allah.” Say, “Will you not reflect?”"
  * **Gloss:** Interrogation one — ownership; the staged concession "They will say, ‘To Allah.’" is, rg-verified, a formula unique to this surah (parallel genre with a bare "Allah" answer at `[10:31]` and `[31:25]`).
* **`[23:86]`** "Say, “Who is the Lord of the seven heavens, and Lord of the Splendid Throne?”" **`[23:87]`** "They will say, “To Allah.” Say, “Will you not become righteous?”"
  * **Gloss:** Interrogation two — lordship of the layered sky and the Throne; "Splendid Throne" (*al-‘arsh al-‘aẓīm*) is this edition's one-off rendering — same Arabic as 27:26's "Sublime Throne," and cf. 85:15 "Glorious Throne," 23:116 "Noble Throne," 21:22 "Lord of the Throne"; the hortatory now asks for *taqwā* ("become righteous").
* **`[23:88]`** "Say, “In whose hand is the dominion of all things, and He protects and cannot be protected from, if you happen to know?”" **`[23:89]`** "They will say, “To Allah.” Say, “Then are you bewitched?”"
  * **Gloss:** Interrogation three — sovereignty plus the protection-paradox (cf. `[36:83]` "in whose hand is the dominion of everything", `[67:1]` "in whose hand is the sovereignty"); the verdict-word "bewitched" (*suḥirū*) is the charge-formula turned back on the accusers (cf. 23:25/69's "possessed," and `[17:47]`, `[26:185]`).
* **`[23:90]`** "In fact, We have given them the truth, and they are liars."
  * **Gloss:** The ledger of 23:38 ("making up lies about Allah") reversed: truth delivered, lie located.
* **`[23:91]`** "Allah has never begotten a son, nor is there any god besides Him. Otherwise, each god would have taken away what it has created, and some of them would have gained supremacy over others. Glory be to Allah, far beyond what they describe."
  * **Gloss:** The son-claim refuted with the plurality thought-experiment — each god absconding with its own creation, then conquest among them; the near-twin argument is `[21:22]`; cf. `[19:88]`–`[19:92]`, `[17:111]`, `[2:116]`, `[39:4]`; this is where the task's remembered "Glory be to Allah" actually lives (23:91, not the close).
* **`[23:92]`** "The Knower of the hidden and the manifest. He is exalted, far above what they associate."
  * **Gloss:** The closing divine name-pair (cf. `[5:116]` "You are the Knower of the hidden") — knowledge as the final attribute before the ruku's end; "what they associate" ties the son to the whole shirk dossier.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`af’ida`** ("the feelings," 23:78):
  * **Rendering:** hearing / eyesight / feelings — the third faculty here as "feelings."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[16:78]`, `[32:9]` — same triplet rendered "the brains" (observable translator drift, rg-verified; the classically debated seat of perception — *Source: agent inference — verify*).
* **`shukr` audit ("little gratitude you show," 23:78):**
  * **Rendering:** the ruku opens with gratitude's minus-sign — the k-shukr note's verdict form (cf. `[16:83]` "recognize Allah’s blessing, but then deny it").
  * **Elsewhere:** `[14:7]` increase-vs-punishment decree; the inventory-to-thanks sequence here mirrors ruku 1's (17–22).
* **`al-‘arsh`** ("the Splendid Throne," 23:86):
  * **Rendering:** one of five Throne epithets across the corpus — Splendid (23:86), Noble (23:116), Sublime (27:26), Glorious (85:15), unadorned Lord of the Throne (21:22); size at `[2:255]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** the 23:17 "seven pathways" now named as heavens — the ruku finishes what ruku 1 opened (*Source: observable structure; the pathways=heavens identity — classical consensus, verify*).
* **`s-ḥ-r` ("bewitched," 23:89):**
  * **Rendering:** "Then are you bewitched?" — the court's last question answered with a diagnosis.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[17:47]` "a man bewitched," `[26:153]`/`[26:185]`, `[15:15]`, `[17:101]` — the accusation genre's stock line, recycled onto its speakers.

## 4. Cross-References (The Web)
* **Within surah:** 23:82–83 ↔ 23:35–37 — the dust-and-bones refrain reprised a ruku later; 23:84–89 ↔ 23:17–22 — the inventory now interrogated: you already concede the Owner of all of it.

* **Son dossier:** `[19:88]`–`[19:92]` ("It is not fitting for the Most Merciful to have a son"), `[43:81]`-class — the k-isa and k-tawhid notes' shared exhibit.
