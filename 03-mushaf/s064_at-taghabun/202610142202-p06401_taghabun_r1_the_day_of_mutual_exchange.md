# Cosmic praise and the Day of Mutual Exchange (64:1–10)
**UID:** 202610142202
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 64:1–10 (Ruku 1 of 2 in at-Taghabun)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[64:1]` – `[64:10]` (10 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The whole cosmos praises a King who knows every heart; the record of destroyed unbelieving towns and a sworn resurrection-oath press the present audience to believe in Allah, His Messenger, and the sent-down Light — because the Day of Gathering is a Day of Mutual Exchange: integrity trades misdeeds for gardens, denial trades revelations for the Fire.
* **Placement:** Ruku 1 of 2; the doxological-summmons half. Its opening joins the five-surah praise cluster (57/59/61/62/64); its two verdicts (64:9–10) hand off to ruku 2's inward pivot at 64:11.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[64:1]`** "Everything in the heavens and the earth praises Allah. To Him belongs the Kingdom, and to Him all praise is due, and He is Able to do all things."
  * **Gloss:** Doxology before address — three ascriptions: universal praise, kingdom, and total capability. The clause "to Him all praise is due" makes this opening the only member of the five whose praise-formula is doubled by a hamd clause.
* **`[64:2]`** "It is He who created you. Some of you are unbelievers, and some of you are believers. And Allah perceives what you do."
  * **Gloss:** The Creator of "you" is named; the first sorting runs through the present audience, not through history — and "perceives" puts every act on record.
* **`[64:3]`** "He created the heavens and the earth with truth, and He designed you, and designed you well, and to Him is the final return."
  * **Gloss:** Creation "with truth" (bil-haqq), human design "well," and "the final return" — origin, craft, and destination in one line; 64:7's oath will enforce the third term.
* **`[64:4]`** "He knows everything in the heavens and the earth, and He knows what you conceal and what you reveal. And Allah knows what is within the hearts."
  * **Gloss:** Omniscience escalates three steps — cosmos, speech (concealed/revealed), hearts. The interior is not a refuge; this arms 64:14's warning about the nearest people.
* **`[64:5]`** "Has the news not reached you, of those who disbelieved before? They tasted the ill consequences of their conduct, and a painful torment awaits them."
  * **Gloss:** The rhetorical question opens the case file; "tasted" makes consequence sensory, and the verdict splits tense — tasted (past), awaits (future).
* **`[64:6]`** "That is because their messengers came to them with clear explanations, but they said, “Are human beings going to guide us?” So they disbelieved and turned away. But Allah is in no need. Allah is Independent and Praiseworthy."
  * **Gloss:** The crime is the refusal of the *mode* of guidance — human messengers. The double close "Allah is in no need. Allah is Independent and Praiseworthy" states the indifference of the rejected Sender.
* **`[64:7]`** "Those who disbelieve claim that they will not be resurrected. Say, “Yes indeed, by my Lord, you will be resurrected; then you will be informed of everything you did; and that is easy for Allah.”"
  * **Gloss:** Denial of resurrection is answered by a divine oath ("by my Lord"), then informed of everything — and the Hour is called "easy" — an answer to 64:3's "final return."
* **`[64:8]`** "So believe in Allah and His Messenger, and the Light which We sent down. Allah is Aware of everything you do."
  * **Gloss:** The command names three objects — Allah, Messenger, and "the Light which We sent down." What the Light is is not glossed in the text (classical reading: the Quran/revelation — *Source: classical — verify*); "Aware" (khabir) echoes 64:2, 64:4.
* **`[64:9]`** "The Day when He gathers you for the Day of Gathering—that is the Day of Mutual Exchange. Whoever believes in Allah and acts with integrity, He will remit his misdeeds, and will admit him into gardens beneath which rivers flow, to dwell therein forever. That is the supreme achievement."
  * **Gloss:** The name-word verse: "gathers you" → "the Day of Gathering" → "the Day of Mutual Exchange" (yawm at-taghabun, form VI = mutual exchange). The believer's wage in three strokes: misdeeds remitted, gardens, permanence — named "the supreme achievement."
* **`[64:10]`** "But as for those who disbelieve and denounce Our revelations—these are the inmates of the Fire, dwelling therein forever; and what a miserable fate!"
  * **Gloss:** The matching verdict in the same three-part shape — identity (denouncers), dwelling (inmates of the Fire), qualifier ("what a miserable fate!") — the exchange closes symmetrically.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`yusabbihu` (tasbih)**: declaring Allah above every associate.
  * **Rendering:** "praises" in 64:1 and 61:1; "Glorifying Allah is..." in 57:1 and 59:1; "glorifies" with the name-expansion "the Sovereign, the Holy" in 62:1.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[57:1]`, `[59:1]`, `[61:1]`, `[62:1]` — the four sister openings (all verbatim-checked); in-surah doxologies `[59:24]` and `[24:41]`, and `[17:44]` "Praising Him are the seven heavens, and the earth, and everyone in them."
* **`yawm at-taghabun`**: the surah's name-word, occurring once.
  * **Rendering:** "the Day of Mutual Exchange" — rg confirms "Mutual Exchange" is corpus-unique to `[64:9]`; the popular "Day of Mutual Loss and Gain" is a classical gloss, not this edition's text. *Source: translator's term choice (observable in corpus); gloss — classical, verify.*
  * **Elsewhere:** the gathering verb recurs at `[4:87]` "He will gather you to the Day of Resurrection, in which there is no doubt" — the sharpest outside gloss of the first label.
* **`al-Ghani, al-Hamid`**: "Allah is Independent and Praiseworthy" (64:6).
  * **Rendering:** the exact pair recurs at `[57:24]` "Allah is the Independent, the Praiseworthy"; `[35:15]` drifts to "the Rich, the Praiseworthy."
  * **Elsewhere:** 64:6 places it at the end of a rejection narrative — rejection costs Him nothing.
* **`al-Light al-munazzal`**: "the Light which We sent down" (64:8).
  * **Rendering:** capital-L Light as a revealed object sent down, paired with Messenger.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[5:15]` "A light from Allah has come to you"; `[57:28]` "...will give you a light by which you walk" — the sent-down light given a function.
