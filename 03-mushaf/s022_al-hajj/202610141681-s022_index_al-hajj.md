## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** CLASSICALLY DISPUTED — Meccan, Medinan, or mixed, depending on the list; do not force a single answer. Several transmitted lists call it a mixed surah: the opening earthquake passage (1–2ff) is read as Meccan, while the permission-to-fight pair (39–40) and the Hajj-law block (26–37) attach to Medina per transmitted reports; other lists count the whole surah Medinan. *Source: classical lists — verify in print (al-Itqan-type surah introductions).*
* **Order:** 103 per the Ibn-Abbas-derived enumeration table. *Source: transmitted nuzul-order tables (same convention as the s016/s013 indices) — verify.*
* **Size:** 78 ayahs, 10 ruku (`data/ruku_map.json`: 10+12+3+8+5+10+9+7+8+6 = 78 ✓). Mushaf position 22, between al-Anbiya (21) and al-Mu'minun (23).
* **Name:** al-Hajj — "The Pilgrimage"; the Hajj charter (26–37) is the surah's legal heart, but the book opens on the Hour's earthquake, and the name-word itself never occurs as a proper noun in the text (Itani renders the Hajj-root in 22:27 as "announce the pilgrimage").
* **Theme checks the task asked to verify honestly (against the corpus):** `[22:2]` earthquake scene ✓ — corpus wording: "every nursing mother will discard her infant" (the "distracted" phrasing belongs to other translations; not this edition's). `[22:11]` ✓ verbatim "worships Allah on edge." `[22:37]` ✓ verbatim "Neither their flesh, nor their blood, ever reaches Allah. What reaches Him is the righteousness from you" — the word "righteousness" here is *taqwa* (cf. 22:32 "the piety of the hearts"), so the sacrifice-vs-piety center genuinely belongs to the taqwa dossier; the shukr note's connection is real but secondary (22:36 "that you may be thankful"; 22:38 "ungrateful"; 22:66 "The human being is unappreciative"). `[22:39–40]` ✓ verbatim "Permission is given to those who are fought against." `[22:26–27]` ✓ Abrahamic charter; corpus of 22:27 says "on foot, and on every transport" — the task's "every lean camel" is not this edition's wording (classical readers take the mounts to be swift lean camels; *Source: classical tafsir — verify*). `[22:78]` ✓ "strive for Allah, with the striving due to Him... the faith of your father Abraham. It is he who named you Muslims before, and in this." The task's "whoever is grateful—only for his own soul" echo (22:??) does **not exist in this surah**: the self-profit clause of gratitude is `[31:12]` ("Whoever is appreciative—is appreciative for the benefit of his own soul"), and s022 has only the shukr-motifs above — recorded as a gap.

## 3. Structural Arc
* **The Hour and its evidence (1–22):** the earthquake summons "O people" to consciousness; the creation ladder (dust→infancy→age, dead earth→spring) proves the resurrection claim; three failed portraits answer — the ignorant arguer (3–4, 8–10), the edge-worshipper (11–13), the despairing schemer (15) — while creation itself prostrates (18) and the two adversaries get their verdicts (19–22).
* **The House and its charter (23–38):** the Garden's decorations (23–24) bracket the Sacred Mosque's equal right for all mankind (25); Abraham is shown the site, told to purify it and proclaim the Pilgrimage (26–29); sanctities and sacraments are venerated from piety of hearts (30–33); the cattle-rite culminates in the surah's theological center — flesh and blood reach no one; *taqwa* reaches Him (34–37); "Allah defends those who believe" (38) turns the page toward permission.
* **Permission and its grammar (39–48):** fight-back is granted to the evicted-for-their-faith (39–40), and the war's stated purpose is the protection of every house where God's name is remembered (40); the empowered community's platform is prayer, zakat, enjoining right, forbidding wrong (41); then the deniers' roster and the ruined towns (42–45), the blind-heart diagnosis (46), and the thousand-year time-scale against those who demand the threat now (47–48).
* **The message defended (49–57):** the messenger is only a plain warner (49–51); when Satan interjects in a prophet's recitation, Allah nullifies the interjection and affirms His revelations (52–54) — revelation's integrity clause; the doubters stay in hesitation until the Hour arrives suddenly (55–57).
* **Provision, patience, and the cosmos (58–72):** the emigrant killed or dying gets the Best of Providers' fine provision (58–59); retaliation is capped and the twice-wronged are assisted (60); night and day merge, water greens the earth, ships sail, the sky is held up (61–65) — while "the human being is unappreciative" (66); every congregation gets its rite, disputes are handed to God's judgment (67–70), and warrant-less gods draw only fire-threats (71–72).
* **The closing commission (73–78):** the fly parable demolishes the invoked-without-Allah (73–74); God chooses messengers from angels and from the people (75–76); the surah ends as a congregation-instruction: kneel, prostrate, do good, strive with the striving due to Him, wear the name "Muslims" from your father Abraham, pray, give zakat, cleave to Allah — "What an excellent Protector" (77–78).
