# Fine provision for the emigrants, capped retaliation, and the merging of night and day (22:58–64)
**UID:** 202610141689
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 22:58–64 (Ruku 8 of 10 in al-Hajj)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[22:58]` – `[22:64]` (7 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** After the sword-permission, the unit settles the accounts of violence: whoever emigrates for Allah and is killed or dies gets fine provision from the Best of Providers and an admittance that pleases (58–59); whoever retaliates in measure and is then wronged again is definitely assisted by a Pardoning God (60); and the ground of all of it is the cosmos itself — the God who merges night into day, Hearing and Seeing, Reality against vanity (61–62), who greens the earth with sky-water, is Kind and Aware (63), owns everything, and is the Rich, the Praised (64).
* **Placement:** Ruku 8; the consolation-and-law interlude between the revelation-defense (ruku 7) and the closing cosmic polemic (ruku 9–10).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[22:58]`** "Those who emigrate in Allah's cause, then get killed, or die, Allah will provide them with fine provisions. Allah is the Best of Providers."
  * **Gloss:** Emigration plus either martyrdom or natural death — the two exits are joined in one promise; "fine provisions" (husn al-rizq) answers the loss of home and life in the ruku 6 frame.
* **`[22:59]`** "He will admit them an admittance that will please them. Allah is Knowing and Clement."
  * **Gloss:** The entry itself (karda — a pleasing admission) is the reward's form; the closing names pair knowledge (of their sacrifice) and clemency.
* **`[22:60]`** "That is so! Whoever retaliates similarly to the affliction he was made to suffer, and then he is wronged again, Allah will definitely assist him. Allah is Pardoning and Forgiving."
  * **Gloss:** The retaliation law in one line: exact equivalence is the ceiling; if the other side escalates after your measured reply, assistance is promised — and God's own disposition toward the first wrong is pardon.
* **`[22:61]`** "That is because Allah merges the night into the day, and He merges the day into the night, and because Allah is Hearing and Seeing."
  * **Gloss:** The physics of the promise: the God who runs the alternating day-night clock has time and attention — "Hearing and Seeing" means no complaint of the wronged is lost.
* **`[22:62]`** "That is because Allah is the Reality, and what they invoke besides Him is vanity, and because Allah is the Sublime, the Grand."
  * **Gloss:** The ontological cut: He is al-haqq; the invoked-without-Him are al-batil; the names "Sublime, Grand" close the argument for why only He can assist.
* **`[22:63]`** "Do you not see that Allah sends down water from the sky, and the land becomes green? Allah is Kind and Aware."
  * **Gloss:** The visible sign — dead earth to green — restating 22:5's resurrection-proof in two breaths; latif and khabir: subtle and intimately aware.
* **`[22:64]`** "To Him belongs everything in the heavens and everything on earth. Allah is the Rich, the Praised."
  * **Gloss:** Total ownership as the unit's capstone, with the self-sufficient-and-praiseworthy pair — nothing reaches Him (cf. 22:37) because He already owns all, yet He is praised.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`muhajir / "emigrate in Allah's cause"`** `[22:58]`:
  * **Rendering:** "Those who emigrate in Allah's cause, then get killed, or die."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[4:100]` — anyone leaving home "emigrating to Allah and His Messenger" and overtaken by death has his compensation on Allah; `[3:169]` — the killed in Allah's cause are "alive, at their Lord, well provided for" (the same rizq root); `[2:154]` — "do not say of those who are killed in the cause of Allah, 'Dead.'"
* **`da'afa / "retaliate similarly"`** `[22:60]`:
  * **Rendering:** "Whoever retaliates similarly to the affliction he was made to suffer."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[16:126]` "If you were to retaliate, retaliate to the same degree as the injury done to you" — the measure-law twin; `[42:40]` "The repayment of a bad action is one equivalent to it" — and both note that pardon/patience outranks it (the pardoning cap is in 22:60 itself).
* **`yuliji / "merges"`** `[22:61]`:
  * **Rendering:** "Allah merges the night into the day, and He merges the day into the night."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[31:29]`, `[35:13]`, `[57:6]` — the identical verb-pair in three other surahs, always with the day-night alternation as proof of sovereignty; the idiom is stable across renderings.
* **`al-Ghani / al-Hamid ("the Rich, the Praised")`** `[22:64]`:
  * **Rendering:** "Allah is the Rich, the Praised."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[31:26]` — verbatim twin ("Allah is the Rich, the Praised"); `[35:15]` "Allah is the Rich, the Praiseworthy" (drift); cf. `[47:38]` "Allah is the Rich, while you are the needy" — the self-sufficiency clause behind 22:37's "nothing reaches Him."

## 4. Cross-References (The Web)
* **Parallel:** `[3:169]` + `[2:154]` — the martyred emigrants' alive-and-provided-for status is the doctrine 22:58 rewards with "fine provisions."
* **Parallel:** `[16:126]` and `[42:40]` — the equivalence ceiling of `[22:60]`; an-Nahl immediately adds "but if you resort to patience—it is better for the patient," the preference that 22:60 states as Allah's own "Pardoning and Forgiving."
* **Parallel:** `[2:255]` — "He is the Most High, the Great" beside `[22:62]`'s "Sublime, the Grand" — the same name-pair rendered two ways (observable drift).
* **Within surah:** `[22:58]` is the payoff of `[22:40]` (the evicted-for-their-confession now get the admittance); `[22:63]` restates `[22:5]`'s earth-vibration sign; `[22:62]` repeats `[22:6]`'s "Allah is the truth"; `[22:64]` is the ownership premise for `[22:37]`'s "nothing reaches Him."

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** the emigrant-martyr clause is read against the Hijrah community's dead (mixed lists: Medinan); the whole-surah lists vary and are not resolved here. *Source: classical lists — verify.*
* **Asbab:** isolated reports attach `[22:60]` to specific retaliation cases (e.g., a companion wronged twice after measured reprisal), but the genre's notices vary. *Source: transmitted asbab-type reports — agent reconstruction, verify.*
