# A plain warner; revelation is not left to Satan's interjection (22:49–57)
**UID:** 202610141688
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 22:49–57 (Ruku 7 of 10 in al-Hajj)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[22:49]` – `[22:57]` (9 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The messenger's job is re-scoped — "I am only a plain warner" — with the two destinies stated flatly (49–51); then the unit's load-bearing verse: no messenger or prophet is sent but that, at his reciting, Satan may interject in his wishes, and Allah nullifies what Satan interjects and affirms His revelations (52) — a doctrine of revelation's integrity that explains trial (53), converts the knowing (54), and leaves the disbelievers in hesitation until the Hour comes suddenly (55), when sovereignty settles on God alone (56–57).
* **Placement:** Ruku 7; applies the history-of-denial of ruku 6 to the texture of revelation itself, and its "suddenly" close re-echoes ruku 1's earthquake opening.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[22:49]`** "Say, “O people, I am only a plain warner to you.”"
  * **Gloss:** The fourth "O people" address; the prophet's self-description is minimized (warner, plainly) — authority is not in the messenger but in the clarity of the warning.
* **`[22:50]`** "Those who believe and work righteousness—for them is forgiveness and a generous provision."
  * **Gloss:** The fixed reward couplet: forgiveness (maghfira) plus rizq karim — the generous provision phrase the corpus reuses verbatim elsewhere (see §3).
* **`[22:51]`** "But those who strive against Our revelations—these are the inmates of Hell."
  * **Gloss:** The counterpart: contesting the verses (sa'y in enmity) is the crime; "inmates of Hell" closes the pair.
* **`[22:52]`** "We never sent a messenger before you, or a prophet, but when he had a desire Satan interfered in his wishes. But Allah nullifies what Satan interjects, and Allah affirms His revelations. Allah is Omniscient and Wise."
  * **Gloss:** The integrity clause: the prophetic function (tala) — wishing/reciting — is exposed to satanic interjection (alqa), but the sequence is law: whatever is interjected is nullified (yabtul), whatever is revealed is affirmed (yumahid), and the double name "Omniscient and Wise" guarantees the sorting.
* **`[22:53]`** "In order to make Satan's suggestions a trial for those whose hearts are diseased, and those whose hearts are hardened. The wrongdoers are in profound discord."
  * **Gloss:** The permission's purpose: interjection becomes a sieve — diseased hearts (maraḍ) and hardened hearts fail the test, while the wrongdoers sit in "profound discord" (shiqaq).
* **`[22:54]`** "And so that those endowed with knowledge may know that it is the truth from your Lord, and so believe in it, and their hearts soften to it. Allah guides those who believe to a straight path."
  * **Gloss:** The other half of the sieve: ulu al-ilm recognize the truth, believe, and their hearts soften (khasha'a) — the same verb-pair as 39:23's skins and hearts softening; guidance to a straight path is the outcome.
* **`[22:55]`** "Those who disbelieve will continue to be hesitant about it, until the Hour comes upon them suddenly, or there comes to them the torment of a desolate Day."
  * **Gloss:** Hesitation (mumtarin, "those in doubt") is named as a permanent posture until the sudden arrival; "a desolate Day" (qarin) — a day that leaves nothing behind.
* **`[22:56]`** "Sovereignty on that Day belongs to Allah; He will judge between them. Those who believe and do good deeds will be in the Gardens of Bliss."
  * **Gloss:** The royal claim of the Day (cf. 40:16's question-form) with judgment; the believers' destination is jannat al-na'im — the "Gardens of Bliss" that 5:65 also uses.
* **`[22:57]`** "But those who disbelieve and reject Our revelations—these will have a humiliating punishment."
  * **Gloss:** The mirror verse: rejection earns humiliation (madhafun) — closing the unit's two-line ledger.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`nudhur mubin` (plain warner)** `[22:49]`:
  * **Rendering:** "I am only a plain warner to you."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[7:184]` "He is but a plain warner" — identical phrase about the same mission; the self-description formula recurs across the corpus for Muhammad's role.
* **`abṭala / yumahidu (nullifies / affirms)`** `[22:52]`:
  * **Rendering:** "Allah nullifies what Satan interjects, and Allah affirms His revelations."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[13:39]` "Allah abolishes whatever He wills, and He affirms. With Him is the source of the Scripture" — the same verb-pair as a general law of revelation-management (kalam doctrine); `[47:1]` "He nullifies their works."
* **`qalb mariḍ / qalb qas / khasha'a` (diseased, hardened, softened hearts)** `[22:53–54]`:
  * **Rendering:** "hearts are diseased... hearts are hardened... hearts soften."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:10]` "In their hearts is sickness" (the hypocrite's maraḍ — same noun, rendered "sickness" there); `[2:74]` "your hearts hardened... as rocks, or even harder"; `[39:23]` "their skins and their hearts soften up to the remembrance of Allah" — the soften-verb twin of 22:54.
* **`rizq karim` (generous provision)** `[22:50]`:
  * **Rendering:** "forgiveness and a generous provision."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[8:4]`, `[24:26]`, `[33:31]`, `[34:4]` — the identical couplet; the formula's five corpus homes are the book's standard consolation line.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** classically read as addressing the Meccan charge that the recitation mixes divine and satanic speech; the mixed-list reports attach nothing specific here. *Source: classical reading — verify; the surah's Mecca/Medina status remains disputed (see index).*
* **Asbab / narrative context:** the tafsir genre transmits an occasion for 22:52 involving an interjected mention of exalted goddesses during a recitation, followed by its nullification; muhaddith and mufassir critics (e.g., Ibn Kathir) rejected the report's soundness even while transmitting it. *Source: transmitted reports and their classical refutation — agent reconstruction, verify.* The ruku's own text makes no such claim beyond 52–54 as stated.
