# Robes of silk and the equal right of the Sacred Mosque (22:23–25)
**UID:** 202610141684
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 22:23–25 (Ruku 3 of 10 in al-Hajj)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[22:23]` – `[22:25]` (3 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The shortest ruku of the surah is a hinge of contrast: the admitted believers are dressed in gold, pearls, and silk and guided to "purity of speech" and "the path of the Most Praised" (23–24), while the unit's real teeth are in 25 — the disbelievers who bar from Allah's path and from the Sacred Mosque, a sanctuary designated *for all mankind equally, resident or visitor*, whose would-be sacrilegeur is promised a painful punishment.
* **Placement:** Ruku 3; closes the fire/reward sorting of ruku 1–2 and opens the House theme that ruku 4 (26–33) will charter through Abraham.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[22:23]`** "But Allah will admit those who believe and do good deeds into Gardens beneath which rivers flow. They will be decorated therein with bracelets of gold and pearls, and their garments therein will be of silk."
  * **Gloss:** The reward inventory in three registers — adornment (gold, pearls), dress (silk), setting (rivers beneath); "believe and do good deeds" is the fixed pair.
* **`[22:24]`** "They were guided to purity of speech. They were guided to the path of the Most Praised."
  * **Gloss:** The interior reward matches the exterior: guidance to the good/pure word (qawl al-tayyib) and to the path of the One who is praised — the only path worth walking; the doubled "They were guided" makes guidance itself the treasure.
* **`[22:25]`** "As for those who disbelieve and repel from Allah's path and from the Sacred Mosque— which We have designated for all mankind equally, whether residing therein or passing through—and seek to commit sacrilege therein—We will make him taste of a painful punishment."
  * **Gloss:** Three crimes compound — disbelief, blocking others from the path/Mosque, and intending ilhad (deviation/sacrilege) inside it; the parenthesis is a constitutional clause: the Mosque is set for all people, equal between local and visitor; the punishment is again framed as "taste" (cf. 22:9–10, 22:22).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`bracelets of gold and pearls / silk`** `[22:23]`:
  * **Rendering:** "decorated therein with bracelets of gold and pearls, and their garments therein will be of silk."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[35:33]` — near-verbatim twin ("adorned therein with gold bracelets and pearls, and their garments therein will be of silk"); `[18:31]` "bracelets of gold... green garments of silk and brocade"; `[76:21]` renders the adornment as "bracelets of silver" — the corpus's three robe-lists differ slightly, an observable of the translator's word choices.
* **`qawl al-tayyib` (purity of speech)** `[22:24]`:
  * **Rendering:** "They were guided to purity of speech."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[35:10]` "To Him ascends speech that is pure" — the same tayyib-of-speech idiom; `[14:24]` "A good word is like a good tree" — the parable form; the good/pure word ascends and is planted — here it is *guidance to* it.
* **`ilhad` (sacrilege)** `[22:25]`:
  * **Rendering:** "seek to commit sacrilege therein."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:194]` "sacrilege calls for retaliation" — same noun; deviating (m-l-h-d, root of mulhid) inside a sanctified boundary — the word's only two corpus uses both concern the Haram.
* **`al-Masjid al-Haram` (Sacred Mosque)** `[22:25]`:
  * **Rendering:** "the Sacred Mosque— which We have designated for all mankind equally."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:144]` (the qiblah), `[2:191]` (do not fight there unless they fight you), `[5:2]` ("people who barred you from the Sacred Mosque"), `[5:97]` "Allah has appointed the Kaabah, the Sacred House, a sanctuary for the people" — the same "appointed/designated + people" grammar (`[22:25]` shares verbs and audience with 5:97).

## 4. Cross-References (The Web)
* **Parallel:** `[2:125]` "We made the House a focal point for the people, and a sanctuary" — the universal-public clause of `[22:25]`'s parenthesis, stated in Baqarah as Abraham's charter; the two verses read as one constitution.
* **Parallel:** `[5:97]` — "appointed... a sanctuary for the people" pairs with "designated for all mankind equally"; both use i-j-' al (appointed/designed) with the same audience.
* **Parallel:** `[2:217]` "to bar others from Allah's path, and to disbelieve in Him, and to prevent access to the Holy Mosque, and to expel its people from it, are more deplorable with Allah" — the exact crime-cluster of `[22:25]`.
* **Parallel:** `[47:1]` "Those who disbelieve and repel from the path of Allah" — verbatim twin of the opening of `[22:25]`.
* **Within surah:** `[22:24]`'s "guided" answers `[22:16]` "Allah guides whomever He wills"; `[22:25]`'s Sacred Mosque is what ruku 4 (26–33) will narrate the founding of.
