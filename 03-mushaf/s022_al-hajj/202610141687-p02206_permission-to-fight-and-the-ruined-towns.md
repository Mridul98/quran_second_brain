# Permission for those wronged — and the graveyard of denied towns (22:39–48)
**UID:** 202610141687
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 22:39–48 (Ruku 6 of 10 in al-Hajj)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[22:39]`–`[22:48]` (10 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The Qur'anic permission-to-fight text: force is authorized to those fought and expelled from their homes solely for saying "Our Lord is Allah," and the stated purpose of God's repelling of people by means of others is that monasteries, churches, synagogues, and mosques should survive; the empowered community's platform is prayer, zakat, enjoining right, forbidding wrong (41) — and the unit then arms that grammar with history: the deniers' roster (42–44), the ruined towns (45), the blind-heart diagnosis (46), and the thousand-year time-scale against those who demand the threat on their schedule (47–48).
* **Placement:** Ruku 6; the surah's hinge — the defense-claim of 22:38 ("Allah defends those who believe") opens the sword-passage, and 42–48 supplies the sociology of denial that ruku 7 (49–57) will apply to the messenger's own reception.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[22:39]`** "Permission is given to those who are fought against, and Allah is Able to give them victory."
  * **Gloss:** The permission is passive and conditional — given to those already being fought; the power clause is placed *after* the permission, as its promise, not its provocation.
* **`[22:40]`** "Those who were unjustly evicted from their homes, merely for saying, “Our Lord is Allah.” Were it not that Allah repels people by means of others: monasteries, churches, synagogues, and mosques—where the name of Allah is mentioned much—would have been demolished. Allah supports whoever supports Him. Allah is Strong and Mighty."
  * **Gloss:** The wrong is named — eviction for confession alone; the counterbalance clause says human force-checking-human-force is what keeps every house of prayer standing; "Allah supports whoever supports Him" makes divine aid reciprocal to human stand; the four-house list is the corpus's only survey of surviving sanctuaries.
* **`[22:41]`** "Those who, when We empower them in the land, observe the prayer, and give regular charity, and command what is right, and forbid what is wrong. To Allah belongs the outcome of events."
  * **Gloss:** Power is defined by its use: prayer, zakat, moral policing without coercion over outcomes — "the outcome of events" belongs to God, an anti-authoritarian cap on the empowered.
* **`[22:42]`** "If they deny you—before them the people of Noah, and Aad, and Thamood also denied."
  * **Gloss:** Consolation by genealogy: the denial has a lineage; the roster begins.
* **`[22:43]`** "And the people of Abraham, and the people of Lot."
  * **Gloss:** The roster extends to the Abrahamic household's own neighbors — denial was never marginal to prophecy.
* **`[22:44]`** "And the inhabitants of Median. And Moses was denied. Then I reprieved those who disbelieved, but then I seized them. So how was My rejection?"
  * **Gloss:** Madyan and Moses close the list; the divine voice shifts to first person and the rhythm to warning: reprieve, then seizure — "how was My rejection?" is a question meant to chill.
* **`[22:45]`** "How many a town have We destroyed while it was doing wrong? They lie in ruins; with stilled wells, and lofty mansions."
  * **Gloss:** The archaeological sermon: wells no longer flowing and tall houses fallen — the two infrastructure proofs of a vanished people.
* **`[22:46]`** "Have they not journeyed in the land, and had minds to reason with, or ears to listen with? It is not the eyes that go blind, but it is the hearts, within the chests, that go blind."
  * **Gloss:** Travel is examination; the senses are intact but the receivers — hearts — are the organ that goes blind; the surah's central epistemology verse.
* **`[22:47]`** "And they ask you to hasten the punishment. But Allah never breaks His promise. A day with your Lord is like a thousand years of your count."
  * **Gloss:** The impatient demand (known from 22:55ff) meets two facts: God's promise is kept on His clock; and His day is a thousand of yours — the delay is not default.
* **`[22:48]`** "How many a town have I reprieved, although it was unjust? Then I seized it. To Me is the destination."
  * **Gloss:** The reprieve-seizure pattern repeated (cf. 44) with the destination clause — every journey ends at God.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`udhina / "permission is given"`** `[22:39]`:
  * **Rendering:** "Permission is given to those who are fought against."
  * **Elsewhere:** unique in the corpus — no other "permission is given" construction; 2:190 states the fight-with-limit clause, 2:216 states obligation ("Fighting is ordained for you"); this verse states authorization.
* **`daf'/dafa'a (repel)`** `[22:40]`:
  * **Rendering:** "Were it not that Allah repels people by means of others."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:251]` "Were it not for Allah restraining the people, some by means of others, the earth would have gone to ruin" — the same noun-construction with "restraining" (observable drift); the clause is a fixed formula across both surahs.
* **`nasr (support/victory)`** `[22:39–40]`:
  * **Rendering:** "Allah is Able to give them victory... Allah supports whoever supports Him."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[47:7]` "If you support Allah, He will support you, and will strengthen your foothold" — near-verbatim reciprocity; `[61:13]` "support from Allah, and imminent victory."
* **`al-amr bi-l-ma'ruf wa-l-nahy 'an al-munkar`** `[22:41]`:
  * **Rendering:** "command what is right, and forbid what is wrong."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[3:104]` "calling to virtue, and advocating righteousness, and deterring from evil"; `[9:71]` "They advocate virtue, forbid evil"; `[31:17]` "advocate righteousness, forbid evil" — the formula's four English costumes across the corpus (drift table).

## 6. Context Notes
* **Text claims, stated neutrally:** the passage authorizes fighting only for those already fought and expelled for their faith; names protection of all named houses of worship as the purpose of the world's force-balance; and binds the empowered community to prayer, zakat, right-command, wrong-forbid, leaving outcomes to God. It does not itself state aggressors' identity, geography, or a date.
* **Revelation context:** classically transmitted as the first permission to fight (reports traced to Ibn Abbas to that effect are collected in the tafsir literature; some place it at Badr-era Medina); mixed-list assignments mark 39–46 Medinan. *Source: transmitted reports — agent reconstruction, verify.*
* **Asbab:** reports attaching 22:39–40 to the Meccan community's expulsion precede; the genre varies in wording. *Source: classical asbab notices — verify.*
