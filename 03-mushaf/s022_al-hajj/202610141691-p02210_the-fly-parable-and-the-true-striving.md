# The fly that outwits its worshippers, and the striving that names you Muslims (22:73–78)
**UID:** 202610141691
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 22:73–78 (Ruku 10 of 10 in al-Hajj)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[22:73]` – `[22:78]` (6 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah's finale: a parable of pure weakness — invoked gods who cannot create a fly, and cannot recover what the fly steals (73–74) — then the election logic: Allah chooses messengers from angels and from people, knows before and behind, and all matters return to Him (75–76); then the commission to the believers: kneel, prostrate, worship, do good, strive with the striving due to Him, receive the name "Muslims" from your father Abraham, bear witness as the Messenger witnesses over you — pray, give zakat, cleave to Allah, "for What an excellent Protector, and what an excellent Helper" (77–78).
* **Placement:** Ruku 10; the closing address answers the opening one (1–2): the surah that began "O people, be conscious" ends "O you who believe" — the universal summons has found its named community.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[22:73]`** "O people! A parable is presented, so listen to it: Those you invoke besides Allah will never create a fly, even if they banded together for that purpose. And if the fly steals anything from them, they cannot recover it from it. Weak are the pursuer and the pursued."
  * **Gloss:** The fourth and last "O people"; the parable's wit: the comparison is with the weakest creature, and even its theft is unappealable — pursuer and pursued are equally powerless, so the invoked rank below the robbed.
* **`[22:74]`** "They do not value Allah as He should be valued. Allah is Strong and Powerful."
  * **Gloss:** The failure is doxological: not appraising God at His worth — the same idiom 6:91 uses verbatim — while He is the Strong (aziz), the Powerful (qawi).
* **`[22:75]`** "Allah chooses messengers from among the angels, and from among the people. Allah is Hearing and Seeing."
  * **Gloss:** Election belongs to Him across both intelligent orders — angelic and human messengership is one choosing (against the 25:7 demand for an angel); His hearing and seeing ground the choosing.
* **`[22:76]`** "He knows what is before them, and what is behind them. To Allah all matters are referred."
  * **Gloss:** Total knowledge in both temporal directions (the Throne-verse idiom) and the destination of all affairs — the court of appeal for the weak and wronged of the surah.
* **`[22:77]`** "O you who believe! Kneel, and prostrate, and worship your Lord, and do good deeds, so that you may succeed."
  * **Gloss:** The ritual quartet — ruku', sujud, worship of the Lord, social good — ordered so that bodily prayer and ihsan are one act of success (falāh); the same two postures the House was purified for (22:26).
* **`[22:78]`** "And strive for Allah, with the striving due to Him. He has chosen you, and has not burdened you in religion—the faith of your father Abraham. It is he who named you Muslims before, and in this. So that the Messenger may be a witness over you, and you may be witnesses over the people. So pray regularly, and give regular charity, and cleave to Allah. He is your Protector. What an excellent Protector, and what an excellent Helper."
  * **Gloss:** The surah's identity charter: striving at its fullest measure; election without burden (the milla of Abraham named as the un-burdened religion); the naming "Muslims" before and in this — a lineage claim; the witness-pair (Messenger over community, community over the nations) exported from Baqarah's qiblah moment; then the closing trio: prayer, zakat, cleaving to Allah — walayah answered with the two exclamations of Protector and Helper.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`dhubab / fly`** `[22:73]`:
  * **Rendering:** "will never create a fly."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:26]` "Allah does not shy away from making an example of a gnat, or something above it" — different Arabic for the tiny-parable strategy; 22:73 is the fly (dhubab) as the unmakeable benchmark, 2:26 the gnat as the unembarrassing example.
* **`qadarū / "value Allah as He should"`** `[22:74]`:
  * **Rendering:** "They do not value Allah as He should be valued."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[6:91]` — verbatim twin of the idiom; `[39:67]` "They have not esteemed Allah as He ought to be esteemed" — the same construction in third rendering (observable drift: value/esteem).
* **`Islam / "named you Muslims"`** `[22:78]`:
  * **Rendering:** "It is he who named you Muslims before, and in this."
  * **Elsewhere:** the root's other named moments — `[3:67]` Abraham was "a Monotheist, a Muslim"; `[3:102]` "do not die except as Muslims"; `[28:53]` "we were Muslims prior to it"; `[2:131–132]` Abraham: "I have submitted... do not die unless you have submitted"; `[61:7]` "invited to Islam" — the s-l-m family's five renderings; 22:78 alone states the *naming* act.
* **`jahada / "striving due to Him"`** `[22:78]`:
  * **Rendering:** "strive for Allah, with the striving due to Him" (juhdakum fiha / haqqa jihadihi).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[25:52]` "strive against them with it, a mighty struggle" — same verb + source of striving (the Qur'an); `[29:69]` "As for those who strive for Us—We will guide them in Our ways"; `[49:15]`, `[61:11]` — striving with wealth and selves: the four forms bracket the physical, verbal, and economic registers.

* **Parallel:** `[6:91]` / `[39:67]` — the under-valuing of God as the root error the fly parable exposes.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** the closing commission reads to many classical lists as the surah's mature Medinan voice (community law: prayer, zakat, striving, witness function); mixed and whole-surah readings still differ. *Source: classical lists — verify.*
* **Asbab:** "who named you Muslims before" is glossed in transmitted reports as Abraham's naming of his progeny and as prior prophets' usage; no single occasion-verse is reported for the ruku. *Source: classical glosses — agent reconstruction, verify.*
