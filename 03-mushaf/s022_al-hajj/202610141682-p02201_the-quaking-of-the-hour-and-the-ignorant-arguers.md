# The Quaking of the Hour and the ignorant arguers (22:1–10)
**UID:** 202610141682
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 22:1–10 (Ruku 1 of 10 in al-Hajj)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[22:1]` – `[22:10]` (10 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah opens not with pilgrimage law but with cosmic dread: "O people" are summoned to taqwa before the Hour's quaking, the resurrection is proven out of the same creation-ladder that runs dust→infant→age→green earth, and the unit sorts the opposition — the defiant arguer who follows "every defiant devil" appears twice (3–4, 8–10) as the foil to the summoned.
* **Placement:** Ruku 1 of 10; sets the Hour-frame that the surah keeps returning to (22:7, 22:47, 22:55–56), before the House and the sword appear.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[22:1]`** "O people, be conscious of your Lord. The quaking of the Hour is a tremendous thing."
  * **Gloss:** A universal address (the first of four "O people" in the surah — 1, 5, 49, 73) opening on taqwa; the Hour is named by its physical event — a "quaking" (rajfa) of staggering scale.
* **`[22:2]`** "On the Day when you will see it: every nursing mother will discard her infant, and every pregnant woman will abort her load, and you will see the people drunk, even though they are not drunk—but the punishment of Allah is severe."
  * **Gloss:** Three images of the scene: the nursing mother forgets the nursling, the pregnant woman miscarry, sober people staggering as if intoxicated — the destruction of every human anchor (maternal instinct, bodily control, sobriety). Note: this edition says "will discard her infant," not the famous "distracted" phrasing of other translations.
* **`[22:3]`** "Among the people is he who argues about Allah without knowledge, and follows every defiant devil."
  * **Gloss:** First portrait: argument without knowledge (jadala), sourced from rebellion (shaytan marin) — disputation about God/the Hour as a symptom, not an intellectual position.
* **`[22:4]`** "It was decreed for him, that whoever follows him—he will misguide him, and lead him to the torment of the Blaze."
  * **Gloss:** The devil's contract is already written (kutiba); following him carries an inevitable double movement — misguidance now, the Blaze (sa'ir) later.
* **`[22:5]`** "O people! If you are in doubt about the Resurrection—We created you from dust, then from a small drop, then from a clinging clot, then from a lump of flesh, partly developed and partly undeveloped. In order to clarify things for you. And We settle in the wombs whatever We will for a designated term, and then We bring you out as infants, until you reach your full strength. And some of you will pass away, and some of you will be returned to the vilest age, so that he may not know, after having known. And you see the earth still; but when We send down water on it, it vibrates, and swells, and grows all kinds of lovely pairs."
  * **Gloss:** The longest verse of the ruku and the surah's evidentiary engine: creation-ladder (dust→drop→clot→flesh), the settled womb-term, the life-curve (infancy→strength→death or second childhood), and the dead-earth-to-green-spring cycle — four resurrections-in-miniature against doubt.
* **`[22:6]`** "That is because Allah is the truth, and because He gives life to the dead, and because He is Capable of everything."
  * **Gloss:** The "because" clauses read the signs back to their source: He is the Real (al-haqq), the giver of life, the omni-capable (qadir).
* **`[22:7]`** "And because the Hour is coming—there is no doubt about it—and because Allah will resurrect those in the graves."
  * **Gloss:** Two more objects of the prior "That is because": the Hour's certainty and the resurrection of what is in the graves — the doubt of 22:3 answered by what 22:5 just displayed.
* **`[22:8]`** "And among the people is he who argues about Allah without knowledge, or guidance, or an enlightening scripture."
  * **Gloss:** The arguer returns, now specified: his argument has none of the three legitimating sources — knowledge, guidance, or an illuminating book (a triple negation).
* **`[22:9]`** "Turning aside in contempt, to lead away from the path of Allah. He will have humiliation in this world, and on the Day of Resurrection We will make him taste the agony of burning."
  * **Gloss:** His posture (turning aside in arrogance, iftina') aims to divert others from God's path; the sentence is twofold: worldly humiliation plus the Day's burning agony.
* **`[22:10]`** "That is for what your hands have advanced, and because Allah is not unjust to the servants."
  * **Gloss:** The verdict's ground: what one's own hands sent ahead, and the denial of divine injustice — retribution is earned, not imposed.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`rajfa` (quaking)** `[22:1]`:
  * **Rendering:** "The quaking of the Hour" — Itani keeps the seismic concrete; unique in the corpus (only 22:1 uses "quaking" this way).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[79:6]` "On the Day when the Quake quakes" — the same word-form describing the Hour; `[99:1]` "When the earth is shaken with its quake" — the same root's surah-title (az-Zalzalah).
* **`shaytan marin` (defiant devil)** `[22:3]`:
  * **Rendering:** "every defiant devil."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[37:7]` "And guarded it against every defiant devil" — identical phrase (the heavens guarded from the marin; here unguarded hearts follow one).
* **`ardhal al-'umr` (vilest age)** `[22:5]`:
  * **Rendering:** "returned to the vilest age."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[16:70]` "Some of you will be brought back to the worst age, so that he will no longer know anything, after having acquired knowledge" — near-verbatim twin (rendering drift: vilest/worst); the decay of memory as resurrection-proof in both.
* **`kutiba` (decreed)** `[22:4]`:
  * **Rendering:** "It was decreed for him."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:180]` "It is decreed for you" (testament law); `[39:42]` "those for which He has decreed death" — the passive of kataba as divine registration.

## 4. Cross-References (The Web)
* **Parallel:** `[3:182]` "This is on account of what your hands have forwarded, and because Allah is not unjust towards the creatures" and `[8:51]` — near-verbatim twins of `[22:10]`; a fixed eschatological formula.
* **Parallel:** `[50:15]` "they are in doubt of a new creation" — the exact doubt `[22:5]` confronts.
* **Parallel:** `[2:28]` "you were dead and He gave you life, then He will put you to death, then He will bring you to life" — the same creation→death→revival proof-chain the ruku builds from embryology and spring.
* **Within surah:** the "O people" address recurs at `[22:5]`, `[22:49]`, `[22:73]`; the still-earth→water→vibrates image is reused at `[22:63]`.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** the opening earthquake verses are read as among the earliest of the surah in the mixed-list readings (Meccan per those reports); whole-surah Medinan lists also exist. *Source: classical lists — verify; agent labeling of the dispute.*
* **Asbab / narrative context:** classical surah-introduction notices relate that these verses came down at night and the companions marveled at their weight. *Source: transmitted reports in the tafsir genre — agent reconstruction, verify.*
