# Shuaib: full measure, the lurker's road, and beautiful trust (7:85–93)
**UID:** 202610141431
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 7:85–93 (Ruku 11 of 24 in al-A’raf)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[7:85]` – `[7:93]` (9 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Madyan's shirk is a business: the refrain arrives with weights-and-measures attached — cheat no one, lurk on no road, don't corrupt after reformation — because their religion lived in their ledger; the arrogant elite threaten eviction, Shuaib answers with the surah's richest tawakkul sentence (fabricate-not, will-of-God, knowledge-enbracing, trust-placed, decide-between-us), and the quake lands with Shuaib's own economics verdict: those who rejected him were the losers — as if they had never prospered.
* **Placement:** The fifth town closes the cycle's moral range: Noah (idolatry), Aad (stature), Thamud (a sign-murder), Lot (bodily transgression), Madyan (commercial fraud) — the surah has now listed every gate of isrāf; 7:87–89's patience-and-trust speech is the covenant endurance-text (cf. the Sabr-and-taqwa note), and "Allah is the Best of Deciders" hands the case to the same court as 7:8–9's scales.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[7:85]`** "And to Median, their brother Shuaib. He said, “O my people, worship Allah; you have no god other than Him. A clear proof has come to you from your Lord. Give full measure and weight, and do not cheat people out of their rights, and do not corrupt the land once it has been set right. This is better for you, if you are believers.”"
  * **Gloss:** Refrain + proof + the shop-floor law: creed and accounting in one breath, graded by "better for you."
* **`[7:86]`** "“And do not lurk on every path, making threats and turning away from the path of Allah those who believe in Him, seeking to distort it. And remember how you were few, and how He made you numerous. So note the consequences for the corrupters.”"
  * **Gloss:** Highway robbery as theology: ambushing travelers and faith alike; the number-blessing (few→many) is their thank-you-note to fail.
* **`[7:87]`** "“Since some of you believed in what I was sent with, and some did not believe, be patient until Allah judges between us; for He is the Best of Judges.”"
  * **Gloss:** Sabr as litigation strategy: the community splits, and the case is adjourned to the Best of Judges.
* **`[7:88]`** "The arrogant elite among his people said, “O Shuaib, We will evict you from our town, along with those who believe with you, unless you return to our religion.” He said, “Even if we are unwilling?”"
  * **Gloss:** The threat: conversion-by-eviction; the retort is two words — exile or apostasy, we choose exile.
* **`[7:89]`** "“We would be fabricating falsehood against Allah, if we were to return to your religion, after Allah has saved us from it. It is not for us to return to it, unless Allah, our Lord, wills. Our Lord embraces all things in knowledge. In Allah we place our trust. Our Lord, decide between us and our people in truth, for You are the Best of Deciders.”"
  * **Gloss:** The tawakkul ladder in five rungs: no-forge, salvation-already-given, divine-will hedge, knowledge-scope, trust-placed, decision-requested.
* **`[7:90]`** "The elite of his people who disbelieved said, “If you follow Shuaib, you will be losers.”"
  * **Gloss:** The market-analysts' verdict — loss framed in the ledger's own vocabulary, which verse 92 will cash literally.
* **`[7:91]`** "Thereupon, the quake struck them; and they became lifeless bodies in their homes."
  * **Gloss:** Verbatim echo of 7:78 — Thamud's end reissued for Madyan, one town's punishment becoming a citation.
* **`[7:92]`** "Those who rejected Shuaib—as if they never prospered therein. Those who rejected Shuaib—it was they who were the losers."
  * **Gloss:** The double-strike line: prosperity retroactively voided; 7:90's accusation reversed onto the accusers.
* **`[7:93]`** "So he turned away from them, and said, “O my people, I have delivered to you the messages of my Lord, and I have advised you, so why should I grieve over a disbelieving people?”"
  * **Gloss:** The prophet's release from grief — delivery done, advice given; compare 7:79 and 18:6's self-consuming sorrow question.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **"full measure and weight" (qisṭ/wazn):** worship in the marketplace.
  * **Rendering:** "Give full measure and weight, and do not cheat people out of their rights" (`[7:85]`).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[11:85]` (Shuaib in Hud's surah — twin commission, "give full measure and full weight, in all fairness"); `[6:152]` "give full weight and full measure, equitably" — and the same verse says "fulfill your covenant with Allah": measures and mithaq in one sentence; `[17:35]` "weigh with accurate scales. That is fair"; `[83:3]` "when they measure or weigh to others, they cheat" — the Mutaffifin woe this command pre-empts; `[21:47]` and 7:8's Day-scales are the ledger's audit.
* **"lurk" (qa'd):** the ambush-road.
  * **Rendering:** "do not lurk on every path, making threats" (`[7:86]`) — "lurk" is unique to this line in the corpus (`rg`-verified).
  * **Elsewhere:** within-surah: "turning away from the path of Allah...seeking to distort it" ≈ 7:45's Fire-residents' profile — path-hindering is the surah's recurring class of crime (also `[7:27]`'s ambush frame from Iblis downward).
* **"Best of Judges / Best of Deciders":** the venue clause.
  * **Rendering:** 7:87 "Best of Judges"; 7:89 "Best of Deciders" — two superlatives for one court, with the formula's third twin at `[6:57]` "He is the Best of Judges."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[3:54]` "Allah is the Best of planners" — the same superlative pressed against 7:99's "Allah's plan" (ruku 12); within-surah `[7:155]` "Best of Forgivers" (Moses' line, later ruku).
* **"losers" (khāsirūn):** the commercial verdict.
  * **Rendering:** "you will be losers" (`[7:90]`) / "it was they who were the losers" (`[7:92]`) / "never prospered therein" (7:92) ↔ `[11:68]` "As if they had never prospered therein" (Thamud-line in Hud — a formula reused across towns, *Source: observable doublet*).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[7:9]` "lost their souls" (ruku 1) — the surah opened with loss defined soul-side and closes the cycle with it ledger-side; the khusrf-trade pairs with the Infraq-vs-riba note.
