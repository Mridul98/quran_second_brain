# Forty nights: the Speech, the Tablets, and the proud (7:142–147)
**UID:** 202610141437
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 7:142–147 (Ruku 17 of 24 in al-A'raf)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[7:142]` – `[7:147]` (6 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The mountaintop interview: the appointment kept (30+10 nights), the request to *see* refused and answered by a shattered mountain, the election to Speech confirmed, the Tablets inscribed with "decisive explanation of all things" — and immediately the surah discloses the counter-law: revelation is turned away from the proud on earth, who will neither believe any sign nor walk any right road (7:146).
* **Placement:** The theological summit of the Moses dossier (7:103–157) and the surah's central revelation-as-speech passage; the 7:146 hardening clause is the key that unlocks the calf episode (7:148–157) and the dog parable (7:175–177) — it restates 7:40 and 7:101 in first-person divine voice.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[7:142]`** "And We appointed to Moses thirty nights, and completed them with ten; and thus the time appointed by his Lord was forty nights. And Moses said to his brother Aaron: “Take my place among my people, and be upright, and do not follow the way of the mischiefmakers.”"
  * **Gloss:** The appointment math (30+10=40) and the deputy charge to Aaron — "be upright" (*islah*) and anti-*mufsidun*; the two-axis ethics of the surah (reform vs corruption) handed to the regent.
* **`[7:143]`** "And when Moses came to Our appointment, and his Lord spoke to him, he said, “My Lord, allow me to look and see You.” He said, “You will not see Me, but look at the mountain; if it stays in its place, you will see Me.” But when his Lord manifested Himself to the mountain, He turned it into dust, and Moses fell down unconscious. Then, when he recovered, he said, “Glory be to you, I repent to you, and I am the first of the believers.”"
  * **Gloss:** The corpus's fullest theophany scene: Speech granted, Vision refused, mountain flattened, Moses swoons — and the recovery is doxology, repentance, and a claim to primacy in belief.
* **`[7:144]`** "He said, “O Moses, I have chosen you over all people for My messages and for My Words. So take what I have given you, and be one of the thankful.”"
  * **Gloss:** Election named as the ground of the two gifts — *risalat* (messages) and *kalam* (Words); the response commanded is thankfulness, not triumph.
* **`[7:145]`** "And We inscribed for him in the Tablets all kinds of enlightenments, and decisive explanation of all things. “Hold fast to them, and exhort your people to adopt the best of them. I will show you the fate of the sinners.”"
  * **Gloss:** Revelation now *written* — the Tablets carry "all kinds of enlightenments" and a "decisive explanation of all things"; the charge: grip them and command the *best* of them.
* **`[7:146]`** "I will turn away from My revelations those who behave proudly on earth without justification. Even if they see every sign, they will not believe in it; and if they see the path of rectitude, they will not adopt it for a path; and if they see the path of error, they will adopt it for a path. That is because they denied Our revelations, and paid no attention to them."
  * **Gloss:** The proud are programmatically immunized against evidence; the path-of-error clause is the mirror-image of Fatihah's petition (1:6–7) answered in reverse.
* **`[7:147]`** "Those who deny Our revelations and the meeting of the Hereafter—their deeds will come to nothing. Will they be repaid except according to what they used to do?"
  * **Gloss:** The closure clause: denial of the Hereafter voids deeds; repayment equals earned works.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **"spoke to him / His Words" (divine speech):** `[7:143]`, `[7:144]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[4:164]` "And Allah spoke to Moses directly" — the corpus's proof-text pairing with this ruku, the k-kalam note's anchor; `[7:158]` (next ruku) "who believes in Allah and His words" — the same "Words" credential extended to the ummic Prophet.
* **"Tablets" (the plural is unique to al-A'raf):** "We inscribed for him in the Tablets" `[7:145]`; "he threw down the tablets" `[7:150]` (next ruku); "he took up the tablets" `[7:154]`.
  * **Rendering:** "Tablets" as Mosaic objects occurs at 7:145/150/154 only (verified `rg -i tablet`); distinguished from the singular "Preserved Tablet" of `[85:22]` (heavenly register).
  * **Elsewhere:** the inscribe-verb arc: "We inscribed for him" `[7:145]` ↔ the people's request "inscribe for us goodness" `[7:156]` — same English verb, heaven-to-Moses then people-to-God (observable).
* **"turn away" (divine repulsion):** "I will turn away from My revelations those who behave proudly" `[7:146]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** within surah `[7:40]` "reject Our revelations and are too arrogant... the doors of Heaven will not be opened" — the same pride-blocks-evidence law from the opening section; `[7:101]` "Thus Allah seals" — the mechanism named twice (seal / turn-away) around this ruku.
* **"look and see You" (vision):** `[7:143]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[6:103]` "No vision can grasp Him, but His grasp is over all vision" — the doctrinal twin of the refusal.
