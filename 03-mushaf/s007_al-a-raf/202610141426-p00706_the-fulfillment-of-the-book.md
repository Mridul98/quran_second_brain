# The third voice speaks, religion in jest, and the Book's due date (7:48–53)
**UID:** 202610141426
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 7:48–53 (Ruku 6 of 24 in al-A’raf)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[7:48]` – `[7:53]` (6 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The men of the Elevations get their speaking part — they mock the hoarders' arrogance and hand the scorned mercy-betters into the Garden — then the Fire's inmates beg water and are refused; and the ruku pivots from scenery to diagnosis: those who took religion as play, whom the world deceived, are met by a Scripture already detailed with knowledge, of which nothing remains to wait for but its fulfillment.
* **Placement:** The second half of the A‘rāf diptych (ruku 5–6) — the surah's title-unit completed; 7:52–53 then close the whole opening block (ruku 1–6) by returning to the Book-claim of 7:2–3, now with an expiry date.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[7:48]`** "And the dwellers of the Elevations will call to men they recognize by their features, saying, “Your hoardings did not avail you, nor did your arrogance.”"
  * **Gloss:** The threshold-men's first address targets the arrogant rich by name — hoarding (their wealth parked out of Allah's road) and kibr, the crime that started the whole drama at 7:13.
* **`[7:49]`** "“Are these the ones you swore Allah will not touch with mercy?” “Enter the Garden; you have nothing to fear, and you will not grieve.”"
  * **Gloss:** The oath of the scornful ("Allah will never mercy these") is reversed on the spot, and the no-fear formula is spoken as admission — the same clause 7:35 promised as the messenger-test's wage.
* **`[7:50]`** "The inmates of the Fire will call on the inhabitants of the Garden, “Pour some water over us, or some of what Allah has provided for you.” They will say, “Allah has forbidden them for the disbelievers.”"
  * **Gloss:** The beggars of 7:44 now beg water; the answer is not the Garden's refusal but an older verdict — provision was forbidden at the gate of choice.
* **`[7:51]`** "Those who took their religion lightly, and in jest, and whom the worldly life deceived. Today We will ignore them, as they ignored the meeting on this Day of theirs, and they used to deny Our revelations."
  * **Gloss:** The indicted class defined by three verbs: played, deceived-by-the-world, ignored — and the Day's justice is pure mirror: they are ignored exactly as they ignored.
* **`[7:52]`** "We have given them a Scripture, which We detailed with knowledge—guidance and mercy for people who believe."
  * **Gloss:** What they had in hand: a detailed Book — the same self-description as 7:32's "We thus detail the revelations for people who know," now tagged "guidance and mercy."
* **`[7:53]`** "Are they waiting for anything but its fulfillment? The Day its fulfillment comes true, those who disregarded it before will say, “The messengers of our Lord did come with the truth. Have we any intercessors to intercede for us? Or, could we be sent back, to behave differently from the way we behaved before?” They ruined their souls, and what they used to invent has failed them."
  * **Gloss:** The Book has a due date; when it matures, the jesting becomes the true-confession line (verbatim with 7:43), then the two doomed requests — intercession and a rewind.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **"hoardings":** wealth withheld from Allah's road.
  * **Rendering:** "Your hoardings did not avail you" (`[7:48]`).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[3:180]` "They will be encircled by their hoardings on the Day of Resurrection" — the same noun-image; the verb family at `[9:34]` "hoard gold and silver, and do not spend them in Allah's cause" — the infraq-versus-riba economy in one word.
* **"lightly, and in jest":** religion as play.
  * **Rendering:** "took their religion lightly, and in jest" (`[7:51]`).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[6:70]` verbatim twin — "those who take their religion for play and pastime, and whom the worldly life has deceived"; `[45:35]` "you took Allah's revelations for a joke"; mockery's insider form at `[2:14]` ("we were only ridiculing") — the nifaq register.
* **"ignore" (nisyān):** mutual forgetting.
  * **Rendering:** "Today We will ignore them, as they ignored the meeting" (`[7:51]`) — one English verb doing the mirror-justice.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[38:26]` "for having ignored the Day of Account"; `[46:3]` "continue to ignore the warnings" — the same divine-ignoring idiom.
* **"its fulfillment":** the Book's maturity-date.
  * **Rendering:** "Are they waiting for anything but its fulfillment?" (`[7:53]`) — "fulfillment" elsewhere only at `[12:100]` ("the fulfillment of my vision") in this edition.
  * **Elsewhere:** if 7:53 and 12:100 share the root taʾ'wīl (arrival of a truth's consequence), the two verses gloss each other — *agent inference — verify.*

## 4. Cross-References (The Web)
* **Formula cluster:** `[7:49]` "nothing to fear, and you will not grieve" ↔ `[2:38]`, `[6:48]`, `[7:35]` — the no-fear formula, here spoken at the moment of admission rather than promised at the moment of belief.
* **Confession repeat:** `[7:53]` "The messengers of our Lord did come with the truth" = verbatim the Garden's doxology of `[7:43]` — the same sentence true on both sides of the partition, said too late on one side.
* **Intercession refusal:** `[26:100]` "Now we have no intercessors"; `[6:94]` "We do not see with you your intercessors—those you claimed were your partners"; `[2:48]` "nor will any intercession be accepted" — 7:53's doomed wish is answered across the corpus.
* **Disregard-and-seize:** `[6:44]` — "when they disregarded what they were reminded of, We opened for them the gates of all things...We seized them suddenly" — 7:53's "disregarded" class rehearsed in history (and staged again in ruku 12, `[7:94–95]`).
* **Within surah:** `[7:48]` continues 7:46's "recognize...by their features"; `[7:52]` answers 7:32's detailing; `[7:53]`'s rewind-request mirrors 7:15–18's respite logic — delay granted to the enemy, denied to his followers.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Meccan; the "religion in jest" indictment fits the Meccan mockery scene (`[2:14]`'s secret ridicule is Medinan but names the same type); *Source: agent inference from motif distribution — verify against chronological tafsir.*
* **Structural fact (observable):** 7:51–53 are the surah's own recap of ruku 1's commission (warn with the revealed Book) now inverted into its rejection — the opening and this ruku form a Book-frame around the A‘rāf scene.
