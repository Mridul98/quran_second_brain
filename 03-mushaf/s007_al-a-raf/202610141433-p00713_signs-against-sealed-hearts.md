# Signs against sealed hearts: the Moses dossier opens (7:100–108)
**UID:** 202610141433
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 7:100–108 (Ruku 13 of 24 in al-A'raf)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[7:100]` – `[7:108]` (9 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah's repeated lesson of destroyed towns becomes a warning to the *heirs*: if God can seal the hearts of those who inherit the land, then Moses' arrival at Pharaoh's court with two plain signs is mercy, not mere spectacle — the longest narrative in the surah begins under the shadow of 7:101's "Thus Allah seals the hearts of the disbelievers."
* **Placement:** The hinge of the surah: 7:100–102 close the general warning cycle (7:4–9, 94–99), 7:103 pivots to the Moses–Pharaoh dossier that runs through 7:157, and 7:104–108 set the confrontation's opening exchange (claim → demand for a miracle → staff and hand).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[7:100]`** "Is it not guidance for those who inherit the land after its inhabitants, that if We willed, We could strike them for their sins? And seal up their hearts, so that they would not hear?"
  * **Gloss:** Address to the heirs of vanished nations: the lesson (*guidance*) is that divine striking and heart-sealing are always held in reserve.
* **`[7:101]`** "These towns—We narrate to you some of their tales. Their messengers came to them with the clear signs, but they would not believe in what they had rejected previously. Thus Allah seals the hearts of the disbelievers."
  * **Gloss:** The surah's own town-stories (7:59–93) are now evidence; unbelief is self-reinforcing — rejection of the first news licenses rejection of the last.
* **`[7:102]`** "We found most of them untrue to their covenants; We found most of them corrupt."
  * **Gloss:** The verdict on the chain of nations is covenant-failure plus corruption (*fasad*) — the two charges the surah keeps returning to.
* **`[7:103]`** "Then, after them, We sent Moses with Our miracles to Pharaoh and his establishment, but they denounced them. So consider the end of the evildoers."
  * **Gloss:** The pivot verse: Moses enters the dossier and Pharaoh's elite reject the signs sight unseen; the reader is told to *consider* the end before it is narrated.
* **`[7:104]`** "Moses said, “O Pharaoh, I am a messenger from the Lord of the Worlds.”"
  * **Gloss:** A slave of a slave addresses the self-god by the titulary "Lord of the Worlds" — the whole confrontation compressed into one sentence.
* **`[7:105]`** "“It is only proper that I should not say about Allah anything other than the truth. I have come to you with clear evidence from your Lord, so let the Children of Israel go with me.”"
  * **Gloss:** Two claims: veracity about God (the surah's recurring sin is saying things about Allah one does not know, 7:28/33) and the political demand — let my people go.
* **`[7:106]`** "He said, “If you brought a miracle, then present it, if you are truthful.”"
  * **Gloss:** Pharaoh accepts the wager format: proof is demanded on his terms.
* **`[7:107]`** "So he threw his staff, and it was an apparent serpent."
  * **Gloss:** Sign one — the staff becomes a real ("apparent") serpent, not a trick of light.
* **`[7:108]`** "And He pulled out his hand, and it was white to the onlookers."
  * **Gloss:** Sign two — the radiant hand, seen by all present; note the book's mid-line capitalization "He pulled" (Moses is the subject; verbatim per the extraction-artifacts fleeting).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`khatm` (seal):** sealing the heart as the consequence of sustained rejection.
  * **Rendering:** "seal up their hearts" `[7:100]`; "seals the hearts of the disbelievers" `[7:101]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:7]` "Allah has set a seal on their hearts and on their hearing"; `[16:108]` "sealed their hearts, and their hearing, and their sight... they are the heedless"; `[63:3]` "they believed, and then disbelieved; so their hearts were sealed" — the cluster marks sealing as *post-knowledge* defection, exactly 7:101's logic.
* **`ayah` (sign/miracle):** the word for evidence in this confrontation.
  * **Rendering:** heavy drift inside one ruku: "clear signs" `[7:101]`, "Our miracles" `[7:103]`, "clear evidence" `[7:105]`, "a miracle" `[7:106]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[27:12]` "among nine miracles to Pharaoh and his people" — the only place the corpus counts Pharaoh's signs; `[7:73]` the she-camel "is a sign for you" (an earlier dossier using the same word).
* **`staff / white hand`:** the twin sign-package of the Moses commission.
  * **Rendering:** `[7:107]`–`[7:108]`, given in answer to a demand.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[20:18]`–`[20:20]` the staff at the burning-ground commission ("it became a moving serpent"); `[28:31]`–`[28:32]` the same two signs "to Pharaoh and his dignitaries"; `[27:12]` "white, without blemish" — the hand sign recurs in three commission scenes.
* **`rabb al-'alamin` (Lord of the Worlds):** Moses' credential line.
  * **Rendering:** "the Lord of the Worlds" `[7:104]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[1:2]` the Fatihah's opening address; `[26:16]` "We are the Messengers of the Lord of the Worlds"; within-surah drift: `[7:54]` renders the divine title "Lord of all beings."
