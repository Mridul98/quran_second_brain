# The first deception, the first repentance, and the descent (7:11–25)
**UID:** 202610141422
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 7:11–25 (Ruku 2 of 24 in al-A’raf)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[7:11]` – `[7:25]` (15 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah's Adam-drama: creation honored by a command to bow, refused by an arrogance of origin ("I am better than he"), answered by a sworn ambush on God's own straight path — and then the Garden scene where the same swearer, now posing as a "sincere advisor," strips the first humans of covering, only for them to answer with the surah's prototype prayer: "Our Lord, we have done wrong to ourselves."
* **Placement:** Direct payoff of 7:10 ("We have established you firmly on earth"): the descent (7:24–25) is the origin story of that earth-station. The clothing motif started here (nakedness/leaves, 7:20–22) is completed in ruku 3's garment theology (`[7:26]`).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[7:11]`** "We created you, then We shaped you, then We said to the angels, “Bow down before Adam;” so they bowed down, except for Satan; he was not of those who bowed down."
  * **Gloss:** The address slides to first person plural ("We created YOU") — the whole species is present in its father's honor; one refuser breaks the ranks.
* **`[7:12]`** "He said, “What prevented you from bowing down when I have commanded you?” He said, “I am better than he; You created me from fire, and You created him from mud.”"
  * **Gloss:** The first heresy: a material-comparison argument against a divine command — origin traded for rank.
* **`[7:13]`** "He said, “Get down from it! It is not for you to act arrogantly in it. Get out! You are one of the lowly!”"
  * **Gloss:** Arrogance (kibr) named as the crime; the downward verb (ihbit) begins the surah's descent vocabulary.
* **`[7:14]`–`[7:15]`** "He said, “Give me respite, until the Day they are resurrected.”" / "He said, “You are of those given respite.”"
  * **Gloss:** The reprieve is granted — and will be granted again verbatim-style at `[7:183]`; the enemy's leash is part of the test design.
* **`[7:16]`** "He said, “Because you have lured me, I will waylay them on Your straight path." *(quote left open in the corpus — book typography [sic])*
  * **Gloss:** The ambush declaration, blasphemously possessive — "YOUR straight path": he will patrol God's own road.
* **`[7:17]`** "Then I will come at them from before them, and from behind them, and from their right, and from their left; and you will not find most of them appreciative.”"
  * **Gloss:** Four directions (the missing below/above is exegetically noted but not Quranic — *Source: agent inference — verify*); his predicted harvest is the ungrateful majority, echoing 7:10's "rarely do you give thanks."
* **`[7:18]`** "He said, “Get out of it, despised and vanquished. Whoever among them follows you—I will fill up Hell with you all." *(open quote [sic])*
  * **Gloss:** The verdict doubles as the motive for every subsequent prophet-narrative: this is the war the towns in ruku 4–12 are losing.
* **`[7:19]`** "And you, Adam, inhabit the Garden, you and your wife, and eat whatever you wish; but do not approach this tree, lest you become sinners.”"
  * **Gloss:** Permission with one boundary-line ("do not approach" — not even near); the dress of the Garden before the fall.
* **`[7:20]`** "But Satan whispered to them, to reveal to them their nakedness, which was invisible to them. He said, “Your Lord has only forbidden you this tree, lest you become angels, or become immortals.”"
  * **Gloss:** The whisper weaponizes ambition: the prohibition is rebranded as jealousy of human ascension.
* **`[7:21]`** "And he swore to them, “I am a sincere advisor to you.”"
  * **Gloss:** Deception's signature move — an oath attached to counterfeit counsel; the prophets will claim the real version ("a trustworthy adviser," `[7:68]`).
* **`[7:22]`** — three clause-chunks:
  * "So he lured them with deceit. And when they tasted the tree, their nakedness became evident to them, and they began covering themselves with the leaves of the Garden."
  * "And their Lord called out to them, “Did I not forbid you from this tree, and say to you that Satan is a sworn enemy to you?”"
  * **Gloss:** The wage of the deal: transparency of shame, hand-made coverings; God's call is a reminder that the enemy had been pre-disclosed.
* **`[7:23]`** "They said, “Our Lord, we have done wrong to ourselves. Unless You forgive us, and have mercy on us, we will be among the losers.”"
  * **Gloss:** The fall's aftermath done right: confession, self-blame, petition — the surah's repentance template (compare 7:151, 7:156).
* **`[7:24]`–`[7:25]`** "He said, “Fall, some of you enemies to one another. On earth you will have residence and livelihood for a while.”" / "He said, “In it you will live, and in it you will die, and from it you will be brought out.”"
  * **Gloss:** The descent sentence fixes the terms: enmity, temporary residence, and resurrection out of the same earth.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`sujud` (bowing down):** the honor-command to all angels.
  * **Rendering:** "Bow down before Adam" (`[7:11]`, `[7:12]`).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:34]`, `[20:116]`, `[17:61]`, `[18:50]` — the command scene retold at least four more times (cf. also 15 and 38 Adam-dramas); 7:11 uniquely frames it as addressed to "you" (the whole species).
* **`inẓār` (respite):** the granted delay to the Day.
  * **Rendering:** "Give me respite" / "You are of those given respite" (`[7:14]–[7:15]`).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[68:45]` "And I will give them respite. My plan is firm" (same words Allah says of the rejecters at `[7:183]`); `[3:178]` — respite as accumulation of sin, not favor.
* **`ghurūr` (deceit/luring):** Iblis's declared method.
  * **Rendering:** "Because you have lured me" (`[7:16]`); "So he lured them with deceit" (`[7:22]`) — the same verb lures the lurer first, then the pair.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[15:39]` "since You have lured me away... I will lure them all away"; `[31:33]` "nor let illusions deceive you regarding Allah" — the Deceiver's trade-name; `[2:36]` "Satan caused them to slip from it."
* **"sworn enemy" (`aduww mubīn`):** the pre-disclosed status of Satan.
  * **Rendering:** "Satan is a sworn enemy to you" (`[7:22]`).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[36:60]` — covenant-addressed version: "Did I not covenant with you, O Children of Adam, that you shall not serve the devil? That he is your sworn enemy?"; `[12:5]`.

* **Path motif:** `[1:6]` — the petition "Guide us to the straight path" is answered here from the enemy's side: it is precisely that path he swore to ambush (7:16).
