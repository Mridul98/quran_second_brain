# Am I not your Lord? — and the dog that pants either way (7:172–181)
**UID:** 202610141442
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 7:172–181 (Ruku 22 of 24 in al-A'raf)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[7:172]` – `[7:181]` (10 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah digs under all its covenant stories to the covenant before history — every descendant of Adam summoned and made to testify "Am I not your Lord?" (7:172) — so that no one can plead ancestry or ignorance (7:173); and against that backdrop the dog parable cuts: a man *given* the signs sheds them, clings to the ground, and pants whatever you do to him (7:175–177) — guidance and loss are then handed to God's discretion (7:178), the heedless sorted into cattle-worse (7:179), the Beautiful Names commanded (7:180), and the truth-guiding community re-appeared, this time among all creation (7:181).
* **Placement:** The theological bedrock of the closing movement: 7:172 is the covenant note's primordial layer, 7:175–177 the surah's bleakest individual case-study, and 7:181's "community... guide by truth" is the exact twin of 7:159 — the ruku sits at the center of a deliberate within-surah mirror pair.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[7:172]`** "And when Your Lord summoned the descendants of Adam, and made them testify about themselves. “Am I not your Lord?” They said, “Yes, we testify.” Thus you cannot say on the Day of Resurrection, “We were unaware of this.”"
  * **Gloss:** Pre-creation roll-call; testimony extracted so that ignorance is forever closed as a defense — tawhid as memory, not argument.
* **`[7:173]`** "Nor can you Say, “Our ancestors practiced idolatry before; and we are their descendants who came after them; will you destroy us for what the falsifiers did?”"
  * **Gloss:** The second excuse — inherited shirk — also pre-empted; (book typography: mid-line capital "Say", quoted verbatim).
* **`[7:174]`** "We thus elaborate the revelations, so that they may return."
  * **Gloss:** Explanation exists for the sake of return — the surah's own rationale for its repetition.
* **`[7:175]`** "And relate to them the story of him to whom We delivered Our signs, but he detached himself from them, so Satan went after him, and he became one of the perverts."
  * **Gloss:** The unnamed man: signs *delivered* (given into his keeping), then shed; the word "perverts" marks the turn as deviation, not mere error.
* **`[7:176]`** "Had We willed, We could have elevated him through them; but he clung to the ground, and followed his desires. His metaphor is that of a dog: if you chase it, it pants; and if you leave it alone, it pants. Such is the metaphor of the people who deny Our signs. So tell the tale, so that they may ponder."
  * **Gloss:** The signs were his lift; he chose gravity; the dog parable — panting under pursuit and under rest alike — images a soul whose agitation is self-generated, then generalizes: "the people who deny Our signs."
* **`[7:177]`** "Evil is the metaphor of the people who reject Our signs and wrong themselves."
  * **Gloss:** The verdict clause: the metaphor is evil, and the rejection is self-harm (echo of 7:23's "wrong to ourselves" formula).
* **`[7:178]`** "Whomever Allah guides is the guided one. And whomever He sends astray—these are the losers."
  * **Gloss:** Guidance and misguidance rest with God; the only loser-class is the self-sent-astray.
* **`[7:179]`** "We have destined for Hell multitudes of jinn and humans. They have hearts with which they do not understand. They have eyes with which they do not see. They have ears with which they do not hear. These are like cattle. In fact, they are further astray. These are the heedless."
  * **Gloss:** The organs exist; the use is forfeit — cattle-simile plus "further astray," the surah's lowest anthropological grade.
* **`[7:180]`** "To Allah belong the Most Beautiful Names, so call Him by them, and disregard those who blaspheme His names. They will be repaid for what they used to do."
  * **Gloss:** The liturgical answer to 7:179's deaf ears: call by the Names; leave the name-blasphemers to repayment.
* **`[7:181]`** "Among those We created is a community—they guide by truth, and do justice thereby."
  * **Gloss:** The universal-community clause mirroring 7:159 ("Among the people of Moses...") — inside Israel a remnant; inside creation, a nation.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **"Am I not your Lord?":** the primordial question `[7:172]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[6:130]` — on that Day the assembly of jinn and humans is asked, and answers "We testify against ourselves" — the courtroom scene inverted: testimony given at birth vs testimony forced at the end (verified).
* **"detached himself" (the shedding):** `[7:175]` "he detached himself from them."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[63:3]` "they believed, and then disbelieved; so their hearts were sealed, and they cannot understand" (verified) — the only corpus line whose *mechanism* (post-knowledge defection) matches 7:175 exactly.
* **"dog":** "His metaphor is that of a dog" `[7:176]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** the word dog appears at only three corpus sites: `[18:18]` "their dog stretching its paws across the threshold" (with `[18:22]` counting it into the sleepers' number) and `[5:4]` "what trained dogs and falcons catch for you" (verified); the cave-dog shares the company of the righteous, the trained dog shares the lawful hunt — only 7:176 makes the animal a censored metaphor (the image-ambiguity is corpus-observable; classical interpretation of the panting dog is not).
* **"like cattle... further astray":** `[7:179]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[25:44]` "They are just like cattle, but even more errant in their way" — same graded simile (verified); hearts/eyes/ears triple: `[22:46]` "It is not the eyes that go blind, but it is the hearts, within the chests" (verified), `[47:24]` "locks upon their hearts" (verified).
* **"the Most Beautiful Names":** `[7:180]`.
  * **Rendering:** `[20:8]` "His are the Most Beautiful Names" (exact same phrase); `[59:24]` same phrase; `[17:110]` renders the same title "the Best Names" — observable drift (all sites verified).
