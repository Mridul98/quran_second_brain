# Who forbade Allah's finery? The forbidden list and the great lie (7:32–39)
**UID:** 202610141424
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 7:32–39 (Ruku 4 of 24 in al-A’raf)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[7:32]` – `[7:39]` (8 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The ruku detonates the self-made prohibitions of religion — "Who forbade Allah's finery?" — and replaces them with the real forbidden list (indecency, sin, aggression, partnerless-partners, saying about Allah what is not known); then the hourglass turns: every nation has an appointed time, the messenger test is announced for all of Adam's children, and the scene cuts to the Fire where the lie-inventors, jinn and human crowds, curse each other in chains of precedence.
* **Placement:** Completion of ruku 3's dress/appetite argument (7:31 → 7:32 is one breath across the ruku boundary); 7:35–36 generalize the messenger-theme that the prophet-cycle (ruku 8–11) will dramatize; 7:38's "first and last" blaming session pre-stages 7:44ff's dialogues between the two homes.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[7:32]`** "Say, “Who forbade Allah’s finery which He has produced for His servants, and the delights of livelihood?” Say, “They are for those who believe, in this present world, but exclusively theirs on the Day of Resurrection.” We thus detail the revelations for people who know."
  * **Gloss:** The rhetorical blast against invented ascetic bans; the good things are shared now but esoterically owned by the believers later — luxury gets an eschatology.
* **`[7:33]`** "Say, “My Lord has forbidden immoralities—both open and secret—and sin, and unjustified aggression, and that you associate with Allah anything for which He revealed no sanction, and that you say about Allah what you do not know.”"
  * **Gloss:** The real ḥarām list, five rungs from manners to shirk to theology — ending on the same crime as 7:28, "say about Allah what you do not know."
* **`[7:34]`** "For every nation is an appointed time. When their time has come, they cannot delay it by one hour, nor can they advance it."
  * **Gloss:** The deadline clause that paces the rest of the surah's town-narratives; twin verbatim at `[10:49]`.
* **`[7:35]`** "O Children of Adam! When messengers from among you come to you, relating to you My revelations—whoever practices piety and reforms—upon them shall be no fear, nor shall they grieve."
  * **Gloss:** The fourth "O Children of Adam" — the universal rule of the messenger-cycle, with the Quran's no-fear-no-grieve formula as its wage.
* **`[7:36]`** "But as for those who reject Our revelations, and are too proud to accept them—these are the inmates of the Fire, where they will remain forever."
  * **Gloss:** The counter-rule; "too proud" (istakbarā) picks up Iblis's own crime-word from 7:13 and passes it to every rejector.
* **`[7:37]`** "Who does greater wrong than he who invents lies about Allah, or denies His revelations? These—their share of the decree will reach them. Until, when Our envoys come to them, to take their souls away, they will say, “Where are they whom you used to pray to besides Allah?” They will say, “They have abandoned us,” and they will testify against themselves that they were faithless."
  * **Gloss:** The lie-inventors' file closes in three frames: their allotted portion runs out, the death-envoys ask where the partners are, and the partners are declared absent — self-incriminating testimony.
* **`[7:38]`** "He will say, “Join the crowds of jinn and humans who have gone into the Fire before you.” Every time a crowd enters, it will curse its sister-crowd. Until, when they are all in it, the last of them will say to the first of them, “Our Lord, these are the ones who misled us, so inflict on them a double punishment in the Fire.” He will say, “Each will have a double, but you do not know.”"
  * **Gloss:** The chain of follow-my-leader: jinn-and-human convoys cursing each other; the followers demand double, and double is granted — to both ranks.
* **`[7:39]`** "The first of them will say to the last of them, “You have no advantage over us, so taste the torment for what you used to earn.”"
  * **Gloss:** Precedence is void in the Fire: the leaders tell the followers they earned the same — each soul for its own earning (cf. 6:164).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **"finery" and "delights of livelihood":** Allah's produced goods.
  * **Rendering:** "Allah’s finery which He has produced for His servants, and the delights of livelihood" (`[7:32]`).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[5:87]` "Do not prohibit the good things Allah has permitted for you" — the law-side twin of this Meccan question; `[7:26]`'s "luxury" continues the same goods-vocabulary across the ruku boundary.
* **"no sanction":** the authority requirement for any prohibition.
  * **Rendering:** "no sanction" (`[7:33]`); the same concept-phrase appears as "no authority" at `[7:71]` (Hud), `[12:40]`, `[53:23]` — likely one Arabic word with two English renderings (*agent inference — verify*).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[3:151]` "partners for which He revealed no sanction" — verbatim twin outside this surah.
* **`ajal` (appointed time):** the national deadline.
  * **Rendering:** "an appointed time" (`[7:34]`).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[10:49]` verbatim ("they can neither postpone it by one hour, nor advance it"); `[16:61]`, `[18:59]`, `[54:46]`, `[44:40]` — the term paces every destruction narrative.
* **`iftirāʾ` (inventing lies about Allah):** the surah's chief crime.
  * **Rendering:** "invents lies about Allah" (`[7:37]`), "fabricating falsehood against Allah" (`[7:89]`), "attributing to Allah what you do not know" (`[7:28]`).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[6:21]`, `[10:17]`, `[29:68]`, `[18:15]` — the identical "Who does greater wrong than..." superlative; `[6:93]` widens it to claiming fake revelation.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Meccan; the finery/prohibition argument reads against both invented tribal taboos and over-ascetic tendencies — *Source: classical tafsir situation-reports on 7:32 (circumambulation in nakedness, self-imposed dietary bans — Tabari/Ibn Kathir genre) — verify before teaching details.*
* **Structural fact (observable):** 7:33 and 7:37 both close on "say about Allah what you do not know" / "invent lies about Allah" — the ruku's envelope is the speech-crime, the same envelope the whole surah keeps returning to (7:28, 7:89).
