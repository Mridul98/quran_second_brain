# The Elevations: a needle, a partition, and rancor removed (7:40–47)
**UID:** 202610141425
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 7:40–47 (Ruku 5 of 24 in al-A’raf)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[7:40]` – `[7:47]` (8 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah draws the map of the two homes and then discovers a third address on the map: the Elevations (al-A‘rāf) — men between, who know everyone by their features, greet the Garden's people with peace while not yet entering, and pray to be kept off the Fire's side of the partition; the section opens with the surah's most quoted impossibility (camels and needles) and closes its middle with rancor surgically removed from the Garden's hearts.
* **Placement:** The title-word of the surah appears here for the first time (7:46, 7:48 — the name "THE ELEVATIONS" is this edition's surah header); the two-homes dialogue set up by 7:36–39 (ruku 4) now gets its geography, continuing into ruku 6.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[7:40]`** "Those who reject Our revelations and are too arrogant to uphold them—the doors of Heaven will not be opened for them, nor will they enter Paradise, until the camel passes through the eye of the needle. Thus We repay the guilty."
  * **Gloss:** Two locked doors — heaven's gate and paradise's gate — with an impossibility clause as the key; kibr (7:13 again) is the bolt.
* **`[7:41]`** "For them is a couch of hell, and above them are sheets of fire. Thus We repay the wrongdoers."
  * **Gloss:** Hospitality imagery inverted: a bed and drapes, made of fire.
* **`[7:42]`** "As for those who believe and do righteous works—We never burden any soul beyond its capacity—these are the inhabitants of the Garden; abiding therein eternally."
  * **Gloss:** The law of moral arithmetic — no demand exceeds the soul — inserted mid-picture as reassurance.
* **`[7:43]`** "We will remove whatever rancor is in their hearts. Rivers will flow beneath them. And they will say, “Praise be to Allah, who has guided us to this. Had Allah not guided us, we would never be guided. The messengers of our Lord did come with the truth.” And it will be proclaimed to them, “This is the Garden you are made to inherit, on account of what you used to do.”"
  * **Gloss:** The Garden's first surgery is relational (rancor out), its first doxology is anti-self-credit ("Had Allah not guided us, we would never be guided"), and its deed-note is a gift ("made to inherit").
* **`[7:44]`** "And the inhabitants of the Garden will call out to the inmates of the Fire, “We found what our Lord promised us to be true; did you find what your Lord promised you to be true?” They will say, “Yes.” Thereupon a caller will announce in their midst, “The curse of Allah is upon the wrongdoers.”"
  * **Gloss:** The verification question across the partition, and the one-word answer ("Yes") that costs everything; a public curse-call interrupts.
* **`[7:45]`** "Those who hinder from the path of Allah, and seek to distort it, and who deny the Hereafter."
  * **Gloss:** The accused are charged by profile — road-blockers, path-distorters, Hereafter-deniers (same two crimes as Shuaib's people, 7:86).
* **`[7:46]`** "And between them is a partition, and on the Elevations are men who recognize everyone by their features. They will call to the inhabitants of the Garden, “Peace be upon you.” They have not entered it, but they are hoping."
  * **Gloss:** The surah's namesake image: a barrier, an elevation above it, and men who know everybody's faces, greet with salām, and hang in hope on the threshold.
* **`[7:47]`** "And when their eyes are directed towards the inmates of the Fire, they will say, “Our Lord, do not place us among the wrongdoing people.”"
  * **Gloss:** The Elevations-men look the other way and pray — suspense as a permanent state; they are not safe, and say so.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **"the Elevations" (al-A‘rāf):** the height between the two homes.
  * **Rendering:** "on the Elevations are men" (`[7:46]`), "the dwellers of the Elevations" (`[7:48]`) — this English word occurs nowhere else in the corpus (verified by `rg`), making the ruku the visible core of the surah's name.
  * **Elsewhere:** the root ‘-r-f (heights) is classically read as the tallest place of the barrier; identities of its men differ in classical tafsir — *Source: classical disagreement (Tabari-type collection; e.g. "equal in deeds") — verify.*
* **"eye of the needle":** the impossibility clause.
  * **Rendering:** "until the camel passes through the eye of the needle" (`[7:40]`) — unique in the corpus (`rg "needle"` returns only this line).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[15:14–15]` — "Even if We opened for them a gateway into the sky, and they began to ascend through it. They would still say, 'Our eyes are hallucinating'" — the same closed-sky image, but the arrogance refuses even open doors.
* **`saymāʾ` (features):** recognition marks.
  * **Rendering:** "recognize everyone by their features" (`[7:46]`), "men they recognize by their features" (`[7:48]`).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:273]` "You will recognize them by their features" — the only other corpus use of the phrase-pattern (there, for the unassuming poor) — a knowing that persists after all faces change.
* **"beyond its capacity" (wus‘):** the mercy clause.
  * **Rendering:** "We never burden any soul beyond its capacity" (`[7:42]`).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[23:62]` verbatim twin ("We never burden any soul beyond its capacity. And with Us is a record that tells the truth"); `[2:286]`, `[6:152]`, `[65:7]` — the same sentence in law, prayer, and charity contexts.

## 4. Cross-References (The Web)
* **Garden doxology:** `[7:43]` "Had Allah not guided us, we would never be guided" — the purest guidance-as-grace confession in the corpus (pairs with the Guidance knowledge note); its this-worldly mirror is 7:30's split, "Some He has guided, and some have deserved misguidance."
* **Inheritance formula:** `[43:72]` — "Such is the Garden you are made to inherit, because of what you used to do" — verbatim twin of 7:43's proclamation.
* **Rancor twin:** `[15:47]` — "We will remove all ill-feelings from their hearts—brothers and sisters, on couches" — the same surgery; note the couch-inversion: 7:41's couch of hell vs 15:47's couches of peace.
* **Within surah:** `[7:36]` → `[7:40]` — "reject Our revelations" twice in five verses, now with arrogance spelled out; `[7:38]` → `[7:44]` — the cursing crowds of ruku 4 answer the curse-call here; `[7:45]` → `[7:86]` — "seek to distort it" is Shuaib's people's exact crime (ruku 11).
* **Hope on the threshold:** `[26:129]` — "you set up fortresses, hoping to live forever?" — "hoping" is the same English rendering for Thamud's deluded hope and the Elevations-men's patient hope (whether one Arabic root stands behind both is *agent inference — verify*); the difference is the object of hope.
* **Capacity + record:** `[23:62]` cited above; `[2:286]` the community's own prayer-version of 7:42's clause.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Meccan — the two-homes/eschatological-map passages are the surah's Meccan spine, placed between the Adam-fall and the prophet-cycle to frame every following town's story as a choice of addresses.
* **Interpretive caution:** who exactly the "men of the Elevations" are is not resolved by the translation; the balanced-deeds reading and the martyrs' reading are classical positions — *Source: classical tafsir disagreement — verify; the text itself supplies only: partition, features, greeting, hope, and prayer (observable).*
