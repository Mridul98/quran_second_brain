# Hud to Aad: names with no authority, stature with no thanks (7:65–72)
**UID:** 202610141429
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 7:65–72 (Ruku 9 of 24 in al-A’raf)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[7:65]` – `[7:72]` (8 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The template repeats with one variable — history: Hud reminds Aad that they are Noah's successors, upgraded in stature, and the crowd's whole theology collapses into ancestor-names ("what our ancestors used to worship"), which Hud answers with the surah's sharpest creed-test: names your forebears invented, "for which Allah sent down no authority."
* **Placement:** Second destruction-narrative (after Noah, ruku 8); its successor-argument (7:69) converts 7:10's earth-firming into genealogy, and its ancestor-excuse re-primes 7:28's "we found our parents doing this" for the towns' final round (7:95).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[7:65]`** "And to Aad, their brother Hud. He said, “O my people! Worship Allah; you have no god other than Him. Will you not take heed?”"
  * **Gloss:** "Their brother" — the messenger is kin; the refrain from 7:59 returns, now with the taqwa-question ("Will you not take heed?").
* **`[7:66]`** "The elite of his people who disbelieved said, “We see foolishness in you, and we think that you are a liar.”"
  * **Gloss:** The establishment's two-step: diagnose (safāhah, "foolishness"), then verdict (liar) — same script as 7:60.
* **`[7:67]`** "He said, “O my people! There is no foolishness in me, but I am a messenger from the Lord of the Worlds." *(open quote in corpus [sic])*
  * **Gloss:** Denial of the diagnosis, assertion of the identity — verbatim 7:61's defense.
* **`[7:68]`** "“I convey to you the messages of my Lord, and I am a trustworthy adviser to you." *(stray open quote [sic])*
  * **Gloss:** The delivery-plus-nasiha pair again (7:62), but the word for trustworthy (amīn) is added — the honest adviser sworn by the counterfeit at 7:21.
* **`[7:69]`** "“Are you surprised that a reminder has come to you from your Lord, through a man from among you, to warn you? Remember how He made you successors after the people of Noah, and increased you greatly in stature. And remember Allah’s blessings, so that you may prosper.”"
  * **Gloss:** The wonder-question plus the genealogy: succession after Noah, physical supremacy — remember it as blessing, not birthright.
* **`[7:70]`** "They said, “Did you come to us to make us worship Allah alone, and abandon what our ancestors used to worship? Then bring us what you threaten us with, if you are truthful.”"
  * **Gloss:** Their tell: worship-alone is the offense; the ancestor-appeal plus the death-dare ("bring us what you threaten") — the dare that recurs at 7:77 and 29:29.
* **`[7:71]`** "He said, “Condemnation and wrath have befallen you from your Lord. Are you arguing with me over names, which you and your ancestors invented, for which Allah sent down no authority? Just wait; I am waiting with you.”"
  * **Gloss:** The verdict pre-announced, then the creed-test: invented names with zero downloaded authority (sulṭān) — and the prophet's strange last weapon: I'll wait with you.
* **`[7:72]`** "So We saved him and those with him, by mercy from Us, and We cut off the roots of those who rejected Our revelations and were not believers."
  * **Gloss:** The cycle's closing move: mercy-rescue, and a root-and-stem ending ("cut off the roots") for the rejectors.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **"names...invented...no authority":** the shirk-diagnosis.
  * **Rendering:** 7:71 "names, which you and your ancestors invented, for which Allah sent down no authority."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[12:40]` — "names you have named, you and your ancestors, for which Allah has sent down no authority" (Joseph's verbatim twin); `[53:23]` — "nothing but names, which you have devised...no authority" — the three-verse dossier on idol-naming; within-surah variant rendering "no sanction" at `[7:33]` (*same Arabic word likely — agent inference — verify*).
* **"successors" (khalāʾif):** the history-argument.
  * **Rendering:** "made you successors after the people of Noah" (`[7:69]`), "successors after Aad" (`[7:74]`, Saleh).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[6:165]` "made you successors on the earth...in order to test you"; `[10:14]` "made you successors on earth after them, to see how you would behave"; `[27:62]`, `[35:39]` — succession is always a test, never a trophy; answers 7:10's "established you firmly".
* **"foolishness" (safāhah):** the elite's insult-currency.
  * **Rendering:** "We see foolishness in you" (`[7:66]`) / "There is no foolishness in me" (`[7:67]`).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:13]` — "Shall we believe as the fools have believed? In fact, it is they who are the fools" — the same word at the Medinan hypocrite-front; `[72:4]` "the fools among us used to say nonsense about Allah."
* **"stature" (bastah fī l-khalq):** Aad's signature blessing.
  * **Rendering:** "increased you greatly in stature" (`[7:69]`).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:247]` "increased him in knowledge and stature" (Saul) — the noun used for bodily and rank advantage; pair with 7:74's mansions-and-mountains architecture (within-surah).

## 4. Cross-References (The Web)
* **Succession doublet:** `[10:73]` — after the Noah drowning, "We made them successors" — 7:69 cites precisely this chain; succession theology links ruku 8→9.
