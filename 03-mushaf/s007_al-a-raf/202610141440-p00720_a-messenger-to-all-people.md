# Say, O people: the universal call and the twelve springs (7:158–162)
**UID:** 202610141440
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 7:158–162 (Ruku 20 of 24 in al-A'raf)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[7:158]` – `[7:162]` (5 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The dossier's private history becomes a public address — "O people, I am the Messenger of Allah to you all" — and immediately the camera cuts to Israel's desert record as a case study in universal provision and local breach: a truth-guiding community inside Moses' people (7:159), twelve springs each knowing its draw, manna, quails, cloud-shade (7:160), and one town that was told to enter humbly and instead swapped the words and ate a plague from the sky (7:161–162).
* **Placement:** The pivot out of the Moses-only narrative (7:103–157) toward the surah's own mission claim (7:158) — the Meccan surah's Medinan-scope moment; 7:160–162 pre-loads the Sabbath-town interrogation (7:163) with the theme of provision-answered-by-substitution.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[7:158]`** "Say, “O people, I am the Messenger of Allah to you all—He to whom belongs the kingdom of the heavens and the earth. There is no god but He. He gives life and causes death.” So believe in Allah and His Messenger, the Unlettered Prophet, who believes in Allah and His words. And follow him, that you may be guided."
  * **Gloss:** The corpus's clearest universal-mission declaration inside a Meccan-length surah: "to you all," grounded in kingdom, lone godhead, life-and-death, and closed by the follow-me-for-guidance clause.
* **`[7:159]`** "Among the people of Moses is a community that guides by truth, and thereby does justice."
  * **Gloss:** A faithful remnant inside the failed majority — the near-identical twin of 7:181 ("Among those We created..."), one inside Israel, one cosmic.
* **`[7:160]`** "We divided them into twelve tribal communities. And We inspired Moses, when his people asked him for something to drink: “Strike the rock with your staff.” Whereupon twelve springs gushed from it. Each group recognized its drinking-place. And We shaded them with clouds, and We sent down upon them manna and quails: “Eat of the good things We have provided for you.” They did not wrong Us, but they used to wrong their own selves."
  * **Gloss:** Provision in four registers (water, shade, meat, order); the closing clause is the surah's theodicy formula — the wrong returns to the wrongdoer, never to God.
* **`[7:161]`** "And it was said to them, “Settle this town, and eat therein whatever you wish, and speak modestly, and enter the gate in humility— We will forgive your sins, and will promote the righteous.”"
  * **Gloss:** The offer: abundance plus forgiveness on three conditions — settle, speak humbly ("speak modestly"), enter low — verbatim family of 2:58's "enter the gate humbly, and say, 'Pardon'".
* **`[7:162]`** "But the wicked among them substituted other words for the words given to them; so We sent down upon them a plague from the sky, because of their wrongdoing."
  * **Gloss:** Word-substitution as the crime — the same act and same punishment pair as 2:59; the desert mercy arc ends in a sky-strike.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **"to you all" (universal claim):** `[7:158]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[7:172]` (next ruku) — the address to *all* descendants of Adam grounds the universal mission in the primordial covenant (observable that the surah moves "O people" → "Your Lord summoned the descendants of Adam" within four verses).
* **"guides by truth, and thereby does justice":** `[7:159]`.
  * **Rendering/Elsewhere:** `[7:181]` "they guide by truth, and do justice thereby" — near-identical twin, one clause-order flip (verified within surah); `[11:116]` "people with wisdom, who spoke against corruption on earth—except for the few whom We saved" — the remnant motif's kin; `[3:104]` "a community calling to virtue, and advocating righteousness... These are the successful" — the ummah's version of the same community-spec.
* **"substituted other words":** `[7:162]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:59]` "the wrongdoers among them substituted words other than those given to them, so We sent down... a plague from heaven" — the tightest verbal twin in the corpus for this pair of verses (verified); within surah, `[7:139]` "their deeds are based on falsehoods" and `[7:148]` the calf as an adopted substitute — substitution is the dossier's recurring verb.
* **"the good things We have provided":** `[7:160]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:57]` verbatim-twin including cloud-shade, manna, quails and the same "did not wrong Us" close (verified); `[20:80]` "We have delivered you from your enemy... sent down to you manna and quails" — Ta-Ha's register; drift: "plague from the sky" (7:162) vs "plague from heaven" (2:59) — same event-word rendered two ways (observable).
