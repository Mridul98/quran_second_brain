# Ask them about the town by the sea (7:163–171)
**UID:** 202610141441
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 7:163–171 (Ruku 21 of 24 in al-A'raf)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[7:163]` – `[7:171]` (9 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The Sabbath town is a three-lane road: the violators become apes (7:163–166), the enforcers of good are saved (7:165), and a third party *argues about the futility of admonition* (7:164) — then the ruku zooms out to the whole Israelite ledger: scattering, testing, successors who grab the world's materials while saying "We will be forgiven" (7:169), the Scripture-holders who pray and are promised no wasted reward (7:170), and the mountain held above them like an umbrella (7:171).
* **Placement:** The interrogation ruku ("Ask them," 7:163) turns the surah's Israel material into evidence *presented to the Prophet's audience*; it closes the covenant-enforcement theme (7:159–162 provision → 7:163–171 test) right before the primordial covenant itself (7:172).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[7:163]`** "Ask them about the town by the sea, when they violated the Sabbath. When they observed the Sabbath, their fish would come to them abundantly. But when they violated the Sabbath, their fish would not come to them. Thus We tried them because they disobeyed."
  * **Gloss:** The test is calibrated: fish appear only on the day of prohibition — abundance as an obedience instrument, not a blessing.
* **`[7:164]`** "And when a group of them said, “Why do you counsel a people whom Allah will annihilate, or punish with a severe punishment?” They said, “As an excuse to your Lord, and so that they may become righteous.”"
  * **Gloss:** The internal debate: admonish the doomed? — the answer is twofold, discharge of duty ("an excuse to your Lord") plus hope of repentance.
* **`[7:165]`** "Then, when they neglected what they were reminded of, We saved those who prohibited evil, and We seized those who did wrong with a terrible punishment, because of their sinfulness."
  * **Gloss:** The three groups resolve: the warners saved, the wrongdoers seized — the saved class is named purely by what they forbade.
* **`[7:166]`** "Then, when they rebelled against the commands to refrain, We said to them, “Be despicable apes.”"
  * **Gloss:** The transformation word-for-word twin of 2:65 ("Be despicable apes!") — rebellion-after-restraint is the trigger in both tellings.
* **`[7:167]`** "Your Lord has announced that, He would send against them, until the Day of Resurrection, those who would inflict terrible suffering upon them. Your Lord is swift in retribution, yet He is Forgiving and Merciful."
  * **Gloss:** A standing sentence through history — and the surah refuses to end it without naming mercy in the same breath.
* **`[7:168]`** "And We scattered them into communities on earth. Some of them righteous, and some of them short of that. And We tested them with fortunes and misfortunes, so that they may return."
  * **Gloss:** Dispersion as discipline: mixed quality by design, alternating pressure with one aim — return.
* **`[7:169]`** "They were succeeded by generations who inherited the Scripture and chose the materials of this world, saying, “We will be forgiven.” And should similar materials come their way, they would again seize them. Did they not make a covenant to uphold the Scripture, and to not say about Allah except the truth? Did they not study its contents? But the Home of the Hereafter is better for the cautious; will you not understand?"
  * **Gloss:** The heirs take the Book and the merchandise simultaneously, front-running repentance ("We will be forgiven") — and the verse cross-examines them with their own covenant clause.
* **`[7:170]`** "Those who adhere to the Scripture, and practice prayer—We will not waste the reward of the reformers."
  * **Gloss:** The counter-portfolio: hold + pray; the payoff word is *muslihun* (reformers), the surah's own highest label.
* **`[7:171]`** "And when We suspended the mountain over them, as if it was an umbrella, and they thought it would fall on them: “Hold fast to what We have given you, and remember what it contains, so that you may be saved.”"
  * **Gloss:** The covenant sealed under a lifted mountain — and the command is doubled: hold *and remember what is in it*.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **"violated the Sabbath":** `[7:163]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:65]` "those of you who violated the Sabbath. We said to them, 'Be despicable apes!'" — the phrase and the sentence occur only here and there (verified two-site pair); `[5:60]` "He turned some of them into apes, and swine, and idol worshipers" — the third telling, where the curse-list is widened (verified).
* **"those who prohibited evil":** `[7:165]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[3:104]` "a community calling to virtue, and advocating righteousness, and deterring from evil. These are the successful"; `[11:116]` the few who "spoke against corruption on earth" — the saved remnant grammar (verified pair with 7:159/7:181, this surah's own community-specs).
* **"the materials of this world":** `[7:169]`.
  * **Rendering:** low-value goods greedily seized; the same event-word rendered "the gains" at `[48:15]` — "Those who lagged behind will say when you depart to collect the gains" (verified; also cited in the k-nifaq note).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[18:28]` "desiring the glitter of this world" — the same grabbing vocabulary in the cave-surah's company-ethic (verified).
* **"umbrella / suspended the mountain":** `[7:171]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:63]` "We received a pledge from you, and raised the Mount above you: 'Take what We have given you earnestly, and remember what is in it'" — same event, same twin-command (verified); `[2:93]` "We made a covenant with you, and raised the Mount above you" — the covenant-word added in Baqarah's version (observable: al-A'raf places the mountain-lifting *after* the town story, Baqarah inside Sinai-chronology).
