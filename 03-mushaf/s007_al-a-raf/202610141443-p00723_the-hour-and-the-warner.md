# Gradual lure, the Hour, and the warner's limits (7:182–188)
**UID:** 202610141443
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 7:182–188 (Ruku 23 of 24 in al-A'raf)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[7:182]` – `[7:188]` (7 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The ruku of measured retreat: rejection is answered not by lightning but by graduality — "We will gradually lead them from where they do not know" (7:182) — then three probes are driven into the audience (do they not think? observe? their friend has no madness, 7:184–185), the Hour's knowledge is locked entirely with Allah (7:187), and the warner's credentials are published with a negative: no control over benefit or harm, no knowledge of the future (7:188).
* **Placement:** The surah's eschatological hinge between the dog-parable block (ending 7:181) and the closing creation-to-recitation movement (7:189–206); 7:187's "It weighs heavily on the heavens and the earth" answers the "government of the heavens and the earth" gaze of 7:185, and both prepare the Hour language of the final ruku's listeners.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[7:182]`** "As for those who reject Our messages, We will gradually lead them from where they do not know."
  * **Gloss:** Istidraj — the most frightening patience in the book: destruction by instalments, imperceptible to the consumer.
* **`[7:183]`** "And I will encourage them. My plan is firm."
  * **Gloss:** First-person divine address (matching 7:156's register): respite is granted, and the stratagem is declared unbreakable.
* **`[7:184]`** "Do they not think? There is no madness in their friend. He is but a plain warner."
  * **Gloss:** The madness-slander — a sibling of the dossier's stock insults ("We see foolishness in you" `[7:66]`, "we think that you are a liar") — answered with the plainest credential: he is a warner, visibly.
* **`[7:185]`** "Have they not observed the government of the heavens and the earth, and all the things that Allah created, and that their time may have drawn near? Which message, besides this, will they believe in?"
  * **Gloss:** The cosmic argument (milk of Meccan kalam): sovereignty-observation plus death-proximity; the closing question makes this Book the final message.
* **`[7:186]`** "Whomever Allah misguides has no guide. And He leaves them blundering in their transgression."
  * **Gloss:** 7:178's symmetry completed: guidance secured from Allah, misguidance leaves no substitute guide (cf. 7:30's devil-patrons).
* **`[7:187]`** "They ask you about the Hour, “When will it come?” Say, “Knowledge of it rests with my Lord. None can reveal its coming except He. It weighs heavily on the heavens and the earth. It will not come upon you except suddenly.” They ask you as if you are responsible for it. Say, “Knowledge of it rests with Allah,” but most people do not know."
  * **Gloss:** The Hour-verse of this dossier: asked twice, answered twice — knowledge is God's, weight is the cosmos', timing is sudden, and the Prophet is not its guardian.
* **`[7:188]`** "Say, “I have no control over any benefit or harm to myself, except as Allah wills. Had I known the future, I would have acquired much good, and no harm would have touched me. I am only a warner, and a herald of good news to a people who believe.”"
  * **Gloss:** The warner's self-limitation charter: no self-profit engineering, no future-knowledge — proof by admission that he is not the source.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **"gradually lead them":** `[7:182]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[68:44]` "We will proceed against them gradually, from where they do not know" — the near-verbatim twin (verified); `[6:47]` "punishment descended on you suddenly or gradually" — the same adverb for divine strike-modes; inverted by `[17:106]`/`[76:23]` — the Quran itself "unfolded gradually"/"a gradual revelation" (verified): one root-family descends as cure on the attentive and as lure on the rejecters.
* **"the Hour" (al-saa'a):** asked about at `[7:187]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[79:42]`–`[79:43]` "They ask you about the Hour, 'When will it take place?' You have no knowledge of it" — the opening clause is a near-verbatim twin; `[33:63]` same question-form; `[31:34]` "With Allah rests the knowledge of the Hour" — the lock-verse; `[47:18]` "Are they just waiting until the Hour comes to them suddenly?" — "suddenly" as the standing mode (all verified).
* **"government of the heavens and the earth":** `[7:185]`.
  * **Rendering:** the corpus's odd-duck English for mulk here; the same domain rendered "kingdom" at `[7:158]` (previous ruku), "dominion" at `[9:116]`, and "sovereignty" at `[67:1]` (verified) — four English garments for one possession-claim across one idea.
* **"no control over benefit or harm":** `[7:188]` — the phrase occurs exactly here in the corpus (verified).
  * **Elsewhere (concept):** `[10:66]` — "Those who invoke other than Allah do not follow partners; they follow only assumptions" — the partners have no benefit/harm standing (verified); `[25:55]` "they serve what neither profits them nor harms them" (verified) — the same profit/harm test moved from the Prophet's humility to the idols' bankruptcy.
