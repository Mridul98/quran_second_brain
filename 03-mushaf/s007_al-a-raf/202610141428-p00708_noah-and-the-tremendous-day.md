# Noah: the one-god refrain and the tremendous Day (7:59–64)
**UID:** 202610141428
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 7:59–64 (Ruku 8 of 24 in al-A’raf)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[7:59]` – `[7:64]` (6 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The prophet-cycle begins with its template: a messenger "from among you" opens with the bare tawhid line ("Worship Allah; you have no god other than Him") and a dated fear ("a tremendous Day"); the dignitaries diagnose error; he re-identifies himself as messenger, advisor, and knower-of-the-unseen; they call him a liar and drown — while the ruku's real argument is 7:63's single wonder-question: why is a reminder from a man of your own species surprising?
* **Placement:** First of four destruction-narratives (Noah → Aad → Thamud → Lot, ruku 8–10; then Madyan, ruku 11) that dramatize exactly the messenger-rule stated at 7:35 (ruku 4) — the surah's thesis testing itself on record.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[7:59]`** "We sent Noah to his people. He said, “O my people! Worship Allah; you have no god other than Him. I fear for you the punishment of a tremendous Day.”"
  * **Gloss:** The commission, the creed-line, the date: no miracle demanded, no story told yet — just worship and a Day.
* **`[7:60]`** "The dignitaries among his people said, “We see that you are in obvious error.”"
  * **Gloss:** The establishment's reply is a diagnosis, not an argument — "we see you" (same verb as 7:66's "we see foolishness in you").
* **`[7:61]`** "He said, “O my people, I am not in error, but I am a messenger from the Lord of the Worlds.”"
  * **Gloss:** He answers on identity alone; "Lord of the Worlds" is the 7:54 sovereignty-name pressed into prophetic credentials.
* **`[7:62]`** "“I deliver to you the messages of my Lord, and I advise you, and I know from Allah what you do not know.”"
  * **Gloss:** Three claims — delivery, nasiha (advice), and God-taught knowledge; the advisor-claim is the exact phrase Iblis forged at 7:21 — here it is authentic.
* **`[7:63]`** "“Do you wonder that a reminder has come to you from your Lord, through a man from among you, to warn you, and to lead you to righteousness, so that you may attain mercy?”"
  * **Gloss:** The wonder-question: the messenger's humanity is the point (from among you = accountable kin); reminder/warning/mercy chain back to 7:2's Book-function.
* **`[7:64]`** "But they called him a liar. So We saved him and those with him in the Ark, and We drowned those who rejected Our revelations. They were blind people."
  * **Gloss:** The pattern's first execution: liar's verdict → salvation-by-vessel → drowning; the closing insult "blind people" answers 7:46's "recognize by their features" — faces that refused to see.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **"Worship Allah; you have no god other than Him":** the prophet-refrain.
  * **Rendering:** identical in 7:59 (Noah), 7:65 (Hud), 7:73 (Saleh), 7:85 (Shuaib) — the cycle's one unchanging sentence.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[11:61]` (Saleh to Thamud) and `[11:84]` (Shuaib) verbatim in Hud's surah; `[23:32]` "Serve Allah. You have no god other than Him" — the same refrain in the believers'-surah catalog; pairs with the Tawhid knowledge note.
* **"adviser/advice" (nāṣiḥ/nasiḥa):** the honest vs. the counterfeit office.
  * **Rendering:** "I advise you" (`[7:62]`), "a trustworthy adviser" (`[7:68]`, Hud), "those who give advice" (`[7:79]`, Saleh) vs "I am a sincere advisor to you" (`[7:21]`, Satan's oath).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[11:34]` — Noah's own version: "My advice will not benefit you, much as I want to advise you"; the office is real, the counterfeiter swears it.
* **"dignitaries/elite/notables":** the power-class that always answers first.
  * **Rendering:** "dignitaries" (`[7:60]`), "elite" (`[7:66]`, 7:75, 7:88, 7:90), "notables" (`[7:109`, out of range, in the Moses cycle) — likely one Arabic word, three English renderings (*agent inference — verify*).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[26:34]` (Pharaoh's dignitaries), `[28:32]`, `[43:46]` (Moses to "Pharaoh and his dignitaries") — the same class name across the Moses–Pharaoh dossier.
* **"in the Ark" (fulk):** the rescue-vessel.
  * **Rendering:** "We saved him and those with him in the Ark" (`[7:64]`).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[10:73]` verbatim twin including the successors-clause; `[11:37]`, `[23:27–28]` (build-commands); note homonym risk: "the Ark" at `[2:248]` is the covenant chest, not Noah's ship (observable collision of renderings).

## 4. Cross-References (The Web)
* **Synoptic Noah-doublet:** `[10:73]` — "they denounced him, so We saved him and those with him in the Ark, and We drowned those who rejected Our signs" — the Yunus pair confirms every keyword; the build-command scene sits at `[11:37]` and `[23:27]` (both rg-verified).
* **Wonder-question twin:** `[6:130]` — "did there not come to you messengers from among you, relating to you My revelations, and warning you of the meeting of this Day?" — 7:35's rule and 7:63's sermon quoted at the Day itself; cf. `[7:69]` for Hud's identical wonder-question.
* **Advisor contrast (within surah):** `[7:21]` ↔ `[7:62]` — the oath "I am a sincere advisor" against the messenger who simply advises; the whole prophet-cycle is a test of which adviser you follow (pairs with the Nifaq note's counterfeit-inside logic).
* **Blindness motif:** `[7:46]`/`[7:48]` (features recognized on the Elevations) ↔ `[7:64]` "They were blind people" — sight as moral organ, the surah's own coinage of it.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Meccan; the destruction-cycle (Noah onward) is the Meccan sermon's stock argument — the Quraysh are asked to remember successorship (7:69, 74, 129) the way Aad was.
