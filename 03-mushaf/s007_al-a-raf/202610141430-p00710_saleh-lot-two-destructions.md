# Saleh's she-camel and Lot's rain: two towns, two verdicts (7:73–84)
**UID:** 202610141430
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 7:73–84 (Ruku 10 of 24 in al-A’raf)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[7:73]` – `[7:84]` (12 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Thamud get a revealed clarification plus a live miracle — Allah's she-camel, a grazing boundary not to be crossed — and answer by ham-stringing the sign and daring the punishment, dropping in a single quake into lifeless bodies; then the camera cuts to Lot's people, whose "lewdness no people ever committed" ends in inverted cities and a rain: the ruku proves that violating a sign and violating a boundary meet the same end.
* **Placement:** Third and fourth towns of the Meccan cycle (after Noah, Aad); the split-class pattern (arrogant elite vs believing commons, 7:75–76) is the surah's social anatomy of rejection, and the she-camel's "do her no harm" is the surah's own limits-motif — al-A'raf's heights humbled (7:74's carved mountains fall in one quake).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[7:73]`** — three clause-chunks:
  * "And to Thamood, their brother Saleh. He said, “O my people! Worship Allah; you have no god other than Him. Clarification has come to you from your Lord."
  * "This she-camel of Allah is a sign for you. So leave her to graze on Allah’s earth, and do her no harm, lest a painful penalty seizes you.”"
  * **Gloss:** Refrain, then a delivered proof ("Clarification") embodied in an animal: the sign comes with a grazing contract — the miracle's whole law is harmlessness.
* **`[7:74]`** "“And remember how He made you successors after Aad, and settled you in the land. You make for yourselves mansions on its plains, and carve out dwellings in the mountains. So remember Allah’s benefits, and do not roam the earth corruptingly.”"
  * **Gloss:** The genealogy again (after Aad, 7:69), now with architecture bragged — plains-palaces and mountain-homes; the blessing-list closes with the anti-corruption clause (7:56's law applied).
* **`[7:75]`** "The elite of his people, who were arrogant, said to the common people who had believed, “Do you know that Saleh is sent from his Lord?” They said, “We are believers in what he was sent with.”"
  * **Gloss:** Class-split as theology: the arrogant quiz the weak-in-status believers, who answer with the surah's cleanest sentence of imān.
* **`[7:76]`** "Those who were arrogant said, “We reject what you believe in.”"
  * **Gloss:** Two words of unambiguous kufr — rejection declared as hostility to the believers' content, not to evidence.
* **`[7:77]`** "So they hamstrung the she-camel, and defied the command of their Lord, and said, “O Saleh, bring upon us what you threaten us with, if you are one of the messengers.”"
  * **Gloss:** The sign murdered, the command defied, the dare repeated (7:70's script) — the triple crime in one verse.
* **`[7:78]`** "Whereupon the quake overtook them, and they became lifeless bodies in their homes."
  * **Gloss:** One rajfa-swallow: the carved mountain-homes become tombs — the elevation-motif's leveling.
* **`[7:79]`** "Then he turned away from them, and said, “O my people, I have delivered to you the message of my Lord, and I have advised you, but you do not like those who give advice.”"
  * **Gloss:** The prophet's farewell line: delivery, nasiha, and the diagnosis — societies that hate honest advisers.
* **`[7:80]`** "And Lot, when he said to his people, “Do you commit lewdness no people anywhere have ever committed before you?”"
  * **Gloss:** New town, new boundary-crime: a lewdness (fāḥishah again, 7:28's root) with no precedent — the surah's consent-scope widens from an animal to a body.
* **`[7:81]`** "“You lust after men rather than women. You are an excessive people.”"
  * **Gloss:** The specification and the label: israf — "excessive" — the same word-class as 7:31's eating-drinking ban.
* **`[7:82]`** "And his people's only answer was to say, “Expel them from your town; they are purist people.”"
  * **Gloss:** Their one-sentence reply: expulsion as policy, purity mocked — the town's only argument.
* **`[7:83]`** "But We saved him and his family, except for his wife; she was of those who lagged behind."
  * **Gloss:** Rescue with one exception: his wife classified among the laggards (the word used for Lot's own backward-glance rules elsewhere).
* **`[7:84]`** "And We rained down on them a rain; note the consequences for the sinners."
  * **Gloss:** The rain — stones in the Hud parallel (11:82) — falls, and the narrator turns to the audience: "note the consequences."

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **"she-camel of Allah":** the living sign with a contract.
  * **Rendering:** "This she-camel of Allah is a sign for you" (`[7:73]`); "hamstrung the she-camel" (`[7:77]`).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[91:13]` — "The messenger of Allah said to them, 'This is the she-camel of Allah, so let her drink'" — the possessive title verbatim; `[11:64]` near-twin ("a sign for you...Let her graze"); `[17:59]` "We gave Thamood the she-camel, a visible sign, but they mistreated her"; `[54:27]`, `[26:155]`.
* **"lifeless bodies":** the quake's residue.
  * **Rendering:** "they became lifeless bodies in their homes" (`[7:78]` and `[7:91]` — the exact pair-bookend of this ruku and Shuaib's).
  * **Elsewhere:** none in this rendering (`rg`-verified unique to s007) — the image belongs to al-A'raf's vocabulary; cf. `[11:65]`'s three-day respite variant.
* **"excessive" (isrāf):** one word, two sin-species.
  * **Rendering:** "You are an excessive people" (`[7:81]`); "do not be excessive" (`[7:31]`, appetite).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[20:81]` "Eat of the good things...but do not be excessive" (Moses to Israel); `[17:27]` "The extravagant are brethren of the devils" — isrāf as the umbrella crime over table and body alike.
* **"lagged behind":** the wife's class.
  * **Rendering:** "she was of those who lagged behind" (`[7:83]`).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[37:135]` — "Except for an old woman who lagged behind" (same story); `[27:57]` — "his wife, whom We destined to be among the laggards" (same verse, third rendering: "lagged"/"laggards"); `[48:15–16]` — the hypocrites' lagging at mobilization — the word for every inward laggard who ends outward (*root-identity is agent inference — verify*).

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Meccan; the paired Thamud–Lot destructions suit the surah's town-tourism (7:4, 7:97) — the audience passes through the ruins by sermon, not by travel.
* **Interpretive caution:** how the she-camel "was" (rock formation, real animal, vision) is not decided by the translation; *Source: classical tafsir disagreement (Tabari/Ibn Kathir genres on 7:73) — verify; the Quranic data are: Allah's she-camel, a sign, a grazing-share, a harm-ban, and a hamstringing (observable).*
