## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Meccan per the classical surah-lists. *Source: transmitted classification (Ibn-Abbas-derived/al-Itqan-type lists; the Tanzil-type Revelation_Order table checked live 2026-10-14) — verify in print.* The same transmitted table flags "except 16–20 from Medina" — a minority annotation on the prostrate-prayer passage, recorded not adopted (see p03202 §6).
* **Order:** 75 in the standard transmitted enumeration — the task's guessed "order 75?" verifies ✓ under this brain's convention (al-Anbiya 73, al-Mu'minun 74, as-Sajdah 75, At-Tur 76). *Source: transmitted nuzul-order tables — agent labeling; verify.*
* **Size:** 30 ayahs, 3 ruku (`data/ruku_map.json`: 11+11+8 = 30 ✓). Mushaf position 32, between Luqman (31) and al-Ahzab (33).
* **Name:** as-Sajdah — "Prostration"; corpus title PROSTRATION; the name verse is `[32:15]` "when reminded of them, fall down prostrate" — the surah that names itself after the posture of its believers. (The liturgical association with dawn prayer — the Prophet reciting 32 and 76 at fajr — is from transmitted reports, not from this text — Source: classical reports — verify.)
* **Letter ring:** `[32:1]` "Alif, Lam, Meem." closes the four-surah ALM run 29–32 (all rg-verified) — see [Muqatta'at fleeting note](../../01-fleeting/202610132007-f-muqattaat_letters_untranslated.md).
* **Central theme:** Three certainties against three denials. The Book's descent is beyond doubt (2) though they say "He made it up" (3); man's origin is clay-plus-spirit (7–9) though they ask "shall we be in a new creation?" (10); the return is certain (11–14) though the guilty only beg "send us back" too late (12). Between the two panoramas of the end (12–22) sits the surah's constitution verse: leadership by God's command, earned by patience and certainty (24) — then destroyed generations, water to dead land, and the taunt "When is this victory?" answered by a single imperative: turn away, and wait (30).

### Theme checks the task asked to verify honestly (against the corpus)
* `[32:2]` ✓ verbatim "The revelation of the Book, without a doubt, is from the Lord of the Universe." — the kalam link is supported (note "revelation of the Book" = tanzil rendering; doubt-grammar twin `[10:37]`).
* `[32:7–9]` ✓ creation stages present, but this edition reads: "He who perfected everything He created, and originated the creation of man from clay" / "an extract of an insignificant fluid" / "Then He proportioned him, and breathed into him of His Spirit" — the task's "made everything He created good" and "began man's creation from clay" follow other translations.
* `[32:12]` ✓ the remorse scene exists — corpus wording "the guilty, bowing their heads" (not "hang their heads"); the plea "Our Lord, we have seen and we have heard, so send us back, and we will act righteously; we are now convinced" is verbatim.
* `[32:23]` ✗ the task's "no dispute except ones given it after knowledge came" is NOT in 32:23. Corpus 32:23: "We gave Moses the Book; so do not be in doubt regarding His encounter; and We made it a guidance for the Children of Israel." The "dispute only after knowledge came" line is `[45:17]`; as-Sajdah's dispute-motif appears one verse later at 32:25 (judgment over what they disputed — itself a verbatim twin of 45:17's closing clause). Recorded as a correction.
* `[32:24]` ✓ imamate-by-patience verbatim: "We appointed leaders from among them, guiding by Our command, as long as they persevered and were certain of Our communications" — sabr note anchor; twin `[21:73]`.
* Closing night/dawn signs: ✗ no night/dawn sign-verses close the surah — the night motif is 32:16 ("Their sides shun their beds"); the close is 32:26–30 (lost generations, water to a dry land, "When is this victory?", turn-away-and-wait). Likewise no "the Hour — no doubt about it" in the final verses: "without a doubt" attaches to the Book at 32:2; the Day named at the end is "the day of victory" (32:28–29), and resurrection-judgment is 32:25. Recorded as a correction.

## 3. Structural Arc
* **The Book & its Maker (1–6):** letters → doubt-proof claim → the "He made it up" rebuttal → six-day Throne-regent with no master or intercessor → affairs ascending on a Day-measured-thousand-years → the Knower of unseen and seen.
* **The human dossier (7–11):** clay → extract of insignificant fluid → proportioned + spirit → hearing, eyesight, brains ("rarely do you give thanks") → the denial of re-creation → the angel of death as the appointed answer.
* **The two audiences on display (12–22):** the guilty with bowed heads begging return (12–14) against the prostrate, bed-shunning, spending believers promised hidden eye's-delight (15–17); the believer/sinner inequality executed as Gardens of Shelter vs. the in-out Fire (18–20); the lesser torment as pedagogy (21) and the turn-away criminal's verdict (22).
* **Israel's charter & the closing signs (23–30):** Moses' Book without doubt (23) → leaders by patience and certainty (24) → resurrection judgment over disputes (25) → the walked-through ruins of destroyed generations (26) → water to a dry land, the resurrection's weekly proof (27) → "When is this victory?" (28) → faith-useless-on-that-day (29) → "So turn away from them, and wait. They too are waiting" (30).
