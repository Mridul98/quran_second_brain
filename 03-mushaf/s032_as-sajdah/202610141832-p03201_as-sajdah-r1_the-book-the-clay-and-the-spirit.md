# Letters, an undoubted Book, and man from clay and spirit (32:1–11)
**UID:** 202610141832
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 32:1–11 (Ruku 1 of 3 in as-Sajdah)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[32:1]` – `[32:11]` (11 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Before any command, the ruku stacks three rebuttals of doubt: the Book's descent is "without a doubt" (2–3), the heavens' Maker sits on the Throne with no rival intercessor (4–6), and man's own dossier — clay, fluid, spirit, senses — proves a re-creation the deniers find absurd (7–11), answered not with argument but with an appointment: the angel of death (11).
* **Placement:** Ruku 1 of 3; it sets the surah's triad — Book, Maker, human — which ruku 2 resolves at the Day (the guilty vs. the prostrate) and ruku 3 into history (Moses, leaders, the dry land).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[32:1]`** "Alif, Lam, Meem."
  * **Gloss:** The fourth and last letter-cluster of the consecutive ALM run `[29:1]`, `[30:1]`, `[31:1]`, `[32:1]` — the densest letters-ring in the Mushaf (all rg-verified); untranslated, per the Fleeting note.
* **`[32:2]`** "The revelation of the Book, without a doubt, is from the Lord of the Universe."
  * **Gloss:** Tanzil as event: descent affirmed with a triple-emphatic "without a doubt" — the kalam note's self-claim verse; "Lord of the Universe" is this edition's variant of the Fatihah formula (`[1:2]` "Lord of the Worlds"; `[7:54]` "Lord of all beings" — rg-checked drift of the same rabb phrase).
* **`[32:3]`** "Yet they say, “He made it up.” In fact, it is the Truth from your Lord, to warn a people who received no warner before you, that they may be guided."
  * **Gloss:** The forgery charge and its rebuttal; "a people who received no warner before you" verbatim-twin of `[28:46]` — the Meccan unaddressed; the purpose-clause "that they may be guided" keeps the guidance-note frame.
* **`[32:4]`** "Allah is He who created the heavens and the earth and everything between them in six days, and then established Himself on the Throne. Apart from Him, you have no master and no intercessor. Will you not reflect?"
  * **Gloss:** The six-days-Throne formula — "established Himself on the Throne" verbatim twin of `[7:54]` (vs. `[25:59]`/`[13:2]` "settled" — rendering drift); sovereignty with no master (wali) and no intercessor — the tawhid exclusion, cf. `[10:3]` "no intercessor except after His permission"; ends "Will you not reflect?"
* **`[32:5]`** "He regulates all affairs, from the heavens, to the earth. Then it ascends to Him on a Day the length of which is a thousand years by your count."
  * **Gloss:** Affairs descending-ascending — governance twins `[10:3]` "governing all things", `[13:2]` "He manages all affairs"; the thousand-year Day pairs with `[22:47]` ("a day with your Lord is like a thousand years of your count") and is dwarfed by `[70:4]` ("fifty thousand years").
* **`[32:6]`** "That is the Knower of the Invisible and the Visible, the Powerful, the Merciful."
  * **Gloss:** The omniscience caption; the exact "Knower of the Invisible and the Visible" formula recurs at `[9:94]`, `[13:9]`, `[62:8]` — the power-and-mercy bracket (al-Aziz ar-Rahim — the same pair addressed to the Messenger at `[26:9]`, rg-checked).
* **`[32:7]`** "He who perfected everything He created, and originated the creation of man from clay."
  * **Gloss:** Creation's triptych opens — "perfected everything" (muhsin) tops the clay-origin; task's "made everything He created good" is another translation's wording — logged at index. Clay-man twins: `[6:2]` "created you from clay", `[38:71]` "I am creating a human being from clay", `[23:12]` "an extract of clay."
* **`[32:8]`** "Then made his reproduction from an extract of an insignificant fluid."
  * **Gloss:** The stage (nutfah) — "insignificant fluid" is this edition's rendering; twins `[77:20]` "Did We not create you from an insignificant fluid?", `[76:2]` "a liquid mixture"; generation-after-generation, not just first Adam.
* **`[32:9]`** "Then He proportioned him, and breathed into him of His Spirit. Then He gave you the hearing, and the eyesight, and the brains— but rarely do you give thanks."
  * **Gloss:** The spirit moment — verbatim twin of the divine speech at `[15:29]`/`[38:72]` "breathed into him of My spirit"; the sense-triple "hearing, eyesight, brains" is word-for-word `[16:78]` (with "that you may give thanks" vs. here "rarely do you give thanks" — the same organ-list, opposite verdicts; cf. `[23:78]` "the feelings").
* **`[32:10]`** "And they say, “When we are lost into the earth, shall we be in a new creation?” In fact, they deny the meeting with their Lord."
  * **Gloss:** The denial stated as incredulity (the unique phrase "lost into the earth" — rg-checked, only here); the real diagnosis is denial of the meeting (liqa'), the `[13:2]`/`[29:5]` phrase for which this ruku just built the case.
* **`[32:11]`** "Say, “The angel of death put in charge of you will reclaim you. Then to your Lord you will be returned.”"
  * **Gloss:** Not a rebuttal but an appointment: malak al-mawt — the phrase "angel of death" occurs nowhere else in the corpus (rg-checked), yet is definite: "put in charge of you" — an already-delegated functionary; "then to your Lord you will be returned" = the ruku's closing refrain (cf. 32:12's scene).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`tanzil`** (revelation-descent): 32:2 "The revelation of the Book... is from the Lord of the Universe."
  * **Rendering:** "revelation of the Book" for tanzil al-kitab.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[10:37]` "There is no doubt about it—it is from the Lord of the Universe" — the doubt-rebuttal formula twin; `[31:2]` opposite mode: the Book's own verses.
* **`istawa`** (establishment on the Throne): 32:4 "established Himself on the Throne."
  * **Rendering:** "established Himself" (also `[7:54]`); drift: `[25:59]`, `[13:2]`, `[57:4]` "settled"; `[10:3]` "settled over".
  * **Elsewhere:** the six-days cluster: `[7:54]`, `[10:3]`, `[11:7]`, `[25:59]`, `[50:38]`, `[57:4]`.
* **`nutfah`** (insignificant fluid) and clay (teen): the two-material origin, 32:7–8.
  * **Rendering:** "originated the creation of man from clay" → "extract of an insignificant fluid".
  * **Elsewhere:** `[22:5]` dust→drop→clot→lump stage-list; `[23:12–14]` extract-of-clay sequence — the embryology dossier the surah's own name-verse (32:15) will answer with prostration.
* **`ruh`** (Spirit): 32:9 "breathed into him of His Spirit."
  * **Rendering:** capitalized "His Spirit" (divine attribution without specification); cf. `[15:29]`, `[38:72]` "of My spirit" — first-person in the Adam-speech, third-person here.
  * **Elsewhere:** the spirit-proportion complex is the tafsir minefield (38:72's prostration command binds it to this surah's name-verse 32:15 — Source: agent observation on the corpus; classical readings — verify).
