# Moses' Book, leaders by patience, and the day of victory (32:23–30)
**UID:** 202610141834
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 32:23–30 (Ruku 3 of 3 in as-Sajdah)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[32:23]` – `[32:30]` (8 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah closes by giving Israel its charter — Moses' Book, and leadership by God's command earned through patience and certainty (23–24) — then turns the camera on Mecca's walk-through ruins and the water sent to dead land (26–27), so that the taunt "When is this victory?" (28) is answered once: on that day faith will not profit, and until then, turn away and wait (29–30).
* **Placement:** Final ruku of 3; it converts ruku 2's Day-judgment into history's evidence (ruins, rain) and hands the Messenger the surah's last posture: strategic turn-away, mutual waiting.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[32:23]`** "We gave Moses the Book; so do not be in doubt regarding His encounter; and We made it a guidance for the Children of Israel."
  * **Gloss:** The giving-formula "We gave Moses the Book/Scripture" recurs verbatim at `[28:43]`, `[25:35]`, `[6:154]`; the doubt-clause repeats 32:2's certainty grammar, now about the encounter (liqa') — and the task-expected line "no dispute except ones given it after knowledge" is NOT here — verified: it is `[45:17]` "They fell into dispute only after knowledge came to them, out of mutual rivalry"; as-Sajdah's dispute-motif surfaces at 32:25 below — recorded at index.
* **`[32:24]`** "And We appointed leaders from among them, guiding by Our command, as long as they persevered and were certain of Our communications."
  * **Gloss:** The imamate-by-patience verse: a'imma guiding "by Our command" — verbatim twin `[21:73]` ("We made them leaders, guiding by Our command"), while the counter-charter is `[28:41]` "leaders calling to the Fire"; the two conditions — sabr + yaqin — are the sabr note's covenant-stamina pair, and Luqman's private sermon (31:17) here becomes communal constitution.
* **`[32:25]`** "Your Lord will judge between them on the Day of Resurrection regarding everything they had disputed."
  * **Gloss:** The dispute-motif lands here, and the verse is a verbatim judgment-formula twin of `[45:17]`'s close: "Your Lord will judge between them on the Day of Resurrection regarding the things they differed about" (rg-checked; cf. also the equitable judgment scene `[39:75]`).
* **`[32:26]`** "Is it not a lesson for them, how many generations We have destroyed before them, in whose habitations they walk? Surely in that are signs. Do they not hear?"
  * **Gloss:** The walk-through-the-ruins argument: the Meccan caravans pass destroyed peoples' homes — twins `[29:38]` "It has become clear to you from their dwellings", `[27:52]` "Here are their homes, in ruins", `[46:25]` "nothing to be seen except their dwellings" (all rg-checked); the sense-audit continues: "Do they not hear?" after ruku 1's hearing-and-sight gift (32:9).
* **`[32:27]`** "Do they not see how We conduct the water to a dry land, and with it We produce vegetation, from which their livestock eat, and themselves? Do they not see?"
  * **Gloss:** Rain-to-dead-earth as resurrection's weekly proof — the phenomenon-formula of `[22:5]` ("you see the earth still; but when We send down water on it, it vibrates"), the exact "conduct the water to a dry land" phrasing unique (rg-checked); "Do they not see?" twice in one verse — the surah's sensory irony.
* **`[32:28]`** "And they say, “When is this victory, if you are truthful?”"
  * **Gloss:** The timing-taunt with the truth-condition — family `[27:71]`, `[34:29]` "When is this promise, if you are truthful?"; this edition names the question-object "victory" (fath), not "decision" — rendering choice.
* **`[32:29]`** "Say, “On the day of victory, the faith of those who disbelieved will be of no avail to them, and they will not be granted respite.”"
  * **Gloss:** The answer: belief-then-useless — the no-profit clause verbatim-family of `[6:158]` "no soul will benefit from its faith unless it had believed previously" (rg-checked); "not granted respite" (la yunzarun) closes the delay they requested in 32:12/63:10 — the respite was now, in 32:21's lesser torment.
* **`[32:30]`** "So turn away from them, and wait. They too are waiting."
  * **Gloss:** The closing command-pair — "So wait and watch. They too are waiting and watching" `[44:59]` and `[6:158]` "Say, 'Wait, we too are waiting'" are the twins; the surah that opened with an undoubted Book ends on mutual waiting — certainty deferred to the day of victory.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`a'imma`** (leaders/imams): 32:24.
  * **Rendering:** "We appointed leaders from among them, guiding by Our command."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[21:73]` verbatim twin for the good imamate; `[28:41]` "leaders calling to the Fire" for the anti-imamate — same noun, opposite command-line.
* **`sabr`** (perseverance): condition #1 of leadership.
  * **Rendering:** "as long as they persevered" (32:24) — for the participle-root family.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[31:17]` "be patient over what has befallen you" (individual); `[31:31]`/`[14:5]` "persevering, thankful" (the storm-proof couplet); the sabr note's stamina verb now institutionalized.
* **`yaqin`** (certainty): condition #2 — "certain of Our communications."
  * **Rendering:** "were certain of Our communications" (ayatuna — same object 31:3's "Verses").
  * **Elsewhere:** `[31:4]` "are certain of the Hereafter"; `[45:20]` "mercy for people who believe with certainty" (rg-checked); 13:2 "that you may be certain of the meeting with your Lord" — certainty always about something seen-and-heard, which 32:12's too-late confession proves.
* **`fath`** (victory/decision): 32:28–29.
  * **Rendering:** "this victory" / "the day of victory" — unique naming of the Day in this surah (the same fath-family elsewhere means verdict/opening: e.g., `[48:1]` title-concept — agent labeling; verify rendering range).
  * **Elsewhere:** the taunt-answers sit in the "When is this promise" cluster `[27:71]`, `[34:29]`.
