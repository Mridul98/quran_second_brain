# Surah 80: HE FROWNED (‘Abasa)
**UID:** 202610142269
**Type:** Index / Surah Map
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Meccan — agreed in the classical type-lists; a minority of transmitted reports attach the opening scene to a later Medinan moment (see §5b). No revelation-order number is asserted — *Source: classical type-lists — verify against a printed table.* The unnumbered Basmala heads the surah (corpus line 3).
* **Size:** 42 ayahs, **1 ruku** (`ruku_map` verified: 80:1–80:42, ayah_count 42) — the whole surah is a single documentation unit in this edition's partition.
* **Central theme:** The surah opens on an interruption and ends on a partition. A frown is reproved (80:1–10); the reproved moment is then promoted to the status of the "Lesson… on honorable pages… by the hands of scribes" (80:11–16); the thankless human is examined from his creation and his food (80:17–32); and the Day of the Deafening Noise separates the fleeing man from his nearest kin, and all faces into two groups (80:33–42). Its argument is that **attention is the test**: whom you attend to, what you consider, and what you have forwarded.
* **Reading note for this surah:** because it is one ruku, the passage note carries the full 42-ayah arc; the two audits (origin 80:17–23, provision 80:24–32) are the hinge between the rebuke and the Day.

## 3. Structural Arc
* **The scene (80:1–10):** "He frowned and turned away" — no subject is named; the cause is "the blind man approached him"; then the counter-factual (he might purify himself, or be reminded and benefit); then the other man, the indifferent one who got attention, with the exoneration clause "you are not liable if he does not purify himself"; and the reversal: "To him you were inattentive."
* **The interlude of status (80:11–16):** "Do not. This is a Lesson." — the Lesson's credentials: whoever wills remembers it; it is on honorable pages, exalted and purified, by the hands of scribes, noble and devoted.
* **The first audit — origin (80:17–23):** "Perish man! How thankless he is!" — from a sperm drop, enabled, eased, put to death, buried, resurrected "when He wills"; verdict: "he did not fulfill what He has commanded him."
* **The second audit — provision (80:24–32):** "Let man consider his food" — water poured, soil cracked, grains, grapes and herbs, olives and dates, luscious gardens, fruits and vegetables: "Enjoyment for you, and for your livestock."
* **The Day (80:33–42):** the Deafening Noise; the flight from brother, mother, father, consort and children; each self "will have enough to preoccupy him"; then the two faces — radiant, laughing, rejoicing / covered with misery, overwhelmed by remorse — and the final identification: "These are the faithless, the vicious."

## 5b. Batch-Return Verification Flags (this index)
* **Hint renderings corrected against the corpus:** "the momentous News" belongs to 78, not here; 80:2 is **"When the blind man approached him"** (not "came to him"); 80:3–4 are **"But how do you know? Perhaps he was seeking to purify himself. / Or be reminded, and the message would benefit him."**
* **"a crux passage" accepted:** 80:1–4 names **no subject and no object** — the corpus lines give only third-person pronouns ("He… him… you"). The identification of the frowner with the Prophet and of the blind man with Ibn Umm Maktud, and the Quraysh-notables occasion, are **classical reports (Source: asbab al-nuzul literature — verify)**; the surah's own text is handled neutrally in the passage note.
* **80:11–16 corrected:** "this is a message" → **"Do not. This is a Lesson."**; "in honored pages, elevated, purified, by hands of scribes, noble, righteous" → **"On honorable pages. Exalted and purified. By the hands of scribes. Noble and devoted."** (verified — "righteous" → **"devoted"**).
* **"the single sperm" clause the task attached to 79 is here:** `[80:19]` "From a sperm drop He created him, and enabled him." (rg "sperm" → 53:46, 80:19, 18:37 only — verified).
* **80:33–37 corrected:** the Day's name here is **"the Deafening Noise"**; 80:37 is **"Every one of them, on that Day, will have enough to preoccupy him."** The hint's "each has enough concern" is a paraphrase, not the line.
* **No tawhid-of-accountability phrase exists in the text**; the surah's own close is the two-face partition and "These are the faithless, the vicious" (80:38–42, verified).
