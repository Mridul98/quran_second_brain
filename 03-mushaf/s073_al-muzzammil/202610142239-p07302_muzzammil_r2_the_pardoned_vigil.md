# The pardoned vigil: read what is possible, and lend Allah a generous loan (73:20)
**UID:** 202610142239
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 73:20 (Ruku 2 of 2 in al-Muzzammil)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[73:20]` – `[73:20]` (1 ayah — a single-ayah ruku, start=end in the ruku map)
* **Thesis:** The surah closes with its own field-report: God records the community's actual night-standing (two-thirds, half, one-third, with a group), designs night and day, acknowledges human inability, pardons — and swaps the vigil's fixed hours for a five-item program: recite what is possible, pray, give charity, lend Allah a generous loan, seek forgiveness.
* **Placement:** Ruku 2 is the easing of ruku 1's charge (73:2–6); the surah's arc is obligation → economy, and this verse is the surah's only direct feeder to the infraq dossier.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[73:20]`** "Your Lord knows that you stay up nearly two-thirds of the night, or half of it, or one-third of it, along with a group of those with you. Allah designed the night and the day. He knows that you are unable to sustain it, so He has pardoned you. So read of the Quran what is possible for you. He knows that some of you may be ill; and others travelling through the land, seeking Allah’s bounty; and others fighting in Allah’s cause. So read of it what is possible for you, and observe the prayers, and give regular charity, and lend Allah a generous loan. Whatever good you advance for yourselves, you will find it with Allah, better and generously rewarded. And seek Allah’s forgiveness, for Allah is Forgiving and Merciful."
  * **Gloss (clause by clause):**
  * The record: the measured fractions answer 73:2–4's elastic hours — the command had been taken literally (verified internal ring).
  * "Allah designed the night and the day… He has pardoned you" — the lawgiver knows the frame He made; taysir, not abandonment.
  * "read of the Quran what is possible" (twice) — recitation now proportional to capacity; cf. 64:16 "be conscious of Allah as much as you can" (verified ease-twin).
  * The excuse-list: illness, travel "seeking Allah's bounty" (cf. 2:198 "seeking bounty from your Lord" — verified near-twin), fighting — mission survives absence.
  * The substitution: prayer + regular charity + "lend Allah a generous loan" — the qard formula, verbatim twin of 2:245 (verified).
  * The audit clause: "Whatever good you advance… you will find it with Allah" — exact twin of 2:110 "whatever good you forward for yourselves, you will find it with Allah" (verified).
  * The close: istighfar + "Forgiving and Merciful" — Noah's chain verb (71:10 "ask your Lord for forgiveness") ends the juz's first night-surah (Source: agent observation).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`qard hasan`** (generous loan): rendering identical to `[2:245]` "Who is he who will offer Allah a generous loan" (verified); the k-infraq note's formula cluster (2:245, 57:18, 64:17) now has a juz-27 feeder.
* **`taqdim`** (advance/forward): "Whatever good you advance" ↔ 2:110 "whatever good you forward" (verified — same sentence-frame, two renderings).
* **`taysir`** (what is possible): 73:20 twice — the surah's hinge word; 64:16 "as much as you can" (verified twin).
