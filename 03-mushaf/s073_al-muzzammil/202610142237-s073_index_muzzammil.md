## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Meccan per the type-lists for 73:1–19; the transmitted exception reports single out 73:20 as Medinan (the vigil-pardon) — the paired claim is recorded in the ruku 2 note. *Source: classical type-lists + transmitted abrogation reports — verify.* Revelation order 4 in the transmitted lists (among the earliest). *Source: classical revelation-order lists — verify against a printed table.* The unnumbered Basmala heads the surah.
* **Size:** 20 ayahs, 2 ruku — and the brain's only single-ayah ruku here (73:20, start=end in the ruku map): one verse that eases the nineteen that preceded it.
* **Central theme:** The night as installation-site. The Enwrapped one is charged to stay up, half or a little less or more, and to chant the Quran rhythmically (73:1–4), because a heavy word is inbound (73:5); the night is the effective medium (73:6), the day the lengthy work (73:7); then dhikr, wholehearted devotion, exclusive trust (73:8–9), patient endurance and polite withdrawal (73:10), a "leave them to Me" clause (73:11), the Day's physics and Pharaoh's precedent (73:12–18), and the path-for-whoever-wills close (73:19).
* **The recitation-standings note (task-flag, placed here per the index's duty):** this surah is the qiyam dossier of the brain — the fixed charge (73:2–4, "chant the Quran rhythmically" = tartil, 73:4), the medium's fit ("The vigil of night is more effective, and better suited for recitation," 73:6 — verified rendering; the task's "more imposing and more sustaining" is a different English), the community precedent (3:113–114, verified), the added-vigil twin (17:79, verified) — and 73:20's recorded actual standings ("nearly two-thirds… or half… or one-third… along with a group of those with you"), where sustained obligation gives way to "read of the Quran what is possible for you," prayer, charity, and the generous loan. *Source: observable corpus structure.*

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Fit the vessel (73:1–4):** address → night-standing with elastic hours → measured chanting.
* **Why night (73:5–7):** the heavy word inbound; vigil is more effective, better matched to recitation; the day has its own claim.
* **Anchor the heart (73:8–9):** remember the Name, devote wholeheartedly — Lord of East and West, no god but He, take Him as Trustee.
* **Social stance (73:10–11):** endure their speech; withdraw politely; "leave Me to the deniers of luxury, and grant them a short respite."
* **The dossier (73:12–18):** shackles, Fierce Fire, choking food; the trembling earth; the witness-messenger like Pharaoh's; the Day that grays children; the shattering heaven — "His promise is always fulfilled."
* **The open door (73:19):** "This is a reminder. So whoever wills, let him take a path to his Lord."
* **The easing (73:20):** the record of actual standings, the pardon, the five-item program — obligation becomes economy (loan, prayer, charity, istighfar).

## 5b. Batch-Return Verification Flags (this index)
* Task renderings corrected against the corpus: "recite the Recitation with measured recitation" → "chant the Quran rhythmically" (73:4); "We will cast upon you a weighty word" → "We are about to give you a heavy message" (73:5); "more imposing and more sustaining" → "more effective, and better suited for recitation" (73:6); "devote to Him entire devotion" → "devote yourself to Him wholeheartedly" (73:8).
* "So leave them to play with it" — not in 73:9 (the verse is the Trustee clause); nearest corpus sites: 6:91 "leave them toying away in their speculation" and 15:3 "Leave them to eat, and enjoy" (verified).
* "If they turn away, say: Allah suffices me" — not in 73:20; it is 9:129 "Allah is enough for me… in Him I have put my trust" (verified); its 73-side twin is 73:9 "take Him as a Trustee." 73:20 closes instead on istighfar: "Allah is Forgiving and Merciful."
