# The night charge: stand, chant, and brace for the heavy word (73:1–19)
**UID:** 202610142238
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 73:1–19 (Ruku 1 of 2 in al-Muzzammil)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[73:1]` – `[73:19]` (19 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Before the message lands, the vessel is fitted: the Enwrapped one is commissioned to the night vigil with measured recitation, because a heavy word is coming; then dhikr, exclusive trust, patient withdrawal, a divine "leave them to Me," the Day and Pharaoh as precedent — closing with the will-cluster's path formula.
* **Placement:** Opens surah 73 and the juz's commissioning pair (twin of 73:20's easing in ruku 2, and of 74:1–7's morning-mission); the surah's k-kalam thesis-claim sits at 73:5.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[73:1]`** "O you Enwrapped one."
  * **Gloss:** Address-form unique pairing with `[74:1]` "O you Enrobed one" (verified — the corpus renders muzzammil/muddathir only here, 73:1/74:1).
* **`[73:2]`–`[73:3]`** "Stay up during the night, except a little. For half of it, or reduce it a little."
  * **Gloss:** Command to qiyam al-layl with elastic hours — obligation plus flexibility in the same breath.
* **`[73:4]`** "Or add to it; and chant the Quran rhythmically."
  * **Gloss:** tartīl: "chant… rhythmically" is this edition's only site (verified) — pacing as a mode of reception, matching the word's weight.
* **`[73:5]`** "We are about to give you a heavy message."
  * **Gloss:** qawl thaqīl — weight announced before it descends; the same noun qawl will be demoted to "the word of a mortal" (74:25): the juz's central speech-contest.
* **`[73:6]`** "The vigil of night is more effective, and better suited for recitation."
  * **Gloss:** medium matches message: night stillness steadies speech ("vigil" rendered here only — cf. 17:79's "keep vigil").
* **`[73:7]`** "In the daytime, you have lengthy work to do."
  * **Gloss:** the day's claim — the excuse-list that 73:20 will generalize (sick, travelers, fighters).
* **`[73:8]`** "So remember the Name of your Lord, and devote yourself to Him wholeheartedly."
  * **Gloss:** dhikr of the Name and total devotion (tabattul) — interiorization before public mission.
* **`[73:9]`** "Lord of the East and the West. There is no god but He, so take Him as a Trustee."
  * **Gloss:** geography's Lord + the shahada clause + wakil rendered "Trustee" (verified unique; 6:102, 39:62, 9:129 render the same root otherwise).
* **`[73:10]`** "And endure patiently what they say, and withdraw from them politely."
  * **Gloss:** sabr under verbal attack + polite hajr — the mission's social stance (cf. 15:94 "turn away from the polytheists").
* **`[73:11]`** "And leave Me to those who deny the truth, those of luxury, and give them a brief respite."
  * **Gloss:** divine first-person case-taking — twin formula of "Leave Me to him whom I created alone" (74:11, verified); luxury (mutra'ūn) is the charge.
* **`[73:12]`–`[73:13]`** "With Us are shackles, and a Fierce Fire. And food that chokes, and a painful punishment."
  * **Gloss:** the stock-in-trade of the Hereafter; near-twin 76:4 "chains, and yokes, and a Searing Fire" (verified).
* **`[73:14]`** "On the Day when the earth and the mountains tremble, and the mountains become heaps of sand."
  * **Gloss:** the Day's physics; 52:9–10 "the heaven sways… the mountains go into motion" (verified twin).
* **`[73:15]`–`[73:16]`** "We have sent to you a messenger, a witness over you, as We sent to Pharaoh a messenger. But Pharaoh defied the Messenger, so We seized him with a terrible seizing."
  * **Gloss:** the prophet is a witness (33:45, 48:8 "a witness, and a bearer of good news, and a warner" — verified); Pharaoh = precedent answering the respite of 73:11.
* **`[73:17]`** "So how will you, if you persist in unbelief, save yourself from a Day which will turn the children gray-haired?"
  * **Gloss:** "gray-haired" unique corpus site (verified); terror-physics of the Day (cf. 76:10–11, 74:9–10).
* **`[73:18]`** "The heaven will shatter thereby. His promise is always fulfilled."
  * **Gloss:** the sky's fragility — the sky-guarded surahs (72:8) note the heaven's defenses; here it will not hold.
* **`[73:19]`** "This is a reminder. So whoever wills, let him take a path to his Lord."
  * **Gloss:** the will-cluster begins: 74:54–55, 76:29–30, 81:27–28 (all verified) — guidance as a path to a Lord.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`muzzammil`** (the Enwrapped): 73:1 — paired with 74:1 muddathir "Enrobed" (verified; the two wrapped-surahs bracket the juz).
* **`tartīl`**: "chant the Quran rhythmically" (73:4, verified unique rendering).
  * **Elsewhere:** 17:106 "unfolded gradually… revealed in stages" and 17:79 "keep vigil with it during parts of the night" (verified) — the recitation-vigil family.
* **`qawl thaqīl`**: "a heavy message" (73:5) vs. 74:25 "the word of a mortal" (verified) — same word qawl, opposite valence: the kalam dispute in two adjectives.
* **`wakīl`**: "Trustee" (73:9) — renderings drift: 6:102 "responsible for everything," 39:62 "in Charge of all things," 9:129 "in Him I have put my trust" (all verified).
