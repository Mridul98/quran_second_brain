## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Meccan by the classical type-lists and by the surah's whole register (Resurrection, the two registers, "Woe" motifs); some transmitted reports connect 83:1–6 to the Medinan market-context ("when we came to Medina, the worst trade was short measure…"), which is why certain lists call the opening Medinan. *Source: classical type-lists; the market report is a transmitted asbab for 83:1–3 — verify in Ibn Kathir/Tabari apparatus.* Revelation order: **transmitted nuzul tables — verify** (corpus states no nuzul; unnumbered Basmala heads the surah).
* **Size:** 36 ayahs, 1 ruku (ruku_map global 525 — verified). The longest of the batch's four surahs; classically the nearest thing to a commercial-ethics surah in the Juz 'Amma run.
* **Title:** al-Mutaffifin — Itani's header "THE DEFRAUDERS"; the plural noun names a profession of petty theft, and the surah hangs an eschatology from it.
* **Central theme:** Weights here, registers there. The defrauders who cheat the scale will stand on "a Great Day… when mankind will stand before the Lord of the Worlds" (83:5–6), where two filing systems are named — the wicked's record in **Sijjeen**, "a numerical book" (83:7–9), and the righteous's in **Elliyyeen**, "witnessed by those brought near" (83:18–21). Between them: the corroded heart, the "Legends of the ancients" reflex, and the comic reversal in which today's mockers become tomorrow's laughed-at (83:29–36).
* **Reading hint:** the surah's architecture is mirror-perfect — two "Not at all" dossiers with the identical definition "A numerical book" (83:9 = 83:20, verbatim — rg-verified); read the pair, then read 82:10–12 backward, and the watchers' job description finally names its two archives.

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Indictment (83:1–3):** "Woe to the defrauders" — the asymmetry defined precisely: take full, give short.
* **The audit clause (83:4–6):** resurrection questioned back at them; the Great Day defined by one image — standing before the Lord of the Worlds.
* **Sijjeen dossier (83:7–17):** the wicked's record, the interrogation "how can you know what Sijjeen is?", the woe of the deniers, the psychology catalog (aggressor → "Legends of the ancients" → corroded hearts → screened from their Lord).
* **Elliyyeen dossier (83:18–28):** the mirrored register of the righteous, the bliss tableau, the sealed wine whose seal is musk, Tasneem.
* **The laughter reversal (83:29–36):** the crime-scene of mockery replayed line-by-line, then flipped: "on that Day, those who believed will laugh at the unbelievers" — closing on the verdict-question "Have the unbelievers been repaid for what they used to do?"

* **Final sequence:** 83:29–31 = present-tense mocker behavior; 83:34–36 = the Day's reversal and repayment-question. The surah ends on a question, not a proclamation.
