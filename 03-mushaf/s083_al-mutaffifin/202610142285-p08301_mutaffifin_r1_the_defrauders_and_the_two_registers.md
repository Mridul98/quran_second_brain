# Woe to the Defrauders: The Two Registers and the Reversal of Laughter (83:1–36)
**UID:** 202610142285
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 83:1–36 (Ruku 1 of 1 in al-Mutaffifin)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[83:1]` – `[83:36]` (36 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** A commercial sin is prosecuted with eschatological machinery: the scales cheated in the marketplace prefigure the record filed in Sijjeen or Elliyyeen — "a numerical book" both times — and the surah's last twist is comic-precise: the mockers who laughed at believers "on that Day" will be the laughed-at, asked as a verdict: "Have the unbelievers been repaid for what they used to do?"
* **Placement:** Ruku 1 of 1 — two mirrored dossiers (wicked/righteous) framed by two "Woe"/bliss bookends. Direct continuation of 82's watchers: there the recorders "know everything you do"; here their files are named and filed.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[83:1]`–`[83:3]`** "Woe to the defrauders. / Those who, when they take a measure from people, they take in full. / But when they measure or weigh to others, they cheat."
  * **Gloss:** The crime is one-directional precision: full for the taking, short for the giving — cheating (tatfif) defined as an asymmetry, not an error.
* **`[83:4]`–`[83:6]`** "Do these not know that they will be resurrected? / For a Great Day? / The Day when mankind will stand before the Lord of the Worlds?"
  * **Gloss:** The audit clause: resurrection is asserted against heedlessness; the Day gets one image — universal standing before "the Lord of the Worlds" (exact phrase corpus-unique here — rg-verified).
* **`[83:7]`–`[83:9]`** "Not at all. The record of the wicked is in Sijjeen. / But how can you know what Sijjeen is? / A numerical book."
  * **Gloss:** The wicked's dossier, named (Sijjeen), interrogated (the "what will convey"-family in its "how can you know" rendering), then defined as a written, numbered register — a book, not a rumor.
* **`[83:10]`–`[83:12]`** "Woe on that Day to the deniers. / Those who deny the Day of Reckoning. / But none denies it except the sinful aggressor."
  * **Gloss:** Woe re-dated to the Day; denial diagnosed: "Day of Reckoning" (hisab — the accountancy word — corpus-unique rendering here, rg-verified) is denied only by a moral type: the aggressive sinner, not the honest skeptic.
* **`[83:13]`–`[83:14]`** "When Our revelations are recited to him, he says, “Legends of the ancients.” / Not at all. Their hearts have become corroded by what they used to earn."
  * **Gloss:** The reflex-line ("Legends of the ancients" — exact phrase shared with 16:24, rg-verified) is answered not with argument but with pathology: earning corrodes the organ of recognition (the rana/covering motif).
* **`[83:15]`–`[83:17]`** "Not at all. On that Day, they will be screened from their Lord. / Then they will roast in Hell. / Then it will be said, “This is what you used to deny.”"
  * **Gloss:** The corroded heart becomes a screened soul: the same root-idea of covering (hijab) matures into exclusion; the sentence is spoken back to them in the second person — denial made permanent experience.
* **`[83:18]`–`[83:21]`** "No indeed; the record of the righteous is in Elliyyeen. / But how can you know what Elliyyoon is? / A numerical book. / Witnessed by those brought near."
  * **Gloss:** The mirror-dossier: same "Not at all", same interrogation, same definition ("A numerical book" — verbatim twin of 83:9, rg-verified), plus one privilege absent from Sijjeen's file: it is witnessed by the brought-near (muqarrabun). Note the one-line spelling drift in this edition: Elliyyeen (83:18) vs Elliyyoon (83:19) — quote as printed.
* **`[83:22]`–`[83:24]`** "Indeed, the righteous will be amid bliss. / On thrones, looking on. / You will recognize on their faces the radiance of bliss."
  * **Gloss:** Bliss given furniture (thrones), direction (looking on), and a visible glow — "the righteous will be amid bliss" is 82:13's formula-twin with rendering drift (virtuous/righteous — rg-verified).
* **`[83:25]`–`[83:28]`** "They will be given to drink a sealed wine. / Whose seal is musk—this is what competitors should compete for. / Its mixture is of Tasneem. / A spring from which those brought near drink."
  * **Gloss:** The drink dossier: sealed, musked, mixed with Tasneem — one named spring; and the surah's one odd, electric line: a competitive slogan ("this is what competitors should compete for") inserted at the description's height.
* **`[83:29]`–`[83:33]`** "Those who committed crimes used to laugh at those who believed. / And when they passed by them, they would wink at one another. / And when they went back to their families, they would go back exulting. / And if they saw them, they would say, “These people are lost.” / Yet they were not sent as guardians over them."
  * **Gloss:** The mockery replayed in five frames — laugh, wink, exult, slur ("These people are lost" — the astray-charge spoken BY the mocked, not a separate sin-list), then the jurisdictional correction: no guardianship was delegated to them over anyone.
* **`[83:34]`–`[83:36]`** "But on that Day, those who believed will laugh at the unbelievers. / On luxurious furnishings, looking on. / Have the unbelievers been repaid for what they used to do?"
  * **Gloss:** The reversal, beat-for-beat (they laughed → will be laughed at; they looked down from arrogance → the believers look on from couches), and the close is a question whose answer is the whole surah: yes, repayment is exactly what this book-files system delivers.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`tatfif` (defrauding)**: shorting the measure — giving less than weighed-out.
  * **Rendering:** "the defrauders" (83:1), "they cheat" (83:3).
  * **Elsewhere:** the Shuaib refrain cluster, all rg-verified: `[26:183]` "do not defraud people of their belongings" (same root), `[7:85]` "Give full measure and weight, and do not cheat people out of their rights", `[11:85]` "Give full measure and full weight, in all fairness", `[17:35]` "weigh with accurate scales. That is fair, and the best determination". — k-infraq's flank, now fully renderings-verified.
* **`Sijjeen` / `Elliyyeen`**: the two registers' names — each glossed by the identical definition "A numerical book".
  * **Rendering:** names left transliterated (kitab marqum-type: "A numerical book" ×2 — rg-verified); 83:19 prints "Elliyyoon" against 83:18's "Elliyyeen" — in-book variance, *Source: observable in corpus; class-2 typography per the artifacts fleeting note*.
  * **Elsewhere:** no other register-names in the corpus; dossier-mates: `[50:4]` "a comprehensive book", `[17:13]` "a book which he will find spread open".
* **`“Legends of the ancients”`**: the denier's canned reply (asatir al-awwalin).
  * **Rendering:** identical phrase `[16:24]` (the only twin, rg-verified); variant "Tales of the ancients" at `[25:5]`, `[46:17]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** the rebuttal pattern is k-kalam's territory (channel-claims vs. provenance-charges — see 69:41, 81:22 cited in its Practical Reading).
* **`ranā’ / "corroded"` and `hijab / "screened"`**: earning → coating → exclusion (83:14–15).
  * **Rendering:** "hearts have become corroded by what they used to earn" (83:14); "screened from their Lord" (83:15).
  * **Elsewhere:** "screened" family rg-verified: `[41:5]` "Our hearts are screened", `[18:101]` "whose eyes were screened to My message", `[19:17]` (she screened herself) — the covering motif's English-chain. (*Source: classical reading of 83:14 as sin-layering — rana — Ibn Kathir/Tabari on 83:14 — verify before quoting detail.*)
