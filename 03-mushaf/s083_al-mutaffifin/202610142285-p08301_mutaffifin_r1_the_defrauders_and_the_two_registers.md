# Woe to the Defrauders: The Two Registers and the Reversal of Laughter (83:1–36)
**UID:** 202610142285
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 83:1–36 (Ruku 1 of 1 in al-Mutaffifin)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[83:1]` – `[83:36]` (36 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** A commercial sin is prosecuted with eschatological machinery: the scales cheated in the marketplace prefigure the record filed in Sijjeen or Elliyyeen — "a numerical book" both times — and the surah's last twist is comic-precise: the mockers who laughed at believers "on that Day" will be the laughed-at, asked as a verdict: "Have the unbelievers been repaid for what they used to do?"
* **Placement:** Ruku 1 of 1 — two mirrored dossiers (wicked/righteous) framed by two "Woe"/bliss bookends. Direct continuation of 82's watchers: there the recorders "know everything you do"; here their files are named and filed.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[83:1]`** "Woe to the defrauders."
  * **Gloss:** The opening verdict: woe (wayl) pronounced against the mutaffifin — the crime named before its definition.
* **`[83:2]`** "Those who, when they take a measure from people, they take in full."
  * **Gloss:** The first half of the asymmetry: receiving-side exactitude — the defrauder's precision when he is the beneficiary.
* **`[83:3]`** "But when they measure or weigh to others, they cheat."
  * **Gloss:** The second half: giving-side shortfall — cheating (tatfif) defined as an asymmetry, not an error; the crime is one-directional precision.
* **`[83:4]`** "Do these not know that they will be resurrected?"
  * **Gloss:** The audit opens with a question: resurrection asserted against heedlessness — the rhetorical "do they not know" indicts their certainty-gap.
* **`[83:5]`** "For a Great Day?"
  * **Gloss:** The Day named by its magnitude: "Great Day" (yawm 'azim) — the scale of the event answers the smallness of their cheating.
* **`[83:6]`** "The Day when mankind will stand before the Lord of the Worlds?"
  * **Gloss:** Universal standing before "the Lord of the Worlds" — exact phrase corpus-unique here (rg-verified); the marketplace cheat now stands before the cosmic Judge.
* **`[83:7]`** "Not at all. The record of the wicked is in Sijjeen."
  * **Gloss:** The wicked's dossier named: Sijjeen — introduced with the veto-word "Not at all" (kalla); the register's name precedes its definition.
* **`[83:8]`** "But how can you know what Sijjeen is?"
  * **Gloss:** The interrogation: "how can you know" — the adrāka-family rendering invites the listener into the archive's mystery before defining it.
* **`[83:9]`** "A numerical book."
  * **Gloss:** The definition: a written, numbered register — kitab marqum; a book, not a rumor; verbatim twin of 83:20 (rg-verified).
* **`[83:10]`** "Woe on that Day to the deniers."
  * **Gloss:** Woe re-dated to the Day: the verdict pronounced in eschatological time, not merely earthly.
* **`[83:11]`** "Those who deny the Day of Reckoning."
  * **Gloss:** The defining clause of the deniers: rejection targets the accounting-Day itself — hisab as the precise term for settlement-day in this edition's rendering.
* **`[83:12]`** "But none denies it except the sinful aggressor."
  * **Gloss:** Denial diagnosed: "Day of Reckoning" is denied only by a moral type — the aggressive sinner (mujrim mu'tad), not the honest skeptic; the crime is character, not intellect.
* **`[83:13]`** "When Our revelations are recited to him, he says, "Legends of the ancients.""
  * **Gloss:** The reflex-line: "Legends of the ancients" (asatir al-awwalin) — exact phrase shared with `[16:24]` (rg-verified); the denier's canned reply to revelation.
* **`[83:14]`** "Not at all. Their hearts have become corroded by what they used to earn."
  * **Gloss:** The pathology-diagnosis: not argument but etiology — earning corrodes the organ of recognition (the rana/covering motif); "Not at all" (kalla) vetoes their self-assessment.
* **`[83:15]`** "Not at all. On that Day, they will be screened from their Lord."
  * **Gloss:** The corroded heart becomes a screened soul: the same root-idea of covering (hijab) matures into exclusion; "Not at all" vetoes any hope of unmediated access.
* **`[83:16]`** "Then they will roast in Hell."
  * **Gloss:** The sentence executed: roasting (yaslā) follows screening — exclusion from God answered by immersion in fire.
* **`[83:17]`** "Then it will be said, "This is what you used to deny.""
  * **Gloss:** The sentence spoken back to them in the second person — denial made permanent experience; the echo-formula ("what you used to deny") mirrors `[52:14]` and `[44:50]` (rg).
* **`[83:18]`** "No indeed; the record of the righteous is in Elliyyeen."
  * **Gloss:** The mirror-dossier opens: same veto-word ("No indeed" = kalla), the righteous register named — Elliyyeen; note the spelling drift Elliyyeen (here) vs Elliyyoon (83:19) — genuine in-book variance per the artifacts fleeting note.
* **`[83:19]`** "But how can you know what Elliyyoon is?"
  * **Gloss:** The interrogation repeated for the righteous register — note the spelling drift "Elliyyoon" here vs "Elliyyeen" at 83:18, genuine in-book variance per the artifacts fleeting note.
* **`[83:20]`** "A numerical book."
  * **Gloss:** The identical definition verbatim twin of 83:9 — both registers share the same format: numbered, written, archived.
* **`[83:21]`** "Witnessed by those brought near."
  * **Gloss:** The privilege absent from Sijjeen's file: the righteous register is witnessed by the brought-near (muqarrabun) — attestation as honor, the archive has an audience.
* **`[83:22]`** "Indeed, the righteous will be amid bliss."
  * **Gloss:** Bliss declared: "the righteous will be amid bliss" is 82:13's formula-twin with rendering drift (virtuous/righteous — rg-verified).
* **`[83:23]`** "On thrones, looking on."
  * **Gloss:** Bliss materialized as posture and gaze: elevated seating with outward vision — the opposite of the screened-from-their-Lord state at 83:15.
* **`[83:24]`** "You will recognize on their faces the radiance of bliss."
  * **Gloss:** Bliss made visible: the radiance (nadra) on their faces — recognition by others completes the portrait; the face-camera idiom shared with batch neighbors `[75:22]`, `[80:38]`, `[80:40]` (rg-verified).
* **`[83:25]`** "They will be given to drink a sealed wine."
  * **Gloss:** The drink dossier opens: sealed wine — the container's integrity signals untouched purity, the opposite of earthly adulteration.
* **`[83:26]`** "Whose seal is musk—this is what competitors should compete for."
  * **Gloss:** The seal's fragrance doubles as a marketplace call: the reward is not merely consumed but competed-for — desire sanctified rather than abolished.
* **`[83:27]`** "Its mixture is of Tasneem."
  * **Gloss:** The named ingredient: Tasneem — a spring-name unique to this verse in the corpus, the celestial mixer that distinguishes the righteous' cup from any earthly vintage.
* **`[83:28]`** "A spring from which those brought near drink."
  * **Gloss:** The spring's clientele named: "those brought near" (muqarrabun) — the same privileged category that witnesses the register at 83:21; Tasneem is their exclusive source.
* **`[83:29]`** "Those who committed crimes used to laugh at those who believed."
  * **Gloss:** The mockery replayed, frame one: laughter directed at believers — the criminals' (mujrimun) signature gesture.
* **`[83:30]`** "And when they passed by them, they would wink at one another."
  * **Gloss:** Frame two: winking as conspiratorial solidarity — the mockers signal to each other, performing contempt as a group ritual.
* **`[83:31]`** "And when they went back to their families, they would go back exulting."
  * **Gloss:** Frame three: exultation carried home — the mockery is not just public performance but private satisfaction; the family-space becomes the echo-chamber of contempt.
* **`[83:32]`** "And if they saw them, they would say, "These people are lost.""
  * **Gloss:** Frame four: the slur spoken directly — "These people are lost" (astray-charge) spoken BY the mocked about the believers, not a separate sin-list.
* **`[83:33]`** "Yet they were not sent as guardians over them."
  * **Gloss:** The jurisdictional correction: no guardianship was delegated to them over anyone — the mockers' authority is self-appointed, void.
* **`[83:34]`** "But on that Day, those who believed will laugh at the unbelievers."
  * **Gloss:** The reversal begins: they laughed → will be laughed at — beat-for-beat inversion; the believers now hold the gaze.
* **`[83:35]`** "On luxurious furnishings, looking on."
  * **Gloss:** The vantage-point of laughter: luxurious furnishings (raha'il) — verbatim-twin `[52:20]`, `[56:15]` (rg); they looked down from arrogance → the believers look on from couches.
* **`[83:36]`** "Have the unbelievers been repaid for what they used to do?"
  * **Gloss:** The close is a question whose answer is the whole surah: yes, repayment is exactly what this book-files system delivers; "for what they used to do" — the retributive refrain (cf. `[52:19]`, `[16:32]` — rg).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`tatfif` (defrauding)**: shorting the measure — giving less than weighed-out.
  * **Rendering:** "the defrauders" (83:1), "they cheat" (83:3).
  * **Elsewhere:** the Shuaib refrain cluster, all rg-verified: `[26:183]` "do not defraud people of their belongings" (same root), `[7:85]` "Give full measure and weight, and do not cheat people out of their rights", `[11:85]` "Give full measure and full weight, in all fairness", `[17:35]` "weigh with accurate scales. That is fair, and the best determination". — k-infraq's flank, now fully renderings-verified.
* **`Sijjeen` / `Elliyyeen`**: the two registers' names — each glossed by the identical definition "A numerical book".
  * **Rendering:** names left transliterated (kitab marqum-type: "A numerical book" ×2 — rg-verified); 83:19 prints "Elliyyoon" against 83:18's "Elliyyeen" — in-book variance, *Source: observable in corpus; class-2 typography per the artifacts fleeting note*.
  * **Elsewhere:** no other register-names in the corpus; dossier-mates: `[50:4]` "a comprehensive book", `[17:13]` "a book which he will find spread open".
* **`“Legends of the ancients”`**: the denier's canned reply (asatir al-awwalin).
  * **Rendering:** identical phrase `[16:24]` (the only twin, rg-verified); variant "Tales of the ancients" at `[25:5]`, `[46:17]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** the rebuttal pattern is k-kalam's territory (channel-claims vs. provenance-charges — see 69:41, 81:22 cited in its Practical Reading).
* **`ranā’ / "corroded"` and `hijab / "screened"`**: earning → coating → exclusion (83:14–15).
  * **Rendering:** "hearts have become corroded by what they used to earn" (83:14); "screened from their Lord" (83:15).
  * **Elsewhere:** "screened" family rg-verified: `[41:5]` "Our hearts are screened", `[18:101]` "whose eyes were screened to My message", `[19:17]` (she screened herself) — the covering motif's English-chain. (*Source: classical reading of 83:14 as sin-layering — rana — Ibn Kathir/Tabari on 83:14 — verify before quoting detail.*)
