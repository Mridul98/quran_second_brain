## 3. Structural Arc
* **Origin-claim (76:1–3):** unmentionable nothing → mixed liquid, made hearing-and-seeing → guided to the way, two-way verdict: appreciative or unappreciative.
* **The split (76:4–6):** the faithless' equipment (chains, yokes, a Searing Fire) against the righteous' drink (aroma-mixture, Allah's servants' gushing spring).
* **The grateful profile (76:7–10):** vows fulfilled; dread of the widespread-ill Day; feeding the three named classes; the audited sentence "We want from you neither compensation, nor gratitude."
* **The reward dossier (76:11–22):** protection, radiance, joy; Garden and silk for patience; climate-free thrones; hanging fruit; silver-crystal tableware measured exactly; Zanjabeel and Salsabeel by name; pearl-like youths; the vast kingdom; pure drink; the appreciation-declaration (76:22).
* **Ruku 2 pivot (76:23–26):** revelation addressed to the Prophet — patience charge, dhikr morning/evening, night prostration and long-night glorification.
* **The rival crowd + will-cluster (76:27–31):** fleeting-life lovers leaving the Heavy Day behind; frame-strength and replacement power; the reminder and the path; the will-hedge; mercy selective, punishment prepared.
