# From nameless nothing to appreciated effort: the tested man and the gratitude profile (76:1–22)
**UID:** 202610142255
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 76:1–22 (Ruku 1 of 2 in al-Insan)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[76:1]` – `[76:22]` (22 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The human dossier: created from a mixed liquid to be tested, made hearing-and-seeing, guided to the way — then sorted into appreciative or unappreciative; the grateful profile is drawn in four moves (vows, dread, feeding the three named classes, refusing thanks from the fed) and answered with the full reward inventory, sealed by the declaration "This is a reward for you. Your efforts are well appreciated."
* **Placement:** Ruku 1 of 2 — the two-profiles half; ruku 2 (76:23–31) turns from the rewarded to the Messenger who was sent the reminder.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[76:1]`** "Has there come upon man a period of time when he was nothing to be mentioned?" — the opening claim-stinger: before naming, there was no nameable thing (concept-twin `[19:67]` "We created him before, when he was nothing?" — rg-verified).
* **`[76:2]`** "We created man from a liquid mixture, to test him; and We made him hearing and seeing." — origin (amshāj — "liquid mixture," task's "mixed drop" ✗), purpose (test), equipment (the senses — end-sense twins `[67:23]`, `[23:78]`, both terminating in thanks, rg-verified).
* **`[76:3]`** "We guided him to the way, be he appreciative or unappreciative." — THE hinge: shākir/kafūr as one pair — gratitude or ingratitude as the only two exits from guidance (k-shukr centerpiece junction; forward link).
* **`[76:4]`** "We have prepared for the faithless chains, and yokes, and a Searing Fire." — the ungrateful's equipment (yokes-family `[13:5]`, `[40:71]`, `[34:33]`; "Searing Fire" corpus-unique here — rg).
* **`[76:5]`** "But the righteous will drink from a cup whose mixture is aroma." — kāfūr rendered as translated "aroma" (naming-practice drift — see §3).
* **`[76:6]`** "A spring from which the servants of Allah will drink, making it gush abundantly." — tafjir: the free-flowing ownership — servants who served the poor now served by a spring.
* **`[76:7]`** "They fulfill their vows, and dread a Day whose ill is widespread." — profile move #1–2: vows kept (dossier joins 22:29, 3:35, 33:23, 68:17 — rg) + dread with horizon ("widespread" is 76:7-only, rg).
* **`[76:8]`** "And they feed, for the love of Him, the poor, and the orphan, and the captive." — move #3: the triad of the no-reciprocity classes; "for the love of Him" admits both readings (for Allah's love / in His love — Source: classical dispute — verify; the captive is the third name the world forgets).
* **`[76:9]`** "“We only feed you for the sake of Allah. We want from you neither compensation, nor gratitude." — move #4, spoken by the fed-or-fed-to: the ANTI-shukr-for-show clause — the very word shukr ("gratitude") renounced as a claim on others (k-infraq's intent-pure pole: 2:264's voiding "reminders" excluded in advance).
* **`[76:10]`** "We dread from our Lord a frowning grim Day.”" — the motive clause: dread, not display ("frowning" corpus-unique, rg); completes 7–10 as one quoted act of worship.
* **`[76:11]`** "So Allah will protect them from the ills of that Day, and will grant them radiance and joy." — the "so": the dread Day met with protection; nuḍūra — the same radiance-root as 75:22's faces (forward to `[80:38]` family, rg).
* **`[76:12]`** "And will reward them for their patience with a Garden and silk." — sabr priced (k-sabr): patience here names the whole vow-vigil-fast-and-feed economy of 7–10 (Source: agent inference from the profile — verify).
* **`[76:13]`** "Reclining therein on the thrones; experiencing therein neither sun, nor frost." — climate abolished (frost corpus-unique, rg); thrones-family `[52:20]` "luxurious furnishings" (rg).
* **`[76:14]`** "Its shade hovering over them, and its fruit brought low within reach." — the hang-low abundance (verbatim-family twin `[69:23]` "Its pickings are within reach" — rg; cf. `[19:25]` palm shaken down, rg).
* **`[76:15]`** "Passing around them are vessels of silver, and cups of crystal." — the tableware inventory ("crystal" occurrences are 76:15–16 only, rg).
* **`[76:16]`** "Crystal of silver—they measured them exactly." — qadru-mastery: the vessels fitted to need ("measured" family ↔ 77:23 "We measured precisely" — cross-surah echo, rg).
* **`[76:17]`** "They will be served therein with a cup whose flavor is Zanjabeel." — ginger-note transliterated where 76:5's kāfūr was translated — this edition's naming drift (see §3).
* **`[76:18]`** "A spring therein named Salsabeel." — the only named spring here, transliterated (cf. 83:27–28 "Tasneem" kept by name, rg).
* **`[76:19]`** "Passing among them are eternalized youths. If you see them, you would think them sprinkled pearls." — servants like scattered pearls ("eternalized" corpus-unique; the same scene-family at `[52:24]` "youths like hidden pearls" and `[56:17]` "immortalized youth" — rg-verified drift).
* **`[76:20]`** "Wherever you look, you see bliss, and a vast kingdom." — the panoramic audit: no dead pixels ("vast kingdom" corpus-unique, rg).
* **`[76:21]`** "Upon them are garments of green silk, and satin. And they will be adorned with bracelets of silver. And their Lord will offer them a pure drink." — dress code complete (silk-family `[22:23]`, `[35:33]`; raḥīq — "pure drink" twin `[37:45]`, rg-verified); task's "pure drink at 76:11" corrected: it is here.
* **`[76:22]`** "“This is a reward for you. Your efforts are well appreciated.”" — the appreciation-declaration: sa'y rendered "efforts… well appreciated" — exact idiom-twin `[17:19]` "their effort will be appreciated" (rg; task's 52:21 pairing ✗ — 52:21 is the hostage-verse, already s074's twin).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`shākir / kafūr`** (appreciative/unappreciative): 76:3 — the surah's sorting verb-pair (root sh-k-r).
  * **Rendering:** "appreciative or unappreciative" at 76:3; the same root as "gratitude" (76:9) and "ingrate" territory — while 76:24 renders a kāfūr-family word "blasphemer" (drift within one surah — Source: translator's term choice, observable; Arabic-root identity verify).
  * **Elsewhere:** k-shukr dossier cites `[31:12]` "Whoever is appreciative—" — same word (verified in note); `[76:3]` is the pair-form, the note's missing centerpiece.
* **`subūl`** (compensation): 76:9 — payment demanded by preachers and traders.
  * **Rendering:** "neither compensation, nor gratitude" — refuse the fee AND the thanks.
  * **Elsewhere:** "compensation" for the preacher-wage formula `[6:90]`, `[34:47]` (rg-verified; cf. `[25:57]` "I ask of you no payment" — rg).
* **Spring-names (kāfūr / Zanjabeel / Salsabeel / Tasneem):** 76:5, 17, 18.
  * **Rendering:** only kāfūr is translated ("aroma"); Zanjabeel/Salsabeel transliterated; 83:27–28 transliterates Tasneem (rg) — naming-practice inconsistent across the edition (Source: translator's term choice, observable — the transliterate-vs-translate rule is unclear; fleeting-grade, reported to batch).
* **`sa'y`** (effort/labor): 76:22 — labor appreciated, not merely repaid.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[17:19]` verbatim twin (rg) — the appreciated-labor line belongs to this idiom family, not 52:21.
