# A gradual revelation, a patient Messenger, morning-and-evening remembrance, and the grammar of will (76:23–31)
**UID:** 202610142256
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 76:23–31 (Ruku 2 of 2 in al-Insan)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[76:23]` – `[76:31]` (9 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The camera turns from the rewarded to the addressed: this book is a piecemeal descent — so endure your Lord's decision, ignore the sinner and the ingrate-in-disguise, answer with dhikr morning and evening and prostration through the night; the rival crowd (fleeting-life lovers, Heavy-Day deserters) is named, the replacement-power stated, and the whole surah exits through the will-cluster: the reminder offered, the path taken by whoever wills — hedged by "you cannot will unless Allah wills" — mercy admitting, punishment prepared.
* **Placement:** Ruku 2 of 2 — the charge-and-close half; ruku 1 described the two profiles, this ruku assigns the Messenger his posture between them.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[76:23]`** "It is We who sent down the Quran upon you—a gradual revelation." — the channel claim in one breath: nazala + tanjīman — descent in installments (k-kalam node; twin `[17:106]` "We unfolded gradually... revealed it in stages" — rg-verified).
* **`[76:24]`** "So be patient for the decision of your Lord, and do not obey the sinner or the blasphemer among them." — the sabr imperative aimed at a verdict still pending; the two names of the pressure from outside (the second noun tracks the same sh-k-r-root family as 76:3 — drift, see §3).
* **`[76:25]`** "And mention the Name of your Lord, morning and evening." — the dhikr bracket: the same clock-phrase as `[33:42]` "glorify Him morning and evening" and `[48:9]` "praise Him morning and evening" (rg-verified).
* **`[76:26]`** "And for part of the night, prostrate yourself to Him, and glorify Him long into the night." — the night shift (sajda + tasbīḥ) — the Muzzammil pair: `[73:2]` "Stay up during the night, except a little", `[73:6]` "The vigil of night is more effective" (rg-verified), and `[17:79]`'s extra vigil (rg).
* **`[76:27]`** "As for these: they love the fleeting life, and leave behind a Heavy Day." — the counter-profile in one line — near-verbatim twin of `[75:20–21]` "you love the fleeting life. And you disregard the Hereafter" (rg-verified; "Heavy Day" corpus-unique here).
* **`[76:28]`** "We created them, and strengthened their frame; and whenever We will, We can replace them with others like them." — the power clause: frame-strength is lent, replacement is reserved (twin `[70:41]` "To replace them with better than they" — rg; cf. `[4:56]` skin-replacement, rg).
* **`[76:29]`** "This is a reminder; so whoever wills, let him take a path to his Lord." — the offer: tadhkira + the path — exact twin `[25:57]` "only that whoever wills may take a path to his Lord" (rg-verified) — and the Fatihah petition (1:6) in the same grammar.
* **`[76:30]`** "Yet you cannot will, unless Allah wills. Allah is Knowing and Wise." — the hedge that is also the mercy-anchor (will-cluster twins `[74:55–56]`, `[81:28–29]` — rg-verified).
* **`[76:31]`** "He admits into His mercy whomever He wills. But as for the wrongdoers, He has prepared for them a painful punishment." — the close: selective admission, prepared consequence — "prepared" echoes 76:4's "We have prepared" for the faithless (rg within s076 — the two-preparations bracket).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`tanjīman`** (gradual revelation): 76:23 — the adverb as theology: descent paced to reception.
  * **Rendering:** "a gradual revelation" — "gradual" elsewhere only in `[17:106]` (unfolded gradually) and `[6:47]`/`[7:182]` punishment-graduality (rg).
* **"Heavy Day"**: 76:27 — the Day as weight pressing on the forgetful.
  * **Rendering:** "a Heavy Day" — corpus-unique (rg); its twin verb "leave behind" is 75:21's "disregard" (rg).
* **"the sinner or the blasphemer"**: 76:24 — the two pressure-names.
  * **Rendering drift:** the root rendered "unappreciative" (76:3) and "gratitude" (76:9) surfaces here under "blasphemer"; the "blasphemer" word elsewhere tracks kāfir-class scenes (`[2:258]`, `[29:68]`, `[46:3]`, `[86:17]` — rg-verified) — Source: translator's term choice (observable; Arabic-root identity of 76:24's second noun verify).
* **`sabeel/path`**: 76:29 — the will-directed road.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[25:57]` exact twin (rg) — path as the answer to a no-fee reminder (k-guidance junction).
