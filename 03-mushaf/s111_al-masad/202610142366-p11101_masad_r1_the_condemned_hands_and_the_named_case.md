# The Condemned Hands: A Named Man, His Wealth, and His Wife (111:1–5)
**UID:** 202610142366
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 111:1–5 (Ruku 1 of 1 in al-Masad)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[111:1]` – `[111:5]` (5 ayahs; global ruku 553 of 556 — verified)
* **Thesis:** A full surah as a single case-file: Abee Lahab — the only contemporary of the Prophet named by name anywhere in the Quran — is condemned personally, his wealth and earnings declared useless as ransom, and his wife joined to him carrying the fuel and wearing the rope; the text converts one man's curse-word into an everlasting verdict.
* **Placement:** Ruku 1 of 1; third door of the closing bracket. The unit's opposition-track: 109 disavows a CATEGORY (disbelievers, unnamed), 111 condemns a PERSON (named) — the two poles bracket the creed-core (112) and refuge-pair (113–114).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[111:1]`** "Condemned are the hands of Abee Lahab, and he is condemned."
  * **Gloss:** The ancient curse-idiom (*tabbat yada…*, "may his hands perish") re-cast as a verdict in force: "Condemned… and he is condemned" — doubled, once to the hands (metonym for power/agency) and once to the man himself, which is the idiom's own unpacking (*Source: classical idiom-reading — verify; observable: the doubling and the "hands" body-part kept*). NOTE (hint corrections): this edition says neither "perish" nor "two hands" — "two hands" has zero corpus sites (rg-verified).
* **`[111:2]`** "His wealth did not avail him, nor did what he acquired."
  * **Gloss:** The two assets named and nullified — inherited wealth and self-made earnings (*kasb*) — neither "avails": the Quran's recurring eschatology-of-worthlessness formula; exact twins: `[39:50]` "what they had earned did not avail them" and `[15:84]` "All they had acquired was of no avail to them" (rg-verified).
* **`[111:3]`** "He will burn in a Flaming Fire."
  * **Gloss:** The man whose very kunya is "father of flame" (Lahab) gets the Flame as abode — the corpus's cruelest naming-h symmetry (wordplay is in the Arabic, not Itani's English; agent note — verify). The capitalized "Flaming Fire" belongs to the Fire-name cluster incl. `[85:10]` "the punishment of Burning" (rg-verified).
* **`[111:4]`** "And his wife—the firewood carrier."
  * **Gloss:** The em-dash epithet: she is defined by service to his fuel. Same English word at `[72:15]` "firewood for Hell" (different Arabic — observable coincidence of register, not a term-link). Classical reports make her enmity active (thorn-carrying on the Prophet's road, or slander-transport) — transmitted gloss, not in the verse (§6).
* **`[111:5]`** "Around her neck is a rope of thorns."
  * **Gloss:** The surah-title verse: *masad* rendered "thorns" (classically a twisted palm-fiber rope — the hint's "palm-fiber" is tafsir gloss, not this translation). Her neck-loop mirrors her trade: what she carried for others she now wears (agent reading of the epithet's placement — verify). English "thorns" elsewhere renders a DIFFERENT word at `[88:6]` "no food except thorns" (rg-verified — do not merge the dossiers).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`tabba / Condemned`**: the doom-verb of the curse idiom.
  * **Rendering:** "Condemned are… and he is condemned" — doubled, passive-form.
  * **Elsewhere:** the active curse sense appears at `[8:2]`? — unverified, NOT cited; this phrase-form is unique in the corpus (rg-verified: no other "Condemned are").
* **`yad (hands)`**: agency, power, earning.
  * **Rendering:** "the hands of Abee Lahab" — no numeral (the hint's "two hands" is the Arabic dual's literal shape, absent from Itani — rg-verified, zero sites).
  * **Elsewhere:** hand-as-power: `[5:64]` "Allah’s hand is tied… His hands are outstretched"; `[48:10]` "The hand of Allah is over their hands" (covenant-hand verse — k-covenant already runs it); hand-as-earning: `[2:79]` "what their hands have written", `[78:40]` "a person will observe what his hands have produced"; regret-hands: `[18:42]` "wringing his hands", `[25:27]` "will bite his hands" (all rg-verified). 111:1 belongs to the first family: hands = the power that just failed at 111:2.
* **`avail (agma / afada family)`**: eschatological uselessness of assets.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[39:50]`, `[15:84]`, `[26:207]` "Of what avail to them will be their past enjoyments?" (rg-verified) — a small, precise dossier 111:2 anchors.
