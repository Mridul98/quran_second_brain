## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Meccan per the classical type-lists — the corpus states nothing (header = title/source/basmala only). *Source: classical type-lists — verify.* Revelation order: **transmitted nuzul tables — verify**.
* **Size:** 5 ayahs, 1 ruku (ruku_map global 553 — verified). Third door of the closing bracket 109–114.
* **Title:** al-Masad — Itani's header "THORNS" tracks `[111:5]` "a rope of thorns"; the word *masad* itself is a rope (classically of palm-fiber), so the header leans figurative — the hint's "palm-fiber" is the classical gloss, not this edition's wording (see §5b).
* **Central theme:** The only Quranic verdict addressed to a NAMED contemporary individual: Abee Lahab and his wife — wealth, earnings, and status all "did not avail," and the two go to the Fire and the rope. Between 110 (victory) and 112 (creed) the unit inserts a single concrete case-file: triumph's enemy is not defeated by force but by an announced ruin that his own assets could not ransom.
* **Reading hint:** the surah's cruelty is its precision — a name, a wife, a rope. Contrast with 109, which never names: category-vs-person is the closing unit's two-track treatment of opposition.

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Verdict + confirmation (111:1):** "Condemned are the hands of Abee Lahab, and he is condemned." — the curse-idiom doubled: the hands sentenced, then the man; Itani keeps the idiom's body-part ("hands") untranslated in reference (see passage §3).
* **Why the verdict stands (111:2):** "His wealth did not avail him, nor did what he acquired." — assets and earnings, the two things Abu Lahab actually owned, nullified as ransom (*Source: observable clause-pair*).
* **His end (111:3):** "He will burn in a Flaming Fire." — the man who said "what of your religion?" (classical occasion, §6) gets a fire named twice over (*lahab* = flame inside *al-lahab* wordplay is untranslatable; agent note — verify against Arabic).
* **Her end (111:4–5):** "And his wife—the firewood carrier. / Around her neck is a rope of thorns." — the em-dash carries the epithet; the pair completes the house-curse: both burn, she in service of the fuel.
* **Unit position:** the named-case interlude between the victory-sign (110) and the creed-core (112); the closing unit answers opposition three ways — 109 separation, 110+111 verdicts, 112–114 refuge.

## 5b. Batch-Return Verification Flags (this index)
* **Hint renderings corrected:** "perish the two hands of Abu Lahab and he has perished" → "**Condemned are the hands of Abee Lahab, and he is condemned.**" — (a) "two hands" appears NOWHERE in this corpus (rg-verified; Itani simply says "the hands"); (b) the name is spelled "**Abee** Lahab" in this edition; (c) *tabban* rendered "Condemned," not "perish". "into a flame" → "**He will burn in a Flaming Fire.**"; "the wood-carrier" → "**the firewood carrier**" (with em-dash before); "a rope of palm-fiber" → "**a rope of thorns**" (palm-fiber = classical gloss, not Itani).
* **Name-census (honest version of the hint's claim):** "Lahab" occurs at 111:1 ONLY (rg-verified) — Abu Lahab IS the only named *contemporary* of the Prophet and the only individual condemned by name in a whole surah. But the blanket "only named individual opponent in the Quran" is FALSE as phrased: named past opponents exist — `[28:6]`/`[40:24]` "**Hamaan**" and "**Quaroon**" (this edition's spellings — rg-verified; "Pharaoh" itself is a title, not a name). Census claim must carry these exceptions.
* **Classical occasion (Safa proclamation + Abu Lahab's "may you perish") = TRANSMITTED REPORT** — the surah names the man but narrates no scene; label verify (§6).
* **Spouse-paired doom form — VERIFIED twin:** `[66:10]` names the wives of Noah and Lot betraying their righteous husbands — "They availed them nothing against Allah, and it was said, 'Enter the Fire…'" — the same avail-nullifier as 111:2 and the same husband-wife verdict-frame as 111:1+4; `[66:11]` (the wife of Pharaoh, believing) is the mirror case. 111 is the only such pair where BOTH spouses are condemned AND the man is named (all lines rg-verified; typology = agent inference — verify).
* **Two-hands idiom verdict:** hint's candidates located — `[5:64]` (Allah's "hand"/"hands are outstretched") and `[48:10]` ("The hand of Allah is over their hands") are the corpus's hand-as-power sites; the regret-hands idiom kin is `[18:42]` "wringing his hands" and `[25:27]` "bite his hands" — 111:1 sits with the power-idiom family, all rg-verified; the "2:??" slot resolved to `[2:79]`/`[2:95]` (hands as agency/earning).

* **Name-pun echo (observable):** the condemned man's own kunya *Lahab* ("flame") meets 111:3's "Flaming Fire" — the name IS the sentence in the Arabic (root l-h-b, per classical observation — verify); in English it survives as transliteration-echo only ("Lahab"… "Flaming") — do not present the wordplay as this translation's device.
* **"Abee Lahab" spelling census (rg-verified):** this edition transliterates with -ee ("Abee"); "Lahab" has exactly one corpus site (111:1) — no other surah or passage references him.
