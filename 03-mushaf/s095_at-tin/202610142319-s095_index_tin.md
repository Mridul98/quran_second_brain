## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Meccan — agreed in the classical type-lists; the sanctuary-oaths (fig/olive, Sinai, "this safe land") suit a Meccan audience for whom the third site is their own city. *Source: classical type-lists — verify.* Revelation order: **transmitted nuzul tables — verify** (corpus states no nuzul; unnumbered Basmala heads the surah).
* **Size:** 8 ayahs, 1 ruku (ruku_map global 537 — verified). Four oaths, one anthropology, one question, one verdict-name.
* **Title:** at-Tin — Itani's header "THE FIG" tracks `[95:1]` "By the fig and the olive"; "fig" is corpus-unique (rg-verified) — the only verse in the book using the word.
* **Central theme:** The human estate sworn by sacred geography: humanity made "in the best design" (95:4) then "reduced… to the lowest of the low" (95:5) — with one exception class, "those who believe and do righteous deeds," whose reward is "without end" (95:6). If that is the human plot, "So why do you still reject the religion?" (95:7) — the surah's operative question — because "Is Allah not the Wisest of the wise?" (95:8), i.e., the Judge of such stories exists and is wise. The oaths land where revelation landed: grove-land, Sinai, Mecca.
* **Reading hint:** trace the elevation/demotion line: "best design" (aḥsan-e taqwīm) → "lowest of the low" (asfala safilīn) → exception → "without end" — a four-beat fall-and-rescue plot compressed into three verses; then notice the oath-sites are all places of divine speech (Tor, and by classical reading the groves) — the sworn geography preaches the Judge (95:8). (*Source: structural observation; the identification of sites with revelation-geography is transmitted tafsir — verify.*)

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Four oaths (95:1–3):** "By the fig and the olive. / And Mount Sinai. / And this safe land." — produce-and-oil land, the mountain of the Mosaic address (2:63 "raised the Mount above you" — rg-verified; 23:20 "a tree springing out of Mount Sinai, producing oil" — rg-verified), and the sworn secure city — Abraham's answered prayer `[2:126]` "make this a peaceful land" and `[14:35]` "make this land peaceful" (rg-verified), Quraysh's security `[106:4]` "has secured them against fear" (rg-verified), `[90:1]` "I swear by this land" (rg-verified).
* **The anthropology (95:4–6):** created in the best design → reduced to the lowest of the low → except the believing doers, "a reward without end" — the exact phrase of `[11:108]` (the happy in Paradise, "a reward without end" — rg-verified two-site cluster).
* **The courtroom turn (95:7–8):** the diversity-of-estates fact becomes an indictment: what still makes you "reject the religion"? (the *dīn* object exactly as in `[107:1]` "him who denies the religion" — rg-verified) — closed by the name-claim "the Wisest of the wise," spoken elsewhere only by Noah, `[11:45]` (rg-verified).
* **Pairing with 96:** 95 ends asking what makes you deny the Judge; 96 opens with the command "Read" from the Lord who teaches — judgment-question followed by revelation-answer (structural adjacency; 95's "creation in design" rhymes 96:2 "Created man from a clot" — same creation-to-judgment ladder). (*Source: structural observation — agent inference.*)
* **The interrogative hinge (95:7):** the "you" is singular — the surah pivots mid-breath from third-person human-estate reportage to a second-person indictment (classical readers debate each-denier vs a named individual — transmitted — verify).

## 5b. Batch-Return Verification Flags (this index)
* **Hint renderings corrected:** "man in the best **fabric**" → "We created man in the best **design**" (95:4); "**brought down** to the lowest" → "Then **reduced him** to the lowest of the low" (95:5); "**boundless** reward" → "a reward **without end**" (95:6, exact twin of 11:108 — rg-verified); "what then makes you **deny the Judgment**" → "So why do you still **reject the religion**?" (95:7 — *bi-d-dīn* rendered "the religion," never "the Judgment," here and at 107:1); "God is the **best of judges**" → "Is Allah not **the Wisest of the wise**?" (95:8 — the phrase "best of judges" does not occur anywhere in this corpus — rg-verified; "Wisest of the wise" occurs twice: 11:45, 95:8).
* **"Sinai" located:** "Mount Sinai" appears only at 95:2 and `[23:20]` (rg-verified); the 2:63 mountain event is rendered "the Mount" — same *Ṭūr* referent per classical reading, verify.
* **"Olive" cluster:** "fig" corpus-unique (95:1 only — rg-verified); "olives" at `[6:141]`, `[80:29]`, and the Sinai oil-tree `[23:20]` (rg-verified) — the sworn pair is the corpus' own agriculture.
* **"Safe land" is not unique in kind:** 2:126/14:35 Abraham's prayer + 29:67 "Secure Sanctuary" + 106:4 — four-site security cluster (rg-verified); 95:3 swears by the answered prayer.
* **"Mount Sinai" vs "the Mount":** 2:63's pledge event is rendered "the Mount," while 95:2/23:20 say "Mount Sinai" — the referent-identity is standard reading, verify against the apparatus (the English forms differ; rg-verified).
* **11:108 twin quoted exactly:** "a reward without end" — both sites verbatim (rg-verified); the exception-class (95:6) and the-happy-in-Paradise class (11:108) share their reward-phrase word-for-word.
