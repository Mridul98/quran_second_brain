# Best Design, Lowest of Low: the human estate sworn by sacred land (95:1–8)
**UID:** 202610142320
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 95:1–8 (Ruku 1 of 1 in at-Tin)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[95:1]` – `[95:8]` (8 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Sworn by fig-and-olive, Mount Sinai, and "this safe land," the surah files its exhibit: humanity created in the best design and reducible to the lowest of the low — rescued only by the belief-and-works exception class with its endless reward — and then turns to the audience with the one question the exhibit makes unreasonable: "So why do you still reject the religion?" — because "Is Allah not the Wisest of the wise?"
* **Placement:** Ruku 1 of 1; the batch's shortest full arc of the fall-and-rescue plot. After the 93–94 prophet-consolation diptych, 95 widens from the prophet's estate to the human estate; 96 will answer 95's judgment-question with the revelation-command.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[95:1]`–`[95:2]`** "By the fig and the olive. / And Mount Sinai."
  * **Gloss:** Oath one: the fruit-and-oil pair — "fig" is the corpus' only occurrence of the word (rg-verified); "olives" belongs to the blessing-inventories `[6:141]` "olives and pomegranates" and `[80:29]` "olives and dates" (rg-verified). Oath two: "Mount Sinai" — the phrase occurs only here and at `[23:20]` "a tree springing out of Mount Sinai, producing oil" (rg-verified), the mountain of the pledge `[2:63]` "raised the Mount above you" (rg-verified) — covenant geography inside the produce oath. What the sites are (lands, sanctuaries, revelation places) is transmitted identifications — the text itself only names them (*Source: classical tafsir geography — verify*).
* **`[95:3]`** "And this safe land."
  * **Gloss:** Oath three, deixis the Meccan hearer can see: the sworn secure city — Abraham's answered petition `[2:126]` "make this a peaceful land" / `[14:35]` "make this land peaceful" (rg-verified), `[106:4]` "who… has secured them against fear" (rg-verified), `[29:67]` "We established a Secure Sanctuary" (rg-verified), and `[90:1]` "I swear by this land" (rg-verified). Three oaths: grove, mountain, city — produce, Torah, Quraysh's own security.
* **`[95:4]`** "We created man in the best design."
  * **Gloss:** The oath's answer, a summit: *awsan-e taqwīm* — "best design." Creation-and-shaping twins: `[75:38]` "And He created and proportioned" and `[82:8]` "In whatever shape He willed, He assembled you" (rg-verified) — the design is deliberate, proportioned, willed.
* **`[95:5]`** "Then reduced him to the lowest of the low."
  * **Gloss:** The one-beat fall: *asfala safilīn*. The "lowest" word-family carries the Fire's bottom `[4:145]` "the lowest level of the Fire," the humiliated `[41:29]` "so they become of the lowest," and the defeated word `[9:40]` "made the word of those who disbelieve the lowest" (rg-verified) — so "reduced… to the lowest of the low" reads toward abasement-and-return, the reversal that `[56:3]` "Bringing low, raising high" describes on Judgment day (rg-verified). (*Source: agent inference on word-family; the identity of the "lowest" — senility, hell, both — is transmitted tafsir — verify.*)
* **`[95:6]`** "Except those who believe and do righteous deeds; for them is a reward without end."
  * **Gloss:** The exception clause (*illa*): faith-works people exit the fall-curve; their reward is "without end" — *minnon* — the exact phrase of the Paradise tableaus `[11:108]` "a reward without end" (rg-verified). Two-site cluster: 95's exception class is 11:108's happy-in-paradise class.
* **`[95:7]`** "So why do you still reject the religion?"
  * **Gloss:** With the exhibit filed, the courtroom question lands: *fa-mā yukadhibuka bi-d-dīn* — "the religion" is Itani's consistent rendering of *dīn* here and at `[107:1]` "him who denies the religion" (rg-verified); the whole 107 surah then operationalizes denial (mistreating the orphan) — and 93:9 forbids precisely that. *Dīn* here is also the Reckoning's name (cf. `[1:4]` "Day of Judgment") — the pun is structural: reject the religion and you deny the Judgment. (*Source: translator's term choice observable in corpus; dual sense is classical — verify.*)
* **`[95:8]`** "Is Allah not the Wisest of the wise?"
  * **Gloss:** The verdict-name: *ahkame-l-ḥākimīn* — Itani "the Wisest of the wise," used elsewhere only in Noah's address `[11:45]` "Your promise is true, and You are the Wisest of the wise" (rg-verified). The Judge exists, and judging is wisdom, not caprice: the surah's last word converts the fall-curve into a court with a bench.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`aḥsan-e taqwīm (best design)`**: the summit-stroke of creation.
  * **Rendering:** "the best design" — shaping language.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[75:38]` "created and proportioned"; `[82:8]` "In whatever shape He willed, He assembled you" (rg-verified) — design is willed and variable-capable, hence 95:5's reversal is His prerogative too.
* **`asfala safilīn (lowest of the low)`**: the fall.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[4:145]` "lowest level of the Fire"; `[41:29]` "of the lowest"; `[9:40]` "the lowest" (rg-verified).
* **`dīn (the religion)`**: the rejected object at 95:7 — rendered "religion" (not "Judgment"); exact twin `[107:1]` (rg-verified).
* **`minnon (without end)`**: the exception-class reward.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[11:108]` exact phrase "a reward without end" (rg-verified) — endlessness as the counter to the reduced estate.
* **`aḥkam (Wisest of the wise)`**: 95:8; `[11:45]` only other site (rg-verified) — the judgeship-name in comparative-excellence form.
