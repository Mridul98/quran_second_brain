# Surah 78: THE EVENT (an-Naba’)
**UID:** 202610142261
**Type:** Index / Surah Map
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Meccan — agreed in the classical type-lists. Its neighbours in the mushaf (79, 80, 81) are Meccan too, and the transmitted revelation-order lists put this cluster in the early wave with 73/74; the exact order-number varies between the printed lists, so no number is asserted here — *Source: classical type-lists and revelation-order lists — verify against a printed table.* The unnumbered Basmala heads the surah (corpus line 3).
* **Size:** 40 ayahs, 2 ruku (30 + 10 — `ruku_map` verified); the surah that gives the juz-30 "Event/News" block its name-word.
* **Central theme:** One question and two answers. The surah opens on a crowd disputing about "the Great Event" (78:1–3), answers the dispute with an audit of unarguable favors (78:6–16), then runs the Day-of-Sorting dossier twice: first the ambush of Hell for the oppressors (78:17–30), then the triumph of the righteous under the sovereignty of "Lord of the heavens and the earth… none can argue with Him" (78:31–40). Its argumentative method is the favor-catalog → resurrection → courtroom sequence, closed by an ultimatum to the will: "So whoever wills, let him take a way back to his Lord" (78:39).

## 3. Structural Arc
* **The question (78:1–5):** three lines of stage-setting — what they ask, what it is, that they disagree — then the doubled "they will find out" (78:4–5), which settles the dispute by announcement rather than by argument.
* **The favors audit (78:6–16):** ten rhetorical "Did We not…" clauses, moving from earth-as-cradle and mountain-pegs, through pairs/sleep/night/day, to the seven strong ones, the blazing lamp, the pouring water and the produce — the shukr inventory in interrogative form.
* **The pivot (78:17):** "The Day of Sorting has been appointed" — the Event named as an appointment, and the catalog's addressee told what the favor-giver has scheduled.
* **The scene and the sentence (78:18–30):** trumpet, droves, sky opened into gateways, mountains dissolved to a mirage; Hell lying in ambush with its four specifications (eons, no coolness, boiling water and freezing hail, "a fitting requital"), the two crimes (no anticipation of reckoning; denial of signs utterly) and the ledger clause "We have enumerated everything in writing."
* **The counter-scene (78:31–36):** triumph, gardens and vineyards, splendid spouses, delicious drinks, no gossip or lies — and the same "fitting" word used for both sentences.
* **The courtroom and the ultimatum (78:37–40):** sovereignty named; the Spirit and the angels standing in row, speech permitted or withheld; then "That is the Day of Reality" and the warning "a near punishment," ending on the faithless one's wish to be dust.

## 5b. Batch-Return Verification Flags (this index)
* **Surah title:** the corpus header renders an-Naba’ as **"THE EVENT"**, and 78:2 as "About the Great Event." The task hint's "momentous News" is not this edition's rendering (verified corpus line 6).
* **78:6–16 renderings corrected against corpus:** "a bed" → **a cradle**; "seven firmaments" → **seven strong ones**; "rain" → **pouring water**; "grain and herbage and garden-trees dense" → **grains and vegetation … luxuriant gardens** (all verified).
* **78:7 "pegs" is a corpus hapax** (rg "pegs" → only 78:7). The twin mountain-verses render the same concept as "stabilizers" (21:31, 31:10), "cast mountains… lest it shifts" (16:15), "stabilizers" (15:19), "anchored" (79:32) — verified. **38:22 is not a mountain verse** (it is the start of David's two-disputants parable) — hint rejected.
* **78:39:** the hint's dialogue ("is this the truth? — Yes, by my Lord") is **not in this surah**; 78:39 reads "That is the Day of Reality. So whoever wills, let him take a way back to his Lord." The surah ends at 78:40 with the "O, I wish I were dust" clause; no "sealing them over / eyes wide" clause exists in s078 (verified full corpus file).
* **78:18:** "come in crowds" → **"come in droves"** (same rendering as 99:6, verified).
* **Bonus twin found while verifying 78:40:** the warning-formula "We have warned you of a near punishment" has a near-twin at `[92:14]` "I have warned you of a Fierce Blaze." (verified) — same first-person warning frame, different object.
