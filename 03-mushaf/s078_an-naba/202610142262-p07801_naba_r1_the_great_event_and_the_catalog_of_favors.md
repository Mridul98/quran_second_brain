# The disputed Event and the ten unarguable favors (78:1–30)
**UID:** 202610142262
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 78:1–30 (Ruku 1 of 2 in an-Naba’)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[78:1]`–`[78:30]` (30 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** A crowd is arguing about "the Great Event"; the surah refuses to debate it and instead produces ten "Did We not…" favors (78:6–16) from the same Builder — so that the Day of Sorting (78:17–20) arrives not as a proposition to win but as an appointment already scheduled, with Hell lying in ambush and everything enumerated in writing (78:21–30).
* **Placement:** Ruku 1 of 2; it carries the surah's name-word and the whole argumentative spine, while ruku 2 supplies only the counter-catalog. The favor-catalog here is the same inventory the shukr note tracks at 16:10–18, 14:32–33 and 6:99 — but cast as interrogation, and with no shukr verb demanded on the page.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[78:1]`** "What are they asking one another about?" **`[78:2]`** "About the Great Event." **`[78:3]`** "About which they disagree."
  * **Gloss:** the opening is pure theatre: an unnamed third party, a named subject, and the verdict that the argument is about it. The edition's title-word lands in 78:2 as "the Great Event."
* **`[78:4]`** "Surely, they will find out." **`[78:5]`** "Most certainly, they will find out."
  * **Gloss:** the doubled "they will find out" (kalla… fa-saya'lamūna) closes the dispute by deferring it to experience — no evidence is offered yet.
* **`[78:6]`** "Did We not make the earth a cradle?" **`[78:7]`** "And the mountains pegs?" **`[78:8]`** "And created you in pairs?"
  * **Gloss:** favors 1–3: habitability, stabilization, bipolarity of creation. "Pegs" (awtad) is this edition's unique rendering here.
* **`[78:9]`** "And made your sleep for rest?" **`[78:10]`** "And made the night a cover?" **`[78:11]`** "And made the day for livelihood?"
  * **Gloss:** favors 4–6: the daily rhythm, in the same order as 25:47 ("the night a covering… sleep for rest… the day a revival") — the catalog's domestic block.
* **`[78:12]`** "And built above you seven strong ones?" **`[78:13]`** "And placed a blazing lamp?"
  * **Gloss:** favors 7–8: the layered sky as construction, the sun as a lamp — architecture then illumination.
* **`[78:14]`** "And brought down from the clouds pouring water?" **`[78:15]`** "To produce with it grains and vegetation?" **`[78:16]`** "And luxuriant gardens?"
  * **Gloss:** favors 9–10 with their chain: clouds → water → grain, herbage, and vineyard-gardens — the same water→produce causal spine as 6:99 and 50:9–11.
* **`[78:17]`** "The Day of Sorting has been appointed."
  * **Gloss:** the pivot: the disputed Event is now named as a scheduled date (yawm al-fasl), and "appointed" answers "Did We not…" — the Same Author sets the deadline.
* **`[78:18]`** "The Day when the Trumpet is blown, and you will come in droves." **`[78:19]`** "And the sky is opened up, and becomes gateways."
  * **Gloss:** the Day's opening three moves: sound, assembly in troops, and the sky turned into open gates — the "cover" of 78:10 reversed into exposure.
* **`[78:20]`** "And the mountains are set in motion, and become a mirage."
  * **Gloss:** favor 2 (pegs) is revoked: the stabilizers become the desert-shimmer that promises water and gives nothing (cf. 24:39's mirage simile for unbelievers' works).
* **`[78:21]`** "Hell is lying in ambush." **`[78:22]`** "For the oppressors, a destination."
  * **Gloss:** Hell is stated as both an ambushing presence and the oppressors' "destination" — the waiting they were "not anticipating" (78:27) is already stationed there.
* **`[78:23]`** "Where they will remain for eons." **`[78:24]`** "They will taste therein neither coolness, nor drink." **`[78:25]`** "Except boiling water, and freezing hail."
  * **Gloss:** the sentence spelled out in contraries of 78:9–11 and 78:14: no sleep-rest, no cover, no drink — heat and cold both weaponized.
* **`[78:26]`** "A fitting requital." **`[78:27]`** "For they were not anticipating any reckoning." **`[78:28]`** "And they denied Our signs utterly."
  * **Gloss:** the two-crime etiology (78:27–28); "fitting" (78:26) states the equivalence thesis — punishment as measure, not excess; the paired use of the same English adjective at 78:36 is examined in the ruku-2 note.
* **`[78:29]`** "But We have enumerated everything in writing."
  * **Gloss:** the ledger clause: what the catalog made uncountable as blessing (cf. 16:18) is fully countable as record.
* **`[78:30]`** "So taste! We will increase you only in suffering."
  * **Gloss:** the "taste" imperative answers 78:24's "they will taste"; and the increase-promise is inverted — the shukr-law of 14:7 running in reverse.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`awtad` (pegs)** — 78:7 "And the mountains pegs?"
  * **Rendering:** "pegs" only here (rg "pegs" across corpus → single hit); elsewhere the same stabilization idea is "stabilizers" (`[21:31]`, `[31:10]`), "placed… lest it shifts" (`[16:15]`), "anchored" (`[79:32]`).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[78:20]` — the pegs revoked into a mirage; `[50:7]` "set on it mountains, and grew in it all kinds of delightful pairs" (the same pairing of stabilization and produce).
* **`yawm al-fasl` (the Day of Sorting)** — 78:17 "The Day of Sorting has been appointed."
  * **Rendering:** "Day of Sorting"; `[44:40]` renders the same construction "The Day of Sorting Out is the appointed time for them all" (verified).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[44:40]` is the corpus's only other use of the construction ("The Day of Sorting Out is the appointed time for them all"), so the two surahs share the term; the Day-name is not reused inside s078 — 78:17 hands the label to 78:18's scene.
* **"livelihood" (the day's earning, ma'āsh)** — 78:11 "And made the day for livelihood?"
  * **Rendering:** "livelihood" is this edition's stable English for the concept here (term identification per classical lexicons — verify; the corpus is translation-only).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[51:22]` "in the heaven is your livelihood", `[15:20]` "We created livelihoods for you", `[27:64]` "who gives you livelihood from the sky and the earth? Is there another god with Allah?", `[29:17]` "seek your livelihood from Allah, and worship Him, and thank Him" (all verified) — 27:64 is the exact favors→tawhid inference this ruku performs; 29:17 makes explicit the shukr-demand this ruku leaves implicit.
* **"from the clouds pouring water" (al-mu‘ṣirāt → thajjāj)** — 78:14
  * **Rendering:** "brought down from the clouds pouring water" — the clouds here are named as the pouring agents; classical lexicons read al-mu‘ṣirāt as the rain-bringing winds/clouds (*Source: classical lexicon — verify; the corpus is translation-only*). Cf. `[6:6]` "sent the clouds pouring down abundant rain", `[54:11]` "floodgates of heaven with water pouring down", `[56:31]` "outpouring water" (verified).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[80:25]` "We pour down water in abundance" — the sibling catalog two surahs later, same water→produce chain, different verb-rendering (verified).

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Meccan (classical consensus for the type). The opening's disputing crowd is presented by the text with no name, no creed and no location — the text's own claim is only that they "ask" and "disagree" (78:1–3).
* **Asbab / occasion:** classical reports identify the disputed "Event" as the Quraysh's argument about resurrection and, in some transmissions, attach 78:40's "I wish I were dust" to a specific denier. **Source: classical reports (e.g., the resurrection-dispute readings collected in the tafsir tradition) — verify**; nothing in the corpus line names any individual.
* **Term-choice observation:** "pegs" (78:7), "seven strong ones" (78:12), "blazing lamp" (78:13), "eons" (78:23), "freezing hail" (78:25) are all unique renderings in this corpus (rg-verified) — translator's term choice (observable in corpus), not a doctrinal claim.
