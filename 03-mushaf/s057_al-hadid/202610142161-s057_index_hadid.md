## 3. Structural Arc
* **Creed as cosmos (1–6):** doxology → kingdom/life-death → four names → six days/Throne/with-you → kingship/referral → night-and-day merged, hearts known. The surah's God is defined before the community is charged.
* **The two demands (7–11, 18):** believe (on the pledge, 8) and spend (from inheritance, 7/10) — the loan-question (11) rings again as the plural roster (18): one economy stated twice.
* **The Day as optics (12–15):** light ahead and right → begged, refused, walled → cross-examined → no ransom. Nifaq's settlement scene, quoted by the canonical note.
* **Diagnosis and cure (16–21):** harden not like the long-timed Book-people (16); earth revived after death (17); the world is five words and one yellowing rain (20); race (21).
* **Decree, balance, iron (22–25):** pre-written calamity steadies grief/gloat (22–23); stinginess answered by the Independent (24); Book, Balance, and iron sent down for the invisible-support test (25).
* **Lineage and grace (26–29):** Noah→Abraham→Jesus→monasticism's audit (26–27); fear-and-believe double portion (28); grace-sovereignty closes on the People of the Book's presumption (29) — the surah's first clause (all glorifies Him) and last clause (all grace is His) are one sentence said twice.
* **Loan-frame ring:** 57:11 asks (who will lend?), 57:18 answers (the charitable men and women, multiplied), 57:24 names the counter-position (the stingy who teach stinginess) — the surah's economy stated three times in ten verses; iron (25) and light (12/19/28) are what the two economies are tested against.
