# Prophethood in the line, the invented monkery, and grace in Allah's hand (57:26–29)
**UID:** 202610142165
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 57:26–29 (Ruku 4 of 4 in al-Hadid)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[57:26]` – `[57:29]` (4 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Salvation-history is compressed into one lineage claim — Noah and Abraham, with Prophethood and the Scripture established in their line, "some of them are guided, but many of them are sinners" (26), carried through the messengers to Jesus and his Gospel, then through the followers' God-instilled compassion to the community's self-invented monasticism, which God did not ordain and they did not keep (27); the ruku turns to the present audience with the double-portion promise — mercy, light to walk by, forgiveness (28) — and closes the whole surah by striking the exclusivity claim: the People of the Book must know they have no power over Allah's grace, because all grace is in His hand (29).
* **Placement:** The closing ruku; the surah's prophetic-cycle ledger and its grace-thesis in one movement — answering ruku 1's pledge (57:8) and rhyming the race-verse (57:21) into a double portion.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[57:26]`** "We sent Noah and Abraham, and established in their line Prophethood and the Scripture. Some of them are guided, but many of them are sinners." — **Gloss:** The task's prophetic-cycle verification ✓ (Itani prints "sinners," not "corrupt"). The lineage-claim near-twins `[29:27]` "conferred on his descendants the Prophethood and the Book" and the triple at `[3:79]` (Scripture, wisdom, prophethood) — both rg-verified in the brain (p00610's cluster). Note the two-tone verdict inside one bloodline: guidance and sin are both inherited possibilities — election is not protection (cf. 57:27's repeat "many of them are sinful" — the ruku's refrain).
* **`[57:27]`** "Then We sent in their wake Our messengers, and followed up with Jesus son of Mary, and We gave him the Gospel, and instilled in the hearts of those who followed him compassion and mercy. But as for the monasticism which they invented—We did not ordain it for them—only to seek Allah’s approval. But they did not observe it with its due observance. So We gave those of them who believed their reward, but many of them are sinful." — **Gloss:** The chain runs to Jesus with one book named — "the Gospel" (Injeel: the corpus's Jesus-book term, one of six surah sites, fronted by the Torah-and-Gospel pair at `[3:3]` — rg-verified). Compassion-and-mercy is *instilled by God* — the same divine heart-grammar as 58:22's "inscribed faith in their hearts" and 59:9's greed-protection (cross-batch; Source: observable structural echo — agent inference). The monasticism line is the surah's sharpest history-of-practice claim: self-invented devotion, undertaken to seek Allah's approval, unobserved with its due observance — reward still given to those who believed. p00511 already cites this verse in the brain (verified: "the ruhbānity credited here as affection's cause and famously scrutinized there"). Classical identification of which sect is meant varies — Source: extra-corpus, verify.
* **`[57:28]`** "O you who believe! Fear Allah, and believe in His Messenger: He will give you a double portion of His mercy, and will give you a light by which you walk, and will forgive you. Allah is Forgiving and Merciful." — **Gloss:** The address now to *this* community, and the task's guidance/eschatology thread resolves: the light "by which you walk" is the walking-lamp promised — p04205 and p00610 already pair it with `[6:91]` and `[6:122]` (rg-verified in brain). "a double portion of His mercy" is corpus-unique (rg) — the 2x answer to the 2x claim of 57:27's failed innovation: fear + believe = doubled mercy, light, forgiveness. The taqwa-command brackets the batch: here, `[58:9]` "fear Allah, to Whom you will be gathered," `[59:18]` commanded twice.
* **`[57:29]`** "That the People of the Book may know that they have no power whatsoever over Allah’s grace, and that all grace is in Allah’s hand; He gives it to whomever He wills. Allah is Possessor of Great Grace." — **Gloss:** The task's closing verification ✓ verbatim — and the correction: NO "Living, the Eternal" anywhere in al-Hadid (that pair is `[2:255]` "the Living, the Everlasting" and `[3:2]` "the Living, the Eternal" — rg-verified). The surah ends where 57:21 began: the grace-inclusive (Possessor of Immense / Possessor of Great Grace), with the bestowal-by-will clause reissued. "the People of the Book" — the surah's only ahl-al-Kitāb hit (57:16 used "those who were given the Book") — hands the theme to `[59:2]` "those who disbelieved among the People of the Book" in the next documented surah (cross-batch).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **Prophethood-and-Scripture-in-the-line**:
  * **Rendering:** 57:26 "established in their line Prophethood and the Scripture" — capitalized pair, one surah-unique collocation (rg).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[29:27]` "conferred on his descendants the Prophethood and the Book" (the twins; p00610 verified); `[3:79]` the triple with wisdom.
* **Gospel (Injeel)**: 57:27 the only site in s057; "the Gospel" occurs in six surah files (3, 5, 7, 9, 48, 57 — rg-verified), fronted by the Torah/Gospel pair at `[3:3]`.
* **Monasticism (rahbāniyyah)**: 57:27 — corpus-unique word (rg: no other "monastic" hit). *Source: the classical readings (which innovation, which group) are extra-corpus tafsir — verify.*
* **double portion (kiflayn)**: 57:28 — unique (rg). The duality responds to the ruku's double failure-list (invented, unobserved).
* **grace (faddl) inclusio**: 57:21 ↔ 57:29 — "Possessor of Immense Grace"/"Possessor of Great Grace" (rg-verified pair); the whole surah's closing word-music is bestowal-by-will, answering 57:11/18's loan economics: grace is not earned, only asked and raced to.
