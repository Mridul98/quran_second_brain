# Everything glorifies, everything is referred — believe, pledge, spend (57:1–10)
**UID:** 202610142162
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 57:1–10 (Ruku 1 of 4 in al-Hadid)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[57:1]` – `[57:10]` (10 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The whole cosmos speaks God's praise (1), six creed-lines state who He is — kingdom, First-Last-Outward-Inward, six-day Creator whose knowledge has no private zone (2–6) — and the ruku then cashes the creed into two demands: believe on the strength of a pledge already received (7–9), and spend, because the heavens and earth are inheritance that will pass to Allah anyway (10).
* **Placement:** Ruku 1 of 4; opens the surah's doxology-and-omniscience frame that ruku 2–4 spend on the loan, the light, the rain, and grace.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[57:1]`** "Glorifying Allah is everything in the heavens and the earth. He is the Almighty, the Wise." — **Gloss:** The inverted tasbih formula ("Glorifying Allah is...") — two names only; the task's "nine-names doxology" ✗ (see index ledger). Head of the Musabbihat cluster with `[59:1]`.
* **`[57:2]`** "To Him belongs the kingdom of the heavens and the earth. He gives life and causes death, and He has power over all things." — **Gloss:** The dominion claim the task flagged ("57:2?5? — locate"): it is BOTH here ("kingdom") and at 57:5 ("kingship... all matters are referred") — one root, two renderings, bracketing the four-names verse. Life-death-power = the shahadah-sovereignty formula, twin of `[44:8]` (verified — p04402 gloss).
* **`[57:3]`** "He is the First and the Last, and the Outer and the Inner, and He has knowledge of all things." — **Gloss:** The four-names verse ✓ as the task asked: First/Last (temporal totality), Outer/Inner (spatial totality), sealed by knowledge of all things. Task's appended "the Merciful, the Generous" ✗ — no such pair anywhere in s057 (rg: only "generous reward" 57:11/18).
* **`[57:4]`** "It is He who created the heavens and the earth in six days, then settled over the Throne. He knows what penetrates into the earth, and what comes out of it, and what descends from the sky, and what ascends to it. And He is with you wherever you may be. Allah is Seeing of everything you do." — **Gloss:** The task's tawhid-verification ✓: six-days/Throne (istawa rendering here "settled over" — drift family: `[10:3]` verbatim "settled over the Throne"; `[7:54]`/`[32:4]` "established Himself on"; `[13:2]`/`[25:59]` "settled on" — all rg-verified; the p03201 ledger already tracks it). The "with-you clause" is plain divine presence-plus-omniscience, no qualifier; verbatim sibling is `[58:7]` "He is with them wherever they may be" — a cross-batch pair (documented in p05802).
* **`[57:5]`** "To Him belongs the kingship of the heavens and the earth, and to Allah all matters are referred." — **Gloss:** The second dominion line; "referred" (rujūʿ) makes the return of all affairs the ground of the spending-demand in 57:7–10.
* **`[57:6]`** "He merges the night into the day, and He merges the day into the night; and He knows what the hearts contains." — **Gloss:** Night/day intercession as sovereignty-proof — the same verb-pair the p02209 ledger tracks at `[31:29]`, `[35:13]`, `[22:61]`. Corpus prints "what the hearts contains" (sic — quote as-is; see artifacts note, class 2).
* **`[57:7]`** "Believe in Allah and His Messenger, and spend from what He made you inherit. Those among you who believe and give will have a great reward." — **Gloss:** The two demands land: belief + spending. "What He made you inherit" twins `[33:27]` "He made you inherit their land..." (rg-verified) — wealth is custodial, which is k-infraq's premise; k-infraq cites 57:18 but NOT 57:7/10/11 (rg-checked) — flagged.
* **`[57:8]`** "What is the matter with you that you do not believe in Allah, when the Messenger calls you to believe in your Lord, and He has received a pledge from you, if you are believers?" — **Gloss:** The charge is covenantal: a pledge (akhadha mīthāqan/khaṭara — "He has received a pledge from you") is already on record; verbatim grammar of `[5:12]` "Allah received a pledge from the Children of Israel" (rg-verified). This is s057's only pledge/covenant word-form (rg over the full file) — and k-covenant cites neither 57:8 nor 5:12 — flagged for orchestrator.
* **`[57:9]`** "It is He who sends down upon His servant clear revelations, to bring you out of darkness into the light. Allah is Gentle towards you, Most Compassionate." — **Gloss:** Revelation-as-light (anzala-form: the k-kalam channel-verb) and the darkness→light transit that k-guidance already cites at `[57:9]` ✓ (backlink verified) with `[2:257]`/`[14:5]` as its cluster kin. "Gentle" (raʾūf) + "Most Compassionate" pair with 57:12's good-news and 57:28's mercy.
* **`[57:10]`** "And why is it that you do not spend in the cause of Allah, when to Allah belongs the inheritance of the heavens and the earth? Not equal among you are those who contributed before the conquest, and fought. Those are higher in rank than those who contributed afterwards, and fought. But Allah promises both a good reward. Allah is Well Experienced in what you do." — **Gloss:** The spend-charge repeated, now with community stratification: early contributors (pre-"the conquest" — referent unnamed in corpus; classical identifications vary — Source: agent inference, verify) outrank later ones, yet BOTH get "a good reward" — rank without exclusion. The "Not equal among you" formula twins `[59:20]` "Not equal are the inhabitants of the Fire..." (rg-verified — cross-batch).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`subh` (glorification)**:
  * **Rendering:** "Glorifying Allah is everything in the heavens and the earth" — the inverted-syntax doxology shared verbatim-in-kind only with `[59:1]` "all that exists" (drift: "everything"/"all that exists"; "the Wise"/"the Most Wise").
  * **Elsewhere:** `[61:1]`/`[64:1]` "Everything in the heavens and the earth praises Allah"; `[62:1]` "glorifies Allah the Sovereign, the Holy, the Almighty, the Wise" — the five Musabbihat; `[24:41]` is the non-Musabbihat kin (p02406 already pairs `[57:1]` ↔ 24:41 — verified). No tasbih knowledge note exists (gap reported to orchestrator).
* **`malik/mulk` (kingdom/kingship)**: 57:2 "kingdom", 57:5 "kingship" — one surah, two English nouns for the same dominion root; cf. `[59:23]` "the Sovereign" (al-Malik) — rg-verified.
* **The "wherever you may be" presence formula**: 57:4 "He is with you wherever you may be"; 58:7 "He is with them wherever they may be" — the corpus's two sites (rg) of the claim, both Medinan, both paired with a Seeing/knowledge clause. *Source: observable corpus pairing — the classical maʿiyya debates (with-knowledge vs with-presence) are extra-corpus; verify before use.*
* **`qard` / spend (infāq)**: 57:7 "spend from what He made you inherit"; 57:10 "spend in the cause of Allah" — the loan-questions of ruku 2 (57:11/18) are the same economy; see p05702 §3 and the k-infraq flag.
* **`mīthāq/khaṭara` (pledge)**: 57:8 only — but it is the community's OWN pledge, not Israel's; read with 57:7's inheritance-custody as one covenant grammar. *Source: agent inference — verify.*
