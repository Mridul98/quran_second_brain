# The rain that withers, the Book before it happens, and the iron sent down (57:20–25)
**UID:** 202610142164
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 57:20–25 (Ruku 3 of 4 in al-Hadid)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[57:20]` – `[57:25]` (6 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The world is autopsied in five words — play, distraction, glitter, boasting, rivalry — and in one image, rain that greens, yellows, and becomes debris (20); the counter-command is a race to forgiveness and a Garden as vast as the heavens and earth (21), grounded in a pre-written decree that cauterizes both grief and gloat (22–23), aimed at the stingy (24), and closed by the shipment of history: messengers, proofs, Book, Balance — and iron, "in which is violent force" (25).
* **Placement:** Ruku 3 of 4; the surah's this-world/other-world hinge. It answers ruku 2's hardening-warning (57:16) with the race-verse, and its decree-theme hands off to ruku 4's "grace is in Allah's hand."

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[57:20]`** "Know that the worldly life is only play, and distraction, and glitter, and boasting among you, and rivalry in wealth and children. It is like a rainfall that produces plants, and delights the disbelievers. But then it withers, and you see it yellowing, and then it becomes debris. While in the Hereafter there is severe agony, and forgiveness from Allah, and acceptance. The life of this world is nothing but enjoyment of vanity." — **Gloss:** The task's verification ✓ with two corrections: (1) the list is five nouns (the task's "adornment, boasting, competing in possessions" = "glitter, boasting, rivalry in wealth and children"); (2) the corpus prints "delights the disbelievers" — the task quoted "delights the sowers," the standard rendering of the Arabic phrase (zurrāʿ) — Itani's choice here is either a heterodox reading or a print slip; quote as-is and flag for PDF cross-check (Source: observable rendering — verify against the source PDF and Arabic). The 6:32 twin ✓: "The life of this world is nothing but game and distraction" (`[6:32]`); kin: `[29:64]` "diversion and play," `[47:36]` "play and pastime" (all rg-verified via the p04402 ledger); "enjoyment of vanity" twins `[3:185]` "enjoyment of delusion" (p00319, verified) — and rhymes back to 57:14's "deluded by wishful thinking."
* **`[57:21]`** "Race towards forgiveness from your Lord; and a Garden as vast as the heavens and the earth, prepared for those who believe in Allah and His messengers. That is the grace of Allah; He bestows it on whomever He wills. Allah is the Possessor of Immense Grace." — **Gloss:** The imperative flips the autopsy into a race — nearly verbatim twin of `[3:133]` "Race towards forgiveness from your Lord..." (p00314 verified: "one of the corpus's clearest intra-Quran quotations"). The closing grace-dual: here "Possessor of Immense Grace," at 57:29 "Possessor of Great Grace" — the same root, two adjectives, one surah (rg-verified; see §3).
* **`[57:22]`** "No calamity occurs on earth, or in your souls, but it is in a Book, even before We make it happen. That is easy for Allah." — **Gloss:** The decree-Book: every calamity pre-registered. The book+ease clause is the twin p02209 already flagged against `[22:70]` ("nothing... but it is in a Book... before it happens; that is easy for Allah") — rg-verified in the brain. This is tawhid's omni-science clause (57:4's knowledge inventory) turned existential.
* **`[57:23]`** "That you may not sorrow over what eludes you, nor exult over what He has given you. Allah does not love the proud snob." — **Gloss:** The decree's pastoral payload: two-way emotional restraint — no grief over the missed, no gloating over the gained. "the proud snob" is corpus-unique (rg) — Itani's blunt English for the self-admiring boaster. *Source: observable term choice — the Arabic idiom behind it is extra-corpus — verify before quoting.*
* **`[57:24]`** "Those who are stingy, and induce people to be stingy. Whoever turns away—Allah is the Independent, the Praiseworthy." — **Gloss:** The emotional restraint names its economic opposite: stinginess taught to others. Near-verbatim twin `[4:37]` "Those who are stingy, and exhort people to stinginess..." (drift: induce/exhort — rg-verified); the stinginess-then-default sequence is exactly k-nifaq's 9:75–77 grammar. "the Independent, the Praiseworthy" — this is the corpus's only full pair at this phrasing (rg) — the Self-Sufficient needs no one's loan, answering 57:11/18 head-on.
* **`[57:25]`** "We sent Our messengers with the clear proofs, and We sent down with them the Book and the Balance, that humanity may uphold justice. And We sent down iron, in which is violent force, and benefits for humanity. That Allah may know who supports Him and His messengers invisibly. Allah is Strong and Powerful." — **Gloss:** The surah's name-verse: iron "sent down" (anzala — the same verb as the Book and the Balance; the classical debates over literal vs. provision-sending are extra-corpus — Source: agent note, verify). The Book+Balance pair near-twins `[42:17]` "the Book with the truth, and the Balance" (p04202 verified). "who supports Him... invisibly" (ghayb) is the test-clause; the support-idiom's legal twin is `[47:7]` "If you support Allah, He will support you" (rg-verified) — NOT 58:21, where the task looked (index ledger). The surah ends its middle on force-with-benefit: iron is for testing, not for boasting — tying back to 57:20's rivalry list.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **The play-field** (laʿib/lahw/zeen/rivalet): "play, and distraction, and glitter, and boasting... rivalry in wealth and children" (57:20).
  * **Rendering:** 6:32 compresses the field to "game and distraction"; 29:64 "diversion and play"; 47:36 "play and pastime" — denial-lexicon, never applied to God's making except in negation (the p04402 ledger's conclusion, confirmed here).
  * **Elsewhere:** "wealth and children" as the standard this-world inventory — p01905 already pairs 57:20 with `[18:39]` and `[71:12]` on exactly this clause (verified in brain).
* **`faddl` (grace/bounty)**:
  * **Rendering:** 57:21 "the grace of Allah... the Possessor of Immense Grace"; 57:29 "all grace is in Allah's hand... Possessor of Great Grace" — the inclusio that brackets ruku 3–4 (rg-verified; drift Immense/Great across eight verses).
  * **Elsewhere:** "He bestows it on whomever He wills" (57:21) reissued as "He gives it to whomever He wills" (57:29) — the same bestowal-by-will clause framing the surah's grace-inclusio (rg-verified). k-shukr's niʿma→shukr→kufr circuit runs beside this grace-grammar but the note cites no `[57:]` verse and none of s057–s059 carries a thank/grateful word-form (rg: zero across all three files) — reported.
* **Balance (mīzān)** + Book: 57:25 only in this surah; twin `[42:17]` ✓.
* **Iron (hadīd)**: the surah's name-word; "in which is violent force" (ba'san shadīd) — corpus-unique phrase (rg). *Source: the classical "sword/steel" readings are extra-corpus — verify.*
* **Support (nasr)**: 57:25 "who supports Him and His messengers invisibly" ↔ `[47:7]` "If you support Allah, He will support you, and will strengthen your foothold" — the batch's one verifiable support-pair.
