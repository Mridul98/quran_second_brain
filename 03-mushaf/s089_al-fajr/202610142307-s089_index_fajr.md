# Surah 89: THE DAWN (al-Fajr)
**UID:** 202610142307
**Type:** Index / Surah Map
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Meccan — agreed in the classical type-lists; the oath-of-time + destroyed-cities + provision-trial + Hour-scene + soul-address arc is the classic early-Meccan sermon shape. *Source: classical type-lists — verify.* Revelation order: **transmitted nuzul tables — verify** (corpus states no nuzul; unnumbered Basmala heads the surah).
* **Size:** 30 ayahs, 1 ruku (ruku_map global 531 — verified). The longest of the 86–91 run and its emotional hinge: 86 swore by sky, 87 by the ordering Lord, 88 by the Day — 89 swears by *time itself* (dawn, ten nights, even and odd, receding night) and ends with the one human address the batch allows: "O tranquil soul" (89:27).
* **Title:** al-Fajr — "THE DAWN" tracks `[89:1]` "By the daybreak"; the dawn-word appears in the batch-adjacent corpus as "the white streak of dawn" `[2:187]`, "the Quran at dawn" `[17:78]`, and the peace of qadr "until the rise of dawn" `[97:5]` (rg-verified).
* **Central theme:** Providence is not proof. The oath-objects (time) introduce the destruction archive (Aad's Erum, Thamud's valley-carved rocks, Pharaoh of the Stakes — 89:6–13) with one verdict: "Your Lord is on the lookout" (89:14). Man reads prosperity as honor and scarcity as insult (89:15–16); the surah answers with a social audit — orphan, poor-feeding, inheritance, wealth-love (89:17–20) — then the earth is leveled, the Lord comes with angels row after row, Hell is brought forward, and remembrance arrives too late (89:21–26). The only surviving "I" is the tranquil soul, commanded home: "Return to your Lord, pleased and accepted… Enter My Paradise" (89:27–30).
* **Reading hint:** the surah turns on two "No—"s (89:17 "Not at all"; 89:21 "No—"): the first cancels man's self-reading of provision, the second cancels his complacency about the earth. Between them sit the indictment and the Hour; after them, one address.

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Four oaths + the rational's question (89:1–5):** daybreak, ten nights, the even and the odd, the night as it recedes — "Is there in this an oath for a rational person?"
* **The archive (89:6–13):** "Have you not seen how your Lord dealt with Aad?" — Erum-of-the-pillars (never created in the land), Thamud "who carved the rocks in the valley," Pharaoh of the Stakes; excess and corruption in the lands; "a scourge of punishment" poured down (rg-verified renderings).
* **The lookout clause (89:14):** "Your Lord is on the lookout" — the surveillance hinge tying 89 back to 86:4 and forward to 90:7.
* **The provision test misread (89:15–16):** honored/prospered → "My Lord has honored me"; livelihood restricted → "My Lord has insulted me."
* **The social audit (89:17–20):** the orphan not honored, the feeding of the poor not urged, inheritance devoured, wealth loved with immense love.
* **The Hour (89:21–26):** earth leveled-pounded-crushed; the Lord comes with angels row after row; Hell brought forward; too-late remembrance; "If only I had forwarded for my life"; none punishes or shackles as He does.
* **The soul-address (89:27–30):** "But as for you, O tranquil soul. / Return to your Lord, pleased and accepted. / Enter among My servants. / Enter My Paradise." (*Source: structural observation on corpus renderings.*)

## 5b. Batch-Return Verification Flags (this index)
* **Hint renderings corrected:** "Iram-of-the-pillars" → "**Erum of the pillars**" (89:7; "Erum" corpus-unique, rg-verified; the word "pillars" otherwise appears of the pillarless heavens `[13:2]`/`[31:10]`). "Thamud's carved valley" → "those who carved **the rocks in the valley**" (89:9). "Pharaoh's stakes" → "Pharaoh of the Stakes" (89:10) — exact-phrase twin `[38:12]` (rg-verified). "your Lord's lash" → "a **scourge** of punishment" poured down (89:13; scourge-word twin `[29:34]`). "the earth's trembling" → "when the earth is **leveled, pounded, and crushed**" (89:21) — no trembling word. "stretched and straitened provision" → "honors him, and **prospers** him" / "**restricts his livelihood**" (89:15–16); the expands/restricts provision formula: `[13:26]`, `[17:30]`, `[29:62]`, `[30:37]`, `[34:36]`. "O soul at peace… pleased and pleasing… enter My Garden" → "**O tranquil soul. / Return to your Lord, pleased and accepted. / Enter among My servants. / Enter My Paradise**" (89:27–30) — corpus says tranquil/accepted/Paradise.
* **Hint cross-ref "89↔7:119?" → REJECTED as stated:** 7:119 ("There they were defeated, and utterly reduced") is the drowning of Pharaoh's people in al-A'raf; 89 does not narrate a drowning — its Pharaoh clause is title + scourge. The verified Thamud twin is `[7:74]` "carve out dwellings in the mountains" (plus `[26:149]`, `[15:82]`, rg-verified), and 89:14's lookout pairs with Saleh's own watch-word in the Thamud scene `[11:93]` "So look out; I am on the lookout with you" (rg-verified rendering echo; Arabic nouns differ — agent inference, verify).
* **"ten nights" located honestly:** the phrase is corpus-unique (89:2); the corpus's own ten-night anchors are `[7:142]` (Moses' thirty plus ten = forty nights) and `[2:196]` ("making ten in all") (rg-verified). The classical reading — the ten days of Dhul-Hijja — is transmitted gloss: *Source: classical tafsir — verify*; the corpus supplies no referent.
* **89:22 ("your Lord comes") is quoted verbatim; its theological interpretation is transmitted-disputed and not this translation's claim — verify against the classical apparatus before elaborating.**
