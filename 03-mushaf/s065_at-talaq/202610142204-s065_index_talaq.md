# Surah 65: DIVORCE (at-Talaq)
**UID:** 202610142204
**Type:** Index / Surah Map
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Medinan — agreed case in the type-lists. *Source: classical type-lists — verify.* The corpus carries no nuzul statement; this brain's chain pins slots only for al-Mumtahina 91, al-Munafiqun 104, as-Saff 109, al-Jumu'ah 110, so no number for 65 is guessed here. The unnumbered Basmala heads the surah.
* **Size:** 12 ayahs, 2 ruku. Second member of the household triad (64–66): 64 calls wives and children a trial; 65 legislates the household when it breaks; 66 stages the Prophet's own house under reproof. Classical reports name at-Talaq "the lesser women surah" (Surat an-Nisa' al-Sughra) — *Source: classical report — verify.*
* **Name:** at-Talaq — the law-word sits in the first clause: `[65:1]` "If any of you divorce women, divorce them during their period of purity." The corpus's divorce-law vocabulary is rg-verified across s002 (2:227–241), s033 (33:49), s065, s066. The 'iddah itself is never transliterated — rendered "their term," with the counting command "calculate their term" (65:1).
* **Central theme:** A statute ruku and a cosmos ruku, welded by a refrain. Ruku 1 runs the whole arc of divorce law — timing, term, witnesses, residence, maintenance, nursing — and braids into it the triple promise to whoever fears Allah: a way out, provision from where he never expected, ease, remitted sins, amplified reward — because "Allah has set a measure to all things" (65:3). Ruku 2 cuts to the town that defied that command, then to the sent-down Reminder and the reciting messenger who moves believers from darkness into light, and closes on seven heavens through which the command descends: the God who measures waiting-terms is the God whose knowledge encompasses everything (65:12).

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Statute through the singular address (65:1):** "O Prophet! If any of you divorce women" — communal law voiced through the prophet-address; anti-eviction rules and the limits formula bracket it, and one open window: "Allah may afterwards bring about a new situation."
* **The refrain (65:2, 4, 5):** three "Whoever fears Allah" promise-lines interrupt the legislation — way out and unexpected provision (2–3), things made easy (4), sins remitted and reward amplified (5); 65:1's "be pious before Allah" is the surah's fourth taqwa-line.
* **Counting at the edges (65:4):** menopause, pre-menstruation, pregnancy — the law meets biology exactly, term by term.
* **Emotion into record (65:2):** "call to witness two just people from among you, and give upright testimony for Allah" — endings are made juridical, not private, and the exhortation formula ties the whole procedure to "whoever believes in Allah and the Last Day" (twin of `[2:232]`) before the first promise-line breaks open.
* **Economics of breakdown (65:6–7):** residence, anti-harassment, pregnancy maintenance, nursing wages, graduated spending — capped by "Allah will bring ease after hardship."
* **Case file of the town (65:8–9):** defiance → strict accountability → the taste of the outcome — 64:5's verdict formula re-run on a city.
* **Cosmological close (65:10–12):** "possess intellect" address; Reminder sent down; Verses recited; darkness into light; seven heavens and the descending command — household law sits inside divine amr.

## 5b. Batch-Return Verification Flags (this index)
* Hint relocated: "way out" and "provides" are two ayahs — "He will make a way out for him" ends `[65:2]`; "And will provide for him from where he never expected" opens `[65:3]`. Corpus wording is "from where he never expected," not "from unseen sources."
* "Allah's measured decree" is `[65:3]` ("Allah has set a measure to all things," reinforced by 65:5 "This is the ordinance of Allah"), inside ruku 1 — not part of the close; the hint's seven-heavens close (`[65:12]`) verifies as the final ayah, and it is cosmological, not legal.
* "Law stated neutrally": the register is humane, not cold — anti-eviction, anti-harassment, nursing wages, witnesses commanded, and a statute that itself leaves hope open (65:1's "new situation"). Address: "O Prophet! If any of you..." — communal law in the prophet-address.
* The refrain's fourth line: 65:1 renders the same verb "be pious before Allah" while 65:2/4/5 render it "fears Allah"; 65:10 drifts again ("So beware of Allah") — *Source: translator's term choice (observable in corpus); rg-verified.*
