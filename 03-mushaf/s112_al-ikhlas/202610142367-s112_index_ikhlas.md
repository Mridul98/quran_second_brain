## 3. Structural Arc
* **112:1 — identity + oneness:** "Say, “He is Allah, the One." — the answer to every "describe your Lord" question starts with the name itself.
* **112:2 — the self-subsistence name:** "Allah, the Absolute." — *al-Samad*, rendered uniquely here (zero other corpus sites for "the Absolute" — rg-verified); the hint's "Eternal Refuge/Async" is NOT this edition's wording (see §5b).
* **112:3 — the double begetting-denial:** "He begets not, nor was He begotten." — active then passive, one line negating both directions of divine lineage; the surah's argumentative center, meeting the son-claims dossier head-on.
* **112:4 — the comparison-close:** "And there is nothing comparable to Him.”" — the doctrine's seal and the quotation's full stop; twin at `[42:11]` "There is nothing like Him." (rg-verified).
* **Unit position:** between 111 (a named man's doom) and 113–114 (refuge-practice) — the unit pivots on pure creed: after two verdict-surahs, four lines about God Himself, then two surahs of taking shelter in Him.
