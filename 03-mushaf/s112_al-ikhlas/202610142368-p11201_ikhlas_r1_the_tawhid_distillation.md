# The Tawhid Distillation: Four Lines That Subtract Everything (112:1–4)
**UID:** 202610142368
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 112:1–4 (Ruku 1 of 1 in al-Ikhlas)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[112:1]` – `[112:4]` (4 ayahs; global ruku 554 of 556 — verified)
* **Thesis:** The Quran's shortest positive creed: commanded speech ("Say") that names God, isolates Him (One; the Absolute), cuts both directions of lineage (begets not, was not begotten), and seals with incomparability — four subtractions that leave nothing shareable.
* **Placement:** Ruku 1 of 1; fourth door of the closing bracket. The hinge of the unit: 109 empties worship of partners (negative tawhid), 112 fills the object (positive), 113–114 turn both into daily refuge-practice. Its argumentative target is the son-claim dossier (19:88–92, 2:116, 4:171, 6:101, 72:3 — all rg-verified).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[112:1]`** "Say, “He is Allah, the One."
  * **Gloss:** The commission mirrors 109:1/113:1/114:1 — the closing unit says "Say" four times (rg-verified); the answer begins with the Name, not a definition. "the One" (*ahad*) — the oneness-name also at `[13:16]`/`[40:16]` "The One, the Irresistible" and `[2:163]` "Your God is one God" (all rg-verified).
* **`[112:2]`** "Allah, the Absolute."
  * **Gloss:** A verbless clause — the Name restated with one epithet and nothing else. *Al-Samad* is rendered "the Absolute" here and nowhere else in the corpus (rg-verified: unique). The hint's "Eternal Refuge/Async" is another edition's vocabulary, not Itani's (§5b correction); classical glosses (self-subsistent, sought-to, perfect in lordship) sit BEHIND this one word — transmitted, verify.
* **`[112:3]`** "He begets not, nor was He begotten."
  * **Gloss:** The surah's battlefield line: active then passive — God generates nobody and was generated by nobody; divine lineage, the claim of 19:88/2:116/4:171, is denied in both directions at once. The nearest creed-twins: `[6:101]` "how can He have a son when He never had a companion?" and `[72:3]` "He never had a mate, nor a child" (rg-verified).
* **`[112:4]`** "And there is nothing comparable to Him.”"
  * **Gloss:** The seal: no measure, no counterpart, no analogy — and the closing quotation mark of the whole surah. Verbatim-shape twin `[42:11]` "There is nothing like Him. He is the Hearing, the Seeing" (rg-verified — incomparability paired there with the sensory names; here with bare transcendence).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`ahad` ("the One")**: numeric-unique oneness.
  * **Rendering:** "the One" (112:1); "one God" (waahid) at `[2:163]`, `[4:171]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[13:16]`, `[40:16]` "The One, the Irresistible" — the Day's final answer to "whose is the kingdom?" (rg-verified) — 112:1 states now what 40:16 will concede then.
* **`al-Samad` ("the Absolute")**: the unexplained epithet.
  * **Rendering:** "the Absolute" — corpus-unique (rg-verified); this edition deliberately leaves *samad* un-paraphrased (no "refuge/eternal" wording anywhere in s112).
  * **Note:** k-tawhid's gloss list ("One, Refuge-begetter, unbegotten, unmatched") uses non-Itani vocabulary for this line — flagged; do not propagate.
* **`walada (begetting)`**: the denied direction.
  * **Rendering:** "He begets not, nor was He begotten" — the only Quranic line where begetting is denied BOTH ways in one clause-pair (observable within the verified dossier).
  * **Elsewhere (all rg-verified):** claim-lines `[19:88]`, `[2:116]`, `[10:68]`? — NOT verified, not cited; confirmed claim-sites: 19:88–92, 2:116, 18:4, 37:152; denial-lines: 4:171, 6:101, 17:111, 19:35? — unverified, not cited; 23:91, 72:3, and 112:3.
* **`kufuwan ("comparable")`**: measure/counterpart.
  * **Rendering:** "nothing comparable to Him" — one of only three "comparable" sites in the corpus (112:4, `[5:95]` penalty-equivalence, `[47:10]` punishment-likeness — rg-verified); the theological absolute-use is unique to 112:4.
