## 3. Structural Arc
* **Threefold "He who created" (67:1–3):** blessedness, death/life as testing, the layered heavens — sovereignty established by inventory, not assertion.
* **The empirical dare (67:3–4):** "Look again. Can you see any cracks?" — creation invites its own defect-hunt and fails to yield one.
* **Cosmic security theater (67:5):** lanterns are also ammunition; the beauty of the sky doubles as its weapons systems against eavesdropping devils.
* **The courtroom replay (67:6–11):** the Blaze roars, its keepers interrogate ("Has no warner come to you?"), the inmates self-indict (67:10 — "Had we listened or reasoned..."), and acknowledgment ends in "So away with the inmates of the Blaze" (67:11).
* **Pivot (67:12–14):** the secret-fearers receive forgiveness; speech — hidden or declared — sits under One "Aware of the inner thoughts"; "Would He not know, He Who created?" closes ruku 1 as sovereignty-as-omniscience.
* **Ruku 2's inverted security (67:15–22):** travel and eat (15) — but the earth can collapse (16) or a storm be unleashed (17); birds stay aloft by nothing but the Compassionate (19); no force, no provider but Him (20–21); the surah's hinge-question: the bent walker vs the upright one on a straight path (22).
* **Closing creed (67:23–30):** senses inventoried with "rarely do you give thanks" (23), scattering and rounding-up (24), the "when" mockery answered (25–27), the mercy gambit (28), and the tawakkul sentence (29) before the final water-dare (30).

## 5b. Batch-Return Verification Flags (this index)
* Task "your vision returns humiliated and exhausted" REJECTED for 67:4 — corpus: "your sight will return to you **dazzled** and exhausted" (khasi'). Cognate: `[75:7]` "When vision is dazzled" (rg-verified).
* 67:16–17 VERIFIED with wording drift: "Are you confident that the One in heaven will not cause the earth to collapse beneath you as it spins?" / "…unleash against you a violent storm?" — the task's "swallow" = "collapse" (khusuf); the precedent is `[28:81]` "So We caused the earth to cave in on him and his mansion" (verified). Task's "stone-bomb": the Arabic hasib family; Itani renders "violent storm" — translator's choice, not the word the task quoted. Capitalization drifts within the pair: "the One in heaven" (16) vs "the One in Heaven" (17).
* 67:22 VERIFIED — the guidance-parable hinge is present: "Is he who walks **bent on his own design** better guided, or he who walks **upright on a straight path**?" Task's "headfirst/upside-down" rejected; "better guided" confirmed.
* 67:29: corpus says "He is **the Compassionate**. We have faith in Him, and **in Him we trust**" — task's "Most Merciful/we rely on Him" rejected; the tawakkul clause itself is verified.
* Saqar: in 67 Itani renders the fire "the Blaze" (sa'ir) ×3 (67:5, 10, 11 — counted via rg "the Blaze"); the proper name "Saqar" occurs only in 74 (26, 27, 42) and 54:48 (verified).
* 67:5 lamps-as-missiles VERIFIED: "We have adorned the lower heaven with lanterns, and made them missiles against the devils" — twins `[41:12]` (lamps + protection), `[15:18]` (visible projectile), `[37:6–7]` (planets + guarded) (all rg-verified).
* 67:10–11 pair VERIFIED: "Had we listened or reasoned..." / "So they will acknowledge their sins. So away with the inmates of the Blaze." The "gasping/roaring" belongs to 67:7–8 ("roaring, as it seethes" / "almost bursts with fury").
