# Are you harder to make, or the heaven? (79:27–46)
**UID:** 202610142267
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 79:27–46 (Ruku 2 of 2 in an-Nazi’at)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[79:27]`–`[79:46]` (20 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah turns from Pharaoh's file to its audience's own construction: are you the harder build, or the heaven He raised, proportioned, darkened and lit — and then the earth spread with its water, pasture and anchored mountains, "a source of enjoyment for you and for your animals" (79:27–33)? Against that credit line the two shelters are assigned (79:34–41), and the surah ends by confiscating the Hour's date from everyone: "To your Lord is its finality" (79:42–46).
* **Placement:** Ruku 2 of 2, and the argument's payoff: ruku 1 swore the Resumption in and showed what refusal costs; ruku 2 proves the resurrection by the same logic as 78:6–16 and 80:24–32 — the Builder of the heaven can rebuild the human — then closes the question-frame the neighboring surahs keep reopening.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[79:27]`** "Are you more difficult to create, or the heaven? He constructed it."
  * **Gloss:** the question the whole juz-30 block keeps asking — cf. 37:11 "Are they more difficult to create, or the others We created?" (verified) — with the heaven as the comparator, and the answer implied by "He constructed it."
* **`[79:28]`** "He raised its masses, and proportioned it." **`[79:29]`** "And He dimmed its night, and brought out its daylight."
  * **Gloss:** acts 2–3 on the heaven: elevation, measure, then darkness-management — the verbs matching 78:10's "night a cover" / 78:11's "day for livelihood" (78 ruku 1's domestic block).
* **`[79:30]`** "And the earth after that He spread." **`[79:31]`** "And from it, He produced its water and its pasture."
  * **Gloss:** acts 4–5: spreading the earth and extracting from it both water and grazing; the order ("after that") is the classic crux (see §6).
* **`[79:32]`** "And the mountains, He anchored." **`[79:33]`** "A source of enjoyment for you and for your animals."
  * **Gloss:** act 6 and the purpose clause: the mountains of 78:7's "pegs" question reappear as a statement, and the catalog terminates in enjoyment for humans and their animals — the same function as 80:32 "Enjoyment for you, and for your livestock" (verified).
* **`[79:34]`** "But when the Great Cataclysm arrives."
  * **Gloss:** the Day enters on a third unique sound-name (hapax, verified), the "But when…" frame identical to 80:33's opening.
* **`[79:35]`** "A Day when man will remember what he has endeavored."
  * **Gloss:** remembrance, not information: what one "endeavored" surfaces on that Day — same slot as 78:40's "observe what his hands have produced" and 81:14's "what it has readied" (all verified).
* **`[79:36]`** "And Hell will be displayed to whoever sees."
  * **Gloss:** Hell becomes visible; the qualification "to whoever sees" leaves the seeing itself at issue (79:9's "sights downcast" still governs).
* **`[79:37]`** "As for him who was defiant." **`[79:38]`** "And preferred the life of this world."
  * **Gloss:** the defendant's two traits, the first repeating Pharaoh's verb from 79:21 ("defied") — the file of ruku 1 is now the general profile.
* **`[79:39]`** "Then Hell is the shelter."
  * **Gloss:** the first of two identically worded verdicts; "shelter" is the surah's own word for the outcome of both parties.
* **`[79:40]`** "But as for him who feared the Standing of his Lord, and restrained the self from desires."
  * **Gloss:** the counter-profile in two acts: standing-fear and self-restraint — the same phrase (with different capitalization in the book) as 55:46 "the standing of his Lord" (verified).
* **`[79:41]`** "Then Paradise is the shelter."
  * **Gloss:** the mirrored verdict: one word, two directions — the shelter is whichever way the fear went.
* **`[79:42]`** "They ask you about the Hour, “When will it take place?”"
  * **Gloss:** the opening question of the surah's world returns: 78:1's crowd asking, now asking the messenger directly.
* **`[79:43]`** "You have no knowledge of it."
  * **Gloss:** the answer is not a date but a competence limit placed on the messenger himself.
* **`[79:44]`** "To your Lord is its finality."
  * **Gloss:** the terminal jurisdiction ("finality") — the same noun as 53:42 "to your Lord is the finality" (verified).
* **`[79:45]`** "You are just a warner for whoever dreads it."
  * **Gloss:** the warner's scope is narrowed to the already-fearing, the "whoever fears" of 79:26.
* **`[79:46]`** "On the Day when they witness it—as though they only stayed an evening, or its morning."
  * **Gloss:** the surah's last line collapses the waiting: one evening, or its morning — the life between becomes a shift; cf. 20:104 "You have lingered only a day."

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **"the Great Cataclysm"** — 79:34
  * **Rendering:** unique in this corpus (rg-verified); 79:6 already had "the Quake", and 80:33 will use "the Deafening Noise" (verified).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[78:18]` Trumpet; `[36:49]` "a single blast, which will seize them while they feud"; `[50:20]` "the Trumpet is blown: 'This is the Promised Day'" (verified) — three surahs, three nouns, one event.
* **"the shelter"** — 79:39 / 79:41
  * **Rendering:** the same word twice, "the shelter" for Hell and for Paradise (both lines verified) — an English-level antonym-pair with no evaluative shading.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[78:22]` "For the oppressors, a destination" occupies the same slot in the parallel surah — same structural claim, different word (agent observation — verify); `[18:90]` "no shelter from it" uses the word physically (verified).
* **"the Standing of his Lord"** — 79:40
  * **Rendering:** with "feared", and paired inside the same line with "restrained the self from desires" (both renderings hapaxes, verified).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[55:46]` "But for him who feared the standing of his Lord are two gardens." (verified — lowercase "standing" there, capitalized here: the book's own casing drift, flagged by the corpus-artifacts fleeting note).
* **"finality"** — 79:44
  * **Rendering:** "To your Lord is its finality" — the Hour's terminus is jurisdictional, not temporal.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[53:42]` "And that to your Lord is the finality" and `[6:67]` "For every happening is a finality, and you will surely know" (both verified) — one English word carrying the "it ends where He decides" idea.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Meccan (surah-level consensus); the Hour-question of 79:42 is reported by the text without naming the questioners. Classical reports assign it to Quraysh's asking "if you are truthful, when is this?" — **Source: classical reports — verify**; nothing in the corpus identifies the askers.
* **"And the earth after that He spread" (79:30):** the sequencing ("after that") generated a classical debate about whether the earth was created after the heaven, or spread after being created — the famous exchange centered on this line vs 41:9–12. **Source: classical tafsir discussion (verify against Ibn Kathir / al-Tabari on 79:30)** — the text itself only states the order it states.
* **"restrained the self from desires" (79:40):** the phrase is the only occurrence of this rendering in the corpus (verified). The counter-image is the messenger's own claim, 53:3 "Nor does he speak out of desire" (verified).
* **Task-quote check:** the hint's "the eye of the transgressor is…" has no referent in s079 (full corpus file read; no such clause) — recorded as not present.
