## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Meccan — agreed in the classical type-lists; the transmitted revelation-order lists place it beside 78 in the early wave, but the order-number differs between lists, so none is asserted here — *Source: classical type-lists and revelation-order lists — verify against a printed table.* The unnumbered Basmala heads the surah (corpus line 3). Folder name follows the corpus file (`s079_an-nazi-at.md`).
* **Size:** 46 ayahs, 2 ruku (26 + 20 — `ruku_map` verified). Named from its first word, an oath-class of agents the text never identifies by name.
* **Central theme:** The surah proves the Resumption by compression. Five anonymous oath-agents (79:1–5) swear in for a Day that collapses the cosmos into "a single nudge" (79:13); the Moses–Pharaoh dossier (79:15–26) is then run as the case of one who was warned, refused, claimed godhood, and was seized — "In this is a lesson for whoever fears" (79:26). Ruku 2 weighs the audience against the heaven they find so unremarkable (79:27–33), sorts them into two "shelters" (79:34–41), and answers the Hour-question by removing it from human competence (79:42–46).

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Oath block (79:1–5):** five participial classes — snatch violently, remove gently, glide smoothly, race swiftly, regulate events. The oath is the argument's weight, not its content.
* **The Day and its physiology (79:6–9):** the Quake quaked, followed by the Successor; hearts pounding, sights downcast — the scene told from inside the bodies.
* **The deniers' three lines (79:10–12):** "Are we to be restored…?", "When we have become hollow bones?", "This is a losing proposition." — then the rebuttal of compression (79:13–14): one nudge, and they are awake.
* **The Moses file (79:15–26):** call at Tuwa → mission to Pharaoh → the offer of cleansing → the Greatest Miracle → denial, defiance, back-turning, gathering, proclamation → "I am your Lord, the most high" → seizure "in the last and in the first" → lesson clause. Nine beats, no magician contest.
* **The creation-wrong question (79:27–33):** you, or the heaven — its raising and proportioning, its night and daylight, then the earth spread, its water and pasture, the mountains anchored, "a source of enjoyment for you and for your animals."
* **The two shelters (79:34–41):** the Great Cataclysm, remembrance of what one endeavored, Hell displayed; defiant/world-preferring → Hell is the shelter; fearing the Standing of his Lord and restraining the self → Paradise is the shelter.
* **The Hour removed (79:42–46):** they ask "When will it take place?"; the answer is not a date but a jurisdiction ("To your Lord is its finality"), a role ("You are just a warner for whoever dreads it"), and a time-collapse ("as though they only stayed an evening, or its morning").

## 5b. Batch-Return Verification Flags (this index)
* **79:1–5 renderings corrected:** "pull forcibly / urge irresistibly / race forward / execute command" → **"snatch violently / remove gently / glide smoothly / race swiftly / regulate events"** (verified corpus lines).
* **79:6–9:** "eyes humbled" → the text says **"Hearts … pounding"** (79:8) and **"Their sights downcast"** (79:9); "behind it another Blast" → **"And is followed by the Successor"** (79:7, hapax).
* **"created you from a single sperm" is not in s079** (rg "sperm" → `[53:46]`, `[80:19]`, `[18:37]` only). The hint mislocated it; the creation-signs block is **79:27–33**.
* **The magician contest is NOT in s079.** "a mere trick" (rg: no such line), "this is a plot" (no such line), the magicians' prostration (**20:70 / 26:46** — verified), Pharaoh's "you believed before I permitted you?" (**20:71 / 26:49** — verified), "We drowned him and his accomplices all" (**17:103 / 28:40** — verified) and "he will lead them to a blazing Fire" (no corpus hit) all belong to the Ta-Ha / ash-Shu'ara / al-Isra / al-Qasas tellings. 79:15–26 compresses the episode to miracle → denial → lord-claim → seizure, and never mentions the sea. **20:70 judged NOT a "twin within" 79** — it is the parallel telling this surah omits.
* **79:42–46 corrected:** "you are not concerned with it" → **"You have no knowledge of it"** (79:43); "it is the finality of everything" → **"To your Lord is its finality"** (79:44); the hint's "man will mention what he forwarded and what he left behind" is **79:35** ("remember what he has endeavored") conflated with 82:5/59:18; "the eternal abode close" is not this surah's ending — it closes on the evening-or-morning compression (79:46).
