## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Meccan — agreed in the classical type-lists. *Source: classical type-lists — verify.* Revelation order: **transmitted nuzul tables — verify** (corpus states no nuzul; unnumbered Basmala heads the surah).
* **Size:** 25 ayahs, 1 ruku (ruku_map global 526 — verified). The closing member of the batch's reckoning run (82 shattering → 83 defrauding → 84 rupture); shares with 82 the "O man!" address — the corpus has exactly two, and they are these (rg-verified).
* **Title:** al-Inshiqaq — Itani's header "THE RUPTURE" tracks `[84:1]` "When the sky is ruptured"; the shattering-word differs from 82:1's "breaks apart" (infitar) — two vocabularies, one teardown.
* **Central theme:** Obedient cosmos, insolent man. The heaven and the earth each "hearkens to its Lord, as it must" (84:2, 84:5 — an identical refrain, rg-verified), while man laboring toward that same Lord splits by how his book is handed: right hand → easy settlement; behind his back → perdition-cry and the Blaze (84:7–12). The twilight oath swears "You will mount stage by stage" (84:19), and the surah ends on the exception clause — belief, deeds, "an undiminished reward" (84:25), the frame-twin of 95:6 (*see flags*).
* **Reading hint:** 84:21 is the tell — the one act the batch's cosmos performs for free (hearkening) is the act these humans refuse (bowing when the Quran is recited). Read the sky-verses and the book-verses as one argument: creation is already submitted; only man abstains.

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Double obedience (84:1–5):** sky ruptured / earth leveled and emptied — each bracketed by "hearkens to its Lord, as it must"; creation's submission is stated twice to shame the one creature that refuses.
* **The address and the split (84:6–12):** "O man! You are laboring towards your Lord, and you will meet Him" — then the two handings and their two outcomes (easy settlement / death-cry and Blaze).
* **The diagnosed man (84:13–15):** he "used to be happy among his family," "thought he would never return" — and the rebuttal in five words: "In fact, his Lord was watching him."
* **The oath (84:16–19):** twilight, night-and-what-it-covers, the full moon — resolved: "You will mount stage by stage."
* **The family-inversion frame (84:9 ↔ 84:13):** the rewarded man "returns to his family delighted"; the doomed man "used to be happy among his family" — one word, two eternities; the batch's smallest chiasm. (*Source: observable in Itani's two renderings — rhetorical label agent-supplied.*)
* **The incredulity inquest (84:20–25):** why no belief? no bowing when the Quran is read? — denial named, concealment known (84:23), the painful-punishment tiding issued — except the believing doers, "an undiminished reward."

## 5b. Batch-Return Verification Flags (this index)
* **"behind his back" is corpus-unique to 84:10** (rg-verified). The hint's "twin of 69:25": 69:25 is the **left-hand** twin ("given his book in his left hand") — the scene-pair (right/left books) is real (69:19/25, 17:71), but the "behind the back" wording has no 69 parallel. 25:27 ("the wrongdoer will bite his hands") is wish-regret kin, not a ledger verse — cited with that judgment in the passage.
* **Hint phrase rejected:** "this is among the great tidings/gnarled warnings" — no such line exists in s084 (no "tidings" plural, no "gnarled" anywhere in the surah; rg-verified). The oath's resolution is 84:19 "You will mount stage by stage"; the only "inform them" clause is the warning of 84:24.
* **Hint wording adjusted:** "he was among his people joyful" → corpus "He used to be happy among his **family**" (84:13 — the family-frame is the point, since 84:9's rewarded man returns to his family); "his Lord was watching over him" → corpus "In fact, his Lord was watching **him**" (84:15 — no "over"); the hint's oath-triad "twilight… night… dawn when it breathes" → corpus third term is "the moon, as it grows full" (84:18 — no dawn-oath in s084; twilight/night/moon triad rg-verified); "the cosmos's obedience motif — tawhid" confirmed in register, phrased in the corpus as "hearkens to its Lord, as it must."
* **"unfailing reward" → corpus "undiminished reward" (84:25).** Phrase-twin judgment vs 95:6: the exception frame is verbatim parallel — "Except those who believe and do…" in both (rg-verified), classical reading holds one Arabic idiom (ajr ghayr mamnun) — but Itani renders 95:6 "a reward without end" and 84:25 "an undiminished reward"; 11:108's "a reward without end" translates a DIFFERENT Arabic word (madzdud — *Source: agent inference on standard lexemes — verify*). Cite by surah location, not by English phrase.
* **Obedience refrain:** "hearkens to its Lord, as it must" occurs exactly twice in the corpus (84:2, 84:5 — rg-verified); the earth's disgorging twin is `[99:2]` "the earth brings out its loads", which then "will tell its tales" because "your Lord will have inspired it" (99:4–5) — same theology, different surah.
