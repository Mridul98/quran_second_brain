# The Gardens, the two Newses, and Abraham's guests (15:45–60)
**UID:** 202610141585
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 15:45–60 (Ruku 4 of 6 in al-Hijr)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[15:45]` – `[15:60]` (16 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** After Hell's seven doors, the counter-register: the righteous in gardens with springs, ill-feelings surgically removed, no fatigue, no eviction — then the surah's balancing-mandate, "Inform My servants that I am the Forgiver, and that My punishment is the painful punishment" (49–50), followed immediately by the guests of Abraham, who carry precisely those two newses: a son of knowledge to a laughing old woman, and a doom-date for a sinful people with one named exception.
* **Placement:** Ruku 4; the pivot of the surah's double-notification structure (unba/idhar). It answers ruku 3's "sincere servants" with their scene, and hands the second errand to ruku 5.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[15:45]`** "But the righteous will be in gardens with springs."
  * **Gloss:** The adversative "But" against 43–44's meeting-place; verbatim twin `[51:15]` "But the pious are amidst gardens and springs" (rendering drift: "righteous" / "pious" — see Key Terms).
* **`[15:46]`** "“Enter it in peace and security.”"
  * **Gloss:** The welcome-word — safety-peace welded; cf. `[50:34]` "Enter it in peace," the shorter twin.
* **`[15:47]`** "And We will remove all ill-feelings from their hearts—brothers and sisters, on couches facing one another."
  * **Gloss:** Eschatology as surgery on resentment (ghill): every heart-debris extracted; then the social form — "brothers and sisters" (corpus-unique plural tenderness here — verified `rg`), siblings on facing couches (the facing-formula at `[56:16]`, `[44:53]`, `[37:44]`).
* **`[15:48]`** "No fatigue will ever touch them therein, nor will they be asked to leave it."
  * **Gloss:** Two negatives: no touch of fatigue (cf. the residents' own speech `[35:35]` "fatigue will not afflict us"), and no eviction order — permanence as a right.
* **`[15:49]`** "Inform My servants that I am the Forgiver,"
  * **Gloss:** The first news; "My servants" re-echoes 15:40/42 — the same owned class; the name chosen is "the Forgiver" (Ghafur) standing alone (its usual company "the Merciful" is withheld to complete the pair in 50 — observable structure).
* **`[15:50]`** "And that My punishment is the painful punishment."
  * **Gloss:** The second news, symmetric: "I am the Forgiver / My punishment is the painful" — mercy attributed to His being, punishment to His act (Source: classical observation on 15:49–50's asymmetry — Ibn Kathir-type point; agent labeling — verify). The wording-check against "Severe in punishment" (`[40:3]`, `[8:52]`) is filed in the index §2.
* **`[15:51]`** "And inform them of the guests of Abraham."
  * **Gloss:** The concrete illustration of the two-newses mandate — one visit with both envelopes; cf. `[51:24]` "Has the story of Abraham's honorable guests reached you?"
* **`[15:52]`** "When they entered upon him, and said, “Peace.” He said, “We are wary of you.”"
  * **Gloss:** Salam offered, salam withheld: "We are wary" — the word "wary" surfaces in the corpus only here as human fear ("be wary of Allah" at `[2:233]` is the same English word for a different Arabic verb — verified drift) — hospitality etiquette concealing a soaked fear.
* **`[15:53]`** "They said, “Do not fear; we bring you good news of a boy endowed with knowledge.”"
  * **Gloss:** The fear-calming formula (as `[51:28]` "Do not fear... a knowledgeable boy"); the son's quality pre-stated: knowledge (al-'alim — the ulu-l-'ilm cluster: `[3:18]`, `[22:54]`, `[30:56]`).
* **`[15:54]`** "He said, “Do you bring me good news, when old age has overtaken me? What good news do you bring?”"
  * **Gloss:** The arithmetic protest — old-age-contact ("overtaken me") — the same impossibility grammar as Sarah's in 11:72.
* **`[15:55]`** "They said, “We bring you good news in truth, so do not despair.”"
  * **Gloss:** "In truth" (haqq) as the news's warranty; then the first despair-command of the pair.
* **`[15:56]`** "He said, “And who despairs of his Lord’s mercy but the lost?”"
  * **Gloss:** Abraham's counter-question — despair redefined as perdition (the qaum-khasir), not age; the twin confession-command `[39:53]` "do not despair of Allah's mercy"; 12:87's is the same grammar "None despairs... except the disbelieving people."
* **`[15:57]`** "He said, “So what is your business, O envoys?”"
  * **Gloss:** Business pivoted from himself to them — verbatim twin `[51:31]` (verified).
* **`[15:58]`** "They said, “We were sent to a sinful people.”"
  * **Gloss:** The second envelope: the errand of punishment, framed by the moral category — "a sinful people," the doom-errand's standard tag (cf. the exception in `[54:34]` "except for the family of Lot; We rescued them at dawn" — verified).
* **`[15:59]`** "“Except for the family of Lot; we will save them all.”"
  * **Gloss:** The exemption clause, universal-scope ("them all").
* **`[15:60]`** "“Except for his wife.” We have determined that she will be of those who lag behind."
  * **Gloss:** The double-exception (all... except one) resolved by divine determination — "those who lag behind," ruku 2's category (15:24) now has a name; the identical ruling-clause at `[7:83]` "she was of those who lagged behind."

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **"the righteous" (muttaqin):** `[15:45]`.
  * **Rendering:** "the righteous" for the class that the guidance note tracks as 2:2's "righteous" (hudan lil-muttaqin) — and rendered "the pious" at the verbatim twin `[51:15]` (drift observable within one formula).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:2]` "a guide for the righteous"; `[13:35]` "the Garden promised to the righteous" — this is the Garden-pole of that note's road.
* **"good news" (bishara/bashshara):** `[15:53–55]`, three-times-weighted.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[11:71]` "We gave her good news of Isaac" (the near-twin scene); `[51:28]` "the good news of a knowledgeable boy" — the guests' news is the same event in three surahs; the word's punishment-side in 15:49–50 shows bashara cannot be separated from indhar (warning).
* **"mercy" (rahmah):** `[15:56]` "his Lord's mercy."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[39:53]` (despair-command + "Allah forgives all sins. He is indeed the Forgiver, the Clement" — verified); the despair-cluster 15:56 ↔ 12:87 ↔ 39:53 forms the mercy-safety-net triangle.
* **"the painful punishment" (al-'adhab al-alim):** `[15:50]`.
  * **Rendering:** the definite threat-pair "the painful punishment" — classically *al-'adhab al-alim* (Source: standard vocabulary — verify); the corpus-ending twin is `[3:77]` "They will have a painful punishment" (verified verbatim tail).
  * **Elsewhere:** contrast the other threat-formula, "Severe in punishment": `[40:3]` ("Severe in punishment" inside the Forgiver-clause itself) and `[8:52]` — 15:49–50 keeps "Forgiver + painful" in the same breath while `[40:3]` welds "Forgiver + Severe" in one verse; see index §2 wording-check.

## 4. Cross-References (The Web)
* **The two-newses mandate:** `[15:49–50]` ↔ `[40:3]` (one verse welding "Forgiver of sins" with "Severe in punishment") and `[18:58]` ("Your Lord is the Forgiver, Possessor of Mercy... but they have an appointment") — the surah's own balance-law for how God is to be announced.
* **Guest-cycle twins:** `[15:51–60]` ↔ `[11:69–76]` and `[51:24–30]` — same visit: fear → calming → knowledge-boy → business-question (51:31 verbatim) → the sinful destination; Abraham's pleading in 11:74 is this ruku's untold middle.
* **Exception clause:** `[15:59–60]` ↔ `[7:83]` (wife-lag verbatim kin), `[54:34]` ("except for the family of Lot; We rescued them at dawn") — the exemption executed in ruku 5.
* **Within surah:** 15:45's gardens ↔ 15:44's seven doors (paired registers); "My servants" 15:49 ← 15:40/42 (the sincere under guarantee); "lag behind" 15:60 ← 15:24 (foreknowledge named in the roll-call).
