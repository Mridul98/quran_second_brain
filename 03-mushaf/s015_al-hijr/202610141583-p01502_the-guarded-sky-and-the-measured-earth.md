# The guarded sky, the measured earth, and the Inheritors (15:16–25)
**UID:** 202610141583
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 15:16–25 (Ruku 2 of 6 in al-Hijr)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[15:16]` – `[15:25]` (10 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Immediately after "We will surely preserve it" (15:9), the surah widens the guard-camera to the cosmos: the sky is ornamented and militarily patrolled against eavesdropping devils, the earth is spread, staked, and metered out in "proper measure," every store is divine, the winds are fertilizers — and the inventory terminates in "We are the Inheritors," the Gathering, and the Two Names that close the argument: the Wise, the Knowing.
* **Placement:** Ruku 2; the cosmological half of the opening pair (ruku 1 = the preserved Message, ruku 2 = the protected world). Its provision-clauses (20–22) feed directly into the tawhid-from-favors pattern, and its "lag behind" clause (24) pre-announces Lot's wife (60).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[15:16]`** "We placed constellations in the sky, and made them beautiful to the beholders."
  * **Gloss:** Ornament before weaponry: the sky's watchtowers are also eye-feast — beauty for those who look, not just fortification.
* **`[15:17]`** "And We guarded them from every outcast devil."
  * **Gloss:** The same "outcast" (rajim) epithet later flung at Iblis (34) and used in the seeking-refuge formula `[16:98]`; the sky is a protected channel — the physical analogue of 15:9's preservation.
* **`[15:18]`** "Except one who steals a hearing, and is followed by a visible projectile."
  * **Gloss:** The eavesdropping exception: jinn-snatched fragments of the unseen, pursued by a "visible projectile" (aflame-star); the twin scene at `[37:8–10]`, the jinn's own confession at `[72:8–9]`.
* **`[15:19]`** "We spread the earth, and placed stabilizers in it, and in it We grew all things in proper measure."
  * **Gloss:** Earth's three verbs — spread, staked, grown-with-measure; "stabilizers" is Itani's consistent mount-word (elsewhere at 21:31, 31:10, 41:10).
* **`[15:20]`** "And in it We created livelihoods for you, and for those for whom you are not the providers."
  * **Gloss:** Livelihoods doubled: yours, and the un-watered dependents (cattle, birds, the future — classical gloss; agent label) whose provider you never are — provision leaks past your intention by design.
* **`[15:21]`** "There is not a thing but with Us are its stores, and We send it down only in precise measure."
  * **Gloss:** The khozayn-claim: every thing's depot is divine; down-send is metered (compare `[54:49]` "Everything We created is precisely measured").
* **`[15:22]`** "We send the fertilizing winds; and send down water from the sky, and give it to you to drink, and you are not the ones who store it."
  * **Gloss:** Weather de-privatized: the winds impregnate, the water descends, the drinking is gifted — "you are not the ones who store it" answers the pre-Islamic rain-attribution (Source: classical note on 15:22's context — Ibn Kathir cites the people's "we were given rain by such-and-such star"; agent labeling — verify).
* **`[15:23]`** "It is We who give life and cause death, and We are the Inheritors."
  * **Gloss:** Life-and-death in one hand; "We are the Inheritors" — the exact twin of `[28:58]` "And We became the Inheritors" after a town's erasure.
* **`[15:24]`** "And We know those of you who go forward, and We know those who lag behind."
  * **Gloss:** The roll-call of history: vanguard and rear-guard known; the "lag" word lands grimly on Lot's wife (60, and 7:83).
* **`[15:25]`** "It is your Lord who will gather them together. He is the Wise, the Knowing."
  * **Gloss:** The Gathering (hashr) as the cosmos' last verb; the couple "the Wise, the Knowing" appears elsewhere only at `[51:30]`, in the guests' own speech — two rare occurrences, one surah-cluster.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`buruj` (constellations):** `[15:16]`.
  * **Rendering:** "constellations."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[25:61]` "Blessed is He who placed constellations in the sky" — near-verbatim twin; `[85:1]` "By the sky with the constellations" — the oath-name; cf. the fortress-irony of `[4:78]` "even if you were in fortified towers" — towers you hide in fail, while God's buruj guard the sky (Source: agent inference on the two images' contrast; some classical reports link buruj to sky-fortresses — verify).
* **`rajim` (outcast):** `[15:17]`, `[15:34]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[16:98]` "Satan the outcast" (the refuge formula), `[3:36]` Mary's descendants commended "from Satan the outcast", `[38:77]` "You are an outcast!" — the curse-word that ties ruku 2's patrolled sky to ruku 3's evicted Iblis.
* **"stabilizers":** `[15:19]`.
  * **Rendering:** Itani's mount-word (rawasi) — "stabilizers," never "mountains" at these four sites.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[21:31]`, `[31:10]`, `[41:10]` (verified) — all with the "lest it shifts/sways with you" clause; the earth's anti-wobble as a named favor.
* **`khozayn` (stores):** `[15:21]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** corpus-unique in this sense (`rg 'stores'` — 15:21–22 only; 12:48's "stored" is a verb of grain) — the metaphor is depot-and-drop: rain, sustenance, revelation all "sent down" from the same vocabulary (cf. the kalam note's channel-verbs).
