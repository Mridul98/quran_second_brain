# Clay, breathed spirit, and the first refusal: Iblis reprieved and bounded (15:26–44)
**UID:** 202610141584
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 15:26–44 (Ruku 3 of 6 in al-Hijr)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[15:26]` – `[15:44]` (19 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah's anthropology scene: the human being's make (clay, molded mud, breathed spirit) is announced to the angels, the prostration command is obeyed by all except Satan — whose refusal is argued from material origins, answered with eviction, curse, and a reprieve to the appointed Day, and whose declared counterattack ("I will glamorize… I will lure them all away") is met with a limit: over God's sincere servants he has no authority — and Hell, seven doors, each door an assigned class.
* **Placement:** Ruku 3; the answer to ruku 1's sneers: the present mockery re-stages the first mockery. The scene is the surah's refusal-archetype, and 15:40/42's "sincere servants / My servants" clause is the security guarantee that 15:9 gave the Message now extended to persons.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[15:26]`** "We created the human being from clay, from molded mud."
  * **Gloss:** Two-stage material: clay, then "molded mud" (the surah's salsal/ṭīn pair — Source: agent root identification; classical vocabularies map these to aged, ringing clay — verify).
* **`[15:27]`** "And the jinn We created before, from piercing fire."
  * **Gloss:** The rival stock introduced first, before the human: jinn from "piercing fire"; the twin make at `[55:15]` "a fusion of fire" (verified drift-pair).
* **`[15:28]`** "Your Lord said to the angels, “I am creating a human being from clay, from molded mud.”"
  * **Gloss:** Divine announcement *to* the angels — the material repeated aloud; near-twin `[38:71]` (verified).
* **`[15:29]`** "“When I have formed him, and breathed into him of My spirit, fall down prostrating before him.”"
  * **Gloss:** The elevation-verb is breath: "of My spirit" (addition to Himself — honor, not incarnation, per classical caution; Source: consensus-style gloss — verify); the command follows the breath. Verbatim twin `[38:72]`; cf. `[32:9]`.
* **`[15:30]`** "So the angels prostrated themselves, all together."
  * **Gloss:** Unanimity ("all together") — the scene's score before the one dissent.
* **`[15:31]`** "Except for Satan. He refused to be among those who prostrated themselves."
  * **Gloss:** The exclusion clause; refusal framed as refusing *to be among the prostrators* — rank, not disbelief-yet.
* **`[15:32]`** "He said, “O Satan, what kept you from being among those who prostrated themselves?”"
  * **Gloss:** The question that lets the excuse speak itself into evidence.
* **`[15:33]`** "He said, “I am not about to prostrate myself before a human being, whom You created from clay, from molded mud.”"
  * **Gloss:** The materialist snobbery, quoted verbatim against his own maker's verbatim words (26/28) — he cites creation to dispute command; the same argument at `[38:76]`, `[7:12]`, `[17:61]`.
* **`[15:34]`** "He said, “Then get out of here, for you are an outcast”."
  * **Gloss:** Eviction with the epithet that armed the sky in 15:17; twin judgment `[38:77]` (corpus ends this line with a straight closing quote after the period [sic] — see index §7).
* **`[15:35]`** "“And the curse will be upon you until the Day of Judgment.”"
  * **Gloss:** The curse is time-bound — to the very Day whose arrival he will beg to delay.
* **`[15:36]`** "He said, “My Lord, reprieve me until the Day they are resurrected.”"
  * **Gloss:** The first "My Lord" from Satan: a petition for respite, not forgiveness; near-twin `[7:14]` (verified).
* **`[15:37]`** "He said, “You are of those reprieved.”"
  * **Gloss:** Granted — but as one of a class: "of those reprieved," membership in a category whose deadline is set elsewhere (the "of those" grammar recurs at 15:43's meeting-place and 15:60 — Source: agent observation — verify).
* **`[15:38]`** "“Until the Day of the time appointed.”"
  * **Gloss:** But not to *his* requested Day: "the Day of the time appointed" — ruku 1's set-time law (15:4–5) now governs him too.
* **`[15:39]`** "He said, “My Lord, since You have lured me away, I will glamorize for them on earth, and I will lure them all away.”"
  * **Gloss:** Vengeance-by-aesthetics: he will make the world glamorous; the second "My Lord" accuses the sentence he received (the lure-ascription echoes `[4:119]`, `[17:62]` "since You have lured me" — cf. `[38:82]` "By Your majesty, I will seduce them all," verified twin).
* **`[15:40]`** "“Except for Your sincere servants among them.”"
  * **Gloss:** His own grand-total ("all") is cut by an exception clause he is granted to hear — the sincere are outside his warrant.
* **`[15:41]`** "He said, “This is a right way with Me.”"
  * **Gloss:** Divine counter-declaration (speaker: Allah — per consensus reading; Source: Ibn Kathir/Tabari take 15:41 as God's speech, some read Iblis continuing — disagreement noted, verify); the straight road is *with* God — the sincerity-exception of 40 is anchored to it.
* **`[15:42]`** "“Over My servants you have no authority, except for the sinners who follow you.”"
  * **Gloss:** The boundary: no dominion over the servants — only over followers who are themselves sinners; twin `[17:65]` "As for My devotees, you have no authority over them" (verified).
* **`[15:43]`** "And Hell is the meeting-place for them all."
  * **Gloss:** The rendezvous of the follower-class (Satan and his, 39–42's "them"); "meeting-place" (miqad) is corpus-unique here (verified).
* **`[15:44]`** "“It has seven doors; for each door is an assigned class.”"
  * **Gloss:** Seven doors, one class per door — the hierarchy of followship mapped to descent (Source: the seven-gates reading per classical reports, e.g. Ibn Kathir citing 15:44's "seven gates" — agent labeling — verify; the corpus itself gives no exegesis).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **"clay, from molded mud":** `[15:26]`.
  * **Rendering:** "molded mud" (classically *ḥama'in masnūn* — molded, shape-given sludge; Source: classical lexemes — verify; observable: the phrase is s015's own, repeated at 28/33 to bracket the refusal).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[37:11]` "sticky clay"; `[55:14]` "hard clay, like bricks"; `[23:12]` "an extract of clay"; `[7:12]`/`[17:61]` bare "mud" — the corpus's make-materials cluster (all verified).
* **`nafkh al-ruh` (breathed spirit):** `[15:29]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[38:72]` verbatim twin; `[32:9]` "breathed into him of His Spirit"; `[21:91]`/`[66:12]` the breath into Mary — the same verb marks human dignity and the Isa conception; the latter is the [Isa correction note](../../02-knowledge/202610141301-k-isa_quranic_jesus_correction.md)'s "spirit from Him" grammar.
* **`mukhlash/mukhlis` (sincere servants):** `[15:40]`.
  * **Rendering:** "Your sincere servants"; the formula-family runs "Except for Allah's sincere servants" at `[37:40/74/128/160]` (verified) and `[38:83]` "your loyal servants" (drift).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[33:8]` "That He may ask the sincere about their sincerity" — the Day-question of 15:92–93 answered in this vocabulary.
* **prostrate / bow down:** `[15:29–33]` vs `[15:98]`.
  * **Rendering:** within one surah, sujud is "prostrating" in the Adam scene (29–33) but "bow down" at the closing command (98); the same Adam-event elsewhere is "Bow down to Adam" `[2:34]`, "bowing down" `[7:12]`, "Bow down before Adam" `[17:61]` (verified) — Itani's single English gesture-word for the Quranic sujud.
