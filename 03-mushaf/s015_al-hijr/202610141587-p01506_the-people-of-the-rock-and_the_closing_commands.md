# The people of the Rock, the Grand Quran, and the closing commands (15:80–99)
**UID:** 202610141587
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 15:80–99 (Ruku 6 of 6 in al-Hijr)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[15:80]` – `[15:99]` (20 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The ledger closes with the surah's namesake — the people of the Rock (Thamud), carvers who felt safe, struck in the morning with their acquisitions useless — and the address pivots from the destroyed to the distressed messenger: creation is with truth and the Hour is coming, so forgive graciously; you have been given seven of the pairs and the Grand Quran, so do not strain your eyes at the couples of the affluent, lower your wing, proclaim, and leave the mockers to Us — your strained heart is answered not with argument but with liturgy: glorify, prostrate, worship until certainty arrives.
* **Placement:** Ruku 6; the surah's peroration. It restates ruku 1's claim pair (the given Book vs the fractured one, 87/90–91) and ruku 1's mockery-scene (6–15 vs 95–97), then resolves the whole accusation-cycle into the closing commands — the liturgical answer that sends the reader back to the Fatihah.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[15:80]`** "The people of the Rock also rejected the messengers."
  * **Gloss:** "also" welds Thamud into the sequence (Sodom, the Woods): the same crime — rejecting messengers (plural, as in 26:176's formula for the Woods); the surah-name-word lands here, not at the header.
* **`[15:81]`** "We gave them Our revelations, but they turned away from them."
  * **Gloss:** Revelation already delivered to them — the fault is posture, not access (echoes 15:13 "They do not believe in it").
* **`[15:82]`** "They used to carve homes in the mountains, feeling secure."
  * **Gloss:** The engineering plus the emotion: security carved in stone — near-twins `[7:74]` "carve out dwellings in the mountains" and `[26:149]` "you skillfully carve houses in the mountains?" (verified).
* **`[15:83]`** "But the Blast struck them in the morning." — **Gloss:** the ruku-5 strike-word reused (15:73), the morning deadline of 15:66 generalized; cf. `[91:14]` "So their Lord crushed them for their sin, and leveled it" (verified Thamud-twin).
* **`[15:84]`** "All they had acquired was of no avail to them." — **Gloss:** the acquisition-void clause — "of no avail" is corpus-unique here (verified; cf. functional kin "cannot benefit you" family elsewhere).
* **`[15:85]`** "We did not create the heavens and the earth, and what lies between them, except with truth. The Hour is coming, so forgive with gracious forgiveness."
  * **Gloss:** Cosmology's "except with truth" — twin `[10:5]`, kin `[29:44]` (verified) — and from there straight to ethics: because the Hour is real, pardon generously (safhan jamilan — "gracious forgiveness" is corpus-unique, verified): the mockers are pardoned, not out-run.
* **`[15:86]`** "Your Lord is the All-Knowing Creator." — **Gloss:** the two-warrant name of the pardon-command: knowing and creating — forgiveness is not naivety.
* **`[15:87]`** "We have given you seven of the pairs, and the Grand Quran."
  * **Gloss:** The prophet's counter-wealth against the affluent's enjoyments (88): "seven of the pairs" is classically the Fatihah (seven oft-repeated verses) — Source: the identification per transmitted reports collected by Ibn Kathir on 15:87, e.g. "the seven oft-repeated verses and the Grand Quran" — agent labeling from reports, verify; "the Grand Quran" — the Quran as such (cf. "a noble Quran" `[56:77]`, verified drift).
* **`[15:88]`** "Do not extend your eyes towards what We have bestowed on some couples of them to enjoy, and do not grieve over them, and lower your wing to the believers."
  * **Gloss:** Three commands in one breath: no covetous gaze, no grief-tax over their rejection, and the wing lowered — the humility-idiom twin `[17:24]` "lower to them the wing of humility" (verified); "couples" (azwaj) = kinds/pairs of them (Source: classical gloss; rendering observable — Itani's literal choice, unique here — verified).
* **`[15:89]`** "And say, “I am the clear warner.”" — **Gloss:** his office, stated with the article: the warner-formula "a clear warner" is the messengers' standard credential (`[71:2]`, `[11:25]`, `[26:115]`, `[46:9]` — verified cluster); 15:89 says THE clear warner — the Meccan surah's own title-page.
* **`[15:90]`** "Just as We sent down to the separatists."
  * **Gloss:** A comparator clause with no stated main verb — "as We sent down to the fracturers [so too...]" — the hanging syntax forces the reader to complete it with the nearest antecedent: the Grand Quran just given (87). *Source: classical readings treat 15:90 as an asseveration or a compressed comparison (Ibn Kathir-type options) — agent labeling, verify.*
* **`[15:91]`** "Those who made the Quran obsolete."
  * **Gloss:** The crime named: those who rendered the Quran discarded/divided (sarf) — neighbors in the corpus: `[30:32]` "those who divided their religion, and became sects" (verified); `[6:91]`'s scroll-theology "displaying them, yet concealing much" (verified) — scripture managed instead of obeyed.
* **`[15:92]`** "By your Lord, we will question them all." — **Gloss:** one of only two "By your Lord" divine oaths in the corpus (the twin at `[19:68]` "By your Lord, We will round them up" — verified); the interrogation-decree, kin of `[7:6]` "We will question those to whom messengers were sent" (verified).
* **`[15:93]`** "About what they used to do." — **Gloss:** the question's object: deeds, not excuses — the record-meter of 15:84's useless acquisitions inverted (here only what was DONE is in evidence).
* **`[15:94]`** "So proclaim openly what you are commanded, and turn away from the polytheists."
  * **Gloss:** The public-preaching command (classically read as the pivot from secret to open da'wah, per reports on 15:94 — Source: transmitted asbab-type readings collected by Ibn Kathir — verify) plus the second verb: turn away — from them, not from the message; method-kin `[16:125]` "call to the way of your Lord with wisdom and good advice" (verified).
* **`[15:95]`** "We are enough for you against the mockers." — **Gloss:** the counter-survival clause: divine sufficiency against ridicule — the "hasb" family (`[3:173]` "Allah is enough for us", `[9:129]` "Allah is enough for me" — verified); closes ruku 1's mockery-cycle (15:11, and cf. `[13:32]` ridicule→respite→seizure).
* **`[15:96]`** "Those who set up another god with Allah. They will come to know." — **Gloss:** the mockers' creed in one line — twin grammar `[34:33]` "set up rivals to Him" (verified; the tawhid note's own 2:22 pattern) — and the deferred threat "They will come to know" repeats 15:3's "They will find out."
* **`[15:97]`** "We are aware that your heart is strained by what they say."
  * **Gloss:** The psychology admitted by God: their speech squeezes the chest — near-twin situation `[11:12]` "you may be stressed because of it" (verified); the surah does not scold the feeling; it prescribes for it.
* **`[15:98]`** "So glorify the praise of your Lord, and be among those who bow down." — **Gloss:** the prescription: tasbih-with-praise and sujud-in-community ("be AMONG those who bow down" — the company clause); formula-twins: `[20:130]` "So bear patiently what they say, and celebrate the praises of your Lord", `[50:39]` "So endure what they say, and proclaim the praises of your Lord" (verified) — the same consolation-surgery performed on the same wound.
* **`[15:99]`** "And worship your Lord in order to attain certainty." — **Gloss:** the stop-condition: worship until yaqin arrives — "certainty" elsewhere at `[102:5–7]` ("knowledge of certainty… the eye of certainty") and `[74:47]` "Until the Inevitable came upon us" (verified drift) — death-euphemism and conviction in one word (Source: classical double-meaning of yaqin here — Ibn Kathir-type note — verify).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **"the Rock" (al-Hijr):** `[15:80]`.
  * **Rendering:** "the people of the Rock"; the phrase is corpus-unique (verified, case-sensitive `rg 'the Rock' corpus/`).
  * **Elsewhere:** Thamud's dossier: `[91:11–15]` (denial, she-camel, crushing — verified), `[89:9]` "those who carved the rocks in the valley", `[7:74]`, `[26:149]` — and the Hud-cycle retelling with its three-day respite at [p01106](../s011_hud/202610141527-p01106_saleh-thamood-the-she-camel-and-the-scream.md).
* **"seven of the pairs" / "the Grand Quran":** `[15:87]`.
  * **Rendering:** both phrases occur once in the corpus (verified).
  * **Elsewhere:** "the seven oft-repeated verses" is the Fatihah's transmitted title — Source: reports collected in classical commentaries (Ibn Kathir on 15:87 citing the hadith-form); the Fatihah's own note: [p00101](../s001_al-fatihah/202610140902-p00101_fatihah_r1_the_quran_in_miniature.md); cf. also 15:1's clarity-claim and `[39:23]` "a Scripture consistent and paired" (mathani — the same "pairs" vocabulary adjacent; Source: classical linkage — verify).
* **`mockers` / `hasb` (enough):** `[15:95–96]`.
  * **Rendering:** "mockers" is corpus-unique (verified); "We are enough for you" vs the servants' confession "Allah is enough for us" (`[3:173]`), "for me" (`[9:129]`).
  * **Elsewhere:** within surah: 15:11 "they ridiculed him" → 15:95 the same verb-class answered by sufficiency; cf. `[13:32]` (ridicule-law) and `[11:12]` (stress from their say-so).
* **`certainty` (yaqin):** `[15:99]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[102:5–7]` (levels: knowledge, then sight of certainty — verified); `[6:75]` Abraham "one of those with certainty"; `[45:20]` "believe with certainty"; `[30:60]` "those who lack certainty" — worship-to-certainty is the road's terminal condition (feeds the guidance note's "answered by seeking").
