# Surah 15: THE ROCK (al-Hijr)
**UID:** 202610141581

## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Meccan — classical consensus; **revelation order 54** in the standard transmitted enumeration (consistent with the adjacent orders recorded in this brain: Hud 52, Yusuf 53; ar-Ra'd later at 96). *Source: classical revelation-order lists (Ibn-Abbas-derived tradition, as tabulated in Tanzil-type lists) — agent labeling from the transmitted chronology tradition; verify against a printed source before teaching the number.* The corpus carries no statement of nuzul; the unnumbered Basmala heads the surah as usual. *See* [Basmala is unnumbered in this edition except 1:1](../../01-fleeting/202610132006-f-basmala_numbering_in_this_edition.md).
* **Size:** 99 ayahs, 6 ruku (per `data/ruku_map.json`: 15+10+19+16+19+20 = 99). *The surah-name fact (observable):* the corpus header names it THE ROCK; the phrase "the Rock" describing dwellings occurs in the corpus only at `[15:80]` — "the people of the Rock," i.e. Thamud of al-Hijr, the stone-carving country north of Hijaz.
* **Central theme:** A consolation surah for a mocked messenger. The Book authenticates itself under accusation — "Surely We revealed the Message, and We will surely preserve it" (`[15:9]`, the centerpiece claim) — while its audience calls the recipient insane (`[15:6]`) and bewitched (`[15:15]`). The same protection that covers the Message covers the sky (`[15:16–18]`), and the whole surah reframes present ridicule as the first refusal replayed: Iblis's materialist snobbery against Adam (`[15:26–44]`), then the itinerary of destroyed towns — Abraham's guests' errand to Sodom (`[15:51–76]`), the Woods, the Rock (`[15:78–84]`) — left on an existing road as readable lessons. The close prescribes the prophet's remedy for a strained heart: gracious forgiveness, the Grand Quran in hand, non-grief, gentle authority, open proclamation, tasbih, prostration, and worship until certainty arrives (`[15:85–99]`).

## 2. Wording check (assignment) — honest verification against the corpus
* **The sneer (15:6):** present, but the words are "you are **insane**" (`[15:6]`); "bewitched" belongs to `[15:15]` ("we are people bewitched") — the twin accusation formula elsewhere is "You are surely one of the bewitched" `[26:153]`, `[26:185]`. Assignment wording merged the two.
* **15:49–50:** the actual pair is "Inform My servants that **I am the Forgiver**, / **And that My punishment is the painful punishment**." The assigned phrasing "your Lord is the Forgiving, the Merciful — and your Lord is the Severe in punishment" is NOT this surah's wording; nearest corpus twins: `[18:58]` "Your Lord is the Forgiver, Possessor of Mercy" and `[40:3]` which welds "Forgiver of sins… Severe in punishment" in one verse. Verified via `rg -i 'severe in punishment' corpus/` — zero hits in s015.
* **15:51–76 (assignment):** these verses are the **guests of Abraham** (51–60) and the **mission to Lot** (61–76); the people of **al-Hijr/Thamud** are `[15:80–84]`, not 51–76. Both blocks verified verbatim in corpus.
* **Closing command (15:98):** present — "So glorify the praise of your Lord, and be among those who bow down," with `[15:99]` "And worship your Lord in order to attain certainty" as the true stop.
* **15:9 as kalam centerpiece:** the verse exists as quoted; **gap reported:** the canonical [Kalam note](../../02-knowledge/202610132005-k-kalam_allah_revelation_as_speech.md) does NOT currently cite `[15:9]` (no link site there; its evidence list stops at 4:164). Per no-edit-outside-s015 rule, the kalam note is left untouched; s015 ruku 1 links to it from its side.

## 4. Structural Arc
* **Claim vs. counter-claim (ruku 1, 1–15):** letters → Book → preservation guarantee → the three sneers (insane, angels-or-truth, sky-gate demand) → the law of the set time as the frame under all of it.
* **The protected cosmos (ruku 2, 16–25):** the sky armed against eavesdropping devils, the earth staked and measured, life/death/inheritance in divine hands — the same security protocol as `[15:9]` extended to cosmology, closing on the Gathering.
* **The archetype of refusal (ruku 3, 26–44):** Adam's make and the prostration command; Iblis's refusal, curse, counter-claim ("I will glamorize"), and the limit set on him — "Over My servants you have no authority" — with Hell's seven doors as the destination column.
* **The two Newses (ruku 4, 45–60):** the Gardens of reunited brothers, then the divine self-disclosure "the Forgiver / the painful punishment" — and Abraham's guests carrying both news-types: a son, and a sinful people exempted-only-one.
* **The execution and its readability (ruku 5, 61–79):** Sodom's rush, the Blast at sunrise, the overturned town under baked clay — and the epistemology claim: lessons for those who read signs, on an existing road; the Woods added; "Both are clearly documented."
* **Hijr and the remedy (ruku 6, 80–99):** Thamud's carved security erased; creation "except with truth"; the seven of the pairs and the Grand Quran; the prophet's prescription — no envying eyes, lowered wing, open proclamation, divine sufficiency against mockers, praise-and-prostration, worship until certainty.
