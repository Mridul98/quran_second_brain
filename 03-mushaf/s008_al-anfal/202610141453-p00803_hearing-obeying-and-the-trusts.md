# Hearing, responding, and the trusts: the covenant address (8:20–28)
**UID:** 202610141453
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 8:20–28 (Ruku 3 of 10 in al-Anfal)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[8:20]` – `[8:28]` (9 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Three "O you who believe!" calls run from the ears to the ledger: obey and do not play deaf (20–23), respond to what revives, because Allah knows what sits between you and your own heart (24), and then the communal rules — fear the discord that does not spare only its authors, remember Mecca's helplessness, keep faith with Allah, the Messenger, and the trusts, and know that wealth and children are the test they are (25–28).
* **Placement:** The pivot ruku: the Badr narrative yields to direct legislation; the deaf-and-dumb portrait (22–23) is the surah's hypocrite-question before the label itself appears at 8:49.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[8:20]`** "O you who believe! Obey Allah and His Messenger, and do not turn away from him when you hear."
  * **Gloss:** Obedience defined as hearing-without-turning; the covenant's basic verb-pair.
* **`[8:21]`** "And be not like those who say, “We hear,” when they do not hear."
  * **Gloss:** The counterfeit formula: profession without reception.
* **`[8:22]`** "The worst of animals to Allah are the deaf and dumb—those who do not reason."
  * **Gloss:** Sense-capacity wasted is what is actually being called worst; the comparison is to dumb beasts.
* **`[8:23]`** "Had Allah recognized any good in them, He would have made them hear; and had He made them hear, they would have turned away defiantly."
  * **Gloss:** The counterfactual closes both directions: hearing is a test, not a cure; the "good" question will be re-opened for prisoners at 8:70.
* **`[8:24]`** "O you who believe! Respond to Allah and to the Messenger when He calls you to what will revive you. And know that Allah stands between a man and his heart, and that to Him you will be gathered."
  * **Gloss:** Response is framed as life; divine immanence (the heart's interior) and the gathering (resurrection) bracket the command.
* **`[8:25]`** "And beware of discord which does not afflict the wrongdoers among you exclusively; and know that Allah is severe in retribution."
  * **Gloss:** Contagion law: unaddressed wrongdoing does not stay among its authors (the "severe in retribution" twin of 8:13).
* **`[8:26]`** "And remember when you were few, oppressed in the land, fearing that people may capture you; but He sheltered you, and supported you with His victory, and provided you with good things—so that you may be thankful."
  * **Gloss:** The Meccan memory as gratitude-engine: smallness, fear, shelter, victory, provision.
* **`[8:27]`** "O you who believe! Do not betray Allah and the Messenger, nor betray your trusts, while you know."
  * **Gloss:** Treachery is named as the covenant's opposite; "while you know" removes ignorance as an excuse.
* **`[8:28]`** "And know that your possessions and your children are a test, and that Allah possesses an immense reward."
  * **Gloss:** The two attachments are relabeled as trial; the immense reward is the counter-weight.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **"We hear" (20–23):** the covenant's oral formula.
  * **Rendering:** "those who say, “We hear,” when they do not hear."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[24:51]` the honest formula "We hear and we obey"; `[4:46]` the forged one "We hear and we disobey"; `[5:7]` — "His covenant which He covenanted with you; when you said, “We hear and we obey”" — 8:20–21 is covenant-language quoted against its counterfeit (all sites verified).
* **`khiyanah` / trusts (8:27):** betrayal of entrusted things.
  * **Rendering:** "betray Allah and the Messenger... betray your trusts."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[4:58]` "give back things entrusted to you to their owners"; `[33:72]` the cosmic "Trust"; within surah `[8:58]` "Allah does not like the treacherous" completes the pair (see Ruku 7).
* **`fitna` renderings in this surah:** the same trial-word, three English faces.
  * **Rendering:** "discord" at `[8:25]`, "a test" at `[8:28]`, "persecution" at `[8:39]` — that all three translate forms of one Arabic root (f-t-n) is a lexical claim (*Source: agent inference — verify against a concordance*); the English spread itself is observable.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:193]` "no oppression" and `[2:191]` "Oppression is more serious than murder"; `[2:217]`/`[29:10]` "persecution"; the same command pair across the surahs shows the edition's drift (observable).
* **Deaf and dumb (8:22):**
  * **Rendering:** "the worst of animals... deaf and dumb."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:18]` "Deaf, dumb, blind. They will not return." — the hypocrite dossier's sensory decay; `[8:55]` "the worst of creatures" repeats the superlative two ruku later, and `[98:6]` carries the identical English phrase (verified pairs).
