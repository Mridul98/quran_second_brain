# Desistance, the fifth, and the Day of Distinction (8:38–44)
**UID:** 202610141455
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 8:38–44 (Ruku 5 of 10 in al-Anfal)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[8:38]` – `[8:44]` (7 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The door closes and the ledger opens: desistance is forgiven, persistence invokes the precedent of destroyed ancients (38), the fight is defined as anti-persecution until religion is Allah's alone (39), protection is re-attributed (40) — and then the spoils law arrives with its five-plus-one claim list (41), followed by a slow-motion replay of the day: banks and caravan, the dream of small numbers, the mutual diminishment in both armies' eyes, all of it "a predetermined matter" decided by evidence, not by plan (42–44).
* **Placement:** The surah's legal center: 8:41 answers 8:1's question at last, and "the Day of Distinction" reuses the criterion-word of 8:29 as a day-name.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[8:38]`** "Say to those who disbelieve: if they desist, their past will be forgiven. But if they persist—the practice of the ancients has passed away."
  * **Gloss:** Amnesty for the future, precedent for the threat: what "passed away" is the divine practice toward destroyed peoples.
* **`[8:39]`** "Fight them until there is no more persecution, and religion becomes exclusively for Allah. But if they desist—Allah is Seeing of what they do."
  * **Gloss:** War's aim named as ending persecution; desistance again met with sight, not sword.
* **`[8:40]`** "And if they turn away, know that Allah is your Protector. The Best Protector, and the Best Supporter."
  * **Gloss:** Sovereignty formula closing the option tree: fight, desist, or turn — Allah handles the third.
* **`[8:41]`** — three clause-chunks:
  * "And know that whatever spoils you gain, to Allah belongs its fifth, and to the Messenger, and the relatives, and the orphans, and the poor, and to the wayfarer,"
  * "provided you believe in Allah and in what We revealed to Our servant on the Day of Distinction, the day when the two armies met."
  * "Allah is Capable of everything."
  * **Gloss:** The only fifth-law verse in the corpus: a fifth set apart for Allah and the Messenger, the relatives, the orphans, the poor, and the wayfarer; how the portion divides among these six named parties is classically disputed — faith in the Day of Distinction is made the condition of compliance (*Source: classical juristic disagreement — verify*).
* **`[8:42]`** — three clause-chunks:
  * "Recall when you were on the nearer bank, and they were on the further bank, and the caravan was below you."
  * "Had you planned for this meeting, you would have disagreed on the timing, but Allah was to carry out a predetermined matter, so that those who perish would perish by clear evidence, and those who survive would survive by clear evidence."
  * "Allah is Hearing and Knowing."
  * **Gloss:** The geography of Badr stated as anti-coincidence: the meeting was staged, not scheduled; evidence is the point of the encounter.
* **`[8:43]`** "Allah made them appear in your dream as few. Had He made them appear as many, you would have lost heart, and disputed in the matter. But Allah saved the situation. He knows what the hearts contain."
  * **Gloss:** Sleep as tactics: the dream edited so the march would hold; hearts' contents are Allah's inventory (echoes 8:24).
* **`[8:44]`** "When you met, He made them appear as few in your eyes, and made you appear fewer in their eyes, so that Allah may conclude a predetermined matter. To Allah all matters revert."
  * **Gloss:** Double optics, one purpose: underestimation on both sides delivers the decided matter; all affairs revert.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **"its fifth" (8:41):** the one share-law verse.
  * **Rendering:** "to Allah belongs its fifth" — the word "fifth" occurs elsewhere in the corpus only in the li'an oaths (`[24:7]`, `[24:9]` "the fifth time") — different sense; here it is the spoils share (single site for this legal sense, rg-verified).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[59:7]` — the fay' list "belongs to Allah, and to the Messenger, and to the relatives, and to the orphans, and to the poor, and to the wayfarer" — the near-identical claimant list with its circulation clause "so that it may not circulate solely between the wealthy among you": two distribution verses, one economics (see infraq link).
* **persecution (8:39):**
  * **Rendering:** "no more persecution."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:193]` "fight them until there is no oppression, and worship becomes devoted to Allah alone" — the same command, different nouns ("oppression" vs. "persecution"; observable drift); `[2:217]` "persecution is more serious than killing"; `[29:10]` "he equates the people's persecution with Allah’s retribution" (verified sites).
* **"the practice of the ancients" (8:38):**
  * **Rendering:** "the practice of the ancients has passed away."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[43:8]` "the example of the ancients has passed away" — the twin formula in a threat-context; `[83:13]`/`[26:137]` apply "of the ancients" to revelation-dismissal — the phrase family is contested across surahs (observable).
* **"Day of Distinction" / "predetermined matter" (8:41, 42, 44):**
  * **Rendering:** "the Day of Distinction" (unique corpus site) and "a predetermined matter" twice here, once more at `[8:68]` (rg counts).
  * **Elsewhere:** the day is Badr by classical identification — the corpus never names it in this surah; `[3:123]` is the corpus's only verse that names Badr. Whether Distinction (furqan) and criterion (8:29) share one Arabic root is lexical (*Source: agent inference — verify*).
