# The hypocrite taunt, the Pharaoh paradigm, and treaty law (8:49–58)
**UID:** 202610141457
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 8:49–58 (Ruku 7 of 10 in al-Anfal)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[8:49]` – `[8:58]` (10 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The only hypocrite-label verse in the surah (49) — the taunt "Their religion has deluded these people" — is answered on three levels: the deathbed of the open enemy (50–51), the Pharaoh precedent for why God changes nothing until people change themselves (52–54), and the statutes for treaty-breakers (55–58), whose closing line ("Allah does not like the treacherous") ties the outer war back to the inner betrayal-law of 8:27.
* **Placement:** Mid-surah reckoning: the enemy typology completes (disbelievers, Pharaoh's heirs, hypocrites-with-sickness) just before the surah turns to deterrence and peace.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[8:49]`** "The hypocrites and those in whose hearts is sickness said, “Their religion has deluded these people.” But whoever puts his trust in Allah—Allah is Mighty and Wise."
  * **Gloss:** The label appears once in this surah, paired with the sickness-phrase known from 2:10/47:20; the taunt reads the believers' smallness as delusion, and trust in Allah is the counter-definition (echoing 8:2).
* **`[8:50]`** "If only you could see, as the angels take away those who disbelieve, striking their faces and their backs: “Taste the agony of the Burning.”"
  * **Gloss:** The vision clause — angels as death-agents; faces (front) and backs (retreat) both struck.
* **`[8:51]`** "“That is because of what your hands have committed, and because Allah is not unjust to the servants.”"
  * **Gloss:** Two denials folded together: no punishment without deed, and no divine injustice.
* **`[8:52]`** "Like the behavior of the people of Pharaoh, and those before them. They rejected the signs of Allah, so Allah seized them for their sins. Allah is Powerful, Severe in punishment."
  * **Gloss:** Pharaoh as the template case of seized-for-rejected.
* **`[8:53]`** "That is because Allah would never change a blessing He has bestowed on a people unless they change what is within themselves, and because Allah is Hearing and Knowing."
  * **Gloss:** The surah's sociology-law: blessing is conditional on interior change (13:11's twin).
* **`[8:54]`** "Such was the case with the people of Pharaoh, and those before them. They denied the signs of their Lord, so We annihilated them for their wrongs, and We drowned the people of Pharaoh—they were all evildoers."
  * **Gloss:** The template restated with the drowning detail — repetition as evidence's emphasis.
* **`[8:55]`** "The worst of creatures in Allah's view are those who disbelieve. They have no faith."
  * **Gloss:** The superlative returns (8:22's "worst of animals" now for the open deniers); in this corpus edition the line uniquely prints a straight apostrophe in "Allah's" — quoted here verbatim, not normalized.
* **`[8:56]`** "Those of them with whom you made a treaty, but they violate their agreement every time. They are not righteous."
  * **Gloss:** The treaty-breaker class defined by serial violation, not by initial enmity.
* **`[8:57]`** "If you confront them in battle, make of them a fearsome example for those who follow them, that they may take heed."
  * **Gloss:** Deterrence logic: exemplary treatment of violators protects future agreements.
* **`[8:58]`** "If you fear treachery on the part of a people, break off with them in a like manner. Allah does not like the treacherous."
  * **Gloss:** Even rupture must be symmetric — no silent ambush; the treachery-verb of 8:27, now with its statute.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **"hypocrites ... hearts is sickness" (49):**
  * **Rendering:** the label phrase pairs nifaq with marad exactly as `[2:10]` (“In their hearts is sickness, and Allah has increased their sickness”) and `[47:20]`, `[5:52]`, `[9:125]`, `[74:31]` carry the sickness-half (all verified); label-sites elsewhere: `[4:145]` "The hypocrites will be in the lowest level of the Fire"; `[9:73]` "Strive against the disbelievers and the hypocrites"; `[66:9]` "Strive hard against the disbelievers and the hypocrites".
  * **Elsewhere:** the nifaq note's cluster counts (6 in an-Nisa, 5 in at-Tawbah, 6 in al-Ahzab) do not list this surah — 8:49 is a label-site the dossier omits (anomaly noted at build).
* **"deluded" (49):**
  * **Rendering:** "Their religion has deluded these people."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[57:14]` "became deluded by wishful thinking" — the hypocrites' own post-mortem line; `[17:64]` "Satan promises them nothing but delusion" — delusion as financed expectation (the nifaq note's "losing position").
* **"treaty / agreement / treachery" (56–58):**
  * **Rendering:** "made a treaty... violate their agreement... fear treachery."
  * **Elsewhere:** "treaty" recurs at `[8:72]` (alliance clause) and `[4:90]` "people with whom you have a treaty"; the treacherous are classed with "the treacherous blasphemer" of `[31:32]`; breach-by-oath echoes `[2:100]` "whenever they make a covenant, some of them toss it aside" (verified pairs; mithaq/aqd root-identity = lexical inference — verify).
* **"worst of creatures" (55):**
  * **Rendering:** "The worst of creatures in Allah's view"
  * **Elsewhere:** `[98:6]` "These are the worst of creatures." — same English superlative; within surah the construction twice: "worst of animals" (8:22, deaf-dumb) → "worst of creatures" (8:55, faithless) — the ranking completes (rg-verified).
