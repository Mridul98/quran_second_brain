# Deterrence, peace-inclination, and hearts no money can unite (8:59–64)
**UID:** 202610141458
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 8:59–64 (Ruku 8 of 10 in al-Anfal)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[8:59]` – `[8:64]` (6 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah drafts a statecraft: denial of the enemies' head-start (59), full-spectrum deterrence funded by a God who repays spending in full (60), immediate reciprocity when they incline to peace (61), and, under all of it, the confession that the community's real asset is unity of hearts — an asset the Prophet could not have bought with everything on earth, and did not (62–64): "Count on Allah, and on the believers who have followed you."
* **Placement:** The pivot from tactics (ruku 6–7) to policy; 8:61 is the surah's widest door — peace as a divine option — set precisely between the Pharaoh-paradigm and the prisoner-ratio.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[8:59]`** "Let not the disbelievers assume that they are ahead. They will not escape."
  * **Gloss:** The race-metaphor denied: a head-start is an illusion; the verdict will catch up.
* **`[8:60]`** "And prepare against them all the power you can muster, and all the cavalry you can mobilize, to terrify thereby Allah’s enemies and your enemies, and others besides them whom you do not know, but Allah knows them. Whatever you spend in Allah’s way will be repaid to you in full, and you will not be wronged."
  * **Gloss:** Deterrence as theology: maximum visible strength, aimed also at unseen third parties; the military budget closes with a repayment clause — no spending lost.
* **`[8:61]`** "But if they incline towards peace, then incline towards it, and put your trust in Allah. He is the Hearer, the Knower."
  * **Gloss:** Symmetric de-escalation: their lean is the command to lean — trust replaces victory-talk.
* **`[8:62]`** "If they intend to deceive you—Allah is sufficient for you. It is He who supported you with His aid, and with the believers."
  * **Gloss:** Bad faith is answered by sufficiency: the double support — heaven's aid and the believers themselves.
* **`[8:63]`** "And He united their hearts. Had you spent everything on earth, you would not have united their hearts, but Allah united them together. He is Mighty and Wise."
  * **Gloss:** The surah's most quoted line of grace: cohesion priced against all the earth's wealth — and lost to it; Allah alone owns that asset.
* **`[8:64]`** "O prophet! Count on Allah, and on the believers who have followed you."
  * **Gloss:** The first "O prophet!" of three: sufficiency includes the people who followed — trust is communal.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **"the power you can muster... cavalry you can mobilize" (60):**
  * **Rendering:** "power" and "cavalry" (the tethered-mounts idiom; the root-claim ribat/quwwah is lexical — agent inference, verify).
  * **Elsewhere:** "cavalry" occurs only here and at `[17:64]` — where it belongs to Satan's arsenal ("rally against them your cavalry and your infantry") — the corpus's two cavalry-lines face each other, deterrence vs. enticement (verified); "repaid to you in full" pairs with `[2:245]` the generous loan multiplied and `[35:29]` "a trade that will not fail" (verified).
* **"incline" (61):**
  * **Rendering:** double verb: "if they incline towards peace, then incline towards it."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[47:35]` "do not waver and call for peace while you have the upper hand"; `[4:90]` "if they... offer you peace, then Allah assigns no excuse for you against them" — the corpus's three peace-inclination statutes rhyme (verified lines).
* **"sufficient / Count on" (62, 64):**
  * **Rendering:** "Allah is sufficient for you"; "Count on Allah."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[39:36]` "Is Allah not enough for His servant?"; `[33:3]` "Allah is enough as a trustee"; `[4:81]` "Allah is Guardian enough" — the hafiz/kafi formula cluster (English-pair verification; Arabic root identity = inference).
* **"united their hearts" (63):**
  * **Rendering:** "He united their hearts... Allah united them together."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[3:103]` "you were enemies, and He reconciled your hearts, and by His grace you became brethren" — the Aws-Khazraj memory in near-identical terms (rendering drift "united/reconciled" observable); within surah, the negative twin is 8:46 ("do not dispute, lest you falter") and 8:1 ("settle your differences").

## 4. Cross-References (The Web)
* **Support doublet:** `[9:40]` — "Allah made His tranquility descend upon him, and supported him with forces you did not see" — 8:62's aid-and-believers formula is the cave-scene's twin (verified line); `[8:9]` "reinforcing you with one thousand angels" is the Badr version — three support-scenes, one argument.

* **Peace law cluster:** 8:61 ↔ 4:90 ↔ 47:35 (all cited above) — note 47:35's conditional ("while you have the upper hand") vs 8:61's unconditional reciprocity: a gradient in the corpus's siyar material (*Source: agent inference — verify before repeating*).
* **Within surah:** 8:60's repayment ↔ `[8:36]`'s sorrow-economy — the two "spending" plots of the surah (enemy spend → loss; believer spend → "will be repaid to you in full") meet here.
