# The criterion for the conscious: the counter-plot and the House's hollow worship (8:29–37)
**UID:** 202610141454
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 8:29–37 (Ruku 4 of 10 in al-Anfal)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[8:29]` – `[8:37]` (9 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** One verse of promise opens the ruku — taqwa buys a criterion, sin-remission, and forgiveness (29) — and the rest shows what needs a criterion: the Meccan plot out-plotted (30), recitation brushed off as "myths of the ancients" (31), a suicide-prayer for stones (32), the two stayings of punishment (33), the custodianship quarrel over the Sacred Mosque (34–35), and the enemy's wealth-spending ending in a heap sorted into Hell (36–37).
* **Placement:** The last "O you who believe!" promise before the surah turns to the other camp; its "criterion" (29) and "Day of Distinction" (41, next ruku) are one word splitting the world.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[8:29]`** "O you who believe! If you remain conscious of Allah, He will give you a criterion, and will remit from you your sins, and will forgive you. Allah is possessor of infinite grace."
  * **Gloss:** The bargain verse: watchfulness purchases discernment, cleansing, and pardon — grace named as the counterparty's character.
* **`[8:30]`** "When the disbelievers plotted against you, to imprison you, or kill you, or expel you. They planned, and Allah planned, but Allah is the Best of planners."
  * **Gloss:** The Hijrah night's three-option plot answered by a better plan; the Qur'an's only verdict "Best of planners" (with 3:54's twin).
* **`[8:31]`** "And when Our revelations are recited to them, they say, “We have heard. Had we wanted, we could have said the like of this; these are nothing but myths of the ancients.”"
  * **Gloss:** The recitation formula of 8:2 re-quoted from the other side: same hearing, opposite fruit.
* **`[8:32]`** "And they said, “Our God, if this is the truth from You, then rain down on us stones from the sky, or visit us with a painful affliction.”"
  * **Gloss:** A conditional self-curse — the "verdict" demand of 8:19 voiced by the Quraysh.
* **`[8:33]`** "But Allah would not punish them while you are amongst them. And Allah would not punish them as long as they seek forgiveness."
  * **Gloss:** Two stayings of punishment: the Prophet's presence and the possibility of istighfar — mercy built into the timeline.
* **`[8:34]`** "Yet why should Allah not punish them, when they are turning others away from the Sacred Mosque, although they are not its custodians? Its rightful custodians are the pious; but most of them do not know."
  * **Gloss:** The stayings have a limit; custody of the sacred is redefined from lineage to piety.
* **`[8:35]`** "Their prayer at the House was nothing but whistling and clapping—so taste the punishment for your blasphemy."
  * **Gloss:** The cultic parody — prayer emptied to sound; unique description in the corpus.
* **`[8:36]`** "Those who disbelieve spend their wealth to repel from Allah’s path. They will spend it, then it will become a source of sorrow for them, and then they will be defeated. Those who disbelieve will be herded into Hell."
  * **Gloss:** The counter-economy: enemy spending aimed at barring the path — spend, regret, defeat, herd, in one sequence.
* **`[8:37]`** "That Allah may distinguish the bad from the good, and heap the bad on top of one another, and pile them together, and throw them in Hell. These are the losers."
  * **Gloss:** The criterion (29) becomes a sorting: the dross heaped and thrown; "the losers" closes the ledger.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`furqan` / "criterion" (8:29):**
  * **Rendering:** "a criterion" (8:29); the same English-root family reappears as "the Day of Distinction" (`[8:41]`) — treating both as one Arabic root (f-r-q) is a lexical claim (*Source: agent inference — verify*).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:53]` "We gave Moses the Scripture and the Criterion" — the furqan of the exodus; 8:29–41 transposes it from salvation-history to discernment-as-combat-logics; `[2:185]` bundles "guidance... and the Criterion" in one breath — the same pairing 8:29 makes between taqwa and discernment.
* **"planned / Best of planners" (8:30):**
  * **Rendering:** "They planned, and Allah planned, but Allah is the Best of planners."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[3:54]` — verbatim twin, "They planned, and Allah planned; but Allah is the Best of planners" (Jesus' plot; observable verbatim); `[34:33]` "your scheming by night and day" — the same reversal vocabulary in a hell-dialogue (English-word pair).
* **"myths of the ancients" (8:31):**
  * **Rendering:** "these are nothing but myths of the ancients."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[68:15]` "When Our Verses are recited to him, he says, “Myths of the ancients!”" and `[6:25]` the identical dismissal — while `[83:13]` renders the same formula "Legends of the ancients" and `[26:137]` "morals of the ancients": the edition's drift on this rebuttal-word is observable corpus fact.
* **"custodians" (8:34) and the pious (muttaqin):**
  * **Rendering:** "Its rightful custodians are the pious." "Custodians" is a single corpus site (rg-verified); "the pious" here renders the piety-word class the guidance note tracks (cf. the guide's own example: muttaqin "believers context in 8:34").
  * **Elsewhere:** `[48:25]` "barred you from the Sacred Mosque" and `[2:217]` "prevent access to the Holy Mosque" — the barring charge is the Hudaybiyyah-era fact the custodianship verdict answers; `[5:2]` the same bar inside the covenant clause.

## 4. Cross-References (The Web)
* **Recitation ring:** `[8:2]` ↔ `[8:31]` — the two "revelations are recited" lines of this surah (rg: exactly these two) define believer and rejector by one identical event; the criterion of 8:29 is what tells them apart.
* **The demanded verdict:** `[8:32]` ↔ `[8:19]` — the stone-prayer is the "verdict" whose arrival Badr announced (within-surah pair, observable).
* **Mercy timeline:** `[8:33]` ↔ `[8:38]` — the two stayings (your presence; seeking forgiveness) answer the next ruku's "if they desist, their past will be forgiven" (see Ruku 5).
* **Sorrow-economy:** `[8:36]` — enemy spending that turns to regret; the mirror of `[8:3]`'s spending that turns to "generous provision" and `[8:60]`'s spending "repaid to you in full" (the infraq note's two-balance-sheet reading fits exactly here).
* **Sorting image:** `[8:37]` "heap the bad on top of one another" — the criterion of 29 executed as metallurgy (one-site phrase; rg-verified).

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Medinan recollection of Meccan events: 8:30's plot (imprison/kill/expel) is the dar al-nadwa decision on the night of the Hijrah (*Source: transmitted sira reports, Ibn Ishaq material — verify*); the corpus itself gives no occasion (observable fact).
* **Classical aside:** 8:33's two stayings are classically read as the Prophet's presence and the people's istighfar as permanent buffers against total punishment (*Source: consensus of classical tafsir on 8:33 — verify*).
