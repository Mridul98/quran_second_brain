## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Meccan — agreed in the classical type-lists; the content (cosmic teardown, the watchers' record, the Day) is the early reckoning register. *Source: classical type-lists — verify.* Revelation order: **transmitted nuzul tables — verify** (corpus states no nuzul; unnumbered Basmala heads the surah).
* **Size:** 19 ayahs, 1 ruku (ruku_map global 524 — verified). Sits in the shattering cluster with 81 (at-Takwir) and 84 (al-Inshiqaq).
* **Title:** al-Infitar — Itani's header "THE SHATTERING" tracks `[82:1]` "When the sky breaks apart"; the surah takes its name from the opening verb (infitar, a bursting-open).
* **Central theme:** The four cosmic ruptures exist so that "Each soul will know what it has advanced, and what it has deferred" (82:5) — and that knowledge is staged as an interrogation: "O man! What deluded you concerning your Lord, the Most Generous?" (82:6), answered by the three-verb craft of creation (82:7–8), the unblinking watchers (82:10–12), and the two camps (82:13–14). The close double-asks what the Day of Justice is, and answers it in one line: no soul avails another, and "the decision on that Day is Allah's" (82:19) — tawhid as the final syntax of judgment.
* **Reading hint:** 82:6–8 is the Quran's shortest creation-sermon — three verbs of making plus "whatever shape He willed" (82:8). Read it against `[96:3]` (the other "Most Generous") and forward into 83: the watchers here become named registers there, then handed books in 84, then the Tablet in 85 — this surah is door one of the batch's escalation.

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Oaths (82:1–4):** sky, stars, oceans, tombs — four "when" clauses unmake the cosmos top-down, ending at the graves.
* **Resolution (82:5):** every soul learns its own ledger — advanced and deferred items.
* **The address (82:6–9):** "O man" — one of exactly two such addresses in the corpus (twin `[84:6]` — rg-verified); delusion diagnosed, generosity (al-Karim) pleaded, creative craft cited (created/formed/proportioned; shaped as He willed), verdict: "But you reject the religion."
* **The watchers (82:10–12):** over you, honest recorders — they know everything you do: the ledger dossier in miniature.
* **The split (82:13–16):** virtuous in bliss, wicked in Hell, entering on the Day of Justice, never absent from it.
* **Permanence in five words (82:16):** "And they will not be absent from it" — the batch's tersest ever-forever clause; its inner twin is 83:15's being "screened from their Lord" (present there, permanent here — *Source: agent inference on batch adjacency — verify*).
* **The double interrogation and close (82:17–19):** "what will convey to you what the Day of Justice is?" ×2 — answered by solitude of souls and total divine decision.

## 5b. Batch-Return Verification Flags (this index)
* **Corpus wording vs task hints (all corrected in the passage note):** "the water-spooling/graves-turned-over" renders as "the oceans are exploded / the tombs are strewn around" (82:3–4); "the Generous" is "the Most Generous" (82:6, pairing uniquely with 96:3 — rg-verified); "proportioned you, balanced you" renders "formed you, and proportioned you" (82:7) with "In whatever shape He willed, He assembled you" (82:8); "noble recorders" is "**Honest** recorders" (82:11).
* **"You deny the Reckoning" NOT the close:** the hint misplaces the denial. The denial line is mid-unit — "But you reject the religion" (82:9, phrase-twin 95:7, rg-verified). The actual close (82:19) is the decision-belongs-to-Allah line. "Day of Reckoning" itself belongs to 83:11.
* **"Day of Justice":** corpus-unique to s082 (82:15,17,18,19 — rg-verified); the same Arabic (yawm ad-din) renders "Day of Judgment" at 1:4 — edition term-map worth knowing.
* **"Oath chain (82:1–5)" hint-range adjusted:** the oath-clauses are 82:1–4; 82:5 is their resolution ("Each soul will know…") — the distinction matters because 82:5's knowing is then tripled by 82:12 (the watchers), 82:17–18 (interrogation), and 82:19 (the decision).
* **Double-interrogation tally:** 82:17–18 carries the "what will convey to you" formula TWICE — as pre-flagged by the s069 index §5b (rg cross-check agrees).
