# The denied return, the unavoidable descent, and the borrowed honor (19:66–82)
**UID:** 202610141656
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 19:66–82 (Ruku 5 of 6 in Maryam)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[19:66]` – `[19:82]` (17 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The man who mocks "will I be brought back alive?" hears the surah's own oldest answer recycled — "We created you before, when you were nothing" (19:67 ↔ 19:9); the assembly scene follows: every one goes down to Hell by unavoidable decree, the devout rescued, the wrongdoers left kneeling (68–72); the status-argument of the prosperous (73–75) is answered by the istidraj-clause and "Allah increases in guidance" (76); and the bargainer who claims "wealth and children" (77–80) and the people who buy gods "for a source of strength" (81–82) are told what 19:40 already said — Allah inherits, and they come to Him alone.
* **Placement:** Ruku 5 of 6; the sorting section — the eschatology hinged at 19:37–40 now worked out; the resurrection-objection of 19:66 is answered with ruku 1's mercy-argument.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[19:66]`** "And the human being says, “When I am dead, will I be brought back alive?”" / **`[19:67]`** "Does the human being not remember that We created him before, when he was nothing?”"
  * **Gloss:** The surah quotes the audience's own objection, then re-runs Zechariah's proof (19:9 verbatim-in-kind): first-creation answers return-creation; "remember" — the argument is one they already know.
* **`[19:68]`** "By your Lord, We will round them up, and the devils, then We will bring them around Hell, on their knees."
  * **Gloss:** A divine oath — the surah's only "By your Lord"; the roundup includes the devils as co-accused; posture: kneeling, the proud made mobile-poor.
* **`[19:69]`** "Then, out of every sect, We will snatch those most defiant to the Most Merciful."
  * **Gloss:** The draft is by defiance-level, and the measure is "to the Most Merciful" — the crime is ingratitude toward the very name this surah keeps speaking.
* **`[19:70]`** "We are fully aware of those most deserving to scorch in it."
  * **Gloss:** Selection is knowledge-based, not arbitrary — "scorch" (sa'sar) keeps the scene precise.
* **`[19:71]`** "There is not one of you but will go down to it. This has been an unavoidable decree of your Lord."
  * **Gloss:** The terrifying universality ("not one of you" — the first direct address of the passage) with "unavoidable decree" (mustawjarah — a "decree passed down"); classical reading distinguishes approaching/crossing from dwelling — §6.
* **`[19:72]`** "Then We will rescue those who were devout, and leave the wrongdoers in it, on their knees."
  * **Gloss:** The rescue-condition is the 19:63 word ("devout"); the kneeling posture of 19:68 recurs — the frame repeats its image at the verdict.
* **`[19:73]`** "When Our clear revelations are recited to them, those who disbelieve say to those who believe, “Which of the two parties is better in position, and superior in influence?”
  * **Gloss:** Recitation again (↔ 19:58 — the same verb for opposite reactions: weeping vs. flexing); the metric proposed is worldly rank and 'izz ("influence").
* **`[19:74]`** "How many a generation have We destroyed before them, who surpassed them in riches and splendor?"
  * **Gloss:** The graveyard-refrain (repeated verbatim-style at 19:98); riches-and-adab (splendor) as the failed proof of favor.
* **`[19:75]`** "Say, “Whoever is in error, the Most Merciful will lead him on.” Until, when they see what they were promised—either the punishment, or the Hour. Then they will know who was in worse position and weaker in forces."
  * **Gloss:** The surah's hardest mercy-clause: even prolongation-in-error is under the Most Merciful's name — istidraj, not approval; the position-question of 19:73 is answered at the seeing.
* **`[19:76]`** "Allah increases in guidance those who accept guidance. And the things that endure— the righteous deeds—have the best reward with your Lord, and the best outcome."
  * **Gloss:** The counter-law: guidance compounds for the guided; "the things that endure" (baqiya) — righteous deeds as the only durable asset.
* **`[19:77]`** "Have you seen him who denied Our revelations, and said, “I will be given wealth and children”?"
  * **Gloss:** One man's contract-with-the-future: denial of revelation paired with presumption about provision — the phrase "wealth and children" is the Qur'anic cliché of this-worldly abundance (cf. 57:20, 18:39, 71:12).
* **`[19:78]`** "Did he look into the future, or did he receive a promise from the Most Merciful?"
  * **Gloss:** Two sources of certainty mocked: unseen-knowledge or a divine covenant ("a promise... from the Most Merciful") — he has neither; the 'ahd-word links to 19:87's "agreement".
* **`[19:79]`** "No indeed! We will write what he says, and will keep extending the agony for him."
  * **Gloss:** Two verdicts: recording ("We will write") and extension (madd — the same root as 19:84's "countdown" and the istidraj of 19:75).
* **`[19:80]`** "Then We will inherit from him what he speaks of, and he will come to Us alone."
  * **Gloss:** The heir-irony completes the thread begun at 19:5–6 (an heir asked) and 19:40 (Allah inherits); "alone" — the word 19:95 will universalize.
* **`[19:81]`** "And they took, besides Allah, other gods, to be for them a source of strength."
  * **Gloss:** 'Izza — strength/honor as the stated purchase-motive of idolatry; the same word the 19:73 flex uses ("influence").
* **`[19:82]`** "By no means! They will reject their worship of them, and become opponents to them."
  * **Gloss:** The bought strength will disavow the buyers (↔ 10:28 "their partners will say, 'It was not us you were worshiping'") — allies turn adversarial (↔ 41:29-family, verify).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`“when you were nothing”`** `[19:67]`: the ex-nihilo proof.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[19:9]` verbatim to Zechariah — mercy-argument recycled as resurrection-proof; `[36:77–79]` "Who will revive the bones when they have decayed?... He who initiated them in the first instance will revive them" — the same objection-answer pair in Ya-Sin.
* **`go down to it`** `[19:71]`: warid — the corpus renders it "go down to it" (unique; `rg "go down to it" corpus/` — single hit).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[19:72]` rescue-immediately-after; classical reading debates approach vs. crossing vs. dwelling. *Source: classical dispute (Tabari/Ibn Kathir genre on 19:71–72) — agent labeling; verify; the English gives only "go down to it."*
* **`lead him on / extending`** `[19:75–79]`: istidraj — mercy's name on deliberate prolongation of the erring.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[19:76]` its twin-law ("Allah increases in guidance those who accept guidance") — the surah states both directions of compound interest; `[19:84]` "We are counting for them a countdown" — respite as arithmetic again in the closing ruku. *Source: istidraj is the classical term for 19:75's clause — verify.*
* **`a source of strength (ʿizz)`** `[19:81]`: the motive of taken-gods; rendered "influence" at 19:73/75 ("superior in influence", "weaker in forces") — one root, two English words in this edition (observable).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[35:10]` "Whoever desires honor—all honor belongs to Allah" — the same root's corrective twin; `[19:87]` (next ruku) gates intercession behind "an agreement with the Most Merciful".

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Meccan (consensus) — resurrection-mockery and status-flexing fit the Qurayshi interlocutors of the surah's setting.
* **Asbab / narrative context:** 19:71–72 classically cited for "I will pass through Hell with all of you, then pull the muttaqun out before them" — read as crossing-through rather than dwelling for the believers. *Source: transmitted reports (Bukhari-Muslim genre cited in Ibn Kathir on 19:71) — agent labeling; verify; not Quran.* 19:77–79 is classically tied to a named individual (al-'As ibi Wa'il-type reports) — *Source: classical asbab reports — verify; text says only "him who."*
