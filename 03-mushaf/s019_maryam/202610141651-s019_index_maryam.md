## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Meccan — consensus of the classical surah-introduction lists. *Source: transmitted classification — verify in print.* Revelation order **41** in the standard transmitted enumeration. *Source: transmitted nuzul-order tables (the local `data/surah_manifest.json` carries no order number — observable); agent labeling — verify.*
* **Size:** 98 ayahs, 6 ruku (`data/ruku_map.json`: 15+25+10+15+17+16 = 98). Mushaf position 19, between al-Kahf (18) and Ta-Ha (20). The corpus header prints the name as "MARY"; transliteration *Maryam* — the only surah named for a woman, and she is named within it at `[19:16]` ("And mention in the Scripture Mary").
* **Central theme:** Mercy as the surah's grammar. The letters at 19:1 give way immediately to "A mention of the mercy of your Lord" (19:2), and the surah then *defines* mercy narratively: two impossible births (John from a barren old couple; Isa from an untouched virgin), Ibrahim's gentle rupture from his father, a prophets' roll whose common denominator is falling down weeping at recitation, and a closing promise that "the Most Merciful will give them love" (19:96). Observable lexicon: the noun "mercy" occurs 4× (`[19:2]`, `[19:21]`, `[19:50]`, `[19:53]`); the two mercy-names fill 16 ayah-lines ("Most Merciful" ×12, "Most Gracious" ×4 — `rg -o` counts on the corpus file).
* **Theme checks the task asked to verify, honestly (verbatim wording governs):** `[19:1]` "Kaf, Ha, Ya, Ayn, Saad." ✓ muqattaat — see [Fleeting: muqattaat](../../01-fleeting/202610132007-f-muqattaat_letters_untranslated.md); `[19:2]` is "A mention of the mercy of your Lord towards His servant Zechariah" — *not* "A mercy from your Lord" (the phrase "a mercy from Us" is at 19:21 instead); old-age du'a ✓ 19:4/19:8; `[19:7]` "a name We have never given before" ✓ (the "first to bear this name" reading is the classical gloss of this exact clause — *Source: classical tafsir on 19:7 — verify*); cradle-speech ✓ 19:29–34, first word verbatim "I am the servant of Allah" (19:30) — pairs with the [Isa dossier](../../02-knowledge/202610141301-k-isa_quranic_jesus_correction.md), which cites this line; Ibrahim correction: **"if you do not turn, I will withdraw from you" is NOT the text** — the threat is the father's ("If you do not desist, I will stone you," 19:46) and Ibrahim answers with peace, a promise to pray forgiveness, *then* withdrawal (19:47–48) — the gentleness is real, the quotation is not; the roll ✓ Musa/Harun/Isma'il + "Enoch" (Itani's rendering of Idris, same at 21:85) at 19:51–58, "These are some of the prophets Allah has blessed" ✓ verbatim 19:58; `[19:96]` verbatim "the Most Merciful will give them love" (not "make them loved"); **"nothing but the speech of the Most Merciful is worth hearing" does not exist in this surah or (by search) the corpus** — nearest are 19:97 "We made it easy in your tongue," 19:98 "hear from them the slightest whisper?" (a *negative* — silence of destroyed generations), and 20:108 "Voices will be hushed before the Merciful, and you will hear nothing but murmur" — probable source of the conflation.

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Mercy narrated (1–40):** two annunciation-births as a matched pair — Zechariah/Mary-the-barren-wife and Mary/untouched-virgin; both fathers-of-no-story ask "how?" (19:8, 19:20) and both get "It is easy for Me" (19:9, 19:21). The ruku-2 close (37–40) hinges the surah toward eschatology: factions, the Day of Regret, divine inheritance.
* **The father motif inverted (41–50):** Ibrahim hears the threat and answers with salam; withdrawal (19:48) is met not with loss but with Isaac, Jacob, prophethood, and "a noble reputation of truth" (19:49–50).
* **The roll and its posture (51–65):** "And mention in the Scripture" four times; the census line 19:58 ("whenever the revelations of the Most Gracious are recited to them, they would fall down, prostrating and weeping") is the surah's definition of a prophet; 19:64–65 answers the "when" of revelation — angels descend only by command, "Your Lord is never forgetful."
* **Sorting the impatient (66–82):** resurrection mocked (66–67), the roundup on knees (68–72), the status-argument (73–76), the man who buys a future of "wealth and children" (77–80), gods taken as "a source of strength" (81–82).
* **The crack and the cure (83–98):** devils incited (83–84), guests and animals parted (85–86), intercession gated by "an agreement with the Most Merciful" (87); the son-claim (88) makes the heavens almost rupture (90), answered by universal 'ubudiyyah (93–95), love (96), the made-easy book (97), and the vanished generations no one can hear (98).
* **Running threads:** *servant* 19:2 → 19:30 → 19:93; *peace* 19:15 → 19:33 → 19:47 → 19:62; *easy* 19:9 → 19:21 → 19:97; *inherit* 19:6 → 19:40 → 19:80; *withdraw/seclude* 19:16/22 → 19:48/49; the destroyed-generation refrain 19:74 → 19:98.
