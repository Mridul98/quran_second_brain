## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Meccan — unanimous in the classical lists. *Source: transmitted classification (al-Itqan-type surah introductions) — verify in print.* **Revelation order:** the task assigns **45** "per the classical lists" — flagged: the standard transmitted enumeration used elsewhere in this brain (Yunus 51, Hud 52, Yusuf 53, al-Hijr 54, an-Nahl 70; Ibn-Abbas-derived tables) seats al-Anbiya at **73**, late-Meccan (between Ibrahim and al-Mu'minun in that table's neighborhood), while **45** is that same table's ordinal for Ta-Ha (20). *Source: classical nuzul-order tables (Tanzil/Wikishia-type, checked 2026-10-14) — agent labeling; discrepancy reported to orchestrator; the corpus itself carries no ordering statement.*
* **Size:** 112 ayahs, 7 ruku (per `data/ruku_map.json`: 10+19+12+9+25+18+19 = 112). Mushaf position 21, between Ta-Ha (20) and al-Hajj (22); the surah opens Juz 17 per the transmitted division (— verify).
* **Name:** al-Anbiya — "THE PROPHETS" (corpus header). The name-word occurs nowhere in the surah's own verses (`rg -c -i "prophet" corpus/s021_al-anbiya.md` → 1 hit, the header line); the title names the roll-call of `[21:48–93]`, where a chain of prophets is recited without ever being called "prophets." *Source: transmitted naming + observable corpus fact.*
* **Central theme:** the prophet-cycle compressed to a roll-call. The surah brackets two nearings — "Mankind's reckoning has drawn near, but they turn away heedlessly" (`[21:1]`) and "The promise of truth has drawn near" (`[21:97]`) — and fills the space with one argument: the same human messengers (`[21:7–8]`), the same single creed every messenger carried — "There is no god but I, so worship Me" (`[21:25]`) — backed by creation's signs (`[21:30–33]`). Then Ibrahim, Lot, Noah, David-and-Solomon, Job, Ishmael/Enoch/Ezekiel, Jonah, Zechariah, the virgin and her son: each unit closed by an answered call — "Thus We save the faithful" (`[21:88]`) — resolving into "We did not send you except as mercy to mankind" (`[21:107]`), the book's last word being a du'a: "My Lord, judge with justice" (`[21:112]`).
* **Task anchors verified against the corpus:** `[21:1–3]` reckoning drawn near / heedless ✓; `[21:30]` carries **both** "We made from water every living thing" and the "one mass… tore them apart" cosmogony — same verse ✓; fire pair: `[21:68]` is the crowd's verdict ("Burn him and support your gods"), **not** a "fuel" line — "fuel for Hell" is `[21:98]` (twins `[2:24]`, `[3:10]`, `[66:6]`); `[21:69]` "coolness and safety" ✓ (unique wording in the corpus); Noah: `[21:77]` reads "so We drowned them all" — the "no dweller" prayer is Noah's own at `[71:26]`, not 21:77; `[21:87]` Jonah's darkness-du'a ✓; `[21:105]` "written in the Psalms, after the Reminder… inherited by My righteous servants" ✓; `[21:16]` "for amusement" ✓ — true twin `[44:38]` ("to play"), plus thematic twin `[38:27]` ("in vain"); the suggested `[46:30]` is the jinn-Scripture scene, **not** a twin; **ending:** no "O servant of mine" address occurs — `[21:112]` reads "He said, “My Lord, judge with justice.” And, “Our Lord is the Gracious, Whose help is sought against what you allege.”" (nearest: "My righteous servants," `[21:105]`).

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Indictment and epistemics (1–10):** near reckoning, heedless hearts, the "mortal like you" charge, the four accusations — answered by the human-messenger axiom and the command to ask the people of knowledge; the ruku lands on the sent-down Book "containing your message."
* **Creed spine (11–29):** history (crushed towns → silent ashes), cosmology (not created for amusement), the tireless heavenly court, the two interrogations of earth-born gods — then the surah's thesis verse: one creed, all messengers (25), sealed with the intercession protocol and the boastful-god threat (28–29).
* **Signs and mortality (30–41):** ratq split, life from water, stabilizers, the protected ceiling, swimming orbits — then no immortality, death tasted for everyone, adversity/prosperity as test, ridicule, haste, "when?", and the sudden encircling of the mockers.
* **The gods fail (42–50):** who guards you at night and day? gods who cannot defend themselves; the land gradually reduced; the deaf; the woe-cry; the mustard-seed scales; Scripture-chain (Moses-Aaron's Criterion → "This too is a blessed message").
* **The Ibrahim exemplar (51–75):** integrity given, statues shamed by their silence, the pyre reversed into coolness — rescue into the blessed land, leaders guiding by God's command, prayer and charity; Lot's judgment and mercy (the pattern already asserted at 21:9).
* **The roll-call (76–93):** nine answered units in 18 verses — Noah, David-Solomon, Job, the steadfast three, Jonah in the darkness, Zechariah's secret call, the virgin's son — snapped shut by the ecclesiology verse: one community; they splintered.
* **Endgame and mercy (94–112):** records, the ban on return, Gog and Magog, the third woe; worshippers and gods as one fuel, the faithful deaf to its hissing; heaven folded like a book; the Psalms-decree; mercy to mankind; tawhid restated as the answer to the opening "mortal like you"; the closing du'a closes the nearness bracket.
