# The one mass torn apart — and every living thing made of water (21:30–41)
**UID:** 202610141674
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 21:30–41 (Ruku 3 of 7 in al-Anbiya)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[21:30]` – `[21:41]` (12 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Four cosmological signs — the joined mass torn apart, life made from water, earth stabilized with passages for guidance, the sky a protected ceiling, night/day/sun/moon each swimming an orbit — pivot the surah from creed-argument to creation-evidence; then mortality is handed to the mockers: no human gets immortality, every soul tastes death, adversity and prosperity are the test, and the "when?" they keep asking will arrive suddenly and bewilder them, encircling them as mockery encircled every mocker before.
* **Placement:** Ruku 3 of 7; the signs-block between the creed-spine (ruku 2) and the failure of the gods (ruku 4). Its double "Will they not..." (`[21:30]`) re-poses 21:6's question in cosmological key.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[21:30]`** "Do the disbelievers not see that the heavens and the earth were one mass, and We tore them apart? And We made from water every living thing. Will they not believe?"
  * **Gloss:** One verse, two origins: cosmogony (ratq → torn apart) and biology (water → every living thing); the twin claims of the task anchor both live here ✓.
* **`[21:31]`** "And We placed on earth stabilizers, lest it sways with them, and We placed therein signposts and passages, that they may be guided."
  * **Gloss:** Mountains as anchors, roads as signage — the earth made legible for guidance; "signposts" ↔ `[16:16]` "landmarks."
* **`[21:32]`** "And We made the sky a protected ceiling; yet they turn away from its wonders."
  * **Gloss:** The sky as mahfuz (kept/guarded) — cf. `[2:22]` "the sky a structure," `[52:5]` "the elevated roof"; wonder-avoidance as the real disease.
* **`[21:33]`** "It is He who created the night and the day, and the sun and the moon; each floating in an orbit."
  * **Gloss:** The astral procession — verbatim-grade twin `[36:40]` "Each floats in an orbit"; time itself is created furniture.
* **`[21:34]`** "We did not grant immortality to any human being before you. Should you die, are they then the immortal?"
  * **Gloss:** The messenger's death is not an anomaly (echoes `[21:8]` "nor were they immortal"); the question shatters the wish for a permanent leader — or permanent religion-by-personality.
* **`[21:35]`** "Every soul will taste death. We burden you with adversity and prosperity—a test. And to Us you will be returned."
  * **Gloss:** Near-verbatim twin `[29:57]` ("Every soul will taste death. Then to Us you will be returned"); also `[3:185]`. Death, the test-seesaw (fitna by bad and good), and the return — three facts in one line.
* **`[21:36]`** "When those who disbelieve see you, they treat you only with ridicule: “Is this the one who mentions your gods?” And they reject the mention of the Merciful."
  * **Gloss:** The mockery resumes the prosecution (cf. `[21:3]`); the second clause is the ruku's diagnosis: their disease is inability to tolerate dhikr — mention — of the Merciful at all.
* **`[21:37]`** "The human being was created of haste. I will show you My signs, so do not seek to rush Me."
  * **Gloss:** Huin as anthropology ("created of haste" ↔ `[17:11]` "very hasty" — the same root, drifted rendering); the sign-demand of `[21:5]`/`[21:38]` is re-framed as impatience with God's clock (↔ `[16:1]` "so do not rush it").
* **`[21:38]`** "And they say, “When will this promise come true, if you are truthful?”"
  * **Gloss:** Near-verbatim twin `[67:25]` "When will this promise be fulfilled, if you are truthful?" — the stock Meccan refrain.
* **`[21:39]`** "If those who disbelieve only knew, when they cannot keep the fire off their faces and off their backs, and they will not be helped."
  * **Gloss:** An uncompleted wish-clause ("only knew...") — the knowledge they lack is phenomenological: fire from every angle, no shield, no help.
* **`[21:40]`** "In fact, it will come upon them suddenly, and bewilder them. They will not be able to repel it, and they will not be reprieved."
  * **Gloss:** Suddenness (baghta) — ↔ `[43:66]` "Are they only waiting for the Hour to come upon them suddenly, while they are unaware?"; three denials: cannot repel, cannot defer.
* **`[21:41]`** "Messengers before you were also ridiculed, but those who jeered were surrounded by what they had ridiculed."
  * **Gloss:** Near-verbatim twin `[6:10]` — the mockery that ends encircling the mocker; the ruku closes the sign-demand with its historical verdict, re-using the precedent-logic of `[21:6]`.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`ratq / fatq` (one mass... tore apart)** `[21:30]`:
  * **Rendering:** "the heavens and the earth were one mass, and We tore them apart" — "one mass" is unique in the corpus (`rg "one mass" corpus/` → 21:30 only, observed).
  * **Elsewhere:** the cosmogony's two halves pair with the water-life line in the same verse; `[24:45]` "Allah created every living creature from water" and `[25:54]` "from fluid, created the human being" are the biological twins; `[2:164]` runs the water→revived-earth sign-list this ruku abbreviates.
  * *Source: classical reading "joined then separated" (reports at Ibn Kathir/Tabari on 21:30) — agent labeling; verify.*
* **`every soul will taste death`** `[21:35]`:
  * **Elsewhere:** `[29:57]` near-verbatim; `[3:185]` "Every soul will have a taste of death"; the reversal at `[44:56]` — the saved "will not taste death, beyond the first death."
* **`mahfuz ceiling / orbit`** `[21:32–33]`:
  * **Rendering:** "a protected ceiling" (unique in corpus); "each floating in an orbit."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:22]` sky-as-structure; `[52:5]` elevated roof; `[36:40]` swimming orbit — the sky is architecture and machinery, never object of worship.
* **`huin (haste)`** `[21:37]`:
  * **Rendering:** "created of haste" (the adjective huun used as substance-noun).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[17:11]` "The human being is very hasty" — same word-family, drifted rendering; haste is the anthropology behind the "when?" of 21:38.
