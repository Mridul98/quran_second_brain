# The town that ate its own security (16:111–119)
**UID:** 202610141606
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 16:111–119 (Ruku 15 of 16 in an-Nahl)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[16:111]` – `[16:119]` (9 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Day-of-solo-pleading opens the ruku (111), answered by the town-parable: secure, peaceful, livelihood arriving from every direction — then unappreciative of Allah's blessings, made to taste "the robe of hunger and fear" (112), with a messenger from among them denounced in the midst (113); the moral then cashes into table-law — eat lawful and good, thank the blessings, if Allah is the one you serve (114); the forbidden list with its necessity-clause (115); the warning against tongue-made law ("This is lawful, and this is unlawful," 116–117); the Jewish prohibitions as penal history, not arbitrary divine taste (118); and the universal door: wrong in ignorance, then repent and reform — "your Lord thereafter is Forgiving and Merciful" (119).
* **Placement:** The application ruku of the surah's gratitude-doctrine: the town shows what ingratitude costs collectively; 16:114–119 then legislate the eater's conscience — closing the 16:35 "prohibited anything besides His prohibitions" loophole with a concrete table-law.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[16:111]`** "On the Day when every soul will come pleading for itself, and every soul will be paid in full for what it has done, and they will not be wronged."
  * **Gloss:** Solo-representation justice: advocacy dead (cf. 2:48/2:123 "no intercession"), full payment, zero wrong — the courtroom of ruku 12 without its witnesses' luxury.
* **`[16:112]`** "And Allah cites the example of a town that was secure and peaceful, with its livelihood coming to it abundantly from every direction. But then it turned unappreciative of Allah’s blessings, so Allah made it taste the robe of hunger and fear, because of what they used to craft."
  * **Gloss:** The corpus's most compact security-economics parable: aman + risq from every taraf → kufr al-ni'ma → the twin robe (joub of hunger and fear) — what God clothed them in, their ingratitude tailored (cf. the protective garments of 16:81 — same clothing-metaphor polarity).
* **`[16:113]`** "A messenger from among them had come to them, but they denounced him, so the punishment seized them in the midst of their wrongdoing."
  * **Gloss:** The town's ingratitude had an epistemic cause: they rejected their own insider messenger — the 16:36 pattern (messenger to every community) narrated in miniature.
* **`[16:114]`** "Eat of the lawful and good things Allah has provided for you, and be thankful for Allah’s blessings, if it is Him that you serve."
  * **Gloss:** The table-command: halalan tayyiban + shukr + the service-test ("if it is Him that you serve") — provision and worship explicitly welded.
* **`[16:115]`** "He has forbidden you carrion, and blood, and the flesh of swine, and anything consecrated to other than Allah. But if anyone is compelled by necessity, without being deliberate or malicious, then Allah is Forgiving and Merciful."
  * **Gloss:** The four-item list — identical core to 2:173 and 6:145; necessity exits the list with a condition-set (not deliberate, not malicious).
* **`[16:116]`** "And do not say of falsehood asserted by your tongues, “This is lawful, and this is unlawful,” in order to invent lies and attribute them to Allah. Those who invent lies and attribute them to Allah will not succeed."
  * **Gloss:** The anti-invention clause: tongue-legislation against God is iftira' — the same verdict word as 16:56/105; dietary claims are now under the authorship-indictment law.
* **`[16:117]`** "A brief enjoyment—then they will have a painful punishment."
  * **Gloss:** The lie's profit-margin: mata' qalil — short enjoyment, then the torment — 16:96's runs-out/keeps economics in miniature.
* **`[16:118]`** "For those who are Jews, We have prohibited what We related to you before. We did not wrong them, but they used to wrong their own selves."
  * **Gloss:** The prior precedent: those extra prohibitions were penal, not arbitrary ("what We related to you before" → 6:146), and self-wrong explains the tightening.
* **`[16:119]`** "But towards those who do wrongs in ignorance, and then repent afterwards and reform, your Lord thereafter is Forgiving and Merciful."
  * **Gloss:** The ignorance-clause: wrong done bi-jahala, then tawba + islah → the same " thereafter" mercy-formula (16:110) — the door left open for the tongue-legislators.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`robe (libas) of hunger and fear`** `[16:112]`:
  * **Rendering:** "Allah made it taste the robe of hunger and fear" — hunger and fear tailored as clothing.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[16:81]` "garments to protect you from the heat, and garments to protect you from your violence" — the blessings were also libas-family (sarabil); the robe-idiom pairs taste×clothing uniquely here; `[34:19]` the Saba town "made history, and We scattered them in every direction" — the parable's strongest candidate referent in classical reading. *Source: Saba identification is one classical opinion among several (others read Mecca) — verify.*
* **`pleading for itself`** `[16:111]`:
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:48]` "no soul will avail another in the least, nor will any intercession be accepted on its behalf"; `[2:123]` same formula — the Day's advocacy-ban; ironic sibling: `[11:74]` Abraham "started pleading with Us concerning the people of Lot" — intercession permitted to prophets in narrative, banned for all on the Day.
* **`lawful and good (halal tayyib)`** `[16:114]`:
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:168]` "Eat of what is lawful and good on earth, and do not follow the footsteps of Satan"; `[5:88]` "Eat of the lawful and good things Allah has provided for you; and be conscious of Allah" — the same three-beat command (eat / be conscious / no Satan-steps).
* **`invent lies and attribute them to Allah`** `[16:116]`:
  * **Elsewhere:** `[6:144]` "Who does greater wrong than he who invents lies and attribute them to Allah, in order to mislead people without knowledge?" — verbatim twin, in an-An'am's taboo-invention context (6:143–144), which is exactly 16:35's accusation; `[6:93]` the invention-of-revelation statute.
