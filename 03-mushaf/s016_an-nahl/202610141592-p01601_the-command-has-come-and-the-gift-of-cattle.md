# The command has come — and cattle are counted as mercy (16:1–9)
**UID:** 202610141592
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 16:1–9 (Ruku 1 of 16 in an-Nahl)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[16:1]` – `[16:9]` (9 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah opens with the Hour already at the door ("do not rush it"), restates the creed in one breath (no god but Me), then — instead of argument — lays out the inventory: human, livestock, horses, mules — warmth, beauty, cargo-hauling, luxury — as the evidence that the Creator is "Clement and Merciful," closing on God's right to point out the paths while leaving the flaw in them human.
* **Placement:** Ruku 1 of 16; the opening pair of the surah's two moves — command/creed (1–4) and provision-inventory (5–8) — that the whole surah will elaborate (ruku 2 completes the inventory; ruku 7–11 run the gratitude audit).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[16:1]`** "The command of Allah has come, so do not rush it. Glory be to Him; exalted above what they associate."
  * **Gloss:** The Hour's onset is stated as fait accompli ("has come"); the disbelievers' impatience (a Meccan motif) is answered with transcendence — tanzih brackets the surah's first word.
* **`[16:2]`** "He sends down the angels with the Spirit by His command, upon whom He wills of His servants: “Give warning that there is no god but Me, and fear Me.”"
  * **Gloss:** Revelation is an commissioned descent: angels "with the Spirit"; the delivered message is a single-line creed plus fear. Selection is "upon whom He wills" — initiative stays divine.
* **`[16:3]`** "He created the heavens and the earth with justice. He is exalted above the associations they attribute."
  * **Gloss:** Cosmology as verdict: creation is bi-l-haqq, "with justice"; the shirk charge is repeated — the bookend of 16:1.
* **`[16:4]`** "He created the human being from a drop of fluid, yet he becomes an open adversary."
  * **Gloss:** Anthropology by proportion: a nubfa from a drop to the Qurayshi's courtroom argument against resurrection — man is "mubin," openly contentious.
* **`[16:5]`** "And the livestock—He created them for you. In them are warmth and benefits for you, and of them you eat."
  * **Gloss:** The inventory begins: an'am (camels, cattle, sheep, goats) created "for you"; three uses — daf' (warmth/pelts), benefits, food. For the desert listener, this is the primary GDP.
* **`[16:6]`** "And there is beauty in them for you, when you bring them home, and when you drive them to pasture."
  * **Gloss:** Aesthetic utility: joy at the herd's return at dusk and its drive to pasture — provision includes pleasure, not just calories.
* **`[16:7]`** "And they carry your loads to territory you could not have reached without great hardship. Your Lord is Clement and Merciful."
  * **Gloss:** Logistics as mercy: the camel reaches the unreachable "without great hardship" (shafaqa); the verse closes with ra'uf rahim — "Clement and Merciful," the pair that names the inventory's giver.
* **`[16:8]`** "And the horses, and the mules, and the donkeys—for you to ride, and for luxury. And He creates what you do not know."
  * **Gloss:** Ride-animal triad with a candid catch-all: "for luxury" (zinsha) — ornament is not ashamed of itself; "and He creates what you do not know" keeps the inventory open-ended.
* **`[16:9]`** "It is for Allah to point out the paths, but some of them are flawed. Had He willed, He could have guided you all."
  * **Gloss:** After the physical inventory, the road-inventory: guidance is Allah's address; crooked paths are real; compulsion is declined — the surah's freedom-with-risk axiom, pre-loading 16:93 and 16:125.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`an-Nahl / daf' (warmth)`** `[16:5]`:
  * **Rendering:** "In them are warmth and benefits" — Itani takes daf'un as the warmth of pelts/fleece. *Source: classical variant — al-Tabari's collection records readers taking daf' as benefit/repelling and a minority reading (haf'un, "carrying") — agent reconstruction; verify in print.*
  * **Elsewhere:** the word-form is unique here in the corpus (`rg "warmth" corpus/` — only 16:5).
* **`ruḥ (Spirit)`** `[16:2]`:
  * **Rendering:** "the angels with the Spirit" — comitative; the Spirit accompanies the angelic descent.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[40:15]` "He conveys the Spirit, by His command, upon whomever He wills of His servants" — near-verbatim twin; `[97:4]` "the angels and the Spirit" descend; `[16:102]` "The Holy Spirit has brought it down" (same surah, ruku 14); `[2:87]` Jesus "supported... with the Holy Spirit." Whether Ruh = Gabriel or revelation is classically disputed. *Source: dispute reported in the classical commentaries (also 17:85 declines the answer: "The Spirit belongs to the domain of my Lord") — agent inference, verify.*
* **`Clement and Merciful (ra'uf / rahim)`** `[16:7]`:
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:263]` "Allah is Rich and Clement"; `[24:20]` "Allah is Clement and Merciful"; `[59:10]` same pair — a stable mercyn-name pair in the corpus.
* **`the paths`** `[16:9]`:
  * **Rendering:** "It is for Allah to point out the paths, but some of them are flawed."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[16:69]` "go along the pathways of your Lord" (the bee's tracked paths — same al-alfab, same idiom, inside this surah); `[6:161]`/`[1:6]` for the single straight way. Guidance link below.
