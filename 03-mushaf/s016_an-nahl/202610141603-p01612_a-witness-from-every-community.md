# A witness from every community — and partners who call their worshippers liars (16:84–89)
**UID:** 202610141603
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 16:84–89 (Ruku 12 of 16 in an-Nahl)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[16:84]` – `[16:89]` (6 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The courtroom ruku: a witness is raised from every community (84) — no permission, no excuse for the disbelievers; punishment grants no relief or reprieve (85); the idolaters' "associates" answer their own advocates with "Surely you are liars" (86); late submission is offered and the invented gods abandon their inventors (87); those who disbelieve and bar others from the path get punishment stacked on punishment (88); and the scene resolves into 16:89's twin-of-4:41 — this Messenger made witness against this community, and the Book revealed "as an explanation of all things, and guidance, and mercy and good news for those who submit."
* **Placement:** Ruku 12 is the surah's forensic center — the witnesses (prophets, communities, the final Messenger, the Book) all testify, while the false partners perjure nothing but recriminate; the evidence table then turns to law in ruku 13.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[16:84]`** "On the Day when We raise up a witness from every community—those who disbelieved will not be permitted, nor will they be excused."
  * **Gloss:** Every ummah gets its own witness (its messenger, cf. 16:36); the defendants lose both procedural motions: not permitted (to speak), not excused (to plead).
* **`[16:85]`** "When those who did wrong see the punishment, it will not be lightened for them, nor will they be reprieved."
  * **Gloss:** No mitigation of sentence, no extension of term — the respite-policy (16:61) has expired.
* **`[16:86]`** "And when the idolaters see their associates, they will say, “Our Lord, these are our associates whom we used to invoke besides You.” They will strike back at them with the saying, “Surely you are liars.”"
  * **Gloss:** The advocacy turns hostile: the invoked ones deny being invoked — "They will strike back... 'Surely you are liars'" — the partners' final function is contradiction.
* **`[16:87]`** "On that Day they will offer their submission to Allah, and what they had invented will abandon them."
  * **Gloss:** Mass islām at the moment it no longer counts; the inventors are abandoned by their inventions — the 16:56/16:105 "invention" family ends in desertion.
* **`[16:88]`** "Those who disbelieve and obstruct from Allah’s path—We will add punishment to their punishment, on account of the mischief they used to make."
  * **Gloss:** Compounded sentence for barrier-crime — disbelief plus deterring others (cf. 16:94's oath-hindrance; 11:19's "those who hinder others from the path of Allah").
* **`[16:89]`** "On the Day when We raise in every community a witness against them, from among them, and bring you as a witness against these. We have revealed to you the Book, as an explanation of all things, and guidance, and mercy and good news for those who submit."
  * **Gloss:** The near-verbatim twin of `[4:41]` — with this surah's addition: the Book itself is the exhibit, tibyanan li-kulli shay'; the four-function formula (explanation, guidance, mercy, glad-news) closes the courtroom with the evidence already on file.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`witness (shahid)`** `[16:84`, `16:89]`:
  * **Rendering:** "a witness from every community" / "bring you as a witness against these."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[4:41]` "when We bring a witness from every community, and We bring you as a witness against these" — the verbatim twin; `[5:117]` Jesus: "I was a witness over them while I was among them"; `[2:143]` "that you may be witnesses to humanity" — the community's own witness-office.
* **`associates (shuraka')`** `[16:86]`:
  * **Elsewhere:** `[46:6]` "And when humanity is gathered, they will be enemies to them, and will renounce their worship of them" — the same betrayal-of-partners verdict from al-Ahqaf; cf. `[16:27]` (ruku 4) where the associates are simply absent at the roll-call.
* **`offer their submission (aslama)`** `[16:87]`:
  * **Elsewhere:** `[16:81]` "so that you may submit" — the voluntary form; `[16:89]` "good news for those who submit" — the saved category; 16:87 is submission coerced by sight, the surah's own counter-definition of islām-as-surrender-before-seeing.
* **`obstruct from Allah's path`** `[16:88`]:
  * **Elsewhere:** `[11:19]` "Those who hinder others from the path of Allah, and seek to make it crooked" — the Hud twin with the added crookedness motif; `[16:94]` "you hindered from Allah's path" — in-surah echo (the oath-breakers' same crime).
