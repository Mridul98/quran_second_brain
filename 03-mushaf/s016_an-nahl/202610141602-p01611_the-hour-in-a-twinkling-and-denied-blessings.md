# The Hour in a twinkling, and blessings recognized then denied (16:77–83)
**UID:** 202610141602
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 16:77–83 (Ruku 11 of 16 in an-Nahl)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[16:77]` – `[16:83]` (7 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The unseen is Allah's, and the Hour's coming is "only as the twinkling of the eye, or even nearer" (77); against that brevity stands the full inventory of gifts — birth-ignorance overcome by hearing, eyesight, and brains (78), birds sustained mid-sky (79), homes, hide-tents, wool and fur (80), shade, mountain refuges, and two registers of garments (81) — "thus He completes His blessings upon you, so that you may submit"; the Messenger's office is only clear communication (82); and the audit closes with the damning one-liner: they recognize Allah's blessing, but then deny it (83).
* **Placement:** Ruku 11 is the second half of the gratitude-audit (paired with ruku 2's inventory and ruku 9's dispensary) and — task-verification honestly recorded — the surah's Hour-verse: 16:77 does NOT say "the Hour will surely come" (that phrasing belongs to `[40:59]` "Indeed, the Hour is coming; there is no doubt about it" and `[34:3]`-type contexts); its claim is imminence-as-brevity, which is the warrant for the patience ethic of 16:96 and 126–128.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[16:77]`** "To Allah belongs the unseen of the heavens and the earth. The coming of the Hour is only as the twinkling of the eye, or even nearer. Allah has power over everything."
  * **Gloss:** Ownership of the ghayb grounds the Hour's suddenness; "or even nearer" tightens the blink; omnipotence makes the timing credible.
* **`[16:78]`** "Allah brought you out of your mothers’ wombs, not knowing anything; and He gave you the hearing, and the eyesight, and the brains; that you may give thanks."
  * **Gloss:** The birth-argument: zero knowledge at entry, the senses installed as gifts; purpose clause: shukr — the tools themselves are addressed to gratitude.
* **`[16:79]`** "Have they not seen the birds, flying in the midst of the sky? None sustains them except Allah. In this are signs for people who believe."
  * **Gloss:** The suspended-birds sign (cf. 67:19's held-up birds) — flight as visible tawhid; audience: "who believe."
* **`[16:80]`** "And Allah has given you in your homes habitats for you, and has provided for you out of the hides of livestock portable homes for you, so you can use them when you travel, and when you camp; and from their wool, and fur, and hair, furnishings and comfort for a while."
  * **Gloss:** Shelter census: settled homes, tents (portable homes), and the fiber triad (wool/fur/hair) — "comfort for a while" marks the worldly term.
* **`[16:81]`** "And Allah has made for you shade out of what He has created, and has given you resorts in the mountains, and has given you garments to protect you from the heat, and garments to protect you from your violence. Thus He completes His blessings upon you, so that you may submit."
  * **Gloss:** Climate kit: shade, mountain refuges, thermal garments, and armor-against-"your violence" — the surah's physical-comfort list; purpose: "so that you may submit" (uslim).
* **`[16:82]`** "But if they turn away, your only duty is clear communication."
  * **Gloss:** The office-limit refrain (16:35) restated as closure of the gift-list — gratitude cannot be conscripted.
* **`[16:83]`** "They recognize Allah’s blessing, but then deny it, as most of them are ungrateful."
  * **Gloss:** The verdict sentence of the whole shukr-dossier: ma'rifa (recognition) followed by inkar (denial) — not ignorance; aktharuhum — "most of them."

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`the unseen (ghayb)`** `[16:77]`:
  * **Elsewhere:** `[6:59]` "With Him are the keys of the unseen; none knows them except He"; `[34:3]` "He is the Knower of the unseen. Not an atom's weight… escapes His knowledge"; `[2:3]` "believe in the unseen" — the believer's first mark. An-Nahl uses ghayb to price the Hour, not to speculate.
* **`twinkling (lamḥ)`** `[16:77]`:
  * **Elsewhere:** `[54:50]` "Our command is but once, like the twinkling of an eye" — the only other use of the idiom in the corpus; both attach it to command-execution speed.
* **`hearing, eyesight, brains (sam' / absar / af'idah)`** `[16:78]`:
  * **Elsewhere:** `[32:9]` "He gave you the hearing, and the eyesight, and the brains—but rarely do you give thanks"; `[23:78]` "the hearing, and the eyesight, and the feelings. But little gratitude you show" — the organ-triad is a fixed gratitude formula (three surahs, one list).
* **`ungrateful (kafur)`** `[16:83]`:
  * **Elsewhere:** `[14:7]` "If you give thanks, I will grant you increase; but if you are ungrateful, My punishment is severe"; `[14:34]` "the human being is unfair and ungrateful"; cf. 16:55 "to show ingratitude for what We have given them" — kufr here stays in its blessing-register meaning, distinct from its creed-register use.
