## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Meccan — consensus of the classical lists. *Source: transmitted classification (al-Itqan-type surah-introductions; no strong dissent recorded) — verify in print.* Revelation order **70** in the Ibn-Abbas-derived enumeration. *Source: transmitted nuzul-order tables (Tanzil-type lists; s013 index used the same convention) — verify.*
* **Size:** 128 ayahs, 16 ruku (per `data/ruku_map.json`: 9+12+4+9+6+10+10+5+5+6+7+6+11+10+9+9 = 128). Mushaf position 16, between al-Hijr (15) and al-Isra (17).
* **Name:** an-Nahl — Arabic plural, "The Bees"; the corpus header prints the singular "THE BEE" (an observable of this edition), and the name-word occurs in the text only at `[16:68]` (`rg -n "the bee" corpus/` — single line-hit; watch for substring noise in a bare `"bee"` search). 16:49–50 is the prostration site in the transmitted recitation-practice lists. *Source: transmitted tilawah-practice lists — verify.*
* **Central theme:** The surah of blessings and gratitude. It reads like a provision audit: cattle (`[16:5–8]`), rain→drink→grain→olives→palms→grapes (`[16:10–11]`), sea-flesh and ornaments and ships (`[16:14]`), mountains, roads, landmarks, stars (`[16:15–16]`), milk from between waste and blood (`[16:66]`), the bee's multicolored healing drink (`[16:69]`), homes, hides, shade, garments (`[16:80–81]`) — capped by the ledger-line: "if you tried to enumerate the favors of Allah, you will not be able to count them" (`[16:18]`; cf. `[14:34]`). The proper response is shukr, and the verdict on the population is that they "recognize Allah's blessing, but then deny it" (`[16:83]`). The cosmos-as-creed inventory works exactly like an-An'am's (`[6:99]`, `[6:97]`), and the same "Is He who creates like him who does not create?" pivot (`[16:17]`) closes idols out of the inventory.
* **Theme checks the task asked to verify honestly (all confirmed against the corpus, with wording notes):** `[16:5–8]` cattle/warmth/eating ✓ (16:5 "In them are warmth and benefits for you, and of them you eat"); `[16:10–13]` rain-and-olive-and-provision cycle ✓ (bees themselves are ruku 9, 16:68–69); "they attribute to Allah what they dislike" — the *daughters* verse is `[16:57]`, the general "what they themselves dislike" is `[16:62]`; oaths-as-bargain — `[16:92]` says "do not use your oaths as means of deception" and the *trade* clause is `[16:95]` "do not exchange Allah's covenant for a small price"; `[16:125]` "Invite to the way of your Lord with wisdom" ✓; the Hour: `[16:77]` does NOT use "will surely come" — it says the Hour's coming is "only as the twinkling of the eye, or even nearer" ✓ verified as such; `[16:61]` "If Allah were to hold mankind for their injustices, He would not leave upon it a single creature" ✓ (respite, not punishment).

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Command → cosmos (1–21):** the Hour is at hand (1) and the creed is one line (2); then the inventory: human, cattle, horses (4–8), water, harvest, stars, sea, mountains, landmarks (10–16), ending where an-An'am ends — the Creator vs. the un-creators (17–21), with the uncountable-favors ledger (18) as the hinge.
* **Sorting and the two deaths (22–34):** "Your God is one God" (22) sorts to the arrogant "Legends of the ancients" (24); the schemers' roof caves in (26); the dying propose peace (28) while the pious are greeted with "Peace" (32) — the surah keeps pairing the two take-aways.
* **Excuses answered (35–50):** fatalism ("had Allah willed," 35 ↔ 6:148) vs. universal messenger-ship (36); oaths denying resurrection (38) vs. "Be, and it becomes" (40); then emigrants and inspired men (41–44), the three seizures (45–47), and all creation bowing in its shadows (48–50).
* **Ingratitude anatomy (51–65):** one God (51–52); blessing→groaning→partnering→ingratitude cycle (53–56); the daughters contradiction (57–62, verbatim check in §1); respite, not exemption (61); Scripture as clarifier (64).
* **Provision lessons, parables, witnesses (66–89):** milk, dates, honey (66–69); unequal rizq and two parables of the powerless (71–76); the twinkling Hour and the blessings inventory (77–81); "they recognize Allah's blessing, but then deny it" (83); the witness-from-every-community pair (84, 89).
* **Law, oaths, and the good life (90–110):** the triad-command (90); covenant/oaths block — 91–95, the surah's covenant spine; patience's payoff and "a good life" (96–97); refuge before recitation (98–100); the substitution-charge, the foreign-tongue objection (101–103); apostasy-under-compulsion and the sealed hearts (106–108), rescued by 110's emigrant clause.
* **The town and the man (111–128):** the secure-town parable (112–113); eat lawful-and-good and be thankful (114–119); Abraham named ummah — devoted, monotheist, grateful (120–123); the summons with wisdom (125), retaliation capped by patience (126–128).
