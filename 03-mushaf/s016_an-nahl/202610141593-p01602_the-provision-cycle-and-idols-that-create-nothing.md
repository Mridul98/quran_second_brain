# The provision cycle — and the gods that cannot make anything (16:10–21)
**UID:** 202610141593
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 16:10–21 (Ruku 2 of 16 in an-Nahl)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[16:10]` – `[16:21]` (12 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The inventory continues — rain to drink to pasture to grain, olives, palms, grapes; night and day, sun, moon, stars "disposed by His command"; diverse-colored earth; a sea that yields tender meat, ornaments, and tracked ships; mountains, rivers, roads, landmarks, and navigation — each stratum closed by a "sign" clause for a graded audience (who think, who ponder, who are mindful) — before the pivot question "Is He who creates like him who does not create?" reduces the invoked gods to dead, created, time-ignorant things.
* **Placement:** The surah's great cosmos-as-creed movement, the structural twin of an-An'am 6:95–99/6:141; it supplies the "favors" (16:18) that the rest of the surah audits for gratitude.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[16:10]`** "It is He Who sends down for you from the sky water. From it is drink, and with it grows vegetation for grazing."
  * **Gloss:** The cycle's first link: one water, three uses — drinking, herbage, pasture for the an'am of ruku 1. The "for you" makes every drop addressed.
* **`[16:11]`** "And He produces for you grains with it, and olives, and date-palms, and grapes, and all kinds of fruits. Surely in that is a sign for people who think."
  * **Gloss:** The Mediterranean-arid crop table (grain, olive, palm, vine) — same quartet-plus as `[6:99]`; audience grade one: "people who think" (the 'aql verb).
* **`[16:12]`** "And He regulated for you the night and the day; and the sun, and the moon, and the stars are disposed by His command. Surely in that are signs for people who ponder."
  * **Gloss:** Time and sky pressed into service ("disposed/musakhkharat"); grade two: "who ponder" (yatakakkaru-n) — a verb root the Reminder repeatedly asks for (cf. `[16:44]` "that they may reflect").
* **`[16:13]`** "And whatsoever He created for you on earth is of diverse colors. Surely in that is a sign for people who are mindful."
  * **Gloss:** Biodiversity itself as evidence; grade three: "who are mindful" (yadh-dhakkaru-n — the dhikr/remembrance audience) — the ladder ends where taqwa begins.
* **`[16:14]`** "And it is He who made the sea to serve you, that you may eat from it tender meat, and extract from it ornaments that you wear. And you see the ships plowing through it, as you seek His bounties, so that you may give thanks."
  * **Gloss:** Sea triple-utility: food (lahm tayr — "tender meat"), jewelry (halya — pearl/coral, cf. `[55:22]`), commerce (ships); the clause ends on the purpose clause "so that you may give thanks" — shukr named for the first time in the surah.
* **`[16:15]`** "And he cast mountains on the earth, lest it shifts with you; and rivers, and roads, so that you may be guided."
  * **Gloss:** Terrestrial stabilizers — mountains as ballast ("lest it shifts"), rivers and roads as transit; "so that you may be guided" moves physical wayfinding into the guidance vocabulary of 16:9. [sic: corpus prints lowercase "he cast".]
* **`[16:16]`** "And landmarks. And by the stars they guide themselves."
  * **Gloss:** Two more wayfinding instruments — a'lam (landmarks/signs) and the stars — the desert's own GPS; echoes `[6:97]` "He created the stars... that you may be guided by them in the darkness of land and sea."
* **`[16:17]`** "Is He who creates like him who does not create? Will you not take a lesson?"
  * **Gloss:** The pivot: after 60+ verses of creation-work, one question exposes the invoked beings as non-makers. This is the surah's tawhid fulcrum.
* **`[16:18]`** "And if you tried to enumerate the favors of Allah, you will not be able to count them. Allah is Forgiving and Merciful."
  * **Gloss:** The favor-ledger's limit — the counting fails; twin at `[14:34]` "if you were to count Allah's blessings, you would not be able to enumerate them." Mercy answers the impossibility: the books are not balanced by enumeration but by rahma.
* **`[16:19]`** "And Allah knows what you hide and what you disclose."
  * **Gloss:** Omniscience bridge — what cannot be counted among favors, He nonetheless comprehends of you.
* **`[16:20]`** "Those they invoke besides Allah create nothing, but are themselves created."
  * **Gloss:** Verdict on the partners: zero creative output, and they are themselves products — passive voice against idols made by hands.
* **`[16:21]`** "They are dead, not alive; and they do not know when they will be resurrected."
  * **Gloss:** Three negations: life, resurrection-awareness — the gods cannot even keep an appointment; cf. `[7:197]` "Those you call upon besides Him cannot help you, nor can they help themselves."

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`musakhkhar (disposed / to serve)`** `[16:12`, `16:14]`:
  * **Rendering:** "the stars are disposed by His command"; "made the sea to serve you."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[14:32–33]` "He committed the ships to your service… and He committed the sun and the moon to your service" — the same sakhkhara verb; `[31:20]` "Allah placed at your service everything in the heavens and the earth"; `[45:13]` "He placed at your service whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on earth—all is from Him." Cosmos as conscripted service is a shared idiom, densest in al-Jathiyah.
* **`sign for people who… (ayah + graded audience)`** `[16:11–13`, `16:65`, `16:69]`:
  * **Elsewhere:** `[6:97–99]` runs the same sign-ladder ("for people who know" 6:97; "for people who understand" 6:98; "signs for people who believe" 6:99); `[10:67]` "signs for people who listen" — a near-twin of 16:65.
* **`tender meat (lahm tayr)`** `[16:14]`:
  * **Elsewhere:** `[35:12]` "from each you eat tender meat, and extract jewelry which you wear" — verbatim-twin verse with the two-seas frame; `[55:22]` "From them emerge pearls and coral" (the ornaments specified).
* **`nujum (stars)`** `[16:12`, `16:16]`:
  * **Rendering:** "the stars are disposed" (pl. masha'iq); "by the stars they guide themselves."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[6:97]` created "that you may be guided by them" (the same guidance-by-stars claim); `[53:1]` "By the star as it goes down" (singular najm); `[52:49]` "at the receding of the stars"; `[37:88]` Abraham "took a glance at the stars"; `[7:54]`/`[55:6]` stars prostrate/subservient. The plural cluster shows an-Nahl uses the cosmic vocabulary to argue, not to swear.

* **Parallel:** `[35:12]` — twin sea-verse (food, ornament, ships, gratitude purpose-clause).
* **Parallel:** `[14:32–34]` — Ibrahim's inventory (created heavens/earth, water, fruits, ships, sun/moon/stars) ending "if you were to count Allah's blessings, you would not be able to enumerate them" — the closest structural sibling to 16:10–18.
* **Within surah:** the "disposed" cosmos returns at `[16:79]` (birds sustained) and `[16:80–81]` (homes, hides, shade, garments); the favor-audit 16:18 is answered by the ingratitude verdict `[16:83]`; "they do not know when they will be resurrected" (16:21) is refuted by the "Be" of `[16:40]`.
