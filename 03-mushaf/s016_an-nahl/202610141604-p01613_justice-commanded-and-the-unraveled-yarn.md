# Justice commanded, and the woman who re-unspins her yarn (16:90–100)
**UID:** 202610141604
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 16:90–100 (Ruku 13 of 16 in an-Nahl)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[16:90]` – `[16:100]` (11 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah's law-center: one verse of comprehensive command (justice, goodness, kin-generosity; forbiddance of immorality, wrong, tyranny — 90); then the oath-block — fulfill covenants with Allah as guarantor (91), do not be the yarn-unspinner who re-breaks strong thread (92), do not treat oaths as deception because one camp prospers more (92–94, with the slipping-foot image), do not sell Allah's covenant for a small price (95); the economics close with "what you have runs out, but what is with Allah remains" (96) and the good-life promise to the righteous, male or female (97); and the ruku seals the reader himself: seek refuge before recitation, for Satan has authority only over the associators (98–100).
* **Placement:** The longest ruku of the surah; the pivot where the gratitude-audit becomes covenant-obligation — the corpus's densest oath-theology — and where the surah turns from argument to address.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[16:90]`** "Allah commands justice, and goodness, and generosity towards relatives. And He forbids immorality, and injustice, and oppression. He advises you, so that you may take heed."
  * **Gloss:** The triad-plus-triad verse — 'adl, ihsan, ata dhi-l-qurba against fahsha', munker, baghy; classical hadith reads it as the most comprehensive single verse of good-and-evil. *Source: frequency of citation in classical ethics (cf. the "most comprehensive verse" reports) — agent labeling, verify.*
* **`[16:91]`** "Fulfill Allah’s covenant when you make a covenant, and do not break your oaths after ratifying them. You have made Allah your guarantor, and Allah knows what you do."
  * **Gloss:** Personal covenant law: ratification (aukd) makes breaking perjury; the guarantor clause makes Allah the co-signer of every oath.
* **`[16:92]`** "And do not be like her who unravels her yarn, breaking it into pieces, after she has spun it strongly. Nor use your oaths as means of deception among you, because one community is more prosperous than another. Allah is testing you thereby. On the Day of Resurrection, He will make clear to you everything you had disputed about."
  * **Gloss:** The image-verse: a spinner who re-shreds her strong yarn — treaty-cancellation as self-waste; the motive named: prospering-ally-switching; the testing-frame + Day-of-exposure clause.
* **`[16:93]`** "Had Allah willed, He would have made you one congregation, but He leaves astray whom He wills, and He guides whom He wills. And you will surely be questioned about what you used to do."
  * **Gloss:** The plurality-of-communities axiom (2:213's shape) — unity declined as option; interrogation sure.
* **`[16:94]`** "And do not use your oaths to deceive one another, so that a foot may not slip after being firm, and you taste misery because you hindered from Allah’s path, and incur a terrible torment."
  * **Gloss:** The same prohibition twice, now with motor imagery: the firm foot that slips — perjury as destabilization, with the barrier-crime of 16:88 attached.
* **`[16:95]`** "And do not exchange Allah’s covenant for a small price. What is with Allah is better for you, if you only knew."
  * **Gloss:** The trade formula (thaman qalil) — 3:77's verbatim pole; covenant-as-asset priced below market.
* **`[16:96]`** "What you have runs out, but what is with Allah remains. We will reward those who are patient according to the best of their deeds."
  * **Gloss:** Run-out vs. remain — the surah's balance-sheet verse; patience (sabr) now named as the payer's channel.
* **`[16:97]`** "Whoever works righteousness, whether male or female, while being a believer, We will grant him a good life—and We will reward them according to the best of what they used to do."
  * **Gloss:** The hayah tayyibah verse: a good life in this world — jointly promised to male and female workers — plus the best-of-deeds reward; the corpus's only "good life" line.
* **`[16:98]`** "When you read the Quran, seek refuge with Allah from Satan the outcast."
  * **Gloss:** The recitation-adab verse — istia'dha before reading; the outcast (rajim) named as the reader's adversary.
* **`[16:99]`** "He has no authority over those who believe and trust in their Lord."
  * **Gloss:** Satan's jurisdictional limit: faith + tawakkul place a person outside his sulthan.
* **`[16:100]`** "His authority is only over those who follow him, and those who associate others with Him."
  * **Gloss:** The remaining two classes: the followers and the mushrikun — his "authority" is itself only over those who gave it to him.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`covenant / oaths (mithaq, aymān)`** `[16:91–95]`:
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:27]` "violate Allah's covenant after its confirmation" (the breach-class); `[3:77]` "exchange the covenant of Allah, and their vows, for a small price" (verbatim twin of 16:95); `[2:224]` "do not allow your oaths in Allah's name to hinder you from virtue, and righteousness, and making peace" — the oath's other failure mode (swearing against good); `[13:20/13:25]` promise/violation pair.
* **`unravels her yarn (naqb)`** `[16:92]`:
  * **Elsewhere:** the image is unique in the corpus (`rg "yarn" corpus/` — only 16:92); the classical asbab-style report names a Meccan woman (Makhdub/Ma'bad bint 'Amr type) who re-spun daily. *Source: transmitted reports differ on her name — agent labeling, verify.*
* **`a small price (thaman qalil)`** `[16:95]`:
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:41]` "do not exchange My revelations for a small price"; `[2:174]`; `[3:187]` — the trade-verb family (shara-yastari) that the infraq/riba economics note tracks; `[16:96]` "runs out" is the price's true size.
* **`good life (hayat tayyibah)`** `[16:97]`:
  * **Elsewhere:** `[20:131]` "Your Lord's provision is better, and more lasting" — the remain-pole of 16:96; `[11:3]` "He will provide you with good sustenance until a stated term" — the closest twin formula (Hud's istighfar-economy).
