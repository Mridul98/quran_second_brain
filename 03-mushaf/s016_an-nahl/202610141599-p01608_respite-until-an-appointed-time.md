# Respite is mercy's grammar, not exemption (16:61–65)
**UID:** 202610141599
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 16:61–65 (Ruku 8 of 16 in an-Nahl)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[16:61]` – `[16:65]` (5 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Why the roof has not yet caved in: if Allah held mankind for their injustices, not one creature would be left — but He postpones to an appointed time that can be neither advanced nor delayed (61); the lie that "theirs is the goodness" (62) is traced to Satan's allurement, the perennial hazard of prior communities (63); the Scripture was sent to clarify what people differ about (64); and the ruku re-grounds its own evidence in the rain that revives the dead earth — a sign "for people who listen" (65).
* **Placement:** Ruku 8 answers the obvious objection to ruku 4 (why no collapse now?): the delay is a policy (respite), bounded by an appointment, and paired with the clarifying function of revelation.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[16:61]`** "If Allah were to hold mankind for their injustices, He would not leave upon it a single creature, but He postpones them until an appointed time. Then, when their time arrives, they will not delay it by one hour, nor will they advance it."
  * **Gloss:** The mercy-premise of the whole respite-theodicy: punishment withheld is not punishment waived; the hour has both an appointed term and zero elasticity.
* **`[16:62]`** "And they attribute to Allah what they themselves dislike, while their tongues utter the lie that theirs is the goodness. Without a doubt, for them is the Fire, and they will be neglected."
  * **Gloss:** The daughters-projection (16:57) generalized: God gets the disliked, their tongues claim the best; "neglected" — no response, no rescue, the respite's end-state.
* **`[16:63]`** "By Allah, We sent messengers to communities before you, but Satan made their deeds appear alluring to them. He is their master today, and they will have a painful punishment."
  * **Gloss:** The history-of-rejection clause (16:36) deepened: the failure was not un-messaged but under-enrolled — "He is their master (mawla) today."
* **`[16:64]`** "We revealed to you the Scripture only to clarify for them what they differ about, and guidance and mercy for people who believe."
  * **Gloss:** Scripture's office restated (cf. 16:39, 16:44): clarification of disagreement — not spectacle — plus the two benefits for believers.
* **`[16:65]`** "Allah sends down water from the sky, with which He revives the earth after its death. In this is a sign for people who listen."
  * **Gloss:** The resurrection's physical analogue, mid-surah: the dead-land reviver is the pattern-book for the dead-soul; audience grade: "who listen" (sam'a).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`postpone / appointed time`** `[16:61]`:
  * **Rendering:** "He postpones them until an appointed time."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[39:42]` "He releases the others until a predetermined time"; `[6:60]` "He raises you up in it, until a fixed term is fulfilled" — the term-grammar is stable across the corpus; the immovability clause has its twins at `[15:5]` "No nation can bring its time forward, nor can they delay it" and `[35:11]` (lifespans held "in a Record").
* **`alluring (zeyyana)`** `[16:63]`:
  * **Elsewhere:** `[27:24]` "Satan made their conduct appear good to them, and diverted them from the path, so they are not guided" — the same zein-verb in the Bilqis scene; 16:63 is the only "appear alluring" collocation in the corpus.
* **`neglected (yuntazar?)`** `[16:62]`:
  * **Rendering:** "they will be neglected" — left, unattended, at the respite's end; the word mirrors 16:61's postponement (delay is what they lose). *Source: the classical pairing of yunzar (respite/provision) with 16:61's ista'khir is noted in the commentaries — agent inference, verify the Arabic forms.*
* **`revives the earth after its death`** `[16:65]`:
  * **Elsewhere:** `[35:9]` "which We drive to a dead land, and thereby revive the ground after it had died. Likewise is the Resurrection" — the explicit resurrection-simile twin; `[43:11]` "so We revive thereby a dead land. Thus you will be brought out"; `[22:63]` "Allah sends down water from the sky, and the land becomes green."
