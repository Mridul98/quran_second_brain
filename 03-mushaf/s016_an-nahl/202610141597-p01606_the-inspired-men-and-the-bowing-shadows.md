# The persecuted emigrants, the inspired men, and every shadow bowing (16:41–50)
**UID:** 202610141597
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 16:41–50 (Ruku 6 of 16 in an-Nahl)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[16:41]` – `[16:50]` (10 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Against the schemers' end, the community's charter: emigration-after-persecution is promised a good settlement here and a greater reward hereafter, defined by patience and trust; then the messenger-question ("why a man?") is answered — every prior revelation came through inspired men, so "ask the people of knowledge"; the Reminder exists to clarify; the three seizures are re-listed against the schemers (with "Your Lord is Gentle and Merciful" as the stay); and the ruku closes with all creation — shadows, living things, angels — prostrating without pride.
* **Placement:** The consolation-and-officer structure pivot: ruku 4–5 argued; ruku 6 addresses the believers directly for the first time (41–44), before the physics of prostration (48–50) make the angels the model the arrogant refused to be (16:22–23, 16:29).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[16:41]`** "Those who emigrate for Allah’s sake after being persecuted, We will settle them in a good place in this world; but the reward of the Hereafter is greater, if they only knew."
  * **Gloss:** Two-stage payoff for displacement: settlement (mas'kan) in this world, greater (akbar) reward in the next; the formula is addressed first to the Meccan emigrants' experience.
* **`[16:42]`** "Those who endure patiently, and in their Lord they put their trust."
  * **Gloss:** The emigrants' identity is two verbs: sabr + tawakkul — the stamina pair of the surah's ethics (cf. 16:96, 127).
* **`[16:43]`** "We did not send before you except men whom We inspired. So ask the people of knowledge, if you do not know."
  * **Gloss:** The scandal of human messengership answered by precedent — and with an epistemic command to consult the knowledgeable, a verse often cited for scholarship as worship.
* **`[16:44]`** "With the clarifications and the scriptures. And We revealed to you the Reminder, that you may clarify to the people what was revealed to them, and that they may reflect."
  * **Gloss:** Revelation's equipment (bayyinat, zubur) and the Prophet's office: clarification, reflection — not miracle-coercion.
* **`[16:45]`** "Do those who scheme evils feel secure that Allah will not cause the earth to cave in with them, or that the punishment will not come upon them from where they do not perceive?"
  * **Gloss:** Re-run of 16:26's vectors: subsidence and the unperceived direction.
* **`[16:46]`** "Or that He will not seize them during their activities? And they will not be able to prevent it."
  * **Gloss:** Third vector: seizure mid-transaction — no exemption for momentum.
* **`[16:47]`** "Or that He will not seize them while in dread? Your Lord is Gentle and Merciful."
  * **Gloss:** Fourth vector: dread itself; then the stay — rafiq: His gentleness withholds the now (contrast with 16:61's wholesale respite).
* **`[16:48]`** "Have they not observed what Allah has created? Their shadows revolve from the right and the left, bowing to Allah as they shrink away."
  * **Gloss:** Shadow-observation: the daily sweep of shadows is a prostration circuit — creation already obeys involuntarily.
* **`[16:49]`** "To Allah bows down everything in the heavens and everything on earth—every living creature, and the angels, and without being proud."
  * **Gloss:** The universal sajda: every dabbah, the angels — and the crucial clause "without being proud": the prostration physics the arrogant (16:23, 29) opt out of.
* **`[16:50]`** "They fear their Lord above them, and they do what they are commanded."
  * **Gloss:** The angelic portrait: fear-plus-compliance — the template the surah's critics refuse to copy.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`emigrate (hijra)`** `[16:41]`:
  * **Rendering:** "Those who emigrate for Allah's sake after being persecuted."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[4:100]` "Anyone who emigrates for the sake of Allah will find on earth many places of refuge, and plentitude"; `[22:58]` "Those who emigrate in Allah's cause, then get killed, or die, Allah will provide them with fine provisions"; `[8:74]` "these are the true believers... forgiveness, and a bountiful provision" — the same promise-structure; and within this surah, the twin at `[16:110]` (see ruku 14) closes the arc.
* **`inspired (wahy)`** `[16:43`, `16:68]`:
  * **Rendering:** "men whom We inspired" — wahy to prophets; 16:68 uses the same verb for the bee's instinct: the root spans revelation and animal guidance.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[20:38]` "When We inspired your mother with the inspiration"; `[28:7]` "We inspired the mother of Moses"; `[42:52]` "We thus inspired you spiritually, by Our command" — the wahy continuum (prophet, mother, bee) is this surah's own spectrum.
* **`the Reminder (dhikr)`** `[16:44]`:
  * **Elsewhere:** `[41:41]` "it is an invincible Book"; `[43:5]` "Shall We hold back the Reminder from you...?"; `[37:3]` "the reciters of the Reminder"; `[21:105]` "We have written in the Psalms, after the Reminder" — dhikr as the Quran's self-name, and as the earlier book's sequel.
* **`bow / prostrate (sajada)`** `[16:48–49]`:
  * **Rendering:** shadows "bowing... as they shrink away"; everything "bows down... without being proud."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[22:18]` "to Allah prostrates everyone in the heavens and everyone on earth, and the sun, and the moon, and the stars, and the mountains, and the trees, and the animals, and many of the people" — the same universal list, with the human exception stated; `[55:6]` "the stars and the trees prostrate themselves"; `[13:15]` "To Allah prostrates everyone in the heavens and the earth, willingly or unwillingly, as do their shadows, in the morning and in the evening" — ar-Ra'd's twin, which adds the will-clause an-Nahl renders as "without being proud."

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Meccan; 16:41–42 is traditionally read against the Abyssinian emigration (the Ja'far party returning to find the reward-verse revealed). *Source: transmitted occasion-reports repeated in classical commentaries (Ibn Kathir-type) on 16:41 — agent labeling; verify.*
