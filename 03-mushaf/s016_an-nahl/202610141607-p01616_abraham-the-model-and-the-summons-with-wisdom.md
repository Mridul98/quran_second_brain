# Abraham the model, the summons with wisdom, and patience as the last word (16:120–128)
**UID:** 202610141607
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 16:120–128 (Ruku 16 of 16 in an-Nahl)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[16:120]` – `[16:128]` (9 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah closes with a portrait and a program: Abraham — "an exemplary leader, devoted to Allah, a monotheist," grateful for His blessings, chosen, guided to a straight path, given goodness in both worlds (120–122) — becomes the pattern the Messenger is told to follow (123); the Sabbath's sectarian burden is quietly unloaded (124); the community's method is then defined: invite with wisdom, good advice, and dignified debate (125), retaliate only at parity, and prefer patience (126), because patience itself is from Allah (127) — until the final clause assigns company, not just reward: "Allah is with those who are righteous and those who are virtuous" (128).
* **Placement:** The ruku that names the surah's practical close: Abraham is the gratitude-ideal (16:121 "Thankful for His blessings" answering 16:18's uncountable favors), and 16:125–128 is the corpus's fullest methodology-verse for dawah and conflict — the patience-doctrine (16:42, 96, 110) as the surah's final breath.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[16:120]`** "Abraham was an exemplary leader, devoted to Allah, a monotheist, and was not of the polytheists."
  * **Gloss:** Four predicates: ummah (a community in one man — or "leader," classically), hanif (devoted/uptight-turned-to-God), muqmin/muslim-monotheist, and the negative clause shared verbatim with the Abrahamic dossier ("was not an idolater/polytheist").
* **`[16:121]`** "Thankful for His blessings. He chose him, and guided him to a straight path."
  * **Gloss:** Shakur — the gratitude virtue of the whole surah personified; election, guidance-to-sirat: the reward of thankfulness is more thankfulness.
* **`[16:122]`** "And We gave him goodness in this world, and in the Hereafter he will be among the righteous."
  * **Gloss:** The two-world goodness promised generically in 16:30 — receipted for Abraham.
* **`[16:123]`** "Then We inspired you: “Follow the religion of Abraham, the Monotheist. He was not an idol-worshiper.”"
  * **Gloss:** The program-verse: the same command given to the Prophet in 2:135/3:95/16:123 — millet Ibrahim as the surah's closing identity.
* **`[16:124]`** "The Sabbath was decreed only for those who differed about it. Your Lord will judge between them on the Day of Resurrection regarding their differences."
  * **Gloss:** Sectarian burden released: the Sabbath was incumbent on those who made the dispute; judgment deferred entirely to Allah — the surah's pluralism axiom in one line.
* **`[16:125]`** "Invite to the way of your Lord with wisdom and good advice, and debate with them in the most dignified manner. Your Lord is aware of those who stray from His path, and He is aware of those who are guided."
  * **Gloss:** Three registers of summons — hikma (wisdom), mauidat hasana (goodly counsel), mujadala best-mannered — bracketed by divine awareness of both strayers and guided: persuasion replaces coercion because God already knows outcomes. "The way of your Lord" is this surah's own phrasing in the corpus.
* **`[16:126]`** "If you were to retaliate, retaliate to the same degree as the injury done to you. But if you resort to patience—it is better for the patient."
  * **Gloss:** Parity law with an upgrade option: equivalence caps vengeance, patience outperforms it — 2:194/42:40's economy of retaliation.
* **`[16:127]`** "So be patient. Your patience is solely from Allah. And do not grieve over them, and do not be stressed by their schemes."
  * **Gloss:** Patience re-source-located (it is His gift, not your stock) and paired with two emotional releases — grief and straitness about their makr (the schemes that opened the surah at 16:26 return as tired opponents).
* **`[16:128]`** "Allah is with those who are righteous and those who are virtuous."
  * **Gloss:** Last word: not reward-language but accompaniment — ma'iyya — for muttaqin and muhsinin; the entire gratitude-audit of the surah ends in divine company as the real blessing.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`ummah (exemplary leader)`** `[16:120]`:
  * **Rendering:** "an exemplary leader" for Abraham alone called ummah; the root elsewhere denotes communities — 16:36 "to every community," 16:84 "a witness from every community," 16:93 "one congregation."
  * **Elsewhere:** the odd singular use is classically glossed "a teacher of good" or "one who gathers the whole of guidance in himself". *Source: classical glosses (varied) — agent labeling, verify.*
* **`hanif / monotheist`** `[16:120`, `16:123]`:
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:135]` "the religion of Abraham, the Monotheist; he was not an idolater"; `[3:67]` "Abraham was neither a Jew nor a Christian, but he was a Monotheist, a Muslim"; `[6:161]` "the creed of Abraham the Monotheist" — the Abrahamic reclamation idiom, verbatim-family.
* **`wisdom (hikmah)`** `[16:125]`:
  * **Rendering:** "Invite to the way of your Lord with wisdom" — here hikma as method, not bestowed prophetic-content.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:269]` "He gives wisdom to whomever He wills. Whoever is given wisdom has been given much good"; `[17:39]` "That is some of the wisdom your Lord has revealed to you" — the nearest sibling (next surah, same root as dawah-clause); `[31:12]`-region Luqman's hikma; the term's best dossier in the corpus is 2:269.
* **`the virtuous (muhsinin)`** `[16:128]`:
  * **Elsewhere:** `[11:115]` "And be patient. Allah will not waste the reward of the virtuous" — verbatim-twin pairing of sabr with ihsan; `[3:134]`-region "Allah loves the virtuous"; the muttaqi-muhsin pairing of 16:128 is the surah's own two-class summation (taqwa ruku 4/13, ihsan throughout the "goodness" verses 16:30, 97, 122).
