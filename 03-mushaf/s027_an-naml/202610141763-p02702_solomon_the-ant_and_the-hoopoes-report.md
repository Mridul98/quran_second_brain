# Solomon, the ant, and the hoopoe: a letter dispatched (27:15–31)
**UID:** 202610141763
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 27:15–31 (Ruku 2 of 7 in an-Naml)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[27:15]` – `[27:31]` (17 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Knowledge is gifted to David and Solomon as a speech-empire — birds taught, an ant heard, a hoopoe reporting — and that empire's only evangelism is a one-page letter whose opening line is the Name the Book itself opens with.
* **Placement:** Ruku 2; the surah's signature narrative. It inherits "appear good" cosmetics from 27:4, hands the throne-test to ruku 3, and plants the submission arc (27:31) that climaxes at 27:44/91.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[27:15]`** "And We gave David and Solomon knowledge. They said, "Praise Allah, who has favored us over many of His believing servants.""
  * **Gloss:** The gift is *knowledge*, not miracle; the response is praise-for-favor — the shukr circuit (ni'ma → praise) in two breaths; cf. the parentage-chain `[6:84]` "We guided... David, and Solomon" (verified).
* **`[27:16]`** "And Solomon succeeded David. He said, "O people, we were taught the language of birds, and we were given from everything. This is indeed a real blessing.""
  * **Gloss:** The only "language of birds" in the corpus (verified); "given from everything" will be mirrored — and contested — at 27:23; "a real blessing" names the audit to come (Correction to task: the gift-rejection is 27:35–37, not 27:16).
* **`[27:17]`** "To the service of Solomon were mobilized his troops of sprites, and men, and birds—all held in strict order."
  * **Gloss:** Three conscript classes; "sprites" (jinn) serve Solomon also at `[34:12]`, mountains-and-birds glorify at `[38:18–19]`/`[21:79]` (verified); "held in strict order" answers at 27:83, where the rejected are "restrained."
* **`[27:18]`** "Until, when they came upon the Valley of Ants, an ant said, "O ants! Go into your nests, lest Solomon and his troops crush you without noticing.""
  * **Gloss:** The surah's namesake: a sermon from an ant to its own kind, preaching evacuation from a crush it cannot assume malice in — small voice, largest court.
* **`[27:19]`** "He smiled and laughed at her words, and said, "My Lord, direct me to be thankful for the blessings you have bestowed upon me and upon my parents, and to do good works that please You. And admit me, by Your grace, into the company of Your virtuous servants.""
  * **Gloss:** The only "smiled" in the corpus (verified); hearing the ant produces *gratitude-prayer*, not power-display — anchor for the Shukr note; "virtuous servants" phrase is unique here (verified).
* **`[27:20]–[27:21]`** "Then he inspected the birds, and said, "Why do I not see the hoopoe? Or is he among the absentees? / I will punish him most severely, or slay him, unless he gives me a valid excuse.""
  * **Gloss:** "Hoopoe" and "absentees" are corpus-unique (verified); the empire keeps attendance rolls — the bird with news will be justified by news.
* **`[27:22]`** "But he did not stay for long. He said, "I have learnt something you did not know. I have come to you from Sheba, with reliable information.""
  * **Gloss:** Knowledge graded — a bird knows what a prophet-king does not; "Sheba" re-appears only in `[34:15]` (Sheba's gardens, gratitude-commanded — verified) — this report is the prequel to that ruin.
* **`[27:23]`** "I found a woman ruling over them, and she was given of everything, and she has a magnificent throne."
  * **Gloss:** "given of everything" mirrors Solomon's own phrase at 27:16 — the rival looks exactly as blessed as the believer; "magnificent throne" ('arsh) sets the ladder to 27:26.
* **`[27:24]`** "I found her and her people worshiping the sun, instead of Allah. Satan made their conduct appear good to them, and diverted them from the path, so they are not guided."
  * **Gloss:** The 27:4 disease on a national scale — same "appear good" verb, now with Satan named as agent (verified); sun-worship is answered by the direct command `[41:37]` "Do not bow down to the sun" and the fact `[22:18]` that the sun itself prostrates (verified).
* **`[27:25]`** "If only they would worship Allah, who brings to light the mysteries of the heavens and the earth, and knows what you conceal and what you reveal."
  * **Gloss:** The hoopoe's creed-line: "brings to light the mysteries" (ghuyub) is corpus-unique (verified); ghayb then drifts across the surah — "the future" (65), "mystery" (75).
* **`[27:26]`** "Allah—There is no god but He, the Lord of the Sublime Throne.""
  * **Gloss:** "Sublime Throne" occurs exactly twice in the corpus, here and at `[9:129]` (verified) — and against *her* magnificent throne (23), the ladder is the surah's argument.
* **`[27:27]–[27:28]`** "He said, "We will see, whether you have spoken the truth, or whether you are a liar. / Go with this letter of mine, and deliver it to them; then withdraw from them, and see how they respond.""
  * **Gloss:** Empiricism inside revelation — truth-claims get a field test; "see how they respond" installs the response-motif cashed at 27:81 ("You can make no one listen, except...").
* **`[27:29]`** "She said, "O Counselors, a gracious letter was delivered to me."
  * **Gloss:** "Counselors" is s027-unique in this corpus (verified); the letter is *karim* — gracious — while its content is a summons.
* **`[27:30]–[27:31]`** "It is from Solomon, and it is, 'In the Name of Allah, the Gracious, the Merciful. / Do not defy me, and come to me submissively.'"
  * **Gloss:** The only numbered mid-surah Basmala in the corpus (rg-verified; see Fleeting note) — a foreign king's letter opens the way revelation itself opens; "defy" belongs to the 'utu cluster (`[6:15]`, `[90:19]`, verified); "submissively" (muslimin) starts the arc: 31 → 38 → 42 → 44 → 81 → 91. Quote stays open through 30 [sic].

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`'arsh` (throne):** "magnificent throne" (27:23) / "the Sublime Throne" (27:26).
  * **Rendering:** Itani distinguishes the adjectives ("magnificent" vs "Sublime"); "Sublime Throne" is welded to `[9:129]`; cousins elsewhere: "Splendid" `[23:86]`, "Noble" `[23:116]`, "Glorious" `[85:15]` (verified).
  * **Elsewhere:** the two adjectives likely render the same Arabic phrase ('arsh 'azim) — Source: agent inference — verify. Either way, the hoopoe sets a local throne under a cosmic one.
* **`sprites` (jinn):** "troops of sprites" (27:17); "An imp of the sprites" (27:39, next ruku).
  * **Rendering:** "sprites" is Itani's standing choice for jinn in Solomon contexts — `[6:100]`, `[34:12]`, `[34:14]` (verified); winds, the other Solomonic corps, are absent from s027 (correction to task).
* **`muslim` arc (submit):** "come to me submissively" (27:31).
  * **Elsewhere:** in-surah 38/42/44/81/91 (verified); the label's own genealogy: `[22:78]` "He named you Muslims before, and in this" — the queen enters the arc before she is named in it (cf. k-guidance's road-with-people).
* **`naba'` (news):** "some news from it" (27:7) → "with reliable information" (27:22).
  * **Rendering:** "news"/"information" — the fire-errand becomes an intelligence briefing; revelation itself is news the king did not have (22: "something you did not know").
