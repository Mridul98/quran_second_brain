# Is there another god with Allah? — the five audits (27:59–66)
**UID:** 202610141766
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 27:59–66 (Ruku 5 of 7 in an-Naml)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[27:59]` – `[27:66]` (8 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Five rival portfolios — rain, ground, the desperate prayer, navigation, creation's re-run — are audited one by one with the same question and five different verdicts, and the final divine prerogative is named: no one in the heavens or earth knows the future except Allah.
* **Placement:** Ruku 5; the surah's creed-center. The destroyed kings and plotters of r4 clear the field; the audits ask who actually runs it. The ghayb-vocabulary planted by the hoopoe (27:25) is harvested here (65) and at 27:75.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[27:59]`** "Say, "Praise Allah, and peace be upon His servants whom He has selected. Is Allah better, or what they associate?""
  * **Gloss:** A liturgical opening — praise and salutation — before the comparative question; "whom He has selected" phrasing is corpus-unique (verified; the elect's naming-cousin `[22:78]` "He named you Muslims"); "Is Allah better" sets the audit's frame: partners weighed against benefit.
* **`[27:60]`** "Or, who created the heavens and the earth, and rains down water from the sky for you? With it We produce gardens full of beauty, whose trees you could not have produced. Is there another god with Allah? But they are a people who equate."
  * **Gloss:** Audit #1 (water→gardens): the trees you *couldn't* grow; verdict-clause #1 "a people who equate" — the adl-verb family (`[6:150]` "those who equate others with their Lord", `[29:10]` — verified); garden-inventory logic belongs to the Shukr note's provision-lists (`[16:10–18]`, `[6:99]`), but here the inventory closes on tawhid, not thanks.
* **`[27:61]`** "Or, who made the earth habitable, and made rivers flow through it, and set mountains on it, and placed a partition between the two seas? Is there another god with Allah? But most of them do not know."
  * **Gloss:** Audit #2 (geography): bed, rivers, anchors, partition; "partition" renders the barzakh of `[25:53]` "a barrier, and an impassable boundary" (verified — word-choice drift); verdict-clause #2 "most of them do not know."
* **`[27:62]`** "Or, who answers the one in need when he prays to Him, and relieves adversity, and makes you successors on earth? Is there another god with Allah? How hardly you pay attention."
  * **Gloss:** Audit #3 (the desperate prayer): answered distress and the successorship — "successors on earth" is the 2:30 viceroyalty-word and `[6:165]` "made you successors on the earth... in order to test you" (verified — and 6:165 ties the post to the *test*, welding r3's theology here); verdict-clause #3 "How hardly you pay attention."
* **`[27:63]`** "Or, who guides you through the darkness of land and sea, and who sends the winds as heralds of His mercy? Is there another god with Allah? Most exalted is Allah, above what they associate."
  * **Gloss:** Audit #4 (navigation and wind); "the darkness of land and sea" is `[6:97]`-verbatim (verified); "heralds of His mercy" twins `[7:57]` "sends the wind ahead of His mercy" (verified — rendering drift); verdict-clause #4 is a doxology; winds serve mercy, not a king (correction to task: no Solomonic wind-service in s027 — cf. `[21:81]`, `[34:12]`).
* **`[27:64]`** "Or, who originates the creation and then repeats it, and who gives you livelihood from the sky and the earth? Is there another god with Allah? Say, "Produce your evidence, if you are truthful.""
  * **Gloss:** Audit #5 (resurrection-prototype + provision); "originates... repeats" is `[30:11]`-near-verbatim, cf. `[29:19]`, `[10:4]` (verified); the sky-and-earth livelihood question rhymes with `[10:31]`; verdict-clause #5 shifts burden of proof — "Produce your evidence."
* **`[27:65]`** "Say, "No one in the heavens or on earth knows the future except Allah; and they do not perceive when they will be resurrected.""
  * **Gloss:** The seal: ghayb-knowledge is non-transferable (twin denials at `[6:50]`, `[7:188]`, questions at `[52:41]`, `[68:47]` — verified); "the future" is the same ghayb-root the hoopoe's "mysteries" (27:25) and 27:75's "mystery" render — three English words, one root (Source: observable rendering; root-identity agent inference — verify).
* **`[27:66]`** "In fact, their knowledge of the Hereafter is confused. In fact, they are in doubt about it. In fact, they are blind to it."
  * **Gloss:** Triple "In fact" (bal) — confusion, doubt, blindness; the certainty-chain snaps (27:3 certain → 66 confused → 82 uncertain, within-surah); "blind" pre-announces 27:80–81.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`Is there another god with Allah?`:**
  * **Rendering:** five occurrences, all in s027 (60–64) — rg-verified corpus-exclusive; cousins: "a god besides Allah" `[52:43]`, "another god besides Allah" `[23:117]`, "set up another god with Allah" `[17:22]` (verified).
  * **Force:** the refrain's *variations* are the verse's rhetoric — five closers (equate / not know / hardly remember / exalted above / produce evidence) diagnose five different failures of the same idol.
* **`khalifah/successors`:** "makes you successors on earth" `[27:62]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:30]` (the announcement), `[6:165]` (succession-as-test), `[10:14]`, `[7:69/74]` (after past nations) — verified; the office is audit-proof: it was given to be answered for.
* **`mudtar` (the one in need):** "who answers the one in need when he prays to Him" `[27:62]`.
  * **Rendering:** "the one in need" (not "the needy" of the zakat lists); the answered desperate is the counter-evidence to silent partners (`[7:194]` "call upon them, and let them answer you" — verified).
* **`nudjub/heralds`:** "the winds as heralds of His mercy" `[27:63]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** the same English noun carries Pharaoh's town-criers (`[7:111]`) and the warner's own office (`[7:188]` "a herald of good news") — verified — mercy, marketing, and mission share one announcer-word in this edition.
