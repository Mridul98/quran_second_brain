# The Trumpet, the passing mountains, and the commanded reciter (27:83–93)
**UID:** 202610141768
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 27:83–93 (Ruku 7 of 7 in an-Naml)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[27:83]` – `[27:93]` (11 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The court session convenes: rationed delegations, a cross-examination that leaves the accused speechless, night and day as standing exhibits, the Trumpet that horrifies everyone except the exempted — and the mountains, imagined solid, are shown moving like clouds; what remains for the prophet is a double command: worship the Lord of this town, and recite.
* **Placement:** Ruku 7; the surah's finale. It answers ruku 6's "when is this promise?" with the Day's own schedule, and closes the recitation bracket: received from an All-Wise, All-Knowing (27:6) → commanded to be recited (27:92); praise opens (27:15) and praise closes (27:93).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[27:83]`** "On the Day when We gather from every community a group of those who rejected Our revelations; and they will be restrained."
  * **Gloss:** A rationed gathering — "a group from every community"; "restrained" (held in ranks/marched) rhymes with Solomon's troops "all held in strict order" (27:17 — within-surah; root-identity agent inference — verify): the empire's formation becomes the condemned's.
* **`[27:84]`** "Until, when they arrive, He will say, "Did you reject My revelations without comprehending them? Or what is it you were doing?""
  * **Gloss:** The cross-examination offers two exits, both closed: rejection without knowledge (the surah's anti-knowledge, cf. 27:55 "ignorant people"), or the ledger of deeds.
* **`[27:85]`** "The Word will come down upon them for their wrongdoing, and they will not speak."
  * **Gloss:** Verdict-sentence falls where the Word fell at 27:82 — speech of God becomes the defendants' silence (within-surah; cf. 7:38-family silence scenes — verify); the surah that gave an ant a voice (18) takes it away here.
* **`[27:86]`** "Do they not see that We made the night for them to rest therein, and the day for visibility? Surely in that are signs for people who believe."
  * **Gloss:** The exhibit before the exhibits: `[10:67]` "made the night for your rest, and the daylight for visibility" — "visibility" shared (verified); `[40:61]`/`[6:96]` twins; night-and-day is Meccan evidence par excellence, re-entering one verse before the Day.
* **`[27:87]`** "On the Day when the Trumpet is blown, everyone in the heavens and the earth will be horrified, except whomever Allah wills; and everyone will come before Him in humility."
  * **Gloss:** The Trumpet-cluster (`[36:51]`, `[39:68]`, `[50:20]`, `[69:13]`, `[74:8]`, `[78:18]` — verified); near-verbatim twin `[39:68]` "everyone in the heavens and the earth will be stunned, except whomever Allah wills" (verified); the exception clause keeps one open door.
* **`[27:88]`** "And you see the mountains, and imagine them fixed, yet they pass, as the passing of the clouds—the making of Allah, who has perfected everything. He is fully Informed of what you do."
  * **Gloss:** The surah's physics sermon: solidity is illusion, drift is craft; "who has perfected everything" twins `[32:7]` "He who perfected everything He created" (verified); moving mountains also at `[78:20]`, crushed at `[56:5]`/`[69:14]` (verified); the "you see... imagine" construction echoes the Cave sleepers "You would think them awake, although they were asleep" `[18:18]` (verified) — perception audited twice in one Quran; closing surveillance-line re-opens the 27:74/93 frame.
* **`[27:89]`** "Whoever brings a virtue will receive better than it—and they will be safe from the horrors of that Day."
  * **Gloss:** Verbatim first clause with `[28:84]` "Whoever brings a virtue will receive better than it" (verified); "horrors" answers 87's "horrified" (within-surah, root-identity agent inference — verify): the Day's fear exempts the virtue-bringer twice.
* **`[27:90]`** "But whoever brings evil—their faces will be tumbled into the Fire. Will you be rewarded except for what you used to do?"
  * **Gloss:** "tumbled into the Fire" phrasing is corpus-unique (verified); the reward-axiom twins `[28:84]`'s second half "rewarded only according to what they used to do" (verified) — the ledger closes on earned deeds.
* **`[27:91]`** ""I was commanded to worship the Lord of this town, who has sanctified it, and to Whom everything belongs; and I was commanded to be of those who submit."
  * **Gloss:** The double command ("I was commanded" ×2): worship and submission; the command-cluster twins `[39:11–12]`, `[10:72]`, `[40:66]` (verified — 40:66 "I was commanded to submit to the Lord of the Worlds" is the nearest); "Lord of this town, who has sanctified it" answers 27:34's devastating kings — cf. `[28:57]` "Safe Sanctuary" and Abraham's prayer `[2:126]` "make this a peaceful land" (verified); "to Whom everything belongs" — the audits' portfolio-owner.
* **`[27:92]`** "And to recite the Quran." Whoever is guided—is guided to his own advantage. And whoever goes astray, then say, "I am one of the warners.""
  * **Gloss:** The third command: recitation — the bracket to 27:6 closes (receiving → reciting; k-kalam); "guided to his own advantage" — correction to the task's "for his own soul": this edition says "advantage" here, "for his own good" at `[17:15]`, "for his own soul" at `[31:12]` (verified); "I am one of the warners" twins `[26:194]`, kin of `[7:188]` (verified) — guidance is neither the reciter's possession nor his power.
* **`[27:93]`** "And say, "Praise belongs to Allah; He will show you His signs, and you will recognize them. Your Lord is not heedless of what you do.""
  * **Gloss:** The surah ends on praise (opened on it, 27:15), on *signs recognized* — the hoopoe's report, the moved thrones, the passing mountains all read back — and on the not-heedless Lord, verbatim-class of `[2:149]` "Allah is not heedless of what you do" (verified); "Ta, Seen" (1) turned out to be the surah's own agenda.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`nafkh/Trumpet`:** "the Trumpet is blown" `[27:87]`.
  * **Rendering:** "Trumpet" (sur) is fixed across the cluster (8 sites, verified); here with the exemption clause (shared only with `[39:68]` — verified).
* **`faza' (horrify/horrors):** "will be horrified" (27:87) / "the horrors of that Day" (27:89).
  * **Rendering:** the noun-verb pair welds the scene to the exemption (within-surah; English collocation unique to s027 — rg-verified; Arabic root-identity agent inference — verify).
* **`amr (commanded):** "I was commanded... and I was commanded... And to recite" `[27:91–92]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** the messenger-command formulas `[39:11–12]`, `[10:72]`, `[40:66]` (verified) — the Solomon-surah's last king-figure is a prophet with no throne, only an order.
* **`muslimun (those who submit):** "to be of those who submit" `[27:91]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** the arc's terminus: 27:31 → 44 → 81 → 91 (verified); the queen's verb has become the reciter's company — and 27:19's prayer ("admit me... into the company of Your virtuous servants") finds its answer-list.
