# Legends of the ancients, the Recorded Book, and the fallen Word (27:67–82)
**UID:** 202610141767
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 27:67–82 (Ruku 6 of 7 in an-Naml)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[27:67]` – `[27:82]` (16 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The resurrection mockers get a travel order and a grief-relief; their "when?" is answered by nearness, not a date; everything secret is already booked in a Clear Book — and the prophet learns his limits: the dead, the deaf and the blind do not hear, until the Word falls and the earth itself produces a witness.
* **Placement:** Ruku 6; the surah's eschatology-and-revelation hinge between the five audits (r5) and the Day scenes (r7). The Book-bracket closes (27:1 → 27:75) and the listening-theme that began with "see how they respond" (27:28) bottoms out at 27:81.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[27:67]`** "Those who disbelieve say, "When we have become dust, and our ancestors, shall we be brought out?"
  * **Gloss:** The dust-doubt cluster (`[36:78]`-family: `[23:35/82]`, `[37:16]`, `[50:3]` — verified); "and our ancestors" is s027's addition, shared only with `[23:83]`; the verb "brought out" plays the surah's own "brings to light" (27:25 — same phrasal verb, root-identity agent inference — verify). Quote opens here, unbalanced [sic].
* **`[27:68]`** "We were promised that before, we and our ancestors—these are nothing but legends of the ancients.""
  * **Gloss:** Near-verbatim with `[23:83]` ("We were promised this before—we and our ancestors...") — verified; "legends of the ancients" is the dismissive label (cf. "tales of the ancients" `[46:17]`, `[37:15]` register).
* **`[27:69]`** "Say, travel through the earth, and observe the fate of the guilty.""
  * **Gloss:** The archaeology command: resurrection argued from ruins (`[16:36]` "travel through the earth, and see what the fate of the deniers was" — near-verbatim, verified; cf. `[29:20]`, `[30:9]` family); Thamood's homes (27:52) are the field evidence.
* **`[27:70]`** "But do not grieve over them, and do not be troubled by what they plot."
  * **Gloss:** The preacher's relief: near-verbatim twin `[16:127]` "do not grieve over them, and do not be stressed by their schemes" (verified); kin of the s026 opening-rebuke `[26:3]` — grief is out of office when plots are already audited (27:74).
* **`[27:71]`** "And they say, "When is this promise, if you are truthful?""
  * **Gloss:** The date-demand, verbatim-class twin `[34:29]` "When is this promise due, if you are truthful?" (verified).
* **`[27:72]`** "Say, "Perhaps some of what you are impatient for has drawn near.""
  * **Gloss:** Non-answer answer: the asked-for nearness is already arriving (`[46:24]` "it is what you were impatient for: a wind" — verified; "has drawn near" family `[21:1]`, `[54:1]`); impatience answered by proximity.
* **`[27:73]`** "Your Lord is gracious towards humanity, but most of them are not thankful."
  * **Gloss:** The grace/ingratitude theorem, near-verbatim with `[40:61]` "Allah is gracious towards the people, but most people do not give thanks" (verified); the shukr-anchor in rebuke-key — third gratitude-verse of the surah after 27:19/40.
* **`[27:74]`** "And your Lord knows what their hearts conceal, and what they reveal."
  * **Gloss:** Verbatim with `[28:69]` (verified); the audits' knower (27:25) now runs the hearts' books.
* **`[27:75]`** "There is no mystery in the heaven and the earth, but it is in a Clear Book."
  * **Gloss:** The archive: every ghayb written — bracket to 27:1 "a book that makes things clear" (within-surah, verified); the Book does not only clarify the future, it records it (k-kalam).
* **`[27:76]`** "This Quran relates to the Children of Israel most of what they differ about."
  * **Gloss:** The clarifying function applied to Israel's disputes; twin construction `[16:64]` "to clarify for them what they differ about" (verified; cf. `[42:10]`, `[45:17]` dispute-judgment family).
* **`[27:77]`** "And it is guidance and mercy for the believers."
  * **Gloss:** Verbatim-formula cousin `[10:57]` "guidance and mercy for the believers", cf. `[16:64]` tail (verified); the surah's own address-list from 27:2 completes here.
* **`[27:78]`** "Your Lord will judge between them by His wisdom. He is the Almighty, the All-Knowing."
  * **Gloss:** Judgment-with-wisdom — the 27:6 divine names (Wise/Knowing) return as the verdict's credentials (within-surah ring, verified); "Your Lord will judge between them" twins `[32:25]`, `[10:93]`, `[45:17]` (verified).
* **`[27:79]`** "So rely on Allah. You are upon the clear truth."
  * **Gloss:** The only "rely on Allah"-command paired with "clear truth" (phrase unique, verified); tawakkul-cousins `[33:48]`, `[11:123]`; the reciter's footing after the mockers' "when?"
* **`[27:80]`** "You cannot make the dead hear, nor can you make the deaf hear the call if they turn their backs and flee."
  * **Gloss:** Near-verbatim `[30:52]` "You cannot make the dead hear, nor can you make the deaf hear the call when they turn away" (verified); hearing is the surah's test-metric — "see how they respond" (28) has found its dead.
* **`[27:81]`** "Nor can you guide the blind out of their straying. You can make no one listen, except those who believe in Our verses; for they are Muslims."
  * **Gloss:** The third incapacity (blind — echoing 27:66's "blind to it", within-surah); and the positive definition: those who listen *are* the muslimūn — the queen's verb (44) is now the community's name (arc 31→44→81→91).
* **`[27:82]`** "And when the Word has fallen on them, We will bring out for them from the earth a creature which will say to them that the people are uncertain of Our revelations."
  * **Gloss:** The dabbat: a speech-act from the soil, testifying at the end of the Word's verdict ("the Word has fallen" / "will come down" 27:85 — within-surah; cf. `[36:70]` "prove the Word against the faithless" — verified); "bring out... from the earth" answers the dust-doubters' "brought out" (67 — phrasal echo observable; root-identity agent inference — verify); "uncertain" closes the yaqin-descent from 27:3.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`kitab mubin` (Clear Book):** "it is in a Clear Book" `[27:75]`.
  * **Rendering:** same mubin family as 27:1 ("a book that makes things clear"), `[26:2]`, `[43:2]` — verified; here the Book is archive as well as address.
* **`qawl` (the Word) as verdict:** "when the Word has fallen" `[27:82]`; "The Word will come down upon them" `[27:85]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[36:70]` "prove the Word against the faithless" (verified); speech that arrives becomes event — the kalam note's channel-grammar in eschatological key.
* **`shukr` in rebuke:** "most of them are not thankful" `[27:73]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[40:61]` verbatim-class twin (verified); with 27:19/40 the surah holds three of the corpus's gratitude-loci — praise, test, rebuke.
* **`sama'/udhur` (hearing/excuse):** the ear-motif — "make the dead hear" (80), "make no one listen" (81), the hoopoe's "valid excuse" (21, root-identity agent inference — verify): the ruku's litmus is who can hear and who will answer.
