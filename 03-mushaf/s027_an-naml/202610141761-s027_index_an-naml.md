# Surah 27: THE ANT (an-Naml)

## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Meccan per the classical surah-lists; label "classical lists — verify". The corpus itself carries no nuzul statement; the Basmala heads the surah unnumbered (see [Basmala numbering](../../01-fleeting/202610132006-f-basmala_numbering_in_this_edition.md)).
* **Order flag (correction):** the task header says "order 93" — that is this surah's **ayah count**, not its position in the transmitted chronology. The standard lists place an-Naml at **48**, and this brain's own indices record the neighbors that way: ash-Shu'ara 47 (`202610141741-s026_index_al-shu-ara.md` §1), al-Qasas 49 (`202610141771-s028_index_al-qasas.md` §1). *Source: classical lists — verify in print.*
* **Size:** 93 ayahs (corpus line count verified: `[27:1]`–`[27:93]`, 93 lines), 7 ruku; map boundaries 14+17+13+14+8+16+11 = 93 ✓.
* **Central theme:** THE SOLOMON SURAH: knowledge as sovereignty. It opens by naming the Quran's sender "an All-Wise, All-Knowing", then shows that knowledge distributed — to Moses (nine signs), to David and Solomon (the language of birds, an ant's warning, a hoopoe's intelligence report), and finally to a queen who trades her magnificent throne for the Lord of the Sublime Throne. Three refused peoples (Pharaoh, Thamood, Lot) show what pride does with signs; five "Is there another god with Allah?" audits strip the rivals of their portfolios; and the surah closes on the Trumpet, mountains sailing like clouds, and a prophet commanded to worship the Lord of this town and to recite.

## 2. Task wording check — verified against the corpus
* `[27:1]` muqatta'at verbatim "Ta, Seen." — **two letters**; the neighbors truncate/expand: `[26:1]` and `[28:1]` both read "Ta, Seen, Meem." (verified). Link: [Muqatta'at](../../01-fleeting/202610132007-f-muqattaat_letters_untranslated.md).

Note the stray space in "All- Wise" — book typography, quoted [sic] (see [extraction artifacts](../../01-fleeting/202610141302-f-corpus_extraction_artifacts_fixed.md) class 2).
* **Ant's warning** `[27:18]` ✓ verbatim ("lest Solomon and his troops crush you without noticing"); "smiled" occurs nowhere else in the corpus (`[27:19]`).
* **Correction — gift-rejection ref:** the task dated Solomon's gift-rejection to 27:16; in this corpus `[27:16]` is "Solomon succeeded David... language of birds," and the gift episode is `[27:35–37]` ("Are you supplying me with money?... It is you who delight in your gift"). Verified.
* **Hoopoe report** `[27:20–28]` ✓ (roll-call 20–21; report 22–24; creed-line 25–26; dispatch 27–28).
* **The Letter's numbered Basmala** `[27:30]` ✓ "It is from Solomon, and it is, 'In the Name of Allah, the Gracious, the Merciful." — the only numbered mid-surah basmala in the whole corpus (rg-verified; only `[1:1]` and `[27:30]`). Opening quote is not closed until `[27:31]` — book typography, [sic].
* **Task wording vs corpus:** "one with knowledge of the Scripture" renders as **"He who had knowledge from the Book"** (`[27:40]`); the gratitude test line is verbatim ✓ "This is from the grace of my Lord, to test me, whether I am grateful or ungrateful" — the surah's strongest anchor for [Shukr](../../02-knowledge/202610141600-k-shukr_gratitude_audit.md) (which does not yet cite s027 — recorded as a gap).
* **Glass floor** `[27:44]` rendering verified: "she thought it was a deep pond, and she bared her legs... It is a palace paved with glass" ("pond" and "glass-floor" wording corpus-unique; "glass" otherwise only in the Light Verse `[24:35]`).
* **Winds/jinn service — correction:** SPRITES (jinn) serve Solomon here (`[27:17]`, `[27:39]` — "sprites" also at `[6:100]`, `[34:12]`, `[34:14]`); the WINDs' Solomonic service is **not in s027** — it belongs to `[21:81]` and `[34:12]`; s027 mentions winds only as "heralds of His mercy" (`[27:63]`, twin of `[7:57]` "sends the wind ahead of His mercy" — rendering drift).
* **Mountains** `[27:88]` ✓ "you see the mountains, and imagine them fixed, yet they pass, as the passing of the clouds—the making of Allah, who has perfected everything" (twin phrase `[32:7]`; cf. eschatological `[78:20]`).
* **Close** `[27:91–92]` ✓ "I was commanded to worship the Lord of this town, who has sanctified it... and I was commanded to be of those who submit. And to recite the Quran." But "guided for his own **soul**" is not this edition's wording: `[27:92]` reads "guided to his own **advantage**"; "for his own good" is `[17:15]`, "for his own soul" is `[31:12]` (verified — recorded as a correction).

## 4. Structural Arc
* **Credentials (1–6):** letters → Clarifying-Book claim → two human types → the sender named; brackets the surah at 27:75/92.
* **Moses dossier (7–14):** fire, staff, hand, nine signs — and rejection "although their souls were certain" (27:14): kufr as cover, answered by the Bilqis arc's unveiling.
* **Solomon empire (15–44):** knowledge → speech-empire (ant, hoopoe) → letter with God's Name → gift refused, throne outflanked, glass floor, submission. The surah's name-ant is the smallest voice in the largest court.
* **Two refusals (45–58):** Thamood's nine plotters and Lot's expelled town — the "see the outcome" formula of 27:14 cashed twice (27:51; rain 27:58 ↔ `[26:173]`).
* **Five audits + the unseen (59–66):** "Is there another god with Allah?" ×5 (corpus-exclusive), each with a different verdict-clause; the seal: none in the heavens or earth knows the future except Allah (27:65 ↔ 27:25 mysteries).
* **Reckoning and command (67–93):** dust-and-ancestors mockery → travel-through-the-earth rebuttal → the Word falls, the creature testifies → Trumpet, terrified heavens, moving mountains → virtue/evil ledger → the reciter commanded (91–92), praise closing (93 ↔ 15).
