# Thamood's nine plotters and Lot's expelled town: refusal's real estate (27:45–58)
**UID:** 202610141765
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 27:45–58 (Ruku 4 of 7 in an-Naml)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[27:45]` – `[27:58]` (14 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** From Sheba's court to the rocky town and the doomed city: two peoples refuse their brother-prophet, and the surah shows the property record — homes in ruins, a rained-upon town — cashing the "see how the outcome was" formula installed at 27:14.
* **Placement:** Ruku 4; the destruction interlude between the Solomon narrative (r2–r3) and the five tawhid audits (r5). Refusal is now staged as *planning*, and planning as self-deception.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[27:45]`** "And We sent to Thamood their brother Saleh: "Worship Allah." But they became two disputing factions."
  * **Gloss:** The brother-summons in one breath (as in the s026 cycles — `[26:141–159]`); the town's answer is polarization, not argument; "two disputing factions" wording is corpus-unique (verified).
* **`[27:46]`** "He said, "O my people, why are you quick to do evil rather than good? If only you would seek Allah's forgiveness, so that you may be shown mercy.""
  * **Gloss:** The indictment is tempo — evil rushed ahead of good; the door left open is forgiveness-for-mercy.
* **`[27:47]`** "They said, "We consider you an ill omen, and those with you." He said, "Your omen is with Allah. In fact, you are a people being tested.""
  * **Gloss:** Superstition laundered as prophecy-aversion; the twin exchange at `[7:131]` ("their omen is with Allah") and the envoys' answer at `[36:18–19]` (verified); "being tested" ties to 27:40's test — same balwa-grammar (within-surah).
* **`[27:48]`** "In the city was a gang of nine who made mischief in the land and did no good."
  * **Gloss:** Nine plotters — the number returns from Moses' nine signs (`[27:12]`, `[17:101]`), now as a strike-team (Source: observable numeral reuse; literary intent — agent inference — verify); "did no good" completes the reversal of 27:46.
* **`[27:49]`** "They said, "Swear by Allah to one another that we will attack him and his family by night, and then tell his guardian, 'We did not witness the murder of his family, and we are being truthful.'""
  * **Gloss:** A conspiracy with a pre-written perjury: night, family, guardian, alibi — the oath itself is a weapon; unbalanced quote brackets [sic, book typography].
* **`[27:50]`** "They planned a plan, and We planned a plan, but they did not notice."
  * **Gloss:** The makr-counter, near-verbatim with `[8:30]` "They planned, and Allah planned, but Allah is the Best of planners"; also `[12:102]` "they plotted and agreed on a plan" (all verified).
* **`[27:51]`** "So note the outcome of their planning; We destroyed them and their people, altogether."
  * **Gloss:** The 27:14 formula returns ("So see how the outcome was for the mischief-makers" — within-surah echo verified); "altogether" removes the faction-question of 27:45.
* **`[27:52]–[27:53]`** "Here are their homes, in ruins, on account of their iniquities. Surely in this is a sign for people who know. / And We saved those who believed and were pious."
  * **Gloss:** Real estate as sermon — "in ruins" recurs at `[22:45]` (verified); the sign is for "people who know," the surah's standing census; the saved formula "believed and were pious" is the taqwa-rescue clause (k-sabr's dialect).
* **`[27:54]`** "And Lot, when he said to his people, "Do you commit lewdness with open eyes?"
  * **Gloss:** The second dossier opens; "with open eyes" makes the crime sighted — knowingly (cf. 27:14's inward-certainty motif, inverted).
* **`[27:55]`** "Do you lust after men instead of women? You are truly ignorant people.""
  * **Gloss:** Near-verbatim twin `[7:81]` "You lust after men rather than women" (verified); the verdict-word is *ignorance* — the surah's anti-knowledge.
* **`[27:56]`** "But the only response of his people was to say, "Expel the family of Lot from your town. They are purist people.""
  * **Gloss:** The town's one-line answer, word-for-word kin to `[7:82]` ("Expel them from your town; they are purist people" — "purist" shared, verified); purity named as an accusation.
* **`[27:57]`** "So We saved him and his family, except for his wife, whom We destined to be among the laggards."
  * **Gloss:** Rescue with one exception; "destined" (qada) forecloses appeal; the same wife is "who will remain behind" at `[29:32–33]` (verified — rendering drift "laggards"/"remain behind").
* **`[27:58]`** "And We rained upon them a rain. Miserable was the rain of those forewarned."
  * **Gloss:** The refrain-twin `[26:173]` "And We rained down on them a rain. Dreadful is the rain of those forewarned" (verified — near-verbatim, tense/adjective drift; the s026 index's "verbatim" label corrected); "those forewarned" also closes `[37:177]`.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`ta'ir` (omen):** "We consider you an ill omen... Your omen is with Allah" `[27:47]`.
  * **Rendering:** "omen"; the transfer-move — relocating luck from bodies to Allah — is `[7:131]`'s exactly; cf. `[36:19]` "Your evil omen is upon you" (verified).
* **`makr` (plan/scheme):** `[27:49–51]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[8:30]` (Allah as best planner), `[12:102]` (plot discovered in narrative past), `[40:5]` ("Every community plotted against their messenger" — verified) — Thamood's nine join a corpus-wide guild.
* **`'aqibah` (outcome):** "So note the outcome" `[27:51]`.
  * **Rendering:** welded here to the destruction report; the same formula at 27:14 (Pharaoh) — the surah runs one argument twice on two maps.
* **`mundhar` (forewarned):** "the rain of those forewarned" `[27:58]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[26:173]`, `[37:177]` (verified); warning is the charge the audits (r5) and the warners (27:92) keep re-billing.
