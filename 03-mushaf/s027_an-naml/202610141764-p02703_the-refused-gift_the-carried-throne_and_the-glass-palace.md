# The refused gift, the carried throne, and the glass palace: Sheba submits (27:32–44)
**UID:** 202610141764
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 27:32–44 (Ruku 3 of 7 in an-Naml)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[27:32]` – `[27:44]` (13 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** A queen's four defenses — council, courage, political realism, bribery — fall one by one, and the throne that out-runs her envoys finally out-runs her eyes: what she mistook for water was glass, and what she mistook for sovereignty was a test of thanks.
* **Placement:** Ruku 3; the second half of the Solomon diptych (r2 dispatches the letter, r3 receives it). The surah's submission-arc peaks here (44), and its gratitude-anchor verse (40) sits at the center.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[27:32]`** "She said, "O counselors, advise me in this matter of mine. I never make a decision unless you are present.""
  * **Gloss:** The counter-portfolio to Solomon's court: rule by consultation; "Counselors" s027-unique (verified).
* **`[27:33]`** "They said, "We are a people of might and great courage, but the decision is yours, so consider what you wish to command.""
  * **Gloss:** Force on tap, authority declined — the military defers to the queen, setting up her realism at 34.
* **`[27:34]`** "She said, "When kings enter a city, they devastate it, and subjugate its dignified people. Thus they always do."
  * **Gloss:** Political theory from a sitting monarch: kingship enters by wrecking; the verse will be out-argued at 91, where one Lord of a town sanctified it (within-surah bracket, observable).
* **`[27:35]`** "I am sending them a gift, and will see what the envoys bring back.""
  * **Gloss:** The fourth defense: money as reconnaissance; "envoys" (rusul) is the corpus's word for *divine* envoys (`[15:57]`, `[11:77]`, `[29:31]` — verified) — her commercial mission echoes a heavenly one.
* **`[27:36]`** "When he came to Solomon, he said, "Are you supplying me with money? What Allah has given me is better than what He has given you. It is you who delight in your gift."
  * **Gloss:** The gift-rejection (task's "27:16" corrected — index §2): Solomon's economics run on givenness, not accumulation — cf. the divine gift addressed to Solomon, `[38:39]` "This is Our gift; so give generously, or withhold" (verified); "delight in your gift" inverts the shukr circuit (gift enjoyed, Giver unthanked).
* **`[27:37]`** "Go back to them. We will come upon them with troops they cannot resist; and we will expel them from there, disgraced and humiliated.""
  * **Gloss:** The ultimatum returned; "cannot resist" phrasing is corpus-unique here (verified); force is stated and *not used* — the next move is a teleport.
* **`[27:38]`** "He said, "O notables, which one of you will bring me her throne before they come to me in submission?""
  * **Gloss:** The test is an epistemic ambush: meet the throne before the monarch does; "come to me in submission" re-quotes the letter's word (31, verified).
* **`[27:39]`** "An imp of the sprites said, "I will bring it to you before you rise from your seat. I am strong and reliable enough to do it.""
  * **Gloss:** "An imp" ifrit — phrase unique in the corpus (verified); his credentials "strong and reliable" are the phrase the Median shepherd-daughter uses of Moses, `[28:26]` "the strong and trustworthy" (verified) — two hires, one vocabulary.
* **`[27:40]`** "He who had knowledge from the Book said, "I will bring it to you before your glance returns to you." And when he saw it settled before him, he said, "This is from the grace of my Lord, to test me, whether I am grateful or ungrateful. He who is grateful, his gratitude is to his own credit; but he who is ungrateful—my Lord is Independent and Generous.""
  * **Gloss:** The unnamed knowledge-man out-bids the ifrit with a blink; his first instinct on miracle-success is the gratitude audit — the surah's anchor for [Shukr]: "Independent" matches `[39:7]`'s self-sufficient-lord-that-rejects-ingratitude, and "gratitude is to his own credit" twins Luqman's `[31:12]` "appreciative... for his own soul" (verified). Identity unnamed in the text; classical exegetes name Asif b. Barkhiya — Source: classical tafsir — verify.
* **`[27:41]`** "He said, "Disguise her throne for her, and we shall see whether she will be guided, or remains one of the misguided.""
  * **Gloss:** "Disguise" unique here (verified); the test re-frames as guidance-or-error — the surah's spine-term lands on the queen personally.
* **`[27:42]`** "When she arrived, it was said, "Is your throne like this?" She said, "As if this is it." "We were given knowledge before her, and we were submissive.""
  * **Gloss:** "As if this is it" — recognition without certainty, the throne made strange; the closing clause's speakers are unnamed in the text (Solomon's court, most naturally — Source: agent inference — verify); "given knowledge before her" answers the hoopoe's "you did not know" (22, within-surah).
* **`[27:43]`** "But she was prevented by what she worshiped besides Allah; she belonged to a disbelieving people."
  * **Gloss:** Worship as a veil on perception: the sun cult blocks the evidence it already half-grasps; direct callback to 27:24 (within-surah).
* **`[27:44]`** "It was said to her, "Go inside the palace." And when she saw it, she thought it was a deep pond, and she bared her legs. He said, "It is a palace paved with glass." She said, "My Lord, I have done wrong to myself, and I have submitted with Solomon, to Allah, Lord of the Worlds.""
  * **Gloss:** Task rendering verified: "deep pond" (word unique corpus-wide), "paved with glass" — "glass" otherwise occurs only in the Light Verse `[24:35]` (verified); the illusion of depth teaches the difference between surface and substance; her confession welds self-blame ("I have done wrong to myself") to submission-with-Solomon — the arc of 31/38/42 completes; "Lord of the Worlds" returns to 27:8's formula.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`balwa/ibtala` (test):** "to test me, whether I am grateful or ungrateful" `[27:40]`.
  * **Rendering:** "test"; the same verb-idea runs the surah — "you are a people being tested" (47) and the knowledge-gifted trial at `[6:165]` "in order to test you through what He has given you" (verified) — blessing *is* the exam.
* **`hadya` (gift):** "I am sending them a gift... It is you who delight in your gift" `[27:35–36]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** English "gift" elsewhere marks *divine* giving — `[38:39]` to Solomon, `[21:72]` to Abraham's line, "a fitting gift" `[78:36]` (verified) — the queen offers the only human gift-verb in the cluster (Source: observable rendering; Arabic-root identity — agent inference — verify).
* **`madinah/mulk` (town/kingship):** "When kings enter a city" `[27:34]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** bracketed by "the Lord of this town, who has sanctified it" `[27:91]` — devastators versus the One whose town is safe (`[28:57]` "Safe Sanctuary... provision from Ourselves" — verified): the surah's politics verse-by-verse.
* **`shukr/kufr` pair at 27:40:** "grateful or ungrateful" — the only such pair-wording in s027; twins `[31:12]` (appreciative for his own soul) and `[39:7]` (He does not approve ingratitude) — verified via the Shukr note's evidence list.
