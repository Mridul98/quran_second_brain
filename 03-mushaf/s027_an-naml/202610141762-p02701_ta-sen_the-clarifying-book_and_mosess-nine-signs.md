# Ta, Seen: the Clarifying Book, the Wise-Knowing sender, and Moses' nine signs (27:1–14)
**UID:** 202610141762
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 27:1–14 (Ruku 1 of 7 in an-Naml)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[27:1]` – `[27:14]` (14 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah files its credentials — letters, Book-claim, two verdicts on two human types, the sender's names — then hands the reader Moses' dossier as evidence: the same clarifying signs were once refused "out of wickedness and pride" by a court that knew better.
* **Placement:** Ruku 1 of 7; the frame of the Solomon surah. The Book-claim brackets the whole surah: 27:1 "a book that makes things clear" ↔ 27:75 "a Clear Book" ↔ 27:92 the command "to recite the Quran."

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[27:1]`** "Ta, Seen. These are the Signs of the Quran— a book that makes things clear."
  * **Gloss:** Untranslated letter-names (two here; `[26:1]`/`[28:1]` carry the expanded "Ta, Seen, Meem." — verified), immediately followed by a Book-claim — the pattern the Muqatta'at note catalogs. Em-dash/lower-case [sic].
* **`[27:2]`** "Guidance and good news for the believers."
  * **Gloss:** The Book's two addresses: direction and cheer; near-twin `[16:102]` "guidance and good news for those who submit" (verified).
* **`[27:3]`** "Those who observe the prayers, and give charity regularly, and are certain of the Hereafter."
  * **Gloss:** The believer census — prayer, zakat, Hereafter-certainty; near-verbatim twins `[31:4]` and `[2:4]` "are certain of the Hereafter" (verified).
* **`[27:4]`** "As for those who do not believe in the Hereafter: We made their deeds appear good to them, so they wander aimlessly."
  * **Gloss:** The inverse: cosmetic deeds, roadless motion ("wander aimlessly" — elsewhere only the desert generation, `[5:26]`); the divine passive anticipates Satanic cosmetics at 27:24 (within surah).
* **`[27:5]`** "It is they who will receive the grievous punishment—and in the Hereafter they will be the greatest losers."
  * **Gloss:** Two-loss verdict; "the greatest losers" recurs only at `[18:103]` (verified).
* **`[27:6]`** "You are receiving the Quran from an All- Wise, All-Knowing."
  * **Gloss:** The sender's names — revelation as delivery from a living address (kalam); the "Wise+Knowing" pair is corpus-unique in this order (`[66:2]` inverts it); "All- Wise" spacing is book typography [sic].
* **`[27:7]`** "When Moses said to his family, "I have glimpsed a fire. I will bring you some news from it; or bring you a firebrand, that you may warm yourselves.""
  * **Gloss:** The dossier opens mid-journey; the fire-glimpse triple: here "glimpsed... firebrand", `[28:29]` "noticed... ember", `[20:10]` "saw... torch" (verified).
* **`[27:8]`** "Then, when he reached it, he was called: "Blessed is He who is within the fire, and He who is around it, and glorified be Allah, Lord of the Worlds."
  * **Gloss:** The blessing formula is corpus-unique; `[28:30]` instead calls "from the right side of the valley, at the Blessed Spot" — the two tellings differ in wording (verified).
* **`[27:9]`** "O Moses, it is I, Allah, the Almighty, the Wise."
  * **Gloss:** Direct self-naming; the Wise-name of 27:6 now speaks to Moses personally.
* **`[27:10]`** "Throw down your staff." But when he saw it quivering, as though it were a demon, he turned around not looking back. "O Moses, do not fear; the messengers do not fear in My presence."
  * **Gloss:** Fear is licensed then revoked; the snake-panic twin `[28:31]` renders it "wiggling, as if it were possessed" (verified); "the messengers do not fear in My presence" is the dossier's charter line.
* **`[27:11]`** "But whoever has done wrong, and then substituted goodness in place of evil. I am Forgiving and Merciful."
  * **Gloss:** The amnesty clause — the conditional left syntactically open [sic, book typography]; repentance is the only crime's sequel.
* **`[27:12]`** "Put your hand inside your pocket, and it will come out white, without blemish— among nine miracles to Pharaoh and his people, for they are immoral people.""
  * **Gloss:** The hand-sign; "pocket... white, without blemish" is verbatim `[28:32]` (verified); the count "nine" echoes here and at 27:48 and is tallied at `[17:101]` "nine clear signs" (verified).
* **`[27:13]`** "Yet when Our enlightening signs came to them, they said, "This is obvious witchcraft.""
  * **Gloss:** The court's verdict; "Our enlightening signs" phrases only otherwise at `[34:43]` "Our enlightening Verses" (verified); the slur family — "plain magic" `[6:7]`, `[37:15]`, "obvious sorcery" `[61:6]`, `[5:110]`.
* **`[27:14]`** "And they rejected them, although their souls were certain of them, out of wickedness and pride. So see how the outcome was for the mischief-makers."
  * **Gloss:** The surah's definition of denial: inward certainty plus outward refusal — kufr-as-cover in [Shukr]'s exact sense (`[16:83]`); the "see the outcome" formula returns at 27:51 for Thamood.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`al-Kitab al-Mubin` family** (that which makes clear):
  * **Rendering:** "a book that makes things clear" `[27:1]`; the cluster is documented in the s026 note — `[26:2]`, `[15:1]`, `[43:2]` — and re-asserted in this surah at `[27:75]` "a Clear Book" (bracket, verified).
  * **Elsewhere:** clarification is the Book's self-staked claim; here it names the *signs* themselves ("These are the Signs").
* **`hudan` (guidance) + `bushra` (good news):** "Guidance and good news for the believers" `[27:2]`.
  * **Rendering:** paired here; `[16:102]` welds them to "those who submit", `[10:57]` re-welds "guidance and mercy for the believers" (27:77's twin).
  * **Elsewhere:** the guided/misguided axis runs the whole surah — 24, 41, 63, 81, 92.
* **`zuwwina` (made to appear good):** `[27:4]` "We made their deeds appear good".
  * **Rendering:** divine passive here; at `[27:24]` Satan is the agent ("Satan made their conduct appear good"); agents elsewhere: `[6:43]`, `[8:48]` (Satan), `[13:33]`, `[40:37]`, `[47:14]` (neutral passive) — verified.
  * **Force:** 27:4 and 27:24 are the same disease at two scales — the wanderers of the opening and the sun-worshippers of the hoopoe report.
* **`yaqin` (certainty):** "certain of the Hereafter" `[27:3]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:4]`, `[31:4]` twins; the surah then *downgrades* certainty across the arc — inward certainty paid for denial (27:14), knowledge "confused... in doubt... blind" (27:66), "people are uncertain of Our revelations" (27:82) — verified within s027.
