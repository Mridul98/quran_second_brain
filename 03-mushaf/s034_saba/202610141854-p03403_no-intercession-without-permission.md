# No intercession without permission (34:22–30)
**UID:** 202610141854
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 34:22–30 (Ruku 3 of 6 in Saba')
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[34:22]` – `[34:30]` (9 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The associates' portfolio is audited to zero — no ownership, no share, no backers, no intercession except by permission — and the reductios stack ("Who provides?"; "Show me those you attached") until the taunt "When is this promise due?" meets a Day that cannot be moved by one hour.
* **Placement:** Ruku 3 of 6; the surah's creedal courtroom. The atom's-weight clause of 34:3 is weaponized here: God's record reaches every atom (3), the partners do not own one (22). The "rightly guided / evident error" binary of 34:24 re-opens the guidance question ruku 1 closed at 34:6.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[34:22]`** "Say, “Call upon those whom you claim besides Allah. They possess not an atom's weight in the heavens or the earth, and they possess no share of either, and He has no backers from among them.”"
  * **Gloss:** Three negatives — no property (even an atom's weight), no partnership share, no co-ownership: "backers" is this corpus's unique rendering of the partner-category (Source: translator's term choice, observable in corpus). The claim is invited to a test: "Call upon those..." — near-verbatim twin at `[17:56]`.
* **`[34:23]`** "Intercession with Him is of no value, except for someone He has permitted. Until, when fear has subsided from their hearts, they will say, “What did your Lord say?” They will say, “The truth, and He is the High, the Great.”"
  * **Gloss:** The intercession dismissal, verified — wording correction: the task's quotation ("on that Day no intercession will benefit except those whom the Most Merciful permits") is `[20:109]`; here the formula is "Intercession with Him is of no value, except for someone He has permitted." The second half stages heaven's council: the angels, fear-calmed, ask what their Lord said, and answer: "The truth" — revelation as speech even above (kalam anchor).
* **`[34:24]`** "Say, “Who provides for you from the heavens and the earth?” Say, “Allah. And Either you, or we, are rightly guided, or in evident error.”
  * **Gloss:** The provider reductio (this — not 34:27 — is the verse the task numbered for the reductio tradition); then the most honest dare in the Quran: the claimant proposes to submit truth to procedure — "Either you, or we, are rightly guided, or in evident error" — with mid-verse capitalization "And Either" as book typography (see Fleeting link).
* **`[34:25]`** "Say, “You will not be asked about our misdeeds, nor will we be asked about what you do.”"
  * **Gloss:** The coin-flip's fine print: no shared ledger; each party answers only for itself — the courtroom is divided before it is convened.
* **`[34:26]`** "Say, “Our Lord will bring us together; then He will judge between us equitably. He is the All-Knowing Judge.”"
  * **Gloss:** The gathering is the referee: "He is the All-Knowing Judge" — knowledge (34:3's premise) installed on the bench.
* **`[34:27]`** "Say, “Show me those you have attached to Him as associates. No indeed! But He is Allah, the Powerful, the Wise.”"
  * **Gloss:** The "Show me those you associated" reductio, verified — located here at 34:27 (the task guessed 34:24): produce the list, name the file, and the answer is a confession instead: "No indeed! But He is Allah, the Powerful, the Wise."
* **`[34:28]`** "We sent you only universally to all people, a herald and warner, but most people do not know."
  * **Gloss:** The mission clause: universal address, dual office (herald-and-warner, verbatim twin of `[25:56]`); the refrain "but most people do not know" — Saba's signature epistemic verdict (again at 34:36).
* **`[34:29]`** "And they say, “When is this promise due, if you are truthful?”"
  * **Gloss:** The timing taunt — the same voice as 34:3's "The Hour will not come upon us," now asking when it will.
* **`[34:30]`** "Say, “You are promised a Day, which you cannot postpone by one hour, nor bring forward.”"
  * **Gloss:** The answer privatizes the schedule from man to Day itself: not one hour's delay or advance (near-twin `[16:61]` "they will not delay it by one hour, nor will they advance it").

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`shafa'a (intercession)`** `[34:23]`:
  * **Rendering:** "Intercession with Him is of no value, except for someone He has permitted" — negation with a permission-gate, not annihilation of the concept.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:255]` ("Who is he that can intercede with Him except with His permission?"), `[20:109]`, `[53:26]`, `[39:44]` ("Say, 'All intercession is up to Allah'"), `[2:48]` — the permission-clause family this verse belongs to.
* **`backers`** `[34:22]`:
  * **Rendering:** "He has no backers from among them" — the unique corpus use; the rival concept is "ally out of weakness" at `[17:111]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[17:111]` (no partner in sovereignty, no ally out of weakness), `[25:3]` (impotent fabricated gods) — the partner-category drained of every corporate function.
* **`herald and warner`** `[34:28]`:
  * **Rendering:** "a herald and warner" (bashir/nadhir pair).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[25:56]` ("We sent you only as a herald of good news and a warner" — verbatim opening "We sent you only"), `[35:24]` ("There is no community but a warner has passed through it" — universalism twin), `[2:119]` (sent "bringing good news, and giving warnings").
* **`postpone... by one hour`** `[34:30]`:
  * **Rendering:** "which you cannot postpone by one hour, nor bring forward."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[16:61]` verbatim-idiom twin ("they will not delay it by one hour, nor will they advance it"); the timing-taunt answer pairs with the Hour-denial oath of `[34:3]` within this surah.
