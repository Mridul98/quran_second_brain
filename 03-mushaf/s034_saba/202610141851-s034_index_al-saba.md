* **Size:** 54 ayahs, 6 ruku (`data/ruku_map.json`: 9+12+9+6+9+9 = 54 ✓). Mushaf position 34, between al-Ahzab (33) and Fatir (35); both neighbor folders are not yet present at write time (BUILD_STATE: s029–s033 in progress).
* **Name:** Saba’ — Sheba; the corpus title is SHEBA and the name's anchor verse is `[34:15]` "In Sheba’s homeland there used to be a wonder: two gardens, on the right, and on the left."
* **Central theme:** Praise audited by ingratitude. The surah opens by giving all praise to the One whose ownership and knowledge reach every atom (1–3), then runs two case-studies of favor — the workshop of David/Solomon ("work with appreciation," 10–14) and the landscape of Sheba ("Eat… and give thanks," 15–19) — both of which show the favor dismantled or withheld when the audit fails. Intercession, associates, wealth, and children are then stripped of protective value (22–30, 31–45), and the surah closes on the Truth that has arrived while the mockers are seized "from a nearby place" and a barrier falls (46–54).
* **Theme checks the task asked to verify honestly (against the corpus):** `[34:1]` praise-doctrine ✓ — but this edition's wording is "Praise be to Allah, to Whom belongs everything in the heavens and the earth; and praise be to Him in the Hereafter" (not "all that is in the heavens and all that is in the earth… to Him belongs the praise"). David 34:10–11 ✓ "And We softened iron for him… 'Make coats of armor, and measure the links well; and work righteousness'" — "full coats of mail" / "righteous deeds" are other translations' words. Two gardens 34:15–19 ✓ — but the word is "wonder" (not "sign"), "give thanks" (not "be grateful"), and "A good land" (not "pleasant land"); the flood of the dam ✓ 34:16, "for their ingratitude" ✓ 34:17. Intercession 34:23 ✓ in concept, but the task's quotation ("on that Day no intercession will benefit except those whom the Most Merciful permits") is `[20:109]`'s wording; 34:23 reads "Intercession with Him is of no value, except for someone He has permitted" plus the fear-subside dialogue. "Our Lord, You have destined this for us?" ✗ — that phrase is nowhere in the corpus; 34:3 is instead the denial "The Hour will not come upon us" answered by an oath, and the Day's blame-dialogue is 34:31–33 ("Were it not for you, we would have been believers" ✓ 34:31). "Show me those you attached to Him as associates" is at `[34:27]`, **not** 34:24 (34:24 is the "Who provides for you?" reductio) — both genuine tawhid candidates in ruku 3. "They will recognize it as they recognize their sons" ✗ in s034 — that is `[2:146]`/`[6:20]`; Saba's cognate is `[34:6]` "Those who received knowledge know that what is revealed to you from your Lord is the truth" (kalam anchor, ruku 1). Closing: "but most people do not believe" ✗ in s034 (it is `[13:1]`, `[11:17]`, `[40:59]`); Saba's refrain is "but most people do not know" (`[34:28]`, `[34:36]`). The actual last verse is `[34:54]` "A barrier will be placed between them and what they desire… They were in disturbing doubt" — there is **no** closing "they say: Praise be to Allah" in Saba (that is `[39:75]`/`[7:43]`/`[10:10]`); praise brackets only the **opening** (34:1).

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Foundation (1–9):** praise → total ownership → four-directional knowledge → the sworn "it will come upon you" → the two recompense classes → the knowers who recognize the revealed truth (6) → the resurrection mockers (7–8) → earth-cave-in/sky-fall sanction, "a sign for every devout servant" (9).
* **Favor case-studies (10–21):** David's iron and the armor commission fused to "work righteousness" (10–11); Solomon's wind, tar-spring, sprites and monuments capped by "O House of David, work with appreciation… but a few of My servants are appreciative" (12–13); the earthworm at the staff exposing the sprites' unseen-ignorance (14); Sheba's two gardens, the eat-and-thank command, the dam-flood, the bitter substitution, "Would We penalize any but the ungrateful?" (15–17); easy travel refused, the people "made… history" (18–19); Satan's assessment confirmed, but only as a test — "Your Lord is Guardian over all things" (20–21).
* **The audit of associates (22–30):** zero ownership/share/backers (22); intercession void without permission and the angels' report "The truth" (23); the provider reductio and the honest coin-flip "Either you, or we, are rightly guided" (24); the deferred mutual judgment (25–26); "Show me those you attached to Him as associates" (27); the universal herald-and-warner claim and the taunt "When is this promise due?" answered by the immovable Day (28–30).
* **The Day's blame-trade (31–36):** "We will never believe in this Quran, nor in what came before it" (31); oppressed vs arrogant re-litigating responsibility (32–33); the affluent reflex as history ("We sent no warner to any town, without its affluent saying…") (34) and the wealth-boast (35); bounty spread or restricted, "but most people do not know" (36).
* **Wealth dethroned (37–45):** nearness bought only by belief-and-work — double reward, the Chambers, safety (37); the counter-summons (38); spend-and-be-replaced, "He is the Best of providers" (39); the angels asked "Was it you these used to worship?" — the jinn-indictment (40–41); the profit/harm freeze (42); the three "nothing but" accusations vs a people who never had a book or a warner (43–44); the one-tenth comparison and "how was My disapproval?" (45).
* **Arrival (46–54):** the single advice, the no-madness defense (46); the zero-fee witness (47); truth-projecting, unseen-knowing Lord (48); "The Truth has come; while falsehood can neither originate, nor regenerate" (49); guidance/error privatized, "He is Hearing and Near" (50); terror seized "from a nearby place," late belief unreachable "from a distant place," allegations "from a far-off place" (51–53); the barrier between them and what they desire — the surah closes in the same epistemic key it opened (3), with doubt (54).
