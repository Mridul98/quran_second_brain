# Neither wealth nor children: spend and be replaced (34:37–45)
**UID:** 202610141856
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 34:37–45 (Ruku 5 of 6 in Saba')
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[34:37]` – `[34:45]` (9 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The economy of nearness is rewritten — wealth and children buy nothing, belief-and-work buys double, and every coin spent is replaced by the Best of providers — while on the Day even the worshiped angels disown their worshipers, and the deniers of every age repeat the same three "nothing but" accusations against the recited truth.
* **Placement:** Ruku 5 of 6; the positive law (37–39) following ruku 4's wealth-boast (35–36), then the Day's cross-examination (40–42) and the accusation-history of the Book (43–45). Verse 38 closes the "seeking to undermine them" bracket opened at 34:5.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[34:37]`** "It is neither your wealth nor your children that bring you closer to Us, but it is he who believes and does good deeds. These will have a double reward for what they did; and they will reside in the Chambers, in peace and security."
  * **Gloss:** Direct repeal of 34:35's immunity-assets; the wage is doubled, the address is "the Chambers" (unique in this corpus), the atmosphere "in peace and security" (the Paradise formula of 15:46/44:55).
* **`[34:38]`** "But those who work against Our revelations, seeking to undermine them—those will be summoned to the punishment."
  * **Gloss:** The bracket closes: "seeking to undermine them" verbatim from 34:5 — the same class that got "a painful plague" there is "summoned to the punishment" here; the revelation-war is the surah's named crime.
* **`[34:39]`** "Say, “My Lord extends the provision to whomever He wills of His servants, or withholds it. Anything you spend, He will replace it. He is the Best of providers.”"
  * **Gloss:** The spend-and-be-replaced axiom — the infaq economy's cash leg: provision is a distribution, not a possession, and replacement is promised as an attribute of God ("the Best of providers," his fixed title, here with the spend-command).
* **`[34:40]`** "On the Day when He gathers them all together, then say to the angels, “Was it you these used to worship?”"
  * **Gloss:** The cross-examination of the highest claimant class: even angels must answer for being worshiped — the question itself is the verdict (cf. 46:5's "Who is more wrong than him who invokes... those who will not answer him").
* **`[34:41]`** "They will say, “Be You glorified; You are our Master, not them. In fact, they used to worship the jinn, and most of them had faith in them.”"
  * **Gloss:** The renunciation (46:6's "they will be enemies to them, and will renounce their worship" staged in direct speech) and the exposure: the real addressees were the jinn — the same attribution-line as 6:100/6:128's jinn-assembly.
* **`[34:42]`** "“Today, none of you has the power to profit or harm the other.” And We will say to those who did wrong, “Taste the agony of the Fire which you used to deny.”"
  * **Gloss:** The mutual freeze: worshiper and worshiped can neither help nor hurt (the gods of 25:3 lack even self-help; here they lack each other); the Fire is addressed as the thing "which you used to deny."
* **`[34:43]`** "And when Our enlightening Verses are recited to them, they say, “This is nothing but a man who wants to divert you from what your ancestors used to worship.” And they say, “This is nothing but a fabricated lie.” And when the Truth comes to them, the blasphemers say of the Truth, “This is nothing but plain magic.”"
  * **Gloss:** The triple "nothing but": a man (against the messenger), a fabricator (against the message), a magician (against the effect) — the same accusation cluster as `[25:4]` ("a lie that he made up") and `[28:36]` ("fabricated magic"); "divert" is the path-security verb of 6:116/6:153.
* **`[34:44]`** "But We gave them no book to study, and We did not send them any warner before you."
  * **Gloss:** The defense collapses on evidence: the accused ancestors never had a book or a warner — the universal warner-history of 34:34/35:24 cuts the other way here: this audience's excuse is invention, not inheritance.
* **`[34:45]`** "Those before them also denied the Truth, yet they have not attained one-tenth of what We had given them. They rejected My messengers, so how was My disapproval?"
  * **Gloss:** The comparison: stronger predecessors denied and fell; "one-tenth" (unique in this corpus) measures present privilege as under-age; "how was My disapproval?" — the fixed rhetorical verdict, near-verbatim at `[67:18]`.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`double reward / the Chambers`** `[34:37]`:
  * **Rendering:** "a double reward for what they did; and they will reside in the Chambers, in peace and security."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[33:31]` ("We will give her a double reward; and We have prepared for her a generous provision" — the only other "double reward"); "the Chambers" unique here; "in peace and security" at `[15:46]`, `[44:55]`.
* **`spend... He will replace it`** `[34:39]`:
  * **Rendering:** "Anything you spend, He will replace it. He is the Best of providers." ("will replace it" — unique clause in this corpus.)
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:245]` and `[57:11]` (the loan-reward grammar); "Best of providers" at `[22:58]`, `[62:11]`, `[5:114]`, `[23:72]` — the title welded to the economy.
* **`worship the jinn`** `[34:41]`:
  * **Rendering:** "In fact, they used to worship the jinn, and most of them had faith in them."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[6:128]` ("O assembly of jinn, you have exploited multitudes of humans"), `[6:100]` (sprites attributed as partners) — the jinn-cult exposed at both ends of revelation.
* **`fabricated lie / plain magic / My disapproval`** `[34:43–45]`:
  * **Rendering:** the triple "nothing but" (man / fabricated lie / magic); "how was My disapproval?"
  * **Elsewhere:** `[25:4]` ("a lie that he made up"), `[28:36]` ("fabricated magic"), `[67:18]` ("Those before them also denied the truth; and how was My disapproval?") — accusation-and-verdict formulas common to the Meccan polemical corpus.
