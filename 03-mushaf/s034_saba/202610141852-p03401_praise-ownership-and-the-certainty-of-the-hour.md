# Praise, ownership, and the certainty of the Hour (34:1–9)
**UID:** 202610141852
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 34:1–9 (Ruku 1 of 6 in Saba')
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[34:1]` – `[34:9]` (9 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah's foundation stone: praise belongs to Allah in this world and the next because everything in the heavens and earth — down to the atom and smaller — is His property and His knowledge, so the Hour the mockers deny is as certain as the record that already lists it.
* **Placement:** Ruku 1 of 6; the creedal premise (owner → knower → reckoner) on which the favor case-studies (ruku 2) and the Day's dialogues (ruku 4–6) run. Its "Knower of the unseen" (3) is answered at the surah's close (48).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[34:1]`** "Praise be to Allah, to Whom belongs everything in the heavens and the earth; and praise be to Him in the Hereafter. He is the Wise, the Expert."
  * **Gloss:** The opening praise-doctrine, verified verbatim (the task's "to Him belongs the praise in the Hereafter" is a paraphrase; this edition doubles the praise: "and praise be to Him in the Hereafter"). Ownership grounds doxology; "the Wise, the Expert" pairs arranging creation with knowing it through and through.
* **`[34:2]`** "He knows what penetrates into the earth, and what comes out of it, and what descends from the sky, and what ascends to it. He is the Merciful, the Forgiving."
  * **Gloss:** Knowledge in four directions — in, out, down, up — no corridor unwatched; and the total surveillance is named with mercy-names, not threat-names.
* **`[34:3]`** "Those who disbelieve say, “The Hour will not come upon us.” Say, “Yes indeed, by my Lord, it will come upon you. He is the Knower of the unseen.” Not an atom's weight in the heavens and the earth, or anything smaller or larger, escapes His knowledge. All are in a Clear Record."
  * **Gloss:** The denial named, then refuted with an oath. Note for the series ledger: the task's line "Our Lord, You have destined this for us?" does not occur here or anywhere in the corpus — 34:3 is denial-plus-oath, and the Day's dialogue begins at 34:31. The atom's-weight clause (verified verbatim) makes cosmology into bookkeeping: "All are in a Clear Record."
* **`[34:4]`** "That He may recompense those who believe and do good works. Those will have forgiveness, and a generous provision."
  * **Gloss:** The purpose clause of the record; "forgiveness, and a generous provision" is a fixed pair elsewhere (`[22:50]` near-verbatim twin).
* **`[34:5]`** "As for those who strive against Our revelations, seeking to undermine them—for them is a punishment of a painful plague."
  * **Gloss:** The counter-category: not passive denial but active efforts against the revelations; "plague" here, verbatim phrase "seeking to undermine them" recurs at `[34:38]` — the bookend that ruku 5 closes.
* **`[34:6]`** "Those who received knowledge know that what is revealed to you from your Lord is the truth; and it guides to the path of the Majestic, the Praiseworthy."
  * **Gloss:** This surah's recognition verse — the cognate of the "they recognize it as they recognize their sons" motif, which is actually `[2:146]`/`[6:20]` and occurs nowhere in Saba (correction on the ledger). Revelation is called truth and named as a road: "the path of the Majestic, the Praiseworthy" — the only "Majestic, the Praiseworthy" collocation in the corpus.
* **`[34:7]`** "Those who disbelieved said, “Shall we point out to you a man, who will tell you that, once torn into shreds, you will be in a new creation?"
  * **Gloss:** The mockers' caucus, gossip-register; "torn into shreds" is this edition's rendering of the disintegration scene (cf. `[13:5]` "When we have become dust, will we be in a new creation?"). The quote opens and never closes — genuine book typography in this edition, not an extraction error (see Fleeting link).
* **`[34:8]`** "Did he invent a lie about Allah, or is there madness in him?” Indeed, those who do not believe in the Hereafter are in torment, and far astray."
  * **Gloss:** The two stock accusations — fabrication and madness — the pair answered inside this very surah at 34:43 and 34:46; then the narrator rules on the accusers instead of the accused.
* **`[34:9]`** "Do they not reflect upon what lies before them and behind them, of the heaven and the earth? If We will, We can make the earth cave in beneath them, or make pieces of the sky fall down on them. In that is a sign for every devout servant."
  * **Gloss:** The four-directional sky/earth of 34:2 becomes the courtroom overhead; the sanction (earth caving — verbatim twin verb at `[28:82]` after Qarun) is on standby; the audience filter closes: "a sign for every devout servant."

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`hamd` (praise)** `[34:1]`:
  * **Rendering:** "Praise be to Allah" — the fixed opening formula; doubled here for "in the Hereafter."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[1:2]`, `[6:1]`, `[18:1]`, `[35:1]`, `[39:75]` — the same formula the saved eventually say on high (the praise bracket of this surah is only at its start; its closing bracket is elsewhere in the corpus).
* **`the Wise, the Expert`** `[34:1]`:
  * **Rendering:** pair closes the praise verse.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[6:18]`, `[6:73]` — verbatim pair, both in creeds about total knowledge; the Expert (khabir) is the inside-knower the Record of 34:3 implies.
* **`Knower of the unseen`** `[34:3]`:
  * **Rendering:** "He is the Knower of the unseen"; at `[34:48]` the same title appears as "Knower of the Unseen" (capitalization drift in this edition, observable).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[9:78]`, `[5:109]` — where even messengers confess they have no knowledge of it.
* **`atom's weight` / `Clear Record`** `[34:3]`:
  * **Rendering:** "Not an atom's weight in the heavens and the earth, or anything smaller or larger, escapes His knowledge. All are in a Clear Record."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[10:61]` near-verbatim twin ("nor is there anything smaller or larger, but is in a clear record"); `[99:7–8]` (the atom's-weight deeds shown); `[4:40]` (no atom's weight of injustice); `[6:59]`, `[36:12]` ("Clear Record").

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Meccan (classical lists); the mockery of bodily resumption (7–8) fits the surah's Meccan argumentative scene (Source: classical lists — verify in print).
