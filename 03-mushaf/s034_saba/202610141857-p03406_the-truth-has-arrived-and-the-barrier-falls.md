# One piece of advice: the Truth has arrived, the barrier falls (34:46–54)
**UID:** 202610141857
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 34:46–54 (Ruku 6 of 6 in Saba')
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[34:46]` – `[34:54]` (9 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The case closes with a single piece of advice — stand before Allah, in pairs or alone, and reflect: the messenger asks no fee, the Truth has arrived and falsehood can neither originate nor regenerate — and the surah ends where its praise could not reach: terror seized "from a nearby place," late belief unreachable "from a distant place," and a barrier between the mockers and what they desire.
* **Placement:** Ruku 6 of 6; the verdict chamber. The guidance coin-flip of 34:24 ("Either you, or we...") is resolved into private responsibility (50); the "Knower of the Unseen" of 34:3 returns at 34:48; the recitation-objections of 34:43 get their final answer at 34:49.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[34:46]`** "Say, “I offer you a single advice: devote yourselves to Allah, in pairs, or individually; and reflect. There is no madness in your friend. He is just a warner to you, before the advent of a severe punishment.”"
  * **Gloss:** The one-advice verse: stand before God — the courtroom of the whole surah reduced to a posture test; "reflect" reopens 34:9's "Do they not reflect"; "no madness in your friend" — verbatim-twin phrasing at `[7:184]` — answers 34:8's madness-charge inside the same surah; the office is modest: a warner, with a punishment ahead.
* **`[34:47]`** "Say, “Whatever compensation I have asked of you, is yours. My compensation comes only from Allah, and He is Witness over all things.”"
  * **Gloss:** The zero-fee clause: the message is not a transaction — the gift-economy of 34:39 (spend and be replaced) extends to the preacher himself; "Witness over all things" (fixed clause: `[4:33]`, `[33:55]`) makes sincerity auditable only by God. Ledger note: the task's "recognize it as they recognize their sons" quotation belongs to `[2:146]`/`[6:20]` — 34:47 is this compensation verse, and Saba's recognition verse is 34:6.
* **`[34:48]`** "Say, “My Lord projects the truth. He is the Knower of the Unseen.”"
  * **Gloss:** Revelation as emission: the Lord "projects" the truth — sends it toward the messenger as the surah has been saying since 34:6; the unseen-title of 34:3 closes the ruku's ring.
* **`[34:49]`** "Say, “The Truth has come; while falsehood can neither originate, nor regenerate.”"
  * **Gloss:** The surah's axiological sentence: truth has arrived and falsehood has lost both birth and re-birth — the corpus's harder cousin of `[17:81]` ("falsehood has withered away; for falsehood is bound to wither away"); the kalam anchor: what came is "the Truth" that heaven itself reported at 34:23.
* **`[34:50]`** "Say, “If I err, I err only to my own loss; but if I am guided, it is by what my Lord inspires me. He is Hearing and Near.”"
  * **Gloss:** Guidance privatized (answering 34:24's collective wager); the channel named — "what my Lord inspires me" — and the God of praise (1) is "Hearing and Near": nearness as address (cf. 2:186), before 34:51 makes nearness as capture.
* **`[34:51]`** "If you could only see when they are terrified, and there is no escape, and they are seized from a nearby place."
  * **Gloss:** The "If you could only see" cut (second use after 34:31) shows the seizure: no escape ("they will realize that they have no escape" — `[41:48]`), and from "a nearby place" — the twin image of `[50:41]`'s caller who calls from a nearby place.
* **`[34:52]`** "And they say, “We have believed in it.” But how can they attain it from a distant place?"
  * **Gloss:** The late conversion is a distance-paradox: faith in "it" (the Truth of 49/the Hour of 3) is claimed, but the window has become unreachable — not through God's refusal but through the geography of the moment.
* **`[34:53]`** "They have rejected it in the past, and made allegations from a far-off place."
  * **Gloss:** Diagnosis: they alleged from "a far-off place" — safe-distance slander (cf. their "Shall we point out to you a man..." of 34:7) — the old rejection priced now.
* **`[34:54]`** "A barrier will be placed between them and what they desire, as was done formerly with their counterparts. They were in disturbing doubt."
  * **Gloss:** The actual final verse of the surah (verified against the corpus — it is not a praise-verse; "and they say: Praise be to Allah" is the closing of 39:75 and the greeting of 7:43/10:10, while Saba's praise stays at its opening 34:1): the barrier (hijab-family: the "invisible barrier" of `[17:45]` placed during the recitation becomes eschatological; `[18:52]`'s barrier after the failed call on partners), the counterparts of 34:45, and the final diagnosis — "disturbing doubt."

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`your friend / madness`** `[34:46]`:
  * **Rendering:** "There is no madness in your friend."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[7:184]` "There is no madness in their friend. He is but a plain warner" — verbatim twin; within the surah the charge itself is quoted at `[34:8]`; the phrase family also includes `[54:47]` ("The wicked are in confusion and madness") as its opposite state.
* **`compensation`** `[34:47]`:
  * **Rendering:** "Whatever compensation I have asked of you, is yours. My compensation comes only from Allah."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[6:90]` ("Say, 'I ask of you no compensation for it; it is just a reminder for all mankind'"), `[23:72]` ("Or are you asking them for a payment? The revenue from your Lord is better") — the prophet's free-of-charge speech, the kalam note's economics.
* **`Truth / falsehood`** `[34:48–49]`:
  * **Rendering:** "My Lord projects the truth... The Truth has come; while falsehood can neither originate, nor regenerate" (capitalized "Truth" for the arrived reality, lower-case for the projected content — observable drift).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[17:81]` ("The truth has come, and falsehood has withered away"); `[13:1]`/`[10:94]` ("...is the truth"/"The truth has come to you from your Lord") — the arrival-verb family this surah's refrain "but most people do not know" (28, 36) keeps disputing.
* **`barrier` / `nearby place`** `[34:51,54]`:
  * **Rendering:** "seized from a nearby place"; "A barrier will be placed between them and what they desire."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[17:45]` ("We place between you and those who do not believe in the Hereafter an invisible barrier" — during recitation), `[18:52]` ("We will place between them a barrier" — after the failed call on partners), `[41:5]` ("between us and you is a barrier" — the deniers' own words), `[50:41]` (the caller "from a nearby place"), `[2:186]`/`[50:16]` God's "near" — same word, two directions.
