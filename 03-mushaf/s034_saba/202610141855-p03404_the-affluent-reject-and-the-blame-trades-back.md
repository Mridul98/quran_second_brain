# The affluent reject; the blame trades back (34:31–36)
**UID:** 202610141855
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 34:31–36 (Ruku 4 of 6 in Saba')
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[34:31]` – `[34:36]` (6 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah's shortest ruku runs one continuous trial: a double refusal ("We will never believe in this Quran, nor in what came before it") is replayed on the Day as a blame-trade between the arrogant and the oppressed, until the distributor of bounty — not the hoarders of wealth and children — has the last word.
* **Placement:** Ruku 4 of 6; the sociology of the ruku-3 courtroom — leaders and followers re-litigating who owned their unbelief. The affluent-reflex (34) universalizes the mission history ("We sent no warner to any town"), and verse 36 hands the economy argument to ruku 5 (37–39).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[34:31]`** "Those who disbelieve say, “We will never believe in this Quran, nor in what came before it.” If you could only see the wrongdoers, captive before their Lord, throwing back allegations at one another. Those who were oppressed will say to those who were arrogant, “Were it not for you, we would have been believers.”"
  * **Gloss:** One verse, three camera moves: the present blanket refusal of *all* Scriptures; the "If you could only see" cut to the Day (same device as 34:51); and the first blame — the oppressed crediting their leaders with their own loss of faith. This is the Day-dialogue the task looked for at 34:3 ("You have destined this for us" is not the corpus's wording anywhere — see index ledger).
* **`[34:32]`** "Those who were arrogant will say to those who were oppressed, “Was it us who turned you away from guidance when it came to you? No indeed, you yourselves were sinful.”"
  * **Gloss:** The leadership defense: guidance came, you declined; "No indeed" — the same rebuttal formula as 34:27 — privatizes the sin.
* **`[34:33]`** "And those who were oppressed will say to those who were arrogant, “It was your scheming by night and day; as you instructed us to reject Allah, and to set up rivals to Him.” They will hide their remorse when they see the retribution. We will put yokes around the necks of those who disbelieved. Will they be repaid for anything other than what they used to do?"
  * **Gloss:** The counter-charge names the pedagogy of disbelief: round-the-clock "scheming," a curriculum of "reject Allah... set up rivals" — the tawhid of ruku 3 charged as *taught*; then the yokes (the collared class of 13:5/40:71/76:4) and the equivalence of repayment to their own deeds.
* **`[34:34]`** "We sent no warner to any town, without its affluent saying, “We reject what you are sent with.”"
  * **Gloss:** The verdict generalized into historiography: every town's rejection was class-coded — "its affluent" (mutraf), the same reflex as `[17:16]` where the affluent of a town transgress at its destruction.
* **`[34:35]`** "And they say, “We have more wealth and more children, and we will not be punished.”"
  * **Gloss:** The audit's two assets read as immunity: wealth-and-children, the very pair that 34:37 will dethrone ("It is neither your wealth nor your children that bring you closer to Us"); Qarun's "the ungrateful never prosper" (28:82) is the counter-verdict.
* **`[34:36]`** "Say, “My Lord spreads out His bounty to whomever He wills, or restricts it; but most people do not know.”"
  * **Gloss:** The distribution prerogative answered by the refrain "but most people do not know" (second occurrence, after 34:28) — the refrain's twin "most of them do not know" recurs at 28:57/31:25/39:29; the variant "most people do not believe" (11:17/13:1/40:59) is a different Saba-adjacent formula — ledger note.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`affluent (mutraf)`** `[34:34]`:
  * **Rendering:** "its affluent saying, 'We reject what you are sent with.'"
  * **Elsewhere:** `[17:16]` — "We command its affluent ones, they transgress in it, so the word becomes justified against it": the class that triggers a town's destruction; cognate social pair at `[7:75]`, rendered there "The elite... who were arrogant" against "the common people who had believed."
* **`yokes`** `[34:33]`:
  * **Rendering:** "We will put yokes around the necks of those who disbelieved."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[13:5]` ("yokes around their necks" — same new-creation deniers as 34:7), `[40:71]` ("the yokes are around their necks, and they will be dragged by the chains"), `[76:4]` ("chains, and yokes, and a Searing Fire").
* **`set up rivals`** `[34:33]`:
  * **Rendering:** "to set up rivals to Him" — the partners-verb of the whole surah (34:27 "those you have attached to Him as associates").
  * **Elsewhere:** `[6:1]` — "Yet those who disbelieve ascribe equals to their Lord" (the same concept, rendered "equals" there — translation drift observable); `[25:3]` — fabricated gods "that create nothing."
* **`most people do not know`** `[34:36]`:
  * **Rendering:** fixed refrain twice in this surah (34:28, 34:36).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[28:57]`, `[31:25]`, `[39:29]` ("but most of them do not know") — Saba's epistemic verdict, distinct from "most people do not believe" (`[11:17]`, `[13:1]`, `[40:59]`).
