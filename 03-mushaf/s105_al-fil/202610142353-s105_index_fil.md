# Surah 105: THE ELEPHANT (al-Fil)
**UID:** 202610142353
**Type:** Index / Surah Map
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Meccan per the classical type-lists; the corpus carries no classification data (header fields only — rg-verified). Revelation order ≈ 19 in the transmitted nuzul tables (list-dependent) — *transmitted tables — verify*.
* **Size:** 5 ayahs, 1 ruku (ruku_map global 547 — verified). Five verbs carry the whole surah: considered / made-go-wrong / sent / throwing / leaving-as — a demolition told as a grammar exercise in divine counter-action.
* **Title:** al-Fil — Itani's header "THE ELEPHANT" tracks `[105:1]` "the People of the Elephant"; the elephant is the Quran's only war-machine named by its beast (the word fīl occurs nowhere else in the corpus — rg-verified).
* **Central theme:** How God dismantles an expedition: the plan itself is re-routed (v2), the counter-army comes from the air (v3), its ammunition is geological (v4), and the result is agriculture imagery — an army reduced to eaten crop (v5). No names, no dates, no moral clause inside the surah: the event is cited as something the addressees *already know* ("Have you not considered"), which is why the classical apparatus (Abrahah, the Year, the Ka'bah target) does so much interpretive work — all of it outside the text (§5b).
* **Reading hint:** 105 and 106 are a designed couplet — the failed attack on the House (105) is the shadow behind the security of the House's neighbors (106); read them as one favor-audit: defense and provision.

## 3. Structural Arc
* **v1 — the question that presumes a known answer:** "Have you not considered how your Lord dealt with the People of the Elephant?" — the formula (your Lord dealt with) is shared exactly with `[89:6]` "Have you not seen how your Lord dealt with Aad?" (rg-verified in English). The considered/seen divergence is Itani's rendering drift; whether the two openings are one identical Arabic formula is an extra-corpus reading — *Source: standard Arabic reading of 105:1/89:6 — verify against a mushaf*.
* **v2 — the counter-move on the intangible:** "Did He not make their plan go wrong?" — the first damage is to strategy itself; no weapon has appeared yet.
* **v3–v4 — the counter-army:** "He sent against them swarms of birds. / Throwing at them rocks of baked clay." — sky becomes firing line; the ammunition is described by manufacture ("baked," sijjīl), tying it to the Overturned Cities' stones (`[11:82]`, `[15:74]` — rg-verified twins).
* **v5 — the afterimage:** "Leaving them like chewed-up leaves." — the sentence ends not on the fire or the flood but on a field: the army becomes crop residue (*Source: structural observation on corpus renderings*).
* **Bracket position:** ruku 547; the destruction-narrative pole of the 104–108 cluster (individual Crusher → civic demolition → civic favor → social audit → individual consolation).
* **Cross-surah form note:** 105 has no woe-line — the demolition is narrated, not pronounced; compare 104 (woe + Crusher) and the 77 litany ("Woe on that Day…" ×10, rg-counted): three registers of the same verdict — addressed, narrated, refrained. (*Source: structural observation across the three surahs — verify.*)
* **Composition count (observable):** two negative-interrogative openers (vv1–2) then three declaratives (vv3–5: sent / throwing / leaving) — the surah asks the audience to recall, then supplies the mechanism in a three-beat descent from sky to field.

## 5b. Batch-Return Verification Flags (this index)
* **Hint renderings corrected (all rg-verified against `corpus/s105_al-fil.md`):** "Elephant-people" → "**the People of the Elephant**"; "plot misdirected" → "**Did He not make their plan go wrong?**" (the plan *goes wrong*, is not "misdirected"); "flocks of birds" → "**swarms of birds**"; "baked clay stones" → "**rocks of baked clay**" (stones is 11:82/15:74's word for the same material); "they became like eaten straw" → "**Leaving them like chewed-up leaves**" — straw is not Itani's word; "chewed-up leaves" is (the agricultural image is preserved).
* **Abrahah expedition = classical asbab, NOT in the text — CONFIRMED:** no "Abrahah," no "Year," no "Mecca," no "Ka'bah" occurs in 105 (or in the corpus as 105's opponent — rg-verified; "Abraham" hits are unrelated). The expedition details (Yemeni viceroy, the church at San'a, the aimed destruction of the House, the Year of the Elephant as a dating era) come from the sira/tafsir tradition (Ibn Ishaq's account is the fullest early form). *Source: classical transmitted reports — verify in primary before stating any detail as "the Quran says."*
* **Destruction-of-tyrant motif cluster w/ 89:5–14 — judged honestly:** the pairing is REAL at formula level (105:1 ↔ 89:6 "how your Lord dealt with" — exact in English apart from Itani's considered/seen drift) and at imagery level (89:7 "Erum of the pillars," 89:10 "Pharaoh of the Stakes" — built-power humbled). But 89:11–12 supplies the moral clause explicitly ("Those who committed excesses in the lands / And spread much corruption therein"), while 105 supplies none — its point is made by the *known identity of the target* (the House, via 106 adjacency), not by an indictment inside the surah. Verdict: cite the cluster as motif-family; do not import 89's moral clause as if 105 stated it.
* **Basmala-header observation:** the corpus prints its own "Basmala: In the name of Allah…" header line for BOTH s105 and s106 (observable in the two corpus files) — so any joint-recension reading (§1) is a manuscript-tradition matter outside this edition's numbering; cite each surah independently here.
* **Ammunition-material identity:** `[11:82]` "We rained down on it stones of baked clay" / `[15:74]` "rained down upon them stones of baked clay" — the only other "baked clay" sites (Lot cities), and both add the "turned it upside down" mechanic the Elephant story's target would have suffered had the plan succeeded (*Source: agent inference on the shared material — verify*).
