# The Army Brought Low: Plot, Birds, Stones, Straw (105:1–5)
**UID:** 202610142354
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 105:1–5 (Ruku 1 of 1 in al-Fil)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[105:1]` – `[105:5]` (5 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The Quran's shortest war report: an expedition against the House is undone not by a counter-army of men but by its own plan being mis-run (v2) and by a conscripted sky — flocks of birds throwing manufactured stones (v3–4) — until the force reads like crop residue (v5). The surah asserts the event as common knowledge ("Have you not considered") and declines every name, date, and motive: the argument is the *fact of intervention*, not its dossier.
* **Placement:** Ruku 547 of 556; the civic-demolition pole of the 104–108 cluster. Its five verbs answer 104's one delusion: wealth counted as permanence (104:2–3) vs an army un-counted out of existence in four lines. Immediately precedes 106, whose "security" (īlāf) is the flip side of this failed raid.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[105:1]`** "Have you not considered how your Lord dealt with the People of the Elephant?"
  * **Gloss:** The consideration-interrogative (the family also runs 107:1, 89:6, 45:23 — rg-verified): knowledge is presumed, not argued. "Your Lord" — the God of the addressee is the one who dealt; "People of the Elephant" names the enemy by their tech, a war-machine so novel it served as the event's nickname.
* **`[105:2]`** "Did He not make their plan go wrong?"
  * **Gloss:** First damage is strategic: the plan itself is undone — it missed, mis-routed, came to nothing. No weapon described yet; the campaign fails at the level of intention-outcome fit. (*Source: classical reading of 105:2 as the plan's dissolution — verify; the corpus shows only "make their plan go wrong."*)
* **`[105:3]`** "He sent against them swarms of birds."
  * **Gloss:** The counter-force is aerial and numerous (ṭayran abābīl — flocks in ranks; *Source: classical lexicon — verify*); the divine "sent" is the corpus's own send-verb for winds, storms, and messengers alike (observable: Itani uses "sent/sends" across those hosts) — birds conscripted as a host.
* **`[105:4]`** "Throwing at them rocks of baked clay."
  * **Gloss:** The ammunition gets a manufacturing label: sijjīl — fired clay (the same material-word family the corpus uses for the Overturned Cities: `[11:82]`, `[15:74]` "stones of baked clay"). Small arms, precisely aimed: participation ("throwing at them," not "raining down") marks it as targeted, not weather.
* **`[105:5]`** "Leaving them like chewed-up leaves."
  * **Gloss:** The tail-clause: an army ends as a field's aftermath — ʿaṣfan maʾkūl, crop-eaten straw (the translator's "chewed-up leaves"). The mightiest column on the Arabian plain is dismissed with farming imagery: consumed, scattered, fertilizer.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`ahl al-Fīl` (People of the Elephant)**: the enemy, named by their beast.
  * **Rendering:** "the People of the Elephant" (105:1); the surah title-word fīl occurs nowhere else in the corpus (rg-verified — unique). The people are a "ahl of" a thing rather than a tribe: the Quran's one ethno-label built on hardware.
  * **Elsewhere:** the "dealt with the people of…" grammar recurs at `[14:45]` "how We dealt with them" (the wronged-ones' homes), `[89:6]` "how your Lord dealt with Aad?" — the formula-cluster this surah joins.
* **`makr/kayd → "plan"` (105:2)**: strategy, device — undone divinely.
  * **Rendering:** "Did He not make their plan go wrong?" — singular "plan"; no counter-plan verb, only the going-wrong.
  * **Elsewhere:** the divine-counterplot dossier, all rg-verified: `[27:50]` "They planned a plan, and We planned a plan, but they did not notice.", `[12:76]` "Thus We devised a plan for Joseph", `[14:46]` "their plans are known to Allah, even if their plans can eliminate mountains" — 105:2 is the same theology run on a military expedition, with We's counter-move left unstated (only the failure is described).
* **`ṭayran abābīl` (swarms of birds)**: flocks — abābīl carries the plural-in-ranks sense (classical lexicon; *Source: classical dictionaries of 105:3 — verify wording*).
  * **Rendering:** "swarms of birds" (105:3); the swarm-English recurs for locusts: `[54:7]` "as if they were swarming locusts", `[21:96]` Gog and Magog "swarm down from every mound" (rg-verified) — Itani's swarm-word family mixes human and animal hosts.
  * **Elsewhere:** birds-as-soldiers is unique to this site; `[2:260]`'s four revived birds and `[109? none]` are the corpus's other bird-moves — none martial.
* **`sijjīl` (baked clay)**: fired-clay ammunition — the Overturned Cities' material.
  * **Rendering:** "rocks of baked clay" (105:4) vs `[11:82]`/`[15:74]` "stones of baked clay" — rocks/stones drift, material identical in English.
  * **Elsewhere:** only those two sites (rg-verified) — the shared gloss links the Elephant's stones to the stones on Lot's people: sky-ammunition reserved for expeditions against sanctified places and their messengers (*Source: agent inference on the material identity — verify*).
* **`ʿaṣfan maʾkūl` (chewed-up leaves)**: consumed crop residue.
  * **Rendering:** "like chewed-up leaves" — unique in corpus (rg-verified "chewed").
  * **Elsewhere:** the harvest-failure imagery twin is `[18:42]` "ruin closed in on his crops" — agrarian loss as the book's stock picture of come-to-nothing (verdict: imagery family, not lexicon twin).

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Meccan per classical type-lists (corpus carries none — rg-verified). Order ≈ 19 — *transmitted tables — verify*. A public, recent, unverifiable-by-the-audience event suits Meccan confrontation with Quraysh: "the Quraysh cannot deny what happened at their own city's gate" is the surah's silent rhetoric (*Source: agent inference on audience and genre — verify*).
* **Asbab / narrative context:** Abrahah the Yemeni viceroy, his church, his march to destroy the Ka'bah, the disease that broke his column, and the dating "Year of the Elephant" — all transmitted (earliest fullest form in Ibn Ishaq's sira; tafsir reports on 105). **None of it is in the text**: no Abrahah, no year, no Mecca, no Ka'bah-word in 105 (rg-verified). *Source: classical transmitted asbab — verify in primary; quote as tradition, never as "the surah says."*
* **Jewish/Christian parallel reports** (the stones leaving marks, the leader's death before San'a) belong to the same transmitted apparatus — flagged for a future verification pass, not asserted here.
