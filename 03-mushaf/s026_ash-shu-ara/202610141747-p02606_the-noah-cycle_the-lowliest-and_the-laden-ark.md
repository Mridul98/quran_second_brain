# The Noah cycle: the lowliest, the stoning threat, the laden Ark (26:105–122)
**UID:** 202610141747
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 26:105–122 (Ruku 6 of 11 in ash-Shu'ara')
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[26:105]` – `[26:122]` (18 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Cycle one installs the template the next four will replay: the people "disbelieved the messengers" (plural — the office, not just Noah), their brother summons with "Do you not fear?", his credential is a faithful messenger-ship paid only by God, the elite objection is purely social ("the lowliest who follow you"), the threat is stoning, and the judgment is the Ark and drowning — sealed by the refrain pair.
* **Placement:** Ruku 6; first of the five refrain cycles (105–191). It is the cycle with the least economic/sexual content — its sin is class contempt — so the template's skeleton (formula → summons → payment-clause → dismissal → destruction → refrain) shows bare.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[26:105]`** “The people of Noah disbelieved the messengers.”
  * **Gloss:** The formula line — plural object ("the messengers") though one was sent; the surah's charges are always against the whole prophetic office (Source: agent observation on the formula — verify).
* **`[26:106] / [26:107] / [26:108]`** “Their brother Noah said to them, ‘Do you not fear? / I am to you a faithful messenger. / So fear Allah, and obey me.”
  * **Gloss:** Kinship ("their brother"), then the credential, then the summons; note the question is bare — "Do you not fear?" without an expressed object; the fear's object is named only in the next line ("fear Allah").
* **`[26:109]`** “I ask of you no payment for this. My payment is only from the Lord of the Worlds.”
  * **Gloss:** The payment-clause (one of five; the "this/it" preposition drift begins here — index §2); prophecy is kept out of the money relation, answering the reward-bid of `41–42` structurally.
* **`[26:110]`** “So fear Allah, and obey me.”
  * **Gloss:** The bracket repeats — Noah and Hud and Salih get the doublet; the middle slot (111–116) holds their specific pathology.
* **`[26:111]`** “They said, ‘Shall we believe in you, when it is the lowliest who follow you?’”
  * **Gloss:** The elite dismissal in its purest form: the argument is a census; who follows is treated as evidence about what is followed.
* **`[26:112] / [26:113] / [26:114] / [26:115]`** “He said, ‘What do I know about what they do? / Their account rests only with my Lord, if you have sense. / And I am not about to drive away the believers. / I am only a clear warner.’”
  * **Gloss:** Four-part rebuttal: the followers' account is not his to audit; expulsion of the lowly is refused (against `58:20`'s inverse); he re-scopes his job to warning.
* **`[26:116]`** “They said, ‘If you do not refrain, O Noah, you will be stoned.’”
  * **Gloss:** The answer to losing the argument is the scaffold — the only other "stoned" line in the corpus is Shu'ayb's people (`[11:91]`, verified): the last cycle re-runs the first cycle's threat.
* **`[26:117]–[26:118]`** “He said, ‘My Lord, my people have denied me. / So judge between me and them decisively, and deliver me and the believers who are with me.’”
  * **Gloss:** The prayer transfers the case to God — a decisive judgment (the same root as the Day's "account," `113`); twins in the plea-dossier: `[54:10]` "I am overwhelmed, so help me" and `[23:26]` "help me, for they have rejected me" (both verified).
* **`[26:119]–[26:120]`** “So We delivered him and those with him in the laden Ark. / Then We drowned the rest.”
  * **Gloss:** Salvation by vessel, judgment by water; "laden Ark" twins `[54:13]` "a craft of planks and nails" and `[69:11]` "the cruising ship" (verified); the drowning line mirrors `[7:64]` "We drowned those who rejected Our revelations" (verified).
* **`[26:121]–[26:122]`** “In that is a sign, but most of them are not believers. / Your Lord is the Almighty, the Merciful.”
  * **Gloss:** Refrain — the short variant ("In that is a sign," no "Surely"; in-surah drift, index §2); the drowning narrative still ends on mercy-naming.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **the formula** ("disbelieved the messengers," `105`):
  * **Rendering:** exact string occurs 5× corpus-wide, all in s026 (`105/123/141/160/176` — rg-verified); the structural twin without the formula is the Elijah cycle `[37:123–124]` ("Do you not fear?" — verified).
  * **Elsewhere:** singular variant "they denied him" (`189`), "called him a liar" (`37:127` — verified) — the surah grades the same verb.
* **`the lowliest`** (`111`): social rank as counter-argument.
  * **Rendering:** "the lowliest"; twin `[58:20]` "among the lowliest" (verified); dossier `[11:27]` "only the worst among us have followed you" (verified).
  * **Force:** the follow-motif thread — `40` ("follow the magicians"), `111`, `215`, `224` (all in-surah, verified) — the surah's sociology is one verb.
* **`clear warner`** (`115`): the job description.
  * **Rendering:** "I am only a clear warner"; the identical formula on Muhammad's lips at `[29:50]`, `[67:26]`, `[38:70]` and Nuh's at `[71:2]` (all verified) — every messenger recites the same scope-of-work clause.
* **`amin`** ("faithful," `107`): trustworthy as credential.
  * **Rendering:** "faithful messenger" ×4 in the cycles; only Shu'ayb drifts to "trustworthy" (`178` — verified, index §2).
