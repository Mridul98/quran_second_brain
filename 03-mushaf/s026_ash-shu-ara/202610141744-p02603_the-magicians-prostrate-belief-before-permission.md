# The magicians fall prostrate: belief before permission (26:34–52)
**UID:** 202610141744
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 26:34–52 (Ruku 3 of 11 in ash-Shu'ara')
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[26:34]` – `[26:52]` (19 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** A state-sponsored contest turns into its opposite: Pharaoh's council reads the staff as a land-grab by magic, hires the best magicians for a reward-bid, and the winning side's first act on seeing the truth is prostration and a faith declared "before permission" — teaching the follow-motif (`40`) what real following looks like (`46–51`).
* **Placement:** Ruku 3; the mid-section of the Moses narrative (ruku 2–4). Its reward-bargain (`41–42`) is the foil against which the cycles' "I ask of you no payment" (`109` etc.) will ring, and its amputation decree (`49`) prefigures the Day's reversals.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[26:34]–[26:35]`** "He said to the dignitaries around him, 'This is a skilled magician. / He intends to drive you out of your land with his magic, so what do you recommend?'"
  * **Gloss:** The elite diagnosis: sign → threat; the national-security reading recurs in Pharaoh's own mouth at `[20:57]` (verified twin).
* **`[26:36]–[26:37]`** "They said, 'Delay him and his brother, and send recruiters to the cities. / To bring you every experienced magician.'"
  * **Gloss:** Procedural patience as suppression; expertise marshaled against revelation.
* **`[26:38] / [26:39] / [26:40]`** "So the magicians were gathered for the appointment on a specified day. / And it was said to the people, 'Are you all gathered? / That we may follow the magicians, if they are the winners.'"
  * **Gloss:** The spectacle is civic liturgy — "a specified day" (cf. `155`, `54:46`, `78:17` verified); the people's stated motive is *following* on the winning side — the surah's follow-question posed in the open (answers at `111`, `215`, `224`).
* **`[26:41]–[26:42]`** "When the magicians arrived, they said to Pharaoh, 'Is there a reward for us, if we are the winners?' He said, 'Yes, and you will be among those favored.'"
  * **Gloss:** The transaction explicit: *ajr* from a king; the cycles' messenger will refuse exactly this (`109–180` cluster — verified).
* **`[26:43] / [26:44] / [26:45]`** "Moses said to them, 'Present what you intend to present.' / So they threw their ropes and their sticks, and said, 'By the majesty of Pharaoh, we will be the winners.' / Then Moses threw his staff, and behold, it began swallowing their trickery."
  * **Gloss:** Moses lets them maximize; their oath is by majesty, his stake is a stick — and the staff eats the appearance ("their trickery").
* **`[26:46] / [26:47] / [26:48]`** "And the magicians fell down prostrating. / They said, 'We have believed in the Lord of the Worlds. / The Lord of Moses and Aaron.'"
  * **Gloss:** Instant sujud; the trial-answer of `23–28` now spoken by the regime's own hired hands — the surah's first "Lord of the Worlds" confession after the definitions.
* **`[26:49]`** "He said, 'Did you believe in Him before I have given you permission? He must be your chief, who taught you magic. You will soon know. I will cut off your hands and feet on opposite sides, and I will crucify you all.'"
  * **Gloss:** Permission-theology: belief as licensed property; the amputation-crucifixion formula is verbatim-kindred with `[5:33]` "hands and feet cut off on opposite sides" (verified).
* **`[26:50]–[26:51]`** "They said, 'No problem. To our Lord we will return. / We are eager for our Lord to forgive us our sins, since we are the first of the believers.'"
  * **Gloss:** The shortest heroism in the surah; return-to-Lord reframes death as commute; "first of the believers" claims preeminence from the last place.
* **`[26:52]`** "And We inspired Moses: 'Travel with My servants by night. You will be followed.'"
  * **Gloss:** Scene-change by inspiration; near-verbatim twin `[44:23]` "Set out with My servants by night—you will be followed" (verified) — forewarning that becomes `61–62`.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`ajr`** (reward, `41`): the worldly wage asked of Pharaoh.
  * **Rendering:** "a reward"; the same root is refused as *payment* in all five cycles ("I ask of you no payment," `109/127/145/164/180`, twins `[25:57]`, `[23:72]`, `[52:40]` — verified): here reward is sought from a tyrant, there declined from the people.
* **`tabi'a` / follow** (`40`, `51`): "that we may follow the magicians" vs "the lowliest who follow you" (`111`) vs "the deviators follow them" (`224`) vs "warn... those of the believers who follow you" (`215`) — one verb threading the whole surah's sociology (all verified in-surah).
* **sujud** ("fell down prostrating," `46`): the magicians' body argues before their mouths (`47`); the surah later valorizes rising devotion — "He Who sees you when you rise... and your devotions amidst the worshipers" (`218–219`, verified).
* **`specified day`** (`38`): "the appointment on a specified day" (maw'iad yawm ma'lum?); phrase-family verified against `155` (the camel's drinking turn "on a specified day") and `[54:46]`/`[78:17]` (appointed Hour) — the pagan spectacle and the covenant miracle share day-grammar; the real appointment is the Hour.
