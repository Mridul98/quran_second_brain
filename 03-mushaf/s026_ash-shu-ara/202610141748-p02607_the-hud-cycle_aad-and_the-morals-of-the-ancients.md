# The Hud cycle: Aad's immortality architecture and "morals of the ancients" (26:123–140)
**UID:** 202610141748
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 26:123–140 (Ruku 7 of 11 in ash-Shu'ara')
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[26:123]` – `[26:140]` (18 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Cycle two indicts builders: Aad raises a monument on every height "for vanity's sake" and fortresses as if death were optional, strikes without mercy — and their dismissal is not an argument but apathy ("whether you lecture us, or do not lecture"), reducing the message to "morals of the ancients"; Hud's counter-move is to audit their supply-list (livestock, children, gardens, springs) and end on the doublet, with destruction welded into the refrain ayah itself.
* **Placement:** Ruku 7; second refrain cycle — the template's middle slot (128–135) carries architecture-and-violence where Noah's carried class.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[26:123] / [26:124] / [26:125] / [26:126]`** “Aad disbelieved the messengers. / When their brother Hud said to them, ‘Do you not fear? / I am to you a faithful messenger. / So fear Allah, and obey me.”
  * **Gloss:** Formula + brother + fear-question + credential + summons, all template-strict (`123 = 105` grammar; `124` adds the "When" that `106` lacks — verified drift, index §2).
* **`[26:127]`** “I ask of you no payment for this. My payment is only from the Lord of the Worlds.”
  * **Gloss:** Payment-clause #2, "for this" variant; the money-free prophecy repeats before any charge is named.
* **`[26:128] / [26:129] / [26:130]`** “Do you build a monument on every height for vanity's sake? / And you set up fortresses, hoping to live forever? / And when you strike, you strike mercilessly?”
  * **Gloss:** Three indictments: skyline as vanity, real estate as immortality-project, violence without limit — the built environment as creed; "monument" and "live forever" expose the theology of permanence (twins verified: `[7:74]` "mansions... dwellings in the mountains"; `[59:2]` "their fortresses would protect them").
* **`[26:131] / [26:132] / [26:133] / [26:134]`** “So fear Allah, and obey me. / And reverence Him, who supplied you with everything you know. / He supplied you with livestock and children. / And gardens and springs.”
  * **Gloss:** Doublet closes; the supply-audit: every listed blessing is *razq* — the gratitude audit's inventory (cf. Abraham's favor-list `78–82`, same grammar of first-person vs second-person blessing).
* **`[26:135]`** “I fear for you the punishment of an awesome Day.”
  * **Gloss:** The messenger's fear re-performs the summons ("Do you not fear?") as its content; "an awesome Day" joins the surah's dread-day series (`156`, `189` — in-surah, verified).
* **`[26:136] / [26:137] / [26:138]`** “They said, ‘It is the same for us, whether you lecture us, or do not lecture. / This is nothing but morals of the ancients. / And we will not be punished.’”
  * **Gloss:** The apatheia of the wealthy: preaching is indifferent; revelation is reclassified as inherited custom; and the punishment is denied outright — the laziest elite dismissal in the block (ancestralism twins verified: `[43:22]`, `[31:21]`, `[7:28]`).
* **`[26:139]`** “So they denied him, and We destroyed them. Surely in this is a sign, but most of them are not believers.”
  * **Gloss:** Denial → destruction → refrain, all in one ayah — the welding (no method of destruction named here; the wind-dossier of Aad lives in `[7:65]`/`[46:25]` — verified — while 26 keeps the template bare).
* **`[26:140]`** “Your Lord is the Almighty, the Merciful.”
  * **Gloss:** Refrain line two — power and mercy over a people erased.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`monument... for vanity's sake`** (`128`): building as doxic display.
  * **Rendering:** "a monument on every height for vanity's sake" — the string is corpus-hapax (rg-verified); functional twin `[7:74]` "mansions on its plains" (verified).
  * **Elsewhere:** the "every height" universal — sin as aesthetic habit, not one bad building (Source: agent observation — verify).
* **`live forever`** (`129`): the fortresses' hidden prayer.
  * **Rendering:** "hoping to live forever"; twin mood `[59:2]` "they thought their fortresses would protect them from Allah" (verified) — security-architecture answered by eviction (`57–59`).
* **`morals of the ancients`** (`137`): revelation reclassified as custom.
  * **Rendering:** "morals of the ancients" — phrase-hapax (verified); note the surah's own contrast: the same appeal to "the ancients" appears reverently at `196` "the scriptures of the ancients" — the people say *morals*, the Book says *scriptures* (Source: observable translator's collocation — verify the Arabic akhlaq/zubur pair).
* **`supplied`** (`132–134`): provision as argument.
  * **Rendering:** "supplied you with everything you know / livestock and children / gardens and springs"; the gardens-and-springs inventory is the surah's blessing-roulade — lost by Pharaoh (`57–58`), audited for Aad (`133–134`), boasted by Thamood (`147`) (all in-surah, verified).
