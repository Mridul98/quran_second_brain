# Revelation, devils, and the poets: the surah authenticates itself (26:192–227)
**UID:** 202610141752
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 26:192–227 (Ruku 11 of 11 in ash-Shu'ara')
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[26:192]` – `[26:227]` (36 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The courtroom turns on itself: after five rehearsals of denied messengers, the surah states its own channel (revelation, the Honest Spirit, upon the heart, a clear Arabic tongue), files its credentials (ancient scriptures, Israel's scholars), disowns the rival channel (devils barred from hearing, descending only on sinful liars), commissions the messenger (kin, wing lowered, trust), and finally judges poetry — condemning poets whom the deviators follow, excepting those who believe, do good, remember Allah much, and defend themselves after being wronged.
* **Placement:** Ruku 11, the closing ruku; its four movements answer the opening frame (`2` the Clarifying Book; `6` "news of what they ridiculed"), and the last word of the surah is the overturning the refrain-cycles were a census of.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[26:192] / [26:193] / [26:194] / [26:195]`** “It is a revelation from the Lord of the Worlds. / The Honest Spirit came down with it. / Upon your heart, that you may be one of the warners. / In a clear Arabic tongue.”
  * **Gloss:** The channel in four lines: source (Lord of the Worlds — the cycles' punchline word now claimed for the Book itself), carrier (the Honest Spirit), landing site (the heart), function (warning — the office named at `115`/`173`/`208`), medium (clear Arabic). NOTE: the assignment's "sent down it" grammar is not in 192–195 — the descent-language belongs to the nazala cluster (verified: `[15:9]` "Surely We revealed the Message, and We will surely preserve it"; `[44:3]`; `[2:185]`) — this is the kalam-note twin (index §2).
* **`[26:196]–[26:197]`** “And it is in the scriptures of the ancients. / Is it not a sign for them that the scholars of the Children of Israel recognized it?”
  * **Gloss:** Two paper trails: the zubur of the ancients (twin verified: `[87:18–19]` "This is in the former scriptures. The Scriptures of Abraham and Moses"), and living expertise — the phrase is "the scholars of the Children of Israel recognized it," not "the knowledge of the rabbis" (assignment misquote; "rabbis" is this edition's rendering elsewhere, e.g. `[5:44]` "the rabbis and the scholars" — verified).
* **`[26:198]–[26:199]`** “Had We revealed it to one of the foreigners. / And he had recited it to them, they still would not have believed in it.”
  * **Gloss:** The counterfactual: the Arabic tongue is not an accident but a fit — and the fit would not have saved them; the recipients' pathology is in them, not the medium ("foreigners" — corpus-hapax here, verified).
* **`[26:200]–[26:201]`** “Thus We make it pass through the hearts of the guilty. / They will not believe in it until they witness the painful punishment.”
  * **Gloss:** Near-verbatim twin verified: `[15:12]` "Thus We slip it into the hearts of the guilty" — the same passive "thus" as ruku 11's theology of hardening.
* **`[26:202] / [26:203] / [26:204]`** “It will come to them suddenly, while they are unaware. / Then they will say, ‘Are we given any respite?’ / Do they seek to hasten Our punishment?”
  * **Gloss:** The deadline scene: the respite-request (external twins verified: `[73:11]`, `[68:45]`) answers the sign-demands of the cycles (`154`, `187`) — they demanded it fast; speed becomes the punishment's mode (cf. `54:46` family).
* **`[26:205] / [26:206] / [26:207]`** “Have you considered: if We let them enjoy themselves for some years. / Then there comes to them what they were promised. / Of what avail to them will be their past enjoyments?”
  * **Gloss:** Long-prosperity thought-experiment — the Aad-Thamood answer from inside the frame: decades of `133–134`/`147–149`-style blessing, cashed at zero at arrival.
* **`[26:208]–[26:209]`** “Never did We destroy a town, but it had warners. / As a reminder—We are never unjust.”
  * **Gloss:** The theodicy of the whole cycle-block in two lines — no destruction without warning, so no complaint survives (`173` "those forewarned" generalizes); twin verified `[28:59]` ("never destroys cities without first sending a messenger..."), and the office definition `[6:48]`.
* **`[26:210] / [26:211] / [26:212]`** “It was not the devils that revealed it. / It is not in their interests, nor in their power. / They are barred from hearing.”
  * **Gloss:** The rival-channel rebuttal: devils cannot transmit what they cannot overhear — the eavesdropping doctrine verified (`[37:8]` "They cannot eavesdrop on the Supernal Elite", `[72:9]`, `[67:5]` "missiles against the devils").
* **`[26:213]`** “So do not pray to another god with Allah, else you will be of those tormented.”
  * **Gloss:** The one creal demand of the closing movement — tawhid with a consequence; twins verified: `[17:39]` "Do not set up with Allah another god...", `[72:18]`.
* **`[26:214] / [26:215] / [26:216]`** “And warn your close relatives. / And lower your wing to those of the believers who follow you. / And if they disobey you, say, ‘I am innocent of what you do.’”
  * **Gloss:** Commission etiquette: begin at home (hapax "close relatives" as command, verified; Safa-mission asbab per classical reports — Source: consensus of classical tafsir on 26:214 — verify), humility idiom (verbatim twin `[15:88]` "lower your wing to the believers"), and dissociation without malice (`43:26` Abraham formula — the ruku-5 thread re-ties here).
* **`[26:217] / [26:218] / [26:219] / [26:220]`** “And put your trust in the Almighty, the Merciful. / He Who sees you when you rise. / And your devotions amidst the worshipers. / He is indeed the Hearer, the Aware.”
  * **Gloss:** Trust in the refrain-names themselves (`9`–`191` drumbeat now becomes the command's object); divine attention (`218–220`) closes the surah's hearing-and-seeing bracket opened at `15` ("We will be with you, listening") — verified in-surah frame.
* **`[26:221] / [26:222] / [26:223]`** “Shall I inform you upon whom the devils descend? / They descend upon every sinful liar. / They give ear, and most of them are liars.”
  * **Gloss:** The counter-channel's real clients; "sinful liar" verbatim twin `[45:7]` ("Woe to every sinful liar", verified); "They give ear" is the inverse of `212` — the barred ones give ear to liars instead (hapax phrase verified).
* **`[26:224] / [26:225] / [26:226]`** “And as for the poets—the deviators follow them. / Do you not see how they ramble in every style? / And how they say what they do not do?”
  * **Gloss:** The verdict on the title-citizens (assignment's "the lost" — actual rendering "the deviators", verified); the charge is formlessness, not meter; the last clause has the surah's strongest external twin: `[61:3]` "most hateful to Allah that you say what you do not do" (verified); the accusation-family around Muhammad-as-poet is answered elsewhere (`[37:36]` "a mad poet", `[52:30]`, `[69:41]`, `[36:69]` "We did not teach him poetry" — all verified).
* **`[26:227]`** “Except for those who believe, and do good deeds, and remember Allah frequently, and defend themselves after they are wronged. As for those who do wrong, they will know by what overturning they will be overturned.”
  * **Gloss:** The exception has FOUR marks, not three (assignment omitted the fourth): faith, deeds, much remembrance (twin `[33:35]` "remember Allah frequently", verified), self-defense after wrong (twin `[22:39]` "Permission is given to those who are fought against", verified) — and the surah ends on knowing, answering `6` ("soon will come to them the news of what they ridiculed") frame-to-frame; "overturning" is corpus-hapax (verified).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`ar-Ruh al-Amin`** ("the Honest Spirit," `193`): the carrier.
  * **Rendering:** phrase-hapax (rg-verified); classical identification with Gabriel is the standard reading — Source: consensus of classical tafsir on 26:193 — verify.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:97]` — "it is he [Gabriel] who revealed it to your heart by Allah's leave" (verified): the same landing-site grammar makes the identification coherent.
* **`clear Arabic tongue`** (`195`): the medium as credential.
  * **Rendering:** verbatim tail-twin `[16:103]` "this is a clear Arabic tongue" (verified); cognate doctrine `[20:113]` "an Arabic Quran" (verified).
  * **Elsewhere:** the kalam note's "sent down / brought by / made into a light" cluster (42:51–52, 44:2–3) is this ruku's genre — [Kalam](../../02-knowledge/202610132005-k-kalam_allah_revelation_as_speech.md) cites 26:192–193 as named evidence (checked in the k-note — verified).
* **`scholars`** (`197`): the recognition experts.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[5:44]` "the rabbis and the scholars" — the same Arabic pair, here truncated to "scholars of the Children of Israel" (hapax phrase; verified) — the argument is testimonial, not genealogical (Source: agent observation — verify).
* **`shu-ara` (poets)** (`224`): the title-word's only verdict.
  * **Elsewhere:** accusation-doctrine `[37:36]`, `[52:30]`, `[21:5]`, refutation `[36:69]`, `[69:41]` (all verified) — the surah answers the charge by analyzing the profession: followership, rambling, broken promises — then exempts the four-mark remnant.
