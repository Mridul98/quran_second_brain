## 1. Orientation

The corpus carries no nuzul statement; the unnumbered Basmala heads the surah (see [Basmala numbering](../../01-fleeting/202610132006-f-basmala_numbering_in_this_edition.md)).
* **Size:** 227 ayahs, 11 ruku (map boundaries 9+24+19+17+35+18+18+19+16+16+36 = 227 — verified against corpus line count). Name fact (observable): the corpus header names it THE POETS; the only "poets" verdict is `[26:224]`.
* **Central theme:** Authentication by rehearsal. The surah proves the Book's origin by replaying one scene five times — a messenger sent to a people, one formula of summons, one elite refusal, one destruction, one closing refrain — then turns the lens on itself: the revelation cannot be devils' chatter (`[26:210–212]`), is recognized by the scholars of Israel (`[26:197]`), and ends by demoting the poets who are followed by the deviators, excepting the believing remnant (`[26:224–227]`).

## 2. Wording check (assignment) — verified against the corpus
* **"Six cycles":** the formula "X disbelieved the messengers" occurs exactly **five** times corpus-wide, all here — `[26:105/123/141/160/176]` (rg-verified, zero hits outside s026). Moses (`10–68`) is a longer, non-formulaic sixth mission; Abraham (`69–104`) a seventh story with no destruction refrain.
* **Brother formula drifts:** `[26:106]` "Their brother Noah" (no "When"); `[26:124/142/161]` "When their brother Hud/Saleh/Lot"; `[26:177]` "When Shuaib said to them" — **no "brother" at all**, and his people are "The People of the Woods" (`[26:176]`, phrase unique in corpus), elsewhere "Median" (`[7:85]`, `[11:85]`, `[29:36]` — verified). Source: classical identification of the two audience-names as one people — verify.
* **"Faithful messenger":** `[26:107/125/143/162]` render *amin* "faithful"; `[26:178]` alone drifts to "trustworthy" (corpus-wide only these five lines match; verified).
* **"I ask you no reward" loci (exact):** `[26:109]`, `[26:127]`, `[26:145]`, `[26:164]`, `[26:180]` — rendered "payment," and the preposition drifts: "for this" (109/127) → "for it" (145/164/180). NOT at 26:27 (that is Pharaoh's "This messenger of yours... is crazy") nor 26:214 ("And warn your close relatives"); the Moses cycle never says it. Nearest external twins: `[25:57]`, `[23:72]`, `[52:40]` (verified).
* **"Fear Allah, and obey me" doublet:** twice in Noah (`108+110`), Hud (`126+131`), Saleh (`144+150`); once in Lot (`163`) and Shuaib (`179`) — each cycle's middle slot carries that people's specific indictment (`128–130`, `146–149`, `165–166`, `181–184`).
* **Elite dismissal is NOT one line:** "the lowliest who follow you" = Noah's people only (`[26:111]`; twin `[58:20]`, full dossier `[11:27]`); "nothing but a man like us" = Saleh's (`[26:154]`) and Shuaib's (`[26:186]`); Aad answers with apathy (`136–138`), Lot's people with expulsion (`167`).
* **Closing refrains per cycle:** Noah `121–122` ("In that is a sign" — no "Surely"); Hud `139–140` (welded to the destruction sentence); Saleh `158–159` (welded); Lot `174–175`; Shuaib `190–191`. "but most of them are not believers" occurs **8×** in-surah (`8, 67, 103, 121, 139, 158, 174, 190`); "Almighty, the Merciful" **9×** (`9` "Most surely", `68` "Surely", `104/122/140/159/175/191` bare, `217` within the trust-command).
* **Assignment line-fixes:** Noah cycle = `105–122` (not 10–20; `10–20` is Moses' commission). "We will be with you, listening" = `[26:15]`, God→Moses (twin `[20:46]` "I am with you, I hear and I see"); "my Lord is with me, He will guide me" = `[26:62]` at the sea — neither belongs to Noah. Hud = `123–140` (not 123–135). Saleh `141–159`, Lot `160–175`, Shuaib `176–191` all confirmed; commerce clause `181–183` ↔ `[17:35]` near-verbatim, cf. `[11:85]`, `[7:85]`, `[83:1–3]`.
* **Abraham:** commission `[26:69]` closes ruku 4; scene `70–104` is ruku 5. `[26:80]` verbatim: "And when I get sick, He heals me."; "a sound heart" `[26:89]` twin `[37:84]`.
* **Poets:** actual `[26:224]` "And as for the poets—the deviators follow them." (not "the lost"); the exception of `[26:227]` has **four** clauses — believe, do good deeds, remember Allah frequently, *and defend themselves after they are wronged* (assignment omitted the fourth). The surah ends on "they will know by what overturning they will be overturned" — a "Lord of the Mighty Dominion" ending does **not** exist ("Mighty Dominion": zero corpus hits).
* **Revelation cluster `192–199`:** wording is "It is a revelation from the Lord of the Worlds / The Honest Spirit came down with it / Upon your heart... / In a clear Arabic tongue" — a "We sent down it" grammar belongs to `[15:9]` ("Surely We revealed the Message, and We will surely preserve it"), the verified kalam twin; `[26:196]` "And it is in the scriptures of the ancients."; `[26:197]` actually "the scholars of the Children of Israel recognized it" (not "the knowledge of the rabbis"; cf. `[5:44]` "the rabbis and the scholars"); `[26:195]` "In a clear Arabic tongue" has a verbatim twin tail at `[16:103]` (verified).

## 4. Structural Arc
* **Frame and refusal (1–9):** letters → Book-claim → grief-rebuke → the optative forced sign declined → creation already evidences → refrain installed (it will recur 8×).
* **The Mosaic trial (10–68):** commission, court argument over "the Lord of the Worlds," the magicians' conversion, the sea — each block closed by the sign-refrain; accompaniment ("with you, listening," 15; "my Lord is with me," 62) is the narrative's creed-spine.
* **Abraham's disputation (69–104):** worship audited by benefit; the favor-list (78–82); the sound heart (89); the Blaze scene rehearses the Day.
* **The five refrain cycles (105–191):** identical skeleton with per-people indictments — class snobbery (Noah), monumental vanity (Aad), security delusion + the she-camel (Thamood), sexual transgression (Lot), commercial fraud (the Woods) — each ending in destruction + refrain.
* **Self-authentication (192–227):** the channel defended (revelation, Honest Spirit, Arabic tongue, Israel's scholars, devils barred), the messenger instructed (213–220), and poetry judged (221–227) — the follow-motif opened at 39–40 is closed at 224.
