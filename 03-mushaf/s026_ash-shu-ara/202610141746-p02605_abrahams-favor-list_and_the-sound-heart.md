# Abraham's favor-list and the sound heart: worship audited by causal power (26:70–104)
**UID:** 202610141746
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 26:70–104 (Ruku 5 of 11 in ash-Shu'ara')
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[26:70]` – `[26:104]` (35 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The one invitation scene with no destruction refrain: Abraham tests his idols by causality (do they hear, benefit, harm?), the people's only defense is ancestry, and he answers with a first-person favor-list — created, guides, feeds, waters, heals, kills, revives — ending on the "sound heart," while the Day-scene (90–102) rehearses the idols' final incompetence: no help, no intercessor, no sincere friend, only the wish for "another chance."
* **Placement:** Ruku 5; the hinge between the Moses block and the five refrain cycles. The commission line `[26:69]` ("And relate to them the story of Abraham") sits at the tail of ruku 4 — the boundary splits command from scene (observable in the map: ruku 4 = 53–69).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[26:70]–[26:71]`** “When he said to his father and his people, ‘What do you worship? / They said, ‘We worship idols, and we remain devoted to them.’”
  * **Gloss:** The opening question forces the creed into the open — worship first, truth later; devotion ("remain devoted") is honest and misplaced.
* **`[26:72]–[26:73]`** “Do they hear you when you pray? / Or do they benefit you, or harm you?”
  * **Gloss:** The causal audit: hearing, benefit, harm — three tests a god must at least be able to fail on purpose; they cannot even be asked.
* **`[26:74]`** “They said, ‘But we found our ancestors doing so.’”
  * **Gloss:** The defense collapses from function to inheritance — the Quran's standing reply to traditionalism (twins verified: `[21:53]`, `[43:22]`, `[31:21]`, `[7:28]`).
* **`[26:75]–[26:76]`** “Have you considered what you worship. / You and your ancient ancestors?”
  * **Gloss:** The same ancestry weapon turned back: the object and its lineage, both on trial.
* **`[26:77]`** “They are enemies to me, but not so the Lord of the Worlds.”
  * **Gloss:** The dissociation formula — the whole created order is adversary except Rabb al-alamin; the surah's keyword returns as Abraham's boundary line (cf. his own use at `47–48`).
* **`[26:78] / [26:79] / [26:80] / [26:81] / [26:82]`** “He who created me, and guides me. / He who feeds me, and waters me. / And when I get sick, He heals me. / He who makes me die, and then revives me. / He who, I hope, will forgive my sins on the Day of the Reckoning.”
  * **Gloss:** The favor-list: eight first-person acts of care (create, guide, feed, water, heal, kill, revive, hope-for-forgive) — tawhid stated as biography, a gratitude audit of one life; the note that sin-forgiveness enters only under "hope" (`yarju`, "I hope") keeps even Abraham's eschatology deferential (Source: agent observation on Itani's rendering — verify the classical hope/ certainty discussion).
* **`[26:83] / [26:84] / [26:85] / [26:86]`** “My Lord! Grant me wisdom, and include me with the righteous. / And give me a reputation of truth among the others. / And make me of the inheritors of the Garden of Bliss. / And forgive my father—he was one of the misguided.”
  * **Gloss:** Four petitions: wisdom, truthful fame, paradise, father; the last is the tenderest and will be answered by withdrawal elsewhere (`[19:49]` "he withdrew from them" — verified twin).
* **`[26:87] / [26:88] / [26:89]`** “And do not disgrace me on the Day they are resurrected. / The Day when neither wealth nor children will help. / Except for him who comes to Allah with a sound heart.”
  * **Gloss:** The fifth petition pivots to the Day; the currency of rescue is redefined — not wealth, not lineage (answering `74`'s ancestor-argument) but a "sound heart" (twin verified: `[37:84]`).
* **`[26:90] / [26:91] / [26:92] / [26:93]`** “And Paradise will be brought near for the righteous. / And the Blaze will be displayed to the deviators. / And it will be said to them, ‘Where are those you used to worship? / Besides Allah? Can they help you, or help themselves?’”
  * **Gloss:** The Day-scene restages `72–73` verbatim-in-substance: the causal audit becomes a roll-call; the idols fail in front of the saved.
* **`[26:94] / [26:95] / [26:96]`** “Then they will be toppled into it, together with the seducers. / And the soldiers of Satan, all of them. / They will say, as they feud in it.”
  * **Gloss:** Worshipped and worshippers fall together; "soldiers of Satan" is corpus-hapax here (rg-verified) — the devotional army reversed into a routed one.
* **`[26:97] / [26:98] / [26:99]`** “‘By Allah, We were in evident error. / For equating you with the Lord of the Worlds. / No one misled us except the sinners.’"
  * **Gloss:** The posthumous confession uses the surah's own oath and the exact sin: equating (`98`, "equating you with the Lord of the Worlds") — tawhid's negation named from inside the Blaze.
* **`[26:100] / [26:101] / [26:102]`** “‘Now we have no intercessors. / And no sincere friend. / If only we could have another chance, we would be among the faithful.’”
  * **Gloss:** The inventory of lost capital — intercessors, friend, chance; the wish is the family phrase of the regretful (`[6:27]`, `[2:167]`, `[39:58]` — all verified twins).
* **`[26:103]–[26:104]`** “Surely in this is a sign, but most of them are not believers. / Your Lord is the Almighty, the Merciful.”
  * **Gloss:** Refrain pair #3 (in-surah `rg`-count: 8/9 sites, index §2) closes the only story with no destruction verb — Abraham's people are not destroyed here; the argument is left running into the cycles.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`qalb salim`** (a sound heart, `89`): unscarred, whole; the sole cargo at judgment.
  * **Rendering:** "a sound heart" — phrase-hapax in the corpus (verified), its twin verb-phrase at `[37:84]` "When he came to his Lord with a sound heart."
  * **Elsewhere:** the negative counterpart is the surah's own ruku 11: revelation "passes through the hearts of the guilty" (`200`) — hearts as reception apparatus, sound or infected.
* **`Garden of Bliss`** (`85`): the petitioned inheritance.
  * **Rendering:** "the Garden of Bliss"; elsewhere only `[70:38]` "aspiring to be admitted into a Garden of Bliss" (verified).
  * **Elsewhere (in-surah):** contrast with the *unasked* inheritance at `59` — Pharaoh's gardens passed to Israel by eviction; true inheritance is requested, not seized (Source: agent observation — verify).
* **`reputation of truth`** (`84`): a good name that speaks truth — memory as afterlife.
  * **Rendering:** "a reputation of truth"; twin `[19:50]` "gave them a noble reputation of truth" (verified) — same translator's collocation only in the Abraham dossier.
* **the help-audit** (`72–73`, `93`): same verbs re-used at the Day — "help you, or help themselves"; the scene pair that makes `90–102` the verdict on `70–74`.
