# The Lot cycle: the expulsion threat, the old woman, the rain of the forewarned (26:160–175)
**UID:** 202610141750
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 26:160–175 (Ruku 9 of 11 in ash-Shu'ara')
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[26:160]` – `[26:175]` (16 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Cycle four indicts the disordering of desire — "Do you approach the males of the world? / And forsake the wives your Lord created for you?" — and receives the template's politest threats ("unless you refrain... you will be expelled"); Lot deploys with his family, one exception (an old woman among those who tarried), and the city dies under a rain, the surah's own wordplay closing: this is "the rain of those forewarned" — the warners' ministry (`194`, `208`) named at its fatal payoff.
* **Placement:** Ruku 9; fourth refrain cycle. Its middle slot (165–166) is the shortest indictment of the five; its single obey-line (163) follows immediately, with no second doublet after the charge (verified against 108/110, 126/131, 144/150 — the doublet pattern breaks at Lot and Shu'ayb).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[26:160] / [26:161] / [26:162] / [26:163]`** “The people of Lot disbelieved the messengers. / When their brother Lot said to them, ‘Do you not fear? / I am to you a faithful messenger. / So fear Allah, and obey me.”
  * **Gloss:** Template-strict opener; brother-formula intact ("their brother Lot") where Shu'ayb's will drop it (index §2).
* **`[26:164]`** “I ask of you no payment for it. My payment is only from the Lord of the Worlds.”
  * **Gloss:** Payment-clause #4, "for it" variant.
* **`[26:165]–[26:166]`** “Do you approach the males of the world? / And forsake the wives your Lord created for you? Indeed, you are intrusive people.”
  * **Gloss:** The charge: a boundary crossed "of the world" (addressed to all humanity's decency, or "beyond the worlds'" — the rendering keeps the phrase; twins verified: `[29:29]` "You approach men, and cut off the way", `[27:54–55]` "commit lewdness... lust after men instead of women"); the verdict-name "intrusive people" is corpus-hapax (rg-verified) against 27:55's "ignorant people".
* **`[26:167]`** “They said, ‘Unless you refrain, O Lot, you will be expelled.’”
  * **Gloss:** The elite dismissal as immigration threat — the cycle's only threat that is not murder or sky-fall; irony-verified twin: `[7:82]` "Expel them from your town; they are purist people" (the cities' claim to purity inverting the surah's "sound heart" motif, `89`).
* **`[26:168]`** “He said, ‘I certainly deplore your conduct.’”
  * **Gloss:** "deplore" is corpus-hapax (rg-verified): the prophet's affect in the presence of the people's program — distance stated, then enacted.
* **`[26:169]`** “‘My Lord, save me and my family from what they do.’”
  * **Gloss:** The rescue petition: salvation from (min) their deeds, not merely from their stones.
* **`[26:170] / [26:171] / [26:172]`** “So We saved him and his family, altogether. / Except for an old woman among those who tarried. / Then We destroyed the others.”
  * **Gloss:** The family saved "altogether" — then the single exception, "an old woman among those who tarried"; 26 never calls her his wife, while the parallel tellings do (`[7:83]` "except for his wife; she was of those who lagged behind"; `[27:57]` "his wife, whom We destined to be among the laggards"; `[37:135]` "an old woman who lagged behind"; `[66:10]` names both Noah's and Lot's wives as betrayers — all verified): this surah's editorial silence is observable (Source: agent observation).
* **`[26:173]`** “And We rained down on them a rain. Dreadful is the rain of those forewarned.”
  * **Gloss:** The stones-and-overturning of other tellings (`15:73–74` "the Blast... at sunrise... turned it upside down... stones of baked clay", verified) is here one word: rain — heaven's gift-mode inverted; the exact verse-twin is `[27:58]` "Miserable was the rain of those forewarned" and the family phrase `[37:177]` "miserable will be the morning of those forewarned" (verified).
* **`[26:174]–[26:175]`** “Surely in this is a sign, but most of them are not believers. / Your Lord is the Almighty, the Merciful.”
  * **Gloss:** Refrain #7 of the 8-site in-surah count (verified) — the mercy-name held over a drowned city.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`indhar`/`nudhur` — warning** (`173`, `194`, `208`): the n-dh-r root threading the surah.
  * **Rendering:** "those forewarned" at `173`; "one of the warners" `194`; "it had warners" `208`; the warner's title itself: "I am only a clear warner" `115` (all in-surah, verified).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[6:48]` "We sent the messengers only as bearers of good news and as warners" (verified) — the same office the whole surah is auditing.
* **`approach`** (`165`): violation as proximity-grammar.
  * **Rendering:** "approach the males of the world"; twin `[29:29]` "approach men, and cut off the way" (verified) — the crime bound to a second social crime (highway) in the longer dossier.
* **`tarried`** (`171`): the exception by inertia.
  * **Rendering:** "among those who tarried"; twins render the same fate "lagged behind" (7:83), "among the laggards" (27:57), "who lagged behind" (37:135) (verified) — one sin, three translator shades: delay, rear-guard, lag.
* **`intrusive people`** (`166`): the verdict-name.
  * **Rendering:** corpus-hapax (verified); sharpened by `[7:80]`-family where the people's own word for themselves is purity (7:82, verified).
