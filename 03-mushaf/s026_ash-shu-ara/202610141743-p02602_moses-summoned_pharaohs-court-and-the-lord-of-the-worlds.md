# Moses summoned, Pharaoh cross-examined: the Lord of the Worlds on trial (26:10–33)
**UID:** 202610141743
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 26:10–33 (Ruku 2 of 11 in ash-Shu'ara')
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[26:10]` – `[26:33]` (24 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The commission scene: Moses' three fears answered with divine accompaniment ("We will be with you, listening"), a courtroom exchange in which Pharaoh mocks memory and status while Moses re-defines "the Lord of the Worlds" three times, and two visibly plain signs handed over uncoerced.
* **Placement:** Ruku 2; opens the longest narrative in the surah (ruku 2–4, 10–68). Its taqwa question (`11`) pre-echoes the five refrain cycles; its accompaniment line (`15`) is the surah's creed-spine, twin at `[20:46]` and payoff at `[26:62]`.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[26:10]–[26:11]`** "Your Lord called to Moses, 'Go to the tyrannical people. / The people of Pharaoh. Will they not fear?'"
  * **Gloss:** Mission framed as address; the very first content of the commission is the fear-question — the refrain's verb before the refrain exists.
* **`[26:12] / [26:13] / [26:14]`** "He said, 'My Lord, I fear they will reject me. / And I become stressed, and my tongue is not fluent, so send Aaron too. / And they have a charge against me, so I fear they will kill me.'"
  * **Gloss:** Three candid fears — rejection, speech, blood-charge; the petition names Aaron itself. Twin petition `[20:27]` "And untie the knot from my tongue" (verified).
* **`[26:15]`** "He said, 'No. Go, both of you, with Our proofs. We will be with you, listening.'"
  * **Gloss:** The divine answer is accompaniment, not competence: *ma'iyyah* + hearing — the strongest tawhid/guidance anchor in the scene; verbatim register twin `[20:46]` "Do not fear, I am with you, I hear and I see" (verified).
* **`[26:16]–[26:17]`** "Go to Pharaoh, and say, 'We are the Messengers of the Lord of the Worlds. / Let the Children of Israel go with us.'"
  * **Gloss:** The claim ("Messengers of the Lord of the Worlds") precedes the demand (let my people go) — politics derives from theology here.
* **`[26:18]–[26:19]`** Pharaoh: "Did we not raise you among us as a child... And you committed that deed you committed, and you were ungrateful."
  * **Gloss:** Status-attack: foster-child, killer, ingrate — the elite dismissal mode before the cycles formalize it.
* **`[26:20] / [26:21] / [26:22]`** Moses: "I did it then, when I was of those astray... my Lord gave me wisdom, and made me one of the messengers... Is that the favor you taunt me with, although you have enslaved the Children of Israel?"
  * **Gloss:** The deed is admitted, contextually minimized, and out-argued: the real grievance is their slavery.
* **`[26:23]–[26:24]`** "Pharaoh said, 'And what is the Lord of the Worlds?' He said, 'The Lord of the heavens and the earth, and everything between them, if you are aware.'"
  * **Gloss:** The surah's central question, asked in bad faith; the answer is cosmos-plus-inter-space, closed with an intelligence appeal.
* **`[26:25]–[26:26]`** "He said to those around him, 'Do you not hear?' He said, 'Your Lord and the Lord of your ancestors of old.'"
  * **Gloss:** Mockery to the court; Moses escalates from cosmos to kinship — the same "Lord of your ancestors" counter in `[37:126]` (Elijah scene, verified).
* **`[26:27]–[26:28]`** "He said, 'This messenger of yours, who is sent to you, is crazy.' He said, 'Lord of the East and the West, and everything between them, if you understand.'"
  * **Gloss:** Diagnostic dismissal ("crazy") met with a third definition — axes of the compass now; the assignment's "no reward" guess lands wrong here: 26:27 is this insult, the payment formula is in the cycles (index §2).
* **`[26:29] / [26:30] / [26:31]`** "If you accept any god other than me, I will make you a prisoner.' He said, 'What if I bring you something convincing?' He said, 'Bring it, if you are being truthful.'"
  * **Gloss:** Power closes the debate; Moses re-opens it with an offered proof — signs come uncoerced, against `26:4`'s declined forced-sign.
* **`[26:32]–[26:33]`** "So he cast his staff; and it was a serpent, plain to see. / And he pulled his hand; and it was white, for all to see."
  * **Gloss:** Two signs, both "plain/for all to see" — visible as the Book is clarifying (`26:2` echo); staff-miracle near-twin `[28:31]` (verified).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`Rabb al-alamin`** (Lord of the Worlds): the trial's keyword — asked `23`, answered `24/26/28`.
  * **Rendering:** stable "the Lord of the Worlds"; in this surah it is the confession-word of the magicians (`47–48`), Abraham's exception (`77`), and the payment-clause's punchline (`109/127/145/164/180`).
  * **Elsewhere:** the formula saturates the corpus; here it functions as the answer to "what is God?" — definition, not slogan (Source: agent observation on 26's usage cluster — verify).
* **`ma'iyyah + sami'`** ("We will be with you, listening," `15`):
  * **Rendering:** "listening" for the divine accompaniment (the Arabic pairs hearing with seeing; Itani's 26:15 gives one verb).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[20:46]` "I hear and I see"; the surah's own payoff is `62` "my Lord is with me" and its closing attribute `220` "the Hearer, the Aware" (verified) — the ruku-frame of this narrative block is hearing-and-seeing.
* **`taqwa`** ("Will they not fear?", `11`): the commission's point of order; the cycles' summoning verb `106/124/142/161/177` (verified ×5) and near-twin in the Elijah cycle `[37:124]` (verified).
* **`burhan`-family** ("something convincing," `30`):
  * **Rendering:** "something convincing"; the Shuaib parallel is "a clear proof" `[7:85]` (verified).
  * **Force:** proof offered ≠ proof coerced — keep with `26:4`.
