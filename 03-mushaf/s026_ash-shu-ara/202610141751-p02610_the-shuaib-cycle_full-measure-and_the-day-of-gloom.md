# The Shu'ayb cycle: full measure, accurate scales, and the day of gloom (26:176–191)
**UID:** 202610141751
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 26:176–191 (Ruku 10 of 11 in ash-Shu'ara')
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[26:176]` – `[26:191]` (16 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Cycle five — and the template's last confession: the people is named twice over ("People of the Woods," elsewhere Median), the brother-formula drops entirely, "faithful" drifts to "trustworthy," and the middle slot is a commerce sermon: full measure, accurate scales, no defrauding, no corruption — so the surah's theology ends where money is weighed; the dismissal is the standard stack ("bewitched," "man like us," "bring pieces of the sky"), and the punishment is a weather event, "the day of gloom."
* **Placement:** Ruku 10; final refrain cycle. Its economic charge completes the sin-census of the block (class, vanity, security, desire, fraud), and its failure to call Shu'ayb "brother" marks the formula's outer limit (index §2).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[26:176] / [26:177] / [26:178] / [26:179]`** “The People of the Woods disbelieved the messengers. / When Shuaib said to them, ‘Do you not fear? / I am to you a trustworthy messenger. / So fear Allah, and obey me.”
  * **Gloss:** Formula opener with the drifts: no "brother," "trustworthy" not "faithful" (both verified in index §2); "The People of the Woods" is corpus-hapax here, twin `[15:78]` "The people of the Woods were also wrongdoers" and the same people as Median in `[7:85]`/`[11:84]` (verified).
* **`[26:180]`** “I ask of you no payment for it. My payment is only from the Lord of the Worlds.”
  * **Gloss:** Payment-clause #5 — the cycle-block's money-frame closes as it opened; the merchants' prophet cannot be bought.
* **`[26:181] / [26:182] / [26:183]`** “Give full measure, and do not cheat. / And weigh with accurate scales. / And do not defraud people of their belongings, and do not work corruption in the land.”
  * **Gloss:** The commerce clause in four negatives-plus-positives: measure, weigh, belongings, land — the closest near-verbatim twins in the corpus: `[17:35]` "give full measure when you measure, and weigh with accurate scales" and `[11:85]`/`[7:85]` "Give full measure and... do not cheat the people out of their rights... do not spread corruption" (verified).
* **`[26:184]`** “And fear Him who created you and the masses of old.”
  * **Gloss:** "masses of old" is corpus-hapax (verified) — market ethics is grounded in origins: the same Creator made you and the generations you now defraud.
* **`[26:185] / [26:186] / [26:187]`** “They said, ‘You are one of those bewitched. / And you are nothing but a man like us; and we think that you are a liar. / So bring down on us pieces from the sky, if you are truthful.’”
  * **Gloss:** The full dismissal stack — possession, species, veracity — plus the most aggressive sign-demand in the block; "pieces from the sky" has its twin in `[34:9]` "make pieces of the sky fall down on them" (verified); cf. `153` (Salih) and `27` (Moses) for the dossier (p02602 §4).
* **`[26:188]`** “He said, ‘My Lord is Well Aware of what you do.’”
  * **Gloss:** No sign is summoned — the ledger is handed to divine awareness; the surah's closing attribute (`220` "the Hearer, the Aware") is rehearsed here (in-surah, verified).
* **`[26:189]`** “But they denied him. So the punishment of the day of gloom gripped them. It was the punishment of a great day.”
  * **Gloss:** Denial (singular, "denied him," like `139`'s grammar) → the shaded day — the cloud/scorching-wind tradition needs no props here; "gloom" external twins are Day-faces (22:22, 75:24, 67:27 — verified) while the revenge on this people is stated at `[15:79]` "So We took revenge upon them" (verified); "a great day" closes the dread-series `135`/`156`/`189`.
* **`[26:190]–[26:191]`** “Surely in this is a sign, but most of them are not believers. / Your Lord is the Almighty, the Merciful.”
  * **Gloss:** Refrain pair #8 — the block ends mid-surah: five peoples, one skeleton, every ending named "the Almighty, the Merciful."

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`keyal`/`mizan` — measure and weight** (`181–182`):
  * **Rendering:** "Give full measure... weigh with accurate scales"; `[17:35]` verbatim on "accurate scales" (verified); `[11:85]` adds "in all fairness"; `[7:85]` renders "measure and weight" as a pair.
  * **Elsewhere:** the indictment side: `[83:1–3]` "Woe to the defrauders... when they measure or weigh to others, they cheat" (verified) — the surah whose name (Defrauders) is built from this clause-family.
* **`defraud... belongings`** (`183`): `bukhtān`-family — reducing people's assets.
  * **Rendering:** "do not defraud people of their belongings"; twins "cheat people out of their rights" (`7:85`), "cheat the people out of their rights" (`11:85`) (verified).
* **`People of the Woods`** (`176`): the doubly-named audience.
  * **Rendering:** "The People of the Woods" (corpus-hapax in this capitalization; `[15:78]` twin, verified); the Median identification (`7:85`, `11:84`, `29:36`) is classical — Source: consensus of classical tafsir — verify.
* **`trustworthy`** (`178`): the single drift of `amin`.
  * **Elsewhere:** only Shu'ayb gets "trustworthy"; the other four cycles and the Elijah-skeleton share "faithful" (index §2) — the one word changed in a five-fold repetition, a translator's signal (Source: observable translator choice).
