# The sea parted: "No; my Lord is with me, He will guide me" (26:53–69)
**UID:** 202610141745
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 26:53–69 (Ruku 4 of 11 in ash-Shu'ara')
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[26:53]` – `[26:69]` (17 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Trap and deliverance: Pharaoh re-counts his majority against a "small gang," pursues to the water's edge, and the crowd panics — while Moses utters the surah's creed-verse, "No; my Lord is with me, He will guide me"; the sea obeys, the blessing-inventory of Egypt changes hands, and the refrain closes the longest narrative before the Abraham commission hinges the surah onward.
* **Placement:** Ruku 4; the payoff of `26:15` ("We will be with you, listening") and the surah's high-water mark for the accompaniment motif; `69` is a one-line hinge into ruku 5.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[26:53] / [26:54] / [26:55] / [26:56]`** "Pharaoh sent heralds to the cities. / 'These are a small gang. / And they are enraging us. / But we are a vigilant multitude.'"
  * **Gloss:** The elite's census-sneer answered in the surah's own arithmetic — numbers are the weak argument, "the lowliest who follow you" (`111`) is its twin.
* **`[26:57] / [26:58] / [26:59]`** "So We drove them out of gardens and springs. / And treasures and noble dwellings. / So it was. And We made the Children of Israel inherit them."
  * **Gloss:** The exodus in reverse as property transfer; the blessing-list (gardens/springs) is the same inventory Thamood will boast of (`147`) and Pharaoh's own people called home — inheritance-of-blessing as divine jurisprudence (Source: agent observation on the shared vocabulary — verify).
* **`[26:60]`** "And they pursued them at sunrise."
  * **Gloss:** The hour detail — pursuit at the most propitious time, which becomes the site of ruin.
* **`[26:61]`** "When the two groups sighted each other, the followers of Moses said, 'We are being overtaken.'"
  * **Gloss:** The believers' panic is quoted, not suppressed — the surah keeps the doubt of the saved alongside the faith of Moses.
* **`[26:62]`** "He said, 'No; my Lord is with me, He will guide me.'"
  * **Gloss:** The surah's shortest sermon: accompaniment (`15`) plus guidance; cf. Abraham's "He who created me, and guides me" (`78`) — the same yahdini verb (verified in-surah).
* **`[26:63]`** "We inspired Moses: 'Strike the sea with your staff.' Whereupon it parted, and each part was like a huge hill."
  * **Gloss:** The second inspiration in two ayahs (`52`, `63`); the staff's strike-grammar is the family of `[2:60]` "Strike the rock with your staff" (verified); "like a huge hill" is corpus-unique here (`rg`, verified).
* **`[26:64]`** "And there We brought the others near."
  * **Gloss:** Divine proximity as prosecution: the pursuers are delivered to the dry path they thought was theirs.
* **`[26:65]–[26:66]`** "And We saved Moses and those with him, all together. / Then We drowned the others."
  * **Gloss:** "All together" / "the others" — the sorting is total and binary; cf. Lot's parallel pair in ruku 9 (`170–172`).
* **`[26:67]–[26:68]`** "In that there is a sign, but most of them are not believers. / Surely, your Lord is the Almighty, the Merciful."
  * **Gloss:** Refrain #2 — variant "In that there is a sign" (index §2); the drowning scene still ends in mercy-and-power naming.
* **`[26:69]`** "And relate to them the story of Abraham."
  * **Gloss:** The recital-command: the surah reveals its method — stories as testimony, delivered on order (Source: agent observation; the command verb opens the Abraham block — verify).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`ma'iyyah`** ("my Lord is with me," `62`): accompaniment as the answer to being "overtaken" (`61`).
  * **Rendering:** "is with me, He will guide me"; paired upstream with `26:15` "We will be with you, listening" and outside with `[20:46]` "I am with you, I hear and I see" (verified) — three registers (plural divine, personal confession, Ta-Ha oracle) of one claim.
* **`huda`** ("He will guide me," `62`):
  * **Elsewhere:** `78` "created me, and guides me" — Abraham's first-person version; `26:62/78` are the surah's two guidance-verbs (verified in-surah).
  * **Force:** guidance here is route-finding under pursuit — the road-with-conditions of the guidance note, in its hardest case.
* **`fulk`/strike-staff:** `63` "Strike the sea with your staff."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:60]` (rock, same staff-grammar, verified); the staff also swallows at `45` and is thrown at `32` — one object, four acts, the surah's running miracle.
* **`inherit`** (`59`): "We made the Children of Israel inherit them" — blessing-transfer verb; cf. the Garden of Bliss inheritance request `85` ("make me of the inheritors," verified in-surah): true inheritance is asked for, not taunted with.
