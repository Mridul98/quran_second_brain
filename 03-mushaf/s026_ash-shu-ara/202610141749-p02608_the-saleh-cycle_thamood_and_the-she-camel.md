# The Salih cycle: Thamood's security delusion and the she-camel's turn (26:141–159)
**UID:** 202610141749
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 26:141–159 (Ruku 8 of 11 in ash-Shu'ara')
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[26:141]` – `[26:159]` (19 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Cycle three indicts the complacently secure: Thamood is asked whether they expect to be "left secure" in gardens, springs, fields, palms and mountain-carved houses — and their sign is not a spectacle but a scheduling: a she-camel with her turn of drinking and theirs, "on a specified day"; they kill her, feel remorse, and are overtaken — destruction welded to the refrain, one line.
* **Placement:** Ruku 8; third refrain cycle — the middle slot (`146–152`) trades Aad's monuments for prosperity-as-property and a ban on obeying "the extravagant"; the sign-demand of `154` receives the least spectacular sign in the Quran: an animal with a water-allocation.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[26:141] / [26:142] / [26:143] / [26:144]`** “Thamood disbelieved the messengers. / When their brother Saleh said to them, ‘Do you not fear? / I am to you a faithful messenger. / So fear Allah, and obey me.”
  * **Gloss:** Template-strict opener; "Saleh" is this edition's spelling of Salih (observable — see the Salih dossier in `[7:73]` rendered "Saleh" too, verified).
* **`[26:145]`** “I ask of you no payment for it. My payment is only from the Lord of the Worlds.”
  * **Gloss:** Payment-clause #3 — the preposition drifts here to "for it" (145/164/180 vs 109/127 "for this"; index §2).
* **`[26:146] / [26:147] / [26:148] / [26:149]`** “Will you be left secure in what is here? / In gardens and springs? / And fields, and palm-trees whose fruits are delicious? / And you skillfully carve houses in the mountains?”
  * **Gloss:** The security question: the inventory (gardens/springs — third occurrence in the surah, cf. `57–58`, `133–134`) plus fields, palms, carved homes — prosperity read as permanence; twin `[7:74]` "carve out dwellings in the mountains" (verified).
* **`[26:150] / [26:151] / [26:152]`** “So fear Allah, and obey me. / And do not obey the command of the extravagant. / Who spread turmoil on earth, and do not reform.”
  * **Gloss:** The doublet closes with its negative: two commands of obedience — God's, and never that of the "extravagant" whose signature is anti-reform (the reform verb-cluster pairs verified: `[6:48]` "believe and reform", `[5:39]` "repents... and reforms").
* **`[26:153]–[26:154]`** “They said, ‘You are surely one of the bewitched. / You are nothing but a man like us. So bring us a sign, if you are truthful.”
  * **Gloss:** The dismissal stack: magic-possession ("bewitched" — same charge Pharaoh throws at Moses in `[17:101]`, verified; and the people of the table in `[15:15]`), then the human-objection (twin `[11:27]` "nothing but a man like us"), then the sign-on-commission demand (`4` already declined forced signs; cf. `30`, `187`).
* **`[26:155]`** “He said, ‘This is a she-camel; she has her turn of drinking, and you have your turn of drinking—on a specified day.”
  * **Gloss:** The sign with a schedule: alternating water-shares, one day each — the miracle is a resource-sharing agreement (twin `[54:28]` "the water is to be shared between them; each share of drink made available", verified; `[7:73]` "This she-camel of Allah is a sign for you", `[11:64]`, `[91:13]` — all verified).
* **`[26:156]`** “And do not touch her with harm, lest the punishment of a great day seizes you.”
  * **Gloss:** The protective rider on the contract; "a great day" joins the dread-series (`135`, `189`).
* **`[26:157]`** “But they slaughtered her, and became full of remorse.”
  * **Gloss:** They kill the schedule; remorse arrives on time but is useless — "became full of remorse" belongs to the regret-doctrine (`34:33`, `10:54`, `80:41` — verified); note the rendering drift: 7:77/91:14 say "hamstrung" (verified) where 26 says "slaughtered" (observable translator choice).
* **`[26:158]`** “So the punishment overtook them. Surely in this is a sign, but most of them are not believers.”
  * **Gloss:** Welded destruction+refrain (same fuse as `139`); twin mood `[11:66]` "We saved Saleh... from the disgrace of that day" (verified).
* **`[26:159]`** “Your Lord is the Almighty, the Merciful.”
  * **Gloss:** Refrain line two, cycle three of five.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`nauba` / "turn of drinking"** (`155`):
  * **Rendering:** "she has her turn of drinking, and you have your turn of drinking—on a specified day" — corpus-hapax construction (rg-verified); twin 54:28 above.
  * **Elsewhere:** "on a specified day" echoes `38` (the magicians' "appointment on a specified day", verified) — the pagan spectacle and the covenant miracle share day-grammar (flagged in p02603 §3).
* **`she-camel`** (155): God's animal as sign.
  * **Rendering:** "a she-camel" (indefinite here; `[7:73]`/`[11:64]`/`[91:13]` have "the she-camel of Allah" — verified).
  * **Elsewhere:** destruction-mode twins: `[54:31]` "a single Scream... like crushed hay"; `[91:14]` "they called him a liar, and hamstrung her. So their Lord crushed them" (verified).
* **`the extravagant`** (`151`): the politics of the middle slot.
  * **Rendering:** "the command of the extravagant" — the word-locus for elite capture of Thamood's water economy (Source: agent observation — verify against 11:116's "élite"/"transgressors" dossier).
* **`remorse`** (`157`): the surah's first late regret.
  * **Elsewhere:** `203` "Are we given any respite?" is the same emotion after the deadline — the surah's whole plot says remorse is only currency before the act (Source: agent observation).
