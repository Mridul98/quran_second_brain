# Surah 92: THE NIGHT (al-Layl)
**UID:** 202610142313
**Type:** Index / Surah Map
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Meccan — agreed in the classical type-lists; the night/day/male-female oath set and the two-ways judgment register sit in the early-Meccan consolation-and-sorting cluster. *Source: classical type-lists — verify.* Revelation order: **transmitted nuzul tables — verify** (corpus states no nuzul; unnumbered Basmala heads the surah).
* **Size:** 21 ayahs, 1 ruku (ruku_map global 534 — verified). The long center of the 91–97 oath-surah run: longer than its neighbors but built the same way — oath, thesis, sorting.
* **Title:** al-Layl — Itani's header "THE NIGHT" tracks `[92:1]` "By the night as it covers"; the same oath-word recurs of the night at `[91:4]`, `[74:33]`, `[81:17]`, `[89:4]` (rg-verified).
* **Central theme:** Diversity of endeavor as the sworn fact: "Your endeavors are indeed diverse" (92:4) — and the surah then shows the diversity collapsing into TWO directions: the giver-righteous-confirmer is eased toward ease (92:5–7), the stingy-complacent-denier eased toward difficulty (92:8–10). Money, which the second track hoards, is declared useless "when he plummets" (92:11); guidance and both abodes belong to Allah (92:12–13); the Fierce Blaze takes the denier-turnaway (92:14–16); the purifying giver, seeking "nothing but the acceptance of his Lord" (92:20), "will be satisfied" (92:21).
* **Reading hint:** watch the easing-verb run both tracks (92:7, 92:10 — same verb *yassara*, opposite destinations) and hear the cluster-echoes: 87:8 "We will ease you into the Easy Way" and the 94:5–6 ease promise are the same vocabulary. The surah's moral psychology is one line: what you give toward, or hoard from, is the direction you are eased into.

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Triple oath (92:1–3):** night-covering, day-revealing, the Creator of male and female — time's two faces and the primal pair. The day-reveal/night-cover pair verbatim-shadow the adjacent surah `[91:3–4]` "And the day as it reveals it. / And the night as it conceals it" (rg-verified) — 92 re-swears 91's cosmos in reverse order.
* **Thesis verse (92:4):** "Your endeavors are indeed diverse" — the sworn conclusion, one line, the hinge of the surah.
* **Mirror-triad A (92:5–7):** gives / is righteous / confirms the Good → "We will ease his way towards ease."
* **Mirror-triad B (92:8–10):** stingy / complacent / denies the Good → "We will ease his way towards difficulty." — the two triads are strict antitheses, verse-for-verse (gives↔stingy, righteous↔complacent, confirms↔denies; ease↔difficulty). (*Source: structural observation on corpus renderings.*)
* **The money test (92:11):** "And his money will not avail him when he plummets" — hoarding's asset frozen at the one deadline; pairs with 63:10's deathbed petition (k-infraq's anchor).
* **Sovereignty bracket (92:12–13):** "It is upon Us to guide. / And to Us belong the Last and the First." — the divine claim; `[53:25]` carries the identical line "To Allah belong the Last and the First" (rg-verified); `[57:3]` "He is the First and the Last" inverts the order.
* **Fire and its tenant (92:14–16):** warning of "a Fierce Blaze"; "None will burn in it except the very wicked. / He who denies and turns away" — the same deny-and-turn formula returns in `[96:13]` "how he disbelieved and turned away" (rg-verified).
* **The devout and his audit (92:17–21):** the devout avoids it — giving money to become pure (92:18; cf. 87:14 "Successful is he who purifies himself"), "Seeking no favor in return" (cf. 74:6), "Only seeking the acceptance of his Lord, the Most High" (87:1 twin), closing on "And he will be satisfied" — the same closing word as `[93:5]` (rg-verified).

## 5b. Batch-Return Verification Flags (this index)
* **Hint renderings corrected:** "your efforts are indeed diverse" → "Your **endeavors** are indeed diverse" (92:4); "eased toward ease/hardship" → "We will **ease his way towards** ease / **towards difficulty**" (92:7, 92:10); "wealth won't save at death" → "And his **money** will not avail him **when he plummets**" (92:11); "ours is the guidance" → "**It is upon Us** to guide" (92:12); "the blazing fire" → "a **Fierce Blaze**" (92:14); "the pious one" → "the **devout**" (92:17); "gives his wealth to purify himself" → "gives his **money** to become pure" (92:18); "seeking only his Lord's **Face**" → "Only seeking the **acceptance** of his Lord, the Most High" (92:20 — the idiom this corpus renders "for the sake of Allah," `[2:272]`/`[76:9]`, and capital-F "Faces" elsewhere are literal — not here); "soon We will please him" → "And he will be satisfied" (92:21 — no "soon," no divine first-person in this verse).
* **"Giver-warner-confirmer" rejected as verse-triad:** the corpus triad is "gives **and is righteous**" + "confirms goodness" (92:5–6); a *warner* appears only as the divine voice at 92:14 "I have warned you" — not as a human type in the triad.
* **"Male-female oaths" located:** 92:3 "And He who created the male and the female" — exact-form twins `[75:39]`, `[53:45]` (rg-verified).
* **92:8 "complacent" and 96:7 "considers himself exempt"** plausibly share one Arabic root (*istaghna*) — NOT verifiable from the English corpus alone; Source: agent inference — verify.
