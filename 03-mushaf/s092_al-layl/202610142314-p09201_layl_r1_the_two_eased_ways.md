# The Two Eased Ways: diverse endeavors sorted night and day (92:1–21)
**UID:** 202610142314
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 92:1–21 (Ruku 1 of 1 in al-Layl)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[92:1]` – `[92:21]` (21 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Sworn on the covering night, the revealing day, and the Creator of the male-female pair, the surah states its fact — "Your endeavors are indeed diverse" (92:4) — then folds all diversity into two tracks: the giving, God-fearing, Good-confirming man is eased *towards ease*, the stingy, self-sufficing, Good-denying man eased *towards difficulty*; money avails nothing at the plummet, guidance and both abodes are Allah's, and the surah ends on the purifier who seeks nothing but his Lord's acceptance — "And he will be satisfied."
* **Placement:** Ruku 1 of 1; the sorting-sermon heart of the 91–97 oath run. 91 swore by the soul and its elevation/failure; 92 swears by cosmic time and gender and sorts *endeavors*; 93 answers the same "satisfied" word with consolation. The mirror-triads here are the template the later Meccan short surahs keep re-loading.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[92:1]`–`[92:3]`** "By the night as it covers. / And the day as it reveals. / And He who created the male and the female."
  * **Gloss:** A triple oath climbing from cosmic time to biological duality: night wrapping (`[92:1]`, *yaghshā*) and day uncovering (`[92:2]`, *yajlī*) — the same verbs as `[91:3–4]` "the day as it reveals it… the night as it conceals it" in mirrored order (rg-verified); then the Creator *of* the pair (`[92:3]`) — male/female as exact twins at `[75:39]`, `[53:45]` (rg-verified). The sworn conclusion arrives in one verse.
* **`[92:4]`** "Your endeavors are indeed diverse."
  * **Gloss:** The thesis: human striving (*saʿy*) is sworn to be "diverse" — Itani's "endeavors" has exactly one English twin in the corpus, `[20:15]` "each soul will be paid for what it endeavors" (rg-verified). Diversity is not praised — it is indicted as a fact requiring sorting.
* **`[92:5]`–`[92:7]`** "As for him who gives and is righteous. / And confirms goodness. / We will ease his way towards ease."
  * **Gloss:** Track A in three beats — outward generosity (gives, `[92:5]`), inward God-consciousness (is righteous/*wattaqā*), and cognitive assent at `[92:6]` ("confirms goodness" — the *ṣaddaqa bi-l-ḥusnā* clause). The reward is directional, not transactional: *We will ease him toward ease* (`[92:7]`) — the easing-verb answers the endeavor; cf. `[87:8]` "We will ease you into the Easy Way" (rg-verified).
* **`[92:8]`–`[92:10]`** "But as for him who is stingy and complacent. / And denies goodness. / We will ease his way towards difficulty."
  * **Gloss:** Track B mirrors beat-for-beat: hoards (stingy), self-suffices ("complacent" — the same posture as `[96:7]` "when he considers himself exempt", same probable root — agent inference — verify), and denies the same *ḥusnā* (`[92:9]`). The verb of 92:7 repeats: he too is "eased" — toward difficulty — divine confirmation of a direction chosen, the surah's darkest mechanic. (*Source: textual observation; classical reading calls this istidrāj "leading-on-by-degrees" — verify before quoting the term.*)
* **`[92:11]`** "And his money will not avail him when he plummets."
  * **Gloss:** Track B's asset neutralized at the fall — "plummets" is corpus-unique English (rg-verified). Death as the deadline pairs with 63:10's deathbed "give from what We have provided for you" (k-infraq's anchor; rg-verified).
* **`[92:12]`–`[92:13]`** "It is upon Us to guide. / And to Us belong the Last and the First."
  * **Gloss:** The sovereignty bracket: guidance is Allah's *obligation* ("upon Us") — the k-guidance note's strongest divine-commitment form, uncited there (rg-verified); and both abodes — "the Last and the First" — an exact twin of `[53:25]`, with `[57:3]` giving the inverse order "the First and the Last" (rg-verified).
* **`[92:14]`–`[92:16]`** "I have warned you of a Fierce Blaze. / None will burn in it except the very wicked. / He who denies and turns away."
  * **Gloss:** The divine voice enters with the warner-verb (*anḏarnā* — the revelation-function word, cf. `[26:194]` "that you may be one of the warners", rg-verified). "Fierce Blaze" (*sa'īr*, rg-cluster: `[25:11]`, `[26:91]`, `[67:5/10/11]`, `[40:7]`, `[4:10]`, `[37:68]`). The tenant is one trait in two acts: "denies and turns away" — the exact formula mirrored in `[96:13]` "he disbelieved and turned away" (rg-verified); the "very wicked" (`[92:15]`, *shaqī*) echoes `[91:12]` "its most wicked" and `[87:11]` "the wretched will avoid it" (rg-verified).
* **`[92:17]`–`[92:19]`** "But the devout will avoid it. / He who gives his money to become pure. / Seeking no favor in return."
  * **Gloss:** The counter-tenant, defined again by giving — but now the giving is *for purification*: "to become pure" (`[92:18]`, *yuzakkī nafsahu* — exact verbal twin `[87:14]` "Successful is he who purifies himself" and `[80:7]` "if he does not purify himself", rg-verified; institutional form at `[9:103]` "to purify them and sanctify them"). "Seeking no favor in return" — no reciprocal gift-economics; twin posture `[74:6]` "And show no favor seeking gain" (rg-verified).
* **`[92:20]`–`[92:21]`** "Only seeking the acceptance of his Lord, the Most High. / And he will be satisfied."
  * **Gloss:** The audit clause: one motive only — "the acceptance of his Lord" (Itani's word is *acceptance*, not "Face"; when intent-direction is idiomatically "Face," this corpus renders "for the sake of Allah" — `[2:272]`, `[76:9]`; capital-F "Faces" elsewhere are literal faces on that Day — `[75:22]`, `[80:38]`, `[88:8]`, rg-verified), "the Most High" (`[87:1]` "Praise the Name of your Lord, the Most High" — rg-verified). The close: "And he will be satisfied" — the *raḍwān* word that `[93:5]` will say to the Prophet himself, and `[20:130]` sets as the goal of glorification (rg-verified three-site cluster).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`diverse (endeavors)`**: the surah's thesis-word.
  * **Rendering:** "Your endeavors are indeed diverse" — plural, moral, not neutral; "endeavors" (= *saʿy*) has its one twin at `[20:15]` (rg-verified).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[71:20]` "that you may travel its diverse roads" (roads); `[59:14]` "their hearts are diverse" (hearts) — rg-verified: *diverse* marks roads, hearts, and here works.
* **`ease / difficulty (yusr–ʿusr)`**: the easing-verb frames both tracks (92:7, 92:10).
  * **Rendering:** "We will ease his way towards ease / towards difficulty."
  * **Elsewhere (rg-verified):** `[87:8]` "We will ease you into the Easy Way"; `[94:5–6]` "With hardship comes ease" ×2; `[2:185]` "Allah desires ease for you, and does not desire hardship for you"; `[18:16]` "will set your affair towards ease."
* **`Fierce Blaze (saʿīr)`**: the sworn Fire.
  * **Rendering:** "a Fierce Blaze" (92:14) — Itani's fixed rendering of *sa'īr*.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[25:11]`, `[26:91]`, `[67:5/10/11]`, `[40:7]`, `[4:10]`, `[37:68]` (rg-verified) — 92:14–16 adds its tenant-list.
* **`the Last and the First`**: both abodes as Allah's property.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[53:25]` identical order; `[57:3]` "the First and the Last" (rg-verified) — fits k-tawhid's "to whom belongs…" interrogation method.
* **`satisfied (raḍiya)`**: the surah's last word.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[93:5]`, `[20:130]` (rg-verified) — satisfaction as divine gift, not human achievement.
