# The scriptured believers and guidance's divine prerogative (28:51–60)
**UID:** 202610141777
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 28:51–60 (Ruku 6 of 8 in al-Qasas)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[28:51]` – `[28:60]` (10 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The delivered Word meets its sample reactions: the prior Scripture-people believe on hearing, take a double reward, and answer vanity with peace (52–55), while Mecca's council pleads fear of displacement (57) though it sits in a provisioned sanctuary — and at the center stands the spine verse: guidance is God's prerogative, not the preacher's affection (56), bracketed by the due-process rule of destruction (59) and the weighing of currencies (60).
* **Placement:** Ruku 6 of 8; opens the surah's weighing-movement; 28:56 is the verse the guidance knowledge-note carries in its own evidence list.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[28:51]`** "We have delivered the Word to them, that they may remember."
  * **Gloss:** Delivery complete, response pending: "We have delivered the Word to them, that they may remember" — the definite Word as Quran (cf. `[23:68]` "Have they not pondered the Word?", `[39:18]` "Those who listen to the Word, and follow the best of it").
* **`[28:52]`** "Those to whom We gave the Scripture before it believe in it."
  * **Gloss:** The prior recipients as counter-example: "Those to whom We gave the Scripture before it believe in it" — the believing remnant of `[3:199]` ("Among the People of the Scripture are those who believe..."), with `[46:12]`'s "before it was the Book of Moses, a model and a mercy" behind them.
* **`[28:53]`** "When it is recited to them, they say, “We have believed in it; it is the truth from our Lord; we were Muslims prior to it.”"
  * **Gloss:** Their confession: "it is the truth from our Lord; we were Muslims prior to it" — submission predates the label's arrival; `[22:78]`: "It is he who named you Muslims before, and in this."
* **`[28:54]`** "These will be given their reward twice, because they persevered; and they counter evil with good; and from Our provisions to them, they give."
  * **Gloss:** The conduct-audit in three motions — reward twice (phrase unique here), perseverance, and two action-clauses: "they counter evil with good" and "from Our provisions to them, they give," both verbatim-twins of `[13:22]` ("repel evil with good"; "spend from Our provisions to them"; cf. `[41:34]`).
* **`[28:55]`** "And when they hear vain talk, they avoid it, and say, “We have our deeds, and you have your deeds; peace be upon you; we do not desire the ignorant.”"
  * **Gloss:** Their politics of distraction: vain talk is declined, not debated — "peace be upon you; we do not desire the ignorant" — the Merciful-servants' etiquette of `[25:63]` (they say, "Peace") and the prophetic command of `[7:199]` ("avoid the ignorant").
* **`[28:56]`** "You cannot guide whom you love, but Allah guides whom He wills, and He knows best those who are guided."
  * **Gloss:** The spine verse, verbatim: "You cannot guide whom you love, but Allah guides whom He wills, and He knows best those who are guided" — the prerogative clause "Allah guides whom He wills" at `[2:272]` (in a charity-context), `[14:4]`, `[16:93]`, `[74:31]`; the line the guidance note cites.
* **`[28:57]`** "And they say, “If we follow the guidance with you, we will be snatched from our land.” Did We not establish for them a Safe Sanctuary, to which are brought all kinds of fruits, as provision from Ourselves? But most of them do not know."
  * **Gloss:** The Meccan excuse quoted — "If we follow the guidance with you, we will be snatched from our land" — answered by the sanctuary already given: "a Safe Sanctuary, to which are brought all kinds of fruits" (cf. `[5:97]` "a sanctuary for the people"; Abraham's prayer of `[2:126]` for a peaceful land fed with fruits; `[95:3]` "this safe land"); the closer "But most of them do not know" returns from 28:13.
* **`[28:58]`** "And how many a city did We destroy for turning unappreciative of its livelihood? Here are their homes, uninhabited after them, except for a few. And We became the Inheritors."
  * **Gloss:** The precedent of ruined towns: destroyed "for turning unappreciative of its livelihood" — the `[16:112]` parable verbatim in stem (secure town, abundant livelihood, unappreciative, the robe of hunger and fear); the emptied homes are an archaeological sermon, and "And We became the Inheritors" mirrors `[15:23]` and inverts 28:5: heirs of promise, heirs of rubble.
* **`[28:59]`** "Your Lord never destroys cities without first sending a messenger in their midst, reciting to them Our revelations. And We never destroy the cities, unless their people are wrongdoers."
  * **Gloss:** Due-process theology: "Your Lord never destroys cities without first sending a messenger in their midst, reciting to them Our revelations... unless their people are wrongdoers" — the law-twin `[17:15]` "nor do We ever punish until We have sent a messenger"; 28:47's excuse-scenario is the stated reason.
* **`[28:60]`** "Whatever thing you are given is but the material of this world, and its glitter. But what is with Allah is better, and longer lasting. Do you not comprehend?"
  * **Gloss:** The currency comparison: "the material of this world, and its glitter" (cf. `[11:15]` "the worldly life and its glitter"; `[57:20]` "play, and distraction, and glitter") versus "what is with Allah is better, and longer lasting" (`[16:96]`'s "what is with Allah remains"); "Do you not comprehend?" — the faculties addressed since 28:51 are being asked to calculate.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`the Word`** `[28:51]`:
  * **Rendering:** "We have delivered the Word to them, that they may remember."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[23:68]` "Have they not pondered the Word?"; `[39:18]` listening and following the best; `[27:82]` "when the Word has fallen on them" — one definite Word, three tested responses (ponder, follow, fall).
* **`Allah guides whom He wills`** `[28:56]`:
  * **Rendering:** "You cannot guide whom you love, but Allah guides whom He wills, and He knows best those who are guided."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:272]` verbatim twin-clause (the task's 2:272 pairing confirmed in corpus: "Their guidance is not your responsibility, but Allah guides whom He wills"); `[14:4]`, `[16:93]`, `[74:31]` bind the same clause to astray-and-guide pairs — the formula's home is prerogative (Source: observable corpus distribution; agent gloss — verify).
* **`unappreciative`** `[28:58]`:
  * **Rendering:** "for turning unappreciative of its livelihood."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[16:112]` the town-parable; `[22:66]` "The human being is unappreciative"; `[31:12]` and `[76:3]` set the word as shukr's exact opposite — the gratitude note's kufr-as-cover stem, here in city-scale grammar.
* **`glitter`** `[28:60]`:
  * **Rendering:** "the material of this world, and its glitter."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[11:15]`, `[57:20]`, `[18:28]` "the glitter of this world" — one stable English rendering across four surahs, always yoked to desire-or-deception warnings.
