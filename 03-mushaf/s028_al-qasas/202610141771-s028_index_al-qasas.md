## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Meccan per the classical surah-lists. *Source: classical lists — verify in print (al-Itqan-type surah introductions); standard lists record no Medinan minority for this surah.*
* **Order:** 49 per the classical enumeration of revelation order (same convention as the s016/s013/s022 indices). *Source: classical lists — verify.*
* **Size:** 88 ayahs, 8 ruku (`data/ruku_map.json`: 13+8+7+14+8+10+15+13 = 88 ✓). Mushaf position 28, between al-Naml (27) and al-Ankabut (29).
* **Name:** al-Qasas — "The Histories/The Stories"; the corpus title is HISTORY, and the word earns it at `[28:3]` "We narrate to you from the history of Moses and Pharaoh—in truth—for people who believe" (the "We narrate" qasas-formula also at `[7:101]`, `[11:120]`, `[12:3]`, `[20:99]`).
* **Central theme:** The Moses-surah of the Quran: one continuous biography (1–42) stages the surah's governing contrast — *exaltation* (tughyan) versus *the promise*. Pharaoh "exalted himself in the land" (28:4) and ends a leader calling to the Fire (28:41); the oppressed are made "leaders... inheritors" (28:5–6); Qarun repeats Pharaoh's error inside Moses' own community (28:76) and is swallowed. Between these, guidance is declared God's prerogative alone (28:56), the Home of the Hereafter is reserved for those who want no superiority (28:83), the Messenger is promised a return (28:85), and everything closes on "His is the judgment, and to Him you will be returned" (28:88).
* **Theme checks the task asked to verify honestly (against the corpus):** `[28:4]` slaughter of sons ✓ verbatim "slaughtering their sons, while sparing their daughters." Family restoration 28:7–13 ✓ — but the sister's line is "Shall I tell you about a family that can raise him for you, and will look after him?" (28:12); a "nurse" wording belongs to other translations (twin `[20:40]` "Shall I tell you about someone who will take care of him?"). Fire episode 28:29–35 ✓ — corpus wording "I have glimpsed a fire" (also `[27:7]`), not "I perceived a fire." The clause "My Hearing and Seeing is with you" does **not** occur in s028 — it is `[20:46]` "I am with you, I hear and I see"; the nearest s028 warrant is 28:31 "you are perfectly safe" + 28:35 — recorded as a correction. Tower ✓ but at `[28:38]` (inside ruku 4, not a 36–43 block): "fire-up the bricks... build me a tower, that I may ascend to the God of Moses, though I think he is a liar" (twin `[40:36–37]`). Qarun: "belonged to the clan of Moses, but he oppressed them" (28:76) — "among Pharaoh's people" is another translation's gloss, not this edition's; "I was given all this on account of knowledge I possess" ✓ verbatim (28:78); "Do not exult; Allah does not love the exultant" ✓ (28:76, "love" not "like"). 28:56 ✓ verbatim, spine verse of the guidance note (which already cites it). 28:83 ✓ — wording "superiority," not "exaltation." 28:85 ✓ "He Who ordained the Quran for you will return you Home" — the Hijrah promise in Itani's rendering. Closing: 28:87–88 ✓ "do not invoke with Allah any other god... His is the judgment, and to Him you will be returned" — but the night-and-day creation argument is 28:71–73 (ruku 7), not the closing verses; recorded as a correction.

## 3. Structural Arc
* **The promised child (1–13):** the surah's first word after the letters is a promise-grammar — the mother told "do not fear, nor grieve; We will return him to you" (28:7); the slaughterer's own household raises the child (28:8–9) and his sister engineers his return to her breast (28:11–13). "Allah's promise is true. But most of them do not know" (28:13) installs the key the closing ruku turns.
* **The formation of Moses (14–28):** wisdom and knowledge (28:14), a justified blow and a forgiven sin (28:15–17), flight "fearful and vigilant" (28:18–21), and in Median the prayer of the desperate shade — "I am in dire need of whatever good you might send down" (28:24) — answered by marriage and an eight-year contract (28:25–28).
* **The commission and the collision (29–42):** the fire, the two proofs, Aaron attached to the mission (28:29–35); Pharaoh answers a clear Book with bricks and a tower (28:38) and the sea takes him; the drowned become "leaders calling to the Fire" (28:36–42).
* **The reciter addressed (43–50):** the camera turns on Muhammad ﷺ — he was not at the Mount, not in Median, not among the witnesses (28:44–46); this un-eyewitnessed Scripture is itself the miracle; the demand "give us the like of what Moses was given" is answered by the challenge of 28:49 and the diagnosis of 28:50.
* **The weighing (51–75):** the prior Scripture-people believe on hearing (51–55); guidance is God's prerogative, not the preacher's affection (56); the Sanctuary and the ruined ungrateful towns (57–59); then the scales: the glitter vs what is "better, and longer lasting" (60–67), the choosing Lord (68–70), the night-and-day mercy argument ending "that you may give thanks" (71–73), and the silenced associates (74–75).
* **The Qarun seal (76–88):** wealth claimed on self-knowledge (78) collapses under the bounty-distribution of 82; the Home is assigned to the non-exalted (83); virtue gets better-than-it (84); the return-Home promise (85) forbids supporter-hood (86–87); nothing remains but the tawhid of 88.
