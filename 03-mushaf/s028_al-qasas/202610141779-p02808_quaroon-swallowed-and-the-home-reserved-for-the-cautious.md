# Quaroon swallowed, and the Home reserved for the cautious (28:76–88)
**UID:** 202610141779
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 28:76–88 (Ruku 8 of 8 in al-Qasas)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[28:76]` – `[28:88]` (13 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The closing case-study: Quaroon — from Moses' own people, oppressing them — converts blessing into boast ("I was given all this on account of knowledge I possess") and the earth audits him; yesterday's covetous spectators wake to the theology of spread-and-restricted bounty; the Home is then assigned to those who want no superiority and no corruption, virtue is rewarded upward, the reciter is promised a return Home — and the unexpected Scripture forbids supporter-hood, sealing on "no god but He... His is the judgment, and to Him you will be returned."
* **Placement:** Ruku 8 of 8; the final ruku completes the surah's exaltation-anti-theme (28:4's Pharaoh finds his internal twin in Qarun) and closes the return-ring (28:39 denied → 28:70 affirmed → 28:88 final word).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[28:76]`** "Quaroon belonged to the clan of Moses, but he oppressed them. We had given him treasures, the keys of which would weigh down a group of strong men. His people said to him, “Do not exult; Allah does not love the exultant."
  * **Gloss:** Corruption inside the liberation-community: "Quaroon belonged to the clan of Moses, but he oppressed them" — verbatim this corpus (the common gloss "among Pharaoh's people" is not this edition's wording); the treasures whose "keys... would weigh down a group of strong men" (image unique here); the kin's counsel "Do not exult; Allah does not love the exultant" — the anti-exultation grammar of `[57:23]` ("nor exult over what He has given you").
* **`[28:77]`** "But seek, with what Allah has given you, the Home of the Hereafter, and do not neglect your share of this world. And be charitable, as Allah has been charitable to you. And do not seek corruption in the land. Allah does not like the seekers of corruption.”"
  * **Gloss:** The positive program in one breath: seek the Home with what is given; "do not neglect your share of this world" (unique clause — the anti-monastic note); "be charitable, as Allah has been charitable to you" (the ihsan-reciprocity of 28:14's "Thus do We reward the virtuous"); "do not seek corruption in the land" (`[7:56]` "do not corrupt on earth after its reformation"; 28:4's "corrupter" is the villain-word).
* **`[28:78]`** "He said, “I was given all this on account of knowledge I possess.” Did he not know that Allah destroyed many generations before him, who were stronger than he, and possessed greater riches? But the guilty will not be asked about their sins."
  * **Gloss:** Task-verified line, located at 28:78: "I was given all this on account of knowledge I possess" — the wealth-ingratitude utterance, the inverse of the gratitude note's 14:7 increase-decree; counter-audit: generations "stronger than he, and possessed greater riches" destroyed (`[40:21]`, `[6:6]`); "the guilty will not be asked about their sins" — `[55:39]` ("no human and no jinn will be asked about his sins") verbatim-shaped.
* **`[28:79]`** "And he went out before his people in his splendor. Those who desired the worldly life said, “If only we possessed the likes of what Quaroon was given. He is indeed very fortunate.”"
  * **Gloss:** The parade of splendor and the crowd's audit-failure: "If only we possessed the likes of what Quaroon was given. He is indeed very fortunate" — the worldly-desire clause verbatim-twins `[11:15]` "Whoever desires the worldly life and its glitter" (cf. `[10:7]`); the fortune-word is the one `[41:35]` reserves for the persevering.
* **`[28:80]`** "But those who were given knowledge said, “Woe to you! The reward of Allah is better for those who believe and do righteous deeds.” Yet none attains it except the steadfast."
  * **Gloss:** The knowledge-party's counter-audit: "Woe to you! The reward of Allah is better for those who believe and do righteous deeds" — the better-currency of 28:60–61 restated; "Yet none attains it except the steadfast" — near-verbatim twin `[41:35]` "none will attain it except those who persevere" (one sentence, two sabr-renderings across surahs).
* **`[28:81]`** "So We caused the earth to cave in on him and his mansion. He had no company to save him from Allah, and he could not defend himself."
  * **Gloss:** The audit executed: "We caused the earth to cave in on him and his mansion" — the cave-in verdict-family: `[16:45]` (the schemers threatened), `[34:9]`, `[17:68]`, and `[29:40]`, which lists the punishments ("Some We caused the ground to cave in beneath them... And some We drowned"); "no company to save him... could not defend himself" — 28:63's intercession-denial operationalized.
* **`[28:82]`** "Those who had wished they were in his position the day before were saying, “Indeed, it is Allah who spreads the bounty to whomever He wills of His servants, and restricts it. Had Allah not been gracious to us, He would have caved in on us. No wonder the ungrateful never prosper.”"
  * **Gloss:** Yesterday's wishers become today's theologians: "it is Allah who spreads the bounty to whomever He wills of His servants, and restricts it" — `[34:36]` "spreads out His bounty... or restricts it" near-verbatim, the expand/restrict formula of `[29:62]`/`[17:30]`/`[30:37]`; survival read as grace, not merit; "the ungrateful never prosper" — the prosper-law of `[10:17]` ("The guilty will never prosper") with `[7:92]`/`[11:68]`'s never-prospered idiom behind it.
* **`[28:83]`** "That Home of the Hereafter—We assign it for those who seek no superiority on earth, nor corruption. And the outcome is for the cautious."
  * **Gloss:** Task-verified: 28:83 — "That Home of the Hereafter—We assign it for those who seek no superiority on earth, nor corruption. And the outcome is for the cautious" (the task's "exaltation" is rendered "superiority" here): the two banned motives are the surah's two villains — Pharaoh's self-exaltation (28:4; cf. `[23:24]` "gain superiority over you") and Qarun's corruption (28:77); `[7:128]` "the future belongs to the righteous" is the twin outcome-formula.
* **`[28:84]`** "Whoever brings a virtue will receive better than it. But whoever brings evil—the evildoers will be rewarded only according to what they used to do."
  * **Gloss:** Reward-mechanics, asymmetric by design: "Whoever brings a virtue will receive better than it" (upward over-weight; cf. `[6:160]` "ten times its like"); "But whoever brings evil—the evildoers will be rewarded only according to what they used to do" — exact equivalence only on the bad ledger (6:160's "they will not be wronged").
* **`[28:85]`** "He Who ordained the Quran for you will return you Home. Say, “My Lord knows best who comes with guidance, and who is in manifest error.”"
  * **Gloss:** Task-verified: 28:85 "He Who ordained the Quran for you will return you Home" — the task's "He who made you return to the place of return" is other translations' rendering of the same verb; Itani's Home-word echoes 28:77's "the Home of the Hereafter"; the epistemic humility survives the promise: "My Lord knows best who comes with guidance, and who is in manifest error" (`[39:22]`'s manifest-error phrase).
* **`[28:86]`** "You did not expect the Scripture to be transmitted to you, except as mercy from your Lord. Therefore, do not be a supporter of the disbelievers."
  * **Gloss:** The Scripture as unexpected gift: "You did not expect the Scripture to be transmitted to you, except as mercy from your Lord" — ruku 5's unwitnessedness (28:44–46) now grounds ethics, not triumph (cf. `[42:52]` "You did not know what the Scripture is"); "Therefore, do not be a supporter of the disbelievers" — verbatim internal echo of 28:17's forgiven-man's vow (criminals → disbelievers).
* **`[28:87]`** "And do not let them divert you from Allah’s revelations after they have been revealed to you. And pray to your Lord, and never be of the polytheists."
  * **Gloss:** The diversion-warning: "do not let them divert you from Allah's revelations after they have been revealed to you" — the divert-family of `[6:116]`, `[6:153]`, `[43:62]`; the positive command is a rare collocation: "And pray to your Lord" — as in `[108:2]` "So pray to your Lord and sacrifice" — "and never be of the polytheists."
* **`[28:88]`** "And do not invoke with Allah any other god. There is no god but He. All things perish, except His presence. His is the judgment, and to Him you will be returned."
  * **Gloss:** The seal: "do not invoke with Allah any other god. There is no god but He. All things perish, except His presence" — the twin of `[55:26]`–`[55:27]` ("Everyone upon it is perishing. But will remain the Presence of your Lord"); "His is the judgment" verbatim-twins `[6:62]` ("Unquestionably, His is the judgment"); "and to Him you will be returned" — 28:70's ring-closer now the surah's final word, answering 28:39's denial.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`exult`** `[28:76]`:
  * **Rendering:** "Do not exult; Allah does not love the exultant."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[57:23]` "nor exult over what He has given you. Allah does not love the proud snob" — same grammar, same gift-context; `[31:18]` "Allah does not love the arrogant showoffs" completes the love-denial cluster.
* **`spreads the bounty / restricts`** `[28:82]`:
  * **Rendering:** "it is Allah who spreads the bounty to whomever He wills of His servants, and restricts it."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[34:36]` near-verbatim "spreads out His bounty... or restricts it"; `[29:62]`, `[17:30]`, `[30:37]` the expand/restrict provision-pairs — the distribution-theology the spectators learn by earthquake.
* **`superiority`** `[28:83]`:
  * **Rendering:** "for those who seek no superiority on earth, nor corruption."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[23:24]` "who wants to gain superiority over you" — the same English noun in another villain-council; the word-family link to 28:4's "exalted himself" is an English-corpus inference only (Source: agent inference — verify).
* **`His presence`** `[28:88]`:
  * **Rendering:** "All things perish, except His presence."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[55:26]`–`[55:27]` "perishing / will remain the Presence" — the twin pair; `[18:28]` "desiring His Presence" shows the capitalized rendering of the same word.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Meccan (Source: classical lists — verify); Qarun is classically read as an Israelite of Moses' own clan ("belonged to the clan of Moses" — the verbatim corpus phrase at 28:76); the earth-caving is alluded to without names at `[29:40]` (Source: structural observation).
* **Asbab / narrative context:** 28:85's "return you Home" is classically read of the Prophet's longing for Mecca after the Hijrah began — a return-promise parallel to 28:7's "We will return him to you" (Moses returned to his mother; the reciter will be returned to his home); other classical readings: death, or the Day's return (Source: classical reports — verify).
