## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** **Meccan** — uncontested in the classical lists; the task's "order 46? verify" **VERIFIES ✓**: in the Ibn 'Abbas-based transmitted chronology (al-Zanjani/Tanzil table — the same lineage whose Cairo-chain this brain logged through al-Ahqaf 66) the run is 45 Taa-Haa → **46 Al-Waaqia** → 47 Ash-Shu'araa, and tradition ties its revelation to before Umar's conversion (he is reported to have embraced on hearing it — *Source: transmitted reports — verify in print*). One noted exception within that table: `[56:81]`–`[56:82]` ("Is it this discourse that you take so lightly? And you make it your livelihood to deny it?") are classed **Medinan** by those lists — they fall inside ruku 3. *Source: classical chronology tables — verify in print; not derivable from the corpus.*
* **Size:** 96 ayahs, 3 ruku (`data/ruku_map.json`: 56:1–38, 39–74, 75–96 = 38+36+22 ✓; corpus file ends `[56:96]` ✓ — and the final line is missing its closing period, quote as-is per the artifacts fleeting note). Mushaf position 56, directly after ar-Rahman (55) — the canonical adjacency: the litany of favors, then the Event that meets the deniers.
* **Name:** al-Waqi'ah — the surah self-names from its first word: `[56:1]` "When the inevitable occurs." (header "THE INEVITABLE"). The task's "Event" is other translations' English (also its Wikidata aliases); Itani keeps "the inevitable" at all four corpus sites of the word: `[20:129]`, `[25:77]`, `[53:57]`, here — always eschatological.
* **Central theme:** A three-class census of humanity at the Day: the forerunners (the nearest), the people of the Right (a throng, described longest), the people of the Left (deferred until their menu arrives). Between description and verdict the surah runs its proof-engine: four "Have you seen… is it you, or are We…?" audits (emission, cultivation, rain, fire-starter) strip every productive act from human hands — this edition's *tawhid* scene — and a closing oath by "the locations of the stars" seals the Book's self-claim ("a noble Quran, in a well-protected Book… a revelation from the Lord of the Worlds"), its deepest *kalam* text. The death-bed rerun of the class-map (83–94) converts eschatology into immediate biography: "We are nearer to it than you are, but you do not see." Twin doxologies bracket the proof: mid-surah "your Great Lord" (74) and the close "your Lord, the Magnificent" (96 — verbatim formula shared with `[69:52]` "So glorify the name of your Lord, the Magnificent.", but for the Name casing and 96's missing terminal period — [sic]).

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Quake and census (1–10):** the Event, unlied; earth shaken, mountains to "scattered dust"; "you become three classes" — and the three are named with their suspense-formulas ("what of those on the Right?").
* **The forerunners (11–26):** the nearest, Gardens of Bliss, a throng of ancients + a small band of latecomers; furnishings, immortalized youth, chosen fruit and meat, lovely companions as treasured pearls — paid for by deeds, greeted only "Peace, peace."
* **The Right (27–40):** orchards, shade, outpouring water, unbarred fruit, mattresses — and a frank line about their spouses, "We have created them of special creation" — then the same throng-plus-throng census.
* **The Left (41–56):** searing wind, boiling water, smoke-shadow — the lifestyle charge (luxury, "immense wrongdoing," resurrection-sneering), the gathering answer ("the first and the last… a familiar Day"), the Tree of Bitterness banquet and thirsty-camel drink.
* **The audits (57–73):** "We created you—if only you would believe!" then emission/growth/water/fire — each closed "or are We the Creator/Grower/Sender/Producer?", with the death-decree and the first-formation memory (60–62) as the hinge — sealed by the mid-surah tasbih (74).
* **The Book and the throat (75–96):** star-oath for a noble Quran in a well-protected Book, touched only by the purified; the rebuke (81–82, the Medinan-exception couple); the soul reaching the throat and the nearness-audit; the class-map rerun as three death-bed verdicts; "This is the certain truth" and the final glorification (96).
