# The Inevitable sorts humanity: the forerunners and the throng of the Right (56:1–38)
**UID:** 202610142144
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 56:1–38 (Ruku 1 of 3 in al-Waqi'ah)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[56:1]` – `[56:38]` (38 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The Event occurs, the earth is pulverized, and humanity becomes "three classes" — and this ruku pays out two of the three names in full: the forerunners ("the nearest," with a *small band* of latecomers) and the people of the Right (two throngs), while the Left keeps only its suspense-tag, its menu deferred to ruku 2.
* **Placement:** Opening census of the surah's arc (index §3 "Quake and census → forerunners → Right"); the class-map set here is rerun as death-bed verdicts at `[56:88]`–`[56:94]`.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[56:1]`** "When the inevitable occurs." — **Gloss:** the surah self-names from its first word; `[56:2]` "Of its occurrence, there is no denial." — it cannot be belied or denied; `[56:3]` "Bringing low, raising high." — its first act is rank-inversion, the vector the whole census follows.
* **`[56:4]`** "When the earth is shaken with a shock." **`[56:5]`** "And the mountains are crushed and crumbled." **`[56:6]`** "And they become scattered dust." — **Gloss:** quake → crush → dust (the task's "despised fluid (56:6?)" is wrong; 56:6 = scattered dust, cf. `[25:23]` "will turn them into scattered dust").
* **`[56:7]`** "And you become three classes." **`[56:8]`** "Those on the Right—what of those on the Right?" **`[56:9]`** "And those on the Left—what of those on the Left?" **`[56:10]`** "And the forerunners, the forerunners." **`[56:11]`** "Those are the nearest." — **Gloss:** the class-list, with two suspense-tags ("what of…?" = verdict held open, paid at 27+/41+) and the forerunners doubled for emphasis, glossed at once as "the nearest" (q-r-b root; rerun as "those brought near," 88).
* **`[56:12]`** "In the Gardens of Bliss." **`[56:13]`** "A throng from the ancients." **`[56:14]`** "And a small band from the latecomers." — **Gloss:** address and arithmetic: many ancients, a *few* latecomers — the only scarcity line in the surah; its twin census (39–40) makes the Right two throngs.
* **`[56:15]`** "On luxurious furnishings." **`[56:16]`** "Reclining on them, facing one another." — **Gloss:** brocade-lined thrones, face-to-face (task's "on thrones facing each other" = 15–16); cf. `[55:54]` "Reclining on furnishings lined with brocade."
* **`[56:17]`** "Serving them will be immortalized youth." **`[56:18]`** "With cups, pitchers, and sparkling drinks." **`[56:19]`** "Causing them neither headache, nor intoxication." — **Gloss:** cup-bearers who never age, and wine without the hangover — pleasure minus its invoice; cf. `[76:19]` "eternalized youths… you would think them sprinkled pearls," `[52:24]` "youths like hidden pearls."
* **`[56:20]`** "And fruits of their choice." **`[56:21]`** "And meat of birds that they may desire." — **Gloss:** two menus, each framed by human wanting ("choice," "may desire").
* **`[56:22]`** "And lovely companions." **`[56:23]`** "The likenesses of treasured pearls." — **Gloss:** spouses likened to pearls *treasured* (hidden from touch), against 55's "rubies and corals" (`[55:58]`, companions at `[55:56]`).
* **`[56:24]`** "As a reward for what they used to do." — **Gloss:** the ledger clause: this scenery is paid — deed-and-reward, not audition; the same formula marks the Left's due at `[56:56]`, and cf. `[32:19]` "hospitality for what they used to do."
* **`[56:25]`** "Therein they will hear no nonsense, and no accusations." **`[56:26]`** "But only the greeting: “Peace, peace.”" — **Gloss:** an acoustic census: only the doubled peace-word (unique in the corpus as a reduplicated greeting; the book's inner curly quotes kept as-is).
* **`[56:27]`** "And those on the Right—what of those on the Right?" — **Gloss:** the suspense-tag returns verbatim (from 8), now actually cashing out; the Right's section runs to the ruku's end.
* **`[56:28]`** "In lush orchards." **`[56:29]`** "And sweet-smelling plants." **`[56:30]`** "And extended shade." **`[56:31]`** "And outpouring water." — **Gloss:** four landscape gifts — garden, fragrance, shade, flowing water — each mirrored by the Left's anti-version at 42–44.
* **`[56:32]`** "And abundant fruit." **`[56:33]`** "Neither withheld, nor forbidden." **`[56:34]`** "And uplifted mattresses." — **Gloss:** fruit without closure or prohibition (the "withheld/forbidden" pair reappears inverted at `[56:44]` "Neither cool, nor refreshing"); raised bedding.
* **`[56:35]`** "We have created them of special creation." **`[56:36]`** "And made them virgins." **`[56:37]`** "Tender and un-aging." — **Gloss:** the frank line about their spouses — the first loud "We"-speech in the passage ("special creation" is corpus-unique), rehearsing the divine-verb register the audits will weaponize (57–73).
* **`[56:38]`** "For those on the Right." — **Gloss:** all of it "for those on the Right" — the section's closing tag; the throng census follows immediately (39–40, ruku 2).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`al-Wāqi'ah` — "the inevitable"**: the Event, eschatological by construction.
  * **Rendering:** Itani keeps "the inevitable" at all four corpus sites of the word: here `[56:1]`, `[20:129]`, `[25:77]`, `[53:57]` — no drift (rg-verified; "waqi'ah" as header "THE INEVITABLE").
  * **Elsewhere:** `[53:57]` "The inevitable is imminent." — the same word as near-horizon warning; `[25:77]` "the inevitable will happen" — tied to denial, exactly this surah's 2.
* **`ṣaff` — the throng formula ("A throng from the ancients / …from the latecomers")**: the census idiom for the two generations.
  * **Rendering:** "throng" (*ṣaff*) twice for the ancients (13, 39), then "a small band" (14) vs "a throng" (40) for the latecomers — the one quantitative asymmetry in the surah.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[39:71]`/`[39:73]` — "in throngs" as processions into Fire and Paradise; the same numbering motif outside s056. Within: 13–14 ↔ 39–40 are the twin rows (index table).
* **`sabiqāt` — "the forerunners"**: the first class, reduplicated.
  * **Rendering:** "And the forerunners, the forerunners" (10) — doubling as dread-and-honor device, mirroring the "what of those…?" tags.
  * **Elsewhere:** corpus-unique ("forerunners" — rg: one hit, here); glossed inside the surah by "the nearest" (11) and rerun as "those brought near" (88) — q-r-b ties the ruku to the death-bed map.
* **`ḥūr / pearls` — "lovely companions… treasured pearls"**: the spouse-imagery cluster.
  * **Rendering:** "lovely companions" (22) + "The likenesses of treasured pearls" (23); the pearl-figure drifts across editions ("hidden pearls" `[52:24]`; "rubies and corals" `[55:58]`).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[55:56]` "maidens restraining their glances, untouched before…" — the 56:23 "treasured" (kept out of reach) answers 55's "untouched."
