# The Left, the bitter tree, and the four-fold creation audit — first glorification (56:39–74)
**UID:** 202610142145
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 56:39–74 (Ruku 2 of 3 in al-Waqi'ah)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[56:39]` – `[56:74]` (36 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The Left's deferred menu finally arrives — searing wind, boiling water, the Tree of Bitterness — and then the surah switches from description to proof: four "Have you seen…? Is it you, or are We…?" audits (emission, cultivation, rain, fire) strip every productive act from human hands, sealed by the mid-surah glorification "your Great Lord" (74).
* **Placement:** The ruku closes the census (Left), then pivots the whole surah from *describing the Day* to *arguing for the Creator* — this edition's tawhid scene (index §4); the hinge is the death-decree (60–62) between audits one and two.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[56:39]`** "A throng from the ancients." **`[56:40]`** "And a throng from the latecomers." — **Gloss:** the Right's census completes the twin row (13–14↔39–40): here both sides are throngs.
* **`[56:41]`** "And those on the Left—what of those on the Left?" — **Gloss:** the suspense-tag held since 9 is cashed out now — the ruku 1 promise kept.
* **`[56:42]`** "Amid searing wind and boiling water." **`[56:43]`** "And a shadow of thick smoke." **`[56:44]`** "Neither cool, nor refreshing." — **Gloss:** an anti-landscape, item-for-item against the Right's orchards/shade/water (28–31); even its "shadow" fails — "Neither cool, nor refreshing" inverts `[56:33]` "Neither withheld, nor forbidden." (the "neither…nor" construction).
* **`[56:45]`** "They had lived before that in luxury." **`[56:46]`** "And they used to persist in immense wrongdoing." — **Gloss:** the lifestyle charge: soft living and unrepentant sin ("immense wrongdoing" — *ʿaẓīm*, corpus-family of `[89:20]` "immense love" of wealth).
* **`[56:47]`** And they used to say, “When we are dead and turned into dust and bones, are we to be resurrected? **`[56:48]`** And our ancient ancestors too?” — **Gloss:** the resurrection-sneer, dust-and-bones (their own future, 6's "scattered dust" thrown back at them); the book's quote opens at 47 unclosed and runs to the curly close at 48's end — quoted as-is (artifacts-note regime).
* **`[56:49]`** Say, “The first and the last. **`[56:50]`** Will be gathered for the appointment of a familiar Day.” — **Gloss:** the gathering answer: first and last, one appointed Day (quote opens at 49, closes at 50 — as-is); cf. `[39:71]`/`[39:73]` driven/led in throngs to that Day.
* **`[56:51]`** "Then you, you misguided, who deny the truth." — **Gloss:** direct address; the surah's only *ḍalāl* hit ("misguided" — guidance's absence, index §4).
* **`[56:52]`** "Will be eating from the Tree of Bitterness." **`[56:53]`** "Will be filling your bellies with it." **`[56:54]`** "Will be drinking on top of it boiling water." **`[56:55]`** "Drinking like thirsty camels drink." — **Gloss:** the banquet: zaqqūm (Tree of Bitterness, `[37:62]`, `[44:43]`), gorging, then water-on-top-of-it, gulping like camels parched (the similitude is corpus-unique; cf. the camels pointed at creation `[88:17]`).
* **`[56:56]`** "That is their hospitality on the Day of Retribution." — **Gloss:** "hospitality" ironically tags the menu — the very word `[37:62]` turns into a contest ("Is this a better hospitality, or the Tree of Bitterness?"); "Day of Retribution" is corpus-unique as a name here.
* **`[56:57]`** "We created you—if only you would believe!" — **Gloss:** PIVOT: from census to proof; the "We"-register of `[56:35]` ("We have created them") now addressed to the whole room ("—if only you would believe!" — optative, repeated at 62).
* **`[56:58]`** "Have you seen what you ejaculate?" **`[56:59]`** "Is it you who create it, or are We the Creator?" — **Gloss:** AUDIT 1 (emission): the most private productive act, claimed from human hands.
* **`[56:60]`** "We have decreed death among you, and We will not be outstripped." **`[56:61]`** "In replacing you with your likes, and transforming you into what you do not know." **`[56:62]`** "You have known the first formation; if only you would remember." — **Gloss:** the hinge: death is decreed, unescapable; replacement/transformation follows; the "first formation" (creation-at-first, cf. `[21:104]` "as We began the first creation, We will repeat it", `[50:15]`) is the memory that makes the audits bite (cf. `[39:42]` souls taken at death).
* **`[56:63]`** "Have you seen what you cultivate?" **`[56:64]`** "Is it you who make it grow, or are We the Grower?" — **Gloss:** AUDIT 2 (cultivation): you sow, but growing is not yours.
* **`[56:65]`** "If We will, We can turn it into rubble; then you will lament." **`[56:66]`** “We are penalized. **`[56:67]`** No, we are being deprived.” — **Gloss:** the crop-loss scene, their lament voiced directly (quote opens at 66, closes at 67 — as-is; "penalized… deprived" — blame shifted to fate, then confession of deprivation).
* **`[56:68]`** "Have you seen the water you drink?" **`[56:69]`** "Is it you who sent it down from the clouds, or are We the Sender?" — **Gloss:** AUDIT 3 (rain): weather-ownership — the pre-Islamic Arabs' own claim to rain by the stars (the tawhid note's "weather-ownership" root, cf. `[2:21–22]`).
* **`[56:70]`** "If We will, We can make it salty. Will you not be thankful?" — **Gloss:** the water-audit's punchline: the gift could have been brackish — *shukr* named explicitly (the gratitude audit's test-case).
* **`[56:71]`** "Have you seen the fire you kindle?" **`[56:72]`** "Is it you who produce its tree, or are We the Producer?" — **Gloss:** AUDIT 4 (fire): you strike it, but the fire-tree's origin is claimed from you.
* **`[56:73]`** "We have made it a reminder, and a comfort for the users." — **Gloss:** fire doubled: a sign (reminder of the Fire? and of its Maker) plus worldly utility ("comfort for the users" — *matāʿ*, provision-with-briefness, cf. `[16:80]`).
* **`[56:74]`** "So glorify the Name of your Great Lord." — **Gloss:** FIRST TASBIH: the only response to the audit-result is glorification — "your Great Lord" (corpus-unique address; "your" keeps the Lord set against *their* claims).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **The audit formula — "Have you seen…? Is it you who…, or are We the ___?"**: the four-fold courtroom question.
  * **Rendering:** enumerate each — `[56:58–59]` emission → "or are We the Creator?"; `[56:63–64]` cultivation → "or are We the Grower?"; `[56:68–69]` rain → "or are We the Sender?"; `[56:71–72]` fire → "or are We the Producer?" Agent-nouns capitalized, one each in the corpus.
  * **Elsewhere:** "Have you seen" occurs 8× in the corpus — half of them right here; the paired "is it you… or are We the…" construction is **s056-only** (rg); the method (ask who owns X; they answer "Allah") parallels the courtroom rungs of `[23:84–89]`.
* **`zaqqūm` — "the Tree of Bitterness"**: the Left's food.
  * **Rendering:** "Tree of Bitterness" consistently (Itani's choice for zaqqūm at all sites).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[37:62]` "Is this a better hospitality, or the Tree of Bitterness?" — the word *hospitality* staged as a contest, exactly what `[56:56]` then asserts; `[44:43]` "The Tree of Bitterness." (the Doom surah's menu).
* **`shukr` — "Will you not be thankful?" (`[56:70]`)**: the audit's intended verdict.
  * **Rendering:** "thankful" for the shākir root, as usual in this edition.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[27:73]` "most of them are not thankful"; `[6:63]` "We will surely be among the thankful" (the deliverance-vow); immediately downstream of ar-Rahman's refrain litany ("which of your Lord's marvels will you deny?", `[55:13]` passim) — same favor-auditing posture, here answered with tasbih (74).
* **`sabbiḥ — "So glorify the Name of your Great Lord" (`[56:74]`)**: the liturgical command-form.
  * **Rendering:** "Great Lord" — corpus-unique pairing; the same verb opens `[87:1]` "Praise the Name of your Lord, the Most High."
  * **Elsewhere:** the surah's second tasbih, `[56:96]` "your Lord, the Magnificent", is the same command with a different attribute-name; the 74/96 bracket is the structural doxology of the whole proof (see p05603 §4).
