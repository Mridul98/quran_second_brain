# The fulfilled vision and the community portrait (48:27–29)
**UID:** 202610142110
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 48:27–29 (Ruku 4 of 4 in al-Fath)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[48:27]` – `[48:29]` (3 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The shortest ruku of the surah pays out its promises: the Messenger’s vision of entering the Sacred Mosque in security — shaven heads, unshorn, unafraid — is fulfilled in truth, with an imminent victory thrown in as a dividend (27); the mission statement rides the corpus's tripled formula (28); and the book closes on the companions' charter — Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah, those with him stern-then-merciful, prostration-marked, described in Torah and Gospel like a plant steadying its own stem — forgiveness and a great reward promised (29).
* **Placement:** The surah's cash-register; the plant parable converts the pledge crowd of ruku 1/3 into a growing organism. A truce surah ends not with a border but with a botany and a biography.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[48:27]`** "Allah has fulfilled His Messenger’s vision in truth: “You will enter the Sacred Mosque, Allah willing, in security, heads shaven, or hair cut short, not fearing. He knew what you did not know, and has granted besides that an imminent victory.”"
  * **Gloss:** Task-verified scene: the ru'ya fulfilled — the fulfillment verb echoes Abraham's "You have fulfilled the vision" (`[37:105]`); entry markers (security, shaving/trimming, no fear) answer 48:25's barring and the offering-law of `[2:196]` (shaving tied to hady arrival); "Allah willing" — the community taught its own adab of hope; "He knew what you did not know" — the divine-knowledge refrain of 48:18 returns; "imminent victory" (fath qarib) closes the bracket begun at 48:1.
* **`[48:28]`** "It is He who sent His Messenger with the guidance and the religion of truth, to make it prevail over all religions. Allah suffices as Witness."
  * **Gloss:** The prevail-verse — exact formula-twins `[9:33]`, `[61:9]` (the only three "prevail over all religions" sites, rg-verified), with this copy shortened and sealed by "Allah suffices as Witness" instead of "even though the idolaters dislike it" — the witness-clause picks up 48:8's office-triple.
* **`[48:29]`** "Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah. Those with him are stern against the disbelievers, yet compassionate amongst themselves. You see them kneeling, prostrating, seeking blessings from Allah and approval. Their marks are on their faces from the effects of prostration. Such is their description in the Torah, and their description in the Gospel: like a plant that sprouts, becomes strong, grows thick, and rests on its stem, impressing the farmers. Through them He enrages the disbelievers. Allah has promised those among them who believe and do good deeds forgiveness and a great reward."
  * **Gloss:** Task claim ✓ with renderings corrected to this edition: "stern against the disbelievers, yet compassionate amongst themselves" (not "merciful among themselves"); "forgiveness and a great reward" (not "magnificent"). The name-Muhammad formula — fourth and final of the corpus's four in-text mentions (`[3:144]`, `[33:40]`, `[47:2]`, `[48:29]`), and this surah's bookend to `[47:2]` "sent down to Muhammad." "Severe/merciful" inversion-twin: `[5:54]` "kind towards the believers, stern with the disbelievers." Description-in-Torah-and-Gospel ↔ `[7:157]` "whom they find mentioned in the Torah and the Gospel" and `[9:111]`'s triple-scripture promise. The plant parable — seedling, shoot, thickening, stem-resting, farmer-impressing — is the agricultural victory-image; the generic rise-and-wither farm parable twin is `[57:20]` ("like a rainfall that produces plants") — same field, opposite moral: one crop collapses, this one steadies. "Kneeling, prostrating" — ruku-and-sujud as identity; faces marked by prostration: the honest visible sign, against 47:30's surmisable tone and 55:41's seized marks — bodies as testimony. "Through them He enrages the disbelievers" — the truce's sting without a sword drawn. The closing promise: forgiveness + great reward — the same pair promised to the pledge-fulfillers at `[48:10]` ("a great reward") and to the believers at `[48:5]`; the surah ends on its opening contract. *Adjudication of the task's "companions' charter":* the text's referent is "those with him" — the pledge generation; classical readings extend the description to all succeeding believers via the plant-growth image. *Source: classical tafsir extension — verify in print.*

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **ru'ya (vision):** 48:27 "Allah has fulfilled His Messenger’s vision in truth."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[37:105]` "You have fulfilled the vision" (Abraham) — the only other fulfillment-of-a-dream scene in this shape; 12's dream-cycle uses "dream" renderings — *Source: agent note — verify before citing Yusuf parallels.*
* **fath (victory):** 48:27 "an imminent victory" — the title-word's last strike; 48:1 opened the bracket.
  * **Rendering:** "imminent victory" (48:27) vs "imminent conquest" (48:18) for the same fathan-qariban phrase-pattern, rg-verified — Itani varies the noun by context.
* **shid'da—rahma (stern—compassionate):** "stern against the disbelievers, yet compassionate amongst themselves."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[5:54]` — "kind towards the believers, stern with the disbelievers": the same double-register, mirrored order; also 48:26's rage (enemy) vs serenity (believers) — the surah's emotional polarization in one adjective.
* **athar (marks on faces):** "Their marks are on their faces from the effects of prostration."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[55:41]` guilty "recognized by their marks"; `[47:30]` hypocrites recognized by tone — the corpus keeps three registers of legibility: marks (55:41), tone (47:30), and this prostration-sheen (48:29): the only mark here is produced by worship, not sin.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Medinan, Hudaybiyya horizon; the "vision" is classically the dream preceding the march — the text narrates its fulfillment without narrating the dream. *Source: classical asbab — verify in print.*
* **Asbab:** the Umrat al-qada' (the following year's executed umrah) is transmitted as the fulfillment event; heads shaven/trimmed mirrors the 2:196 ihram law made for exactly this prevented-pilgrim situation. *Source: classical reports (Ibn Kathir on 48:27) — verify.*
