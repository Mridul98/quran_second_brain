# Conspicuous victory, tranquility, and the hand above hands (48:1–10)
**UID:** 202610142107
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 48:1–10 (Ruku 1 of 4 in al-Fath)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[48:1]` – `[48:10]` (10 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** God opens by handing out the victory — a truce read as conquest — whose cash value is forgiveness, completed favor, guidance, and support (1–3); the engine of the whole passage is *sakina*, tranquility injected into believers' hearts so faith stacks on faith (4), while the hypocrites and idolaters get the reverse ledger (5–6); the ruku ends where heaven touches procedure: pledging allegiance to the Prophet is pledging Allah, "the hand of Allah is over their hands" (10).
* **Placement:** The surah's opening grant; every keyword it plants (fath, sakina, bay'a, straight path) bears fruit in ruku 3–4.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[48:1]`** "We have granted you a conspicuous victory."
  * **Gloss:** Task claim ✓ (Itani: "conspicuous," not "manifest" — other editions' word). The royal plural opens; the event-name Hudaybiyya is classical ascription, not text: the corpus never says "Hudaybiyya" (rg-verified, 0 hits). *Source: classical asbab — verify in print.*
* **`[48:2]`** "That Allah may forgive you your sin, past and to come, and complete His favors upon you, and guide you in a straight path."
  * **Gloss:** The victory's purpose-clause: forgiveness "past and to come" (corpus-unique phrase — rg); the favor-completion twin of `[5:3]`; straight-path guidance enters the surah's ledger here.
* **`[48:3]`** "And help you with an unwavering support."
  * **Gloss:** The help clause — "unwavering" support, a help that cannot be outflanked (corpus is English-only; no Arabic claim made here).
* **`[48:4]`** "It is He who sent down tranquility into the hearts of the believers, to add faith to their faith. To Allah belong the forces of the heavens and the earth. Allah is Knowing and Wise."
  * **Gloss:** SAKINA occurrence 1 of 3 in this surah (task's locate-all: 48:4, 18, 26 — verified complete; rg "tranquility|serenity" in s048). Downward motion ("sent down") and inward destination (hearts); faith compounding on faith — the momentum formula also at `[9:124]` ("it increases them in faith"), with the opposite result for the sick-hearted (cf. 47:16).
* **`[48:5]`** "He will admit the believers, male and female, into Gardens beneath which rivers flow, to abide therein forever, and He will remit their sins. That, with Allah, is a great triumph."
  * **Gloss:** The gender-symmetric promise; "a great triumph" (fawz 'azim) — victory language transferred from battlefield to hereafter.
* **`[48:6]`** "And He will punish the hypocrites, male and female, and the idolaters, male and female, those who harbor evil thoughts about Allah. They are surrounded by evil; and Allah is angry with them, and has cursed them, and has prepared for them Hell—a miserable destination."
  * **Gloss:** The mirror-punishment, "male and female" again — the exact roster of `[9:67]`–`[9:68]`; "harbor evil thoughts about Allah" (classical idiom zan al-saw' — Source: classical lexicography — verify in print) ↔ `[3:154]` "thoughts of ignorance."
* **`[48:7]`** "To Allah belong the troops of the heavens and the earth. Allah is Mighty and Wise."
  * **Gloss:** The ruku's internal refrain against 48:4 ("forces"/"troops" — junud) — whoever owns the troops can afford tranquility for the few.
* **`[48:8]`** "We sent you as a witness, and a bearer of good news, and a warner."
  * **Gloss:** The office-triple, near-verbatim of `[33:45]` — the Trench-surah's own commissioning line; a hint the two Medinan crisis-surahs are in dialogue.
* **`[48:9]`** "That you may believe in Allah and His Messenger, and support Him, and honor Him, and praise Him morning and evening."
  * **Gloss:** The people's counterpart-duty: belief, support, honor, and praise morning-and-evening (the formula also at `[33:42]`, `[24:36]`, `[76:25]`); "support Him, and honor Him" pairs with `[7:157]` "respect him, and support him."
* **`[48:10]`** "Those who pledge allegiance to you are pledging allegiance to Allah. The hand of Allah is over their hands. Whoever breaks his pledge breaks it to his own loss. And whoever fulfills his covenant with Allah, He will grant him a great reward."
  * **Gloss:** The bay'a keystone: the hand over hands — "hand of Allah" is corpus-unique here (rg; contrast the polemic at `[5:64]` "Allah’s hand is tied"). Breach self-harms; fulfillment is covenant-keeping (dh-covenant note already cites this verse — backlink exists ✓).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **fath (victory):** 48:1 "conspicuous victory" — the surah's title-word; the same "imminent" phrase returns as "conquest" (48:18) and "victory" (48:27) — Itani varies the noun, rg-verified. ("Gains" at 15/19/20 is a different spoils-word, not this root.)
  * **Elsewhere:** the "great triumph/fawz" of 48:5 keeps the winning-frame spiritual; the word-form "victory" itself: 48:1, 48:27 in this surah.
* **sakina (tranquility):** 48:4 here; the word-family occurs corpus-wide at `[2:248]` (ark), `[9:26]`, `[9:40]` (Hunayn, cave), `[30:21]` (mates).
  * **Rendering drift inside one surah:** "tranquility" at 48:4 vs "serenity" at 48:18, 48:26 — same descent, two English words (observable translator choice).
* **bay'a (pledge):** 48:10 "pledge allegiance... pledging allegiance to Allah."
  * **Elsewhere:** Israel's pledges (`[2:63]`, `[3:187]`, `[4:154]`, `[5:12]`–`[5:14]` broken-pledge dossier); the oath-counterfeit grammar at `[9:75]`–`[9:77]` (k-nifaq); fulfillment-frame `[33:23]` "true to what they pledged."
* **zan al-saw' (evil thoughts):** 48:6 "harbor evil thoughts about Allah."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[3:154]` "thinking of Allah thoughts that were untrue—thoughts of ignorance"; `[48:12]` repeats the verb against the laggers — the thought-crime runs through the whole surah.
