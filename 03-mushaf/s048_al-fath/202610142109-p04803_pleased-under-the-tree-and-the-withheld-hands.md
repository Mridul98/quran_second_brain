# Pleased under the tree: serenity, gains, and withheld hands (48:18–26)
**UID:** 202610142109
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 48:18–26 (Ruku 3 of 4 in al-Fath)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[48:18]` – `[48:26]` (9 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah's emotional peak: Allah was *pleased* with the pledgers under the tree, knew what was in their hearts, answered it with serenity and an imminent conquest (18); the ruku then unpacks what "victory" actually consisted of — gains expedited, people’s hands restrained (20), the enemy's flight pre-written (22), Meccan violence withheld in the valley (24), and the hidden believers inside the enemy camp protected from unwitting harm (25) — ending where it began: jahiliyya-rage in the enemy's hearts, Allah's serenity and the words of righteousness imposed on the believers (26).
* **Placement:** The middle ruku answers ruku 1's sakina-grant (4) with its documentary proof, and ruku 2's "gains" grab (15) with God's distribution of them (19–20).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[48:18]`** "Allah was pleased with the believers, when they pledged allegiance to you under the tree. He knew what was in their hearts, and sent down serenity upon them, and rewarded them with an imminent conquest."
  * **Gloss:** SAKINA occurrence 2 of 3 (rendered "serenity" here — task's locate claim ✓ complete set is 48:4, 18, 26). "The tree" is corpus-unique (rg "under the tree"); the ridwan pleasure formula pairs divine knowledge-of-hearts (cf. 47:30's exposure) with downward calm. "Imminent conquest" (fathan qariban) — the first fath-adjacent payoff.
* **`[48:19]`** "And abundant gains for them to capture. Allah is Mighty and Wise."
  * **Gloss:** Plunder named as reward without apology — the gains ruku 2's laggers tried to join late (15) are here distributed to those who stayed pledged.
* **`[48:20]`** "Allah has promised you abundant gains, which you will capture. He has expedited this for you, and has restrained people’s hands from you; that it may be a sign to the believers, and that He may guide you on a straight path."
  * **Gloss:** The restraint motif, first strike: hands held back (cf. `[5:11]` "He restrained their hands from you"); straight-path guidance restated from 48:2 — victory is framed as *guidance infrastructure*, "a sign to the believers."
* **`[48:21]`** "And other things, of which you were incapable, but Allah has encompassed them. Allah is Capable of everything."
  * **Gloss:** The overfill clause: beyond the expedited gains lie outcomes they couldn't reach — God has "encompassed" them; capacity belongs to the Capable (qadir).
* **`[48:22]`** "If those who disbelieve had fought you, they would have turned back and fled, then found neither protector nor helper."
  * **Gloss:** The counterfactual rout: their one war would have ended in flight + "neither protector nor helper" — this exact pair is corpus-unique here (rg); "no helper" clusters abound (`[40:21]`, `[3:192]`, `[17:69]`, and 47:13 within the sibling surah).
* **`[48:23]`** "It is Allah’s pattern, ongoing since the past. You will never find any change in Allah’s pattern."
  * **Gloss:** The sunna verse — corpus-rare word "pattern" for sunna: the only other site is `[33:38]` "Such is the pattern of Allah among those who passed before" (rg-verified); "no change" again against 48:15's changers — the divine pattern is the un-changeable Word at the scale of history.
* **`[48:24]`** "It is He who withheld their hands from you, and your hands from them, in the valley of Mecca, after giving you advantage over them. Allah is Observer of what you do."
  * **Gloss:** The geography: "valley of Mecca" — the name Mecca occurs once in the corpus, here (rg-verified); "after giving you advantage" — the truce's military premise; mutual restraint as mercy, "Observer" sealing the clause.
* **`[48:25]`** "It is they who disbelieved, and barred you from the Sacred Mosque, and prevented the offering from reaching its destination. Were it not for faithful men and faithful women, whom you did not know, you were about to hurt them, and became guilty of an unintentional crime. Thus Allah admits into His mercy whomever He wills. Had they dispersed, We would have punished those who disbelieved among them with a painful penalty."
  * **Gloss:** The grievance inventory: barry from the Sacred Mosque + prevented hady — near-verbatim of `[5:2]` "people who barred you from the Sacred Mosque"; the offering-shaving law sits at `[2:196]`. The unknown "faithful men and faithful women" inside Mecca — providential ignorance protecting secret believers; "unintentional crime" (khat' bighair 'ilm) — the non-liable error clause; mercy-admission again by divine will (cf. 48:14).
* **`[48:26]`** "Those who disbelieved filled their hearts with rage—the rage of the days of ignorance. But Allah sent His serenity down upon His Messenger, and upon the believers, and imposed on them the words of righteousness— of which they were most worthy and deserving. Allah is aware of everything."
  * **Gloss:** SAKINA occurrence 3 of 3 — near-verbatim twin of `[9:26]` "Then Allah sent down His serenity upon His Messenger, and upon the believers" (Hunayn verse — rg-verified). "Jahiliyya-rage" (hamiyyat al-jahiliyya) — the only "days of ignorance" phrase in this surah; its thought-twin is `[3:154]` "thoughts of ignorance." "The words of righteousness" (kalimat al-taqwa — "words of Godwaring/consciousness"): imposed — a root-anchored kalima note for k-kalam; worthy-and-deserving: the believers as its natural owners.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **sakina (serenity here):** 48:18, 48:26 (and "tranquility" at 48:4).
  * **Rendering:** the drift "tranquility"→"serenity" is observable; the same word-group: `[9:26]`, `[9:40]` (cave), `[2:248]` (ark), `[30:21]` (marriage bed).
* **kaff (restrain/withhold):** 48:20 "restrained people’s hands," 48:24 "withheld their hands... and your hands."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[5:11]` "He restrained their hands from you" — the same mercy-grammar for a different assassination plot; victory-as-restraint is this surah's signature reversal.
* **sunnat Allah (pattern):** 48:23 (twice in one line).
  * **Rendering drift, rg-verified:** the word is "pattern" only at 33:38 and 48:23 in this edition; the same constancy-doctrine is rendered "precedent/system" at `[33:62]` ("You will find no change in Allah’s system") and "practice" at `[35:43]` ("You will not find any change in Allah’s practice... any substitute") — 48:23 is that dossier's victory-campaign instance.
* **hamiyya (rage):** 48:26 "the rage of the days of ignorance."
  * **Elsewhere:** corpus-unique "days of ignorance" phrase (rg); `[3:154]` supplies the twin *thought*crime ("thoughts of ignorance") — rage and presumption as the jahili-heart's two temperatures.
