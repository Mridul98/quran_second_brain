## 3. Structural Arc
* **The grant package (1–7):** victory → forgiveness "past and to come" → completed favor → straight path → unwavering support (1–3); tranquility descending into the hearts of the believers (4); gardens vs. the hypocrite-and-idolater pair, "male and female" both times (5–6); forces/troops of heavens and earth (4, 7).
* **The office and the oath (8–10):** witness/bearer-of-good-news/warner (8); believe-support-honor-praise morning and evening (9); the pledge that is pledging Allah — "the hand of Allah is over their hands" (10), the surah's covenant keystone.
* **The audit of the lagging (11–17):** tongue-without-heart excuses (11), the evil-thought confession (12), kingdom-and-will (13–14), the attempt "to change the Word of Allah" for the gains (15), the future call-up against a people of great might (16), the disability exemptions (17).
* **The tree, the spoils, the restraint (18–26):** divine pleasure under the tree + serenity + imminent conquest (18–19); expedited gains and restrained hands (20–21); the unchanging pattern (22–23); the valley of Mecca, the barred mosque, the prevented offering, the unknown believers in the enemy's town (24–25); jahiliyya-rage vs. the words of righteousness (26).
* **The dream and the portrait (27–29):** the vision fulfilled, heads shaven in security, plus an imminent victory (27); the sent-with-guidance verse, tripled in the corpus (28); Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah — and those with him, described in Torah and Gospel, marked by prostration, promised forgiveness and a great reward (29).
