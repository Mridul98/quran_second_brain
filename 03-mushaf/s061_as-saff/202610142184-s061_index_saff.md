## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Medinan per the majority of classical reports; the type is one of the *disputed* classifications in the lists (a minority opinion counts it Meccan) — there is no "mixed" surah report strong enough to split verses between the two in the standard lists. *Source: classical type-lists (consensus of most reports; dispute recorded in 'ulum al-Quran tabulations) — verify against a printed source.* Revelation order **109** in the enumeration this brain's indices use (same list: al-Mumtahina 91, al-Munafiqun 104, al-Jumu'ah 110) — *Source: classical lists — verify.* The corpus carries no nuzul statement; the unnumbered Basmala heads the surah.
* **Size:** 14 ayahs, 2 ruku. Named for the battle-rank of 61:4 ("in ranks... as though they were a compact structure").
* **Title etymology:** as-Saff = "the row/rank" — the same root appears in the name of as-Saffat (37, "the aligners," `[37:1]`), but this surah's rank is human and martial, not angelic; *Source: observable corpus renderings.*
* **Reading hint:** the surah runs on a hate/love register: "most hateful... that you say what you do not do" (61:3) against "Allah loves those who fight in His cause, in ranks" (61:4) — one adjective pair framing the whole indictment; the trade offer of ruku 2 then prices love in transactions ("That is best for you," 61:11).
* **Central theme:** Alignment between speech and reality. The cosmos already performs what believers are accused of faking: everything praises Him (61:1), yet the community says what it does not do (61:2–3) — the worst hatred in the surah. The counter-images stack up: the ranked fighters (61:4), the rejected messengers Moses and Jesus with their prediction (61:5–6), the un-quenchable Light and the religion sent to prevail (61:8–9). Ruku 2 turns the indictment into an offer sheet: a trade that saves (61:10–12), near victory (61:13), and the disciples' answer "We are Allah's supporters" (61:14).

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Cosmic baseline (61:1):** the tasbih formula opens the surah — creation is already in "rank"; the human problem is catch-up, not initiation. Cluster: `[57:1]`, `[59:1]`, `[61:1]`, `[62:1]`, `[64:1]` — five openings (plus the in-surah echo `[59:24]`), all verified in corpus; Itani splits renderings between "praises" (61/64) and "glorifies" (62), with 57/59 fronting the participle ("Glorifying Allah is...").
* **Indictment (61:2–4):** "most hateful" (kinara) attaches to speech without deed; the beloved alternative is the fighter-rank compared to a compact structure.
* **Salvation-history precedent (61:5–7):** Moses hurt by those who "knew"; Jesus announcing "a messenger... whose name is Ahmad" and called sorcery; the "does not guide" formula twice (61:5 sinful people, 61:7 wrongdoing people).
* **The Light and the commission (61:8–9):** the extinguishing attempt fails; the Messenger is sent with guidance and the true religion to prevail (formula-twins at 9:33, 48:28).
* **Offer and answer (61:10–14):** the trade (belief + striving with wealth and life), the double payoff (forgiveness, Gardens — and "something else you love": support and imminent victory), closed by the ansar precedent from the disciples.
* **Verdict chain:** "Allah does not guide the sinful people" occurs exactly twice in the corpus — 61:5 and 63:6 (verified couplet); its "wrongdoing people" sibling runs 61:7 → 62:5. The two short Medinan surahs of this wave (61, 63) are stitched by this formula. *Source: rg-verifiable corpus fact.*
