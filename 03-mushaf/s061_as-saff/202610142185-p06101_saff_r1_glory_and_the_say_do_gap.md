# Glory first, then the indictment: the say–do gap and the un-quenchable Light (61:1–9)
**UID:** 202610142185
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 61:1–9 (Ruku 1 of 2 in as-Saff)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[61:1]` – `[61:9]` (9 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** All creation is already in formation, praising Allah (61:1) — so the believers' sin of saying what they do not do is the one off-rhythm note in the cosmos (61:2–3); the remedy is rank (61:4), the precedent is Moses and Jesus, both rejected by people who "knew" (61:5–6), and the verdict is cosmically fixed: the Light cannot be mouthed out (61:8), the commissioned religion will prevail (61:9).
* **Placement:** Opening ruku; sets the surah's courtroom staging (cosmic testimony → human indictment → salvation-history precedent → commission), which ruku 2 answers with the trade offer (61:10–14).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[61:1]`** "Everything in the heavens and the earth praises Allah. He is the Almighty, the Wise."
  * **Gloss:** The tasbih formula, opened front-on: the whole cosmos already performs the alignment 61:2–3 will demand of humans.
* **`[61:2]`** "O you who believe! Why do you say what you do not do?"
  * **Gloss:** A question, not a command — the believers are asked to explain their own gap.
* **`[61:3]`** "It is most hateful to Allah that you say what you do not do."
  * **Gloss:** The superlative ("most hateful") matches the cosmic praise of 61:1: one sentence up, everything glorifies; one sentence down, empty speech is hated. *Source: structural observation on adjacent verses.*
* **`[61:4]`** "Allah loves those who fight in His cause, in ranks, as though they were a compact structure."
  * **Gloss:** The beloved counter-image to hated speech: bodies aligned (saff) and fused like masonry — the surah's name-verse; Itani renders *bunyan marsus* as "a compact structure" (no "wall" word appears in this corpus line).
* **`[61:5]`** "When Moses said to his people, “O my people, why do you hurt me, although you know that I am Allah’s Messenger to you?” And when they swerved, Allah swerved their hearts. Allah does not guide the sinful people."
  * **Gloss:** The first precedent: knowledge without loyalty — they "knew" and still hurt him; the recompense image is a swerving mirrored by God ("Allah swerved their hearts"), capped by the negative guidance formula.
* **`[61:6]`** "And when Jesus son of Mary said, “O Children of Israel, I am Allah’s Messenger to you, confirming what preceded me of the Torah, and announcing good news of a messenger who will come after me, whose name is Ahmad.” But when he showed them the miracles, they said, “This is obvious sorcery.”"
  * **Gloss:** The second precedent: Jesus re-staged as predictor — Torah-confirmer, bearer of good news about "a messenger... whose name is Ahmad" (the sole corpus occurrence of the name), and received with "obvious sorcery."
* **`[61:7]`** "And who is a greater wrongdoer than he who attributes falsehoods to Allah, when he is being invited to Islam? Allah does not guide the wrongdoing people."
  * **Gloss:** The charge generalized (attributing falsehood while being called to submission); second "does not guide" verdict, now of the "wrongdoing people."
* **`[61:8]`** "They want to extinguish Allah’s Light with their mouths; but Allah will complete His Light, even though the disbelievers dislike it."
  * **Gloss:** The mouth (which lied in 61:2–3, and spat "sorcery" in 61:6) returns as the organ of attempted extinguishing — and fails; the Light is Allah's to complete.
* **`[61:9]`** "It is He who sent His Messenger with the guidance and the true religion, to make it prevail over all religions, even though the idolaters dislike it."
  * **Gloss:** The commission clause: guidance + true religion + prevalence; closes ruku 1 where the surah began — a cosmos whose Maker has a sent plan.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`sabbaha` (praise/glorify):** `[61:1]` "praises Allah".
  * **Rendering:** the cluster splits renderings: "praises" (61:1, 64:1) vs "glorifies" (62:1) vs fronted participle "Glorifying Allah is..." (57:1, 59:1).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[57:1]`, `[59:1]`, `[62:1]`, `[64:1]` — the five-surah tasbih-openings, plus the in-surah echo `[59:24]` "Whatever is in the heavens and the earth glorifies Him" — all lines verified in corpus; the openings bracket the Medinan cluster around this surah.
* **the say–do formula:** "say what you do not do" `[61:2]`–`[61:3]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[3:167]` "They say with their mouths what is not in their hearts", `[48:11]` "They say with their tongues what is not in their hearts" — the mouth–heart split the nifaq note calls the mechanism (that note cites 3:167/48:11 but not 61:2–3 — recorded in the batch return).
* **`saff` / `bunyan marsus` (rank / compact structure):** `[61:4]` — name of the surah; ranks of fighters "as though they were a compact structure."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[37:1]` "By the aligners aligning" (as-Saffat, the same s-f-f root in Its "ranks" set) — observable root-link across the corpus.
* **`Ahmad` / `nur Allah`:** `[61:6]` "whose name is Ahmad" (hapax); `[61:8]` "Allah's Light".
  * **Elsewhere:** `[9:32]` — verbatim twin of 61:8 ("They want to extinguish Allah's light with their mouths, but Allah refuses except to complete His light..."); the Light-as-revelation channel recurs at `[5:15]` ("A light from Allah has come to you") and `[7:157]` ("the light that came down with him").

## 4. Cross-References (The Web)
* **Parallel:** `[9:32]` — same extinguishing clause; the pair's difference is divine response ("refuses except to complete" vs "will complete") — *Source: observable corpus comparison.* (48:29 carries NO light clause — it is the companions'-description verse; the light-twin is 9:32 alone.)
* **Parallel:** `[9:33]`, `[48:28]` — the prevail-over-all-religions formula of 61:9, verbatim-family across three surahs.
* **Parallel:** `[33:69]` — "do not be like those who abused Moses" — the 61:5 hurt-Moses precedent made a direct behavioral command.
* **Formula pair:** `[63:6]` — "Allah does not guide the sinful people" occurs exactly twice in the corpus: 61:5 and 63:6 (verified) — the two as-Saff/al-Munafiqun verdicts form a Medinan couplet.
* **Within surah:** `[61:14]` (ruku 2) — the disciples as ansar answer 61:4's ranks: speech aligned with deed.
