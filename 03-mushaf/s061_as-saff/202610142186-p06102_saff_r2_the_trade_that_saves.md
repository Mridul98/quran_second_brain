# The trade that saves from a painful torment (61:10–14)
**UID:** 202610142186
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 61:10–14 (Ruku 2 of 2 in as-Saff)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[61:10]` – `[61:14]` (5 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah's indictment converts into a sales pitch: Allah offers a trade — believe and strive with possessions and selves — whose returns are forgiveness, the Gardens, "the supreme success," and a bonus the community already loves: support and imminent victory; the ruku closes by telling the believers to be Allah's supporters, quoting the disciples' answer to Jesus (61:14).
* **Placement:** Closing ruku; the offer sheet answers ruku 1's hated speech-gap (61:2–3) with a transaction whose currency is deed, and its rank-motif (the disciples' alignment) recalls the surah's name (61:4).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[61:10]`** "O you who believe! Shall I inform you of a trade that will save you from a painful torment?"
  * **Gloss:** A rhetorical vendor's question (the same figure as 61:5's "who is a greater wrongdoer...?"); the trade's product is salvation from torment — the business vocabulary announced before its terms.
* **`[61:11]`** "That you believe in Allah and His Messenger, and strive in the cause of Allah with your possessions and yourselves. That is best for you, if you only knew."
  * **Gloss:** The terms: faith in the commissioned Messenger of 61:9, and jihad with both capital and self; "if you only knew" — the knowledge the hurt-Moses people lacked (61:5 "although you know").
* **`[61:12]`** "He will forgive you your sins; and will admit you into gardens beneath which rivers flow, and into beautiful mansions in the Gardens of Eden. That is the supreme success."
  * **Gloss:** The returns — forgiveness, rivers-gardens, mansions in Eden — labeled *al-fawz al-ʿazim*, "the supreme success."
* **`[61:13]`** "And something else you love: support from Allah, and imminent victory. So give good news to the believers."
  * **Gloss:** A bonus on the believers' own demand curve: nasr and fath qarib — this-worldly help, soon; the command to give good news attaches to those who believe the offer.
* **`[61:14]`** "O you who believe! Be supporters of Allah, as Jesus son of Mary said to the disciples, “Who are my supporters towards Allah?” The disciples said, “We are Allah's supporters.” So a group of the Children of Israel believed, while another group disbelieved. We supported those who believed against their foe, so they became dominant."
  * **Gloss:** The historical precedent of the trade: the hawariyyun answered exactly this call; belief split Israel; the supporters were supported and made dominant — the payoff clause of 61:9/61:13 demonstrated.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`tijara` (trade):** `[61:10]` "a trade that will save you".
  * **Rendering:** "trade" kept as market vocabulary in the salvation register.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[9:111]` — "Allah has purchased from the believers their lives and their properties in exchange for Paradise... that is the supreme triumph" (the twin contract, with 61:12's "supreme success"); `[35:29]` — "expect a trade that will not fail"; `[2:16]` — the hypocrites' barter "does not profit"; `[2:254]` — "Spend from what We have given you, before a Day comes in which there is neither trading, nor friendship". All lines verified in corpus; the k-nifaq note already reads 61:10/9:111/35:29 as one offer sheet (backlink confirmed).
* **`jāhada bi-amwālikum wa-anfusikum` (strive with possessions and selves):** `[61:11]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[9:41]` — "strive with your possessions and your lives in the cause of Allah. That is better for you, if you only knew" — near-verbatim twin including the closing clause (verified); the economic half of the trade is the infraq idiom — "possessions" first, as in 2:254/57:18.
* **`ansar` (supporters of Allah):** `[61:14]` "Be supporters of Allah... “We are Allah's supporters.”"
  * **Elsewhere:** `[3:52]` — the same scene, rendered "Who are my allies towards Allah?... We are Allah's allies" — the term drifts between "supporters" (61:14) and "allies" (3:52) in this corpus; `[9:117]` "the Supporters" for the historical ansar — same word, community-title.
* **`fath qarib` (imminent victory):** `[61:13]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[110:1]` — "When there comes Allah's victory, and conquest" — the same pair (nasr + fath) as the surah that signals the end of the mission (verified line).

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Medinan by majority report (see index; type disputed in lists). The mobilization register ("fight in His cause, in ranks"; "strive... with your possessions") suits the expeditionary horizon classically assigned to this surah.
* **Asbab / narrative context:** Classical reports tie 61:10–13 to a community saying what it would do and not doing it; no occasion is stated in the corpus. *Source: classical tafsir reports on 61:10–11 — agent labeling; verify.* The "something else you love" (61:13) is read by the same reports as the believers' appetite for spoils/victory — *Source: classical tafsir — verify.*
