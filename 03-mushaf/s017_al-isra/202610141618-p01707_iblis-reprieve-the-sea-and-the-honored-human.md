# Iblis' reprieve, the sea, and the honored human (17:61–70)
**UID:** 202610141618
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 17:61–70 (Ruku 7 of 12 in al-Isra’)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[17:61]` – `[17:70]` (10 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The Adam-scene is retold with its demand for respite and its chilling answer — Satan gets sway over all of Adam's descendants "except My devotees" (61–65); the same unprotected human is then shipped across a sea he prays in, saved from, and forgets (66–67), with a land that could cave and a wind that could drown still one intention away (68–69) — and the unit lands on the counter-ayah: the Children of Adam honored, carried, fed, and favored (70). Honor is real; 4–8 and 67 prove it is not immunity.
* **Placement:** Ruku 7 of 12; the surah's anthropology hub between the cursed-tree test (60) and the national ledger (71).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[17:61]`** When We said to the angels, “Bow down before Adam,” they bowed down, except for Satan. He said, “Shall I bow down before someone You created from mud?”
  * **Gloss:** The bowing command, the exception, and the mud-based objection — the Adam-Iblis scene retold, one of the corpus' set-tellings (cf. `[2:34]`, `[7:11]`, `[15:28–29]`, `[18:50]`, `[20:116]`, `[38:71–72]`); note the corpus names "Satan" here, not "Iblis".
* **`[17:62]`** He said, “Do You see this one whom You have honored more than me? If You reprieve me until the Day of Resurrection, I will bring his descendants under my sway, except for a few.”
  * **Gloss:** "Whom You have honored more than me?" — envy as the first politics; the reprieve requested until the Day, and the declared program: his descendants under sway, "except for a few."
* **`[17:63]`** He said, “Begone! Whoever of them follows you—Hell is your reward, an ample reward.”
  * **Gloss:** "Begone!" — the cast-out one's followers get Hell as "an ample reward": the curse-register that called adultery "an evil way" (17:32) now names the payoff.
* **`[17:64]`** “And entice whomever of them you can with your voice, and rally against them your cavalry and your infantry, and share with them in wealth and children, and make promises to them.” But Satan promises them nothing but delusion.
  * **Gloss:** The conscription kit — voice, cavalry and infantry, partnership in wealth and children, promises — and the parenthesis that ends it: "Satan promises them nothing but delusion" (4:120's verbatim twin; note how "wealth and children" here re-runs 17:6's gift).
* **`[17:65]`** “As for My devotees, you have no authority over them.” Your Lord is an adequate Guardian.
  * **Gloss:** The exemption clause: "As for My devotees (ibadi), you have no authority over them" (15:42's twin) — and "Your Lord is an adequate Guardian (hafizh)."
* **`[17:66]`** Your Lord is He who propels for you the ships at sea, that you may seek of His bounty. He is towards you Most Merciful.
  * **Gloss:** The sea-grace: ships propelled for bounty-seeking, framed by "He is towards you Most Merciful" — seafaring as mercy with the rahim name (cf. 31:31's "persevering, thankful" pair).
* **`[17:67]`** When harm afflicts you at sea, those you pray to vanish, except for Him. But when He saves you to land, you turn away. The human being is ever thankless.
  * **Gloss:** The wave-trial and the double betrayal: at sea every prayed-to vanishes except Him; once ashore they turn away — "The human being is ever thankless (kafur)" — the gratitude audit failing twice in one storm.
* **`[17:68]`** Are you confident that He will not cause a track of land to cave in beneath you, or unleash a tornado against you, and then you find no protector?
  * **Gloss:** Land-security questioned: a track of land caving, a tornado (hasibah), and no protector (wakil) — 34:9/67:17's imagery in al-Isra' shorthand.
* **`[17:69]`** Or are you confident that He will not return you to it once again, and unleash a hurricane against you, and drown you for your ingratitude? Then you will find no helper against Us.
  * **Gloss:** Sea-security questioned twice over: returned to it, hurricane (qasf), drowned "for your ingratitude" (kafuran) — no helper against Us; the drowning-grammar already rehearsed on Pharaoh's side (103).
* **`[17:70]`** We have honored the Children of Adam, and carried them on land and sea, and provided them with good things, and greatly favored them over many of those We created.
  * **Gloss:** The counter-ayah, karramna: the Children of Adam honored, carried on land and sea, provided good things, "greatly favored over many of those We created" — the same honor that 17:4–8 made Israel's and 17:67 squandered.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`bow down before Adam`** `[17:61]`:
  * **Rendering:** the command, the exception, and "created from mud" (hama').
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:34]`, `[7:11]`, `[15:28]`, `[18:50]`, `[20:116]`, `[38:72]`; Satan's material-complaint with mud/clay at `[7:12]` ("You created him from mud") and `[15:33]`, `[38:76]` — the six-scene dossier; 17:62 adds the "honored more than me" grievance, echoed in `[38:34]`? ( Solomon's fall only) — *verify: only 17:62 uses this framing here (agent `rg` check on "honored more than me" — 1 hit).*
* **`authority / sway (sultan)`** `[17:62,65]`:
  * **Rendering:** Satan's promised sway over descendants; revoked over "My devotees."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[15:42]` "Over My servants you have no authority, except for the sinners who follow you"; `[38:82–83]` the same exception, "your loyal servants"; `[39:10]` "Say, O My devotees (ibadi)" — the ibadi-exemption is the surah's comfort clause.
* **`kafur (thankless / ingratitude)`** `[17:67,69]`:
  * **Rendering:** "The human being is ever thankless"; "drown you for your ingratitude."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[16:83]` recognize-then-deny; `[25:50]` "persist in thanklessness"; `[80:17]` "Perish man! How thankless he is!"; `[2:88]` cursed for ingratitude — all one dossier with 17:3/19's shukr pole.
* **`honored the Children of Adam (karramna)`** `[17:70]`:
  * **Rendering:** honor, carry, good things, favor — four verbs of endowment.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[45:16]` "We provided them with the good things; and We gave them advantage over all other people" — near-twin; `[36:60]` "Did I not covenant with you, O Children of Adam" — the honored addressed as covenanted; the "Children of Adam" address-cluster at `[7:26–35]`.
