# The Spirit, the joint challenge, and the sign-demanders (17:85–93)
**UID:** 202610141621
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 17:85–93 (Ruku 10 of 12 in al-Isra’)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[17:85]` – `[17:93]` (9 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Three knowledge-edges in a row: the Spirit declined to the questioners — "the domain of my Lord," with "only little knowledge" given to you (85); the revelation itself could be taken away, were it not for a mercy (86–87); and mankind and jinn together could not produce the like of this Quran (88), though every kind of similitude has been displayed and most persist in denial (89). Then the ultimatum-list: springs, palm-gardens, a falling sky, Allah and the angels before them, a house of gold, an ascension — capped by a readable book — answered by the surah's own humility line: "Glory be to my Lord. Am I anything but a human messenger?" (90–93).
* **Placement:** Ruku 10 of 12; the inimitability ruku; 85's "little knowledge" rhymes 52's "stayed only a little," and 90–93 is the practical answer to 59's withheld-signs clause.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[17:85]`** And they ask you about the Spirit. Say, “The Spirit belongs to the domain of my Lord; and you were given only little knowledge.”
  * **Gloss:** The Spirit-question answered with a deflection to the divine domain (min amri rabbi) and a census-statement on human knowledge — "only little" (qalil), the Quran's only use of that phrase for knowledge (rg-verified, 1 hit).
* **`[17:86]`** If We willed, We could take away what We revealed to you. Then you will find for yourself no protecting guardian against Us.
  * **Gloss:** If We willed, We could take away the revealed — no guardian against Us (same construction as 17:69/75's "no helper against Us"); the threat is not of loss but of the fact that retention is sheer favor.
* **`[17:87]`** Except through a mercy from your Lord. His favors upon you have been great.
  * **Gloss:** The exception: "except through a mercy from your Lord" — His favors on the Prophet "great" — revelation is a held gift, not a property.
* **`[17:88]`** Say, “If mankind and jinn came together to produce the like of this Quran, they could never produce the like of it, even if they backed up one another.”
  * **Gloss:** The challenge widened to a joint venture of mankind and jinn, backing each other — and still no mithl; the pairing of both creation-intelligences at a single literary task is unique (`rg "mankind and jinn"` — 1 hit).
* **`[17:89]`** We have displayed for mankind in this Quran every kind of similitude, but most people insist on denying the truth.
  * **Gloss:** "Every kind of similitude" displayed for mankind — the surah's own catalogue-method — and most insist on denying the truth (anidan: stubborn persistence).
* **`[17:90]`** And they said, “We will not believe in you unless you make a spring burst from the ground for us.
  * **Gloss:** Ultimatum one: a spring bursting from the ground — the very sign already granted Moses (2:60, 7:160 twelve springs).
* **`[17:91]`** Or you have a garden of palms and vines; then cause rivers to gush pouring through them.
  * **Gloss:** Ultimatum two: a garden of palms and vines with rivers gushing — the Meccan horticulture fantasy (cf. 2:266's test-garden).
* **`[17:92]`** Or make the sky fall on us in pieces, as you claim, or bring Allah and the angels before us.
  * **Gloss:** Ultimatum three and four: the sky in pieces "as you claim" (52:44's lumps-of-sky answer), or Allah and the angels brought face to face — the theophanic demand (25:21's twin).
* **`[17:93]`** Or you possess a house of gold. Or you ascend into the sky. Even then, we will not believe in your ascension, unless you bring down for us a book that we can read.” Say, “Glory be to my Lord. Am I anything but a human messenger?”
  * **Gloss:** Ultimatum five and six: a house of gold (43:53's treasure-demand), an ascension — and even then, only "a book that we can read": the demand for paper-revelation, met by "Glory be to my Lord. Am I anything but a human messenger?" — the surah's own 6:7 answer to touch-and-see faith.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`al-Ruh (the Spirit)`** `[17:85]`:
  * **Rendering:** "The Spirit belongs to the domain of my Lord" — deferral; the answer declines substance.
  * **Elsewhere:** the Ruh-dossier: `[16:2]` angels "with the Spirit"; `[16:102]` "The Holy Spirit has brought it down"; `[26:193]` "The Honest Spirit came down with it"; `[40:15]` "He conveys the Spirit, by His command"; `[97:4]` angels and the Spirit descend; `[78:38]`; `[70:4]`; on 'Isa: `[2:87]`/`[2:253]` "supported him with the Holy Spirit", `[4:171]` "a Spirit from Him", `[15:29]`/`[32:9]`/`[38:72]` the breathed spirit — the question of whether Ruh = Gabriel or revelation is classically disputed (*Source: dispute recorded in the commentaries; 17:85 itself is the Quran's own decline — agent labeling*).
* **`the like of this Quran (mithl)`** `[17:88]`:
  * **Rendering:** produce the like of it, jointly, backing one another.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:23]` "produce a chapter like these"; `[10:38]` "a single chapter like it"; `[11:13]` "ten chapters like it, invented"; `[8:31]` the counterfeit claim "we could have said the like of this" — 17:88 is the maximal version of the challenge-scale.
* **`similitude (mathal)`** `[17:89]`:
  * **Rendering:** "every kind of similitude."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[18:54]` verbatim-twin "We have elaborated in this Quran for the people every kind of example"; `[16:75]`–`[16:76]` the powerlessness parables an-Nahl cites next door to this surah.
* **`human messenger (bashar)`** `[17:93]`:
  * **Rendering:** "Am I anything but a human messenger?"
  * **Elsewhere:** `[17:94]` the scandal named in the next ayah; `[6:8]` "Why was an angel not sent down to him"; `[25:7]` "What sort of messenger is this, who eats food, and walks in the marketplaces?" — the bashar-messenger defense is a Meccan set-piece.
