# Say the best, the withheld signs, and the cursed tree (17:53–60)
**UID:** 202610141617
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 17:53–60 (Ruku 6 of 12 in al-Isra’)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[17:53]` – `[17:60]` (8 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** A unit of speech-ethics and sign-economics: tell the servants to say what is best because Satan sows discord (53); the Prophet is not their advocate (54); God knows the heavens, ranks the prophets, and gave David the Psalms (55) — while the invoked "gods" are themselves worshippers seeking nearness (56–57), every doomed city is written in the Book (58), signs are withheld because the ancients called them lies (the she-camel), sent only to terrify (59), and the shown vision plus the cursed tree are tests that increase only flight (60).
* **Placement:** Ruku 6 of 12; connects the kalam-ruku (5) to the Iblis-ruku (7) — idols fall silent, prophetic ranks rise, and the sign-history of 59–60 pre-loads the sign-demands of 90–93.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[17:53]`** Tell My servants to say what is best. Satan sows discord among them. Satan is to man an open enemy.
  * **Gloss:** Social ethics issued as divine address: "say what is best" — because Satan yufizz (sows discord) — "Satan is to man an open enemy," the 2:168 phrase re-deployed as community hygiene.
* **`[17:54]`** Your Lord knows you best. If He wills, He will have mercy on you; and if He wills, He will punish you. We did not send you as their advocate.
  * **Gloss:** Divine sovereignty over mercy or punishment, "if He wills" — and the office declared: "We did not send you as their advocate" — the prophet is not the guarantor of his audience (cf. 6:107).
* **`[17:55]`** Your Lord knows well everyone in the heavens and the earth. We have given some prophets advantage over others; and to David We gave the Psalms.
  * **Gloss:** The heavenly census: God knows all in heavens and earth; some prophets given advantage over others; and to David — the Psalms, the named scripture of the ranked.
* **`[17:56]`** Say, “Call upon those you claim besides Him. They have no power to relieve your adversity, nor can they change it.”
  * **Gloss:** The challenge to client-gods: call on them; they cannot relieve adversity nor transfer it — powerlessness argued in a line (cf. 34:22's near-identical commission).
* **`[17:57]`** Those they call upon are themselves seeking means of access to their Lord, vying to be nearer, and hoping for His mercy, and fearing His punishment. The punishment of your Lord is to be dreaded.
  * **Gloss:** The knife-twist: those they invoke are themselves seeking means of access (wasīlah) to God, vying for nearness, hoping, fearing — the worshippers are worse off than their worshipped; "The punishment of your Lord is to be dreaded."
* **`[17:58]`** There is no city but We will destroy before the Day of Resurrection, or punish it with a severe punishment. This is inscribed in the Book.
  * **Gloss:** No city but will be destroyed or severely punished before the Day — "This is inscribed in the Book" — the urban ledger of 16/17 restated as written decree.
* **`[17:59]`** Nothing prevents Us from sending miraculous signs, except that the ancients called them lies. We gave Thamood the she-camel, a visible sign, but they mistreated her. We do not send the signs except to instill reverence.
  * **Gloss:** Why signs are withheld: the ancients' precedent of calling them lies; Thamood's she-camel — a visible sign they mistreated; "We do not send the signs except to instill reverence" — the sign is an alarm, not a proof.
* **`[17:60]`** We said to you that your Lord encompasses humanity. We did not make the vision We showed you, except as a test for the people, and the tree cursed in the Quran. We frighten them, but that only increases their defiance.
  * **Gloss:** "Your Lord encompasses humanity"; the vision shown is made "a test for the people," and the tree cursed in the Quran — threatening them "only increases their defiance" — the third increase-verse of the surah.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`wasilah (means of access)`** `[17:57]`:
  * **Rendering:** "seeking means of access to their Lord, vying to be nearer."
  * **Elsewhere:** the phrase-form "means of access" occurs only here (`rg` — 1 hit); `[5:35]` "seek the means of approach to Him" (the devotional twin of the same root), `[39:3]` the idolaters' defense: "they may bring us nearer to Allah" — 17:57 turns their own nearness-idiom against them.
* **`Psalms (Zabur)`** `[17:55]`:
  * **Rendering:** "to David We gave the Psalms."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[4:163]` "And We gave David the Psalms" — verbatim pair; `[21:105]` "We have written in the Psalms, after the Reminder" — the only other Zabur mention; the ranked-prophets clause pairs verbatim with `[2:253]` "These messengers: We gave some advantage over others."
* **`she-camel (naqah)`** `[17:59]`:
  * **Rendering:** "We gave Thamood the she-camel, a visible sign, but they mistreated her."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[7:73]` "This she-camel of Allah is a sign for you"; `[11:64]`; `[26:155]`; `[54:27]` "We are sending the she-camel as a test for them; so watch them and be patient"; `[91:13]` — six corpus hits, all Thamood; 17:59 adds the withholding-theology ("except to instill reverence").
* **`the tree cursed in the Quran`** `[17:60]`:
  * **Rendering:** "the tree cursed in the Quran" — unnamed here.
  * **Elsewhere:** identified by description with the "Tree of Bitterness" of `[37:62–64]` ("a tree that grows from the bottom of Hell"), `[44:43–46]`, `[56:52]` — *Source: classical identification, unanimous in tafsir (Zaqqum); observable in corpus because Itani renders Zaqqum as "Tree of Bitterness" and never transliterates the name (`rg -i "zaqqum"` — 0 hits), a translation-choice fact worth noting.*

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Meccan (consensus).
* **Asbab / tafsir:** the "vision" of 17:60 is read in the commentaries either as the Isra' sights of 17:1 or as a dream; "the tree cursed" is glossed as Zaqqum (the Quraysh mockery of "a tree in Hell" is reported in the occasion-reports). *Source: Ibn Kathir / al-Tabari on 17:60 — agent labeling; verify in print.* The corpus itself keeps both indefinite.
