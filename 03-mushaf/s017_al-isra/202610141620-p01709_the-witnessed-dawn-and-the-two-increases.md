# The witnessed dawn and the two increases (17:78–84)
**UID:** 202610141620
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 17:78–84 (Ruku 9 of 12 in al-Isra’)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[17:78]` – `[17:84]` (7 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** From sun-decline to night-darkness, and the dawn recitation that is "witnessed" — prayer as the ledger-keeping body's own time-keeping (78); a vigil added beyond the obligatory, and a "laudable position" perhaps in view (79); then the traveler's double prayer for a true entry and a true exit (80), the proclamation that truth has come and falsehood withered (81), and the Book's two-directional effect — healing and mercy for believers, pure loss for wrongdoers (82) — ending on the human portrait: turns away when blessed, despairs when afflicted, each running on his own disposition while God alone knows who is best guided (83–84).
* **Placement:** Ruku 9 of 12; the worship-rhythm answer to the decree (22–40) and the time-table version of 17:12's night-and-day signs; 82 closes the surah's three-fold increase-motif before 109 reverses it.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[17:78]`** Perform the prayer at the decline of the sun, until the darkness of the night; and the Quran at dawn. The Quran at dawn is witnessed.
  * **Gloss:** Prayer-appointed: from the sun's decline (duluk) to the night's darkness, plus "the Quran at dawn" — dawn recitation mashhudah, "witnessed" (the angels' attendance is the classical gloss; cf. 11:103 a Day to be witnessed).
* **`[17:79]`** And keep vigil with it during parts of the night, as an extra prayer. Perhaps your Lord will raise you to a laudable position.
  * **Gloss:** Tahajjud — vigil "with it" (the Quran) in parts of the night, as extra (nafilatan); the horizon: "perhaps your Lord will raise you to a laudable position" (maqam mahmud — classically the station of intercession).
* **`[17:80]`** And say, “My Lord, lead me in through an entry of truth, and lead me out through an exit of truth, and grant me from You a supporting power.”
  * **Gloss:** Entry-of-truth, exit-of-truth, and a supporting power (sultanan nasira) — the Hijrah-dua shape: in and out named as truth-ports, echoing 17:76's threatened exit from the land.
* **`[17:81]`** And say, “The truth has come, and falsehood has withered away; for falsehood is bound to wither away.”
  * **Gloss:** The battle-cry of the moment: truth has come and falsehood has withered — and the general law under it: falsehood is "bound to wither" (zahaq — cf. 34:49's twin line).
* **`[17:82]`** We send down in the Quran healing and mercy for the believers, but it increases the wrongdoers only in loss.
  * **Gloss:** The third increase-ayah: the Quran as shifa' (healing) and rahma for the believers; for the wrongdoers it "increases… only in loss" (khusr — the whole of surah 103 in three words).
* **`[17:83]`** When We bless the human being, he turns away and distances himself. But when adversity touches him, he is in despair.
  * **Gloss:** The human triad completed: blessed → turns away, distances; afflicted → despairs (ya'us) — 41:51's near-verbatim portrait; haste (11), ingratitude (67), despair (83) are this surah's three self-portraits of man.
* **`[17:84]`** Say, “Each does according to his disposition. Your Lord knows best who is better guided in the way.”
  * **Gloss:** "Each does according to his disposition (shakl)" — temperaments recognized as real drivers; "Your Lord knows best who is better guided in the way" — guidance as the only rank that counts (returns to 17:9's sorting).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`witnessed (mashhud)`** `[17:78]`:
  * **Rendering:** "The Quran at dawn is witnessed."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[11:103]` "a Day to be witnessed" — the word "witnessed" in this phrase-form has exactly two corpus hits, `[11:103]` and `[17:78]`; the dawn's witnessing is classically read as angelic assembly (Source: classical tafsir on 17:78 — verify).
* **`laudable position`** `[17:79]`:
  * **Rendering:** "raise you to a laudable position" — maqam + mahmud, the Quran's only occurrence of the phrase.
  * **Elsewhere:** the passive of the same root names God "the Praised" (`[22:64]`, `[31:26]`, `[42:28]`, `[60:6]`); the intercession-station identification is transmitted, not textual — *Source: classical tafsir on 17:79 (hadith-derived maqam mahmud, Ibn Kathir/Tabari) — verify in print.*
* **`healing (shifa')`** `[17:82]`:
  * **Rendering:** "healing and mercy for the believers."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[10:57]` "advice from your Lord, and healing for what is in the hearts, and guidance and mercy"; `[41:44]` "a guidance and a healing" for believers with the deaf-disbelievers clause; `[16:69]` the bees' drink "containing healing for the people" — physical and Qur'anic healing share the word.
* **`in loss (khusr)`** `[17:82]`:
  * **Rendering:** "increases the wrongdoers only in loss."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[103:2]` "The human being is in loss"; `[48:10]` "whoever breaks his pledge breaks it to his own loss"; `[22:11]` "that is the obvious loss"; `[34:50]` "If I err, I err only to my own loss" — the loss-vocabulary of the ledger surahs.
