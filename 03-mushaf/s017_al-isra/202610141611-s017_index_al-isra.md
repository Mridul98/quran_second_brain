## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Meccan — consensus of the classical surah-lists. *Source: transmitted classification (al-Itqan-type introductions; no strong dissent recorded) — verify in print.* Revelation order **50** in the Ibn-Abbas-derived / Tanzil-type enumeration (same convention as the s013/s016 indexes). *Source: transmitted nuzul-order tables — verify.*
* **Size:** 111 ayahs, 12 ruku (per `data/ruku_map.json`, globals 240–251; per-ruku ayah counts 10+12+8+10+12+8+10+7+7+9+7+11 = 111 ✓, matching `corpus/s017_al-isra.md` and `data/surah_manifest.json`). Mushaf position 17, between an-Nahl (16) and al-Kahf (18).
* **Name:** al-Isra’ — “The Night Journey”; the corpus header prints “THE NIGHT JOURNEY” (observable, edition A). The name-event is only 17:1 (“journeyed His servant by night”); the surah is bracketed by tasbih — opens “Glory to Him” (`[17:1]`), breaks at “Be He glorified” (`[17:43]`), closes “glorify Him constantly” (`[17:111]`).
* **Central theme:** Two scriptures and one ledger. The surah moves from the gifted vision (17:1) to the gifted Book: the Torah was made “a guide for the Children of Israel” (17:2) whose double corruption and double restoration is prophesied inside it (17:4–8); now “this Quran guides to what is most upright” (17:9). Everything between is account-keeping: fate tied to each neck (17:13), every people called with its leader (17:71), a decree ring of ten prohibitions bracketed twice by “Do not set up another god” (17:22–39), prayer and vigil as the believer's ledger-entry (17:78–79), and a closing doxology of the Names (17:110–111).
* **Theme checks the task asked to verify honestly (all against the corpus):** Night journey ✓ `[17:1]` Sacred Mosque → Farthest Mosque, “whose precincts We have blessed,” “the Listener, the Beholder.” Decalogue-like decree ✓ and the range is exactly `[17:22]`–`[17:39]`: parents (23–24), no killing of children (31), chastity (32), the sacred soul (33), orphan/pledge (34), measure (35), “do not occupy yourself with what you have no knowledge of” (36), pride (37), bracketed by 22/39 — note it straddles ruku 2–4 (17:22 closes ruku 2). Israel's double corruption ✓ `[17:4]`–`[17:8]` (“commit evil on earth twice”; two promise-cycles; “if you revert, We will revert”) — but the *gathering* of nations is not in 4–8: it is the ledger scene at `[17:71]` and “We will bring you all together” at `[17:104]`. “Nor do We ever punish until We have sent a messenger” ✓ `[17:15]`. Nation-by-nation ledger ✓ `[17:71]`. The Quran's humbling effect ✓ but the exact “increases only in aversion” line is a composite: verbatim increase-motif at `[17:41]` “only adds to their rebellion,” `[17:60]` “increases their defiance,” `[17:82]` “increases the wrongdoers only in loss”; “an invisible barrier” is `[17:45]` and “they turn their backs in aversion” verbatim is `[17:46]`; the positive mirror is `[17:109]` “adds to their humility.” Ibrahim's “fire, be coolness and safety” twin at `[21:69]` — **not in this surah and no Ibrahim mention at all** (`rg -i "abraham|Ibrahim" corpus/s017_al-isra.md` — 0 ayah-lines); what IS here is the Moses–Pharaoh cycle (17:2–8, 101–104), Noah (3, 17), David (55), the she-camel (59), the Adam–Satan scene (61–65), and the “tree cursed in the Quran” (60) = the “Tree of Bitterness” of `[37:62]`/`[44:43]`/`[56:52]`. Closing tasbih ✓ `[17:110]` (“Call Him Allah, or call Him the Most Merciful… the Best Names”); the “sends down” language sits one pair earlier, `[17:105]` “With the truth We sent it down” (also `[17:82]` “We send down in the Quran healing”), not in 110–111.

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Vision → book → sorting (1–10):** the journey ayah hands the surah its tasbih frame, then the Torah's guide-office and Israel's twice-told corruption cycle, then the Quran's parallel claim (9) and the two-audience cut (10) that recurs at 45.
* **The human ledger (11–22):** hasty man, day-and-night clocks, the fate tied to the neck, the personal scroll, the messenger-before-punishment rule (15), the affluent-town mechanism (16), and the two desires (18–21) — ending exactly where the decree begins (22).
* **The decree ring (22–40):** worship-one-God and parents (23–25), wealth ethics (26–30), the seven prohibitions (31–37), summary verdict (38), bracket-closing wisdom line (39), and the concrete shirk charge answered (40).
* **The dividing book (41–60):** three increase-verses (41, the barrier of 45–46, and 82's twin) around the cosmos' counter-praise (42–44); resurrection mockery answered (49–52); speech ethics, prophetic ranks, silent idols, the withheld signs, the test-vision and cursed tree (53–60).
* **From Iblis to honor (61–77):** the Adam scene and the devotees' exemption (61–65); sea-survival and the thankless turn (66–69); the honored Children of Adam (70); the national roll-call (71–72) and the prophet's near-miss with a firmness from God (73–77).
* **Prayer, challenge, closure (78–111):** the commanded day-parts and the vigil (78–79), the truth-vs-falsehood proclamation and healing/loss (80–84); the Spirit, the inimitability challenge, the six sign-demands, the human-messenger scandal (85–100); the Moses coda (101–104), the revealed-in-stages book and the prostrating scholars (105–109), the Names and the closing hamd-tasbih (110–111).
