# Varied explanations, cosmic praise, and the bone-yard (17:41–52)
**UID:** 202610141616
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 17:41–52 (Ruku 5 of 12 in al-Isra’)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[17:41]` – `[17:52]` (12 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The Book announces its dividing effect: explained in every turning, it only adds to the rebels' rebellion (41); between the reciter and the Hereafter-deniers God sets an invisible barrier, covers on hearts and weight in ears (45–46) — while the cosmos, deaf men cannot hear, is full of praise (44); the mockers' bone-yard question (49) gets its hardening escalation (50–51) and its answer: the Originator restores, and the deniers will rise realizing they stayed "only a little" (52).
* **Placement:** Ruku 5 of 12; the surah's kalam-ruku — its first "increase" verse (41) opens the 41/60/82 triad, and 45 re-runs 10's sorting as perceptual architecture.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[17:41]`** We have explained in this Quran in various ways, that they may remember, but it only adds to their rebellion.
  * **Gloss:** Sarafna — "We have explained… in various ways," the Quran turned this way and that so they may remember; the result inverted: "it only adds to their rebellion" — the first of the surah's three increase-verses.
* **`[17:42]`** Say, “If there were other gods with Him, as they say, they would have sought a way to the Lord of the Throne.”
  * **Gloss:** The partners-reductio: rival gods would have to "seek a way to the Lord of the Throne" — polytheism collapses as a jurisdictional absurdity before the Throne.
* **`[17:43]`** Be He glorified. He is exalted, far above what they say.
  * **Gloss:** The surah's mid-point tasbih break — "Be He glorified… far above what they say" — answering its own 42 (cf. 17:1, 17:111).
* **`[17:44]`** Praising Him are the seven heavens, and the earth, and everyone in them. There is not a thing that does not glorify Him with praise, but you do not understand their praises. He is indeed Forbearing and Forgiving.
  * **Gloss:** Cosmic liturgy: the seven heavens, the earth, all in them praise Him; "there is not a thing that does not glorify Him with praise, but you do not understand their praises" — universal doxology, particular deafness; closed by Forbearing and Forgiving.
* **`[17:45]`** When you read the Quran, We place between you and those who do not believe in the Hereafter an invisible barrier.
  * **Gloss:** When you read the Quran, an invisible barrier is interposed between the reciter and the Hereafter-deniers — recitation itself becomes a sorting event.
* **`[17:46]`** And We drape veils over their hearts, preventing them from understanding it, and heaviness in their ears. And when you mention your Lord alone in the Quran, they turn their backs in aversion.
  * **Gloss:** Covers on hearts, weight in ears; when your Lord is mentioned alone in the Quran, "they turn their backs in aversion" — the word *aversion* verbatim, here and not at 41/82.
* **`[17:47]`** We know well what they listen to, when they listen to you, as they conspire, when the wrongdoers say, “You only follow a man bewitched.”
  * **Gloss:** God knows their listening-as-conspiring, and the wrongdoers' label: "a man bewitched" — the same slur Pharaoh will say to Moses at 17:101.
* **`[17:48]`** Note what they compared you to. They are lost, and unable to find a way.
  * **Gloss:** "Note what they compared you to" — and the verdict: lost, unable to find a way (cf. 25:9's near-verbatim).
* **`[17:49]`** And they say, “When we have become bones and fragments, shall we really be resurrected as a new creation?”
  * **Gloss:** The bone-yard sneer: bones and fragments resurrected "as a new creation" — the formula quoted verbatim again at 17:98.
* **`[17:50]`** Say, “Even if you become rocks or iron.
  * **Gloss:** The counter-escalation begins: "Even if you become rocks or iron" — matter harder than dust.
* **`[17:51]`** Or some substance, which, in your minds, is even harder.” Then they will say, “Who will restore us?” Say, “The One who originated you the first time.” Then they will nod their heads at you, and say, “When will it be?” Say, “Perhaps it will be soon.”
  * **Gloss:** "…or some substance harder in your minds" — then their "Who will restore us?" answered: "The One who originated you the first time" (cf. 36:79); head-shaking, "When will it?" — "Perhaps soon."
* **`[17:52]`** On the Day when He calls you, you will respond with His praise, and you will realize that you stayed only a little.
  * **Gloss:** The Day of the call: they respond with His praise and realize they stayed "only a little" — the brief-life idiom of 79:46, 23:113, 10:45; and *a little* rhymes 17:85's "little knowledge."

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`explained in various ways (sarfna)`** `[17:41]`:
  * **Rendering:** "We have explained in this Quran in various ways, that they may remember."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[6:46]` "how We explain the revelations in various ways, yet they still turn away"; `[7:58]` "Thus We explain the revelations in various ways for people who are thankful" — same verb-formula, opposite outcomes; the Quran's self-description by its own turning.
* **`adds to their rebellion`** `[17:41]`:
  * **Rendering:** "but it only adds to their rebellion" (ziyada-shiqāq).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[5:68]` "Allah will increase many of them in rebellion and disbelief" — the only other increase-by-recitation twin; the motif continues at `[17:60]` "increases their defiance," `[17:82]` "increases the wrongdoers only in loss," `[17:109]` "adds to their humility."
* **`barrier (hijab) / covers (aghshiyah)`** `[17:45–46]`:
  * **Rendering:** "an invisible barrier"; "We drape veils over their hearts."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[36:9]` "a barrier in front of them, and a barrier behind them"; `[41:5]` "between us and you is a barrier"; `[6:25]` "covers over their hearts… heaviness in their ears"; `[18:57]` the same with "coverings" — the deaf-hearts cluster is a Meccan stock scene (also `[41:44]`).
* **`bones and fragments / new creation`** `[17:49]`:
  * **Rendering:** "When we have become bones and fragments, shall we really be resurrected as a new creation?"
  * **Elsewhere:** `[36:78]` "Who will revive the bones when they have decayed?"; `[75:3]` "reassemble his bones?"; `[56:47]`, `[37:16]`, `[23:82]` the dust-and-bones sneer; "a new creation" also `[13:5]`, `[32:10]`, `[34:7]`, `[50:15]`.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Meccan (consensus); the barrier/cover scene belongs to the Meccan hardening vocabulary (cf. `[2:7]`, `[6:25]`, `[18:57]`).
* **Note:** "rocks or iron" (50) has no prior Quranic pair in this exact escalation; iron appears as material elsewhere (`[34:10]` softened for David; `[57:25]` "sent down iron") but the bone-yard's hardening counter is this surah's own image. *Source: agent observation over `rg` — verify.*
