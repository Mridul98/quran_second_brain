# Every people with its leader, and the pressure to invent (17:71–77)
**UID:** 202610141619
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 17:71–77 (Ruku 8 of 12 in al-Isra’)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[17:71]` – `[17:77]` (7 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The Day is a roll-call: every people with its imam, and the right-handed read their record unhindered (71); blindness now is blindness then, and further from the way (72). Then the surah pulls back the curtain on the gravest pressure a messenger can carry: a near-success attempt to seduce him into inventing revelation, friendship as the price, stability from God as the only reason it failed, double tasting had he inclined, and the expulsion plot whose "tradition of the messengers" has no amendment (73–77).
* **Placement:** Ruku 8 of 12; the nation-ledger (71) closes the book-bracket opened at 13–14, and 73–77 is the surah's kalam-integrity centerpiece before the prayer ruku.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[17:71]`** On the Day when We call every people with their leader. Whoever is given his record in his right hand—these will read their record, and they will not be wronged one bit.
  * **Gloss:** The national ledger: every people called with its leader (imaman) — the right-handed read their own record, "not wronged one bit" — collectivity by banner, accountability by book.
* **`[17:72]`** But whoever is blind in this, he will be blind in the Hereafter, and further astray from the way.
  * **Gloss:** Blind in this world → blind in the Hereafter → "further astray from the way" — the 20:124–125 blind-gathering and 25:34's worse-position phrasing fused.
* **`[17:73]`** They almost lured you away from what We have revealed to you, so that you would invent something else in Our name. In that case, they would have taken you for a friend.
  * **Gloss:** They nearly lured you away from the revealed, to forge something in Our name — "they would have taken you for a friend": flattery as the mechanism of fabrication.
* **`[17:74]`** Had We not given you stability, you might have inclined towards them a little.
  * **Gloss:** "Had We not given you stability, you might have inclined a little" — the Prophet's protection from a *little* inclination is stated as pure divine favor, not natural immunity.
* **`[17:75]`** Then We would have made you taste double in life, and double at death; then you would have found for yourself no helper against Us.
  * **Gloss:** The hypothetical penalty: doubled in life and doubled at death, no helper — the double-suffering of 33:30–31's register, applied to the office itself.
* **`[17:76]`** They almost provoked you, to expel you from the land. In that case, they would not have lasted after you, except briefly.
  * **Gloss:** The expulsion plot: nearly provoked you out of the land — and their own tenure after him would have been brief: the 8:30 plan (imprison, kill, expel) answered by history.
* **`[17:77]`** The tradition of the messengers We sent before you—you will find no change in Our rules.
  * **Gloss:** "The tradition of the messengers We sent before you" — send, rejected, expelled, vindicated — "you will find no change in Our rules" (sunnah vocabulary; cf. 48:23, 35:43).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`imam (leader)`** `[17:71]`:
  * **Rendering:** "We call every people with their leader."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[21:73]` "We made them leaders, guiding by Our command"; `[28:41]` "leaders calling to the Fire" — imams exist on both sides; `[16:120]` Abraham called an "exemplary leader" (the word, not the man — Abraham is absent from s017 itself).
* **`record in the right hand`** `[17:71]`:
  * **Rendering:** right-handed reading, zero-wronging.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[69:19]` "Here, take my book and read it"; `[84:7]` given in the right hand — the same hand-idiom of 13–14 now in national scale; `[4:41]` a witness from every community pairs with the roll-call.
* **`stability`** `[17:74]`:
  * **Rendering:** "Had We not given you stability."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[14:26]`–`[14:27]` — the bad word "has no stability," while "Allah gives firmness to those who believe, with the firm word" — divine steadying as the paired image; the word "stability" itself has exactly two corpus hits (`[14:26]`, `[17:74]`).
* **`tradition / no change in rules`** `[17:77]`:
  * **Rendering:** "The tradition of the messengers… no change in Our rules."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[48:23]` "Allah's pattern, ongoing since the past. You will never find any change"; `[35:43]` "You will not find any change in Allah's practice"; `[18:55]` "the precedent of the ancients" — the sunnah-word here is about messenger-fates, not prayer-law.

## 4. Cross-References (The Web)
* **Parallel:** `[69:44–46]` — "Had he falsely attributed some statements to Us, We would have seized him by the right arm, then slashed his lifeline" — the only other fabrication-threat with the same conditional grammar; 17:73–75 is its twin.
* **Parallel:** `[8:30]` — the Meccan counterfactual "to imprison you, or kill you, or expel you" ↔ `[17:76]`; `[14:13]` messengers threatened with expulsion — the tradition stated.
* **Parallel:** `[25:34]` — "further astray from the way" verbatim ↔ `[17:72]`; `[20:124–125]` the blind-gathering Q&A.
* **Within surah:** `[17:71]` ↔ `[17:13–14]` personal scroll / national banner; `[17:76–77]` ↔ `[17:5–8]` the same reversal-and-restoration cycle Israel was warned inside; `[17:73]` "invent" ↔ `[17:39]` "the wisdom revealed" — invention vs. revelation define the prophet's office.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Meccan (consensus), yet 73–77 and 76 read as pre-Hijrah pressure and expulsion-threat; occasion-reports classically tie 17:76 to Meccan expulsion scheming. *Source: transmitted asbab-type notices (Ibn Kathir on 17:76) — agent labeling; verify.*
* **Theological note:** 17:74 is cited in classical debates on prophetic protection (isma) since it admits a *hypothetical* inclination ("a little") averted only by divine steadying. *Source: consensus framing of the commentators; agent summary — verify in print.*
