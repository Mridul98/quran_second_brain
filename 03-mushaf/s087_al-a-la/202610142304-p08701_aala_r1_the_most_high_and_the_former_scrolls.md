# The Most High, the Read-Promise, and the Former Scrolls (87:1–19)
**UID:** 202610142304
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 87:1–19 (Ruku 1 of 1 in al-A'la)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[87:1]` – `[87:19]` (19 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah answers its own command — "Praise the Name of your Lord, the Most High" — with the evidence of ordered creation (87:2–5), then secures the messenger's memory and mission (87:6–8), sorts hearers by how they receive the reminder (87:9–13), prices the two lives (87:14–17), and finally shows the message is not an innovation but archive material: "The Scriptures of Abraham and Moses" (87:18–19).
* **Placement:** Ruku 1 of 1; the batch's doxology hinge between 86's Decisive Word and 88's Day of two faces. Its reminder-clause (87:9) is the shorter-sister of 88:21.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[87:1]`** "Praise the Name of your Lord, the Most High."
  * **Gloss:** The imperative opens with a Name, not a description; "the Most High" is the surah's title-word. The same glorification command, with different epithets, at `[56:74]` ("your Great Lord"), `[56:96]` and `[69:52]` ("the Magnificent") (rg-verified) — a four-site praise-formula cluster.
* **`[87:2]`–`[87:3]`** "He who creates and regulates. / He who measures and guides."
  * **Gloss:** Two participial pairs: making-then-ordering, apportioning-then-leading. The direct twin is Moses' answer `[20:50]`: "Our Lord is He who gave everything its existence, then guided it" (rg-verified) — guidance is built into the make.
* **`[87:4]`–`[87:5]`** "He who produces the pasture. / And then turns it into light debris."
  * **Gloss:** One season inside: green growth → brittle residue — the parable-engine the batch will cite for this-worldly life: `[57:20]` "…then it becomes debris. …The life of this world is nothing but enjoyment of vanity", with the same rendering at `[39:21]` and `[18:45]` (rg-verified).
* **`[87:6]`–`[87:7]`** "We will make you read, so do not forget. / Except what Allah wills. He knows what is declared, and what is hidden."
  * **Gloss:** The recitation is promised, guarded by the divine-will caveat (87:7a), and backed by total knowledge (87:7b). Kin: the first-revelation command `[96:1]`–`[96:5]` "Read… Taught man what he never knew" (rg-verified); the declared/hidden pair is this surah's entry in the corpus's secrets-cluster — cf. `[2:33]`, `[49:18]`, `[59:22]` (rg-verified), and the disclosure counterpart `[86:9]`.
* **`[87:8]`** "We will ease you into the Easy Way."
  * **Gloss:** Not merely method but road: God eases the prophet *into* ease — kin `[94:5]`–`[94:6]` "With hardship comes ease" and the legislation-clause `[2:185]` "Allah desires ease for you, and does not desire hardship for you" (rg-verified).
* **`[87:9]`–`[87:11]`** "So remind, if reminding helps. / The reverent will remember. / But the wretched will avoid it."
  * **Gloss:** The mission gets a conditional and a sociology: reminder works on the reverent (muttaqi), is shunned by the wretched (shaqiyy) — the same two-column hearing as 88:8–16 and 50:45's "So remind by the Quran whoever fears My warning" (rg-verified); the reminder-formula also at `[52:29]` "So remind." and `[51:55]` "And remind, for the reminder benefits the believers" (rg-verified).
* **`[87:10]`** "The reverent will remember."
  * **Gloss:** The positive reception: the muttaqi receives the reminder — memory as the fruit of reverence, the first column of the hearing-sociology.
* **`[87:12]`–`[87:13]`** "He who will enter the Gigantic Fire. / Where he will neither die, nor live."
  * **Gloss:** "Gigantic Fire" (an-nar al-kubra) is corpus-unique here (rg-verified). The neither-death-nor-life construction is the near-twin of `[20:74]` "…for him is Hell, where he neither dies nor lives" (same figure, tense differs — rg-verified): the hint's 87↔20:74 cross-ref confirmed at the wording level.
* **`[87:14]`–`[87:15]`** "Successful is he who purifies himself. / And mentions the name of his Lord, and prays."
  * **Gloss:** The success-formula "Successful is he who…" opens 91:9 verbatim ("Successful is he who purifies it," rg-verified) — one formula split across the batch: self-purification here, soul-purification there; prayer-as-Name-mention rings back to 87:1 and pairs with `[20:14]` "practice the prayer for My remembrance" (rg-verified).
* **`[87:16]`–`[87:17]`** "But you prefer the present life. / Though the Hereafter is better, and more lasting."
  * **Gloss:** The plural turn ("you") indicts preference, not possession; the comparative pair is fixed idiom here and at `[42:36]` "what Allah possesses is better and more lasting" and `[28:60]` (rg-verified), with the delight-in-world clause at `[13:26]`.
* **`[87:18]`–`[87:19]`** "This is in the former scriptures. / The Scriptures of Abraham and Moses."
  * **Gloss:** The archive close: "former scriptures" also at `[20:133]`; the Abraham/Moses pair reversed in order at `[53:36]`–`[53:37]` "the Scrolls of Moses? And of Abraham, who fulfilled?" and the honorable-pages at `[80:13]` (rg-verified). No "first and last" descriptor in this corpus (see index §5b).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`al-A'la` (the Most High)**: the comparative name of rank.
  * **Rendering:** 87:1 "Praise the Name of your Lord, the Most High"; header "THE MOST HIGH".
  * **Elsewhere:** the glorify-the-Name formula at `[56:74]`, `[56:96]`, `[69:52]`; the phrase "the Most High" itself is rare: `[2:255]` "He is the Most High, the Great", `[92:20]` "his Lord, the Most High" (rg-verified).
* **`qaddara wa hada` (measures and guides)**: apportioning as guiding.
  * **Rendering:** "He who measures and guides" (87:3).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[20:50]` (existence-then-guidance); the regulation half at `[43:11]` "sends down water… in due proportion" (rg-verified).
* **`debris`**: the withered residue of pasture.
  * **Rendering:** "light debris" (87:5).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[39:21]`, `[18:45]` ("debris, scattered by the wind"), `[57:20]` — consistently the this-worldly-life figure (rg-verified); "stubble" variant at `[10:24]`.
* **`purifies himself` (zakka nafsahu)**: the success verb of this surah.
  * **Rendering:** "Successful is he who purifies himself" (87:14).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[20:76]` "the reward for him who purifies himself"; `[91:9]` "Successful is he who purifies it" (soul-object); the prophetic purifier mission `[2:129]`, `[62:2]` (rg-verified).
