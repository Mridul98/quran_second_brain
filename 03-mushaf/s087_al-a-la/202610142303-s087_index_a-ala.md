## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Meccan — agreed in the classical type-lists; the doxology-then-reminder arc and the Abraham/Moses scroll-claim sit in the early Meccan register. *Source: classical type-lists — verify.* Revelation order: **transmitted nuzul tables — verify** (corpus states no nuzul; unnumbered Basmala heads the surah). Transmitted liturgical associations (recitation in witr and the eid prayer) are hadith-apparatus material, not in this translation — see §5b.
* **Size:** 19 ayahs, 1 ruku (ruku_map global 529 — verified). Second door of the 86–91 night-oath run; the batch's doxology-surah: it opens by commanding praise of a Name and closes by locating its message in the oldest scriptures.
* **Title:** al-A'la — "THE MOST HIGH" tracks `[87:1]` "Praise the Name of your Lord, the Most High"; the same glorification formula, with different divine epithets, runs `[56:74]` "your Great Lord," `[56:96]`/`[69:52]` "the Magnificent" (rg-verified) — a four-site tasbih cluster.
* **Central theme:** The Most High who orders everything — creates and regulates, measures and guides, sprouts pasture and dries it to debris (87:2–5) — secures His messenger's reading (87:6–8), limits the reminder's reach (87:9–13), and prices the two lives (87:14–17). The surah's frame-claim: this order-and-reminder message is not novel; it is "in the former scriptures. The Scriptures of Abraham and Moses" (87:18–19).
* **Reading hint:** follow the Name — the command falls on "the Name of your Lord" (87:1) and the success clause on him who "mentions the name of his Lord, and prays" (87:15): the whole surah is sandwiched by Name-mention, with the created order (87:2–5) as the evidence that the Name is high.

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Doxology (87:1–5):** command to glorify; three participial clauses (creates/regulates; measures/guides; produces pasture/turns it to debris) — the Most High profiled as orderer of creatures and of seasons.
* **Prophetic security (87:6–8):** "We will make you read, so do not forget. Except what Allah wills" — memory, revelation, and ease ("We will ease you into the Easy Way") handed down, not earned.
* **The reminder's reach (87:9–13):** remind *if it helps*; the reverent remember, the wretched avoid and enter the Gigantic Fire "Where he will neither die, nor live."
* **The two-life trade (87:14–17):** success to the purifier who prays; loss to those who "prefer the present life. Though the Hereafter is better, and more lasting."
* **Archive close (87:18–19):** "This is in the former scriptures. The Scriptures of Abraham and Moses." (*Source: structural observation on corpus renderings.*)

## 5b. Batch-Return Verification Flags (this index)
* **Hint renderings corrected:** "created-proportioned-destined-guided" → "He who **creates and regulates**. / He who **measures and guides**" (87:2–3) — no word "proportioned" here (that verb lands at `[91:7]` "proportioned it," rg-verified). "purifying and praying" ✓ (87:14–15). "the scrolls of Abraham and Moses" ✓ (87:19) — but **"the first and the last" is NOT in this corpus**: 87:18 reads "This is in the former scriptures," and that exact phrase-pair is matched only at `[20:133]` (rg-verified); a "first and last" descriptor is transmitted gloss — verify before use.
* **Hint REJECTED — doxology:** "Ever-Living and Sustainer doxology" appears nowhere in s087 (rg-verified). The corpus's Living/Eternal name-pair lives in other surahs: `[2:255]` "the Living, the Everlasting," `[3:2]`, `[20:111]` "the Living, the Eternal," `[25:58]` "the Living, the One who never dies," `[40:65]`. The recited glorification "Glory to my Lord the Most High" (tied to 87:1 in prayer practice) is transmitted liturgical report — *Source: transmitted reports — verify*; not quotation-material from this translation.
* **"pasture then black straw" →** "He who produces the pasture. / And then turns it into **light debris**" (87:4–5); "debris" is this edition's fixed rendering of the withered-crop word at `[39:21]`, `[18:45]`, `[57:20]` (rg-verified); "black straw" is a gloss, not the corpus.
* **Cross-ref hint CONFIRMED:** 87:12–13 ↔ `[20:74]` — the neither-dying-nor-living figure pairs 87:13 "Where he will neither die, nor live" with 20:74 "where he neither dies nor lives" (near-twin construction, tense differs; rg-verified — the "neither dies" form is corpus-single at 20:74); the wretched-shape of 87:11 pairs with `[91:12]` "its most wicked" in this batch (same root, rendered "wretched"/"most wicked" — rg-verified drift).
