# Fruits of various colors, the trade that will not fail, and the scream of the Fire (35:27–37)
**UID:** 202610141905
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 35:27–37 (Ruku 4 of 5 in Fatir)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[35:27]` – `[35:37]` (11 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The one water produces variegated fruits, variegated mountains, variegated creatures — and the one response that fits is the awe of the learned; from that awe flows the surah's economics: reciting, praying, and spending people expect a trade that will not fail, being paid in full plus increase by the Forgiving, Appreciative, while the Book they recite passes to a chosen people split three ways and ends in two scenes — the saved praising that sorrow has been lifted, and the ingrates screaming to be let out, answered by their own long-enough life.
* **Placement:** Ruku 4 of 5; the two-economies drama between the warner-ruku (15–26) and the closing audit (38–45). It converts 35:9/12's creation evidence into an epistemology (knowledge→fear, 28) and a commerce (29–30).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[35:27]`** "Have you not seen that Allah sends down water from the sky? With it We produce fruits of various colors. And in the mountains are streaks of white and red—varying in their hue—and pitch-black."
  * **Gloss:** The "Have you not seen" vision-formula; one water, many colors (*thulfan*). Ledger verification the task requested: the parable of "grapes, date-palms, pomegranates — similar and dissimilar — eat of its fruit... and give its due on the day of harvesting" is **not in Fatir** — it is `[6:141]` verbatim (with the growth-parable at `[6:99]`, both lines "similar and dissimilar"); Fatir's cognate is this colors-verse, with no harvest-dues command. The nearest color-parallels: `[13:4]` ("We make some taste better than others"), `[16:11]`, `[16:13]` ("of diverse colors... a sign for people who are mindful"). "Streaks" (patha'iju) is this edition's word, corpus-unique as a noun.
* **`[35:28]`** "Likewise, human beings, animals, and livestock come in various colors. From among His servants, the learned fear Allah. Allah is Almighty, Oft-Forgiving."
  * **Gloss:** Variegation culminates in the observers themselves; the epistemic chain is the surah's sharpest: colors → the learned (*ulama'*) → khashya (fear of Allah). "The learned" appears at `[29:43]` ("none grasps them except the learned"), and the knowledge-elevation pairing at `[58:11]`. Closing on "Almighty, Oft-Forgiving" pre-installs the Forgiving-Appreciative pair of 35:30/34 — power and pardon bracket the whole economics unit.
* **`[35:29]`** "Those who recite the Book of Allah, and perform the prayer, and spend of what We have provided for them, secretly and publicly, expect a trade that will not fail."
  * **Gloss:** The task's "those who read the Book... will never be at loss" ✓ in substance: "recite" for read (*yatluna*), and "a trade that will not fail" — the failure-word is *bukar* (losslessness); other editions' "never be at a loss." Four acts: recite, pray, spend of the provision, expect. Twins: `[2:274]` "spend their wealth by night and day, privately and publicly" (case-drift secretly/privately); `[14:31]` "give from what We have given them, secretly and publicly, before a Day comes in which there is neither trading nor friendship" — the trade negated on that Day vs the trade that survives it here; `[61:10]` "a trade that will save you from a painful torment." The provision-source clause ties back to 35:3's provider and 35:12's bounty. Note: the k-nifaq ledger line already cites 35:29 ✓ (verified in `02-knowledge/202610141300-...`).
* **`[35:30]`** "He will pay them their dues in full, and will increase them from His bounty. He is Forgiving and Appreciative."
  * **Gloss:** Full payment plus bounty-increase (the double payout; cf. `[64:17]` "He will multiply it for you, and will forgive you"); "Forgiving and Appreciative" (*ghafur shakur*) is a verbatim pair-twin at `[42:23]`, and Appreciative (*shakur*) recurs at `[2:158]`, `[4:147]`. Ledger: the task's "He is the Forgiving, the Grateful" is another edition's rendering — this corpus says "Appreciative," and the pair sits at 35:30/35:34, **not** at `[34:17]` (Saba's verse penalizes ingratitude: "Would We penalize any but the ungrateful?").
* **`[35:31]`** "What We inspired in you, of the Book, is the truth, confirming what preceded it. Allah is Well-Informed of His servants, All-Seeing."
  * **Gloss:** The kalam anchor: the inspiration (*wahy*) is named *haqq* and *musaddiq* — "confirming" is the fixed self-description of the Quran's relation to prior scripture (`[2:91]`, `[2:97]` Gabriel "revealed it to your heart... confirming what preceded it", `[2:101]`). "Well-Informed... All-Seeing" (Khabir–Basir) opens the knowledge bracket that closes at 35:38 and 35:45.
* **`[35:32]`** "Then We passed the Book to those of Our servants whom We chose. Some of them wrong their souls, and some follow a middle course, and some are in the foremost in good deeds by Allah’s leave; that is the greatest blessing."
  * **Gloss:** The Book is inherited (*awrathna* — cf. `[21:105]` "the earth will be inherited by My righteous servants") by a chosen community that is internally tripartite: soul-wrongers, middling, and the foremost (*sabiqun*) — by Allah's leave, not momentum. "The foremost" re-appears as "the forerunners, the forerunners... those are the nearest" at `[56:10–11]` — the same social structure of the saved, word-choice drift (foremost/forerunners). Source: transmitted reports divide this verse as a total self-description of the ummah with all three classes inside it (classical tafsir on 35:32 — agent labeling; verify in print).
* **`[35:33]`** "The Gardens of Eden, which they will enter. They will be adorned therein with gold bracelets and pearls, and their garments therein will be of silk."
  * **Gloss:** The adornment scene — near-verbatim twin of `[22:23]` ("decorated therein with bracelets of gold and pearls, and their garments therein will be of silk"); cf. `[18:31]` (gold bracelets, green silk and brocade), `[76:21]` (silver bracelets — material drift gold/silver across editions of the scene).
* **`[35:34]`** "And they will say, “Praise Allah, who has lifted all sorrow from us. Our Lord is Most Forgiving, Most Appreciative."
  * **Gloss:** The saved speak hamd — the surah's opening praise (35:1) returned from inside the reward; the *ghafur-shakur* pair restated superlatively. The typographic quote opened here stays open across 35:35 and closes there — book typography [sic], faithfully carried (see the edition-typography class in the Fleeting link).
* **`[35:35]`** "He Who settled us in the Home of Permanence, by His grace, where boredom will not touch us, and fatigue will not afflict us.”"
  * **Gloss:** "Home of Permanence" (*dar al-qarar*) is verbatim-twin of `[40:39]` "the Hereafter is the Home of Permanence"; the fatigue-word (*nasab*) has one striking counter-use: `[50:38]` "no fatigue touched Us" — the created rest because the Creator did not tire. Boredom + fatigue = the two exhaustions of *this* life, banned by name.
* **`[35:36]`** "As for those who disbelieve, for them is the Fire of Hell, wherein they will never be finished off and die, nor will its punishment be lightened for them. Thus We will repay every ingrate."
  * **Gloss:** The counter-scene in mirror structure: no dying and no relief. The "never be finished off" clause is corpus-unique at this wording (verified by `rg "finished off"`), while the "not lightened" formula is attested elsewhere (`[16:85]` "it will not be lightened for them"; `[2:86]` "the punishment will not be lightened for them") — Fatir stacks both. "Every ingrate" (*kaffar*) is the shukr-audit's hard case — cf. `[2:276]` "no love for every sinful ingrate" and the previous surah's verdict `[34:17]` "Would We penalize any but the ungrateful?"
* **`[35:37]`** "And they will scream therein, “Our Lord, let us out, and we will act righteously, differently from the way we used to act.” Did We not give you a life long enough, in which anyone who wanted to understand would have understood? And the warner did come to you. So taste. The evildoers will have no helper."
  * **Gloss:** The lifetime-regret scene ✓ verbatim (task): the scream, the re-offer ("we will act righteously, differently"), and the two-part refusal — the verse glosses itself ("a life long enough, in which anyone who wanted to understand would have understood"; the underlying word is the "age/lifespan" noun, classically tied to the age of full reasoning — agent labeling; verify) and the warner's actual arrival: the claims of 35:23–24 become exhibit A. "The evildoers will have no helper" — verbatim family at `[3:192]`, `[2:270]`; `[17:69]` even pairs no-helper with drowning "for your ingratitude" (the shukr ledger again). The quote opens and closes within the line ✓.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`ulama' + khashya` (the learned fear)** `[35:28]`:
  * **Rendering:** "From among His servants, the learned fear Allah" — reverent, status-aware fear (servants→Lord); *khashya* is the classical term here (lexical note — agent labeling, verify in print, since the corpus is English-only).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[21:49]` "fear their Lord in private" (the inward-fear class 35:18 warns); `[29:43]` "none grasps them except the learned"; `[58:11]` Allah elevates those given knowledge.
* **`tijara` (trade)** `[35:29]`:
  * **Rendering:** "expect a trade that will not fail" — one of the Quran's two positive trade valuations: `[61:10]` "a trade that will save you from a painful torment" is its sister; the failure-word here is losslessness itself.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:16]` "their trade does not profit them" — the losing position (the nifaq note's ledger); `[14:31]` no "trading" on the Day — the market's suspension is what makes 35:29's survival claim count.
* **`shakur` (Appreciative)** `[35:30]`/`[35:34]`:
  * **Rendering:** "Forgiving and Appreciative" (35:30, 42:23 verbatim pair); superlative in the saved's speech (35:34).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:158]` "Allah is Appreciative, Well-Informed"; `[4:147]` "Allah is Appreciative, All-Knowing"; `[64:17]` "Allah is Appreciative and Forbearing" — the divine name that rewards the smallest circulation of thanks.
* **`kaffar` (ingrate)** `[35:36]`:
  * **Rendering:** "Thus We will repay every ingrate" — kufr at its root meaning, ungratitude, in an eschatological verdict.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:276]` "every sinful ingrate"; `[34:17]` the ungrateful penalized; `[17:69]` drowning "for your ingratitude" — the shukr-note's audit-failure class, with 35:37 its courtroom transcript.
