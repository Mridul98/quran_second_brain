# Successors, the partner-nullity, the heavens held up, and the deferral (35:38–45)
**UID:** 202610141906
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 35:38–45 (Ruku 5 of 5 in Fatir)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[35:38]` – `[35:45]` (8 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The closing audit: the God who knows the unseen and the hearts installed humanity as successors on earth, so disbelief recoils on its owner alone; the partners are re-examined and found empty (no creation, no share, no book); the cosmos itself is held together only by Him — which is exactly why the oath-breakers' patterned doom and the deferred punishment are not mercy's absence but its stated-term administration.
* **Placement:** Ruku 5 of 5; the surah's verdict column. It redeploys the ruku-1 vocabulary (knowledge 35:38 ↔ 35:2; the partners interrogation 35:40 ↔ 35:3/13–14) and closes on the *ajal* word first sounded at 35:13 — the sun, the moon, and now the nations all run to a stated term, watched by the Observant.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[35:38]`** "Allah is the Knower of the future of the heavens and the earth. He knows what the hearts contain."
  * **Gloss:** The omniscience premise, in two registers: cosmic unseen and interior hearts. Note the edition's word choice — *ghayb* rendered "the future" here, against its standard "unseen" (`[2:3]`, `[34:3]`); the same "future" rendering sits in the messenger's disclaimer `[6:50]` "nor do I know the future" — the disclaimer here becomes the claim, for Allah alone. Verbatim twin of the hearts-clause at `[39:7]` ("He is aware of what the hearts contain").
* **`[35:39]`** "It is He who made you successors on earth. Whoever disbelieves, his disbelief will recoil upon him. The disbelief of the disbelievers adds only to their Lord's disfavor of them. The disbelief of the disbelievers adds only to their perdition."
  * **Gloss:** Successorship (`[2:30]` "I am placing a successor on earth"; near-verbatim `[10:14]` "Then We made you successors on earth after them, to see how you would behave") means the office survives the occupant. The triple "adds only to" formula privatizes the damage of disbelief — recoil, *sukht* (rendered "disfavor", this edition's unique wording; the concept-word elsewhere is "wrath", `[6:147]`, `[20:81]`), and *tabab* "perdition" (twins `[6:70]`, `[47:8]`, `[71:28]`).
* **`[35:40]`** "Say, “Have you considered those partners of yours that you worship instead of Allah? Show me what they have created on earth. Or do they have any share in the heavens?” Or have We given them a book whose clear teachings they follow? In fact, the wrongdoers promise one another nothing but delusions."
  * **Gloss:** The tawhid reductio in three tests — creation, co-ownership, credentials — near-verbatim twin of `[46:4]` ("Show me which portion of the earth they have created. Or do they own a share of the heavens? Bring me a scripture prior to this one"), and the previous surah's zero-portfolio verse `[34:22]`; cf. `[7:191]` "Do they idolize those who create nothing, and are themselves created?" and `[17:56]` (they "have no power to relieve your adversity"). "Clear teachings" (*bayyinat*) ties back to 35:25; the verdict "nothing but delusions" (*ghurur*) names the same commodity the Tempter traded at 35:5 — word-choice drift: "the Tempter" there, "delusions" here, same root.
* **`[35:41]`** "Allah holds the heavens and the earth, lest they fall apart. And were they to fall apart, there is none to hold them together except He. He is Most Clement, Most Forgiving."
  * **Gloss:** The cosmological proof stated as ongoing maintenance: the "fall apart" (task's "lest they slip" — this edition's words are "fall apart"; corpus-unique phrase) phrasing answers the mockers' security-in-idols fantasy with the fact that even the sky's coherence is His single-handed work. Near-twin: `[22:65]` "That He holds up the sky lest it falls on earth—except by His permission". The closing names — Clement (*ra'uf*; elsewhere "Clement" at `[24:20]`, `[2:263]`, `[39:53]`) and Forgiving — are the two reasons 35:45's deferral exists.
* **`[35:42]`** "And they swore by Allah with their solemn oaths, that if a warner came to them, they would be more guided than any other people. Yet when a warner came to them, it only increased them in aversion."
  * **Gloss:** The oath-scene: sworn pre-commitment, revoked on delivery. Same formula at `[6:109]` ("They swear by Allah, with their most solemn oaths, that if a miracle were to come to them, they would believe in it... Even if it did come, they still would not believe"); "more guided" is a corpus-unique comparative (verified by search), and the word "warner" is now the arriving one — 35:23–24 has come to their door (the task's theme-check: the surah's warner-universalism becomes this scene's evidence, as at 35:37).
* **`[35:43]`** "Priding themselves on earth, and scheming evil. But evil scheming overwhelms none but its authors. Do they expect anything but the precedent of the ancients? You will not find any change in Allah’s practice, and you will not find any substitute to Allah’s practice."
  * **Gloss:** The twin disease (arrogance + scheme) and its self-imploding rule — verbatim intra-surah echo of 35:10 ("the planning of these will fail"). Then the *sunnah* declaration: "Allah's practice" admits no change and no substitute — twins `[33:62]` ("no change in Allah's system"), `[48:23]` ("It is Allah's pattern, ongoing since the past... no change"), `[17:77]` ("tradition of the messengers... no change in Our rules").
* **`[35:44]`** "Have they not journeyed in the land and observed the fate of those who preceded them? They were superior to them in strength. But nothing can defeat Allah in the heavens or on Earth. He is indeed Omniscient and Omnipotent."
  * **Gloss:** The travel-audience argument (cf. `[40:82]` "Have they not journeyed through the land, and seen the outcome for those before them?... greater power", `[30:9]`, `[16:36]`): superior strength is the control variable — if they were destroyed, the equation is scale-independent. "Omniscient and Omnipotent" — the exact phrase-twin is `[16:70]` (two corpus occurrences — verified). Note "Earth" capitalized in this line [sic — book typography].
* **`[35:45]`** "If Allah were to punish the people for what they have earned, He would not leave a single living creature on its surface. But He defers them until a stated time. Then, when their time has arrived—Allah is Observant of His creatures."
  * **Gloss:** The mercy of deferral, stated as restraint: total accounting would empty the earth of every crawling thing — near-verbatim twin `[16:61]` ("He would not leave upon it a single creature, but He postpones them until an appointed time... when their time arrives, they will not delay it by one hour"). The "stated time" is the same *ajal* word as 35:13's sun and moon — cosmology and history run on one deadline schedule. The final word "Observant" (*basir*) brackets 35:31's "All-Seeing" and 35:38's heart-knowledge: the surah ends with watchful eyes, not a closed door.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`ghayb`**: the hidden — rendered "the future" at `[35:38]` ("Knower of the future") and at `[6:50]`, `[27:65]` (both "knows the future"-type clauses); standard rendering "unseen" at `[2:3]`, `[34:3]`.
  * **Rendering:** drift observable within the same edition; "future" sharpens the 35:38→35:45 deadline frame.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:3]` belief's first object; `[34:3]` the previous surah's atom-weight clause — the two neighboring surahs hand the title back and forth.
* **`khalifa` (successor)**: one installed after a predecessor.
  * **Rendering:** "successors on earth" `[35:39]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:30]` the angelic question at the first appointment; `[10:14]` near-verbatim twin with the test-clause ("to see how you would behave") — successorship is staged audit, the shukr-note's grammar.
* **`sunnah` (practice/pattern)**: the invariant divine procedure.
  * **Rendering:** "Allah's practice... no change... no substitute" `[35:43]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[33:62]`, `[48:23]`, `[17:77]` — the word's Quranic use is almost exclusively this warning-idiom: history has a rule, and the rule is not negotiable.
* **`ajal` (stated term)**: the fixed deadline.
  * **Rendering:** "each running for a stated term" `[35:13]`; "defers them until a stated time" `[35:45]`; also `[35:11]`'s lifespan in a Record.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[31:29]` verbatim twin for sun/moon; `[16:61]` "appointed time" for nations; `[71:4]`, `[11:3]` Noah's and Hud's sermons sell the same respite — one word governing astronomy, biography, and history.
