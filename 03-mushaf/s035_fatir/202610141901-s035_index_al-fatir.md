## 3. Structural Arc
* **Sovereignty (1–7):** hamd to the Originator whose adding-to-creation reaches even angel-wings; mercy's faucet in His hand alone; "O people!" interrogation #1 (creator/provider) → #2 (the promise, the Tempter) → the two classes. Consolation for the rejected messenger (4) brackets the sequence.
* **Evidence against the self-deceived (8–14):** the inner parable (8), the outer ones — wind, cloud, dead land, two seas, merged night and day (9, 12, 13) — and the court record: speech ascends, deeds elevate, plots perish (10, 11); the invoked gods fail hearing, answering, and disavow partnership on the Day (13b–14).
* **The human position (15–26):** "O people!" #3 — you are the poor; Allah the Rich — the no-burden-transfer axiom, the four unequals, and the warner's job description (not a hearer-forcer): "There is no community but a warner has passed through it" (24), sealed with the former-nations precedent (25–26).
* **The two economies of the Book [An Naml 37](../s027_an-naml/202610141761-s027_index_an-naml.md):** variegated creation produces awe in the learned (27–28); reciting–praying–spending people expect a trade that will not fail and get dues-plus-increase from the Forgiving, Appreciative (29–30); the Book is inherited by a chosen people split three ways (31–32); the saved praise with sorrow lifted, in the Home of Permanence (33–35); the ingrate scream to be returned, and are answered by their own long enough life (36–37).
* **The closing audit (38–45):** heart-knowledge (38), successorship whose disbelief recoils (39), the partner reductio (40), the cosmos held lest it slip (41), the broken oath and the immutable pattern (42–43), the journeyed-to fate (44), and the deferral — "when their time has arrived—Allah is Observant of His creatures" (45).
