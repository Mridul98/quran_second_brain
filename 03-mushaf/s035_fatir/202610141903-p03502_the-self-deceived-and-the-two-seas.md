# The self-deceived, the ascending pure word, and the speck-owning gods (35:8–14)
**UID:** 202610141903
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 35:8–14 (Ruku 2 of 5 in Fatir)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[35:8]` – `[35:14]` (7 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Creation as evidence against the man whose evil looks good to him: guidance and misguidance are Allah's to give (8), so the wind-driven rain that revives dead ground (9), the ascending pure speech and elevated deeds (10), the dust-to-Record biography of every person (11), the two unequal seas yielding one bounty (12), and the merged night and day running to a stated term (13) all testify that the beings people invoke own not even a speck and will disown their worshippers on the Day (14).
* **Placement:** Ruku 2 of 5; the surah's evidence block. The night-day rotation the task flagged is here (`[35:13]`), not in the 19–23 parable pair. Its closing "None informs you like an Expert" hands off to the middle ruku's interrogation of need.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[35:8]`** "What of him whose evil deed was made attractive to him, and so he regards it as good? Allah leads astray whomever He wills, and He guides whomever He wills. Therefore, do not waste yourself sorrowing over them. Allah knows exactly what they do."
  * **Gloss:** The self-deceiver — inner parable opening the evidence block (cf. `[6:122]` "the doings of disbelievers are made to appear good to them"). The will-and-guidance clause is the guidance-note's divine-sovereignty leg verbatim; "do not waste yourself sorrowing" is the parallel idiom of `[18:6]` "Perhaps you may destroy yourself with grief" — English idiom-twins; the Arabic verbs differ (*ta'maq* vs *tubqi* — classical lexical note, agent labeling; verify).
* **`[35:9]`** "Allah is He who sends the winds, which agitate clouds, which We drive to a dead land, and thereby revive the ground after it had died. Likewise is the Resurrection."
  * **Gloss:** A weather report converted into an eschatological proof; the chain wind→cloud→dead land→revival verbatim-twins `[30:24]` ("with it He revives the earth after it was dead") and `[2:164]`, and the programmatic "to revive dead lands thereby" of `[25:49]`. "Likewise is the Resurrection" makes the hydrological cycle an exemplum, not a nature poem.
* **`[35:10]`** "Whoever desires honor—all honor belongs to Allah. To Him ascends speech that is pure, and He elevates righteous conduct. As for those who plot evil, a terrible punishment awaits them, and the planning of these will fail."
  * **Gloss:** Honor's ownership answers the seeker of status through tribe or god. "All honor belongs to Allah" is verbatim-twin of `[4:139]` "All glory belongs to Allah" (*'izza*, rendered honor/glory across the two — observable drift). Pure *speech* ascends to Him and righteous *conduct* He raises: the good-word-as-living-tree parable of `[14:24–26]` is the kalam-note's cognate, and the elevation of believers-and-learned echoes `[58:11]` / `[12:76]`. Plotting boomerangs — the theme 35:43 restates verbatim in kind ("evil scheming overwhelms none but its authors").
* **`[35:11]`** "Allah created you from dust, then from a small drop; then He made you pairs. No female conceives, or delivers, except with His knowledge. No living thing advances in years, or its life is shortened, except it be in a Record. That is surely easy for Allah."
  * **Gloss:** Biographies are recorded: the creation sequence verbatim-twins `[22:5]` ("We created you from dust, then from a small drop..."), the pairs of `[53:45]`, and the conception/delivery clause is near-verbatim at `[41:47]`; lengthening and shortening life in a Record belongs with `[57:22]` ("it is in a Book, even before We make it happen") and `[13:8]` ("everything is by measure"). "That is surely easy for Allah" is the fixed refrain (cf. `[22:70]`, `[4:30]`).
* **`[35:12]`** "The two seas are not the same. One is fresh, sweet, good to drink, while the other is salty and bitter. Yet from each you eat tender meat, and extract jewelry which you wear. And you see the ships plowing through them, so that you may seek of His bounty, so that you may give thanks."
  * **Gloss:** The two-seas parable — verbatim-twin of `[25:53]` (fresh/sweet vs salty/bitter, a barrier between) and `[55:19–22]` (two seas meeting; pearls and coral emerge), with the bounty-from-both motif from `[16:14]` ("tender meat... ornaments... ships plowing"). Note the drift: 35:12 "jewelry" vs 16:14 "ornaments" for the same *libas*; both end so that you may seek His bounty / give thanks — the shukr anchor of the surah.
* **`[35:13]`** "He merges the night into the day, and He merges the day into the night; and He regulates the sun and the moon, each running for a stated term. Such is Allah, your Lord; His is the sovereignty. As for those you call upon besides Him, they do not possess a speck."
  * **Gloss:** The night-day rotation the task flagged (here, not at 19–23): verbatim twin of `[31:29]` ("each running for a stated term"), cognate of `[39:5]` "He wraps the night around the day." Sovereignty is then handed to the sole owner, and the called-upon gods shrink to "not possess a speck" (*qitmir* — the date-stone's slit; cf. `[4:53]` "not give people a speck"; and the previous surah's atom-weight clause `[34:22]`).
* **`[35:14]`** "If you pray to them, they cannot hear your prayer. And even if they heard, they would not answer you. And on the Day of Resurrection, they will reject your partnership. None informs you like an Expert."
  * **Gloss:** The gods' three failures: no hearing, no answering, no loyalty on the Day — the near-verbatim twin is `[46:5–6]` ("those who will not answer him until the Day of Resurrection... they will be enemies to them, and will renounce their worship"). The closing *khabir* formula ("None informs you like an Expert") names the counter-witness: the Expert who knows 35:13's ownership down to the speck.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`'izza` (honor/glory)** `[35:10]`:
  * **Rendering:** "Whoever desires honor—all honor belongs to Allah."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[4:139]` the same totalizing clause as "glory" — the drift matters: the nifaq-note's hypocrites seek 'izz from the wrong quarter, and 35:10 tells them the currency's only issuer. The clause *kull al-'izzati lillah* occurs exactly twice in the corpus (verified by `rg "honor belongs|glory belongs"`): here and 4:139.
* **`qitmir` (speck)** `[35:13]`:
  * **Rendering:** "they do not possess a speck."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[4:53]` "would not give people a speck" — identical English rendering, but 4:53's Arabic is the *naqir* (classical lexical note — agent labeling; verify), so this is a translation convergence, not a repeated word; the zero-ownership scale continues at `[34:22]` ("not an atom's weight... no share").
* **`shakir/tashakkur` axis — "give thanks"** `[35:12]`:
  * **Rendering:** closing purpose clause "so that you may give thanks," paired with 16:14's identical teleology.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[4:147]` "if you have given thanks, and have believed? Allah is Appreciative"; the shukr audit's bounty-then-thanks grammar runs the whole surah (35:3, 12, 29–30, 34, 36).
* **`khabir` (Expert)** `[35:14]`:
  * **Rendering:** "None informs you like an Expert."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[34:1]` "the Wise, the Expert"; `[6:18]`/`[6:73]`; `[35:31]` within this surah — the knowledge-attribute that makes the speck-clause auditable.
