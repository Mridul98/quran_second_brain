# The hushed summons, the Throne-verse echoes, and Adam's covenant (20:105–115)
**UID:** 202610141667
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 20:105–115 (Ruku 6 of 8 in Ta-Ha)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** ``[20:105]`` – ``[20:115]`` (11 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Mountains to level waste, the summons obeyed without deviation, voices hushed before the Merciful: the scene narrows to two lines that re-quote the Throne-verse almost verbatim (intercession by permission; knowledge before-and-behind), then the Book shows its own credentials — “an Arabic Quran… diversified the warnings” — and the prophet is told not to be hasty, but to pray for more knowledge; and the ruku ends on the surah's whole anthropology in one epitaph: “We covenanted with Adam before, but he forgot, and We found in him no resolve.”
* **Placement:** Ruku 6 of 8; opens on the Meccan interrogation motif (“they ask you about the mountains”), closes on 20:115 — the seed-verse of the Adam drama that ruku 7 unpacks.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[20:105]`** And they ask you about the mountains. Say, “My Lord will crumble them utterly.”
  * **Gloss:** The mountains question answered: “My Lord will crumble them utterly” — cf. [56:5] “crushed and crumbled,” [19:90] “fall and crumble” (both verified).
* **`[20:106]`** And leave them desolate waste.
  * **Gloss:** “desolate waste.” — the one-word aftermath, flat as the terrain it describes.
* **`[20:107]`** You will see in them neither crookedness, nor deviation.”
  * **Gloss:** “neither crookedness, nor deviation.” — the plain; the deviation-word returns at once in the next line's obedience.
* **`[20:108]`** On that Day, they will follow the caller, without any deviation. Voices will be hushed before the Merciful, and you will hear nothing but murmur.
  * **Gloss:** All follow the caller “without any deviation”; voices hushed before *the Merciful* — the judgment name chosen is mercy (cf. [50:41] “the caller calls from a nearby place,” verified) — and only a “murmur” is heard.
* **`[20:109]`** On that Day, intercession will not avail, except for him permitted by the Merciful, and whose words He has approved.
  * **Gloss:** Intercession void unless double-qualified: permitted by the Merciful, and his words approved — the exact grammar of [2:255] “Who is he that can intercede with Him except with His permission?” and [53:26] “permission to whomever He wills, and approves” (both verified).
* **`[20:110]`** He knows what is before them and what is behind them, and they cannot comprehend Him in their knowledge.
  * **Gloss:** “He knows what is before them and what is behind them” — Throne-verse language re-imported ([2:255] carries the same clause with its comma [sic]); humans “cannot comprehend Him in their knowledge.”
* **`[20:111]`** Faces will be humbled before the Living, the Eternal. Whoever carries injustice will despair.
  * **Gloss:** Faces humbled before “the Living, the Eternal” — [2:255]'s pair rendered “the Living, the Everlasting” there (observable drift, verified); the injustice-carrier despairs.
* **`[20:112]`** But whoever has done righteous deeds, while being a believer—will fear neither injustice, nor grievance.
  * **Gloss:** The ballot's second printing: “whoever has done righteous deeds, while being a believer—will fear neither injustice, nor grievance” — the 20:74–75 formula re-cut with a security clause: the surah says its verdict twice.
* **`[20:113]`** Thus We have revealed it an Arabic Quran, and We have diversified the warnings in it, that perhaps they would become righteous, or it may produce a lesson for them.
  * **Gloss:** The medium claim: “an Arabic Quran… diversified the warnings” — same phrase at [39:28] (“An Arabic Quran, without any defect”) and [43:3] (both verified); the k-kalam note already cites this line — dedup confirmed, link, do not re-create.
* **`[20:114]`** Exalted is Allah, the True King. Do not be hasty with the Quran before its inspiration to you is concluded, and say, “My Lord, increase me in knowledge.”
  * **Gloss:** Recitation etiquette: no haste “before its inspiration to you is concluded” (the twin scene at [75:16–17] — reference, wording unchecked), and the prophet's only recorded petition for increment: “My Lord, increase me in knowledge” — “increase me” occurs nowhere else in the corpus (verified).
* **`[20:115]`** And We covenanted with Adam before, but he forgot, and We found in him no resolve.
  * **Gloss:** The bridge-verse — verified as 20:115: “And We covenanted with Adam before, but he forgot, and We found in him no resolve.” — the task's wording checked (“We found in him no resolve”); twin of [2:37–38] (received words; guidance clause); set against [20:52] “nor does He forget”: the God who never forgets covenanting with the man who did.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`shafā'a` (intercession)** [20:109]
  * **Rendering:** negated universally, then unlocked by two conditions (permission + approved words).
  * **Elsewhere:** [2:255], [53:26] (both verified) — the permission-grammar is stable across the corpus; Ta-Ha keeps the Merciful's name in both clauses.
* **“the Living, the Eternal”** [20:111]
  * **Elsewhere:** [2:255] “the Living, the Everlasting” — same pair, second English word differs (observable drift, per the artifacts note's class-2 caveat).
* **“an Arabic Quran”** [20:113]
  * **Elsewhere:** [39:28], [43:3] (verified); already anchored inside the k-kalam note — this passage links to it instead of duplicating.
* **“increase me in knowledge”** [20:114]
  * **Rendering:** unique in the corpus (verified) — the Book's own etiquette: reception before recitation.
