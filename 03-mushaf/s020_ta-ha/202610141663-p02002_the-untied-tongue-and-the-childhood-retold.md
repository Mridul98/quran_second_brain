# The untied tongue, the childhood retold, and Pharaoh's question (20:25–54)
**UID:** 202610141663
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 20:25–54 (Ruku 2 of 8 in Ta-Ha)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** ``[20:25]`` – ``[20:54]`` (30 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The mission produces prayer, not confidence: Moses asks for heart, task, tongue, and a colleague — and God answers with a résumé of favors (basket, sister, mother, a killing survived, years in Median), then sends the brothers to Pharaoh with soft words; the whole royal interrogation “Who is your Lord?” ends in a sermon on design, guidance, and pasture.
* **Placement:** Ruku 2 of 8; completes the commissioning (25–44) and stages the first audience (45–54), handing the sign-contest to ruku 3.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[20:25]`** He said, “My Lord, put my heart at peace for me.  **`[20:26]`** And ease my task for me.  **`[20:27]`** And untie the knot from my tongue.  **`[20:28]`** So they can understand my speech.
  * **Gloss:** The prophet's four-clause prayer: heart at ease, task eased, “untie the knot from my tongue,” comprehension — the speech-difficulty is the text's own datum; the stutter as cause is a classical gloss. *Source: consensus of classical tafsir — verify.*
* **`[20:29]`** And appoint an assistant for me, from my family.  **`[20:30]`** Aaron, my brother.  **`[20:31]`** Strengthen me with him.  **`[20:32]`** And have him share in my mission.
  * **Gloss:** “Appoint an assistant for me, from my family. Aaron, my brother” — the request names the answer; twin at [25:35] “appointed his brother Aaron as his assistant” (verified wording).
* **`[20:33]`** That we may glorify You much.  **`[20:34]`** And remember You much.  **`[20:35]`** You are always watching over us.”
  * **Gloss:** Purpose clauses: glorify much, remember much — the mission's liturgy before its logistics; “You are always watching over us.” closes on being-seen.
* **`[20:36]`** He said, “You are granted your request, O Moses.  **`[20:37]`** We had favored you another time.
  * **Gloss:** “You are granted your request” — petition answered on the spot — and the pivot to memory: “We had favored you another time.”
* **`[20:38]`** When We inspired your mother with the inspiration.
  * **Gloss:** “When We inspired your mother with the inspiration” — wahy to a woman; the Qasas twin [28:7] “We inspired the mother of Moses: ‘Nurse him…’” (verified).
* **`[20:39]`** `Put him in the chest; then cast him into the river. The river will wash him to shore, where an enemy of Mine and an enemy of his will pick him up. And I have bestowed upon you love from Me, so that you may be reared before My eye.
  * **Gloss:** Chest → river → “an enemy of Mine and an enemy of his” → “reared before My eye” — providence staged through the enemy's own house.
* **`[20:40]`** When your sister walked along, and said, ‘Shall I tell you about someone who will take care of him?' So We returned you to your mother, that she may be comforted, and not sorrow. And you killed a person, but We saved you from stress; and We tested you thoroughly. And you stayed years among the people of Median. Then you came back, as ordained, O Moses.
  * **Gloss:** The whole résumé in one verse: sister's scout, return to mother (comforted, “not sorrow”), the killing and rescue “from stress,” being “tested… thoroughly,” “years among the people of Median” (Itani's spelling of Madyan, corpus-wide), and “Then you came back, as ordained” — the itinerary as appointment.
* **`[20:41]`** And I made you for Myself.  **`[20:42]`** Go, you and your brother, with My signs, and do not neglect My remembrance.
  * **Gloss:** “And I made you for Myself.” — the hardest line of election in the surah; then the double send with signs, bracketed by “do not neglect My remembrance” (pairing with [20:14]'s prayer-for-remembrance).
* **`[20:43]`** Go to Pharaoh. He has tyrannized.  **`[20:44]`** But speak to him nicely. Perhaps he will remember, or have some fear.”
  * **Gloss:** “Go to Pharaoh. He has tyrannized. But speak to him nicely. Perhaps he will remember, or have some fear.” — soft-word diplomacy to the tyrant; the capital He [sic] as at 20:24.
* **`[20:45]`** They said, “Lord, we fear he may persecute us, or become violent.”  **`[20:46]`** He said, “Do not fear, I am with you, I hear and I see.
  * **Gloss:** The brothers' honest fear of violence; the divine answer is presence: “I am with you, I hear and I see.”
* **`[20:47]`** Approach him and say, `We are the messengers of your Lord; so let the Children of Israel go with us, and do not torment them. We bring you a sign from your Lord, and peace be upon him who follows guidance.  **`[20:48]`** It was revealed to us that the punishment falls upon him who disbelieves and turns away.'“
  * **Gloss:** The scripted speech: let Israel go, no torment, “peace be upon him who follows guidance” — the phrase occurs nowhere else in the corpus (verified) — and the revealed warning on whoever disbelieves and turns away.
* **`[20:49]`** He said, “Who is your Lord, O Moses.”  **`[20:50]`** He said, “Our Lord is He who gave everything its existence, then guided it.”
  * **Gloss:** Pharaoh's one question — “Who is your Lord, O Moses” (dangling quote [sic]) — and the answer that does not name a tribe: “Our Lord is He who gave everything its existence, then guided it” — twin idiom [87:2–3] “He who creates and regulates… He who measures and guides” (verified).
* **`[20:51]`** He said, “What about the first generations?”  **`[20:52]`** He said, “Knowledge thereof is with my Lord, in a Book. My Lord never errs, nor does He forget.”
  * **Gloss:** “What about the first generations?” — the theodicy question — answered: knowledge “in a Book. My Lord never errs, nor does He forget.” — the adjacent-surah echo [19:64] “Your Lord is never forgetful” (verified); the anti-forgetfulness line the surah will set against Adam, who forgot ([20:115]).
* **`[20:53]`** He who made the earth a habitat for you; and traced in it routes for you; and sent down water from the sky, with which We produce pairs of diverse plants.
  * **Gloss:** The habitat sermon: earth spread, routes traced, water sent, “pairs of diverse plants” — provision as proof.
* **`[20:54]`** Eat and pasture your livestock. In that are signs for those with understanding.
  * **Gloss:** “Eat and pasture your livestock. In that are signs for those with understanding.” — the inventory ends where the shukr note says inventories must end: with use and discernment.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`wazir` (assistant)** [20:29]
  * **Rendering:** “appoint an assistant for me, from my family”; the same request as completed fact at [25:35] “appointed his brother Aaron as his assistant.”
  * **Elsewhere:** [20:42] then sends *both*: “Go, you and your brother” — the granted petition reshapes the mission.
* **`wahy` to the mother** [20:38]
  * **Rendering:** “We inspired your mother with the inspiration” — cognate accusative made literal.
  * **Elsewhere:** [28:7] verbatim twin (“We inspired the mother of Moses”); cf. k-kalam note: inspiration as speech-event.
* **design-guidance** [20:50]
  * **Rendering:** “He who gave everything its existence, then guided it.”
  * **Elsewhere:** [87:2–3] “He who creates and regulates… measures and guides” — the same doxology, Itani's wording split across two verses (verified).
* **`la yansā` (never forgetful)** [20:52]
  * **Elsewhere:** [19:64] “Your Lord is never forgetful” (verified) — and the surah's own forgetting-thread: [20:88] (people claim Moses' God forgot), [20:115] (Adam forgot), [20:126] (you will be forgotten as you forgot).
