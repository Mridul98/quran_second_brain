# The appointed day, the magicians' prostration, and the two ballots (20:55–76)
**UID:** 202610141664
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 20:55–76 (Ruku 3 of 8 in Ta-Ha)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** ``[20:55]`` – ``[20:76]`` (22 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Resurrection asserted as geology (“From it We created you…”) grounds the showdown: Pharaoh re-frames revelation as eviction-by-magic, the duel is fixed at festival noon, the conjured ropes make Moses falter — and the contest's losers become its first believers, prostrate under a mutilation threat; the ruku closes with the surah's ballot-box: the guilty, and “whoever comes to Him a believer, having worked righteousness—these will have the highest ranks.”
* **Placement:** Ruku 3 of 8; the miracle-contest and the first conversion inside the narrative; verses 74–76 are a hinge the surah returns to verbatim-sister at 20:112 (ruku 6). Correction for the record: the “high ranks” line is [20:75], the close of the contest — NOT the surah's closing (that is [20:135]).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[20:55]`** From it We created you, and into it We will return you, and from it We will bring you out another time.
  * **Gloss:** Earth's three-stage claim — made from it, returned into it, brought out again — resurrection stated as soil; the warrant under everything that follows.
* **`[20:56]`** We showed him Our signs, all of them, but he denied and refused.  **`[20:57]`** He said, “Did you come to us to drive us out of our land with your magic, O Moses?  **`[20:58]`** We will produce for you magic like it; so make an appointment between us and you, which we will not miss—neither us, nor you—in a central place.”
  * **Gloss:** Signs shown entire; denial entire. Pharaoh's counter-narrative — “to drive us out of our land with your magic” — and his own appointment: “which we will not miss—neither us, nor you—in a central place” (the phrase will boomerang at 20:97).
* **`[20:59]`** He said, “Your appointment is the day of the festival, so let the people be gathered together at mid-morning.”
  * **Gloss:** Moses sets the terms: “the day of the festival… at mid-morning” — the public square as theater; the day the people rest is the day the argument is staged.
* **`[20:60]`** Pharaoh turned away, put together his plan, and then came back.
  * **Gloss:** One-verse montage of state mobilization: “Pharaoh turned away, put together his plan, and then came back.”
* **`[20:61]`** Moses said to them, “Woe to you. Do not fabricate lies against Allah, or He will destroy you with a punishment. He who invents lies will fail.”  **`[20:62]`** They disagreed among themselves over their affair, and conferred secretly.
  * **Gloss:** Moses' warning to the magicians: “He who invents lies will fail” (cf. [10:69] “Those who fabricate lies about Allah will not succeed,” verified); they confer in secret.
* **`[20:63]`** They said, “These two are magicians who want to drive you out of your land with their magic, and to abolish your exemplary way of life.  **`[20:64]`** So settle your plan, and come as one front. Today, whoever gains the upper hand will succeed.”
  * **Gloss:** Their read: two magicians, one cultural eviction, and a merger: “settle your plan, and come as one front. Today, whoever gains the upper hand will succeed.”
* **`[20:65]`** They said, “O Moses, either you throw, or we will be the first to throw.”
  * **Gloss:** Courteous opening move: “either you throw, or we will be the first to throw.”
* **`[20:66]`** He said, “You throw.” And suddenly, their ropes and sticks appeared to him, because of their magic, to be crawling swiftly.  **`[20:67]`** So Moses felt apprehensive within himself.
  * **Gloss:** “You throw.” And the ropes and sticks — [26:44] “their ropes and their sticks” is the same kit (verified) — crawl, “because of their magic,” and “Moses felt apprehensive within himself”: the prophet's fear, unedited.
* **`[20:68]`** We said, “Do not be afraid, you are the uppermost.  **`[20:69]`** Now throw down what is in your right hand—it will swallow what they have crafted. What they have crafted is only a magician’s trickery. But the magician will not succeed, no matter what he does.”
  * **Gloss:** Revelation reassures mid-frame: “you are the uppermost”; the staff swallows the conjuring; “the magician will not succeed, no matter what he does” — the axiom of 61 restated as verdict.
* **`[20:70]`** And the magicians fell down prostrate. They said, “We have believed in the Lord of Aaron and Moses.”
  * **Gloss:** “And the magicians fell down prostrate” — [26:46] prints the identical line (verified); their faith-announcement reverses the usual order, unique in the corpus: “the Lord of Aaron and Moses” (elsewhere always “Moses and Aaron,” e.g. [7:122], [26:48], [21:48] — observable fact).
* **`[20:71]`** He said, “Did you believe in him before I have given you permission? He must be your chief, who has taught you magic. I will cut off your hands and your feet on alternate sides, and I will crucify you on the trunks of the palm-trees. Then you will know which of us is more severe in punishment, and more lasting.”
  * **Gloss:** Pharaoh's counter: belief without permission, the implied chief-teacher charge, hands-feet-crucifixion — might as argument; “which of us is more severe… and more lasting” — he unknowingly adopts the surah's comparative.
* **`[20:72]`** They said, “We will not prefer you to the proofs that have come to us, and Him who created us. So issue whatever judgment you wish to issue. You can only rule in this lowly life.  **`[20:73]`** We have believed in our Lord, so that He may forgive us our sins, and the magic you have compelled us to practice. Allah is Better, and more Lasting.”
  * **Gloss:** The new believers decline both: no preference over “Him who created us”; “You can only rule in this lowly life”; and the doxology that the surah will keep quoting to itself: “Allah is Better, and more Lasting.”
* **`[20:74]`** Whoever comes to his Lord guilty, for him is Hell, where he neither dies nor lives.
  * **Gloss:** Ballot one: the guilty come to Hell “where he neither dies nor lives” — the phrase occurs nowhere else in the corpus (verified).
* **`[20:75]`** But whoever comes to Him a believer, having worked righteousness—these will have the highest ranks.
  * **Gloss:** Ballot two — the task's “closing” line, in fact the contest's epilogue: “But whoever comes to Him a believer, having worked righteousness—these will have the highest ranks.”
* **`[20:76]`** The Gardens of Perpetuity, beneath which rivers flow, dwelling therein forever. That is the reward for him who purifies himself.
  * **Gloss:** “The Gardens of Perpetuity, beneath which rivers flow” — the capitalized twin of [16:31] (verified); “the reward for him who purifies himself” — the standard moved inward.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`iftirā'` (fabricated lies)** [20:61]
  * **Rendering:** “Do not fabricate lies against Allah… He who invents lies will fail.”
  * **Elsewhere:** [10:69] “Those who fabricate lies about Allah will not succeed” — same verdict; also [10:59], [5:103] — the lie-attribution idiom's stable home.
* **sujud of the magicians** [20:70]
  * **Rendering:** “fell down prostrate” = [26:46] verbatim; [7:120] renders the same fall “to their knees.”
  * **Elsewhere:** their confession “the Lord of Aaron and Moses” is the corpus's only Aaron-first order; the A'raf/Shu'ara versions say “Lord of the Worlds… the Lord of Moses and Aaron” ([7:121–122], [26:47–48]). *Source: observable corpus fact; any reason for the reversal is classical gloss — agent inference — verify.*
* **“the highest ranks”** [20:75]
  * **Rendering:** unique phrase in the corpus (verified); the formula's return at [20:112] adds the security clause “will fear neither injustice, nor grievance.”
  * **Elsewhere:** the reward-image “Gardens of Perpetuity” recurs verbatim at [16:31].
