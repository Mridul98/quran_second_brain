## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Meccan — consensus of the classical surah-classifications; no strong dissent recorded. *Source: transmitted classification (al-Itqan-type surah-introductions) — verify in print.* Revelation order **42** in the classical nuzul-lists this series follows. *Source: transmitted nuzul-order tables (series convention; printed tables vary, many giving 45 for Ta-Ha) — agent note, verify.*
* **Size:** 135 ayahs, 8 ruku (per `data/ruku_map.json`: 24+30+22+13+15+11+13+7 = 135). Mushaf position 20, between Maryam (19) and al-Anbiya (21).
* **Name:** Ta-Ha — the opening letters (`[20:1]` "Ta, Ha."), transliterated and untranslated; [20:1] is on the surveyed list in the muqatta'at fleeting note. The valley-word of `[20:12]` (“Tuwa”) also gives the surah a second classical name. *Source: transmitted naming reports — agent note, verify.*
* **Central theme:** A surah of reassurance built around two chosen, fallen, restored men — Moses and Adam. It opens by denying that revelation is misery — "We did not reveal the Quran to you to make you suffer." (`[20:2]`) — and closes that promise cashed in the Adam-drama: the follower of guidance "will not go astray, nor suffer" (`[20:123]`; the suffer-word links 2, 117, 123 — observable in this translation). Between the frame: the fire and the untied tongue, the festival-noon duel and the magicians' prostration, the night flight and the lowing calf, the hushed Trumpet and the humbled faces, the Tree of Immortality and the three-verb pardon, then the prophet's own instruction — a day ringed with glorification, eyes off the splendor, ending "Say, “Everybody is waiting, so wait. You will know who the people of the straight path are, and who is rightly-guided." (`[20:135]`).
* **Theme checks the task asked to verify (against the corpus, with corrections):** `[20:2–3]` ✓ but the wording is "We did not reveal the Quran to you to make you suffer." — "miserable" does not occur here; the guidance k-note likewise contains no "miserable/suffer" line (checked), so the live link is 20:3's conditional audience. `[20:12]` ✓ — "I—I am your Lord. Take off your shoes. You are in the sacred valley of Tuwa." says **shoes**, not "sandals". `[20:59]` the chosen appointment ✓ (festival day; the rendezvous itself is quoted at `[20:80]`, "promised you by the right side of the Mount"). `[20:96]` — the task's "I had what my hand concealed" is NOT this corpus: it prints "He said, “I saw what they did not see, so I grasped a handful from the Messenger’s traces, and I flung it away. Thus my soul prompted me.”" (the "concealed-in-hand" idiom belongs to other English translations — agent note, verify). `[20:115]` ✓ — "And We covenanted with Adam before, but he forgot, and We found in him no resolve.". The "repentance pair" = 121–122: the fall and "But then his Lord recalled him, and pardoned him, and guided him.", twin of `[2:37]`. `[20:131]` ✓ number correct — “do not extend your glance towards what We have given some classes of them to enjoy” (corpus: And do not extend your glance towards what We have given some classes of them to enjoy—the splendor of the life of this world— that We may test them thereby. The "high ranks" line is `[20:75]` ("But whoever comes to Him a believer, having worked righteousness—these will have the highest ranks.") — the close of the magicians' contest (ruku 3), NOT the surah's closing; its sister formula is `[20:112]`, and the genuine close is `[20:135]`.

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Reassurance & credentials (1–8):** the letters, the not-a-burden denial, the reminder for him who fears, the Throne-doxy and the Most Beautiful Names — the Book defines itself before it narrates.
* **Moses act one (9–48):** fire → valley → staff and hand → the petition's four clauses (heart, task, tongue, hearing) → childhood as a résumé of favors → soft words ordered to Pharaoh ("speak to him nicely," 44).
* **The duel (49–76):** the royal question answered with design-and-guidance (50); the festival-noon appointment; ropes that crawl and a prophet who flinches (66–67); prostration under a mutilation threat; the two ballots (74–76) the surah reprints at 112.
* **Salvation & betrayal (77–98):** night flight, dry path, drowned state ("Pharaoh misled his people, and did not guide them," 79); right-side rendezvous, manna and quails, the rushed appointment; the Samarian (named in this surah alone across the corpus) and the lowing calf; Aaron's plea; creed re-set at 98.
* **Hour & horizon (99–115):** reports of times gone by as cargo; the Trumpet, the blue muster, the ten-that-was-a-day; mountains crumbled, voices hushed, intercession double-qualified (2:255's grammar); the Arabic Quran's credentials; the no-haste etiquette and "increase me in knowledge"; Adam's epitaph as bridge (115).
* **Adam act (116–128) & charge (129–135):** refusal, needlessness, the immortality bait, fall, three-verb pardon, the guidance/not-suffer cash; then the prophet's own day — prior-word respite, tasbih clock, uncovetous eyes, "Everybody is waiting, so wait."
