# The Book sent down in truth: sincere devotion to the One, the Prevailing (39:1–9)
**UID:** 202610141942
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 39:1–9 (Ruku 1 of 8 in az-Zumar)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[39:1]` – `[39:9]` (9 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The overture stamps the Book's origin ("from Allah, the Mighty and Wise"), extracts its single demand ("devoting your religion to Him"), hears the associators state their own motive ("that they may bring us nearer to Allah"), and then settles the argument with the creation ledger — cosmos, embryo, cattle-pairs — before auditing the human heart's default settings: ingratitude-until-adversity, rivals-after-relief, and the unanswerable rank-question "Are those who know and those who do not know equal?"
* **Placement:** Ruku 1 of 8; every theme the surah later harvests (kalam 1–2, intercession 3, son-reductio 4, provision/gratitude 7, the shrine-pivot heart 45, the mercy-hope 9→53) is seeded here.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[39:1]`** "The revelation of the Book is from Allah, the Mighty and Wise."
  * **Gloss:** The download-stamp: origin, not authorship-by-human; the two names (Mighty, Wise) bracket the whole surah's project — power behind the verdicts, wisdom in the pleadings. Task wording-check: this edition says "revelation," not "sending down," and joins the names "Mighty **and** Wise."
* **`[39:2]`** "We sent down to you the Book with the truth, so serve Allah, devoting your religion to Him."
  * **Gloss:** The divine voice takes over ("We") — kalam anchor; the Book's credential is "with the truth" and its only executive demand is *ikhlas*: religion emptied of middlemen.
* **`[39:3]`** "Is not to Allah that sincere faith is due? As for those who take guardians besides Him, “We only worship them that they may bring us nearer to Allah.” Allah will judge between them regarding their differences. Allah does not guide the lying blasphemer."
  * **Gloss:** The rhetorical question universalizes 39:2's demand; the polytheists' defense is quoted verbatim — the earliest documented excuse of intercession-logic; the verdict is deferred to divine judgment and the guidance-door is welded shut for "the lying blasphemer."
* **`[39:4]`** "If Allah wanted to have a son, He could have selected from His creation at will. Glory be to Him. He is Allah, the One, the Prevailing."
  * **Gloss:** The son-reductio turned inside out: if sonship were wantable, sonship would be choosable from creation — which makes the claim creature-talk, not divine-talk; sealed by "the One, the Prevailing" (the only "Prevailing" rendering in the corpus).
* **`[39:5]`** "He created the heavens and the earth with reason. He wraps the night around the day, and He wraps the day around the night. And He regulates the sun and the moon, each running along a specific course. He is indeed the Almighty, the Forgiver."
  * **Gloss:** The creation-signs passage, verified with exact rendering: "with reason" (the task's "in truth" is other editions' wording), and the *takwir* image "wraps... around" (the night-coiling-over idiom appears in this corpus only here; elsewhere the same physics is the "merges... into" formula). Closing names couple might with forgiveness — the seed of 39:53.
* **`[39:6]`** "He created you from one person, then made from it its mate, and brought down livestock for you—eight kinds in pairs. He creates you in the wombs of your mothers, in successive formations, in a triple darkness. Such is Allah, your Lord. His is the kingdom. There is no god but He. So what made you deviate?"
  * **Gloss:** Anthropology as argument: species from one person → mate (the task's "single soul" is `[4:1]`'s rendering of the same *nafs* motif); "brought down" (tanzil's own verb) livestock in eight paired kinds — provision and creed in one breath; embryology "in successive formations, in a triple darkness"; the crescendo "His is the kingdom. There is no god but He" ends on the accusation: "So what made you deviate?"
* **`[39:7]`** "If you disbelieve, Allah is Independent of you, yet He does not approve ingratitude on the part of His servants. And if you are thankful, He will approve that in you. No bearer of burden can bear the burden of another. Then to your Lord is your return; and He will inform you of what you used to do. He is aware of what the hearts contain."
  * **Gloss:** The gratitude audit: His independence is total, His approval of ingratitude is nil, His approval of thankfulness is *in you* — credit that lands on the servant; then the personal-ledger axiom and the return-report: the hearts' contents already known. This is the line the canonical shukr note quotes verbatim.
* **`[39:8]`** "When some adversity touches the human being, he prays to his Lord, repenting to Him. But then, when He confers on him a grace of His, he forgets what he was praying for before, and he attributes rivals to Allah, in order to lead astray from His way. Say, “Enjoy your disbelief for a little while; you will be among the inmates of the Fire.”"
  * **Gloss:** The human default in one cycle: distress → exclusive prayer → relief → forgetting → rivals. "In order to lead astray from His way" is this edition's sharp reading of the verse's purpose-clause (Source: translator's term choice, observable in corpus); the sentence is irony measured in "a little while."
* **`[39:9]`** "Is he who worships devoutly during the watches of the night, prostrating himself and standing up, mindful of the Hereafter, and placing his hope in the mercy of his Lord? Say, “Are those who know and those who do not know equal?” Only those possessed of reason will remember."
  * **Gloss:** The night-worshipper's portrait (postures: prostration and standing; motives: fear and hope) is left grammatically hanging — the comparison's other term is everyone else; the rank-question answers itself, and "the mercy of his Lord" is the hope-thread that 39:53 will cut.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`revelation of the Book` (tanzil)** `[39:1]`:
  * **Rendering:** "The revelation of the Book is from Allah, the Mighty and Wise" — and 39:2's verb-family "We sent down to you the Book with the truth."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[32:2]` "The revelation of the Book, without a doubt, is from the Lord of the Universe"; `[45:2]` "The revelation of the Book is from Allah, the Exalted in Might, the Wise" — the 39:1 near-twin; `[39:6]` "brought down livestock" uses the same download-verb for provision.
* **`sincere faith / devoting` (ikhlas)** `[39:2–3]`:
  * **Rendering:** "devoting your religion to Him" (v.), "Is not to Allah that sincere faith is due?" (n.); 39:14 returns it as "sincere in my faith in Him."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[31:32]` "they call upon Allah, devoting their religion to Him" (the storm-sincerity that dries up on shore); `[2:139]` "we are sincere to Him"; `[40:65]` "pray to Him, devoting your religion to Him" — Ghafir's twin close.
* **`wraps` (takwir)** `[39:5]`:
  * **Rendering:** "He wraps the night around the day, and He wraps the day around the night" — mutual coiling, reciprocal and bidirectional.
  * **Elsewhere:** the same phenomenon in the merge-idiom: `[31:29]`, `[22:61]`, `[35:13]` "merges the night into the day"; `[57:6]` merges + "knows what the hearts contains"; the sun-and-moon with "a specific course" here vs. "a stated term" (`[31:29]`, `[35:13]`) and "a specified period" (`[13:2]`) — task-verified: no "cover(s) the night/day" wording exists in this corpus.
* **`ingratitude / thankful` (kufr–shukr)** `[39:7]`:
  * **Rendering:** "He does not approve ingratitude... And if you are thankful, He will approve that in you" — kufr rendered here by its gratitude-side meaning, matching the shukr note's thesis.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[16:83]` "They recognize Allah's blessing, but then deny it, as most of them are ungrateful"; `[31:12]` Luqman's circuit ("does not approve ingratitude" — the k-shukr note cites 31:12 alongside this very 39:7 line); `[2:152]` "thank Me, and do not be ungrateful."
