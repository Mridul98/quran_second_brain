## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Meccan per the classical surah-classification lists (unanimous in the printed nuzul lists; individual-verses-Medinan reports exist in the genre for neighbors but are not attached to az-Zumar's core). *Source: classical lists — verify in print.*
* **Order:** 59 per the transmitted chronology — the task's "order 59?" verifies ✓ under this brain's own convention: the Saba' index (`202610141851-s034_index_al-saba.md` §1) already reserved "the later Meccan band before az-Zumar 59," and the widely transmitted enumeration (the one printed in the standard mushaf chronologies, e.g. Cairo-1924 lineage: Luqman 57, Saba 58, **az-Zumar 59**, Ghafir 60, Fussilat 61) agrees. *Source: classical lists — verify in print; not derivable from the corpus.*
* **Size:** 75 ayahs, 8 ruku (`data/ruku_map.json`: 9+12+10+10+11+11+7+5 = 75 ✓). Mushaf position 39, between Sad (38) and Ghafir (40) — the pair is a matched Meccan diptych: Ghafir opens "The revelation of the Book is from Allah, the Exalted in Might..." (`[45:2]` is its Jathiyah cognate; Ghafir's 40:1–2 mirrors 39:1–2) and closes on the same praise formula az-Zumar ends with.
* **Name:** the corpus title is THRONGS (az-Zumar, "the groups"); the name-word surfaces exactly twice, in the surah's closing pageant: "driven to Hell **in throngs**" `[39:71]` and "led to Paradise **in throngs**" `[39:73]` — the only "throngs" renderings in the corpus (kin plurals: "droves" `[78:18]`/`[99:6]`, "troops" `[37:173]`/`[27:17]`). The task's "THROUNGS/ARMIES" conflated two things: the name is *throngs* (crowds at the gates), and the surah's culminating image is the **Throne** (`[39:75]`), not an army. *Source: translator's rendering + naming convention — classical ascription; verify.*
* **Central theme:** The Book comes from the Mighty and Wise, and its single demand is *devotion*: "serve Allah, devoting your religion to Him" (39:2). From there the surah is one long audit of where humans have secretly lent divine functions — nearness-bringing guardians (3), intercession (43–44), provision (52), the unreturnable death-sleep (42), the folded heavens (67) — and every audit ends the same way: "All intercession is up to Allah... To Him belongs the kingdom." The counter-weight is the surah's mercy-climax, 39:53 — "do not despair of Allah's mercy, for Allah forgives all sins" — issued immediately before the deadline verses (54–59) and followed by the two throngs at the gates. Kalam, tawhid, shukr, sabr, and guidance all have anchor verses here.

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Overture (1–9):** origin of the Book → devotion command → the guardians' own stated motive ("bring us nearer") → the son-reductio sealed by "the One, the Prevailing" → macro/micro creation signs (cosmos, embryo, cattle-pairs) → the gratitude audit (7) → the adversity-then-rivals reflex (8) → the knowing worshipper vs. the unknowing, "Are those who know... equal?"
* **The commanded litany (10–21):** five "Say" moves (10, 11–12, 13, 14–15) — devotees commissioned, religion exclusive, fear of the horrendous Day, the dare "worship whatever you wish"; then the two architecture shots: fire-layers above and below vs. mansions upon mansions, joined by "Allah never breaks a promise" and the water→debris reminder.
* **The Book and the heart (22–31):** opened vs. hardened hearts; the "best of narrations" whose effect runs skin→heart; the dread-face question; the precedent of penalty "from where they did not perceive"; the defectless Arabic Quran and the two-master parable; "You will die, and they will die" → the Day's quarrel.
* **Sufficiency and its opposite (32–41):** the liar-vs-truth-bringer binary with its two Hell-rooms; "Is Allah not enough for His servant?"; the creator-reductio that the associators themselves answer ("Allah") — then the impotence test and "Allah suffices for me"; the mutual-letdown declaration; the Book "for mankind in truth," guidance and loss privatized, "You are not their overseer."
* **Death, intercession, ingratitude (42–52):** the sleep/death soul-office; intercessors who "have no power over anything, and are devoid of reason"; "All intercession is up to Allah"; the shrink/joy heart-test; the Originator's judgment prayer; the unbuyable Day; the knowledge-claim ("I have attained this by virtue of my knowledge") and the spread/constrict ledger.
* **Mercy and deadline (53–63):** the forgiveness-all-sins call; turn-and-submit *before*; follow the best of what is revealed *before*; the three "if only"s answered by "My Verses did come to you"; blackened faces vs. the place of salvation; Creator, in Charge, holding the reins.
* **Majesty and muster (64–75):** the voiding of works under idolatry; worship-and-appreciation; the grip and the folded heavens; the two blasts — stun, then rising witnesses; the shining earth, the Book in place, judgment equitably; then the two processions at the two sets of gates, the angels about the Throne, and "Praise be to Allah, Lord of the Worlds."
