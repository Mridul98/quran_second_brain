# O My devotees: the steadfast paid in full without reckoning, and the commanded "Say" litany (39:10–21)
**UID:** 202610141943
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 39:10–21 (Ruku 2 of 8 in az-Zumar)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[39:10]` – `[39:21]` (12 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah's second movement is spoken in the first person plural of command — five "Say" moves commission the devotees (taqwa, vast earth, wage without reckoning), stage the Prophet's own exclusivity litany (commanded, first of those who submit, fearing, sincere), let the dare "worship whatever you wish" fall on the losers, bracket fire-layers and mansions with "Allah never breaks a promise," and end on the water-to-debris reminder — the same horizon 39:5 opened.
* **Placement:** Ruku 2 of 8; the obedience-and-reckoning hinge between ruku 1's creed-arguments and ruku 3's Book-and-heart anatomy. The "listen to the Word, follow the best of it" line (39:18) is the surah's own listening-rule for what follows.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[39:10]`** "Say, “O My devotees who have believed, keep your duty to your Lord. For those who do good in this world, is goodness. And Allah’s earth is vast. The steadfast will be paid their wages in full, without reckoning.”"
  * **Gloss:** The commission: devotees (not servants) addressed, taqwa ordered, goodness doubled (this world's and the wage's), exile-room promised ("Allah's earth is vast"), and the patient paid on an unledged account — the sabr note's verbatim anchor.
* **`[39:11]`** "Say, “I was commanded to serve Allah, devoting my religion exclusively to Him."
  * **Gloss:** First person of the commanded Prophet; the quotation is left open here and closes only in 39:12 (book typography — see Fleeting link).
* **`[39:12]`** "And I was commanded to be the first of those who submit.”"
  * **Gloss:** The second command: precedence in submission — the Prophet as pattern, not privilege.
* **`[39:13]`** "Say, “I fear, if I disobeyed my Lord, the punishment of a horrendous Day.”"
  * **Gloss:** The litany's hinge: even the first-of-submitters keeps a "if I disobeyed" clause — the surah's anti-infallibility footnote, and the word "horrendous" is this corpus's sole rendering here.
* **`[39:14]`** "Say, “It is Allah I worship, sincere in my faith in Him.”"
  * **Gloss:** Fronted object ("It is Allah") = exclusivity as syntax; the split pair 39:14–15 runs one utterance across the ayah boundary (the dare that follows is the believer's speech, not a permission).
* **`[39:15]`** "“But you can worship whatever you wish besides Him.” Say, “The losers are those who lose their souls and their people on the Day of Resurrection.” That is indeed the obvious loss."
  * **Gloss:** The famous dare — leave-them-to-it irony; then the losers redefined: the bankruptcy is self and dependents lost. Task check: this is the "deeds in vain" verse's *partner*, but the "works will be in vain" wording itself lives at `[39:65]`.
* **`[39:16]`** "They will have layers of Fire above them, and layers beneath them. That is how Allah strikes fear into His servants—“O My servants! Beware of Me!”"
  * **Gloss:** Architecture of doom as pedagogy: the fire-canopy/shadow pair exists to make the living wise; the call "O My servants! Beware of Me!" is the surah's first mercy-framing of fear (the address is to servants, not enemies).
* **`[39:17]`** "As for those who avoid the worship of idols, and devote themselves to Allah—theirs is the good news. So give good news to My servants."
  * **Gloss:** The counter-architecture: quit the taghut (rendered "idols"), turn to Allah — and the reward named is *being told good news*, the same verb the surah will use at its gates (39:73).
* **`[39:18]`** "Those who listen to the Word, and follow the best of it. These are they whom Allah has guided. These are they who possess intellect."
  * **Gloss:** The epistemic virtue: audition first, selection of the best second, guidance and intellect as the twin results — the kalam note's listening-rule; "follow the best of it" is re-issued as a command at 39:55.
* **`[39:19]`** "What about someone who has deserved the sentence of punishment? Is it you who can save those in the Fire?"
  * **Gloss:** The "What about someone..." (a-faman) chain starts — four rhetorical comparisons run 19–20, 22, 24; here the question to the would-be rescuer closes the door on any human save-faculty.
* **`[39:20]`** "But those who fear their Lord will have mansions upon mansions, built high, with streams flowing beneath them. The promise of Allah; and Allah never breaks a promise."
  * **Gloss:** Stacked mansions vs. stacked fire-layers (16) — the surah sets its two blueprints side by side and bolts them with the promise-keeping axiom, the covenant-grammar verse (no *mithaq* term, same contract).
* **`[39:21]`** "Have you not considered how Allah sends down water from the sky, then He makes it flow into underground wells, then He produces with it plants of various colors, then they wither and you see them yellowing, then He turns them into debris? Surely in this is a reminder for those with understanding."
  * **Gloss:** Consideration-command on the water→spring→colorful-plants→yellowing→debris chain: the same tanzil-verb as the Book's sending-down, played out in agriculture; life's quick compost is the surah's standing resurrection-proof.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`without reckoning`** `[39:10]`:
  * **Rendering:** "The steadfast will be paid their wages in full, without reckoning" — sabr's wage escapes the ledger entirely.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[3:37]` "Allah provides to whom He wills without reckoning"; `[24:38]` "Allah provides for whomever He wills without reckoning" — the only two partners; the k-sabr note quotes this line verbatim in its body.
* **`My devotees / My servants`** `[39:10, 16–17]`:
  * **Rendering:** "O My devotees who have believed" (10); "His servants... O My servants! Beware of Me!" (16); "give good news to My servants" (17) — the surah keeps re-marking the category.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[17:65]` "As for My devotees, you have no authority over them"; `[29:56]` "O My servants who have believed: My earth is vast, so worship Me alone" — the nearest near-verse to 39:10 anywhere in the corpus (vast-earth + exclusive worship).
* **`first of those who submit`** `[39:12]`:
  * **Rendering:** "I was commanded to be the first of those who submit."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[6:14]` "I am instructed to be the first of those who submit"; `[6:163]` "I am the first of those who submit"; `[40:66]` "I was commanded to submit to the Lord of the Worlds" — the submission-precedence formula belongs to the "Say" litany family.
* **`possess intellect`** `[39:18]`:
  * **Rendering:** "These are they who possess intellect" — the ulu al-albab cohort, here earned by listening-and-following.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[65:10]` "So beware of Allah, O you who possess intellect and have faith"; with 39:9's "those possessed of reason" and 39:21's "those with understanding" (cf. `[20:54]`) this is the surah's three-tier audience ladder.

## 6. Context Notes
