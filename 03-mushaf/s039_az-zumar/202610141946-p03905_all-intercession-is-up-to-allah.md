# Souls taken at death and in sleep; all intercession is up to Allah (39:42–52)
**UID:** 202610141946
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 39:42–52 (Ruku 5 of 8 in az-Zumar)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[39:42]` – `[39:52]` (11 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The two offices no guardian ever performed are reclaimed: the soul-office (Allah takes the souls at death *and* in sleep, retaining or releasing by decree) and the intercession-office ("All intercession is up to Allah" — the task's interrogative form is `[2:255]`'s; here the verdict is declarative monopoly). The heart-test verse (39:45) then names the disease: shrink at Allah-alone, joy at the besides-Him; the Originator-prayer hands the differences to the only Judge; the unbuyable Day (47–48), the self-credit reflex ("I have attained this by virtue of my knowledge"), and the spread/constrict provision ledger (52) finish the audit of everything humans pretend to hold.
* **Placement:** Ruku 5 of 8; the surah's death-and-intercession courtroom — direct callback to ruku 1's return-verse (39:7) and the lead-in to the mercy-climax (39:53).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[39:42]`** "Allah takes the souls at the time of their death, and those that have not died during their sleep. He retains those for which He has decreed death, and He releases the others until a predetermined time. In that are signs for people who reflect."
  * **Gloss:** Sleep as minor death: the same tawaffi verb governs both, a nightly rehearsal of the retain/release decision — the strongest resurrection-sign argument the surah makes, "for people who reflect."
* **`[39:43]`** "Or have they chosen intercessors other than Allah? Say, “Even though they have no power over anything, and are devoid of reason?”"
  * **Gloss:** The chosen-intercessors question with its own answer built in: the nominated shafi's are power-zero and mind-zero — "devoid of reason" strips even the plea-of-ignorance defense.
* **`[39:44]`** "Say, “All intercession is up to Allah. To Him belongs the kingdom of the heavens and the earth. Then to Him you will be returned.”"
  * **Gloss:** The intercession monopoly, verified as the ruku's center — total intercession (kullaha), sovereignty as the deed, return as the schedule. This declarative form is what the task's `[2:255]`-style question answers to; both belong to the permission-gate family.
* **`[39:45]`** "When Allah alone is mentioned, the hearts of those who do not believe in the Hereafter shrink with resentment. But when those other than Him are mentioned, they become filled with joy."
  * **Gloss:** The diagnostic verse: ishma'zz — the flinch at the bare name, the glow at the rivals' names; the pathognomic sign that the problem is not evidence but allegiance (cf. 39:3's guardians).
* **`[39:46]`** "Say, “Our God, Initiator of the heavens and the earth, Knower of all secrets and declarations. You will judge between your servants regarding what they had differed about.”"
  * **Gloss:** The taught prayer: fatir-address, knower-of-hidden-and-open, differences handed to judgment — the courtroom's opening bell (cf. Joseph's identical address, `[12:101]`).
* **`[39:47]`** "If those who did wrong owned everything on earth, and the like of it with it, they would redeem themselves with it from the terrible suffering on the Day of Resurrection. But there will appear to them from Allah what they never anticipated."
  * **Gloss:** The ransom-economy collapse: the whole earth plus its like, offered — and the ledger's surprise line: what was never computed for.
* **`[39:48]`** "There will appear to them the evils of their deeds, and they will be surrounded by what they used to ridicule."
  * **Gloss:** Two appearances: their own crimes shown up, and the ridiculed punishment enclosing them — mockery converts to encirclement (the 6:5 "news of what they used to ridicule" motif fulfilled).
* **`[39:49]`** "When adversity touches the human being, he calls on Us. But then, when We favor him with a blessing from Us, he says, “I have attained this by virtue of my knowledge.” However, it is a test, but most of them do not know."
  * **Gloss:** Ruku 1's cycle-verse retold with its punchline upgraded: now the granted blessing is claimed as self-earned — "by virtue of my knowledge," the Qarun reflex (verbatim kin at `[28:78]`); verdict: it was a test.
* **`[39:50]`** "Those before them said it, but what they had earned did not avail them."
  * **Gloss:** Precedent clause: the same sentence, the same zero-earnings — earning is not the save-file.
* **`[39:51]`** "The evils of their deeds caught up with them. And the wrongdoers among these will also be afflicted by the evils of what they earned, and they cannot prevent it."
  * **Gloss:** The catch-up law, stated for then and now — "these" = the Prophet's own addressees; no escape clause.
* **`[39:52]`** "Do they not know that Allah extends the provision to whomever He wills, and constricts it? In that are signs for people who believe."
  * **Gloss:** The provision ledger closed: if blessing is self-made (49), provision follows will — az-Zumar says basṭ/qabr are His alone; "signs for people who believe" answers 39:42's "for people who reflect" — the ruku brackets itself with its two audiences.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`takes the souls` (tawaffa)** `[39:42]`:
  * **Rendering:** "Allah takes the souls at the time of their death, and those that have not died during their sleep."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[6:60]` "It is He Who takes you by night... then He raises you up" — the sleep-death twin verse; `[32:11]` "The angel of death put in charge of you will reclaim you" (agent-form); `[12:101]` "Receive my soul in submission" — the task's "take my soul in Islam?" motif, which belongs to Joseph's prayer, not to az-Zumar.
* **`intercession` (shafa'a)** `[39:43–44]`:
  * **Rendering:** declarative monopoly: "All intercession is up to Allah" — the concept is centralized, not abolished (permission-gate family, not denial family).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:255]` "Who is he that can intercede with Him except with His permission?" — the interrogative mother-form; `[53:26]` angels' intercession avails nothing "except after Allah gives permission"; `[34:23]` "Intercession with Him is of no value, except for someone He has permitted"; `[2:48/123]`, `[74:48]` — the Day-dismissal set; p03403 already links 39:44 into this family.
* **`by virtue of my knowledge`** `[39:49]`:
  * **Rendering:** "I have attained this by virtue of my knowledge."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[28:78]` "I was given all this on account of knowledge I possess" — Qarun's line, the idiom's named archetype; both end with "but most of them do not know" (39:49; cf. 16:75).
* **`extends / constricts` (bast–qabr)** `[39:52]`:
  * **Rendering:** "Allah extends the provision to whomever He wills, and constricts it."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[34:39]` "My Lord extends the provision to whomever He wills of His servants, or withholds it"; `[28:82]` "Allah... spreads bounty... and restricts it" — the provision-sovereignty formula the infraq note's economy shares (verdict: az-Zumar has no spend-command, only this ledger).
