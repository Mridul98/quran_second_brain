# Is it other than Allah you instruct me to worship? The earth in His grip and the two trumpet blasts (39:64–70)
**UID:** 202610141948
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 39:64–70 (Ruku 7 of 8 in az-Zumar)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[39:64]` – `[39:70]` (7 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The ruku is the surah's majesty-montage: the worship-dare asked one last time ("Is it other than Allah you instruct me to worship, you ignorant ones?"), the inter-generational warning that idolatry voids works (the deeds-in-vain verse the task asked for — it lives here at 39:65, with 39:15 as its loser-definition partner), the command to worship and be among the appreciative — and then the estimation verse: the earth in His grip, the heavens folded in His right, the two trumpet blasts (stun, then rise), the earth shining with its Lord's Light, the Book put in place, judgment passed equitably, every soul compensated in full.
* **Placement:** Ruku 7 of 8; the scale-shift from the moral to the cosmic — ruku 6 emptied the plea-system, this ruku empties the sky-holders too, setting up the processions of ruku 8.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[39:64]`** "Say, “Is it other than Allah you instruct me to worship, you ignorant ones?”"
  * **Gloss:** The worship-dare in its most colloquial form — "you ignorant ones" (jahihin: the people of jahiliyya-logic) answered with a question instead of an argument; the 39:14 litany inverted.
* **`[39:65]`** "It was revealed to you, and to those before you, that if you idolize, your works will be in vain, and you will be of the losers."
  * **Gloss:** The deeds-in-vain verse, verified verbatim: the warning is inter-generational ("to you, and to those before you" — kalam claim: one law across all revelation); "idolize" is this edition's verb for shirk at its root (make others like Him), and the sanction is not new punishment but bankruptcy: works voided, loser-status. The task's trailing clause "they will see the truth" is not here — az-Zumar's seeing-motif is the Day-vision of 67–70; the losers-definition stands at 39:15.
* **`[39:66]`** "Rather, worship Allah, and be of the appreciative."
  * **Gloss:** The positive pivot in five words: worship + shukr — the surah's whole economy compressed (39:7's approval-of-thankfulness returns as command); "the appreciative" (shakirin) is the same cohort the shukr note tracks.
* **`[39:67]`** "They have not esteemed Allah as He ought to be esteemed. The entire earth will be in His grip on the Day of Resurrection, and the heavens will be folded in His right. Immaculate is He, and Transcendent He is beyond the associations they make."
  * **Gloss:** The estimation failure (ma qadaru Allah haqqa qadrihi) answered by the anatomy-shrinking image: whole earth — a handful; heavens — a folded scroll in the right hand (qabda/yamin; the corpus's only "grip" in this scale-sense and its only "folded"), then the transcendent seal "beyond the associations they make" — the shirk of 65 measured against the grip of 67.
* **`[39:68]`** "And the Trumpet will be sounded, whereupon everyone in the heavens and the earth will be stunned, except whomever Allah wills. Then it will be sounded another time, whereupon they will rise up, looking on."
  * **Gloss:** The two blasts: sa'qa — swoon/stun for all, with the exception-clause; then the second sound and the standing-up gaze: the audience is drafted, not scattered; the same exception-clause as `[27:87]`.
* **`[39:69]`** "And the earth will shine with the Light of its Lord; and the Book will be put in place; and the prophets and the witnesses will be brought in; and Judgment will be passed among them equitably, and they will not be wronged."
  * **Gloss:** Four courtroom moves: lighting (the earth lit by its Lord's Light — the only "shine" of the earth in the corpus), the register laid down, the plaintiff-panel (prophets + witnesses) convened, equity sealed — and "they will not be wronged" as the trial's bill of rights.
* **`[39:70]`** "And every soul will be fully compensated for what it had done. He is well aware of what they do."
  * **Gloss:** Full compensation (wafayat) with no shrinkage across the transfer from deeds to wage; the awareness-clause grounds the verdict in present-tense knowledge ("what they do"), not memory.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`idolize / works in vain`** `[39:65]`:
  * **Rendering:** "if you idolize, your works will be in vain" — hubṭ (voidance) applied to 'amal; shirk rendered by its shape (making others like Him).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[6:41]`, `[7:152]`, `[7:191]` — the verb-idiom "idolize" family; `[5:5]` "whoever rejects faith, his work will be in vain"; `[18:105]` "their works are in vain"; `[49:2]` "lest your works be in vain without you realizing"; `[2:217]` the apostasy-case ("whose works will come to nothing") — hubt's five registers; 39:65 is its prophetic-address form.
* **`grip / folded`** `[39:67]`:
  * **Rendering:** "The entire earth will be in His grip... and the heavens will be folded in His right."
  * **Elsewhere:** "grip" otherwise only in punishment-scale uses: `[11:102]` "Such is the grip of your Lord when He seizes the towns," `[69:10]`, `[23:76]`, `[26:189]`; the "folded" verb is unique here — the classically noted scroll-image (cf. `[21:104]` "as We rolled up the scroll of creation" — verify) is the nearest cousin; Source: agent inference on the attribute-discipline — the verse states no interpretation.
* **`the Trumpet` (sur)** `[39:68]`:
  * **Rendering:** "the Trumpet will be sounded... stunned... sounded another time... rise up, looking on."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[27:87]` "everyone in the heavens and the earth will be horrified, except whomever Allah wills" — the near-twin blast-verse; `[18:99]` "the Trumpet will be blown, and We will gather them"; `[50:20]`, `[74:8]`, `[78:18]` — "you will come in droves," the muster the very next ruku stages.
* **`appreciative` (shakir)** `[39:66]`:
  * **Rendering:** "be of the appreciative" — shukr as worship's civic identity.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[76:3]` "We guided him to the way, be he appreciative or unappreciative"; `[3:144/145]` "Allah will reward the appreciative"; `[2:243]` "most people are not appreciative" — the cohort the k-shukr note defines; 39:66 fuses the note's two verbs: worship Allah *and* be appreciative.
