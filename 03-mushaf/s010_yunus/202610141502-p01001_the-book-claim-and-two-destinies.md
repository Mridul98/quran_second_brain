# The Wise Book, the wonder of a human warner, and the two destinies (10:1–10)
**UID:** 202610141502
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 10:1–10 (Ruku 1 of 11 in Yunus)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[10:1]` – `[10:10]` (10 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The lettered opening authenticates the recital as "the Verses of the Wise Book" delivered through a merely human warner — a claim the disbelievers answer "This is a manifest sorcerer" — and the ruku lays out the creed that makes the recital credible: one Lord who created in six days, settled over the Throne, governs all things, and holds every intercessor under His permission; the cosmos becomes a calendar, humanity splits by whether it hopes to meet God, and the garden's final word is "Praise be to Allah, Lord of the Worlds."
* **Placement:** Opening ruku of the surah's courtroom argument; its intercession-clause (10:3) is the creed anchor that 10:18 will weaponize, and its "do not hope to meet Us" class (10:7) reappears verbatim as the demanders of a different Quran in 10:15.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[10:1]`** "Alif, Lam, Ra. These are the Verses of the Wise Book."
  * **Gloss:** Letters, then a Book-claim; "Wise Book" — the same phrase as 31:2 (verified) — frames the whole surah's evidence exhibit.
* **`[10:2]`** "Is it a wonder to the people that We inspired a man from among them: “Warn mankind, and give good news to those who believe that they are on a sound footing with their Lord”? The disbelievers said, “This is a manifest sorcerer.”"
  * **Gloss:** The messenger's humanity is the scandal; "on a sound footing" renders *ka'annahum* — the note of standing well with the Lord (unique phrase in the corpus, verified); the immediate rebuttal is the sorcerer-label.
* **`[10:3]`** "Your Lord is Allah, who created the heavens and the earth in six days, then settled over the Throne, governing all things. There is no intercessor except after His permission. Such is Allah, your Lord—so serve Him. Will you not reflect?"
  * **Gloss:** One verse of compressed creed: creation, enthronement, ongoing governance (*yudabbiru al-amr*), and the deflation of intercession to permission-dependence; ends in the interrogation "Will you not reflect?" — the exact twin of 32:4's closing (verified).
* **`[10:4]`** "To Him is your return, altogether. The promise of Allah is true. He originates creation, and then He repeats it, to reward those who believe and do good deeds with equity. As for those who disbelieve, for them is a drink of boiling water, and agonizing torment, on account of their disbelief."
  * **Gloss:** Return, true promise, origination-then-repetition (the same formula as 30:11, verified) — equity for one side, boiling drink (*saqir*) for the other (cf. 6:70 "scalding water," verified).
* **`[10:5]`** "It is He who made the sun radiant, and the moon a light, and determined phases for it— that you may know the number of years and the calculation. Allah did not create all this except with truth. He details the revelations for a people who know."
  * **Gloss:** Astronomy as calendar and worship-grammar; the moon's "phases" twin is 36:39 (verified); creation "except with truth" answers the forged-Book charge before it is spoken.
* **`[10:6]`** "In the alternation of night and day, and in what Allah created in the heavens and the earth, are signs for people who are aware."
  * **Gloss:** The same nature-signs that carry 2:164 and 3:190 (verified) — here addressed to "people who are aware."
* **`[10:7]`** "Those who do not hope to meet Us, and are content with the worldly life, and are at ease in it, and those who pay no heed to Our signs."
  * **Gloss:** The first class: no hope of the encounter, settling for the near life, heedless (the same heedlessness Pharaoh's body will rebuke in 10:92).
* **`[10:8]`** "These—their dwelling is the Fire—on account of what they used to do."
  * **Gloss:** Sentence passed on deeds, not on God's whim.
* **`[10:9]`** "As for those who believe and do good deeds, their Lord guides them in their faith. Rivers will flow beneath them in the Gardens of Bliss."
  * **Gloss:** The counter-class: guidance granted along with faith — the road with conditions, stated in one line.
* **`[10:10]`** "Their call therein is, “Glory be to You, our God.” And their greeting therein is, “Peace.” And the last of their call is, “Praise be to Allah, Lord of the Worlds.”"
  * **Gloss:** Paradise liturgy — glorification, greeting of peace (cf. 14:23 and 33:44, verified), closing with the Fatihah's own line, "Praise be to Allah, Lord of the Worlds" (1:2).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`Alif, Lam, Ra.`**: the disconnected letters, numbered ayah 1 in this edition.
  * **Rendering:** letter-names transliterated, not translated.
  * **Elsewhere:** exactly five surahs open with this string — `[10:1]`, `[11:1]`, `[12:1]`, `[14:1]`, `[15:1]` (rg-verified) — each followed by a Book-claim; see the Muqatta'at fleeting note.
* **`intercessor` (shafi'):** the surah's creedal test case.
  * **Rendering:** "There is no intercessor except after His permission" (10:3) — permission as precondition, not prohibition.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:255]` "Who is he that can intercede with Him except with His permission?" (near-verbatim twin, verified); `[20:109]` "intercession will not avail, except for him permitted by the Merciful"; `[32:4]` — the six-days-Throne verse again with "no master and no intercessor" (both verified).
* **`six days` + `Throne`:** creation-with-governance.
  * **Rendering:** "created the heavens and the earth in six days, then settled over the Throne, governing all things" (10:3).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[32:4]`, `[7:54]`, `[57:4]`, `[25:59]`, `[50:38]`, `[11:7]` (all verified) — 32:4 is Yunus 10:3's twin down to the intercession clause and the closing question.
* **`the meeting/encounter` (liqa'):** the hinge sorting 10:7–8 from 10:9–10.
  * **Rendering:** "Those who do not hope to meet Us" (10:7); reappears at `[10:11]` "those who do not expect Our encounter" and `[10:45]` "denied the meeting with Allah."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[25:21]` "Those who do not expect to meet Us say..." (same class, same phrasing, verified); `[29:5]` "Whoever looks forward to the meeting with Allah"; `[45:14]` "those who do not hope for the Days of Allah" (verified).

## 4. Cross-References (The Web)
* **Parallel:** `[7:63]` — Noah's people: "Do you wonder that a reminder has come to you from your Lord, through a man from among you?" — the same *wonder* objection 10:2 raises, voiced inside this surah's own Noah story (ruku 8).
* **Within surah:** `[10:3]` ↔ `[10:18]` — the creed clause here becomes the interrogation of the intercessor-claim there; `[10:7]` ↔ `[10:15]` — the no-hope class reappears demanding a swapped Quran.
* **Parallel:** `[6:7]` — "Had We sent down upon you a book on paper, and they had touched it with their hands, those who disbelieve would have said, 'This is nothing but plain magic'" — the material-proof counterfactual matching 10:2's sorcerer-label.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Meccan (classical consensus); the sorcerer-label of 10:2 belongs to the Meccan accusation register (cf. `[40:24]`, `[51:52]`, `[43:49]`, all verified — "lying sorcerer", "a sorcerer or a madman", Pharaoh's own "O sorcerer").
* **Term note:** Itani renders 10:2's *sha'ir* as "sorcerer"; classically the word is read "poet" in Meccan-rejection contexts — *Source: classical tafsir — verify against printed editions; the corpus line stands as quoted.*
