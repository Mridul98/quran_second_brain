# Surah 10: JONAH (Yunus)
**UID:** 202610141501
**Type:** Index / Surah Map
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Meccan — classical consensus. **Revelation order 51** in the standard transmitted enumeration. *Source: classical revelation-order lists; agent labeling from the transmitted chronology tradition — verify against a printed source before teaching the number.* The basmala is unnumbered here, as in every surah header in this edition — see [Basmala is unnumbered in this edition except 1:1](../../01-fleeting/202610132006-f-basmala_numbering_in_this_edition.md).
* **Size:** 109 ayahs, 11 ruku (Hanafi/Idad-e-Quran partition per `data/ruku_map.json`). It opens with `Alif, Lam, Ra.` — the same three letters as the immediately adjacent surahs Hud (11:1) and Yusuf (12:1) — see [Muqatta'at letters](../../01-fleeting/202610132007-f-muqattaat_letters_untranslated.md).
* **Central theme:** A courtroom surah on the **credibility of revelation**. It opens by authenticating the recital ("the Verses of the Wise Book," 10:1) and the human messenger it is delivered through (10:2 — met with "This is a manifest sorcerer"), then keeps returning to the charge: replace it or change it (10:15); "He has forged it" answered by the single-chapter challenge (10:37–38); verify me with the Scripture-readers before you (10:94); close by binding the prophet to the revealed word (10:109). The verdict scene is built with twin instruments: a **tawhid interrogation** whose expected answer is "Allah" (10:31–35), with intercession deflated to "no intercessor except after His permission" (10:3) and the idols' intercessor-claim exposed (10:18), and a **messenger-history sequence** proving 10:47's law "Every community has a messenger": Noah's dare and drowning (10:71–73), the sealed-chain of later messengers (10:74), Moses–Pharaoh with the sorcerer showdown (10:75–82), Pharaoh's rejected deathbed creed (10:90–92), Israel's settlement (10:93) — and the single counter-example, Jonah's town, whose belief actually profited (10:98). The close moves from verdict to coexistence: no compulsion (10:99), the religion-line disavowal (10:104), and the final pair of commands, follow and be patient (10:109).

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Authentication (ruku 1–2):** letters + Book-claim + the "wonder" of a man inspired from among his own people (10:1–2); creed compressed in one verse — six days, Throne, governance, intercession under permission only (10:3); cosmos as calendar (10:5–6); the two destinies (7–10). Then the counter-requests: accelerate the harm (11), swap the Quran (15), produce a miracle (20) — each answered from the prophet's inability: "I only follow what is revealed to me" (15).
* **Unmasking (ruku 3–4):** situational sea-faith exposed (22–23); the world-parable (24); the Day's courtroom separation of partners from their claimants (28–30). Then the interrogation sequence (31–35) — provision, hearing/sight, living/dead, governance, origination, guidance — all answered "Allah," reducing the partners to non-guides; conjecture indicted (36); the Book's self-defense and the one-chapter challenge (37–38); the crowd splits (40).
* **Verdict mechanics (ruku 5–7):** mutual disavowal of deeds (41); the prophet's non-coercive limits restated twice (42–43); every community had a messenger with an immovable time (47–49); faith-at-sighting mocked and refused (51–53); the ransom refused (54); the Book announced as advice and healing (57); the total record (61); the friends of Allah defined — "those who believe and are aware" (62–63); the son-claim asked for its proof (68).
* **The messenger-history (ruku 8–9):** Noah (71–73) → sealed chain (74) → Moses and Aaron before Pharaoh (75–82), with the showdown left unwitnessed — in Yunus the sorcerers never speak; their prostration belongs to 7:120–126 and 20:70. The drowning scene here belongs to **Pharaoh himself**: his creed "Now?" refused, his body preserved as sign (90–92).
* **Resolution (ruku 10–11):** Israel settled (93); the doubt-clause directed to prior Scripture (94); the hardened cannot believe even at every sign (96–97); Jonah's town, the exception that repented and profited (98); the counterfactuals that close coercion forever (99–100); the religion line (104–105) and the guidance-for-one's-own-soul declaration (108); the two closural commands — follow the revealed, be patient until judgment (109).
