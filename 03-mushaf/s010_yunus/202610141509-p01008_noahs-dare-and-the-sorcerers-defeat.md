# Noah's dare, the drowned and the successors, and the sorcerers' defeat before Pharaoh (10:71–82)
**UID:** 202610141509
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 10:71–82 (Ruku 8 of 11 in Yunus)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[10:71]` – `[10:82]` (12 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The messenger-history enters as evidence for 10:47's law: Noah, cornered, throws the case on Allah — "in Allah I have put my trust... come to a decision, you and your partners... then carry out your decision on me" — asks no wage, is commanded to be of those who submit; he is denounced, saved with his company, made successors, and his opponents drowned ("So consider the fate of those who were warned"); after him a chain of messengers meets people who will not believe what they already rejected — hearts sealed; then Moses and Aaron before Pharaoh: truth named "clearly sorcery," the experienced sorcerers assembled, their production pronounced magic that Allah will make fail — and the ruku closes on the principle, "Allah upholds the truth with His words, even though the sinners detest it."
* **Placement:** Eighth ruku — the surah's narrative exhibit A. Note the honest limits of the text: the magicians never speak or believe here (that scene belongs to 7:120–126 and 20:70, verified); Yunus keeps only the confrontation and the law of the outcome; the drowning and creed are deferred to ruku 9 (10:90).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[10:71]`** "And relate to them the story of Noah, when he said to his people, “O my people, if my presence among you and my reminding you of Allah’s signs is too much for you, then in Allah I have put my trust. So come to a decision, you and your partners, and do not let the matter perplex you; then carry out your decision on me, and do not hold back.”"
  * **Gloss:** Total tawakkul plus an open dare — the "you and your partners" address that 10:28 will answer on the Day.
* **`[10:72]`** "“But if you turn away, I have not asked you for any wage. My wage falls only on Allah, and I was commanded to be of those who submit.”"
  * **Gloss:** The non-transactional claim — no wage (cf. 26:109, 38:86, 42:23, verified) — and the command to be among the submitters (27:91, 6:163, 39:12, verified).
* **`[10:73]`** "But they denounced him, so We saved him and those with him in the Ark, and We made them successors, and We drowned those who rejected Our signs. So consider the fate of those who were warned."
  * **Gloss:** The cycle in one verse — denunciation, salvation, succession, drowning; near-verbatim twin at 7:64 (verified); "consider the fate" = the surah's pedagogy (10:39, 10:101–102).
* **`[10:74]`** "Then, after him, We sent messengers to their people. They came to them with the clear proofs, but they would not believe in anything they had already rejected. Thus We set a seal on the hearts of the hostile."
  * **Gloss:** The chain generalizes Noah's sociology: pre-rejection hardens into seal — seal formula cf. 2:7, 40:35 (verified).
* **`[10:75]`** "Then, after them, We sent Moses and Aaron with Our proofs to Pharaoh and his dignitaries. But they acted arrogantly. They were sinful people."
  * **Gloss:** The two-brother mission — Aaron is named only here in the surah (10:87 will say "Moses and his brother", observable in corpus); arrogance as the pharaonic trait.
* **`[10:76]`** "And when the truth came to them from Us, they said, “This is clearly sorcery.”"
  * **Gloss:** The same first-word verdict as 10:2 on the Prophet himself — sorcery-label twins at 40:24, 34:43, 51:52, 28:36 (verified).
* **`[10:77]`** "Moses said, “Is this what you say of the truth when it has come to you? Is this sorcery? Sorcerers do not succeed.”"
  * **Gloss:** The retort-of-principle: 20:69 "the magician will not succeed, no matter what he does" (near-verbatim, verified).
* **`[10:78]`** "They said, “Did you come to us to divert us from what we found our ancestors following, and so that you become prominent in the land? We will never believe in you.”"
  * **Gloss:** The motive diagnosis — inheritance-religion and power-anxiety; ancestor-clause twin 28:36 "We never heard of this from our ancestors of old" (verified).
* **`[10:79]`** "Pharaoh said, “Bring me every experienced sorcerer.”"
  * **Gloss:** The regime's answer is technical: 26:37 "every experienced magician" assembled (near-verbatim, verified).
* **`[10:80]`** "And when the sorcerers came, Moses said to them, “Throw whatever you have to throw.”"
  * **Gloss:** The court session opens; cf. 20:65 "either you throw, or we will be the first to throw" (verified).
* **`[10:81]`** "And when they threw, Moses said, “What you produced is sorcery, and Allah will make it fail. Allah does not foster the efforts of the corrupt.”"
  * **Gloss:** Sorcery named from the other side and declared structurally failing — corruption (ifsād) and its non-fostered efforts, cf. 2:205, 28:4 (verified).
* **`[10:82]`** "“And Allah upholds the truth with His words, even though the sinners detest it.”"
  * **Gloss:** The ruku's law-statement: truth is upheld by divine words — the kalam clause under the trial (corpus-unique wording, verified).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`put my trust` (tawakkul):** Noah's posture, echoed by his people's heirs.
  * **Rendering:** "then in Allah I have put my trust" (10:71); answered at 10:85 "In Allah we have put our trust" (internal).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[7:89]` "In Allah we place our trust" (Hud's people-to-God plea, verified); `[11:56]` "I have placed my trust in Allah, my Lord and your Lord" (verified); `[60:4]` "In You we trust" (verified).
* **`wage` (ajr):** the prophet's non-transaction.
  * **Rendering:** "My wage falls only on Allah" (10:72).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[26:109]`, `[11:51]`, `[38:86]`, `[42:23]`, `[12:104]`, `[36:21]` (verified) — the wage-denial formula across the prophet-sermons.
* **`seal` (khatm):** the hardening of pre-rejection.
  * **Rendering:** "Thus We set a seal on the hearts of the hostile" (10:74).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:7]` "Allah has set a seal on their hearts" (verified); `[40:35]` "Thus Allah seals the heart of every proud bully" (verified); cf. `[45:23]` sealed hearing and heart (verified).
* **`sorcery` (sihr):** the shared first word of every regime in this surah.
  * **Rendering:** "This is clearly sorcery" (10:76) — same verdict as 10:2's "manifest sorcerer" against the warner himself.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[40:24]` "A lying sorcerer"; `[34:43]` "plain magic" at the arrival of truth; `[51:52]` "A sorcerer or a madman" — the recurring stock label (verified).
* **`Allah upholds the truth with His words` (yuhiqq al-haqq bi-kalimātih):** 10:82.
  * **Rendering:** verbatim clause; corpus-unique (verified) — the words themselves are the instrument of truth's victory.
  * **Elsewhere:** the same divine-words grammar appears at `[6:115]` "The Word of your Lord has been completed, in truth and justice" (verified) and is refuted-negatively at 10:64 "no alteration to the words of Allah".
