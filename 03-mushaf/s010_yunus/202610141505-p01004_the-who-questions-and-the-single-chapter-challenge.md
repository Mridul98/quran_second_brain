# The who-questions, and "He has forged it" answered by one chapter (10:31–40)
**UID:** 202610141505
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 10:31–40 (Ruku 4 of 11 in Yunus)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[10:31]` – `[10:40]` (10 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** A rapid-fire interrogation — who provides, who controls hearing and sight, who produces living from dead, who governs the Order, who originates and repeats creation, who guides to truth? — expects the crowd's own answer "Allah" and uses it to evict the partners from every divine function; most people nonetheless "follow nothing but assumptions," which is where the Book's credibility defense lands: this Quran could not have been produced by anyone other than Allah, and the counter-charge "He has forged it" is answered with the wager of a single chapter.
* **Placement:** Ruku 4 is the argument's center of gravity: ruku 1's creed (10:3) has become cross-examination, and the inimitability challenge here (37–38) is the courtroom's evidence test; the ruku's two halves — tawhid interrogation and recital defense — are the surah's thesis stated as technique.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[10:31]`** "Say, “Who provides for you from the heaven and the earth? And who controls the hearing and the sight? And who produces the living from the dead, and produces the dead from the living? And who governs the Order?” They will say, “Allah.” Say, “Will you not be careful?”"
  * **Gloss:** Four jurisdiction questions with a scripted answer; provision twin 35:3, living/dead twin 30:19 and 6:95, hearing/sight seizure twin 6:46 (all verified); the closing "Will you not be careful?" matches 23:85's "Will you not reflect?" family (verified).
* **`[10:32]`** "Such is Allah, your Lord—the True. What is there, beyond the truth, except falsehood? How are you turned away?"
  * **Gloss:** An aphorism without corpus twin (verified) — truth's complement set is only falsehood; turning away is therefore self-deception, not choice.
* **`[10:33]`** "Thus your Lord’s Word proved true against those who disobeyed, for they do not believe."
  * **Gloss:** The Word as settled verdict — same construction as 41:25 "the Word proved true against them" (verified); will be quoted back at 10:96.
* **`[10:34]`** "Say, “Can any of your partners initiate creation, and then repeat it?” Say, “Allah initiates creation, and then repeats it. How are you so deluded?”"
  * **Gloss:** Origination-resurrection removed from partner competence (30:11 and 27:64 twins, verified).
* **`[10:35]`** "Say, “Can any of your partners guide to the truth?” Say, “Allah guides to the truth. Is He who guides to the truth more worthy of being followed, or he who does not guide, unless he himself is guided? What is the matter with you? How do you judge?”"
  * **Gloss:** Guidance too is exclusive — the unguided cannot even lead themselves (cf. 22:54, 24:46, verified); the double question "What is the matter with you? How do you judge?" recurs verbatim at 37:154 and 68:36 (verified).
* **`[10:36]`** "Most of them follow nothing but assumptions; and assumptions avail nothing against the truth. Allah is fully aware of what they do."
  * **Gloss:** The epistemic indictment — conjecture (zan) is the partners' only fuel; near-verbatim twin 53:28 (verified).
* **`[10:37]`** "This Quran could not have been produced by anyone other than Allah. In fact, it is a confirmation of what preceded it, and an elaboration of the Book. There is no doubt about it—it is from the Lord of the Universe."
  * **Gloss:** Impossibility claim plus function claim: it confirms prior scripture and elaborates the Book — the same twin verbs as 12:111 and 2:97 and 3:3 (verified).
* **`[10:38]`** "Or do they say, “He has forged it”? Say, “Then produce a single chapter like it, and call upon whomever you can, apart from Allah, if you are truthful.”"
  * **Gloss:** The surah's charge in one line — "He has forged it" — answered with the minimized challenge (one chapter; cf. 2:23 "a chapter like these" and 11:13 "ten chapters," verified: the challenge ladder).
* **`[10:39]`** "In fact, they deny what is beyond the limits of their knowledge, and whose explanation has not yet reached them. Thus those before them refused to believe. So note the consequences for the wrongdoers."
  * **Gloss:** The psychology of denial: rejecting what exceeds their grasp before its interpretation arrives — cf. 53:28 "They have no knowledge of that" (verified).
* **`[10:40]`** "Among them are those who believe in it, and among them are those who do not believe in it. Your Lord is fully aware of the mischief-makers."
  * **Gloss:** The crowd splits; the mischief-makers (mufsidūn) are known — the word ties to 10:81's "efforts of the corrupt" and 2:205 (verified).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`the Order` (al-amr):** 10:31's fourth jurisdiction.
  * **Rendering:** "who governs the Order?" — noun form of the same verb as 10:3's "governing all things."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[13:2]` "He manages all affairs... that you may be certain of the meeting with your Lord" (verified) — governance aimed at the encounter 10:7 already sorted people by.
* **`assumptions` (ẓann):** conjecture as the partners' religion.
  * **Rendering:** "follow nothing but assumptions; and assumptions avail nothing against the truth" (10:36).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[53:28]` "They only follow assumptions, and assumptions are no substitute for the truth" (near-verbatim twin, verified); `[53:23]` idols as "nothing but names... They follow nothing but assumptions" (verified); internally `[10:66]` "they follow only assumptions" (same line reused at 10:36, verbatim twin, verified).
* **`forged/invented` (iftirā'):** the charge and the counter-charge.
  * **Rendering:** "He has forged it" (10:38) — the assignment's "He invented it" appears in this edition with this verb choice; the parallel charge at `[11:13]` is "He invented it?" and at `[46:8]` "He invented it himself?" (verified).
  * **Elsewhere:** the accusation is reflected back as fabrication-against-Allah at 10:17/10:59–60/10:69 (internal twins of 6:21 and 16:116, verified).
* **`confirmation` (muṣaddiq) + `elaboration` (tafsīr):** the Book's twin functions in 10:37.
  * **Rendering:** "a confirmation of what preceded it, and an elaboration of the Book."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:97]` "confirming what preceded it, and guidance and good news for the believers"; `[3:3]`; `[12:111]` "a confirmation of what came before it, and a detailed explanation of all things"; `[11:1]` "perfected, then elaborated" (all verified).
