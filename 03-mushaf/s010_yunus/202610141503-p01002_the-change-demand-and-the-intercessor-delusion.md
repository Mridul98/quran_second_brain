# The demand to change the recital, the man who cannot, and the intercessors unmasked (10:11–20)
**UID:** 202610141503
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 10:11–20 (Ruku 2 of 11 in Yunus)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[10:11]` – `[10:20]` (10 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Three crowd-demands are refused in order — accelerate the bad as you accelerate the good (11), "Bring a Quran other than this, or change it" (15), "If only a miracle was sent down" (20) — and the prophet's answer each time is incapacity: he follows, he cannot swap, the unseen is not his to produce; between the refusals sit the precedent of destroyed successor-generations (13–14), the charge of lie-fabrication (17), and the exposure of the "intercessors with Allah" as entities Allah knows nothing of (18).
* **Placement:** Second ruku of the credibility trial; it converts ruku 1's creed clause "no intercessor except after His permission" into an interrogation of the idols' claim, and its "change it" refusal is the surah's first inimitability moment, answered in kind at 10:37–38.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[10:11]`** "If Allah were to accelerate the ill for the people, as they wish to accelerate the good, their term would have been fulfilled. But We leave those who do not expect Our encounter to blunder in their excesses."
  * **Gloss:** Divine delay is mercy's architecture; the impatient are left to blunder (the same verb as 6:110 and 7:186, verified).
* **`[10:12]`** "Whenever adversity touches the human being, he prays to Us—reclining on his side, or sitting, or standing. But when We have relieved his adversity from him, he goes away, as though he had never called on Us for trouble that had afflicted him. Thus the deeds of the transgressors appear good to them."
  * **Gloss:** All postures of desperate prayer, then instant amnesia — human faithfulness measured by adversity's half-life (twin scene: 41:51, verified).
* **`[10:13]`** "We destroyed generations before you when they did wrong. Their messengers came to them with clear signs, but they would not believe. Thus We requite the sinful people."
  * **Gloss:** The historical precedent clause — messenger + clear signs + refusal + requital, the surah's cycle formula in embryo.
* **`[10:14]`** "Then We made you successors on earth after them, to see how you would behave."
  * **Gloss:** Succession (*khalā'if*) is staged as examination — cf. 7:129 "then He will see how you behave" and 6:165 (verified).
* **`[10:15]`** "And when Our clear revelations are recited to them, those who do not hope to meet Us say, “Bring a Quran other than this, or change it.” Say, “It is not for me to change it of my own accord. I only follow what is revealed to me. I fear, if I disobeyed my Lord, the torment of a terrible Day.”"
  * **Gloss:** The trial's central demand — swap or edit the recital; the defendant's plea is total non-ownership of the speech-act ("I only follow what is revealed to me").
* **`[10:16]`** "Say, “Had Allah willed, I would not have recited it to you, and He would not have made it known to you. I have lived among you for a lifetime before it. Do you not understand?”"
  * **Gloss:** The recital is an event that overtook a known life — the biographical check (the "lifetime among you" claim is unique in the corpus, verified).
* **`[10:17]`** "Who does greater wrong than someone who fabricates lies about Allah, or denies His revelations? The guilty will never prosper."
  * **Gloss:** The forgery charge turned back on its holders — near-verbatim twin of 6:21 (verified).
* **`[10:18]`** "And they worship, besides Allah, what neither harms them nor benefits them. And they say, “These are our intercessors with Allah.” Say, “Are you informing Allah about what He does not know in the heavens or on earth?” Glorified be He, High above the associations they make."
  * **Gloss:** Ruku 1's creed clause detonated: the intercessor-list is an accusation against God's knowledge (paired interrogation: 21:66, 25:55, 39:43–44, verified).
* **`[10:19]`** "Mankind was a single community; then they differed. Were it not for a prior decree from your Lord, the matters over which they had disputed would have been settled."
  * **Gloss:** The one-community thesis — difference is the condition of history, deferred judgment its mercy (twin: 2:213, verified).
* **`[10:20]`** "And they say, “If only a miracle was sent down to him from his Lord.” Say, “The realm of the unseen belongs to Allah; so wait, I am waiting with you.”"
  * **Gloss:** The third demand refused with an ownership-claim (27:65: "No one in the heavens or on earth knows the future except Allah," verified) and the wait-formula this surah will repeat at 10:102.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`change it` (tabdīl):** the demand of 10:15.
  * **Rendering:** "Bring a Quran other than this, or change it" — corpus-unique pairing of swap-and-edit (verified: "Quran other than" has no other corpus site).
  * **Elsewhere:** answered structurally at `[10:37–38]` — the Book cannot be produced by any, and the challengers are called to one chapter; and at `[10:109]` "follow what is revealed to you" — the prophet's permanent answer to edit-requests.
* **`fabricates lies about Allah` (yaftarī 'alā Allah):** the crime both sides charge.
  * **Rendering:** "Who does greater wrong than someone who fabricates lies about Allah, or denies His revelations?" (10:17).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[6:21]` verbatim twin (verified); `[16:116]` "in order to invent lies and attribute them to Allah. Those who invent lies and attribute them to Allah will not succeed" (10:59–60's food-law application, verified); `[20:61]` "He who invents lies will fail" (verified).
* **`successors` (khulafā'):** 10:14's address to the present audience.
  * **Rendering:** "We made you successors on earth after them, to see how you would behave."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[6:165]`, `[7:69]` (successors *after the people of Noah* — this surah's own ruku 8, verified), `[7:129]`, `[24:55]`, `[35:39]` (verified).
* **`single community` (ummah wāhida):** 10:19's anthropology.
  * **Rendering:** "Mankind was a single community; then they differed."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:213]` "Humanity used to be one community; then Allah sent the prophets, bringing good news and giving warnings" (verified) — the difference-and-prophets pair; `[21:92]`, `[43:33]`, `[42:8]`, `[11:118]` (verified).
