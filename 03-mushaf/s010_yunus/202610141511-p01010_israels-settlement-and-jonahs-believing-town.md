# Israel settled, the Word justified — except Jonah's town; and no compulsion (10:93–103)
**UID:** 202610141511
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 10:93–103 (Ruku 10 of 11 in Yunus)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[10:93]` – `[10:103]` (11 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The history exhibit closes where every warning-story ends — settlement: Israel placed in a position of honor, provisioned, and divided only after knowledge came; then the doubter is sent to the Scripture-readers before him (94–95), because some are beyond signs entirely: those against whom the Lord's Word is justified will not believe even if every sign comes (96–97) — with one exception ever, in the whole human record: the people of Jonah, who believed, and whose disgrace was lifted (98); therefore coercion is ruled out of God's own procedure — had He willed, all on earth would believe (99), faith is by Allah's leave (100), the command is to look, not to compel (101), and the rest is waiting for the days of the forefathers, until the closing law: We save Our messengers and those who believe — a promise binding on Us (102–103).
* **Placement:** Tenth ruku; the verdict on hardening plus the surah's anti-coercion settlement — the theological ground for the religion-line of ruku 11; the Jonah exception is the surah's namesake moment and its only happy ending in the history cycle.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[10:93]`** "And We settled the Children of Israel in a position of honor, and provided them with good things. They did not differ until knowledge came to them. Your Lord will judge between them on the Day of Resurrection regarding their differences."
  * **Gloss:** Settlement — the "position of honor" phrase is corpus-unique (verified); difference-after-knowledge is the 42:14 formula (verified); judgment deferred, as in 10:19.
* **`[10:94]`** "If you are in doubt about what We revealed to you, ask those who read the Scripture before you. The truth has come to you from your Lord, so do not be of those who doubt."
  * **Gloss:** The verification clause of the credibility trial: consult the prior recitations (pair: 16:43, 21:7 "ask the people of knowledge," verified) — yet sealed immediately by the truth-arrival claim.
* **`[10:95]`** "And do not be of those who deny Allah’s revelations, lest you become one of the losers."
  * **Gloss:** The loser-warning formula (cf. 39:65's "you will be of the losers", verified).
* **`[10:96]`** "Those against whom your Lord’s Word is justified will not believe."
  * **Gloss:** The 10:33 Word-clause recycled as a class-definition — divine speech can function as a verdict (cf. 41:25, verified).
* **`[10:97]`** "Even if every sign comes to them—until they see the painful punishment."
  * **Gloss:** Sign-saturation cannot reverse the justified Word — the mechanism of 10:51–53, 10:88b, and 10:91 in one line.
* **`[10:98]`** "If only there was one town that believed and benefited by its belief. Except for the people of Jonah. When they believed, We removed from them the suffering of disgrace in the worldly life, and We gave them comfort for a while."
  * **Gloss:** The counter-exception to 96–97: the only town whose late-ish belief profited; the surah's namesake prophet finally appears by his people's repentance — pair: 37:147–148 "We sent him to a hundred thousand, or more. And they believed, so We gave them enjoyment for a while" (verified); "disgrace" pair 11:66 (verified); the Jonah himself story: 21:87 (verified).
* **`[10:99]`** "Had your Lord willed, everyone on earth would have believed. Will you compel people to become believers?"
  * **Gloss:** The will-counterfactual (cf. 42:8, 11:118, verified) plus the rhetorical ban on compulsion — the verse-side twin of 2:256 "There shall be no compulsion in religion" (verified); cf. 18:29's "Whoever wills—let him believe" (verified).
* **`[10:100]`** "No soul can believe except by Allah’s leave; and He lays disgrace upon those who refuse to understand."
  * **Gloss:** Faith's license sits with God (permission-mechanism again — cf. 10:3, internal); disgrace is poured on those who refuse to understand — cf. 11:66 "the disgrace of that day" (verified).
* **`[10:101]`** "Say, “Look at what is in the heavens and the earth.” But signs and warnings are of no avail for people who do not believe."
  * **Gloss:** The observational command — same imperative type as 16:36's "travel through the earth and see... the fate of the deniers" (verified) — addressed to a crowd already defined as sign-insensitive.
* **`[10:102]`** "Do they expect anything but the likes of the days of those who passed away before them? Say, “Then wait, I will be waiting with you.”"
  * **Gloss:** The days-of-the-forefathers clause (cf. 45:14 and 14:5 "Days of Allah," verified) closes with the wait-formula first spoken at 10:20 — the surah's ring composition (verified internally).
* **`[10:103]`** "Then We save Our messengers and those who believe. It is binding on Us to save the believers."
  * **Gloss:** The cycle's ending law — God underwrites His own rescue promise (cf. 21:104 "a promise binding on Us," verified); the ruku's hardening clause (96) does not discharge this one (96 is about belief, 103 about salvation — agent observation).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`people of Jonah` (qawm Yūnus):** the exception.
  * **Rendering:** "Except for the people of Jonah. When they believed, We removed from them the suffering of disgrace."
  * **Elsewhere:** "Jonah" mentioned by name at `[21:87]` (the storm-out, the cry in the darkness), `[37:139–148]` (the full mini-cycle: "We sent him to a hundred thousand, or more. And they believed," 37:147–148, verified), `[6:86]`, `[4:163]` (name-lists, verified) — but the "people of Jonah" phrase occurs only at 10:98 in this corpus (verified); it is the sole success report of a warned town in the Quran, which is why the surah carries his name.
* **`compel` (ikrāh):** the banned procedure.
  * **Rendering:** "Will you compel people to become believers?"
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:256]` "There shall be no compulsion in religion; the right way has become distinct from the wrong way" (verified); `[18:29]` "The truth is from your Lord. Whoever wills—let him believe" (verified); `[16:106]` compelled apostasy excused while the heart rests in faith (verified) — the same word used in the other direction: coercion voids the claim, it never creates faith.
* **`Allah's leave` (idhn):** faith's license.
  * **Rendering:** "No soul can believe except by Allah’s leave."
  * **Elsewhere:** the permission-mechanism runs through this surah — 10:3 intercession "except after His permission"; 10:59 "Did Allah give you permission?" (internal, verified); externally cf. `[20:109]` intercession "except for him permitted by the Merciful" (verified) — belief, intercession, and dietary law all turn on the same license (agent observation).
* **`Days of Allah` (ayyām Allāh):** the expected event.
  * **Rendering:** "the likes of the days of those who passed away before them."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[45:14]` "forgive those who do not hope for the Days of Allah"; `[14:5]` "remind them of the Days of Allah" (both verified) — the idiom's home ground is the destruction-cycle Yunus has just recited.
* **`binding on Us` (waʿdan ʿalainā):** the rescue guarantee.
  * **Rendering:** "It is binding on Us to save the believers."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[21:104]` "a promise binding on Us. We will act" (verified); `[9:111]` "a promise binding on Him in the Torah, and the Gospel, and the Quran" (verified) — promise-language that also grounds 10:55's "the promise of Allah is true".

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Meccan (classical consensus); the no-compulsion settlement here belongs to the Meccan phase's non-coercive preaching — *Source: agent inference from the surah's position — verify against chronological tafsir; the Medinan 2:256 sits in a different legal context.*
