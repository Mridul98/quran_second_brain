# My religion and yours: the monotheist line, and patience until the Best of judges (10:104–109)
**UID:** 202610141512
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 10:104–109 (Ruku 11 of 11 in Yunus)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[10:104]` – `[10:109]` (6 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The verdict having been read, the surah closes with the prophet's public religion-line — "if you are in doubt about my religion: I do not serve what you serve apart from Allah... I was commanded to be of the believers" — the hanif command ("dedicate yourself to the true religion... never be of the polytheists"), the prohibition of calling on what profits and harms nothing, the monopoly of harm-removal and grace-repelling in Allah's hand, and the responsibility declaration ("whoever accepts guidance is guided for his own soul... I am not a guardian over you"); then, to the Messenger himself, the two final commands: follow what is revealed to you, and be patient until Allah issues His judgment — for He is the Best of judges.
* **Placement:** The closing ruku; it answers the opening ruku's challenge (10:2 "Warn mankind") with the warner's bounded role, and converts ruku 2's "I only follow what is revealed to me" (10:15) into the surah's last word — follow, and be patient. The "commanded to be of those who submit" of Noah (10:72) returns as "commanded to be of the believers" (10:104): the prophet-cycle closes on the same command.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[10:104]`** "Say, “O people, if you are in doubt about my religion—I do not serve those you serve apart from Allah. But I serve Allah, the one who will terminate your lives. And I was commanded to be of the believers.”"
  * **Gloss:** The religion-line — service separated without coercion; death in God's hand alone ("the one who will terminate your lives" — cf. 39:42 souls taken at death, verified); the command-formula repeats Noah's (10:72; cf. 27:91, 6:163, 39:12, verified).
* **`[10:105]`** "And dedicate yourself to the true religion—a monotheist—and never be of the polytheists."
  * **Gloss:** The hanif command (toward the prophet, classically — Source: consensus of classical tafsir on 10:105); verbatim twin of 30:30's "This is the true religion" frame and its twin verb "devote/turn your face" (verified); cf. 13:36 "I am commanded to worship Allah, and to never associate anything with Him" (verified).
* **`[10:106]`** "And do not call, apart from Allah, on what neither benefits you nor harms you. If you do, you are then one of the wrongdoers."
  * **Gloss:** The powerless-object prohibition, restating 10:18's description in command form (internal); twin renderings at 25:55 "serve what neither profits them nor harms them" and 21:66 Abraham's question (verified); the loss-logic is 39:65's "if you idolize, your works will be in vain" (verified).
* **`[10:107]`** "If Allah afflicts you with harm, none can remove it except He. And if He wants good for you, none can repel His grace. He makes it reach whomever He wills of His servants. He is the Forgiver, the Merciful."
  * **Gloss:** Removal and repelling both monopolized — near-twins 6:17 "if Allah touches you with adversity, none can remove it except He" and 35:2 none can withhold or release mercy (verified); worship is re-anchored in that monopoly.
* **`[10:108]`** "Say, “O people, the truth has come to you from your Lord. Whoever accepts guidance is guided for his own soul; and whoever strays only strays to its detriment. I am not a guardian over you.”"
  * **Gloss:** The responsibility clause — verbatim family of 17:15 "Whoever is guided—is guided for his own good..." and of 6:104 "I am not a guardian over you" (both verified); the truth-arrival line restates 10:94 (internal ring).
* **`[10:109]`** "And follow what is revealed to you, and be patient until Allah issues His judgment, for He is the Best of judges."
  * **Gloss:** The two closural commands: adhere to the recital (cf. 33:2, 43:43 "So adhere to what is revealed to you," verified) and hold patience until judgment — 76:24 "be patient for the decision of your Lord" and 52:48 "So patiently await the decision of your Lord" (verified); "Best of judges" is the same doxology as Hud's prayer "You are the Best of Deciders" (7:89, verified).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`my religion` (dīnī):** 10:104's boundary word.
  * **Rendering:** "if you are in doubt about my religion—I do not serve those you serve apart from Allah."
  * **Elsewhere:** the exchange-formula peaks in `[109:1–6]`: "I do not worship what you worship... You have your way, and I have my way" (verified — Itani renders 109:6 "way," not "religion"); within Yunus the deeds-version is 10:41 "I have my deeds, and you have your deeds" (verified) — three gradations of the same disavowal (agent observation).
* **`monotheist` (ḥanīf):** the 10:105 stance.
  * **Rendering:** "dedicate yourself to the true religion—a monotheist—and never be of the polytheists."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[30:30]` "So devote yourself to the religion of monotheism—the natural instinct Allah has instilled in mankind" (verified); `[6:161]` "an upright religion, the creed of Abraham the Monotheist, who was not a polytheist" (verified) — hanif = the Abraham-orientation of wholehearted turning (agent gloss on the two cited renderings).
* **`guardian` (wakīl):** the prophet's disclaimed office.
  * **Rendering:** "I am not a guardian over you."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[6:104]` identical closing line (verified); `[6:107]` "We did not appoint you as a guardian over them, and you are not a manager over them"; `[42:48]` "We did not send you as a guardian over them. Your only duty is communication" (both verified) — guardianship belongs to God alone, which is also 10:30's "their True Master".
* **`Best of judges` (khayr al-ḥākimīn):** the doxology that ends the surah.
  * **Rendering:** "He is the Best of judges."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[7:89]` "You are the Best of Deciders" — Hud's people's prayer, same superlative construction (verified) — the prophet-cycle's own words return as the surah's final comfort.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Meccan (classical consensus); the religion-line and the non-guardian clause suit the Meccan preaching phase's powerless-address style — *Source: agent inference from genre — verify.*
* **Wording check (assignment):** the closural patience/command verse is exactly `[10:109]` — follow + be patient + God the Best of judges; there is no later verse in this surah, and no anti-innovation (bid'a) clause anywhere in it (observable corpus fact).
