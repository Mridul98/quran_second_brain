# The trusting few, Moses' terrible prayer, and Pharaoh's refused creed at the drowning (10:83–92)
**UID:** 202610141510
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 10:83–92 (Ruku 9 of 11 in Yunus)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[10:83]` – `[10:92]` (10 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Belief in Moses is socially impossible — "none believed in Moses except some children of his people," out of fear of Pharaoh — so Moses converts their fear into trust conditional on submission, and they answer with trust and two prayers; God moves the community's infrastructure (homes in Egypt made places of worship) and answers Moses' devastating counter-prayer — obliterate their wealth, harden their hearts, they will not believe until they see the painful torment — with "Your prayer has been answered, so go straight." Then the crossing: Pharaoh pursues, and at the moment of drowning confesses the creed of the Children of Israel's God — and it is refused ("Now?"), his body preserved instead as a sign.
* **Placement:** Ninth ruku: the live case of the surah's two hardest doctrines — trust under persecution, and faith-at-sighting as no faith (10:51's question, answered here dramatically); the fear-to-trust turn instantiates 10:62–63's definition of Allah's friends.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[10:83]`** "But none believed in Moses except some children of his people, for fear that Pharaoh and his chiefs would persecute them. Pharaoh was high and mighty in the land. He was a tyrant."
  * **Gloss:** The faith-scarcity clause (corpus-unique phrasing, verified); the tyranny double-portrait — "high and mighty" also unique to this verse (verified); cf. 28:4 Pharaoh exalting and factioning (verified).
* **`[10:84]`** "Moses said, “O my people, if you have believed in Allah, then put your trust in Him, if you have submitted.”"
  * **Gloss:** Faith and submission named as the premises; trust (tawakkul) as the conclusion — the logic of 10:71 restated for the new remnant.
* **`[10:85]`** "They said, “In Allah we have put our trust. Our Lord, do not make us victims of the oppressive people.”"
  * **Gloss:** The answer-formula mirrors 7:89 and 60:4 (verified); the victim-prayer anticipates the homes-as-sanctuaries order.
* **`[10:86]`** "“And deliver us, by Your mercy, from the disbelieving people.”"
  * **Gloss:** Mercy as the operative cause of deliverance — cf. 10:21's tasted mercy, internal.
* **`[10:87]`** "And We inspired Moses and his brother, “Settle your people in Egypt, and make your homes places of worship, and perform the prayer, and give good news to the believers.”"
  * **Gloss:** Liturgy decentralized under tyranny — homes become mosques (cf. 72:18 "The places of worship are for Allah," verified); good-news repeats 10:2's charge (internal).
* **`[10:88]`** "Moses said, “Our Lord, you have given Pharaoh and his chiefs splendor and wealth in the worldly life. Our Lord, for them to lead away from Your path. Our Lord, obliterate their wealth, and harden their hearts, they will not believe until they see the painful torment.”"
  * **Gloss:** One of the Quran's most severe prophet-prayers — wealth erased, hearts sealed for sight, the term fixed (hardening formula cf. 2:74, 39:22, 6:43, verified).
* **`[10:89]`** "He said, “Your prayer has been answered, so go straight, and do not follow the path of those who do not know.”"
  * **Gloss:** The dual answer — the tyrant's ruin and the people's guidance granted at once; "go straight" (fastaqim) is 11:112's command (verified); the path-of-ignorance clause is 45:18's (verified).
* **`[10:90]`** "And We delivered the Children of Israel across the sea. Pharaoh and his troops pursued them, defiantly and aggressively. Until, when he was about to drown, he said, “I believe that there is no god except the One the Children of Israel believe in, and I am of those who submit.”"
  * **Gloss:** Pursuit (cf. 20:78 "the sea overwhelmed them," verified); the deathbed creed — worded uniquely in this corpus, borrowing the language of the very people he enslaved (verified).
* **`[10:91]`** "Now? When you have rebelled before, and been of the mischief-makers?"
  * **Gloss:** The one-word refusal — the "Now?" of 10:51 spoken to its test case; transaction closed at sighting.
* **`[10:92]`** "Today We will preserve your body, so that you become a sign for those after you. But most people are heedless of Our signs."
  * **Gloss:** The body as exhibit — corpus-unique phrase (verified) — and the closing jab: heedlessness (ghafla) again the crowd's disease (cf. 10:7 internal, verified).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`put your trust` (tawakkal):** the remnant's act.
  * **Rendering:** "then put your trust in Him, if you have submitted" (10:84); "In Allah we have put our trust" (10:85).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[7:89]`, `[11:56]`, `[60:4]` (verified); internally `[10:71]` — Noah's dare-formula answered by Israel's youth.
* **`places of worship` (masjid):** 10:87's domestic sanctuary.
  * **Rendering:** "make your homes places of worship."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[72:18]` "The places of worship are for Allah. So do not call, besides Allah, upon anyone else"; `[2:114]` (remembrance in places of worship); `[9:17–18]` (who may attend them) (all verified) — a persecuted community's portable temple.
* **`harden` (qaswa):** the request against the enemy's heart.
  * **Rendering:** "harden their hearts, they will not believe until they see the painful torment."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:74]` "your hearts hardened... as rocks, or even harder"; `[39:22]` "whose hearts are hardened against the mention of Allah"; `[6:43]` "their hearts hardened" at calamity (verified) — Moses asks for his people's opposite number what Israel itself feared (agent observation).
* **`go straight` (istaqāma):** the answered-prayer command.
  * **Rendering:** "so go straight."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[11:112]` "So be upright, as you are commanded" (verified — same verb root); `[41:30]` "then go straight, the angels will descend... 'Do not fear'" (verified) — straightness is the friends-of-God biography of 10:62–63.
* **`drowning / body` (gharq, jasād):** the refused creed and its exhibit.
  * **Rendering:** "when he was about to drown... I believe... Now?"; "We will preserve your body."
  * **Elsewhere:** drowning scene `[20:78]` and `[7:136]` (verified, without any creed speech); the body line has no corpus parallel (verified) — Yunus alone stages Pharaoh's mouth.
