# Quit of your deeds: the deaf audience, the appointed time, and faith when it falls (10:41–53)
**UID:** 202610141506
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 10:41–53 (Ruku 5 of 11 in Yunus)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[10:41]` – `[10:53]` (13 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** If the verdict is rejection, the split is final — "I have my deeds, and you have your deeds; you are quit of what I do, and I am quit of what you do" — because the prophet cannot unlock ears or eyes anyway (42–43), Allah wrongs no one (44), and the gathering Day will compress a lifetime into an hour (45); the ruku then fixes the law behind all timetable-mockery: every community had a messenger, and every nation has an appointed time no hour can move (47–49), so belief that arrives only when punishment falls is arrival too late (51–53).
* **Placement:** Fifth ruku; it hands the courtroom its procedural rules — disavowal, non-coercion, appointed times — and its two formulas ("wait" from 10:20 returns implicitly; "When will this promise?" will return at 10:102's days-of-the-forefathers close). The deeds-disavowal here is the surah's tolerance moment — the assignment's "to you your religion" belongs closer to 10:104, not this verse (verified in the text).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[10:41]`** "If they accuse you of lying, say, “I have my deeds, and you have your deeds. You are quit of what I do, and I am quit of what you do.”"
  * **Gloss:** Mutual discharge of works — the "quit of" formula is Abraham's disavowal in 60:4 (verified); deeds separated, persons left to their return.
* **`[10:42]`** "And among them are those who listen to you. But can you make the deaf hear, even though they do not understand?"
  * **Gloss:** An audience that hears without understanding — sensory siege, cf. 43:40 and the hypocrite-dossier's "Deaf, dumb, blind" (2:18, verified).
* **`[10:43]`** "And among them are those who look at you. But can you guide the blind, even though they do not see?"
  * **Gloss:** Watching without seeing; the twin questions appear at 30:53 and 27:81, and the pairing at 6:39 (all verified).
* **`[10:44]`** "Allah does not wrong the people in the least, but the people wrong their own selves."
  * **Gloss:** The theodicy clause; same form as 30:9 and 9:70 (verified).
* **`[10:45]`** "On the Day when He rounds them up—as if they had tarried only one hour of a day— they will recognize one another. Those who denied the meeting with Allah will be the losers. They were not guided."
  * **Gloss:** The roundup; a life shrinks to an hour (corpus-unique compression, verified); the losers of 10:7's "meeting" motif are sentenced here.
* **`[10:46]`** "Whether We show you some of what We promise them, or take you, to Us is their return. Allah is witness to everything they do."
  * **Gloss:** The prophet's indifference clause — show or take, the return is not his to manage (near-twin 13:40: "your duty is to inform, and Ours is the reckoning," verified).
* **`[10:47]`** "Every community has a messenger. When their messenger has come, judgment will be passed between them with fairness, and they will not be wronged."
  * **Gloss:** The epistemic-law clause — no community un-messengered; verbatim theme-twin 16:36 "To every community We sent a messenger" (verified); it licenses the whole ruku 8–9 story block.
* **`[10:48]`** "And they say, “When will this promise be fulfilled, if you are truthful?”"
  * **Gloss:** The timetable demand — verbatim twin of 67:25 (verified).
* **`[10:49]`** "Say, “I have no power to harm or benefit myself, except as Allah wills. To every nation is an appointed time. Then, when their time arrives, they can neither postpone it by one hour, nor advance it."
  * **Gloss:** The messenger's self-description (7:188 twin, verified) plus the immovable term (16:61, 23:43, 34:30 — verified).
* **`[10:50]`** "Say, “Have you considered? If His punishment overtakes you by night or by day, what part of it will the guilty seek to hasten?”"
  * **Gloss:** The hastening mocked: what part of the thing you hurried toward? — the night-or-day overtaking reuses 10:24's parable wording ("Our command descends upon it by night or by day," internal).
* **`[10:51]`** "“Then, when it falls, will you believe in it? Now? When before you tried to hasten it?”"
  * **Gloss:** Faith-at-sighting refused; the "Now?" that will be spoken to Pharaoh himself at 10:91 (internal).
* **`[10:52]`** "Then it will be said to those who did wrong, “Taste the torment of eternity. Will you be rewarded except for what you used to do?”"
  * **Gloss:** Reward strictly equal to deeds — the surah's equity refrain (10:4, 10:8, 10:27).
* **`[10:53]`** "And they inquire of you, “Is it true?” Say, “Yes, by my Lord, it is true, and you cannot evade it.”"
  * **Gloss:** The oath-clause — "Yes, by my Lord, it is true"; the only oath-in-disputation in this surah (observable in corpus).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`quit of` (barā'ah):** the discharge formula of 10:41.
  * **Rendering:** "You are quit of what I do, and I am quit of what you do."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[60:4]` "We are quit of you, and what you worship apart from Allah" (verified); the nearest religion-level exchange is `[109:6]` "You have your way, and I have my way" (verified) — 10:41 splits deeds, 109:6 splits ways of worship.
* **`appointed time` (ajal):** 10:47–49's immovable term.
  * **Rendering:** "To every nation is an appointed time... they can neither postpone it by one hour, nor advance it."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[16:61]` "He postpones them until an appointed time... they will not delay it by one hour, nor will they advance it"; `[23:43]`; `[34:30]` (all verified).
* **`deaf / blind`:** the audience metaphor.
  * **Rendering:** "can you make the deaf hear, even though they do not understand?... can you guide the blind, even though they do not see?"
  * **Elsewhere:** `[43:40]` "Can you make the deaf hear, or guide the blind, and him who is in evident error?"; `[30:53]`; `[27:81]`; `[6:39]` "deaf and dumb, in total darkness"; and the nifaq dossier's `[2:18]` "Deaf, dumb, blind. They will not return." (all verified).
* **`meeting with Allah` (liqā'):** 10:45's loser-clause, closing the motif opened in 10:7/10:11.
  * **Rendering:** "Those who denied the meeting with Allah will be the losers."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[6:31]` "Losers are those who deny the encounter with Allah" (verified) — the same verdict grammar (faaz-like "losers") in al-An'am.
