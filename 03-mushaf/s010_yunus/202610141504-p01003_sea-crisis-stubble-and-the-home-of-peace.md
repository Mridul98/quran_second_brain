# Crisis-faith at sea, the harvest turned to stubble, and the Home of Peace (10:21–30)
**UID:** 202610141504
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 10:21–30 (Ruku 3 of 11 in Yunus)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[10:21]` – `[10:30]` (10 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The ruku diagnoses a religion of convenience — men scheme against the revelations the moment mercy returns (21), pray with pure devotion while the sea closes over them and commit tyranny the moment their feet touch land (22–23) — sets the world as a crop that turns to stubble overnight (24), then opposes to it the Home of Peace to which Allah invites and guides whomever He wills (25), the two faces of the two classes (26–27), and the Day when the disowned partners testify: "It was not us you were worshiping" (28–30).
* **Placement:** Third ruku of the credibility trial; it supplies the anthropology of unbelief (situational faith) that ruku 1–2's courtroom needs, and its Day-scene separation pre-runs the interrogation of 10:31–35 and the partner-trial of ruku 7.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[10:21]`** "When We make the people taste mercy after some adversity has touched them, they begin to scheme against Our revelations. Say, “Allah is swifter in scheming.” Our envoys are writing down what you scheme."
  * **Gloss:** Mercy's return triggers counter-scheming; the retort "Allah is swifter in scheming" is corpus-unique (verified); the record-clause twin is 43:80 (verified).
* **`[10:22]`** "It is He who transports you across land and sea. Until, when you are on ships, sailing in a favorable wind, and rejoicing in it, a raging wind arrives. The waves surge over them from every side, and they realize that they are besieged. Thereupon they pray to Allah, professing sincere devotion to Him: “If You save us from this, we will be among the appreciative.”"
  * **Gloss:** The crisis liturgy: winds, waves from every side, besiegement — and pure, exclusive devotion improvised under water.
* **`[10:23]`** "But then, when He has saved them, they commit violations on earth, and oppose justice. O people! Your violations are against your own souls. It is the enjoyment of the present life. Then to Us is your return, and We will inform you of what you used to do."
  * **Gloss:** The covenant-free answer to rescue: bagh (violence) on dry land; the recoil formula — "against your own souls" (cf. 35:39, verified).
* **`[10:24]`** "The likeness of the present life is this: water that We send down from the sky is absorbed by the plants of the earth, from which the people and the animals eat. Until, when the earth puts on its fine appearance, and is beautified, and its inhabitants think that they have mastered it, Our command descends upon it by night or by day, and We turn it into stubble, as if it had not flourished the day before. We thus clarify the revelations for people who reflect."
  * **Gloss:** The harvest parable — mastery-illusion cut overnight; the twin parable is 18:45's garden become "debris, scattered by the wind" (verified).
* **`[10:25]`** "Allah invites to the Home of Peace, and guides whomever He wills to a straight path."
  * **Gloss:** The counter-abode named — "the Home of Peace" appears otherwise only at 6:127 (verified) — with the straight-path clause answering the Fatihah's petition.
* **`[10:26]`** "For those who have done good is goodness, and more. Neither gloom nor shame will come over their faces. These are the inhabitants of Paradise, abiding therein forever."
  * **Gloss:** "and more" — the classical ziyadah reading takes this of seeing God (Source: classical tafsir — verify); the face without gloom answers the faces of 10:27.
* **`[10:27]`** "As for those who have earned evil deeds: a reward of similar evil, and shame will cover them. They will have no defense against Allah—as if their faces are covered with dark patches of night. These are the inmates of the Fire, abiding therein forever."
  * **Gloss:** Equity stated twice in the two faces: light without gloom, darkness like night-pieces.
* **`[10:28]`** "On the Day when We will gather them altogether, then say to those who ascribed partners, “To your place, you and your partners.” Then We will separate between them, and their partners will say, “It was not us you were worshiping.”"
  * **Gloss:** The courtroom separation of clients from claimed patrons — the partners themselves disavow (cf. 30:13 "they will disown their partners," verified).
* **`[10:29]`** "“Allah is sufficient witness between us and you. We were unaware of your worshiping us.”"
  * **Gloss:** The objects' testimony — ignorance plea answered by divine witness (cf. 41:47's associates-without-witness scene, verified).
* **`[10:30]`** "There, every soul will experience what it had done previously; and they will be returned to Allah, their True Master; and what they used to invent will fail them."
  * **Gloss:** The invented patrons vanish — the formula "what they used to invent has failed them" recurs at 6:94 and 7:53 (verified).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`sincere devotion` (mukhliṣīn):** the drowning men's temporary creed.
  * **Rendering:** "they pray to Allah, professing sincere devotion to Him" (10:22).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[29:65–66]` "they pray to Allah, devoting their faith to Him; but once He has delivered them... they attribute partners" and `[31:32]` "they call upon Allah, devoting their religion to Him. But when He has delivered them... some of them waver" (both verified) — the identical sea-scene as a three-surah dossier (agent observation).
* **`scheming` (makr):** 10:21's exchange of moves.
  * **Rendering:** "Allah is swifter in scheming" — corpus-unique superlative (verified).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[43:80]` "Our messengers are by them, writing down" (verified); `[7:99]` "None feel safe from Allah's plan" (verified).
* **`Home of Peace` (dār as-salām):** the invitation's destination.
  * **Rendering:** "Allah invites to the Home of Peace" (10:25).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[6:127]` "For them is the Home of Peace with their Lord, and He is their Master" (verified) — the only two corpus sites.
* **`straight path` (ṣirāṭ):** 10:25's guide-clause.
  * **Rendering:** "guides whomever He wills to a straight path."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[1:6]` the petition; `[24:46]` same wording "guides whomever He wills to a straight path" (verified); `[42:52]`, `[43:43]`, `[6:153]` (verified) — see [Guidance (hudan)](../../02-knowledge/202610132000-k-guidance_hudan_lil_muttaqin.md).
