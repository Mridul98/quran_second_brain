# The atom-weight record, Allah's friends without fear, and the son-claim without proof (10:61–70)
**UID:** 202610141508
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 10:61–70 (Ruku 7 of 11 in Yunus)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[10:61]` – `[10:70]` (10 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Nothing is unwatched — no situation, no recitation, no deed; not even an atom's weight outside a clear record (61) — and that surveillance is good news, because Allah's friends (awliya) are the believing, God-conscious ones who have nothing to fear and whose good news runs in this life and the next, with no alteration possible in Allah's words (62–64); therefore the prophet need not grieve at the rival claims — all power is Allah's (65), every claimed partner is assumption and guessing (66), and the loudest claim, "Allah has taken a son," is met with one question: Do you have any proof for this? (68).
* **Placement:** Seventh ruku; the surah's watchfulness-and-friendship hinge between the Book-function announcement (10:57) and the prophet-cycle: the friends of Allah are precisely the trusting remnant of ruku 8–9, while the son-claim's refutation (68–70) closes the tawhid interrogation opened in ruku 4.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[10:61]`** "You do not get into any situation, nor do you recite any Quran, nor do you do anything, but We are watching over you as you undertake it. Not even the weight of an atom, on earth or in the sky, escapes your Lord, nor is there anything smaller or larger, but is in a clear record."
  * **Gloss:** Three registers — life, recital, deed — all watched; the atom-and-record clause twins 34:3 and 6:59 (verified).
* **`[10:62]`** "Unquestionably, Allah’s friends have nothing to fear, nor shall they grieve."
  * **Gloss:** The fear-formula (la khawf) transferred from its usual eschatological scenes to a present-tense category: His friends.
* **`[10:63]`** "Those who believe and are aware."
  * **Gloss:** Two words define the category — faith and taqwa; the same pairing as 46:13's "say Our Lord is Allah, then lead a righteous life" and 7:35's "practices piety and reforms" (verified).
* **`[10:64]`** "For them is good news in this life, and in the Hereafter. There is no alteration to the words of Allah. That is the supreme triumph."
  * **Gloss:** Good news across both worlds; the immutability clause twins 6:115/6:34 (verified); "supreme triumph" — same phrase as 9:111 and 37:60 (verified).
* **`[10:65]`** "And let not their sayings dishearten you. All power is Allah’s. He is the Hearer, the Knower."
  * **Gloss:** Console the warner — power (not their sayings) is the operative variable; verbatim "all power is Allah's" also at 2:165, and 63:8 "power belongs to Allah" answers the hypocrites' boast (verified).
* **`[10:66]`** "Certainly, to Allah belongs everyone in the heavens and everyone on earth. Those who invoke other than Allah do not follow partners; they follow only assumptions, and they only guess."
  * **Gloss:** Ruku 6's ownership clause (10:55) quoted and applied: partners are grammar, not entities — the assumptions-line verbatim from 10:36, twin 53:23 (verified).
* **`[10:67]`** "It is He who made the night for your rest, and the daylight for visibility. Surely in that are signs for people who listen."
  * **Gloss:** Creation returns as the proof-court (6:96 "the night for rest," 25:47 "sleep for rest," verified); "people who listen" — the audience 10:42 could not reach.
* **`[10:68]`** "And they said, “Allah has taken a son.” Be He glorified. He is the Self-Sufficient. His is everything in the heavens and everything on earth. Do you have any proof for this? Or are you saying about Allah what you do not know?"
  * **Gloss:** The surah's climax of the partners-argument — "taken a son" rendered uniquely here (elsewhere "begotten a son": 2:116, 19:88, verified); the counter-questions: proof (27:64, 21:24 formula, verified) and knowledge (cf. 10:18's informing-God charge, internal).
* **`[10:69]`** "Say, “Those who fabricate lies about Allah will not succeed.”"
  * **Gloss:** The verdict line of 10:17 restated as future tense; near-twin 16:116b "will not succeed" (verified).
* **`[10:70]`** "Some enjoyment in this world; then to Us is their return; then We will make them taste the severe punishment on account of their disbelief."
  * **Gloss:** The deferred-term sentence (cf. 10:11, 10:23 internal) — enjoyment, return, tasting; the boiling-drink pair is 6:70 "a drink of scalding water" (verified, cf. 10:4), and "taste" is the surah's own sentencing verb (10:52, internal).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`Allah's friends` (awliyā' Allāh):** the category of 10:62.
  * **Rendering:** "Allah's friends have nothing to fear, nor shall they grieve."
  * **Elsewhere:** the no-fear formula is elsewhere eschatological-addressed — `[7:35]` (messengers from among you... upon them shall be no fear), `[41:30]` (say Our Lord is Allah, then go straight — angels descend: "Do not fear"), `[46:13]` (verified); `[2:112]` (whoever submits... nothing to fear, verified) — Yunus makes it a named present category (agent observation).
* **`clear record` (kitāb mubīn):** 10:61's file.
  * **Rendering:** "but is in a clear record."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[34:3]` "All are in a Clear Record" — with the identical atom-weight clause (verified); `[6:59]` — grain, leaf, unseen-keys "in a clear record" (verified); cf. `[11:6]` "Clear Book" (verified).
* **`assumptions / guess`:** the partner-epistemic.
  * **Rendering:** "they follow only assumptions, and they only guess" (10:66) — verbatim half-line from 10:36 (internal, verified).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[53:23]` "nothing but names... They follow nothing but assumptions" (verified) — the vocabulary-corpus of invented intercessors.
* **`proof`:** the demand of 10:68.
  * **Rendering:** "Do you have any proof for this?"
  * **Elsewhere:** `[21:24]` "Say, 'Bring your proof'" (verified); `[27:64]` "Say, 'Produce your evidence, if you are truthful'" (verified); 53:23's idols have "no authority" sent down (verified).
* **`Self-Sufficient`:** the name answering the son-claim.
  * **Rendering:** "He is the Self-Sufficient" — corpus-rare: this edition uses it only here and at 60:6 (verified).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:116]` "Rather, His is everything in the heavens and the earth; all are obedient to Him" — the same rebuttal shape (verified).
