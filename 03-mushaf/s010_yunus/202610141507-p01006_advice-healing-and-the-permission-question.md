# The refused ransom, the Book as advice and healing, and who made your food unlawful (10:54–60)
**UID:** 202610141507
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 10:54–60 (Ruku 6 of 11 in Yunus)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[10:54]` – `[10:60]` (7 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The verdict scene closes — the wrongdoer would offer the whole earth as ransom and it will not be taken (54), because everything in the heavens and earth already belongs to Allah, whose promise is true, who gives life and death (55–56) — and the ruku pivots to the address it exists for: "O people! There has come to you advice from your Lord, and healing for what is in the hearts" (57), worth more than anything hoarded (58); the one live case of false jurisdiction then exposed is dietary prohibition itself — did Allah give you permission, or do you fabricate lies? (59–60).
* **Placement:** Sixth ruku; the surah's mid-course announcement of what the Book is *for* (advice, healing, guidance, mercy) — its positive self-description between the challenge of ruku 4 and the son-claim rebuttal of ruku 7; the hoard-clause is this surah's single economics moment.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[10:54]`** "Had every soul which had done wrong possessed everything on earth, it would offer it as a ransom. They will hide the remorse when they witness the suffering, and it will be judged between them equitably, and they will not be wronged."
  * **Gloss:** The earth-as-ransom offered and unusable — same clause at 5:36 (plus the earth-full-of-gold at 3:91, verified); judgment "equitably" repeats 10:4's equity.
* **`[10:55]`** "Assuredly, to Allah belongs everything in the heavens and the earth. Assuredly, the promise of Allah is true. But most of them do not know."
  * **Gloss:** Ownership grounds the ransom's refusal; the double "assuredly" (inna) opens the son-claim verse of 10:66 too (internal).
* **`[10:56]`** "He gives life and causes death, and to Him you will be returned."
  * **Gloss:** Life-and-death monopoly — same clause in the messenger-claim of 7:158 (verified).
* **`[10:57]`** "O people! There has come to you advice from your Lord, and healing for what is in the hearts, and guidance and mercy for the believers."
  * **Gloss:** The Book's four-function announcement; its near-twin is 17:82 "We send down in the Quran healing and mercy for the believers" (verbatim phrase, verified), cf. 41:44 (verified).
* **`[10:58]`** "Say, “In Allah’s grace and mercy let them rejoice. That is better than what they hoard.”"
  * **Gloss:** The economy verse: rejoicing in grace over hoarded wealth — hoard (kaniz) is 9:34's word (verified); 9:111 supplies the same "rejoice" verb for the best transaction (verified).
* **`[10:59]`** "Say, “Have you considered the sustenance Allah has sent down for you, some of which you made unlawful, and some lawful?” Say, “Did Allah give you permission, or do you fabricate lies and attribute them to Allah?”"
  * **Gloss:** Dietary claims audited at their warrant — permission or fabrication; 16:116 is the near-verbatim twin ("This is lawful, and this is unlawful... to invent lies and attribute them to Allah," verified).
* **`[10:60]`** "What will they think—those who fabricate lies and attribute them to Allah—on the Day of Resurrection? Allah is bountiful towards the people, but most of them do not give thanks."
  * **Gloss:** The liars' dividend on that Day, bracketed by Allah's bounty — bounty (fadl) explains the delay of 10:11's punishment.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`ransom` (fidā'/iftidā'):** the refused currency of 10:54.
  * **Rendering:** "Had every soul which had done wrong possessed everything on earth, it would offer it as a ransom."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[5:36]` "even if they owned everything on earth, and the like of it with it... it will not be accepted" (verified); `[3:91]` "even the earth full of gold would not be accepted... were he to offer it for ransom" (verified); Day-refusals also take intercession: `[2:48]`, `[2:123]` (verified).
* **`advice` (mawʿiẓah):** 10:57's unusual rendering.
  * **Rendering:** "There has come to you advice from your Lord" — Itani's choice where classical renderings use "admonition/enforcement" (Source: classical lexicography of *m-w-ʿ*; agent note — verify word choice in a printed glossary).
  * **Elsewhere:** the verse's other functions are the surah's own vocabulary — "guidance" (hudā, cf. guidance note cluster) and "mercy" (rahmah, cf. 10:21 "taste mercy" internal).
* **`healing` (shifā'):** for what is in the hearts.
  * **Rendering:** "healing for what is in the hearts, and guidance and mercy for the believers."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[17:82]` "We send down in the Quran healing and mercy for the believers" (near-verbatim, verified); `[41:44]` "For those who believe, it is guidance and healing" (verified); `[16:69]` honey "containing healing for the people" (verified — creation and recital heal alike).
* **`hoard` (kanz):** the counter-grace.
  * **Rendering:** "That is better than what they hoard."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[9:34]` "Those who hoard gold and silver, and do not spend them in Allah's cause" (verified); `[9:35]` "This is what you hoarded for yourselves; so taste what you used to hoard" (verified).
* **`permission` (idhn):** the dietary audit's test.
  * **Rendering:** "Did Allah give you permission, or do you fabricate lies...?"
  * **Elsewhere:** `[16:116]` (verified twin); the permission-mechanism is also how 10:3 handles intercession — same root logic: nothing licensed without Him (*Source: agent observation on the root idhn in the cited renderings — verify Arabic collocations*).

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Meccan (classical consensus); the food-unlawful question of 10:59 targets the Meccan sanctification system (bahira/saiba family — cf. 5:103's "fabricate lies about Allah" in the same breath, verified) more naturally than any Medinan dietary law.
* **Term note:** "advice" for mawʿiẓah appears only here in the surah (observable in corpus).
