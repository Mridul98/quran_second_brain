# Ha, Meem: the sending-down, the mute gods, and Israel's hidden witness (46:1–10)
**UID:** 202610142052
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 46:1–10 (Ruku 1 of 4 in al-Ahqaf)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[46:1]` – `[46:10]` (10 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The family's last Ha, Meem fronting a Book-claim opens a courtroom scene: the invoked gods cannot answer until the Resurrection (5–6), the Book's critics can produce neither prior scripture nor trace of knowledge (4), and the prophet disowns every claim beyond warning (9) — until the verdict turns on an unnamed Israelite who recognized the pattern and believed (10).
* **Placement:** Ruku 1 of 4; the creedal-forensic frame (letters → sender → purposeful creation → audit of refusal) under which the parents passage (15), the Aad precedent (21–26), and the jinn counter-audience (29–32) will run. Its "warnings they receive" (3) names the surah's disease: hearing without heeding.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[46:1]`** "Ha, Meem."
  * **Gloss:** Muqatta'at letters, transliterated and untranslated, numbered as ayah 1 — the seventh and final Ha-Meem opening (40:1–46:1, all rg-verified). The edition refuses to gloss them (see Fleeting link).
* **`[46:2]`** "The sending down of the Scripture is from Allah, the Honorable, the Wise."
  * **Gloss:** The Book-claim the letters front: revelation has a route ("sending down") and a sender qualified by might-and-wisdom. Ledger: the task's "revelation of the Book… Mighty, the Wise" is `[45:2]`'s wording ("the Exalted in Might, the Wise"); here the same divine name (al-'Aziz) renders "the Honorable" — one of only two "Honorable" sites in the corpus (the other `[42:19]`).
* **`[46:3]`** "We did not create the heavens and the earth and what lies between them except with reason, and for a finite period. But the blasphemers continue to ignore the warnings they receive."
  * **Gloss:** Creation is purposeful and time-bound — near-verbatim twin `[30:8]` ("except with reason, and for a specific duration"); the second clause names the disease. Rendering drift: "the blasphemers" here for the same Arabic class Itani renders "those who disbelieve" at `[46:7]`, `[46:11]` and "the faithless" at `[46:20]`.
* **`[46:4]`** "Say, “Have you considered those you worship instead of Allah? Show me which portion of the earth they have created. Or do they own a share of the heavens? Bring me a scripture prior to this one, or some trace of knowledge, if you are truthful.”"
  * **Gloss:** The double audit: a creation-audit (which portion did they create? do they share the heavens?) and an evidence-audit (prior scripture or trace of knowledge — "trace of knowledge" is corpus-unique, rg-verified). The gods fail both.
* **`[46:5]`** "Who is more wrong than him who invokes, besides Allah, those who will not answer him until the Day of Resurrection, and are heedless of their prayers?"
  * **Gloss:** The superlative of injustice: worship addressed to entities that are not merely mute till the Resurrection but *heedless of the prayer itself* — the worse than silence. Cf. Abraham's line to his people: worship of "what can neither benefit you in anything, nor harm you" (`[21:66]`).
* **`[46:6]`** "And when humanity is gathered, they will be enemies to them, and will renounce their worship of them."
  * **Gloss:** The assembly reverses the relationship: the invoked become adversaries and disown the devotion. Ha-Meem twin `[43:67]` — "On that Day, friends will be enemies of one another, except for the righteous."
* **`[46:7]`** "When Our revelations are recited to them, plain and clear, those who disbelieve say of the truth when it has come to them, “This is obviously magic.”"
  * **Gloss:** The recitation test: clarity itself is read as sorcery (the magic-charge is Moses's signature accusation, `[20:57]`). Ledger: the task's "When the Scripture is replaced?" is not this edition's wording — 46:7 is *recited* (tutla); the replace-the-Book demand is `[10:15]` ("Bring a Quran other than this, or change it").
* **`[46:8]`** "Or do they say, “He invented it himself”? Say, “If I invented it myself, there is nothing you can do to protect me from Allah. He knows well what you are engaged in. He is sufficient witness between me and you. He is the Forgiver, the Merciful.”"
  * **Gloss:** The second charge, "invented," met with a self-incriminating challenge (if I forged it, you cannot save me from the consequence) and the witness formula — near-twin `[13:43]` "Allah is a sufficient witness between me and you, and whoever has knowledge of the Scripture," which also pre-announces verse 10. Forgiver-and-Merciful closes even the courtroom sentence.
* **`[46:9]`** "Say, “I am not different from the other messengers; and I do not know what will be done with me, or with you. I only follow what is inspired in me, and I am only a clear warner.”"
  * **Gloss:** The office-statement — the surah's kalam anchor: no novelty, no knowledge of the outcome for anyone (agnostic claim ✓ — task's "not an innovation among the messengers" is another translation's phrasing, not this edition's), passive reception ("I only follow what is inspired in me" — verbatim-shape twins `[6:50]`, `[7:203]`), and the limit: "only a clear warner" (`[7:188]`).
* **`[46:10]`** "Say, “Have you considered? What if it is from Allah and you disbelieve in it? A witness from the Children of Israel testified to its like, and has believed, while you turned arrogant. Allah does not guide the unjust people.”"
  * **Gloss:** The thought experiment hardened into evidence: an Israelite witness testifies to *its like* — pattern-recognition across scriptures — while the opponents "turned arrogant." Guidance closes to the unjust (the guidance-note formula). The text names nobody; the identification Abdullah ibn Salam is transmitted gloss (see §6).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`Ha, Meem`**: transliterated letter-names, non-semantic.
  * **Rendering:** "Ha, Meem." exactly, as ayah 1 — the family signature of surahs 40–46.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[40:1]`, `[41:1]`, `[42:1]`, `[43:1]`, `[44:1]`, `[45:1]` — all six siblings rg-verified; 46 is the family's last letter-opening, and 46:2 repeats 40:2's Book-claim structure, the evidence the letters work as a header.
* **`al-'Aziz` (the Honorable)**: mighty/honored, the name fronting the sender.
  * **Rendering:** "the Honorable, the Wise" (46:2); family drift across the Ha-Meem band: "the Almighty, the Omniscient" (`[40:2]`), "the Exalted in Might, the Wise" (`[45:2]`).
  * **Elsewhere:** "the Honorable" occurs in the corpus only twice — here and `[42:19]` "He is the Powerful, the Honorable" (rg-verified) — both inside this letter-family.
* **`shahid` (witness)**: one who testifies; the ruku's hinge.
  * **Rendering:** divine witness, "He is sufficient witness between me and you" (46:8); human witness, "A witness from the Children of Israel testified to its like, and has believed" (46:10).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[13:43]` — the twin verse welding both senses: Allah as sufficient witness "and whoever has knowledge of the Scripture."
* **`sihr` (magic)**: the accusation named at 46:7.
  * **Rendering:** "This is obviously magic."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[20:57]` — Pharaoh-side magicians' framing of Moses, "your magic, O Moses" — the charge recurs precisely at scripture's clearest moments.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Meccan per classical lists; order 66, the Ha-Meem family's terminus (see index ledger; *Source: classical lists — verify in print*).
* **Asbab / narrative context:** the witness of `[46:10]` is unnamed in the text. The popular identification as Abdullah ibn Salam rests on a report via Ibn 'Abbas collected by Ibn Kathir (*Source: transmitted report — verify in print*); other classical readings exist. Agent note: the anonymity is rhetorically load-bearing — a nameless cross-tradition recognizer is exactly the pattern-evidence the surah describes.
