## 3. Structural Arc
* **Letters → Book → cosmos (1–3):** Ha, Meem hands the Scripture to its Honorable-Wise sender; creation is stated as purposeful and time-bound; the blasphemers "ignore the warnings they receive" — the surah's disease named before its cases.
* **The audit of refusals (4–11):** creation-audit against the gods (4), the mute-worship paradox (5–6), "magic" (7), "invented" (8), the prophet's office-statement (9), the cross-tradition witness and the arrogance verdict (10), precedence-envy and "an ancient lie" (11).
* **Two books, two sons (12–18):** Moses's Book "a model and a mercy" and *this* confirming Arabic Book (12); the no-fear formula (13–14); the grateful son's forty-year prayer (15–16) against the sneering son, "Enough of you!" (17); sentence justified on communities "of jinn and humans" (18) — the jinn return at 29 as believers' counterparty.
* **Degrees and the account (19–20):** ranks for all; the enjoyment-audit at the Fire — squandered, enjoyed, shamed.
* **Aad on trial (21–26):** the nameless brother of Aad at the dunes; "bring us what you threaten" answered by the cloud misread as rain; the powers-and-senses inventory: "neither their hearing, nor their sight, nor their minds availed them."
* **The reversal of audiences (27–35):** townships around the hearers as evidence; intercession exposed as fabrication; the jinn *dispatched to listen* — "Pay attention!" — and going home to warn; the caller answered or unavoidable (32); the tiredless Creator (33); the second Fire-scene (34); patience-command of the ulu-l-'azm and the hour-compression of the promised Day (35).
