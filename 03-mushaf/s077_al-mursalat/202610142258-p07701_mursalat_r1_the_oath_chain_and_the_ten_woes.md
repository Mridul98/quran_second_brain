# The unleashed oath-chain and the sevenfold drumbeat: the Did-We-Not audits (77:1–40)
**UID:** 202610142258
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 77:1–40 (Ruku 1 of 2 in al-Mursalat)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[77:1]` – `[77:40]` (40 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Six nameless oaths certify the promised Day; the refrain "Woe on that Day to the…" — struck 7× inside this ruku (10× in the surah, rg-counted) — drums under three "Did We not" audits (destroyed ancients, made-from-fluid origins, the earth-homestead) until the denied Fire arrives as an un-shading shadow and the Day revokes speech and apology.
* **Placement:** Ruku 1 of 2 — the countdown half; ruku 2 (77:41–50) flips the scene and completes the drumbeat.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`mursalāt`** (the unleashed): 77:1 — title-word and oath-word, left unnamed; the "unleashed" verb elsewhere drives winds/punishment (`[54:19]`, `[69:7]`, `[51:41]`, `[34:16]` — rg), favoring the wind-read (Source: agent inference — verify).
* **`yawm al-fasl`**: 77:13/14 "Day of Decision" vs 77:38 "Day of Separation" — one Day-label, two English names inside one surah; cf. `[37:21]` "Separation", `[44:40]` "Sorting Out", `[78:17]` "Sorting" (rg) — Source: translator's term choice — verify against the Arabic.
* **The woe-refrain** ("Woe on that Day to the…"): ×10, seven here; rotating nouns liars/rejecters/falsifiers/deniers ×3/3/2/2 (rg-counted). Corpus-rivals: `[52:11]` ×1, s083 ×1 — this density is unrivaled; 75:34–35's "Woe to you; and woe" is the address-family, not this refrain (rg).
* **`qadara`** (measure): 77:23 "We measured precisely. We are the best to measure." ↔ `[76:16]` "they measured them exactly" (rg) — the batch's measure-pair: precisely made, precisely served.
