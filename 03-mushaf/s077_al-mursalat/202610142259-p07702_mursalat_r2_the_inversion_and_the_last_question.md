# Shades, fountains, and the last three drumbeats: "In what message, beyond this, will they believe?" (77:41–50)
**UID:** 202610142259
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 77:41–50 (Ruku 2 of 2 in al-Mursalat)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[77:41]` – `[77:50]` (10 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The inversion: the same scene-grammar flips to the righteous (shades, fountains, fruits, the eat-and-drink reward formula), while the deniers get a mockery-twin ("Eat and enjoy yourselves a little; you are indeed criminals") — and the surah's tenfold woe-drumbeat closes over one final act of stubbornness: told to kneel, they do not; the book ends by handing the audience its own verdict-question: "In what message, beyond this, will they believe?"
* **Placement:** Ruku 2 of 2 — the verdict-and-exit half of the drumbeat surah; completes refrains 8–10 of the 10 (rg-counted: 7 in ruku 1, 3 here).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[77:41]`** "The righteous will be amidst shades and fountains." — the anti-shadow resolved: real shades at last (77:30's false "shadow of three different masses" inverted — observable pair, rg).
* **`[77:42]`** "And fruits as they desire." — fruit at will (cf. 76:14's brought-low fruit — the batch's abundance-family, rg).
* **`[77:43]`** "“Eat and drink pleasantly, for what you used to do.”" — the reward-formula: "eat and drink" + causal-for ("for what you used to do") — near-verbatim twin `[52:19]` "Eat and drink happily, for what you used to do" (rg-verified; family `[69:24]` — s069 note).
* **`[77:44]`** "This is how We reward the doers of good." — the method-line made explicit (twins `[37:105]`, `[37:110]` "Thus We reward the doers of good" — rg-verified; echo of 77:18's guilty-method line — same "how We deal/reward" structure both directions).
* **`[77:45]`** "Woe on that Day to the deniers." — DRUMBEAT #8 — "deniers" re-struck; now inside the blessing-scene, the refrain reads as a bracket, not a break.
* **`[77:46]`** "“Eat and enjoy yourselves a little; you are indeed criminals." — the mockery-twin: the same imperative "eat," now rationed ("a little") with a verdict attached (criminals-family: `[36:59]` "step aside today, you criminals", `[32:22]` vengeance on the criminals — rg-verified).
* **`[77:47]`** "Woe on that Day to the liars." — DRUMBEAT #9 — "liars," the cycle's first noun, now penultimate.
* **`[77:48]`** "And when it is said to them, “Kneel”, they do not kneel." — the final sin staged as one refused posture (kneel-family: `[22:77]` "Kneel, and prostrate", `[3:43]`, `[96:19]` "kneel down, and come near" — rg-verified: the last-revealed surah's command answered with refusal here).
* **`[77:49]`** "Woe on that Day to the rejecters." — DRUMBEAT #10 — the refrain completes its decalogue; the drumbeat stops exactly when the surah's argument is done.
* **`[77:50]`** "In what message, beyond this, will they believe?" — the closing question — "In what message" corpus-unique (rg); it rings back to 77:5–6 ("Delivering a message. Excusing or warning.") — the message was delivered, the excuse-window (77:36) closed: nothing follows this book.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`wayl`** (woe) refrain (completion): vv. 45, 47, 49 — bringing the surah to 10 (ruku 1 had 7).
  * **Rendering:** nouns at these three: deniers/liars/rejecters — the full rotation across the surah ×3 liars, ×3 rejecters, ×2 falsifiers, ×2 deniers (rg-counted).
* **"enjoy yourselves"**: 77:46 — "Eat and enjoy yourselves a little" — enjoyment with an expiry tag ("a little" — diminutive of respite; Source: agent inference on rendering scope — verify).
* **kneel**: 77:48 — the bowing posture commanded and refused.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[96:19]` "kneel down, and come near" — nearness paired with kneeling (rg); `[22:77]` command-pair "Kneel, and prostrate" (rg) — 77:48 is the same call issued to a crowd that will not bend (Source: classical reading ties the refusal to arrogance — verify).
* **"message"** (hadith-class word): 77:50 — the same English noun as 77:5's delivery-line — "message/discourse/statement" territory; "In what message" is corpus-unique (rg).
  * **Elsewhere:** the near-identity twin is `[52:34]` "So let them produce a discourse like it, if they are truthful" (rg-verified) — the corpus's two "what speech next?" ultimatums.
