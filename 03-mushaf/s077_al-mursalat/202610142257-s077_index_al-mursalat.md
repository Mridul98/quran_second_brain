## 3. Structural Arc
* **Oath chain (77:1–6):** six beats — unleashed, storming, scattering, separating, delivering, excusing-or-warning — the messengers' own function swore in.
* **Certainty and collapse (77:7–11):** the promise lands; stars obliterated, sky fractured, mountains blown away, messengers alerted.
* **Naming the Day (77:12–14):** "Until which day is it deferred? Until the Day of Decision" — then the Woe-refrain starts its tenure (77:15).
* **Audit #1 — history (77:16–19):** the ancients destroyed, others succeeded, the guilty method; woe.
* **Audit #2 — origin (77:20–24):** insignificant fluid, secure lodging, known term, "We measured precisely. We are the best to measure"; woe.
* **Audit #3 — habitat (77:25–28):** earth homestead for living and dead, mountains, pure water; woe.
* **Fire's irony (77:29–34):** proceed to the shadow that shades nothing; sparks like castles, yellow-camel light; woe.
* **Silence and assembly (77:35–40):** no speech, no apology accepted; the Day of Separation gathers first and last; "if you have a strategy, use it against Me"; woe.
* **Ruku 2 — inversion (77:41–50):** shades, fountains, fruits, the eat-and-drink reward formula; the criminals' counter-taunt "Eat and enjoy yourselves a little"; three final woes bracket the kneeling-refusal and the closing question.
