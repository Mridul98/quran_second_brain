# Pharaoh without a refrain: the multitude defeated, the measured command, the assembly of virtue (54:41–55)
**UID:** 202610142138
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 54:41–55 (Ruku 3 of 3 in al-Qamar)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[54:41]` – `[54:55]` (15 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The fifth warning-case (Pharaoh) deliberately breaks the machinery — warnings arrive (41), the signs are rejected "all of them," and the seizure lands *without* the court-question or the ease-invitation that sealed every prior episode (42); the pattern's silence is the argument: the hearers are not outside the series ("Are your unbelievers better than all those?" 43), their confidence ("We are united") is answered with a defeat-prophecy (44–45), the Hour is re-fixed as *their* appointment (46), and the last fifteen lines alternate decree (49–53) and final seats — dragged faces (48) versus "an assembly of virtue, in the presence of an Omnipotent King" (55).
* **Placement:** Ruku 3 of 3; closes the five-case drumbeat, cashes the "everything has its time" note of 3, and hands the surah's last word to sovereignty — the pair-bookend to an-Najm's "So bow down to Allah, and worship!" (`[53:62]`).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[54:41]`** "The warnings also came to the people of Pharaoh."
  * **Gloss:** The docket-line "also" welds Pharaoh into the series — the warnings that came to him are the same nudhur-family of 23, 33, and 5.
* **`[54:42]`** "They rejected Our signs, all of them, so We seized them—the seizure of an Almighty Omnipotent."
  * **Gloss:** Total rejection (not some signs — *all*), total seizure; and *no refrain*. The docket that should carry "So how were My punishment and My warnings?" ends instead on the attribute-pair "Almighty Omnipotent" — the court declines the question because the case is appeal-proof. The task's "×5 each ending with the refrain" is thus a correction point: four interrogatives + two imperatives, never Pharaoh (index ledger).
* **`[54:43]`** "Are your unbelievers better than all those? Or do you have immunity in the scriptures?"
  * **Gloss:** The fourth wall drops: *your* unbelievers — the Meccan audience named into the comparison; "immunity in the scriptures" is the same privilege-hunger that produced "the message given to him, out of all of us?" (25): a written safe-conduct for a people who refuse the writing that exists.
* **`[54:44]`** "Or do they say, 'We are united, and we will be victorious'?"
  * **Gloss:** The second privilege-claim: coalition-as-security. (This verse sits inside the transmitted Medinan exception-claim band 44–46, read as a Badr prophecy — see §6.)
* **`[54:45]`** "The multitude will be defeated, and they will turn their backs."
  * **Gloss:** The divine answer in future tense: the *group* routed, backs turned — the surah's only open prediction against a living party; military reversal as eschatological sign.
* **`[54:46]`** "The Hour is their appointed time—the Hour is more disastrous, and most bitter."
  * **Gloss:** Appointment re-fixed after their false schedules ("tomorrow" of 26, "united" of 44); comparative-superlative stack — the Day outbids every destruction just narrated (task's "devastating and bitterer" = other-translation wording ✓ substance). *Medinan-exception band continues here — verify in print.*
* **`[54:47]`** "The wicked are in confusion and madness."
  * **Gloss:** The series' residents re-described: the "going astray" Thamud dreaded (24) re-assigned to its mockers — confusion and frenzy as their actual present state.
* **`[54:48]`** "The Day when they are dragged upon their faces into the Fire: 'Taste the touch of Saqar.'"
  * **Gloss:** The taste-refrain's final appearance, now without "and My warnings": pure imperative; "Saqar" — a Fire-name otherwise found only in surah 74 (`[74:26]`, `[74:27]`, `[74:42]`, rg-verified); face-dragging inverts the upright "scrambling towards the Caller" of 8.
* **`[54:49]`–`[54:50]`** "Everything We created is precisely measured. / And Our command is but once, like the twinkling of an eye."
  * **Gloss:** The decree-doctrine at the surah's center of gravity: creation itself is qadar-patterned (the noun echoes `[97:1]` "Night of Decree" — same word-line), and divine speech is a single event executed in a blink — the anti-image of the slow refusals in 1–8 (the same formula-grammar as `[36:82]` "to say to it, 'Be,' and it comes to be").
* **`[54:51]`** "We have destroyed your likes. Is there anyone who would ponder?"
  * **Gloss:** The refrain's sixth appearance and the series' punchline: not the ark-sign, not the Book-ease — *your own counterparts* already deleted; "ponder" completes the four-verb ladder (learn/remember/understand/memorize → now reflect) in echo-form without the Quran clause (index ledger).
* **`[54:52]`–`[54:53]`** "Everything they have done is in the Books. / Everything, small or large, is written down."
  * **Gloss:** The record: deeds in *zubur*-class "Books" (the scrolls-grammar an-Najm cited at `[53:36]`–`[53:37]` — the archive that rebuts private-knowledge claims); total coverage "small or large" — no rounding error in the ledger.
* **`[54:54]`–`[54:55]`** "The righteous will be amidst gardens and rivers. / In an assembly of virtue, in the presence of an Omnipotent King."
  * **Gloss:** The last frame: gardens-and-rivers (the standard paradise geometry, cf. the Garden of Repose near the Lotus Tree at `[53:15]`), then "an assembly of virtue" — the seat-of-truth image of the task's wording ("seat of truth with a Mighty Sovereign") rendered so differently in this edition that the drift itself is the lesson (*Source: translator's term choice, observable between editions cited in the task; the corpus is English-only — the Arabic words behind "assembly/virtue" and "King/Omnipotent" are not verifiable here — verify in print*); the same Omnipotence that seized Pharaoh (42) now hosts the rescued. The "righteous" here (muttaqīn-class in Arabic — *Source: agent inference — verify in print*) reopens the surah's address to the reader: the invitation (17–40) was for this seat.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`qadar` (measure/decree):** 49 "precisely measured" — the surah's answer to fake schedules (26, 44).
  * **Rendering:** verb-form "precisely measured" (49); noun family of `[97:1]` "Night of Decree".
  * **Elsewhere:** `[36:82]` "Be," and it comes to be" — the execution-grammar; the flood's waters met "for a purpose already destined" (12) was the same doctrine in narrative form.
* **`Azīz Muqtadir / Malīk Muqtadir`:** the surah's bracket-names — "Almighty Omnipotent" (42) and "Omnipotent King" (55).
  * **Elsewhere:** both full phrase-forms are unique to s054 (rg-verified); the adjective "Omnipotent" itself recurs as an attribute-line elsewhere, e.g. `[59:23]` "the Almighty, the Omnipotent" and `[30:54]`.
* **`zubur / Books`:** 52's record-archive.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[87:18]`–`[87:19]` "the former scriptures. The Scriptures of Abraham and Moses"; `[53:36]`–`[53:37]` (an-Najm's scrolls) — the two Book-loci that make 43's "immunity in the scriptures" a real question.
* **`Saqar`:** Fire-name; appears only in s054 (48) and s074 (`[74:26]`, `[74:27]`, `[74:42]`) — rg-verified.
