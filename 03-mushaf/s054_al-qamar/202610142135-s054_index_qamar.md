## 3. Structural Arc
* **Sign refused (1–8):** the Hour nears, the moon splits, every "miracle" is re-labelled "Continuous magic"; "everything has its time" (3) then "warnings are of no avail" (5) — the surah states its own futility-condition in advance; the Caller's Day opens (6–8).
* **Four refrain-cases (9–40):** Noah (rejected servant, floodgates, the craft "Sailing before Our eyes" — 16 court-question, 17 ease-invite), Aad (screaming wind, palm-trunks — 18/21 double court-question, 22 ease), Thamud (she-camel, water-share, "wicked liar" — 30 court-question, 31 single Scream, 32 ease), Lot (stones at dawn, "Thus We reward the thankful," guests lusted after — the court-question becomes "taste," twice: 37, 39; 40 ease).
* **The broken pattern (41–46):** Pharaoh's warnings (41), total sign-rejection, seizure — and silence where the refrain should be (42); then the fourth-wall turn: "Are your unbelievers better than all those?" (43), the "We are united" claim answered "The multitude will be defeated" (44–45), the Hour re-named as appointment (46).
* **Decree and record (47–53):** the measured Command that is "but once, like the twinkling of an eye" (49–50); "We have destroyed your likes" — the final echo-invite (51); everything small or large in the Books (52–53).
* **The two seats (54–55):** the righteous amidst gardens and rivers versus the dragged faces (48) — and the last word is a throne-picture: "in the presence of an Omnipotent King."
