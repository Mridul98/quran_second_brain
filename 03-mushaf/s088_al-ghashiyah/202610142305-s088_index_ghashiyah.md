# Surah 88: THE OVERWHELMING (al-Ghashiyah)
**UID:** 202610142305
**Type:** Index / Surah Map
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Meccan — agreed in the classical type-lists; the two-faces Day-tableau and the limited-reminder office fit the Meccan courtroom register. *Source: classical type-lists — verify.* Revelation order: **transmitted nuzul tables — verify** (corpus states no nuzul; unnumbered Basmala heads the surah). Transmitted reports of reciting 88 at the eid prayer are hadith-apparatus, not translation content — verify.
* **Size:** 26 ayahs, 1 ruku (ruku_map global 530 — verified). Second-longest of the 86–91 batch (behind only al-Fajr's 30); the surah that turns 87's "remind, if reminding helps" into a full office-charter (88:21–22).
* **Title:** al-Ghashiyah — "THE OVERWHELMING" tracks `[88:1]` "Has there come to you the news of the overwhelming?"; the overwhelm-verb itself is applied elsewhere to the sea `[20:78]`, to humiliated eyes `[70:44]`, to evil scheming `[35:43]`, and to the hypocrites "whom Allah Himself has overwhelmed" `[4:88]` (rg-verified) — the Day is what finally overwhelms everything that overcame people.
* **Central theme:** The Overwhelming Day sorts faces — "shamed. Laboring and exhausted" (88:2–3) against "joyful. Satisfied with their endeavor" (88:8–9) — each column drawn in its sensory detail (fire with thorn-food and a flaming spring; garden with springs, raised beds, cushions, carpets). The proof-move is a creation-survey of camels, sky, mountains, earth (88:17–20); the office-limit follows: remind, no control (88:21–22); the close reserves return and reckoning to God (88:25–26).
* **Reading hint:** the surah's hidden comparison is labor-for-labor: column one's faces are "Laboring and exhausted" (88:3), column two's are "Satisfied with their endeavor" (88:9) — same human effort, two endpoints. Then notice the survey's odd first item: camels before sky — revelation addresses a caravan culture's nearest miracle.

## 3. Structural Arc
* **News-formula (88:1):** "Has there come to you the news of the overwhelming?" — the opened case-file; the same formula-verb at `[85:17]` "Has there come to you the story of the legions?" (rg-verified).
* **Face column one (88:2–7):** shamed, laboring, exhausted, roasting, flaming-spring drink, thorn-food "that neither nourishes, nor satisfies hunger."
* **Face column two (88:8–16):** joyful, satisfied, lofty garden, no nonsense heard, flowing spring, raised beds, cups set, cushions in rows, carpets spread — comfort inventory in three "In it" clauses (88:11–13) plus the "And…" furnishings (rg-counted within surah).
* **The survey (88:17–20):** camels/how created; sky/how raised; mountains/how installed; earth/how spread out — four look-moves to plain production.
* **The office (88:21–22):** "So remind. You are only a reminder. You have no control over them."
* **The verdict reserve (88:23–26):** the turner-away gets "the greatest punishment"; "To Us is their return. Then upon Us rests their reckoning." (*Source: structural observation on corpus renderings.*)

## 5b. Batch-Return Verification Flags (this index)
* **Hint renderings corrected:** "humble laboring faces" → "Faces on that Day will be **shamed**. / **Laboring and exhausted**" (88:2–3) — "humble" (khāshi'a) is the gloss sense, not the corpus word. "blissful grateful ones" → "Faces on that Day will be **joyful**. / **Satisfied with their endeavor**" (88:8–9) — no gratitude vocabulary in column two (rg-verified). "the garden" ✓ "a lofty Garden" (88:10). "do they not look?" ✓ "Do they not look at the camels—how they are created?" (88:17) with sky/mountains/earth following (88:18–20). "reminder — you are not over them a controller" → "So remind. You are only a reminder. / **You have no control over them**" (88:21–22); "to Us their return; Our reckoning" ✓ verbatim (88:25–26).
* **Hint REJECTED — "the judge/force clauses":** no judging-verse and no "force" clause exists in this corpus rendering of s088 (rg-verified across s088 lines). The surah's final four lines are turn-away → greatest punishment → return → reckoning. Any "the Mighty Judge" / "the strong will struggle" language in the hint belongs to other texts — do not import.
* **Near-twin located:** 88:22's "no control over them" (muṣayṭir-form) has its strongest corpus twin at `[50:45]` "you are not a dictator over them. So remind by the Quran…", with the controllers-rhetorical at `[52:37]` and the guardian-disclaimers at `[10:108]`, `[6:104]`, `[11:86]` (all rg-verified).
* **Face-cluster:** "Faces on that Day…" recurs in batch neighbors — `[75:22]` "radiant", `[80:38]` "radiant", `[80:40]` "covered with misery" (rg-verified): 88 shares the idiom but uniquely pairs shamed-vs-joyful.
