# Deliver what was revealed and do not fear the people: the Messiah who worshipped (5:67–77)
**UID:** 202610141211
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 5:67–77 (Ruku 10 of 16 in al-Ma’idah)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[5:67]` – `[5:77]` (11 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The Messenger is ordered to deliver the whole revelation with a personal guarantee — "Allah will protect you from the people" — and the content he must not withhold is precisely the hard correction: the covenant-killers of Israel, the deified Messiah answered by the Messiah's own words ("worship Allah, my Lord and your Lord"), a third-of-three denied, a mother of truth named, and People of the Scripture warned against exaggerating their religion beyond the truth.
* **Placement:** The surah's mission pivot: 5:67 answers the mockers of ruku 9 (deliver anyway, you are protected), and 5:72–77 is the Christological correction's core, bracketed by the inclusive promise of 5:69 and closing the surah's longest "People of the Scripture" address before the weeping Christians of ruku 11.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[5:67]`** — "O Messenger, convey what was revealed to you from your Lord. But if you do not, then you would not have delivered His message. And Allah will protect you from the people. Allah does not guide the disbelieving people."
  * **Gloss:** The total delivery command; withholding one word = delivering nothing; protection promised — the task and the security are both Allah's (direct link to the kalam note).
* **`[5:68]`** — "Say, “O People of the Scripture! You have no basis until you uphold the Torah, and the Gospel, and what is revealed to you from your Lord.” But what is revealed to you from your Lord will increase many of them in rebellion and disbelief, so do not be sorry for the disbelieving people."
  * **Gloss:** The books they possess are their own condemnation until upheld; the hard fact that revelation hardens many is stated twice in this surah (cf. 5:64).
* **`[5:69]`** — "Those who believe, and the Jews, and the Sabians, and the Christians—whoever believes in Allah and the Last Day, and does what is right—they have nothing to fear, nor shall they grieve."
  * **Gloss:** The inclusive promise restated with its triple condition (belief, Last Day, righteous act) — the corpus's sister verse is `[2:62]`.
* **`[5:70]`** — "We made a covenant with the Children of Israel, and We sent to them messengers. Whenever a messenger came to them with what their souls did not desire, some of them they accused of lying, and others they put to death."
  * **Gloss:** The covenant's record: lie-accusation and prophet-killing as the two reflexes against unwanted speech.
* **`[5:71]`** — "They assumed there would be no punishment, so they turned blind and deaf. Then Allah redeemed them, but then again many of them turned blind and deaf. But Allah is Seeing of what they do."
  * **Gloss:** The punishment-assumption, the blindness-deafness cycle, and the renewed seeing of Allah — the sense-vocabulary of conscience.
* **`[5:72]`** — two clause-chunks:
  * "They disbelieve those who say, “Allah is the Messiah the son of Mary.” But the Messiah himself said, “O Children of Israel, worship Allah, my Lord and your Lord."
  * "Whoever associates others with Allah, Allah has forbidden him Paradise, and his dwelling is the Fire. The wrongdoers have no saviors.”"
  * **Gloss:** The correction staged as quotation: Jesus preaching against his own deification; the shirk-clause gives the surah its sharkest soteriology ("no saviors").
* **`[5:73]`** — "They disbelieve those who say, “Allah is the third of three.” But there is no deity except the One God. If they do not refrain from what they say, a painful torment will befall those among them who disbelieve."
  * **Gloss:** The Trinity named as "third of three"; the One God answered in the same breath; the warning restricted by the text itself — "those among them who disbelieve" (observable clause-limit).
* **`[5:74]`** — "Will they not repent to Allah and ask His forgiveness? Allah is Forgiving and Merciful."
  * **Gloss:** The correction is not a door slammed: repentance invited immediately.
* **`[5:75]`** — "The Messiah son of Mary was only a messenger, before whom other Messengers had passed away, and his mother was a woman of truth. They both used to eat food. Note how We make clear the revelations to them; then note how deluded they are."
  * **Gloss:** Three creaturely facts as proof: he died-messenger, she a ṣiddīqa, both ate — embodiment as evidence of creaturehood.
* **`[5:76]`** — "Say, “Do you worship, besides Allah, what has no power to harm or benefit you?” But Allah: He is the Hearer, the Knower."
  * **Gloss:** The harm/benefit test against any deity-minus; the Hearer-Knower seal (the answer formula of the idol-question).
* **`[5:77]`** — "Say, “O People of the Scripture! Do not exaggerate in your religion beyond the truth; and do not follow the opinions of people who went astray before, and misled many, and themselves strayed off the balanced way.”"
  * **Gloss:** The diagnosis behind the claims: *ghuluww* — excess in religion — traced to ex-aggressive leaders; the "balanced way" (qasīd) echoes 5:66's middle community.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **"convey" (balligh):**
  * **Rendering:** `[5:67]` "convey what was revealed"; "delivered His message" in the same verse.
  * **Elsewhere (within-surah triplet):** `[5:92]` "the duty of Our Messenger is clear communication"; `[5:99]` "The Messenger's sole duty is to convey"; and `[13:40]` "your duty is to inform", `[16:82]` "your only duty is clear communication", `[42:48]` — the delivery-verse family (verified; core support for the kalam note).
* **"Sabians" / "Sabeans":**
  * **Rendering:** "the Sabians" at `[5:69]`; "the Sabeans" at `[2:62]` and `[22:17]` — the same group spelled two ways in this edition (observable corpus fact; translator's term drift).
  * **Elsewhere:** the four-class list "believe... Jews... Christians... Sabeans" is `[2:62]` ≈ `[5:69]`; `[22:17]` adds Zoroastrians and polytheists at judgment — the list's full range.
* **"exaggerate in your religion" (taghlū):**
  * **Rendering:** `[5:77]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[4:171]` "O People of the Scripture! Do not exaggerate in your religion, and do not say about Allah except the truth" — near-verbatim twin (verified), with 4:171's "The Messiah... is the Messenger of Allah" supplying the same correction.
* **"a woman of truth" (ṣiddīqa):**
  * **Rendering:** `[5:75]` for Mary.
  * **Elsewhere:** the truth-verb/noun family: `[3:17]` "the truthful"; `[5:119]` "the truthful will benefit from their truthfulness" (surah close) — Mary's title rhymes with the surah's final category (within-surah web).

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Medinan; 5:67's protection clause is classically said to have been revealed when the Prophet feared assassination, after which guards attended him (*Source: classical reports on 5:67 — verify*).
* **Interfaith reading:** 5:69 (the inclusive promise) and 5:72–77 (the correction) sit in one ruku — the surah judges and invites in the same breath; whether 5:69 abrogates, continues, or specifies `[2:62]` is a classical dispute (*Source: classical dispute on 2:62/5:69 — verify*).
