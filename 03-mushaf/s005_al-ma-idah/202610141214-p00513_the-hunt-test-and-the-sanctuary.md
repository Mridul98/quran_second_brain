# The hunt as divine test, the Kaabah and its economy, and the Messenger's sole duty (5:94–100)
**UID:** 202610141214
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 5:94–100 (Ruku 13 of 16 in al-Ma’idah)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[5:94]` – `[5:100]` (7 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Allah closes the dietary-covenant circle by *testing* the community with game their own hands and spears can reach — sanctity honored unseen is the point — then legislates the penalty ladder for the violator, frees the sea's catch, and installs the Kaabah as the sanctuary's anchor: the sacred months, the offerings, the garlanded all appointed "that you may know that Allah knows everything in the heavens and the earth."
* **Placement:** The return of the opening taboo: 5:1–2 forbade hunt in pilgrim sanctity with no penalty; ruku 13 supplies the penalty (`[5:95]`), the sea clause (`[5:96]`), and the sanctuary institutions (`[5:97]`) — the frame-closure of the surah's law movements, before the testimony ruku and the heavenly courtroom.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[5:94]`** — "O you who believe! Allah will test you with something of the game your hands and spears obtain, that Allah may know who fears Him at heart. Whoever commits aggression after that will have a painful punishment."
  * **Gloss:** The test's design: near-at-hand game as the instrument; its purpose is to *make manifest* who fears Allah unseen — taqwa defined by the unwatched trigger.
* **`[5:95]`** — five clause-chunks:
  * "O you who believe! do not kill game while you are in pilgrim sanctity. Whoever of you kills any intentionally, its penalty shall be a domestic animal comparable to what he killed, as determined by two honest persons among you—an offering delivered to the Kaabah."
  * "Or he may atone by feeding the needy, or its equivalent in fasting, so that he may taste the consequences of his conduct."
  * "Allah forgives what is past."
  * "But whoever repeats, Allah will take revenge on him."
  * "Allah is Almighty, Avenger."
  * **Gloss:** The violation's three-track penalty (comparable offering judged by two honest men / feeding the needy / equivalent fasting), its pedagogical purpose (tasting consequences), the amnesty for the past, and the revenge-clause for repetition — the surah's sharpest "Allah is Avenger" seal.
* **`[5:96]`** — "Permitted for you is the catch of sea, and its food—as sustenance for you and for travelers. But forbidden for you is the game of land while you are in pilgrim sanctity. And fear Allah, to whom you will be gathered."
  * **Gloss:** The sea's catch exempted; the land's game bound to sanctity; the gathering (to Allah) as the verse's eschatological hinge.
* **`[5:97]`** — "Allah has appointed the Kaabah, the Sacred House, a sanctuary for the people, and the Sacred Month, and the offerings, and the garlanded. That you may know that Allah knows everything in the heavens and the earth, and that Allah is Cognizant of all things."
  * **Gloss:** The four appointments of 5:2 now said to be *Allah's instituting* — the sanctuary order is pedagogy about divine knowledge.
* **`[5:98]`** — "Know that Allah is severe in retribution, and that Allah is Forgiving and Merciful."
  * **Gloss:** The double attribute bracket — severity and mercy stated in one breath, the surah's constant resolution.
* **`[5:99]`** — "The Messenger’s sole duty is to convey. Allah knows what you reveal and what you conceal."
  * **Gloss:** The delivery-doctrine's third statement (after 5:67, 5:92); responsibility splits: conveyance is his, disclosure-reckoning is Allah's.
* **`[5:100]`** — "Say: “The bad and the good are not equal, even though the abundance of the bad may impress you. So be conscious of Allah, O you who possess intelligence, that you may succeed.”"
  * **Gloss:** The weigh-not-the-count clause: quality over quantity; addressed to "possessors of intelligence," sealed with success.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **"pilgrim sanctity" again:** the bracket word.
  * **Rendering:** `[5:95]`, `[5:96]` — closing the 5:1–2 frame (occurrences corpus-wide: only 5:1, 5:2, 5:95, 5:96 — verified).
  * **Elsewhere:** ḥajj-conduct kin `[2:196–197]` ("do not shave your heads until the offering has reached its destination"; "whoever decides to perform the Hajj—there shall be no sexual relations, nor misconduct") — the sanctity's companion rules (verified lines).
* **"Kaabah" / "Sacred House" / "the House":**
  * **Rendering:** `[5:95]` "delivered to the Kaabah"; `[5:97]` "the Kaabah, the Sacred House, a sanctuary for the people."
  * **Elsewhere:** the word *Kaabah* occurs only at 5:95 and 5:97 in the corpus (verified); `[2:125]` "We made the House a focal point for the people, and a sanctuary" (near-verbatim "sanctuary for the people"); `[22:29]` "circle around the Ancient House"; `[2:158]` "whoever makes the Pilgrimage to the House" — the sanctuary's other names (verified).
* **"two honest persons" (dhawā ʿadl):**
  * **Rendering:** `[5:95]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[65:2]` "call to witness two just people from among you, and give upright testimony for Allah" (verified) and within-surah `[5:106]` "two reliable persons" — the two-witness instrument of this surah's law (and ruku 14's).
* **"severe in retribution / severe in punishment":**
  * **Rendering:** `[5:98]`; `[5:2]` (same phrase-pair at the frame's start).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[40:3]` "Severe in punishment, Bountiful in bounty" — the attribute-pair's nearest theological twin (verified).
