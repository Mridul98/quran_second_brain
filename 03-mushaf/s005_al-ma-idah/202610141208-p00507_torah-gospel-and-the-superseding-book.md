# Torah, Gospel, and the superseding Book: compete in righteousness (5:44–50)
**UID:** 202610141208
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 5:44–50 (Ruku 7 of 16 in al-Ma’idah)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[5:44]` – `[5:50]` (7 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The judgment question of 5:43 is answered by a trilogy of revealed Books — Torah (guidance and light), Gospel (confirming and counseling), and this Book (confirming and *superseding* them) — each with the same clause "those who do not rule by what Allah revealed," closing with the pluralism verse: one nation was possible, but instead the law of the covenant is *competition in righteousness*.
* **Placement:** The surah's scripture argument at its center; the "race towards goodness" of `[2:148]` is answered here verbatim-by-idea; the three-fold failure label (unbelievers/evildoers/sinners, 5:44/45/47) is an internal ring before the ummah's own command (5:48–49).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[5:44]`** — three clause-chunks:
  * "We have revealed the Torah, wherein is guidance and light. The submissive prophets ruled the Jews according to it, so did the rabbis and the scholars, as they were required to protect Allah’s Book, and were witnesses to it."
  * "So do not fear people, but fear Me. And do not sell My revelations for a cheap price."
  * "Those who do not rule according to what Allah revealed are the unbelievers."
  * **Gloss:** Torah honored with its own inner description ("guidance and light"); its guardians held as responsible; the failure-label one of three.
* **`[5:45]`** — two clause-chunks:
  * "And We wrote for them in it: a life for a life, an eye for an eye, a nose for a nose, an ear for an ear, a tooth for a tooth, and an equal wound for a wound; but whoever forgoes it in charity, it will serve as atonement for him."
  * "Those who do not rule according to what Allah revealed are the evildoers."
  * **Gloss:** The qisas list quoted as Torah-content, with forgoing-as-atonement inside the same clause — retaliation *and* remission are both law; failure-label two.
* **`[5:46]`** — "In their footsteps, We sent Jesus son of Mary, fulfilling the Torah that preceded him; and We gave him the Gospel, wherein is guidance and light, and confirming the Torah that preceded him, and guidance and counsel for the righteous."
  * **Gloss:** Jesus' book carries the same twin descriptor as the Torah; the prophetic relay ("in their footsteps") is the surah's scripture-chains motif.
* **`[5:47]`** — "So let the people of the Gospel rule according to what Allah revealed in it. Those who do not rule according to what Allah revealed are the sinners."
  * **Gloss:** The Gospel's own people addressed; failure-label three (unbelievers/evildoers/sinners — observable gradient in the surah's own wording).
* **`[5:48]`** — four clause-chunks:
  * "And We revealed to you the Book, with truth, confirming the Scripture that preceded it, and superseding it."
  * "So judge between them according to what Allah revealed, and do not follow their desires if they differ from the truth that has come to you."
  * "For each of you We have assigned a law and a method. Had Allah willed, He could have made you a single nation, but He tests you through what He has given you. So compete in righteousness."
  * "To Allah is your return, all of you; then He will inform you of what you had disputed."
  * **Gloss:** The Quran as *muhaymin* ("superseding"); the plural-law sentence makes the competition theodicy: differences of law are the testing ground, not the scandal.
* **`[5:49]`** — two clause-chunks:
  * "And judge between them according to what Allah revealed, and do not follow their desires. And beware of them, lest they lure you away from some of what Allah has revealed to you."
  * "But if they turn away, know that Allah intends to strike them with some of their sins. In fact, a great many people are corrupt."
  * **Gloss:** The command repeated once more (5:48→5:49) with a warning; deserters are left to their own sins — a sovereignty clause, not a sword clause.
* **`[5:50]`** — "Is it the laws of the time of ignorance that they desire? Who is better than Allah in judgment for people who are certain?"
  * **Gloss:** The closing rhetorical pair: *jahiliyyah* judgment named and mocked against Allah's judgment; certainty as the criterion.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **"guidance and light" (hudan wa-nūr):**
  * **Rendering:** for the Torah `[5:44]` and the Gospel `[5:46]` — the identical phrase (observable corpus fact; the pairing occurs only in these two verses in this corpus).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[7:52]` "a Scripture... detailed with knowledge—guidance and mercy for people who believe"; `[2:185]` the Quran as "guidance for humanity" — the descriptor family of revealed books (links to the kalam note directly).
* **"superseding it" (muhaymin):**
  * **Rendering:** "confirming the Scripture that preceded it, and superseding it" `[5:48]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** the same *musaddiq* ("confirming") half appears for the Gospel at `[5:46]`, `[61:6]` ("confirming what preceded me of the Torah"), and `[3:3]` ("confirming what came before it"); *muhaymin* as a Book-title adjective is corpus-unique to `[5:48]` (verified via `rg`).
* **"a law and a method" (shirʿah wa-minhāj):**
  * **Rendering:** `[5:48]` — the pluralism key-phrase, corpus-unique.
  * **Elsewhere:** the concept's kin: `[42:13]` "He prescribed for you the same religion He enjoined upon Noah" (religion one, law plural — verified phrase "prescribed for you the same religion").
* **"compete in righteousness" (mustabaqāt al-khayr):**
  * **Rendering:** "So compete in righteousness" `[5:48]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:148]` "To every community is a direction towards which it turns. Therefore, race towards goodness" — the same command at the qiblah turn; the echo this ruku was designed to answer; contrast within-surah: `[5:62]` "competing with one another in sin and hostility" (the inverted race).

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Medinan, in the surah's judicial phase; classically tied to requests for the Prophet to arbitrate between Jewish claimants (*Source: classical reports on 5:42–43 — verify*).
* **Interpretive honesty:** whether 5:44–47 addresses the People of the Book only, or Muslims by extension, is a classical dispute; the verses' grammar ("the Jews... the people of the Gospel") reads as addressed to them, while 5:48–49 addresses the Prophet's community — *Source: agent observation of the address shifts in the corpus — verify.*
