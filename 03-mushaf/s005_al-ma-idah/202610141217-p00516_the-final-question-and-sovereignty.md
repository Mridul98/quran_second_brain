# The final question to Jesus, and the sovereignty that closes the book (5:116–120)
**UID:** 202610141217
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 5:116–120 (Ruku 16 of 16 in al-Ma’idah)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[5:116]` – `[5:120]` (5 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah's last court-session asks the deified himself: "Did you say to the people, take me and my mother as gods rather than Allah?" — and Jesus' answer is pure tawhid-adab: it was not his right to say what he never said, Allah alone knows his inner, and his entire message reduces to "worship Allah, my Lord and your Lord"; when Allah took him, Allah became the Watcher — and the surah ends on truthfulness rewarded, mutual divine pleasure, and sovereignty over everything.
* **Placement:** Ruku 16 completes the interrogation diptych begun at 5:109 (the messengers, now Jesus personally); it answers ruku 10 (5:72–75) at the eschatological bar instead of the legal one, and its closing sovereignty-verse rings back to `[5:17]` — the surah's first Christological verdict.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[5:116]`** — three clause-chunks:
  * "And Allah will say, “O Jesus son of Mary, did you say to the people, `Take me and my mother as gods rather than Allah?'“"
  * "He will say, “Glory be to You! It is not for me to say what I have no right to. Had I said it, You would have known it. You know what is in my soul, and I do not know what is in your soul. You are the Knower of the hidden."
  * **Gloss:** The question is framed so that the claim, if it existed, is not traced to his own mouth; his defense is not evidence but admission of epistemic humility: You know my inner, I do not know Yours. (Corpus line as quoted, ending un-closed — verbatim.)
* **`[5:117]`** — two clause-chunks:
  * "I only told them what You commanded me: that you shall worship Allah, my Lord and your Lord. And I was a witness over them while I was among them; but when You took me to Yourself, you became the Watcher over them—You are Witness over everything."
  * **Gloss:** His message = the command he received (identical to 5:72's creed-quote); his office changed at the taking: witness → Watcher transferred to Allah — the surah's final word on the authority over the Christian community.
* **`[5:118]`** — "“If You punish them, they are Your servants; but if You forgive them, You are the Mighty and Wise.”"
  * **Gloss:** The surrender of the verdict without presuming it: punishment just, forgiveness worthy — the sentence's referent (whom he speaks of) is classically disputed; the text itself leaves it open (observable fact; *Source: classical dispute on 5:118 — verify*).
* **`[5:119]`** — three clause-chunks:
  * "Allah will say, “This is a Day when the truthful will benefit from their truthfulness.”"
  * "They will have Gardens beneath which rivers flow, wherein they will remain forever. Allah is pleased with them, and they are pleased with Him."
  * "That is the great attainment."
  * **Gloss:** The reward-sentence the whole surah has built toward: truthfulness itself profits — the oath-law, testimony-law, and interrogation-scenes (5:89, 106–108, 109–118) resolve into one quality; the divine mutual-pleasure formula (98:8) crowns it.
* **`[5:120]`** — "To Allah belongs the sovereignty of the heavens and the earth and what lies in them, and He has power over everything."
  * **Gloss:** The ring-verse: sovereignty-total, power-total — answering `[5:17]` ("To Allah belongs the sovereignty... and Allah has power over everything") and closing the surah on the tawhid its Christological corrections served.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **"my Lord and your Lord" (rabbī wa-rabbikum):**
  * **Rendering:** `[5:117]` as Jesus' sum of his message.
  * **Elsewhere:** within-surah `[5:72]` (Messiah's own preaching-quote); `[3:51]`, `[19:36]`, `[43:64]` "Allah is my Lord and your Lord, so worship Him. That is a straight path" (all verified) — the formulaic creed-sentence of the Jesus-material across three surahs.
* **"Witness over everything" (shahīd):**
  * **Rendering:** `[5:117]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[6:19]` "Allah is Witness between you and me" (verified); within-surah `[5:44]` (guardians "were witnesses to it") and `[5:83]` ("count us among the witnesses") — the surah's testimony-vocabulary finds its divine ceiling here.
* **"the truthful" (ṣādiqīn):**
  * **Rendering:** `[5:119]` — those whose truthfulness profits on the Day.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[3:17]` "The patient, and the truthful, and the reverent..." (verified); `[9:119]` "Be conscious of Allah, and be with the sincere" (verified — "truthful/sincere" drift for the same root); within-surah `[5:75]` Mary "a woman of truth" — the ṣ-d-q family completes its surah arc at the last judgment.
* **"Allah is pleased with them, and they are pleased with Him":**
  * **Rendering:** `[5:119]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[98:8]` "Allah is pleased with them, and they are pleased with Him. That is for whoever fears his Lord" (verified); `[9:100]`; `[48:18]` (pledge-under-the-tree pleasure) — the mutual-pleasure formula's cluster.
* **"sovereignty" (mulk):**
  * **Rendering:** `[5:120]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[3:189]`, `[2:107]`, `[67:1]` "Blessed is He in whose hand is the sovereignty, and Who has power over everything" (verified) and within-surah `[5:17]` — the ring pair.

## 4. Cross-References (The Web)
* **Ring:** `[5:120]` ↔ `[5:17]` — the surah's first and last Christological-environment verses are the same sovereignty sentence (observable verbatim parallel).
* **Diptych:** `[5:116]` ↔ `[5:109]` — the messengers' interrogation (109) generalizes; Jesus' interrogation (116) particularizes: no messenger is a god, no saint a partner (tawhid note's courtroom form).
* **Answer to ruku 10:** `[5:117]` ↔ `[5:72]` — "worship Allah, my Lord and your Lord" quoted from his ministry, then re-quoted from his defense — the correction's two registers (ministry, judgment).
