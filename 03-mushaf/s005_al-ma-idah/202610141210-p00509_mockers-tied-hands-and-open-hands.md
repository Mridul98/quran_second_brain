# The mockers, the tied hand, and the outstretched hands (5:57–66)
**UID:** 202610141210
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 5:57–66 (Ruku 9 of 16 in al-Ma’idah)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[5:57]` – `[5:66]` (10 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** From the ban on befriending those who mock the call, the ruku swings to the taunt "Allah's hand is tied" — answered by both outstretched hands, the extinguisher of every kindled war-fire — and ends with the surah's tenderest conditional: *had* they believed and observed the Torah, the Gospel, and this revelation, blessings would have opened from above and below; among them there is a moderate community.
* **Placement:** Ruku 8 fenced the party; ruku 9 explains why the fence exists (mockery, perjury, stinginess-theology) — and then cracks the door with two "Had they..." sentences (`[5:65–66]`) that return to covenant conditionality.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[5:57]`** — "O you who believe! Do not befriend those who take your religion in mockery and as a sport, be they from among those who were given the Scripture before you, or the disbelievers. And obey Allah, if you are believers."
  * **Gloss:** The alliance-ban now specified by behavior, not identity: mockers *from Scripture-people or from the polytheists* — the fence is moral, symmetric.
* **`[5:58]`** — "When you call to the prayer, they take it as a joke and a trifle. That is because they are people who do not reason."
  * **Gloss:** The named mockery: the adhan; the diagnosis is a failure of reasoning, not of revelation.
* **`[5:59]`** — "Say, “O People of the Scripture! Do you resent us only because we believe in Allah, and in what was revealed to us, and in what was revealed previously; and most of you are sinners?”"
  * **Gloss:** The believers' grievance stated: their only "crime" is belief in *all* the revelations — the same creed-list as `[5:68]`, `[2:62]`.
* **`[5:60]`** — "Say, “Shall I inform you of worse than that for retribution from Allah? He whom Allah has cursed, and with whom He became angry; and He turned some of them into apes, and swine, and idol worshipers. These are in a worse position, and further away from the right way.”"
  * **Gloss:** The counter-image: cursed, angered, transformed — apes and swine (the dietary markers of 5:3 as metamorphosis) and *awsan*-worshippers; those who weaponize the covenant signs.
* **`[5:61]`** — "When they come to you, they say, “We believe,” though they have entered with disbelief, and they have departed with it. But Allah is well aware of what they hide."
  * **Gloss:** The greeting-creed: belief as door-policy; Allah's knowledge of concealment as the ruku's recurring court.
* **`[5:62]`** — "You see many of them competing with one another in sin and hostility, and their consuming of what is illicit. What they have been doing is truly evil."
  * **Gloss:** The inverted race: competition in sin — the dark doublet of `[5:48]` "compete in righteousness" (observable within-surah mirror).
* **`[5:63]`** — "Why do the rabbis and the priests not prevent them from speaking sinfully and from consuming forbidden wealth? Evil is what they have been doing."
  * **Gloss:** The clergy's dereliction: sinners' rulers are the *rabbis and priests* who fail to restrain — responsibility climbs the ladder of authority.
* **`[5:64]`** — four clause-chunks:
  * "The Jews say, “Allah’s hand is tied.” It is their hands that are tied, and they are cursed for what they say."
  * "In fact, His hands are outstretched; He gives as He wills."
  * "Certainly, what was revealed to your from your Lord will increase many of them in defiance and blasphemy. And We placed between them enmity and hatred, until the Day of Resurrection." (Corpus reads "to your from your Lord" verbatim; quoted as-is.)
  * "Whenever they kindle the fire of war, Allah extinguishes it. And they strive to spread corruption on earth. Allah does not love the corrupters."
  * **Gloss:** The theology-argument: a stingy god is refuted by two open hands; the revealed word hardens the defiant; internal enmity is itself the decree; and the war-fire's lifespan is Allah's extinguisher.
* **`[5:65]`** — "Had the People of the Scriputure believed and been righteous, We would have remitted their sins, and admitted them into the Gardens of Bliss." (*"Scriputure" is the corpus's typographical rendering, quoted verbatim — see Anomalies.)*
  * **Gloss:** The first counterfactual blessing: faith + righteousness → remission + Gardens of Bliss (the phrase recurs `[31:8]`, `[37:43]`, `[56:12]` — verified).
* **`[5:66]`** — two clause-chunks:
  * "Had they observed the Torah, and the Gospel, and what was revealed to them from their Lord, they would have consumed amply from above them, and from beneath their feet."
  * "Among them is a moderate community, but evil is what many of them are doing."
  * **Gloss:** The second counterfactual: *observe the books* and material blessing opens; the "moderate community" (ummatan wasatan — the 2:143 self-description of this ummah) already exists *inside* the older communities — an unexpected concession as the ruku closes.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **"hand tied" (yad mughlūla) vs. "outstretched" (mabṣūṭān):**
  * **Rendering:** `[5:64]` — the taunt, the retort, and the double-hand image.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[17:29]` "do not keep your hand tied to your neck, nor spread it out fully" — the same hand-metaphor for generosity in human ethics; `[3:181]` "Allah is poor, and we are rich" — the identical scandal of divine stinginess. The taunt pair is thus double-attested in the corpus (observable).
* **"apes" (qirada) and "swine" (khanāzīr):**
  * **Rendering:** transformation classes in `[5:60]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:65]` "Be despicable apes!" and `[7:166]` — the Sabbath-transgressors' metamorphosis; "flesh of swine" is a prohibited *food* at `[5:3]`, a *metamorphosis-class* here — the dietary markers inverted into shame-images (within-surah web, observable).
* **"moderate community" (ummatan wasaṭan):**
  * **Rendering:** `[5:66]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:143]` "Thus We made you a moderate community, that you may be witnesses to humanity" — the same term now located among the People of the Scripture (term-choice identity verified via `rg`; interpretive significance: classical readings differ on whether 5:66 praises a remnant or the just community of each book — *Source: classical — verify*).
* **"idol worshipers" (awthān-worshippers / wasaniyyūn):**
  * **Rendering:** "apes, and swine, and idol worshipers" `[5:60]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[5:90]` "idolatry" (*ansāb*/idols in the ritual list, next ruku) — the corruption of cult within-surah (observable lexical link).

## 4. Cross-References (The Web)
* **Mockery kin:** `[5:58]` ↔ `[15:95]` "We are enough for you against the mockers" — the divine answer pre-empting the ban; cf. `[25:41]`, `[18:106]`.
* **Inverted competition:** `[5:62]` ↔ `[5:48]` "compete in righteousness" — sin-race vs. virtue-race (within-surah mirror, observable).
* **Clergy failure:** `[5:63]` ↔ `[5:44]` (rabbis/scholars *required to protect Allah's Book* — the guardianship named and here violated; within-surah, verified phrase "rabbis").
* **War-fire:** `[5:64]` "kindle the fire of war, Allah extinguishes it" ↔ `[2:17]` (kindled fire extinguished — hypocrite parable; thematic kin) and ↔ `[5:2]` "severe in punishment" as the surah's constant: hostility kindled by people is bounded by Allah.
* **Covenant conditionality:** `[5:65–66]` "Had they..." ↔ `[7:96]` "Had the people of the towns believed and turned righteous, We would have opened for them the blessings of the heaven and the earth" — the verbatim counterfactual-blessing formula; and ↔ the surah's own pledge structure of ruku 3.
* **Two-hands generosity:** `[5:64]` "He gives as He wills" ↔ `[5:1]` "Allah decrees whatever He wills" — the ruku 1 sovereignty formula returns as open-handedness (observable within-surah echo).

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Medinan; the mockery verses are classically linked to specific individuals jeering the call to prayer (*Source: classical occasion reports on 5:57–58 — verify*).
* **Corpus anomaly:** `[5:65]` reads "People of the Scriputure" (typographical, this edition's line) — quoted verbatim above; no other surah-file carries the error (observable corpus fact).
* **Fiqh honesty:** "idol worshipers"/"wasaniyyūn" and the transformation class of 5:60 carry heavy classical lore (literal vs. metaphorical metamorphosis) — *Source: classical dispute — verify*; the text's list stands as quoted.
