# The interrogation of the messengers and the table from heaven (5:109–115)
**UID:** 202610141216
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 5:109–115 (Ruku 15 of 16 in al-Ma’idah)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[5:109]` – `[5:115]` (7 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The Day's first docket: the messengers themselves are asked "What response were you given?" — knowledge disclaimed to the Knower of the unseen — and Jesus' file is reopened grace by grace (the Spirit, the crib, Scripture and wisdom, clay birds, blind, lepers, the dead: all "by My leave"), the disciples' inspired submission, and their requested table: Jesus' public prayer earns a descent — and an unprecedented ultimatum for whoever disbelieves after it.
* **Placement:** The surah turns from legislation to eschatology: the interrogation frame of 5:109 receives its second, harder docket in ruku 16 (5:116–118, to Jesus personally); the table-scene supplies the surah's own name, planted at its narrative apex rather than its title position.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[5:109]`** — "On the Day when Allah will gather the messengers, then say, “What response were you given?” They will say, “We have no knowledge; it is you who are the Knower of the unseen.”"
  * **Gloss:** The messengers interrogated, not excused: their report is surrendered to divine knowledge — the delivery-doctrine's Day-side mirror (5:99).
* **`[5:110]`** — three clause-chunks:
  * "When Allah will say, “O Jesus son of Mary, recall My favor upon you and upon your mother, how I supported you with the Holy Spirit. You spoke to the people from the crib, and in maturity."
  * "How I taught you the Scripture and wisdom, and the Torah and the Gospel. And recall that you molded from clay the shape of a bird, by My leave, and then you breathed into it, and it became a bird, by My leave. And you healed the blind and the leprous, by My leave; and you revived the dead, by My leave."
  * "And recall that I restrained the Children of Israel from you when you brought them the clear miracles. But those who disbelieved among them said, `This is nothing but obvious sorcery.'“"
  * **Gloss:** Grace listed so that the worship cannot misfire: every miracle carries "by My leave" — four times in this single verse (observable count) — and the favor includes survived intent-to-harm (cf. 5:11). (Corpus backtick quoting kept verbatim.)
* **`[5:111]`** — "“And when I inspired the disciples: `Believe in Me and in My Messenger.' They said, `We have believed, so bear witness that We have submitted.'“"
  * **Gloss:** Inspiration (wahi to the disciples) and their covenant-response, identical in wording to 3:52's "bear witness that we submit" — the purest submission-quotation in the Jesus material.
* **`[5:112]`** — "“And when the disciples said, 'O Jesus son of Mary, is your Lord able to bring down for us a feast from heaven?' He said, 'Fear Allah, if you are believers.'“"
  * **Gloss:** The request that names the surah (ma'ida: "feast"); Jesus' reflex is taqwa, not spectacle.
* **`[5:113]`** — "They said, “We wish to eat from it, so that our hearts may be reassured, and know that you have told us the truth, and be among those who witness it.”"
  * **Gloss:** Their stated motive: reassurance, confirmation, witness — not doubt in the demand's grammar, though the answer treats it as dangerous (5:115).
* **`[5:114]`** — "Jesus son of Mary said, “O Allah, our Lord, send down for us a table from heaven, to be a festival for us, for the first of us, and the last of us, and a sign from You; and provide for us; You are the Best of providers.”"
  * **Gloss:** The prayer in full: an eid (festival) binding first and last generations, a sign, and provision as the ask; "Best of providers" is the liturgical seal (cf. 23:72).
* **`[5:115]`** — "Allah said, “I will send it down to you. But whoever among you disbelieves thereafter, I will punish him with a punishment the like of which I never punish any other being.”"
  * **Gloss:** The grant with its sting: post-sign revelation raises the accountability ceiling — the surah's most severe consequence sentence (echoing 5:12's "whoever... disbelieves afterwards," observable within-surah formula).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **"disciples" (ḥawāriyyūn):**
  * **Rendering:** `[5:111]`, `[5:112]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[3:52]` "The disciples said, 'We are Allah's allies; we have believed in Allah, and bear witness that we submit'" and `[61:14]` "as Jesus son of Mary said to the disciples, 'Who are my supporters towards Allah?'" (both verified) — the same circle with ally/supporter term drift across surahs (observable).
* **"from the crib" (mahdan):**
  * **Rendering:** `[5:110]` "You spoke to the people from the crib, and in maturity."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[3:46]` "He will speak to the people from the crib, and in adulthood" (verified — near-identical; "maturity"/"adulthood" drift) and `[19:29]` "How can we speak to an infant in the crib?" (verified) — the crib-speech is triple-attested (observable).
* **"the blind and the leprous":**
  * **Rendering:** `[5:110]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[3:49]` — Jesus' sign-list: "...and I heal the blind and the leper..." (verified near-kin; leprous/leper drift) — the same healing pair.
* **"table" / "feast" (māʾidah):**
  * **Rendering:** "feast" at `[5:112]`, "table" at `[5:114]` — the two renderings of the one word inside four verses (observable drift; the surah is named by it). The English nouns "table/feast" in this sense occur nowhere else in the corpus (verified via `rg`).
