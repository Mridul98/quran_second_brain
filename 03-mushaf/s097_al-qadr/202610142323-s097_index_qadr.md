**UID:** 202610142323

## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Meccan per the classical type-lists, with a genuine transmitted disagreement (some reports attach it to Medina); *Source: classical type-lists and variant reports — verify.* Revelation order: **transmitted nuzul tables — verify** (corpus states no nuzul; unnumbered Basmala heads the surah). Its content — the descent-night of the Quran — makes it the natural companion of 96 (the command that was revealed) regardless of rank-order.
* **Size:** 5 ayahs, 1 ruku (ruku_map global 539 — verified). The shortest in this batch; among the shortest named-surahs in the book.
* **Title:** al-Qadr — Itani's header "DECREE" tracks `[97:1]` "the Night of Decree" (*laylat al-qadr*, which occurs only here in the corpus — 97:1/2/3, rg-verified); the same noun-family gives measure-language at `[13:8]` "everything is by measure" and the standalone noun at `[44:5]` "A decree from Us" (rg-verified) — qadr, decree and measure in one root-family, two English words (term-choice observable in corpus).
* **Central theme:** Five verses on one night: a first-person-plural descent declaration ("We sent it down on the Night of Decree," 97:1) — the book naming its own arrival-night — an amazement-formula ("But what will convey to you what the Night of Decree is?", 97:2, the same magnification-device as `[82:17–18]`, rg-verified), the value-judgment ("better than a thousand months," 97:3), the traffic of the night ("the angels and the Spirit descend… with every command," 97:4), and its moral weather: "Peace it is; until the rise of dawn" (97:5). Time is weighed, not measured: one night outweighs a lifetime (83 years' arithmetic is transmitted — the text itself states only "a thousand months").
* **Reading hint:** read 96 then 97 as one event from two angles: 96 is the *command* ("Read!") and 97 the *night* ("We sent it down"); 96 teaches by the pen, 97 by descending decree; both end nearness-language (96:19 "come near"; 97:5 "Peace").

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Verse 1 — the fact:** divine plural, descent verb, named night: "We sent it down on the Night of Decree." (Object "it" = the Quran, understood from the cluster: `[2:185]` Ramadan "the month in which the Quran was revealed" and `[44:2–3]` "By the Enlightening Scripture. We have revealed it on a Blessed Night" — rg-verified.)
* **Verse 2 — the amazement:** *mā adrāka* formula, doubling the name "Night of Decree" (97:2–3 back-to-back) to hold the question open.
* **Verse 3 — the value:** "better than a thousand months" — worth-language; the stretched-time twins `[22:47]`, `[32:5]` speak "a thousand years" (rg-verified) — months vs years is a live translation detail; cite both carefully.
* **Verse 4 — the traffic:** "In it descend the angels and the Spirit, by the leave of their Lord, with every command." — the Spirit-with-angels pair matches `[26:193]` "The Honest Spirit came down with it" and `[16:102]` "The Holy Spirit has brought it down from your Lord" (rg-verified); "by the leave of their Lord" is the 2:97/7:58/34:12 permission-formula (rg-verified).
* **Verse 5 — the weather:** "Peace it is; until the rise of dawn." — *salām* predicate + dawn boundary; cf. `[56:26]` the greeting "Peace, peace" and the dawn-witnessed recitation `[17:78]` (rg-verified).
* **Twin-surah:** 44:2–5 is the longer parallel: Blessed Night, every wise command distinguished "In it," "A decree from Us" (rg-verified) — 97 and 44:2–5 are the two night-descent passages; each should cite the other (k-kalam already pairs them — see §4).

## 5b. Batch-Return Verification Flags (this index)
* **Hint renderings corrected:** "angels + the Spirit descend **by permission** with **every decree**" → "by the **leave** of their Lord, with **every command**" (97:4 — Itani's permission-word here is "leave"; "decree" in this surah belongs only to the night's name — rg-verified); "peace until dawn" → "**Peace it is**; until the **rise of** dawn" (97:5); "what conveys what the Night is" → "But what will convey to you what **the Night of Decree** is?" (97:2 — full name in the question, not "the Night"); "We sent it down on the Night of Decree" — hint VERIFIED exactly (97:1, rg-verified).
* **"The twin in 44" located:** `[44:2–5]` "By the Enlightening Scripture. / We have revealed it on a Blessed Night— We have warned. / In it is distinguished every wise command. / A decree from Us." (rg-verified) — note the *verb drift inside one theme*: 97:1 "We sent it down," 44:3 "We have revealed it" (anzala family, two English renderings — observable term choice); "every wise command" (44:4) vs "every command" (97:4).
* **"2:185" located verbatim:** "Ramadan is the month in which the Quran was revealed… Allah desires ease for you… and celebrate Allah for having guided you, so that you may be thankful." (rg-verified) — 97's night, 2:185's month, 44:3's blessed night are the one descent-event in three calendared tellings (*Source: structural observation; the classical identity "Laylat al-Qadr = a Ramadan night" is transmitted hadith — verify — the text never says it in 97 itself*).
* **"thousand months" is corpus-unique** (97:3 — rg-verified); the stretched-time formulae with "a thousand years" at `[22:47]`, `[32:5]` (rg-verified) are kin, not twins — different unit.
