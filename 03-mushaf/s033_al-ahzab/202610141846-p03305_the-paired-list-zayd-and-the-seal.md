# The paired list, the closed choice, Zayd, and the seal of the prophets (33:35–40)
**UID:** 202610141846
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 33:35–40 (Ruku 5 of 9 in al-Ahzab)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[33:35]` – `[33:40]` (6 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah universalizes what the household was offered: a ten-pair virtue-list — the Muslim men and women, believing, obedient, truthful, patient, humble, charitable, fasting, chaste-guarding, and frequent-remembering ones — all receive "a pardon, and an immense reward"; once Allah and His Messenger decide a matter, no believer has "liberty of choice"; then the Zayd episode is narrated — the Prophet "hid within yourself what Allah was to reveal" — to legalize marriage to a former adopted-son's wife; and the ruku seals with "Muhammad is not the father of any of your men; but he is… the seal of the prophets."
* **Placement:** Ruku 5 of 9; the legal-theological pivot of the household arc (ruku 4–7). 33:35 answers 33:29's "magnificent compensation"; 33:36 enforces 33:6's prophetic authority; 33:37 closes ruku 1's adopted-son ruling (33:4–5); 33:40 defines the Messenger's office.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[33:35]`** "Muslim men and Muslim women, believing men and believing women, obedient men and obedient women, truthful men and truthful women, patient men and patient women, humble men and humble women, charitable men and charitable women, fasting men and fasting women, men who guard their chastity and women who guard, men who remember Allah frequently and women who remember—Allah has prepared for them a pardon, and an immense reward."
  * **Gloss:** The equality-list: ten virtue-pairs stated for men and women in full symmetry, closing on the twin rewards "a pardon, and an immense reward" — the surah's broadest statement of equal salvific address (cf. `[4:124]`-family; the *muhaymin*-style pair "remember Allah frequently" ties to 33:41–42).
* **`[33:36]`** "It is not for any believer, man or woman, when Allah and His Messenger have decided a matter, to have liberty of choice in their decision. Whoever disobeys Allah and His Messenger has gone far astray."
  * **Gloss:** The binding clause: no *khiyarat* (option) once Allah and His Messenger have decreed an affair — "man or woman" echoing 33:35 — with the guidance-frame penalty "has gone far astray" (the Guidance note's road).
* **`[33:37]`** "When you said to him whom Allah had blessed, and you had favored, “Keep your wife to yourself, and fear Allah.” But you hid within yourself what Allah was to reveal. And you feared the people, but it was Allah you were supposed to fear. Then, when Zaid ended his relationship with her, We gave her to you in marriage, that there may be no restriction for believers regarding the wives of their adopted sons, when their relationship has ended. The command of Allah was fulfilled."
  * **Gloss:** The Zayd verse (task-verified verbatim): the Prophet counsels the man Allah had blessed "Keep your wife to yourself"; he "hid within yourself what Allah was to reveal"; fearing people more than Allah is corrected; and when "Zaid ended his relationship with her," God gives her to the Prophet — the stated purpose being to abolish the taboo on an adopted son's ex-wife. The text's *claim* is legal; Zaynab is never named, and the domestic biography is Source: classical — verify.
* **`[33:38]`** "There is no blame on the Prophet regarding what Allah has ordained for him. Such is the pattern of Allah among those who passed before. The command of Allah is an absolute decree."
  * **Gloss:** The general immunity-clause: "no blame on the Prophet regarding what Allah has ordained for him"; the *sunnah*-formula "the pattern of Allah among those who passed before" (cf. 33:62); "the command of Allah is an absolute decree."
* **`[33:39]`** "Those who deliver the messages of Allah, and fear Him, and never fear anyone except Allah. Allah is sufficient as a reckoner."
  * **Gloss:** The prophetic function defined — deliver the *risalat* (messages), fear none but Allah: a *tawhid* of fear; "sufficient as a reckoner" (cf. 33:3, 48).
* **`[33:40]`** "Muhammad is not the father of any of your men; but he is the Messenger of Allah, and the seal of the prophets. Allah is Cognizant of everything."
  * **Gloss:** The seal-verse: the biological claim ("not the father of any of your men") resolves the Zayd-adoptive issue, and the office-claim "the seal of the prophets" (*khatam an-nabiyyin* — rg-verified unique in this corpus) closes the prophetic chain opened by the covenant of 33:7.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`"the seal of the prophets" (khatam)`** `[33:40]`:
  * **Rendering:** "the Messenger of Allah, and the seal of the prophets."
  * **Elsewhere:** the phrase "seal of the prophets" occurs once in the corpus (rg-verified); the *khatam* root elsewhere means sealing hearts (`[2:7]`, `[7:101]`, `[63:3]`) or sealing the Cave-sleeper's ears (`[18:11]`) or a stoppered wine (`[83:25–26]`); only here does it name an office — finality of prophethood (Source: classical reading — verify).
* **`"hid within yourself what Allah was to reveal" (tukfi)`** `[33:37]`:
  * **Rendering:** the concealment is of what Allah "was to reveal," paired with "you feared the people, but it was Allah you were supposed to fear."
  * **Elsewhere:** the mouth/heart concealment idiom belongs to the nifaq-cluster (`[3:167]`, `[48:11]`), but here it is the Prophet's own *pedagogical* concealment, not hypocrisy (Source: agent inference — verify; the classical debate on what was concealed is transmitted, not in the corpus).
* **`"liberty of choice" (khiyar)`** `[33:36]`:
  * **Rendering:** "not… to have liberty of choice in their decision."
  * **Elsewhere:** the binding-when-decided idiom has no verbatim twin; it operationalizes `[33:2]` "follow what is revealed to you from your Lord."
* **`"absolute decree" (marfuqa/fawran)`** `[33:38]`:
  * **Rendering:** "The command of Allah is an absolute decree."
  * **Elsewhere:** pairs the kalam-motif "command of Allah" of `[33:37]` "The command of Allah was fulfilled."
