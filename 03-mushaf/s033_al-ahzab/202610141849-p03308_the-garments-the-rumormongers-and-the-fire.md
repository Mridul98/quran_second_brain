# Garments, the rumormongers of the City, and the regret of the Fire (33:59–68)
**UID:** 202610141849
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 33:59–68 (Ruku 8 of 9 in al-Ahzab)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[33:59]` – `[33:68]` (10 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah widens from the Prophet's house to the whole city's public order: the women of the believers are told to lengthen their garments "so they will be recognized and not harassed"; the three destabilizers of Madinah — hypocrites, the sick-hearted, and the rumormongers — are warned they will be incited against and evicted; the Hour is deferred to Allah's knowledge alone; and the disbelievers' end is drawn — the cursed Blaze, faces flipped into the Fire, and the regretful prayer "If only we had obeyed Allah and obeyed the Messenger," blaming the "superiors and dignitaries" who led them astray.
* **Placement:** Ruku 8 of 9; the public-law and eschatological climax of the honor-arc, transitioning from domestic etiquette (ruku 7) to the closing warnings (ruku 9).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[33:59]`** "O Prophet! Tell your wives, and your daughters, and the women of the believers, to lengthen their garments. That is more proper, so they will be recognized and not harassed. Allah is Forgiving and Merciful."
  * **Gloss:** The *jilbab*/garment command: from the Prophet's women outward to "the women of the believers," the stated reason public recognition-and-protection ("recognized and not harassed") — the outer-honor counterpart to 33:53's inner screen.
* **`[33:60]`** "If the hypocrites, and those with sickness in their hearts, and the rumormongers in the City, do not desist, We will incite you against them; then they will not be your neighbors there except for a short while."
  * **Gloss:** Task-verified triplet: the three City-destabilizers — hypocrites, "sickness in their hearts" (the *marad* cluster), and the *ifshah*-rumormongers; the ultimatum "do not desist → incited against," eviction from "the City."
* **`[33:61]`** "They are cursed; wherever they are found, they should be captured and killed outright."
  * **Gloss:** The curse and the enforcement clause on the persisting agitators ("captured and killed outright") — the severity bracket of 33:60's warning (Source: the verses read as a legal-warn pair; the historical application is classical — verify).
* **`[33:62]`** "Such has been Allah’s precedent with those who passed away before. You will find no change in Allah’s system."
  * **Gloss:** The *sunnah*-formula — "Allah's precedent with those who passed before" — verbatim-twin of 33:38; the unchangeable "system" (*nahj*) of divine consequence.
* **`[33:63]`** "The people ask you about the Hour. Say, “The knowledge thereof rests with Allah. But what do you know? Perhaps the hour is near.”"
  * **Gloss:** The Hour-deferral: knowledge "rests with Allah," and the rhetorical "Perhaps the hour is near" (the verbatim twin `[42:17]` "what will make you realize that perhaps the Hour is near?") — the eschatological pivot into the Fire-scene.
* **`[33:64]`** "Allah has cursed the disbelievers, and has prepared for them a Blaze."
  * **Gloss:** The curse and "a Blaze" (*sa'ir*) prepared — the pairing of curse + Fire now generalized to the disbelievers (cf. 33:57's curse).
* **`[33:65]`** "Dwelling therein forever, not finding a protector or a savior."
  * **Gloss:** Eternity and abandonment: "neither friend nor helper" restated (33:17) as "no protector or a savior" — the tawhid-of-protection's negative image.
* **`[33:66]`** "The Day when their faces are flipped into the Fire, they will say, “If only we had obeyed Allah and obeyed the Messenger.”"
  * **Gloss:** Task-verified verbatim: the face-flipped scene ("their faces are flipped into the Fire" — cf. `[54:48]`) and the regretful counterfactual — "obeyed Allah and obeyed the Messenger" — the surah's obedience-motif (33:36, 71) inverted into longing.
* **`[33:67]`** "And they will say, “Lord, we have obeyed our superiors and our dignitaries, but they led us away from the way."
  * **Gloss:** The blame-redistribution to leaders — "our superiors and our dignitaries… led us away from the way" — the follow-the-leader regret scene whose corpus twins are `[2:167]` ("those who followed… we will disown them") and `[14:21]` ("the weak will say to those who were proud, 'We were your followers…'") (Source: structural observation; cf. 25:42's "led us away").
* **`[33:68]`** "Lord, give them double the punishment, and curse them with a great curse.”"
  * **Gloss:** The double-punishment request against the leaders — verbatim-twin of `[7:38]` "inflict on them a double punishment in the Fire" — the surah's "doubled-reward" grammar (33:30–31) mirrored in doubled-punishment (Source: structural observation).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`"lengthen their garments" (jilbab)`** `[33:59]`:
  * **Rendering:** "to lengthen their garments… so they will be recognized and not harassed."
  * **Elsewhere:** the modesty-cluster pairs with 33:53's screen and 24:31's "draw their coverings over their breasts" (`[24:31]` — an-Nur); "lengthen their garments" is unique in the corpus (rg-verified).
* **`"rumormongers in the City" (mumshifun/afki)`** `[33:60]`:
  * **Rendering:** the third City-disturber; cf. 24:11–12's *ifk* (the slander) — the same speech-attack genre.
  * **Elsewhere:** "the City" = Madinah (Yathrib named only at 33:13); `[63:8]` uses the same "the City" in a hypocrite-slogan ("If we return to the City…") — the two Medinan City-references bracket the hypocrite-material (rg-verified).
* **`"obeyed Allah and obeyed the Messenger"`** `[33:66]`:
  * **Rendering:** the regret-formula in the Fire.
  * **Elsewhere:** the positive twin is 33:71 "Whoever obeys Allah and His Messenger has won a great victory"; the obedience-word runs through 33:36, 53 — the surah's central imperative (Source: structural observation).
* **`"faces are flipped into the Fire"`** `[33:66]`:
  * **Rendering:** eschatological inversion of the face.
  * **Elsewhere:** verbatim-twin `[54:48]` "their faces will be dragged/flipped into the Fire"; cf. `[2:167]`, `[14:21]` the leader-blame scene.
