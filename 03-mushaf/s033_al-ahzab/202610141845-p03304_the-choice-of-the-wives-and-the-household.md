# The wives' choice and the purified Household (33:28–34)
**UID:** 202610141845
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 33:28–34 (Ruku 4 of 9 in al-Ahzab)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[33:28]` – `[33:34]` (7 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah turns to the Prophet's own house: he is told to offer his wives the choice — the life of this world and its finery, with a kind release, or Allah, His Messenger, and the Home of the Hereafter, with a magnificent compensation; the wives who commit a proven indecency face doubled punishment, and those who are obedient a double reward; and the household is charged to "settle in your homes," to pray, give charity, and obey — for Allah "desires to remove all impurity from you, O People of the Household, and to purify you thoroughly."
* **Placement:** Ruku 4 of 9; the pivot from the outer front (ruku 2–3, the armies) to the inner front (ruku 4–7, the household). This is the domestic material the task briefly mislocated at 33:25.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[33:28]`** "O Prophet! Say to your wives, “If you desire the life of this world and its finery, then let me compensate you, and release you kindly."
  * **Gloss:** The *takhyir* (option) opens: the option of the world and its "finery" (*zakhariha*) answered not with blame but with "compensate you, and release you kindly" (a divorce-with-beauty).
* **`[33:29]`** "But if you desire Allah, His Messenger, and the Home of the Hereafter, then Allah has prepared for the righteous among you a magnificent compensation.”"
  * **Gloss:** The counter-option — Allah, His Messenger, and "the Home of the Hereafter" (*dar al-akhirah*) — with "a magnificent compensation" (*ajr caravan*) for "the righteous among you" (the two economies of the world vs the Home; cf. the infaq note).
* **`[33:30]`** "O wives of the Prophet! Whoever of you commits a proven indecency, the punishment for her will be doubled. And that would be easy for Allah."
  * **Gloss:** The elevated accountability of proximity: "a proven indecency" (*fahisha*) → doubled punishment; nearness to the Messenger raises, not lowers, the standard (Source: the verse's explicit "doubled"; the referent of *fahisha* is agent inference — verify).
* **`[33:31]`** "But whoever of you remains obedient to Allah and His Messenger, and acts righteously, We will give her a double reward; and We have prepared for her a generous provision."
  * **Gloss:** The mirror clause — obedience and righteousness → "a double reward" and "a generous provision" — the same doubled-scale grammar as 33:30, now on the positive side.
* **`[33:32]`** "O wives of the Prophet! You are not like any other women, if you observe piety. So do not speak too softly, lest the sick at heart lusts after you, but speak in an appropriate manner."
  * **Gloss:** Distinction conditional on *taqwa* ("if you observe piety"); the speech-rule — not "too softly," lest "the sick at heart lusts after you," but "an appropriate manner" (*ma'ruf*); the *marad* ("sick at heart") word links to 33:12, 33:60.
* **`[33:33]`** "And settle in your homes; and do not display yourselves, as in the former days of ignorance. And perform the prayer, and give regular charity, and obey Allah and His Messenger. Allah desires to remove all impurity from you, O People of the Household, and to purify you thoroughly."
  * **Gloss:** Task-verified PRESENT: "And settle in your homes" (Itani's rendering of *qarna fi buyutikunna*), the counter to "the former days of ignorance" (*jahiliyyah* — the only corpus site); then the four duties (prayer, zakat, obedience) and the *tathir* verse — Allah "desires to remove all impurity from you, O People of the Household, and to purify you thoroughly."
* **`[33:34]`** "And remember what is recited in your homes of Allah's revelations and wisdom. Allah is Kind and Informed."
  * **Gloss:** The Household's charge to remember "what is recited in your homes of Allah's revelations and wisdom" — a kalam-line: the house is where the recitation dwells; "Allah is Kind and Informed" closes.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`"settle in your homes" (qarna)`** `[33:33]`:
  * **Rendering:** "And settle in your homes; and do not display yourselves, as in the former days of ignorance."
  * **Elsewhere:** "settle in your homes" is a unique phrase in this corpus (rg → 1 hit); "former days of ignorance" is likewise the sole such site — the same root-word appears at `[48:26]` ("the rage of the days of ignorance") in a different collocation (both rg-verified).
* **`People of the Household (Ahl al-Bayt)`** `[33:33]`:
  * **Rendering:** "Allah desires to remove all impurity from you, O People of the Household, and to purify you thoroughly."
  * **Elsewhere:** "People of the Household" occurs exactly once in the corpus (rg-verified, here); the "purify" verb also grounds the ablution verse `[5:6]` "He intends to purify you," and `[74:4]` "purify your clothes."
* **`"the Home of the Hereafter" (dar al-akhirah)`** `[33:29]`:
  * **Rendering:** the object of the better choice; the same world-vs-Home contrast frames `[29:64]` "the Home of the Hereafter—that is the Life."
  * **Elsewhere:** the paired choice is verbatim-twin `[28:77]` "seek, with what Allah has given you, the Home of the Hereafter, and do not neglect your share of this world."
* **`"sick at heart" (marad)`** `[33:32]`:
  * **Rendering:** "lest the sick at heart lusts after you."
  * **Elsewhere:** the sickness-cluster of 33:12, 33:60 and `[8:49]`/`[47:20]` (Nifaq note) — one diagnosis-word across the surah.

## 4. Cross-References (The Web)
* **Within-surah:** 33:29's "Home of the Hereafter" sets the choice answered by 33:35's pardon-and-reward for the ten paired categories; 33:32's "sick at heart" echoes 33:12 and pre-announces 33:60.
* **Within-surah:** the wives-as-"mothers" status of `[33:6]` (ruku 1) governs the marriage-ban at `[33:53]` (ruku 7) — one family-law arc.
* **Parallel:** `[29:64]` — "the Home of the Hereafter—that is the Life" — the world-vs-Home axis of 33:28–29.
* **Kalam echo:** `[33:34]`'s "what is recited in your homes of Allah's revelations" is the domestic register of `[33:2]` "follow what is revealed."

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Medinan; the option-to-the-wives (*takhyir*) and *tathir* verses are classically read as a single household-revelation block (Source: classical asbab — verify).
* **Asbab / narrative context:** the *takhyir* is tied in the reports to the wives requesting greater provision; the identity of the "People of the Household" (the Prophet's wives alone, or the broader *Ahl al-Kisa'*) is a classical and sectarian-reading point the corpus line does not settle — the surrounding verses (28–34) address the wives directly (Source: the verses' own address; the wider referent is transmitted — verify).
