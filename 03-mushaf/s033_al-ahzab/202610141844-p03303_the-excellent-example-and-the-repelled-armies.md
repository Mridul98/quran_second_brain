# The excellent example, the truthful word, and the armies turned back (33:21–27)
**UID:** 202610141844
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 33:21–27 (Ruku 3 of 9 in al-Ahzab)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[33:21]` – `[33:27]` (7 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The Trench resolves: the Prophet is set out as "an excellent example" for anyone who seeks Allah and remembers Him much; the believers, seeing the confederates, read the siege as fulfilled promise — "Allah and His messenger have told the truth" — and are only increased in faith and submission; the men true to their pledge stand against the hypocrites whom God may punish or pardon; and Allah repels the disbelievers "in their rage," spares the believers combat, brings down the backstabbing People of the Book from their strongholds, and hands their land to the believers.
* **Placement:** Ruku 3 of 9; the resolution of the outer-front drama opened in ruku 2. 33:22's truth-word answers 33:12's "illusion," and 33:25's repelling answers 33:13–20's flight-talk.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[33:21]`** "You have an excellent example in the Messenger of Allah; for anyone who seeks Allah and the Last Day, and remembers Allah frequently."
  * **Gloss:** The *uswa hasana* verse — the surah's portrait of prophetic conduct, addressed to "anyone who seeks Allah and the Last Day, and remembers Allah frequently"; the same phrase-model returns of Abraham's band at `[60:4, 6]`.
* **`[33:22]`** "And when the believers saw the confederates, they said, “This is what Allah and His messenger have promised us; and Allah and His messenger have told the truth.” And it only increased them in faith and submission."
  * **Gloss:** Task-verified verbatim: the believers' affirmation reading the threatened armies as promised-and-true; the crisis *increased* them (the idiom of `[3:173]`, `[9:124]`) "in faith and submission" (*Islam*) — the sabr/guidance payoff of ruku 2's test.
* **`[33:23]`** "Of the believers are men who are true to what they pledged to Allah. Some of them have fulfilled their vows; and some are still waiting, and never wavering."
  * **Gloss:** The covenant-keepers: "true to what they pledged" (*sadaqa*), some already martyred ("fulfilled their vows"), some "still waiting, and never wavering" — the *mithaq* note's fulfillment as the beloved state.
* **`[33:24]`** "That Allah may reward the truthful for their truthfulness; and punish the hypocrites, if He wills, or pardon them. Allah is Forgiving and Merciful."
  * **Gloss:** The recompense clause; the crucial discretion: "punish the hypocrites, **if He wills, or pardon them**" — nifaq is not a closed caste (the Nifaq note's exit-line).
* **`[33:25]`** "Allah repelled the disbelievers in their rage; they gained no advantage. Allah thus spared the believers combat. Allah is Strong and Mighty."
  * **Gloss:** The divine turn: the confederates repelled "in their rage" (cf. `[48:26]` "the rage of the days of ignorance"), the believers "spared combat" — the Trench closed without the pitched battle ruku 2's hypocrites predicted. Task-flagged "33:25 as domestic" ✗ — this is the battle-resolution verse; see ruku 4–5.
* **`[33:26]`** "And He brought down from their strongholds those of the People of the Book who backed them, and He threw terror into their hearts. Some of them you killed, and others you took captive."
  * **Gloss:** The siege's aftermath against "those of the People of the Book who backed them" (classically Banu Qurayza); terror cast into their hearts, with killing and captivity — the *rinql* of `[8:12]`'s "I will cast terror into the hearts of those who disbelieve."
* **`[33:27]`** "And He made you inherit their land, and their homes, and their possessions, and a region you have never stepped on. Allah has power over all things."
  * **Gloss:** Inheritance (*awradakum*) of the strongholds' land, homes, possessions, and "a region you have never stepped on" — the military boon; "Allah has power over all things" as the closer.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`"excellent example" (uswa hasana)`** `[33:21]`:
  * **Rendering:** "You have an excellent example in the Messenger of Allah."
  * **Elsewhere:** the same *uswa hasana* formula is directed at Abraham and his band at `[60:4]` and `[60:6]` — the Messenger set as exemplar exactly as the Abrahamic community was (Source: structural observation on the corpus).
* **`"told the truth" / "true to their pledge" (sidq)`** `[33:22, 23]`:
  * **Rendering:** 33:22 "Allah and His messenger have told the truth"; 33:23 "true to what they pledged."
  * **Elsewhere:** the *sadaqa* root frames ruku 1's purpose-verse `[33:8]` "He may ask the sincere about their sincerity" — truthfulness is the surah's positive pole against *nifaq*.
* **`"if He wills, or pardon them"`** `[33:24]`:
  * **Rendering:** discretion on the hypocrites' fate: "punish the hypocrites, if He wills, or pardon them."
  * **Elsewhere:** the same openness the Nifaq note flags at `[4:146]` "Except those who repent, and reform… These are with the believers."
* **`"repelled… in their rage / spared combat"`** `[33:25]`:
  * **Rendering:** "Allah repelled the disbelievers in their rage; they gained no advantage. Allah thus spared the believers combat." ("spared the believers combat" is a unique phrase, rg → 1 hit.)
  * **Elsewhere:** the "rage" is `[48:26]` "the rage of the days of ignorance" (the confederate hearts); the repelling answers ruku 2's flight-coveting (33:13, 16, 20) — the battle they tried to dodge was itself withheld.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Medinan; classically read as the Trench's resolution plus the Banu Qurayza action (5 AH) (Source: classical asbab — verify; the corpus names neither the battle nor the tribe).
* **Asbab / narrative context:** "those of the People of the Book who backed them" (33:26) is identified in the tradition as Banu Qurayza; "a region you have never stepped on" (33:27) is glossed variously (future conquests, or Khaybar). None of these names is in the corpus line (Source: classical reports — verify).
