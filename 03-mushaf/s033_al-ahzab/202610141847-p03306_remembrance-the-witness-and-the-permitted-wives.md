# Frequent remembrance, the witness-Messenger, and the permitted wives (33:41–52)
**UID:** 202610141847
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 33:41–52 (Ruku 6 of 9 in al-Ahzab)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[33:41]` – `[33:52]` (12 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The ruku brackets the Messenger's office with the community's worship: first the command of frequent remembrance and glorification morning and evening, with Allah and His angels "reaching out" to bring the believers out of darkness into light, and their greeting on meeting Him "Peace"; then the Prophet is defined as witness, bearer of good news, warner, caller, and illuminating beacon; and finally the domestic legislation resumes — divorce before consummation (no waiting period), the Prophet's permitted wives, and the closing limit "no other women are permissible for you."
* **Placement:** Ruku 6 of 9; the worship-and-mission center of the surah, linking ruku 4–5's household to ruku 7's screen-and-salawat.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[33:41]`** "O you who believe, remember Allah with frequent remembrance."
  * **Gloss:** *dhikr katheer* — the command to remember often, the trait of 33:35's last pair and of the exemplar at 33:21.
* **`[33:42]`** "And glorify Him morning and evening."
  * **Gloss:** *subhan* morning and evening (cf. `[40:55]`, `[48:9]`) — the day-framed doxology.
* **`[33:43]`** "It is He who reaches out to you, and His angels, to bring you out of darkness into the light. And He is Ever-Merciful towards the believers."
  * **Gloss:** Divine *salat* (reaching-out/blessing) with the angels, the transit-image "out of darkness into the light" — the Guidance note's axis (verbatim twin `[57:9]`; cf. `[2:257]`).
* **`[33:44]`** "Their greeting on the Day they meet Him is, “Peace,” and He has prepared for them a generous reward."
  * **Gloss:** *Salam* as the eschatological greeting (cf. `[10:10]`, `[14:23]`); "a generous reward" echoing 33:31.
* **`[33:45]`** "O prophet! We have sent you as a witness, and a bearer of good news, and a warner."
  * **Gloss:** The mission-triple — *shahid, bashir, nadhir* (the same office given to other messengers, e.g. `[2:119]`, `[48:8]`).
* **`[33:46]`** "And a caller towards Allah by His leave, and an illuminating beacon."
  * **Gloss:** The mission-completion: caller by permission (*siraan idhan Allah*) and "an illuminating beacon" (*siraaj munir*) — light-language of the Kalam note.
* **`[33:47]`** "And give the believers the good news that for them is a great reward."
  * **Gloss:** The *bishara* function restated with its content, "a great reward."
* **`[33:48]`** "And do not obey the blasphemers and the hypocrites, and ignore their insults, and rely on Allah. Allah is a sufficient protector."
  * **Gloss:** The 33:1 refrain returns ("do not obey the… hypocrites," here "blasphemers"), with the instruction to "ignore their insults" and rely — "Allah is a sufficient protector" (the *wakeel*-idiom of 33:3, 39).
* **`[33:49]`** "O you who believe! When you marry believing women, but then divorce them before you have touched them, there is no waiting period for you to observe in respect to them; but compensate them, and release them in a graceful manner."
  * **Gloss:** Family law: unconsummated divorce carries no *'iddah*; compensate and "release them in a graceful manner" (cf. `[2:236]`, the same ruling).
* **`[33:50]`** "O Prophet! We have permitted to you your wives to whom you have given their dowries, and those you already have, as granted to you by Allah, and the daughters of your paternal uncle, and the daughters of your paternal aunts, and the daughters of your maternal uncle, and the daughters of your maternal aunts who emigrated with you, and a believing woman who has offered herself to the Prophet, if the Prophet desires to marry her, exclusively for you, and not for the believers. We know what We have ordained for them regarding their wives and those their righthands possess. This is to spare you any difficulty. Allah is Forgiving and Merciful."
  * **Gloss:** The permission-list for the Prophet's marriages: dowried wives, what Allah granted, the emigrated cousin-sets, and the woman who "offered herself," all "exclusively for you, and not for the believers" — a prophetic-exception clause, closed by the ordinance-acknowledgment and "to spare you any difficulty."
* **`[33:51]`** "You may defer any of them you wish, and receive any of them you wish. Should you desire any of those you had deferred, there is no blame on you. This is more proper, so that they will be comforted, and not be grieved, and be content with what you have given each one of them. Allah knows what is within your hearts. Allah is Omniscient and Clement."
  * **Gloss:** Equal-turn latitude granted, its purpose stated: the wives "comforted, and not be grieved"; God's knowledge of the hearts closes.
* **`[33:52]`** "Beyond that, no other women are permissible for you, nor can you exchange them for other wives, even if you admire their beauty, except those you already have. Allah is Watchful over all things."
  * **Gloss:** The limit-clause: beyond the permitted set, no further marriages and no exchange — "Allah is Watchful over all things."

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`dhikr (remembrance)`** `[33:41]`:
  * **Rendering:** "remember Allah with frequent remembrance… glorify Him morning and evening."
  * **Elsewhere:** the same *dhikr katheer* phrase-closure of 33:35 "men who remember Allah frequently"; `[48:9]` pairs "glorify Him morning and evening" — the day-framed dhikr.
* **`"bring you out of darkness into the light"`** `[33:43]`:
  * **Rendering:** "It is He who reaches out to you, and His angels, to bring you out of darkness into the light."
  * **Elsewhere:** verbatim-twin `[57:9]` "to bring you out of darkness into the light"; the transit-idiom of `[2:257]`, `[14:5]`, `[65:11]` — the Guidance note's road-image (note cites 57:9, 14:5).
* **`"witness, bearer of good news, warner"`** `[33:45–46]`:
  * **Rendering:** the office-quadruple with "an illuminating beacon."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[48:8]` "We have sent you as a witness, a bearer of good news, and a warner" — the same triple for the same Messenger; `[2:119]` for the bashir/nadhir pair.
* **`"offered herself to the Prophet"`** `[33:50]`:
  * **Rendering:** "a believing woman who has offered herself to the Prophet… exclusively for you, and not for the believers."
  * **Elsewhere:** the exclusivity-clause is unique to the Prophet's marriage-law; cf. 33:51–52's parallel latitude and limit (Source: the corpus lines).

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Medinan; the worship-commands (41–44) and the marriage-permissions (50–52) are classically grouped in the Madinan period (Source: classical lists — verify).
