# Moses cleared, the Trust accepted, and the closing verdict (33:69–73)
**UID:** 202610141850
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 33:69–73 (Ruku 9 of 9 in al-Ahzab)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[33:69]` – `[33:73]` (5 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The closing ruku binds speech to accountability and the surah to the human condition: do not be like those who abused Moses, whom "Allah cleared of what they said"; be conscious of Allah and "speak in a straightforward manner" so conduct is rectified and sins forgiven, for "whoever obeys Allah and His Messenger has won a great victory"; and then the cosmic frame — the Trust offered to the heavens, earth, and mountains, refused by all but accepted by the human being, "unfair and ignorant" — ending on the verdict: Allah punishes the hypocrite men and women and the idolater men and women, and redeems the believing men and women, "Allah is Ever-Forgiving, Most Merciful."
* **Placement:** Ruku 9 of 9; the surah's finale. 33:69 vindicates the 33:57–58 insult-law with the Moses precedent; 33:71 restates the obedience-motif as victory; 33:72 universalizes the covenant of 33:7–8 into the Trust; 33:73 is the closing sorting that answers the hypocrite-dossier of ruku 2.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[33:69]`** "O you who believe! Do not be like those who abused Moses; but Allah cleared him of what they said. He was distinguished with Allah."
  * **Gloss:** The honor-precedent: the believers must not "be like those who abused Moses" (*adha*, the same root as 33:53/57/58); "Allah cleared him of what they said" — vindication is God's act, "distinguished with Allah" — the warning that the Messenger insulted in 33:57 has a scriptural type in Moses.
* **`[33:70]`** "O you who believe! Be conscious of Allah, and speak in a straightforward manner."
  * **Gloss:** The *qawid* / *qawlan sadidan* command — "speak in a straightforward manner" — the speech-ethic that closes the surah's long treatment of words (hypocrite-speech 33:12–13; the slander-law 33:58; here its upright opposite).
* **`[33:71]`** "He will rectify your conduct for you, and will forgive you your sins. Whoever obeys Allah and His Messenger has won a great victory."
  * **Gloss:** Task-verified verbatim: straightforward speech brings rectified conduct and forgiven sins; the closing promise — "Whoever obeys Allah and His Messenger has won a great victory" (Itani's "a great victory," not "a mighty conquest"; the idiom twins `[4:13]` "the great attainment" and `[24:52]` "these are the winners").
* **`[33:72]`** "We offered the Trust to the heavens, and the earth, and the mountains; but they refused to bear it, and were apprehensive of it; but the human being accepted it. He was unfair and ignorant."
  * **Gloss:** The Trust verse (exact rendering verified): the *amanah* proposed to the cosmos, all refusing "and were apprehensive of it," until "the human being accepted it" — described as "unfair and ignorant" (*zhaluman jahula*), not "man unjust." The verse universalizes ruku 1's covenant (33:7–8): the pledge is now the load every human voluntarily took.
* **`[33:73]`** "Allah will punish the hypocrites, men and women, and the idolaters, men and women. And Allah will redeem the believers, men and women. Allah is Ever-Forgiving, Most Merciful."
  * **Gloss:** The closing sorting, in the surah's ten-pair grammar: hypocrite men/women and idolater men/women punished, believing men/women "redeemed" — a mercy-turn after the verdict — and the surah ends, after the punishment-clause, on "Allah is Ever-Forgiving, Most Merciful" (the *tawbah*-mercy closer; Source: the corpus line — the Arabic root of "redeem" is not recoverable from an English-only corpus).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`"the Trust" (amanah)`** `[33:72]`:
  * **Rendering:** "We offered the Trust to the heavens… the human being accepted it. He was unfair and ignorant."
  * **Elsewhere:** the capital-T "Trust" is a unique offering in this corpus (rg-verified sole site); the *amanah*-family recurs in the plural as moral deposits — `[8:27]` "nor betray your trusts," `[4:58]` "give back things entrusted to you," `[23:8]`/`[70:32]` "faithful/honor their trusts and pledges" — 33:72 is the cosmic prototype of that trust-ethics.
* **`"abuse/harm" (adha)`** `[33:69]`:
  * **Rendering:** "do not be like those who abused Moses; but Allah cleared him of what they said."
  * **Elsewhere:** the same *adha* verb runs 33:53, 57, 58 — the surah's one word for offending the Messenger and his community; the Moses-vindication is the precedent-type (Source: structural observation).
* **`"straightforward manner" (qawlan sadidan)`** `[33:70]`:
  * **Rendering:** "speak in a straightforward manner."
  * **Elsewhere:** the upright-speech idiom; the *sidq* family here (33:70) is the positive pole of the whole surah — 33:8 sincerity, 33:22 "told the truth," 33:23 "true to what they pledged" (Covenant/Sabr notes).
* **`"a great victory"`** `[33:71]`:
  * **Rendering:** "Whoever obeys Allah and His Messenger has won a great victory."
  * **Elsewhere:** the *falah*-family — `[4:13]` "the great attainment," `[9:89]`/`[57:12]` "the great triumph"; 33:71's is the obedience-conditioned variant, matching `[24:52]` "these are the winners" (rg-verified). The male-and-female paired-address grammar of 33:73's verdict returns from 33:35's ten-pair list, framing reward and judgment by gender-symmetry (Source: structural observation).

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Medinan; the Moses-abuse precedent (33:69) is classically tied to the slander-accusations of the City, and the Trust to the covenant-people (Source: classical asbab — verify).
* **Asbab / narrative context:** what the "Trust" *is* (obligation, accountability, divine commands) and why the heavens/earth/mountains "refused" are debated in classical tafsir and are not defined in the corpus line (Source: classical reports; agent inference — verify). The Moses-abuse is not specified in-text (various reports: the slap-accusation, the *adha* of the spies) — Source: classical — verify.
