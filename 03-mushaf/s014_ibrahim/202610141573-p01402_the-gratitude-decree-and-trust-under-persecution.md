# The gratitude decree and the first messengers' trust under persecution (14:7–12)
**UID:** 202610141573
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 14:7–12 (Ruku 2 of 7 in Abraham/Ibrahim)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[14:7]` – `[14:12]` (6 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The Lord Himself proclaims the surah's governing exchange — thankfulness buys increase, ingratitude buys severe punishment (14:7) — Moses hedges the deal so no one mistakes God for a needy counterparty (14:8), and the deep historical archive (Noah, Aad, Thamood, and uncounted nations after, 14:9) supplies the recurring script: human messengers rejected as "humans like us" (14:10) who answer with divine elective favor and tawakkul, and open-ended perseverance under persecution (14:11–12).
* **Placement:** Ruku 1 named its readers ("every patient and thankful person," 14:5); this ruku states the decree those readers live under and then dramatizes them in the earliest communities — the surah's engine installed here.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[14:7]`** “And when your Lord proclaimed: “If you give thanks, I will grant you increase; but if you are ungrateful, My punishment is severe.””
  * **Gloss:** The economy verse: a two-sided, first-person promise — gratitude met with *ziyādah* (increase), ingratitude (*kufr* here so rendered) met with severity; the whole surah runs on this clause.
* **`[14:8]`** “And Moses said, “Even if you are ungrateful, together with everyone on earth—Allah is in no need, Worthy of Praise.””
  * **Gloss:** The hedge: the exchange does not make God dependent; earth's total ingratitude would not dent His self-sufficiency — praise is His attribute regardless.
* **`[14:9]`** “Has not the story reached you, of those before you, the people of Noah, and Aad, and Thamood—and those after them? None knows them except Allah. Their messengers came to them with the clear proofs, but they tried to silence them, and said, “We reject what you are sent with, and we are in serious doubt regarding what you are calling us to.””
  * **Gloss:** The archive opens: three named nations plus an unnumberable tail ("None knows them except Allah") — the pattern repeats: clear proofs, a hand over the mouth, rejection quoted word-for-word.
* **`[14:10]`** “Their messengers said, “Is there any doubt about Allah, Maker of the heavens and the earth? He calls you to forgive you your sins, and to defer you until a stated term.” They said, “You are only humans like us; you want to turn us away from what our ancestors worshiped; so bring us a clear proof.””
  * **Gloss:** The messengers' counter: doubt about the Maker is category-absurd; the offer is forgiveness and a respite to a stated term. The people's counter-counter: your humanity disqualifies you — bring a miracle (the surah's miracle-demand voice, cf. `[13:7]`).
* **`[14:11]`** “Their messengers said to them, “We are only humans like you, but Allah favors whomever He wills from among His servants. We cannot possibly show you any proof, except by leave of Allah. In Allah let the faithful put their trust.””
  * **Gloss:** Humanity conceded, election claimed: the difference is not species but divine favor; proofs come only "by leave of Allah" — and the clause turns address-wise into the creed line "In Allah let the faithful put their trust."
* **`[14:12]`** ““And why should we not trust in Allah, when He has guided us in our ways? We will persevere in the face of your persecution. And upon Allah the reliant should rely.””
  * **Gloss:** The tawakkul sealed with three reasons: He guided our paths, we will endure your *fitna* (persecution/testing) without flinching, and reliance is His right, ours to perform.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`give thanks / grant you increase`** (*shukrtum... layazīdannakum*): the decree's verbs.
  * **Rendering:** "If you give thanks, I will grant you increase" (14:7) — formula unique in the corpus; gratitude elsewhere is commanded or modeled ("And thank Me, and do not be ungrateful," `[2:152]`; "Whoever is appreciative—is appreciative for the benefit of his own soul," `[31:12]`).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[39:7]` — the closest twin of the whole clause-complex: "if you are thankful, He will approve that in you" + Allah Independent of the disbelievers; `[34:15]` — Sheba's "Eat of your Lord's provision, and give thanks to Him" is the decree cast as a land's biography.
* **`tawakkul`** (reliance): 14:11–12 renders it twice — "let the faithful put their trust," "the reliant should rely."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[27:79]` "So rely on Allah. You are upon the clear truth"; `[39:38]` "Say, 'Allah suffices for me. On Him the reliant rely'" — same imperative shape after a sovereignty argument.
* **`persecution`** (*fitna*): rendered "persecution" in 14:12 — "We will persevere in the face of your persecution."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:177]` "patiently persevere in the face of persecution, hardship, and in the time of conflict" — the virtue-verse pairs sabr with the same word; `[2:217]` "persecution is more serious than killing"; also "trial" for the same root in 14:6.
* **`stated term`** (*ajil musammā*): the respite of 14:10.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[35:45]` "He defers them until a stated time" — the deferral doctrine Ibrahim will restate at `[14:42]` and `[14:44]`.
