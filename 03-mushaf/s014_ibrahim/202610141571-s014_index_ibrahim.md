## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Meccan per the classical lists (Ibn `Abbas, Jabir, Mujahid, Qatadah transmitted as Meccan; al-Suyuti's standard enumeration). A Medinian *asbab* dispute exists and is real but narrow: an-Nahhas (via Mujahid from Ibn `Abbas) and Qatadah report the surah is Meccan **except `[14:28–29]`** ("those who exchanged the blessing of Allah with blasphemy"), which at-Tabari transmits about the slain Quraysh leaders of Badr (`Ali, Ibn `Abbas, Sa`id b. Jubayr, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd: "the polytheists of Badr"; the "two most wicked clans of Quraysh" report from `Umar/`Ali). *Source: asbab reports web-retrieved 2026-10-14 from quranpedia (an-Nahhas 537), hodaalquran, and at-Tabari on 14:28 — verify in print before quoting.* A whole-surette Medinian claim was NOT confirmed in this check; the dispute is about 28–29, not the surah.
* **Revelation order:** 56 in the classical descent enumeration (late-Meccan texture: miracle-scepticism, persecution/expulsion threats, resurrection mockery). *Source: standard nuzul-order tables (al-Itqan-type lists) — agent labeling, verify; at least one modern scheme (The Last Dialogue) places it ~72th in late Meccan, so ordinal claims need a named table.*
* **The "witness" material — verified honestly:** the phrase "the Day We raise up a witness for every nation" does **not** occur in Ibrahim (`rg -n "witness" corpus/s014_ibrahim.md` → 0 hits; observable). That formula belongs to an-Nahl (`[16:84]`, `[16:89]`) and an-Nisa' (`[4:41]`). Ibrahim's cognate material is the *arising-for-judgment* scene: "They will emerge before Allah, altogether" (`[14:21]`, repeated `[14:48]`), Satan's post-verdict address (`[14:22]`), and the closing reckoning (`[14:51]`); the surah's own Day-anchor is "the Day the Reckoning takes place" (`[14:41]`). *Source: corpus grep (observable); the equation of the two scenes is agent inference.*
* **Size:** 52 ayahs, 7 ruku (`data/ruku_map.json`: 6+6+9+6+7+7+11 = 52). Mushaf position 14, after ar-Ra'd, before al-Hijr; its opening letters "Alif, Lam, Ra." join the Yunus–Hud–Yusuf–Ibrahim–al-Hijr cluster.
* **Central theme:** Gratitude is the surah's engine. The Book is sent to move humanity from darkness to light (`[14:1]`), Moses' commission repeats the itinerary and names its readers — "every patient and thankful person" (`[14:5]`) — and the Lord's own proclamation sets the economy: "If you give thanks, I will grant you increase; but if you are ungrateful, My punishment is severe" (`[14:7]`). History (Noah, Aad, Thamood, the unnamed many, `[14:9]`), the tree-parable of the good and bad word (`[14:24–26]`), and the favor-inventory that cannot be counted (`[14:34]`) all run on that axis, until Abraham himself prays for a settled progeny "that they may be thankful" (`[14:37]`) and the surah closes as a proclamation "that people of understanding may remember" (`[14:52]`).

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Commission (1–6):** the Book's purpose-statement (darkness→light) is proven by the law of linguistic revelation (4) and instantiated in Moses (5–6).
* **Decree + archive (7–12):** gratitude/increase (`[14:7]`) frames the historical archive — Noah, Aad, Thamood "and those after them, none knows them except Allah" (`[14:9]`) — whose messengers model tawakkul and perseverance under persecution (10–12).
* **Collapse of the persecutors (13–21):** the expulsion ultimatum flips into divine settlement-promise; tyrants come to disappointment; works turn to ash; all parties emerge before Allah and the follower–leader chain starts breaking (21).
* **The verdict scene and the word made botanical (22–27):** Satan speaks when "the issue is settled"; the good word grows like a rooted tree yielding fruit every season, the bad word is torn up rootless; Allah firms the believers "with the firm word" (27).
* **The Makkan case (28–41):** the blessing-exchangers named (28–30); the counter-liturgy commanded — prayer and spending, secret and public (31); the uncountable-favors inventory (32–34); then Abraham's five du'as reclaim the House and its settlement (35–41) — the surah's namesake speaks only here, all in direct address to God.
* **Respite and reckoning (42–52):** wrongdoers deferred, not forgotten; the changed earth, the chained, the tar garments — then the colophon: "This is a proclamation for mankind... that He is One God" (52), answering `[14:1]`'s Book-claim.
