# Respite is not heedlessness: the changed earth and the proclamation for mankind (14:42–52)
**UID:** 202610141578
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 14:42–52 (Ruku 7 of 7 in Abraham/Ibrahim)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[14:42]` – `[14:52]` (11 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah closes its court: God's silence toward the wrongdoers is reclassified from negligence to scheduling (14:42), and the schedule is shown — necks outstretched, gaze unblinking, hearts void (14:43); the wicked's late bid to defer "a little while" is answered by their own prior oaths and their forefathers' demolished homes (14:44–45); plans that could move mountains are "known to Allah" (14:46), and the promise to His messengers will not be broken (14:47); then the cosmos itself is swapped — another earth, another heavens, the sinners chained in tar-garments, each soul repaid by a God "Quick in reckoning" (48–51) — until the colophon names the book it finishes: "This is a proclamation for mankind... that He is One God, and that people of understanding may remember" (14:52).
* **Placement:** The eschatological finale answering the surah's commission: 14:1 said the Scripture was sent to bring humanity into light; 14:52 states its delivery terms — warning, tawhid, remembrance. Abraham's "Day the Reckoning takes place" (14:41) arrives by name and scenery.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[14:42]`** “Do not ever think that Allah is unaware of what the wrongdoers do. He only defers them until a Day when the sights stare.”
  * **Gloss:** The theodicy correction — the "do not ever think" verb, restated for the promise at 14:47: deferral, not indifference; "the sights stare" fixes the Day's first symptom at eye level.
* **`[14:43]`** “Their necks outstretched, their heads upraised, their gaze unblinking, their hearts void.”
  * **Gloss:** Four anatomical clauses: the stretched neck of the condemned, the lifted gaze that cannot blink, and hearts that have emptied of everything — a portrait of terror as paralysis, unique phrasing in the corpus (`rg "hearts void|unblinking"` → only here).
* **`[14:44]`** “And warn mankind of the Day when the punishment will come upon them, and the wicked will say, “Our Lord, defer us for a little while, and we will answer Your call and follow the messengers.” Did you not swear before that there will be no passing away for you?””
  * **Gloss:** The warning-job (the same verb as 14:5's "remind") stages the ultimate delayed request — a little respite to do exactly what the messengers asked first — cross-examined on their earlier oaths that life had no exit.
* **`[14:45]`** “And you inhabited the homes of those who wronged themselves, and it became clear to you how We dealt with them, and We cited for you the examples.”
  * **Gloss:** The evidence is real estate and case-law: they lived in the ruins of the self-wrongers (14:9's archive, now with addresses) and were given the *amthāl* (14:24–26's method) — no excuse survives the tour.
* **`[14:46]`** “They planned their plans, but their plans are known to Allah, even if their plans can eliminate mountains.”
  * **Gloss:** *Makr* vs. foreknowledge: even a plot that would remove mountains (the ar-Ra'd spectacle-clause inverted, `[13:31]`) is already in the file.
* **`[14:47]`** “Do not ever think that Allah will break His promise to His messengers. Allah is Strong, Able to Avenge.”
  * **Gloss:** The second "do not think," now about promise-keeping — the same formula as `[13:31]` "Allah never breaks a promise" and `[3:9]`; the names close it: `Azīz, Dhū-l-Intiqām`.
* **`[14:48]`** “On the Day when the earth is changed into another earth, and the heavens, and they will emerge before Allah, the One, the Irresistible.”
  * **Gloss:** The stage itself is swapped; "they will emerge before Allah" repeats 14:21 verbatim — the court from ruku 3 convenes on new terrain; the divine name pair is the corpus's Qahhar-formula (cf. `[40:16]`, `[13:16]`).
* **`[14:49]`** “On that Day, you will see the sinners bound together in chains.”
  * **Gloss:** "Bound together" — the sinners shackled as paired defendants: the chained-punishment family of `[38:38]` "fettered in chains," `[76:4]` "chains, and yokes," and `[40:71]` "dragged by the chains."
* **`[14:50]`** “Their garments made of tar, and the Fire covering their faces.”
  * **Gloss:** Clothing as fuel: *qirāṭ* (tar) is a hapax in the corpus (`rg "\btar\b"` → only this line); the faces that turned up to nothing (14:43) are now veiled by fire.
* **`[14:51]`** “That Allah may repay each soul according to what it has earned. Allah is Quick in reckoning.”
  * **Gloss:** The purpose clause individualizes the repay (each *nafs*, its *kasb*) — and the reckoning is swift: the exact clause of `[2:202]`; the delay was never slowness.
* **`[14:52]`** “This is a proclamation for mankind, that they may be warned thereby, and know that He is One God, and that people of understanding may remember.”
  * **Gloss:** The book describes itself: *balāgh* (delivery/proclamation, cf. `[3:138]`, `[46:35]`), three intents — warning, the oneness creed, remembrance — and the audience formula (ulū al-albāb, `[3:190]`) names who actually takes it in.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`defers`** (respite): the delay-verb of 14:42 ("He only defers them...") and of the wicked's plea "defer us" (14:44).
  * **Rendering:** "He only defers them until a Day..."; "defer us for a little while."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[35:45]` "But He defers them until a stated time" — the same doctrine; 14:10's "defer you until a stated term" is its offer-side (both verified).
* **`the One, the Irresistible`** (*al-Wāḥid, al-Qahhār*): 14:48's confession-name on the Day.
  * **Elsewhere:** the name-pair occurs exactly three times in the corpus (`rg "Irresistible" corpus/`): `[13:16]` — after ar-Ra'd's "darkness and light equal?" argument; `[40:16]` — on the Day they emerge, the sovereignty-question answered with the identical pair; and here at 14:48.
* **`tar`** (*qirāṭ*): 14:50's garment — corpus hapax (observable via `rg`).
* **`proclamation`** (*balāgh / nibā'* register): 14:52 "This is a proclamation for mankind."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[3:138]` "This is a proclamation to humanity, and guidance, and advice for the righteous"; `[46:35]` "A proclamation: Will any be destroyed except the sinful people?" — the self-label of delivered warning.
* **`people of understanding`** (*ulū al-albāb*): the remembrance-capable audience.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[3:190]` the creation-alternation signs "for people of understanding"; `[2:179]`, `[20:128]` — the Quran's standing address to intellects that remember (`dhikr`), matching 14:5's "remind them."

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Meccan finale (index §1); the respite-grammar doubles as consolation for a persecuted community and warning to an unpunished one — the same two audiences of 14:12–13.
* **Asbab resonance (labeled):** the Badr-period reports on 14:28–29 have `Ali/`Umar say the slain clan's partners "are given enjoyment for a while" — the deferral doctrine of 14:42 in exegetical dress. *Source: at-Tabari transmitted reports retrieved 2026-10-14 — labeled tradition, verify.*
