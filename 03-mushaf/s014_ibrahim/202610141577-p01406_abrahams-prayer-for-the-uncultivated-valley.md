# Abraham's du'as: peace, idols, the uncultivated valley, and the Day of Reckoning (14:35–41)
**UID:** 202610141577
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 14:35–41 (Ruku 6 of 7 in Abraham/Ibrahim)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[14:35]` – `[14:41]` (7 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah's namesake finally speaks — and he speaks only as a supplicant: five consecutive du'as asking peace for the land and protection from idol-worship for himself and his sons (14:35), defining affiliation by following rather than lineage (14:36), settling some offspring in a valley with no vegetation beside the Sacred House so that prayer may be established and hearts may incline and fruits come "that they may be thankful" (14:37), praising the God who hides nothing and who gave him Ishmael and Isaac in old age (38–39), begging prayer-continuity down his lines (14:40), and closing on forgiveness for himself, his parents, and the believers "on the Day the Reckoning takes place" (14:41).
* **Placement:** The emotional and theological center of the surah: the gratitude-engine of 14:5–7 arrives inside Abraham's own prayer as the *purpose* of the settlement — and the Day he names in 14:41 is the Day ruku 7 will depict.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[14:35]`** “Recall that Abraham said, “O my Lord, make this land peaceful, and keep me and my sons from worshiping idols.””
  * **Gloss:** Two petitions, one for place (peace on this land — the Haram), one for lineage (distance from *wathan*): the founder's fear is not poverty but idolatry.
* **`[14:36]`** ““My Lord, they have led many people astray. Whoever follows me belongs with me; and whoever disobeys me—You are Forgiving and Merciful.” [sic — corpus line opens the quotation and never closes it before 14:37's new opening quote]
  * **Gloss:** The follower-grammar rewritten: affiliation to Abraham is affiliation into "me" (the believer's company), disobedience is handed to God's forgiveness — no eternalizing of the errand by the ancestor.
* **`[14:37]`** ““Our Lord, I have settled some of my offspring in a valley of no vegetation, by Your Sacred House, our Lord, so that they may perform the prayers. So make the hearts of some people incline towards them, and provide them with fruits, that they may be thankful.””
  * **Gloss:** The settlement prayer: agriculture-less land + Sacred House + prayer + inclined hearts + fruits, and the *telos* is gratitude — the whole surah's engine stated by its patriarch as an act of obedience.
* **`[14:38]`** ““Our Lord, You know what we conceal and what we reveal. And nothing is hidden from Allah, on earth or in the heaven.””
  * **Gloss:** The omniscience clause anchors the audacity of the previous petitions: what looks like an absurd settlement is filmed from inside divine knowledge.
* **`[14:39]`** ““Praise be to Allah, Who has given me, in my old age, Ishmael and Isaac. My Lord is the Hearer of Prayers.””
  * **Gloss:** The praise-formula (al-ḥamdu lillāh — the 1:2 register) opens: the sons named together (the only du'a of praise in the corpus pairing Ishmael and Isaac as old-age gifts), and the self-name "Hearer of Prayers" seals the session.
* **`[14:40]`** ““My Lord, make me one who performs the prayer, and from my offspring. Our Lord, accept my supplication.””
  * **Gloss:** The founder cannot guarantee his line's prayer — he asks for it: *muqīm al-ṣalāh* for himself and "of my offspring," followed by the plain "accept my supplication."
* **`[14:41]`** ““Our Lord, forgive me, and my parents, and the believers, on the Day the Reckoning takes place.””
  * **Gloss:** The closing ask widens to the whole believing family of humanity, timed precisely: the Day the ḥisāb stands up — the surah's own eschatology, named.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`idols`** (*wathan*): 14:35's only fear.
  * **Rendering:** "worshiping idols."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[26:71]` "We worship idols, and we remain devoted to them"; `[29:17]` "You worship idols besides Allah... they cannot provide you with livelihood"; `[6:74]` Abraham-to-Azar opener; the Day-side verdict `[30:13]` "no intercessors from among their idols" — the same object-class 14:35 flees and 14:22's Satan disavows.
* **`Sacred House`** (*al-Bayt al-Ḥarām*): 14:37's coordinate.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[5:97]` "Allah has appointed the Kaabah, the Sacred House, a sanctuary for the people" — the corpus's own gloss of the term; `[5:2]` "those heading for the Sacred House."
* **`valley of no vegetation`** (*wādin ghayri dhī zarʿ* — a valley having no cultivation): the barren settlement-site.
  * **Rendering:** unique phrase in the corpus (`rg "vegetation"` returns only this valley and irrigation-growth verses like `[32:27]`).
  * **Classical identification:** the valley is Bakka/Makkah, the Sacred House the Ka'bah, the "some of my offspring" Ishmael's line. *Source: consensus of classical tafsir on 14:37; not stated in the text — agent labeling, verify.*
* **`the Day the Reckoning takes place`** (*yawm tāqūmu al-ḥisāb*): 14:41's timestamp.
  * **Elsewhere:** the reckoning-root of the surah's close: `[14:51]` "Allah is Quick in reckoning"; the wider idiom `[2:202]` "Allah is swift in reckoning."

## 4. Cross-References (The Web)
* **Twin prayer:** `[2:126]` — "O My Lord, make this a peaceful land, and provide its people with fruits—whoever of them believes in Allah and the Last Day": the same du'a in al-Baqarah, with the faith-conditional fruits that 14:37's "that they may be thankful" unpacks; Abraham's Baqarah trio `[2:126–129]` (peace, House, messenger) is the cousin-cluster of Ibrahim's pentad (35–41).
* **The exemplar and its exception:** `[60:4]` — "You have had an excellent example in Abraham... *Except for the words of Abraham to his father, 'I will ask forgiveness for you'*": the Qur'an's own footnote on 14:41's "forgive me... my parents," resolved by `[9:114]` — the istighfar was for a promise, then he disowned him when enmity became clear. The text supplies its own timeline; classical harmonization places 14:41's prayer before the disavowal. *Source: corpus trio (14:41/60:4/9:114) observable; the before/after sequencing is classical tafsir — verify.*
* **Follower-grammar contrast:** 14:36's "Whoever follows me belongs with me" against the ruku 3–4 collapse of every other chain — `[14:21–22]`, `[2:166–167]`, `[37:30]` — Abraham builds affiliation on following, which the Day will not sever, unlike the leaders of 14:21. *Source: agent inference from the three scenes' shared vocabulary — verify.*
* **Old-age motif:** `[15:54]` ("old age has overtaken me? What good news..."), `[11:72]` (Sarah's astonishment), `[19:8]`, `[3:40]` — the impossibility-framing the praise of 14:39 closes; the Isaac-with-Jacob gifting lines `[6:84]`, `[21:72]` complete the offspring-dossier.
* **Within surah:** 14:37 "that they may be thankful" = the 14:5/14:7 engine as *purpose* of the settlement; 14:35 "make this land peaceful" answers 14:14's settlement-promise; 14:41's Day feeds ruku 7's 42–52; "nothing is hidden" (14:38) pre-answers "Allah is unaware" negation at 14:42.
