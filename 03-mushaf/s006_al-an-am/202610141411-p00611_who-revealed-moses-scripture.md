# Who revealed the Scripture Moses brought? (6:91–94)
**UID:** 202610141411
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 6:91–94 (Ruku 11 of 20 in al-An'am)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[6:91]` – `[6:94]` (4 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** One slogan — "Allah did not reveal anything to any human being" — is tried against an undeniable fact: the Scripture Moses brought, displayed on scrolls yet largely concealed; and whoever still counterfeits revelation dies with angels' hands outstretched, arriving alone, every invented intercessor cut.
* **Placement:** Direct sequel to the prophets' chain, which closed "Those are they whom Allah has guided, so follow their guidance" (`[6:90]`); the ruku now interrogates the Book behind that guidance, opening the surah's final movement (revelation's credibility → creation's signs → the covenant of 6:151–154).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[6:91]`** — four clause-chunks:
  * "They do not value Allah as He should be valued, when they say, “Allah did not reveal anything to any human being.”"
  * "Say, “Who revealed the Scripture which Moses brought— a light and guidance for humanity?”"
  * "You put it on scrolls, displaying them, yet concealing much. And you were taught what you did not know—neither you, nor your ancestors."
  * "Say, “Allah;” then leave them toying away in their speculation."
  * **Gloss:** The counter-question lets the opponent's own book condemn his claim; the charge pivots from denial to mishandled disclosure ("displaying them, yet concealing much"); "Say, 'Allah;'" hands the verdict back and abandons the disputant to his game.
* **`[6:92]`** "This too is a Scripture that We revealed— blessed—verifying what preceded it, that you may warn the Mother of Cities and all around it. Those who believe in the Hereafter believe in it, and are dedicated to their prayers."
  * **Gloss:** The Quran pleads the same credential — a confirming, blessed book aimed at the navel-city and its hinterland; belief in the Hereafter is the stated key that recognizes it, visible in prayer.
* **`[6:93]`** — three clause-chunks:
  * "Who does greater wrong than someone who invents falsehood against Allah, or says, “It was revealed to me,” when nothing was revealed to him, or says, “I will reveal the like of what Allah revealed”?"
  * "If only you could see the wrongdoers in the floods of death, as the angels with arms outstretched: “Give up your souls."
  * "Today you are being repaid with the torment of shame for having said about Allah other than the truth, and for being too proud to accept His revelations.”"
  * **Gloss:** The crime widens from denying revelation to forging it — three escalating counterfeit claims; the "if only you could see" death-scene makes the forger's end a public humiliation.
* **`[6:94]`** — two clause-chunks:
  * "You have come to Us individually, just as We created you the first time, leaving behind you everything We gave you."
  * "We do not see with you your intercessors—those you claimed were your partners. The link between you is cut, and what you had asserted has failed you."
  * **Gloss:** The courtroom empties: solo arrival, assets surrendered, claimed partners unseated — the patronage "link" severed.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`quddara Allah haqqa qudrih`** ("value Allah as He should be valued"): failing to estimate God's worth, here by making revelation impossible for Him.
  * **Rendering:** the identical English phrase at `[6:91]` and `[22:74]` (rg-verified).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[22:74]` "They do not value Allah as He should be valued. Allah is Strong and Powerful" — there it answers oaths exalting human partisans; here it answers an oath that dethrones revelation.
* **`ṣuḥuf` ("scrolls"):** transcribed revelation.
  * **Rendering:** "scrolls" in `[6:91]` — displayed and concealed at once.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[53:36]` "what is in the Scrolls of Moses"; `[74:52]` scrolls as the parchment the disbelievers wish they were handed.
* **`shufafa'` ("intercessors"):** claimed heavenly patrons.
  * **Rendering:** 6:94's "intercessors—those you claimed were your partners"; cf. "no protector or intercessor" at `[6:51]` and "no helper or intercessor" at `[6:70]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[10:18]`, `[39:43]`, `[7:53]`, `[30:13]`, `[26:100]`, `[74:48]` — the claim recurs and fails in every one (rg-verified).
* **`farda` ("individually"):** bare, one-by-one.
  * **Rendering:** "individually" at `[6:94]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[34:46]` "devote yourselves to Allah, in pairs, or individually" — the same solitary-standing image turned into the summons of arrival.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Meccan (classical consensus).
* **Asbab / narrative context:** the slogan of 6:91a is reported in the tafsirs variously for a Jewish disputant, for Quraysh, and for a claimant to prophecy — reports differ and the text itself names none (Source: Ibn Kathir on 6:91, collecting the variants — verify); "Mother of Cities" = Mecca is the consensus gloss. The rhetorical pivot — the opponent's own Scripture testifying against his claim — is agent analysis — verify.
