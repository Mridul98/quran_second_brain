# Invented taboos on Allah's provision (6:130–140)
**UID:** 202610141416
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 6:130–140 (Ruku 16 of 20 in al-An'am)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[6:130]` – `[6:140]` (11 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The Day's subpoena reaches both species — messengers came, the world seduced you, you testify against yourselves — and the verdict is grounded in this world's practice: a people who apportion crops and cattle between Allah and their idols, kill children at their "partners'" counsel, and tax the womb by decree have lost the right to legislate over God's provision.
* **Placement:** The reckoning scene begun in ruku 15 completes here, then the surah pivots to its longest legal dispute — the fabricated food-and-offering taboos — which runs through ruku 17–18 and is answered by the honest list of 6:145.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[6:130]`** — two clause-chunks:
  * "“O assembly of jinn and humans, did there not come to you messengers from among you, relating to you My revelations, and warning you of the meeting of this Day of yours?” They will say, “We testify against ourselves.”"
  * "The life of the world seduced them. They will testify against themselves that they were disbelievers."
  * **Gloss:** The universal-providence objection is built into the summons: messengers "from among you" — same species, own language — leaving no appeal.
* **`[6:131]`** "That is because your Lord would not destroy towns for injustice while their inhabitants are unaware."
  * **Gloss:** Destruction is never unjust-by-surprise: notice precedes doom — the theodicy clause of the whole scene.
* **`[6:132]`** "They all have ranks according to what they did; and your Lord is not unaware of what they do."
  * **Gloss:** Hierarchy in the beyond is payroll by deeds; "not unaware" rhymes the "unaware" of 6:131.
* **`[6:133]`** "Your Lord is the Rich Beyond Need, the Possessor of Mercy. If He wills, He can do away with you, and substitute whomever He wills in your place, just as He produced you from the descendants of another people."
  * **Gloss:** Self-sufficiency plus mercy, then extinction-with-substitution — the Surah's repeated replace-humanity option (cf. 6:165's succession).
* **`[6:134]`** "What you are promised is coming, and you cannot thwart it."
  * **Gloss:** Promise-continuity and human impotence in six words.
* **`[6:135]`** "Say, “O my people! Work according to your ability, and so will I.” You will come to know to whom will belong the sequel of the abode.” The wrongdoers will not prevail."
  * **Gloss:** A stand-off verse — the Prophet matches their energy and leaves the endgame to fate; "the sequel of the abode" answers their confidence (Source: the reading of 6:10's mockery-besieged pattern as its twin — agent inference — verify). (Corpus [sic]: a stray closing quote after "abode." — book typography.)
* **`[6:136]`** "And they set aside for Allah a share of the crops and the livestock He created, and they say, “This is for Allah,” according to their claim, “and this is for our idols.” But the share of their idols does not reach Allah, yet the share of Allah reaches their idols. Evil is their judgment."
  * **Gloss:** The accounting farce: their ledger runs downhill — offerings meant for God leak to the idols, none ever the reverse.
* **`[6:137]`** "Likewise, their idols entice many idolaters to kill their children, in order to lead them to their ruin, and confuse them in their religion. Had Allah willed, they would not have done it; so leave them to their fraud."
  * **Gloss:** The budget extends to blood: "partners" counsel infanticide; the verse insulates God's will from the crime before the next consolation.
* **`[6:138]`** — two clause-chunks:
  * "And they say, “These animals and crops are restricted; none may eat them except those we permit,” by their claims, and animals whose backs are forbidden, and animals over which they do not pronounce the name of Allah—fabricating lies against Him."
  * "He will repay them for what they used to invent."
  * **Gloss:** The taboo zoo: claimed reserve species, back-banned animals, un-named slaughter — one line against each fabricated category.
* **`[6:139]`** "And they say, “What lies in the wombs of these animals is exclusively for our males, and prohibited to our wives.” But if it is stillborn, they can share in it."
  * **Gloss:** The self-refuting rule: wombs taxed by gender until death intervenes — purity law that flips on carrion.
* **`[6:140]`** "Lost are those who kill their children foolishly, with no basis in knowledge, and forbid what Allah has provided for them—innovations about Allah. They have gone astray. They are not guided."
  * **Gloss:** The verdict in staccato: lost, foolish, knowledge-less, astray, unguided — provision-forbidding and child-killing named as the same sin of innovation.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **"messengers from among you":**
  * **Rendering:** 6:130 — divine address via one's own species.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[7:35]` "when messengers from among you come to you, relating to you My revelations" — near-verbatim twin, with the believer's clause added (rg-verified); also `[2:151]`.
* **"kill their children":**
  * **Rendering:** `[6:137]` (enticed by their partners) and `[6:140]` (foolishly, without knowledge).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[17:31]` "And do not kill your children for fear of poverty. We provide for them, and for you" — the same crime answered at the root of economy; `[60:12]` — child-killing listed among the pledging-away sins; within the surah, `[6:151]` legislates it into the covenant.
* **"ranks":**
  * **Rendering:** `[6:132]` — ranks by deeds, this side and that.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[6:165]` "raised some of you in ranks over others, in order to test you" — the worldly hierarchy re-described as trial, completing this ruku's claim that all station is earned and provisional (rg-verified pair).
* **"the Rich Beyond Need, the Possessor of Mercy":**
  * **Rendering:** `[6:133]` — divine self-sufficiency paired with mercy-owner, then the substitution threat.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[35:15]` "O people! It is you who are the poor, in need of Allah; while Allah is the Rich, the Praiseworthy" — the mirror image of the same two names (rg-verified); within the surah, "He has inscribed for Himself mercy" `[6:12]` and "your Lord has prescribed mercy for Himself" `[6:54]` are the earlier drafts.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Meccan (classical consensus).
* **Asbab / narrative context:** the taboos of 6:136–139 are the classical Bahira/Saiba/Wasila/Hami complex and the sanctuary-reserve customs (Source: consensus of classical tafsir on these verses; cf. `[5:103]`); the killing of children is reported both as female infanticide and as votive child-slaughter — the reports differ (Source: Ibn Kathir and Tabari on 6:137 — verify). "Their partners" in 6:137 reads as the devil-figures or chieftains who legislate; the text does not resolve which (observable).
