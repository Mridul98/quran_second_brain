# The blessed Scripture and the tested successors (6:155–165)
**UID:** 202610141420
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 6:155–165 (Ruku 20 of 20 in al-An'am)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[6:155]` – `[6:165]` (11 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah's closing sermon: follow this blessed Scripture before excuses or angels or final signs make following impossible; weigh deeds on tenfold grace; walk Abraham's upright creed with your whole life — prayer, sacrifice, living, dying — because you are successors on earth, ranked in degrees, under test.
* **Placement:** The final ruku answers the excuses pre-staged in 6:91 ("neither you, nor your ancestors") and closes the surah where it opened: praise to Allah, Lord of the Worlds (6:1 ↔ 6:162), the path of 6:153 now walked by its prophet in 6:161.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[6:155]`** "This too is a blessed Scripture that We revealed; so follow it, and be righteous, that you may receive mercy."
  * **Gloss:** "This too" — the same blessedness claimed for Moses' book in 6:92; the only response clause is follow + righteous, the only aim stated: mercy.
* **`[6:156]`** "Lest you say, “The Scripture was revealed to two parties before us, and we were unaware of their teachings.”"
  * **Gloss:** Excuse one, pre-empted: the two parties (classically: Jews and Christians) cannot excuse illiteracy once a book of their own language has come (Source: classical identification — verify).
* **`[6:157]`** — two clause-chunks:
  * "Or lest you say, “Had the Scripture been revealed to us, we would have been better guided than they.”"
  * "Clarification has come to you from your Lord, and guidance, and mercy. Who then does greater wrong than he who gives the lie to Allah's messages, and turns away from them? We will repay those who turn away from Our messages with the worst kind of punishment, because of their turning away."
  * **Gloss:** Excuse two — the hypothetical presumption — answered by a third-person verdict: clarification IS here; the greatest-wrong refrain returns (6:21, 6:93, 6:144) for the liars-and-turners.
* **`[6:158]`** — two clause-chunks:
  * "Are they waiting for anything but for the angels to come to them, or for your Lord to arrive, or for some of your Lord’s signs to come?"
  * "On the Day when some of your Lord’s signs come, no soul will benefit from its faith unless it had believed previously, or had earned goodness through its faith. Say, “Wait, we too are waiting.”"
  * **Gloss:** The waiting-game ends at a closed door: seeing-when-signs-arrive is not believing; the earlier 6:93 "too proud to accept His revelations" is now unprofitable forever.
* **`[6:159]`** "As for those who divided their religion and became sects—you have nothing to do with them. Their case rests with Allah; then He will inform them of what they used to do."
  * **Gloss:** The sect-dividers are excommunicated from the Prophet's concern but not from God's audit — a separation clause with the judgment deferred.
* **`[6:160]`** "Whoever comes up with a good deed will have ten times its like; and whoever comes up with an evil deed will be repaid only with its equivalent—they will not be wronged."
  * **Gloss:** The grace-scales: 10:1 for good, 1:1 for evil, and the closing "they will not be wronged" makes generosity the asymmetry of the system.
* **`[6:161]`** "Say, “My Lord has guided me to a straight path, an upright religion, the creed of Abraham the Monotheist, who was not a polytheist.”"
  * **Gloss:** Guidance, religion, and pedigree in one line — the path (6:126/6:153) is traced back to the Abraham of 6:74–83, the surah's first exemplar.
* **`[6:162]`** "Say, “My prayer and my worship, and my life and my death, are devoted to Allah, the Lord of the Worlds."
  * **Gloss:** Total devotion: cultic acts and the two bookends of biography, all "devoted" (nusuk — classically glossed as sacrifice/act of worship; Source: Ibn Kathir on 6:162 citing Ibn Abbas — verify). (Corpus [sic]: quotation opens and is never closed.)
* **`[6:163]`** "No associate has He. Thus I am commanded, and I am the first of those who submit."
  * **Gloss:** The creed-clause mid-devotion; "first of those who submit" re-quotes the charge of 6:14 word-for-word — the surah bookends the Prophet's mandate.
* **`[6:164]`** "Say, “Am I to seek a Lord other than Allah, when He is the Lord of all things?” No soul gets except what it is due, and no soul bears the burdens of another. Then to your Lord is your return, then He will inform you regarding your disputes."
  * **Gloss:** Lordship-question, personal-earnings rule, no-burden-transfer rule, return, adjudication — five clauses that undo every intercession-economy of 6:94 and 6:136.
* **`[6:165]`** — two clause-chunks:
  * "It is He who made you successors on the earth, and raised some of you in ranks over others, in order to test you through what He has given you."
  * "Your Lord is Quick in retribution, and He is Forgiving and Merciful."
  * **Gloss:** The final word-picture: khala'if — inheritors under audit; inequality reframed as testing material, not status; and the surah ends, as it preached, with both hands of God extended (6:12, 6:54, 6:133, 6:147).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **"a blessed Scripture":**
  * **Rendering:** `[6:155]` — "blessed" applied to the Book, its second application in this surah (6:92; rg-verified).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[38:29]` "A blessed Book that We sent down to you, that they may ponder its Verses" — the same adjective with a contemplation-purpose; `[21:50]` — the reminder is "blessed" too (rg list).
* **"sects" (shiya'):**
  * **Rendering:** "divided their religion and became sects" `[6:159]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[30:32]` "those who divided their religion, and became sects; each faction pleased with what they have" — near-verbatim twin with the self-satisfaction clause added; `[23:53]` "they tore themselves into sects" — same picture (both rg-verified; the two are this note's cross-commentary).
* **"ten times its like":**
  * **Rendering:** `[6:160]` — the corpus's only explicit ten-fold multiplier for a single good deed (rg-verified).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[28:84]` — the same 10:1-vs-equivalent grammar with "better" for good; `[2:261]` — the infaq-parable multiplies seven hundred-fold: ten is the floor, not the ceiling of grace (the infraq note's ledger).
* **"successors" (khalā'if):**
  * **Rendering:** "made you successors on the earth" `[6:165]`; the same verb-family underwrites 6:133's "substitute whomever He wills."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:30]` "I am placing a successor on earth" — the angelic question's answer-scene is now the surah's last word; `[10:14]` "Then We made you successors on earth after them, to see how you would behave" — successorship defined as examination (both rg-verified).

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Meccan (classical consensus); 6:159 is classically cited beyond the pagans as a warning about communal fragmentation (Source: the 73-sect reports collected by Ibn Kathir on 6:159 — verify).
* **Asbab / narrative context:** 6:158's "some of your Lord's signs" is read in classical tafsir as the final cosmic signs (Sun from the west, the Beast, the Dajjal — per the report in Sahih Muslim cited by Ibn Kathir on 6:158; the verse itself names none — observable); "the two parties" of 6:156 is classically the Jews and the Christians (Source: consensus gloss — verify).
