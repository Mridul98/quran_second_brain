# The keys of the unseen (6:56–60)
**UID:** 202610141407
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 6:56–60 (Ruku 7 of 20 in al-An’am)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[6:56]` – `[6:60]` (5 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Three “Say” disavowals — forbidden worship, clear evidence held, no power to hasten — climb to the surah’s most quoted creed line: with Him are the keys of the unseen, none knows them but He, down to the falling leaf, the buried grain, the wet and the dry, all in a clear record; and the same exhaustive knowledge is exercised as intimacy — He takes you by night, knows what you earn by day, raises you again until the fixed term.
* **Placement:** Ruku 7; the epistemic summit of the Meccan half. Ruku 5’s disclaimer (“nor do I know the future,” 6:50) is answered here: the future belongs to a lock only Allah holds — and the ruku hands off to 6:61’s death-envoys (ruku 8) on the word “return.”

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[6:56]`** "Say, “I am forbidden from worshiping those you pray to besides Allah.” Say, “I will not follow your desires; else I would be lost and not be of those guided.”"
  * **Gloss:** Revelation (not desire) steers the prophet — the answer to 18:28-type pressure to re-seat the audience; the guided/un-guided pair opens the ruku’s guidance motif.
* **`[6:57]`** — two clause-chunks:
  * "Say, “I stand on clear evidence from my Lord, and you have rejected Him."
  * "I do not possess what you seek me to hasten; the decision belongs solely to Allah. He states the truth, and He is the Best of Judges.”"
  * **Gloss:** Evidence-claim, hastening-denial, verdict-transfer: God judges, and He “states the truth” — speech as final court. (Book typography: “you have rejected Him” where the Arabic pronoun refers to the evidence/Book — quoted as printed.)
* **`[6:58]`** "Say, “If I possessed what you seek me to hasten, the matter between you and me would have been settled. Allah is well aware of the unjust.”"
  * **Gloss:** The 6:8 thought re-performed from the prophet’s mouth: demanded doom would already have landed — reprieve is grace, not weakness.
* **`[6:59]`** — three clause-chunks:
  * "With Him are the keys of the unseen; none knows them except He. And He knows everything on land and in the sea."
  * "Not a leaf falls but He knows it; and there is not a single grain in the darkness of earth,"
  * "nor is there anything wet or dry, but is in a clear record."
  * **Gloss:** The creed of total knowledge in three zooms: the locked keys, the falling leaf and buried grain, the wet-and-dry inventory — and every micro-event is simultaneously in a record: knowing and writing are one act.
* **`[6:60]`** "It is He Who takes you by night, and He knows what you earn by day. Then He raises you up in it, until a fixed term is fulfilled. Then to Him is your return, then He will inform you of what you used to do."
  * **Gloss:** Sleep as the nightly rehearsal of death and resurrection — the omni-knowledge of 6:59 descends into daily biography, with the 6:2 “term” re-asserted.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`keys of the unseen`** (mafātīḥ al-ghayb):
  * **Rendering:** `[6:59]` — “keys” used only twice in the corpus: here and of Qarun’s coffers, `[28:76]` “We had given him treasures, the keys of which would weigh down a group of strong men” — human keys bow under weight; divine keys bow to no knower but Him (observable placement).
  * **Elsewhere:** the exclusivity clause rhymes with `[74:31]` “None knows the soldiers of your Lord except He”; classical ḥadīth reports count the keys of the unseen as five, linking this verse to `[31:34]` and `[41:47]` (*Source: classical transmitted reports on 6:59 — verify*).
* **`the unseen / the future`** (ghayb):
  * **Rendering:** “the unseen” here `[6:59]`, but `[6:50]` renders the same field “the future” — “nor do I know the future” (verified two verses before, same surah); `[2:3]` “believe in the unseen”; `[34:3]` “He is the Knower of the unseen.”
  * **Elsewhere:** `[27:65]` “No one in the heavens or on earth knows the future except Allah” — the twin of 6:59’s exclusivity clause in the “future” costume (all refs verified via `rg`).
* **`a clear record`** (kitāb mubīn):
  * **Rendering:** `[6:59]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[34:3]` “All are in a Clear Record”; `[10:61]` “but is in a clear record”; `[36:12]` “We have tallied all things in a Clear Record”; `[11:6]` “Everything is in a Clear Book” — the record-formula’s four cousins, all verified; within-surah twin: `[6:38]` “We neglected nothing in the Scripture.”
* **`the Best of Judges`** (khayr al-ḥākimīn):
  * **Rendering:** “He is the Best of Judges” `[6:57]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[7:87]` Shuʿayb’s “for He is the Best of Judges”; `[10:109]` “be patient until Allah issues His judgment, for He is the Best of judges” — the title belongs to patience-scenes: the judge is best because the verdict is delayed (verified).

## 4. Cross-References (The Web)
* **Detail twins:** `[31:34]` — “With Allah rests the knowledge of the Hour… He knows what the wombs contain. No soul knows what it will reap tomorrow…” and `[41:47]` — “No fruit emerges from its sheath… except with His knowledge” — the leaf-and-grain precision restated; the classical pairing of these with 6:59’s “keys” is report-based (*Source: classical — verify*).
* **Within surah — settled matter:** `[6:58]` “the matter… would have been settled” ↔ `[6:8]` “Had We sent down an angel, the matter would have been settled” — verbatim echo; the angel-demand of ruku 1 answers the hastening of ruku 7.
* **Within surah — night vocabulary:** `[6:59]` “darkness of earth” ↔ `[6:60]` “takes you by night” ↔ `[6:13]` “whatever rests in the night and the day” — darkness is where hidden-ownership is demonstrated, to be answered by stars-as-guides at `[6:97]` (“darkness of land and sea”, verified).
* **Within surah — term chain:** `[6:60]` “until a fixed term is fulfilled” ↔ `[6:2]` (ruku 1) — the determined term now governs nightly resurrection.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Meccan (classical consensus; order 55) — the “decision belongs solely to Allah” line later entered political history as a slogan; nothing in the ruku carries a Medinan ruling (observable).
* **Asbab / narrative context:** none reported; 6:57’s “what you seek me to hasten” continues the sign-demand sequence of 6:7–9/6:37 without new occasion (Source: observable within-corpus sequence).
