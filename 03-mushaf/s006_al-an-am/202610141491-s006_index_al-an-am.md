# Surah 6: The Cattle (al-An'am)
**UID:** 202610141491
**Type:** Index / Surah Map
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Meccan — unanimous at surah level in the classical lists; placed 55th in the order of revelation. Source: classical surah-metadata/revelation-order lists. The argument's texture matches the label: it debates living Meccan cult practice (apportioned crops and cattle, child-killing at partners' counsel, taboo zoology — ruku 16–17), with no household-law cascade of the Medinan type.
* **Size:** 165 ayahs, 20 ruku — classically described as the longest Meccan surah. Source: standard surah descriptions. The 20 ruku fall in an almost deliberate rhythm of tens and elevens around the hinge ruku 9–10 (Abraham and the prophets' chain).
* **Central theme:** Al-An'am builds creed out of cosmos: praise to the Creator of the darkness/light pair opens a sustained argument — travel the earth and observe, read the grain-split and the dawn-split, the guiding stars, the rain-fed orchards — against anyone who "ascribes equals," an Originator who cannot have a son and whom "no vision can grasp." Abraham's dialogue with Azar and the dethroning of the star, moon, and sun by their own setting (6:74–83) make the argument flesh, and the prophets' chain (6:83–90) receipts it: "That was Our argument which We gave to Abraham" — follow their guidance. The surah then lands the creed at the table and the street: invented food-taboos are indicted (6:136–144), the honest forbidden-list is four items (6:145), and the moral testament of 6:151–153 gathers everything onto one road — "This is My path, straight, so follow it" (6:153, the Fatihah's petition answered; see the guidance knowledge note below) — closing with the believer's whole life as prayer and sacrifice to the Lord of the Worlds (6:162–164).

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Creed through cosmos (ruku 1–4):** praise → "travel the earth and observe" → the first "Say" chain (sovereignty, inscribed mercy, the testimony question) → the Day-scene trilogy and this world as game — with the consolation hinge (6:33–34) and the cosmic inventory of 6:38 answering the sign-demand.
* **The respite-and-seizure arc, socially turned (ruku 5–8):** divine sociology (hardening, istidraj, sudden seizure) → the messenger's fourfold disclaimer → the lowly callers protected against a counterfeit seating-economy → the epistemic summit of the keys of the unseen (6:59) → the death-staff, the darkness-instinct of 6:63, and the etiquette of disengagement.
* **Abraham and the chain (ruku 9–10):** the creed-sermon against zero-effect idols; the star-moon-sun dethroning and the security-definition (6:82) → receipts by the prophets' chain and the duty "follow their guidance" (6:90).
* **Book versus world in court (ruku 11–14):** the "nothing was revealed" slogan tried against Moses' Scripture → creation's five signs exhibit the Revealer → no vision grasps Him, the son-claim dies → even dumped miracles wouldn't convince, because the Word is complete and unchangeable, and the test lands at the dinner table (6:118–121).
* **Hearts, taboos, testament (ruku 15–19):** lamp-walker versus squeezed heart → both species subpoenaed, fabricated apportionments indicted → constructive harvest-law and the cross-examined eight pairs → the honest four-item list and the collapsed fatalist alibi → the ten-command testament onto the one straight path (6:153).
* **Successors (ruku 20):** follow before the excuse-window closes; tenfold grace; the whole life as prayer and sacrifice — "we are successors on earth, ranked in degrees, under test" (6:165) — praise bookended: 6:1 ↔ 6:162–164.
