# Opened hearts on the straight path (6:122–129)
**UID:** 202610141415
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 6:122–129 (Ruku 15 of 20 in al-An'am)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[6:122]` – `[6:129]` (8 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Two physiology parables sort humanity — a man raised from death walking with a lamp, and a heart squeezed like a man climbing the sky — then the scene cuts to the Day when jinn and humans convict each other: the opened hearts inherit the Home of Peace, and the path between is named, once, as "the straight path of your Lord."
* **Placement:** The surah's emotional mid-pivot: after the food-law humiliation of 6:116–121, guidance is re-internalized (hearts, not tables), and the reckoning scenes that began at 6:93–94 resume in the jinn–mankind dialogue which 6:130 completes.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[6:122]`** "Is he who was dead, then We gave him life, and made for him a light by which he walks among the people, like he who is in total darkness, and cannot get out of it? Thus the doings of disbelievers are made to appear good to them."
  * **Gloss:** The believer is a resurrection résumé — dead, given life, given a lamp, walking in public; the pagan is the same body with the light removed.
* **`[6:123]`** "And thus We set up in every city its leading wicked sinners, to conspire in it, but they conspire only against themselves, and they do not realize it."
  * **Gloss:** The social twin of 6:122: every town has its mafia of the fatwa (6:121's "followers"), and their plot backfires by construction.
* **`[6:124]`** "When a sign comes to them, they say, “We will not believe unless we are given the like of what was given to Allah’s messengers.” Allah knows best where to place His message. Humiliation from Allah and severe torment will afflict the criminals for their scheming."
  * **Gloss:** The demand for egalitarian revelation is answered by election: message-placement is God's knowledge, not a quota.
* **`[6:125]`** — two clause-chunks:
  * "Whomever Allah desires to guide, He spreads open his heart to Islam; and whomever He desires to misguide, He makes his heart narrow, constricted,"
  * "as though he were climbing up the sky. Allah thus lays defilement upon those who do not believe."
  * **Gloss:** Guidance is elasticity, misguidance is suffocation — submission to God as breathing, defiance as climbing a vertical void (Source: the sky-climbing gloss per classical tafsir, Ibn Kathir on 6:125 — verify).
* **`[6:126]`** "This is the straight path of your Lord. We have explained the revelations in detail for people who recollect."
  * **Gloss:** The road named mid-sura, with the surah's own credential formula ("We have explained… in detail," cf. 6:97–98, 6:119) stamped on it.
* **`[6:127]`** "For them is the Home of Peace with their Lord, and He is their Master—because of what they used to do."
  * **Gloss:** The path's terminus: a "Home of Peace" (dār al-salām), earned by conduct, held by a protecting Friend.
* **`[6:128]`** — three clause-chunks:
  * "On the Day when He gathers them all together: “O assembly of jinn, you have exploited multitudes of humans.”"
  * "Their adherents among mankind will say, “Our Lord, we have profited from one another, but we have reached the term that you have assigned for us.”"
  * "He will say, “The Fire is your dwelling, wherein you will remain, except as Allah wills. Your Lord is Wise and Informed."
  * **Gloss:** The courtroom opens on both species: exploitation charged, complicity confessed, sentence passed with the door of exception left ajar — "except as Allah wills." (Corpus [sic]: the last quotation opens and is never closed — book typography, not extraction damage.)
* **`[6:129]`** "Thus We make some of the wrongdoers befriend one another, because of what they used to do."
  * **Gloss:** The verdict generalized into a law of moral gravity: the wicked are magnetically paired with their own kind.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **"a light by which he walks":**
  * **Rendering:** "made for him a light" `[6:122]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[57:28]` "He will… give you a light by which you walk" — the same walking-lamp promised at the end of the corpus; within this surah the lamp traces back to 6:91's "a light and guidance."
* **"spreads open / narrow, constricted" (sharḥ ṣadr, ḍayyiq):**
  * **Rendering:** 6:125's double physiology.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[39:22]` "What about someone whose heart Allah has opened to Islam, so that he follows a light from His Lord? Woe to those whose hearts are hardened…" — the identical opened/woe contrast, and the hardened-heart verdict of `[6:43]` within this surah (rg-verified).
* **"straight path":**
  * **Rendering:** "This is the straight path of your Lord" `[6:126]` — one of two surah-placements that name it (the other being 6:161; 6:153 will say "My path").
  * **Elsewhere:** `[1:6]` the petition for the path; `[10:25]` — in a single verse, the Home of Peace AND the straight path, exactly as 6:126–127 pair them; `[37:118]` "And We guided them upon the straight path" (the prophets of 6:83–90).
* **"the Home of Peace" (dār al-salām):**
  * **Rendering:** 6:127 — a place, with a Lord resident in it.
  * **Elsewhere:** the phrase occurs only at `[6:127]` and `[10:25]` (rg-verified) — a two-site term, making Yunus the best commentary on this verse.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Meccan (classical consensus).
* **Asbab / narrative context:** 6:124's taunt is classically read as the chieftains' demand for equivalent prophetic status (Source: Ibn Kathir on 6:124 — verify); the "except as Allah wills" caveat in 6:128's sentence is left unexplained in the text itself (observable) — later theology reads it variously; do not flatten it.
