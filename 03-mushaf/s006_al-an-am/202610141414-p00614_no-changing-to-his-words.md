# "There is no changing to His words" (6:111–121)
**UID:** 202610141414
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 6:111–121 (Ruku 14 of 20 in al-An'am)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[6:111]` – `[6:121]` (11 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Even dumped miracle-evidence would not produce faith, because the real contest is over the Word — which is complete, unchangeable, and humanly unpopular: most of earth would divert you from Allah's path, so the test shrinks to the plainest obedience left, naming Allah over your food and refusing to name Him where they never did.
* **Placement:** The hinge of the surah's second half: 6:111–115 close the sign-demand argument; 6:116–121 land it at the dinner table, setting up the fabricated-taboo indictment of 6:136–144 and the honest list of 6:145.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[6:111]`** "Even if We sent down the angels to them, and the dead spoke to them, and We gathered all things before them, they still would not believe, unless Allah wills; but most of them are ignorant."
  * **Gloss:** The three fantasy evidences of 6:109's sworn oaths are granted in imagination and still fail — belief stays within Allah's will.
* **`[6:112]`** — two clause-chunks:
  * "Likewise, We have assigned for every prophet an enemy—human and jinn devils— inspiring one another with fancy words in order to deceive."
  * "But had your Lord willed, they would not have done it. So leave them to their fabrications."
  * **Gloss:** Opposition is systematized ("for every prophet"), and its weapon is aesthetic rhetoric — "fancy words," the same counterfeit the lies of 6:93 forge.
* **`[6:113]`** "So that the hearts of those who do not believe in the Hereafter may incline to it, and be content with it, and that they may perpetrate whatever they perpetrate."
  * **Gloss:** The purpose clause names the consumers of fancy speech: hearts with no Hereafter, who are left to their purchases.
* **`[6:114]`** — two clause-chunks:
  * "“Shall I seek a judge other than Allah, when He is the One who revealed to you the Book, explained in detail?”"
  * "Those to whom We gave the Book know that it is the truth revealed from your Lord. So do not be of those who doubt."
  * **Gloss:** To arbitrate truth, God Himself is the only judge — and He has already filed the opinion: the detailed Book, acknowledged even by the Book's custodians.
* **`[6:115]`** "The Word of your Lord has been completed, in truth and justice. There is no changing to His words. He is the Hearer, the Knower."
  * **Gloss:** The ruku's keystone: completed (kalima), truthful, just, unchangeable — four predicates the earlier "words" of threat and mercy already performed (cf. 6:34).
* **`[6:116]`** "If you were to obey most of those on earth, they would divert you from Allah’s path. They follow nothing but assumptions, and they only conjecture."
  * **Gloss:** Majority is demoted from evidence to noise: zann twice — assumption then conjecture.
* **`[6:117]`** "Your Lord knows best who strays from His path, and He knows best the guided ones."
  * **Gloss:** Guidance and straying are God's registered populations — the survey is not run by earth's majority.
* **`[6:118]`** "So eat of that over which the Name of Allah was pronounced, if you indeed believe in His revelations."
  * **Gloss:** "If you believe" attached to an act of eating: creed made verifiable at the table.
* **`[6:119]`** — two clause-chunks:
  * "And why should you not eat of that over which the Name of Allah is pronounced, when He has detailed for you what is prohibited for you, unless you are compelled by necessity?"
  * "Many lead astray with their opinions, through lack of knowledge. Your Lord knows best the transgressors."
  * **Gloss:** The prohibition-list is already "detailed" — the innovation of extra taboos is opinion, not revelation (the same verb as 6:97–98's sign-detailing).
* **`[6:120]`** "So abandon sin, outward and inward. Those who commit sins will be repaid for what they used to perpetrate."
  * **Gloss:** A one-line ethic bracketing the food rules: the taboos that matter are sins, public and secret (answered at 6:151).
* **`[6:121]`** "And do not eat from that over which the Name of Allah was not pronounced, for it is abomination. The devils inspire their followers to argue with you; but if you obey them, you would be polytheists."
  * **Gloss:** The silent-name meat is fisq — "abomination" — and the argument behind it is devil-inspiration (6:112's method), so obeying it is treaty with partners.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **"His words" (kalām/kalimāt):**
  * **Rendering:** 6:115 "There is no changing to His words"; the identical clause at `[6:34]` ("There can be no change to Allah's words") — rg-verified; `[18:27]` reuses the phrase for the recited Book.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[6:114]` — the Book "explained in detail" is one of those words; the kalam-note's channel-question is answered here by immutability.
* **"judge" (ḥakam):**
  * **Rendering:** "Shall I seek a judge other than Allah" `[6:114]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[6:57]` "the decision belongs solely to Allah… He is the Best of Judges" — the same courtroom image earlier in the surah (rg-verified).
* **"conjecture" (ẓann):**
  * **Rendering:** "They follow nothing but assumptions, and they only conjecture" `[6:116]` — one Arabic noun doubled into two English words (observable).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[10:36]` "Most of them follow nothing but assumptions; and assumptions avail nothing against the truth"; `[6:148]` — the polytheists' fatalist plea is "nothing but conjecture" (same ruku-arc).
* **"pronounce the Name" (dhakra):**
  * **Rendering:** 6:118/119/121 — the Name's presence or absence is the line between belief and "abomination."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[22:36]` "So pronounce Allah's name upon them as they line up" — the command's ritual form on offerings; `[6:138]` — animals over which they "do not pronounce the name of Allah," the corruption already named (rg-verified: "pronounced" appears at 6:118/119/121 only within this surah).

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Meccan (classical consensus); the meat-controversy is the Meccan table, not a Medinan dietary code.
* **Asbab / narrative context:** classical commentators read 6:116 as barring the derivation of truth from human majority — it is cited in the usul tradition against majority-as-proof (Source: agent inference — verify the usul citation); "compelled by necessity" in 6:119 previews 6:145 and mirrors `[2:173]` ("without desiring or exceeding").
