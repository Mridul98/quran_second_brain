# The harvest due and the eight pairs (6:141–144)
**UID:** 202610141417
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 6:141–144 (Ruku 17 of 20 in al-An'am)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[6:141]` – `[6:144]` (4 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Against the fabricated taboos, the orchard and the herd are legislated: gardens trellised and untrellised are to be eaten, tithed on the harvest day, and never wasted; and the eight livestock pairs are put on cross-examination — did God forbid the males, the females, or what the wombs carry? were you present when He enjoined it?
* **Placement:** The constructive answer to ruku 16's indictments: 6:99's orchard-sign becomes 6:141's harvest-law — the surah's most direct sign-to-statute link — before 6:145 completes the diet with the short, honest list.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[6:141]`** — three clause-chunks:
  * "It is He who produces gardens, both cultivated and wild, and date-palms, and crops of diverse tastes, and olives and pomegranates, similar and dissimilar."
  * "Eat of its fruit when it yields, and give its due on the day of its harvest, and do not waste."
  * "He does not love the wasteful."
  * **Gloss:** Creation inventory in, command out: eat, give the due the same day (no delay, no excuse of uncertainty), and the due is followed by a ceiling on excess — provision is for circulation, never for hoarding or waste.
* **`[6:142]`** "Among the livestock are some for transportation, and some for clothing. Eat of what Allah has provided for you, and do not follow the footsteps of Satan. He is to you an outright enemy."
  * **Gloss:** The animals' three God-given uses — ride, wear, eat — are stated against 6:138's back-ban; the taboo is renamed "footsteps of Satan," the enmity the surah has been building (cf. 6:121 "polytheists").
* **`[6:143]`** — two clause-chunks:
  * "Eight pairs: two of the sheep, and two of the goats."
  * "Say, “Did He forbid the two males, or the two females, or what the wombs of the two females contain? Inform me with knowledge, if you are truthful.”"
  * **Gloss:** The enumeration (sheep ×2, goats ×2) with a cross-examination attached: no sex, and no unborn offspring, is off the table by anyone's decree.
* **`[6:144]`** — three clause-chunks:
  * "And two of the camels, and two of the cattle."
  * "Say, “Did He forbid the two males, or the two females, or what the wombs of the two females contain? Were you present when Allah enjoined this upon you?”"
  * "Who does greater wrong than he who invents lies and attributes them to Allah, in order to mislead people without knowledge? Allah does not guide the wicked people."
  * **Gloss:** The second half of the eight pairs raises the interrogation to eyewitness demand — "were you present?" — and the third rung returns to the surah's greatest-wrong refrain (6:21, 6:93), now applied to dietary forgery.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **"give its due" (ḥaqqahu):**
  * **Rendering:** "give its due on the day of its harvest" `[6:141]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[51:19]` "in their wealth, there was a share for the beggar and the deprived"; `[70:24]` "in whose wealth is a rightful share" — the due is person-shaped: a named claimant's right, not the owner's charity decision (the two A'raf/Hadid verses are the corpus's fixed gloss).
* **"waste / the wasteful" (isrāf):**
  * **Rendering:** "do not waste… He does not love the wasteful" `[6:141]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[25:67]` "neither wasteful nor stingy, but choose a middle course"; `[17:27]` "The extravagant are brethren of the devils" — the same root is rendered "excessive" at `[7:31]` ("He does not love the excessive"), a translation-family worth tracking (rg-verified).
* **"livestock" (anʿām) — the surah's namesake:**
  * **Rendering:** "livestock" at `[6:142]`; the surah-title itself (LIVESTOCK, corpus header of s006).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[39:6]` "brought down livestock for you—eight kinds in pairs… He creates you in the wombs of your mothers" — one verse spans this ruku's entire vocabulary (livestock, eight pairs, wombs, deviation); `[22:30]` "All livestock are permitted to you, except what is recited to you" — the open-then-exception rule that 6:143–144's interrogation assumes.
* **"similar and dissimilar":**
  * **Rendering:** identical phrase at `[6:99]` and `[6:141]` — exactly two sites in the whole corpus (rg-verified), binding the orchard-sign to the orchard-law.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Meccan (classical consensus).
* **Asbab / narrative context:** "cultivated and wild" is glossed classically as trellised and untrellised gardens alike (Source: Tabari and Ibn Kathir on 6:141 — verify wording); whether "its due" was an independent obligation later specified by zakat, or a recommended harvest-charity, is a real early disagreement — Ibn Kathir reports the Ibn Abbas charity-at-harvest practice and notes both readings (Source: Ibn Kathir on 6:141 — verify).
