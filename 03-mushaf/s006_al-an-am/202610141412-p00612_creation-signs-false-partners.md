# Creation signs answer invented partners (6:95–100)
**UID:** 202610141412
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 6:95–100 (Ruku 12 of 20 in al-An'am)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[6:95]` – `[6:100]` (6 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Five creation signs — the split of grain, the split of dawn, the guiding stars, the one human origin, the rain-fed orchard cascade — exhibit the God who "brings the living from the dead"; against that exhibit, the partners attributed to Him are jinn He created, and His "sons and daughters" are inventions "without any knowledge."
* **Placement:** The evidentiary turn after the courtroom of 6:91–94: the Book could not prove the Revealer, so the world does — each sign closing with "Such is Allah. So how could you deviate?"

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[6:95]`** "It is Allah Who splits the grain and the seed. He brings the living from the dead, and He brings the dead from the living. Such is Allah. So how could you deviate?"
  * **Gloss:** Resurrection argued as agronomy: every sprout is a live thing issued from a dead husk, and the same agency runs the cosmos' bookkeeping both directions.
* **`[6:96]`** "It is He Who breaks the dawn. And He made the night for rest, and the sun and the moon for calculation. Such is the disposition of the Almighty, the All-Knowing."
  * **Gloss:** The second "split" is horological — light out of black night, luminaries turned into a calendar; "Almighty, the All-Knowing" answers the claim that revelation is beneath God's ways.
* **`[6:97]`** "And it is He Who created the stars for you, that you may be guided by them in the darkness of land and sea. We thus explain the revelations for people who know."
  * **Gloss:** Stars re-issued as gifts for navigation; the signature formula "We thus explain the revelations" (recurs at `[6:98]`, `[6:105]`, `[6:126]`) marks the sign-list as itself revelation.
* **`[6:98]`** "And it is He who produced you from a single person, then a repository, then a depository. We have detailed the revelations for people who understand."
  * **Gloss:** Human origin from one self, then two cryptic stations ("repository," "depository") — descent and lodging of the embryo; Itani keeps the pair opaque rather than harmonizing it (observable term choice).
* **`[6:99]`** — three clause-chunks:
  * "And it is He who sends down water from the sky. With it We produce vegetation of all kinds, from which We bring greenery, from which We produce grains in clusters."
  * "And palm-trees with hanging clusters, and vineyards, and olives, and pomegranates—similar and dissimilar. Watch their fruits as they grow and ripen."
  * "Surely in this are signs for people who believe."
  * **Gloss:** One water-source cascades into six crop-families, the chain ("from which… from which…") mimicking germination itself — and an imperative is hidden inside the sign: "Watch."
* **`[6:100]`** "Yet they attributed to Allah partners—the sprites—although He created them. And they invented for Him sons and daughters, without any knowledge. Glory be to Him. He is exalted, beyond what they describe."
  * **Gloss:** The punchline names the crime the signs refute: made things made partners for the Maker; the genealogy-fantasy closes with the corpus's stock transcendence-formula, "Glory be to Him."

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **"splits / breaks" (fa-ṭ-r):**
  * **Rendering:** "splits the grain and the seed" `[6:95]` and "breaks the dawn" `[6:96]` — one opening-action image across seed-coat and horizon (Source: classical lexicon on fataqa/falq — verify the root unity).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[113:1]` "daybreak" — the same dawn-split as refuge-object; `[42:11]` "Originator of the heavens and the earth."
* **"the living from the dead":**
  * **Rendering:** stated both directions in `[6:95]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[10:31]` "Who… brings the living out of the dead, and the dead out of the living?" — the identical double clause as a rhetorical question to the idolaters.
* **"a single person" (nafs wāḥida):**
  * **Rendering:** "produced you from a single person" `[6:98]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[4:1]`, `[7:189]`, and especially `[39:6]` "He created you from one person, then made from it its mate, and brought down livestock for you—eight kinds in pairs" — the same verse runs from one-self to the eight pairs, exactly the sequence 6:98 → 6:142–144 will legislate.
* **"the sprites" (jinn):**
  * **Rendering:** "sprites" for the attributed partners at `[6:100]`; the same choice appears at `[27:17]`, `[27:39]`, `[34:12]`, `[34:14]`, while "jinn" is retained elsewhere in this surah (`[6:112]`, `[6:128]`, `[6:130]`) — translator term-choice drift (observable in corpus).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[37:158]` "And they invented a relationship between Him and the jinn" — the nearest paraphrase of 6:100a.

## 4. Cross-References (The Web)
* **Within surah:** `[6:59]` ↔ `[6:95]` — "not a single grain in the darkness of earth… is in a clear record" (God's knowledge of the seed) meets 6:95 (God's splitting of it): one atom, two attributes.

* **Parallel:** `[37:158]` — invented jinn-kinship; 6:100's "partners—although He created them" names the same fabrication.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Meccan (classical consensus); the sign-catalog is the Meccan sermon's home form.
* **Asbab / narrative context:** classical tafsir reads 6:100b ("sons and daughters") against the Arab claim that the angels — or a divine-jinn kinship — were God's offspring (Source: Ibn Kathir on 6:100; cf. `[37:158]` — verify); 6:100c's "Glory be to Him" is the formula the corpus attaches to every such attribution (observable).
