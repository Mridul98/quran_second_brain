# The chain of guidance (6:83–90)
**UID:** 202610141410
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 6:83–90 (Ruku 10 of 20 in al-An’am)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[6:83]` – `[6:90]` (8 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The ruku that receipts Abraham’s argument: “That was Our argument which We gave to Abraham” — and then unfolds the chain: Noah, Abraham’s sons and descendants, David, Solomon, Job, Joseph, Moses, Aaron, Zechariah, John, Jesus, Elias, Ishmael, Elijah, Jonah, Lot — each guided, each favored, the Book-and-wisdom-and-prophethood given to a line that, if these reject it, passes “to others who do not reject them”; the single human duty follows: follow their guidance — and the messenger takes no wage for it.
* **Placement:** Ruku 10; closes the Abraham sequence opened at ruku 9. It converts one household’s argument (6:74–83) into a salvation history, and its “had they associated, their deeds would have gone in vain” is the surah’s sharpest soteriological clause before the law sections (rukus 18–19).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[6:83]`** "That was Our argument which We gave to Abraham against his people. We elevate by degrees whomever We will. Your Lord is Wise and Informed."
  * **Gloss:** The retuning verb (rafaʿa) appears: elevation is distributed by will within a Wise-Informed frame — degrees as trial, not caste (cf. 6:53).
* **`[6:84]`** — two clause-chunks:
  * "And We gave him Isaac and Jacob—each of them We guided. And We guided Noah previously; and from his descendants David, and Solomon, and Job, and Joseph, and Moses, and Aaron."
  * "Thus We reward the righteous."
  * **Gloss:** The roster proper: sons, the Noahic priority, then six descendants — a kingship-and-prophecy bundle (David, Solomon, Joseph) with the lawgivers (Moses, Aaron) — closed by the reward formula.
* **`[6:85]`** "And Zechariah, and John, and Jesus, and Elias—every one of them was of the upright."
  * **Gloss:** Second row of names; Jesus stands mid-list with no qualification beyond uprightness (ṣāliḥ — see Isa note link).
* **`[6:86]`** "And Ishmael, and Elijah, and Jonah, and Lot—We favored each one of them over all other people."
  * **Gloss:** Third row — Ishmael enters before the mission, Lot as the rescued exile; “favored… over all other people” makes election explicit.
* **`[6:87]`** "And of their ancestors, and their descendants, and their siblings—We chose them, and guided them to a straight path."
  * **Gloss:** The list spills across genealogy — chosen and guided, the Fatihah petition given its historical population.
* **`[6:88]`** "Such is Allah’s guidance. He guides with it whomever He wills of His servants. Had they associated, their deeds would have gone in vain."
  * **Gloss:** The chain is named: this is guidance — singular, owned; and one conditional clause makes shirk the solvent of every work in the list.
* **`[6:89]`** "Those are they to whom We gave the Book, and wisdom, and prophethood. If these reject them, We have entrusted them to others who do not reject them."
  * **Gloss:** The triple gift (Book, wisdom, prophethood); then the transfer clause — rejection by the addressees does not bankrupt the message; a not-yet-rejecting other is in trust.
* **`[6:90]`** "Those are they whom Allah has guided, so follow their guidance. Say, “I ask of you no compensation for it; it is just a reminder for all mankind.”"
  * **Gloss:** The command, the disclaimer, the scope: follow the chain’s guidance; take no wage; it is a reminder for all mankind — the ruku’s particular line becomes a universal address.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`elevate by degrees`** (darajāt):
  * **Rendering:** `[6:83]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[17:21]` “See how We have favored some of them over others; yet the Hereafter is greater in ranks”; `[43:32]` “We elevated some of them in rank above others, that some of them would take others in service” — degrees as social trial (verified); within-surah: `[6:165]` “raised some of you in ranks over others, in order to test you.”
* **`the Book, and wisdom, and prophethood`** (al-Kitāb wa-l-ḥikma wa-l-nubuwwa):
  * **Rendering:** `[6:89]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[3:79]` “No person to whom Allah has given the Scripture, and wisdom, and prophethood would ever say…” — the exact triple, with its anti-godmaking rider; `[29:27]` “conferred on his descendants the Prophethood and the Book” — the Abrahamic line again; `[57:26]` “established in their line Prophethood and the Scripture” (all verified).
* **`favored… over all other people`** (faḍḍala ʿalā al-ʿālamīn):
  * **Rendering:** `[6:86]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[44:32]` “And We chose them knowingly over all other people” — near-twin of 6:86–87; `[2:47]` “…favored you over all nations” — the same claim to Israel in Moses’ address (verified; note the book’s grammar at 2:47, “and I that favored,” quoted as printed).
* **`gone in vain`** (baṭala al-ʿamal):
  * **Rendering:** “their deeds would have gone in vain” `[6:88]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[39:65]` “if you idolize, your works will be in vain” — the conditional addressed to the prophet himself, classically the surah’s gravest verse; `[5:5]` “whoever rejects faith, his work will be in vain” (verified).

## 4. Cross-References (The Web)
* **Within surah:** `[6:83]` “That was Our argument” — the deictic close of ruku 9’s Abraham scene (6:74–82); the roster is the argument’s invoice.
* **Reward-formula twin:** `[37:105]` — “Thus We reward the doers of good” in the Abraham-of-the-vision scene (As-Saffat) — the 6:84 clause’s nearer twin; its ironic inversion at `[21:29]` “Thus We reward the wrongdoers” shows the formula’s range (verified).
* **Name dossier:** Zechariah and John narrated at `[19:7]` (“whose name is John, a name We have never given before”) and `[3:39]` — the roster’s bare names carry whole surahs of backstory (verified); the roster’s “Elijah” (6:86, al-Yasaʿ classically Elisha) vs. “Elias” (6:85) vs. `[38:48]` “Elisha” vs. `[37:130]` “Elijah” (for Ilyās) — term drift reported, not fixed: see Fleeting below.
* **Transfer clause:** `[62:3]` — “And others from them, who have not yet joined them” — the not-yet-rejecting community of 6:89’s closest register (Source: classical pairing on 6:89 — verify).
* **Wage-disclaimer family:** `[11:51]` “I ask you no wage for it; my wage lies with Him who originated me”; `[25:57]` “I ask of you no payment for this” — the same ujr in three English dressings (compensation/wage/payment, verified); reminder-for-mankind twin: `[81:27]` “It is only a Reminder to all mankind” (verified).
* **Straight-path echo:** `[6:87]` “guided them to a straight path” ↔ `[1:6]` ↔ within-surah `[6:39]` — the petition and the predestination clause meet in the chain.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Meccan (classical consensus; order 55) — the roster answers the sign-demanding crowd with ancestry rather than miracle: credentials by chain.
* **Asbab / narrative context:** 6:89’s “others who do not reject them” is classically read as the succession of communities (a people who would accept the Book if this one refuses) — the verse that the tradition hears as foretelling the Arab umma (*Source: consensus of classical tafsir on 6:89 — verify*). The corpus gives no explicit target (observable).
