# What is truly forbidden (6:145–150)
**UID:** 202610141418
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 6:145–150 (Ruku 18 of 20 in al-An'am)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[6:145]` – `[6:150]` (6 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The honest forbidden-list is four items long and mercy keeps the door open at the edge of necessity; the long lists are human fabrications — and the fatalist alibi ("had Allah willed, we would not have associated") collapses because Allah's argument, not their excuse, is the conclusive one.
* **Placement:** The verdict that answers ruku 16–17's counterfeit taboos; 6:145's short list becomes the covenant's moral list in 6:151–153, and 6:148–150's polemic closes the surah's argument with unbelief before the testament begins.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[6:145]`** — two clause-chunks:
  * "Say, “In what was revealed to me, I find nothing forbidden to a consumer who eats it, except carrion, or spilled blood, or the flesh of swine—because it is impure—or a sinful offering dedicated to other than Allah."
  * "But if someone is compelled by necessity, without being deliberate or malicious—your Lord is Forgiving and Merciful."
  * **Gloss:** A claimed revelation that limits revelation: the inventory is closed at four, with the necessity-clause as the mercy-valve. (Corpus [sic]: the quotation opens and never closes — book typography.)
* **`[6:146]`** — three clause-chunks:
  * "For the Jews We forbade everything with claws."
  * "As of cattle and sheep: We forbade them their fat, except what adheres to their backs, or the entrails, or what is mixed with bone."
  * "This is how We penalized them for their inequity. We are indeed truthful."
  * **Gloss:** Scripture's other diet-history: a heavier list, explicitly declared penal ("for their inequity") — contrast with the short list's framing: one is punishment, one is mercy with a necessity door.
* **`[6:147]`** "If they accuse you of lying, say, “Your Lord is Possessor of infinite mercy, but His wrath cannot be averted from the guilty people.”"
  * **Gloss:** Mercy first, wrath second — the order itself is the argument; the mercy-Possessor of 6:133 now stated as infinite, under the same consolation frame as 6:34.
* **`[6:148]`** — two clause-chunks:
  * "The polytheists will say, “Had Allah willed, we would not have practiced idolatry, nor would have our forefathers, nor would we have prohibited anything.”"
  * "Likewise those before them lied, until they tasted Our might. Say, “Do you have any knowledge that you can produce for us? You follow nothing but conjecture, and you only guess.”"
  * **Gloss:** The fatalist alibi quoted verbatim — its own three crimes match 6:136–140 — then disarmed with the knowledge-demand of 6:143–144 and the conjecture-verdict of 6:116.
* **`[6:149]`** "Say, “To Allah belongs the conclusive argument. Had He willed, He would have guided you all.”"
  * **Gloss:** The counter-move: God's will is not their excuse but His method — His conclusive argument is the guidance He actually sent (Source: Ibn Kathir on 6:149 — verify), exactly the ḥujja once given to Abraham (`[6:83]`).
* **`[6:150]`** — two clause-chunks:
  * "Say, “Produce your witnesses who would testify that Allah has prohibited this.” If they testify, do not testify with them."
  * "And do not follow the whims of those who deny Our revelation, and those who do not believe in the Hereafter, and those who equate others with their Lord."
  * **Gloss:** The epistemology ends where it began — witnesses to a divine prohibition; and the three follower-types (deniers, no-Hereafter, comparers) are the surah's whole enemy-file compressed.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **The four-item list ("carrion… blood… swine… offering"):**
  * **Rendering:** `[6:145]` — the corpus's other three full diet-lists are `[2:173]`, `[5:3]`, `[16:115]` (rg-verified); 6:145 alone adds "spilled" to blood and "because it is impure" to swine.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[16:115]` "only carrion, and blood, and the flesh of swine, and anything consecrated to other than Allah" — the nearest twin in wording and in the necessity-clause.
* **"compelled by necessity" (ḍarūra):**
  * **Rendering:** 6:145 "without being deliberate or malicious" — mirrored word-for-word at `[16:115]`, and in `[2:173]` as "without desiring or exceeding" (rg-verified; translation-family).
  * **Elsewhere:** within the surah at `[6:119]` — the necessity-clause appears first as a legal aside and then as creed.
* **"everything with claws":**
  * **Rendering:** `[6:146]` — corpus hapax (rg-verified).
  * **Elsewhere:** conceptually paired by `[3:93]` "All food was permissible to the Children of Israel, except what Israel forbade for itself before the Torah was revealed" — the corpus distinguishes self-imposed from God-imposed penalties on the same table.
* **"whims" (ahwā'):**
  * **Rendering:** "do not follow the whims" `[6:150]` — the phrase occurs nowhere else in the corpus (rg-verified); cf. "I will not follow your desires" `[6:56]` — the same root rendered "desires."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[6:119]` "Many lead astray with their opinions, through lack of knowledge" and `[6:116]`'s conjecture — the same polemic family inside this surah (root-identity across the English variants "whims"/"desires"/"opinions" is classical lexicon; here labeled agent inference — verify).

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Meccan (classical consensus); several classical sources treat 6:145 as among the Meccan food-verses with 5:3 as the Medinan completion (Source: classical disagreement, recorded by Ibn Kathir — verify).
