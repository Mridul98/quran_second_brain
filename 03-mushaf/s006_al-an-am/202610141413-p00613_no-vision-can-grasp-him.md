# No vision can grasp Him (6:101–110)
**UID:** 202610141413
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 6:101–110 (Ruku 13 of 20 in al-An'am)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[6:101]` – `[6:110]` (10 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The creation signs consolidate into creed: an originator God cannot have a son, no sight can catch Him, and He alone is worship-worthy — so the Prophet is ordered to follow the revealed Book and turn away, since the miracle-demand was never the problem: hearts that first refused are now being turned away.
* **Placement:** The creed pivot between 6:95–100's evidence and 6:111+ will-testimony; verses 101–103 close the partner-argument, 104–108 re-cast the Prophet's mandate, 109–110 explain the hardening already narrated in 6:25 and 6:46.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[6:101]`** "Originator of the heavens and the earth— how can He have a son when He never had a companion? He created all things, and He has knowledge of all things."
  * **Gloss:** The son-claim dies on the origin-question: genealogy presupposes a consort; the Creator of all has knowledge of all — ancestry is not His mode.
* **`[6:102]`** "Such is Allah, your Lord. There is no god except He, the Creator of all things; so worship Him. He is responsible for everything."
  * **Gloss:** The shahada drawn from the exhibit of 6:95–101; "worship Him" is the only fitting response, "responsible for everything" evacuating every other dependency.
* **`[6:103]`** "No vision can grasp Him, but His grasp is over all vision. He is the Subtle, the Expert."
  * **Gloss:** The epistemic ceiling: God is seen-by, never sees-caught; the closing names make the asymmetry gentle rather than cold.
* **`[6:104]`** "“Insights have come to you from your Lord. Whoever sees, it is to the benefit of his soul; and whoever remains blind, it is to its detriment. I am not a guardian over you.”"
  * **Gloss:** The Book itself is the "insight" (baṣā'ir, pl. of basīra — Source: classical lexicon — verify); sight is self-profit; the Prophet disclaims custodial power over belief.
* **`[6:105]`** "We thus diversify the revelations, lest they say, “You have studied,” and to clarify them for people who know."
  * **Gloss:** Repetition and variety in the signs are preemptive answers to the charge that the Prophet recycles learned material — the accusation of `[6:25]`'s "myths of the ancients."
* **`[6:106]`** "Follow what was revealed to you from your Lord. There is no god but He. And turn away from the polytheists."
  * **Gloss:** Three commands in one breath: follow, confess, disengage — guidance as a walkable line, not a debate to be won.
* **`[6:107]`** "Had Allah willed, they would not have practiced idolatry. We did not appoint you as a guardian over them, and you are not a manager over them."
  * **Gloss:** Sovereignty defuses mission-anxiety: the unbelievers' will is not the Prophet's assignment; "manager" (wakīl) reinforces the 6:104 disclaimer twice.
* **`[6:108]`** "Do not insult those they call upon besides Allah, lest they insult Allah out of hostility and ignorance. We made attractive to every community their deeds. Then to their Lord is their return, and He will inform them of what they used to do."
  * **Gloss:** An ethics of polemic: provoking the insult-back is a net loss for truth; judgment is deferred to the return.
* **`[6:109]`** "They swear by Allah, with their most solemn oaths, that if a miracle were to come to them, they would believe in it. Say, “The miracles are only with Allah.” But how do you know? Even if it did come, they still would not believe."
  * **Gloss:** Oaths promising faith-on-sign are met with the counter-claim that miracles are not the negotiable currency here.
* **`[6:110]`** "And We turn away their hearts and their visions, as they refused to believe in it the first time, and We leave them blundering in their rebellion."
  * **Gloss:** The seal-theology of `[6:25]` and `[6:46]` restated as active divine verbs: hearts and eyes turned *because* of a prior refusal — hardening is consequence, not caprice (Source: consensus reading of the "as they refused" clause — verify).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **"There is no god except/but He":**
  * **Rendering:** twice inside this ruku, `[6:102]` ("except He") and `[6:106]` ("but He") — micro-drift in one passage (observable).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:163]` "Your God is one God. There is no god but He…" — the formula's fullest early placement (rg-verified across the corpus).
* **"the Subtle, the Expert" (laṭīf, khabīr):**
  * **Rendering:** "the Subtle, the Expert" at `[6:103]`; "the Subtle" is a corpus hapax in this translation (rg-verified: one hit), while "the Expert" pairs with "the Supreme… the Wise, the Expert" at `[6:18]` within this surah.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[67:14]` and `[34:1]` carry the Expert-name (rg list); the pairing at 6:103 caps the vision-verse: what cannot be seen is what cannot be finessed.
* **"guardian / manager" (ḥafīẓ, wakīl):**
  * **Rendering:** "I am not a guardian over you" `[6:104]`; "We did not appoint you as a guardian… you are not a manager" `[6:107]`; cf. God's "guardians" watching over you at `[6:61]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[10:108]` "I am not a guardian over you" and `[42:48]` "We did not send you as a guardian over them" — the identical Prophetic disclaimer in three locations (rg-verified).
* **"insult" (sabb):**
  * **Rendering:** "Do not insult those they call upon besides Allah" `[6:108]` — the corpus hapax for the verb in this sense (49:11 uses "revile"/mockery for believers' speech; observable).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[49:11]` — the same etiquette internalized for the community.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Meccan (classical consensus).
* **Asbab / narrative context:** 6:108 is classically tied to exchanges of abuse — the Muslims' cursing of the pagans' gods provoking curses on Allah in return (Source: Tabari and Ibn Kathir on 6:108, reporting the occasion — verify); 6:110's turning is the same seal-hadith theology the guidance note tracks from `[2:7]`.
