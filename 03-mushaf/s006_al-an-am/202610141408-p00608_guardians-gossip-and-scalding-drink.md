# Guardians, darknesses, and the scalding drink (6:61–70)
**UID:** 202610141408
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 6:61–70 (Ruku 8 of 20 in al-An’am)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[6:61]` – `[6:70]` (10 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Allah’s sovereignty runs through staff — guardians, death-envoys who never fail, the Swiftest of reckoners — yet humans recall Him only in the darknesses of land and sea, and associate after deliverance (6:63–64); the warning then names four ends (affliction above, underfoot, faction, mutual violence), and the community receives its etiquette: turn away from the gossipers of the revelations, remember that the righteous carry no accountability for them, and leave the play-takers — for no soul is pledged beyond what it earned, no equivalent accepted, no drink but scalding water.
* **Placement:** Ruku 8; the conduct-ruku built on ruku 7’s all-knowing Lord. Its two halves — death-management (61–65) and social boundary (66–70) — are welded by the accountability formula already given at 6:52 and the “play” verdict already given at 6:32.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[6:61]`** "He is the Conqueror over His servants, and He sends guardians over you, until, when death overtakes one of you, Our envoys take him away, and they never fail."
  * **Gloss:** The 6:18 title in a new costume (“Conqueror” for “Supreme”); heavenly bureaucracy with a no-slack clause.
* **`[6:62]`** "Then they are brought back to Allah, their True Master. Unquestionably, His is the judgment, and He is the Swiftest of reckoners."
  * **Gloss:** Return, ownership, judgment, speed — four clauses, one verdict: the reckoning outruns every excuse.
* **`[6:63]`** "Say, “Who delivers you from the darkness of land and sea?” You call upon Him humbly and inwardly: “If He delivers us from this, We will surely be among the thankful.”"
  * **Gloss:** The 6:59 inventory becomes an experience: in the dark of land and sea everyone is monotheist by instinct.
* **`[6:64]`** "Say, “It is Allah who delivers you from it, and from every disaster. Yet then you associate others with Him.”"
  * **Gloss:** The punchline of the distress-cycle (cf. 6:41, 39:8): deliverance general, gratitude defaulting into shirk.
* **`[6:65]`** — two clause-chunks:
  * "Say, “He is Able to send upon you an affliction, from above you, or from under your feet."
  * "Or He can divide you into factions, and make you taste the violence of one another. Note how We explain the revelations, so that they may understand.”"
  * **Gloss:** Sky-strike, earth-swallow, or sectarian civil war — the third option is stated as a warning, not a mystery; the explanation-refrain lands mid-threat.
* **`[6:66]`** "But your people rejected it, though it is the truth. Say, “I am not responsible for you.”"
  * **Gloss:** The disavowal of responsibility (wakāla) — the prophet’s hands off, truth still on the table.
* **`[6:67]`** "For every happening is a finality, and you will surely know."
  * **Gloss:** Every report (nabaʾ) has its settlement-date — 6:5’s “soon will reach them the news of what they used to ridicule” rewritten as a law.
* **`[6:68]`** "When you encounter those who gossip about Our revelations, turn away from them, until they engage in another topic. But should Satan make you forget, do not sit after the recollection with the wicked people."
  * **Gloss:** The sitting-rule: disengage from the idle talk about the verses, and if forgetfulness happens, re-leave when remembered.
* **`[6:69]`** "The righteous are in no way accountable for them; it is only a reminder, that they may be careful."
  * **Gloss:** The mirror clause of 6:52’s mutual non-accountability: the muttaqūn bear no debt for the mockers — the reminder is for them.
* **`[6:70]`** — three clause-chunks:
  * "So leave alone those who take their religion for play and pastime, and whom the worldly life has deceived. But remind with it, lest a soul becomes damned on account of what it has earned."
  * "It has no helper or intercessor besides Allah. Even if it offers every equivalent, none will be accepted from it. These are the ones who are delivered to perdition by their actions."
  * "They will have a drink of scalding water, and a painful punishment, because they used to disbelieve."
  * **Gloss:** Leave them, but still remind: the soul is pledged alone, ransom void, drink scalding — the ruku’s staff-and-deliverance imagery ends at its opposite pole.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`guardians`** (muʿaqqibāt):
  * **Rendering:** “He sends guardians over you” `[6:61]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[13:11]` “He has a succession; before him and behind him, protecting him by Allah’s command” — the same post, named “succession” there (verified; identity of term per classical reading — *Source: agent inference — verify*).
* **`Swiftest of reckoners`** (asraʿ al-ḥāsibīn):
  * **Rendering:** `[6:62]` — the superlative occurs only here in the corpus (verified via `rg "reckon"`).
  * **Elsewhere:** the positive is common: `[2:202]`, `[5:4]`, `[24:39]` “Allah is swift in reckoning” — here it is escalated to the superlative on the Day-frame.
* **`helper or intercessor`** (nāṣir/shafīʿ):
  * **Rendering:** `[6:70]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[6:51]` “no protector or intercessor apart from him” — the ruku-6 twin; `[40:18]` “no intimate friend, and no intercessor to be obeyed”; `[26:100]` the pit’s confession “Now we have no intercessors” (verified).
* **`scalding water`** (ḥamīm):
  * **Rendering:** “a drink of scalding water” `[6:70]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[22:19]` “scalding water will be poured over their heads”; the same Arabic family rendered “boiling water”: `[47:15]` “that cuts-up their bowels”, `[56:42]` “Amid searing wind and boiling water”, `[78:25]` “Except boiling water, and freezing hail” — two English renderings for one torment-word, verified.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Meccan (classical consensus; order 55) — yet 6:65’s “divide you into factions” is the surah’s most Medinan-sounding line, classically read as foretelling the umma’s sectarian future (*Source: classical — verify*).
* **Asbab / narrative context:** none in the corpus; 6:68’s sitting-rule is classically taken as general toward mockery of the verses (*Source: consensus of classical tafsir — verify*).
