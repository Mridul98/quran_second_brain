## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Meccan — agreed in the classical type-lists; the sworn-cosmos → creation → Day → counter-plot register fits the early Meccan pressure period. *Source: classical type-lists — verify.* Revelation order: **transmitted nuzul tables — verify** (corpus states no nuzul; unnumbered Basmala heads the surah).
* **Size:** 17 ayahs, 1 ruku (ruku_map global 528 — verified). First door of the six-surah night-oath run 86–91: at-Tariq, al-A'la, al-Ghashiyah, al-Fajr, al-Balad, ash-Shams — short sworn surahs each built on cosmos-oaths, a creation/destruction argument, and a paired verdict close.
* **Title:** at-Tariq — the name is transliterated, not translated, at `[86:1]`–`[86:2]`, then glossed as "The Piercing Star" at `[86:3]`; the header "THE NIGHTLY VISITOR" gives the Arabic's night-comer sense (rg-verified: the *piercing* word-family also yields "a piercing projectile" `[37:10]` and "piercing fire" `[15:27]`).
* **Central theme:** Watchfulness and exposure. Every soul has a Protector over it (86:4); man is recalled to his humble origin (86:5–7) so that the Returner is recalled (86:8); on the Day the secrets are disclosed he has "no strength, and no supporter" (86:9–10). The second oath — the returning sky, the cracking earth (86:11–12) — certifies "It is a Decisive Word. It is no joke" (86:13–14); the enemies' counter-plot is met by divine counter-plot and one brief, deliberate respite (86:15–17).
* **Reading hint:** follow the hidden/exposed axis — the night-comer arrives unseen, the soul is guarded unseen, the fluid origin is unseen, and everything ends in disclosure: "On the Day when the secrets are disclosed" (86:9). The surah is one long answer to the question of what escapes surveillance.

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Oath I (86:1–3):** "By the sky and at-Tariq" — the riddling name, then its answer, "The Piercing Star": cosmos-entry + night-striker.
* **The guarded soul (86:4):** "There is no soul without a Protector over it" — the surah's hinge: nothing is unwatched.
* **Creation argument (86:5–7):** "Let man consider what he was created from" — gushing liquid, issuing "from between the backbone and the breastbones."
* **Return and disclosure (86:8–10):** He "is certainly able to return him"; the Day of exposed secrets strips strength and supporter alike.
* **Oath II and the verdict on the Book (86:11–14):** the sky "that returns" and the earth "that cracks open" underwrite the claim that "It is a Decisive Word. It is no joke."
* **Plot and respite (86:15–17):** "They plot and scheme. But I plot and scheme" — and the only command left to the messenger is a brief stay of execution. (*Source: structural observation on corpus renderings.*)
* **Ring to note:** oath I opens with the sky (86:1), oath II resumes with the sky (86:11) — the pair closes over the soul-and-argument core like brackets. (*Source: structural observation on corpus renderings.*)

## 5b. Batch-Return Verification Flags (this index)
* **Hint renderings corrected:** "night-comer" → the corpus **transliterates** the name ("at-Tariq," 86:1–2) and glosses it "The Piercing Star" (86:3); header "THE NIGHTLY VISITOR". "gushing fluid" → "gushing **liquid**" (86:6). "ribs" → "**breastbones**" (86:7; the "breastbones" token is a two-site corpus pair — 86:7 and [75:26] "when it has reached the breastbones," rg-verified — and *ṣulb*/"backbone" stands alone at 86:7, rg-verified). "heaven and earth separating as a decisive word" → the oath is "By the sky **that returns**. / And the earth that **cracks open**" (86:11–12); the Decisive-Word predication (86:13–14) is a separate clause whose subject "It" is left open in the corpus (classically the Quran — *Source: transmitted gloss — verify*). "the plotting and Your respite" → "They plot and scheme. / But I plot and scheme. / Therefore, give the blasphemers respite, a brief respite" (86:15–17) — the respite is commanded, not petitioned.
* **"nearness/pressure theme" located honestly:** the corpus never glosses *tariq* as "pressing"; the stated nearness is 86:4's Protector "over" every soul (kin: [13:11] "protecting him by Allah's command"; [50:16] "We are nearer to him than his jugular vein," rg-verified) and 86:9's exposure. The night-pressing sense is lexical tradition, not this translation's surface.
* **"the plotting" is a matched pair:** "plot and scheme" is corpus-rare — 86:15–16 and [12:5] (Ya'qub on the brothers), plus Allah's "My plan is firm" at [68:45] (rg-verified).
* **Revelation order:** no numeric order claimed — the brain's chains confirm none; transmitted nuzul tables — verify.
