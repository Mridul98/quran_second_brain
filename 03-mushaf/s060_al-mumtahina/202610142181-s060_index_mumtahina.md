## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Medinan — classical consensus for the whole surah; its material is treaty material (the examined women, the compensation clauses) tied by transmitted reports to Hudaybiyyah and its aftermath. Revelation order **91** in the transmitted enumeration this brain's indices use (same list that seats al-Anfal 88, Aal-Imran 89, an-Nisa 92, al-Ma'idah 112). *Source: classical revelation-order lists — the task flag "order 104?" does NOT match this list (104 belongs to al-Munafiqun) — verify against a printed 'ulum al-Quran table before teaching the number.* The corpus carries no nuzul statement; the unnumbered Basmala heads the surah.
* **Size:** 13 ayahs, 2 ruku. Named for the women "tested" at `[60:10]` — the surah's title is a procedure, not a character.
* **Central theme:** The boundaries of loyalty in a community under treaty. Ruku 1 forbids secretly befriending "My enemies and your enemies" and grounds the ethic in Abraham's disavowal (`60:1–6`); ruku 2 softens and legislates — enmity is not final (`60:7`), hostility (not kinship or faith-claim) is the legal criterion (`60:8–9`), and the treaty's hardest clause (examined emigrant women, compensation, the women's pledge) is worked out verse by verse (`60:10–12`), closing on the loyalty warning restated (`60:13`).
* **Title etymology:** al-Mumtahina = "she who is examined/tested" — the passive participle of the test-clause `[60:10]` ("test them"); the surah is named after a procedure applied to its most exposed litigants. *Source: observable from the corpus line and surah header.*
* **Reading-order tip:** read 60:1–6 and 60:7–9 as one argument about *initiative*: humans may not smuggle affection across the battle-line (60:1); Allah may plant affection across it when He wills (60:7). The book's moral geography is conduct, not blood (60:3) — hence 60:8's permission and 60:9's prohibition share one criterion (fight/expel/aid expulsion).

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Audit (60:1–3):** a secret love is exposed ("I know what you conceal and what you reveal"); relatives and children will not save at the Resurrection — loyalty is detribalized.
* **Exemplar (60:4–6):** Abraham's "We are quit of you" becomes the template; his one exception (prayer for his father) is quarantined as a prior promise, later reversed at `[9:114]`.
* **Hope and limit (60:7–9):** perhaps Allah will *plant* the affection humans were forbidden to smuggle; meanwhile, the non-hostile get kindness and equity (`60:8` — "Allah loves the equitable"), the hostile get no intimacy (`60:9` — "these are the wrongdoers").
* **Treaty casuistry (60:10–12):** test the emigrating women; no return of wives; compensate what was spent, in both directions (60:10–11); accept the women's pledge on the six articles of `60:12`, whose first article — "will not associate anything with Allah" — is tawhid stated as the community's entry condition. The bay'ah grammar of 60:12 matches the men's pledge scene at `[48:10]` (verified).
* **Bookend (60:13):** the opening prohibition returns with a theological definition of the un-befriendable: people with whom "Allah has become angry" — and the despair doublet ("as the faithless have despaired of the occupants of the graves") makes the disloyal invisible twice: in the Hereafter they despair, like deniers of resurrection itself.
* **Loyalty register (observable counts):** the *wali*-family prohibition vocabulary runs at `[60:1]` ("supporters"), `[60:9]` ("befriending"/"friends"), `[60:13]` ("befriend") — three statements of one rule at the surah's head, middle, and tail; *Source: rg-verifiable within corpus/s060.*
* **Pair-watch:** 60:8–9 is a matched diptych (permission/prohibition on identical criteria); 60:1↔60:13 and 60:4↔60:6 (twice "excellent example") are the surah's bracket-structures.
* **Verdict ladder (observable in renderings):** 60:1 "has strayed from the right way" → 60:9 "these are the wrongdoers" → 60:13 names instead the object of the ban ("people with whom Allah has become angry") — the censure moves from the act, to the actor, to the company kept.
