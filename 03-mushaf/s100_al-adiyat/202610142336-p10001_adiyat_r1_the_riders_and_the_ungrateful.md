# Dawn-raiders swear the verdict: the ungrateful witness, the opened graves, the mined hearts (100:1–11)
**UID:** 202610142336
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 100:1–11 (Ruku 1 of 1 in al-’Adiyat)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[100:1]` – `[100:11]` (11 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Five asyndetic oaths by a dawn cavalry raid — the peak of human ferocity and motion — are spent on one quiet indictment: the human being is "ungrateful to his Lord," knows it (he "bears witness to that"), and is only as fierce about wealth as about the raid; therefore "Does he not know?" that two excavations are scheduled — graves scattered open, hearts' contents obtained — after which the only predicate that matters is that "their Lord… is fully informed of them."
* **Placement:** Ruku 1 of 1. Between 99's speaking earth and 101's named Fire, this surah interiorizes the audit: the outer Day is stated (100:9), the inner one is stated (100:10), the Judge is not described except as KNOWING (100:11).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[100:1]`–`[100:5]`** "By the racers panting. / Igniting sparks. / Raiding at dawn. / Raising clouds of dust. / Storming into the midst."
  * **Gloss:** The oath-chain runs as one breath in five frames: galloping (panting as the sound-track), striking fire (hoof or friction — "Igniting sparks"), the hour chosen ("Raiding at dawn"), the blind storm raised ("clouds of dust"), and the penetration ("Storming into the midst") — the target of the raid is never named; the riders are the whole image.
* **`[100:6]`** "Indeed, the human being is ungrateful to his Lord."
  * **Gloss:** The sworn conclusion (lām-taḥqīq "Indeed"): not "the disbeliever" but THE HUMAN — a present-tense anthropology; *kafūr* rendered "ungrateful," the favor-covering kufr of the shukr circuit (see §3).
* **`[100:7]`** "And he bears witness to that."
  * **Gloss:** Grammatically open: either the human testifies against himself (his conduct is the confession) or a witness affirms it — the English preserves the ambiguity; classical readers split here (Source: two transmitted readings on 100:7 — verify before choosing).
* **`[100:8]`** "And he is fierce in his love of wealth."
  * **Gloss:** The third indictment and the proof-line: the same creature is "fierce" (ḥirṣ) about wealth — the raid-oaths become a metaphor for greed's real object; his intensity matches the chargers'.
* **`[100:9]`** "Does he not know? When the contents of the graves are scattered around."
  * **Gloss:** The knowledge-probe ("Does he not know?" — exact-form twin `[96:14]` "Does he not know that Allah sees?" — rg-verified two-site pair) plus the first When: the buried scattered UP — the earth's "loads" of 99:2 in raid-lexicon ("scattered around" echoes the raiders' dust).
* **`[100:10]`** "And the contents of the hearts are obtained."
  * **Gloss:** The second When, the inward twin: what lies buried in chests (*ṣudūr*, rendered "hearts") is mined ("obtained") — the real raid is God's, and His target was never the grave but the heart.
* **`[100:11]`** "Their Lord, on that Day, is fully informed of them."
  * **Gloss:** The close with no threat verb: divine khubr ("fully informed") — omniscience stated as the sufficient eschatology (Source: structural observation — restraint-as-rhetoric; classical readers note the surah ends with knowledge where others end with fire — verify).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`al-’Ādiyāt` ("the racers")**: the oathed chargers.
  * **Rendering:** "By the racers panting" — five consecutive participal images, all corpus-unique in Itani's wording (rg-verified).
  * **Elsewhere:** the dawn-motif elsewhere is mercy/worship, not assault: `[51:18]` "at dawn, they would pray for pardon"; `[3:17]` "seekers of forgiveness at dawn"; `[54:34]` Lot's family "rescued at dawn"; `[17:78]` "The Quran at dawn is witnessed" (all rg-verified) — 100:3 is the corpus's only violent dawn (Source: observation on rg results).
* **`kafūr` ("ungrateful")**: covering favor.
  * **Rendering:** "ungrateful to his Lord" (100:6).
  * **Elsewhere:** exact-phrase twin `[17:27]` — "the devil is ever ungrateful to his Lord" (rg-verified — the human shares Iblis' register); portrait twin `[14:34]` "The human being is unfair and ungrateful"; the audit-fork `[27:40]` "whether I am grateful or ungrateful"; the definition-site `[16:83]` "They recognize Allah’s blessing, but then deny it." K-shukr note: cites all the twins above EXCEPT [100:6] (rg-verified) — this passage carries 100:6 as the note's strongest anthropology-claim until appended.
* **`"he bears witness"`**: self-testimony.
  * **Elsewhere:** the divine/creature witness cluster is large (`[3:18]`, `[4:166]`, `[63:1]` — rg-verified); 100:7 is the only site where the human "bears witness TO THAT" — his own ingratitude as acknowledged fact (Source: agent observation — verify).
* **`ḥirṣ` ("fierce in his love of wealth")**: greed as temperament.
  * **Elsewhere (rg-verified):** `[89:20]` "And you love wealth with immense love" — the Medinan-cousin line; `[3:180]` "They will be encircled by their hoardings on the Day of Resurrection" — the verdict-form of the same vice (k-infraq's economy).
* **`khubr` ("fully informed")**: 100:11's closing predicate.
  * **Rendering:** "is fully informed of them" — phrase corpus-unique (rg-verified).
  * **Elsewhere (concept-family):** `[2:234]` "Allah is fully acquainted with what you do"; `[86:9]` "On the Day when the secrets are disclosed" — the exact eschatological twin of 100:10 (rg-verified); "Knower of secrets and declarations" sites: `[6:73]`, `[39:46]`, `[59:22]`.
