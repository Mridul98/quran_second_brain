# Surah 100: THE RACERS (al-’Adiyat)
**UID:** 202610142335
**Type:** Index / Surah Map
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Meccan in the majority of transmitted lists; a transmitted minority reads it Medinan. The war-steed oath-register and the raw anthropology of 100:6–8 favor Meccan; nothing internal decides it. Labeled as found. *Source: transmitted type-lists — verify.* Revelation order: **transmitted nuzul tables — verify** (corpus asserts no nuzul; unnumbered Basmala heads the surah).
* **Size:** 11 ayahs, 1 ruku (ruku_map global 542 — verified). Zero "Allah" in the numbered verses; exactly two relational Lord-mentions — "his Lord" (100:6) and "Their Lord" (100:11) — which bracket the whole human indictment inside a personal address (rg-verified counts).
* **Title:** al-’Adiyat — corpus header "THE RACERS" tracks 100:1 "By the racers panting": the oaths' galloping raiders give the surah its name; the five-oath chain (100:1–5) is lexically corpus-unique (rg-verified: "racers", "panting", "Raiding at dawn", "clouds of dust", "Storming into the midst" have no other sites in Itani).
* **Central theme:** The most violent motion humanity can picture — dawn raid, sparks, dust, the breach into the ranks — is sworn to prove not war but WORRY: "Indeed, the human being is ungrateful to his Lord" (100:6), a verdict the human himself witnesses (100:7), rooted in "fierce… love of wealth" (100:8). "Does he not know?" answers: the graves will be emptied, the hearts mined — and "Their Lord, on that Day, is fully informed of them" (100:9–11).
* **Reading hint:** the surah's motion decelerates from raid-speed (1–5) to stillness under interrogation (9–11): what no dawn-raider can reach is "the contents of the hearts" — only divine informing (khubr) gets there.

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Five oaths (100:1–5):** racers → sparks → dawn raid → dust → breaching the midst: one cinematic beat per phrase, no conjunctions (Source: observable asyndeton in the corpus lines).
* **Three indictments (100:6–8):** ingratitude toward "his Lord"; self-witness ("And he bears witness to that"); "fierce in his love of wealth" — the raider-oaths frame a creature worse than idle: ungrateful.
* **The counter-question and two "When"s (100:9–10):** "Does he not know?" then the twin excavations — graves scattered around, hearts' contents obtained — the outer and inner burdens of 99:2's single earth-moment (batch link).
* **The closing knowledge (100:11):** "Their Lord, on that Day, is fully informed of them" — the surah's only future-of-God clause: no punishment-line at all, just informing (Source: structural observation — the restraint is the rhetoric).

* **Hint renderings corrected:** "snorting chargers" → "**By the racers panting**" (100:1); "what is in graves is brought out" → "the contents of the graves are **scattered around**" (100:9); "what is in breasts is seized" → "the contents of the **hearts** are **obtained**" (100:10 — sudūr rendered "hearts"; the English "breasts" appears in this corpus only at `[24:31]`, a different word — rg-verified); "man intensely ungrateful" → "Indeed, the human being is ungrateful to his Lord" (100:6 — "intensely" belongs to the 89:20 twin's phrase, see passage).
* **"three when clauses" — corrected:** the corpus has TWO explicit "When" clauses (100:9, 100:10) plus the "on that Day" close (100:11); the hint counted the Day-clause as a third.
* **VERIFIED as hinted:** "fully informed of them" (100:11 — rendering of khubr, corpus-unique phrase); "witness to that" (100:7 — "And he bears witness to that"); love-of-wealth core (100:8, twin `[89:20]` "you love wealth with immense love").
