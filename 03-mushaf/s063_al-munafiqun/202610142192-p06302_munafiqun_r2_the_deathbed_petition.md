# The deadline: possessions, children, and the petition that will not be granted (63:9–11)
**UID:** 202610142192
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 63:9–11 (Ruku 2 of 2 in al-Munafiqun)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[63:9]` – `[63:11]` (3 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah's final turn: the audience is no longer "the hypocrites" but "O you who believe" — the same distraction that 63:7 weaponized against the community is now indicted inside the community's own households (wealth and children vs remembrance, 63:9), the remedy is spending now, before the deathbed's "delay me for a short while" is revealed as a request that will never be granted (63:10–11), and the surah closes on Allah "Informed of what you do."
* **Placement:** Closing ruku; it converts ruku 1's dossier into self-examination, and joins the as-Saff/al-Jumu'ah complex — 61:10–11 (the trade), 62:9–11 (the scramble), 63:9–11 (the deadline) are one three-surah sermon on attention and money.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[63:9]`** "O you who believe! Let neither your possessions nor your children distract you from the remembrance of Allah. Whoever does that— these are the losers."
  * **Gloss:** The two classical distractors named together (cf. `[9:69]` "more wealth and children... It is they who are the losers" — verified twin); the verdict-word is the surah's own economics: loss (khusran), answering 63:7's attempted defunding.
* **`[63:10]`** "And give from what We have provided for you, before death approaches one of you, and he says, “My Lord, if only You would delay me for a short while, so that I may be charitable, and be one of the righteous.”"
  * **Gloss:** The deathbed petition: reprieve measured in "a short while," and its stated purpose — charity and righteousness; the verse makes infaq the last words of the dying, i.e., the one use-case that proves the trade of as-Saff (61:11) was real.
* **`[63:11]`** "But Allah will not delay a soul when its time has come. Allah is Informed of what you do."
  * **Gloss:** The refusal clause and the closing surveillance formula: no reprieve at the term, and being-known — the same "aware/informed of what you do" cadence that bracketed al-Mumtahina (60:3) and as-Saff's swerving hearts.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`dhikr Allah` (remembrance):** `[63:9]` — the thing the two distractors pull away from.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:152]` — "So remember Me, and I will remember you. And thank Me" (the k-shukr note's welding of dhikr and gratitude — cited there); `[62:9]`–`[62:10]` — hasten to remembrance, then remember Allah much — the sibling legislation one surah before (verified).
* **`infaqa` (give/spend):** `[63:10]` "give from what We have provided for you".
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:254]` — "Spend from what We have given you, before a Day comes in which there is neither trading, nor friendship" — same "before a Day/_before death_" frame (verified); `[61:11]` — striving "with your possessions" — the trade the k-infraq note maps; 63:10 is cited in that note (backlink verified).
* **`inẓar` (delay/reprieve):** `[63:10]` "delay me for a short while"; `[63:11]` "will not delay a soul".
  * **Elsewhere:** `[15:36]`–`[15:37]` — Iblis's "reprieve me until the Day they are resurrected" and its grant (verified) — the same verb-family granted to the curse's bearer and refused at the human deathbed; `[4:18]` — repentance foreclosed "when death approaches one of them, he says, “Now I repent”" (verified twin of the deathbed clause). The task's guess that 63:10's twin is `[7:176]` is rejected: 7:176 is the dog-parable of the one who clung to the earth, with no deathbed petition (see its example-link at 62:5).
* **`khusran` (loss):** `[63:9]` "these are the losers".
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:27]` (covenant-severers "are the losers"), `[9:69]` (wealth-and-children cohort "are the losers"), `[39:63]` — the term is the book's verdict on every failed trade (verified lines; k-nifaq reads 63:9–10 as the hypocrite's own ledger — cited there).
