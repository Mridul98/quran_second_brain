# Victory, Multitudes, and the Close of Forgiveness (110:1–3)
**UID:** 202610142364
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 110:1–3 (Ruku 1 of 1 in an-Nasr)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[110:1]` – `[110:3]` (3 ayahs; global ruku 552 of 556 — verified)
* **Thesis:** The surah is a clock with a commission: WHEN Allah's victory and conquest arrive and the crowds enter the religion — then, and only then, the commanded act is not celebration but praise-plus-istighfar, grounded in one attribute: "He is the Accepter of Repentance." Triumph is met with renewal.
* **Placement:** Ruku 1 of 1; second door of the closing bracket. 109 separated the ways; 110 shows the winning way filling up — and immediately folds success into repentance, pre-empting any triumphalist reading before the unit's verdict-surah (111) even speaks.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[110:1]`** "When there comes Allah’s victory, and conquest."
  * **Gloss:** A bare temporal clause — no main verb yet; the surah is built as "when X… then do Y." The paired nouns (*nasr* + *fath*) are rg-verified twins at `[61:13]` ("support from Allah, and imminent victory") and `[48:18]` ("an imminent conquest"); the phrase "Allah’s victory" itself recurs at `[2:214]` ("When is Allah’s victory? Indeed, Allah’s victory is near") — 110:1 reads as that question's answer (*Source: observable corpus pairs; eschatological "nearness" link = agent inference — verify*).
* **`[110:2]`** "And you see the people entering Allah’s religion in multitudes."
  * **Gloss:** The spectacle: witnessing ("you see") mass conversion — "in multitudes" is adverbial and unique here (rg-verified; noun-kin at `[6:128]`, `[7:179]` — "multitudes of jinn and humans"). *Din* is rendered "religion" — the SAME word 109:6 softened to "way": two verses, two registers of the partition (observable drift).
* **`[110:3]`** "Then celebrate the praise of your Lord, and seek His forgiveness. He is the Accepter of Repentance."
  * **Gloss:** The "then" resolves the clock: two imperatives — fused praise-glorification (sabbih bi-hamdi: rg-verified near-twin `[20:130]` "celebrate the praises of your Lord before the rising of the sun…") and istighfar — then a ground-clause naming the attribute that makes the command possible. The Accepter-of-Repentance epithet has exactly two "Accepter of Repentance" sites in the corpus, `[2:54]` and here (third kin-site `[40:3]` "Accepter of repentance" — rg-verified): the other is the calf-breach renewal verse — 110:3 imports the covenant-renewal vocabulary wholesale (*Source: corpus census + agent inference — verify the covenant framing*).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`nasr` (victory/support)**: help-as-triumph.
  * **Rendering:** "victory" (110:1, surah header "VICTORY"); "support" at `[61:13]` — same root, two registers (observable).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:214]`, `[3:123]` (Badr: "Allah had given you victory at Badr, when you were weak"), `[5:52]` "perhaps Allah will bring about victory" (rg-verified cluster) — nasr is promised-long-before-it-comes; 110's "When there comes" closes a waiting-thread.
* **`fath` (conquest/opening)**: the decisive opening.
  * **Rendering:** "conquest" (110:1; only 3 sites render the fath-family thus: `[48:18]` "an imminent conquest", `[57:10]` "before the conquest" — rg-verified).
  * **Elsewhere:** 48 is the treaty-surah ("The Dawn of Victory" register) — 110:1's pair reads as the 48-vocabulary brought to its end-state (*agent inference — verify*).
* **`Tawwab` ("the Accepter of Repentance")**: God's renewing name.
  * **Rendering:** "He is the Accepter of Repentance." — the surah's final words.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:54]` (calf — "So He turned to you in repentance. He is the Accepter of Repentance, the Merciful"), `[40:3]` ("Forgiver of sins, Accepter of repentance, Severe in punishment" — the attribute paired with severity, exactly the 110-farewell mood; rg-verified).
* **`istighfar` cluster (seeking forgiveness)**: the unit's hidden thread.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[41:6]` "be upright towards Him, and seek forgiveness from Him" (a "Say"-surah twin); `[11:61]`, `[27:46]`, `[73:20]`, and the dawn-istighfar pair `[3:17]`/`[51:18]` (all rg-verified) — 110:3 sits mid-cluster, but only here is istighfar the commanded response to *victory*.
