# All perishes but His Presence — the warning litany (55:26–45)
**UID:** 202610142141
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 55:26–45 (Ruku 2 of 3 in ar-Rahman)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[55:26]` – `[55:45]` (20 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The favor-litany turns annihilative: everything on the battered earth perishes and only the Presence of your Lord remains; every creature in the heavens and earth *asks Him* while He is at work each day — and the two weighty audiences, jinn and humans, are shown that the cosmos's borders cannot be crossed without authorization, that the sky will split like rose-paint, that guilt will be read off foreheads, and that the Hell they called lie is now standing: nine refrains drive the descent from metaphysics to the seething bath.
* **Placement:** Ruku 2 of 3 — the surah's hinge. The refrain's verb ("deny") is now spoken *inside* a warning, and `[55:43]` "Hell that the guilty denied" reuses the very verb of the litany: denial-of-favor and denial-of-Hell are one act (observable in the English).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[55:26]`** "Everyone upon it is perishing." — **Gloss:** The earth's entire inventory (55:10–24) just received: annihilation clause — every named favor-holder is finite.
* **`[55:27]`** "But will remain the Presence of your Lord, Full of Majesty and Splendor." — **Gloss:** *Wajh* — rendered "Presence"; the permanence-only-He claim, the tawhid core of the ruku. Verbatim-shape twin: `[28:88]` "All things perish, except His presence." The phrase "Full of Majesty and Splendor" is corpus-unique (rg) and returns only at `[55:78]` — the surah's ring.
* **`[55:28]`** "So which of your Lord’s marvels will you deny?" — **Gloss:** Refrain 7/31 — first strike after the turn: is *perishing* not a marvel as much as provision?
* **`[55:29]`** "Everyone in the heavens and the earth asks Him. Every day He is managing." — **Gloss:** The universe petitioning, God never unoccupied (*sha'n*); the answer-verse across the corpus is `[2:186]` "I Am near; I answer the call of the caller." Asking-and-answering *is* the daily management.
* **`[55:30]`** "So which of your Lord’s marvels will you deny?" — **Gloss:** Refrain 8/31.
* **`[55:31]`** "We will attend to you, O prominent two." — **Gloss:** *Thaqalān* — the two weighty ones, jinn and humans; "prominent two" is corpus-unique in this sense (the other "prominent" sites — `[9:86]`, `[34:18]`, `[10:78]` — render a different word, *mustakbar*, "the prominent/arrogant": collision-warning, see §3). Attention (*fa-sa-nafarughu*) is ominous: the Judge takes their case in hand.
* **`[55:32]`** "So which of your Lord’s marvels will you deny?" — **Gloss:** Refrain 9/31.
* **`[55:33]`** "O society of jinn and humans! If you can pass through the bounds of the heavens and the earth, go ahead and pass. But you will not pass except with authorization." — **Gloss:** The escape-audit: try the cosmos's seams — *sulṭān*, "authorization," the same English word as `[4:91]` "clear authorization." The challenge genre mirrors `[11:13]` and the ten-surahs dare — but here the escape route, not a rival book, is mocked.
* **`[55:34]`** "So which of your Lord’s marvels will you deny?" — **Gloss:** Refrain 10/31.
* **`[55:35]`** "You will be bombarded with flares of fire and brass, and you will not succeed." — **Gloss:** The sky's defense system: *shihāb* (flares) and *nufrāṣ* (molten brass/foam-fire); paired motif at `[67:5]` — lanterns "made them missiles against the devils." Flight is firewalled.
* **`[55:36]`** "So which of your Lord’s marvels will you deny?" — **Gloss:** Refrain 11/31.
* **`[55:37]`** "When the sky splits apart, and becomes rose, like paint." — **Gloss:** The Hour's sky-color: splitting, rose-red, bubbling-oil sheen — the same heavens the auditors tried to breach now collapse in paint-terms (corpus-unique simile, rg "like paint").
* **`[55:38]`** "So which of your Lord’s marvels will you deny?" — **Gloss:** Refrain 12/31.
* **`[55:39]`** "On that Day, no human and no jinn will be asked about his sins." — **Gloss:** The silence of self-evidence — nothing to declare because everything is displayed; cf. the double-audience formula again (human/jinn), and the "marks" next verse as the *reason* questioning is moot. (Read classically as: no *questioning* after the marks, vs `[15:92]` "We will certainly ask them all" — a known reconciliation locus; *Source: classical tafsir disagreement — verify in print.*)
* **`[55:40]`** "So which of your Lord’s marvels will you deny?" — **Gloss:** Refrain 13/31.
* **`[55:41]`** "The guilty will be recognized by their marks; they will be taken by the forelocks and the feet." — **Gloss:** Physiognomy of guilt and the seizure-idiom: *nāsiyah* (forelock) — the same hostage-image as `[96:15]`–`[96:16]` "We will drag him by the forelock. A deceitful, sinful forelock."
* **`[55:42]`** "So which of your Lord’s marvels will you deny?" — **Gloss:** Refrain 14/31.
* **`[55:43]`** "This is Hell that the guilty denied." — **Gloss:** Deixis — *this* is it: the demonstrative answers `[55:31]`'s promise of attention. The verb "denied" echoes the refrain's own verb: the deniers of marvels are the deniers of Hell (one *kidhb*, observable in English).
* **`[55:44]`** "They circulate between it and between a seething bath." — **Gloss:** The round-trip economy of Hell — circuit (*ṭawāf*, the pilgrim-word put to torment) between the Fire and *ḥamīm*, "seething bath"; the seething-water cluster: `[52:6]` "the seething sea," `[44:46]` (s044's ledger).
* **`[55:45]`** "So which of your Lord’s marvels will you deny?" — **Gloss:** Refrain 15/31 — the last strike before the Gardens: six refrains were earned by bounty here; nine are spent on warning.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`wajh → "Presence"`**: 55:27 "the Presence of your Lord."
  * **Rendering:** "Presence" for *face* — the same choice at `[28:88]` ("except His presence"); the task's "face of your Lord" is other translations' literalism.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[3:38]`, `[3:8]` render *ladun* "from Your presence" — one English word, two Arabic sources; flag before quoting "presence" as a unity.
* **`thaqalān → "the prominent two"`**: 55:31.
  * **Rendering:** "O prominent two" — the dual weighty ones (jinn + humankind — *Source: classical lexicon; verify*).
  * **Elsewhere:** mustakbar "the prominent" at `[9:86]`, `[10:78]`, `[34:18]` — unrelated word, same English: a false-trace warning for `rg "prominent"`.
* **`sulṭān → "authorization"`**: 55:33.
  * **Rendering:** "except with authorization" — the passing-power nobody has.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[4:91]` "clear authorization" (same rendering, same root-class); the flares pair with `[67:5]` "missiles against the devils."
* **`kidhb (deny) as one verb`**: refrain ×9 in this ruku; 43 "the guilty denied."
  * **Rendering:** refrain "will you deny," 43 "denied" — litany and verdict share their verb.
  * **Elsewhere:** the shukr note's definition of kufr, `[16:83]` "recognize Allah's blessing, but then deny it" — the circuit this surah performs.
