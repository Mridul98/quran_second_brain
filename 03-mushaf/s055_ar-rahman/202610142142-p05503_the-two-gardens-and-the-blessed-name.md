# The two pairs of Gardens — and the blessed Name (55:46–78)
**UID:** 202610142142
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 55:46–78 (Ruku 3 of 3 in ar-Rahman)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[55:46]` – `[55:78]` (33 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** For the one who feared his Lord's standing, the litany's last movement: two gardens, described in eight bounty-lines each punctuated by the refrain, then a second pair beneath them in deep green — until the economy itself is named ("Is the reward of goodness anything but goodness?") and the surah ends where its grammar began, doubled into doxology: "Blessed be the name of your Lord, Full of Majesty and Splendor."
* **Placement:** Final ruku; the refrain's 16 remaining strikes (16–31 of 31) alternate bounty/interrogation in strict lockstep — the only surah-structure in the corpus built as call-and-response. The task's "55:46–62" verifies as **46–61** (first pair) + **62–77** (second pair), with 78 the seal.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[55:46]`** "But for him who feared the standing of his Lord are two gardens." — **Gloss:** The turn from Hell's circuit: fear-of-the-standing (the Day one will stand before Him) cashes out double; "two gardens" echoes Saba's charter `[34:15]` "two gardens, on the right, and on the left. Eat of your Lord's provision, and give thanks" (the k-shukr note's own site).
* **`[55:47]`** refrain 16/31 — **Gloss:** "So which of your Lord’s marvels will you deny?" — first of the sixteen.
* **`[55:48]`** "Full of varieties." — **Gloss:** *Fanun* — two kinds, every sort: density of delight.
* **`[55:49]`** refrain 17/31.
* **`[55:50]`** "In them are two flowing springs." — **Gloss:** Water within the pair — the garden's secret (cf. the "rivers beneath" idiom throughout; here *ma'* two springs).
* **`[55:51]`** refrain 18/31.
* **`[55:52]`** "In them are fruits of every kind, in pairs." — **Gloss:** Answering `[55:11]`'s earth-fruits: the inventory now served at source.
* **`[55:53]`** refrain 19/31.
* **`[55:54]`** "Reclining on furnishings lined with brocade, and the fruits of the two gardens are near at hand." — **Gloss:** *Furush* lined with *istaqabraq* — brocade: the corpus ledger of paradise furniture (s044's tally): `[37:44]` "On furnishings, facing one another," `[44:53]`, `[56:15]`.
* **`[55:55]`** refrain 20/31.
* **`[55:56]`** "In them are maidens restraining their glances, untouched before by any man or jinn." — **Gloss:** *Qāṣirātu-ṭ-ṭarf* — glances contracted to their mates ("restraining their glances" is corpus-unique here, rg; the kin-site `[37:48]` renders the same idea "bashful women with lovely eyes" — motif twin, not verbatim); the man/jinn pair returns the dual audience one last time.
* **`[55:57]`** refrain 21/31.
* **`[55:58]`** "As though they were rubies and corals." — **Gloss:** *Lu'lū' wa-marjān* — "coral" again (55:22): the sea's jewelry now walks; cf. pearl-similes `[56:23]`, `[37:49]`, `[52:24]`, `[76:19]`.
* **`[55:59]`** refrain 22/31.
* **`[55:60]`** "Is the reward of goodness anything but goodness?" — **Gloss:** THE PIVOT — *iḥsān* answered by *iḥsān*: the surah's whole exchange economy stated as a question with one answer; the refrain's question momentarily inverted (corpus-unique verse, rg).
* **`[55:61]`** refrain 23/31.
* **`[55:62]`** "And beneath them are two gardens." — **Gloss:** The second pair (*min dūnihimā*) — lower in rank or nearer the ground: both read classically (*Source: classical tafsir disagreement — verify in print*).
* **`[55:63]`** refrain 24/31.
* **`[55:64]`** "Deep green." — **Gloss:** *Mudhāmirān* — dark verdant (the word alone as a verse — density again; corpus-unique).
* **`[55:65]`** refrain 25/31.
* **`[55:66]`** "In them are two gushing springs." — **Gloss:** *Biddāhā* — gushing ("gushing" recurs in the corpus only at `[86:6]`, "gushing liquid," a different word — don't harmonize the two).
* **`[55:67]`** refrain 26/31.
* **`[55:68]`** "In them are fruits, and date-palms, and pomegranates." — **Gloss:** The earth-list of `[55:11]` returns item-for-item — palms, fruits — with pomegranates added: the An'am inventory verses carry the same triad, `[6:99]`, `[6:141]` (which ends "give its due on the day of its harvest").
* **`[55:69]`** refrain 27/31.
* **`[55:70]`** "In them are good and beautiful ones." — **Gloss:** *Khayrāt* and *ḥisān* — good and fair to look upon.
* **`[55:71]`** refrain 28/31.
* **`[55:72]`** "Companions, secluded in the tents." — **Gloss:** *Quur* — companions hooped in pavilions ("tent" sites: corpus-unique here, rg).
* **`[55:73]`** refrain 29/31.
* **`[55:74]`** "Whom no human has touched before, nor jinn." — **Gloss:** Mirror of `[55:56]` — the untouched clause restated for the second pair; the audience-duality (man/jinn) is the surah's final signature.
* **`[55:75]`** refrain 30/31.
* **`[55:76]`** "Reclining on green cushions, and exquisite carpets." — **Gloss:** *Abqariyya* — fine carpets: the furniture ledger's last colors (green upon green).
* **`[55:77]`** refrain 31/31 — **Gloss:** The litany's final strike; 6+9+16 = 31, rg-verified.
* **`[55:78]`** "Blessed be the name of your Lord, Full of Majesty and Splendor." — **Gloss:** *Tabāraka* — the same doxology-verb as `[67:1]` "Blessed is He in whose hand is the sovereignty," `[27:8]`, `[23:14]` "Most Blessed is Allah"; here on *the Name* — the surah began with the Name bare (55:1) and ends blessing it; "Full of Majesty and Splendor" closes the ring from 55:27 (the task's "possessor of majesty and honor" is other translations' wording).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`two gardens (jannatān)`**: 46 and 62.
  * **Rendering:** the doubled pair structure is this ruku's skeleton (first pair 46–61, second 62–77 — the task's "46–62?" boundary is one line off).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[34:15]` — the Saba twin: two gardens + the thank-command (k-shukr's site); `[18:32–44]` — the parable of two gardens, whose moral is "goodness is Allah's."
* **`marjān (coral)`**: 22 and 58.
  * **Rendering:** "coral"/"corals" — s055 owns the word in this corpus (rg).
  * **Elsewhere:** none — the earth-sea's wealth becomes the Garden's people; the closest register-partner is the pearl cluster `[56:23]`, `[37:49]`, `[52:24]`, `[76:19]`.
* **`iḥsān → "goodness"`**: 60.
  * **Rendering:** "Is the reward of goodness anything but goodness?" — requital folded onto itself.
  * **Elsewhere:** the reward-for-deeds grammar of `[56:24]` "As a reward for what they used to do" — ar-Rahman's version is stricter: the reward *is* the goodness returned.
* **`Full of Majesty and Splendor (dhul-jalāl wal-ikrām)`**: 27 and 78.
  * **Rendering:** fixed epithet, corpus-unique pair; the task's "honor" vs Itani's "Splendor" — ledger.
  * **Elsewhere:** the Name-cluster "The Compassionate"/kin (s055 header) and the *tabāraka* family `[67:1]`, `[23:14]`, `[27:8]`.
