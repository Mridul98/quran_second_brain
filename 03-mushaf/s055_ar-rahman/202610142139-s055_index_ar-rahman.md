## 3. Structural Arc
* **Teaching-framing (1–4):** the Name, then three taught things — the Quran, the human, *clear expression*; speech-ability itself is a divine lesson before any argument begins (kalam anchor).
* **Order and balance (5–9):** sun/moon on calculation, stars/trees in prostration, sky raised, *mizan* imposed — cosmic law and commercial justice in one breath, "do not transgress in the balance" (the measure-and-scale flank of the infaq ledger).
* **Favors litany, round 1 (10–25):** earth laid for creatures; fruits, palms, grains, fragrant plants — refrain enters at 13 and alternates through creation-tables: two creations (clay/bricks, fire-jinn), two Easts/two Wests, the merged seas and their barrier, pearls, coral, landmark-ships.
* **Annihilation turn (26–36):** everything perishes but the Presence; every creature asks Him, every day He is managing; the "prominent two" are attended to and shown the sky's armed boundary (authorization, flares).
* **Judgment and Hell (37–45):** the rose-paint sky, the unasked Day, the marked guilty, the denied Hell, the seething bath — refrain now punctuates threat, not table-setting.
* **The Gardens double (46–77) and the Name blessed (78):** two gardens for the one who feared, then two beneath them — inventories answered line-by-line by the refrain's last 16 strikes; "Is the reward of goodness anything but goodness?" (60) is the economy's pivot; the doxology closes on Majesty and Splendor.
