# Surah 104: THE BACKBITER (al-Humazah)

**Type:** Index / Surah Map
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Meccan per the classical type-lists; the corpus carries no classification data (header = title/source/basmala only — rg-verified). Revelation order ≈ 30 in the transmitted nuzul tables (list-dependent) — *transmitted tables — verify*.
* **Size:** 9 ayahs, 1 ruku (ruku_map global 546 — verified). Second-shortest-surah territory: 104–108 sit at 9/5/4/7/3 ayahs, the dense final Meccan cluster before the closing bracket (109–114, ruku 551–556).
* **Title:** al-Humazah — Itani's header "THE BACKBITER" tracks `[104:1]` "Woe to every slanderer backbiter."; the twin nouns (humazah, lammaz) are compressed into one two-word English line — the title names only the first (*Source: translator's term choice, observable in corpus*).
* **Central theme:** Two indictments and one demolition. The tongue (scorn and backbiting, v1) and the purse (wealth gathered, counted, and mistaken for immortality, v2–3) are the same posture: a self built on what it takes and what it says about others. "By no means" (v4) flips to the Crusher — God's kindled Fire, which laps to the *hearts* (v7) and closes in on them in extended columns (v8–9). The man who thought his wealth extended his life finds the columns extended over him.
* **Reading hint:** the surah's arithmetic is deliberate — he counts (v2), so he will be enclosed (v8–9); counting is the sin the k-shukr note defines as the audit that cannot be run on blessings (`[16:18]`, `[14:34]`: favors are uncountable *by design*). 104 is its criminal portrait: a man who runs the wrong count.

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Verse 1 — the woe:** an open indictment of a *type*, not a person — "every slanderer backbiter" (the "every" is the text's own generalizer; see §5b on classical person-identifications).
* **Verses 2–3 — the dossier:** second-person singular closes in: "Who gathers wealth and counts it over. / Thinking that his wealth has made him immortal." The participle chain moves from tongue to purse to *thought* — the crime matures into a belief about time.
* **Verse 4 — the refutation:** "By no means." (the clause-killing *kallā* — same family as 83:7 "Not at all. The record of the wicked is in Sijjeen" in the woe-sister surah). Then the sentence: "He will be thrown into the Crusher."
* **Verses 5–7 — the amplification interrogative:** "And what will make you realize what the Crusher is?" — the 556-ruku corpus's signature device (cf. `[101:2–3]`, `[90:12]`), answered with two lines: "Allah's kindled Fire. / That laps to the hearts."
* **Verses 8–9 — the enclosure:** "It closes in on them. / In extended columns." The surah ends as it began the flip: from singular "he" (v4) to plural "them" (v8–9) — the type has a crowd in it (*Source: structural observation on corpus renderings*).

## 5b. Batch-Return Verification Flags (this index)
* **Hint renderings corrected (all rg-verified against `corpus/s104_al-humazah.md`):** "scorner/slanderer" → "**Woe to every slanderer backbiter.**"; "amasses wealth and counts it thinking it makes him immortal" → "**Who gathers wealth and counts it over. / Thinking that his wealth has made him immortal.**"; "God's kindled fire reaching the hearts" → "**Allah's kindled Fire. / That laps to the hearts.**"; "extended over them on stretched columns" → "**It closes in on them. / In extended columns.**" — "stretched" is not Itani's word; "Crusher" (×2, v4–5) IS, so the hint's "the Crusher" verifies.
* **Opponent unnamed in text — CONFIRMED:** no personal name appears in 104 (or anywhere in the corpus) for the indicted type. Classical asbab reports attach the verses to named Quraysh figures (transmitted in the tafsir corpora on 104:1). *Source: classical transmitted reports — verify in primary (Ibn Kathir/Tabari on 104) before quoting any name; the text's "every" generalizes beyond any single cause.*
* **Woe-refrain cluster verified:** `[83:10]` "Woe on that Day to the deniers."; 77 carries the refrain ten times (15/19/24/28/34/37/40/45/47/49 — rg count verified); `[45:7]` "Woe to every sinful liar." is 104:1's structural twin — same "Woe to every + two participles" mold (the only two such sites in the corpus).
* **"By no means" rendering cluster (edition-observation, rg-verified):** 104:4 "By no means" belongs to a five-site "By no means!" family (19:82, 23:100, 70:15, 74:16) while cognate refusal-clauses elsewhere run "Not at all" (83:7, 83:14) / "No indeed" (83:18) — Itani's drift is English-side only; quote 104:4 exactly as "By no means."
* **Type-not-person (text-internal):** 104 never names anyone; 104:1's "every" + the participles make the dossier a portrait, which is why the classical person-reports (see above) can attach without the text endorsing any one of them.
* **"Extended columns" ↔ the architecture cluster:** `[89:7]` "Erum of the pillars." and `[89:10]` "Pharaoh of the Stakes." — same built-tyrant imagery family; whether Itani's "extended columns" (104:9) deliberately echoes 89's "pillars"/"Stakes" is *agent inference — verify* (different Arabic nouns: ʿamūd vs ʿimād/awtād). **Exact-lexicon twin, rg-verified:** `[90:20]` "Upon them is a padlocked Fire." — Itani's "padlocked" and 104:8's "It closes in on them" are the same enclosure-word; 90:20 supplies this edition's gloss of it.
