# Woe to the Wealth-Counter: The Crusher That Laps to the Hearts (104:1–9)
**UID:** 202610142352
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 104:1–9 (Ruku 1 of 1 in al-Humazah)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[104:1]` – `[104:9]` (9 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** A two-instrument indictment — the tongue that scorns, the purse that counts — reaches its verdict in a single image reversal: the man who thought his gathered wealth had made him immortal (v3) is thrown into the Crusher, God's kindled fire that laps to the hearts (v7) and closes in on him in extended columns (v8–9): what he extended over life is now extended over him.
* **Placement:** Ruku 546 of 556; sits between al-Asr's bare "the human being is in loss" (103:2) and al-Fil's civic demolition — the individual dossier bracketed by the species verdict and the army precedent. Its wealth-Counting vocabulary is the negative twin of the cluster 102 ("Abundance distracts you") and 100 ("fierce in his love of wealth") immediately before it.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[104:1]`** "Woe to every slanderer backbiter."
  * **Gloss:** The woe-word (waylun) opens on a doubled active participle: one who habitually scorns, one who habitually gashes behind backs — Itani packs humazah and lammaz into "slanderer backbiter," a binomial of the mouth. "Every" makes the line a type-warrant, not a named charge.
* **`[104:2]`** "Who gathers wealth and counts it over."
  * **Gloss:** The relative clause follows the same type: hoarding (ya jmaʿu) plus re-counting (ya'dudduhu) — wealth's two verbs are acquisition and audit. The second verb is the tell: he knows the number.
* **`[104:3]`** "Thinking that his wealth has made him immortal."
  * **Gloss:** The count becomes a cosmology — "thinking" (yahsabu) names the operative sin: an arithmetic that concluded permanence. Immortality (khuld) is the exact asset the book elsewhere says no human holds (`[21:34]`).
* **`[104:4]`** "By no means. He will be thrown into the Crusher."
  * **Gloss:** The clause-killer "By no means" (kallā) cancels the thought of v3 outright, then the punishment is named, not described: the Crusher (al-Ḥuṭamah) — a crushing-noun that will be interrogated before it is explained.
* **`[104:5]`** "And what will make you realize what the Crusher is?"
  * **Gloss:** The amplification-interrogative — the register-shift device that says *what you are imagining is smaller than what is* (cf. `[101:2–3]`, `[83:8]`).
* **`[104:6]`** "Allah's kindled Fire."
  * **Gloss:** The answer is an ascription and an adjective: it is Allah's (the genitive makes the Crusher owned, not wild), and kindled — a fire deliberately lit (`[56:71]` "the fire you kindle" is the human verb; here the fire has a divine Kindler).
* **`[104:7]`** "That laps to the hearts."
  * **Gloss:** The fire's route bypasses the skin: it *laps* straight to the seats of what was wrong — the heart is where the "thinking" of v3 lived; the punishment is organ-specific.
* **`[104:8]`** "It closes in on them."
  * **Gloss:** Enclosure: locked, windowless containment — the opposite of the open ledger he kept; the plural switch ("them") convicts the crowd the "every" (v1) gathered. The corpus's own lexicon for this word is 90:20's "padlocked" (same image, there applied to the Fire itself — rg-verified).
* **`[104:9]`** "In extended columns."
  * **Gloss:** The architecture of the cell: stretched pillars/columns — the tyrant's building-imagery (89's "Erum of the pillars," "Pharaoh of the Stakes") inverted into scaffolding for the imprisoned. A hoarder gets a structure he cannot count out of.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`humazah / lammaz`** (slanderer / backbiter): habitual-scorn and habitual-gossip nouns.
  * **Rendering:** "slanderer backbiter" — one line, both nouns (104:1). The lammaz root surfaces once elsewhere: `[49:12]` "do not spy on one another, nor backbite one another" — the only other backbiting site in the corpus (rg-verified), with the corpse-eating simile attached.
  * **Elsewhere:** the "Woe to every X" mold has exactly two sites: `[104:1]`, `[45:7]` "Woe to every sinful liar." (rg-verified) — 45:7 proves the type-warrant, not the individual case, is the genre.
* **`ya'dudduhu` (counts it over)**: iterative counting/enumera­tion of wealth.
  * **Rendering:** "gathers wealth and counts it over" (104:2).
  * **Elsewhere:** the counting-audit cluster, all rg-verified: `[102:1]` "Abundance distracts you. / Until you visit the graveyards." (the plural of the same error), `[100:8]` "he is fierce in his love of wealth", `[111:2]` "His wealth did not avail him, nor did what he acquired.", `[9:34]` "Those who hoard gold and silver, and do not spend them in Allah's cause" — while k-shukr's uncountability verses (`[16:18]`, `[14:34]`) state that God's favors cannot be enumerated: 104's man enumerates the wrong account (Source: agent inference on the cluster — verify against tafsir of 104:2).
* **`al-Ḥuṭamah` (the Crusher)**: the fire's nickname — a crushing noun.
  * **Rendering:** "the Crusher," ×2 (104:4–5), left untranslated-by-definition until v6 makes it "Allah's kindled Fire."
  * **Elsewhere:** unique in the corpus (rg-verified); its name + interrogation + answer format is the `[101:1–3]` "The Shocker. / What is the Shocker? / What will explain to you what the Shocker is?" family — 104:5 is the shorter cousin.
* **`khuld` (immortality)**: what v3's arithmetic wrongly purchased.
  * **Rendering:** "made him immortal" (104:3).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[21:34]` "We did not grant immortality to any human being before you. Should you die, are they then the immortal?" — the doctrine 104:3 violates, stated as fact; `[7:20]` Satan's pitch "lest you become angels, or become immortals" — the same delusion at the first temptation (both rg-verified).
