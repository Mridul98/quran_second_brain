* **Central theme:** The certainty-and-provision surah: four oath-creatures certify "What you are promised is true, Judgment will take place" (1–6); a second oath-cycle (the woven sky) indicts divided speech and the "imposters... dazed in ignorance" (7–11); the pious portrait (sleepless nights, dawn-pardon, a wealth-share for the beggar) shows what the promise buys (15–19); "in the heaven is your livelihood" swears provision by the Lord of heaven and earth (22–23); then the guest-scene of Abraham (annunciation) and the demolition reel (Lot, Moses/Pharaoh, Aad, Thamood, Noah) argue that the threat is historical fact (24–46); the universe built, spread, paired, expanded (47–49); "So flee towards Allah" (50); and the purpose-clause: humans and jinn created to worship, God fed by nothing — "Allah is the Provider, the One with Power, the Strong" (56–58); the close: woe on that promised Day (60).

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Oath-certification (1–6):** four anonymous oaths → "What you are promised is true. Judgment will take place" — the oath's point is the promise's certainty.
* **Speech-audit (7–14):** the woven sky swears; "You differ in what you say"; whoever is diverted stays diverted; perish the imposters; the taunt "When is the Day?" answered by presentation to the Fire and "Taste your ordeal. This is what you used to challenge."
* **The pious portrait (15–19):** gardens and springs, sleep-a-little-at-night, dawn-pardon (the `[3:17]` twin), and the wealth-share for beggar and deprived (the `[70:24–25]` twin) — eschatology with a line-item in the budget.
* **Cosmic evidence + provision oath (20–23):** signs in the earth and in yourselves; livelihood in heaven; sworn as true "as the fact that you speak."
* **Narrative evidence (24–46):** Abraham's guests (peace, fatted calf, fear-not, the knowledgeable boy, the mission to the guilty city, the single Muslim household) → Moses and Pharaoh's sea → Aad's wind → Thamood's lightning → Noah's immorality: the promise has receipts.
* **Credo close (47–60):** built universe, spread earth, pairs, expansion → flee-to-Allah charge → the sorcerer-or-madman refrain → the Prophet's release ("turn away... you are not to blame") → remind → worship-purpose, no-needing → the turn-of-counterparts → final woe.
