# The universe built and expanding: flee to Allah, created to worship, fed by none (51:47–60)
**UID:** 202610142121
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 51:47–60 (Ruku 3 of 3 in adh-Dhariyat)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[51:47]` – `[51:60]` (14 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The cosmos itself testifies — built with power, still expanding, spread out, everything in pairs — and from that witness the surah draws its single command: "So flee towards Allah," the warner's one message across all messengers (50–52); the Prophet is released from blame (54), the reminder keeps working (55), and the creed lands on the purpose verse: humans and jinn created to worship, God needing no provision from anyone — "Allah is the Provider" (56–58) — with the disbelievers' turn reserved (59) and a final woe on the promised Day (60).
* **Placement:** The close of the certainty-provision surah: ruku 1 swore the Promise, ruku 2 paid it in history; ruku 3 pays it in cosmology and creed.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[51:47]`** "We constructed the universe with power, and We are expanding it."
  * **Gloss:** The cosmos-clause (sa'a — "I am expanding it," first-person plural) — the only corpus use of "expanding" (rg); the modern resonance with cosmological expansion is an interpretive comparison, not a textual claim (*Source: agent inference — verify before quoting as concordism*).
* **`[51:48]`** "And the earth—We spread it out—How well We prepared it!"
  * **Gloss:** The spread earth — the same English verb as `[50:7]` "how We spread it out" (rg; the surah-pair's shared exhibit) — plus an exclamation of workmanship: "How well We prepared it!" — the only builder-praise in the trio of cosmos-verses.
* **`[51:49]`** "We created all things in pairs, so that you may reflect and ponder."
  * **Gloss:** Pair-creation as curriculum: the azwaj-family dossier (rg-verified): `[50:7]` "delightful pairs", `[78:8]` "created you in pairs?", `[39:6]` "eight kinds in pairs", `[55:52]` "fruits of every kind, in pairs"; purpose-clause "that you may reflect and ponder" — the tadhkira machinery of ruku 1 (51:20-21 "Do you not see?") running to the end of the surah.
* **`[51:50]`** "“So flee towards Allah. I am to you from Him a clear warner.”"
  * **Gloss:** THE flee-verse (the task's "So flee to Allah... do not set up another god" belongs HERE, 51:50-51 — not "52:50": at-Tur has only 49 ayahs; corpus-verified correction): flight as the correct response to a built-and-expanding cosmos — the verb of hijra turned existential; and the surah's answer to the 50:2 wonder: everything is evidence, so run to its Maker.
* **`[51:51]`** "“And do not set up any other god with Allah. I am to you from Him a clear warner.”"
  * **Gloss:** The flight's negative clause: one command, one prohibition, warner-refrain twice in two verses — the whole prophetic program reduced to the k-tawhid formula (pair: `[52:43]` "Or do they have a god besides Allah?" — forward-twin).
* **`[51:52]`** "Likewise, no messenger came to those before them, but they said, “A sorcerer or a madman.”"
  * **Gloss:** The universal history of rejection: the identical insult from ruku 2's Pharaoh (51:39) now generalized across all messengers — Itani's exact words both times (rg); the Meccan insult-dictionary's siblings: `[52:29]` "neither a soothsayer, nor a madman", `[37:36]` "a mad poet" — the refusers reuse the file.
* **`[51:53]`** "Did they recommend it to one another? In fact, they are rebellious people."
  * **Gloss:** The accusation of coordination: the insult is a passed-down script, mutual recommendation of a rejection-strategy; verdict: "rebellious people" — the aggression-family of `[50:25]` "aggressors" in Itani's English (root-kinship per classical lexicography — verify).
* **`[51:54]`** "So turn away from them; you are not to blame."
  * **Gloss:** The Prophet's release #1 (taballigha-clause: no liability for their refusal — pairs with `[52:45]` "So leave them until they meet their Day" and `[50:45]` "you are not a dictator over them": the three Meccan neighbor-surahs hand the warner the same discharge-paper).
* **`[51:55]`** "And remind, for the reminder benefits the believers."
  * **Gloss:** The commission survives the release: remind — because tadhkira only lands on those already qualified (the receiver-theory of 50:37 "whoever possesses a heart" in one clause; near-twin of 50:45's "remind by the Quran whoever fears My warning" — rg).
* **`[51:56]`** "I did not create the jinn and the humans except to worship Me."
  * **Gloss:** THE purpose-of-creation verse (task claim ✓ verbatim): creation has an except-clause — li-ya'budun ("to worship Me"; the worship/ibadah reading is standard, and the classical alternative "to know Me" is transmitted (*Source: classical tafsir disagreement — verify*; the English "worship" is the corpus fact)). Divine needlessness precedes the command: God creates for relation, not revenue.
* **`[51:57]`** "I need no livelihood from them, nor do I need them to feed Me."
  * **Gloss:** The Unfed: rizq reversed — the Creator does not draw sustenance from the worshipped; the prophetic economic formula is its twin: "I ask of you no payment" `[25:57]`, `[26:109]`+ (the messengers' refrain, rg-verified); read with k-tawhid's "no hidden benefactors" thesis: worship to a God-with-needs is idolatry wearing monotheist clothing (Source: agent synthesis of 51:56–58 — verify).
* **`[51:58]`** "Allah is the Provider, the One with Power, the Strong."
  * **Gloss:** The triple name-verse settles it: al-Razzaq (Provider — the 51:22 "in the heaven is your livelihood" answered at its source), dhu-l-quwwa (possessor of strength), al-matin (the Firm — "the Strong" rendering; no other corpus use of this divine adjective — rg). Provision runs down, never up.
* **`[51:59]`** "Those who do wrong will have their turn, like the turn of their counterparts, so let them not rush Me."
  * **Gloss:** The disbelievers' portion — but not as the task phrased it: no "share of the Fire" wording here; "their turn" (dhaqb — a portion-day of punishment matching "the turn of their counterparts" — twin-line `[34:54]` "as was done formerly with their counterparts" — rg) + the "do not rush Me" clause answering 51:14's "what you used to challenge": the timing-question gets a reply without a date.
* **`[51:60]`** "So woe to those who disbelieve because of that Day of theirs which they are promised."
  * **Gloss:** The surah's closing verse (task's locate-verify: the final ayah is a woe, not a Fire-share line): "woe" (wayl — the corpus's warning-genre word — cf. `[52:11]` "Woe on that Day to the deniers" — forward-pair) + "that Day of theirs" (the promise-possessive) + which they are promised — the wa'd-thread of 51:5/22 ties off here: the Promise, kept, is the warning's last word.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **ibadah — "to worship Me":** 51:56 — the purpose-clause of human and jinn creation.
  * **Rendering:** "except to worship Me"; "know Me" is transmitted alternative gloss (*Source: classical tafsir — verify*).
  * **Elsewhere:** the k-tawhid note's frame "It is You we worship" (`[1:5]`) is the Fatihah-side of the same verb — the brain's worship-definition lives there.
* **razzaq/Provider — livelihood-language:** 51:57–58.
  * **Elsewhere:** 51:22 "in the heaven is your livelihood" (same English word, rizq — rg-internal): the provision-oath of ruku 1 is answered at its source by the Provider-name of 51:58; read with k-infraq: the share given downward (51:19) mirrors the Provider who receives nothing (51:57).
* **tadhkira — "the reminder benefits the believers":** 51:55.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[50:45]` remind-by-the-Quran; 51:8-11's divided speech is the reminder's failed reception — the surah's own A/B test (see k-guidance).
* **dhaqb (turn/portion of punishment):** 51:59 — "will have their turn, like the turn of their counterparts."
  * **Elsewhere:** the pairing-formula's twin is `[34:54]` "as was done formerly with their counterparts" — the only verified lexical echo (rg).
