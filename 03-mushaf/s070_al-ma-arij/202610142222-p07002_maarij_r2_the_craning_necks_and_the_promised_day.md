# Can each of them wish to be admitted to a Garden of Bliss? The craning crowds and the promised Day (70:36–44)
**UID:** 202610142222
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 70:36–44 (Ruku 2 of 2 in al-Ma'arij)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[70:36]` – `[70:44]` (9 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The camera snaps from the honored exception-list to the crowd gawking at the prophet from right and left, each of them privately hoping for a Garden — a hope annulled by one sentence ("We created them from what they know," 70:39); God swears by the Lord of the Easts and the Wests that He can replace them with better (40–41), the prophet is released to leave them to blunder and play until their promised Day (42), and the surah ends on the tombs-rush: hurrying as though to a target, eyes cast down, humiliation complete — "This is the Day which they were promised" (43–44).
* **Placement:** Closing ruku; the surah's verdict on the questioners of 70:1. The whole surah is bracketed by question-and-answer mockery: 70:1's demanded torment ↔ 70:44's delivered Day ("This is the Day which they were promised").

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[70:36]`** "What is with those who disbelieve, stretching their necks towards you." — task-flag resolved: the image is neck-crane (ihbab, gazers), not "rushing": the disbelief of 70:1's questioner has become a staring crowd.
* **`[70:37]`** "From the right, and from the left, banding together?" — the encirclement in parties (shita): the gawkers organize — the surround reads like a tribunal of mockers around the warner.
* **`[70:38]`** "Is every one of them aspiring to be admitted into a Garden of Bliss?" — the rhetorical strike: the same people mocking the warning privately hope for paradise — jannah al-na'im, the "Garden of Bliss" title-twin `[26:85]` (rg-verified; plural at 56:12/31:8 — counted).
* **`[70:39]`** "No indeed! We created them from what they know." — the denial plus the double-sentence (Itani literal): creation from what they themselves know — i.e., from base stuff they recognize — and/or "from what is known of them"; reading left deliberately hard by the translation — *Source: translator's literal rendering; classical glosses say "from what they know of their own origin" — verify.*
* **`[70:40]`** "I swear by the Lord of the Easts and the Wests, that We are Able." — the surah's only oath; plural easts/wests (mashariq/maghārib) against the dual of `[55:17]` "Lord of the two Easts and... two Wests" (rg-verified drift) — the east-west axis as the sweep over which replacement power runs.
* **`[70:41]`** "To replace them with better than they, and We are not to be outdone." — the replacement-threat: the generation after the mockers may be better — sovereignty proven by substitutability (kin-logic to 67:30's water-dare).
* **`[70:42]`** "So leave them to blunder and play, until they meet their Day which they are promised." — task-flag VERIFIED verbatim (above); the "leave" formula's exact twin is `[43:83]` and the divine-delegation kin is 68:44 "leave Me" (rg-verified) — patience's outer limit is release, not coercion.
* **`[70:43]`** "The Day when they will emerge from the tombs in a rush, as though they were hurrying towards a target." — the tombs become a stampede; "towards a target" (nasab — a goal they strain toward; *Source: standard lexicon — verify*) — the swarm-image twin `[21:96]` "they swarm down from every mound" (rg-verified).
* **`[70:44]`** "Their eyes cast down; overwhelmed by humiliation. This is the Day which they were promised." — the closing answer to 70:1: the promised thing arrives with lowered eyes — the same irony-family as 67:27 "This is what you used to call for" (rg-verified).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`Garden of Bliss`** (jannat al-na'im): 70:38 — singular here; `[26:85]` "the Garden of Bliss" exact (rg-verified), plural "Gardens of Bliss" at `[56:12]`, `[31:8]`, `[37:43]` (counted).
* **`mashariq / magharib`** (Easts / Wests): 70:40 — the plural vs 55:17's dual (rg-verified) — *Source: classical note treats the plural as seasonal east-west points; agent flags translation-visible form — verify.*
* **`yamdahuun`** (stretching their necks): 70:36 — the stare made posture: the surah's last picture of unbelief is a crowd looking at the prophet while he looks past them to the Day.
