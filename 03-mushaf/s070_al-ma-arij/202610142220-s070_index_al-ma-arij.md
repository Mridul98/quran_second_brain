## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Meccan — agreed in the classical type-lists. *Source: classical type-lists — verify.* Revelation order: **transmitted nuzul tables — verify** (no chain-confirmed number at this slot; corpus carries no nuzul statement; unnumbered Basmala heads the surah).
* **Size:** 44 ayahs, 2 ruku: 70:1–35 / 70:36–44 (ruku_map globals 502–503 — verified).
* **Title:** al-Ma'arij = "the Ways of Ascent" — God's epithet at 70:3 ("Lord of the Ways of Ascent"), corpus-unique (rg-counted: the phrase occurs only here and in the file header). The surah is also known by transmitted titles ("the questioner") — *verify; the corpus offers only the header "WAYS OF ASCENT."*
* **Central theme:** A mockery-question ("when is this torment?") answered at Day-scale. The ascent frames the Day (angels and the Spirit rise; its measure fifty thousand years, 70:4); the tableau dissolves every human security — friend, family, all earth as ransom (70:10–14), the fire stripping scalps (70:15–16); human default panic (halu'a — fretful, ungenerous, 70:19–21) is broken only by the exception-list of ten verbs (prayer, wealth-share, Day-affirmation, fear, chastity, trusts, testimony — 70:22–34), who end "honored in Gardens" (70:35). Ruku 2 turns to the gawking crowds and leaves them to play until the promised Day.
* **Reading hint:** the surah's arithmetic is deliberate — 50,000 years (70:4) against the 1,000-year Days of `[32:5]` and `[22:47]` (rg-verified): the Day longer than all human counting.

## 3. Structural Arc
* **The question and its owner (70:1–5):** an actual questioner, an actual torment "inevitable... none can repel," from the Lord of the Ascents; then the first patience-command — "with sweet patience" (70:5).
* **Perspective flip (70:6–7):** "They see it distant. But We see it near."
* **Cosmic dissolve (70:8–16):** sky-brass, wool-mountains, friend-indifference (70:10 — cf. 69:35), the ransom list (children → spouse → brother → family → everyone on earth), refused: "By no means! It is a Raging Fire. It strips away the scalps."
* **The invitée profile (70:17–18):** the fire calls the one who turned back, fled, accumulated and hoarded — hoarding named as eschatology.
* **Anthropology + exception (70:19–35):** manu' created halu'a (restless; fretful in harm, ungenerous in good) — "Except the prayerful," and the ten traits that carve a class out of the default, closing in honor in Gardens.
* **Ruku 2 (70:36–44):** the craning crowds from right and left; the Garden-of-Bliss aspiration denied ("We created them from what they know"); the oath of the Lord of Easts-and-Wests able to replace them; "leave them to blunder and play"; the promised Day with tombs-rush and cast-down eyes.

## 5b. Batch-Return Verification Flags (this index)
* 70:1–3 VERIFIED as the opening: "A questioner questioned the imminent torment. For the disbelievers; none can repel it. From Allah, Lord of the Ways of Ascent." — task's "Ask about the punishment—inevitable" is a close paraphrase; corpus is third-person report, not imperative.
* 70:4 VERIFIED: "on a Day the duration of which is fifty thousand years" — "fifty thousand" corpus-unique (rg); Spirit-pair `[97:4]`, thousand-year Days `[32:5]`+`[22:47]` (rg-verified).
* 70:11–14 VERIFIED as the ransom list (children/spouse/brother/family-that-sheltered/everyone-on-earth). Task's "companions? owners?" → 70:10's word is "No friend will care about his friend" (hamim); the "no ties of kinship" formula is `[23:101]` "no relations between them will exist on that Day" (rg-verified — 23, not 70). Task's "the skin?" → 70:16 "It strips away the **scalps**"; skin-testimony belongs to `[41:20–22]` and melting skins to `[22:20]` (rg-verified) — not this surah.
* Task's "self-reproach? that is 75" CONFIRMED by location: the oath "I swear by the blaming soul" is `[75:2]` (rg-verified — Itani renders nafs lawwama as "the blaming soul"; 70 has no such oath). The cognate corpus-use is 68:30 "blaming one another."
* **70:25–27 task-flag REJECTED and fixed:** the "gives and fears and confirms" trio is NOT 70's wording — 70:24–27 reads "in whose wealth is a rightful share / For the beggar and the deprived / And those who affirm the Day of Judgment / And those who fear the punishment of their Lord." The explicit "confirms the Best/al-Husna" formula belongs to `[92:5–6]`: "As for him who gives and is righteous. And confirms goodness" (rg-verified; Itani renders al-husna here as "goodness"). The triplet belongs to al-Layl; 70's list is the ten-traits exception-clause.
* 70:36–39: task cited 36–38; the line "We created them from what they know" is **70:39** (corpus-verified); 70:36–37 "stretching their necks... from the right, and from the left, banding together" — task's "rushing from every side" adjusted.
* 70:42 VERIFIED: "So leave them to blunder and play, until they meet their Day which they are promised." — exact-phrase twin `[43:83]` (rg-verified; 70:43–44 add the tombs-rush and cast-down eyes).
