# A questioner questioned the imminent torment: the 50,000-year Day and the exception-list (70:1–35)
**UID:** 202610142221
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 70:1–35 (Ruku 1 of 2 in al-Ma'arij)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[70:1]` – `[70:35]` (35 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** A real mockery-question receives a real answer measured in ascent: the torment is inevitable and its Day lasts fifty thousand years (1–4); when it comes, sky and mountains melt, no friend is asked after, and the criminal's ransom bid — children, spouse, brother, family, the whole earth — is refused by a fire that strips scalps (6–18); the human default (restless, fretful, ungenerous) is carved out by ten exception-traits, prayer and a wealth-share at their head (19–34), and "these will be honored in Gardens" (35).
* **Placement:** Opening ruku; the surah's full argument in one sweep. Ruku 2 (70:36–44) re-frames the same Day against the gawking crowds.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[70:1]`** "A questioner questioned the imminent torment." — the surah reports the mockery as a historical speech-act; "imminent" (waqi') already rules.
* **`[70:2]`** "For the disbelievers; none can repel it." — the scope and the defenselessness in six words.
* **`[70:3]`** "From Allah, Lord of the Ways of Ascent." — the title-ayah (ma'arij corpus-unique — rg): ascent-names the source of what descends as warning.
* **`[70:4]`** "Unto Him the angels and the Spirit ascend on a Day the duration of which is fifty thousand years." — the ascent-view; pair `[97:4]` "In it descend the angels and the Spirit" (rg-verified — direction-reversed twins); Day-length kin: `[32:5]`, `[22:47]` (rg-verified).
* **`[70:5]`** "So be patient, with sweet patience." — the first sabr-command; jamil rendered "sweet" where 12:18 has "beautiful" and 12:83 "a virtue" (rg-verified drift).
* **`[70:6]`** "They see it distant." — epistemic, not optical: the Day's distance is a belief-position.
* **`[70:7]`** "But We see it near." — the divine-voice counter; 6–7 is the surah's shortest courtroom.
* **`[70:8]`** "On the Day when the sky will be like molten brass." — the teardown begins with color; brass-family drink of 18:29's Fire-scene (rg-verified cognate image).
* **`[70:9]`** "And the mountains will be like tufted wool." — weight becomes fluff; exact-phrase twin `[101:5]` (rg-verified — Qari'ah and Ma'arij share the similes).
* **`[70:10]`** "No friend will care about his friend." — hamim: the intimate made indifferent; twin-clause 69:35 "he has no friend here today" (rg-verified pair).
* **`[70:11]`** "They will be shown each other. The criminal wishes he would be redeemed from the punishment of that Day by his children." — sight restored just in time for the failed transaction: children offered as fida.
* **`[70:12]`** "And his spouse, and his brother." — the ransom ladder descends through the household.
* **`[70:13]`** "And his family that sheltered him." — the sheltering clan, the same unit named as the one they hoarded for (cf. `[8:28]` "your possessions and your children are a test" — rg-verified).
* **`[70:14]`** "And everyone on earth, in order to save him." — the bid ends planetary: the whole earth as one man's price — refused by 70:15.
* **`[70:15]`** "By no means! It is a Raging Fire." — the veto; "Raging Fire" (dalwah) renders the fire's name — exact twin `[101:11]` "A Raging Fire" (rg-verified).
* **`[70:16]`** "It strips away the scalps." — the task's "skin?" answer: the scalp-shaving fire (tashawa al-ammah — *Source: standard reading — verify*); skin-testimony is elsewhere (`[41:20–22]` rg-verified — different fire-sensory).
* **`[70:17]`** "It invites him who once turned his back and fled." — the fire as caller (yad'u) — the invitations of this life (prostration called, 68:43 — rg-kin) reversed.
* **`[70:18]`** "And accumulated and hoarded." — 'ajjafa: hoarding named — the economic trait in the eschatological dossier; k-infraq's thesis in two verbs (forward).
* **`[70:19]`** "Man was created restless." — halu'a: the default anthropology; "restless" is corpus-unique here (rg-counted).
* **`[70:20]`** "Touched by adversity, he is fretful." — juzu': panic at harm.
* **`[70:21]`** "Touched by good, he is ungenerous." — manu': withholding at gain — the garden-owners' disease (68:24 — rg-kin) in anthropological form.
* **`[70:22]`** "Except the prayerful." — the exception-clause opens with salat ("the prayerful" = al-musallin — *Source: standard idiom — verify*); the list's load-bearing verb.
* **`[70:23]`** "Those who are constant at their prayers." — dawam: continuity, not intensity — patience's liturgical form (k-sabr link — forward).
* **`[70:24]`** "And those in whose wealth is a rightful share." — a known, earmarked portion (haqq ma'lum — *Source: standard reading — verify*); twin `[51:19]` "a share for the beggar and the deprived" (rg-verified).
* **`[70:25]`** "For the beggar and the deprived." — al-sa'il wal-mahrum: the two claimants; 68:24's barred poor in statute form (rg-kin).
* **`[70:26]`** "And those who affirm the Day of Judgment." — the day-affirming answer to 70:1's questioner — tawhid's eschatological clause (task's "confirms the Best [al-Husna]" is 92:5–6 wording, not here — corrected).
* **`[70:27]`** "And those who fear the punishment of their Lord." — khara: the negative motive, named first-class.
* **`[70:28]`** "Their Lord’s punishment is not to be taken for granted." — divine security refused: the answer to 67:16's "Are you confident?"-question (rg-kin).
* **`[70:29]`** "And those who guard their chastity." — furuj-guarding: the body counted among the exception-traits.
* **`[70:30]`** "Except from their spouses or those living under their control, for then they are free of blame." — the lawful exception; near-identical to `[23:6]` "Except from their spouses, or their dependents—for then they are free from blame" (rg-verified couplet with 23:5–7).
* **`[70:31]`** "But whoever seeks to go beyond that— these are the transgressors." — the same 23:7 boundary-line ("these are the transgressors" — rg-verified).
* **`[70:32]`** "And those who honor their trusts and their pledges." — amanat + mithaq (cf. k-covenant's pledge-grammar — forward).
* **`[70:33]`** "And those who stand by their testimonies." — shahada-honoring: speech kept honest (contrast 63's forged testimony — rg-kin).
* **`[70:34]`** "And those who are dedicated to their prayers." — the list circles back to salat, opened and closed by it (inclusio — corpus fact).
* **`[70:35]`** "These will be honored in Gardens." — makramun: the restless one's counter-portrait; gardens of the exception, after 68:17's hoarded one.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`ma'arij`** (Ways of Ascent): 70:3 — epithet-only, corpus-unique (rg-counted); the title-word belongs to God's lordship over ascent, then 70:4 makes it the angels' traffic pattern.
* **`halu'a`** (restless): 70:19 — unique render (rg); its two faces are 70:20 juzu' (fretful) and 70:21 manu' (withholding) — the psychology the exception-list carves out.
* **`hamim`** (intimate friend): 70:10 — the indifference-word; twin clause `[69:35]` (rg-verified).
