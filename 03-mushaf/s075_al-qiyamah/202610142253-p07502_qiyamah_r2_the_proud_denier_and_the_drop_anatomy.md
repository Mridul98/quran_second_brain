# The proud denier, doubled woe, and the drop-to-pair anatomy: "Is He not Able to revive the dead?" (75:31–40)
**UID:** 202610142253
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 75:31–40 (Ruku 2 of 2 in al-Qiyamah)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[75:31]` – `[75:40]` (10 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah names its defendant at last — the man who neither believed nor prayed, denied, turned away, and swaggered home — hits him with a doubled "Woe to you" twice over, then answers his existential status-question ("left without purpose?") by replaying his own assembly-line: semen → clot → shaped → pair — landing the whole surah on one closing question that is also its title.
* **Placement:** Ruku 2 of 2 — the verdict-half; ruku 1 staged the Day, this unit puts one man in the dock and proves ability from his own origin-stuff.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[75:31]`** "He neither believed nor prayed." — the two-verb indictment: assent and devotion both absent (the faith/prayer pair as minimum unit — cf. 74:43's catalogue opening "We were not of those who prayed," rg-verified in s074 note).
* **`[75:32]`** "But he denied and turned away." — the second pair: rejection then retreat — denial is not neutral, it walks out (twin profile-move `[74:23]` "Then he turned back and was proud," rg-verified).
* **`[75:33]`** "Then he went to his family, full of pride." — the social exit: back to his clan, swaying with self-importance — pride as the closing posture of a public denial (Source: the swagger-reading of the root is classical — verify; "full of pride" rendering is Itani's).
* **`[75:34]`** "Woe to you; and woe." — the address-woe (waylakum), doubled in one breath: not the surah-wide refrain-family of 77 ("Woe on that Day…" — different idiom, rg-counted).
* **`[75:35]`** "Then again: Woe to you; and woe." — repetition as drumbeat: the same compound struck twice more — four woes on one man (the "then again" keeps the rhythm unresolved).
* **`[75:36]`** "Does man think that he will be left without purpose?" — the second and last "Does man think" of the surah (75:3, 75:36 — rg-counted): sudan — abandoned, unchecked, sent-meaningless (corpus-unique rendering, rg).
* **`[75:37]`** "Was he not a drop of ejaculated semen?" — the answer-archive begins: the humblest start imaginable (cluster twins: `[76:2]` "liquid mixture," `[77:20]` "insignificant fluid" — forward to this batch, rg-verified).
* **`[75:38]`** "Then he became a clot. And He created and proportioned?" — clot ('alaqah) — twin of `[96:2]` "Created man from a clot" (the first-revealed surah, classical reports — verify) and stage 2 of `[22:5]`'s full sequence (rg-verified); the shaping (khalaqa fa-sawwā) is the fingerprint-work of 75:4.
* **`[75:39]`** "And made of him the two sexes, the male and the female?" — the pair-creation formula, exact twin `[53:45–46]` "created the two kinds—the male and the female. From a sperm drop, when emitted" (rg-verified).
* **`[75:40]`** "Is He not Able to revive the dead?" — the ring-question: what began at "reassemble his bones" ends at "revive the dead" — the expected answer is the surah's own "Yes indeed" of 75:4 (formula twin `[46:33]` "is Able to revive the dead? Yes indeed" — rg-verified).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`sudan`** (without purpose): 75:36 — the skeptic's real claim: not "no God" but "no reckoning aimed at me."
  * **Rendering:** "left without purpose" (task's "without control" ✗ — different edition idiom).
  * **Elsewhere:** corpus-unique word (rg); exact concept-twin `[23:115]` "Did you think that We created you in vain, and that to Us you will not be returned?" (rg-verified) — the same think-question plus the return that 75:12/75:30 bracket.
* **`'alaqah`** (clot): 75:38 — the second stage of man's dossier.
  * **Rendering:** "clot" — identical at `[96:2]`; `[22:5]` "a clinging clot" (rg-verified).
* **`nutfah`** (drop): 75:37 "a drop of ejaculated semen."
  * **Rendering:** full phrase here; `[53:46]` "a sperm drop, when emitted" (rg) — the batch's drop-cluster (75:37 / 76:2 / 77:20–21) is its most concentrated evidence-run.
* **`wayl`** (woe): 75:34–35 — the address-form "Woe to you" (corpus elsewhere: `[20:61]`, `[28:80]`, `[46:17]` — rg-verified) versus 77's tenfold "Woe on that Day" refrain (rg-counted: 77 ×10, 52 ×1, 83 ×1).
