## 3. Structural Arc
* **Oath pair (75:1–2):** the Day sworn, then the in-house witness — the blaming soul.
* **Refutation (75:3–6):** bones-question → fingertips-answer → the psychology of denial ("wants to deny what is ahead of him") → the sneer "When is the Day of Resurrection?"
* **Collapse and audit (75:7–15):** dazzled vision, eclipsed moon, joined sun; no refuge; the full ledger (sent-forward and left-behind); man self-evidencing, excuses useless.
* **Revelation interlude (75:16–19):** do not hurry the recitation — collection, recitation, explanation are on Allah (k-kalam node).
* **Inverted loves + two faces (75:20–25):** fleeting-life love, Hereafter disregard; radiant faces looking toward their Lord; gloomy faces awaiting the back-breaker.
* **Deathbed now (75:26–30):** breastbones, "Who is the healer?", the parting realized, leg entwined with leg — "To your Lord on that Day is the drive" (brackets 75:12 "the settlement").
* **Ruku 2 (75:31–40):** the four-beat profile (no faith, no prayer, denial-and-turning, swagger home); doubled woe ×2; the "without purpose" question answered by the semen→clot→proportion→pair sequence; closing ability-question.
