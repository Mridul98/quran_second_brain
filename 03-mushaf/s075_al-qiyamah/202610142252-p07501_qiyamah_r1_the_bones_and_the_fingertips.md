# The Day and the blaming soul sworn over the fingertip proof: collapse, ledger, two faces, deathbed now (75:1–30)
**UID:** 202610142252
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 75:1–30 (Ruku 1 of 2 in al-Qiyamah)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[75:1]` – `[75:30]` (30 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Two oaths — the Day itself and the blaming soul within man — certify the resurrection against the bones-skeptic; the ruku then runs the Day in three registers (cosmos collapsing, ledger opened, faces splitting) and lands it in the present tense of a dying body, bracketed by "the settlement" and "the drive" to the Lord.
* **Placement:** Ruku 1 of 2; opens the surah's courtroom and contains every public scene. Ruku 2 (75:31–40) supplies the defendant's profile and the anatomy rebuttal.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[75:1]`** "I swear by the Day of Resurrection." — the disputed object swears in the argument: the Day itself as oath-certifier.
* **`[75:2]`** "And I swear by the blaming soul." — second oath, inside the human: the self that accuses the self (nafs lawwāma — this ayah is the locus classicus; Source: classical tafsir reads it as self-reproach on that Day — verify).
* **`[75:3]`** "Does man think that We will not reassemble his bones?" — the skeptic's thesis, stated as the question the surah will autopsy.
* **`[75:4]`** "Yes indeed; We are Able to reconstruct his fingertips." — the rebuttal at maximum resolution: not merely bones but fingertips (banān — corpus-unique, rg); if the pattern survives, identity survives.
* **`[75:5]`** "But man wants to deny what is ahead of him." — the motive revealed: denial is wish, not evidence (task's "persist in sin" ✗ for this edition, rg).
* **`[75:6]`** "He asks, “When is the Day of Resurrection?”" — the mock-timing question; twin of `[51:12]` "They ask, “When is the Day of Judgment?”" (rg-verified).
* **`[75:7]`** "When vision is dazzled." — the answer: not a calendar date but a state of the eye — the first collapse is sensory.
* **`[75:8]`** "And the moon is eclipsed." — light-registry failing (twin `[81:2]` "the stars are dimmed", `[82:2]` "the planets are scattered" — rg-verified).
* **`[75:9]`** "And the sun and the moon are joined together." — the two lights forced into one: orbit abolished; cf. `[81:1]` "When the sun is rolled up" (rg).
* **`[75:10]`** "On that Day, man will say, “Where is the escape?”" — the question flips: the mocker now asks; the "where is" of flight.
* **`[75:11]`** "No indeed! There is no refuge." — the verdict-word (malas) — twin clause `[42:47]` "there will be no refuge on that Day" (rg-verified).
* **`[75:12]`** "To your Lord on that Day is the settlement." — first bracket-line: the destination is a Person, not a place.
* **`[75:13]`** "On that Day man will be informed of everything he put forward, and everything he left behind." — the ledger's two columns (twin `[36:12]` "what they have forwarded, and their traces"; `[82:5]` "advanced... deferred" — rg-verified).
* **`[75:14]`** "And man will be evidence against himself." — the prosecution rests on the defendant's own file — 75:2's blaming soul externalized.
* **`[75:15]`** "Even as he presents his excuses." — the excuse does not void the evidence: self-witness outruns self-defense (nearest concept outside: `[100:10–11]` the contents of the hearts obtained — rg; task's "Allah sees what is in the breast" ✗ s075).
* **`[75:16]`** "Do not wag your tongue with it, to hurry on with it." — voice-cut: the Prophet addressed mid-courtroom — the recitation's mechanics become divine property.
* **`[75:17]`** "Upon Us is its collection and its recitation." — Allah owns the Quran's gathering and delivery (k-kalam node; Source: classical tafsir reads "collection" as in the heart — verify).
* **`[75:18]`** "Then, when We have recited it, follow its recitation." — the human duty reduced to follow-along: reception, not fabrication.
* **`[75:19]`** "Then upon Us is its explanation." — clarity guaranteed: three "Upon Us" claims bracket the interlude (17–19 — observable triple, corpus fact).
* **`[75:20]`** "Alas, you love the fleeting life." — pivot back to the crowd: the diagnosis is affection mis-aimed (near-verbatim twin `[76:27]` — rg).
* **`[75:21]`** "And you disregard the Hereafter." — the inversion clause: love here ⇔ forgetting there (the shukr-audit's failure mode, forward to k-shukr via 76:27).
* **`[75:22]`** "Faces on that Day will be radiant." — face-family opens; verbatim twin of `[80:38]` (rg-verified) — the beatific-vision headline (Source: classical reading of 75:23 as seeing the Lord — verify in print).
* **`[75:23]`** "Looking towards their Lord." — four words that carry an entire creed-debate: the eyes of the blessed busy with their Lord (task's "turned toward their Lord" ≈ this line; "staring fixed" ✗).
* **`[75:24]`** "And faces on that Day will be gloomy." — the mirror face ("gloomy" twin `[67:27]`, family `[88:2]` "shamed", `[80:40]` "misery" — rg-verified).
* **`[75:25]`** "Realizing that a back-breaker has befallen them." — the spine-image: the Day snaps what uprights a person ("back-breaker" is corpus-unique English, rg; Source: agent inference — verify).
* **`[75:26]`** "Indeed, when it has reached the breastbones." — the scene shifts from that Day to this deathbed: the soul at the collarbones (twin anatomy `[86:7]` "between the backbone and the breastbones" — rg).
* **`[75:27]`** "And it is said, “Who is the healer?”" — the room's helpless whisper: who has power now? ("healer" corpus-unique, rg).
* **`[75:28]`** "And He realizes that it is the parting." — the dying one's private certainty: separation, capitalized "He" is an edition/extract oddity (see Fleeting link — verify).
* **`[75:29]`** "And leg is entwined with leg." — calamity braided on calamity (corpus-unique idiom, rg); the last trouble is the angel of death. (Source: classical gloss "the last agony meets the Day" — verify.)
* **`[75:30]`** "To your Lord on that Day is the drive." — closing bracket to 75:12: settlement then drive — both "To your Lord", both corpus-unique as a pair (rg).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`lawwāma`** (the blaming soul): 75:2 — the only oath made from inside the human.
  * **Rendering:** "the blaming soul"; "self-reproaching" = 0 corpus hits (task phrasing from other editions).
  * **Elsewhere:** the verb-family only at `[68:30]` "blaming one another" (rg) — the Garden-owners' morning quarrel previews the self-blame 75:2 swears by.
* **`banān`** (fingertips): 75:4 — resurrection argued at the resolution of finger-patterns.
  * **Rendering:** "reconstruct his fingertips" ("reshape" ✗ task).
  * **Elsewhere:** corpus-unique; the bones-question twin `[36:78]` "Who will revive the bones when they have decayed?" answered by first-instance creation `[36:79]` (rg-verified).
* **`mustaqarr`/`sayr`** (settlement/drive): 75:12 and 75:30 — the bracket pair.
  * **Rendering:** "the settlement" / "the drive" — both lines "To your Lord on that Day…" (rg-counted: exactly these two).
* **"back-breaker"** (the Day's crushing): 75:25 — the force on the spine.
  * **Rendering:** "a back-breaker has befallen them" — corpus-unique English word (rg).
  * **Elsewhere:** the face-family `[80:38–40]`, `[88:2]`, `[67:27]` (rg) sharpen the two-face genre it belongs to.
