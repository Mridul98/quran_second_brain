## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** DISPUTED — transmitted lists split between Meccan and Medinan readings (the austere oath-verdict form reads Meccan to many; a transmitted camp assigns it Medinan). Nothing internal settles it; both camps labeled as found. *Source: transmitted type-lists — verify.* Revelation order: **transmitted nuzul tables — verify** (corpus asserts no nuzul; unnumbered Basmala heads the surah).
* **Size:** 3 ayahs, 1 ruku (ruku_map global 545 — verified) — with al-Kawthar (108) and an-Nasr (110) it is the shortest surah in this edition by ayah-count (3 each — rg-counted). Zero "Allah," zero "Lord" (rg-count 0/0): the rescue clause names no deity and no object of belief — the four exits are stated entirely as human acts, and God appears only as the implicit first clause ("believe").
* **Title:** al-’Asr — header "TIME" tracks 103:1 "By Time": the oath is by a common noun for (the shrinking of) time; no other corpus verse swears by Time as such (rg-verified: "By Time" unique).
* **Central theme:** The Quran's tightest complete argument: an oath (Time), a universal verdict ("The human being is in loss"), and one exception-clause doing all the rescue work — belief, good works, mutual exhortation to truth, and mutual exhortation to patience (103:3). Two verbs of communal speech (encourage/recommend) convert private faith into a community that talks itself toward truth and holds itself in patience; the surah is the counter-diagnosis of 102: wealth kept vs. time lost.
* **Reading hint:** read 103 as the reply to 100–102: 100 indicted the ungrateful wealth-lover, 102 showed abundance distracting unto the graveyards, 103 declares that ALL of humanity is in that same free-fall (loss, khusr) — and gives the only four-vector escape hatch in the book's shortest sentence.

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Oath (103:1):** "By Time." — two words; the sworn thing is the medium in which the verdict happens (Source: structural observation).
* **Verdict (103:2):** "The human being is in loss." — the same generic human of `[100:6]` "Indeed, the human being is ungrateful to his Lord" (exact phrase-pair, rg-verified): one anthropology in two indictments.
* **Exception (103:3):** "Except those who believe, and do good works, and encourage truth, and recommend patience." — four coordinated clauses; the latter two are mutual-speech verbs (tawāṣaw-formula), making the exit a COMMUNITY, not a person. The "except" pattern twins: `[95:6]` "Except those who believe and do righteous deeds; for them is a reward without end" and `[84:25]` "Except those who believe and do good deeds; they will have an undiminished reward" (rg-verified cluster).
* **Zero-sum shape (whole):** the surah has no threat, no reward-scene, no divine name — just diagnosis and exit; its fame in transmitted Islam rests on that compression — *Source: transmitted virtue-reports — verify.*
