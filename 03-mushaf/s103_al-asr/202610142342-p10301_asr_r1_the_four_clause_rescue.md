# By Time: the human being is in loss — except the four-clause exit (103:1–3)
**UID:** 202610142342
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 103:1–3 (Ruku 1 of 1 in al-’Asr)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[103:1]` – `[103:3]` (3 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The Mushaf's shortest complete argument: sworn on Time itself, humanity is declared universally "in loss," and one exception-clause rescues — belief, good works, and the two mutual-speech acts of encouraging truth and recommending patience; the exit is not a deposit of time but a community that counsels, making 103 the mirror-diagnosis of 102 (what abundance distracts, Time prosecutes).
* **Placement:** Ruku 1 of 1; the batch's closing sentence. It quotes 100:6's anthropology ("the human being is…") without its Lord, and answers 102:1's "Abundance distracts you" with the verdict's venue (loss). It is also k-sabr's pre-existing anchor — the only one of my twelve notes whose knowledge note already cites it (rg-verified) — cited back here.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[103:1]`** "By Time."
  * **Gloss:** Two words of oath: the sworn entity is the depleting medium of human existence (‘aṣr — a shrinking/depleting time by standard lexing — *Source: transmitted lexing — verify*). In this edition no other verse swears by Time (rg-verified); its oath-neighbors in the batch swear by cosmos (85:1–3) and war-steeds (100:1–5).
* **`[103:2]`** "The human being is in loss."
  * **Gloss:** The verdict, universal and present-tense: same generic subject as `[100:6]` "Indeed, the human being is ungrateful to his Lord" (exact phrase-pair — rg-verified). The predicate khusr ("loss") bundles bankruptcy-and-perdition: not merely time-spent but self-lost (concept-twin `[17:82]` — the Quran "increases the wrongdoers only in loss"; verdict-twin `[63:9]` — "these are the losers").
* **`[103:3]`** "Except those who believe, and do good works, and encourage truth, and recommend patience."
  * **Gloss:** The exit clause in four coordinated verbs: vertical (believe), horizontal (good works), and two COMMUNAL SPEECH acts — "encourage truth" and "recommend patience" render one repeated Arabic formula (tawāṣaw, "advise one another") split into two English verbs (observable drift). The formula's twin pair is `[90:17]` "…believe, and advise one another to patience, and advise one another to kindness," which `[90:18]` seals: "These are the people of happiness" (rg-verified). Exception-formula cluster: `[95:6]`, `[84:25]` (both "Except those who believe and do…", rg-verified).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`al-‘Aṣr` ("Time")**: the oath.
  * **Rendering:** "By Time" — unique; note the edition's restraint: no personification, no elaboration.
  * **Elsewhere:** nothing swears by Time; 45:24's "they say…" dahr-speech? — NOT verified in this corpus's wording and therefore not cited (kept out per evidence policy).
* **`al-insān` ("The human being")**: the indicted species.
  * **Elsewhere:** 100:6 "ungrateful to his Lord" (piety-fault) and 103:2 "in loss" (fate-fault) — one anthropology, two diagnoses; also `[14:34]` "The human being is unfair and ungrateful" (rg-verified — k-shukr's own anchor).
* **`khusr` ("loss")**: the bankruptcy root.
  * **Rendering:** "in loss" (103:2).
  * **Elsewhere (rg-verified):** `[63:9]` distracted-by-possessions-and-children → "these are the losers" — the takathur-surah's own verdict-word; `[17:82]` Quran as "loss" increase for wrongdoers. The traders' idiom of the whole batch: 99:7–8's ledger, 101:6–9's scales, 103:2's bankruptcy (Source: structural observation — verify).
* **`"do good works"`**: one formula, four renderings across my batch and its neighbors (all rg-verified): 103:3 "do good works"; `[84:25]` "do good deeds"; `[95:6]` "do righteous deeds"; 98:7 "lead a righteous life." Translator drift, same Arabic pairing (āmanū wa-‘amilū).
* **`tawāṣaw` ("encourage/recommend")**: mutual exhortation.
  * **Rendering:** "encourage truth, and recommend patience" (103:3); "advise one another to patience, and advise one another to kindness" (`[90:17]`); "advocate patience" (`[3:200]` "Be patient, and advocate patience, and be united") — three English treatments of the counseling formula (all rg-verified).
  * **Note:** k-sabr maps this as its fourth quadrant — "sabr as social contract (103:3)" — and this passage confirms the formula-pair with 90:17/3:200 as its pedigree.
