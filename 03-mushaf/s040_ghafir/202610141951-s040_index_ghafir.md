## 3. Structural Arc
* **Letters, names, precedent (1–9):** Ha, Meem → the Scripture's descent from the Almighty-Omniscient → the Forgiver's four-name creed with the shahadah ("There is no god but He") → the arguers dismissed → Noah and the confederates as history's seized precedent → the carried Throne and the angels' mercy-fronted prayer for "those who repent and follow Your path."
* **The Day's census (10–20):** the Fire's interrogation; the twice-dead confession; association named as the fatal asymmetry (12); sustenance and heed (13–14); the Spirit and the Day of Encounter (15); the sovereignty question answered (16); hearts at the throats (18); the knower of "the deceptions of the eyes" (19); Allah judges with justice (20).
* **Pharaoh's dossier, first pass (21–27):** travel through the earth as courtroom → messengers rejected, seized → Moses sent with signs and a clear authority → the infant-slaughter revived → Pharaoh's murder plot and Moses's flight-to-God.
* **The hidden believer, the surah's heart (28–50):** speech one (28–37: tawhid defense, confederate fears, Joseph, the tower); speech two (38–44: two homes, the salvation-and-Fire rebuke, the committing of the case); divine rescue (45); Pharaoh's clan exposed morning and evening (46); the Fire's blame-trades (47–50).
* **Crescendo of creed (51–78):** promised support of messengers and believers → Moses's guidance-inheritance → the patience formula (55) → the arguer-without-authority portrait again (56) → heaven-and-earth's greater creation (57) → the answered prayer (60) → the gift litany (61–68) → the deviating arguers, abandoned gods, and the yokes (69–78, closing the messengers' limit: miracles only by leave of Allah).
* **Mercy audit, final case (79–85):** the domestic animals and the ships → "which of Allah's signs will you deny?" → the journeyed-through precedent once more → the too-late exclusive belief → the sunnat-Allah verdict that ends the surah the way `[40:5]` began it: the mockers lost.
