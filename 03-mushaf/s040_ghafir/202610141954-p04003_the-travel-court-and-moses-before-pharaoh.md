# The travel-court; Moses with a clear authority before Pharaoh, Hamaan, Quaroon (40:21–27)
**UID:** 202610141954
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 40:21–27 (Ruku 3 of 9 in Ghafir)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[40:21]` – `[40:27]` (7 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The courtroom procedure of travel — inspect the ruins of stronger civilizations, note that clear proofs arrived and were disbelieved, note the seizure, note that there was "no defender against Allah" — and then the dossier is opened for its most detailed case: Moses sent to Pharaoh, Hamaan, and Quaroon, called a lying sorcerer, answered with the revived infant-slaughter, and met by a man whose only weapon is refuge in "my Lord and your Lord."
* **Placement:** Ruku 3 of 9; the pivot from the universal eschatology of ruku 2 to the surah's controlling narrative (ruku 4–5). Its "no defender against Allah" (21) is quoted back verbatim by the secret believer at 40:33, and its seizure-law (22) pre-rates the rescue of 40:45.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[40:21]`** "Have they not travelled through the earth, and seen the consequences for those before them? They were stronger than them, and they left more impact on earth. But Allah seized them for their sins, and they had no defender against Allah."
  * **Gloss:** The empirical summons: tourism as evidence; the variables are strength and monumentality, and neither bought protection. "Travelled through the earth" is the surah's recurring formula — its twin phrasing returns at `[40:82]` ("Have they not journeyed through the land… what they had achieved availed them nothing").
* **`[40:22]`** "That is because their messengers used to come to them with clear proofs, but they disbelieved, so Allah seized them. He is Strong, Severe in retribution."
  * **Gloss:** The causal clause makes rejection of clear proofs the exact crime (the ruku-1 charge of 40:4 again); the divine name-pair "Strong, Severe in retribution" is this ruku's seal — severity named, as in 40:3, after mercy is offered first.
* **`[40:23]`** "We sent Moses with Our signs, and a clear authority."
  * **Gloss:** First-person plural sending: the dossier's author is God. "Signs + a clear authority" is a fixed missionary kit — the verbatim near-twin is `[23:45]` "We sent Moses and his brother Aaron, with Our signs and a clear authority," and the authority-half alone recurs at `[51:38]` "We sent him to Pharaoh with a clear authority."
* **`[40:24]`** "To Pharaoh, Hamaan, and Quaroon. But they said, “A lying sorcerer.”"
  * **Gloss:** The triangle named — king, minister, financier — and the verdict on Moses delivered before any debate: "a lying sorcerer" fuses the forgery charge and the sorcery charge into one word (cf. Pharaoh's later self-description of the contest, 40:37: "though I think he is lying"). The trio recurs verbatim at `[29:39]` ("And Quaroon, and Pharaoh, and Hamaan—Moses went to them with clear arguments, but they acted arrogantly").
* **`[40:25]`** "Then, when he came to them with the truth from Us, they said, “Kill the sons of those who have believed with him, and spare their daughters.” But the scheming of the unbelievers can only go astray."
  * **Gloss:** The old genocide re-legislated as policy: the slaughter/spare daughters formula is `[28:4]`'s ("slaughtering their sons, while sparing their daughters") — Pharaoh repeating himself is the text's verdict on Pharaoh; the narrator's aside ("scheming… can only go astray") pre-reads 40:37's "Pharaoh's guile was only in defeat."
* **`[40:26]`** "Pharaoh said, “Leave me to kill Moses, and let him appeal to his Lord. I fear he may change your religion, or spread disorder in the land.”"
  * **Gloss:** The private murder proposal, framed as security policy: "change your religion" (dīn) and "disorder in the land" are the twin fears of every tyrant in this surah; the taunt "let him appeal to his Lord" answers itself in 40:27 and then in the whole ruku 4–5 arc.
* **`[40:27]`** "Moses said, “I have sought the protection of my Lord and your Lord, from every tyrant who does not believe in the Day of Account.”"
  * **Gloss:** The counter-move is a sentence: the shared Lordship ("my Lord and your Lord") asserted *to* the claimants of divinity; the filter for tyranny is unbelief in the Day of Account (the Day-name of `[38:16]`, `[38:53]`); the near-verbatim twin is the Dukhan Moses, `[44:20]` "I have taken refuge in my Lord and your Lord, lest you stone me."

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`travelled through the earth`**: history as fieldwork.
  * **Rendering:** "Have they not travelled through the earth, and seen the consequences for those before them?" (40:21); drift to "journeyed through the land" at the surah's second summons, 40:82.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[47:10]` "Have they not journeyed through the earth and seen the consequences for those before them? Allah poured destruction upon them" — same courtroom, same verb-mix; the formula is the Quran's standing evidence-procedure.
* **`a clear authority`**: sulṭān — demonstrable divine sanction.
  * **Rendering:** "Our signs, and a clear authority" (40:23).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[23:45]` verbatim kit-twin; `[51:38]` "to Pharaoh with a clear authority"; `[4:153]` "We gave Moses a clear authority" — the authority survives the calf, which is the point the People of the Scripture miss.
* **`Kill the sons… spare the daughters`**: the demographic weapon.
  * **Rendering: 40:25** phrases it as a policy shout at the arrival of truth.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[28:4]` — the same act as background to Moses's birth ("slaughtering their sons, while sparing their daughters. He was truly a corrupter"); the Korah-financial third joins the trio at `[28:76]` and `[29:39]`.
* **`the Day of Account`**: yawm al-ḥisāb as the tyrant's blind spot.
  * **Rendering:** "every tyrant who does not believe in the Day of Account" (40:27).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[38:16]` "hasten Your writ upon us, before the Day of Account" and `[38:53]` "This is what you are promised for the Day of Account" — the name-cluster of the reckoning Days in this surah's neighborhood.
