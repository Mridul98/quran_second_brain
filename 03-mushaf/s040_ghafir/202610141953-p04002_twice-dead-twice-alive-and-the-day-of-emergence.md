# Twice dead, twice alive: the Day that strips concealment (40:10–20)
**UID:** 202610141953
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 40:10–20 (Ruku 2 of 9 in Ghafir)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[40:10]` – `[40:20]` (11 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The ruku stages the Day from inside it — the Fire's interrogation, a confession that names the exact crime (believing in associates, disbelieving at the call to Allah alone), the sovereignty question with its one answer, and a God who knows "the deceptions of the eyes" — so that concealment, the surah's coming specialty, is declared impossible before the Pharaoh story brings it to life.
* **Placement:** Ruku 2 of 9; the eschatological sorting after ruku 1's creed. Its 40:19 (Allah knows what hearts conceal) is the interpretive hinge for ruku 4's concealed believer and the anti-hinge for the nifaq dossier — same hidden-heart physics, opposite sign.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[40:10]`** "Those who disbelieved will be addressed, “The loathing of Allah is greater than your loathing of yourselves—for you were invited to the faith, but you refused.”"
  * **Gloss:** The interrogation opens with a ratio of loathing: their self-hatred in the Fire is exceeded by divine aversion; the charge is refusal of an invitation already delivered. "The loathing of Allah" is the corpus's sole such phrase (rg-verified).
* **`[40:11]`** "They will say, “Our Lord, you made us die twice, and twice you gave us life. Now we acknowledge our sins. Is there any way out?”"
  * **Gloss:** The confession and the futile exit-question; the "twice" formula restates in first person what `[2:28]` narrates in second ("you were dead and He gave you life, then He will put you to death, then He will bring you to life").
* **`[40:12]`** "That is because when Allah alone was called upon, you disbelieved; but when others were associated with Him, you believed. Judgment rests with Allah the Sublime, the Majestic."
  * **Gloss:** The crime is an asymmetry of response — tawhid rejected, shirk believed instantly; the narrator's verdict interrupts their own speech ("Judgment rests with Allah").
* **`[40:13]`** "It is He who shows you His wonders, and sends down sustenance from the sky for you. But none pays heed except the repentant."
  * **Gloss:** Back to the present-tense audience: wonders and revealed sustenance are already in circulation; heed is reserved for the *awwab* (repentant) — the same class 40:3 said repentance is accepted from.
* **`[40:14]`** "So call upon Allah, with sincere devotion to Him, even though the disbelievers resent it."
  * **Gloss:** The command of exclusive du'a; "with sincere devotion to Him" (ikhlas) is the ruku's positive mirror of the concealment theme — purity of the mouth-heart transaction, resented by the unbelievers.
* **`[40:15]`** "Exalted in rank, Owner of the Throne. He conveys the Spirit, by His command, upon whomever He wills of His servants, to warn of the Day of Encounter."
  * **Gloss:** Revelation's mechanics in one line: rank, throne, command, Spirit, chosen carriers, warning-function — the near-verbatim twin is `[16:2]` ("He sends down the angels with the Spirit by His command... 'Give warning that there is no god but Me'"). "Day of Encounter" and "Owner of the Throne" are each the corpus's only site of the phrase (rg-verified).
* **`[40:16]`** "The Day when they will emerge, nothing about them will be concealed from Allah. “To whom does the sovereignty belong today?” “To Allah, the One, the Irresistible.”"
  * **Gloss:** The ruku's center: emergence into total disclosure, then a question posed and answered on the Day. Ledger: this — not 40:44 — is the verse the task's "the dominion that Day is the Truth for Allah" was reaching for; corpus near-twins are `[25:26]` ("On that Day, true sovereignty will belong to the Merciful") and `[18:44]` ("authority belongs to Allah, the True"); "the One, the Irresistible" recurs verbatim in the tawhid reductio of `[13:16]`.
* **`[40:17]`** "On that Day, every soul will be recompensed for what it had earned. There will be no injustice on that Day. Allah is quick to settle accounts."
  * **Gloss:** Reckoning individual and exact; "quick to settle accounts" is verbatim the clause of `[13:41]`; the double "on that Day" chains verses 16–18 into one scene.
* **`[40:18]`** "And warn them of the Day of Imminence, when the hearts are at the throats, choking them. The evildoers will have no intimate friend, and no intercessor to be obeyed."
  * **Gloss:** The warning duty re-asserted mid-scene; "Day of Imminence" is corpus-unique (rg-verified); the two forfeits — khalil (intimate friend) and obeyed intercessor — echo `[6:51]` ("no protector or intercessor") and `[74:48]` ("the intercession of intercessors will not help them").
* **`[40:19]`** "He knows the deceptions of the eyes, and what the hearts conceal."
  * **Gloss:** The ruku's shortest and densest line: God's object is the micro-expression and the withheld thought; the phrase occurs nowhere else in the corpus (rg-verified). Read against `[3:167]` ("They say with their mouths what is not in their hearts; but Allah knows what they hide" — cited in the nifaq note), it is the same counter-witness Ghafir now places before the story of a man who hid *faith*.
* **`[40:20]`** "Allah judges with justice, while those whom they invoke besides Him cannot judge with anything. It is Allah who is the Hearing, the Seeing."
  * **Gloss:** The verdict-scene closes the opening one: real justice vs zero-judgment of the invoked; and the pair "the Hearing, the Seeing" (recurring with expanded capitals at `[40:56]`, cf. `[4:58]` "All-Hearing, All-Seeing") grounds 40:19's knowledge of concealment.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`die twice... twice gave us life`**: the double death/double life confession.
  * **Rendering:** "you made us die twice, and twice you gave us life" (40:11).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:28]` — the same sequence narrated by the second person ("you were dead and He gave you life, then He will put you to death, then He will bring you to life") — the Baqarah verse is the grammar this confession quotes back.
* **`the Day of Encounter`**: the meeting-day of the two worlds.
  * **Rendering:** corpus-unique phrase, functioning here as the warning-object of the Spirit's mission (40:15).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[29:5]` — "Whoever looks forward to the meeting with Allah—the appointed time of Allah is coming": the same rendezvous idea in the meeting-word; the Day's witnesses (40:51) and Calling Out (40:32) are the surah's sibling Day-names, each also unique.
* **`the Spirit, by His command`**: revelation's delivery mechanism.
  * **Rendering:** "He conveys the Spirit, by His command, upon whomever He wills of His servants, to warn" (40:15).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[16:2]` near-verbatim twin ("sends down the angels with the Spirit by His command, upon whom He wills of His servants: 'Give warning that there is no god but Me'"); the Spirit's hiddenness is answered at `[17:85]` ("The Spirit belongs to the domain of my Lord").
* **`the deceptions of the eyes`**: khā'inat al-a'yun — the eye's traitorous glances.
  * **Rendering:** "He knows the deceptions of the eyes, and what the hearts conceal" — the corpus's only occurrence (rg-verified).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[3:167]`, `[48:11]` (via the nifaq note's mouth/heart formula) — there concealment condemns; here it is merely *known*, and 40:14's "sincere devotion" is the exit.
