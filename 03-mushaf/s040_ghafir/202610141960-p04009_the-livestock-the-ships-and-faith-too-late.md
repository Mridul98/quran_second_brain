# Livestock, ships, denied signs; the faith that comes when might is witnessed (40:79–85)
**UID:** 202610141960
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 40:79–85 (Ruku 9 of 9 in Ghafir)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[40:79]` – `[40:85]` (7 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The last gift-audit: riding-animals and ships as needs-fulfillers and transport, signs shown until the question "which of Allah's signs will you deny?" becomes unanswerable — then the travel-court reconvenes on the stronger, richer dead, and the surah ends on its hardest law: belief issued at the sight of divine might is accepted from nobody, for it is Allah's way with His servants — "and there and then the disbelievers lost," the same clause that closed 40:78.
* **Placement:** Ruku 9 of 9, the surah's close: the creation-argument (79–81) resumes ruku 7's litany, the precedent-argument (82–83) resumes ruku 3's travel-court, and the terminal verdict (84–85) answers the secret believer's warning-cycle (40:30–33) by showing the warning's shape when ignored.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[40:79]`** "Allah is He who created the domestic animals for you—some for you to ride, and some you eat."
  * **Gloss:** The anʿām gift opens the finale: the same inventory as `[16:5]` ("the livestock—He created them for you. In them are warmth and benefits for you, and of them you eat") — here split ride/eat.
* **`[40:80]`** "And in them you have other benefits as well, and through them you satisfy your needs. And on them, and on the ships, you are transported."
  * **Gloss:** Utility beyond riding and eating — needs satisfied, and the transport clause "on them, and on the ships, you are transported" is **verbatim** `[23:22]` in al-Mu'minun, the surah whose title ("The Believers") rhymes with Ghafir's believer; the sea is admitted to the gift-list beside the beast (cf. `[43:12]` "provided you with ships, and animals on which you ride").
* **`[40:81]`** "And He shows you His signs. So which of Allah’s signs will you deny?"
  * **Gloss:** The showing-recapitulation (40:13's "shows you His wonders" upgraded to āyāt) and the rhetorical knockout: after a litany, denial has to pick a target and cannot.
* **`[40:82]`** "Have they not journeyed through the land, and seen the outcome for those before them? They were more numerous than they, and had greater power and influence in the land. But what they had achieved availed them nothing."
  * **Gloss:** The travel-summons returns in its second surah-shape (40:21 travelled → here journeyed, with "influence" added to strength and numbers), and the ledger clause "availed them nothing" is the Quran's standard write-off (`[11:101]` "their gods… availed them nothing when the command of your Lord arrived"; `[66:10]` the betrayers "availed them nothing against Allah").
* **`[40:83]`** "When their messengers came to them with clear proofs, they rejoiced in the knowledge they had, and the very things they used to ridicule besieged them."
  * **Gloss:** The infection inside the gift-audit: the proof arrives and the self-possessed "knowledge" rejoices instead — the same verb as 40:75's false earthly joy; the siege-verb ("besieged") is 40:45's torment-verb re-dated: the ridicule they carried now besieges them.
* **`[40:84]`** "Then, when they witnessed Our might, they said, “We believe in Allah alone, and we reject what we used to associate with Him.”"
  * **Gloss:** The deathbed creed — perfectly worded (tawhid formula plus explicit rejection of associates) and perfectly worthless: right words at the wrong time; the scene's twin is Pharaoh's own profession at `[10:90]` ("I believe that there is no god except the One the Children of Israel believe in…") met by "Now? When you have rebelled before" (`[10:91]`).
* **`[40:85]`** "But their faith could not help them once they witnessed Our might. This has been Allah’s way of dealing with His servants. And there and then the disbelievers lost."
  * **Gloss:** The final law-verse: faith-at-sight-of-might is a null class, "Allah's way of dealing with His servants" (Itani's rendering of the sunnat-Allah formula — the same word he renders "precedent… system" at `[33:62]` and "pattern" at `[48:23]` — observable term drift); the loss-clause "and there and then the disbelievers lost" repeats 40:78's phrase for the vanity-seekers, closing the surah on the verdict its opening ruku's seizure-history (40:5–6) announced.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`domestic animals`** (anʿām): the riding-and-eating gift.
  * **Rendering:** 40:79–80; Itani's choice "domestic animals" here, "livestock" at the twin sites.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[16:5]` (livestock: warmth, benefits, eating), `[39:6]` (az-Zumar: "brought down livestock for you—eight kinds in pairs"), `[43:12]` (ships *and* animals as the ride-pair).
* **`you are transported`** (on them and on the ships): load-bearing grace.
  * **Rendering:** "And on them, and on the ships, you are transported." (40:80).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[23:22]` — verbatim identical line in al-Mu'minun (folder documented; the surah-twin named for "the believers"); the sea-ship as divine favor recurs at `[31:31]` ("signs for every persevering, thankful person" — the shukr couplet Saba's `[34:19]` also carries).
* **`availed them nothing`**: the write-off clause.
  * **Rendering:** "what they had achieved availed them nothing" (40:82); "their faith could not help them" (40:85) — the same verdict in two words.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[11:101]`, `[66:10]` — gods and betrayal alike written off against the command; the internal twin is 40:50's vain prayers.
* **`Allah’s way of dealing with His servants`**: sunnat Allāh.
  * **Rendering:** 40:85 "This has been Allah’s way of dealing with His servants" — one translation's three English faces for the same formula.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[33:62]` "Such has been Allah's precedent… You will find no change in Allah's system"; `[48:23]` "It is Allah's pattern, ongoing since the past… no change in Allah's pattern" — translator's-term observation, Source: observable in the corpus renderings.

(40:29) — the fall is now the only evidence left; 40:85's "and there and then… lost" repeats 40:78; 40:81's sign-question resumes 40:13's wonder-showing.
* **Closing resonance:** the last word "lost" settles 40:37 ("Pharaoh's guile was only in defeat") and 40:5–6's first seizure-report; the surah that opened with "To Him is the ultimate return" ends with the return's verdict ledger.
