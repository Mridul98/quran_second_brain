# Night for rest, day for sight; the Living One who says "Be," and it comes to be (40:61–68)
**UID:** 202610141958
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 40:61–68 (Ruku 7 of 9 in Ghafir)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[40:61]` – `[40:68]` (8 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The gift-litany: night, day, earth, sky, human design, good provision — each struck with the refrain "but most people do not give thanks" — escalating into the shahadah, the prophet's prohibition-claim, the lifespan syllabus from dust to elderhood, and the creative word that needs no rehearsal: "Be," and it comes to be.
* **Placement:** Ruku 7 of 9; the middle panel of the creedal crescendo (51–78), the surah's most concentrated tawhid-from-creation argument, and the shukr passage the gratitude-audit note needs (canonical note currently cites no s40 verse — flagged at the index).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[40:61]`** "It is Allah Who made the night for you, that you may rest therein; and the day allowing sight. Allah is gracious towards the people, but most people do not give thanks."
  * **Gloss:** The task's "We made the night for rest and the day for sight" — ✓ verified verbatim in shape at 40:61, and its near-twin is `[27:86]` "We made the night for them to rest therein, and the day for visibility"; the divine graciousness is stated as fact while the thanks is the audit that fails ("most people do not give thanks" — twins `[10:60]`, `[12:38]`).
* **`[40:62]`** "Such is Allah, your Lord, Creator of all things. There is no god except Him; so how could you turn away?"
  * **Gloss:** Creator-of-all-themes → the one-god clause → and the question of orientation: worship of the Creator alone; "Creator of all things" is the same formula as `[6:102]`, `[13:16]`, `[39:62]`.
* **`[40:63]`** "Thus are turned away those who dispute the signs of Allah."
  * **Gloss:** One clause answering the previous question with the same verb: those who argue away from the signs *are turned away* — the self-inflicted drift, echoing the arguers' trilogy (4, 35, 56) and 40:37's "averted from the path."
* **`[40:64]`** "It is Allah who made the earth a habitat for you, and the sky a structure. And He designed you, and designed you well; and He provided you with the good things. Such is Allah, your Lord; so Blessed is Allah, Lord of the Worlds."
  * **Gloss:** Four gifts in one breath — habitat, canopy, well-shaped design, good provision — capped by "Blessed is Allah, Lord of the Worlds" — the surah's only "Blessed is Allah" (rg-verified; cf. the register of `[67:1]` "Blessed is He in whose hand is the sovereignty").
* **`[40:65]`** "He is the Living One. There is no god except He. So pray to Him, devoting your religion to Him. Praise be to Allah, the Lord of the Worlds."
  * **Gloss:** The Living-One name (corpus-unique phrase, rg-verified) grounds the du'a command and its ikhlas-condition ("devoting your religion to Him" — the exact demand of `[40:14]`), and the line ends on a Fatihah-recital: "Praise be to Allah, the Lord of the Worlds" (this edition's 1:2 reads "Praise be to Allah, Lord of the Worlds." — observable article drift).
* **`[40:66]`** "Say, “I was prohibited from worshiping those you invoke besides Allah, now that clear revelations have come to me from my Lord; and I was commanded to submit to the Lord of the Worlds.”"
  * **Gloss:** The Prophet's own kalam-anchored warrant: prohibition arrived "now that clear revelations have come" — revelation as the event that changes what is possible for a human will — and the counter-command is submission (islam) to the Lord of the Worlds, twice repeated from 64–65.
* **`[40:67]`** "It is He who created you from dust, then from a seed, then from an embryo, then He brings you out as an infant, then He lets you reach your maturity, then you become elderly—although some of you die sooner—so that you may reach a predetermined age, so that you may understand."
  * **Gloss:** The lifespan as syllabus: dust → seedling → embryo → infant → maturity → elderhood, with early death folded into the plan; the purpose clauses ("so that you may reach… so that you may understand") make biology protologic — cf. `[39:6]` "He creates you in the wombs of your mothers, in successive formations, in a triple darkness" and `[53:32]` "you were embryos in your mothers' wombs."
* **`[40:68]`** "It is He who gives life and death; and when He decides on a thing, He just says to it, “Be,” and it comes to be."
  * **Gloss:** Life-and-death held in one sender, and the creative decree without deliberation — the verbatim twin is `[36:82]` ("to say to it, 'Be,' and it comes to be"); the shorter form `[3:47]` "He only says to it, 'Be,' and it is."

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`night… to rest therein / day allowing sight`**: the two-service cosmology.
  * **Rendering:** "the night for you, that you may rest therein; and the day allowing sight" (40:61); "the day for visibility" at the twin site.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[27:86]` — near-verbatim, there as "signs for people who believe"; the night/day couple is Itani's stable creation-signature, and here it answers the du'a-demand of 40:60: the gifted hours are for resting, seeing — and calling.
* **`Creator of all things`**: khāliq al-shay'.
  * **Rendering:** 40:62 — "Such is Allah, your Lord, Creator of all things."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[6:102]` "the Creator of all things; so worship Him", `[13:16]` "Allah is the Creator of all things, and He is The One, the Irresistible", `[39:62]` (previous surah's opening of the same argument) — the formula is always a shirk-refutation engine.
* **`the Living One`**: al-Ḥayy.
  * **Rendering:** "He is the Living One. There is no god except He" (40:65) — corpus-unique phrasing (rg-verified), grounding prayer to the One who cannot be absent or dead like the invoked.
  * **Elsewhere:** within s040 the counter-image is the dead idols who "have abandoned us" (`[40:74]`) and "cannot judge with anything" (`[40:20]`); the life-and-death command is restated at 40:68.
* **`Be, and it comes to be`**: kun.
  * **Rendering:** "He just says to it, 'Be,' and it comes to be" (40:68).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[36:82]` verbatim twin; `[3:47]` short form — the creative word that makes 40:67's long stages the opposite of labor for God.
