# Help is promised; "Pray to Me, and I will respond to you" (40:51–60)
**UID:** 202610141957
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 40:51–60 (Ruku 6 of 9 in Ghafir)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[40:51]` – `[40:60]` (10 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** After the Pharaoh dossier closes with its verdicts, the address shifts to the Prophet and the patient community: support is guaranteed here and at the witnesses' Day, Moses's guidance-inheritance is named, the patience formula lands for the first time — and the ruku's pivot is the surah's own du'a-verse, "Pray to Me, and I will respond to you," the answered-prayer promise that 40:50's "the prayers of the disbelievers… in vain" had just defined by contrast.
* **Placement:** Ruku 6 of 9; the turning from narrative to creedal crescendo (51–60), bracketed by the two argue-without-authority portraits (4 and 35 before; 56 here) and by the second patience-formula at 40:77.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[40:51]`** "Most surely We will support Our messengers and those who believe, in this life, and on the Day the witnesses arise."
  * **Gloss:** The sworn support-promise on two time-scales; "the Day the witnesses arise" is the corpus's only occurrence of the phrase (rg-verified) — the Day when witnesses (prophets, angels, records) stand and the trial closes.
* **`[40:52]`** "The Day when their excuses will not profit the wrongdoers, and the curse will be upon them, and they will have the Home of Misery."
  * **Gloss:** The excuse-economy bankrupt; "Home of Misery" is likewise corpus-unique (rg-verified), the destination-half of the Day.
* **`[40:53]`** "We gave Moses guidance, and made the Children of Israel inherit the Scripture."
  * **Gloss:** The dossier now read forwards as gift: what the clan rejected was "guidance" (hudan) plus an inherited Book — the kalam/election grammar the surah has been demonstrating.
* **`[40:54]`** "A guide and a reminder for those endowed with reason."
  * **Gloss:** The Scripture's double function (hadh-dhakir for the intellects), with the audience filter the surah keeps applying.
* **`[40:55]`** "So be patient. The promise of Allah is true. And ask forgiveness for your sin, and proclaim the praise of your Lord evening and morning."
  * **Gloss:** The first patience-formula (its verbatim twin `[30:60]`; the second occurrence at `[40:77]` will mark the other end of this section): the Prophet is addressed with sabr, istighfar, and the praise-cycle "evening and morning" — the same clock on which Pharaoh's clan is exposed at 40:46, reversed into worship.
* **`[40:56]`** "Those who dispute regarding Allah’s revelations without any authority having come to them—there is nothing in their hearts but the feeling of greatness, which they will never attain. So seek refuge in Allah; for He is the All- Hearing, the All-Seeing."
  * **Gloss:** The third argument-portrait: their heart contains nothing but kibr — **ledger:** the task's "their anger is only in their souls" is not this edition's wording ("anger" occurs nowhere in s040, rg-verified; Itani renders the greatness/feeling-of-grandeur); and the clause "which they will never attain" says the aggrandizement never lands. "the All- Hearing, the All-Seeing" [sic spacing] is the same divine pair as `[40:20]` and `[4:58]`.
* **`[40:57]`** "Certainly the creation of the heavens and the earth is greater than the creation of humanity, but most people do not know."
  * **Gloss:** The argument from scale: the heaven-and-earth build outweighs human make — and the "but most people do not know" refrain is this surah's epistemic tally-keeper.
* **`[40:58]`** "Not equal are the blind and the seeing. Nor are those who believe and work righteousness equal to the sinners. How little you reflect."
  * **Gloss:** Two inequality-pairs: the sense-parable (`[35:19]` "Not equal are the blind and the seeing" verbatim; cf. `[6:50]`, `[13:16]`) and the moral pair, with reflection as the organ that would see it.
* **`[40:59]`** "Indeed, the Hour is coming; there is no doubt about it; but most people do not believe."
  * **Gloss:** The Hour-certainty clause whose refrain "but most people do not believe" has a near-twin at `[13:1]` ("…but most people do not believe"), while Saba's refrain is the knowledge-version (`[34:28]` "but most people do not know" — the pairing is already logged in the s034 index); doubt-denial plus belief-shortfall in one line.
* **`[40:60]`** "Your Lord has said, “Pray to Me, and I will respond to you. But those who are too proud to worship Me will enter Hell forcibly.”"
  * **Gloss:** The du'a-verse the task asked to verify — ✓ verbatim in the corpus, and kalam-anchored by its direct speech-frame "Your Lord has said." **Ledger:** the hemistich "He hears your supplication" is **not in this edition** at 40:60 (zero "supplic" hits in s040, rg-verified); the hearing-names that make the promise plausible stand one verse before and after: `[40:20]`, `[40:56]`. The "too proud" clause ties the punished class to 40:56's "feeling of greatness": pride is the one posture du'a cannot wear.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`the Day the witnesses arise`**: the testimony-Day.
  * **Rendering:** 40:51's support-promise horizon; corpus-unique (rg-verified).
  * **Elsewhere:** surah-coined kin: "the Day of Calling Out" (40:32), "the Day of Imminence" (40:18), "the Day of Encounter" (40:15) — Ghafir's Day-name cluster answers each other; `[34:40]` stages the angels' cross-examination of the worshipped (cf. Saba's ruku 5 note).
* **`be patient… the promise of Allah is true`**: the sabr-formula.
  * **Rendering:** 40:55 (plus istighfar and praise-cycle attached); 40:77 repeats the first two clauses verbatim.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[30:60]` "So be patient. The promise of Allah is true" verbatim; the formula also stands at `[46:17]` in a parent-son scene and `[35:5]` as a warning against "the Tempter."
* **`Pray to Me, and I will respond to you`**: the promise-verse.
  * **Rendering:** 40:60, introduced by "Your Lord has said" — direct speech without mediation.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:186]` — the first-person twin: "I Am near; I answer the call of the caller when he calls on Me"; the pairing is the Quran's whole doctrine of du'a in two verses.
* **`feeling of greatness`**: kibr as the heart's only contents.
  * **Rendering:** "there is nothing in their hearts but the feeling of greatness, which they will never attain" (40:56) — Itani's expansion of the kibr-word, and *not* "anger" (ledger).
  * **Elsewhere:** the proud/arrogant class recurs at `[40:76]` ("What a terrible dwelling for the arrogant") and `[40:47–48]` (the arrogant in the Fire); "those who are too proud to worship Me" (40:60) closes the circle.
