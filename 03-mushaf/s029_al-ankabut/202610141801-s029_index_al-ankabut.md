* **Size:** 69 ayahs, 7 ruku (`data/ruku_map.json`: 13+9+8+14+7+12+6 = 69 ✓). Mushaf position 29, between al-Qasas (28) and ar-Rum (30).
* **Name:** al-Ankabut — "The Spider"; the corpus header is THE SPIDER. The title-word occurs exactly once, at `[29:41]`, inside the parable of the frailest of houses.
* **Central theme:** The testing surah. It opens with the question — "Have the people supposed that they will be left alone to say, 'We believe,' without being put to the test?" (29:2) — and closes with the promise to the same verb's people: "As for those who strive for Us—We will guide them in Our ways" (29:69). Between these brackets: prophetic case studies under pressure (Noah, Abraham in the fire, Lot, Shuaib) and a world-historical roster of the ruined (Aad, Thamood, Quaroon, Pharaoh, Hamaan); the spider-house critique of taking weak patrons besides Allah (29:41); the charter of the reciting community (prayer, debate etiquette, the Book-as-sufficient-miracle, 29:45–51); and an existential re-sorting (vast earth, taste of death, carried provision, sea-confession, the Sanctuary) that terminates in "the Home of the Hereafter is the Life" (29:64).
* **Theme checks the task asked to verify honestly (against the corpus):** The testing question `[29:2–3]` ✓ verbatim ("without being put to the test?... We have tested those before them"), and the sabr link is text-supported: testing sorts truthful from liars (29:3), the persecutable-believer and hypocrite are sorted the same way (29:10–11), and the winners are "Those who endure patiently, and in their Lord they trust" (29:59). Abraham's intercession/withdrawal re Sodom "29:22–27": **correction** — in s029 the destruction scene is `[29:31–32]` (ruku 4): the envoys announce, Abraham asks "Yet Lot is in it," and is told "We are well aware of who is in it"; there is no pleading-and-withdrawal here — that scene is `[11:74]` "he started pleading with Us concerning the people of Lot" (Hud). 29:22–23 is the no-escape/No-mercy declaration; 29:24–27 is fire-salvation, the Day-of-disowning sermon, Lot's belief, and the progeny-grant. Lot's plea "29:26": **correction** — `[29:26]` is the emigration declaration ("I am emigrating to my Lord. He is the Noble, the Wise"), and this edition's syntax attaches it to Lot while classical reports read the speaker as Abraham (agent inference — verify); Lot's *plea* is `[29:30]` "My Lord, help me against the people of corruption." The spider parable `[29:41]` ✓ verbatim — "the most fragile of houses is the spider's house" — and yes, a genuine tawhid-note case: the partners are *protectors* (patrons), indicted as structurally weak, reinforced by 29:22 "no protector and no savior" and 29:25 "no saviors." `[29:6]` ✓ verbatim "Whoever strives, strives only for himself" (this edition: "himself"; "for his own soul" is another translation's phrasing). `[29:46]` ✓ verbatim: "do not argue with the People of the Scripture except in the best manner possible, except those who do wrong among them... our God and your God is One; and to Him we are submissive." `[29:69]` ✓ verbatim, guidance-link confirmed (twin `[14:12]` "guided us in our ways").

## 3. Structural Arc
* **The test and its three claimants (1–13):** "We believe" (2), the harmed believer who trades belief for safety — the hypocrite (10–11), and the recruiters who promise to carry other people's sins (12–13). Testing is stated as divine *knowing* (3, 11) — the courtroom already assembled inside the community.
* **Prophets under trial (14–30):** Noah's century-minus-fifty and the ark as "a sign for all peoples" (14–15); Abraham's livelihood-argument against fabricated gods (16–21), the fire and its salvation (24), the Day when patron-affection flips into disowning and cursing (25), Lot's exit and plea (26, 30).
* **The roster and the parable (31–44):** the envoys, Sodom, the trace left behind (31–35); Shuaib, Aad, Thamood, Quaroon, Pharaoh, Hamaan — four seizures, one verdict: "It was not Allah who wronged them" (36–40); then the surah's title: patrons besides Allah are spider-houses (41), and the examples are grasped only by the learned (43–44).
* **The community's charter (45–51):** recite and pray — "The prayer prevents indecencies and evils" (45); debate etiquette with the People of the Scripture anchored in "our God and your God is One" (46); the unlettered Prophet's Scripture as the settled miracle (47–51).
* **Re-sorting the survivors (52–63):** witness, deferred and sudden punishment (52–55); "My earth is vast, so worship Me alone" against persecution-paralysis (56); death tasted, gardens for the patient (57–59); provision carried by Allah, not by the creature (60–62); the sky-water poll (63).
* **The seal (64–69):** the world as diversion and play (64), the ship that makes them monotheists and the shore that gives them partners (65–66), the Sanctuary security squandered (67), the lie-for-truth trade (68) — and the closing promise that answers the opening question: strivers are guided in His ways (69).
