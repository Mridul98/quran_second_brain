# Belief claimed is belief tested (29:1–13)
**UID:** 202610141802
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 29:1–13 (Ruku 1 of 7 in al-Ankabut)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[29:1]` – `[29:13]` (13 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah's opening question — claimed belief does not exempt anyone from being put to the test — sorts three claimants: the believer who looks forward to meeting Allah (5–7), the harmed man who trades "we believe" for safety and calls persecution Allah's retribution (10–11), and the recruiters who offer to carry other people's sins (12–13); testing is stated as divine *knowing*, and Allah already knows which is which (3, 11).
* **Placement:** Ruku 1 of 7; sets the testing frame the whole surah runs on — prophets under trial (ruku 2–4), the community's charter under debate (ruku 5), and the closing promise to strivers (29:69).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[29:1]`** "Alif, Lam, Meem."
  * **Gloss:** Untranslated letter-cluster (see Fleeting note); the same cluster opens 2:1, 3:1, 30:1, 31:1, 32:1 (rg-verified) — here it fronts a surah about letters becoming law.
* **`[29:2]`** "Have the people supposed that they will be left alone to say, “We believe,” without being put to the test?"
  * **Gloss:** The thesis-question; "left alone" is the same idiom as `[9:16]` "Or do you think that you will be left alone, without Allah identifying which of you will strive" — claim is not credential.
* **`[29:3]`** "We have tested those before them. Allah will surely know the truthful, and He will surely know the liars."
  * **Gloss:** Testing as precedent (the prophets of ruku 2–4 will be the exhibit) and as disclosure: God's "knowing" here is made visible to the knowers — Source: agent inference — verify; cf. `[3:167]` "And that He may know the hypocrites."
* **`[29:4]`** "Or do those who commit sins think they can fool Us? Terrible is their opinion!"
  * **Gloss:** The second "suppose" verb: sin assumes it can outrun surveillance; "fool Us" pairs with `[2:9]` "They seek to deceive Allah and those who believe, but they deceive none but themselves" (rendering drift: "fool"/"deceive").
* **`[29:5]`** "Whoever looks forward to the meeting with Allah—the appointed time of Allah is coming. He is the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing."
  * **Gloss:** The first honest claimant: expectation of the meeting (liqa'); the encounter-word family — `[6:31]` "deny the encounter with Allah", `[10:45]` "denied the meeting with Allah".
* **`[29:6]`** "Whoever strives, strives only for himself. Allah is Independent of the beings."
  * **Gloss:** Striving (juhd) is self-profit, not divine need: twin of `[53:39]` "the human being attains only what he strives for"; "Independent" (self-sufficiency) is this corpus's word for God's needlessness — cf. `[39:7]`, `[27:40]`, and the as-Samad rendering at `[57:24]`.
* **`[29:7]`** "Those who believe and do righteous deeds—We will remit their sins, and We will reward them according to the best of what they used to do."
  * **Gloss:** The payoff couplet — remit + best-rate reward; verbatim tail of `[39:35]`, same formula at `[16:97]`.
* **`[29:8]`** "We have advised the human being to be good to his parents. But if they urge you to associate with Me something you have no knowledge of, do not obey them. To Me is your return; and I will inform you of what you used to do."
  * **Gloss:** The first live test-case: parents who press shirk; kinship piety does not license idolatry — verbatim-logic twin `[31:15]`; the return-and-inform clause matches `[39:7]`.
* **`[29:9]`** "Those who believe and do good works—We will admit them into the company of the righteous."
  * **Gloss:** Belief-with-works enters the righteous' company — the "company" idiom twin `[3:193]` "make us die in the company of the virtuous."
* **`[29:10]`** "Among the people is he who says, “We have believed in Allah.” Yet when he is harmed on Allah’s account, he equates the people's persecution with Allah’s retribution. And if help comes from your Lord, he says, “We were actually with you.” Is not Allah aware of what is inside the hearts of the people?"
  * **Gloss:** The inside trader: equivalence-error (man's persecution = God's retribution) plus bandwagon grammar "We were actually with you" — the nifaq portrait in miniature; hearts-awareness twin `[3:167]` "Allah knows what they hide."
* **`[29:11]`** "Allah certainly knows those who believe, and He certainly knows the hypocrites."
  * **Gloss:** The testing of 29:3 answered in two named classes — the liars of verse 3 are here the hypocrites: the lie is inside the community.
* **`[29:12]`** "Those who disbelieve say to those who believe, “Follow our way, and we will carry your sins.” In no way can they carry any of their sins. They are liars."
  * **Gloss:** Recruitment by liability-transfer — refuted by the burden-law of `[39:7]` "No bearer of burden can bear the burden of another."
* **`[29:13]`** "They will carry their own loads, and other loads with their own. And they will be questioned on the Day of Resurrection concerning what they used to fabricate."
  * **Gloss:** The verdict: double cargo — own sins plus the sins of those they led — "fabricate" threading toward 29:17 and 29:68's lie-attribution.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`put to the test` (bala')** `[29:2–3]`:
  * **Rendering:** "without being put to the test?... We have tested those before them."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:155]` "We will certainly test you with some fear and hunger"; `[47:31]` "We will certainly test you, until We know those among you who strive"; `[3:186]` "You will be tested through your possessions and your persons" — the same sieve-word, always future-tense for this community.
* **`strives` (jahada)** `[29:6]`:
  * **Rendering:** "Whoever strives, strives only for himself."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[22:78]` "strive for Allah, with the striving due to Him"; `[49:15]` "strive for Allah's cause with their wealth and their persons"; `[29:69]` "those who strive for Us" — the surah opens and closes on this verb.
* **`hypocrites` (munafiqun)** `[29:11]`:
  * **Rendering:** paired with "those who believe" as the two known classes.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[3:167]` "And that He may know the hypocrites"; `[9:64]` the hypocrites' fear of a chapter exposing their hearts — 29:10–11 is the same anatomy from the inside.
* **`loads` (athqal)** `[29:13]`:
  * **Rendering:** "They will carry their own loads, and other loads with their own."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[6:31]` "they will carry their burdens on their backs—evil is what they carry" — the cargo metaphor for accrued responsibility.
