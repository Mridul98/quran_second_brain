# Allah suffices as witness: the vast earth and the carried provision (29:52–63)
**UID:** 202610141807
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 29:52–63 (Ruku 6 of 7 in al-Ankabut)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[29:52]` – `[29:63]` (12 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Against the demand to hurry the punishment, the ruku installs the witness who already settles the case, the appointed time that will arrive suddenly, and then turns to "O My servants who have believed" with three liberations: the earth is vast (no persecution-pocket can be your whole map), death is universal and patience paid in full, and provision is carried by Allah — the same Allah the polls of 61/63 make them name before they partner.
* **Placement:** Ruku 6 of 7; the survival-charter after the charter of worship (ruku 5): jurisdiction, respite, emigration, death, provision — handing the surah to its world-vs-home verdict in ruku 7.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[29:52]`** "Say, “Allah suffices as witness between you and me. He knows everything in the heavens and the Earth. Those who believe in vanity and reject Allah—it is they who are the losers.”"
  * **Gloss:** The witness-formula cluster — `[17:96]` "Allah is enough witness between you and me", `[46:8]` "He is sufficient witness between me and you", `[13:43]`; vanity-belief is the loser-definition before any punishment arrives.
* **`[29:53]`** "And they urge you to hasten the punishment. Were it not for a specified time, the punishment would have come to them. But it will come upon them suddenly, while they are unaware."
  * **Gloss:** Respite doctrine: the hour has a term, the rushing is refused; "suddenly, while they are unaware" is the corpus's own warning-refrain (`[43:66]`, `[12:107]`, `[26:202]`).
* **`[29:54]`** "They urge you to hasten the punishment. But Hell will engulf the disbelievers."
  * **Gloss:** The demand repeated — and answered once: "Hell will engulf the disbelievers" verbatim-identical to `[9:49]`; the engulfing verb also at `[16:34]`, `[20:78]`.
* **`[29:55]`** "On the Day when the punishment will envelop them, from above them, and from beneath their feet, He will say, “Taste what you used to do!”"
  * **Gloss:** Envelopment from both directions — twin image of `[39:16]` "layers of Fire above them, and layers beneath them"; the command "Taste" is the doing made consumable.
* **`[29:56]`** "O My servants who have believed: My earth is vast, so worship Me alone."
  * **Gloss:** The address-formula family (`[39:16]`, `[39:53]`, `[43:68]`); the emigration clause — "My earth is vast" recurs at `[39:10]`, and the persecution-excuse is ruled out at `[4:97]` "Was Allah's earth not vast enough for you to emigrate in it?" — tawhid as geography: no corner is outside His jurisdiction.
* **`[29:57]`** "Every soul will taste death. Then to Us you will be returned."
  * **Gloss:** The universal clause — verbatim twin `[21:35]` "Every soul will taste death" (and there, with the testing-frame: "a test"), near-twin `[3:185]` "Every soul will have a taste of death"; return-to-Us seals it.
* **`[29:58]`** "Those who believe and work righteousness—We will settle them in Paradise, in mansions under which rivers flow, dwelling therein forever. Excellent is the compensation for the workers."
  * **Gloss:** The settlement promise: mansions-with-rivers family (`[61:12]` "beautiful mansions", `[39:20]` "mansions upon mansions"); "Excellent is the compensation for the workers" — reward tied to labor.
* **`[29:59]`** "Those who endure patiently, and in their Lord they trust."
  * **Gloss:** The two qualifiers of the settlers: sabr and tawakkul — near-verbatim twin `[16:42]` "Those who endure patiently, and in their Lord they put their trust"; the surah's opening test (29:2) gets its passing grade here.
* **`[29:60]`** "How many a creature there is that does not carry its provision? Allah provides for them, and for you. He is the Hearer, the Knowledgeable."
  * **Gloss:** The provision-argument from animal economics — near-twin `[11:6]` "There is no moving creature on earth but its sustenance depends on Allah"; livelihood-anxiety (the very theme of 29:17) dissolved; Hearer-Knower closes (answers prayer and knows hearts).
* **`[29:61]`** "And if you asked them, “Who created the heavens and the earth and regulated the sun and the moon?” They would say, “Allah.” Why then do they deviate?"
  * **Gloss:** Poll one: the regulate-verb (`[13:2]`, `[39:5]`, `[35:13]`) — the answer is Allah; the question is only "why deviate?"
* **`[29:62]`** "Allah expands the provision for whomever He wills of His servants, and restricts it. Allah is Cognizant of all things."
  * **Gloss:** The expansion-restriction clause of `[30:37]` (near-verbatim) and `[17:30]`; provision-distribution is will-plus-knowledge, not favor-plus-justice — the Quaroon inference (`[28:78]` "I was given all this on account of knowledge I possess") cut at the root.
* **`[29:63]`** "And if you asked them, “Who sends water down from the sky, with which He revives the earth after it had died?” They would say, “Allah.” Say, “Praise be to Allah.” But most of them do not understand."
  * **Gloss:** Poll two: the revive-after-death argument (`[2:164]`, `[16:65]`, `[30:24]`); the scripted response is praise — the shukr reflex that 29:17 commanded and `[31:25]` also scripts; "most of them do not understand."

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`witness`** `[29:52]`:
  * **Rendering:** "Allah suffices as witness between you and me."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[46:8]`, `[17:96]`, `[13:43]`, `[10:29]` — the formula's function is always to end a stalled argument with an omniscient adjudicator; here it answers the demand for a faster verdict (29:53).
* **`earth is vast`** `[29:56]`:
  * **Rendering:** "My earth is vast, so worship Me alone."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[39:10]` "And Allah's earth is vast" (paired there with "the steadfast will be paid their wages in full"); `[4:97]` the interrogation of those who pleaded persecution — the verse-pair of the emigration-law.
* **`endure patiently... trust`** `[29:59]`:
  * **Rendering:** "Those who endure patiently, and in their Lord they trust."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[16:42]` near-verbatim; the same couplet closes the reward-clause at 29:58 — patience as the qualification, trust as the mode.
* **`provision` (rizq)** `[29:60–62]`:
  * **Rendering:** "does not carry its provision... Allah expands the provision."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[11:6]` sustenance-dependency twin; `[30:37]` expands/restricts near-verbatim; 29:17 already routed livelihood to Allah alone — one argument, two registers (idols can't provide; anxiety needn't).
