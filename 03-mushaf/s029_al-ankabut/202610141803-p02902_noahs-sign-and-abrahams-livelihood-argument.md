# Noah's sign and Abraham's livelihood argument (29:14–22)
**UID:** 202610141803
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 29:14–22 (Ruku 2 of 7 in al-Ankabut)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[29:14]` – `[29:22]` (9 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The testing frame gets its first exhibits: Noah survives the Deluge and his ark becomes "a sign for all peoples"; Abraham preaches the counter-economics of worship — idols cannot provide livelihood, Allah originates and reproduces creation — and the ruku closes on the escape-argument: no fugitive leaves God's jurisdiction, and patronage besides Him does not exist.
* **Placement:** Ruku 2 of 7; the first of the surah's prophet-under-trial blocks (Noah → Abraham → Lot → Shuaib), running until the ruined roster of ruku 4.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[29:14]`** "We sent Noah to his people, and He stayed among them for a thousand years minus fifty years. Then the Deluge swept them; for they were wrongdoers."
  * **Gloss:** The career-long test: 950 years of preaching, then the sweep; "a thousand years minus fifty" is unique in the corpus (rg-verified) — patience measured in centuries against the surah's own short-claim about this world (29:64).
* **`[29:15]`** "But We saved him, together with the company of the Ark, and We made it a sign for all peoples."
  * **Gloss:** Salvation is communal ("the company of the Ark") and the event is converted into permanent evidence; the "sign for all peoples" universal-formula (cf. `[25:37]` "a lesson for mankind").
* **`[29:16]`** "And Abraham, when he said to his people, “Worship Allah, and fear Him. That is better for you, if you only knew."
  * **Gloss:** The standard sermon, two verbs — worship and reverence (taqwa-root): near-twin of `[71:3]` "Worship Allah and reverence Him"; "better for you" sets the surah's value-comparison register.
* **`[29:17]`** "You worship idols besides Allah, and you fabricate falsehoods. Those you worship, instead of Allah, cannot provide you with livelihood. So seek your livelihood from Allah, and worship Him, and thank Him. To Him you will be returned.”"
  * **Gloss:** The livelihood-argument: fabricated gods have no provisioning power — twin of `[16:73]` "what possesses no provisions for them in the heavens, nor on earth"; the three-step circuit (seek → worship → thank) terminates in return: the shukr program exactly.
* **`[29:18]`** "If you disbelieve, communities before you have also disbelieved. The Messenger is only responsible for clear transmission."
  * **Gloss:** Unbelief is not news; the messenger's office is narrowed to transmission — the load-shifting that 29:12's recruiters fantasize about in reverse.
* **`[29:19]`** "Have they not seen how Allah originates the creation, and then reproduces it? This is easy for Allah."
  * **Gloss:** The resurrection argument from observed origin; verbatim-family of `[30:11]` "Allah originates creation, and then repeats it" and `[27:64]`.
* **`[29:20]`** "Say, “Roam the earth, and observe how He originated the creation.” Then Allah will bring about the next existence. Allah has power over all things.”"
  * **Gloss:** Empirical pilgrimage: the tourists of ruin (cf. `[30:42]` "Roam the earth, and observe the fate of those who came before"; `[27:69]`) — observation licensed as worship.
* **`[29:21]`** "He punishes whom He wills, and He grants mercy to whom He wills, and to Him you will be restored."
  * **Gloss:** The will-and-restoration clause of `[5:18]`/`[3:129]` ("He forgives whom He wills, and He punishes whom He wills") reversed in order — sovereignty before comfort.
* **`[29:22]`** "You cannot escape, on earth or in the heaven; and you have no protector and no savior besides Allah."
  * **Gloss:** Jurisdiction closes the argument: no exit corridor in either direction, and the patron-word (wali) is denied to every rival — the spider-house indictment stated before the parable itself.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`livelihood` (rizq)** `[29:17]`:
  * **Rendering:** "cannot provide you with livelihood... seek your livelihood from Allah."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[27:64]` "who gives you livelihood from the sky and the earth?"; `[51:57]` "I need no livelihood from them" — provisioning is the measurable proof of who is god; 29:60 will run the same argument on animals.
* **`Roam the earth`** `[29:20]`:
  * **Rendering:** "Roam the earth, and observe how He originated the creation."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[30:42]` verbatim opener "Roam the earth, and observe the fate"; `[16:36]`/`[27:69]` "travel through the earth" — the Quran's ruin-tourism command, here pointed at origination rather than fate.
* **`originates... reproduces`** `[29:19]`:
  * **Rendering:** "originates the creation, and then reproduces it."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[30:11]` "originates creation, and then repeats it" (near-verbatim; rendering drift "reproduces"/"repeats"); `[10:4]` — the bad'-i'ada argument the whole Meccan corpus runs.
* **`protector and savior`** `[29:22]`:
  * **Rendering:** "no protector and no savior besides Allah."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[4:123]` "any protector or savior" verbatim-family; `[9:74]` "no protector and no savior" verbatim — the pair recurs only for fugitives from divine jurisdiction.
