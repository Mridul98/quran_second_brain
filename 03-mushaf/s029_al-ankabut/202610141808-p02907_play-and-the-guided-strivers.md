# The play-world, the sea-confession, and the guided strivers (29:64–69)
**UID:** 202610141808
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 29:64–69 (Ruku 7 of 7 in al-Ankabut)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[29:64]` – `[29:69]` (6 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The closing ruku re-rates the two homes — this world is "diversion and play," the Home of the Hereafter "is the Life" — and exposes the built-in monotheism the sea forces out of them: at storm they pray to Allah alone, on shore they attribute partners; the Sanctuary's safety is squandered on falsehood; the worst crime named is trading truth for lie — and the surah answers its own opening question one last time with the only verb that was ever asked of them: whoever strives for Us, We guide into Our ways.
* **Placement:** Ruku 7 of 7, the surah's seal: 29:2 asked whether "We believe" would be left untested; 29:69 promises guidance to tested strivers — the frame closes on the striving of 29:6.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[29:64]`** "The life of this world is nothing but diversion and play, and the Home of the Hereafter is the Life, if they only knew."
  * **Gloss:** The world-life verdict — the 6:32/57:20 family ("game and distraction"; "play, and distraction, and glitter") in its sharpest double: this world is play, and only the other Home *is* life; "if they only knew" the surah’s own refrain-family — 29:16 "if you only knew", 29:41 "If they only knew", 29:64 — three turns of the same lament (rg-verified).
* **`[29:65]`** "When they embark on a vessel, they pray to Allah, devoting their faith to Him; but once He has delivered them safely to land, they attribute partners to Him."
  * **Gloss:** The storm-confession experiment — verbatim-logic twin of `[31:32]` ("they call upon Allah, devoting their religion to Him. But when He has delivered them to dry land, some of them waver") and `[10:22]`; tawhid is involuntary at sea, optional on shore.
* **`[29:66]`** "To be ungrateful for what We have given them, and to enjoy themselves. They will surely come to know."
  * **Gloss:** The partners are first ingratitude — the delivered blessing denied (cf. `[16:83]` "They recognize Allah's blessing, but then deny it"); "They will surely come to know" defers the invoice, echoing 29:64's "if they only knew."
* **`[29:67]`** "Do they not see that We established a Secure Sanctuary, while all around them the people are being carried away? Do they believe in falsehood, and reject the blessings of Allah?"
  * **Gloss:** The Meccan empirical case (Source: classical reading — the Secure Sanctuary as the surah's own town, verify): safety amid a snatch-economy (twin of `[28:57]` "a Safe Sanctuary... snatched from our land"); the double-indictment: falsehood-belief plus blessing-rejection.
* **`[29:68]`** "And who does greater wrong than he who fabricates lies and attributes them to Allah, or calls the truth a lie when it has come to him? Is there not in Hell a dwelling for the blasphemers?"
  * **Gloss:** The summit of wrong has two faces — inventing about God (the 29:17/29:13 fabricators' final form; near-twin `[39:32]` "who lies about Allah, and denies the truth when it has come to him?") and refusing arrived truth; the rhetorical question closes the dwelling-claim.
* **`[29:69]`** "As for those who strive for Us—We will guide them in Our ways. Allah is with the doers of good."
  * **Gloss:** The seal-promise, verified as the task asked: striving-for-Us (`[22:78]` "strive for Allah, with the striving due to Him"; `[60:1]` "mobilized to strive for My cause") earns guidance into "Our ways" — the plural-roads answer to 1:6's petition and to 29:2's test; "Allah is with the doers of good" (`[16:128]` family: with the righteous and the virtuous) closes the surah on companionship, not distance.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`diversion and play` (la'ib)** `[29:64]`:
  * **Rendering:** "nothing but diversion and play... the Home of the Hereafter is the Life."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[6:32]` "game and distraction"; `[57:20]` "play, and distraction, and glitter"; `[21:16–17]` "We did not create the sky and the earth... for amusement" — the world's category is stated by negation of divine purpose.
* **`devoting their faith` (ikhlas)** `[29:65]`:
  * **Rendering:** "they pray to Allah, devoting their faith to Him."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[31:32]` near-verbatim; `[10:22]` "professing sincere devotion to Him" — the storm strips partnership away; shore re-decorates it (cf. `[7:190]` "they attribute partners to Him in what He has given them").
* **`Secure Sanctuary`** `[29:67]`:
  * **Rendering:** "We established a Secure Sanctuary."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[28:57]` "a Safe Sanctuary, to which are brought all kinds of fruits, as provision from Ourselves" — same institution, previous surah, same argument: safety is a blessing whose denial is ingratitude (29:66–67 run the shukr audit on a whole town).
* **`strive for Us` (jahada)** `[29:69]`:
  * **Rendering:** "As for those who strive for Us—We will guide them in Our ways."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[22:78]`, `[49:15]` "strive for Allah's cause"; `[53:39]` "attains only what he strives for" — the 29:6 verb now paid: the striver's self-profit is guidance.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Meccan (Source: classical lists — verify); 29:67's Secure-Sanctuary appeal is read classically as direct address to Quraysh's precinct-privilege (Source: classical-tafsir reading — verify).
