# Sodom, the ruined roster, and the spider's house (29:31–44)
**UID:** 202610141805
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 29:31–44 (Ruku 4 of 7 in al-Ankabut)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[29:31]` – `[29:44]` (14 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Lot's plea (29:30) is answered: the envoys' Sodom-announcement secures him by name, the town falls under a heaven-sent scourge, and a trace is left for the understanders; the surah then runs the full ruin-roster — Midian, Aad, Thamood, Quaroon, Pharaoh, Hamaan — the four seizures and one verdict ("It was not Allah who wronged them") — and distills the whole exhibit into the title-parable: protectors taken besides Allah are a spider's house, the frailest of structures.
* **Placement:** Ruku 4 of 7; the surah's midpoint and its name-source; the Sodom scene corrects the task's expectation — Abraham's negotiation here is 29:31–32 (no withdrawal scene in s029; that is `[11:74]`), and the roster answers the testing-question of 29:3 ("We have tested those before them").

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[29:31]`** "And when Our envoys brought Abraham the good news, they said, “We are going to destroy the people of this town; its people are wrongdoers.”"
  * **Gloss:** The glad-tidings scene (twin `[11:69]`) pivots mid-verse into a destruction notice — good news and scourge delivered by the same visitors.
* **`[29:32]`** "He said, “Yet Lot is in it.” They said, “We are well aware of who is in it. We will save him, and his family, except for his wife, who will remain behind.”"
  * **Gloss:** Abraham's one protective question — the s029 form of the intercession; the divine reply names the survivor-list, wife excepted (the exception recurs verbatim in 33).
* **`[29:33]`** "Then, when Our envoys came to Lot, they were mistreated, and he was troubled and distressed on their account. They said, “Do not fear, nor grieve. We will save you and your family, except for your wife, who will remain behind.”"
  * **Gloss:** The hospitality crisis (twin `[11:77]` "he was anxious for them... This is a dreadful day"); the "do not fear, nor grieve" formula is the same comfort given to Moses' mother (`[28:7]`), delivered here to the man who pleaded at 29:30.
* **`[29:34]`** "“We will bring down upon the people of this town a scourge from heaven, because of their wickedness.”"
  * **Gloss:** Execution by sky — "scourge from heaven" here; the other tellings specify the mechanism: the Blast at sunrise (`[15:73]`), stones of baked clay (`[11:82]`).
* **`[29:35]`** "And We left behind a clear trace of it, for people who understand."
  * **Gloss:** The ruin becomes readable evidence — twin-function of `[15:75–77]` "lessons for those who read signs... a sign for the believers."
* **`[29:36]`** "And to Median, their brother Shuaib. He said, “O my people, worship Allah and anticipate the Last Day, and do not spread corruption in the land.”"
  * **Gloss:** The roster resumes — verbatim opener of `[7:85]`; Shuaib's brief adds the Last-Day anticipand and the anti-corruption clause his market-context demands.
* **`[29:37]`** "But they rejected him, so the tremor overtook them, and they were left motionless in their homes."
  * **Gloss:** Midian's seizure (twin `[11:94]` "the Blast struck... motionless bodies in their homes") — death at home, upright nowhere.
* **`[29:38]`** "And Aad and Thamood. It has become clear to you from their dwellings. Satan embellished for them their deeds, barring them from the path, even though they could see."
  * **Gloss:** The archaeological address (twin `[14:45]` "you inhabited the homes of those who wronged themselves"; `[46:25]` "nothing to be seen except their dwellings"); the diagnosis: embellished deeds closing the path on people with open eyes.
* **`[29:39]`** "And Quaroon, and Pharaoh, and Hamaan—Moses went to them with clear arguments, but they acted arrogantly in the land. And they could not get ahead."
  * **Gloss:** The compressed villain-trio — the whole al-Qasas cast in one line (cf. `[40:24]` "To Pharaoh, Hamaan, and Quaroon"); "could not get ahead" = no escape-run.
* **`[29:40]`** "Each We seized by his sin. Against some We sent a sandstorm. Some were struck by the Blast. Some We caused the ground to cave in beneath them. And some We drowned. It was not Allah who wronged them, but it was they who wronged their own selves."
  * **Gloss:** The four-method digest: sandstorm (Aad — `[54:19]` "a screaming wind" rendering drift), Blast (Lot's town — `[15:73]`), caving-in (Quaroon — `[28:81]`), drowning (Pharaoh's host — `[26:66]`); the exoneration clause twin of `[11:101]` "We did not wrong them, but they wronged themselves."
* **`[29:41]`** "The likeness of those who take to themselves protectors other than Allah is that of the spider. It builds a house. But the most fragile of houses is the spider’s house. If they only knew."
  * **Gloss:** The title-parable: patronage besides Allah is architecture by the frailest builder — the web is shelter only until it is touched; "If they only knew" marks the parable's unread audience.
* **`[29:42]`** "Allah knows what they invoke besides Him. He is the Almighty, the Wise."
  * **Gloss:** The knowing-and-mighty clause: the patrons are known to be nothing; the spider-house has an audience that is neither fragile nor wise-less — He is the Almighty.
* **`[29:43]`** "These examples—We put them forward to the people; but none grasps them except the learned."
  * **Gloss:** Parable-epistemology: the struck-through examples (amthal) are filtered by knowledge — twin register of `[35:28]` "From among His servants, the learned fear Allah."
* **`[29:44]`** "Allah created the heavens and the earth with truth. Surely in that is a sign for the believers."
  * **Gloss:** The cosmological seal under the parable: creation itself is truth-act (twin `[64:3]` "created the heavens and the earth with truth"; `[6:73]`) — the only solid house left standing.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`protectors` (awliya)** `[29:41]`:
  * **Rendering:** "those who take to themselves protectors other than Allah."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[13:16]` "Have you taken besides Him protectors, who have no power to profit or harm even themselves?"; `[46:32]` "has no protectors besides Him" — the patron-word, and the spider-house is its structural verdict.
* **`spider` / `most fragile of houses`** `[29:41]`:
  * **Rendering:** "that of the spider. It builds a house. But the most fragile of houses is the spider's house."
  * **Elsewhere:** unique in the corpus (rg-verified) — the surah's name-word, carried entirely by one parable.
* **`embellished` (zeyyana)** `[29:38]`:
  * **Rendering:** "Satan embellished for them their deeds, barring them from the path" — the only "embellished" in the corpus (rg-verified).
  * **Elsewhere:** the same sin-decoration verb-family drives `[27:24]` "Satan made their conduct appear good to them, and diverted them from the path" and `[16:63]` "Satan made their deeds appear alluring... He is their master today" — embellishment and false mastery travel together, exactly the spider-house pathology.
* **`the learned`** `[29:43]`:
  * **Rendering:** "none grasps them except the learned."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[35:28]` — the same class as God-fearers; the parable-audience is competence, not sympathy (Source: observable term pairing).
