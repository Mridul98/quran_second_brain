# The two parties: oaths as screens, the written Word, and a spirit from Him (58:14–22)
**UID:** 202610142169
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 58:14–22 (Ruku 3 of 3 in al-Mujadilah)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[58:14]` – `[58:22]` (9 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah's final ruku sorts humanity into two enlisted armies: those who befriended a people under Allah's wrath — oath-screened, property-shielded, swearing to God on the Day exactly as they swear to the community now, "indeed, they themselves are the liars" (14–18) — claimed by Satan, who makes them forget remembrance, "the losers" (19); and the party of Allah, defined by one brutal loyalty-test — no love for those who oppose Allah and His Messenger, "even if they were their parents, or their children, or their siblings" (22a) — secured by a pre-written victory (21) and three interior gifts: faith inscribed, a spirit from Him, and mutual divine pleasure (22b).
* **Placement:** Closing ruku; it cashes the surah's perception-frame (heard/counted/witnessed, ruku 1) and whisper-law (ruku 2) into an alliance verdict — the batch's sharpest covenant-boundary text.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[58:14]`** "Have you considered those who befriended a people with whom Allah has become angry? They are not of you, nor of them. And they swear to a lie while they know." — **Gloss:** Alliance as the crime: friendship (walāʾ) with the divinely-angered; the verdict is a double exclusion — "not of you, nor of them" — the null-citizen formula (cf. `[3:28]`-family walāʾ language elsewhere; not audited this pass — reference only). Swearing-knowingly is the falsified instrument.
* **`[58:15]`** "Allah has prepared for them a terrible punishment. Evil is what they used to do." — **Gloss:** Punishment pre-"prepared" (aʿadda) — the stock preparation-clause the batch has now at 57:19/21-neighborhood and 59:21-neighborhood (internal; cite verified lines only where noted).
* **`[58:16]`** "They took their oaths as a screen, and prevented others from Allah’s path. They will have a shameful punishment." — **Gloss:** The oath-as-screen idiom: verbatim kin of `[63:2]` "They treat their oaths as a cover" (k-nifaq's own quote — brain-verified) — same Arabic figure, Itani drift: "screen" (58:16) / "cover" (63:2). The block-from-the-path clause follows the oaths in both surahs — one portrait, two settings.
* **`[58:17]`** "Neither their possessions nor their children will avail them anything against Allah. These are the inhabitants of the Fire, dwelling therein forever." — **Gloss:** The wealth-and-children shield fails — exactly the inventory 57:20 named as the world's rivalry-terms (cross-batch ✓) and 9:55/63:9's distraction-themes (3-hit cluster unverified this pass — reference only). Fire-dwelling formula shared verbatim-in-kind with `[59:17]` (see p05902).
* **`[58:18]`** "On the Day when Allah will resurrect them altogether—they will swear to Him, as they swear to you, thinking that they are upon something. Indeed, they themselves are the liars." — **Gloss:** The courtroom satire: their swearing habit survives resurrection — they will swear AT God as they swear at the Prophet. "they themselves are the liars" — the emphasis construction that `[59:11]` answers with divine counter-witness: "Allah bears witness that they are liars" (batch pair ✓, cf. `[63:1]` — k-nifaq-verified).
* **`[58:19]`** "Satan has taken hold of them, and so has caused them to forget the remembrance of Allah. These are the partisans of Satan. Indeed, it is Satan’s partisans who are the losers." — **Gloss:** enlistment language: ḥizb al-shayṭān; forgetting-remembrance is Satan's capture-effect — the negative twin of `[59:19]` "those who forgot Allah, so He made them forget themselves" (rg-verified pair). "the losers" — the stock verdict-formula, same one k-nifaq quotes at `[63:9]` "these are the losers" (rg-verified). The task's check resolves: "the losers" is THIS verse's close — NOT 58:22's.
* **`[58:20]`** "Those who oppose Allah and His Messenger are among the lowliest." — **Gloss:** The oppose-formula returns verbatim from 58:5 — and "the lowliest" is its rank-clause; bracket: 58:5 (subdued) ... 58:20 (lowest-rank) ... 58:22 (loyalty-test applied to the believers) — one crime-word, three register-steps. `[59:4]` re-issues the formula in retribution-key (batch ✓).
* **`[58:21]`** "Allah has written: “I will certainly prevail, I and My messengers.” Allah is Strong and Mighty." — **Gloss:** The task's verification — and correction: the "who aids Allah and He aids him" line is NOT here; that idiom is `[47:7]` "If you support Allah, He will support you, and will strengthen your foothold" (rg-verified; nearest kin in-batch: `[57:25]` "who supports Him and His messengers invisibly"). What IS here is the decree-as-text: "Allah has written" (kataba) — the same root the very next verse applies to hearts ("inscribed"), and the decided-Word class already paired by p03705 with `[40:51]`/`[37:172]` (brain-verified), and by p02107 with `[21:104]`'s self-binding promise. Victory is pre-scripted, not negotiated.
* **`[58:22]`** "You will not find a people who believe in Allah and the Last Day, loving those who oppose Allah and His Messenger, even if they were their parents, or their children, or their siblings, or their close relatives. These—He has inscribed faith in their hearts, and has supported them with a spirit from Him. And He will admit them into Gardens beneath which rivers flow, wherein they will dwell forever. Allah is pleased with them, and they are pleased with Him. These are the partisans of Allah. Indeed, it is Allah’s partisans who are the successful." — **Gloss:** The loyalty boundary in its strongest corpus form — the kin-list (parents/children/siblings/close kin) verbatim-rhymes with `[9:24]`'s five-term inventory "your parents, and your children, and your siblings, and your spouses, and your relatives... more dear to you than Allah" (rg-verified; 9:24 adds spouses and runs the threat-direction, 58:22 runs the love-prohibition). Interior gifts: faith INSCRIBED (the same write-root as 58:21 — observable root-play), support with "a spirit from Him" — the exact phrase-family of `[4:171]` applied to Jesus, "a Spirit from Him" (rg-verified), and the support-with-spirit grammar of `[5:110]` "I supported you with the Holy Spirit" (rg-verified); 42:52's revelation-spirit Itani renders as "We thus inspired you spiritually" (drift flagged to k-kalam). Mutual pleasure formula: "Allah is pleased with them, and they are pleased with Him" — shared verbatim-in-kind with `[9:100]`, `[5:119]`, `[98:8]` (all rg-verified). Closure: ḥizb-Allah "who are the successful" — the mirror of 58:19's losers and the verbatim twin of `[59:9]` "it is they who are the successful" (batch ✓; the same success-formula sits at `[7:8]`).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`ḥizb` (party)**: "partisans of Satan... the losers" / "partisans of Allah... the successful" — 58:19/22, one surah, mirrored closes; the corpus's only explicit two-army vocabulary at this density (compare 58:5/20's oppose-gram). *Source: observable structure; the party-idiom's juridical history is extra-corpus.*
* **kataba-wordplay (wrote/inscribed)**: "Allah has written" (58:21) ↔ "He has inscribed faith in their hearts" (58:22) — Itani keeps both in one lexical family; cf. 57:22's pre-calamity Book and `[4:104]`-neighborhood "Allah writes down what they conspire" (`[4:81]` rg-verified — 58:10's conspiracies recorded — *agent note: same root confirmed only at 4:81, verify others*).
* **"a spirit from Him" (rūḥ)**: 58:22 (community) ↔ `[4:171]` (Jesus) ↔ `[5:110]` "Holy Spirit" (Jesus) ↔ `[42:52]` (revelation, rendered "inspired... spiritually") ↔ the breath-clause family `[15:29]`/`[38:72]`/`[32:9]`/`[21:91]`/`[66:12]` "breathed into... of My/Our Spirit" (all rg-verified). JUDGMENT for the task's probe: the "spirit from Him" family belongs to k-kalam's channel-grammar (42:51–52 is its anchor) AND to k-isa's Jesus-naming (4:171); neither note cites 58:22 — both flagged; the Adam-breath sites are a distinct idiom (nafkh), not to be merged.
* **Pleasure formula**: "Allah is pleased with them, and they are pleased with Him" — 58:22/`[9:100]`/`[5:119]`/`[98:8]` (rg-verified quadruple) — always at victory-or-gathering moments; k-guidance/k-nifaq territory: the reward beyond the trade.
