# Allah heard her: the pleading woman and the ladder of expiation (58:1–6)
**UID:** 202610142167
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 58:1–6 (Ruku 1 of 3 in al-Mujadilah)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[58:1]` – `[58:6]` (6 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** A single woman's complaint is answered at cosmic scale: Allah hears — three times in one verse (1) — and the surah that opens with one heard case immediately legislates the whole class of such cases (2–4), then lifts the camera to the Day when every concealed deed is recounted by the One who kept count while the actors forgot (5–6): the same hearing-seeing-witnessing God who subdues every opponent of Allah and His Messenger.
* **Placement:** The opening ruku of the shortest of this batch's three surahs; its divine-perception frame (heard/counted/witness) governs the rest of the book — ruku 2's secret counsel and ruku 3's two parties stand inside it.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[58:1]`** "Allah has heard the statement of she who argued with you concerning her husband, as she complained to Allah. Allah heard your conversation. Allah is Hearing and Seeing." — **Gloss:** The task's opening verification ✓: the divine name-hearing is tripled — "has heard... heard... Hearing and Seeing" — a wordplay on the surah-name (al-Mujadilah = she who argues; the edition titles the surah "THE ARGUMENT"). The woman is unnamed in the corpus; classical reports name her Khawlah bint Tha'labah (Source: classical asbab reports via Aisha-chain transmit — verify in print). "Hearing and Seeing" is the fixed pair at `[31:28]`, `[22:61]`, `[22:75]`; the bare-name variant "the Hearing, the Seeing" at `[42:11]`, `[40:20]` (all rg-verified).
* **`[58:2]`** "Those of you who estrange their wives by equating them with their mothers—they are not their mothers. Their mothers are none else but those who gave birth to them. What they say is evil, and a blatant lie. But Allah is Pardoning and Forgiving." — **Gloss:** The zihar-law, stated text-neutrally as this brain records legal material: the formula "you are to me like my mother" is declared ontologically false ("they are not their mothers") and morally named ("evil, and a blatant lie"), and the verse ends not with penalty but with "Allah is Pardoning and Forgiving" — pardon precedes procedure. "Estrange... by equating them with their mothers" is Itani's unpacking of a single Arabic term (zihar); the idiom is unique to this surah in the corpus (rg: only 58:2/58:3). *Source: observable rendering; classical fiqh elaboration is extra-corpus — verify before legal use.*
* **`[58:3]`** "Those who estrange their wives by equating them with their mothers, then go back on what they said, must set free a slave before they may touch one another. To this you are exhorted, and Allah is well aware of what you do." — **Gloss:** Expiation tier one: freeing a slave — the same instrument as oath-atonement at `[5:89]` "freeing a slave" (rg-verified) and unintentional-killing at `[4:92]` (a believing slave; rg-verified). "Go back on what they said" (ʿawda): the return from the formula to the marriage is what triggers the ladder. "Allah is well aware" — the ruku's surveillance-refrain begins.
* **`[58:4]`** "But whoever cannot find the means must fast for two consecutive months before they may touch one another. And if he is unable, then the feeding of sixty needy people. This, in order that you affirm your faith in Allah and His Messenger. These are the ordinances of Allah. The unbelievers will have a painful punishment." — **Gloss:** Tier two: "two consecutive months" — verbatim phrase of the man-slayer's fast `[4:92]` (rg-verified); tier three: "the feeding of sixty needy people" — the corpus's only sixty-head count (rg; compare `[5:89]`'s ten needy for a broken oath — ladder logic observable, proportional to the case, *Source: agent inference on the ratio*). Purpose-clause: "in order that you affirm your faith"; status-clause: "These are the ordinances of Allah" (hudud); sanction-clause flips register to "the unbelievers will have a painful punishment" — the ruku already thinking past the case toward the parties of ruku 3.
* **`[58:5]`** "Those who oppose Allah and His Messenger will be subdued, as those before them were subdued. We have revealed clear messages. The unbelievers will have a demeaning punishment." — **Gloss:** The oppose-formula ("those who oppose Allah and His Messenger") — this surah's spine-word, repeated verbatim at 58:20 and reissued in 59:4 "Whoever opposes Allah—Allah is stern in retribution" (all rg-verified; see p05803). "Subdued" (rendering of a debasement-root) pairs with "among the lowliest" of 58:20 — one verdict in two images. "We have revealed clear messages" — the k-kalam channel-verb (anzala) inside a law-passus (flagged there).
* **`[58:6]`** "On the Day when Allah resurrects them all, and informs them of what they did. Allah has kept count of it, but they have forgotten it. Allah is Witness over everything." — **Gloss:** The task's resurrection-reckoning verification ✓ — the site is 58:6, not 58:9 (the task hedged "58:6/9"; 58:9 is the etiquette command — see p05802). The arithmetic of remembering: He "kept count" (aḥṣā) while they forgot; "Allah is Witness over everything" — the topos also at `[29:52]` "Allah suffices as witness... He knows everything" (rg-verified). The double "He will inform them of what they did" (58:6, 58:7) rings across into ruku 2 — the ruku boundary is thematic, not rhetorical.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`samiʿ` (hearing)**: 58:1 — three occurrences in one verse: "has heard," "heard," "Hearing and Seeing."
  * **Rendering:** the pair "Hearing and Seeing" — 58:1, 31:28, 22:61, 22:75; "the Hearing, the Seeing" — 42:11, 40:20 (rg-verified: two fixed English faces, no drift within either).
  * **Elsewhere:** the hearing-names' sharpest claim is here: a woman's private litigation is the surah's first datum — k-kalam (revelation as addressed speech) has divine *speech* cited but not divine *hearing* anywhere in its evidence list (rg-checked) — flagged.
* **`zihar` (estranging)**: unique to 58:2/3; the annulment logic is ontological ("their mothers are none else but those who gave birth to them") before it is legal.
* **Expiation ladder (kaffarat grammar)**: free a slave (58:3 ↔ `[5:89]`/`[4:92]`) → "two consecutive months" fast (58:4 ↔ `[4:92]` verbatim) → feed sixty needy (58:4; the ten-needy oath-atonement at `[5:89]`). *Source: the three-step ladder is textual; its fiqh ordering and juristic scope are extra-corpus — verify.*
* **`shahid` (Witness)**: 58:6 "Allah is Witness over everything" — cf. `[29:52]`, and the witness-family (6:19 "Allah is Witness between you and me" — rg-verified).
* **Oppose-formula**: "oppose Allah and His Messenger" — 58:5/58:20/58:22 + 59:4 (batch cluster; the task's "one who aids Allah... He aids him" line is NOT this formula — that idiom is `[47:7]` "If you support Allah, He will support you" — see p05803 ledger); k-nifaq's counterparty vocabulary (63:7 "side with") is its flip side.
