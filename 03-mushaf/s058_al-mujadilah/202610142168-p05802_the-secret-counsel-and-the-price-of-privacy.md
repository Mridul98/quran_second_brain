# He is their fourth: the secret counsel, the etiquette of gatherings, and the price of privacy (58:7–13)
**UID:** 202610142168
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 58:7–13 (Ruku 2 of 3 in al-Mujadilah)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[58:7]` – `[58:13]` (7 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Because no whisper escapes the arithmetic of omniscience — three with a fourth, five with a sixth, "nor less than that, nor more" (7) — the community's secret speech is legislated twice: first against the conspirators who returned to what they were banned from, whose inside-question is "Why does Allah not punish us for what we say?" (8), then for the believers: converse in virtue and piety, make room in gatherings, know that knowledge earns ranks (9–11), and, in the ruku's most fragile law, offer charity before private audience with the Messenger — a duty whose non-performance is met not with penalty but with pardon, prayer, alms, and obedience (12–13).
* **Placement:** Ruku 2 of 3; the panoramic lens between 58:1's single heard complaint and 58:14–22's two parties. Its "He is with them wherever they may be" is verbatim-twin of `[57:4]` "He is with you wherever you may be" — the batch's central perception-pair.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[58:7]`** "Do you not realize that Allah knows everything in the heavens and everything on earth? There is no secret counsel between three, but He is their fourth; nor between five, but He is their sixth; nor less than that, nor more, but He is with them wherever they may be. Then, on the Day of Resurrection, He will inform them of what they did. Allah has knowledge of everything." — **Gloss:** The task's verification with correction: 58:7 is the secret-counsel omniscience verse; the "knows what they conceal and disclose" wording the task floated is not here (and not in s058 at all — rg) — its nearest sites are `[59:22]` "the Knower of secrets and declarations" and `[60:1]` "I know what you conceal and what you reveal" (rg-verified; 60 is not in this batch's scope, cited as reference only). The "with them wherever they may be" clause is verbatim-twin of 57:4 (p05701 §3). The number-play "three... fourth... five... sixth" is the corpus's only other rhyme at `[18:22]`, the sleepers' contested count — "Three, and their fourth being their dog... Five, and their sixth being their dog" (rg-verified; *Source: observable numeric echo — intent unproven*).
* **`[58:8]`** "Have you noted those who were prohibited from conspiring secretly, but then reverted to what they were prohibited from? They conspire to commit sin, and aggression, and defiance of the Messenger. And when they come to you, they greet you with a greeting that Allah never greeted you with. And they say within themselves, “Why does Allah not punish us for what we say?” Hell is enough for them. They will roast in it. What a miserable destiny!" — **Gloss:** The named-none portrait: no "hypocrite" noun appears anywhere in s058 (rg count: zero across all 22 ayahs) — the dossier runs entirely on conduct, exactly k-nifaq's "longest portrait never uses the label" method for 2:8–20. The three-count conspiracy (sin, aggression, defiance) reappears inverted in 58:9 (virtue, piety, obedience-of-the-Messenger). "They greet you with a greeting that Allah never greeted you with" — the greeting-slang claim is opaque without the extra-corpus reports on its wording (Source: classical tafsir — verify);. "What a miserable destiny!" twins `[57:15]` "what an evil fate!" — two wretchedness-exclamations, one build (rg-verified pair).
* **`[58:9]`** "O you who believe! When you converse secretly, do not converse in sin, and aggression, and disobedience of the Messenger; but converse in virtue and piety; And fear Allah, to Whom you will be gathered." — **Gloss:** The same verb-set re-sown for the believers: "disobedience of the Messenger" (58:9) vs "defiance of the Messenger" (58:8) — the identical object, two English faces (observable drift). Mid-line capital "And fear Allah" — quote as-is (class-2 typography). "to Whom you will be gathered" — the gathering-clause (cf. `[57:5]` "all matters are referred" — batch-adjacent). The task's "58:6/9" hedge resolves: the resurrection-informing verse is 58:6; this verse is the command built on it.
* **`[58:10]`** "Conspiracies are from Satan, that he may dishearten those who believe; but he will not harm them in the least, except by leave of Allah. So let the believers put their trust in Allah." — **Gloss:** Whisper's owner named: the conspiracy-gram is Satan's, aim = grief; harm-limit clause "except by leave of Allah" — the same sovereignty-permission formula k-guidance/k-sabr territory; "put their trust in Allah" — the fixed trust-formula at `[3:122]`, `[3:160]`, `[14:11]`, `[64:13]` (rg-verified family).
* **`[58:11]`** "O you who believe! When you are told to make room in your gatherings, make room; Allah will make room for you. And when you are told to disperse, disperse. Allah elevates those among you who believe, and those given knowledge, many steps. Allah is Aware of what you do." — **Gloss:** Etiquette becomes promise: room given ↔ room made, degrees (darajāt) for belief-and-knowledge. "make room" is corpus-unique (rg). The knowledge-elevation pairing was already verified against 35:27-28's learned by p03504 ("the knowledge-elevation pairing at `[58:11]`" — brain-verified). "Allah is Aware" refrain — the surah's surveillance-tag (58:3/6?/7/11/13 — the Aware-chain).
* **`[58:12]`** "O you who believe! When you converse privately with the Messenger, offer something in charity before your conversation. That is better for you, and purer. But if you do not find the means—Allah is Forgiving and Merciful." — **Gloss:** The charity-before-audience law — infaq-grammar as door-practice; notice its mercy escape-hatch in the same breath: "if you do not find the means—Allah is Forgiving and Merciful." k-infraq cites none of 58:12–13 (rg-checked) — flagged: the note's "charity is audited by intent at the edges" could use this edge.
* **`[58:13]`** "Are you reluctant to offer charity before your conversation? If you do not do so, and Allah pardons you, then perform the prayer, and give alms, and obey Allah and His Messenger. Allah is Aware of what you do." — **Gloss:** The clause relaxes inside the corpus itself — the command's non-performance is met by pardon + the standard triad (prayer, zakat, obedience). Classical reports say the duty was abrogated (a one-coin practice held by almost no one — Ibn Kathir citing reports from Ali); the corpus itself shows only the relaxation sequence (Source: classical tafsir on 58:12–13 — verify; observable: 58:12's own escape-clause). "obey Allah and His Messenger" — the obedience-formula that in `[59:7]` gets its take-it/refrain version (batch pair, see p05901).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`najwa` (secret counsel/conversation)**:
  * **Rendering:** "secret counsel" (58:7), "conspiring secretly"/"conspire" (58:8), "converse secretly" (58:9), "Conspiracies" (58:10), "converse privately with the Messenger" (58:12) — one root, five English faces across seven ayahs; the drift itself maps the ruku's argument: the forbidden whisper and the permitted privacy share the word. *Source: observable term choice — verify root-level claim against Arabic.*
  * **Elsewhere:** within the batch, secret/conspiracy language is confined to 58:7–12 (rg-verified); its only theological counterpart in s057–s059 is `[59:22]` "the Knower of secrets and declarations," and inward-gaze kin in 57:6 "He knows what the hearts contains" — whisper-law and name-of-God bookend the three surahs.
* **The fourth/sixth arithmetic**: 58:7 ↔ `[18:22]` — the corpus's only two sites for "their fourth/their sixth" (rg-verified); here the count-names God, there the count-fails humans.
* **Ranks of knowledge**: "Allah elevates... those given knowledge, many steps" (58:11) ↔ 35:27–28's learned cluster — the pairing already recorded in p03504's ledger (brain-verified).
* **Aware-refrain** (`khabīr`-family rendering): "Allah is Aware of what you do" — 58:4? ("well aware" 58:3), 58:11, 58:13 — the ruku closes as the law-closed: with surveillance-as-care. *Source: observable repetition.*
