## 3. Structural Arc
* **Heard → legislated → witnessed (1–6):** a complaint ascends to law: ontology ("they are not their mothers"), ladder (slave → two consecutive months → sixty needy), and the Day's bookkeeping (58:6) as the frame under which any law is kept.
* **The panoramic ear (7–13):** omniscience stated arithmetically (fourth/sixth), then applied: forbidden whisper → commanded room → degrees for knowledge → charity-priced privacy, closed with the corpus's gentlest relaxation (12–13): "if you do not find the means—Allah is Forgiving and Merciful."
* **Draft-day (14–19):** alliance with the divinely-angered produces neither of two communities (14); oaths become screens, property becomes shields (16–17), and the habit of false swearing survives resurrection (18); Satan claims the whole set, and forgetting remembrance is the capture-condition (19).
* **The counter-army (20–22):** the oppose-formula's verdict (20) answers the earlier one (5) — subdued then lowliest; the pre-written victory (21) is then applied to hearts: inscribed faith, spirit, gardens, mutual pleasure, party of the successful (22) — the surah's last word is "the successful," its first word "Allah has heard."
