# Surah 81: THE ROLLING (at-Takwir)
**UID:** 202610142273
**Type:** Index / Surah Map
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Meccan — agreed in the classical type-lists, and transmitted lists place it among the earliest wave (in company with 73/74/80); the order-number differs between the printed lists, so none is asserted here — *Source: classical type-lists and revelation-order lists — verify against a printed table.* The unnumbered Basmala heads the surah (corpus line 3).
* **Size:** 29 ayahs, **1 ruku** (`ruku_map` verified: 81:1–81:29, ayah_count 29) — the whole surah is one documentation unit.
* **Central theme:** Twelve "when" clauses take the cosmos apart (81:1–13) and end on a single line of accounting: "Each soul will know what it has readied." The surah then swears by galaxies, retreating night and breathing dawn (81:15–18) in order to authenticate its messenger — power, proximity to "the Lord of the Throne", obedience, honesty, an unfrightening vision, no stinginess about the Unseen, and no diabolical provenance (81:19–25). Its conclusion returns the reader to the question of direction and of will: "So where are you heading?… But you cannot will, unless Allah wills— The Lord of the Worlds" (81:26–29).

## 3. Structural Arc
* **The unlabeled sequence (81:1–13):** twelve clauses with no stated subject — sun rolled up, stars dimmed, mountains moved, relationships suspended, beasts gathered, oceans fired, souls paired, the buried girl questioned, records public, sky peeled, Fire ablaze, Paradise brought near.
* **The ledger line (81:14):** "Each soul will know what it has readied" — the only thing the sequence is for; the same sentence in 82:5 with a doubled verb (advanced/deferred, verified).
* **The counter-oath (81:15–18):** from collapse to cosmos — galaxies running their courses, night receding, morning breathing: the ordered universe swearing for the ordered speech.
* **The dossier (81:19–25):** seven lines about the bearer of the message (noble messenger; endowed with power and eminent with the Lord of the Throne; obeyed and honest; the friend not possessed; seen on the luminous horizon; not withholding the Unseen; not the word of an accursed devil) — plus the third-party check that the visionary was seen, not imagined.
* **The address (81:26–29):** a question ("So where are you heading?"), a scope ("a Reminder to all mankind"), a condition ("whoever of you wills to go straight"), and a limit ("you cannot will, unless Allah wills").
* **The ring:** the surah opens on conditions with no stated subject and closes on a question with no stated answer, with the only named agent in between being Allah (81:29) — the collapse and the will are both His acts.

## 5b. Batch-Return Verification Flags (this index)
* **81:1–14 renderings corrected against the corpus:** "folded up" → **"rolled up"**; "stars fall" → **"the stars are dimmed"**; "the camels are neglected" → **"When the relationships are suspended."** (81:4 — see the passage note's term-choice flag); "the seas are set ablaze" → **"the oceans are set aflame"**; "the scrolls are unfolded" → **"the records are made public"**; "the heaven stripped" → **"the sky is peeled away"**; "the Garden approached" → **"Paradise is brought near"**; "what it has brought" → **"what it has readied"** (all verified).
* **81:15–18:** the hint's oath "I swear by what you see and what you hide" is **not in s081** — this surah swears "by the galaxies / Precisely running their courses / the night as it recedes / the morn as it breathes". The hint's formula is 69:38–39: "Indeed, I swear by what you see. And by what you do not see." (verified).
* **81:19–24 corrected:** "your companion is not a madman" → **"Your friend is not possessed."**; "the clear horizon" → **"the luminous horizon"**; "not miserly about the unseen" → **"does not withhold knowledge of the Unseen"**; "a mighty one established with" → **"Endowed with power, eminent with"** (all verified). 53-twin judgment: **yes, meaningful** — 53:5–7 (power/highest horizon), 53:13 (a second seeing), 53:3–4 (not desire; revelation) are the closest dossier parallel (verified).
* **81:25–29 close corrected:** no clause "and O mankind, there is no falsehood" and no "you will not walk about except by Allah's will" exists in s081 (full corpus file read). The actual close is 81:25–29 as quoted in §3; the will-limit clause is 81:29 "But you cannot will, unless Allah wills— The Lord of the Worlds", whose twin is 76:30 (verified).
* **81:21 "Obeyed and honest"** is a single corpus occurrence for "Obeyed" (rg-verified) — translator's compression of two attributes into one line.
