# Twelve "when" clauses, a cleared messenger, and an owned will (81:1–29)
**UID:** 202610142274
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 81:1–29 (Ruku 1 of 1 in at-Takwir)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[81:1]`–`[81:29]` (29 ayahs — the entire surah is one ruku in this edition's partition)
* **Thesis:** The surah disassembles the cosmos in an unlabeled sequence of "when" clauses (81:1–13) and says what it was for: "Each soul will know what it has readied" (81:14); it then re-swears over the ordered universe — galaxies, retreating night, breathing dawn — to authenticate the messenger who brought the speech, answering the charges laid against him — power, rank, command, trust, sanity, sight, honesty, provenance (81:19–25) —, and ends by asking the reader where he is heading while removing the will's ownership from his hands (81:26–29).
* **Placement:** Single-ruku surah closing the ‘Abasa block. 81:1–14 is the corpus's tightest cosmic-collapse list and is re-run almost verse-for-verse in the next surah (82:1–5, verified); 81:19–25 is the provenance dossier whose verbatim twin is 69:40–43 and whose mechanism-narrative is 53:3–14 (verified).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[81:1]`** "When the sun is rolled up." **`[81:2]`** "When the stars are dimmed." **`[81:3]`** "When the mountains are set in motion."
  * **Gloss:** the clauses at 81:1–3, each without a stated subject or time: the light-sources de-activated and the stabilizers moved; 81:3 reuses 78:20's exact phrase (verified), and 77:8 "When the stars are obliterated" is the neighbour's version of 81:2 (verified).
* **`[81:4]`** "When the relationships are suspended." **`[81:5]`** "When the beasts are gathered." **`[81:6]`** "When the oceans are set aflame."
  * **Gloss:** the clauses at 81:4–6, the social, the animal and the hydrological: bonds interrupted, beasts assembled, seas ignited (81:5 "beasts" and 81:6 are both unique renderings, verified; cf. 82:3 "When the oceans are exploded").
* **`[81:7]`** "When the souls are paired." **`[81:8]`** "When the girl, buried alive, is asked:" **`[81:9]`** "For what crime was she killed?"
  * **Gloss:** the clauses at 81:7–9 (81:9 is the question carried by 81:8): the pairing of souls (cf. 37:22 "Gather those who did wrong, and their mates", verified) and then the surah's single courtroom image — a murdered infant girl put on trial and asked for her crime, an indictment in the form of a question addressed to the victim.
* **`[81:10]`** "When the records are made public." **`[81:11]`** "When the sky is peeled away."
  * **Gloss:** the clauses at 81:10–11: the archive opened (cf. 78:29 "We have enumerated everything in writing"; 74:52 "scrolls unrolled") and the sky's surface removed (77:9 "And the sky is fractured", verified).
* **`[81:12]`** "When the Fire is set ablaze." **`[81:13]`** "When Paradise is brought near."
  * **Gloss:** the clauses at 81:12–13, the two destinations activated — 26:90 has the identical English "And Paradise will be brought near for the righteous" (verified).
* **`[81:14]`** "Each soul will know what it has readied."
  * **Gloss:** the sequence's payload: twelve "when" clauses were waiting for this sentence; 82:5 states the same line with two verbs ("what it has advanced, and what it has deferred") and 79:35/78:40/59:18 state it with other verbs (all verified).
* **`[81:15]`** "I swear by the galaxies." **`[81:16]`** "Precisely running their courses." **`[81:17]`** "And by the night as it recedes." **`[81:18]`** "And by the morn as it breathes."
  * **Gloss:** four oaths over the cosmos that still works — the retreating night and breathing dawn mirroring the collapse just described (74:33–34 "the night as it retreats. And the morning as it lights up", verified); note this is not the oath of 69:38–39 ("by what you see… by what you do not see").
* **`[81:19]`** "This is the speech of a noble messenger."
  * **Gloss:** the sworn claim: the speech has a bearer; 69:40 is verbatim the same sentence (verified) — the corpus's two lines on one another.
* **`[81:20]`** "Endowed with power, eminent with the Lord of the Throne." **`[81:21]`** "Obeyed and honest."
  * **Gloss:** four attributes of that bearer: power, proximity ("eminent with"), standing command, and trustworthiness — the counter-dossier to "possessed", "poet", "soothsayer", and "devil" (53:5–6 "Taught to him by the Extremely Powerful. The one of vigor. He settled.", verified; 40:15 "Owner of the Throne. He conveys the Spirit, by His command", verified).
* **`[81:22]`** "Your friend is not possessed." **`[81:23]`** "He saw him on the luminous horizon."
  * **Gloss:** two charges answered: madness, and the unverifiability of the vision — the seeing is placed at an horizon ("luminous", unique here), with 53:7 "the highest horizon" and 53:13 "He saw him on another descent" as the parallel tellings (verified); cf. 52:29 "you are neither a soothsayer, nor a madman" (verified).
* **`[81:24]`** "And He does not withhold knowledge of the Unseen."
  * **Gloss:** the stinginess charge answered — the message is not held back (cf. 6:50 "I only follow what is inspired to me"; 64:18/34:48 "the Knower of the Unseen"). Note the book's capital "He" here, where 81:22–23 has "his/He" for the messenger — either the referent is Allah or the casing is the book's own inconsistency: **Source: agent observation on the printed line — verify.**
* **`[81:25]`** "And it is not the word of an accursed devil."
  * **Gloss:** the provenance charge answered — the last of the negations ("accursed" is the corpus's only such rendering, verified), and paired with 69:41–43's chain of "not the speech of a poet… not the speech of a soothsayer… It is a revelation from the Lord of the Worlds" (verified).
* **`[81:26]`** "So where are you heading?"
  * **Gloss:** the one direct question in the surah, and a hapax rendering (verified) — the twelve "when" clauses and the seven dossier lines exist for this address.
* **`[81:27]`** "It is only a Reminder to all mankind." **`[81:28]`** "To whoever of you wills to go straight."
  * **Gloss:** the scope is universal ("all mankind", cf. 12:104 "It is only a reminder for all mankind"; 38:87), the condition is particular ("whoever of you wills", cf. 76:29, 73:19, 80:12, 74:55 — verified).
* **`[81:29]`** "But you cannot will, unless Allah wills— The Lord of the Worlds."
  * **Gloss:** the will's ownership removed, and the surah closes on a divine name; 76:30 is the near-exact twin ("Yet you cannot will, unless Allah wills. Allah is Knowing and Wise.", verified).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **"rolled up" (the surah's name-word)** — 81:1 "When the sun is rolled up."
  * **Rendering:** "rolled up" occurs only here (rg "rolled" → 81:1 plus 74:52's "unrolled", verified); the corpus's cognate image for the same gesture is 21:104 "On the Day when We fold the heaven, like the folding of a book" (verified).
  * **Elsewhere:** 82:1 "When the sky breaks apart" and 77:9 "the sky is fractured" (verified) — three different verbs for the sky's end in three consecutive surahs.
* **"the girl, buried alive"** — 81:8–9
  * **Rendering:** the only such phrase in the corpus (verified); the concept's counterpart appears as an accusation at 16:58–59 ("when one of them is given news of a female infant, his face darkens… Shall he keep it in humiliation, or bury it in the dust?") and at 43:16–17 (both verified).
  * **Elsewhere:** the text stages the scene neutrally — the girl is the one asked, and the question is "For what crime was she killed?", i.e., no charge against her is possible; the indictment falls on the killer (Source: agent observation on the printed lines). The identification of a pre-Islamic Arab practice of live-burial of daughters as the background is classical — **Source: classical reports and lexicons — verify**; no name, place or community is mentioned in 81:8–9.
* **"the speech of a noble messenger"** — 81:19 (with 81:20–21)
  * **Rendering:** "speech" for the medium, "noble messenger" for the bearer; 69:40 has the identical English (verified), and 69:43 supplies the surah's own closing formula, "It is a revelation from the Lord of the Worlds" — the same terminus as 81:29's "The Lord of the Worlds" (verified).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[53:3–5]` (not desire → revelation → taught by the Extremely Powerful) and `[80:15–16]` ("By the hands of scribes. Noble and devoted.") — the angelic-bearer dossier the k-kalam note carries (all verified).
* **"you cannot will"** — 81:28–29
  * **Rendering:** "To whoever of you wills to go straight. / But you cannot will, unless Allah wills" — permission nested inside address.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[76:29–30]` the near-identical pair (verified); `[74:55–56]` and `[80:12]` for the "whoever wills" formula; `[18:29]` for its harder version (all verified).

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Meccan (surah-level consensus). The surah's addressees are unnamed: "they"/"you" only, with "your friend" (81:22) as the sole designation of the messenger — he is never named in this surah.
* **Text-only handling of 81:1–13:** the sequence states conditions without subjects, times, or agents; nothing in these lines identifies who rolls, dims, moves, gathers, or asks. The twelve clauses (81:1–8, 10–13, with 81:9 as the question inside 81:8) are quoted above exactly as printed.
* **Social clause (81:8–9):** the text's claim is that a killed girl will be questioned for a crime she cannot have; the attribution of this to a specific historical practice is a classical reading, not a corpus statement — **Source: classical reports (the practice of female infanticide and its abandonment in the prophetic account; verify against a printed source).**
* **Identity of the "noble messenger" (81:19–21):** the text describes him without naming him; classical readers identify the seen figure of 81:23 as the angel of revelation (with 53:5–18 as the parallel telling), and a minority reading takes 81:19's bearer as the Prophet himself. **Source: classical disagreement — verify.** Recorded, not resolved.
* **81:24's capital "He":** flagged as an edition-typography observation (§2), the same class of book-level variance catalogued in the corpus-artifacts fleeting note.
