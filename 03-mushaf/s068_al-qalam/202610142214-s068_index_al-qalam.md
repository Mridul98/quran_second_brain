## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Meccan in the classical type-lists; *transmitted reports hold part of the opening (about 68:1–16/17) Medinan, read against a Walid-figure's insult* — **transmitted tables — verify** before teaching the partition. Revelation order: no number is confirmed by this brain's chain for this slot — **transmitted nuzul tables — verify** (cf. the s050 convention).
* **Size:** 52 ayahs, 2 ruku — and note the uneven split: ruku 1 is 68:1–33 (33 ayahs), ruku 2 only 68:34–52 (19) (ruku_map globals 498–499 — verified).
* **Title:** al-Qalam = "the Pen"; the surah opens with a lone letter-name — "Noon." — which the [Muqatta'at fleeting note](../../01-fleeting/202610132007-f-muqattaat_letters_untranslated.md) quotes verbatim ("'Noon. By the pen' — 68:1") — a true two-way door.
* **Central theme:** An oath on the medium answers a charge about the messenger. The Pen swears that the accused prophet is not insane, is rewarded without ending, and stands on "a great moral character" (68:1–4); the deniers — compromisers, slanderers, wealth-proud "myths" dismissers (68:8–16) — are then mirror-read in the garden parable (68:17–33): a harvest hoarded against the poor, planned with no reservation, gone by morning. Ruku 2 asks the courtroom questions (scripture? oaths? partners?) up to the Day the Shin is exposed and prostration is impossible (68:34–43), then commands patience against the example of the Fellow of the Fish (68:48–50), closing on glances that "almost stab" and the book's own verdict: "a reminder to all the Worlds."
* **Reading hint:** the surah brackets itself with the madness-charge: 68:2 "you are not insane" ↔ 68:51 "He is crazy!" (rg-verified — the claim named at the start, the claim shouted at the end).

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Oath + vindication (68:1–7):** the Pen and what they inscribe; sanity, endless reward, great character; "You will see, and they will see" — and God knows strayed from guided (68:7).
* **The denier dossier (68:8–16):** compromise-hunger (68:9), seven traits stacked (68:10–13), wealth-and-children as the credential (68:14), "Myths of the ancients!" (68:15), branding on the muzzle (68:16).
* **The garden test (68:17–33):** vow without reservation → night-calamity → dawn-discovery → whisper-plot against the poor → confession arc: glorify / blame one another / "we were domineering" / turning to the Lord — then 68:33 generalizes: "Such is the punishment; but the punishment of the Hereafter is greater."
* **The interrogation (68:34–41):** Gardens of Delight for the righteous — so why treat Muslims as villains? Scripture? oaths? guarantor? partners — "let them produce their partners" (68:41, the k-tawhid courtroom method).
* **The Shin (68:42–43):** exposure, the call to prostrate, inability — the shame of those "invited... when they were sound."
* **Leave-Me and patience (68:44–50):** discourse-rejecters handed to God ("My plan is firm"); no fee, no unseen transcripts; patience commanded — with the Fish-companion as cautionary twin (68:48–50: despair-cry, favor, election).
* **Close (68:51–52):** the glances, the shouted "crazy!" — and the Book's self-naming: "no less than a reminder to all the Worlds."
