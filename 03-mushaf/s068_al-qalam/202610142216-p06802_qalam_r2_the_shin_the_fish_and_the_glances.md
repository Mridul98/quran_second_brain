# The Shin exposed, the Fellow of the Fish, and the stabbing glances (68:34–52)
**UID:** 202610142216
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 68:34–52 (Ruku 2 of 2 in al-Qalam)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[68:34]` – `[68:52]` (19 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** After the garden sample, ruku 2 stages the verdict-scene: why should the Muslims be treated as villains (68:34–35)? The interrogation escalates — a scripture? binding oaths? a guarantor? partners "let them produce" — up to the one Day the Shin is uncovered, prostration is called and impossible (68:36–43); then the discourse-rejecters are handed to God ("My plan is firm"), the prophet is commanded to patience learned against the counter-example of the Fellow of the Fish (68:44–50), and the surah ends where its oath began: eyes raised against him, and a Book "no less than a reminder to all the Worlds."
* **Placement:** Closing ruku; the verdict-half of a surah whose first half was the oath-half (68:33's "punishment of the Hereafter is greater" is the hinge sentence).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[68:34]`** "For the righteous are Gardens of Delight with their Lord." — the counter-garden to 68:17's lost estate; cf. `[56:89]` "Garden of Delights" and `[56:12]` "Gardens of Bliss" (rg-verified).
* **`[68:35]`** "Shall We treat the Muslims like the villains?" — the equivalence-question that answers 68:15's sneer: submission vs. crime will not be flattened.
* **`[68:36]`** "What is the matter with you? How do you judge?" — exact-phrase twins `[37:154]` and `[10:35]`-tail (rg-verified) — the Quran's stock indictment of private verdicts.
* **`[68:37]`** "Or do you have a scripture in which you study." — scripture as evidence-demand: the k-kalam courtroom (where is your document?) — and "scripture" (qira'at/suhuf) plays against 68:1's Pen.
* **`[68:38]`** "Wherein there is whatever you choose?" — entitlement-by-paper: the fantasy of a text that contains "whatever you select" (task's "whatever you choose" ✓).
* **`[68:39]`** "Or do you have oaths from Us, binding until the Day of Resurrection, that you will have whatever you demand?" — the covenant-modal alternative checked and left open (cf. k-covenant's mithaq grammar — forward).
* **`[68:40]`** "Ask them, which of them will guarantee that." — kafil: the guarantor question — finance language in a theology trial (k-infraq echo — the guarantee-market of 30? — keep: agent inference — verify).
* **`[68:41]`** "Or do they have partners? Then let them produce their partners, if they are truthful." — the courtroom demand k-tawhid catalogs at `[23:84–89]` — "produce" as falsifiability test (forward link to k-tawhid; rg-verified absence of 68 there).
* **`[68:42]`** "On the Day when the Shin will be exposed, and they will be called to bow down, but they will be unable." — Task-verified line. Kashf al-saq: the Day's severity idiom; prostration becomes physically impossible for those who refused it when sound. "Shin" occurs nowhere else in the corpus (rg-counted). *Source: classical idiom note (consensus-family on saq) — verify.*
* **`[68:43]`** "Their eyes subdued, shame will cover them. They were invited to bow down when they were sound." — the verdict's two facts: lowered sight (cf. 67:4's dazzled return) and the withheld ability, dated: "when they were sound" — the old invitation on record.
* **`[68:44]`** "So leave Me to those who reject this discourse; We will proceed against them gradually, from where they do not know." — "this discourse" (hadith): the Book self-named — k-kalam's channel-vocabulary; "gradually" — the istidraj formula, near-exact twin `[7:182]` "We will gradually lead them from where they do not know" (rg-verified). Itani renders "discourse" here where `[39:23]` uses "narrations" for the same root (rg-verified drift).
* **`[68:45]`** "And I will give them respite. My plan is firm." — umli (respite) + kayd (plan) — divine strategy stated as patience's inverse.
* **`[68:46]`** "Or do you ask them for a fee, so they are burdened with debt?" — the charge repeated from the prophet's economics; near-exact twin `[52:40]` "Or do you demand a payment from them, and they are burdened by debt?" (rg-verified).
* **`[68:47]`** "Or do they know the future, and so they write it down?" — ghayb + yaktubun: the Pen motif returns as the villains' counterfeit transcription — they claim the unseen and "write" entitlement (k-kalam's record family: cf. 68:1's inscription and `[83:18–21]` "the record of the righteous is in Elliyyeen... a numerical book" — rg-verified).
* **`[68:48]`** "So wait patiently for the Decision of your Lord, and do not be like the Fellow of the Fish who cried out in despair." — Task-verified. Sabr commanded exactly where the surah's vindication-delay stings; the Fish-companion (only "the Fish" line in the corpus, rg-counted) is the counter-example of the same waiting — "cried out in despair" (nidā' min dad). *Source: classical reading tying 68:48–50 to Yunus — consensus-family — verify.*
* **`[68:49]`** "Were it not for his Lord’s favor that reached him, he would have been thrown into the wilderness, fully despised." — the favor-nudge (la'ola an) — the prophet's survival is by ni'ma, not merit: cf. 68:2's "By the grace of your Lord" (internal ring).
* **`[68:50]`** "But his Lord chose him, and made him one of the righteous." — election after the belly: the despair-story ends in ijtaba — the same "righteous" word as 68:34 (internal).
* **`[68:51]`** "Those who disbelieve almost stab you with their glances when they hear the message, and say, “He is crazy!”" — the bracket closes: the madness-claim of 68:2, now shouted; glances as weapons (the evil-eye idiom — *Source: classical reading on the ayn — verify*).
* **`[68:52]`** "But it is no less than a reminder to all the Worlds." — "the message" (68:51) named: dhikr to al-'alamin — the Pen oath's product universalized; family: `[81:27]` "It is only a Reminder to all mankind" (rg-verified).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`hadith` rendered "discourse" (68:44):** the Book's self-reference; Itani's drift vs `[39:23]` "best of narrations" (rg-verified pair) — k-kalam's channel-word.
* **`sāq` rendered "Shin" (68:42):** corpus-unique; the Day's exposure idiom. **Elsewhere:** none (rg-verified) — handle as interpretive rendering, Source: classical idiom — verify.
* **`ṣāḥib al-ḥūt` (Fellow of the Fish, 68:48):** unnamed here — the named twins: `[21:87]` "And Jonah, when he stormed out in fury... cried out in the darkness, 'There is no god but You!...'" and `[37:139–142]` (fled to the laden boat, gambled, swallowed); `[10:98]`: his people alone benefited from belief (all rg-verified).
