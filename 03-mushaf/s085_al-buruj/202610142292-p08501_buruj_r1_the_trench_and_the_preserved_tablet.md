# The Trench, the Witness, and the Preserved Tablet (85:1–22)
**UID:** 202610142292
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 85:1–22 (Ruku 1 of 1 in al-Buruj)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[85:1]` – `[85:22]` (22 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Sworn on the constellationed sky, the Promised Day, and "the witness and the witnessed," the surah opens one historical case-file — a trench of fire in which a people were punished only for believing — and answers mob violence with sovereign doxology: the God whose "onslaught is severe" begins and repeats, holds the Glorious Throne, and closes by anchoring His own speech "In a Preserved Tablet" — the batch's answer to every "Legends of the ancients" jab (83:13).
* **Placement:** Ruku 1 of 1, and the batch's only full narrative scene. 82 installed watchers, 83 named the registers, 84 handed the books — 85 shows the courtroom's real case and its archive's location.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[85:1]`–`[85:3]`** "By the sky with the constellations. / And by the Promised Day. / And by the witness and the witnessed."
  * **Gloss:** A triple oath climbing from cosmos to deadline to courtroom: the decorated sky (same *buruj* as `[25:61]`/`[15:16]`), "the Promised Day" (exact phrase twin — only here and `[50:20]` — rg-verified), and the paired legal nouns "witness and witnessed" (corpus-unique as a phrase — rg-verified).
* **`[85:4]`** "Destroyed were the People of the Trench."
  * **Gloss:** The oath resolves into a verdict-sentence on the perpetrators — "Destroyed were" reads as a curse on "the People of the Trench," a fire-pit crew named only by their crime; no people, place, or date given (text-neutral — see §6).
* **`[85:5]`–`[85:7]`** "The fire supplied with fuel. / While they sat around it. / And were witnessing what they did to the believers."
  * **Gloss:** The scene develops in three camera moves: the fire stoked, the persecutors SEATED (the trench as spectator sport), and the damning reframing — they were "witnessing": the false "witnessed" of 85:3 inverted, their own act becoming the evidence.
* **`[85:8]`–`[85:9]`** "They begrudged them only because they believed in Allah the Almighty, the Praiseworthy. / To Whom belongs the sovereignty of the heavens and the earth. Allah is witness over everything."
  * **Gloss:** The sole charge against the victims: belief — "they begrudged" (*nakamu*) faith itself; and the court is escalated: the believed-in One owns heavens and earth, and "Allah is witness over everything" — the phrase-pair is otherwise only `[41:53]` ("your Lord is witness over everything" — rg-verified), with near-forms at `[22:17]`, `[10:46]`.
* **`[85:10]`** "Those who tempt the believers, men and women, then do not repent; for them is the punishment of Hell; for them is the punishment of Burning."
  * **Gloss:** The verdict generalizes from that trench to every persecution ("tempt" — *fatana*: tried believers male and female and died unrepentant), and the sentence is doubled for emphasis: two punishments named, the second "the punishment of Burning" — fire paid back in kind.
* **`[85:11]`** "Those who believe and do righteous deeds will have Gardens beneath which rivers flow. That is the great triumph."
  * **Gloss:** The victims' counter-sentence; "the great triumph" phrase-cluster: `[48:5]`, `[57:12]` (rg-verified) — the reward-tableau counterpart to 83:22–28's bliss scene (Gardens/triumph here, thrones/wine there; no shared triumph-word with 83 — checked).
* **`[85:12]`–`[85:16]`** "The onslaught of your Lord is severe. / It is He who begins and repeats. / And He is the Forgiving, the Loving. / Possessor of the Glorious Throne. / Doer of whatever He wills."
  * **Gloss:** Five doxology lines answer one fire: severe *dhaqb* (onslaught) — but the same list holds "the Forgiving, the Loving" (a divine-name pairing unique in this corpus — rg-verified), the begin-repeat God (resurrection doctrine — kin `[30:27]`, `[10:34]`), the Throne-owner, and an unconditioned will: the persecutors' fire is not the last word, by constitution.
* **`[85:17]`–`[85:18]`** "Has there come to you the story of the legions? / Of Pharaoh and Thamood?"
  * **Gloss:** A news-formula opening the precedent file — the two canonical destroyed legions; "the story of the legions" re-runs the destruction dossier of `[69:9]` ("Then Pharaoh came, and those before him, and the Overturned Cities") and Thamood's "Overwhelming" (`[69:5]`).
* **`[85:19]`–`[85:20]`** "In fact, those who disbelieve are in denial. / And Allah encloses them from beyond."
  * **Gloss:** The pattern closes on the present audience: denial is their settled state; and the counter-image is encirclement — "encloses… from beyond" is a corpus-unique rendering (rg-verified): no escape outward, because the flanking is already done.
* **`[85:21]`–`[85:22]`** "In fact, it is a Glorious Quran. / In a Preserved Tablet."
  * **Gloss:** The keystone: the defied Book is located — *majid* ("Glorious," name-twin of `[50:1]` "By the Glorious Quran") and *mahfuz* ("Preserved") on a tablet kept safe: the batch's standing reply to every "Legends of the ancients" (83:13) and every heart-corrosion (83:14) — this Book is archived where earning cannot corrode it.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`buruj` (constellations)**: the sky's palaces/high points.
  * **Rendering:** "By the sky with the constellations" (85:1) — oath-grade decoration.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[25:61]` "He who placed constellations in the sky, and placed in it a lamp, and an illuminating moon"; `[15:16]` "We placed constellations in the sky, and made them beautiful to the beholders" (rg-verified) — the sworn sky is the adorned sky.
* **`the witness and the witnessed`**: 85:3's paired legal nouns.
  * **Rendering:** kept as a bare pair — no referent named in the verse (textually open).
  * **Elsewhere (concept-twins, rg-verified):** `[4:41]` "We bring a witness from every community, and We bring you as a witness against these"; `[50:21]` "accompanied by a driver and a witness"; `[17:78]` "The Quran at dawn is witnessed"; and 85:7's persecutors-as-"witnessing" — the scene ironizes the term.
* **`yabda'u wa-yu'idu` ("begins and repeats")**: creation-as-then-return, the resurrection engine.
  * **Rendering:** "It is He who begins and repeats" (85:13) — bare, without the word "creation."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[30:27]` "It is He who initiates creation, and then repeats it, something easy for Him"; `[10:34]` "Allah initiates creation, and then repeats it. How are you so deluded?" (rg-verified) — note 10:34's closer re-opens the 82:6 *ghrur* cluster: begin-repeat is itself an anti-delusion proof.
* **`al-lawh al-mahfuz` ("a Preserved Tablet")**: the Book's address.
  * **Rendering:** "In a Preserved Tablet" (85:22) — the *mahfuz* phrase is corpus-unique (rg-verified; Moses' stone "Tablets" at `[7:145/150/154]` are a different word-use).
  * **Elsewhere (dossier):** `[56:78]` "In a well-protected Book" + `[56:79]` "None can grasp it except the purified"; `[43:4]` "with Us, in the Source Book, sublime and wise"; `[15:9]` "We revealed the Message, and We will surely preserve it" (k-kalam's current anchors) — all rg-verified.
