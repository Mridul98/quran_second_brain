# Surah 85: THE CONSTELLATIONS (al-Buruj)

## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Meccan — agreed in the classical type-lists; the persecution-consolation register (a burned believer-band, Pharaoh and Thamood, a Tablet guarantee) suits the early Meccan period. *Source: classical type-lists — verify.* Revelation order: **transmitted nuzul tables — verify** (corpus states no nuzul; unnumbered Basmala heads the surah).
* **Size:** 22 ayahs, 1 ruku (ruku_map global 527 — verified). The batch's terminal surah — and its only full narrative: one historical scene (85:4–10) wed to a doxology (85:12–16) and a Book-claim close (85:21–22).
* **Title:** al-Buruj — Itani's header "THE CONSTELLATIONS" tracks `[85:1]` "By the sky with the constellations"; the same word appears of the decorated sky at `[25:61]` and `[15:16]` (rg-verified).
* **Central theme:** The witness and the witnessed (85:3). A fire-trench is dug, and its crime is faith: "They begrudged them only because they believed in Allah the Almighty, the Praiseworthy" (85:8) — the first martyr-text in the batch, answered by "the onslaught of your Lord is severe" (85:12), the God "who begins and repeats" (85:13), and the surah's keystone close: "In fact, it is a Glorious Quran. In a Preserved Tablet" (85:21–22).
* **Reading hint:** follow the witness-word through the surah — oath (85:3), spectators "witnessing" their own crime (85:7), "Allah is witness over everything" (85:9): every human witness in this scene ends up on trial; only the divine witness stands. Then see who is really indestructible: not the victims, but the Book (85:22).

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Triple oath (85:1–3):** constellated sky, the Promised Day, the witness and the witnessed — cosmos, deadline, courtroom.
* **The Trench (85:4–9):** "Destroyed were the People of the Trench" — the fire, the seated spectators, the sole charge (belief in the Almighty, the Praiseworthy), and 85:9's sovereignty-plus-witness clause that turns the victims' courtroom into Allah's.
* **The verdicts (85:10–11):** the persecutors who "tempt the believers… then do not repent" get a doubled "punishment of Burning"; the believing doers get Gardens and "the great triumph."
* **The doxology (85:12–16):** severe onslaught; He who begins and repeats; Forgiving, Loving; Possessor of the Glorious Throne; Doer of whatever He wills — five lines answering one fire.
* **The archive and the Book (85:17–22):** the legions of Pharaoh and Thamood as precedent; "Allah encloses them from beyond" (corpus-unique phrase, rg-verified); close on the Glorious Quran "In a Preserved Tablet."
* **Ring to note (85:3 ↔ 85:9):** the oath opens with "the witness and the witnessed" and the scene closes with "Allah is witness over everything" — the courtroom's final seat occupied before the verdicts are read. (*Source: structural observation on corpus renderings.*)

## 5b. Batch-Return Verification Flags (this index)
* **Hint renderings corrected:** "slain were the companions of the Trench" → "**Destroyed were the People of the Trench**" (85:4); "the Exalted in Might, the Praiseworthy" → "**the Almighty, the Praiseworthy**" (85:8); "painful burn of Hell" → "the punishment of Hell; for them is the punishment of **Burning**" (85:10); "your Lord's grip is severe" → "**The onslaught** of your Lord is severe" (85:12); "who re-creates?" → "It is He who **begins and repeats**" (85:13 — same doctrine as 30:27/10:34 "initiates creation, and then repeats it", rg-verified); "Lord of the Throne, Glorious" → "Possessor of the Glorious Throne" (85:15); "hosts" → "**legions**" (85:17).
* **Two hint lines REJECTED — not in the corpus:** (1) the bracketed "[and] you had as witnesses your Lord's favor?" — no such clause anywhere in s085 (85:9 ends "Allah is witness over everything"); (2) "the curse of Allah is on every oppressor" — no curse-verse in s085 at all (its verdict-form is 85:10's double punishment). Both misattributed; nothing in the surah to cite.
* **"Witness and the witnessed" located:** exact phrase is corpus-unique to 85:3 (rg-verified); concept-twins per the hint's query: `[4:41]` (a witness from every community), `[50:21]` (a driver and a witness), `[17:78]` (the dawn Quran "is witnessed"); "the Promised Day" phrase-pair is exactly 85:2 ↔ `[50:20]` (rg-verified two-site cluster).
* **Persecution narrative is text-neutral:** the Quran names no people, place, date, or victims — the classical identification (Himyarite/Najran burning) is from transmitted reports and must be labeled as such, never quoted as "the verse says" (see passage §6).
* **k-kalam "keystone" claim:** true as a claim about the THEME (85:21–22 is the strongest single-line statement of the Preserved Tablet), false as a claim about the NOTE's current state (rg-verified absent) — flagged as append candidate rather than existing citation.
