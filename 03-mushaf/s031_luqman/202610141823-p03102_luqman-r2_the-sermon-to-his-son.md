# Luqman's sermon to his son: tawhid, parents, and the mustard-seed scale of God (31:12–19)
**UID:** 202610141823
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 31:12–19 (Ruku 2 of 3 in Luqman)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[31:12]` – `[31:19]` (8 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Wisdom gets a voice: Luqman's endowment (12) is delivered as a father's sermon to his son — no shirk, gratitude as the whole of religion, omniscience on a mustard-seed scale, prayer-command-patience, and humility written on the stride and the voice — with God Himself interrupting (14–15) to legislate the parents' case.
* **Placement:** The surah's heart; ruku 1 declared the Book Wise, this ruku shows wisdom embodied, and ruku 3's "argues about Allah without knowledge" (20–21) is the anti-Luqman figure — a son without a father's address.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[31:12]`** "We endowed Luqman with wisdom: “Give thanks to Allah.” Whoever is appreciative— is appreciative for the benefit of his own soul. And whoever is unappreciative—Allah is Sufficient and Praiseworthy."
  * **Gloss:** The only named wisdom-endowment in the corpus (Luqman appears nowhere else — rg-checked); its first imperative is shukr, and gratitude is restated as self-interest correctly calculated (cited already in the shukr knowledge note).
* **`[31:13]`** "When Luqman said to his son, as he advised him, “O my son, do not associate anything with Allah, for idolatry is a terrible wrong.”"
  * **Gloss:** The sermon's creed-line: shirk named as zulm — "idolatry is a terrible wrong" (task's "tremendous wrongdoing" is another translation's wording); the tawhid note's primary proof-text pair.
* **`[31:14]`** "We have entrusted the human being with the care of his parents. His mother carried him through hardship upon hardship, weaning him in two years. So give thanks to Me, and to your parents. To Me is the destination."
  * **Gloss:** The divine voice interrupts the sermon — parents enjoined with the mother's hardship-on-hardship pregnancy and the two-year weaning; thanks to God and parents fused in one command.
* **`[31:15]`** "But if they strive to have you associate with Me something of which you have no knowledge, do not obey them. But keep them company in this life, in kindness, and follow the path of him who turns to Me. Then to Me is your return; and I will inform you of what you used to do."
  * **Gloss:** The shirk-command exception clause: disobedience has a strict ceiling (no obedience in idolatry), but kindness is never withdrawn — the two-verse near-twin of `[29:8]` (see Key Terms/Elsewhere); "follow the path of him who turns to Me" makes discipleship chosen, not inherited — anti-31:21.
* **`[31:16]`** "“O my son, even if it were the weight of a mustard-seed, in a rock, or in the heavens, or on earth, Allah will bring it to light. Allah is Kind and Expert."
  * **Gloss:** The sermon resumes — 31:16 opens a quotation that the edition leaves running to 31:19's closing mark (a multi-ayah quote, not an extraction break); omniscience by weight and by hiding-place — a rock added to the heavens/earth of the twin `[21:47]`; Latif-Khabir rendered "Kind and Expert" (unique pairing in this corpus — rg-checked).
* **`[31:17]`** "O my son, observe the prayer, advocate righteousness, forbid evil, and be patient over what has befallen you. These are of the most honorable traits."
  * **Gloss:** Four imperatives — prayer, amar-ma'ruf, nahy-munkar, sabr — the social religion in one breath; "These are of the most honorable traits" renders azm al-umum ("matters of resolve" in other translations) — rendering drift logged below.
* **`[31:18]`** "And do not treat people with arrogance, nor walk proudly on earth. Allah does not love the arrogant showoffs."
  * **Gloss:** The sermon turns to posture — the turned cheek and the striding gait; "Allah does not love the arrogant showoffs" belongs to the recurring divine-hate verdict formula: "the aggressors" `[2:190]`, "any sinful ingrate" `[2:276]`, "the exultant" `[28:76]` (all rg-checked).
* **`[31:19]`** "And moderate your stride, and lower your voice. The most repulsive of voices is the donkey’s voice.”"
  * **Gloss:** The closing couplet on body and voice — the donkey's bray, the Quran's only donkey-simile, a humor line inside creed discourse (unique in corpus — rg-checked).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`hikmah`** (wisdom): the surah's keynote, personified.
  * **Rendering:** "We endowed Luqman with wisdom" (31:12); contrast 31:2 "the Wise Book."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:269]` "He gives wisdom to whomever He wills. Whoever is given wisdom has been given much good" — the definition verse; wisdom also at `[28:14]` (Moses at maturity).
* **`shirk`/`zulm`** (association / wrong): 31:13 names it "a terrible wrong."
  * **Rendering:** "do not associate anything with Allah, for idolatry is a terrible wrong."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[4:48]` "Allah does not forgive association with Him... has devised a monstrous sin"; `[22:26]` "Do not associate anything with Me" (Abraham's command — same idiom); `[6:19]` "I am innocent of your idolatry."
* **parents-twin (31:14–15 / 29:8):** the trust-with-parents clause.
  * **Rendering:** 31:14 "We have entrusted the human being with the care of his parents" vs `[29:8]` "We have advised the human being to be good to his parents" — same root-scene; 31 expands with "hardship upon hardship," the two-year weaning, and the kindness-despite-shirk rider absent from 29:8.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[46:15]` "His bearing and weaning takes thirty months"; `[2:233]` "Mothers may nurse their infants for two whole years" — the weaning law.
* **`azm al-umum`** (matters of resolve): the closing clause of 31:17.
  * **Rendering:** "These are of the most honorable traits" — unique in this corpus (rg-checked); task's "matters of resolve" follows other translations — translator's term choice.
  * **Elsewhere:** "be patient over what has befallen you" has its twin portrait at `[22:35]` "those who endure what has befallen them."
