# The Wise Book and its two audiences (31:1–11)
**UID:** 202610141822
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 31:1–11 (Ruku 1 of 3 in Luqman)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[31:1]` – `[31:11]` (11 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah opens the way al-Baqarah does — untranslated letters, a book that names itself Wise, a portrait of the righteous, and a counter-portrait of the man who buys entertainment instead of guidance — then forces the creation question: if Allah made everything, what did your partners make?
* **Placement:** Ruku 1 of 3; the hinge from the surah's book-claim (1–5) through its first counter-audience (6–9) into the creation argument (10–11) that ruku 2's sermon and ruku 3's serviced-universe passage both lean on.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[31:1]`** "Alif, Lam, Meem."
  * **Gloss:** The letters stand untranslated (see Fleeting note); this is the third of four consecutive surahs opening with exactly this cluster in this edition — `[29:1]`, `[30:1]`, `[31:1]`, `[32:1]` (all rg-verified).
* **`[31:2]`** "These are the Verses of the Wise Book."
  * **Gloss:** The book-declaration is verbatim identical to `[10:1]`; wisdom (hikma) is the surah's own keynote before Luqman is named (31:12).
* **`[31:3]`** "A guide and a mercy for the righteous."
  * **Gloss:** hudan + rahmah for the muttaqin — the twin formula of `[7:52]`, `[27:77]`; this verse is already quoted in the guidance knowledge note.
* **`[31:4]`** "Those who observe the prayer, and pay the obligatory charity, and are certain of the Hereafter."
  * **Gloss:** The righteous get three marks — prayer, zakah, certainty (yaqin) — nearly the program of `[2:3–4]`; "certain of the Hereafter" pre-loads 31:34's Hour-knowledge.
* **`[31:5]`** "These are upon guidance from their Lord. These are the successful."
  * **Gloss:** Verbatim echo of `[2:5]` — the "upon guidance" (ala hudan) locative that makes guidance a road one stands on; the fatihah petition answered again.
* **`[31:6]`** "Among the people is he who trades in distracting tales; intending, without knowledge, to lead away from Allah's way, and to make a mockery of it. These will have a humiliating punishment."
  * **Gloss:** The counter-audience: lahwal-hadith — commerce in idle narrative "without knowledge" (bi-ghayri ilm), the phrase ruku 3 will repeat at 31:21; the sin is trading attention away from the Wise Book.
* **`[31:7]`** "And when Our Verses are recited to him, he turns away in pride, as though he did not hear them, as though there is deafness in his ears. So inform him of a painful punishment."
  * **Gloss:** The recited Book meets a self-inflicted deafness — the kalam test-scene; the identical turn-away verdict recurs verbatim-shaped at `[32:22]` "Who is more wrong than he, who, when reminded of his Lord’s revelations, turns away from them?" (rg-checked).
* **`[31:8]`** "As for those who believe and do good deeds—for them are the Gardens of Bliss."
  * **Gloss:** The pair-contrast resolves: belief + deeds → na'im gardens, the same phrase as `[22:56]`, `[10:9]` (rg-checked).
* **`[31:9]`** "Dwelling therein forever. The promise of Allah is true. He is the Mighty, the Wise."
  * **Gloss:** "The promise of Allah is true" recurs as the surah's bracket (also 31:33); al-Aziz al-Hakim closes — Might and Wisdom match the Wise Book's opening claim.
* **`[31:10]`** "He created the heavens without pillars that you can see, and placed stabilizers on earth lest it shifts with you, and scattered throughout it all kinds of creatures. And from the sky We sent down water, and caused to grow therein of every noble pair."
  * **Gloss:** Five acts in one verse: roofless heavens, mountain-stabilizers ("stabilizers" is Itani's coinage — twins at `[21:31]`, `[15:19]`, `[41:10]`), scattered creatures, sent-down water, noble pairs — the same water-vegetation sign that grounds `[32:27]`.
* **`[31:11]`** "Such is Allah's creation. Now show me what those besides Him have created. In fact, the wicked are in obvious error."
  * **Gloss:** The challenge-verdict: the partners-produce-anything argument, near-verbatim at `[35:40]` "Show me what they have created on earth" — the tawhid note's standard proof-scene.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`Alif, Lam, Meem`** (the letters): untranslated opening cluster.
  * **Rendering:** left as letters, "Alif, Lam, Meem."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:1]`, `[29:1]`, `[30:1]`, `[32:1]` — the 29-surah muqattaat phenomenon (Fleeting note).
* **`Hakim`** (Wise): the Book is "Wise" before its sage appears.
  * **Rendering:** "the Wise Book" (31:2); the divine name pair "the Mighty, the Wise" at 31:9.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[10:1]` verbatim twin "the Wise Book"; wisdom itself given to Luqman at `[31:12]` and defined at `[2:269]` "He gives wisdom to whomever He wills."
* **`lahwal-hadith`** (distracting tales): commerce in idle discourse.
  * **Rendering:** "trades in distracting tales" (31:6) — unique phrase in this corpus (rg-checked; "tales" recurs only at `[7:101]`, `[46:17]`, `[99:4]` with different senses).
  * **Elsewhere:** the same "without knowledge" (bi-ghayri ilm) charge returns at `[31:20–21]`.
* **`ala hudan`** (upon guidance): the standing-on-guidance idiom.
  * **Rendering:** "These are upon guidance from their Lord" (31:5).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:5]` verbatim — the guidance note's spine phrase; Fatihah's "guide us" (1:6) answered twice in identical words.
