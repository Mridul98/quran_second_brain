# The serviced universe and the inexhaustible Words (31:20–34)
**UID:** 202610141824
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 31:20–34 (Ruku 3 of 3 in Luqman)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[31:20]` – `[31:34]` (15 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Everything in the heavens and earth is pressed into human service and blessing is showered "outward and inward" — yet some argue about Allah without knowledge, defaulting to ancestral imitation; the ruku answers with the handle that does not break, the ocean-and-seven-more-inks of God's Words, the sea-terror that purifies prayer, and finally the five secrets — with the Hour's knowledge last.
* **Placement:** Surah-closing movement: ruku 1 proved the Author, ruku 2 taught the son; ruku 3 confronts the anti-Luqman figure (the knowledgeless arguer) and ends where the mustard-seed verse began — Allah "All-Knowing, Well-Informed."

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[31:20]`** "Do you not see how Allah placed at your service everything in the heavens and the earth? ... Yet among the people is he who argues about Allah without knowledge, without guidance, and without an enlightening Scripture."
  * **Gloss:** Universal subservience (sakhkhara) vs the triple-blind arguer — the exact phrase "argues about Allah without knowledge... enlightening scripture" is a verbatim near-twin of `[22:8]`.
* **`[31:21]`** "And when it is said to them, “Follow what Allah has revealed,” they say, “Rather, we follow what we found our parents devoted to.” Even if Satan is calling them to the suffering of the Blaze?"
  * **Gloss:** The inheritance-of-religion refusal — twin of `[2:170]` ("what we found our ancestors following" — here "parents devoted to"); "the Blaze" (al-sa'ir) is also at `[37:68]`, `[26:91]`; direct rebuttal of Luqman's own father-son model: true fatherhood points past the father.
* **`[31:22]`** "Whoever submits himself wholly to Allah, and is a doer of good, has grasped the most trustworthy handle. With Allah rests the outcome of all events."
  * **Gloss:** Islam-as-whole-self-surrender grasps the urwa — "the most trustworthy handle" is verbatim the image of `[2:256]` ("which does not break"); outcomes (ummur) rest with Allah, tying back to 31:17's "most honorable traits."
* **`[31:23]`** "Whoever disbelieves—let not his disbelief sadden you. To Us is their return..."
  * **Gloss:** Consolation to the Messenger; the "let not... sadden you" formula twin at `[36:76]` "So let their words not sadden you."
* **`[31:24]`** "We give them a little comfort; then We compel them to a harsh torment."
  * **Gloss:** The deferred-tactic motif: prorogued enjoyment (muta') then compulsion — same grammar as 31:6–7's humiliating punishment.
* **`[31:25]`** "And if you ask them, “Who created the heavens and the earth?” They will say, “Allah.” Say, “Praise be to Allah.” But most of them do not know."
  * **Gloss:** The fitra-confession experiment — verbatim method of `[39:38]` and `[10:31]`; the gap between correct creed-speech and lived "most of them do not know."
* **`[31:26]`** "To Allah belongs everything in the heavens and the earth. Allah is the Rich, the Praised."
  * **Gloss:** Ownership + self-sufficiency: near-verbatim twin `[22:64]` ("Allah is the Rich, the Praised"); cf. `[35:15]` "O people! It is you who are the poor..."
* **`[31:27]`** "If all the trees on earth were pens, filled by the ocean, with seven more oceans besides, the Words of Allah would not run out..."
  * **Gloss:** The sea-as-ink verse: trees-pens + ocean + seven oceans vs `[18:109]`'s ocean-as-ink that runs out even "if We were to bring the like of it" — the Meccan twin pair (verified verbatim both ways); Allah is Majestic and Wise closes the kalam unit.
* **`[31:28]`** "Your creation and your resurrection are only as a single soul. Allah is Hearing and Seeing."
  * **Gloss:** Cosmic-scale ease: creating and raising all is like one soul — the idiom is unique here; `[4:1]` "created you from a single soul" uses the phrase cosmologically, not as divine-ease simile.
* **`[31:29]`** "Have you not seen how Allah merges the night into the day, and merges the day into the night? That He subjected the sun and the moon, each running for a stated term?..."
  * **Gloss:** The day-night-subjection formula, verbatim family: `[35:13]`, `[22:61]`, `[57:6]`; "stated term" (ajal musamma) also `[71:4]`, `[11:3]`.
* **`[31:30]`** "That is because Allah is the Reality, and what they worship besides Him is falsehood, and because Allah is the Exalted, the Supreme."
  * **Gloss:** Haqq vs batil — the tawhid verdict under the creation signs (cf. 22:62 "Allah is the Reality" — same confession pattern).
* **`[31:31]`** "Have you not seen how the ships sail through the sea, by the grace of Allah, to show you of His wonders? In that are signs for every persevering, thankful person."
  * **Gloss:** Ships by His grace — twins `[22:65]` "ships sail at sea by His command", `[45:12]` "ships may run through it by His command", `[16:14]`, `[42:32]` "ships sailing the sea like flags"; the sabur–shakur couplet rendered HERE "persevering, thankful" vs `[14:5]` "patient and thankful" and `[42:33]` "disciplined, grateful" — one Arabic couplet, three Itani renderings (flagged in the shukr note).
* **`[31:32]`** "When waves, like canopies, cover them, they call upon Allah, devoting their religion to Him. But when He has delivered them to dry land, some of them waver..."
  * **Gloss:** The storm-sincerity scene verbatim-twin `[10:22]` (raging wind, waves from every side, "professing sincere devotion"); "waves like hills" `[11:42]`, "waves, above which are waves" `[24:40]`; the deliverance-waver is the Meccan hypocrite-portrait — no one renounces revelations "except the treacherous blasphemer."
* **`[31:33]`** "O people! Be conscious of your Lord, and dread a Day when no parent can avail his child, nor can a child avail his parent, in anything. The promise of Allah is true..."
  * **Gloss:** The sermon's audience widens to all people; parent-child non-availing inverts Luqman's own pair (father cannot save son; cf. the general `[2:48]` "no soul will avail another in the least"); "The promise of Allah is true" brackets back to 31:9.
* **`[31:34]`** "With Allah rests the knowledge of the Hour. He sends down the rain, and He knows what the wombs contain. No soul knows what it will reap tomorrow, and no soul knows in what land it will die. Allah is All- Knowing, Well-Informed."
  * **Gloss:** The five secrets closing the surah: Hour (twins `[41:47]`, `[43:85]`), rain, wombs (`[13:8]`, `[39:6]`), tomorrow, the land of death; the closing divine pair mirrors 31:16's "Kind and Expert" — the mustard-seed omniscience was the overture to this finale. (Note: corpus prints "All- Knowing" with a stray space — extraction artifact, text otherwise verbatim.)

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`bi-ghayri ilm`** (without knowledge): the ruku's diagnosis.
  * **Rendering:** "argues about Allah without knowledge, without guidance, and without an enlightening Scripture" (31:20) — three negatives where `[22:8]` has two and a half.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[31:6]` used the same charge against the tale-merchant — ruku 1 and 3 rhyme on knowledgelessness.
* **`al-urwa al-wuthqa`** (the trustworthy handle): grasped by whole surrender + good.
  * **Rendering:** "has grasped the most trustworthy handle" (31:22).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:256]` verbatim idiom — there it is renouncing evil + believing; here submission + doing good: the same handle, two entry descriptions.
* **`kalimat Allah`** (Words of Allah): inexhaustible speech.
  * **Rendering:** "the Words of Allah would not run out" (31:27).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[18:109]` "the words of my Lord run out" — the ocean-ink twin; `[31:2]`'s "Verses" (ayat) of the Wise Book are the same speech arrived.
* **`sabur–shakur`** (persevering, thankful): the storm-proof reader.
  * **Rendering:** "every persevering, thankful person" (31:31).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[14:5]` "every patient and thankful person"; `[42:33]` "every disciplined, grateful person" — rendering drift documented; both halves anchor the sabr and shukr notes.
