* **Size:** 34 ayahs, 3 ruku (`data/ruku_map.json`: 11+8+15 = 34 ✓). Mushaf position 31, between ar-Rum (30) and as-Sajdah (32).
* **Name:** Luqman — the sage-named-by-nothing-else surah: Luqman appears exactly once in the Quran, at `[31:12]`–`[31:19]` (a personal name unattested elsewhere in the corpus — rg-verified). The word earns the surah: "We endowed Luqman with wisdom" (31:12).
* **Letter ring (corpus fact):** `[31:1]` "Alif, Lam, Meem." makes Luqman the middle of a four-surah muqattaat run — `[29:1]`, `[30:1]`, `[31:1]`, `[32:1]` all open with the same cluster in this edition's numbering. See [Muqatta'at fleeting note](../../01-fleeting/202610132007-f-muqattaat_letters_untranslated.md).
* **Central theme:** Wisdom as address. The Wise Book (31:2) first sorts its audience — the righteous who profit (3–5) versus the merchant of distracting tales (6–7) — then proves its Author from creation (10–11). The heart is a father's sermon to his son (12–19): tawhid, gratitude, conscience toward parents even unto refusal, the mustard-seed precision of God's knowledge, prayer-command-patience, and the etiquette of the lowered voice. Ruku 3 widens the lens: a universe pressed into human service (20) against those who "argue about Allah without knowledge" (20–21), the inexhaustible Words (27), the storm that prunes fake worship (31–32), and the five secrets closing on the Hour (34).

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Book & audience (1–11):** letters → self-declaration → the righteous portrait (3–5, a near-twin of al-Baqarah's opening census) → the tale-merchant (6–7) → the creation argument (10–11). The surah opens by defining who can hear it.
* **The sermon (12–19):** wisdom personalized — one father, one son, six imperatives pivoting on gratitude (12) and tawhid (13), with the parents-law (14–15) interrupting the sermon like a divine footnote, then the scale of God's knowledge (16) and the etiquette of the body (18–19).
* **The universe argued [Ta Ha 32](../s020_ta-ha/202610141661-s020_index_ta-ha.md):** everything "placed at your service" (20) answered by ancestral-tradition stubbornness (21), the trustworthy handle (22–24), the innate monotheism confession (25–26), the ink-and-ocean Words (27), day-night-ships signs (29–32) ending on the storm-purified prayer.
* **The closing secrets (33–34):** "O people! Be conscious of your Lord" — the parent-child Day, then the five knowledges reserved to Allah (Hour, rain, wombs, tomorrow, the land of death), echoing the mustard-seed verse's "Expert" (Latif, Khabir) at the surah's end.
