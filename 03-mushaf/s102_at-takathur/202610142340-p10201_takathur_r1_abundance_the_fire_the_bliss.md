# Abundance ends at graveyards: the double "you will know," the two-stage seeing, the Bliss audit (102:1–8)
**UID:** 202610142340
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 102:1–8 (Ruku 1 of 1 in at-Takathur)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[102:1]` – `[102:8]` (8 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** A single present-tense diagnosis — "Abundance distracts you, until you visit the graveyards" — is answered by a doubled refrain ("Indeed, you will know. Certainly, you will know."), then by a knowledge-to-sight ladder: were they to know *with knowledge of certainty* they would already see the Inferno; on that Day they will see it *with the eye of certainty* — and the only question that survives is about the Bliss they preferred to the seeing.
* **Placement:** Ruku 1 of 1; the batch's wealth-indictment capstone. 100:8 named the greed (ḥirṣ), 102 names its total career (takathur unto death), 103 will name its cost (loss); the diversion-verb ties it to 63:9 and 24:37 on one side and to 57:20's "rivalry in wealth and children" on the other (all rg-verified).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[102:1]`** "Abundance distracts you."
  * **Gloss:** The subject is not wealth but the *pursuit-of-more* (takāthur rendered "Abundance" — noun-form; the competitive nuance lives in the Arabic root, visible in English only at `[57:20]` "rivalry in wealth and children"); the verb (alhā — the lahw/diversion root; see §3) makes diversion itself the sin: nothing else in the surah happens except being occupied away.
* **`[102:2]`** "Until you visit the graveyards."
  * **Gloss:** Distraction has a terminus, stated as euphemism: "visit" (zūr) — the dead are "visitors" to the graveyards, death treated as a relocation one attends; the word "graveyards" (mazār) occurs nowhere else in the corpus (rg-verified).
* **`[102:3]`–`[102:4]`** "Indeed, you will know. / Certainly, you will know."
  * **Gloss:** The doubled refrain — same sentence, intensified opening adverb — postpones all content to the act of knowing; structural twin: `[78:4–5]` "Surely, they will find out. / Most certainly, they will find out." (rg-verified pair).
* **`[102:5]`–`[102:6]`** "If you knew with knowledge of certainty. / You would see the Inferno."
  * **Gloss:** The unreal conditional: a knowledge qualified as *certain* ("knowledge of certainty" — ‘ilm al-yaqīn by standard correspondence, verify) would convert the unseen Inferno (al-Jaḥīm) into present sight — seeing indexed to knowing, not to distance.
* **`[102:7]`** "Then you will see it with the eye of certainty."
  * **Gloss:** The indicative flip: same verb "see," certainty now an organ ("the eye of certainty" — ‘ayn al-yaqīn correspondence — verify); the ladder's two rungs are the surah's whole epistemology: knowledge-of → sight-through (the classical third rung, ḥaqq al-yaqīn, sits with `[56:95]` "This is the certain truth" — rg-verified phrase, Source: standard tafsir schema — verify).
* **`[102:8]`** "Then, on that Day, you will be questioned about the Bliss."
  * **Gloss:** "na‘īm" capitalized as "the Bliss" — corpus-unique object for the question formula (rg-verified); the audit's shape: not "did you sin?" but "what did you do with enjoying?" — every favor a deposited item; kin-question `[16:93]` "you will surely be questioned about what you used to do" (rg-verified).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`takāthur` ("Abundance")**: competing-in-increase.
  * **Rendering:** bare noun "Abundance" (102:1) — the rivalry-verb root (k-th-r) folded into a static noun; "rivalry in wealth and children" (`[57:20]`) is the nearest English of the same notion (rg-verified).
* **`alhā` ("distracts you")**: the diversion-verb of 102:1 (root l-h-w; the lahw family also names the world itself at `[6:32]` "game and distraction" and `[29:64]` "diversion and play" — rg-verified English pairing).
  * **Elsewhere:** the identical English verb "distract" binds 102:1 to `[63:9]` ("Let neither your possessions nor your children distract you…") and `[24:37]` ("neither trading nor commerce distracts them…") — all three are l-h-w verbs in the standard concordance reading — *Source: agent Arabic knowledge — verify against Arabic; the English-verb cluster is rg-verified.*
* **`mazār` ("graveyards")**: euphemistic site-noun.
  * **Elsewhere (concept-twin):** `[15:99]` "And worship your Lord in order to attain certainty" — the "until…" clause of worship vs. the "until…" clause of distraction: two surahs, same deadline, opposite directions (rg-verified lines; yaqīn-as-death is standard — verify).
* **`al-yaqīn` ("certainty")**: qualified twice, as knowledge (102:5) and as eye (102:7).
  * **Elsewhere (rg-verified family):** `[15:99]` (attaining certainty = the life-term), `[45:20]` "people who believe with certainty," `[6:75]` "one of those with certainty," `[74:31]` "attain certainty," `[30:60]` "those who lack certainty."
* **`na‘īm` ("the Bliss")**: the audited favor.
  * **Elsewhere (rg-verified web):** the Hereafter's own name — "Garden(s) of Bliss" `[37:43]`, `[31:8]`, `[56:12]`, `[10:9]`, `[22:56]`, `[26:85]`, `[70:38]`, `[5:65]`; "amid bliss" `[52:17]`, `[82:13]`, `[83:22]`; `[9:21]` "lasting bliss"; and 101:7 "a pleasant life" — the word for the reward is the same word audited at 102:8: the Bliss questioned is the Bliss aimed at (*Source: observable in corpus renderings*).
* **`questioned` (sū'al):** the Day's verb.
  * **Elsewhere (rg-verified):** `[16:93]` (questioned about deeds), `[55:39]` + `[28:78]` (NOT asked about sins — the counter-formula), `[21:23]` ("He will not be questioned… but they will be questioned"), `[2:119]` (you will not be questioned about the inmates of Hell). 102:8 asks about BLISS specifically — unique object corpus-wide.
