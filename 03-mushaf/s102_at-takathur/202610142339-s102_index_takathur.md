## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** DISPUTED — transmitted lists assign it to both Meccan and Medinan camps (a famous report reads it as revealed about two rival Medinan clans boasting of lineage/numbers; the claim is transmission, not text). Labeled as found. *Source: transmitted type-lists and asbab-reports — verify.* Revelation order: **transmitted nuzul tables — verify** (corpus asserts no nuzul; unnumbered Basmala heads the surah).
* **Size:** 8 ayahs, 1 ruku (ruku_map global 544 — verified). Like 101 and 103: zero "Allah", zero "Lord" in the numbered verses (rg-count 0/0) — the addressee is a "you" with no named questioner; the audit of 102:8 runs in passive voice ("you will be questioned"). The divine-name recession across the batch: 98 (3 lines) → 99 (1 "your Lord") → 100 (2 relational) → 101/102/103 (0).
* **Title:** at-Takathur — corpus header "ABUNDANCE" tracks 102:1 "Abundance distracts you": the verbal noun *takāthur* (competing-in-increase) is rendered as a plain noun ("Abundance"); the rivalry nuance surfaces elsewhere in the corpus only via `[57:20]` "rivalry in wealth and children" (rg-verified).
* **Central theme:** One verb starts the world's distraction ("Abundance distracts you, until you visit the graveyards") and one refrain ends it twice over ("Indeed, you will know. Certainly, you will know."); the conditional ladder — IF you knew with knowledge of certainty, you WOULD see the Inferno — then flips to eschatological indicative: "Then you will see it with the eye of certainty," closing on the day's one question: "you will be questioned about the Bliss" (102:8).
* **Reading hint:** the surah has one subject — a distracted YOU — and shifts the verb-frames: distracted (present), visit (terminus), know (refrain), see (conditional then certain), be questioned (passive end). The reader's own grammar enacts the diversion ending.

## 3. Structural Arc
* **The diagnosis (102:1–2):** distraction by increase, with its deadline — "until you visit the graveyards" (corpus-unique euphemism; "graveyard" occurs nowhere else, rg-verified).
* **The double refrain (102:3–4):** "Indeed, you will know. / Certainly, you will know." — the refrain-twin is `[78:4–5]` "Surely, they will find out. / Most certainly, they will find out." (rg-verified same doubled-refrain engine).
* **The conditional ladder (102:5–6):** "If you knew with knowledge of certainty. / You would see the Inferno." — sight as function of knowledge; the hypothetic unreal.
* **The indicative flip (102:7):** "Then you will see it with the eye of certainty." — the same verb, no longer conditional: certainty becomes organ (an "eye").
* **The audit (102:8):** "Then, on that Day, you will be questioned about the Bliss." — the question motif cluster: `[16:93]` "you will surely be questioned about what you used to do"; `[21:23]` "He will not be questioned… but they will be questioned"; `[28:78]` "the guilty will not be asked about their sins" (all rg-verified).

## 5b. Batch-Return Verification Flags (this index)
* **"No! You will know" — corrected:** the corpus reads "Indeed, you will know. / Certainly, you will know." (102:3–4). Edition A renders this refrain's particle (Arabic *kallā* per standard text — verify) as "Indeed/Certainly," while the same particle elsewhere renders "**By no means**" — rg-verified sites: `[19:82]`, `[23:100]`, `[70:15]`, `[74:16]`, `[104:4]`. The hint's "No!" matches other translations, not this corpus.
* **"diversion/competition formula twins 10:3?/34:?? — REJECTED":** 10:3 (Lordship over creation) and 34:3 (the Hour and the atom's weight) contain no diversion/competition formula; 34:3's atom mention is likely hint-confusion with 99:7–8. VERIFIED diversion-formula twins: `[63:9]` "Let neither your possessions nor your children distract you from the remembrance of Allah. Whoever does that— these are the losers"; `[24:37]` (trade does not distract → prayer+alms); `[57:20]` "play, and distraction, and glitter, and boasting among you, and rivalry in wealth and children"; generic lahw: `[6:32]`, `[29:64]`, heedless hearts `[21:3]` — all rg-verified.
* **Three-step ladder: two steps verified inside 102** ("knowledge of certainty" 102:5; "eye of certainty" 102:7 — exact phrases, corpus-unique pairs); the classical THIRD member (ḥaqq al-yaqīn) corresponds to `[56:95]` "This is the certain truth" (rg-verified phrase; the ladder-ordering itself is classical — *Source: standard tafsir schema — verify*).
* **Final question motif VERIFIED:** "Then, on that Day, you will be questioned about the Bliss" — with "the Bliss" (capitalized definite na‘īm) a corpus-unique object (rg-verified); na‘īm-web: "Gardens/Garden of Bliss" at `[37:43]`, `[31:8]`, `[56:12]`, `[10:9]`, `[22:56]`, `[70:38]`, `[26:85]`, `[5:65]`; "amid bliss" `[52:17]`, `[82:13]`, `[83:22]`; "a pleasant life" `[101:7]` — all rg-verified.
* **Adjacent-surah bridge:** 102:8's audited "Bliss" and 101:7's "pleasant life" are the same na‘īm family — the Fire surah's reward and this surah's audit-object share one word, making 101→102 a single lesson in two orders (rg-verified renderings).
