## 3. Structural Arc
* **Letters, Book, precedent (1–8):** Ha, Meem → the Clear Book sworn, made Arabic, kept in the Source Book → the incredulous question ("Shall We hold back the Reminder…?") → the law of ridicule over every mission → destroyed might as passed example.
* **The creation-court (9–15):** the same pagans' own answers (9) — habitat, pathways, rain, pairs, ships and livestock, the mounting-dua (13–14) — ending in the verdict "Man is clearly ungrateful" (15), the hinge into the daughters-accusation.
* **Two slanders unmade (16–25):** daughters attributed to Him while sons are claimed for you; angels dressed female; the fatalistic excuse (20) and the inheritance-of-error (22–24) answered with vengeance-precedent (25).
* **Abraham's legacy vs the market of rank (26–35):** the disavowal made an enduring word; then the demand that the Quran go to a great man of the two cities, and the silver-and-decorations thought-experiment that names the surah.
* **The companion and the cross-question (36–45):** whoever shuns the Reminder gets a devil-companion; the deaf and blind; the command to adhere — three "straight path" beats begin (43, 61, 64) — and every previous mission is summoned as witness (45).
* **Two case-files (46–67):** Moses vs Pharaoh (plagues, the broken pledge, the Egypt-claim, the drowning, 46–56); Jesus as example rather than son (57–65), closing on the Hour's suddenness and friends at war (66–67).
* **The Day's census and the creedal bracket (68–89):** the servants without fear; trays of gold against demanded bracelets; Malek's verdict; the counter-scheme and the writing messengers; the son-reductio and the Throne-glorification; Allah in heaven and earth; intercession shut; the ring back to 43:9; and the dismissal: "Pardon them, and say, 'Peace.'"
