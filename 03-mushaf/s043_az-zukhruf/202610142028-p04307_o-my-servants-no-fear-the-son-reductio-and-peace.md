# "O My servants, no fear": the trays of gold, Malek's "You are staying," the son-reductio, and the word of Peace (43:68–89)
**UID:** 202610142028
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 43:68–89 (Ruku 7 of 7 in az-Zukhruf)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[43:68]` – `[43:89]` (22 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah's longest ruku holds two auditions at once: the servants' welcome — fear struck from the record, gold served that was mocked as proof — and the criminals' exchange with Malek ("You are staying"); then the present dispute is re-opened one last time (their secrets heard, the writing messengers by them), answered by the great reductio ("If the Most Gracious had a son, I would be the first to worship") and a triple doxology (heaven-and-earth Allah, blessed sovereignty, intercession shut), and finally released: the unnamed complaint "My Lord, these are a people who do not believe" met with "Pardon them, and say, 'Peace.' They will come to know."
* **Placement:** Ruku 7 of 7; the surah's closing bracket. Every ledger-word returns: gold (53→71), the companion-audit (38→67), the Hour (61, 66→85), the creation-court (9→87), and the mockery-motif (7→83, 89) — the surah ends not with the mockers' punishment but with its postponement and the prophet's dismissal into peace.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[43:68]`** "O My servants, you have nothing to fear on that Day, nor will you grieve." — **Gloss:** The address that answers 43:67's exception: the righteous' friendship with God spoken in the second person; the fear-and-grief formula verbatim-twin of `[2:38]` and `[2:62]`.
* **`[43:69]`** "Those who believed in Our revelations, and were submissive." — **Gloss:** The category spelled out: belief in the revelations (the Book of 43:2–4) plus humility — who the "My servants" are.
* **`[43:70]`** "Enter the Garden, you and your spouses, Joyfully." — **Gloss:** Entry as imperative and reunion with spouses (cf. `[36:56]` "They and their spouses, in shades"); the capital-J "Joyfully" is the book's typography.
* **`[43:71]`** "They will be served around with trays of gold, and cups. Therein is whatever the souls desire and delights the eyes. Therein you will stay forever."
  * **Gloss:** The gold surah refused as evidence (43:53) becomes the Garden's service-ware; "whatever the souls desire" answers `[16:31]` "they will have whatever they desire"; "trays of gold" is corpus-unique (rg-verified).
* **`[43:72]`** "Such is the Garden you are made to inherit, because of what you used to do." — **Gloss:** Verbatim-twin of `[7:43]` — inheritance-as-earned, collecting 43:35's promise that the Hereafter "is for the righteous."
* **`[43:73]`** "Therein you will have abundant fruit, from which you eat." — **Gloss:** The table-suites continue; sustenance without season.
* **`[43:74]`** "As for the sinners, they will be in the torment of Hell forever." — **Gloss:** The census flips; "forever" is now assigned to the other register.
* **`[43:75]`** "It will never be eased for them. In it, they will be devastated." — **Gloss:** Two clauses: no relief, no exit — the companion's party ends in despair (cf. `[6:44]` "at once, they were in despair").
* **`[43:76]`** "We did them no injustice, but it was they who were the unjust." — **Gloss:** The theodicy clause of this edition (cf. `[40:31]` "Allah wants no injustice for the servants," `[3:108]`).
* **`[43:77]`** "And they will cry, “O Malek, let your Lord finish us off.” He will say, “You are staying.”"
  * **Gloss:** The Fire's bailiff named — "Malek" occurs nowhere else in this corpus (rg-verified); his reply, two words, reverses their request: what they ask for (termination) is denied them (permanence). *Source: classical tradition identifies Malik as the angel-keeper of Hell — verify in print.*
* **`[43:78]`** "We have given you the truth, but most of you hate the truth."
  * **Gloss:** The address swings back to the living; near-verbatim twin `[23:70]` "he brought them the truth, but most of them hate the truth."
* **`[43:79]`** "Have they contrived some scheme? We too are contriving."
  * **Gloss:** Counter-scheme (makr-family): this edition elsewhere renders the same field as "planned" (`[8:30]` "They planned, and Allah planned") and "scheme" (`[10:21]` "Allah is swifter in scheming") — a rendering drift worth watching.
* **`[43:80]`** "Or do they think that We cannot hear their secrets and their conspiracies? Yes indeed, Our messengers are by them, writing down."
  * **Gloss:** The surah's own surveillance-answer (no eavesdropping-jinn here, contra the task's query): near-twin `[10:21]` "Our envoys are writing down what you scheme"; cf. `[50:17–18]`, `[82:10–12]` — and this retires the "recorded, and questioned" of `[43:19]`.
* **`[43:81]`** "Say, “If the Most Gracious had a son, I would be the first to worship.”"
  * **Gloss:** The reductio par excellence: the speaker hypothetically concedes the premise ("if… had a son") to expose the conclusion as impossible — worship would be due the claim, yet it cannot be. Ledger ✓: at 43:81, but the corpus names "the Most Gracious" (not Merciful) and "the first to worship" (not "of worshippers"). Counter-clause: `[19:92]` "It is not fitting for the Most Merciful to have a son"; `[6:101]` "how can He have a son when He never had a companion?"
* **`[43:82]`** "Glorified be the Lord of the heavens and the earth, the Lord of the Throne, beyond what they describe."
  * **Gloss:** The doxology the task mis-placed as the surah's ending — it is here, mid-ruku; near-verbatim twin `[21:22]` "glory be to Allah, Lord of the Throne, beyond what they allege"; Throne-names cf. `[17:42]`, `[81:20]`.
* **`[43:83]`** "So leave them to blunder and play, until they encounter their Day which they are promised."
  * **Gloss:** Verbatim-twin `[70:42]` ("So leave them to blunder and play, until they meet their Day which they are promised"); the postponement-of-seizure the task sought at 43:47 lives here instead; cf. `[6:44]` sudden seizure after play.
* **`[43:84]`** "It is He who is Allah in heaven, and Allah on earth. He is the Wise, the Knower." — **Gloss:** Corpus-unique double locative: the name "Allah" is His in both domains — the court's names (43:9 "the Mighty, the Knower") sealed.
* **`[43:85]`** "And blessed is He Who has sovereignty over the heavens and the earth and what is between them. He alone has knowledge of the Hour, and to Him you will be returned."
  * **Gloss:** The real sovereignty-answer to Pharaoh's `mulk` (43:51); Hour-knowledge reserved: cf. `[31:34]`, `[41:47]`, and 43:61's portent; "to Him you will be returned" — 43:14's mounting-dua, now verdict.
* **`[43:86]`** "Those they invoke besides Him are incapable of intercession; only those who testify to the truth and have knowledge." — **Gloss:** Intercession shut against the invoked ones, with the exception-clause: testimony to the truth + knowledge (cf. `[53:26]`, `[20:109]`, `[2:255]` — intercession only by permission).
* **`[43:87]`** "And if you asked them, “Who created them?”, they would say, “Allah.” Why then do they deviate?" — **Gloss:** The creation-court's ring closes (43:9); cf. `[31:25]` — the deviation named where the answer is already given.
* **`[43:88]`** "As for his statement: “My Lord, these are a people who do not believe.”"
  * **Gloss:** A quotation introduced without a named speaker — the corpus leaves "his" open; classical readers hear a messenger's complaint (Noah-type twin plea: `[26:117]` "My Lord, my people have denied me"). *Source: classical tafsir disagreement on the referent — agent note: corpus gives no antecedent; quote as-is.*
* **`[43:89]`** "Pardon them, and say, “Peace.” They will come to know."
  * **Gloss:** The surah's last word is amnesty and a greeting, with the threat left to knowledge-to-come (cf. `[15:96]`, `[29:66]`, `[6:135]` "You will come to know"); "Pardon them, and say, 'Peace'" echoes `[7:199]` ("Be tolerant… and avoid the ignorant") and the greeting of guidance in `[20:47]`.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`nothing to fear, nor grieve`**: the formula of the saved.
  * **Rendering:** "you have nothing to fear on that Day, nor will you grieve" (43:68).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:38]`, `[2:62]` (and the 2:112 cluster) — the same formula given to Adam's progeny and to every believer who does good.
* **`Malek`**: the addressee in the Fire.
  * **Rendering:** "O Malek, let your Lord finish us off" (43:77) — corpus-unique name-form (rg-verified); *Source: classical tafsir — the keeper of Hell.*
  * **Elsewhere:** the answer "You are staying" performs the reversal; cf. the unanswered cry in `[7:38]`-type crowd-scenes.
* **`shafa'a (intercession)`**: the closed door.
  * **Rendering:** "incapable of intercession; only those who testify to the truth and have knowledge" (43:86).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[53:26]` (angelic intercession useless without permission), `[20:109]` (on that Day it will not avail), `[2:255]` ("who can intercede with Him except by His permission").
* **`salām and "they will come to know"`**: the closing pair.
  * **Rendering:** "say, 'Peace.' They will come to know." (43:89).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[20:47]` "peace be upon him who follows guidance"; `[15:96]`, `[29:66]`, `[6:135]` — the deferred-knowledge formula.
