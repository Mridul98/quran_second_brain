# The assigned devil-companion, the deaf and the blind, and the question put to every prior mission (43:36–45)
**UID:** 202610142025
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 43:36–45 (Ruku 4 of 7 in az-Zukhruf)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[43:36]` – `[43:45]` (10 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Whoever shuns the Reminder is given a devil as companion — one he thinks is guiding him — and the ruku then states the prophet's limit (can you make the deaf hear?) before pivoting to the surah's command-scene: adhere to what is revealed, *you are upon a straight path*, a message you will be questioned about; and for its content the whole prior apostolate is summoned as witness: "Did We appoint gods besides the Most Gracious to be worshiped?"
* **Placement:** Ruku 4 of 7; the hinge between the creed-arguments (1–35) and the two case-files (Moses 46–56, Jesus 57–67). The evil companion (38) is eschatologically re-served at 43:67 ("friends will be enemies of one another"), and the cross-question (45) answers 43:21's book-demand.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[43:36]`** "Whoever shuns the remembrance of the Most Gracious, We assign for him a devil, to be his companion."
  * **Gloss:** The counter-portion to the Reminder: not abandonment but assignment — a devil as *qarin*/companion; cf. `[20:124]` — whoever turns from the Reminder gets "a confined life" and is raised blind.
* **`[43:37]`** "They hinder them from the path, though they think they are guided."
  * **Gloss:** The definition of misguidance at work: obstruction plus self-deception — the state that 43:20's fatalism and 43:22's inherited course both describe from inside.
* **`[43:38]`** "Until, when He comes to Us, he will say, “If only there were between me and you the distance of the two Easts.” What an evil companion!"
  * **Gloss:** The reunion scene: the companion becomes the wish-against; "the distance of the two Easts" pairs with `[55:17]` "Lord of the two Easts and Lord of the two Wests" — same idiom, opposite valence.
* **`[43:39]`** "It will not benefit you on that Day, since you did wrong. You are partners in the suffering."
  * **Gloss:** The companionship dissolves into shared penalty; cf. `[7:38]` — crowds of jinn and humans entering the Fire together, each cursing its sister-crowd.
* **`[43:40]`** "Can you make the deaf hear, or guide the blind, and him who is in evident error?"
  * **Gloss:** The prophet's limit-formula, verbatim in kind: `[30:52–53]` ("you cannot make the deaf hear the call… nor guide the blind"), `[27:80–81]`, `[10:42–43]`; and the blind-raising of 20:124 is the same image at judgment.
* **`[43:41]`** "Even if We take you away, We will wreak vengeance upon them."
  * **Gloss:** The mission does not depend on the messenger's presence; vengeance is deferred, not cancelled (cf. 43:25).
* **`[43:42]`** "Or show you what We have promised them; for We have absolute power over them."
  * **Gloss:** The alternative: he may live to see it — "for We have absolute power over them"; the either/or is verbatim-typed at `[13:40]` ("Whether We show you some of what We have promised them, or We cause you to die—your duty is to inform, and Ours is the reckoning").
* **`[43:43]`** "So adhere to what is revealed to you. You are upon a straight path."
  * **Gloss:** The command-scene: hold fast to the revealed Book — the first of the surah's three "straight path/way" beats (43, 61, 64); cf. `[3:101]` "Whoever cleaves to Allah has been guided to a straight path."
* **`[43:44]`** "It is a message for you, and for your people; and you will be questioned."
  * **Gloss:** The Book is *dhikr* — honor and audit in one breath (echoing 43:5's "Reminder"); questioning is the surah's refrain (19, 45, 89's "They will come to know").
* **`[43:45]`** "Ask those of Our messengers We sent before you: “Did We appoint gods besides the Most Gracious to be worshiped?”"
  * **Gloss:** The whole apostolate cross-examined as one witness — verbatim in substance `[21:25]` ("no messenger… without inspiring him: There is no god but I, so worship Me") and `[16:36]` ("Worship Allah, and avoid idolatry"). Ledger: the task's "Ask any of Our messengers" is this verse; wording "those of Our messengers"; "gods besides the Most *Gracious*" (not Merciful).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`qarin`-companion (the assigned devil)**: companionship as punishment.
  * **Rendering:** "We assign for him a devil, to be his companion" (43:36); "What an evil companion!" (43:38).
  * **Elsewhere:** within s043, 43:67 "On that Day, friends will be enemies of one another, except for the righteous" — the same bond read at its end; cf. the paired-crowd cursing of `[7:38]`.
* **`two Easts` (masharayn)**: the idiom of maximal distance.
  * **Rendering:** "the distance of the two Easts" (43:38).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[55:17]` "Lord of the two Easts and Lord of the two Wests" — rg-verified as the only other corpus occurrence.
* **`deaf/blind` limit-formula**: prophetic power bounded.
  * **Rendering:** "Can you make the deaf hear, or guide the blind, and him who is in evident error?" (43:40).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[30:52–53]`, `[27:80–81]`, `[10:42–43]` — the triple-twin cluster; `[20:124]` raises the turner-from-Reminder blind at the Resurrection.
* **`sirat mustaqim` (straight path)**: 43:43, this edition "a straight path".
  * **Rendering:** "You are upon a straight path" (43:43); "This is a straight way" (43:61); "this is a straight path" (43:64) — one term, two renderings inside the surah.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[1:6]` the petition; `[3:101]` cleaving = guided to it.
