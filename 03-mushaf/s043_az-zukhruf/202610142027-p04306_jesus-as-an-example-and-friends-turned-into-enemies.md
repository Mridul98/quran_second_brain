# Jesus cited as an example, the portent of the Hour, and friends turned to enemies (43:57–67)
**UID:** 202610142027
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 43:57–67 (Ruku 6 of 7 in az-Zukhruf)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[43:57]` – `[43:67]` (11 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The second case-file: when "the son of Mary" is cited as the precedent-word of 43:56 was meant to be used — an example — his people "opposed" and spat back "Are our gods better, or he?"; the divine reply demotes the dispute itself (he was *just a servant whom We blessed*), re-credits him with the only two things a creature can carry — a portent pointing to the Hour and a straight-path creed — and closes on factions, woe, and the friendship-audit of that Day.
* **Placement:** Ruku 6 of 7; the Jesus dossier the canonical isa note already flags as Zukhruf's anchor cluster (43:57–65); its "example" chain reaches back through 43:56 to 43:8, and its 43:64 is verbatim the ministry-creed of `[3:51]`; the straight-path triplet (43, 61, 64) completes here; the friends-as-enemies verdict (67) cashes the evil-companion promise (38).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[43:57]`** "And when the son of Mary was cited as an example, your people opposed."
  * **Gloss:** The *mathal* word turns: what was an example of destruction (56) becomes, in the dispute-market, "an example" they will not accept; "your people" — the address shifts to the Prophet, tying the scene to the mockery-motif.
* **`[43:58]`** "They said, “Are our gods better, or he?” They cited him only for argument. In fact, they are a quarrelsome people."
  * **Gloss:** The counter-slam: pitted gods against the man; the narrator diagnoses not error but *mood* — "only for argument… a quarrelsome people" (cf. `[40:4]` the arguers).
* **`[43:59]`** "He was just a servant whom We blessed, and We made him an example for the Children of Israel."
  * **Gloss:** The correction in one predicate-chain: *abd* (servant), blessed (gifted from outside himself), example (for Israel, not deity) — the isa note's demotion-clause, quoted there verbatim.
* **`[43:60]`** "Had We willed, We would have made of you angels to be successors on earth."
  * **Gloss:** The angel-swap counterfactual: no special ontology is needed to serve as God's vicegerents — successors (*khalā'if*-type: cf. `[6:165]` "He who made you successors on the earth," `[27:62]`); and it quietly deflates the angel-veneration of 43:19 from the other side.
* **`[43:61]`** "He is a portent of the Hour, so have no doubt about it, and follow Me. This is a straight way."
  * **Gloss:** Jesus (pronoun referent classically contested — see §6) is a *signpost* toward the Hour, not its lord; "have no doubt about it" — the Hour's certainty without its timetable (cf. `[43:85]` "He alone has knowledge of the Hour"); second straight-way beat.
* **`[43:62]`** "And let not Satan divert you. He is an open enemy to you."
  * **Gloss:** The old enemy named after the devil-companion of 43:36 — here open, not intimate.
* **`[43:63]`** "When Jesus came with the clarifications, he said, “I have come to you with wisdom, and to clarify for you some of what you differ about. So fear Allah, and obey me."
  * **Gloss:** His self-commission: clarifications (the *mubin* family of 43:2), wisdom, partial clarification of dispute — and the twin commands "fear Allah, and obey me"; quotation opens here, runs to 43:64 (class-2 typography, quote as-is).
* **`[43:64]`** "Allah is my Lord and your Lord, so worship Him—this is a straight path.”"
  * **Gloss:** The ministry-creed, verbatim-twin of `[3:51]` (also cited in k-isa alongside 5:72): third straight-path beat — the prophet's God is above both him and his hearers; the em-dash is the book's.
* **`[43:65]`** "But the factions differed among themselves. So woe to the wrongdoers from the suffering of a painful Day."
  * **Gloss:** The follow-up history: the sects split; near-verbatim twin `[19:37]` ("But the various factions differed among themselves. So woe to those who disbelieve from the scene of a tremendous Day") — same Jesus-context, Maryam's version.
* **`[43:66]`** "Are they only waiting for the Hour to come upon them suddenly, while they are unaware?"
  * **Gloss:** Ledger ✓: the task asked what 43 actually has near the "projectile" verse — this is it: no eavesdropping-jinn, but the sudden-Hour warning; verbatim-twin `[47:18]` ("Are they just waiting until the Hour comes to them suddenly?").
* **`[43:67]`** "On that Day, friends will be enemies of one another, except for the righteous."
  * **Gloss:** The friendship-audit; cashes `[43:38]` ("What an evil companion!") — every companionship not built on taqwa converts to enmity at the reckoning; the exception clause names the only surviving bond.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`mathal` (example)**: the ruku's ring-word (56→57→59).
  * **Rendering:** "cited as an example" (57); "We made him an example for the Children of Israel" (59).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[25:37]` ("made them a lesson for mankind" — the drowning precedent's twin); `[43:8]` the ancients' passed example — the surah runs one *mathal* theology end to end.
* **`abd` (servant)**: Jesus's office.
  * **Rendering:** "He was just a servant whom We blessed" (43:59).
  * **Elsewhere:** the canonical isa note cites this verse as the demotion clause; cf. `[19:30]` — the crib-word "I am the servant of Allah"; the same title grounds the angels-are-*servants* argument at `[43:19]`.
* **`khalifa/successors on earth`**: human vicegerency.
  * **Rendering:** "angels to be successors on earth" (43:60).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[6:165]` ("He who made you successors on the earth"), `[27:62]` ("makes you successors on earth?") — the office is Adam-kind's, angelic or otherwise.
* **`sirat mustaqim`**: third and fourth strikes — "a straight way" (43:61), "a straight path" (43:64).
  * **Rendering:** drift between "way" (61) and "path" (64) inside one ruku.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[3:51]` verbatim twin of 43:64; `[1:6]` the petition; `[43:43]` the command-scene's first beat.
