# Surah 66: PROHIBITION (at-Tahrim)
**UID:** 202610142207
**Type:** Index / Surah Map
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Medinan — agreed case in the type-lists. *Source: classical type-lists — verify.* The corpus carries no nuzul statement; no revelation-order number is guessed here (this brain's chain pins slots only for 91/104/109/110). The unnumbered Basmala heads the surah.
* **Size:** 12 ayahs, 2 ruku. Third member of the household triad (64–66): 64 named the family a trial; 65 legislated its endings; 66 stages the Prophet's own marriage under direct divine reproof — then widens every lesson outward to all households, and upward to four women who out-argue all of it.
* **Name:** at-Tahrim — the name-word sits in the rebuke-question `[66:1]` "Why do you prohibit what Allah has permitted for you?" The same verb-pair occurs exactly once more, as communal law: `[5:87]` "Do not prohibit the good things Allah has permitted for you." This surah's case is interrogated, not legislated — the law for the ummah stays general while the Prophet's own house is corrected in public recitation. *Source: translator's term choice (observable in corpus); rg-verified pair.*
* **Central theme:** Revelation inside the prophet-household: a self-imposed prohibition reproved, oaths dissolved by decree, a confidence told and disclosed (66:1–3); two conspiring addressees — never named in the text — offered repentance or warned of the counter-alliance of Allah, Gabriel, the righteous believers and the angels, with exchange by "wives better than you" floated (66:4–5). The camera then pans to every family: protect yourselves and your families from the Fire whose fuel is people and stones (66:6), where excuses end (66:7). Ruku 2 reopens the door — sincere repentance and a light its bearers ask to be completed (66:8) — charges the strive against disbelievers and hypocrites (66:9), and closes with the four-women paradigm (66:10–12): prophet-wives lost inside a righteous house, Pharaoh's wife saved inside a tyrant's house, and Maryam — guarded, Spirit-breathed, believing His Words — the surah's last word.

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Reproof and decree (66:1–2):** the question "Why do you prohibit?" names the motive — "seeking to please your wives" — and both halves close on acquittal-names: Forgiving and Merciful; Master, All-Knowing, Most Wise.
* **The confidence narrative (66:3):** the only "in confidence" in the corpus; the Prophet "communicated part of it, and he avoided another part" — tact inside revelation's transcript; her "Who informed you of this?" answered: "The All-Knowing, the All-Informed."
* **The two-address fork (66:4–5):** repent — or band together, against Allah, Gabriel, the righteous believers, the angels; and the exchange-clause: "wives better than you," six virtues, "previously married, or virgins."
* **The household pivot (66:6–7):** from the prophet's families to all families; the angels "never disobey... and they carry out whatever they are commanded" — perfect households as the fire's guards, set against the malfunctioning one in the transcript; then the Day's closing of excuses.
* **Return and light (66:8):** the corpus's only "sincere repentance"; gardens, the Prophet not disappointed, light running ahead — the juz's light-chain (64:8 → 65:11 → 66:8) reaching its fulfilled image.
* **Strive and paradigm (66:9–12):** the 9:73-twin command, then four women as the surah's verdict-graph — marriage to righteousness does not save; faith inside tyranny does; and the final figure is a virgin-mother from the family of Imran, not a wife at all.

## 5b. Batch-Return Verification Flags (this index)
* Hint wording: "why prohibit what Allah made lawful" — corpus `[66:1]` reads "what Allah has **permitted** for you"; "made lawful" is this edition's halala-verb elsewhere (e.g., `[5:5]` "Today all good things are made lawful for you").
* Hint's "oath-expiation clause": corpus `[66:2]` "Allah has decreed for you the **dissolution** of your oaths" — the "expiation/atonement" (kaffarah) reading is classical fiqh, and that word belongs to `[5:89]` ("The atonement for it is by feeding ten needy people..."). Both kept in view in the ruku-1 note. *Source: classical tafsir — verify.*
* Hint's "the two unnamed conspiring wives": precise but worth flagging — the text never names them and never says "two wives": `[66:3]` has "one of his wives" (singular); the pair enters only via the dual address `[66:4]` "if you band together against him." The classical identification is Aisha and Hafsa (honey-hadith, Bukhari; rival Maria reports exist) — *Source: classical reports — verify*; recorded, not asserted.
* Hint's 66:11 quote "build me a house near You in the Garden" — corpus: "build for me, **with you**, a house in **Paradise**" (`[66:11]`).
* Hint's 66:12 quote "guarded her chastity... testified to His words" — corpus: "who guarded her **womb**... she **believed in the truth of** her Lord’s **Words and His Books**, and was one of the devout" (`[66:12]`). Cognate drift rg-verified: `[21:91]` "guarded her virginity"; `[33:35]` "guard their chastity" — one root, three nouns.
* Four-women paradigm 66:10–12 ✓ verified exactly as hinted: Noah's wife and Lot's wife (disbelief-examples), Pharaoh's wife (belief-example, unnamed), Maryam named with patronymic ("daughter of Imran," answering `[3:35]` "The wife of Imran"). Isa-dossier junction confirmed at "Our Spirit" / "His Words" — k-isa forward link recorded above.
