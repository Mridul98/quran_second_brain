# The Clear Evidence ends inertia: one ancient command, two species of creature (98:1–8)
**UID:** 202610142332
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 98:1–8 (Ruku 1 of 1 in al-Bayyinah)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[98:1]` – `[98:8]` (8 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Revelation's arrival is an ending-of-inertia: the disbelieving wings of Scripture-people and Polytheists "were not apart, until the Clear Evidence came," and the same Evidence that should unite exposes the splintering — against which the surah sets the oldest, smallest command (exclusive worship, prayer, alms: "That is the upright religion") and then sorts all of history into "the worst of creatures" and "the best of creatures," closing the best in Gardens of Eternity and God's two-sided pleasure, gated on fear of the Lord.
* **Placement:** Ruku 1 of 1; the batch's hinge surah — the last of the six whose verses name Allah explicitly. It opens the 98–103 run with a definition of religion-as-minimum, which 99–103 then apply as audit (deeds weighed, ingratitude named, distraction indicted, loss diagnosed).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[98:1]`** "Those who disbelieved among the People of the Scripture, and the Polytheists, were not apart, until the Clear Evidence came to them."
  * **Gloss:** Two camps defined by unbelief, one shared verb of inertia — "were not apart" (this edition's striking rendering of Arabic *munfakkīn*, "separate/distinct"): they did not separate, resolve, or move off their position until Evidence forced the issue (Source: translator's term choice — observable; standard reading is "would not leave their religion / be separated till the evidence came" — verify).
* **`[98:2]`–`[98:3]`** "A messenger from Allah reciting purified scripts. / In them are valuable writings."
  * **Gloss:** The Evidence unpacked in three descending beats: a human messenger, from Allah, whose act is READING — over scrolls characterized as "purified" and filled with "valuable writings." Revelation here is recited text, not spectacle.
* **`[98:4]`** "Those who were given the Scripture did not splinter, except after the Clear Evidence came to them."
  * **Gloss:** The 98:1 frame returns with a fault-line verb: *splinter* (tafarruq). Division arrives AFTER evidence — timing makes it culpable, not accidental (rg: "splinter" as communal rupture only here and `[21:93]` "they splintered themselves into factions"; 2:74 uses the word of rocks).
* **`[98:5]`** "They were commanded only to worship Allah, devoting their faith to Him alone, and to practice regular prayer, and to give alms. That is the upright religion."
  * **Gloss:** The ancient minimum, stated as what they were ALREADY commanded: worship + sincerity ("devoting their faith to Him alone," *muḥliṣīna lahu ad-dīn ḥunafāʾ*) + prayer + alms; labeled "the upright religion" (*ad-dīn al-qayyim*) — the same label God's own path carries in `[6:161]`.
* **`[98:6]`** "Those who disbelieve among the People of the Scripture, and the Polytheists, will be in the Fire of Hell, where they will abide forever. These are the worst of creatures."
  * **Gloss:** The 98:1 mirror at verdict-speed; the demotion is onto their whole species-status: "worst of creatures" (*sharr barīya*) — the same phrase-frame used of disbelievers at `[8:55]` "The worst of creatures in Allah's view are those who disbelieve" (rg-verified twin).
* **`[98:7]`** "As for those who believe and lead a righteous life—these are the best of creatures."
  * **Gloss:** The counter-species: belief + life-righteousness; "best of creatures" (*khayr barīya*) — corpus-unique rendering (rg-verified); its constitutional neighbor is `[3:110]` "You are the best community that ever emerged for humanity" (the communal mirror of 98:7's creature-language — Source: agent inference — verify).
* **`[98:8]`** "Their reward is with their Lord: Gardens of Eternity beneath which rivers flow, where they will abide forever. Allah is pleased with them, and they are pleased with Him. That is for whoever fears His Lord."
  * **Gloss:** Reward located "with their Lord"; the dwelling-phrase "Gardens of Eternity" occurs only here and `[40:8]` (rg-verified); the centerpiece is mutual pleasure — God "pleased with them, and they with Him," the riḍwān cluster also at `[9:100]`, `[58:22]`, `[5:119]` (all rg-verified); the surah's final gate is a posture: "That is for whoever fears His Lord" (corpus-unique closing line — rg-verified).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`al-Bayyinah` ("the Clear Evidence")**: the arriving fact that ends excusable inertia.
  * **Rendering:** capitalized "Clear Evidence" (98:1, 98:4) — phrase unique to these two sites corpus-wide (rg-verified).
  * **Elsewhere:** classical identification of the Evidence as the Messenger/the Quran is standard tafsir — *Source: consensus-type reading — verify*; the corpus itself leaves the referent open inside 98:2's unpacking.
* **`qayyim` — one root, two renderings in one passage:** "valuable writings" (98:3) and "the upright religion" (98:5).
  * **Rendering:** drift is observable (rg): *qayyim* as "valuable" for texts, "upright" for religion. The upright-religion line twins `[6:161]` "an upright religion, the creed of Abraham" and `[30:43]` "devote yourself to the upright religion" — the 98:5 "upright" is thus load-bearing in k-guidance's uprightness web.
* **`devoting their faith to Him alone`** (ikhlaṣ + ḥanīf grammar): religion stripped of partners.
  * **Rendering:** one clause carries both words (Source: translator's compression — observable).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[29:65]` "they pray to Allah, devoting their faith to Him" — the sea-storm sincerity that evaporates ashore (rg-verified exact-phrase site); the *ḥanīf* family renders as "the Monotheist" of Abraham: `[2:135]`, `[3:67]`, `[6:161]`, `[16:123]` (rg-verified).
* **`"and to give alms"`** (zakāt): the third pillar-item of the minimum.
  * **Rendering:** "alms" here — the same phrase-pair "performing the prayers, and from giving alms" at `[24:37]` (rg-verified), zakat-as-purification at `[9:103]` (k-infraq's anchor).
* **`"pleased with them, and they are pleased with Him"`** (riḍwān reciprocity): salvation as two-directional satisfaction.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[9:100]` (the Pioneers), `[58:22]` (loyal believers — "These are the partisans of Allah"), `[5:119]` ("the great attainment") — rg-verified cluster; 98:8 unique in gating it on FEAR ("whoever fears His Lord").
