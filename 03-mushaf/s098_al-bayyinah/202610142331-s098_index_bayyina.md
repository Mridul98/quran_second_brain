## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** DISPUTED — the transmitted type-lists split: the majority label it Medinan, a transmitted minority says Meccan. Both readings have textual traction: the addressees "the People of the Scripture, and the Polytheists" (98:1) suit the Medinan arena, while the compact sorting-form suits the Meccan reckoning-run. Not resolved here; labeled as found. *Source: transmitted type-lists — verify.* Revelation order: **transmitted nuzul tables — verify** (the corpus asserts no nuzul; unnumbered Basmala heads the surah).
* **Size:** 8 ayahs, 1 ruku (ruku_map global 540 — verified). The Allah-dense hinge of the batch: the divine name "Allah" appears in 3 numbered verses here (98:2/5/8, rg-verified), while in 99–103 it appears in NO numbered verse — 99 runs on "your Lord" (99:5), 100 on "his/Their Lord" (100:6/11), and 101/102/103 carry neither "Allah" nor "Lord" in any verse. Surah 98 is the last fully named theology before the unnamed audit (Source: structural observation on rg counts).
* **Title:** al-Bayyinah — the corpus header "CLEAR EVIDENCE" tracks "**the Clear Evidence**," a phrase occurring at 98:1 and 98:4 only, corpus-wide (rg-verified). The surah is named for what arrives and ends inertia.
* **Central theme:** Two camps — Scripture-people and Polytheists — "were not apart, until the Clear Evidence came to them" (98:1): a messenger from Allah reciting purified scripts (98:2–3). The schism that follows evidence, not precedes it (98:4), is answered by a minimum that is also the oldest: worship Allah alone, prayer, alms — "That is the upright religion" (98:5). History then splits into two species of creature — worst and best (98:6–7) — and the best are given Gardens of Eternity and the mutual pleasure of God, "That is for whoever fears His Lord" (98:8).
* **Reading hint:** follow "until/except after" (98:1, 98:4) — evidence converts latent division into culpable division; then notice 98:5's four items read like the answer to 98:1's two camps: the same "religion" the Scripture-people were already commanded, stripped of added history.

## 3. Structural Arc
* **The inertia and the arrival (98:1–3):** "were not apart, until the Clear Evidence came" — then the Evidence is unpacked: a messenger, reciting, purified scripts, "In them are valuable writings."
* **The after-timing of schism (98:4):** "did not splinter, except after the Clear Evidence came" — the same phrase-frame returns, now with fault attached.
* **The ancient minimum (98:5):** worship Allah, devoting faith to Him alone + regular prayer + alms — labeled "That is the upright religion."
* **The sorting (98:6–7):** the 98:1 mirror re-run at verdict-speed: Fire and "the worst of creatures" ↔ belief, "lead a righteous life," and "the best of creatures."
* **The reward and its gate (98:8):** Gardens of Eternity; "Allah is pleased with them, and they are pleased with Him"; close on the fear-gate: "That is for whoever fears His Lord" — the surah's last word is a human posture, not a decree (Source: agent observation on corpus renderings).
