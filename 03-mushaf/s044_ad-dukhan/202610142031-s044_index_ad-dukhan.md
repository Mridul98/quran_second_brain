## 3. Structural Arc
* **Book → smoke → precedent (1–29):** the oath-chain (Enlightening Scripture / Blessed Night / wise command / mercy) grounds the warner's authority; doubt "plays around" (9); the smoke day and its conditional cry (10–12) are answered "how can they be reminded?" (13); then the Pharaoh dossier from demand to drowning (17–24), ending on the inventory of abandoned luxury (25–29).
* **Election and its mirror (30–42):** rescue, choosing "knowingly over all other people," "many signs, in which was an obvious test" (30–33) — then "These people say" the one-death creed (34–36), Tubba' as counter-case (37), and the cosmic premise "not created... to play... only for a specific purpose" (38–39) cashing out as the Day of Sorting where friendship avails nothing (40–42).
* **Two destinations rendered (43–57):** zaqqum, the sinner's food, molten-lead bellies, the seize-and-pour interrogation "Taste! You who were powerful and noble" (43–50); then the righteous in "a secure place," gardens and springs, silk and brocade, wed to lovely companions, no death beyond the first, "the supreme salvation" (51–57).
* **The book's own defense (58–59):** "We made it easy in your language, so that they may remember" — the Blessed-Night opening (3) resolves into plain speech — and the surah ends where ruku 1's oath ended: mutual waiting ("So wait and watch," 59).
* **Ring of grace:** "As mercy from your Lord" (6) → the mercy-exception on the Sorting Day ("Except for him upon whom Allah has mercy. He is the Mighty, the Merciful," 42) → "A favor from your Lord" (57): mercy-names bracket might-names across the whole surah.
* **Three named days, one deadline:** the smoke day (10–11), the Day of the Great Assault (16), the Day of Sorting Out (40) — plus Tubba's annihilation and Pharaoh's drowning as already-run precedents; the surah's warning counts down different names of the same verdict.
