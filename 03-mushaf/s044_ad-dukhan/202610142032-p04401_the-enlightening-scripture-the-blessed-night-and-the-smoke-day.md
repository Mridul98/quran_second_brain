# The Enlightening Scripture, the Blessed Night, and the smoke day (44:1–29)
**UID:** 202610142032
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 44:1–29 (Ruku 1 of 3 in ad-Dukhan)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[44:1]` – `[44:29]` (29 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** An oath-sworn Book descends on a Blessed Night from the Lord who "gives life and causes death" — and the same people who call the enlightening messenger "crazy" will one day cry "lift the torment from us"; the ruku seals the argument with Pharaoh: his gardens, plantations and comforts left behind, "passed on to another people," unmourned by heaven and earth.
* **Placement:** Ruku 1 of 3; the surah's full apparatus (Book → warner → smoke day → precedent) in a single sweep, setting up the election-and-creed debate of ruku 2 and the two-destinations finale of ruku 3.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[44:1]`** "Ha, Meem." — **Gloss:** Muqatta'at letters, transliterated and untranslated, numbered as ayah 1; head of the Ha-Meem family (all seven openings of 40–46 rg-verified: 40:1, 41:1, 42:1, 43:1, 44:1, 45:1, 46:1). See Fleeting link.
* **`[44:2]`** "By the Enlightening Scripture." — **Gloss:** The oath is sworn on the Book itself; ledger: the task's "By the Book that makes things clear" is `[43:2]`'s rendering of the same Arabic phrase — here Itani prints "Enlightening Scripture" (see Key Terms).
* **`[44:3]`** "We have revealed it on a Blessed Night— We have warned." — **Gloss:** The night-descent (the dash-then-space is book typography, quote as-is); twins in-corpus: `[97:1]` "We sent it down on the Night of Decree" and `[2:185]` "Ramadan is the month in which the Quran was revealed."
* **`[44:4]`** "In it is distinguished every wise command." — **Gloss:** The night is when the year's decrees are sorted out; "wise command" is corpus-unique in this form (rg); cf. `[97:4]` "with every command."
* **`[44:5]`** "A decree from Us. We have been sending messages." — **Gloss:** The sender signs the descent; "We have been sending" keeps the warners' line running until it is cited against them at 44:13.
* **`[44:6]`** "As mercy from your Lord. He is the Hearer, the Knower." — **Gloss:** Revelation framed as mercy, and the hearing/knowing names attached; the exact name-pair recurs at `[2:127]`, `[2:137]`, `[5:76]`, `[6:115]`, `[8:61]`, `[10:65]`, `[12:34]`, `[21:4]`, `[41:36]` (rg).
* **`[44:7]`** "Lord of the heavens and the earth and what is between them, if you know for sure." — **Gloss:** Lordship formula; the identical "Lord of the heavens and the earth and what is between them" is printed verbatim at `[19:65]`; the "what is between them" span recurs at `[5:17]`, `[43:85]`, `[50:38]`, `[30:8]` and in this surah's own creation-verse `[44:38]` (all rg-verified).
* **`[44:8]`** "There is no god but He. He gives life and causes death—your Lord and Lord of your ancestors of old." — **Gloss:** The shahadah plus sovereignty over life and death (formula twins `[7:158]`, `[10:56]`, `[57:2]`); "ancestors of old" pre-arms the answer to 44:36's demand to have the ancestors brought back.
* **`[44:9]`** "Yet they play around in doubt." — **Gloss:** The verdict on the audience; the same "play in confusion" verb (kh-w-d) stands behind `[6:91]` "leave them toying away in their speculation."
* **`[44:10]`** "So watch out for the Day when the sky produces a visible smoke." — **Gloss:** The title-event (surah-name "SMOKE"); the word *dukhan* also at `[41:11]` (primordial sky "was smoke") and `[56:43]` ("a shadow of thick smoke").
* **`[44:11]`** "Enveloping mankind; this is a painful punishment." — **Gloss:** Ledger: the task's "a punishment?" ✓ — verbatim "this is a painful punishment" is here in 44:11.
* **`[44:12]`** "“Our Lord, lift the torment from us, we are believers.”" — **Gloss:** The smoke-day cry: belief stated as a bargaining condition; ledger: "Lord, ransom us" ✗ — no "ransom" word in s044; the nearest twin is `[7:134]` "If you lift the plague from us, we will believe in you."
* **`[44:13]`** "But how can they be reminded? An enlightening messenger has already come to them." — **Gloss:** Ledger: the task's "Did We not destroy the ancients... a lesson for the later ones" ✗ — 44:13 is the remembrance-cries verse; that destroy-the-ancients/lesson motif is `[32:26]`; within s044 the motif runs 25–29 and 37 below. "Enlightening messenger" rhymes the "Enlightening Scripture" of 44:2.
* **`[44:14]`** "But they turned away from him, and said, “Educated, but crazy!”" — **Gloss:** The rejection charge; "He is crazy!" verbatim twin `[68:51]`; cf. `[26:27]`; "Educated" is s044-unique (rg).
* **`[44:15]`** "We will ease the punishment a little, but you will revert." — **Gloss:** Divine reply: a temporary easing will prove them apostatic; ledger: the task's lesser/greater-punishment verse is `[32:21]` ("We will make them taste the lesser torment, prior to the greater torment, so that they may return"), not 44:48 — 44:15 is this surah's own easing-motif.
* **`[44:16]`** "The Day when We launch the Great Assault—We will avenge." — **Gloss:** The "avenge" verb is God's name-adjacent: `[14:47]` "Allah is Strong, Able to Avenge."
* **`[44:17]`** "Before them We tested the people of Pharaoh; a noble messenger came to them." — **Gloss:** The precedent switch: the smoke-day generation will be measured against Pharaoh's.
* **`[44:18]`** "Saying, “Hand over Allah’s servants to me. I am an honest messenger to you.”" — **Gloss:** Moses's demand: Israel, "Allah's servants," surrendered; quotation typography: the quote-strands here and at 19–21 run unbalanced (class 2 — quote as-is).
* **`[44:19]`** "And, “Do not exalt yourselves above Allah. I come to you with clear authority." — **Gloss:** The demand is reloaded as a warning against arrogance before Allah; "clear authority" is the gift Moses brings in `[40:23]` ("We sent Moses with Our signs, and a clear authority" — rg-verified phrase twin).
* **`[44:20]`** "I have taken refuge in my Lord and your Lord, lest you stone me." — **Gloss:** Flight-to-God formula; the stoning threat against a messenger recurs at `[26:116]` (Noah) and `[11:91]` (Shuaib).
* **`[44:21]`** "But if you do not believe in me, keep away from me.”" — **Gloss:** The ultimatum: if not belief, then at least non-interference; the refusal-pattern rhymes 44:14's turn-away and prepares the appeal of 44:22.
* **`[44:22]`** "He appealed to his Lord: “These are a sinful people.”" — **Gloss:** The complaint that triggers the exodus; cf. `[28:32]` "They are truly sinful people" (same dossier).
* **`[44:23]`** "“Set out with My servants by night—you will be followed." — **Gloss:** The divine answer; near-verbatim twins `[26:52]` ("Travel with My servants by night. You will be followed.") and `[20:77]` ("Travel by night with My servants").
* **`[44:24]`** "And cross the sea quickly; they are an army to be drowned.”" — **Gloss:** The verdict pre-written: the pursuing army drowns; the sea-crossing frame `[2:50]` supplies the rescue side.
* **`[44:25]`** "How many gardens and fountains did they leave behind?" — **Gloss:** The "how many We destroyed" inventory opens here; ledger: "maqams (stations) and treasures" belongs to the twin scene at `[26:57–58]` ("gardens and springs... treasures and noble dwellings"); this edition's 44:25–27 list is gardens/fountains, plantations, splendid buildings, comforts.
* **`[44:26]`** "And plantations, and splendid buildings." — **Gloss:** Cultivated wealth and monumental wealth — the full estate abandoned.
* **`[44:27]`** "And comforts they used to enjoy." — **Gloss:** "Comforts" is corpus-rare (rg: only this line); the point is enjoyment itself forfeited.
* **`[44:28]`** "So it was; and We passed it on to another people." — **Gloss:** Inheritance-transfer of the whole estate; the twin scene names the heirs (`[26:59]` "We made the Children of Israel inherit them") while this verse keeps it general — same phrase "So it was" opening both.
* **`[44:29]`** "Neither heaven nor earth wept over them, nor were they reprieved." — **Gloss:** The epitaph: no cosmic mourning, no extension; "wept" is corpus-unique here (rg); the "not reprieved" formula recurs at `[2:162]`, `[3:88]`, `[6:8]`, `[16:85]`, `[21:40]`, `[22:44/48]`, `[15:37]`.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`kitāb mubīn`** (the making-clear Book, m-b-n root):
  * **Rendering:** "the Enlightening Scripture" (44:2; same phrase also rendered "Enlightening Scripture" at `[35:25]`); the cognate oath is rendered "the Book/Quran that makes things clear" at `[43:2]`, `[15:1]`, `[27:1]`, and "the Illuminating Scripture" at `[3:184]` — one Arabic phrase, three English renderings (translation variance per the artifacts fleeting note, not an artifact).
  * **Elsewhere:** the same root describes the messenger himself at 44:13 "An enlightening messenger," Book and warner sharing the clarity-verb.
* **`dukhan`** (smoke):
  * **Rendering:** "a visible smoke" (44:10) — the surah's title-event; the smoke is *given* by the sky, not lit by men.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[41:11]` — the primordial sky "was smoke"; `[56:43]` — "a shadow of thick smoke" at Judgment; the corpus knows dukhan at creation's start and judgment's end.
* **`layla mubāraka`** (Blessed Night):
  * **Rendering:** "a Blessed Night" (44:3), capitalized in this edition.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[97:1–3]` "the Night of Decree... better than a thousand months"; `[2:185]` Ramadan as the revealed month — the corpus locates the night but never names it "Blessed" outside 44:3.
* **`kashf`** (lifting/easing):
  * **Rendering:** "lift the torment from us" (44:12, the people's plea) and "We will ease the punishment a little" (44:15, God's reply).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[7:134]` "If you lift the plague from us, we will believe in you" — conditional belief on lifting is a recurring psychology; `[32:21]` tastes the "lesser torment prior to the greater."
