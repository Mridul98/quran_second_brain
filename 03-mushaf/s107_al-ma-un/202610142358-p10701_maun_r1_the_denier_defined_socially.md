# The Denier Defined Socially: Orphan, Poor, and the Heedless Prayers (107:1–7)
**UID:** 202610142358
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 107:1–7 (Ruku 1 of 1 in al-Ma'un)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[107:1]` – `[107:7]` (7 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Al-Ma'un answers its own interrogation with a social definition: the denier of the religion is the man of the orphan and the un-urged meal (vv2–3) — then extends the same diagnosis inside the worship-community: woe to the praying who are heedless, performed-for-audience, and withhold the small assistance (vv4–7). Creed is tested at the orphan's door, not the prayer rug; the liturgy of the House (106 just commanded it) can be run by the religion's deniers.
* **Placement:** Ruku 549 of 556; the social-audit pole of the 104–108 cluster, and 106's opposite case: gratitude-to-the-Giver (106) vs. worship-minus-justice (107). Its second half is the Meccan prototype of the Medinan nifaq portrait (4:142's prayer-signature — see §4).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[107:1]`** "Have you considered him who denies the religion?"
  * **Gloss:** The consideration-interrogative (same opener as 105:1; ~20 "Have you considered" sites in the corpus — rg-verified) — the question invites the hearth to picture a denier; Itani keeps *dīn* as "religion" (the Day-of-Judgment shade is classical exegesis — *Source: classical reading of 107:1 — verify*).
* **`[107:2]`** "It is he who mistreats the orphan."
  * **Gloss:** The answer locates denial in conduct: "It is he who" makes v2 the *definition*. The orphan (yamīm) is the book's standing test-case of un-coerced power; "mistreats" translates a pushing-away verb (classically repulse/drive off — *Source: classical lexicon — verify*).
* **`[107:3]`** "And does not encourage the feeding of the poor."
  * **Gloss:** The second symptom is second-hand: not merely a withheld meal, but no *urging* of it — social will-formation around the poor is itself the religion-marker (cf. the near-verbatim twin 89:18 "you do not urge the feeding of the poor" — rg-verified).
* **`[107:4]`** "So woe to those who pray."
  * **Gloss:** The "So" welds the halves and the woe changes venue: from the denier-in-the-world to the pray-ers-in-the-community (the corpus's only woe whose object is prayer-performers — see index §5b).
* **`[107:5]`** "Those who are heedless of their prayers."
  * **Gloss:** Not non-performers but inattentive ones: they pray *and* are heedless (sahw) — worship performed with the heart elsewhere. The exact phrase-twin is 46:5's idols "heedless of their prayers?" (rg-verified) — heedlessness is attributed to worshippers here, to false gods there.
* **`[107:6]`** "Those who put on the appearance."
  * **Gloss:** The audience line (classical root: riya' — seeing-off): Itani's rendering is unique in the corpus (rg-verified); the riya'-family's other three sites are 2:264 "to be seen by the people," 4:142 "showing off in front of people," 8:47 "showing off before the people" (all rg-verified).
* **`[107:7]`** "And withhold the assistance."
  * **Gloss:** The final clause measures sin downward: the withholding is of *ma'un* — small, everyday services/utensils which (classically) nobody refuses except a miser (*Source: classical lexicon/report on 107:7 — verify*). The surah's own list equates two scales: denial of the religion (v1) and refusal of the small (v7) sit in one indictment.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`dīn` (here: "the religion")**: the denied object of v1.
  * **Rendering:** "religion" (107:1) — vs. "the Day of Judgment" `[1:4]`, "the Day of Reckoning" `[83:11]`, "reject the religion" `[95:7]` (all rg-verified): Itani runs *dīn* across judgment/religion poles; 107:1 + 95:7 are the corpus's two "deny/reject the religion" lines, the closest twin.
  * **Elsewhere:** 107:1's religious-denial-by-conduct answers 83:10–11's Day-denial-by-character ("Woe on that Day to the deniers. / Those who deny the Day of Reckoning. / But none denies it except the sinful aggressor") — the definer-a-denier genre, verified.
* **`yatīm` (orphan)**: the social test-case.
  * **Rendering:** "mistreats the orphan" (107:2).
  * **Elsewhere:** the orphan dossier, all rg-verified: `[89:17]` "you do not honor the orphan" (negative twin), `[93:9]` "do not mistreat the orphan" (imperative twin — on the same verb Itani uses here for 107:2's "mistreats"), `[90:15]` "An orphan near of kin" (the ascent's positive), `[2:83]` covenant clause "be good to parents, and relatives, and orphans", `[2:220]`, `[4:2]`, `[6:152]`, `[17:34]` property protections, `[76:8]` feed "the poor, and the orphan, and the captive".
* **`huḍāʾ / heedless of prayers`** (v5): inattention inside worship.
  * **Rendering:** "heedless of their prayers" — exact phrase-twin at `[46:5]` (rg-verified); the broader heedless cluster (`[7:179]`, `[16:108]`, `[21:1]`, `[30:7]`) marks the whole heedless type.
  * **Elsewhere:** contrast 4:142's lazy-standing signature (k-nifaq's ritual list) and `[20:129?]` — no: the sharpening use is 46:5 — idols described as "heedless of their prayers" while 107:5 gives the worshipper the same diagnosis: heedlessness moves from the worshipped to the worshipping (*agent inference on the phrase identity — verify*).
* **`yurāʾūn / "put on the appearance"`** (v6): performance-for-eyes.
  * **Rendering:** unique English in the corpus (rg-verified); the riya'-family's other sites use see/show-off words (2:264, 4:142, 8:47 — verified above).
  * **Elsewhere:** k-nifaq cites 4:142 and 2:264 in its dossier — 107:6 is the same conduct without the confession-formula language; k-nifaq never uses the noun here because the text doesn't either (the hypocrite-label is absent from Meccan surahs — *Source: agent inference — verify*).
* **`māʿūn` (the assistance)**: the withheld small.
  * **Rendering:** "the assistance" (107:7) — the word occurs once (rg-verified).
  * **Elsewhere:** withholding-cluster, all rg-verified: `[9:67]` the hypocrites "prohibit righteousness, and withhold their hands" (k-nifaq's line), `[3:180]` "Those who withhold what Allah has given them", `[47:38]` "whoever withholds is withholding against his own soul" — 107:7 is the smallest-denomination version of the same sin.
