* **Title:** al-Haqqah — Itani renders the Day's self-name "The Reality"; the surah interrogates its own title twice in three lines (69:2–3) — the only "Haqqah" naming of the Day in the corpus.
* **Central theme:** The Reality authenticated by two evidences and one oath. History: four annihilations whose "remnant" cannot be found (69:4–12). Courtroom: exposure, the books handed right or left, and a crime-sheet pairing disbelief-in-God-with-failure-to-feed-the-destitute (69:13–37). Then the channel-claim: sworn on seen and unseen, this is the speech of a noble messenger, and forgery would have been severed at the lifeline (69:38–47) — ending "So glorify the name of your Lord, the Magnificent" (69:52).
* **Reading hint:** the surah's logic runs the k-kalam question — "how does this book know it is God talking?" — and answers it with the strongest self-claim in the corpus (69:40–47), placed immediately after the ledger scenes.

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Self-interrogation (69:1–3):** name, "What is the Reality?", "What will make you understand what the Reality is?" — the Day held up as an unknown the surah will go on to define.
* **Destruction catalogue (69:4–12):** Thamud (the Overwhelming), Aad (the roaring wind, seven nights eight days, palm-stump corpses), Pharaoh + the Overturned Cities, the Flood carried in the "cruising ship" — addressed as "a lesson... so that retaining ears may retain it."
* **Cosmic teardown (69:13–18):** one Trumpet, earth-and-mountains crushed, heaven cracking, angels at the borders, eight bearing the Throne — and "you will be exposed, and no secret of yours will remain hidden."
* **The two verdict speeches (69:19–37):** the right-hand book's relief ("I knew I would be held accountable"), garden ease, eat-and-drink — versus the left-hand book's collapse, the shackles, the seventy-cubit chain, the two-crime sentence (no belief in "Allah the Great"; no advocacy for the destitute's feeding), and the diet of scum.
* **The oath and the authentication (69:38–51):** sworn by seen and unseen: "the speech of a noble messenger" — not poet, not soothsayer — "a revelation from the Lord of the Worlds"; the forgery clause (seized by the right arm, lifeline slashed); the grief-to-disbelievers/absolute-truth pair.
* **Close (69:52):** the doxology-command: "So glorify the name of your Lord, the Magnificent."
