# Hasten at the call, then disperse: the Friday ordering (62:9–11)
**UID:** 202610142189
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 62:9–11 (Ruku 2 of 2 in al-Jumu'ah)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[62:9]` – `[62:11]` (3 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The weekly rehearsal of right ordering: when the call sounds, remembrance wins over business (62:9); when the prayer ends, dispersal and bounty-seeking are not merely lawful but piously commanded — "remember Allah much, so that you may prosper" (62:10); and the ruku ends with its scandal anecdote — the scramble toward merchandise or entertainment, and the Prophet left standing (62:11).
* **Placement:** Closing ruku of the shortest legislation unit in the Quran's Friday material; it converts ruku 1's theory (grace recited, taught, carried) into one practiced hour per week. Its distraction vocabulary is the direct sibling of `[63:9]`'s "let neither your possessions nor your children distract you" — same wave, same diagnosis.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[62:9]`** "O you who believe! When the call is made for prayer on Congregation Day, hasten to the remembrance of Allah, and drop all business. That is better for you, if you only knew."
  * **Gloss:** Four moves: the call (nad'), the named day (the hapax "Congregation Day" that titles the surah), the gait (hasten — the opposite of the hypocrites' lazy standing at `[4:142]`), and the release ("drop all business"). "That is better for you, if you only knew" — the surah's second better-than clause, after 61:11's identical idiom.
* **`[62:10]`** "Then, when the prayer is concluded, disperse through the land, and seek Allah’s bounty, and remember Allah much, so that you may prosper."
  * **Gloss:** The counter-balance: no monasticism — dispersal (intsar) and bounty-seeking are commanded in the same breath as much dhikr; prosperity (falah) hangs on the conjunction "and," not on the either/or.
* **`[62:11]`** "Yet whenever they come across some business, or some entertainment, they scramble towards it, and leave you standing. Say, “What is with Allah is better than entertainment and business; and Allah is the Best of providers.”"
  * **Gloss:** The occasion-ethics verse: a crowd (classically, a caravan with drums arriving during the sermon) visibly re-weights the community's attention while the Prophet stands mid-address; the remedy is a spoken confession — what is with Allah outbids both, and He is the Best of providers (the surah's own final word).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`yawm al-jumu'ah` (Congregation Day):** `[62:9]` — the name-verse; hapax in the corpus (verified single occurrence).
  * **Rendering:** "Congregation Day" — Itani's weekly-congregation rendering; classical fiqh builds the Friday-prayer obligation on this verse plus the call ("when the call is made") as the trigger. *Source: consensus of classical fiqh on 62:9 — verify.*
* **`dhikr` (remembrance):** `[62:9]` "remembrance of Allah"; `[62:10]` "remember Allah much".
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:152]` — "So remember Me, and I will remember you. And thank Me" (the shukr-welding cited in the k-shukr note); `[63:9]` — "Let neither your possessions nor your children distract you from the remembrance of Allah" — the sibling prohibition one surah over (verified).
* **`ibtigha' al-fadl` (seeking bounty):** `[62:10]` "seek Allah’s bounty".
  * **Elsewhere:** `[73:20]` — "others travelling through the land, seeking Allah’s bounty" — the same phrase inside a permission to work amid worship (verified); note `[62:4]` within the surah: the bounty sought after prayer is the fadl whose owner is Allah "Possessor of limitless grace."
* **`lahw` (entertainment/distraction):** `[62:11]` — paired with trade in both the report and the verdict ("better than entertainment and business").
  * **Elsewhere:** `[63:9]` uses "distract" for the possessors-and-children pull — concept-twin with different roots (observable); `[2:256]` "the right way has become distinct from the wrong way" is the wider say–do backdrop: the scramble is the say–do gap of ruku 1 of as-Saff in street form.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Medinan (agreed); the verse-pair 62:9–10 is the locus classicus of the Friday-prayer obligation in classical fiqh. *Source: consensus of classical scholarship — verify.*
* **Asbab / narrative context:** 62:11 is transmitted with the arrival of a trade caravan (classically, Dihya's caravan) with drum/song at Medina during the Prophet's sermon, drawing nearly everyone out and leaving a few with him. *Source: classical tafsir/maghazi reports on 62:11 (Ibn Kathir/Tabari family) — agent labeling; verify.* The reports differ on whether the pull was merchandise, entertainment, or both — the corpus line itself says "some business, or some entertainment."
