# A Scripture perfected and elaborated — total knowledge, total sustenance, the test of conduct (11:1–8)
**UID:** 202610141522
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 11:1–8 (Ruku 1 of 10 in Hud)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[11:1]` – `[11:8]` (8 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The lettered opening authenticates the Book ("perfected, then elaborated, from One who is Wise and Informed"), and the ruku compresses the whole sermon into four pillars — worship none but Allah, seek forgiveness and repent, "To Allah is your return," and the total surveillance that makes religion unnecessary as concealment: He knows the wrapped chest, sustains every creeping thing, owns where it lives and rests, and built the heavens on a Throne above the waters expressly "to test you—which of you is best in conduct."
* **Placement:** Opening ruku of the destruction-cycle surah; sets the epistemic frame (God's knowledge, the Clear Book) that the six prophet-stories will act out and that 11:12–14 will apply to the stressed Messenger.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[11:1]`** "Alif, Lam, Ra. A Scripture whose Verses were perfected, then elaborated, from One who is Wise and Informed."
  * **Gloss:** Letters, then a Book-claim: the verses are made sound/complete (muhkamat), then detailed (mufassalat) — a two-stage origin from a Speaker defined as Wise (hakim) and Informed (khabir).
* **`[11:2]`** "That you shall worship none but Allah. “I am a warner to you from Him, and a bearer of good news.”"
  * **Gloss:** The Book's single content: exclusive worship, announced by a warner who is also bearer of good news — the pair that bounds every prophet-sermon in this surah.
* **`[11:3]`** "“And ask your Lord for forgiveness, and repent to Him. He will provide you with good sustenance until a stated term, and will bestow His grace on every possessor of virtue. But if you turn away, then I fear for you the punishment of a grievous Day.”"
  * **Gloss:** The repentance formula with a this-world promise (good sustenance to a term; grace to the virtuous) and a Day-warning — the same offer Hud, Salih and Shu'ayb will each reissue.
* **`[11:4]`** "“To Allah is your return, and He is Capable of all things.”"
  * **Gloss:** The return (ruju') and the capability: the two premises of accountability.
* **`[11:5]`** "They wrap their chests to hide from Him. But even as they cover themselves with their clothes, He knows what they conceal and what they reveal. He knows what lies within the hearts."
  * **Gloss:** Concealment attempted against the All-Knowing: the folded breast, the pulled cloak — three knowledge-clauses in one verse; the hypocritical posture (mouth-heart split) meets its analytic opposite number.
* **`[11:6]`** "There is no moving creature on earth but its sustenance depends on Allah. And He knows where it lives and where it rests. Everything is in a Clear Book."
  * **Gloss:** Total provision and total registration: every dābbah, its place of movement (qarār) and its deposit (mustaqarr), filed in a Clear Book — sustenance and record in one breath.
* **`[11:7]`** "It is He who created the heavens and the earth in six days—and His Throne was upon the waters—in order to test you—which of you is best in conduct. And if you were to say, “You will be resurrected after death,” those who disbelieve would say, “This is nothing but plain witchcraft.”"
  * **Gloss:** The cosmology clause with its unique archaic detail (the Throne upon the waters) and its stated purpose: creation as examination for conduct (aḥsan ʿamal), not comfort; resurrection met with the charge of "witchcraft" (sihr).
* **`[11:8]`** "And if We postponed their punishment until a stated time, they would say, “What holds it back?” On the Day when it reaches them, it will not be averted from them, and what they used to ridicule will besiege them."
  * **Gloss:** The deferral is pedagogy, not denial: when it comes it surrounds — anticipating the cycle's "when Our command came" moments and answered in-ruku at 11:104.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`Alif, Lam, Ra.`**: the disconnected letters, here numbered ayah 1.
  * **Rendering:** the letter-names transliterated, not translated, and numbered as ayah 1 (observable edition stance).
  * **Elsewhere:** the same three-letter string opens exactly five surahs — `[10:1]`, `[11:1]`, `[12:1]`, `[14:1]`, `[15:1]` (rg-verified) — and Hud sits between Yunus and Yusuf; each of the five is followed by a Book-claim (cf. `[12:1]`, `[15:1]`). See the Muqatta'at fleeting note.
* **`Clear Book` (al-kitāb al-mubīn)**: the registered file of 11:6.
  * **Rendering:** "Clear Book" in 11:6.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[12:1]` "These are the Verses of the Clear Book"; `[28:2]` same; `[27:75]` "no mystery in the heaven and the earth, but it is in a Clear Book" — the same register-phrase applied to unseen secrets (verified).
* **`sustenance` (rizq)**: 11:3's promised "good sustenance"; 11:6 "its sustenance depends on Allah."
  * **Rendering:** "good sustenance until a stated term" (11:3); "its sustenance depends on Allah" (11:6).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[20:132]` "We ask of you no sustenance, but it is We who sustain you"; `[45:5]` "the sustenance Allah sends down from the sky"; `[10:59]` (Yunus) questioning who made sustenance lawful/unlawful — provision as a monotheist proof-text in the adjacent surahs (verified).
* **`best in conduct` (aḥsan ʿamal)**: the test's metric, 11:7.
  * **Rendering:** "which of you is best in conduct" (11:7), matching 18:7 word-for-word (verified).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[18:7]` — "to test them as to which of them is best in conduct" — the only twin in the corpus (verified); the Throne-over-waters detail is unique to 11:7 corpus-wide (verified).

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Meccan (classical consensus); the resurrection-sneer of 11:7 and the deferral-question of 11:8 read as the Meccan pagans' two standing objections — *Source: agent inference from the objections' register — verify against chronological tafsir.*
