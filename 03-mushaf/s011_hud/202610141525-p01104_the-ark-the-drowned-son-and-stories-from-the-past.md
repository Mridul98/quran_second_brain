# The Ark, the drowned son, and the hinge: stories from the past, so be patient (11:36–49)
**UID:** 202610141525
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 11:36–49 (Ruku 4 of 10 in Hud)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[11:36]` – `[11:49]` (14 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The Flood executes: revelation of despair ("none of your people will believe"), ark-building mocked by the passers-by, the volcano as the sentence's opening, the few aboard, the father-son dialogue on waves like hills, the earth's swallow and Judi's rest, and the plea for a son reclassified as "an unrighteous deed" — then the surah's hinge-verse: these unseen stories are revelation-proof, so be patient, for the future belongs to the pious.
* **Placement:** The cycle's first execution (unit 1 of 6) and the structural keystone: 11:49's formula ("stories from the past... be patient... the future belongs to the pious") retrospectively certifies all six narratives and pre-authors the closing commands of ruku 10.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[11:36]`** "And it was revealed to Noah: “None of your people will believe, except those who have already believed, so do not grieve over what they do.”"
  * **Gloss:** Wahy of closure: the missionary window is declared shut; grief is forbidden in advance — the emotional law of the whole surah.
* **`[11:37]`** "“And build the Ark, under Our eyes, and with Our inspiration, and do not address Me regarding those who did wrong; they are to be drowned.”"
  * **Gloss:** Construction under divine observation ("Our eyes"), by divine blueprint; intercession for the wrongdoers pre-banned.
* **`[11:38]`** "As he was building the ark, whenever some of his people passed by him, they ridiculed him. He said, “If you ridicule us, we will ridicule you, just as you ridicule.”"
  * **Gloss:** The landlocked shipyard as comedy for passers-by; Noah answers ridicule with a deferred ridicule — the irony the ruku will perform on them.
* **`[11:39]`** "“You will surely know upon whom will come a torment that will abase him, and upon whom will fall a lasting torment.”"
  * **Gloss:** The wager set: ignominious now or lasting later — knowledge-by-experience, the cycle's epistemology.
* **`[11:40]`** "Until, when Our command came, and the volcano erupted, We said, “Board into it a pair of every kind, and your family—except those against whom the sentence has already been passed—and those who have believed.” But those who believed with him were only a few."
  * **Gloss:** "When Our command came" — the execution-formula's first stroke; the tannur (rendered "the volcano erupted") as the sign; pairs, family-with-exceptions, few believers.
* **`[11:41]`** "He said, “Embark in it. In the name of Allah shall be its sailing and its anchorage. My Lord is indeed Forgiving and Merciful.”"
  * **Gloss:** The bismala over motion and stillness — the journey's whole vocabulary of trust; the verse closes on the two mercy-names even at the drowning's edge.
* **`[11:42]`** "And so it sailed with them amidst waves like hills. And Noah called to his son, who had kept away, “O my son! Embark with us, and do not be with the disbelievers.”"
  * **Gloss:** The surah's most cinematic line: mountain-waves, and the final personal invitation to the son apart (hijāz).
* **`[11:43]`** "He said, “I will take refuge on a mountain—it will protect me from the water.” He said, “There is no protection from Allah’s decree today, except for him on whom He has mercy.” And the waves surged between them, and he was among the drowned."
  * **Gloss:** Topography as false refuge vs mercy as the only shielding; the waves as the closing parenthesis between father and son — a family divided by decree.
* **`[11:44]`** "And it was said, “O earth, swallow your waters,” and “O heaven, clear up.” And the waters receded, and the event was concluded, and it settled on Judi, and it was proclaimed: “Away with the wicked people.”"
  * **Gloss:** The command-duet to earth and sky, the event "cut" (qadiya), the resting-place named — the only Quranic occurrence of Judi — and the first "away with" (suḥban) proclamation, the cycle's funeral refrain.
* **`[11:45]`** "And Noah called to his Lord. He said, “O My Lord, my son is of my family, and Your promise is true, and You are the Wisest of the wise.”"
  * **Gloss:** The post-resurrection-of-the-waters plea: kinship claimed against the sentence, using God's own attributes as argument.
* **`[11:46]`** "He said, “O Noah, he is not of your family. It is an unrighteous deed. So do not ask Me about something you know nothing about. I admonish you, lest you be one of the ignorant.”"
  * **Gloss:** The correction: family is redefined by deed (ʿamal ghayr salih), not blood — the surah's most cited lineage-verdict; and the prophet is disciplined for unqualified intercession.
* **`[11:47]`** "He said, “O My Lord, I seek refuge with You, from asking You about what I have no knowledge of. Unless You forgive me, and have mercy on me, I will be one of the losers.”"
  * **Gloss:** The immediate return to the surah's opening formula (11:3: forgiveness + mercy) — even the greatest prophet runs the repentance track.
* **`[11:48]`** "It was said, “O Noah, disembark with peace from Us; and with blessings upon you, and upon communities from those with you. And other communities We will grant prosperity, and then a painful torment from Us will befall them.”"
  * **Gloss:** Disembarkation as the first salām; the saved lineage blessed; and the future enunciated — prosperity, then torment: the whole six-cycle in one clause.
* **`[11:49]`** "These are some stories from the past that we reveal to you. Neither you, nor your people knew them before this. So be patient. The future belongs to the pious."
  * **Gloss:** The hinge: narrated unseen = revealed speech (the Qur'an's proof of itself); therefore sabr — and the ghāyah (the future/the end) is taqwa's property.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`the Ark` build-formula**: 11:37 "under Our eyes, and with Our inspiration."
  * **Rendering:** "build the Ark, under Our eyes, and with Our inspiration" (11:37).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[23:27]` — "Build the Ark under Our observation and by Our inspiration" — the same commission in al-Mu'minun with eyes→observation drift; both add the drowned-exception clause (verified) — a fixed pre-Quranic narrative datum recut twice.
* **`the volcano erupted`** (tannur): the Flood's start-sign, 11:40.
  * **Rendering:** "when Our command came, and the volcano erupted" (11:40).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[23:27]` — "when Our decree comes to pass, and the oven boils over" — same event, "volcano" vs "oven" rendering (verified; tannur lexically an oven/baker) — *Source: translator's term choice, observable; the classical gloss "the oven" is standard — verify.*
* **`Away with`** (suḥban): the imprecation-refrain.
  * **Rendering:** "it was proclaimed: Away with the wicked people" (11:44).
  * **Elsewhere:** within Hud at 11:44, 60, 68, 95 (verified); `[23:41]` — "So away with the wicked people" — the identical proclamation-formula after a Blast (verified): the cycle's closing stamp across surahs.
* **`the future` (ghāyah) / `be patient` (sabr)**: 11:49's pair.
  * **Rendering:** "The future belongs to the pious" (11:49).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[7:128]` — "the future belongs to the righteous" (pious→righteous drift, verified); 11:31 and 11:123 use the same "future" noun for outcome and for 11:123 "the future of the heavens and the earth" ↔ `[35:38]` verbatim (verified); `[2:153]` patience commanded — see [Sabr and taqwa](../../02-knowledge/202610132003-k-sabr_and_taqwa.md).
