# Hud to Aad: "You do nothing but invent lies" — the forelock-trust on the straight path (11:50–60)
**UID:** 202610141526
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 11:50–60 (Ruku 5 of 10 in Hud)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[11:50]` – `[11:60]` (11 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The second mission — pure tawhid to Aad with a rain-promise attached to repentance — is met by the strangest rebuttal in the cycle ("some of our gods have possessed you with evil"), and Hud answers with total self-surrender: summoning Allah and them as witnesses against their associations, daring a unified scheme, and naming the reason he cannot be afraid: every creature is held by its forelock by a Lord who is on a straight path — the surah's tawakkul summit. The execution is compressed: salvation "by a mercy from Us," a curse in both worlds, and "away with Aad, the people of Hud."
* **Placement:** Ruku 5; the cycle's second unit and its shortest destruction report — the pattern is now recognized by the reader, so the narrative thins and the theology thickens.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[11:50]`** "And to Aad, their brother Hud. He said, “O my people, worship Allah, you have no other god besides Him. You do nothing but invent lies.”"
  * **Gloss:** The brother-phrase (their brother Hud: kin, not stranger) + the sermon + the verdict on their gods: pure invention (iftirāʾ).
* **`[11:51]`** "“O my people, I ask you no wage for it; my wage lies with Him who originated me. Do you not understand?”"
  * **Gloss:** The wage-disclaimer with its own theology: payment from the one who brought him into being (faṭara).
* **`[11:52]`** "“O my people, ask forgiveness from your Lord, and repent to Him. He will release the sky pouring down upon you, and will add strength to your strength. And do not turn away and be wicked.”"
  * **Gloss:** The repentance→rain offer — addressed to a drought-scoured people; grace-for-repentance stated in hydrology.
* **`[11:53]`** "They said, “O Hud, you did not bring us any evidence, and we are not about to abandon our gods at your word, and we are not believers in you.”"
  * **Gloss:** The triple rebuttal: no evidence, no abandonment at command, no belief.
* **`[11:54]`** "“We only say that some of our gods have possessed you with evil.” He said, “I call Allah to witness, and you to witness, that I am innocent of what you associate."
  * **Gloss:** Their diagnosis: one of their gods has touched him badly — possession as explanation for prophecy; Hud's counter: he summons the very God they insult, plus them, as witnesses to dissociation.
* **`[11:55]`** "Besides Him. So scheme against me, all of you, and do not hesitate."
  * **Gloss:** The open challenge: all gods, all at once, no delay — the inventory of shirk dared to a single verdict.
* **`[11:56]`** "I have placed my trust in Allah, my Lord and your Lord. There is not a creature but He holds it by the forelock. My Lord is on a straight path."
  * **Gloss:** The tawakkul argument: shared Lordship (mine and yours), universal forelock-hold (nāṣiya — total sovereignty), and the straight-path commitment of that sovereignty.
* **`[11:57]`** "If you turn away, I have conveyed to you what I was sent to you with; and my Lord will replace you with another people, and you will not cause Him any harm. My Lord is Guardian over all things.”"
  * **Gloss:** Conveyance discharged; replacement threatened (another people), harmlessness of their refusal affirmed; Guardian (ḥafīẓ) as closing name.
* **`[11:58]`** "And when Our decree came, We saved Hud and those who believed with him, by a mercy from Us, and We delivered them from a harsh punishment."
  * **Gloss:** The execution-formula (here "decree" for amr) with the salvation-by-mercy stamp.
* **`[11:59]`** "That was Aad; they denied the signs of their Lord, and defied His messengers, and followed the lead of every stubborn tyrant."
  * **Gloss:** The post-mortem: denial, defiance (ʿaṣā), and the third crime — the clientele of tyrants (every stubborn Jabbār).
* **`[11:60]`** "And they were pursued by a curse in this world, and on the Day of Resurrection. Indeed, Aad blasphemed against their Lord—so away with Aad, the people of Hud."
  * **Gloss:** The curse in two worlds; the proclamation "away with Aad" — second stroke of the refrain (11:44, 60, 68, 95).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`invent lies`** (taftarūn): 11:50's one-line diagnosis of polytheism.
  * **Rendering:** "You do nothing but invent lies" (11:50).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[7:71]` — Hud: "names, which you and your ancestors invented, for which Allah sent down no authority" (verified) — the same crime in al-A'raf's wording; cf. 11:13/18's iftirāʾ cluster — see [Tawhid](../../02-knowledge/202610132001-k-tawhid_the_one_whom_no_rivals.md).
* **`forelock`** (nāṣiya): 11:56's image of total ownership.
  * **Rendering:** "There is not a creature but He holds it by the forelock" (11:56).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[96:15–16]` — "We will drag him by the forelock. A deceitful, sinful forelock"; `[55:41]` — "taken by the forelocks and the feet" (verified) — Hud 11:56 is the positive pole: the same grip as trust, elsewhere as disgrace.
* **`straight path`**: 11:56 — "My Lord is on a straight path."
  * **Rendering:** "My Lord is on a straight path" (11:56).
  * **Elsewhere:** the petition at `[1:6]`; `[6:161]` "My Lord has guided me to a straight path"; `[3:101]` (verified in corpus's 32-site field for the phrase) — here the predicate is of God's own conduct: sovereignty is straight.
* **`another people`**: 11:57's replacement clause.
  * **Rendering:** "my Lord will replace you with another people, and you will not cause Him any harm" (11:57).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[9:39]` — "He will punish you... and will replace you with another people, and you will not harm Him at all" — near-verbatim twin (verified); `[21:11]` — "crushed... and established thereafter another people" (verified).
