# The tested human, the stressed warner, and the ten-chapter challenge (11:9–24)
**UID:** 202610141523
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 11:9–24 (Ruku 2 of 10 in Hud)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[11:9]` – `[11:24]` (16 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Before the prophet-stories begin, the surah autopsies its listeners: the human being who despairs when mercy is withdrawn and gloats when hardship lifts — except the patient doers of good; the Messenger who nearly shrinks under the demand for a treasure or an angel, reassured that his office is only warnerhood and Allah's is responsibility; the escalated challenge of ten chapters; and the final sorting of the two parties — blind-and-deaf versus seeing-and-hearing.
* **Placement:** Ruku 2 completes the opening discourse (ruku 1–2) and supplies the pastoral key ("You are only a warner") to which all six failed missions in ruku 3–8 conform.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[11:9]`** "If We give the human being a taste of mercy from Us, and then withdraw it from him, he becomes despairing and ungrateful."
  * **Gloss:** Reaction pattern #1: withdrawn mercy reads as abandonment.
* **`[11:10]`** "And if We give him a taste of prosperity, after some adversity has afflicted him, he will say, “Troubles have gone away from me.” He becomes excited and proud."
  * **Gloss:** Reaction pattern #2: lifted hardship reads as personal immunity — 7:95's ancestral-rationalization in the singular.
* **`[11:11]`** "Except those who are patient and do good deeds—these will have forgiveness and a great reward."
  * **Gloss:** The exception clause: sabr + ʿamal salih — the ruku's thesis-word, planted at 11:11 for harvest at 11:49 and 11:115.
* **`[11:12]`** "Perhaps you wish to disregard some of what is revealed to you, and you may be stressed because of it, since they say, “If only a treasure was sent down to him, or an angel came with him.” You are only a warner, and Allah is Responsible for all things."
  * **Gloss:** The surah's pastoral center: the burden of the demanded miracle lifted — office defined (warner only), ownership reassigned (Allah is wakīl over all things). *Note: the assignment's phrasing "I am with you, a clear warner" is not 11:12's wording — the corpus says "You are only a warner"; the first-person "clear warner" formula is 11:25.*
* **`[11:13]`** "Or do they say, “He invented it?” Say, “Then produce ten chapters like it, invented, and call upon whomever you can, besides Allah, if you are truthful.”"
  * **Gloss:** The challenge raised to ten chapters — a dare to collaborators; "invented" answers "invented" (iftirāʾ for iftirāʾ).
* **`[11:14]`** "But if they fail to answer you, know that it was revealed with Allah’s knowledge, and that there is no god but He. Will you then submit?"
  * **Gloss:** The failed challenge converts to proof: the Book is knowledge-speech of the Wise and Informed (cf. 11:1); the closing question demands taslim.
* **`[11:15]`** "Whoever desires the worldly life and its glitter—We will fully recompense them for their deeds therein, and therein they will not be defrauded."
  * **Gloss:** The near-life's ledger: paid in full, on the spot, to the last gram.
* **`[11:16]`** "These—they will have nothing but the Fire in the Hereafter. Their deeds are in vain therein, and their works are null."
  * **Gloss:** The same ledger's bottom line: zero carried forward.
* **`[11:17]`** "Is he who possesses a clear proof from his Lord, recited by a witness from Him, and before it the Book of Moses, a guide and a mercy? These believe in it. But whoever defies it from among the various factions, the Fire is his promise. So have no doubt about it. It is the truth from your Lord, but most people do not believe."
  * **Gloss:** The evidenced believer: proof + a witness from Him + Moses' precedent-Book; the factions (aknāf) who defy are warned without ambiguity.
* **`[11:18]`** "Who does greater wrong than he who fabricates lies about Allah? These will be presented before their Lord, and the witnesses will say, “These are they who lied about their Lord.” Indeed, the curse of Allah is upon the wrongdoers."
  * **Gloss:** The iftirāʾ-verdict: arraigned, testified by the witnesses, cursed — the ruku's frame-echo of 11:13's charge.
* **`[11:19]`** "Those who hinder others from the path of Allah, and seek to make it crooked; and regarding the Hereafter, they are in denial."
  * **Gloss:** The crime profile: obstruction, distortion of the road (the crooked against the straight — cf. 7:86's lurkers), denial of the next.
* **`[11:20]`** "These will not escape on earth, and they have no protectors besides Allah. The punishment will be doubled for them. They have failed to hear, and they have failed to see."
  * **Gloss:** No flee, no shield, double tariff — for sensory failure willfully maintained.
* **`[11:21]`** "Those are the ones who lost their souls, and what they had invented has strayed away from them."
  * **Gloss:** The inventory liquidates: the invented gods walk off the stage at the hour of need.
* **`[11:22]`** "Without a doubt, in the Hereafter, they will be the biggest losers."
  * **Gloss:** The superlative of loss attached to the same commercial idiom.
* **`[11:23]`** "As for those who believe and do good deeds, and humble themselves before their Lord—these are the inhabitants of Paradise, where they will abide forever."
  * **Gloss:** The counter-party: belief, works, and tadarruʿ (humility) — the very trait the towns-cycle of 7:94 was engineered to produce.
* **`[11:24]`** "The parable of the two groups is that of the blind and the deaf, and the seeing and the hearing. Are they equal in comparison? Will you not reflect?"
  * **Gloss:** The ruku's closing parable: two groups, four senses-of-the-soul — unequal by any ledger; taqrir-question as exit.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`a taste of mercy / a taste of prosperity`** (dhawwaqa): the trial-by-sampling verb.
  * **Rendering:** 11:9 "a taste of mercy from Us, and then withdraw it"; 11:10 "a taste of prosperity, after some adversity" — one sampling-verb, two directions.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[17:83]` — "When We bless the human being, he turns away and distances himself. But when adversity touches him, he is in despair" — same two reactions inverted in order; `[30:36]` — "When We give people a taste of mercy, they rejoice in it. But when adversity befalls them... they begin to despair" — Hud 11:9–10 is this psychology's three-way corpus cluster (verified).
* **`ten chapters`**: the escalated challenge, unique in the corpus (verified: `rg "ten chapters"` → 11:13 only).
  * **Rendering:** "Then produce ten chapters like it, invented" (11:13) — suwar as "chapters".
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:23]` "produce a chapter like these"; `[10:38]` "produce a single chapter like it" — one chapter in the adjacent surah, ten here; *Source: agent observation — the quantity difference is a translation-visible fact, its reason (Meccan vs Medinan dare) is classical exegetical talk — verify.*
* **`witness from Him`** (shāhid): 11:17's confirming voice.
  * **Rendering:** "recited by a witness from Him" (11:17).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[46:10]` — "A witness from the Children of Israel testified to its like" — the same shāhid-category at the pair-surah al-Ahqaf; `[46:12]` — "before it is the Book of Moses, a model and a mercy" vs 11:17 "a guide and a mercy" — same phrase, drifted rendering (verified).
* **`fabricates lies about Allah`** (iftarā): 11:13, 11:18 (and 11:50's "invent lies").
  * **Rendering:** 11:18 "fabricates lies about Allah"; 11:13 "He invented it?"; 11:50 "You do nothing but invent lies" — one root, three renderings in one surah (verified).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[6:21]`, `[10:17]`, `[29:68]` — the identical "Who does greater wrong than he who fabricates lies about Allah" formula, three times outside Hud (verified) — Hud localizes it against the "He invented it?" charge.
