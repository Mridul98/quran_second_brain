# Noah: the clear warner and the notables' sociology of refusal (11:25–35)
**UID:** 202610141524
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 11:25–35 (Ruku 3 of 10 in Hud)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[11:25]` – `[11:35]` (11 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The first of the six missions: Noah's sermon is the surah-template in miniature (clear warner → worship none but Allah → fear of a Day), and the rebuttal he receives is class-analysis, not evidence — "a man like us," "only the worst follow you," "we think you are liars" — which Noah answers by disclaiming the prophet's supposed advantages (money, treasures, knowledge of the future, angel-status) and by refusing to dismiss the lowly believers, ending where all cycle-sermons end: guidance is advice, but "if Allah desires to confound you," advice does not save.
* **Placement:** Opens the cycle proper (ruku 3–8); ruku 4 carries its execution (the Flood).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[11:25]`** "We sent Noah to his people, “I am to you a clear warner.”"
  * **Gloss:** The commission formula verbatim — the "I am to you a clear warner" line the surah will reissue for every mission.
* **`[11:26]`** "“That you shall worship none but Allah. I fear for you the agony of a painful Day.”"
  * **Gloss:** Content = exclusive worship; motive = fear for them, not for himself — the warner's fear is vicarious.
* **`[11:27]`** "The notables who disbelieved among his people said, “We see in you nothing but a man like us, and we see that only the worst among us have followed you, those of immature judgment. And we see that you have no advantage over us. In fact, we think you are liars.”"
  * **Gloss:** The four-part rejection: same-species argument, follower-class contempt, no-status argument, liar-verdict — the Meccan elites' exact objections voiced through the first people.
* **`[11:28]`** "He said, “O my people, Have you considered? If I stand on clear evidence from my Lord, and He has given me a mercy from Himself, but you were blind to it, can we compel you to accept it, even though you dislike it?”"
  * **Gloss:** The taqrir ("Have you considered?") + apology: evidence and mercy are God-side gifts; blindness to them is not refuted by force — revelation is not coerced.
* **`[11:29]`** "“O my people! I ask of you no money for it. My reward lies only with Allah. And I am not about to dismiss those who believed; they will surely meet their Lord. And I see that you are ignorant people.”"
  * **Gloss:** Financial disclaimer + class defense: the believers' worth is adjudicated at the meeting with their Lord, not by the notables' taste.
* **`[11:30]`** "“O my people! Who will support me against Allah, if I dismiss them? Will you not give a thought?”"
  * **Gloss:** The dismissal refused on accountability grounds — a prophet cannot fire God's guests.
* **`[11:31]`** "“I do not say to you that I possess the treasures of Allah, nor do I know the future, nor do I say that I am an angel. Nor do I say of those who are despicable in your eyes that Allah will never give them any good. Allah is Aware of what lies in their souls. If I did, I would be one of the wrongdoers.”"
  * **Gloss:** The four disclaimers — treasures, ghayb-knowledge, angel-status, private judgments on souls: the prophet is not a surveillance-class being; God alone reads the souls of the despised.
* **`[11:32]`** "They said, “O Noah, you have argued with us, and argued a great deal. Now bring upon us what you threaten us with, if you are truthful.”"
  * **Gloss:** Tiredness with argument; the demand to fast-forward the threat — the cycle's goad (7:70's identical dare).
* **`[11:33]`** "He said, “It is Allah who will bring it upon you, if He wills, and you will not be able to escape.”"
  * **Gloss:** The threat de-privatized: the prophet does not own the punishment.
* **`[11:34]`** "“My advice will not benefit you, much as I may want to advise you, if Allah desires to confound you. He is your Lord, and to Him you will be returned.”"
  * **Gloss:** The ruku's theological deepest line: nasihah has no causal power against a divine decree of confound (ghawāy) — sincerity is not leverage.
* **`[11:35]`** "Or do they say, “He made it up?” Say, “If I made it up, upon me falls my crime, and I am innocent of the crimes you commit.”"
  * **Gloss:** The framing-rebuttal (cf. 11:13): the forger's penalty accepted hypothetically, the audience's crimes returned unshared.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`a clear warner` (nadhir mubīn)**: 11:25's self-definition.
  * **Rendering:** first-person formula here.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[71:2]` — Noah verbatim "I am to you a clear warner"; `[51:50–51]` same line; `[26:115]` Hud "I am only a clear warner"; al-A'raf's Noah is paraphrased — "to warn you" `[7:63]` (verified) — three renderings of one office.
* **`no money / no wage`**: the ministers' poverty clause, 11:29.
  * **Rendering:** "I ask of you no money for it" (11:29) — the wage-clause rendered "money" here, "wage" at 11:51.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[11:51]` Hud "I ask you no wage"; `[10:72]` Yunus-cycle Noah "I have not asked you for any wage. My wage falls only on Allah" — the formula family at 36:21, 38:86, 42:23 (verified); `[20:132]` "We ask of you no sustenance" — prophetic economics as monotheist evidence.
* **`the treasures of Allah... the future... an angel`**: the triple disclaimer, 11:31.
  * **Rendering:** "I do not say to you that I possess the treasures of Allah, nor do I know the future, nor do I say that I am an angel" (11:31).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[6:50]` — the same verse almost verbatim ("the treasuries of Allah, nor do I know the future, nor do I say to you that I am an angel"); `[7:188]` "Had I known the future..." — ghayb rendered "the future" in this edition (cf. 11:49, 11:123; verified).
* **`advice` (nasihah)**: 11:34 — the prophet's honest product and its limit.
  * **Rendering:** "My advice will not benefit you, much as I may want to advise you" (11:34).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[7:62]` Noah "I advise you"; `[7:68]` Hud "I am a trustworthy adviser to you"; `[7:79, 93]` Salih and Shu'ayb "I have advised you" — the adviser-title belongs to this cycle across surahs (verified).
