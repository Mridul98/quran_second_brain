# Salih to Thamood: the she-camel, the three-day respite, the Scream (11:61–68)
**UID:** 202610141527
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 11:61–68 (Ruku 6 of 10 in Hud)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[11:61]` – `[11:68]` (8 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The third mission — to Thamood, carved-dwelling people — opens with the Quran's rarest divine self-description ("My Lord is Near and Responsive") and grounds it in their origin ("He initiated you from the earth, and settled you in it"); the test arrives as one animal — Allah's she-camel, a sign — and one prohibition — do not harm her; they hamstring her, take a three-day tourism at home, and the Scream leaves them motionless, "as if they had never prospered therein" — the cycle's shortest, flattest execution.
* **Placement:** Ruku 6; the pattern is now so established that the sermon, the sin, the respite and the wipe-out take eight verses.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[11:61]`** "And to Thamood, their brother Saleh. He said, “O my people, worship Allah, you have no god other than Him. He initiated you from the earth, and settled you in it. So seek His forgiveness, and repent to Him. My Lord is Near and Responsive.”"
  * **Gloss:** Sermon + creation-from-earth clause + settlement (ista'marakum) + forgiveness-call, sealed with the two-name close: Qarib, Mujib.
* **`[11:62]`** "They said, “O Saleh, we had hopes in you before this. Are you trying to prevent us from worshiping what our parents worship? We are in serious doubt regarding what you are calling us to.”"
  * **Gloss:** The disappointment rebate ("we had high hopes of you"), the ancestors-shield, and frank doubt — three registers of refusal in one reply.
* **`[11:63]`** "He said, “O my people, have you considered? If I stand upon clear evidence from my Lord, and He has given me mercy from Him, who would protect me from Allah, if I disobeyed Him? You add nothing for me except loss.”"
  * **Gloss:** The apology formula reused (cf. 11:28), sharpened: to abandon his mission is disobedience — protection-by-God is the prophet's only shield.
* **`[11:64]`** "“O my people, this is the she-camel of Allah, a sign for you. Let her graze on Allah’s land, and do not harm her, lest an imminent punishment overtakes you.”"
  * **Gloss:** The sign is a shared ecology: her grazing, their obligation of non-harm — a command about an animal that reads the whole heart.
* **`[11:65]`** "But they hamstrung her, and so He said, “Enjoy yourselves in your homes for three days. This is a prophecy that is infallible.”"
  * **Gloss:** The crime in three words; the sentence a sightseeing-notice: three days' lease on their own homes — a promise (wa'd) never falsified.
* **`[11:66]`** "Then, when Our command came, We saved Saleh and those who believed with him, by a mercy from Us, from the disgrace of that day. Your Lord is the Strong, the Mighty."
  * **Gloss:** The execution-formula; salvation stamped by mercy; the day named "disgrace"; the two names — Strong, Mighty — as the sentence's signature.
* **`[11:67]`** "And the Scream struck those who transgressed, and they became motionless bodies in their homes."
  * **Gloss:** The sayha (the cry from above, classically) versus the quake-terms of other tellings; the homes that sheltered the hamstrung camel now file their occupants flat (jāthimīn — crouching corpses, rendered "motionless bodies").
* **`[11:68]`** "As if they had never prospered therein. Indeed, Thamood rejected their Lord, so away with Thamood."
  * **Gloss:** The erasure-clause and third "away with" — the prosperity unwritten.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`she-camel`** (nāqat Allāh): the named sign, 11:64.
  * **Rendering:** "this is the she-camel of Allah, a sign for you" (11:64).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[7:73]` "This she-camel of Allah is a sign for you"; `[26:155]` her drinking-turns; `[54:27]` "sending the she-camel as a test"; `[91:13]` "the she-camel of Allah, so let her drink"; `[17:59]` "We gave Thamood the she-camel, a visible sign, but they mistreated her" (all verified) — the Quran's one animal-miracle, told five ways.
* **`Near and Responsive`** (Qarīb Mujīb): 11:61's Lord-names.
  * **Rendering:** "My Lord is Near and Responsive" (11:61).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:186]` — "I Am near; I answer the call of the caller when he calls on Me" (verified) — first-person twin of the same pair; the nearness is the ground of the forgiveness-call.
* **`the Scream`** (ṣayḥa): 11:67.
  * **Rendering:** "the Scream struck those who transgressed" (11:67).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[54:31]` — "We sent against them a single Scream, and they became like crushed hay" (verified); contrast the Thamood-word at `[7:78]` "the quake overtook them... lifeless bodies in their homes" — different strike-words for the same people across surahs (sayha vs rajfa/zalzafa — *Source: translator term choices observable; Arabic root-assignment is agent inference — verify*).
* **`never prospered` / `motionless bodies`**: the erasure pair.
  * **Rendering:** "they became motionless bodies in their homes" (11:67); "As if they had never prospered therein" (11:68).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[7:92]` "Those who rejected Shuaib—as if they never prospered therein"; `[29:37]` "left motionless in their homes" (verified) — and reused for Madyan at 11:95; the formula belongs to the cycle, not to one people.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Meccan; the same dwellers appear as "the people of the Rock" (`[15:80]`) who "carve homes in the mountains, feeling secure" (`[15:82]`) and are struck by "the Blast... in the morning" (`[15:83]`) — the rock-cut monument landscape of the Hijaz read as sermon — *Source: observable corpus verses (15:80–84, verified); the toponym identification of al-Hijr with Thamood is classical — verify.*
* **Term note:** "a prophecy that is infallible" (11:65) renders wa'd — the promise-word as guarantee — *Source: translator's term choice, observable in corpus.*
