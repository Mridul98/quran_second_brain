# Shu'ayb to Median: the stolen scale, "what is left by Allah," and the Blast (11:84–95)
**UID:** 202610141529
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 11:84–95 (Ruku 8 of 10 in Hud)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[11:84]` – `[11:95]` (12 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The cycle's sixth and fullest sermon: Shu'ayb is the only prophet of the six whose announced crime is commercial — short measure and weight against a people "in good circumstances" — and his counter-economy is one line: "What is left by Allah is best for you, if you are believers." The rebuttal is the secularist taunt of the ages ("does your prayer command you?") answered by the reform formula ("I desire nothing but reform, as far as I can"), the history-warning naming Noah, Hud, Saleh — "and the people of Lot are not far away from you" — the tribe-shield mocked, the challenge issued, and the Blast delivered in the surah's own closing mirror of the Thamood wipe-out.
* **Placement:** Ruku 8, last of the six missions; its destruction-report is deliberately the most patterned ("Away with Median, as was done away with Thamood") — the cycle closes by quoting itself.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[11:84]`** "And to Median, their brother Shuaib. He said, “O my people, worship Allah; you have no god other than Him. And do not short measure or short weight. I see you in good circumstances, but I fear for you the agony of an encompassing Day.”"
  * **Gloss:** Tawhid first, then the scale: idolatry and fraud arrive in one breath; the prophet reads their prosperity (khayr) as exposure, not safety — an "encompassing" (muHīṭ) Day.
* **`[11:85]`** "“O my people! Give full measure and full weight, in all fairness, and do not cheat the people out of their rights, and do not spread corruption in the land."
  * **Gloss:** The three commercial commands: measure up, quittance (bashtariyya — "buy not" — God's rights away from people), no corruption-after-reform.
* **`[11:86]`** "What is left by Allah is best for you, if you are believers. And I am not a guardian over you.”"
  * **Gloss:** The counter-ledger: what remains with God (baqiyatullah) outweighs what is skimmed at the scale; and the prophet's limit-clause — no guardianship, only warning.
* **`[11:87]`** "They said, “O Shuaib, does your prayer command you that we abandon what our ancestors worshiped, or doing with our wealth what we want? You are the one who is intelligent and wise.”"
  * **Gloss:** The taunt: religion as a regulatory rival to inheritance-cult and capital autonomy — with the sarcasm of "forbearing/wise" (ḥalīm, rashīd) worn as irony.
* **`[11:88]`** "He said, “O my people, have you considered? What if I have clear evidence from my Lord, and He has given me good livelihood from Himself? I have no desire to do what I forbid you from doing. I desire nothing but reform, as far as I can. My success lies only with Allah. In Him I trust, and to Him I turn.”"
  * **Gloss:** The apology formula (third reuse — cf. 11:28, 63) + integrity clause (he does not practice what he forbids) + the islah-program bounded by capacity + tawakkul and inabah.
* **`[11:89]`** "“O my people, let not your hostility towards me cause you to suffer what was suffered by the people of Noah, or the people of Hud, or the people of Saleh. The people of Lot are not far away from you.”"
  * **Gloss:** The history-warning — three destroyed peoples by name, then the fourth by geography: their trade-routes pass Lot's wreckage (see §6).
* **`[11:90]`** "“And ask your Lord for forgiveness, and repent to Him. My Lord is Merciful and Loving.”"
  * **Gloss:** The forgiveness-call again (11:3, 52), now closed with the pair Raḥīm,wadūd.
* **`[11:91]`** "They said, “O Shuaib, we do not understand much of what you say, and we see that you are weak among us. Were it not for your tribe, we would have stoned you. You are of no value to us.”"
  * **Gloss:** The full contempt stack: incomprehension, physical weakness, tribal immunity as the only thing between him and a stoning, zero value.
* **`[11:92]`** "He said, “O my people, is my tribe more important to you than Allah? And you have turned your backs on Him? My Lord comprehends everything you do.”"
  * **Gloss:** The counter-question: the tribe they fear is nothing against the God they ignore; "comprehends" (muHīṭ) echoes his own Day-warning word.
* **`[11:93]`** "“O my people, do as you may, and so will I. You will know to whom will come a punishment that will shame him, and who is a liar. So look out; I am on the lookout with you.”"
  * **Gloss:** Symmetry of waiting: compete in expectation; knowledge-by-experience again (cf. 11:39).
* **`[11:94]`** "And when Our command came, We saved Shuaib and those who believed with him, by mercy from Us, and the Blast struck the wrongdoers, and they became motionless bodies in their homes."
  * **Gloss:** The execution-formula with the salvation-stamp and the rajfa — same tableau as 11:67, down to the clause.
* **`[11:95]`** "As though they never flourished therein. Away with Median, as was done away with Thamood."
  * **Gloss:** The erasure-clause and the final "away with" — the fourth — now explicitly comparing cycles.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`full measure and full weight`**: 11:85's commercial mitzvah.
  * **Rendering:** prohibition "do not short measure or short weight" (11:84) answered by command "Give full measure and full weight, in all fairness" (11:85).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[6:152]` "give full weight and full measure, equitably"; `[7:85]` Shu'ayb "Give full measure and weight, and do not cheat people out of their rights" (verified — same sermon); `[17:35]` "give full measure when you measure, and weigh with accurate scales"; `[26:181–182]` "Give full measure, and do not cheat. And weigh with accurate scales" (verified) — weights as worship across four surahs.
* **`corruption in the land`** (fasād): 11:85's ban.
  * **Rendering:** "do not spread corruption in the land" (11:85) — the same Shu'ayb phrase in this edition.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[29:36]` — the same Shu'ayb line, "do not spread corruption in the land" (verified); `[7:56]` "do not corrupt on earth after its reformation"; `[7:85]` "do not corrupt the land once it has been set right" (verified) — and see 11:116's reformers of the close.
* **`What is left by Allah`** (baqiyatullāh): 11:86's counter-capital.
  * **Rendering:** exact phrase unique in the corpus (verified: `rg "left by Allah"` → 11:86 only).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[28:60]` "whatever you are given is but the material of this world, and its glitter. But what is with Allah is better, and longer lasting" (verified) — the same ledger-doctrine re-worded; cf. `[12:88]` "Allah rewards the charitable" in a full-measure context — see [Infraq vs riba](../../02-knowledge/202610132004-k-infraq_vs_riba_two_economies.md).
* **`the Blast`** (rajfa): 11:94.
  * **Rendering:** "the Blast struck the wrongdoers" (11:94).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[15:73]` "the Blast struck them at sunrise" and `[15:83]` "the Blast struck them in the morning" (verified) — the same strike-word used for Lot's people and for Thamood in al-Hijr; `[29:40]` "Some were struck by the Blast" (verified); `[23:41]` (verified).
