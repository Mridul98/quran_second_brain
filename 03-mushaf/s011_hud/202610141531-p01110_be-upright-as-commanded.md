# Be upright as you are commanded: the closural battery of Hud (11:110–123)
**UID:** 202610141531
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 11:110–123 (Ruku 10 of 10 in Hud)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[11:110]` – `[11:123]` (14 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah that watched six peoples end in wipe-out now issues its commands to the surviving community: hold the disputed Scripture with a settled prior Word (110); be upright exactly as commanded, without transgression, with the repentant company at your side (112); no alliance-gestures toward wrongdoers (113); prayer at the day's borders as an evil-effacer (114); patience, because the virtuous are never short-paid (115); regret for the vanished reformers (116); the law that God never destroys towns while their people are righteous (117); the counterfactual of willed human unity, mercy-exception and the final Word (118–119); the stated purpose of all the narrated history — heart-strengthening (120); the two-line ultimatum (121–122); and the close: to Allah belongs the future of the heavens and the earth — worship Him, rely on Him; your Lord is never unaware (123).
* **Placement:** Final ruku; the constructive answer to the destruction-cycle. Classically, 11:112 is the verse reports make Umar say "Hud and its sisters whitened my hair" — the command's weight is measured in gray hair, not length (see §6).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[11:110]`** "We gave Moses the Scripture, but it was disputed. Were it not for a prior word from your Lord, it would have been settled between them. They are in serious doubt concerning it."
  * **Gloss:** Scripture and dispute coexist by decree: the prior Word defers the verdict — the same "deferral" policy as 11:8/104, now applied to books, not punishments.
* **`[11:111]`** "Your Lord will repay each one of them in full for their deeds. He is Aware of everything they do."
  * **Gloss:** Deferral is not loss: full repayment, and awareness as the standing record.
* **`[11:112]`** "So be upright, as you are commanded, along with those who repented with you, and do not transgress. He is Seeing of everything you do."
  * **Gloss:** The keystone: isteqim — the straightness asked of 1:6 becomes a command; the community of penitents is the co-commanded; the limit ("do not transgress") keeps zeal inside the road; "Seeing" closes as audit.
* **`[11:113]`** "And do not incline towards those who do wrong, or the Fire may touch you; and you will have no protectors besides Allah, and you will not be saved."
  * **Gloss:** The alliance-prohibition: even the lean (rukn) counts; the Fire is the touching-distance consequence; the no-protectors clause from 11:20 re-applied to the believer's politics.
* **`[11:114]`** "Perform the prayer at the borders of the day, and during the approaches of the night. The good deeds take away the bad deeds. This is a reminder for those who remember."
  * **Gloss:** The practical mercy-engine: scheduled prayer at day's edges and night's approaches; good erase bad — expiation inside the week, not only at the Day; "reminder for those who remember" is the corpus's only such clause (verified).
* **`[11:115]`** "And be patient. Allah will not waste the reward of the virtuous."
  * **Gloss:** The second sabr-command (after 11:49); the doers of ihsan (muhsinin — same noun as 11:3's "possessor of virtue") cannot be robbed of wages.
* **`[11:116]`** "If only there were, among the generations before you, people with wisdom, who spoke against corruption on earth—except for the few whom We saved. But the wrongdoers pursued the luxuries they were indulged in, and thus became guilty."
  * **Gloss:** The counterfactual wish-lament (lawā): where were the enjoining-good party in the destroyed generations? The saved were few (11:40's "only a few" again); the pursued pleasures prosecuted them.
* **`[11:117]`** "Your Lord would never destroy the towns wrongfully, while their inhabitants are righteous."
  * **Gloss:** The non-destruction-law: the cycle was never arbitrary — righteousness in a town is an exemption the text swears by.
* **`[11:118]`** "Had your Lord willed, He could have made humanity one community, but they continue to differ."
  * **Gloss:** The unity counterfactual: a single ummah was available by will; difference is the fact God did not compel around.
* **`[11:119]`** "Except those on whom your Lord has mercy—for that reason He created them. The Word of your Lord is final: “I will fill Hell with jinn and humans, altogether.”"
  * **Gloss:** The mercy-exception flips the purpose clause: for THAT (the differing, the seeking) He created them; and the final Word, verbatim shared with 32:13, fills the account.
* **`[11:120]`** "Everything We narrate to you of the history of the messengers is to strengthen your heart therewith. The truth has come to you in this, and a lesson, and a reminder for the believers."
  * **Gloss:** The surah's own purpose-statement for the six narratives: heart-firming (tuthabbit), truth, admonition, reminder — the four goods of the unseen-stories.
* **`[11:121]`** "And say to those who do not believe, “Act according to your ability; and so will we.”"
  * **Gloss:** The ultimatum of parallel effort — no coercion (cf. 11:28's refusal to compel).
* **`[11:122]`** "And wait; we too are waiting."
  * **Gloss:** The symmetrical wait of 11:93's "look out; I am on the lookout" — now general.
* **`[11:123]`** "To Allah belongs the future of the heavens and the earth, and to Him all authority goes back. So worship Him, and rely on Him. Your Lord is never unaware of what you do."
  * **Gloss:** The close: the ghāyah/outcome belongs to Allah (the same noun as 11:49's "the future belongs to the pious"), all amr returns; worship + reliance (tawakkul, the forelock-trust of 11:56 recommissioned to the reader); unawareness (ghaflah) finally excluded.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`be upright`** (istaqīmū): 11:112, the command-word.
  * **Rendering:** "So be upright, as you are commanded, along with those who repented with you, and do not transgress" (11:112).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[42:15]` — "be upright as you were commanded" (verified) — the corpus's only twin, in ash-Shura, named in tafsir-tradition among "Hud's sisters" (the hard-command surahs; see §6); cf. the petition `[1:6]` and 11:56's "My Lord is on a straight path" — the road's shape and the walker's verb.
* **`incline`** (rukn): the forbidden lean, 11:113.
  * **Rendering:** "do not incline towards those who do wrong" (11:113).
  * **Elsewhere:** the same verb is positive at `[8:61]` — "if they incline towards peace, then incline towards it" — and `[25:71]` — "has inclined towards Allah with repentance" (verified) — direction is the whole morality of the lean; 11:80's "strong support" (rukn shadid) is the noun's other face.
* **`borders of the day`** (ṭarafata al-nahār): 11:114's prayer-timing.
  * **Rendering:** "at the borders of the day, and during the approaches of the night" (11:114).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[20:130]` — "at the borders of the day" for glorification (verified); the approaches of the night ↔ `[39:9]` "watches of the night" (verified); the effacing mechanic has no twin clause — *Source: observable uniqueness (`rg "take away the bad"` → 11:114 only).*
* **`one community` / `the Word is final`**: 11:118–119.
  * **Rendering:** "He could have made humanity one community" (11:118); "The Word of your Lord is final" (11:119).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[42:8]` "Had Allah willed, He could have made them one community" (verified — same counterfactual); `[5:48]` renders the will-clause "a single nation" (drift, verified); `[32:13]` carries 11:119's Hell-filling verbatim (verified); `[6:115]` "The Word of your Lord has been completed" (verified) and 11:110's "prior word" ↔ `[10:19]` "prior decree" — same decree-noun, two renderings.

* **Wording check (assignment):** no "innovations in religion" phrasing occurs at Hud's close — `rg -i "innovat"` = 0 hits in the corpus file (observable). The closural grammar is uprightness, non-transgression, non-leaning, prayer, patience.
* **Revelation context:** Meccan commands addressed to a small community under pressure — the "those who repented with you" clause (11:112) makes the penitents the co-addressees of straightness — *Source: agent observation of addressee grammar.*
