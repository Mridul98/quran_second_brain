## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Meccan — classical consensus. **Revelation order 52** in the standard transmitted enumeration (some classical lists place it 51). *Source: classical revelation-order lists — agent labeling from the transmitted chronology tradition; verify against a printed source before teaching the number.* No basmala is numbered here; the corpus carries the unnumbered header line as in every Meccan surah except at-Tawbah — see [Basmala is unnumbered in this edition except 1:1](../../01-fleeting/202610132006-f-basmala_numbering_in_this_edition.md).
* **Size:** 123 ayahs, 10 ruku (Hanafi/Idad-e-Quran partition per `data/ruku_map.json`). It opens with `Alif, Lam, Ra.` shared with the immediately adjacent surahs Yunus (10:1) and Yusuf (12:1) — see [Muqatta'at letters](../../01-fleeting/202610132007-f-muqattaat_letters_untranslated.md).
* **Central theme:** The six-prophet succession cycle — Noah (11:25–49), Hud to Aad (11:50–60), Salih to Thamood (11:61–68), Abraham's guests (11:69–76), Lot (11:77–83), Shu'ayb to Median (11:84–95) — each sermon identical in form (worship Allah alone; ask forgiveness and repent; the prophet demands no wage) and each ending in destruction-by-refusal at the formula "when Our command came," followed by the proclamation "away with" the people. The cycle is bracketed by the Scripture's self-authentication (11:1–8) and the prophet's psychology under rejection (11:9–24, anchored in 11:12 "You are only a warner, and Allah is Responsible for all things"), hinged on 11:49 — "These are some stories from the past that we reveal to you... So be patient. The future belongs to the pious" — and distilled into law at 11:100–103 ("These are of the reports of the towns..."). A compressed Moses/Pharaoh interlude (11:96–99) adds the tyrant-followers to the pattern. The close is a battery of commands: be upright as commanded (11:112), do not incline to wrongdoers (11:113), prayer at the borders of the day (11:114), be patient (11:115), and "worship Him, and rely on Him" (11:123).
* **Wording check (assignment):** the closing verses contain **no "innovations in religion" phrasing** — `rg -i "innovat|bid"` returns 0 hits in `corpus/s011_hud.md` (observable corpus fact). The actual closural commands are 11:112 ("So be upright, as you are commanded... and do not transgress"), 11:113 ("do not incline towards those who do wrong"), and 11:123 ("So worship Him, and rely on Him"). The "invention" vocabulary in this surah belongs to the *opening* of each cycle's conflict (11:13 "He invented it?", 11:35, 11:50 "You do nothing but invent lies"), not to a closing anti-innovation verse.

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Authentication (ruku 1–2):** the Book declares its maker (11:1 "perfected, then elaborated, from One who is Wise and Informed"), God's knowledge covers the hidden chest (11:5), every creature's sustenance and dwelling (11:6); the tested human types are sorted (11:9–11); the warner is bounded to his function (11:12) and armed with the challenge (11:13–14); the two groups — blind-deaf vs seeing-hearing — close the frame (11:24).
* **The cycle proper (ruku 3–8):** six prophetic units in succession, each with sermon → elite rebuttal → prophet's apology ("Have you considered?" 11:28, 63, 88) → "when Our command came" → salvation of the believers "by a mercy from Us" (11:58, 66, 94) → destruction and the "away with" proclamation (11:44, 60, 68, 95). Ruku 7 interrupts the sequence mid-cycle with the Abraham–Lot guest-story; ruku 4 closes with the surah's hinge-verse 11:49.
* **The interlude and the distillation (ruku 9):** Moses/Pharaoh in four verses (11:96–99), then the law stated abstractly: "These are of the reports of the towns... We did not wrong them, but they wronged themselves" (11:100–101), the grip (11:102), the Day (11:103–108), ending the doubt clause (11:109).
* **Commands (ruku 10):** uprightness, the Fire-warning against alliances with wrongdoers, prayer-as-effacer, patience, the regret for absent reformers, the non-destruction-law (11:117), the difference counterfactual and the final Word (11:118–119), the purpose of all this narration (11:120), two-line ultimatum (11:121–122), and the closing reliance-verse (11:123).
