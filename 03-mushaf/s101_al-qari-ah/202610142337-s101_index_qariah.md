* **Title:** al-Qāri’ah — corpus header "THE SHOCKER" tracks 101:1: Itani's coinage for the striking Event; the word occurs at 101:1–3 only (rg-verified), and the name-question pair (101:2 "What is the Shocker?" / 101:3 "What will explain to you what the Shocker is?") is the surah's engine.
* **Central theme:** The Shocker is announced, interrogated, and pictured (people like scattered moths, mountains like tufted wool), then the scene narrows to a balance-sheet: scales heavy → "a pleasant life"; scales light → home "the Abyss," interrogated a second time and answered: "A Raging Fire." Eschatology as sudden shock, weight, and heat — three images, no narrative.
* **Reading hint:** count the interrogatives (101:2, 101:3, 101:10) — the surah refuses description twice and answers itself once; the second question-and-answer (101:10–11) is the whole warning compressed: the Abyss is not a place but a temperature.

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Name and double interrogation (101:1–3):** bare title, then "What is the Shocker?", then escalation "What will explain to you what the Shocker is?" — the device that also opens 69 and (next door) 104.
* **The two images (101:4–5):** people "like scattered moths"; mountains "like tufted wool" — swarm and weightlessness: the crowd loses order, the solid loses mass.
* **The scales diptych (101:6–9):** "As for he whose scales are heavy" → pleasant life; "But as for he whose scales are light" → home the Abyss — the exact amma-man doublet that 23:102–103 and 7:8–9 use, and that 92:5–10 re-runs morally (all rg-verified).
* **Second question and answer (101:10–11):** "Do you know what it is? / A Raging Fire." — the surah's only noun for Hell, and it is a temperature-word (ḥāmiya; phrase-twin `[70:15]` "By no means! It is a Raging Fire" — rg-verified).
