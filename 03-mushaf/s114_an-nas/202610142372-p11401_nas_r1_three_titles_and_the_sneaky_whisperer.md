# Three Titles, One Whisperer, and the Book's Last Words (114:1–6)
**UID:** 202610142372
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 114:1–6 (Ruku 1 of 1 in an-Nas — global ruku 556, the LAST of 556 — ruku_map verified)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[114:1]` – `[114:6]` (6 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The Quran's final ruku: mankind is told to seek refuge in the triple-titled God of their own species — Lord, King, God of mankind — from the only enemy whose workplace is the heart: the sneaky whisperer, recruited from both jinn and people; the Book ends not with a verdict but with a defensive prayer on the reader's own lips.
* **Placement:** Second half of the Mu'awwidhatan; the closing bracket's final door (109 separation → 110 victory → 111 verdict → 112 creed → 113 outward refuge → 114 inward refuge). Whatever else the Mushaf's canonical order means, its last words stage the reciter as vulnerable-and-protected: the whole book resolves into "I seek refuge."

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[114:1]`** "Say, “I seek refuge in the Lord of mankind."
  * **Gloss:** The fourth and last commanded "Say" of the closing unit (109/112/113/114 — rg-verified). Note the pair-variation with 113:1: "I TAKE refuge WITH" vs here "I SEEK refuge IN" (observable drift — same Arabic verb, two English phrasings). First title: RABB — the nurturer/owner of this species.
* **`[114:2]`** "The King of mankind."
  * **Gloss:** MALIK — sovereignty over the same "mankind"; this edition's only "The King of mankind" (rg-verified); the malik-register family: `[1:4]` "Master", `[59:23]` "the Sovereign", `[20:114]` "the True King", `[3:26]` "Owner of Sovereignty" — the s001 Fatihah passage already runs 1:4↔114:2 as its own cited drift (backlink received).
* **`[114:3]`** "The God of mankind."
  * **Gloss:** ILAH — the worship-relation, closing the escalation nurture→rule→devotion; answers the unit's own 109: "what I worship/serves" now has its one titled object (structural reading — agent inference; the 112:1 "He is Allah, the One" supplies the creed-premise).
* **`[114:4]`** "From the evil of the sneaky whisperer."
  * **Gloss:** WASWAS — rendered as a name-character; the classical epithet *al-Khannas* ("the one who withdraws") survives only inside "sneaky" (hint correction: no "who withdraws" clause exists in this text — rg-verified; withdrawal-behavior is transmitted gloss — verify). ONE enemy this time, vs 113's four — the interior front needs only one.
* **`[114:5]`** "Who whispers into the hearts of people."
  * **Gloss:** The verb explains the noun: waswasa — the whisper-dossier's own verbs: `[7:20]` "Satan whispered to them" (the garden), `[20:120]` "Satan whispered to him… the Tree of Immortality", `[50:16]` "We know what his soul whispers to him" (rg-verified — Allah nearer than the vein, the whisper known before it lands). Venue = "hearts" (qalb), not the hint's "breasts"; register-shift *nas* → "people" (observable).
* **`[114:6]`** "From among jinn and among people.”"
  * **Gloss:** The Book's final verse: the whisperer's cohort is mixed — jinn AND human people; the Quran's last words are "among people" — the reader included (observable terminal placement; the self-inclusion reading = agent inference — verify). The jinn-and-humankind pair-dossier: `[55:33]` "O society of jinn and humans!", `[6:128]` "O assembly of jinn", `[7:38]`/`[7:179]` "multitudes of jinn and humans" (rg-verified) — 114:6 lands the same pairing at the canon's end.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`nas` (mankind/people)**: the species-word — six-for-six.
  * **Rendering:** "mankind" ×3 (114:1–3) then "people" ×2 (114:5–6) — intra-surah drift (rg-verified; header "MANKIND").
  * **Elsewhere:** `[1:2]` "Lord of the Worlds" (*alamin* — the bracket-word, rg-verified); the whisper-target "hearts of people" = hearts of the same species the titles claim — triple ownership before triple protection (structural observation).
* **`rab/malik/ilah` (Lord/King/God)**: the triple address.
  * **Rendering:** "of mankind" fixed to all three (drumbeat — observable).
  * **Elsewhere:** sovereignty-assembly `[59:23]` ("the Sovereign, the Holy…"), kingship-with-creation `[3:26]`, lordship-of-all-worlds `[1:2]` (rg-verified) — 114's escalation is a one-line surah's worth of that litany aimed at one species.
* **`waswas` (the whisperer/whispers)**: the method-name.
  * **Rendering:** "the sneaky whisperer… Who whispers" — noun+verb same root, one surah (as in 113:5's "an envious when he envies" — the pair's matching micro-device, observable).
  * **Elsewhere:** verbs at `[7:20]`, `[20:120]`; the internalized version at `[50:16]`; "the slightest whisper" `[19:98]` (rg-verified); "the urgings of the devils" `[23:97]` — same assault rendered from the refuge-side.
* **`qalb (hearts)`**: the venue.
  * **Rendering:** "hearts" (hint's "breasts" not this edition).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[18:57]` "We have placed coverings over their hearts, lest they understand it" — hearts are where whisper AND seal both operate (rg-verified; 112-passage pair: the whisper is unseen-transmission vs 50:16's all-hearing).
