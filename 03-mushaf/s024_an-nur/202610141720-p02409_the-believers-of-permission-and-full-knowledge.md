# The believers of permission and the God who knows it all (24:62–64)
**UID:** 202610141720
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 24:62–64 (Ruku 9 of 9 in an-Nur)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[24:62]` – `[24:64]` (3 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah closes its permission-arc at the Prophet's assembly: true believers do not leave a common matter until they have asked; the Messenger is told to grant and to seek forgiveness for them (62); address to the Messenger must differ from address between equals, and the slip-away-with-excuses crowd gets a fitna-warning (63) — then the whole book is sealed on total ownership, total knowledge, and the Day's debrief: "Allah has full knowledge of all things" (64).
* **Placement:** Ruku 9; the shortest ruku of the surah, functioning as its signature: the household knock-law (27–29, 58–61) finished at the community's door, with divine knowledge as the lock.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[24:62]`** "The believers are those who believe in Allah and His Messenger, and when they are with him for a matter of common interest, they do not leave until they have asked him for permission. Those who ask your permission are those who believe in Allah and His Messenger. So when they ask your permission to attend to some affair of theirs, give permission to any of them you wish, and ask Allah’s forgiveness for them. Allah is Forgiving and Merciful."
  * **Gloss:** A definition of belief by assembly-manners: staying until dismissed; the permission-seekers are re-named the believers (the word's honor, not its loophole); the Prophet gets discretion ("any of them you wish") plus a command to seek forgiveness *for* them — leadership as intercession.
* **`[24:63]`** "Do not address the Messenger in the same manner you address one another. Allah knows those of you who slip away using flimsy excuses. So let those who oppose his orders beware, lest an ordeal strikes them, or a painful punishment befalls them."
  * **Gloss:** Speech-discipline at the top of the community; the surveillance clause: Allah "knows" the door-sneakers with their pretext-word "flimsy excuses" (unique to this verse, rg-verified); opposing his orders risks ordeal or painful punishment — the surah's last warning.
* **`[24:64]`** "Surely, to Allah belongs everything in the heavens and the earth. He knows what you are about. And on the Day they are returned to Him, He will inform them of what they did. Allah has full knowledge of all things."
  * **Gloss:** The double-ownership-plus-knowledge closer (cf. 24:42): everything belongs, everything is known, the return is announced, and the surah ends where its Light verse ended (24:35) — God cognizant of everything.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`ask permission`** (ista'dhana): `[24:62]` ×3.
  * **Rendering:** the same verb carried from the home (24:27–28), the household clock (24:58–59), and now the assembly — permission as the surah's repeated civic rite.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[33:53]` "unless you are given permission to come for a meal… and when you have eaten, disperse" — the Ahzab twin: there, enter-with-permission and leave-soon; here, stay-until-dismissed.
* **`a matter of common interest`** (amr jami'): `[24:62]`.
  * **Rendering:** unique in the corpus (rg-verified single hit) — the gathered public affair that binds attendance.
* **`flimsy excuses`** ('udhr): `[24:63]`.
  * **Rendering:** "slip away using flimsy excuses" — the pretext-word also unique to this verse in the corpus (rg-verified); sneaking is done *under cover of* an excuse, named and unmaked.
* **`oppose his orders`**: `[24:63]`.
  * **Rendering:** command-disobedience at the Messenger's line; the ordeal/punishment pair ("lest an ordeal strikes them, or a painful punishment befalls them") is the corpus's standard warning-idiom — *Source: agent inference from the formula's recurrence; individual parallels not quoted here.*
* **`full knowledge of all things`**: `[24:64]`.
  * **Rendering:** the surah's terminal divine attribute.
  * **Elsewhere:** within an-Nur the knowledge-clauses form a chain — `[24:30]`, `[24:35]` "Allah is cognizant of everything", `[24:41]`, `[24:53]` "Allah is experienced with what you do", `[24:64]` — the permission-surah ends policed by knowledge; [correction logged] the task's remembered closer "Allah is Knowing of everything" is not the book's wording.
