# The glorifying cosmos and the turnaway claimants (24:41–50)
**UID:** 202610141717
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 24:41–50 (Ruku 6 of 9 in an-Nur)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[24:41]` – `[24:50]` (10 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Everything glorifies God knowingly — heavens, earth, birds in formation, each with its own prayer — because dominion and return belong to Allah (41–42); the sky's weather, the alternation of night and day, and life from water are lessons for the insight-holders (43–45); enlightenment has been sent down (46) — which is why the claimants "we have believed… and we obey" who then refuse judgment are diagnosed, not excused (47–50).
* **Placement:** Ruku 6; the cosmic-argument ruku: the lit-up houses (36–38) and the granted light (35, 40) now become a praying universe, set against hearts that will not come when called.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[24:41]`** "Do you not realize that Allah is glorified by whatever is in the heavens and the earth, and even by the birds in formation? Each knows its prayer and its manner of praise. Allah knows well what they do."
  * **Gloss:** Tasbih as universal and *knowing* — creation's liturgy is not metaphor; "each knows its prayer and its manner of praise"; God's knowledge answers its creatures'.
* **`[24:42]`** "To Allah belongs the dominion of the heavens and the earth, and to Allah is the ultimate return."
  * **Gloss:** The sovereignty-and-return clause: ownership now, destination later — the unit's creedal hinge (echoed verbatim-style in 64).
* **`[24:43]`** "Have you not seen how Allah propels the clouds, then brings them together, then piles them into a heap, and you see rain drops emerging from its midst? How He brings down loads of hail from the sky, striking with it whomever He wills, and diverting it from whomever He wills? The flash of its lightening almost snatches the sight away."
  * **Gloss:** Meteorology as will: clouds driven, heaped, rain, selective hail, blinding flash — the same sky that gives light can blind (cf. 24:40's unseen hand); [sic] "lightening" spelling is the book's typography.
* **`[24:44]`** "Allah alternates the night and the day. In that is a lesson for those who have insight."
  * **Gloss:** Time's exchange as ʿibra — the abshar ("insight-holders") are the audience the parables were for (24:35).
* **`[24:45]`** "Allah created every living creature from water. Some of them crawl on their bellies, and some walk on two feet, and others walk on four. Allah creates whatever He wills. Allah is Capable of everything."
  * **Gloss:** The biological sign: water as origin, locomotion as variety — power over creation closes with "Capable of everything."
* **`[24:46]`** "We sent down enlightening revelations, and Allah guides whomever He wills to a straight path."
  * **Gloss:** Revelation is the third sign-register (sky, life, Scripture); the guidance-clause repeats 24:35's grammar in the flat idiom of the straight path.
* **`[24:47]`** "And they say, “We have believed in Allah and the Messenger, and we obey,” but some of them turn away afterwards. These are not believers."
  * **Gloss:** The claim-audit: lip-creed + turnaway; the verdict — "These are not believers" — the surah's own nifaq diagnosis.
* **`[24:48]`** "And when they are called to Allah and His Messenger, in order to judge between them, some of them refuse."
  * **Gloss:** The test is the summons-to-judgment; refusal is the tell.
* **`[24:49]`** "But if justice is on their side, they accept it willingly."
  * **Gloss:** Their obedience runs to self-interest, not to God — the criterion is who wins, not what is right.
* **`[24:50]`** "Is there sickness in their hearts? Or are they suspicious? Or do they fear that Allah may do them injustice? Or His Messenger? In fact, they themselves are the unjust."
  * **Gloss:** Four differential diagnoses (sick heart, doubt, fear of divine injustice) then the reversal: the unjust are the refusers themselves.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`glorified`** (sabbaha): `[24:41]`, with `[24:36]` "He is glorified therein."
  * **Rendering:** passive universal — "Allah is glorified by whatever is in the heavens and the earth."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[57:1]` "Glorifying Allah is everything in the heavens and the earth" — word-identical claim, inverted syntax; the corpus's doxology-of-all-things family.
* **`birds in formation`** (ṣāffāt): `[24:41]`.
  * **Rendering:** rows of birds glorifying; each creature with "its prayer (salat) and its manner of praise (tasbih)."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[67:19]` "the birds above them, spreading their wings, and folding them? None holds them except the Compassionate" — the sign-of-birds dossier, a different verb (flight vs. rank); pair them, don't merge them.
* **`enlightening revelations`** (āyāt mubayyinat / mubin): `[24:46]`, `[24:34]` "clarifying revelations", `[24:1]` "clear Verses."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[44:2]` "By the Enlightening Scripture"; `[35:25]` "the Enlightening Scripture"; `[27:13]` "Our enlightening signs came to them, they said, 'This is obvious witchcraft'" — the Book's self-luminous adjective (n-w-r root again, tying 24:46 to 24:35).
* **`alternates`** (naql/yuqallib night and day): `[24:44]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[3:27]` "You merge the night into the day, and You merge the day into the night" — same sign-claim, Ali-Imran's prayer form.
* **`from water`**: `[24:45]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[21:30]` "We made from water every living thing" — near-verbatim twin; the creation-from-water doctrine occurs only at these two poles of the corpus (rg-verified as the two hits for the phrase-family).

## 4. Cross-References (The Web)
* **Parallel:** `[57:1]` — the doxology of everything; with 24:41 the sharpest tasbih-twin in the corpus.
* **Parallel:** `[21:30]` — water-origin of life; read 24:45 against it for the corpus's single creation-from-water pair.
* **Within surah:** `[24:43]`'s blinding flash ↔ `[24:40]`'s invisible hand — light given and light withheld in consecutive image-pairs of the surah; `[24:46]` ↔ `[24:34]` ↔ `[24:1]` — the revelation-inclusio across three ruku (kalam dossier).
* **Parallel:** `[4:77]` — "Have you not considered those who were told, 'Restrain your hands'… when fighting was ordained for them, a faction of them feared the people as Allah ought to be feared" — the same self-interest-obedience diagnosis; 24:49's "if justice is on their side" is its courtroom mirror.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Medinan (classical lists — verify); the cosmic-sign block (41–45) is often read as Meccan-style da'wa material placed in Medinan law — a literary judgment, not a transmission fact (*Source: agent inference — verify*).
* **Asbab / narrative context:** the turnaway-claimants of 47–50 are unnamed in the text; classical readers attach them to hypocrites or to parties avoiding the Messenger's arbitration — *Source: classical tafsir — verify*.
