* **Order:** 102 per the Ibn-Abbas-derived enumeration table (same convention as the s016/s013/s022 indices). *Source: transmitted nuzul-order tables — verify.*
* **Size:** 64 ayahs, 9 ruku (`data/ruku_map.json`: 10+10+6+8+6+10+7+4+3 = 64 ✓). Mushaf position 24, between al-Mu'minun (23) and al-Furqan (25).
* **Name:** an-Nur — "The Light," named for the lamp-parable of 24:35; the light motif frames the surah's cosmos (lamps in glorified houses, 36–38; the mirage and layered dark for the deniers, 39–40; "enlightening revelations," 46). Yet the surah's working title is communal chastity: its legal blocks (2–10, 27–34, 58–61) legislate sex, speech, homes, gazes, thresholds, and tables.

## 3. Structural Arc
* **The chapter's self-claim and its statutes (1–10):** "a chapter that We have revealed, and made obligatory" opens; the lash for adultery (2), the marriage bar (3), eighty lashes + testimony-ban for unproven accusation of chaste women (4–5), and the five-fold oath procedure for spousal accusation (6–10) — law as communal self-defense of honor.
* **The episode inside the law (11–20):** the slander becomes theology: it came from "a band of you"; accepting it is "trivial" only to you, "but according to Allah, it is serious"; four witnesses or liars (13); grace-and-mercy refrains (10, 14, 20) hold the punishment back.
* **Purification and its enemies (21–26):** no footsteps of Satan (21); pardon and overlook for the affluent (22); the slanderers of chaste women cursed, limbs testifying (23–25); the chiasmus "good women for good men" acquits (26).
* **Thresholds, gazes, houses of God (27–40):** permission before entering (27–29), gazes lowered and coverings drawn (30–31), marriage for the single with provision promised (32–33) — then the Light verse (35) with its lit-up houses (36–38) and its twin darkness mirrors (39–40): law and luminance in one arc.
* **The praying cosmos and the testing community (41–57):** everything glorifies, birds in formation (41), clouds/hail/night-and-day as lessons for the insight-holders (43–44), every creature from water (45); then the turnaway claimants (47–50) answered by "We hear and we obey" (51–54) and the succession-security promise (55–57).
* **The household close (58–64):** three privacy hours (58–59), the post-menopausal leniency (60), the exempted and the open tables (61), permission culture at the Prophet's assembly (62–63), and "to Allah belongs everything in the heavens and the earth… Allah has full knowledge of all things" (64).
