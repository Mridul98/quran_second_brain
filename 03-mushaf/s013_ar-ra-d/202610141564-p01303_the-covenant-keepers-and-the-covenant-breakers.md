# The covenant-keepers win the Ultimate Home; the covenant-breakers take the curse (13:19–26)
**UID:** 202610141564
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 13:19–26 (Ruku 3 of 6 in ar-Ra’d)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[13:19]` – `[13:26]` (8 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The froth parable applied to people: one pair — he who knows the revealed truth is the truth versus him who is blind — splits into two biographies, the covenant-keepers (promise kept, ties joined, evil repelled with good) who inherit the Ultimate Home, and the covenant-breakers (promise violated after pledging, ties severed, corruption spread) who take the curse; and the closing verse relativizes the stakes — worldly provision, dispensed and restricted at will, is not honor.
* **Placement:** The ethical pivot of the surah: ruku 1–2 argued from cosmos to parable; ruku 3 is the parable cashed out as covenant behavior, and it is the surah’s densest contribution to the brain’s covenant theme.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[13:19]`** “Is he who knows that what was revealed to your from your Lord is the truth, like him who is blind? Only those who reason will remember.”
  * **Gloss:** The froth parable’s verdict in one question — sight vs. blindness redefined as recognition of revelation; only intellect-people take heed. The corpus line prints “revealed to your from your Lord” — book typo for “to you”, quoted verbatim.
* **`[13:20]`** “Those who fulfill the promise to Allah, and do not violate the agreement.”
  * **Gloss:** Portrait line one: the promise (‘ahd) kept, the contract (‘aqd) intact — covenant vocabulary in the surah’s own words.
* **`[13:21]`** “And those who join what Allah has commanded to be joined, and fear their Lord, and dread the dire reckoning.”
  * **Gloss:** Line two: silat — joining the ties Allah commands (kinship, and by extension every commanded bond); taqwa rendered as dread of the reckoning.
* **`[13:22]`** “And those who patiently seek the presence of their Lord, and pray regularly, and spend from Our provisions to them, secretly and openly, and repel evil with good. These will have the Ultimate Home.”
  * **Gloss:** The five-act compressed life: sabr, prayer, infaq dual-channel (secret and open), evil counter-attacked with good — verdict: “the Ultimate Home.”
* **`[13:23]`** “Everlasting Gardens, which they will enter, along with the righteous among their parents, and their spouses, and their descendants. And the angels will enter upon them from every gate.”
  * **Gloss:** Family reunification conditioned on righteousness (“the righteous among”), and angelic greetings from every gate — no fear entering from any direction.
* **`[13:24]`** ““Peace be upon you, because you endured patiently. How excellent is the Final Home.””
  * **Gloss:** The reward explicitly receipted to *sabr* — the endurance named in 13:22 is what the greeting pays.
* **`[13:25]`** “As for those who violate the promise to Allah, after pledging to keep it, and sever what Allah has commanded to be joined, and spread corruption on earth—these, the curse will be upon them, and they will have the Worst Home.”
  * **Gloss:** The mirror-verse: same three acts inverted (violate after ratifying, sever, corrupt on earth) → curse + “the Worst Home.” This is the surah’s breach-verse (see Covenant link).
* **`[13:26]`** “Allah dispenses the provisions to whomever He wills, and restricts. And they delight in the worldly life; yet the worldly life, compared to the Hereafter, is only enjoyment.”
  * **Gloss:** Wealth-flow is sovereign discretion, not moral endorsement; the delighters are answered with the world’s true size: “only enjoyment.”

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`promise / agreement`** (‘ahd / ‘aqd) `[13:20`, `13:25]`.
  * **Rendering:** “fulfill the promise to Allah, and do not violate the agreement” / “violate the promise to Allah, after pledging to keep it.”
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:27]` “Those who violate Allah’s covenant after its confirmation, and sever what Allah has commanded to be joined” — verbatim twin of 13:25’s second act; `[16:91]` “Fulfill Allah’s covenant when you make a covenant, and do not break your oaths after ratifying them”; `[8:56]` “they violate their agreement every time.”
* **`join / sever what Allah has commanded to be joined`** `[13:21`, `13:25]` (silat / qat‘).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:27]` (as above) — the same paired verbs; the tie to be joined is kinship and every bond God welds. *Source: classical gloss “kinship ties” as standard in tafsir on 13:21/25 — verify.*
* **`repel evil with good`** `[13:22]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[41:34]` “Repel evil with good, and the person who was your enemy becomes like an intimate friend”; `[28:54]` “they counter evil with good” — three sites, one ethic, graded in effect.
* **`Ultimate Home / Final Home / Worst Home`** `[13:22`, `13:24–25`, `13:42]`.
  * **Rendering:** al-ma’abat al-ula — “the Ultimate Home”; its obverse “the Worst Home.”
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:94]` “If the Final Home with Allah is yours alone…” — same phrase used against an exclusive-paradise claim; the frame returns at `[13:42]` (“will know to whom the Ultimate Home is”) closing the surah on this ruku’s verdict.
