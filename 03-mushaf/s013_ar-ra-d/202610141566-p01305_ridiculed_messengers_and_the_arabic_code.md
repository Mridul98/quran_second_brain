# Ridiculed messengers, the Watchful One, and the Arabic code of law (13:32–37)
**UID:** 202610141566
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 13:32–37 (Ruku 5 of 6 in ar-Ra’d)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[13:32]` – `[13:37]` (6 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The consolation of pattern: messengers before you were ridiculed and their mockers seized after respite (32), so stop demanding partners be named — the Watchful One over every soul’s deeds needs no briefing (33); worldly torment and Hereafter torment bracket the schemers (34), the Garden is sketched for those who guard (35), the given-Scripture rejoice while factions cherry-pick (36), and the warning turns on the Messenger himself: this is an Arabic code of law, and after the knowledge, desires have no escort (37).
* **Placement:** The surah’s historical-theological turn: from sign-refusal (ruku 4) to the prophetic-sociology law (“ridicule → respite → seizure”) and the self-limiting clause of 13:37, which prepares ruku 6’s “for every era is a scripture.”

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[13:32]`** “Messengers before you were ridiculed, but I granted the disbelievers respite, and then I seized them. What a punishment it was!”
  * **Gloss:** The divine first-person switches in mid-surah for the memory-of-nations motif: ridicule is not a refutation; respite is not acquittal.
* **`[13:33]`** “Is He who is watchful over the deeds of every soul? Yet they ascribe associates to Allah. Say, “Name them! Or are you informing Him of something on earth He does not know, or is it a show of words?” In fact, the scheming of those who disbelieve is made to appear good to them, and they are averted from the path. Whomever Allah misguides has no guide.”
  * **Gloss:** The question-word opens (the Watchful One — the answer embedded); “Name them!” puts the polytheists’ inventory to a burden of proof; then the diagnosis: schemes beautified, path-blocking, and the no-guide clause.
* **`[13:34]`** “There is for them torment in the worldly life, but the torment of the Hereafter is harsher. And they have no defender against Allah.”
  * **Gloss:** Two-stage penalty; “defender against Allah” repeats the surah’s no-protector family (13:11, 13:16).
* **`[13:35]`** “The likeness of the Garden promised to the righteous: rivers flowing beneath it; its food is perpetual, and so is its shade. Such is the sequel for those who guard against evil, but the sequel of the disbelievers is the Fire.”
  * **Gloss:** The one Garden-simile in the surah — perpetual food, perpetual shade (no heat, no hunger); “sequel” (‘uqba) makes history the verdict column.
* **`[13:36]`** “Those to whom We gave the Scripture rejoice in what was revealed to you, while some factions reject parts of it. Say, “I am commanded to worship Allah, and to never associate anything with Him. To Him I invite, and to Him is my return.””
  * **Gloss:** The People of the Book split between rejoicing partisans and part-rejecting factions; the Messenger’s counter-program is the plainest possible: worship without association, invitation without apology.
* **`[13:37]`** “Thus We revealed it an Arabic code of law. Were you to follow their desires, after the knowledge that has come to you, you would have neither ally nor defender against Allah.”
  * **Gloss:** Revelation has a chosen language — “code of law” (hukm) in Arabic; and the surah’s sharpest address: the Prophet himself is not exempt if desires follow knowledge. The factional pressure of 13:36 is what “their desires” points at.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`ridicule`** `[13:32]`.
  * **Rendering:** passive — “Messengers before you were ridiculed.”
  * **Elsewhere:** `[6:10]` and `[21:41]` — “Messengers before you were ridiculed, but those who mocked them became besieged by what they ridiculed” — formula twins; `[43:7]` “No messenger came to them, but they ridiculed him”; `[36:30]` “Alas for the servants.”
* **`respite … seized`** `[13:32]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[35:45]` “He defers them until a stated time. Then, when their time has arrived—Allah is Observant of His creatures” — the respite clause with its end-marker; cf. `[13:5]`’s inhabitants of Fire and `[13:31]`’s disasters-on-deeds.
* **`Arabic code of law`** `[13:37]`.
  * **Rendering:** Itani’s unique “code of law” for hukm — elsewhere the collocation is “an Arabic Quran.”
  * **Elsewhere:** `[12:2]`, `[20:113]`, `[43:3]`, `[39:28]`, `[41:3]`, `[42:7]` “an Arabic Quran”; `[26:195]` “a clear Arabic tongue”; `[16:103]` the rebuttal — “the tongue of him they allude to is foreign, while this is a clear Arabic tongue.”
* **`follow their desires`** `[13:37]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:120]` and `[2:145]` — “Should you follow their desires, after the knowledge that has come to you, you will have in Allah neither guardian nor helper” / “…you would be in that case one of the wrongdoers” — 13:37 is this formula’s near-verbatim third site; `[5:48–49]` the same warning to the judge-community.

## 4. Cross-References (The Web)
* **Watchful omniscience:** `[57:4]` — “He knows what penetrates into the earth, and what comes out of it… And He is with you wherever you may be” — the Watchful One of 13:33 with His mobility clauses.
* **No defender against Allah:** `[40:33]` — “having no defender against Allah. Whomever Allah misguides has no guide” — 13:33–34’s two clauses welded in one Ghafir verse; also 13:37’s consequence is exactly 40:33’s bracket.
* **Part-belief:** `[2:85]` — “you believe in part of the Scripture, and disbelieve in part” — 13:36’s “factions reject parts of it,” the cherry-picking pathology the nifaq note catalogues as a trade that does not clear.
* **Perpetual Garden:** `[39:20]` — “mansions upon mansions, built high, with streams flowing beneath them. The promise of Allah; and Allah never breaks a promise” — 13:35’s rivers-beneath and 13:31’s promise-clip share their ledger.
* **Within surah:** “scheming/planning” 13:33 → 13:42 (“Those before them planned, but the entire plan is up to Allah”); the averted-from-the-path clause (13:33) is ruku 1’s guidance-claim (13:7) inverted; 13:30 “communities before which others have passed” is retold as the ridicule-respite-seizure law in 13:32.
