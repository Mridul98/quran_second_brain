# Hearts find comfort in remembrance — and no mountain-moving Quran is coming (13:27–31)
**UID:** 202610141565
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 13:27–31 (Ruku 4 of 6 in ar-Ra’d)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[13:27]` – `[13:31]` (5 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The miracle-demand is heard a second time and refused a second time — guidance now runs through repentance, not spectacle (27); the famous comfort-verse (28) names the true miracle the objectors did not ask for, a settled heart (29); and the surah dares the impossible Quran — mountains moved, earth shattered, dead speaking — and predicts the hard hearts would explain even *that* away, because every decision rests with Allah (30–31).
* **Placement:** Mid-surah hinge: ruku 3 sorted people by covenant conduct; ruku 4 sorts them by their appetite for signs, then pivots the surah from argument to consolation.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[13:27]`** “Those who disbelieve say, “If only a miracle was sent down to him from his Lord.” Say, “Allah leads astray whomever He wills, and He guides to Himself whoever repents.””
  * **Gloss:** Demand restated from 13:7 almost verbatim; the answer re-routes sign-seeking through the twin verb-pair — misguiding and guiding — and makes *repentance* the qualifying stance.
* **`[13:28]`** “Those who believe, and whose hearts find comfort in the remembrance of Allah. Surely, it is in the remembrance of Allah that hearts find comfort.””
  * **Gloss:** The surah’s second most-quoted line (with 13:11): the heart’s stillness is a *where*, “in the remembrance of Allah,” doubled by the asseverative clause — the spectacle they demanded is the tranquility they ignored. (Line ends with a stray closing quote in the edition — [sic].)
* **`[13:29]`** “For those who believe and do righteous deeds—for them is happiness and a beautiful return.”
  * **Gloss:** Two-word reward: *happiness* (khayr/“good”) and a “beautiful return” — the ma’ab-family continues from 13:22–25’s Home verses.
* **`[13:30]`** “Thus We sent you among a community before which other communities have passed away, that you may recite to them what We revealed to you. Yet they deny the Benevolent One. Say, “He is my Lord; there is no god but He; in Him I trust, and to Him is my repentance.””
  * **Gloss:** The Messenger’s office re-stated (recitation, not spectacle); the denial is named precisely — denial of *ar-Rahman*, the Benevolent — answered by the creed handshake: no god but He, trust, and repentance back toward Him (mirroring 27’s “whoever repents”).
* **`[13:31]`** “Even if there were a Quran, by which mountains could be set in motion, or by which the earth could be shattered, or by which the dead could be made to speak. In fact, every decision rests with Allah. Did the believers not give up and realize that had Allah willed, He would have guided all humanity? Disasters will continue to strike those who disbelieve, because of their deeds, or they fall near their homes, until Allah’s promise comes true. Allah never breaks a promise.”
  * **Gloss:** The counterfactual Quran that never arrives — the apodosis is withheld: even it would not convert the obstinate (classical reading); sovereignty over every decision (12:40), the scandal that guidance is willed not forced, and the ticking clock of disaster-on-deeds until the promise lands — “Allah never breaks a promise,” the divine side of the covenant ruku.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`remembrance of Allah`** (dhikr) `[13:28]`.
  * **Rendering:** “in the remembrance of Allah” — twice in one verse.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[29:45]` “the remembrance of Allah is greater” (prayer framed inside dhikr); `[57:16]` “surrender their hearts to the remembrance of Allah” — the hearts-command twin; `[39:23]` skins and hearts “soften up to the remembrance of Allah”; `[62:9]` hasten to it on Congregation Day; `[63:9]` wealth and children must not distract from it; `[5:91]` intoxicants and gambling exist to prevent it.
* **`find comfort`** (root ṭ-m-’-n, tranquility) `[13:28]`.
  * **Rendering:** Itani’s four sites for the one root: 13:28 “hearts **find comfort**”; `[2:260]` Abraham’s “to put my heart **at ease**”; `[89:27]` “O **tranquil** soul”; `[20:25]` Moses’ “put my heart **at peace** for me” — quote these exact renderings when tracing the lineage; the brain’s shorthand “hearts find rest” is a paraphrase of 13:28’s “comfort,” not the edition’s word.
* **`leads astray … guides`** `[13:27]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[35:8]` “Allah leads astray whomever He wills, and He guides whomever He wills” — formula twin; `[7:186]` and `[39:23]` “Whomever Allah misguides has no guide” — 13:33 will reuse it; cf. `[10:99]` “Had your Lord willed, everyone on earth would have believed” for 13:31’s closing clause.
* **`Benevolent One`** (ar-Rahman) `[13:30]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:163]` “your God is one God. There is no god but He, the Benevolent, the Compassionate”; `[21:36]` “they reject the mention of the Merciful” — same denial verb against the same name-family. *Source: name-identity (Rahman = Benevolent/Merciful across Itani’s renderings) — observable corpus fact + agent pairing, verify.*

## 4. Cross-References (The Web)
* **Mountain-Quran pair:** `[59:21]` — “Had We sent this Quran down on a mountain, you would have seen it trembling, crumbling” — the Quran’s weight *on a mountain* (59) versus mountains set in motion *as demanded proof* (13:31): one grants the imagery, the other refuses the spectacle.
* **Judgment formula:** `[12:40]` “Judgment belongs to none but Allah” — the twin of 13:31 “every decision rests with Allah,” in the surah that said messengers cannot bring signs except by Allah’s leave (`[14:11]`, coming in ruku 6’s `[13:38]`).
* **Promise-keeping God:** `[3:9]` / `[2:80]` / `[39:20]` — “Allah never breaks a promise” — 13:31’s end-clip places divine fidelity beside 13:20’s human covenant-keeping: breach is human, never His.
* **Within surah:** 13:27 mirrors 13:7 (the demand, twice refused); 13:28’s comfort answers 13:26’s worldly delight; 13:30 “communities before which others have passed” re-loads the respite-seizure pattern of ruku 5 (13:32).

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** disputed (index §1); the Meccan-camp rhetorics (Maududi’s internal evidence) put precisely this kind of sign-refusal in the late-Meccan preaching sequence. *Source: Tafhim intro to 13 — labeled, verify.*
* **Reading of 13:31’s withheld apodosis:** the “Even if there were a Quran…” sentence stops without a consequence; classical grammarians take it as a denial — such a Quran does not exist, and even its miracles would not move the obstinate. *Source: classical conditional-of-denial reading (Zamakhshari-type rhetorical analysis, standard in tafsir on 13:31) — agent inference from the corpus line, verify.*
