# For every era a scripture: deliver, and leave the reckoning — Allah the sufficient witness (13:38–43)
**UID:** 202610141567
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 13:38–43 (Ruku 6 of 6 in ar-Ra’d)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[13:38]` – `[13:43]` (6 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah contracts the Messenger’s office to its legal minimum and its divine maximum: messengers came human, married, childed, and never produced a sign except by Allah’s permission — for every era a scripture, and Allah abolishes or affirms what He wills (38–39); “your duty is to inform, and Ours is the reckoning” (40); Allah judges, unmoved by human planning, reducing the land at its edges and settling accounts fast (41–42); and the final denial — “You are not a messenger” — is answered with the surah’s last word: Allah is witness enough, and whoever has knowledge of the Scripture knows (43).
* **Placement:** Closing ruku. It retrieves every thread: the miracle-demand (13:7, 27) is here declared procedurally impossible without divine leave; the sign-epochs of 13:38 answer ruku 1’s “to every community is a guide”; the Ultimate Home of 13:22 returns as the question-mark of 13:42; the Book-claim of 13:1 closes under Allah’s own testimony (13:43).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[13:38]`** “We sent messengers before you, and We assigned for them wives and offspring. No messenger could bring a sign except with the permission of Allah. For every era is a scripture.”
  * **Gloss:** Normalization of prophecy — human households, no autonomous miracles — and the periodization clause: each age carries its own book-portion; ar-Ra’d’s answer to “why no new miracle?” is that signs run on schedule, not on demand.
* **`[13:39]`** “Allah abolishes whatever He wills, and He affirms. With Him is the source of the Scripture.”
  * **Gloss:** Legislative sovereignty inside revelation itself: erasing and confirming belong to Allah alone; “the source of the Scripture” (umm al-kitab) is where the epochs are held.
* **`[13:40]`** “Whether We show you some of what We have promised them, or We cause you to die—your duty is to inform, and Ours is the reckoning.”
  * **Gloss:** The job description, final version: delivery, not outcome. Death of the Messenger changes nothing — the reckoning was never his. The surah’s closing claim of the clear warner-only office (cf. 13:7).
* **`[13:41]`** “Do they not see how We deal with the earth, diminishing it at its edges? Allah judges; and nothing can hold back His judgment. And He is quick to settle accounts.”
  * **Gloss:** Geography as sermon — land shrinking at its rims (conquest, erosion, loss: classical options); then three short clauses: Allah judges, none intercepts, none is late.
* **`[13:42]`** “Those before them planned, but the entire plan is up to Allah. He knows what every soul earns. Those who disbelieve will know to whom the Ultimate Home is.”
  * **Gloss:** The schemers of every era (13:33) met by the whole-plan verse; earnings fully known; and the surah’s covenant word — “the Ultimate Home” (13:22) — turns into a future lesson for the disbelievers.
* **`[13:43]`** “Those who disbelieve say, “You are not a messenger.” Say, “Allah is a sufficient witness between me and you, and whoever has knowledge of the Scripture.””
  * **Gloss:** The last human sentence in the surah is a denial; the last divine command is an appeal to two witnesses: Allah Himself, and the one(s) with Scripture-knowledge — the Book testifying alongside its Sender.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`permission of Allah` (signs)** `[13:38]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[40:78]` “No messenger can bring a miracle except by leave of Allah” — verbatim twin; `[14:11]` “We cannot possibly show you any proof, except by leave of Allah” — messengers’ own first-person form; cf. `[35:13]`/`[31:29]` the luminaries running “for a stated term” by His regulation.
* **`abolishes … affirms`** `[13:39]`.
  * **Rendering:** “Allah abolishes whatever He wills, and He affirms.”
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:106]` “We never nullify a verse, nor cause it to be forgotten, unless We bring one better than it, or similar to it” — the abrogation-adjacent twin; naskh-theology belongs to the kalam note’s ledger. *Source: classical reading linking 13:39 to 2:106 (abrogation discussions in the tafsir of both) — verify.*
* **`duty is to inform`** `[13:40]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[16:82]` “But if they turn away, your only duty is clear communication”; `[5:92]` “the duty of Our Messenger is clear communication” — the same contract in the surah whose passage note [p00510](../s005_al-ma-idah/202610141211-p00510_deliver-without-fear.md) pairs it with “deliver what has been revealed” (`[5:67]`) and do-not-fear.
* **`sufficient witness`** `[13:43]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[46:8]` “He is sufficient witness between me and you. He is the Forgiver, the Merciful”; `[4:166]` “Allah bears witness to what He revealed to you… Though Allah is a sufficient witness”; `[10:29]` the Day’s formula “Allah is sufficient witness between us and you” — 13:43 is the surah’s signature of that courtroom idiom.
