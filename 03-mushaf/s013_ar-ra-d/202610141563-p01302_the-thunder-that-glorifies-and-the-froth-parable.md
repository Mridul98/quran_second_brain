# The thunder glorifies: omniscience, storm-theology, and the froth parable (13:8–18)
**UID:** 202610141563
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 13:8–18 (Ruku 2 of 6 in ar-Ra’d)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[13:8]` – `[13:18]` (11 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Against the demanded miracle, this ruku stages the miracle already happening: a God who measures every womb and every event, whose thunder praises Him and whose lightning is fear and hope at once, before whom all creation prostrates — and it distills the argument into one parable: truth settles, froth is swept away.
* **Placement:** The surah’s core. Ruku 1 asked “why no miracle?”; ruku 2 answers with sky, worship-cosmos, and parable — the longest ruku and the one carrying the surah’s name-verse `[13:13]`.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[13:8]`** “Allah knows what every female bears, and every increase and decrease of the wombs. With Him, everything is by measure.”
  * **Gloss:** Omniscience at its most intimate (the womb) + the keyword: everything *by measure* — destiny as calibration, not chaos.
* **`[13:9]`** “The Knower of the Invisible and the Visible; the Grand, the Supreme.”
  * **Gloss:** The two-domain name-pair; the surah’s God spans what is hidden and what is seen — matching 13:10’s concealed/declared speech.
* **`[13:10]`** “It is the same; whether one of you conceals his speech, or declares it; whether he goes into hiding by night, or goes out by day.”
  * **Gloss:** Concealment buys nothing before that knowledge — a leveling of secret plots (relevant to the schemers of 13:42).
* **`[13:11]`** “He has a succession; before him and behind him, protecting him by Allah’s command. Allah does not change the condition of a people until they change what is within themselves. And if Allah wills any hardship for a people, there is no turning it back; and apart from Him they have no protector.”
  * **Gloss:** Relay-guards around every soul by Allah’s command; then the surah’s most-quoted civic law: collective condition changes only from inside; outside, no protector.
* **`[13:12]`** “It is He who shows you the lightening, causing fear and hope. And He produces the heavy clouds.”
  * **Gloss:** The storm in two faces: fear (the bolt) and hope (the rain) in one flash; “heavy clouds” pregnant with water — the sign-science of the farmer’s sky. The edition prints “lightening” where “lightning” is meant (book typo — quoted as-is).
* **`[13:13]`** “The thunder praises His glory, and so do the angels, in awe of Him. And He sends the thunderbolts, striking with them whomever He wills. Yet they argue about Allah, while He is Tremendous in might.”
  * **Gloss:** The name-verse: thunder is a doxology, angels its chorus, thunderbolts His arrows — and the absurdity: *people dispute about this God.* Cosomic praise vs. human argument.
* **`[13:14]`** “To Him belongs the call to truth. Those they call upon besides Him do not respond to them with anything—except as someone who stretches his hands towards water, so that it may reach his mouth, but it does not reach it. The prayers of the unbelievers are only in vain.”
  * **Gloss:** The water-thirst parable: dead idols cup hands at unreachable water; the “call of truth” belongs to Allah alone.
* **`[13:15]`** “To Allah prostrates everyone in the heavens and the earth, willingly or unwillingly, as do their shadows, in the morning and in the evening.”
  * **Gloss:** Universal sajdah — voluntary and conscripted; even shadows bow twice a day. (This is the surah’s sajdah-ayah in the transmitted lists.)
* **`[13:16]`** “Say, “Who is the Lord of the heavens and the earth?” Say, “Allah.” Say, “Have you taken besides Him protectors, who have no power to profit or harm even themselves?” Say, “Are the blind and the seeing equal? Or are darkness and light equal? Or have they assigned to Allah associates, who created the likes of His creation, so that the creations seemed to them alike? Say, “Allah is the Creator of all things, and He is The One, the Irresistible.””
  * **Gloss:** A rapid-fire Say- liturgy: lordship, protectors who cannot help themselves, blind/seeing, darkness/light — collapsing to the twin claim: Creator of all, the One, the Irresistible. (Quote-nesting is unbalanced in the edition — quote [sic].)
* **`[13:17]`** “He sends down water from the sky, and riverbeds flow according to their capacity. The current carries swelling froth. And from what they heat in fire of ornaments or utensils comes a similar froth. Thus Allah exemplifies truth and falsehood. As for the froth, it is swept away, but what benefits the people remains in the ground. Thus Allah presents the analogies.”
  * **Gloss:** The truth-vs-vanity parable: flood froth and smelting scum blow away; water-metal-benefit settle. Falsehood’s bulk is its whole substance; truth’s claim is usefulness.
* **`[13:18]`** “For those who respond to their Lord is the best. But as for those who do not respond to Him, even if they possessed everything on earth, and twice as much, they could not redeem themselves with it. Those will have the worst reckoning; and their home is Hell—a miserable destination.”
  * **Gloss:** Response is the only currency; double-worldly wealth cannot ransom; the reckoning itself turns worst for the non-responder.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`ra‘d` (thunder)** — the surah’s namesake.
  * **Rendering:** “The thunder praises His glory” `[13:13]`; cognate field: “lightening [sic: lightning]” `[13:12]`, “thunderbolts” `[13:13]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `rg -i "thunder" corpus/` — the *noun-thunder as doxology* is unique to 13:13; `[2:19]` uses “darkness, and thunder, and lightning… the thunderbolts” inside the hypocrite-parable, `[2:55]`/`[4:153]` thunderbolts as strike on the sight-demanders, `[41:13]`/`[41:17]` “thunderbolt of Aad and Thamood,” `[18:40]` thunderbolts on a garden.
* **`by measure`** `[13:8]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[54:49]` “Everything We created is precisely measured”; `[65:3]` “Allah has set a measure to all things” — qadar as calibration, corpus-wide.
* **`Knower of the Invisible and the Visible`** `[13:9]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[32:6]`, `[62:8]`, `[9:94]` — the same phrase, always paired with return/accountability.
* **`The One, the Irresistible`** `[13:16]` (al-Ahad, al-Qahhar).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[14:48]` and `[40:16]` “the One, the Irresistible” verbatim; cf. `[112:1–2]` “Allah, the One. Allah, the Absolute.”

## 4. Cross-References (The Web)
* **Storm optics:** `[2:19–20]` — thunder/lightning as the hypocrites’ weather (“Whenever it illuminates for them, they walk in it; but when it grows dark over them, they stand still”); ar-Ra’d’s storm is the same sky read as praise rather than opportunism.
* **Unanswered callers:** `[46:5]` — “Who is more wrong than him who invokes, besides Allah, those who will not answer him until the Day of Resurrection” — the water-parable of 13:14 in question form.
* **Universal glorification:** `[57:1]` “Glorifying Allah is everything in the heavens and the earth”; `[24:41]` birds in formation know “its prayer and its manner of praise” — the doxology cosmology of 13:13, 15.
* **Internal change:** `[8:53]` — “Allah would never change a blessing He has bestowed on a people unless they change what is within themselves” — the twin of 13:11’s condition-of-a-people law.
* **Within surah:** water sent down (13:17) reuses the sun/moon/water machinery of 13:2–4; “worst reckoning” (13:18) pre-echoes the “dire reckoning” dread of `[13:21]`.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** the disputed classification lives or dies largely on this ruku — see index §1.
* **Asbab (13:8–14):** at-Tabarani from Ibn Abbas (quoted at length by Ibn Kathir): ‘Amir ibn al-Tufayl and Arbad ibn Qays come to Madinah to kill the Prophet; Arbad’s sword-hand freezes, a thunderbolt kills him, ‘Amir dies of a swelling in a woman of Banu Salul’s house; Ibn Abbas held 13:8–11 (and 10–14 in another route) revealed concerning them. Ibn Kathir flags weak narrators in the chains; Ibn ‘Ashur rejects the reports as feeble. *Source: Ibn Kathir on 13:8–14 citing Tabarani; Ibn ‘Ashur’s critique (web-retrieved 2026-10-14) — labeled, verify.*
* **Thunder-adab:** Ibn az-Zubayr at thunder: “Glory be to the One whom the thunder glorifies with praise of Him, and the angels from fear of Him… a severe warning to the people of the earth” — Malik’s Muwatta and Bukhari in *Adab al-Mufrad* (724); Bukhari notes no sound chain in that chapter. *Source: as transmitted — verify.*
