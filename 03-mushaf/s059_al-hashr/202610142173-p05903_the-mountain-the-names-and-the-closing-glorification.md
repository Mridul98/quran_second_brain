# The accounting for the morrow, the mountain that would crumble, and the litany of the Best Names (59:18–24)
**UID:** 202610142173
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 59:18–24 (Ruku 3 of 3 in al-Hashr)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[59:18]` – `[59:24]` (7 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah's last ruku turns from the case file to the classroom: fear Allah — twice in one verse — and audit what each soul has forwarded for tomorrow (18); do not be the ones whose forgetting of Allah made them forget themselves (19); the Fire-Paradise inequality is absolute (20); and if you doubt the weight of this Book, consider that a mountain, shown the same words, would have trembled and crumbled (21) — which leaves only the silence of praise: three "He is Allah" verses stacking the oneness-shahada, the secret-knower and mercy-names (22), the eight royal names ending "Glory be to Allah, beyond what they associate" (23), and the Creator-Maker-Designer triad with "His are the Most Beautiful Names" — closing, as the surah opened, with everything in the heavens and earth glorifying Him, the Majestic, the Wise (24).
* **Placement:** Closing ruku; the densest single name-cluster in the corpus — the tawhid litany that both answers 59:1's doxology and out-quotes every other name-list in the book.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[59:18]`** "O you who believe! Fear Allah, and let every soul consider what it has forwarded for the morrow, and fear Allah. Allah is Aware of what you do." — **Gloss:** The bracketed taqwa-command — "fear Allah" twice around one audit-verb (nazar), sandwiching "Allah is Aware" (the batch refrain, shared verbatim with 58:4/11/13 — see p05802/p05803). "what it has forwarded" (qaddama) is the earnings-idiom of `[2:95]`, `[3:182]` "what your hands have forwarded" and `[89:24]` "if only I had forwarded for my life" (rg-verified family). The "morrow" (ghad) is the corpus's rarest Day-word — a tomorrow that is the Day. *Source: observable term; day-as-morrow register noted — verify Arabic.*
* **`[59:19]`** "And do not be like those who forgot Allah, so He made them forget themselves. These are the sinners." — **Gloss:** The forgetting-law in reciprocal form: forget Him → forget yourselves. Exact partner across the batch: `[58:19]` "Satan... caused them to forget the remembrance of Allah" (rg-verified) — in 58 the devil does the forgetting; here self-forgetting is the divine sanction. "the sinners" (fusseq) — the same closing verdict-classes as 57:26/27 (batch echo — agent note).
* **`[59:20]`** "Not equal are the inhabitants of the Fire and the inhabitants of Paradise. It is the inhabitants of Paradise who are the winners." — **Gloss:** The inequality-formula, third site in two surahs this batch: `[57:10]` "Not equal among you are those who contributed before the conquest" (rg-verified) and the 35:19 "Not equal are the blind and the seeing" (rg-verified). "the winners" (fā'izūn) — corpus-unique success-word at this phrasing (rg; contrast 59:9 "the successful", 58:22 — observable drift across one build).
* **`[59:21]`** "Had We sent this Quran down on a mountain, you would have seen it trembling, crumbling in awe of Allah. These parables We cite for the people, so that they may reflect." — **Gloss:** The mountain-verse — the Quran's own weight measured against stone: the counter-scene to `[7:171]` "We suspended the mountain over them, as if it was an umbrella... Hold fast to what We have given you" (rg-verified): there the mountain threatens, here it would collapse from awe. The kalam note's anchor material (42:51–52: revelation by command, Book-as-light; 42:52 "We thus inspired you spiritually") speaks to the same channel-verb here — the note cites no `[59:21]` (flagged). "This Quran" — the self-naming, one of the corpus's first-person Book-claims (cf. `[97:1]` descent class in k-kalam).
* **`[59:22]`** "He is Allah. There is no god but He, the Knower of secrets and declarations. He is the Compassionate, the Merciful." — **Gloss:** Litany verse one: shahada + hidden/manifest knowledge + mercy-pair. The task's render-check: the corpus has "secrets and declarations" (not "unseen and witnessed") and reverses the task's guess to "the Compassionate, the Merciful" (rg-line verified). The knowledge-clause absorbs 58:7's whisper-arithmetic into a name (cross-batch ✓; k-tawhid already cites `[59:22]` — the note's only line from these three surahs — backlink verified). Shahada-kin: `[3:2]` "Allah, there is no god but He, the Living, the Eternal" and `[20:8]` "Allah, there is no god but He, His are the Most Beautiful Names" (both rg-verified — 20:8 is the Best-Names twin).
* **`[59:23]`** "He is Allah; besides Whom there is no god; the Sovereign, the Holy, the Peace-Giver, the Faith-Giver, the Overseer, the Almighty, the Omnipotent, the Overwhelming. Glory be to Allah, beyond what they associate." — **Gloss:** Eight royal names after the second shahada-clause — the task's "nine names" intuition belongs HERE, not to 57:1 (index ledger: 57:1 has two names; the count across 59:22–24 = fourteen named attributes + "the Most Beautiful Names" as umbrella — reported). Renderings as-issued: Sovereign/Holy (pair verbatim in `[62:1]`'s doxology — the Musabbihat chain crosses the names too, rg-verified), Peace-Giver/Faith-Giver/Overseer/Almighty/Omnipotent/Overwhelming (one-name English renderings; Arabic identities not audited — verify before quoting tafsir). Close: "Glory be to Allah, beyond what they associate" — the transcendence-formula family (27:63/16:1/30:40/7:190/9:31/23:92 carry "above what they associate" — rg-verified; 59:23's "beyond" phrasing is its own face).
* **`[59:24]`** "He is Allah; the Creator, the Maker, the Designer. His are the Most Beautiful Names. Whatever is in the heavens and the earth glorifies Him. He is the Majestic, the Wise." — **Gloss:** The triad of making — "the Creator, the Maker, the Designer" (the classical Arabic triad for this verse — khāliq/bāri'/muṣawwir — Source: standard term-set, verify; Itani's three-word English run — task guessed "Originator, the Fashioner": ✗, corrected as-issued) — then the Best-Names claim: shared verbatim-in-kind with `[7:180]` "To Allah belong the Most Beautiful Names, so call Him by them," `[20:8]`, and `[17:110]` "to Him belong the Best Names" (all four rg-verified — the dossier the task asked to flag: no knowledge note exists on al-Asma al-Husna; reported as gap, none created per rule 5). The doxology returns full-circle from 59:1: "Whatever is in the heavens and the earth glorifies Him" — the surah now names the glorifiers (24) after the cosmos opened by glorifying (1). Final names "the Majestic, the Wise" — verbatim pair of `[45:37]`'s closing praise (brain-verified p04504) and "Most High, the Great" neighbor of `[2:255]`.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **The litany's structure** ("He is Allah" × 3):
  * **Rendering:** 59:22 "He is Allah. There is no god but He..."; 59:23 "He is Allah; besides Whom there is no god..."; 59:24 "He is Allah; the Creator..." — three anaphoric openings, shahada-wording drifts per verse (secrets-and-declarations / besides-Whom / none) — observable editorial rhythm, not mechanical repetition.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[3:2]` and `[20:8]` carry the same shahada-frame (rg-verified); 20:8 adds the Best-Names claim in one line — making 7:180/17:110/20:8/59:24 the four-site Best-Names dossier (reported as theme-note GAP).
* **`fear Allah` double-bracket (59:18)**: the surah's closing command opens with the law 57:28, 58:9, 58:13 rehearsed — the batch's taqwa-chain (verifiable sites: 57:28, 58:9, 59:18×2 — all cited lines in this build; k-sabr cites none — flagged).
* **Forwarding (qadama)**: 59:18 ↔ `[2:95]`, `[3:182]`, `[89:24]` (rg-verified) — the day-audit verb: wealth forwarded (infraq) or not (glitter) decides what is there "for the morrow"; the k-infraq thesis stated at its eschatological endpoint, uncited — flagged.
* **Self-forgetting (nasī)**: 59:19 ↔ 58:19 (batch pair ✓); kin to 57:14's "arrogance deceived you regarding Allah" (the delusion that forgets its own account).
