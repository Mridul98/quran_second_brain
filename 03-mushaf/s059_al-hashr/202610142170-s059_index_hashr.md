## 3. Structural Arc
* **Praise and case (1–5):** the inverted doxology opens a courtroom: eviction as exhibit, terror as weapon, trees as law-question, lesson as the stated purpose ("take a lesson, O you who have insight" — 59:2).
* **Economics as theology (6–7):** non-combat spoils → six named claims + the Messenger's authority + the fear-clause; 59:7 is the surah's hinge — the circulation verdict that makes ruku 1 a policy-document.
* **Doxology ring:** 59:1 opens with the inverted "Glorifying Allah is..." and 59:24 closes on "Whatever is in the heavens and the earth glorifies Him" — the surah names the glorifiers only after reading the whole case file (rg-verified pair; Musabbihat ledger below).
* **Three hearts (8–10):** refugees, helpers, after-comers — merit defined by possession-surrender, preference-over-self, and malice-free prayer; the roster stays open to all later believers (Source: observable address-shape — agent note).
* **Roster-prayer:** the after-comers' du'a (59:10) — "leave no malice in our hearts towards those who believe" — is the batch's only community-wide purity-of-intention petition; it defines the third generation by prayer, not deed (observable address-shape; kin not audited — verify before widening).
* **The counter-hearts (11–17):** the four-part pledge un-spoken; fear-of-people over fear-of-God; wall-war and disunited unity; the "shortly before them" warning; the devil's disclaimer as the parable of every misaligned loyalty; Fire for both of them.
* **Fear-chain:** 59:7 "And fear Allah. Allah is severe in punishment" leads to 59:13's inverted fear ("Fear of you is more intense in their hearts than fear of Allah") and lands in 59:18's double command ("fear Allah... and fear Allah") — the same verb runs law, diagnosis, and close (cited lines rg-verified in this batch).
* **Account and praise (18–24):** double taqwa + the morrow-audit; self-forgetting; the absolute inequality; the mountain that would crumble under this Quran; three "He is Allah" litanies (14 names + the Best-Names umbrella) closing on the surah's first sentence — the book's densest praise-block ends the case file.
