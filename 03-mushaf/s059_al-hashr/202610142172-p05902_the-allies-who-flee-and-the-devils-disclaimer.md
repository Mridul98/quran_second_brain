# The allies who will not come: hypocrite pledges, fortress-war, and the devil's disclaimer (59:11–17)
**UID:** 202610142172
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 59:11–17 (Ruku 2 of 3 in al-Hashr)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[59:11]` – `[59:17]` (7 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The ruku cross-examines the insider who promises the outsider: hypocrites tell their People-of-the-Book brethren "if you are evicted, we will leave with you... if anyone fights you, we will certainly support you" — and Allah bears witness that they are liars (11), a prophecy the verse then unspounds four times over, each clause a negation of the last (12); their real fear is of the believers, not of Allah (13), their war is wall-war and their unity is a mirage of divided hearts (14); they are like "those shortly before them" who tasted consequences (15), and like the devil who says "Disbelieve" and, when obeyed, disclaims the soul he talked into hell (16) — for whose pair, and for the alliance they brokered, the Fire is the common end (17).
* **Placement:** Ruku 2 of 3; the counter-portrait to 59:8–10's three sincere generations — malice-free hearts vs. heart-divided allies — and the surah's sharpest shirk-autopsy: following a patron who will not own you.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[59:11]`** "Have you not considered those who act hypocritically? They say to their brethren who disbelieved among the People of the Book, “If you are evicted, we will leave with you, and will not obey anyone against you; and should anyone fight you, we will certainly support you.” But Allah bears witness that they are liars." — **Gloss:** The batch's second explicit hypocrite-label (with 57:13; s058 never uses the noun — see p05802). "their brethren" — the kinship of the inside trade exactly as k-nifaq defines it (speech aimed at people, cost avoided); "But Allah bears witness that they are liars" — verbatim-in-kind of `[63:1]` "Allah bears witnesses that the hypocrites are liars" (k-nifaq's own quote, brain-verified) and answered by 58:18's "they themselves are the liars" (batch). Eviction-verb reuses 59:2's — the promise is made about the very event the surah just narrated.
* **`[59:12]`** "If they are evicted, they will not leave with them; and if anyone fights them, they will not support them; and if they go to their aid, they will turn their backs and flee; then they will receive no support." — **Gloss:** The four-fold negation: every clause of 59:11's pledge is un-spoken in order, ending with the reciprocal law — no support received, ever. "turn their backs and flee" — the rout-verb; the pledge decays in the open, no punishment-scene needed yet. *Source: structural observation (negation-order mirrors pledge-order) — agent inference.*
* **`[59:13]`** "Fear of you is more intense in their hearts than fear of Allah. That is because they are a people who do not understand." — **Gloss:** The diagnosis: the fear-thermometer inverted — more awed by the community than by God; the reason-clause "do not understand" repeats verbatim in 59:14 (rg-internal) — a refrain the surah uses to fence the two fortress-peoples (hypocrites and the evicted allies alike). Kin: `[3:173]` "The people have mobilized against you, so fear them" — fear-of-people as faith-test (rg-verified family).
* **`[59:14]`** "They will not fight you all together except from fortified strongholds, or from behind walls. Their hostility towards each other is severe. You would think they are united, but their hearts are diverse. That is because they are a people who do not understand." — **Gloss:** Military sociology of a hollow alliance: wall-war only, intra-block hatred intense, and the external unity a misreading — "hearts are diverse." The fortresses echo 59:2's failed walls (internal ring: they "thought their fortresses would protect them"); the wall-motif of 57:13 (between mercy and agony) is this batch's third architecture of the same sorting (agent note: same English root-family, Arabic identity unverified).
* **`[59:15]`** "Like those shortly before them. They experienced the consequences of their decisions. For them is a painful punishment." — **Gloss:** "Those shortly before them" — unnamed in the corpus; classical identifications run to the Qurayza (or the Meccan dead of Badr — reports differ); Source: classical asbab — verify. The verse welds 59:11's allies to 59:2's evicted: same crime-grammar, already punished once in this very surah's history-section. "experienced the consequences of their decisions" — a decision-earnings phrasing; the same pattern ("tasted/was seized for their decisions") belongs to the destroyed-town topos — *Source: agent inference; exact twins not audited this pass — verify before citing.*
* **`[59:16]`** "Like the devil, when he says to the human being, “Disbelieve.” But when he has disbelieved, he says, “I am innocent of you; I fear Allah, the Lord of the Worlds.”" — **Gloss:** The abandonment formula, exactly as the task probed: verbatim twin of `[8:48]` — Satan at Badr: "he turned on his heels, and said, 'I am innocent of you; I see what you do not see; I fear Allah; Allah is severe in punishment'" (rg-verified pair!) — and the Day-version at `[14:22]` ("I had no authority over you, except that I called you, and you answered me... I reject your associating with me" — rg-verified). The innocent-of-you line is a stable prophetic formula — Abrahamic in `[6:78]`, `[43:26]`-style (rg-hits verified). JUDGMENT for the task's nifaq/shirk probe: 59:16 is the shirk-autopsy — the patron who disowns at the moment of cost — and it also fits k-nifaq's ledger model (a position held for a payout that never clears); it links to BOTH, and the link is honest: k-tawhid's "partners you cannot see" gets its kindest portrait here — the partner who will visibly flee.
* **`[59:17]`** "The ultimate end for both of them is the Fire, where they will dwell forever. Such is the requital for the wrongdoers." — **Gloss:** "Both of them" — devil and his dupe, allies and their patrons, one final settlement; the forever-dwelling formula shared verbatim-in-kind with `[58:17]` (batch — see p05803), and "the wrongdoers" closes the ruku on the surah's generic verdict-word.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **The abandonment formula ("I am innocent of you")**:
  * **Rendering:** 59:16 "I am innocent of you; I fear Allah, the Lord of the Worlds"; `[8:48]` adds "I see what you do not see" and "Allah is severe in punishment".
  * **Elsewhere:** `[14:22]` the Day-sermon; the prophetic-disclaimer use (Abraham 43:26/6:78-family; 26:216 "I am innocent of what you do" — all rg-verified): the same sentence is the devil's cowardice and the messenger's courage — one formula, opposite mouths. *Source: observable phrase-cluster; the theological reading (shirk as misdirected loyalty) is agent inference — verify.*
* **Hypocrisy label count**: rg over the three surahs: one hit in s057 (57:13), zero in s058, one in s059 (59:11 — "those who act hypocritically") — the batch's dossier is deliberately thin on nouns and rich in pledges-broken.
* **Fear-comparative**: 59:13 "Fear of you is more intense in their hearts than fear of Allah" — corpus-unique comparative construction (rg); connects to k-sabr's taqwa-steering (whose absence in this note is flagged at index) and to `[3:173]`'s fear-of-people test.
* **Wall/fortress grammar**: "fortified strongholds... behind walls" (59:14) ↔ 59:2 "fortresses" ↔ 57:13's eschatological "wall" — three walls in two surahs, one build (observable English-word family; Arabic identity per site unverified).
