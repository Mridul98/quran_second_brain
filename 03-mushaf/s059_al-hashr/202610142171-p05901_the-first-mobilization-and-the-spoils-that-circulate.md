# The first mobilization: eviction, the spoils that must circulate, and the three generations (59:1–10)
**UID:** 202610142171
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 59:1–10 (Ruku 1 of 3 in al-Hashr)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[59:1]` – `[59:10]` (10 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The cosmos glorifies the Mighty-Wise (1) and the surah immediately reads that sovereignty's case file: a fortified People of the Book, certain of their walls, evicted from where they never expected, terror cast into their hearts, wrecking their own homes (2) — an event held together by decree (3), retribution against opposition (4), and trees cut or left standing "by Allah's will" (5); the winnings (fay) belong to Allah, the Messenger, and six social claims — "so that it may not circulate solely between the wealthy among you" — sealed by the authority clause and the fear-command (6–7); and the ruku closes as a roster of three generations of recipients: the emigrants who lost everything, the helpers who preferred others over themselves, and those who came after, praying for clean hearts (8–10).
* **Placement:** Ruku 1 of 3; the historical-argument opening of al-Hashr — the surah's doxology, its case study, and its economics in one movement; ruku 2 then exposes the hypocrites' failed alliance, ruku 3 closes with the name-litany.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)

You did not think they would leave, and they thought their fortresses would protect them from Allah. But Allah came at them from where they never expected, and threw terror into their hearts. They wrecked their homes with their own hands, and by the hands of the believers. Therefore, take a lesson, O you who have insight." — **Gloss:** The classical asbab names the Banu Nadir (4 AH/627 CE, the siege and departure) — Source: classical sira/sir reports — verify in print; the corpus itself names no tribe, only "those who disbelieved among the People of the Book" — the same phrase 57:29 ends on (batch pair ✓) and 59:11 quotes back. "the first mobilization" — the surah's title-word (hashr) rendered as mobilization; the noun is corpus-unique (rg); its verb-siblings run through 9:38–47's mobilization-commands and 3:173 (rg-verified family). Terror "thrown into hearts" — the weapon God handles: verbatim kin of `[8:12]` "I will cast terror into the hearts of those who disbelieve" and `[33:26]` (p00317-verified cluster). * **`[59:4]`** "That is because they opposed Allah and His Messenger. * **`[59:5]`** "Whether you cut down a tree, or leave it standing on its trunk, it is by Allah’s will. ## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`fayʾ` (restored property)**: 59:6 "bestowed", 59:7 "restored to His Messenger" — two verbs for one category inside two ayahs; the technical legal term for non-combat acquisition. *Source: observable rendering; the fiqh definition is extra-corpus — verify.*
* **Circulation (dawla)**: 59:7 "so that it may not circulate solely between the wealthy among you" ↔ `[3:140]` alternation-of-days (p00314-verified root-weld). The anti-monopoly clause is the batch's sharpest economic sentence.
* **The six claims + the list kin:** Allah, Messenger, relatives, orphans, poor, wayfarer (59:7). The zakat inventory at `[9:60]` — "the poor, and the destitute, and... the traveler in need" — is the corpus's other recipient roster (rg-verified); the lists overlap on poor/wayfarer but 59:7 adds relatives/orphans and 9:60 adds administrators/debt/hearts — do NOT harmonize without checking: *Source: observable lists; classical legal comparison extra-corpus — verify.*
* **`sadiqun`/winners/successful**: "These are the sincere" (59:8 ↔ 57:19), "the successful" (59:9 ↔ 58:22), "the winners" (59:20 — corpus-unique phrasing, rg) — three success-words stacked across the batch's three surahs; observable drift, Arabic-side identity unverified.
* **Terror (ruʿb)**: 59:2 "threw terror into their hearts" ↔ `[8:12]` "cast terror" ↔ `[33:26]` (p00317-verified) — the weapon belongs to God alone in every site.
