# Surah 72: THE JINN (al-Jinn)
**UID:** 202610142234
**Type:** Index / Surah Map
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Meccan in the majority type-lists; a transmitted Medinan option attaches to the Nakhla listening-event. *Source: classical type-lists + maghazi reports — verify.* Revelation order 40 in the transmitted lists. *Source: classical revelation-order lists — verify.* The unnumbered Basmala heads the surah.
* **Size:** 28 ayahs, 2 ruku; the only surah named for the jinn — an unseen jury delivers its verdict on the Recitation.
* **Central theme:** Kalam authenticated by external audition: "Say, it was revealed to me that a band of jinn listened in, and said, 'We have heard a wondrous Quran'" (72:1) — and their report runs the full creed: tawhid without mate or child (72:3), the sky's revelation-security dossier (72:8–9), a water-test for the steadfast (72:16), pure invocation at the worship-floors (72:18). Ruku 2 hands the Prophet his matching credo ("I pray only to my Lord," 72:20), his office-limits (72:21–23), and the unseen registry: disclosed to no one — except a chosen messenger escorted by guards before and behind, until everything is tallied by number (72:26–28).

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Report (72:1–2):** revelation about a revelation-heard — audition at two removes; belief cashed instantly as anti-shirk.
* **Doxology and its shadow (72:3–5):** the Grandeur without mate/child; "the fools among us used to say nonsense about Allah."
* **Inversions (72:6–7):** humans seeking power through jinn increased them in confusion; resurrection-denial shared across species.
* **Security dossier (72:8–10):** the heaven probed, found full of stern guards and projectiles; listening-posts now lethal — yet the jinn still cannot read the earth's intent (72:10).
* **Taxonomy (72:11–15):** righteous and less; submitters and compromisers — "whoever believes in his Lord fears neither loss, nor burden" (72:13).
* **The water-test and the worship-floors (72:16–18):** rectitude kept → plenty water, explicitly "to test them"; masajid for Allah alone; the crowd pressing the servant who rises calling (72:19) — the hinge to 73's night-charge.
* **Credo and limits (72:20–25):** three "Say"s; Hour-timing withheld from the warner.
* **Unseen registry (72:26–28):** disclosure only to the chosen messenger; guards before and behind; delivery confirmed; "…has tallied everything by number."
