# The messenger's credo and the unseen registry: guards before and behind (72:20–28)
**UID:** 202610142236
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 72:20–28 (Ruku 2 of 2 in al-Jinn)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[72:20]`–`[72:28]` (9 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Three "Say"s give the Prophet the jinn's own creed ("I never associate anyone with Him," answering 72:2), his office's hard limits (no harm, no guidance, no refuge from Allah — except proclamation), and the Hour's withheld timing; then the unseen registry closes the surah: Allah discloses the Invisible to none — except a chosen messenger, escorted by guards before and behind, so that delivery is confirmed and everything stands tallied by number.
* **Placement:** Ruku 2 mirrors ruku 1 at every joint (72:20↔72:2, 72:22↔72:6, 72:27↔72:8–9): the heaven sealed against eavesdroppers is the converse of the messenger escorted so nothing of the message is lost or stolen — the surah's revelation-security architecture.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[72:20]`** "Say, “I pray only to my Lord, and I never associate anyone with Him.”"
  * **Gloss:** the credo ring with 72:2 — jinn and messenger recite the same tawhid, first person now.
* **`[72:21]`** "Say, “It is not in my power to harm you, nor to bring you to right conduct.”"
  * **Gloss:** rashad's third rendering here ("right conduct"); the preacher's-power limit — exactly k-guidance's cited point at 28:56 ("guidance is God's prerogative, not the preacher's power").
* **`[72:22]`** "Say, “No one can protect me from Allah, and I will not find any refuge except with Him."
  * **Gloss:** the refuge-inversion of 72:6 (humans seeking power through jinn) — ma'wa' only with Allah.
* **`[72:23]`** "Except for a proclamation from Allah and His messages. He who defies Allah and His Messenger—for him is the Fire of Hell, in which they will dwell forever."
  * **Gloss:** the exception clause: his only content and power is the proclamation — cf. 5:67 "convey what was revealed… Allah will protect you from the people" (verified twin).
* **`[72:24]`** "Until, when they see what they were promised, they will know who is weaker in helpers, and fewer in numbers."
  * **Gloss:** deferral of the scoreboard; the numbers-motif begins — leading to 72:28's tally.
* **`[72:25]`** "Say, “I do not know whether what you are promised is near, or whether my Lord will extend it for a period.”"
  * **Gloss:** the warner does not hold the calendar — cf. 27:65 "No one in the heavens or on earth knows the future except Allah" (verified; ghayb rendered "the future" there).
* **`[72:26]`** "The Knower of the Invisible; He does not disclose His Invisible to anyone."
  * **Gloss:** bare "Knower of the Invisible" — without 13:9's paired "and the Visible" (verified) — privacy underlined.
* **`[72:27]`** "Except to a Messenger of His choosing. He then dispatches guards before him and behind him."
  * **Gloss:** the single disclosure-channel; the watchers before and behind answer 72:8's "stern guards and projectiles": heaven sealed for stealers, opened with escort for senders.
* **`[72:28]`** "That He may know that they have conveyed the messages of their Lord. He encompasses what they have, and has tallied everything by number."
  * **Gloss:** delivery-confirmation — kalam's chain ends in verified transmission; "tallied everything by number" ↔ 36:12 "We have tallied all things in a Clear Record" (verified); the numbering close answers 74:30–31's Nineteen (same juz).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`ghayb` (the Invisible):** 72:26–27; renderings elsewhere: 27:65 "the future", 13:9 "the Invisible and the Visible" (verified) — Itani's fixed term is "Invisible".
* **`guards before and behind` (72:27):** the escort-formula; English converges with 72:8 "stern guards" (heaven-side) and 74:31 "wardens of the Fire" (zabaniyah — transmitted lexical form — verify), but Arabic identity across the three sites is unverified — flag.
* **`ahsa`/tally:** 72:28 "tallied everything by number" ↔ 36:12 "tallied all things in a Clear Record" (verified pair; two tally-sites corpus-wide).
* **`balagh` (proclamation):** 72:23 "a proclamation from Allah and His messages" ↔ 5:67 "convey" (verified cognate scene) — the messenger's sole authority-item.
