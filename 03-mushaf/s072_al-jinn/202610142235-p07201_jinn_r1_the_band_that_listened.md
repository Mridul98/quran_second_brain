# The band that listened: a wondrous Quran and a guarded heaven (72:1–19)
**UID:** 202610142235
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 72:1–19 (Ruku 1 of 2 in al-Jinn)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[72:1]`–`[72:19]` (19 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** An outside jury reviews the Recitation: jinn hear it, find it guiding to rectitude, and believe — their report braids a creed (Grandeur without mate or child), a security dossier (the heaven probed and found packed with guards and projectiles), a taxonomy (submitters and compromisers), and a water-test, closing on worship-floors reserved for Allah and the crowd pressing the servant who rises calling on Him.
* **Placement:** Ruku 1 of the jinn-surah; its audition-claim is the k-kalam note's strongest external-testimony site, and its standing-servant scene (72:19) opens the night-charge of 73.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[72:1]`** "Say, “It was revealed to me that a band of jinn listened in, and said, ‘We have heard a wondrous Quran."
  * **Gloss:** a revelation about a revelation-heard — three layers of audition in one verse; "wondrous" is the corpus's term for the jinn's verdict (elsewhere only 18:9's "wondrous signs" — verified).
* **`[72:2]`** "It guides to rectitude, so we have believed in it; and we will never associate anyone with our Lord."
  * **Gloss:** belief caused by the guidance-effect, not by the miracle; its first cash-out is tawhid — twin formula of 17:9 "guides to what is most upright" (verified; k-kalam cites 17:9).
* **`[72:3]`** "And Exalted is the Grandeur of our Lord— He never had a mate, nor a child."
  * **Gloss:** jadd rendered "Grandeur" — corpus-unique (verified); direct twin of k-isa's exclusion verse 19:35 "It is not for Allah to have a child" (verified).
* **`[72:4]`–`[72:5]`** "But the fools among us used to say nonsense about Allah. And we thought that humans and jinn would never utter lies about Allah."
  * **Gloss:** the lie named as nonsense; attribution-inversion — cf. 6:21 "Who does greater wrong than someone who fabricates lies against Allah" (verified).
* **`[72:6]`** "Some individual humans used to seek power through some individual jinn, but they only increased them in confusion."
  * **Gloss:** the refuge-economy inverted — humans arming jinn, jinn swelling with confusion ("confusion" also renders 71:24's curse; Arabic identity unverified — flag).
* **`[72:7]`** "They thought, as you thought, that Allah would never resurrect anyone."
  * **Gloss:** resurrection-denial as a shared species-error — the Meccan audience's own disease spoken from outside.
* **`[72:8]`** "We probed the heaven, and found it filled with stern guards and projectiles."
  * **Gloss:** the security audit; hardware twins: 67:5 "lanterns… missiles against the devils," 37:7–8 "guarded… they cannot eavesdrop," 41:12 lamps "for protection" (verified).
* **`[72:9]`** "We used to take up positions to listen in; but whoever listens now finds a projectile in wait for him."
  * **Gloss:** the eavesdropping window closed — 15:18 "a visible projectile", 37:10 "a piercing projectile" (verified); 71:16's sun-lamp re-read here as surveillance hardware (Source: agent structural observation).
* **`[72:10]`** "We do not know whether ill is intended for those on earth, or if their Lord intends goodness for them."
  * **Gloss:** the guards do not concede the unseen — setup for 72:26's registry (ruku 2).
* **`[72:11]`** "Some of us are righteous, but some of us are less than that; we follow divergent paths."
  * **Gloss:** self-taxonomy; the jinn community mirrors humanity's split — 76:3 "We guided him to the way, be he appreciative or unappreciative" (verified) is the same road-and-reaction grammar.
* **`[72:12]`** "We realized that we cannot defeat Allah on earth, and that we cannot escape Him by fleeing."
  * **Gloss:** omnipresence as the limit-case; the same realization Noah's people lacked at 71:25's helper-denial.
* **`[72:13]`** "And when we heard the guidance, we believed in it. Whoever believes in his Lord fears neither loss, nor burden."
  * **Gloss:** "the guidance" names the Quran itself as rashad — the note-k-guidance claim that guidance is a road travelers take.
* **`[72:14]`–`[72:15]`** "Among us are those who are submitting, and among us are the compromisers. As for those who have submitted—it is they who pursue rectitude. But as for the compromisers—they will be firewood for Hell.’"
  * **Gloss:** final taxonomy: muslimun and qasitun ("compromisers" — an uncommon English for the unjust-middle; observable rendering, verify), submission = pursuing rectitude, the 72:2 word returned.
* **`[72:16]`–`[72:17]`** "Had they kept true to the Path, We would have given them plenty water to drink. To test them with it. Whoever turns away from the remembrance of his Lord, He will direct him to torment ever mounting."
  * **Gloss:** prosperity re-marked: the water that was Noah's forgiveness-payout (71:11–12) is here explicitly a trial — same divine first-person formula as 5:66's conditional abundance (verified).
* **`[72:18]`** "The places of worship are for Allah. So do not call, besides Allah, upon anyone else."
  * **Gloss:** the worship-floor clause — invocation (du'a) is the litmus: masajid ("places of worship") belong to Allah alone.
* **`[72:19]`** "And when the servant of Allah got up calling on Him, they almost fell on him in a mass."
  * **Gloss:** the scene that named the surah: the standing servant (cf. 17:79's vigil — verified) swarmed; prelude to 73:2 "Stay up during the night" (Source: agent structural observation).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`rashad` (rectitude):** 72:2 "guides to rectitude", 72:14 "pursue rectitude", 72:21 "bring you to right conduct" — three renderings inside one surah (verified); twins: 17:9 "most upright", 7:146/40:38 "path of rectitude" (verified); 18:13 renders the same root "guidance" (verified drift).
* **`shihab` (projectile):** 72:8–9 — elsewhere 15:18 "visible projectile", 37:10 "piercing projectile" (verified).
* **`jadd` (Grandeur):** 72:3 only (verified); 71:13's 'izama "Greatness" is the human-register twin.
* **`wondrous` ('ajib):** 72:1 ↔ 18:9 "Our wondrous signs" (verified) — the Quran as sign-class, not charm.
