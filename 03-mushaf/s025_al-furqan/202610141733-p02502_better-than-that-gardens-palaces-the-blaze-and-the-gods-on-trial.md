# Better than that — the Blaze, the Garden of Eternity, and the gods on trial (25:10–20)
**UID:** 202610141733
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 25:10–20 (Ruku 2 of 6 in al-Furqan)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[25:10]` – `[25:20]` (11 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The second "Blessed is He" answers the taunt "treasure or a garden!" (25:8) with a better offer — gardens, rivers, palaces — then runs the two ballots of the surah: the Blaze versus the Garden of Eternity (11–16), and the interrogation of the false gods on Day of Gathering (17–19), closing by re-certifying the human-messenger claim of 25:7 (20) and turning the pressure itself into a test: "will you be patient?"
* **Placement:** Ruku 2 of 6; the eschatological hinge of the trial-surah — the prosecution's fantasy wealth is outbid, the competitors are cross-examined, and the messenger-charge is answered inside the same unit (7 ↔ 20).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[25:10]`** Blessed is He who, if He wills, can provide you with better than that—gardens beneath which rivers flow—and He will give you palaces.
  * **Gloss:** Doxology #2: "better than that" — the offered treasure/garden of the mockers (25:8) outbid by divine provision; "palaces" — the word occurs only here in the corpus (verified).
* **`[25:11]`** In fact, they have denied the Hour, and We have prepared for those who deny the Hour a Blaze.
  * **Gloss:** The real indictment is denial of the Hour; its correlate is a prepared "Blaze" — punishment as antecedent fact, not arbitrary threat.
* **`[25:12]`** When it sees them from a distant place, they will hear it raging and roaring.
  * **Gloss:** The fire personified: it "sees them" from afar and they hear its "raging and roaring" — terror at long range.
* **`[25:13]`** And when they are thrown into it, into a tight place, shackled, they will plead there for death.
  * **Gloss:** Then close-up: thrown in "into a tight place, shackled"; death becomes the petition.
* **`[25:14]`** "Do not plead for one death today, but plead for a great many deaths."
  * **Gloss:** The reply in quote-form: even the plea for death is multiplied into mockery — no exit, only more dying.
* **`[25:15]`** Say, "Is this better, or the Garden of Eternity promised to the righteous? It is for them a reward and a destination. [sic — quote opens and never closes; see artifacts note]
  * **Gloss:** The ballot: "Is this better, or…?" — the "Garden of Eternity" (phrase unique in the corpus) promised to "the righteous" (Itani's standing rendering of *muttaqīn*).
* **`[25:16]`** They will have therein whatever they desire, forever. That is upon your Lord a binding promise.
  * **Gloss:** The inventory ("whatever they desire" — cf. [43:71]) anchored in "a binding promise… upon your Lord" — the only corpus occurrence of the exact phrase (verified).
* **`[25:17]`** On the Day when He gathers them, and what they worshiped besides Allah, He will say, "Was it you who misled these servants of Mine, or was it they who lost the way?"
  * **Gloss:** The co-defendants separated: the gods are interrogated too — who misled whom?
* **`[25:18]`** They will say, "Glory be to You. It was not for us to take any lords besides You. But you gave them enjoyments, and their ancestors, until they forgot the Message, and became ruined people."
  * **Gloss:** The gods disavow: the excuse is long enjoyment (mut'a) → forgetting the Message → "ruined people."
* **`[25:19]`** They have denied you because of what you say; so you can neither avert, nor help. Whoever among you commits injustice, We will make him taste a grievous punishment.
  * **Gloss:** Addressed to the worshippers: the gods themselves testify against them; the offer of intercession collapses into "neither avert, nor help."
* **`[25:20]`** We never sent any messengers before you, but they ate food and walked in the marketplaces. And We made some of you tempters for one another—will you be patient? Your Lord is always Observing.
  * **Gloss:** The answer to 25:7 inside the surah: appetite and the marketplace are the sunnah of all messengers; life is mutual temptation; the demand is patience; the watchword "Your Lord is always Observing."

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`saʿīr`** ("a Blaze"): the consuming fire.
  * **Rendering:** "a Blaze" at 25:11; elsewhere "roast in a Blaze" [4:10], "return will be to the Blaze" [37:68], "the Blaze will be displayed" [26:91], "the agony of the Blaze" [40:7], "the punishment of the Blaze" [67:5].
* **`jannat al-khuld`** ("the Garden of Eternity"): the everlasting garden as promised estate.
  * **Rendering/Elsewhere:** the exact phrase "Garden of Eternity" occurs only at 25:15 in the corpus (verified) — here Itani capitalizes it as a title.
* **`ʿahd`** ("a binding promise"): the promise made binding upon God Himself.
  * **Rendering:** "That is upon your Lord a binding promise" — unique exact phrase (verified); adjacent in register to [19:?] gift-promise language, but not the mithaq clause — series ledger: covenant note is adjacent, not forced.
* **`muttaqīn`** ("the righteous"): 25:15 "promised to the righteous."
  * **Elsewhere:** Itani's standing rendering matches the guidance k-note's ledger for *muttaqīn* ("the righteous" in [2:2–3] contexts) — the promise's audience is the same qualified group guidance addresses.
