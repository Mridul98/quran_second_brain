# The shadow, the two seas, and the war over a name — "And what is the Merciful?" (25:45–60)
**UID:** 202610141736
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 25:45–60 (Ruku 5 of 6 in al-Furqan)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[25:45]` – `[25:60]` (16 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The evidence shift from history to physics: a shadow that moves when it could freeze, night worn like clothing, winds as advance agents and water that revives dead ground, two seas merged yet not confusable, human kinship spun from a single fluid — and the created audit ends on the thankless reader (50); the unit then re-credentials the Messenger (no payment, herald and warner, tawakkul in "the Living, the One who never dies") until it detonates on the surah's name-theology: the pagans refuse prostration to "the Merciful" because they do not know the name — and the surah answers by making "the Most Merciful" the seal of its Throne-verse.
* **Placement:** Ruku 5 of 6; the creation-evidence block sitting between the destroyed cities (ruku 4) and the doxology-plus-character-portrait (ruku 6); the "Blessed is He #3" falls one verse after this unit's end, at 25:61.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[25:45]`** Do you not see how your Lord extends the shadow? Had He willed, He could have made it still. And We made the sun a pointer to it.  **`[25:46]`** Then We withdraw it towards Us gradually.
  * **Gloss:** The shadow experiment: it lengthens, it retreats, it is *indexed* to the sun — "a pointer" (unique word in the corpus, verified); the willed stillness never granted — motion as mercy-and-signature.
* **`[25:47]`** And it is He who made the night a covering for you, and sleep for rest; and He made the day a revival.
  * **Gloss:** Night = garment (the corpus renders *libās* as "a covering"; cf. [2:187]'s "a garment" for spouses — same English family, different choice — observable), sleep = rest, day = "a revival" (unique rendering, verified).
* **`[25:48]`** And it is He who sends the winds, bringing advance news of His mercy; and We send down from the sky pure water.  **`[25:49]`** To revive dead lands thereby, and to provide drink for the multitude of animals and humans We created.
  * **Gloss:** Winds as heralds (cf. [27:63] "heralds of His mercy," [30:46] "bearing good news"); *pure water* (same phrase at [77:27], [47:15]) for *dead land* — resurrection analogized daily (twin at [35:9]).
* **`[25:50]`** We have circulated it among them, that they may reflect, but most people persist in thanklessness.
  * **Gloss:** The parables are *turned about* among people (cf. 25:39's "presented the parables"); the verdict is ingratitude, not ignorance — kufr as thanklessness, the shukr note's audit register.
* **`[25:51]`** Had We willed, We could have sent to every town a warner.  **`[25:52]`** So do not obey the disbelievers, but strive against them with it, a mighty struggle.
  * **Gloss:** One warner, many towns — the economy of grace; and the command: strive *"with it"* — the instrument is the Book itself ("a mighty struggle" — the great jihad carried by revelation — *Source: classical reading of the preposition — agent inference — verify*).
* **`[25:53]`** And it is He who merged the two seas; this one fresh and sweet, and that one salty and bitter; and He placed between them a barrier, and an impassable boundary.
  * **Gloss:** Oceanography as tawhid evidence: contact without confutation (twins: [55:19–20] "He merged the two seas… a barrier, which they do not overrun"; [35:12] "The two seas are not the same"; [27:61] "a partition between the two seas").
* **`[25:54]`** And it is He who, from fluid, created the human being. Then He made relationships through marriage and mating. Your Lord is Omnipotent.
  * **Gloss:** From one fluid, kinship-architecture — "relationships through marriage and mating" is the corpus's only such phrase (verified); creation and connection under "Omnipotent."
* **`[25:55]`** And yet, instead of Allah, they serve what neither profits them nor harms them. The disbeliever has always turned his back on his Lord.
  * **Gloss:** The gods fail the profit-harm test — the same clause as 25:3; "turned his back" closes the creature-hymn with treason.
* **`[25:56]`** We sent you only as a herald of good news and a warner.  **`[25:57]`** Say, "I ask of you no payment for this— only that whoever wills may take a path to his Lord."
  * **Gloss:** The office re-certified (twin wording [7:188]; payment-denial formula cf. [26:109], [11:29]); and the price is a *path* — guidance offered as road-choice, not fee.
* **`[25:58]`** And put your trust in the Living, the One who never dies; and celebrate His praise. He suffices as the All-Informed Knower of the faults of His creatures.
  * **Gloss:** Tawakkul in "the Living" (cf. [2:255] "the Living, the Everlasting"; "never dies" is corpus-unique) — while all else perishes ([55:26–27]); "He suffices" returns (twin of 25:31).
* **`[25:59]`** He who created the heavens and the earth and everything between them in six days, then settled on the Throne. The Most Merciful. Ask about Him a well-informed.
  * **Gloss:** The six-days/Throne formula ([7:54], [10:3], [11:7], [32:4], [50:38], [57:4]; "settled on the Throne" also [13:2], [20:5]); then two oddities: "The Most Merciful" stands as a nameless predicate, and "Ask about Him a well-informed" — the *khabīr* summons (elsewhere Allah's own title: [31:34] "All-Knowing, Well-Informed," [35:31] "Well-Informed of His servants") — *Source: referent of "a well-informed" (God vs. a knower) debated in tafsir — agent note, verify.*
* **`[25:60]`** And when it is said to them, "Bow down to the Merciful," they say, "And what is the Merciful? Are we to bow down to whatever you command us?" And it increases their aversion.
  * **Gloss:** The name-war: they do not recognize *al-Rahman* as a divine name; the corpus's only "And what is the Merciful?" (verified); the surah's answer is structural — it keeps calling God "the Merciful" (its servants, 63; its doxology, 61) and [17:110] elsewhere legislates the name: "Call Him Allah, or call Him the Most Merciful."

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`barzakh` ("a barrier")**: 25:53 — between the two seas.
  * **Elsewhere:** [55:20] "a barrier, which they do not overrun"; [23:100] the post-mortem "barrier" — one English word spans oceanography and the inter-death waiting-room (observable in corpus); [27:61] renders the same idea "a partition."
* **`al-Ḥayy` ("the Living… never dies")**: 25:58.
  * **Elsewhere:** [2:255] "the Living, the Everlasting"; [55:26–27] "Everyone upon it is perishing. But will remain the Presence of your Lord" — the mortality-comparative that 25:58 compresses into a trust-object.
* **`al-Raḥmān` ("the Merciful")**: 25:59–60 — the disputed name.
  * **Rendering:** "the Merciful" (elsewhere often "the Most Merciful"); the protest-phrase "And what is the Merciful?" is unique; legislative twin [17:110]; the *name*-density of s025 (7 "Merciful" lines, incl. 25:63 "The servants of the Merciful") is a corpus-verifiable signature of this surah.
* **`jihād` ("strive… a mighty struggle")**: 25:52 — striving "with it" (the Book) as the Meccan-mode combat of argument; *Source: classical reading — agent inference — verify; the term note has no canonical ledger yet.*
