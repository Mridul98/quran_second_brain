## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Meccan — per the classical surah-classification lists. *Source: transmitted classification (al-Itqan-type surah-introductions) — verify in print.* Revelation order **42/43 — classical lists — verify**: printed nuzul-order tables conflict (the s020 agent recorded 42/45/73 discrepancies across tables for Ta-Ha/Furqan neighbors; this series labels the disagreement rather than resolving it — agent note).
* **Size:** 77 ayahs, 6 ruku (per `data/ruku_map.json`: 9+11+14+10+16+17 = 77). Mushaf position 25, between an-Nur (24) and ash-Shu'ara (26).
* **Name:** al-Furqan, "The Criterion" — from `[25:1]` "Blessed is He who sent down the Criterion upon His servant." The doxology verb "Blessed is He" (tabāraka) opens the surah and recurs at `[25:10]` and `[25:61]` — 3 of the 5 corpus occurrences of the phrase sit in this one surah (verified: the other two are [27:8], [67:1]).
* **Central theme:** A Meccan courtroom surah: the Criterion answers the charges pressed against the Messenger — "a lie that he made up" `[25:4]`, "Tales of the ancients" `[25:5]`, "a man under spell" `[25:8]` — by producing the credentials of its Sender (no son, no partner, creator of measure `[25:2]`), the evidence of creation (shadow, winds, two seas, constellations `[25:45–61]`), and the Day-scene where the false gods stand trial and friends turn to enemies (`[25:17–19]`, `[25:27–29]`). It closes with the surah's counter-portrait — the character list of "the servants of the Merciful" (`[25:63–74]`) — and the verdict line `[25:77]` "What are you to my Lord without your prayers?"
* **Theme checks the task asked to verify (against the corpus, with corrections):** `[25:61]` — the task's "Great Bear … glowing moon" is another English version; this corpus prints "Blessed is He who placed **constellations** in the sky, and placed in it a **lamp**, and an **illuminating moon**" (identical verb+noun at [15:16]; 85:1 "By the sky with the constellations"). `[25:63–74]` ✓ as a character list, with wording notes: 63 "when the ignorant address them, they say, 'Peace'"; 64 night prostration; 67 the corpus is "neither **wasteful** nor **stingy**, but choose a **middle course**"; 68 the corpus is "do not **implore** besides Allah any other god" (not "invoke"), "do not kill the soul which Allah has made sacred—**except in the pursuit of justice**" (the "except by right" idiom belongs to other versions), "do not **commit adultery**" (the "do not approach immorality" wording is [17:32]'s, not here), and "Whoever does that will **face penalties**" (not "meet a burden"). `[25:77]` — task quote "my Lord would not care for you without your prayer" is a paraphrase; corpus: "Say, 'What are you to my Lord without your **prayers**?'" `[25:4–6]` ✓ — the corpus prints "Tales of the ancients" at 25:5; "Legends of the ancients" is how the same idiom is rendered at [16:24], [27:68], [23:83], [83:13] (drift observable in this book). `[25:43]` — corpus: "Have you seen him who **chose** his desire **as** his god?" (twin at [45:23] "has **taken** his desire **for** his god"). **Moses/Pharaoh 25:35–40 — correction:** "Pharaoh" occurs **zero times** in this surah (rg-verified); the pair is Moses and Aaron as assistant (35); "the people who rejected Our signs" (36) are classically read as Pharaoh's people — *Source: classical tafsir identification — agent note, verify.* `[25:22]` ✓ — "On the Day when they see the angels— there will be no good news for sinners on that Day."

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Criterion & charges (1–9):** doxology → creed (no son, no partner, measure) → the gods that "create nothing, but are themselves created" → the four charges (lie, tales, treasure/spell) → "Look how they invent examples for you."
* **The second "Blessed is He" (10–20):** better-than-that gardens and palaces; the Blaze described at range and close-up; the ballot "Is this better, or the Garden of Eternity promised to the righteous?" (15); the gods interrogated on trial (17–19); the creedal answer to the messenger-charge: "We never sent any messengers before you, but they ate food and walked in the marketplaces" (20).
* **Meeting the unseen (21–34):** those who "do not expect to meet Us" want angels or a visible Lord; the Day they see the angels brings "no good news"; works become "scattered dust"; the sky cleft with clouds; the wrongdoer bites his hands over "so-andso"; the Messenger's complaint — "my people have abandoned this Quran" (30); the answer to "why not at once?": "We revealed it in stages" (32).
* **Lessons of the destroyed (35–44):** Moses given the Scripture with Aaron as assistant (35 — Ta-Ha's petition answered); Noah, Aad, Thamood, the inhabitants of Arras, the city "drenched by the terrible rain"; the mockers' verdict "who is further away from the way"; the desire-god (43); "just like cattle, but even more errant in their way" (44).
* **Signs & the name (45–60):** the extending shadow and its sun-pointer; night a covering, sleep rest, day a revival; winds as advance news and pure water reviving dead land — "but most people persist in thanklessness" (50); the merged two seas and their barrier; "put your trust in the Living, the One who never dies" (58); Throne and six days (59); and the pagans' dispute over the very name: "And what is the Merciful?" (60).
* **The third "Blessed is He" & the servants (61–77):** constellations, lamp, illuminating moon; night and day made to alternate "for whoever desires to reflect, or desires to show gratitude"; the ten-mark portrait of `ibad al-Rahman` (63–74) with its penalty-triad and its reversal clause (70 "Allah will replace their bad deeds with good deeds"); the Chamber awarded "for their patience"; the surah's last word: prayer (77).
