## 3. Structural Arc
* **113:1 — the commission and the address:** "Say, “I take refuge with the Lord of Daybreak." — commanded, first-person flight; the whole-surah quotation opens here and closes at 113:5 (observable typography, as in 109/112/114).
* **113:2 — open general:** "From the evil of what He created." — no category excluded; note Itani's added "He" (of what HE created — observable choice, theistic re-framing of a bare "creation").
* **113:3 — cosmic hour:** "And from the evil of the darkness as it gathers." — nightfall as an agent-window (hint correction: NOT "night and its darkness"; ghasiq rendered "the darkness as it gathers").
* **113:4 — human technique:** "And from the evil of those who practice sorcery." — the corpus's only sihr-perpetrator-plural line (hint correction: "blowers in knots" is classical-literal; Itani's text has NO "knot" or "blow" anywhere in s113 — rg-verified).
* **113:5 — human heart:** "And from the evil of an envious when he envies.”" — the net ends inside a person's feeling at the moment it acts; closing quotation mark seals the list.
* **Unit position:** 112 says who God IS; 113 says one thing you DO with that — run to Him. 114 completes the gesture inward (to the whisperer).
