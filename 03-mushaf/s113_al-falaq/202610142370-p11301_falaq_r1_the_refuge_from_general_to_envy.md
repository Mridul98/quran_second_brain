# The Refuge from General to Envy: Five Lines of Flight (113:1–5)
**UID:** 202610142370
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 113:1–5 (Ruku 1 of 1 in al-Falaq)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[113:1]` – `[113:5]` (5 ayahs; global ruku 555 of 556 — verified)
* **Thesis:** The first Mu'awwidhah: a commanded act of fleeing ("I take refuge with the Lord of Daybreak") whose four objects tighten like a net — evil at large (what He created), the cosmic hour (gathering darkness), a criminal technique (sorcery-practitioners), and finally a feeling in a chest at the moment it strikes (an envious when he envies).
* **Placement:** Ruku 1 of 1; fifth door of the closing bracket and first half of the refuge-pair. 112 established an unmatched God; 113 performs the first consequence of that — when nothing is comparable to Him, the only safety is in His Lordship. The surah prefaces its twin 114, which names its enemy's method (whispering) where 113 names only occasions of fear.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[113:1]`** "Say, “I take refuge with the Lord of Daybreak."
  * **Gloss:** Qul + first-person flight; the refuge-verb ('a-dh) with the Rabb of *falaq* — the one whose signature act is splitting (dawn from dark, grain from husk — `[6:95]` "It is Allah Who splits the grain and the seed", rg-verified): the Lord of breakings-out is asked for an exit from evils (agent reading of the name-choice — verify). Quotation opens here, closes at 113:5 (observable typography).
* **`[113:2]`** "From the evil of what He created."
  * **Gloss:** The broadest possible object — every created thing's harmful capacity, none specified; Itani's inserted "He" keeps creation explicitly theistic (observable choice). Classical theodicy-note: the evil sought refuge FROM is in what-acts, not in creating-as-such (*Source: classical reading — verify; not stated in text*).
* **`[113:3]`** "And from the evil of the darkness as it gathers."
  * **Gloss:** Nightfall personified as an interval of risk — "as it gathers" (*waqab*: sinking/thickening, per classical glosses — verify) renders time as substance. Darkness-corpus: `[6:1]` "made the darkness and the light", `[14:1]` "from darkness to light" (rg-verified) — in this book darkness is always what rescue MOVES THROUGH, and here it is what one flees; pair with 114's non-visual enemy.
* **`[113:4]`** "And from the evil of those who practice sorcery."
  * **Gloss:** The only named human technique of harm in the surah — Itani generalizes the classical "blowers on knots" phrase away (zero "knot"/"blow" in s113 — rg-verified; hint corrected). The sihr-dossier: `[2:102]` (witchcraft taught "the means to cause separation between man and his wife" — the dossier's most concrete evil, rg-verified), `[7:116]` "a mighty magic", `[20:69]` "a magician's trickery", and the accuser-line "sorcerer or a madman" `[51:39]`/`[51:52]`/`[40:24]`/`[61:6]` (rg-verified) — sorcery as perennial charge-vs-threat pair.
* **`[113:5]`** "And from the evil of an envious when he envies.”"
  * **Gloss:** The net's last ring: not "the envier" as type but "an envious WHEN he envies" — the evil localized at the act-moment (Itani's "when" keeps the temporal clause; hint corrected "envier"→"an envious"). Envy-dossier: `[2:109]` (People of the Book turn believers back "out of envy"), `[2:213]` "out of mutual envy", `[3:19]`, `[4:54]` "Or do they envy the people for what Allah has given them" (rg-verified) — envy is the Quran's repeated pathology of the privileged-religious; 113:5 is the only place the community is told to seek refuge from it.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`falaq` (daybreak/split)**: the breaking of dawn — and of constraint.
  * **Rendering:** "Daybreak" (113:1 + header "DAYBREAK").
  * **Elsewhere:** `[89:1]` "By the daybreak" (same English for *fajr* — observable cross-root merge), `[6:95]` "splits the grain and the seed" (verb, rg-verified), `[97:5]` "Peace it is; until the rise of dawn" — the qadr-night that ends where this surah's Lord reigns (agent pairing — verify).
* **`sharr (evil)`**: the surah's metronome — "From the evil of…" ×4 (113:2–5; "evil" count in s113 = 4 — rg-verified).
  * **Note:** the anaphora begins with bare "From" (113:2) then "And from" (3–5) — observable listing rhythm.
* **`naffath (sorcery-practice)`**:
  * **Rendering:** "those who practice sorcery" — technique unnamed (no "knots" — rg-verified; classical literal rendering lives in tafsir only — transmitted, verify).
  * **Elsewhere:** sihr cluster (2:102, 7:116, 20:69, 51:39/52, 40:24, 61:6 — all rg-verified).
* **`hasud (an envious)`**: the feeling-as-weapon.
  * **Rendering:** "an envious when he envies" — noun+verb from one root in five words (observable).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:109]`, `[2:213]` "out of mutual envy", `[3:19]`, `[4:54]`, and `[48:15]` "you are jealous of us" (rg-verified) — envy is the Quran's standard pathology of the pious-privileged; 113:5 is the only refuge-prayer against it.
