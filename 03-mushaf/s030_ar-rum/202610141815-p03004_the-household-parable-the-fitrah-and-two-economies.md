# The household parable, the fitrah command, and the two economies (30:28–40)
**UID:** 202610141815
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 30:28–40 (Ruku 4 of 6 in ar-Rum)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[30:28]` – `[30:40]` (13 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The argument comes home: would you make your own servants partners in your own wealth? (28) — yet the wrongdoers "follow their desires without knowledge" (29); so set your face to the religion built on the natural instinct Allah folded into mankind (30), pray, and refuse sects (31–32); fair-weather monotheism is arraigned (33–36); provision is God's dial (37); and the audit ends where every Meccan creed ends — money: the relative, the destitute, the wayfarer vs. usury that never multiplies with Allah (38–39), under the four-stage God whom no idol can imitate (40).
* **Placement:** Ruku 4 of 6; the surah's moral center — creed (28–32), character (33–37), cash (38–39), closure (40). Its 30:30 command is re-issued in ruku 5 as 30:43.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[30:28]`** "He illustrates an example for you, from your own selves: do you make your servants full partners in the wealth We have given you? Do you revere them as you revere one another? We thus explain the revelations for a people who understand."
  * **Gloss:** The household-analogy: even masters refuse property-sharing with their own servants — so why the partnership claim against God? Twin-parable `[39:29]` (partners at odds vs one master); the explanation-formula twins `[57:17]` ("We thus explain the revelations for you, so that you may understand") and `[6:65]` ("Note how We explain the revelations, so that they may understand"); allocation-frame `[43:32]`.
* **`[30:29]`** "Yet the wrongdoers follow their desires without knowledge. But who can guide whom Allah leaves astray? They will have no helpers."
  * **Gloss:** The diagnosis: desire leading, knowledge absent — the desire-as-god portrait at `[45:23]` and `[25:43]` "who chose his desire as his god?"; the prerogative clause-twins `[17:97]`, `[16:37]` "Allah does not guide those who misguide. And they will have no saviors"; "no helpers" as at `[3:56]`, `[2:270]`.
* **`[30:30]`** "So devote yourself to the religion of monotheism—the natural instinct Allah has instilled in mankind. There is no altering Allah’s creation. This is the true religion, but most people do not know."
  * **Gloss:** The command: set-your-face (hanif-idiom — cluster: `[10:105]` "dedicate yourself to the true religion—a monotheist", `[6:79]`/`[6:161]`/`[16:120]`); "natural instinct" (fitrah) — corpus-unique (rg-verified); "no altering Allah's creation" rhymes with the words-formula `[10:64]` "no alteration to the words of Allah" and `[18:27]`; the closing motif-line again ("most people do not know" — third time: 6, 8, 30; twin `[34:36]`).
* **`[30:31]`** "Turning towards Him—and be conscious of Him, and perform the prayer, and do not be of the idolaters."
  * **Gloss:** Four instructions in one breath: return (inabah), taqwa, salah, dissociation — "do not be of the idolaters" near-verbatim `[6:14]`.
* **`[30:32]`** "Of those who divided their religion, and became sects; each faction pleased with what they have."
  * **Gloss:** The identity-club warning: verbatim-family `[6:159]` "those who divided their religion and became sects"; the contentment-line twin `[23:53]` "each party happy with what they have" (pleased/happy drift); the prohibition-form `[3:105]` "do not be like those who separated and disputed".
* **`[30:33]`** "When affliction touches the people, they call on their Lord, turning to Him in repentance. But then, when He gives them a taste of His mercy, some of them attribute partners to their Lord."
  * **Gloss:** The weather-religion cycle — near-verbatim twin `[39:8]` ("adversity touches... he prays... then... he attributes rivals to Allah"); also `[10:12]` family, and the mercy-scheme-counterpart `[10:21]`.
* **`[30:34]`** "To show ingratitude for what We have given them. Indulge yourselves—you will surely know."
  * **Gloss:** Kufr named as the point of the cycle (the shukr-note's cover-leg); "Indulge yourselves" is a deferred sentence, not a permission — twin-threat `[39:8]` "Enjoy your disbelief for a little while; you will be among the inmates of the Fire".
* **`[30:35]`** "Have We sent down to them any authority, which speaks in support of their idols?"
  * **Gloss:** The evidence-audit: where is your sulṭān? — the `[6:81]` test ("for which He sent down to you no authority") restated as rebuke; no descending document supports the partners.
* **`[30:36]`** "When We give people a taste of mercy, they rejoice in it. But when adversity befalls them, because of what their hands have perpetrated, they begin to despair."
  * **Gloss:** The same people in both weathers: rejoice-then-despair — the psychology tripled with `[11:9–10]` and `[6:44]` (delight, sudden seizure, despair), cf. `[17:83]`; the earned-adversity formula returns here before its public version at 30:41.
* **`[30:37]`** "Do they not see that Allah expands the provision for whomever He wills, or restricts it? Surely in this are signs for people who believe."
  * **Gloss:** Provision is distributed by will, not merit — the expand/restrict family `[29:62]`, `[17:30]`, `[34:36]`; audience-line "signs for people who believe."
* **`[30:38]`** "So give the relative his rights, and the destitute, and the wayfarer. That is best for those who seek Allah’s presence. Those are the prosperous."
  * **Gloss:** The triad of the road, nearly verbatim `[17:26]` "give the relative his rights, and the poor, and the wayfarer"; the wayfarer-list `[2:215]`, `[8:41]`, `[59:7]`; "seek Allah's presence" = `[13:22]` "seek the presence of their Lord", `[2:115]`, with 2:272's "for the sake of Allah" drift; the "prosperous" (muflihun) are 2:5's "the successful."
* **`[30:39]`** "The usury you practice, seeking thereby to multiply people’s wealth, will not multiply with Allah. But what you give in charity, desiring Allah’s approval—these are the multipliers."
  * **Gloss:** The two economies in one sentence: riba inflates ledgers, not weight; sadaqah-desiring-His-face = "the multipliers" — the riba-law twins `[2:275]` (commerce vs usury), `[2:276]` (Allah condemns usury, blesses charities — "does not love any sinful ingrate" welding to 30:45), the multiply-family `[2:245]` "so He will multiply it for him manifold", `[3:130]`, `[4:161]`; the canonical note already cites this verse (verified).
* **`[30:40]`** "Allah is He who created you, then provides for you, then makes you die, then brings you back to life. Can any of your idols do any of that? Glorified is He, and Exalted above what they associate."
  * **Gloss:** The four-stage lifecycle audited: can your partners originate-provide-kill-revivify? — the partner-challenge-twin `[10:34]` "Can any of your partners initiate creation, and then repeat it?"; the same arc returns at 30:54 (weak→strong→gray).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`fitrah` (natural instinct):**
  * **Rendering:** "the natural instinct Allah has instilled in mankind" — hapax in the corpus (rg-verified; "instilled" is the edition's verb for fatara).
  * **Elsewhere:** no other occurrence; the classical gloss "every child is born upon fitrah" is a hadith read onto this word (*Source: classical tafsir — Bukhari/Muslim-type reports as cited by Ibn Kathir/Tabari on 30:30 — verify*).
* **The hanif cluster (monotheism/upright):**
  * **Rendering:** "religion of monotheism" (30:30) and "the upright religion" (30:43) are the same orientation-word rendered two ways, one surah apart; `[98:5]` "That is the upright religion" closes the idiom.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[6:161]` "an upright religion, the creed of Abraham the Monotheist"; `[16:120]` "Abraham was... a monotheist"; `[10:105]` — the command-verb twin of both ar-Rum verses.
* **`hudud/sect` (divided religion):**
  * **Rendering:** "Of those who divided their religion, and became sects; each faction pleased with what they have."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[6:159]` (the disavowal version), `[23:53]` (happy/pleased drift), `[3:105]` (after clear proofs), `[15:90]` "the separatists".
* **`riba` vs `sadaqah` (multiply):**
  * **Rendering:** "will not multiply with Allah... these are the multipliers" — a single root y-z-r carrying both verdicts.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:276]` near-verbatim thesis twin; `[2:245]` multiply-manifold; `[3:130]`, `[4:161]` the riba indictments — this is the two-economies note's spine-verse, already cited there.
