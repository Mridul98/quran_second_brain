# From weakness to gray hair, the one-hour oath, and the promise kept (30:54–60)
**UID:** 202610141817
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 30:54–60 (Ruku 6 of 6 in ar-Rum)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[30:54]` – `[30:60]` (7 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The body itself preaches resurrection — weak, strong, weak-and-gray, created as He wills (54); on the Day the guilty will swear they tarried but an hour (55), and the people of knowledge-and-faith will correct the stopwatch (56) as excuses expire (57); the Quran's parable-method, restated, is answered with one word — "fakers" (58) — and that refusal is the seal (59); so the surah's last word is the oldest command: "So be patient. The promise of Allah is true. And do not let those who lack certainty belittle you" (60).
* **Placement:** Ruku 6 of 6; the ring-close — 30:60 answers 30:6 ("Allah never breaks His promise"), converting the prophecy of ruku 1 into the patience-command for every reader after it.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[30:54]`** "Allah is He Who created you weak, then after weakness gave you strength, then after strength gave you weakness and gray hair. He creates whatever He wills. He is the Omniscient, the Omnipotent."
  * **Gloss:** A life written as three movements — the curve twin of `[22:5]` ("full strength... the vilest age"), `[40:67]` (dust→seed→embryo→elderly) and `[36:68]` ("Whomever We grant old age, We reverse his development"); "He creates whatever He wills" — `[28:68]` "Your Lord creates whatever He wills, and He chooses."
* **`[30:55]`** "On the Day when the Hour takes place, the sinners will swear they had stayed but an hour. Thus they were deluded."
  * **Gloss:** Perjury about duration: the grave-time shrinks to "an hour" — twins `[10:45]` "as if they had tarried only one hour of a day" and `[79:46]` "as though they only stayed an evening, or its morning"; the delusion is the fourth Hour-clause of the surah (12, 14, 16, 55).
* **`[30:56]`** "But those endowed with knowledge and faith will say, “You remained in Allah’s Book until the Day of Resurrection. This is the Day of Resurrection, but you did not know.”"
  * **Gloss:** The counter-testimony from the people of knowledge-and-faith — the ulu-l-'ilm cluster `[3:18]` (Allah, the angels, and those endowed with knowledge bearing tawhid) and `[22:54]` ("those endowed with knowledge may know that it is the truth"); the record ("Allah's Book") makes residence measurable; their last word to the dead: "but you did not know" — answering 30:7's surface-knowers.
* **`[30:57]`** "On that Day, the sinners’ excuses will not benefit them, nor will they be excused."
  * **Gloss:** The legal window closes — near-verbatim twin `[16:84]` "those who disbelieved will not be permitted, nor will they be excused."
* **`[30:58]`** "We have cited in this Quran for the people every sort of parable. But even if you bring them a miracle, those who disbelieve will say, “You are nothing but fakers.”"
  * **Gloss:** The Book's method self-declared — the three-rendering formula: this verse ("every sort of parable"), `[39:27]` ("every ideal"), `[18:54]` ("every kind of example"); the miracle-demand is the stock refusal (`[13:7]`, `[29:50]`, `[6:109]`, and the prerogative-answer `[40:78]` "No messenger can bring a miracle except by leave of Allah"); "fakers" is corpus-unique (rg-verified).
* **`[30:59]`** "Allah thus seals the hearts of those who do not know."
  * **Gloss:** The seal as self-employment: the "thus" (kadhālika) ties the verdict to the refusal of 58 — near-verbatim twin `[7:101]` "Thus Allah seals the hearts of the disbelievers"; cf. `[2:7]` (set a seal) and `[47:16]` (sealed hearts "and they follow their own desires" — 30:29's diagnosis completed).
* **`[30:60]`** "So be patient. The promise of Allah is true. And do not let those who lack certainty belittle you."
  * **Gloss:** The closing triad: sabr, the guaranteed promise, immunity from discouragement — the first two sentences verbatim in `[40:55]` ("So be patient. The promise of Allah is true. And ask forgiveness...") and the patience-command of `[40:77]`; the "promise" is, in this surah's own terms, the victory foretold at 30:2–4 and guaranteed at 30:6.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **The life-curve (da'f → quwwah → da'f wa shayb):**
  * **Rendering:** "weakness and gray hair" (54) — "gray hair" occurs only here (rg-verified); `[22:5]` renders the same endpoint as "the vilest age."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[22:5]`, `[40:67]`, `[36:68]` — the same arc in resurrection-argument contexts; within s030, 30:20 (dust→spreading) and 30:40 (created→provided→died→revived) are its two earlier cuts.
* **"but an hour" (sa'a):**
  * **Rendering:** 30:55 — the oath about duration, judged as delusion.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[10:45]`, `[79:46]` — the two other duration-shrinkages; the Hour-clause formula itself recurs at 30:12/14/16/55 (Source: observable repetition).
* **"endowed with knowledge and faith":**
  * **Rendering:** 30:56's courtroom experts; knowledge joined to faith, not to credentials.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[3:18]`, `[22:54]` — the same pairing carrying tawhid-testimony and truth-recognition; cf. `[15:53]` "a boy endowed with knowledge."
* **khatm (seal):**
  * **Rendering:** 30:59 "Allah thus seals the hearts of those who do not know" — the seal targets "those who do not know," the surah's chronic class.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[7:101]` near-verbatim; `[2:7]`; `[47:16]` seal-plus-desires — the guidance-note's forfeitability clause (hearts sealed are the road's self-excluded travelers).

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Meccan (Source: classical lists — verify); a patience-command close is this register's signature (verbatim twin 40:55 in another Meccan surah).
* **Structure note:** the surah that opened with history (a dated victory) closes with biography (a dated life: weak→strong→gray) and with the same word — "the promise" (6, 60): patience is the liturgical form of believing 30:2–4 (Source: structural observation; classical reports likewise read 30:60 as the surah's summary command — verify).
