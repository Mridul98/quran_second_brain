# The six signs: dust, mates, languages, sleep, lightning, and the standing heavens (30:20–27)
**UID:** 202610141814
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 30:20–27 (Ruku 3 of 6 in ar-Rum)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[30:20]` – `[30:27]` (8 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Six times "And of His signs" — you from dust, mates for tranquility, languages and colors, sleep and pursuit, lightning and life-giving rain, heaven and earth standing at His disposal — each sign is tuned to a distinct reader (reflect, know, listen, understand), and the inventory closes on total ownership: all are submissive, and His is the highest attribute (26–27).
* **Placement:** Ruku 3 of 6; the empirical heart the ruku 1–2 argument promised; its revivals (24) carry the 30:19 proof forward, and its closing pair (26–27) restates 30:11 with doxology.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[30:20]`** "And of His signs is that He created you from dust; and behold, you become humans spreading out."
  * **Gloss:** Sign one, anthropological: dust to diffused humanity (naashirin) — the ladder `[35:11]` "created you from dust, then from a small drop", `[22:5]`, `[40:67]`, and the taunt-twin `[18:37]`.
* **`[30:21]`** "And of His signs is that He created for you mates from among yourselves, so that you may find tranquility in them; and He planted love and compassion between you. In this are signs for people who reflect."
  * **Gloss:** Sign two, domestic: the spouses-sign (azwaj; twins `[42:11]` "He made for you mates from among yourselves", `[16:72]`, `[4:1]` "created from it its mate") — stated as sign-discourse, not legislation; "He planted" ja'ala as making-found; the audience: reflecters.
* **`[30:22]`** "And of His signs is the creation of the heavens and the earth, and the diversity of your languages and colors. In this are signs for those who know."
  * **Gloss:** Sign three: difference itself — "languages" is corpus-unique here (rg-verified); the color-family `[35:27–28]` (fruits, mountains, humans "various colors" — where "the learned fear Allah"), `[16:13]`, `[16:69]`; audience: the knowing.
* **`[30:23]`** "And of His signs are your sleep by night and day, and your pursuit of His bounty. In this are signs for people who listen."
  * **Gloss:** Sign four: the daily liturgy of rest and labor — sleep-as-covering twin `[25:47]` "He made the night a covering for you, and sleep for rest; and He made the day a revival"; sa'y (pursuit) paired with bounty as in 28:73; audience: listeners.
* **`[30:24]`** "And of His signs is that He shows you the lightning, causing fear and hope. And He brings down water from the sky, and with it He revives the earth after it was dead. In this are signs for people who understand."
  * **Gloss:** Sign five: the storm — verbatim twin `[13:12]` "It is He who shows you the lightening, causing fear and hope. And He produces the heavy clouds" (edition's "lightening" spelling there, [sic]); the rain-revival with 19 and 50 forms the surah's triad (`[16:65]`, `[57:17]`, `[29:63]` as externals); audience: understanders.
* **`[30:25]`** "And of His signs is that the heaven and the earth stand at His disposal. And then, when He calls you out of the earth, you will emerge at once."
  * **Gloss:** Sign six, cosmic: maintenance and moment — "stand at His disposal" (bi-amrihi qa'imatan) is corpus-unique (rg-verified); the one-call resurrection echoes `[20:55]` "from it We will bring you out another time"; no friction between the two halves of the verse.
* **`[30:26]`** "To Him belongs everyone in the heavens and the earth. All are submissive to Him."
  * **Gloss:** Ownership then posture: qunut as universal submission — the prostration family `[13:15]` "To Allah prostrates everyone in the heavens and the earth, willingly or unwillingly" and `[22:18]`; whatever worshipers deny, the cosmos performs.
* **`[30:27]`** "It is He who initiates creation, and then repeats it, something easy for Him. His is the highest attribute, in the heavens and the earth. He is the Almighty, the Wise."
  * **Gloss:** 30:11 restated with its cost-account ("something easy for Him" — `[29:19]` "This is easy for Allah"; `[10:34]` the same challenge-question); "the highest attribute" (mathal) = `[16:60]` "Allah sets the Highest Example" — one word, two renderings (drift observable); doxology: Almighty, the Wise.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`azwaj` (mates) + `sakinah` (tranquility):**
  * **Rendering:** 30:21 "mates from among yourselves, so that you may find tranquility in them" — task's "find rest" is other editions; this one: tranquility.
  * **Elsewhere:** spouses: `[4:1]`, `[16:72]`, `[42:11]`; sakina elsewhere is battlefield/Ark peace — `[2:248]` "bringing tranquility from your Lord", `[9:40]`, `[48:4]` "sent down tranquility into the hearts of the believers" — 30:21 is the corpus's only domestic sakinah (rg-verified).
* **The audience ladder (reflect / know / listen / understand):**
  * **Rendering:** 21 ta-fakkarun → 22 ya-lamun → 23 yasma'un → 24 ya-qilun, four sign-clauses, four verbs.
  * **Elsewhere:** each endpoint exists standalone: `[16:65]` "a sign for people who listen", `[2:164]` "signs for people who understand", `[10:67]` "people who listen" — ar-Rum stacks them (Source: agent inference — the ladder as rhetoric, verify).
* **`ra'ban wa rahban` (fear and hope):**
  * **Rendering:** lightning "causing fear and hope" (30:24).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[13:12]` verbatim twin; `[2:19–20]` lightning as parable-state; the pair is the believer's weather — mercy in sight, thunder still audible.
* **`mathal al-a'la` (the highest attribute/example):**
  * **Rendering:** "His is the highest attribute, in the heavens and the earth" (30:27).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[16:60]` "Allah sets the Highest Example" — same Arabic superlative, different English noun; the parable-cluster (16:60/75/76) is its home field.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Meccan (Source: classical lists — verify); a litany of ayat — the classical Meccan register of creation-signs.
* **Text-claim note (30:21):** the verse states a made-for-you tranquility and a planted love/compassion as a sign for reflecters; it legislates nothing here — the family-law kin (divorce/kindness verses in Surah 4, e.g., `[4:19]` "live with them in kindness") is the legal corpus, and this verse is its doxological warrant. Classical glosses split mawadda/rahma variously (desire-and-pity, divine-arrangement-and-mercy). *Source: classical tafsir on 30:21 (Ibn Kathir/Tabari variants) — verify.*
* **Shukr-circuit note:** unlike the 16:10–18 style inventories, 30:21–25 terminate in audience-labels, not in "give thanks" — the thanks-demand arrives at `[30:46]` (next-but-one ruku) on the same rain/winds material (Source: observable structure; cf. the shukr note's "Practical Reading").
