## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Meccan per the classical surah-lists; no Medinan minority recorded for this surah. *Source: classical lists — verify in print (al-Itqan-type surah introductions).*
* **Order flag:** the task header asks whether revelation order is **43**. This brain's own indices record the neighbors in a different transmitted enumeration — ash-Shu'ara 47 (`202610141741-s026_index_al-shu-ara.md` §1), an-Naml 48 (`202610141761-s027_index_an-naml.md` §1), al-Qasas 49 (`202610141771-s028_index_al-qasas.md` §1) — in which the consecutive block places ar-Rum after al-Ankabut (50+), not at 43. Two classical sequences genuinely disagree; "43" is retained here only with its verify-label. *Source: classical lists — verify.*
* **Size:** 60 ayahs, 6 ruku (map boundaries 10+9+8+13+13+7 = 60 ✓). Mushaf position 30, between al-Ankabut (29) and Luqman (31).
* **Name:** ar-Rum — "THE ROMANS" (corpus title line). The word for the nation occurs only at `[30:2]` "The Romans have been defeated." (rg-verified: "Romans" appears nowhere else in the corpus).
* **Central theme:** The surah puts a dated, falsifiable promise at the center of history — the defeated Romans will triumph "in a few years", and "Allah never breaks His promise" (30:2–6). Around that hinge it runs one resurrection argument: the God who reverses defeats originates creation and repeats it (11, 27), brings the living from the dead and revives dead land (19, 24, 50), and plants love and compassion between mates (21); the signs are legible to the reflecters, knowers, listeners, understanders (21–24), while those who "know an outer aspect of the worldly life" (7) end as the dead who cannot be made to hear (52–53). Corruption is earned and tasted "so that they might return" (41); the command is to set the face to the upright religion (30, 43) and the surah seals: "So be patient. The promise of Allah is true" (60) — a ring with 30:6.
* **Task wording checks, verified against the corpus:** (1) Prophecy — the task's quotation "in the nearest land — but they, after their defeat, will overcome" is NOT this edition: `[30:3]` reads "In a nearby territory. But following their defeat, they will be victorious." ("overcome"→"will be victorious"; "nearest land"→"a nearby territory" — recorded as correction). (2) `[30:41]` ✓ verbatim as tasked, with the purpose clauses: "in order to make them taste some of what they have done, so that they might return". (3) `[30:21]` — the task's "find rest / He made affection and mercy" vs corpus "find **tranquility** in them; and He **planted love and compassion** between you" (correction); it is sign-discourse, not legislation — family-law kin (4:19 "live with them in kindness"; 4:1 "created from it its mate") confirmed as a text-claim, not a law-claim. (4) `[30:22]` ✓ "the diversity of your languages and colors" — "languages" is corpus-unique (rg-verified). (5) Dead-rise: ✓ `[30:19]` "He revives the land after it had died" and ✓ `[30:24]` "He revives the earth after it was dead" — **plus `[30:50]`**, which the task missed (30:19/24/50 form an internal triad). (6) Hanifiyyah: the command pair is `[30:30]` ("the religion of monotheism—the natural instinct") and `[30:43]` ("the upright religion, before there comes from Allah a Day that cannot be averted"); `[30:44]` is consequence, not command; and neither closes the surah — the close is the patience command `[30:60]` (recorded as correction to "closing").

## 3. Structural Arc
* **History as evidence (1–10):** a dated promise in public affairs is the surah's opening miracle; "The matter is up to Allah, in the past, and in the future" (4) universalizes it; the epistemic diagnosis (7–8) and the travel-audit (9–10) say how to read such signs.
* **Return and sorting (11–19):** the Hour's three clauses ("On the Day when the Hour takes place" ×3: 12, 14, 16) sort the guilty into despair and the believers into meadows; the doxology (17–18) answers with the living/dead exchange (19) — a bridge into the signs list.
* **The signs inventory (20–27):** six "wa-min ayatihi" clauses escalate from dust→mates→languages→sleep→lightning→the heaven-and-earth standing at His disposal (bi-amrihi); audiences escalate: reflect, know, listen, understand (21–24).
* **From cosmos to household (28–40):** "an example for you, from your own selves" (28) converts cosmology into creed (29–32), creed into character (33–37), and character into economics — the relative/destitute/wayfarer vs usury-that-does-not-multiply (38–39).
* **Corruption and mercy (41–53):** the moral diagnosis (41) is answered by the same mercy-inventory now run on winds, ships, and clouds (46, 48), terminating in thanks; the prophet is told his limits twice (52–53) — speech survives, acceptance does not (58).
* **The ring (54–60):** the body's own weak→gray arc preaches resurrection (54); time compresses to "an hour" (55); excuses expire (57); "So be patient. The promise of Allah is true" (60) closes against "Allah never breaks His promise" (6).
