# Blessed Moses & Aaron, Elijah against Baal, Lot's exit: the peace-refrain at work (37:114–138)
**UID:** 202610141925
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 37:114–138 (Ruku 4 of 5 in as-Saffat)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[37:114]` – `[37:138]` (25 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Three cycles at full speed: Moses & Aaron receive blessing, deliverance, victory, the Clarifying Scripture and the straight path with a sealed peace (114–122); Elijah's Baal-reductio gets lied about and peace on "the House of Elijah" (123–132); Lot is saved whole except one, his ruined people left as a roadside exhibit for the passers-by (133–138).
* **Placement:** Ruku 4; the surah's compression test — the same crisis-rescue-salam-refrain kit now runs without any courtroom detail, and the Fir'awn confrontation the task expected is absent: the exodus survives only as "the terrible disaster" (115).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[37:114]`** "And We blessed Moses and Aaron. **`[37:115]`** And We delivered them and their people from the terrible disaster. **`[37:116]`** And We supported them, and so they were the victors."
  * **Gloss:** Blessing, deliverance (with "their people" — the exodus compressed), divine support flipping them into victors: no confrontation scene, only outcome.
* **`[37:117]`** "And We gave them the Clarifying Scripture. **`[37:118]`** And We guided them upon the straight path."
  * **Gloss:** The Book and the path pair — Scripture as clarification, guidance as its function (the guidance-note's own program).
* **`[37:119]`** "And We left with them for later generations. **`[37:120]`** Peace be upon Moses and Aaron. **`[37:121]`** Thus We reward the righteous. **`[37:122]`** They were of Our believing servants."
  * **Gloss:** The seal kit on the dual name — refrain here rendered "the righteous" (task drift verified: 121 ≠ "doers of good", that is 105/110).
* **`[37:123]`** "Also Elijah was one of the messengers. **`[37:124]`** He said to his people, “Do you not fear?"
  * **Gloss:** The kit re-applies to a named-but-unstory'd prophet; his whole sermon starts with the fear-question (taqwa verb).
* **`[37:125]`** "Do you call on Baal, and forsake the Best of creators? **`[37:126]`** Allah is your Lord, and the Lord of your ancestors.”"
  * **Gloss:** The Baal reductio: name one god, lose the Best of creators; then the ancestor-counter (Moses' answer to Pharaoh verbatim-twin, `[26:26]`, verified).
* **`[37:127]`** "But they called him a liar, and thus they will be brought forward. **`[37:128]`** Except for Allah’s sincere servants."
  * **Gloss:** Denial, arraignment-bring, and the fourth exception-bracket (40/74/128/160) — the surah's recurring rescue-clause.
* **`[37:129]`** "And We left with him for later generations. **`[37:130]`** Peace be upon the House of Elijah. **`[37:131]`** Thus We reward the virtuous. **`[37:132]`** He was one of Our believing servants."
  * **Gloss:** The only peace formula addressed to a *household*; refrain's third English ending, "the virtuous" (drift logged in index §1).
* **`[37:133]`** "And Lot was one of the messengers. **`[37:134]`** We saved him and his family, all of them."
  * **Gloss:** Lot enters mid-cycle — saved with family "all of them", the claim about to be qualified.
* **`[37:135]`** "Except for an old woman who lagged behind. **`[37:136]`** Then We annihilated the others."
  * **Gloss:** The one exception inside the rescue (twin phrasing `[26:171]` "an old woman among those who tarried", verified); annihilation closes the case.
* **`[37:137]`** "You pass by them in the morning. **`[37:138]`** And at night. Do you not understand?"
  * **Gloss:** The ruined land as daily itinerary — the Meccan caravans drive past the evidence twice a day, yet need asking "Do you not understand?"

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`al-Kitab al-Mubin`** (the Clarifying Scripture): `[37:117]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[26:2]` "These are the Verses of the Clarifying Book" (verified) — one word-family linking the Torah here to the Quran's self-title.
* **`siratan mustaqim`** (the straight path): `[37:118]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[1:6]` the petition; `[3:101]` cleaving to Allah is guidance; `[16:121]` Abraham guided to a straight path (all verified) — the Mubin-Book pair (16:76 too) shows Scripture-as-path grammar.
* **`khayr al-khaliqin`** (the Best of creators): `[37:125]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[23:14]` "Most Blessed is Allah, the Best of Creators" (verified; case-drift "Creators") — Elijah's polemic word is the creators-hierarchy superlative.
* **`Baal`**: `[37:125]` — corpus-unique name (verified); the translation transliterates without glossing the deity. *Source: observable corpus fact; the idol's identification = classical tafsir, verify in print.*
