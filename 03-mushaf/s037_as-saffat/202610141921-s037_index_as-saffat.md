## 3. Structural Arc
* **Oath and cosmos (1–10):** three heavenly crews swear; the verdict "Your God is indeed One" (4–5); the lower heaven adorned and guarded against eavesdropping devils (6–10).
* **The anthropology of mockery (11–21):** "more difficult to create?" — sticky clay; ridicule, magic-charge, resurrection-denial (12–17); "Yes indeed, and you will be totally subdued" → the single nudge and the Day of Separation announced (18–21).
* **The sorting scene (22–74):** the herd driven to Hell and stopped for questioning (22–24); followers and leaders trading blame (25–39); the bracket "Except for Allah's sincere servants" (40) opening the paradise suite (41–61) answered by the Tree of Bitterness suite (62–68); inherited misguidance closes the bracket (69–74).
* **The peace-and-reward cycles (75–148):** Noah (75–82), Abraham (83–113), Moses & Aaron (114–122), Elijah (123–132), Lot (133–138), Jonah (139–148) — each compressed to crisis, rescue, "Peace be upon…", "Thus We reward…", "one of Our believing servants."
* **The daughters-reductio and the angelic rejoinder (149–173):** "Ask them" (149–157); jinn-kinship refuted (158–160); seduction denied (161–163); the ranks themselves testify — "we are the arrangers… the glorifiers" (164–166), the ring back to verse 1; the counterfactual regret (167–170); God's Word issued to the messenger-servants — support and victory (171–173).
* **Wait, watch, worship (174–182):** "So disregard them… avoid them… they will soon see" (174–179, the paired command); the exalted-close generalizes the five peaces over all messengers and returns praise to Allah, Lord of the Worlds (180–182).
