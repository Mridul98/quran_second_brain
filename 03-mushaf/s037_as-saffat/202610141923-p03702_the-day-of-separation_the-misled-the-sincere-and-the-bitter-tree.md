# The Day of Separation in court: the misled, the sincere servants, and the Tree of Bitterness (37:22–74)
**UID:** 202610141923
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 37:22–74 (Ruku 2 of 5 in as-Saffat)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[37:22]` – `[37:74]` (53 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The Day of Separation is staged as a procession and a cross-examination: the wrongdoers are herded with their "mates" and stopped for questioning (22–24), followers and leaders trade blame (25–39), and the bracket "Except for Allah's sincere servants" (40, repeated 74) encloses the paradise suite — cup, pearls, the saved man spotting his old mocker in the pit — against the counter-suite of the Tree of Bitterness (62–68).
* **Placement:** Ruku 2; the surah's longest unit and its eschatological engine room — the sorting promised at 37:19–21 is executed here in detail, then handed to the prophetic case-law of ruku 3–5.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[37:22]`** "Gather those who did wrong, and their mates, and what they used to worship. **`[37:23]`** Besides Allah, and lead them to the way to Hell. **`[37:24]`** And stop them. They are to be questioned.”"
  * **Gloss:** The court's arrest-and-halt order: the guilty, their paired kind, and their false gods all collected; "stop them" makes the road itself an interrogation point.
* **`[37:25]`** "What is the matter with you? Why do you not help one another? **`[37:26]`** In fact, on that Day, they will be submissive. **`[37:27]`** They will come to one another, questioning one another. **`[37:28]`** They will say, “You used to come at us from the right.”"
  * **Gloss:** Old solidarity collapses into submission and mutual accusation; the followers charge the leaders who "came at us from the right" (rendered literally — the power/ambush idiom is unresolved in the translation; Source: agent inference — verify).
* **`[37:29]`** "They will say, “You yourselves were not believers. **`[37:30]`** We had no authority over you. You yourselves were rebellious people."
  * **Gloss:** The leaders' defense: no faith, no coercion — you were your own rebels.
* **`[37:31]`** "The Word of our Lord has been realized against us. We are tasting it. **`[37:32]`** We seduced you. We were seducers.”"
  * **Gloss:** Both sides confess the same thing from different angles: the Word came due; seduction admits only seducers (the Word motif pairs with 37:171 — agent observation).
* **`[37:33]`** "On that Day, they will share in the punishment. **`[37:34]`** Thus We deal with the sinners."
  * **Gloss:** Verdict: joint liability; the sentence doubles as case-law ("Thus We deal…", twin formula at `[21:29]`, verified).
* **`[37:35]`** "When it was said to them, “There is no god except Allah,” they grew arrogant. **`[37:36]`** And said, “Are we to abandon our gods for a mad poet?” **`[37:37]`** In fact, he came with the truth, and he confirmed the messengers."
  * **Gloss:** The earthly cause-of-action: tawhid answered with arrogance and the poet-slander; God's one-line vindication — truth-bringer, confirmer of the messengers before him.
* **`[37:38]`** "Most assuredly, you will taste the painful punishment. **`[37:39]`** And you will be repaid only for what you used to do. **`[37:40]`** Except for Allah’s sincere servants."
  * **Gloss:** Exact-reciprocity justice, then the bracket opens: the mukhlasun are exempt — the surah's recurring rescue-class (37:74, 128, 160 repeat it).
* **`[37:41]`** "For them is a known provision. **`[37:42]`** Fruits; and they will be honored. **`[37:43]`** In the Gardens of Bliss. **`[37:44]`** On furnishings, facing one another."
  * **Gloss:** The inventory begins: rizq "known" — recognized, appointed; fruits, honor, Bliss, couch-facing communion.
* **`[37:45]`** "They will be offered a cup of pure drink. **`[37:46]`** White; a delight to those who drink. **`[37:47]`** Never polluted, and never intoxicating."
  * **Gloss:** Wine-language minus every wine defect — purity, color, delight, no ghaul (headache/pollution), no intoxication.
* **`[37:48]`** "With them will be bashful women with lovely eyes. **`[37:49]`** As if they were closely guarded pearls."
  * **Gloss:** Chaste, wide-eyed companions "as if closely guarded pearls" — the simile twin of `[56:23]` "treasured pearls" (verified).
* **`[37:50]`** "Then they will approach one another, questioning. **`[37:51]`** One of them will say, “I used to have a friend. **`[37:52]`** Who used to say, “Are you of those who believe? **`[37:53]`** That after we die and become dust and bones, we will be called to account?”"
  * **Gloss:** The saved believer recounts his mocker-friend (37:16–17's taunt in new mouth); the nested quote opens at 52 and is never closed in this edition (artifact — quote as-is).
* **`[37:54]`** "He will say, “Will you have a look?” **`[37:55]`** He will look, and will see him in the pit of Hell."
  * **Gloss:** Perspective reversal: the mocker is spotted mid-Hell from the terrace of Bliss.
* **`[37:56]`** "He will say, “By Allah, you almost ruined me. **`[37:57]`** Were it not for the grace of my Lord, I would have been among the arraigned.”"
  * **Gloss:** Salvation read as grace narrowly-kept: near-ruin by bad company; "the grace of my Lord" is the shukr-note's deliverance-grammar.
* **`[37:58]`** "“We will not die. **`[37:59]`** Except for our first death, and we will not be punished.”"
  * **Gloss:** The inhabitants of Bliss declare the end of dying — one death behind, no penalty ahead.
* **`[37:60]`** "This is the supreme triumph. **`[37:61]`** For the like of this let the workers work."
  * **Gloss:** Falah defined and the rally-cry: for this — compete.
* **`[37:62]`** "Is this a better hospitality, or the Tree of Bitterness? **`[37:63]`** We made it an ordeal for the unjust. **`[37:64]`** It is a tree that grows from the bottom of Hell."
  * **Gloss:** The counter-hospitality named: Zaqqum (this edition's "Tree of Bitterness"), an ordeal rooted at Hell's floor.
* **`[37:65]`** "Its fruits are like the devils’ heads. **`[37:66]`** They will eat from it, and fill their bellies with it."
  * **Gloss:** Fruit-repellence in one image — devils' heads eaten to fullness: the "provision" parody of 37:41.
* **`[37:67]`** "Then, on top of it, they will have a brew of boiling liquid. **`[37:68]`** Then their return will be to the Blaze. **`[37:69]`** They had found their parents astray. **`[37:70]`** And rushed along in their footsteps."
  * **Gloss:** Boiling chaser and return to the Blaze; the anthropology of imitation — found astray, rushed after, no reflection (guidance-note negative case).
* **`[37:71]`** "And most of the ancients before them went astray. **`[37:72]`** Even though We sent messengers to warn them. **`[37:73]`** So observe the end of those who were warned."
  * **Gloss:** History as evidence: majority-misguidance *despite* warners; "observe the end" turns the audience into witnesses.
* **`[37:74]`** "Except for the sincere servants of Allah."
  * **Gloss:** The bracket closes exactly where it opened (40) — the mukhlasun are the only exit in the whole sweep.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`al-mukhlasin`** (the sincere servants): the exempt class.
  * **Rendering:** "Allah's sincere servants" (`[37:40]`, `[37:128]`, `[37:160]`) and "the sincere servants of Allah" (`[37:74]`) — word order flips across the four.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[15:40]` "Except for Your sincere servants among them" (verified) — in al-Hijr it is Iblis's own concession; as-Saffat grants it four times as the surah's true exception-clause.
* **`zaqqum`** (Tree of Bitterness): `[37:62–65]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[44:43]` "The Tree of Bitterness" and `[56:52]` "Will be eating from the Tree of Bitterness" (verified) — one named tree across three Meccan surahs; 37's is the hospitality-framed telling.
* **`kaws`** (the cup): `[37:45]` "a cup of pure drink".
  * **Elsewhere:** `[76:21]` "your Lord will offer them a pure drink" and `[56:18–19]` "cups, pitchers, and sparkling drinks… neither headache, nor intoxication" (verified) — defect-free-drink is a fixed eschatological image.
* **`rizq ma`lum`** (known provision): `[37:41]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** corpus-unique wording (verified) — "known" read as appointed/recognized by the dwellers; Source: agent inference — verify against other rizq verses at documentation leisure.
