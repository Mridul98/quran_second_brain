# Noah answered, Abraham sound-hearted: idol-smashing, the fire, the dream, and the great sacrifice (37:75–113)
**UID:** 202610141924
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 37:75–113 (Ruku 3 of 5 in as-Saffat)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[37:75]` – `[37:113]` (39 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The case-law begins: Noah's distress-call is answered and sealed with "Peace be upon Noah" (75–82); then Abraham — "of his kind" and with a *sound heart* — smashes the idols, out-argues "what you carve / what you manufacture", survives the pyre, and passes the evident test in which a son's commanded patience becomes a ransomed submission (83–113).
* **Placement:** Ruku 3; the surah's narrative pivot from the Day's sorting (ruku 2) to the prophets as living exhibits of "Thus We reward…" — the formula of 80/105/110 is the thesis sentence of all five cycles.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[37:75]`** "And Noah called out to Us, and We are the Best of responders. **`[37:76]`** And We saved him and his family from the great calamity. **`[37:77]`** And We made his descendants the survivors."
  * **Gloss:** Call, rescue, lineage: one verse of prayer answered by three verses of deliverance — the pattern every cycle will repeat.
* **`[37:78]`** "And We left mention of him among those who succeeded. **`[37:79]`** Peace be upon Noah among all people."
  * **Gloss:** Posthumous speech preserved; then the surah's first *salam* formula (corpus says "among all people" — the task's "among the worlds" is another translation's wording).
* **`[37:80]`** "We thus reward the righteous. **`[37:81]`** He was one of Our believing servants. **`[37:82]`** Then We drowned the others."
  * **Gloss:** The refrain enters — "the righteous" here, "doers of good" at 105/110 — plus the servant-dossier, plus the drowned counter-example in one line.
* **`[37:83]`** "Of his kind was Abraham. **`[37:84]`** When he came to his Lord with a sound heart."
  * **Gloss:** Kinship-by-religion across the centuries; his qualification is a *sound heart* — the same phrase saved for the Day's only profitable heart at `[26:89]` (verified).
* **`[37:85]`** "He said to his father and his people, “What are you worshiping? **`[37:86]`** Is it falsified gods, instead of Allah, that you want? **`[37:87]`** So what is your opinion about the Lord of the Worlds?”"
  * **Gloss:** Three questions against inherited idols — ending in the opinion-test about Rabb al-alamin (the same trial-question Pharaoh's court will fail, 26:23 twin).
* **`[37:88]`** "Then he took a glance at the stars. **`[37:89]`** And said, “I am sick.” **`[37:90]`** But they turned their backs on him, and went away."
  * **Gloss:** The stratagem: an astronomical glance, a polite illness, and a departure — the idols' festival emptied by design.
* **`[37:91]`** "Then he turned to their gods, and said, “will you not eat? **`[37:92]`** What is it with you, that you do not speak?” **`[37:93]`** Then he turned on them, striking with his right hand."
  * **Gloss:** Mocking the foodless, speechless gods, then the blow — the corpus's lowercase mid-quote at 91 is an extraction artifact (quote as-is).
* **`[37:94]`** "And they came running towards him. **`[37:95]`** He said, “Do you worship what you carve? **`[37:96]`** When Allah created you, and what you manufacture?”"
  * **Gloss:** The run to the scene meets the doctrine: Creator of makers — your carved things are your own manufacture.
* **`[37:97]`** "They said, “Build a pyre for him, and throw him into the furnace.” **`[37:98]`** They wished him ill, but We made them the losers."
  * **Gloss:** The pyre sentence and its reversal (the fire-story's deliverance told in four words; twins at `[29:24]`/`[21:69]`, verified).
* **`[37:99]`** "He said, “I am going towards my Lord, and He will guide me.” **`[37:100]`** “My Lord, give me one of the righteous.” **`[37:101]`** So We gave him good news of a clement boy."
  * **Gloss:** Hijrah-with-hope, the child-petition, and the basharah: "a clement boy" — the same adjective elsewhere a divine name ("the Clement", `[39:53]`, verified).
* **`[37:102]`** "Then, when he was old enough to accompany him, he said, “O My son, I see in a dream that I am sacrificing you; see what you think.” He said, “O my Father, do as you are commanded; you will find me, Allah willing, one of the steadfast.”"
  * **Gloss:** The whole test in one ayah: consultation, not command, from the father; consent, sabr, and "Allah willing" from the son — the surah's submission-scene (corpus-unique "in a dream", verified).
* **`[37:103]`** "Then, when they had submitted, and he put his forehead down. **`[37:104]`** We called out to him, “O Abraham! **`[37:105]`** You have fulfilled the vision.” Thus We reward the doers of good."
  * **Gloss:** Two submissions (the verb is plural) completed the act; the call stops it; the fulfillment is credited, refrain #2 with "doers of good".
* **`[37:106]`** "This was certainly an evident test. **`[37:107]`** And We redeemed him with a great sacrifice."
  * **Gloss:** Named as the manifest trial; redemption by the great sacrifice — corpus says "redeemed", not "ransomed" (task drift noted in index).
* **`[37:108]`** "And We left with him for later generations. **`[37:109]`** Peace be upon Abraham. **`[37:110]`** Thus We reward the doers of good. **`[37:111]`** He was one of Our believing servants."
  * **Gloss:** The cycle's closing seal — mention, salam, refrain, servant-dossier, exactly the Noah kit.
* **`[37:112]`** "And We gave him good news of Isaac, a prophet, one of the righteous. **`[37:113]`** And We blessed him, and Isaac. But among their descendants are some who are righteous, and some who are clearly unjust to themselves."
  * **Gloss:** The second glad-tidings names Isaac as prophet-righteous; the blessing extends, then splits the descendants honestly — lineage does not guarantee taqwa.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`qalb salim`** (sound heart): `[37:84]` — Abraham's entrance credential.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[26:89]` "Except for him who comes to Allah with a sound heart" (verified) — ash-Shu'ara gives the Day's only profitable heart; as-Saffat names its first owner.
* **`halim`** (clement): `[37:101]` "a clement boy".
  * **Rendering:** applied to the promised son; the same root as a divine attribute is "Clement" elsewhere (`[39:53]`, `[16:7]`, `[2:263]`, verified).
* **`sabrun`** (steadfast): `[37:102]` "one of the steadfast" — the son's self-description.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[13:24]` "Peace be upon you, because you endured patiently" (verified) — sabr's greeting, the same salam-sabr pairing as the cycles.
* **`salam`** (Peace be upon…): `[37:79]`, `[37:109]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** within the surah at 120, 130, and generalized at 181 (verified); "among all people" is unique to the Noah formula in the corpus.
