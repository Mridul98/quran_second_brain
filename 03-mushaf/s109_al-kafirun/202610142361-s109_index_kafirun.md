## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Meccan per the classical type-lists; transmitted lists preserve a minority Medinan assignment, and the corpus carries no classification data (header = title/source/basmala only — rg-verified). *Source: classical type-lists — verify.* Revelation order: **transmitted nuzul tables — verify**.
* **Size:** 6 ayahs, 1 ruku (ruku_map global 551 — verified). Opens the Quran's closing six-surah unit (109–114, global ruku 551–556) — the disavowal-and-refuge bracket around the book's end.
* **Title:** al-Kafirun — Itani's header "THE DISBELIEVERS" tracks `[109:1]` "O disbelievers."; the vocative names the *category*, never a person — the exact inverse of `[111:1]`, the only individual *named* in this unit (see s111).
* **Central theme:** A six-line declaration of worship-severance. Two mirrored pairs (2–3 "worship", 4–5 "serve") state what the two parties do NOT share, and v6 partitions the ways: "You have your way, and I have my way." No invective, no argument — pure demarcation; the disavowal (tabarru') formula distilled to its minimum.
* **Reading hint:** the whole surah is ONE utterance in this edition — Itani's quotation marks open at 109:1 and close only at 109:6 (observable typography): the four denials and the parting-line are a single commissioned speech-act launched by "Say".

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Commission + vocative (109:1):** "Say, 'O disbelievers." — address at maximum distance: the other is named only by its rejection.
* **First mirror-pair (109:2–3):** "I do not worship what you worship. / Nor do you worship what I worship." — A→B then B→A; the symmetry freezes both parties' worship as present, settled fact (*Source: structural observation on corpus renderings*).
* **Second mirror-pair (109:4–5):** "Nor do I serve what you serve. / Nor do you serve what I serve." — the same four-beat device re-run with the verb re-rendered ("serve"); Itani does NOT mark a tense-change here (see §5b) — the doubling itself is the emphasis.
* **Partition (109:6):** "You have your way, and I have my way." — the unit's only possessive line; the denial resolves into two owned roads.
* **Unit position:** 109 is the closing cluster's outer disavowal-pole — negative tawhid (whom-not), answered inside the same bracket by 112 (whom) and 113–114 (refuge) (*Source: agent structural inference — verify against the mushaf sequence*).
