## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Meccan per the classical surah-classification lists. Transmitted chronology tables attach one exception-claim — `[53:32]` marked Medinan in some chains (it lands inside ruku 2). *Source: classical lists (tanzil-class tables) — verify in print.*

* **Size:** 62 ayahs, 3 ruku (`data/ruku_map.json`: 53:1–25, 26–32, 33–62 = 25+7+30 = 62 ✓; corpus file ends at `[53:62]` ✓; manifest: "THE STAR"/an-Najm, 62 ✓). Mushaf position 53: the at-Tur (52) folder does not exist at write time (BUILD_STATE: completed through 46; 47–52 never documented — orchestrator gap, flagged, not filled); al-Qamar (54) is created in this same batch and is the surah's true twin [index](../s054_al-qamar/202610142135-s054_index_qamar.md).
* **Name:** an-Najm from the `[53:1]` oath "By the star as it goes down." — singular, at-setting; cf. the other star-oaths `[56:75]` "I swear by the locations of the stars" and `[81:15]`–`[81:16]` "I swear by the galaxies… Precisely running their courses." The oath is the hinge: what falls in the sky is matched to a messenger who does *not* stray (2).
* **Central theme:** The revelation-mechanics surah: an oath, a triple denial ("has not gone astray, nor has he erred… Nor does he speak out of desire"), and a positive claim ("It is but a revelation revealed. Taught to him by the Extremely Powerful," 1–5) — then the vision narrative (6–18): nearness, the horizon, the Lotus Tree, the sight that held steady. The second half audits what people claim for themselves: goddess-names with no authority (19–28), intercession without permission (26), the Scrolls-of-Moses creed ledger of personal responsibility and divine attributes (33–54), and a closing address that ends on a command to prostrate (55–62).
* **Task ledger — proposed verifications checked against the corpus:** the ruku-1 quotes ✓ in substance, ✗ in wording where the task used other translations: `[53:1]` is "By the star as it goes down" (not "by the stars when they set"); `[53:2]` is "Your friend…" (not "your companion… deluded"); `[53:7]` is "the highest horizon" (not "clear horizon"). "desire" in `[53:3]` ("out of desire") is the same Itani word-line as `[45:23]` ("him who has taken his desire for his god") and `[25:43]` ("him who chose his desire as his god") ✓ — and the word recurs inside s053 itself (23, 24, 29). The vision range is `[53:6]`–`[53:10]` (task's "6?–9?" closes at 10: "Then He revealed to His servant what He revealed"). `[53:11]`–`[53:13]` ✓ verbatim-adjacent ("The heart did not lie…"; "another descent"). `[53:14]`–`[53:17]` ✓ with drift: "Lotus Tree of the Extremity" (not "lote-tree of the end"), "Garden of Repose" (not "Refuge"), "The sight did not waver, nor did it exceed" (not "swerve nor transgress"). The responsibility run `[53:38]`–`[53:42]` ✓ — twins `[6:164]`, `[2:286]`, `[35:18]` verified (see ruku 3 note).
* **Correction 1:** the task's "He destroys the first and the last" for `[53:43]` is not this corpus — 43 reads "it is He who causes laughter and weeping"; "gives death and life" (44) is inverted from the task's "gives life and death"; "next existence" (47), not "next creation."
* **Correction 2:** the destruction catalog (Sirius, "the first Aad," "Thamood, sparing no one," Noah "most unjust and most oppressive," "toppled the ruined cities") is `[53:49]`–`[53:54]`, *not* 55–56; `[53:55]` is instead the lone borrowing of ar-Rahman's refrain — "So which of your Lord's marvels can you deny?" ≈ `[55:13]` (×31 there, rg-verified). The close `[53:56]`–`[53:62]` ✓ ("just like the first warnings"; "The inevitable is imminent"; "None besides Allah can unveil it"; "And laugh, and do not weep? / Lost in your frivolity?"; "So bow down to Allah, and worship!"). The task's "far return" phrase matches nothing in this range (closest corpus facts: "finality" 42 ↔ `[79:44]`; "next existence" 47 ↔ `[29:20]`) — logged as other-translation residue.

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Oath → denial → mechanism (1–5):** the going-down star frames a messenger cleared of error, delusion and self-desire; revelation is named as *taught* speech from "the Extremely Powerful."
* **The vision narrative (6–18):** nearness measured ("within two bows' length, or closer"), the highest horizon, the heart that did not lie, a second descent, the Lotus Tree of the Extremity and the Garden of Repose — perception at its limit ("The sight did not waver, nor did it exceed").
* **The counter-cult (19–25):** al-Lat, al-Uzza, Manat — "nothing but names, which you have devised"; the males/females distribution mocked ("What a bizarre distribution"); "Or is the human being to have whatever he desires?" ends ruku 1 on desire again — the ring back to verse 3.
* **The audit of the heavens (26–32):** intercession needs permission; the turners-away from "Our remembrance" who "desire nothing but the present life"; then the wide horizon of "Vast Forgiveness" and the warning against self-acclamation.
* **The ledger scroll (33–47):** "him who turned away… gave a little, and held back" opens the citation of "the Scrolls of Moses… and of Abraham, who fulfilled": no soul bears another's burden, each gets only what he strives for, and the creed run — laughter/weeping, death/life, the pairs, "the next existence."
* **Attributes, destructions, address (48–62):** enricher/impoverisher, "the Lord of Sirius," the roll of the destroyed (first Aad, Thamood, Noah's people, the toppled cities); the borrowed refrain (55); "This is a warning, just like the first warnings" (56); the closing three-question rebuke and "So bow down to Allah, and worship!" (59–62).
