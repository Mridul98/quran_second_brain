# Angels whose intercession avails nothing: permission, assumptions, Vast Forgiveness (53:26–32)
**UID:** 202610142133
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 53:26–32 (Ruku 2 of 3 in an-Najm)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[53:26]` – `[53:32]` (7 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The shortest ruku of the surah runs one wire from heaven's bureaucracy to the human heart: angels are numerous but intercession is useless until Allah "gives permission… and approves" (26); the female-name project is diagnosed as unbelief in the Hereafter misnaming His servants (27); knowledge is replaced by assumptions that "are no substitute for the truth" (28); the turner-away is defined by what he desires (29); then Allah's knowledge re-divides the scene — strayed or guided (30), evil repaid, good rewarded "with the best" (31), and a "Vast Forgiveness" wide enough for minor lapses but hostile to self-acclamation (32).
* **Placement:** The pivot between the vision-defense (ruku 1) and the Scrolls ledger (ruku 3): ruku 1 said revelation comes *by permission through a sent messenger* (`[53:5]`, `[53:10]`); ruku 2 generalizes permission to all heavenly intercession; ruku 3's "no soul bears the burdens of another" (`[53:38]`) is the ledger-version of the same sovereignty.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[53:26]`** "How many an angel is there in the heavens whose intercession avails nothing, except after Allah gives permission to whomever He wills, and approves?"
  * **Gloss:** The one-verse counter to `[53:19]`–`[53:25]`'s named goddesses: rank in heaven buys no leverage; two conditions gate all intercession — His willing permission and His approval. The syntax leaves the goddesses unnamed here on purpose: the angels themselves lose, not just their idols.
* **`[53:27]`** "Those who do not believe in the Hereafter give the angels the names of females."
  * **Gloss:** The etiology of the cult: disbelief in the Hereafter (the same Hereafter whose near-geography was just shown at 15) produces misgendering of God's servants — "give… the names" is a naming-crime, echoing "names, which you have devised" (`[53:23]`).
* **`[53:28]`** "They have no knowledge of that. They only follow assumptions, and assumptions are no substitute for the truth."
  * **Gloss:** Epistemology verdict: *ẓann* (assumption) cannot stand where *ḥaqq* (truth) is required — the ruku's middle hinge, and the surah's second strike on assumption-following (after `[53:23]`).
* **`[53:29]`** "So avoid him who has turned away from Our remembrance, and desires nothing but the present life."
  * **Gloss:** The social command — *avoid*, not coerce — aimed at a type: turner + desire-constrained-to-this-life; "Our remembrance" (dhikr) is the revealed Book itself (kalam register), so the turn is from the address.
* **`[53:30]`** "That is the extent of their knowledge. Your Lord knows best who has strayed from His path, and He knows best who has accepted guidance."
  * **Gloss:** "Their" swings the 29's "him" into a class — the stray/guided sorting re-stages `[2:5–7]`'s census; the double "knows best" reserves the sorting to Allah, deferring the verdict the avoidance-command already enacted socially.
* **`[53:31]`** "To Allah belongs whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on earth. He will repay those who do evil according to their deeds, and recompense those who do good with the best."
  * **Gloss:** Ownership sentence (heavens+earth = the Throne-verse's opening move, cf. `[2:255]`) then the two-track economy: evil repaid *according to* deeds, good recompensed *with the best* — asymmetric grace is the ledger-rule the surah will apply to "him who turned away" in ruku 3.
* **`[53:32]`** "Those who avoid gross sins and indecencies—except for minor lapses—your Lord is of Vast Forgiveness. He knows you well, ever since He created you from the earth, and ever since you were embryos in your mothers’ wombs. So do not acclaim your own virtue; He is fully aware of the righteous."
  * **Gloss:** The mercy clause with its famous exception-dash ("except for minor lapses"), then the origin-memory ("from the earth… embryos") grounding "He knows you well"; the payoff command: no self-acclamation, since Allah is the one "fully aware of the righteous." *Source: 53:32 is the ayah flagged Medinan in some transmitted lists — classical chronology tables; verify in print.*

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`shafā'a` (intercession):** heavenly leverage; negated except by permission.
  * **Rendering:** "whose intercession avails nothing, except after Allah gives permission… and approves" (26).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:255]` "Who is he that can intercede with Him except with His permission?"; `[34:23]` "Intercession with Him is of no value, except for someone He has permitted"; `[39:44]` "All intercession is up to Allah" — the permission-condition cluster (cf. passage note p03905, which already cites `[53:26]`).
* **`ẓann` (assumption/guess):** the failed substitute for truth.
  * **Rendering:** "They only follow assumptions, and assumptions are no substitute for the truth" (28); "nothing but assumptions" (23).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[10:36]` "Most of them follow nothing but assumptions; and assumptions avail nothing against the truth" — near-verbatim twin; `[43:20]` "they are merely guessing" — same diagnosis of the daughters-claim.
* **`dhikr` (remembrance):** here = the revealed message one turns from (29).
  * **Elsewhere:** the ease-refrain's object in `[54:22]` (sibling surah: "Is there anyone who would remember?"); k-kalam's address-register.
* **`al-Maghfirah al-Wāsi'ah` (Vast Forgiveness):** width-language for pardon; hapax rendering in this corpus (rg "Vast Forgiveness": only `[53:32]`).
  * **Elsewhere:** no twin of the phrase-form in the corpus (rg-verified); the gross/minor sins distinction echoes `[4:31]` "If you avoid the worst of what you are forbidden, We will remit your sins".
