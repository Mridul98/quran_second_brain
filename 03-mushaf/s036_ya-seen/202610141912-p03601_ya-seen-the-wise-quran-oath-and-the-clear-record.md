# Ya, Seen — by the Wise Quran: the oath that verifies the messenger, and the Record that tallies everything (36:1–12)
**UID:** 202610141912
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 36:1–12 (Ruku 1 of 5 in Ya-Sin)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[36:1]` – `[36:12]` (12 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah swears by the Wise Quran that the addressee is genuinely a messenger on a straight path, commissioned to warn a warner-less line — then sorts its audience before it argues: most are sealed with the Word already realized against them (7–10), while the warning finds its mark only on the one who fears the Most Gracious inwardly (11), both verdicts resting on the God who revives the dead and has tallied all things in a Clear Record (12).
* **Placement:** Ruku 1 of 5; the surah's oath-and-venue setup. Its 36:7 "The Word" bracket closes at 36:70; its "straight path" (4) brackets with 36:61; its revival-and-writing competence (12) is litigated by the whole surah (33, 49–53, 78–82).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[36:1]`** "Ya, Seen."
  * **Gloss:** Muqatta'at — transliterated letter-names, numbered as ayah 1, deliberately untranslated (style matches `[28:1]` "Ta, Seen, Meem."; see the canonical fleeting link); letters open, and immediately an oath on the Book — the 50:1–2 / 68:1 pattern class.
* **`[36:2]`** "By the Wise Quran."
  * **Gloss:** The oath (wah) that fronts 3–6; "the Wise Quran" is unique in the corpus (rg-verified) — cousin phrases: "the Glorious Quran" `[50:1]`, `[85:21]`; "the Book that makes things clear" `[43:2]`.
* **`[36:3]`** "You are one of the messengers."
  * **Gloss:** The sworn answer to the letters: prophetic verification, plain predicate — the task's "truly" is other editions' amplification, absent here (corpus-verified correction).
* **`[36:4]`** "On a straight path."
  * **Gloss:** Sirat — the Fatihah petition (`[1:6]` "Guide us to the straight path.") spoken of the Messenger himself; re-cited at `[36:61]` as the covenant's own definition (intra-surah bracket).
* **`[36:5]`** "The revelation of the Almighty, the Merciful."
  * **Gloss:** The Book's source-tag (kalam anchor; task's "a revelation from the Mighty" — corpus: "The revelation of the Almighty, the Merciful"); power-name then mercy-name, in that order, framing the warning project as might qualified by rahmah.
* **`[36:6]`** "To warn a people whose ancestors were not warned, and so they are unaware."
  * **Gloss:** The addressee clause: an un-warnered ancestor-line, hence ghaflah (heedlessness) — the Meccan Quraysh claim; Saba's cognate is `[34:44]` "But We gave them no book to study, and We did not send them any warner before you" (verified).
* **`[36:7]`** "The Word was realized against most of them, for they do not believe."
  * **Gloss:** "The Word" (kalima) as settled sentence — divine speech already fact before the fact; bookended at 36:70 "prove the Word against the faithless" (the kalam note's verdict-side).
* **`[36:8]`** "We placed shackles around their necks, up to their chins, so they are stiff-necked."
  * **Gloss:** Guidance- refusal figured as ironwork: yokes welded to chins force the head up — inability to bow; the Fire-scene twin is `[40:71]` "When the yokes are around their necks, and they will be dragged by the chains" (verified).
* **`[36:9]`** "And We placed a barrier in front of them, and a barrier behind them, and We have enshrouded them, so they cannot see."
  * **Gloss:** Blind-boxed front and back — the sealed are not merely unpersuaded but sightless; the same front/behind idiom returns as the warning's content at 36:45 (intra-surah echo). No verbatim twin of this enshrouding line in the corpus (rg-checked).
* **`[36:10]`** "It is the same for them, whether you warn them, or do not warn them—they will not believe."
  * **Gloss:** The warner's function collapse for the sealed; sits in productive tension with 36:6's purpose clause and is re-qualified by 36:11 — warning is never wasted, it just has a hearer-condition.
* **`[36:11]`** "You warn only him who follows the Message, and fears the Most Gracious inwardly. So give him good news of forgiveness, and a generous reward."
  * **Gloss:** The audience filter: dhikr-follower + secret-khashya; "fears the Most Gracious inwardly" is verbatim-twin `[50:33]` (also `[35:18]`); the bishara pair "forgiveness, and a generous reward" rhymes with `[34:4]` "forgiveness, and a generous provision" and the idiom `[57:18]`, `[33:44]`.
* **`[36:12]`** "It is We who revive the dead; and We write down what they have forwarded, and their traces. We have tallied all things in a Clear Record."
  * **Gloss:** The dossier verse: two divine competences — resurrection and bookkeeping — that the surah will spend on proof (33, 51–53) and verdict (47–50, 76–83); "what they have forwarded" twin `[59:18]`, echo `[89:24]`; "Clear Record" twin `[34:3]` (back-cited at `[6:59]`, lowercase there — case drift observable), and p03401 already cites this verse.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`the Wise Quran`**: the oath-object of 36:2.
  * **Rendering:** "By the Wise Quran." — hakam-as-"Wise" applied to the Book; unique collocation in the corpus (rg-verified).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[50:1]` "the Glorious Quran"; `[43:2]` "the Book that makes things clear"; the surah self-describes again at 36:69 "a reminder, and a Clear Quran" — oath-open, definition-close.
* **`sirat (straight path)`**: 36:4, and adjudicated at 36:61.
  * **Rendering:** "On a straight path." / "This is a straight path." — the same phrase bookending the surah's argument.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[1:6]` the petition; `[6:153]` "This is My path, straight, so follow it"; `[3:101]` cleaving to Allah is being "guided to a straight path" (all verified).
* **`Clear Record`**: 36:12's tally of all things.
  * **Rendering:** "We have tallied all things in a Clear Record."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[34:3]` "All are in a Clear Record" (s034's note cross-quotes 36:12 in return); `[6:59]` lowercase "in a clear record" — intra-book case drift; `[45:28]` every community "called to its Book" on the Day.
