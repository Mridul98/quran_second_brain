## 3. Structural Arc
* **Oath and mandate (1–12):** letters → Book-oath → messenger-verification → straight path → revelation-source → the unwarned addressees; then the sealed majority (7–10) contrasted with the one who hears (11), all under the twin divine competences of 12: reviving the dead and writing everything down.
* **The town on trial (13–32):** a parable that is also a mirror — denial (15), claim of human impossibility, stoning threat (18), the running believer's creed-reductio (22–24), instant honor (26–27), a single Cry and silence (28–29), universalized: "Alas for the servants" (30–32).
* **Sign dossiers (33–44):** dead land → gardens → pairs → night/day → sun/moon orbits → the laden Ark and its like; each layer is resurrection-and-providence evidence addressed to "them" (the sealed audience of 7–10 re-argued cosmically).
* **Refusal and its answer [Al Jathiyah 59](../s045_al-jathiyah/202610142041-s045_index_al-jathiyah.md):** the warning-address, the turning-away pattern, the spending mockery, the "when is this promise" taunt — answered by the blast amid the feud, the rush from tombs, the Day's two performative utterances ("Peace—a saying" / "step aside today, you criminals").
* **Covenant interrogation (60–67):** the Adamic covenant cited on the stand, "This is a straight path" closing the bracket opened at 36:4; then the sealing of mouths and the testimony of limbs.
* **The resurrection lawsuit (68–83):** old age reversed, the Book re-claimed as reminder not poetry, cattle subdued and ungrateful idolaters, the bone-question answered from first initiation to "Be," and the closing doxology of dominion and return.
