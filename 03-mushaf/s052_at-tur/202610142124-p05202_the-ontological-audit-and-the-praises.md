# The ontological audit and the praise-hours: the "Or…?" cascade (52:29–49)
**UID:** 202610142124
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 52:29–49 (Ruku 2 of 2 in at-Tur)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[52:29]` – `[52:49]` (21 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** "So remind" — and the surah audits the accusers with a cascade of fifteen "Or…?" questions over twelve verses (poet? madman? fabricator? self-creators? treasury-holders? stairway-eavesdroppers? daughter-fathers? fee-earners? future-scribes? conspirators? rival-gods?): each claim is met not with refutation but with the dare ("produce a discourse like it"), the dilemma (created from nothing? — "they are not certain"), and the discharge ("leave them"), until the surah hands the Prophet the only remaining equipment: patient waiting, since he is "before Our Eyes," and praise at fixed hours — rising, night, and the receding of the stars.
* **Placement:** The audit-half; ruku 1 swore the two verdicts, ruku 2 interrogates the people who found them implausible; its close answers the release-commissions of the neighbor pair (51:54–55; 50:39–40's praise-frame verbatim-kin).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[52:29]`** "So remind. By the grace of your Lord, you are neither a soothsayer, nor a madman."
  * **Gloss:** Commission plus disclaimer in one breath — the same grace-buffer as `[68:2]` "By the grace of your Lord, you are not insane" (near-verbatim twin, rg); "soothsayer" (kahin) occurs elsewhere only at `[69:42]` "Nor is it the speech of a soothsayer" (rg) — the two Quran-self-classification surahs defend the same office.
* **`[52:30–31]`** "Or do they say, “A poet—we await for him a calamity of time”? Say, “Go on waiting; I will be waiting with you.”"
  * **Gloss:** The poet-accusation dossier: `[21:5]` (the triple "jumble of dreams / made it up / a poet"), `[37:36]` "a mad poet", `[69:41]`, `[36:69]` "We did not teach him poetry" (all rg); "calamity of time" (dayh al-dahr — fortune's circling) is this edition's only such phrase (rg); the mutual-wait refrain: `[7:71]`, `[9:52]`, `[10:20]`, `[10:102]`, `[32:30]` "and wait. They too are waiting" (rg).
* **`[52:32]`** "Or is it that their dreams compel them to this? Or are they aggressive people?"
  * **Gloss:** Two more "Or"s mid-verse: the ahlam-family of `[21:5]`'s "jumble of dreams" (rg); "aggressive" kin to `[50:25]` "aggressor" and `[49:9]` "aggressing group" (rg; root-kinship = classical lexicography — *Source: verify*).
* **`[52:33–34]`** "Or do they say, “He made it up”? Rather, they do not believe. So let them produce a discourse like it, if they are truthful."
  * **Gloss:** "Made it up" cluster: `[32:3]`, `[21:5]`, `[11:35]`, `[46:8]` "He invented it himself" (rg); the production-dare family: `[10:38]` "produce a single chapter like it", `[11:13]` "ten chapters", `[17:88]` "produce the like of this Quran", and their counter-claim `[8:31]` "Had we wanted, we could have said the like of this" (rg) — ruku 1 swore by the inscribed Book (52:2–3); here the Book's literacy is put under challenge: k-kalam's two halves.
* **`[52:35–36]`** "Or were they created out of nothing? Or are they the creators? Or did they create the heavens and the earth? In fact, they are not certain."
  * **Gloss:** THE dilemma: no self-origination, no self-creation, no cosmos-building — and the verdict names not their error but their epistemic state: "they are not certain" (task's "they have no certainty" ✓ other edition); concept-kin: `[19:9]` "I created you before, when you were nothing" and `[76:1]` "a period of time when he was nothing to be mentioned?" (rg); within the surah-pair, the heavens they cannot claim are the ones 51:47 swore it built "with power", and the yaqin-logic runs 51:20 "signs for the convinced" → 51:23's speech-yardstick → this verse's privation; the syllogistic reading of these three "Or"s is classical kalām territory (*Source: classical tafsir — verify*).
* **`[52:37]`** "Or do they possess the treasuries of your Lord? Or are they the controllers?"
  * **Gloss:** Treasuries-twin `[38:9]` near-verbatim "Or do they possess the treasuries of the mercy of your Lord—the Majestic, the Giver?" (rg; cf. `[6:50]`, `[17:100]`); "controllers" (musallitūn) — corpus-unique here (rg): gatekeeping revelation is claimed by people who own neither the treasury nor the turnstile.
* **`[52:38]`** "Or do they have a stairway by means of which they listen? Then let their listener produce a clear proof."
  * **Gloss:** The eavesdropping dossier: `[37:8]` "They cannot eavesdrop on the Supernal Elite" (+ 37:6–7 the guarding, `[15:18]` the stolen hearing and projectile, `[67:5]` lanterns-as-missiles, `[72:1]` the jinn band that "listened in" — all rg); stairways-to-heaven: `[6:35]`, `[43:33]` (rg); the burden-shifting "clear proof" formula mirrors `[37:156]` "Or do you have some clear proof?" (rg) in the very same argument-cluster.
* **`[52:39]`** "Or for Him the daughters, and for you the sons?"
  * **Gloss:** The daughters-attribution dossier: `[37:149]` near-verbatim "Are the daughters for your Lord, while for them the sons?" (+ 37:150), the next surah's `[53:21–22]` "Are you to have the males, and He the females? What a bizarre distribution", `[16:57]`, `[43:16]`, `[6:100]` (all rg) — the calumny is audited across three adjacent Meccan surahs.
* **`[52:40]`** "Or do you demand a payment from them, and they are burdened by debt?"
  * **Gloss:** `[68:46]` near-verbatim twin "Or do you ask them for a fee, so they are burdened with debt?" (rg); the accusation imagines precisely what the prophets' own formula disclaims — "I ask of you no payment" `[25:57]`, `[26:109]`+ (rg) — the k-infraq axis: revelation is not a business.
* **`[52:41]`** "Or do they know the future, and they are writing it down?"
  * **Gloss:** "The future" (ghayb) is God's monopoly: `[27:65]` "No one in the heavens or on earth knows the future except Allah", `[6:50]`, `[3:179]` (rg); the sneer-clause "and they are writing it down" — the corpus's other "writing it down" is `[21:94]`, where WE record the believer's righteous deed (rg): they claim to transcribe what only the Recorder writes.
* **`[52:42]`** "Or are they planning a conspiracy? The conspiracy will befall the disbelievers."
  * **Gloss:** The recoil-law of plot: twin `[35:43]` "evil scheming overwhelms none but its authors" (rg); the word "conspiracy" elsewhere only at `[7:123]` (Pharaoh's accusation — rg); within the pair: 51:53 "Did they recommend it to one another?" raises the same coordination-suspicion (rg).
* **`[52:43]`** "Or do they have a god besides Allah? Allah transcends what they associate."
  * **Gloss:** The audit's terminal question — the last "Or" is k-tawhid's own formula; "Allah transcends what they associate" in this exact wording is corpus-unique (rg; subhan-exaltations recur in variant phrasings); the cascade bottoms out: behind every question that came before it stands one rival.
* **`[52:44]`** "Even if they were to see lumps of the sky falling down, they would say, “A mass of clouds.”"
  * **Gloss:** Sky-fall threat-twin `[34:9]` "make pieces of the sky fall down on them" (rg); "a mass of clouds" — corpus-unique (rg); the hardened optic: even the sign they demanded (v.38's stairway, 6:35's tunnel) would be re-classified on arrival — the "elevated roof" of 52:5 becomes the thing they would misread.
* **`[52:45–47]`** "So leave them until they meet their Day in which they will be stunned. The Day when their ploys will avail them nothing; and they will not be helped. For those who do wrong, there is a punishment besides that; but most of them do not know."
  * **Gloss:** The discharge: "leave them" joins 51:54 "turn away… not to blame", `[50:45]` "you are not a dictator over them", `[88:22]` "You have no control over them" (all rg — the release-formula of three neighbor surahs); "stunned" occurs elsewhere only at `[39:68]`'s trumpet scene (rg); "ploys" corpus-unique (rg); "a punishment besides that" — the phrase "besides that" carries the opposite sign at `[48:27]` "granted besides that an imminent victory" (rg); the classical reading: a barzakh-punishment before the promised Day (*Source: classical tafsir on 52:47 — verify*); "most of them do not know" — the corpus's stock refrain: `[16:101]` ("You are an impostor." But most of them do not know"), `[27:61]`, `[39:29]`, `[28:13]` (rg).
* **`[52:48]`** "So patiently await the decision of your Lord, for you are before Our Eyes; and proclaim the praises of your Lord when you arise."
  * **Gloss:** The patient-await twins near-verbatim: `[76:24]` "So be patient for the decision of your Lord", `[68:48]` "So wait patiently for the Decision of your Lord" (rg); "before Our Eyes" occurs elsewhere only of the Ark: `[54:14]` "Sailing before Our eyes" (rg) — the watched vessel as type of the watched warner; k-sabr's command-side, replacing the Fire's worthless patience (52:16).
* **`[52:49]`** "And glorify Him during the night, and at the receding of the stars."
  * **Gloss:** The praise-hours frame: `[50:39–40]` is the near-duplicate ("proclaim the praises of your Lord before the rising of the sun… glorify Him during the night, and at the end of devotions" — rg); also `[20:130]`, `[76:25–26]`, `[17:79]` (rg); "receding" corpus-unique (rg) — the task's "setting of the stars" is the next surah's phrasing, `[53:1]` "By the star as it goes down" (rg; cf. `[56:75]` "the locations of the stars"); what the dubbar-hour precisely is — classical lexicography (*Source: verify*). The surah whose oaths swore an unavertible punishment (52:7–8) ends by giving the Prophet a timetable: the counter-move to terror is doxology at fixed hours.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **am — the "Or…?" audit-particle:** 15 "Or"-clauses across 12 verses here (30, 32×2, 33, 35×2, 36, 37×2, 38, 39, 40, 41, 42, 43 — rg count); ten read "Or do they/Or are they".
  * **Rendering:** Itani keeps the interrogation bare — no conjunction-gloss; the s052 index's earlier "nine-question" count is corrected to this rg census.
  * **Elsewhere:** the cascade recurs at `[68:46–47]`: "Or do you ask them for a fee, so they are burdened with debt? Or do they know the future, and so they write it down?" — the fee- and future-questions of this ruku have verbatim-kin there (rg).
* **ujra — "payment/fee":** 52:40 "demand a payment… burdened by debt."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[68:46]` verbatim twin; the prophets' refrain `[25:57]`, `[26:109]`+ (rg) — see k-infraq's no-payment thesis.
* **burhan — "clear proof":** 52:38 "let their listener produce a clear proof."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[37:156]` same dare in the same daughters-cluster; the proof-demand corpus: `[14:10]`, `[18:15]`, `[7:85]` (rg).
* **tasbih — "proclaim the praises… glorify":** 52:48–49.
  * **Rendering:** two verbs, one command-hour-list; "receding of the stars" — unique English collocation (rg).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[50:39–40]` near-verbatim twin; `[20:130]`, `[76:26]` (rg) — the brain's praise-hours file.
