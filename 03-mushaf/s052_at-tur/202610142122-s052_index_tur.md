Burn in it — patience or impatience is the same," 9–16) and the righteous' Garden-inventory (gardens, bliss, luxurious furnishings, gorgeous spouses, reunited offspring, harm-free cups, pearl-like pages — and the saved ones' gratitude testimony, 17–28). Ruku 2 is the audit of the accusers in a chain of "Or do they...?" questions — soothsayer? madman? poet? fabricator? self-creators? treasury-holders? stairway-eavesdroppers? — answered by the ontological dilemmas (created out of nothing? not certain?) and closed with the Prophet's release: patiently await, for you are before Our Eyes, and praise at the rising, the night, and the receding of the stars (29–49).

## 3. Structural Arc
* **The sworn certainty (1–8):** five oaths (mount, book, scroll, house, roof, sea) → the punishment "is coming," unavertible — the Book-in-the-oath (2–3) previews ruku 2's revelation-audits (34).
* **The Day's landscape (9–12):** heaven swaying, mountains in motion, woe to the deniers, "Those who play with speculation" — the 51:10 imposter-family in the next surah's same register (rg-verified lexical kin).
* **Fire-interrogation (13–16):** shoved in; "This is the Fire which you used to deny. Is this magic, or do you not see?"; patience-or-not equals; repayment-for-what-you-used-to-do.
* **Garden-inventory (17–28):** gardens and bliss; spared the suffering; the food-and-drink blessing; furnishings and spouses; the offspring-reunion and the hostage clause ("Every person is hostage to what he has earned" — 74:38 verbatim twin); fruit, meat, harm-free cups, pearl-pages, mutual inquiry; the gratitude testimony that ends with "He is the Good, the Compassionate."
* **The audit (29–49):** "So remind" — neither soothsayer, madman, poet, nor fabricator; produce a discourse like it (17:88 kin); the fifteen-clause "Or…?" cascade over 12 verses (30, 32×2, 33, 35×2, 36, 37×2, 38, 39, 40, 41, 42, 43 — rg census; the earlier "nine-question" tally was an undercount, corrected in p05202 §3); sky-lumps dismissed as "a mass of clouds"; leave them to the stunned Day; a punishment besides that for the wrongdoers; the Prophet's discharge (patiently awaiting, "before Our Eyes") and the praise-hours close.
