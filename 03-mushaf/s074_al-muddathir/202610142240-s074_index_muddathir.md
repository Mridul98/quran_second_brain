# Surah 74: THE ENROBED (al-Muddathir)
**UID:** 202610142240
**Type:** Index / Surah Map

## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Meccan — an agreed case in the type-lists; revelation order 5 in the transmitted lists (paired with 73 as among the very first) — *Source: classical type-lists and revelation-order lists — verify against a printed table.* The unnumbered Basmala heads the surah.
* **Size:** 56 ayahs, 2 ruku; the longest surah of the juz's opening block, and the twin-commission surah to 73 ("Enrobed" vs "Enwrapped" — verified address-pair).
* **Central theme:** From installation to interrogation. Seven imperative commands mobilize the Enrobed one (74:1–7); the Trumpet Day splits the scene (74:8–10); one rich man is tried as a case-study in self-attribution (74:11–25), his verdict Saqar with nineteen over it (74:26–31) and the number itself declared a trial (74:31); the right-people in Gardens interrogate the criminals for the four-failure catalogue (74:40–48); and the present crowd flees the Reminder like donkeys (74:49–53) into the will-cluster close: "He is the Source of Righteousness, and the Source of Forgiveness" (74:56).

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Mobilization (74:1–7):** arise and warn; magnify the Lord; purify garments; flee the filth; do not give to gain more; be constant — mission, creed, purity, economy, stamina.
* **The Day arrives (74:8–10):** Trumpet blown; "a difficult day… not easy for the disbelievers."
* **The case-study (74:11–25):** "Leave Me to him whom I created alone" — wealth, witness-children, smoothness, greed for more, stubbornness, the seven-beat analysis scene, and the two counter-claims: "magic from the past… the word of a mortal."
* **Saqar and the number (74:26–31):** neither leaves nor spares; nineteen wardens, angels only; the number a stumbling-block with four declared effects; "None knows the soldiers of your Lord except He."
* **The great oath + hostage-law (74:32–39):** moon, retreating night, lighting dawn; "Every soul is hostage to what it has earned — except for those on the Right."
* **The catalogue (74:40–48):** Gardens interrogating the criminals; four failures (prayer, feeding the destitute, indulging with the indulgent, denying the Day) + intercession worthless.
* **The present scene (74:49–56):** panicked donkeys from a lion; every one wants unrolled scrolls; the Reminder stands; will belongs to Allah; Lord of taqwa and of ghufran.
