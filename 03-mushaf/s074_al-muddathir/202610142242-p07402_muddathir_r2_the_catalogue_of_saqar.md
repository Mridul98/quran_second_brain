# The catalogue of Saqar: hostage, four failures, and the Lord of taqwa and forgiveness (74:32–56)
**UID:** 202610142242
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 74:32–56 (Ruku 2 of 2 in al-Muddathir)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[74:32]`–`[74:56]` (25 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Sealed by three time-oaths, the surah states the constitutional law — every soul is hostage to what it has earned, except the people on the Right — then stages the interrogation scene whose answer is the four-failure catalogue (no prayer, no feeding the destitute, indulgence with the indulgent, denial of the Day) and the bankruptcy of intercession; finally the present crowd, fleeing the Reminder like donkeys from a lion while each demands unrolled scrolls, is answered by the will-cluster and the twin-title close: the Source of Righteousness and the Source of Forgiveness.
* **Placement:** Closing ruku; its catalogue is the juz's negative mirror — 51:17–19 and 76:8–12 (verified) are the same four deeds performed successfully, and 69:33–44/26:94–102 are its confession-family.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[74:32]`** "Nay! By the moon." **`[74:33]`** "And the night as it retreats." **`[74:34]`** "And the morning as it lights up."
  * **Gloss:** triple time-oath — twin of 81:15–18 "by the night as it recedes… by the morn as it breathes" (verified); the surah's own night–dawn axis closes the ruku-pair's opening (74:2 "Arise").
* **`[74:35]`** "It is one of the greatest." **`[74:36]`** "A warning to the mortals." **`[74:37]`** "To whomever among you wishes to advance, or regress."
  * **Gloss:** "It" — Saqar or the Trumpet Day: classical reference disputed — verify; "wishes to advance, or regress" = the will-grammar twin of 81:28 "whoever of you wills to go straight" (verified).
* **`[74:38]`** "Every soul is hostage to what it has earned." **`[74:39]`** "Except for those on the Right."
  * **Gloss:** the ransom-law (rahn); exact twin verified: 52:21 "Every person is hostage to what he has earned" — placed inside a Garden scene, exactly as here — the task's "52:??" question resolves at 52:21.
* **`[74:40]`** "In Gardens, inquiring." **`[74:41]`** "About the guilty." **`[74:42]`** "“What drove you into Saqar?”"
  * **Gloss:** the interrogation inverts privilege: the saved ask, the lost confess; the name Saqar returns (74:26/27 → 74:42, verified) one rung below its dossier.
* **`[74:43]`** "They will say, “We were not of those who prayed."
  * **Gloss:** Failure #1 — salah: the same deficit the wrapped-surahs answered (73:20 "observe the prayers"; 2:45/2:153 prayer-with-sabr, cited in k-sabr).
* **`[74:44]`** "Nor did we feed the destitute."
  * **Gloss:** Failure #2 — it'am al-miskin; twin verdict 69:34 "Nor would he advocate the feeding of the destitute" (verified); positive twins 51:19 "a share for the beggar and the deprived" and 76:8 "they feed… the poor, and the orphan, and the captive" (verified) — the k-infraq counter-case at the level of cash-flow.
* **`[74:45]`** "And we used to indulge with those who indulge."
  * **Gloss:** Failure #3 — the vain-indulgence verb: direct twin verified at 9:69, "And you indulged, as they indulged" (khawd-family address to the hypocrites — same surah as the k-nifaq dossier's 63 cousin); the positive-space command uses a different root, laghw: 25:72 "when they come across indecencies, they pass by with dignity" (verified — root-shift flagged).
* **`[74:46]`** "And we used to deny the Day of Judgment." **`[74:47]`** "Until the Inevitable came upon us.”"
  * **Gloss:** Failure #4 — takdhib al-din, the root of 43–45; "the Inevitable" = yaqin (death-certainty), rendered "certainty" at 15:99 "worship your Lord in order to attain certainty" (verified drift).
* **`[74:48]`** "But the intercession of intercessors will not help them."
  * **Gloss:** the mediation-economy voided — twins 26:100 "Now we have no intercessors" and 2:48/2:123/2:254, 39:44, 53:26 (verified cluster) — tawhid's courtroom exclusion.
* **`[74:49]`** "Why are they turning away from the Reminder?" **`[74:50]`** "As though they were panicked donkeys." **`[74:51]`** "Fleeing from a lion?"
  * **Gloss:** the present scene mirrors the interrogated scene; donkey-simile (cf. 31:19 "the most repulsive of voices is the donkey's voice" — verified, different use); lion corpus-unique here (verified).
* **`[74:52]`** "Yet every one of them desires to be given scrolls unrolled." **`[74:53]`** "No indeed! But they do not fear the Hereafter."
  * **Gloss:** scroll-demand maximalism — each wants his own delivered scripture; refusal-twins: 20:133 "Were they not given enough miracles in the former scriptures?" and 6:91 "You put it on scrolls, displaying them, yet concealing much" (verified) — the same people who received the Reminder want a bigger channel (Source: agent reading).
* **`[74:54]`** "Nevertheless, it is a reminder." **`[74:55]`** "So whoever wills, shall remember it."
  * **Gloss:** the Reminder stands regardless; will-cluster site (73:19; 76:29; 81:27–28 — verified).
* **`[74:56]`** "But they will not remember, unless Allah wills. He is the Source of Righteousness, and the Source of Forgiveness."
  * **Gloss:** the exception-clause (twin of 76:30 "you cannot will, unless Allah wills" — verified) lands on the two titles the catalogue failed to earn — and the welding of taqwa + maghfira is exactly 3:133's "race towards forgiveness from your Lord, and a Garden… prepared for the righteous" (verified); the juz's first surah's ghaffar (71:10) closes the round (Source: agent structural observation — verify).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`rahn` (hostage):** 74:38 ↔ 52:21 (verified exact-twin pair; two sites for the English "hostage" corpus-wide by this search).
* **`khawd` (vain indulgence):** 74:45 ↔ 9:69 "And you indulged, as they indulged" (verified English twin); positive-space commands use laghw instead (25:72 — verified root-shift; "vain talk" family, two Arabic words — verify).
* **`yaqin` (the Inevitable):** 74:47 ↔ 15:99 "certainty" (verified drift).
* **`shafa'a` (intercession):** 74:48 — cluster 2:48/2:123/2:254, 26:100, 39:44, 53:26 (all verified) — k-tawhid's intercessors-as-friends dossier (39:3 cited in the note).
* **`rabbi'l-taqwa wa rabbi'l-ghufran`:** "the Source of Righteousness, and the Source of Forgiveness" — the English "Source of" occurs only here (verified); Itani renders rabb as "Source" in this title-pair (observable choice).
