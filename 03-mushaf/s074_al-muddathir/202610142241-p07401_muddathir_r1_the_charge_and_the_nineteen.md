# Arise and warn — and the nineteen: the charge, the case, the number (74:1–31)
**UID:** 202610142241
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 74:1–31 (Ruku 1 of 2 in al-Muddathir)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[74:1]`–`[74:31]` (31 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The Enrobed one is mobilized in seven commands; the Trumpet Day is announced; then one man is put on trial as the case-study of self-attribution — created alone, loaded with wealth and witness-children, demanding more, reasoning, sneering "magic… the word of a mortal" — and his sentence opens Saqar's file: nineteen wardens, angels only, the number declared a deliberate trial, "None knows the soldiers of your Lord except He."
* **Placement:** Ruku 1 of the twin-commission pair (73:1–4 at night; 74:1–7 at dawn); its kalam-counter-claim (74:24–25) is the surah-pair's central dispute, and its sickness-clause (74:31) is already harvested by the k-nifaq dossier (verified two-way door).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[74:1]`** "O you Enrobed one."
  * **Gloss:** the twin address of `[73:1]` "O you Enwrapped one" — the two wrapped-surahs open their commissions back to back (verified pair).
* **`[74:2]`** "Arise and warn." **`[74:3]`** "And magnify your Lord."
  * **Gloss:** mission (nadhir — the verb of 71:1 "Warn your people," verified chain) followed by creed: magnify (kabir) the Lord — worship-space cleared before speech begins.
* **`[74:4]`** "And purify your clothes." **`[74:5]`** "And abandon abominations."
  * **Gloss:** purity outer then inner; "abominations" = rijs, the same word as 5:90 "abominations of Satan's doing" and 22:30 "the abomination of idols" (verified) — garments and gods both to be cleansed.
* **`[74:6]`** "And show no favor seeking gain." **`[74:7]`** "And be constant for your Lord."
  * **Gloss:** the economy command: no transactional giving (the riba-instinct in charity-form — k-infraq's counter-thesis); then stamina: "be constant" (sabr imperative, standard lexicon correspondence — verify) for His Lord's sake, not yours.
* **`[74:8]`** "When the Trumpet is blown." **`[74:9]`** "That Day will be a difficult day." **`[74:10]`** "For the disbelievers—not easy."
  * **Gloss:** the Day dropped into the commission; Trumpet-twins 39:68, 50:20, 78:18, 27:87, 20:102 (verified cluster); "difficult" answered by 76:10–11 "a frowning grim Day… Allah will protect them" (verified mirror).
* **`[74:11]`** "Leave Me to him whom I created alone." **`[74:12]`** "And gave him vast wealth." **`[74:13]`** "And children as witnesses."
  * **Gloss:** divine first-person case-taking (cluster: 73:11, 68:44, 40:26 — verified); the gifts inventoried — "vast wealth," "children as witnesses" (this edition's rendering; source-gloss disputed — verify).
* **`[74:14]`** "And smoothed things for him." **`[74:15]`** "Then he wants Me to add yet more!" **`[74:16]`** "By no means! He was stubborn towards Our revelations."
  * **Gloss:** grace heaped, greed inferred, "more" demanded — 104:2–3's twin fantasy "thinking that his wealth has made him immortal" (verified); the verdict: stubbornness against ayat.
* **`[74:17]`** "I will exhaust him increasingly." **`[74:18]`** "He thought and analyzed."
  * **Gloss:** the ascent-verb (salq — "exhaust him increasingly") answers his self-elevation; then the inner scene opens: he thought, he sized the matter up.
* **`[74:19]`** "May he perish, how he analyzed." **`[74:20]`** "Again: may he perish, how he analyzed."
  * **Gloss:** the doubled imprecation — the labor of rejection marked twice.
* **`[74:21]`** "Then he looked." **`[74:22]`** "Then he frowned and whined." **`[74:23]`** "Then he turned back and was proud."
  * **Gloss:** the seven-beat portrait concludes in body-language: glance, scowl, retreat, kibir — reception of kalam as a facial expression.
* **`[74:24]`** "And said, “This is nothing but magic from the past." **`[74:25]`** "This is nothing but the word of a mortal."
  * **Gloss:** the two counter-claims — sihr mastur, qawl al-bashar; the answer-site is 69:40–43 "It is the speech of a noble messenger… And it is not the speech of a poet… Nor is it the speech of a soothsayer… It is a revelation from the Lord of the Worlds" (verified; k-kalam's own 26:192–193 is its twin), and 73:5 re-asserts the qawl's true weight: "a heavy message."
* **`[74:26]`** "I will roast him in Saqar." **`[74:27]`** "But what will explain to you what Saqar is?"
  * **Gloss:** the name Saqar stands (untranslated — observable translator choice); its only other corpus site is 54:48 "Taste the touch of Saqar" (verified).
* **`[74:28]`** "It neither leaves, nor spares." **`[74:29]`** "It scorches the flesh."
  * **Gloss:** two negations then one burn — totality stated in negatives before the numbers appear.
* **`[74:30]`** "Over it are Nineteen."
  * **Gloss:** the most compressed sentence in the surah; the capitalized "Nineteen" is this edition's styling (observable).
* **`[74:31]`** "We have appointed only angels to be wardens of the Fire, and caused their number to be a stumbling block for those who disbelieve; so that those given the Scripture may attain certainty; and those who believe may increase in faith; and those given the Scripture and the believers may not doubt; and those in whose hearts is sickness and the unbelievers may say, “What did Allah intend by this parable?” Thus Allah leads astray whom He wills, and guides whom He wills. None knows the soldiers of your Lord except He. This is nothing but a reminder for the mortals."
  * **Gloss:** the number dossier — text-only: wardens are angels alone; the number is made "a stumbling block for those who disbelieve" (Itani's English; standard fitna-equivalence — verify) with four declared effects (People of the Scripture attain certainty; believers increase; neither doubt; the sick-hearted and the unbelievers ask "what parable?"); "soldiers" (junud) is verified as a two-site cluster with 26:95 "the soldiers of Satan" — the only "soldiers" renderings corpus-wide; the will-clause lands inside the punishment file — guidance is Allah's prerogative (k-guidance's point). Classical expansions (the warden angels' names; the ten-for-one boast) are transmitted — verify.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`rijs` (abominations):** 74:5 ↔ 5:90, 22:30 (verified cluster) — filth as creed-word, paired with garment-purity.
* **`Saqar`:** 74:26/27/42 + 54:48 (verified — four corpus sites total, only these two surahs; transliterated, not translated — translator's term choice, observable).
* **`qawl`:** 74:25 "the word of a mortal" vs 73:5 "a heavy message" (verified) — one noun, two theologies.
* **`junud` (soldiers):** 74:31 ↔ 26:95 "soldiers of Satan" (verified) — the unseen order sized only by God.
* **`trial/stumbling block`:** 74:31 "caused their number to be a stumbling block" — the only "stumbling block" rendering in the corpus (verified); standard equivalence = fitna (trial) — *Source: lexical correspondence — verify*.
