# Abraham's House, Abraham's prayer for a messenger (2:122–129)
**UID:** 202610140925
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 2:122–129 (Ruku 15 of 40 in al-Baqarah)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[2:122]` – `[2:129]` (8 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The Israel-address gets one last reprise of favor-and-Day (2:122–123), then the camera lifts to Abraham: tested by words and appointed leader of humanity — with the pledge's fine print, "My pledge does not include the wrongdoers" — then the House established as focal point and sanctuary, and the two builders' four-part prayer: acceptance, submission, shown rites, and a raised messenger from among them, who recites, teaches, and purifies.
* **Placement:** The hinge from Israel to Islam as Abrahamic inheritance: the surah transfers covenant-vocabulary (pledge, words, House) from lineage to obedience; 2:129's prayer is quoted back to the community as *answered* in 2:151 — this ruku is the application, ruku 18 the acceptance letter.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[2:122]`** "O Children of Israel! Remember My blessing which I bestowed upon you, and that I have favored you over all people."
  * **Gloss:** The third round of "remember My blessing" (2:40, 2:47, 2:122) — same frame, now "all people" rather than "all nations" (Source: observable variant across the three addresses).
* **`[2:123]`** "And beware of a Day when no soul will avail another soul in any way, and no ransom will be accepted from it, and no intercession will benefit it, and they will not be helped."
  * **Gloss:** The Day-fine-print, near-verbatim reprise of 2:48 — ransom and intercession both barred; the frame before Abraham means *fatherhood will not save either* (→ 2:124's clause).
* **`[2:124]`** "And when his Lord tested Abraham with certain words, and he fulfilled them. He said, 'I am making you a leader of humanity.' He said, 'And my descendants?' He said, 'My pledge does not include the wrongdoers.'"
  * **Gloss:** The scene in five beats: test, words, fulfillment, appointment, and the asked-about heirs — with the verdict that the pledge "does not include" the wrongdoers: the covenant-pledge (*mithāq* — see the covenant note) runs past lineage to moral state; Abraham, who "fulfilled" the words, is the anti-type of 2:27's covenant-violators.
* **`[2:125]`** "And We made the House a focal point for the people, and a sanctuary. Use the shrine of Abraham as a place of prayer. And We commissioned Abraham and Ishmael, 'Sanctify My House for those who circle around it, and those who seclude themselves in it, and those who kneel and prostrate.'"
  * **Gloss:** House = "focal point" for the people (a return-place) plus sanctuary; prayer-station attached to Abraham; four worship postures listed for its upkeep: circling (*ṭawāf*), seclusion (*ʿākif*), kneeling, prostrating — the whole liturgy of 2:43 ("kneel with those who kneel") founds here.
* **`[2:126]`** "When Abraham said, 'O My Lord, make this a peaceful land, and provide its people with fruits—whoever of them believes in Allah and the Last Day.' He said, 'And whoever disbelieves, I will give him a little enjoyment, then I will consign him to the punishment of the Fire; how miserable the destiny!'"
  * **Gloss:** The prayer for security and fruit; God's answer adds the open list: provision extends to disbelievers too, briefly — and the "whoever believes in Allah and the Last Day" criterion of 2:62 re-appears *inside Abraham's prayer*.
* **`[2:127]`** "As Abraham raises the foundations of the House, together with Ishmael, 'Our Lord, accept it from us, You are the Hearer, the Knower."
  * **Gloss:** The raising with the son; the prayer's first word is acceptance — works offered to the *Hearer* (the name-pair Hearer/Knower frames the whole petition-sequence).
* **`[2:128]`** "Our Lord, and make us submissive to You, and from our descendants a community submissive to You. And show us our rites, and accept our repentance. You are the Acceptor of Repentance, the Merciful."
  * **Gloss:** Four requests: personal submission, a submitting descendant-community, *shown rites* (manāsik — the liturgy taught, not invented), repentance accepted — the Tawwāb/Raḥīm name-pair from 2:37 returns, the Adam-cadence inside Abraham's mouth.
* **`[2:129]`** "Our Lord, and raise up among them a messenger, of themselves, who will recite to them Your revelations, and teach them the Book and wisdom, and purify them. You are the Almighty, the Wise."
  * **Gloss:** The climax-petition: a messenger *from their own*, with a four-function brief — recite, teach Book, teach wisdom, purify; the surah will later address the audience of this exact prayer (2:151: "Just as We sent to you a messenger from among you...").

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`bait`** (the House): 2:125 "We made the House a focal point", 2:127 "the foundations of the House".
  * **Elsewhere:** `[3:97]` "Pilgrimage to the House is a duty to Allah" and its "the Station of Abraham" — vs 2:125's "the shrine of Abraham": one Arabic phrase (*maqām Ibrāhīm*), two English renderings (Source: refs verified via corpus; drift observable).
  * **Within surah:** `[2:158]` "Whoever makes the Pilgrimage to the House" (forward, ruku 19) — the word stays "House" through the Hajj sections (Source: observable).
* **`kalimāt`** (words): 2:124 "tested Abraham with certain words."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:37]` "Adam received words from his Lord" — the two "words-scenes" of the surah's proto-history: Adam receives words and repents; Abraham fulfills words and is made leader (Source: observable pair, verified).
* **`imām`** (leader): 2:124 "I am making you a leader of humanity."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[28:5]` "to make them leaders" (the oppressed made *aʾimmah*) — same word, now granted to Abraham personally (Source: ref verified via corpus).
* **`ḥikmah`** (wisdom): 2:129 "teach them the Book and wisdom."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:251]` "Allah gave him sovereignty and wisdom" (David); `[2:269]` "He gives wisdom to whomever He wills" (forward — the surah's own definition: "Whoever is given wisdom has been given much good"); `[5:110]`, `[62:2]` — the Book+wisdom teaching-pair (Source: refs verified; 62:2 and 5:110 confirmed via corpus).

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Medinan; Abraham's association with the Kaʿbah is the assumption on which the later qiblah-material (2:144) stands — this ruku installs the Abrahamic claim *before* the direction-change uses it (Source: observable sequencing; classical tafsir reads it likewise, Ibn Kathir on 2:125–127).
* **Asbab / narrative context:** the "certain words" of 2:124 are enumerated variously in reports (rites of ḥajj, circumcision, the five virtues of 2:177-style conduct...) — transmitted disagreement, none in-text (Source: named disagreement, Ibn Kathir/al-Tabari on 2:124).
