# The ten-clause covenant, and believing in half the book (2:83–86)
**UID:** 202610140920
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 2:83–86 (Ruku 10 of 40 in al-Baqarah)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[2:83]` – `[2:86]` (4 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The covenant text itself — one breath of ten obligations from pure monotheism to praying and giving alms — then the indictment in the same breath: you shed your own blood, expel your own, conspire against them, and ransom captives you were forbidden to free; the verdict names the disease: "you believe in part of the Scripture, and disbelieve in part."
* **Placement:** The legal heart of the Israel-section: 2:83 is the surah's fullest covenant-clause list (quoted verbatim in the Covenant knowledge note); the "part-belief" charge of 2:85 becomes the surah's permanent warning against selective revelation-handling, echoing into 2:89–91.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[2:83]`** "We made a covenant with the Children of Israel: 'Worship none but Allah; and be good to parents, and relatives, and orphans, and the needy; and speak nicely to people; and pray regularly, and give alms.' Then you turned away, except for a few of you, recanting."
  * **Gloss:** Vertical clause (worship alone), horizontal clauses (kin, orphans, needy, speech), ritual clauses (prayer, alms) — then the reflex: turning away, a few excepted.
* **`[2:84]`** "And We made a covenant with you: 'You shall not shed the blood of your own, nor shall you evict your own from your homes.' You agreed, and were all witnesses."
  * **Gloss:** A prior, specific clause about brotherhood's blood and homes, with the audience sworn as witnesses — covenant with attestation.
* **`[2:85]`** (clause-chunk a) "But here you are, killing your own, and expelling a group of your own from their homes—conspiring against them in wrongdoing and hostility."
  * **Gloss:** The 2:84 clause violated point-by-point, plus the aggravator: conspiracy (*ithm* and *ʿudwān* — "wrongdoing and hostility").
* **`[2:85]`** (clause-chunk b) "And if they come to you as captives, you ransom them, although it was forbidden to you. Is it that you believe in part of the Scripture, and disbelieve in part?"
  * **Gloss:** The tortuous consistency: they honored the ransom clause while breaking the non-expulsion clause — partial obedience read as cherry-picking; the surah's sharpest epistemic charge.
* **`[2:85]`** (clause-chunk c) "What is the reward for those among you who do that but humiliation in this life? And on the Day of Resurrection, they will be assigned to the most severe torment. Allah is not unaware of what you do."
  * **Gloss:** Two-world wages; the refrain "Allah is not unaware" (from 2:74) returns to close the ledger.
* **`[2:86]`** "Those are they who bought the present life for the Hereafter, so the punishment will not be lightened for them, nor will they be helped."
  * **Gloss:** The trade-formula again (2:16, 2:27 vocabulary): purchase of the near life; no mitigation, no helper — both Quranic mercy-valves shut.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`mithāq`** (covenant): 2:83 "We made a covenant with the Children of Israel"; 2:84 "We made a covenant with you."
  * **Rendering:** "covenant" (here the Arabic *mithāq*); Itani elsewhere renders the same word "pledge" (2:63) — drift within four verses' reach (Source: observable; Arabic identity agent inference — verify).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:40]` pledge; `[2:63]` "We received a pledge from you"; `[2:93]` "And We made a covenant with you, and raised the Mount above you"; `[2:100]` "whenever they make a covenant"; `[2:124]` "My pledge does not include the wrongdoers" — the word's full chain runs through the surah (Source: observable, verified via full-file read).
* **`tawallā`** (turn away): 2:83 "Then you turned away."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:64]` "But after that you turned away" — the same verdict two ruku earlier; `[2:137]` "But if they turn away, then they are in schism" (forward, in the Abraham-section — the verb of breach persists beyond Israel) (Source: observable).
* **`qalīl`** (a few): 2:83 "except for a few of you."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:249]` "they drank from it, except for a few of them" — the same minority-of-obedient motif in Saul's test (forward, Source: observable; Arabic phrase-identity agent inference — verify).

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Medinan; 2:83–85 presumes a community that *knows* its own text — the charge is not ignorance but bookkeeping fraud against a known ledger (Source: agent observation; classical tafsir similarly reads 2:85's partial-belief as knowledge-plus-violation, Ibn Kathir).
* **Asbab / narrative context:** reports tie 2:84–85 to inter-tribal warfare in Medina where allied clans fought each other while ransoming their own captives — transmitted historical background, not text (Source: named classical references, Ibn Kathir/al-Tabari on 2:84–85 — verify exact reports when citing).
