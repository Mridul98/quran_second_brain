# Mutual disowning, divine presence, and the God who says "Be" (2:113–121)
**UID:** 202610140924
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 2:113–121 (Ruku 14 of 40 in al-Baqarah)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[2:113]` – `[2:121]` (9 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The two saved communities disown each other's scripture while both read it — so judgment is deferred to the Resurrection; then the surah answers the geography-and-lineage quarrel with pure sovereignty (east and west are Allah's; whichever way you turn is His presence), dissolves the son-claim with the Originator's single word "Be," refuses the demand "let Allah speak to us" by citing its universal precedent, warns that approval is conditional on following creed, and finally names the true readers of Scripture: those who follow it as it ought to be followed.
* **Placement:** The creedal crest between the "part-belief" indictment (2:83–96) and the qiblah section (2:142+): 2:115's "whichever way you turn" lays the theological groundwork *before* the direction-change controversy arrives — the surah answers a question its next ruku will ask.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[2:113]`** "The Jews say, 'The Christians are not based on anything;' and the Christians say, 'The Jews are not based on anything.' Yet they both read the Scripture. Similarly, the ignorant said the same thing. Allah will judge between them on the Day of Resurrection regarding their differences."
  * **Gloss:** Mutual excommunication with the punchline attached ("they both read the Scripture"); the same-saying of the *ignorant* universalizes the fault; judgment postponed to the Day.
* **`[2:114]`** "Who is more unjust than him who forbids the remembrance of Allah’s name in places of worship, and contributes to their ruin? These ought not to enter them except in fear. For them is disgrace in this world, and for them is a terrible punishment in the Hereafter."
  * **Gloss:** The superlative-of-injustice question (*man aẓlamu*) — the crime is blocking *dhikr* in God's houses and ruining them; the two-world disgrace formula (cf. 2:85).
* **`[2:115]`** "To Allah belong the East and the West. Whichever way you turn, there is Allah’s presence. Allah is Omnipresent and Omniscient."
  * **Gloss:** Geography surrendered to ownership; *waǧh Allāh* rendered "Allah's presence" — direction universalized in one line, the precondition for the qiblah ruling to come.
* **`[2:116]`** "And they say, 'Allah has begotten a son.' Be He glorified. Rather, His is everything in the heavens and the earth; all are obedient to Him."
  * **Gloss:** The son-claim met with transcendence (*subḥānahu* → "Be He glorified") and the counter-claim of total ownership: everything belongs to Him — sonship is category-error.
* **`[2:117]`** "Originator of the heavens and the earth. Whenever He decrees a thing, He says to it, 'Be,' and it becomes."
  * **Gloss:** *Bādiʿ* — creation without precedent or partner; the *kun fayakūn* formula replaces begetting with commanding.
* **`[2:118]`** "Those who do not know say, 'If only Allah would speak to us, or a sign would come to us.' Thus said those who were before them. Their hearts are alike. We have made the signs clear for people who are certain."
  * **Gloss:** The speech-demand archived as a genre — "their hearts are alike" flattens the centuries; the answer: signs are already made clear, for the certain.
* **`[2:119]`** "We have sent you with the truth—bringing good news, and giving warnings. You will not be questioned about the inmates of Hell."
  * **Gloss:** The messenger's role bounded: truth-bearer, bringer of news, warner — with disavowal of liability for the refusal of others.
* **`[2:120]`** "The Jews and the Christians will not approve of you, unless you follow their creed. Say, 'Allah’s guidance is the guidance.' Should you follow their desires, after the knowledge that has come to you, you will have in Allah neither guardian nor helper."
  * **Gloss:** Approval's price is apostasy by creed-following; the counter-slogan — "Allah's guidance is the guidance" — absolute, definite-article hidāyah; the guardian-helper formula (2:86/107) turns back on the believer if he defects.
* **`[2:121]`** "Those to whom We have given the Scripture follow it, as it ought to be followed— these believe in it. But as for those who reject it—these are the losers."
  * **Gloss:** The true readership defined by *following*; "as it ought to be followed" (*ḥaqqa tilāwatihi*) — recitation as performance, not possession; "the losers" closes the trade-motif circle (2:27).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`mashriq / maghrib`** (east/west): 2:113-115 (also 2:115 "whichever way you turn").
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:177]` "Righteousness does not consist of turning your faces towards the East and the West" (forward — the same geography re-used to redefine righteousness); `[55:17]` "Lord of the two Easts and Lord of the two Wests" (Source: refs verified via corpus).
* **`masājid`** (places of worship): 2:114 "forbids the remembrance of Allah's name in places of worship."
  * **Rendering:** "places of worship" here; the same root renders "mosques" at `[2:187]` ("retreat at the mosques") and the definite "Sacred Mosque" at `[2:144]` — a three-tier English spread of one word (Source: observable; 2:187 and 2:144 verified via full-file read).
* **`dhikr`** (remembrance): 2:114 — the first *dhikr*-node of the surah's remembrance-chain.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:152]` "remember Me, and I will remember you" (forward, ruku 18); `[2:198]`, `[2:200]`, `[2:203]` — the Hajj section's repeated "remember Allah" (Source: observable chain, verified via full-file read).
* **`kun fayakūn`** ("Be," and it becomes): 2:117.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[16:40]` "When We intend for something to happen, We say to it, 'Be,' and it becomes"; `[19:35]` "To have anything done, He says to it, 'Be,' and it becomes" — verbatim pairs answering the son-claim scenes (Source: refs verified via corpus).

## 4. Cross-References (The Web)
* **Within surah:** `[2:115]` ↔ `[2:142–150]` — the ground before the qiblah storm: "whichever way you turn" is the theology; "turn your face towards the Sacred Mosque" is the practice; the surah orders them exactly so (Source: observable sequencing; forward refs in ruku 17–18 range).
* **Within surah:** `[2:118]` ↔ `[2:55]` ("we will not believe in you unless we see Allah plainly") and ↔ `[2:108]` (questioning the Messenger) — the demand-for-direct-revelation file, three entries deep (Source: observable).
* **Within surah:** `[2:120]` "neither guardian nor helper" ↔ `[2:86]`, `[2:107]` — the formula shuttles between peoples and individuals; here it is pointed *at the believers* as a defection-warning (Source: observable).
* **Within surah:** `[2:121]` ↔ `[2:146]` "Those to whom We have given the Book recognize it as they recognize their own children" (forward) — readership defined twice, by following and by recognition; 2:146 shows recognition *without* submission, which 2:121's "these believe in it" quietly requires.
* **Answer to petition:** `[1:6]` — "Allah's guidance is the guidance" (2:120) is the surah's strictest monotheism-of-guidance claim: the road belongs to Him, not to either faction of 2:113.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Medinan; 2:116's "they say" is not attributed to a named group in-text — classical reports assign it variously (a Jewish saying, a Christian doctrine, or an pre-Islamic claim about angels as "daughters"), and the note keeps the claim anonymous as the text does (Source: named disagreement among classical references on 2:116; Ibn Kathir al-Tabari both cite variants).
* **Asbab / narrative context:** 2:114's ruiners are identified in reports variously as Nebuchadnezzar, Titus, or the archetype of oppressors — unverified specifics; the verse's own categories ("disgrace in this world... terrible punishment in the Hereafter") are left to do the work (Source: reports per classical tafsir; treat as background).
