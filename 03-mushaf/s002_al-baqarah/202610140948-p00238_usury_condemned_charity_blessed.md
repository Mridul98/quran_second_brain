# The war on interest, the blessing on giving, and the day of no wronging (2:274–281)
**UID:** 202610140948
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 2:274–281 (Ruku 38 of 40 in al-Baqarah)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[2:274]` – `[2:281]` (8 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The economy parables get their verdict: charity is a seed that God blesses, usury a claim that God condemns; the two are named in one breath (2:276), the survivors of the swap are given amnesty for the past and capital-only for the present — and the whole sequence closes on the one sentence that makes both economies legible: a Day when every soul is paid fully and no one is wronged.
* **Placement:** Final verdict-block of the charity-vs-usury sequence (ruku 36–38); 2:281 hands off to the surah's longest legal verse (the debt record, ruku 39) with the eschatology already in place.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[2:274]`** "Those who spend their wealth by night and day, privately and publicly, will receive their reward from their Lord. They have nothing to fear, nor shall they grieve."
  * **Gloss:** Charity generalized — all times, all audiences — receives the surah's salvation-refrain for the third time in this run.
* **`[2:275]`** "Those who swallow usury will not rise, except as someone driven mad by Satan's touch. That is because they say, “Commerce is like usury.” But Allah has permitted commerce, and has forbidden usury. Whoever, on receiving advice from his Lord, refrains, may keep his past earnings, and his case rests with Allah. But whoever resumes—these are the dwellers of the Fire, wherein they will abide forever."
  * **Gloss:** The hoarders' standing-scene: they rise (at the resurrection) as one touched by Satan staggers — the inverse of the garden-givers; their defense ("Commerce is like usury") is answered by a divine two-line statute (permitted/forbidden), then amnesty for the past, capital's limit for the future, and the Fire for whoever resumes.
* **`[2:276]`** "Allah condemns usury, and He blesses charities. Allah does not love any sinful ingrate."
  * **Gloss:** The surah's economic thesis in a sentence — the verb-pair yamhaqu/yurbi: usury is liquidated, charity is made fertile; divine love names the two characters it refuses (sinful, ungrateful).
* **`[2:277]`** "Those who believe, and do good deeds, and pray regularly, and give charity—they will have their reward with their Lord; they will have no fear, nor shall they grieve."
  * **Gloss:** The four-act profile restates the opening muttaqin description (2:3–4): prayer plus zakat, now with its verdict — the surah closes the ring it opened.
* **`[2:278]`** "O you who believe! Fear Allah, and forgo what remains of usury, if you are believers."
  * **Gloss:** The command is now to the believers themselves: outstanding interest is the last idol to break; "if you are believers" makes the clause a test, not a flourish.
* **`[2:279]`** "If you do not, then take notice of a war by Allah and His Messenger. But if you repent, you may keep your capital, neither wronging, nor being wronged."
  * **Gloss:** The severest sanction in the surah: war, not merely punishment; the settlement formula — principal intact, neither party wronged — defines the Quranic alternative to the interest economy.
* **`[2:280]`** "But if he is in hardship, then deferment until a time of ease. But to remit it as charity is better for you, if you only knew."
  * **Gloss:** Insolvency protocol: the creditor becomes a time-lender; total remission becomes the charity option — the debtor's hardship is re-classified as the creditor's opportunity.
* **`[2:281]`** "And guard yourselves against a Day when you will be returned to Allah; then each soul will be rewarded fully for what it has earned, and they will not be wronged."
  * **Gloss:** The last warning verse of the legislation arc (classically, the final revealed ayah of the surah per some reports): full payment, zero injustice — the premise on which 2:279's "neither wronging nor being wronged" is built.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`usury`** (ribā): growth decoupled from risk and time.
  * **Rendering:** "swallow usury" (2:275); "Allah condemns usury" (2:276); "forgo what remains of usury" (2:278).
  * **Elsewhere:** the verb-economy pairs it against trade's permission in 2:275 ("Commerce is like usury... Allah has permitted commerce"), while `[2:254]` voids "trading" on that Day — trade survives here, commerce dies there, interest dies both (Source: pairing observable; the two-verse synthesis agent-made — verify).
* **`swallow`** (yaʾkuluʾl-ribā): eating as the usurer's posture.
  * **Rendering:** 2:275; 2:279 "you may keep your capital."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:174]` — concealers "swallow nothing but fire"; `[2:188]` — "do not consume one another's wealth by unjust means" — the surah's three swallowing-crimes all end in the belly (verified).
* **`war`** (ḥarb): the sanction's register.
  * **Rendering:** "a war by Allah and His Messenger" (2:279).
  * **Elsewhere:** the only "war" of divine sanction in this surah (verified via rg); its grammar reverses the combat verses: 2:190 permits war against aggressors, 2:279 declares war against the unrepenting ledger — the surah reserves both senses of the word (Source: observable contrast; the reading is agent-made).
* **`wronged`** (ẓulm): the Day's audit standard.
  * **Rendering:** "neither wronging, nor being wronged" (2:279); "they will not be wronged" (2:281).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:272]` "will be repaid to you in full, and you will not be wronged"; `[2:57]` "They did not wrong Us, but they used to wrong their own souls" — the repayment-justice root threads ruku 37–38 (verified).

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Medinan; classical reports call 2:281 (or 2:278–281) among the last verses revealed of the surah — transmitted, non-textual (Source: reports in Ibn Kathir/Tabari on 2:281 — verify).
