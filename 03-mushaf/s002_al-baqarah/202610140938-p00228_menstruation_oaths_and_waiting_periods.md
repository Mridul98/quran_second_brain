# Bodies, oaths, and the discipline of waiting (2:222–228)
**UID:** 202610140938
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 2:222–228 (Ruku 28 of 40 in al-Baqarah)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[2:222]` – `[2:228]` (7 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The law descends into the household's most intimate material — blood, sex, sworn words, and the counting of waiting-months — and insists each be handled within stated limits, with Allah's love reserved for the repentant and the clean.
* **Placement:** Third "They ask you" block (2:222 continues the question-series of ruku 27) and the opening of the divorce cascade that runs unbroken through 2:237 — the longest family-law stretch in the Quran's longest surah. ## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[2:222]`** "And they ask you about menstruation: say, “It is harmful, so keep away from women during menstruation. And do not approach them until they have become pure. Once they have become pure, approach them in the way Allah has directed you.” Allah loves the repentant, and He loves those who keep clean.”
  * **Gloss:** Question answered with a suspension-window, not a stigma ("It is harmful" = hurtful/offensive, adha); the closing pair — loves the repentant, loves the clean — makes hygiene and tawba kin. (Note: the corpus line closes this sentence with an unmatched, dangling ” — a source artifact, reproduced verbatim.)
* **`[2:223]`** "Your women are cultivation for you; so approach your cultivation whenever you like, and send ahead for yourselves. And fear Allah, and know that you will meet Him. And give good news to the believers."
  * **Gloss:** Once purity returns, initiative is wide open ("whenever you like") — and the field-metaphor adds agency: the cultivators choose when and how, while sowing for the meeting with God.
* **`[2:224]`** "And do not allow your oaths in Allah's name to hinder you from virtue, and righteousness, and making peace between people. Allah is Listener and Knower."
  * **Gloss:** Oaths must not become hostage-taking of conscience: the sworn name may not block birr, taqwa, or reconciliation.
* **`[2:225]`** "Allah does not hold you responsible for your unintentional oaths, but He holds you responsible for your intentions. Allah is Forgiving and Forbearing."
  * **Gloss:** Locus of accountability shifts to intention; slip-of-the-tongue is forgiven, contracted resolve is held.
* **`[2:226]`** "Those who vow abstinence from their wives must wait for four months. But if they reconcile—Allah is Forgiving and Merciful."
  * **Gloss:** The īlāʾ vow (a man swearing off his wife) is given a four-month fuse — after which the suspended marriage forces a decision, not a drift.
* **`[2:227]`** "And if they resolve to divorce—Allah is Hearing and Knowing."
  * **Gloss:** If the four months run out toward divorce, the courtroom of God has already heard the case; the sentence is two lines, deliberately blunt.
* **`[2:228]`** "Divorced women shall wait by themselves for three periods. And it is not lawful for them to conceal what Allah has created in their wombs, if they believe in Allah and the Last Day. Meanwhile, their husbands have the better right to take them back, if they desire reconciliation. And women have rights similar to their obligations, according to what is fair. But men have a degree over them. Allah is Mighty and Wise."
  * **Gloss:** The ʿiddah: three cycles to certify the womb and to cool the decision; concealment of pregnancy is made a faith-breach; reconciliation-right sits with the husband "if they desire reconciliation" — and the fairness formula makes spousal rights reciprocal "according to what is fair."

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`the repentant`** (tawwābīn): those who turn back.
  * **Rendering:** "Allah loves the repentant" (2:222).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:160]` "Except those who repent, and reform, and proclaim"; `[2:279]` "But if you repent, you may keep your capital" (ruku 38) — divine love at the body's limits and economic amnesty after usury use the same verb (verified).
* **`cultivation`** (ḥarth): field/tilth metaphor for the marital relationship.
  * **Rendering:** "Your women are cultivation for you" (2:223); 2:187's paired image is "They are a garment for you, and you are a garment for them."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:187]` (ruku 23) — garment = covering/mutual protection; [2:223] cultivation = productive choice — two of the Quran's whole-body marriage metaphors, both in this legislative run (verified).
* **`oaths`** (aymān): sworn use of Allah's name.
  * **Rendering:** "do not allow your oaths in Allah's name to hinder you" (2:224); "unintentional oaths... your intentions" (2:225).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:204]` — the corruptor "calls Allah to witness what is in his heart" — oath-as-instrument appears as fraud there and is legislated here (verified).
* **`wait by themselves`** (tarabbus/ʿiddah): the mandated waiting.
  * **Rendering:** "three periods" (2:228).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:234]` widows "wait by themselves for four months and ten days"; `[2:226]` the īlāʾ husband "must wait for four months" — the surah legislates three clocks: cycles, months, months-and-days (verified).
