# To Allah belongs all: the confession, the answer, and the last plea (2:284–286)
**UID:** 202610140950
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 2:284–286 (Ruku 40 of 40 in al-Baqarah)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[2:284]` – `[2:286]` (3 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The longest surah ends where it began — on total ownership and human capacity: everything in the heavens and earth belongs to Allah, the Messenger and the believers say "We hear and we obey" (the exact reversal of 2:93's "We hear and disobey"), and the final threefold prayer asks to never be re-burdened as burdens were laid on those before.
* **Placement:** Closure ruku of the surah and of this batch; 2:284 answers the concealment-audits of ruku 39, 2:285 restates the opening creed of 2:3–4, and 2:286's "Allah does not burden any soul beyond its capacity" quotes 2:233 as doctrine at the moment of mercy.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[2:284]`** "To Allah belongs everything in the heavens and the earth. Whether you reveal what is within your selves, or conceal it, Allah will call you to account for it. He forgives whom He wills, and He punishes whom He wills. Allah is Able to do all things."
  * **Gloss:** Ownership premise, then audit of the hidden, then sovereignty over verdicts — forgiveness and punishment both placed under divine will, and no claim left to anyone else.
* **`[2:285]`** "The Messenger has believed in what was revealed to him from his Lord, as did the believers. They all have believed in Allah, and His angels, and His scriptures, and His messengers: “We make no distinction between any of His messengers.” And they say, “We hear and we obey. Your forgiveness, our Lord. To you is the destiny.”"
  * **Gloss:** The community's answer-speech: four-object confession plus non-distinction among messengers (restating 2:136), then the triple assent — hearing, obeying, seeking forgiveness — with the destination named: "To you is the destiny."
* **`[2:286]`** "Allah does not burden any soul beyond its capacity. To its credit is what it earns, and against it is what it commits."
  * **Gloss:** Divine engineering principle — load-limits — and moral bookkeeping: earned credit, committed debit, each soul its own account; the sentence quotes 2:233 verbatim in doctrine.
* **`[2:286]`** "“Our Lord, do not condemn us if we forget or make a mistake. Our Lord, do not burden us as You have burdened those before us. Our Lord, do not burden us with more than we have strength to bear; and pardon us, and forgive us, and have mercy on us. You are our Lord and Master, so help us against the disbelieving people.”"
  * **Gloss:** The surah's last words are a communal dua of three "Our Lord"s: slip-and-forget, historical burden-comparison, strength-limit — then three petitions (pardon, forgiveness, mercy) and the final ask for help against disbelief: the Fatihah's "guide us... nor of those who are misguided" re-voiced as a people's closing line.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`We hear and we obey`** (samiʿnā wa aṭaʿnā): the covenant formula answered.
  * **Rendering:** 2:285.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:93]` — "They said, “We hear and disobey”" — the surah's single most deliberate echo: the new community's self-description is the old one's mirror-image (verified).
* **`make no distinction`** (no furūq): all messengers believed in.
  * **Rendering:** "We make no distinction between any of His messengers" (2:285).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:136]` — "We make no distinction between any of them" — same formula in the Abrahamic-creed verse, now spoken by the whole community at the end (verified).
* **`burden beyond capacity`** (taklīf): load and limit.
  * **Rendering:** 2:286 opening + threefold plea.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:233]` — "No soul shall be burdened beyond its capacity" (nursing-law, ruku 30); `[2:220]` — "Had Allah willed, He could have overburdened you" — the legal principle of the middle surah becomes the closing worship (verified).
* **`reveal / conceal`** (al-ẓāhir wa al-bāṭin): the account's scope.
  * **Rendering:** "Whether you reveal what is within your selves, or conceal it" (2:284).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:33]` — "what you reveal and what you conceal"; `[2:77]` — "Allah knows what they conceal and what they reveal"; `[2:228]`/`[2:283]` — hidden wombs, hidden testimony — one audit-motif running from the Adam pericope to the final verse (verified).
