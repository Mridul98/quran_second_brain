# Eat the lawful and the good — and never invent prohibitions (2:168–176)
**UID:** 202610140931
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 2:168–176 (Ruku 21 of 40 in al-Baqarah)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[2:168]` – `[2:176]` (9 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The ruku turns creation's signs (the previous ruku) into a table law: eat what is lawful and good, thank the Provider, accept the four stated prohibitions — and refuse both Satanic invention of bans and the ancestors' uncritical imitation, because the same concealment-of-revelation that distorts food law distorts the Book itself.
* **Placement:** The first legal-address block after the creedal/signs run (2:163–167, ruku 19–20); it opens the surah's long Medinan legislative arc that runs through 2:286. Source: placement is an observable reading of the ruku map plus range content — agent inference.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[2:168]`** "O people! Eat of what is lawful and good on earth, and do not follow the footsteps of Satan. He is to you an open enemy."
  * **Gloss:** A universal address ("O people") sets permission, not restriction, as the baseline of eating; inventing prohibitions is re-framed as following Satan.
* **`[2:169]`** "He commands you to do evil and vice, and to say about Allah what you do not know."
  * **Gloss:** The mechanism exposed: dietary bans of human origin are "saying about Allah what you do not know."
* **`[2:170]`** "And when it is said to them, “Follow what Allah has revealed,” they say, “We will follow what we found our ancestors following.” Even if their ancestors understood nothing, and were not guided?"
  * **Gloss:** Second rebuttal: inherited custom is not revelation; the rhetorical question rejects ancestry as an evidence-category.
* **`[2:171]`** "The parable of those who disbelieve is that of someone who calls upon someone who hears nothing except screaming and yelling. Deaf, dumb, and blind—they do not understand."
  * **Gloss:** A beast-of-burden image: callers who respond only to noise, not to meaning — unguided imitation rendered as deafness.
* **`[2:172]`** "O you who believe! Eat of the good things We have provided for you, and give thanks to Allah, if it is Him that you serve."
  * **Gloss:** The address narrows to believers; gratitude for food becomes a test of exclusive service.
* **`[2:173]`** "He has forbidden you carrion, and blood, and the flesh of swine, and what was dedicated to other than Allah. But if anyone is compelled, without desiring or exceeding, he commits no sin. Allah is Forgiving and Merciful."
  * **Gloss:** The explicit prohibitions — four items — plus the necessity exception, closing with divine names of pardon.
* **`[2:174]`** "Those who conceal what Allah revealed in the Book, and exchange it for a small price— those swallow nothing but fire into their bellies. And Allah will not speak to them on the Day of Resurrection, nor will He purify them, and they will have a painful punishment."
  * **Gloss:** The food imagery reverses: concealers of revelation "swallow nothing but fire" — the belly motif judges the heart.
* **`[2:175]`** "It is they who exchange guidance for error, and forgiveness for punishment. But why do they insist on the Fire?"
  * **Gloss:** The trade formula (echo of 2:16) names the deal being made: certainty sold for loss.
* **`[2:176]`** "That is because Allah has revealed the Book in truth; and those who differ about the Book are in deep discord."
  * **Gloss:** Grounding: the Book came "in truth"; dissent about it is not innocent variation but "deep discord."

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`footsteps of Satan`** (khuaṭāt al-shayṭān): Satanic approach in slow, stepwise increments.
  * **Rendering:** "the footsteps of Satan" in 2:168; repeated verbatim in 2:208.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:208]` — the same command reappears at "Enter into submission, wholeheartedly," tying table discipline to total Islam; `[2:36]` — Satan "caused them to slip," the first fall by a step.
* **`good things`** (ṭayyibāt): wholesome, clean provision.
  * **Rendering:** "what is lawful and good" in 2:168; "the good things We have provided for you" in 2:172.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:57]` — "Eat of the good things We have provided for you" (manna and quails, with ingratitude as the sin); `[2:267]` — "Give of the good things you have earned," completing the arc from eating good to giving good.
* **`say about Allah what you do not know`**: the Quran's standing formula for invented religion.
  * **Rendering:** "say about Allah what you do not know" in 2:169.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:80]` — the same accusation formula ("saying about Allah what you do not know") against presumption about the Fire — verified in corpus.
* **`conceal`** (kitmān): suppression of revealed content for price.
  * **Rendering:** "conceal what Allah revealed in the Book" (2:174); cf. "suppress the proofs" at 2:159.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:42]`, `[2:146]`, `[2:159]` — concealment of truth by Book-holders, cursed in 2:159; the food-ruling dispute is folded into that same indictment.
