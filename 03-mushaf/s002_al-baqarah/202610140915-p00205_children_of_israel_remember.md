# The address begins: remember, fulfill the pledge, believe, fear (2:40–46)
**UID:** 202610140915
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 2:40–46 (Ruku 5 of 40 in al-Baqarah)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[2:40]` – `[2:46]` (7 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The long address to the Children of Israel opens with four imperatives — remember My blessings, fulfill the pledge (and I fulfill Mine), believe what I reveal, fear Me — then charges them with the scholar's hypocrisy of preaching virtue while forgetting themselves, and closes with the endurance formula: seek help through patience and prayer, difficult except for the devout.
* **Placement:** The pivot of the opening movement: after humanity (2:21–29) and Adam (2:30–39), the surah now names the previous covenant-holders; 2:40–46 is the *invitation* section before the *indictment* recital (2:47–61) and the legal case-studies (2:62+).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[2:40]`** "O Children of Israel! Remember My blessings which I bestowed upon you, and fulfill your pledge to Me, and I will fulfill My pledge to you, and fear Me."
  * **Gloss:** The covenant re-opened as reciprocity: remember, fulfill, and I-fulfill — the pledge is mutual and conditional; "and fear Me" caps the clause sequence (the address-word *wa-lā khawf* grammar appears at 2:6, 2:38 — this ruku is the "khawf" chapter).
* **`[2:41]`** "And believe in what I revealed, confirming what is with you; and do not be the first to deny it; and do not exchange My revelations for a small price; and be conscious of Me."
  * **Gloss:** The specific belief-demand: this Book as *confirming* (muṣaddiqan) — credential language, not competition; "a small price" (*thaman qalīl*) starts the bribery motif (2:79, 2:174 reuse it verbatim).
* **`[2:42]`** "And do not mix truth with falsehood, and do not conceal the truth while you know."
  * **Gloss:** Two information-crimes: blending and burying — the concealment thread runs to 2:76–77, 2:140, 2:159, 2:174 (Source: observable chain, full-file read).
* **`[2:43]`** "And attend to your prayers, and practice regular charity, and kneel with those who kneel."
  * **Gloss:** Liturgy re-joins them to the community: the odd, deliberate phrasing "kneel with those who kneel" (*wa-rākiʿū maʿa al-rākiʿīn*) commands join-the-row humility rather than solo piety.
* **`[2:44]`** "Do you command people to virtuous conduct, and forget yourselves, even though you read the Scripture? Do you not understand?"
  * **Gloss:** The famous rebuke of the preaching-while-absent scholar; the taunt's edge is "even though you read the Scripture" — the medicine beside the wound.
* **`[2:45]`** "And seek help through patience and prayer. But it is difficult, except for the devout."
  * **Gloss:** The endurance formula appears *first here* (and again at 2:153 nearly verbatim, addressed to believers); *istāʿīn* — help sought through sabr and salah as a pair; "the devout" (*khāshiʿīn*) — those whose whole posture softens.
* **`[2:46]`** "Those who know that they will meet their Lord, and that to Him they will return."
  * **Gloss:** The definition of the devout by horizon: meeting/return — 2:28's biography-argument becomes 2:46's psychology.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`ʿahd / mithāq`** (pledge/covenant): 2:40 "fulfill your pledge to Me, and I will fulfill My pledge to you."
  * **Rendering:** "pledge" (here the Arabic is *ʿahd*); the next-cited *mithāq* of 2:63 is also rendered "pledge," while 2:83/2:93/2:100 render *mithāq* as "covenant" — Itani varies the English across the Arabic pair (Source: observable rendering drift; the Arabic identification is agent inference — verify).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:83]` "We made a covenant with the Children of Israel"; `[2:63]` "We received a pledge from you"; `[2:100]` "whenever they make a covenant, some of them toss it aside"; `[2:124]` "My pledge does not include the wrongdoers."
* **`muṣaddiq`** (confirming): 2:41 "confirming what is with you."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:89]` "a scripture came... confirming what they have"; `[2:97]` "confirming what preceded it"; `[2:101]` "a messenger from Allah, confirming what they had"; `[61:6]` "confirming what preceded me of the Torah" — the Quran's own credential-word, used four times in this surah alone (Source: refs verified; 61:6 verified via corpus).
* **`ṣabr`** (patience): 2:45 "through patience and prayer."
  * **Rendering:** "patience."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:153]` "Seek help through patience and prayers" — the near-verbatim repeat (the Knowledge note's pairing anchor); `[2:250]` "pour down patience on us" (forward, Source: observable).
* **`thaman qalīl`** (a small price): 2:41 "do not exchange My revelations for a small price."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:79]` "that they may exchange it for a little price" — same phrase, different English adjective; `[2:174]` "exchange it for a small price" (Source: observable, refs verified — the forgery-for-payment motif).

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Medinan; the Children-of-Israel address marks the shift to confronting the existing covenant communities of Medina — the *form* is invitation before indictment (2:40–46 vs 2:47–61) (Source: classical consensus on the surah's Medinan address-scope; the structural observation is agent inference — verify).
* **Asbab / narrative context:** none transmitted for these seven verses as a unit; 2:43's "kneel with those who kneel" is cited in classical works as a command to join the Muslim prayer-rows in humility even while retaining theological dispute (Source: consensus-style tafsir reading; treat as interpretation, not text).
