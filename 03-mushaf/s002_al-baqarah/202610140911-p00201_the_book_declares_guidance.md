# The Book declares itself guidance — and sorts humanity by its answer (2:1–7)
**UID:** 202610140911
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 2:1–7 (Ruku 1 of 40 in al-Baqarah)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[2:1]` – `[2:7]` (7 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The untranslated letters hand over to a threefold declaration — the Book, no doubt in it, a guide for the righteous — then lists the righteous by five traits, seals them "upon guidance," and immediately sketches the counter-type: the disbelievers whose hearts, hearing, and sight are sealed shut.
* **Placement:** The opening ruku of the longest surah and the longest ruku-unit of the Quran's preface; al-Baqarah begins exactly where al-Fatihah ends — the petition "Guide us to the straight path" (1:6) receives its first answer here: *this* Book is the guidance, and these are the guided.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[2:1]`** "Alif, Lam, Meem."
  * **Gloss:** The recitation opens on letter-names, not meaning — a boundary marker of what translation can carry (see Fleeting note below).
* **`[2:2]`** "This is the Book in which there is no doubt, a guide for the righteous."
  * **Gloss:** The book's self-declaration: identity (the Book), epistemic status (no *rayb*), function (*hudan*), address (the *muttaqin*).
* **`[2:3]`** "Those who believe in the unseen, and perform the prayers, and give from what We have provided for them."
  * **Gloss:** Traits one through three: creed (unseen), ritual (prayer), economics (spending) — belief shows in wallet and wrist.
* **`[2:4]`** "And those who believe in what was revealed to you, and in what was revealed before you, and are certain of the Hereafter."
  * **Gloss:** Traits four and five: an inter-textual faith (this Book *and* the earlier ones) and eschatological certainty — the community of 2:4 is defined by accepting revelation as a series.
* **`[2:5]`** "These are upon guidance from their Lord. These are the successful."
  * **Gloss:** Verdict one: the guided are *upon* guidance (carried, not owning it); "the successful" (*mufliḥūn*) — the Quran's winning word.
* **`[2:6]`** "As for those who disbelieve—it is the same for them, whether you have warned them, or have not warned them—they do not believe."
  * **Gloss:** Verdict two begins: the warning's efficacy is not the warner's to guarantee — the same for them, warned or not.
* **`[2:7]`** "Allah has set a seal on their hearts and on their hearing, and over their vision is a veil. They will have a severe torment."
  * **Gloss:** The three inputs (heart, hearing, sight) are closed from above; the seal is stated as divine action after 2:6's hardened stance (Source: consensus of classical tafsir on the ordering — the seal follows persistent rejection, e.g., Ibn Kathir on 2:6–7).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`Alif, Lam, Meem`** (muqatta'at): letter-names; Itani transliterates and never glosses.
  * **Rendering:** "Alif, Lam, Meem." — non-meaning held open.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[3:1]`, `[19:1]`, `[42:1]`, `[46:1]`, `[68:1]` — 29 surahs open this way; a striking number are followed immediately by Book-claims (Source: observable corpus fact; tracked in the Fleeting note).
* **`hudan`** (guide/guidance): the ruku's load-word, appearing as the Book's function (2:2) and the guided's state (2:5).
  * **Rendering:** "a guide" (2:2), "guidance" (2:5).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:38]` "whenever guidance comes to you from Me"; `[2:185]` "Guidance for humanity"; `[3:4]` "as guidance for mankind" — guidance is what *comes*, addressed, not possessed (links to Knowledge note).
* **`muttaqin`** (the righteous — *al-muttaqīn*): the Book's designated addressees.
  * **Rendering:** "the righteous" in 2:2.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:66]` "a lesson for the righteous"; `[2:177]` ends "these are the pious" — the same root drifts "righteous" → "pious" across the surah (Source: translator's term choice, observable in corpus).
* **`khatama`** (seal): 2:7 "set a seal on their hearts."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:88]` "Our hearts are sealed" — the claim the hypocrites and objectors make of their own hearts is the state 2:7 describes of them; note the Arabic of 2:88's slogan uses a different root (*ghulf*, "veiled/uncased") and Itani's "sealed" creates the English echo (Source: agent inference — verify against Arabic lexicons).

## 4. Cross-References (The Web)
* **Answered petition:** `[1:6–7]` — "Guide us to the straight path. The path of those You have blessed..." is answered verse-by-verse in 2:2–5: the Book *is* the guidance, and the guided have traits, not just claims (Source: classical unity-of-Quran reading, per Ibn Kathir/al-Qurtubi on 1:6–7 / 2:2–5; also flagged in the Fatihah example passage note).
* **Within surah:** `[2:5]` "These are upon guidance" ↔ `[2:16]` (next ruku) "bartered error for guidance... and they are not guided" — the second group, the hypocrites, stands between 2:5's success and 2:7's seal; the ruku sequence is the sorting of humanity by reaction to the Book.
* **Within surah:** `[2:4]` "believe in... what was revealed before you" ↔ `[2:136]` and `[2:285]` — the creed-lists at the surah's mid-point and end restate this mark of true belief.
* **Parallel:** `[46:1–2]` "Ha, Meem. The sending down of the Scripture is from Allah..." — letters then Book-claim, the structural signature the Fleeting note tracks; `[2:1–2]` is its first instance in the Mushaf after al-Fatihah.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Al-Baqarah is Medinan by classical consensus (reports of Ibn Abbas and the Madani-Makki classification literature); its opening prefaced the community-building years. That a "creedal" sorting of humanity opens a law-heavy Medinan surah is itself programmatic: identity before legislation (Source: consensus of classical tafsir; the reading is agent inference — verify).
* **Asbab / narrative context:** none transmitted for 2:1–7 as a unit; classical reports on the muqatta'at's meaning run from *tawqif* ("God knows best") to letter-numerology — the translation refuses to gloss them, which is an interpretive stance worth remembering (Source: Fleeting note, already verified).
