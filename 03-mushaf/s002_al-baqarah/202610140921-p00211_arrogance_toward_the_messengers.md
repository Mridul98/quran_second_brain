# The messenger-file: arrogance, a curse, and the love of life (2:87–96)
**UID:** 202610140921
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 2:87–96 (Ruku 11 of 40 in al-Baqarah)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[2:87]` – `[2:96]` (10 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The indictment closes by file: Moses given the Scripture, messengers sent in succession, Jesus given proofs and the Holy Spirit — and the pattern of response: whenever a messenger brings what the soul dislikes, arrogance, some called impostors, some killed; their slogan "Our hearts are sealed" is answered with curse, their exclusivist hopes with a challenge to wish for death, and the whole confession-of-Paradise exposed by the telling fact: of all mankind they are the most eager to stay alive.
* **Placement:** The recap ruku — it deliberately re-quotes the calf (2:92↔2:51), the Mount (2:93↔2:63), turning-away (2:83/2:64) — the surah reviewing its own evidence before the Gabriel dispute (2:97+).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[2:87]`** "We gave Moses the Scripture, and sent a succession of messengers after him. And We gave Jesus son of Mary the clear proofs, and We supported him with the Holy Spirit. Is it that whenever a messenger comes to you with anything your souls do not desire, you grew arrogant, calling some impostors, and killing others?"
  * **Gloss:** Credential list (Scripture, succession, proofs, Spirit) against behavior list (arrogance, slander, homicide); "a succession" (*ʿaqaba*) is the word for relayed appointment.
* **`[2:88]`** "And they said, 'Our hearts are sealed.' Rather, Allah has cursed them for their ingratitude. They have little faith."
  * **Gloss:** Their one-line defense — a claimed sealed heart — met with the counter-declaration: not seal but curse (*laʿanahum*), and a quantitative verdict on faith: "little."
* **`[2:89]`** "And when a scripture came to them from Allah, confirming what they have—although previously they were seeking victory against those who disbelieved—but when there came to them what they recognized, they disbelieved in it. So Allah’s curse is upon the disbelievers."
  * **Gloss:** The cruel irony: they used to invoke the coming Book against their own pagans; when it came and they *recognized* it, they refused — recognition without submission is the surah's definition of kufr-in-knowledge.
* **`[2:90]`** "Miserable is what they sold their souls for—rejecting what Allah has revealed, out of resentment that Allah would send down His grace upon whomever He chooses from among His servants. Thus they incurred wrath upon wrath. And there is a demeaning punishment for the disbelievers."
  * **Gloss:** The sale-price named: envy that grace (*faḍl*) can be sent outside their line — "wrath upon wrath," the stacking image from 2:61.
* **`[2:91]`** "And when it is said to them, 'Believe in what Allah has revealed,' they say, 'We believe in what was revealed to us,' and they reject anything beyond that, although it is the truth which confirms what they have. Say, 'Why did you kill Allah’s prophets before, if you were believers?'"
  * **Gloss:** The part-belief slogan (2:85) with its own rebuttal: a book you truly held would have held your hands off its messengers.
* **`[2:92]`** "Moses came to you with clear proofs, yet you adopted the calf in his absence, and you were in the wrong."
  * **Gloss:** Recap: the calf file re-opened (→ 2:51, 54).
* **`[2:93]`** "And We made a covenant with you, and raised the Mount above you: 'Take what We have given you firmly, and listen.' They said, 'We hear and disobey.' And their hearts became filled with the love of the calf because of their disbelief. Say, 'Wretched is what your faith commands you to do, if you are believers.'"
  * **Gloss:** The Mount file re-opened (→ 2:63) with a savage addition: "We hear and disobey" — the covenant's anti-oath (which 2:285 will answer with "We hear and we obey"); and the calf-love is now located *in the heart*.
* **`[2:94]`** "Say, 'If the Final Home with Allah is yours alone, to the exclusion of all other people, then wish for death if you are sincere.'"
  * **Gloss:** The exclusivity claim tested by the death-wish dare — sincerity should make meeting one's promised home desirable.
* **`[2:95]`** "But they will never wish for it, because of what their hands have forwarded. Allah is aware of the evildoers."
  * **Gloss:** The dare refused in advance: their forwarded deeds make death an audit, not a homecoming.
* **`[2:96]`** "You will find them, of all mankind, the most eager for life, even more than the polytheists. Every one of them wishes he could live a thousand years; but to be granted a long life will not nudge him from the punishment. Allah is Seeing of what they do."
  * **Gloss:** The verdict-portrait: life-appetite worse than the pagans', the thousand-year fantasy, and the one-line anatomy of that appetite — lengthening cannot defer punishment (Source: "what their hands have forwarded" ↔ 2:85's ransom/homicide ledger — agent observation).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`rūḥ al-qudus`** (the Holy Spirit): 2:87 "We supported him with the Holy Spirit."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:253]` "We strengthened him with the Holy Spirit" — the near-verbatim doublet inside one surah; `[16:102]` "The Holy Spirit has brought it down from your Lord" — here the Spirit is the revealer himself; `[5:110]` "I supported you with the Holy Spirit" (Source: refs verified via corpus; the referent (Gabriel) is classical consensus, Ibn Kathir on 2:87 — not in text).
* **`laʿna`** (curse): 2:88 "Allah has cursed them"; 2:89 "So Allah’s curse is upon the disbelievers."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:159–161]` "Allah curses them, and the cursers curse them... the curse of Allah, and of the angels, and of all humanity" (forward — the curse formula reaches its fullest form in ruku 19); `[5:60]` "He whom Allah has cursed... turned some of them into apes" (links 2:65's apes-verdict to the curse-grammar); `[33:61–62]` "They are cursed... Such has been Allah's precedent" (Source: refs verified).
* **`ghaḍab`** (wrath): 2:90 "wrath upon wrath."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:61]` "incurred wrath from Allah" — the stack adds a second layer; also `[5:60]` "with whom He became angry" (Source: refs verified).
* **`khatam`/"sealed"** claim: 2:88 "Our hearts are sealed."
  * **Rendering:** Itani's "sealed" echoes 2:7's seal ("set a seal on their hearts"), making the slogan sound like a confession of the preface's diagnosis; the Arabic of 2:88's slogan uses a different root (*ghulf*, "veiled/encased") — the English rhyme is a translation choice (Source: agent inference — verify against Arabic lexicons; flagged in ruku 1 note).

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Medinan; the "seeking victory" of 2:89 is classically read as the pre-Islamic monotheists' practice of invoking a coming prophet against their pagans — transmitted background, and the classical sources explicitly caution that individual reports about which verse revealed whom vary (Source: named classical references, Ibn Kathir on 2:89; label background not text).
* **Asbab / narrative context:** none transmitted for the ruku as a unit; 2:88's slogan is quoted speech in the text itself, the only direct transcript of their defense.
