# Blocked remarriage, two years of nursing, and the writing before the tie (2:232–235)
**UID:** 202610140940
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 2:232–235 (Ruku 30 of 40 in al-Baqarah)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[2:232]` – `[2:235]` (4 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** After the counted divorces, the law protects what divorce tends to damage — the ex-husband's remarriage, the nursing child, the widow's term and dignity, the betrothed's secret thoughts — and seeds the surah's closing principle early: no soul is burdened beyond its capacity.
* **Placement:** Third unit of the family-law cascade (2:222–242); 2:233's burden-doctrine will be the surah's last consolation (2:286), making this short ruku a quiet doctrinal hub.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[2:232]`** "When you divorce women, and they have reached their term, do not prevent them from marrying their husbands, provided they agree on fair terms. Thereby is advised whoever among you believes in Allah and the Last Day. That is better and more decent for you. Allah knows, and you do not know."
  * **Gloss:** Guardians (and possibly jealous ex-husbands) may not block a woman's remarriage once terms are fair; the address filters: this advice is for those who believe in the Last Day.
* **`[2:233]`** "Mothers may nurse their infants for two whole years, for those who desire to complete the nursing-period. It is the duty of the father to provide for them and clothe them in a proper manner. No soul shall be burdened beyond its capacity. No mother shall be harmed on account of her child, and no father shall be harmed on account of his child. The same duty rests upon the heir. If the couple desire weaning, by mutual consent and consultation, they commit no error by doing so. You commit no error by hiring nursing-mothers, as long as you pay them fairly. And be wary of Allah, and know that Allah is Seeing of what you do."
  * **Gloss:** Even the child's feeding is a contract with sides: two years as the ceiling, provision as the father's duty, mutual consent for weaning, fair pay for hired nurses — and the engine of the whole verse, "No soul shall be burdened beyond its capacity." (Corpus line previously read "nursingmothers" — a line-wrap hyphen extraction artifact, corrected 2026-10-14.)
* **`[2:234]`** "As for those among you who die and leave widows behind, their widows shall wait by themselves for four months and ten days. When they have reached their term, there is no blame on you regarding what they might honorably do with themselves. Allah is fully acquainted with what you do."
  * **Gloss:** The widow's ʿiddah is fixed — longer than the divorce waiting of 2:228 — and, once past, her honorable choices are hers; "Allah is fully acquainted" closes surveillance where human blame ends.
* **`[2:235]`** "You commit no error by announcing your engagement to women, or by keeping it to yourselves. Allah knows that you will be thinking about them. But do not meet them secretly, unless you have something proper to say. And do not confirm the marriage tie until the writing is fulfilled. And know that Allah knows what is in your souls, so beware of Him. And know that Allah is Forgiving and Forbearing."
  * **Gloss:** Courtship is granted honesty ("Allah knows that you will be thinking about them") but bounded: no secret promises, no consummated tie before the written contract — interior desire is forgiven, procedural secrecy is not.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`burdened beyond its capacity`** (wusʿ): the law's load-limit.
  * **Rendering:** "No soul shall be burdened beyond its capacity" (2:233).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:286]` — "Allah does not burden any soul beyond its capacity" — the same sentence becomes the surah's final doctrine (verified); `[2:220]` — "Had Allah willed, He could have overburdened you" — the negative image of the same root (verified).
* **`their husbands`** (azwājihim): the contested referent.
  * **Rendering:** "do not prevent them from marrying their husbands" (2:232) — ambiguous between remarriage to the original husband and to a new one; the translation keeps the ambiguity (Source: translator's rendering keeps both classical readings open; the dispute is standard in tafsir on 2:232 — verify).
* **`the writing`** (al-kitāb): the contract document.
  * **Rendering:** "do not confirm the marriage tie until the writing is fulfilled" (2:235).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:282]` — the debt verse makes writing a commanded act with scribe and witnesses; the surah's two writing-laws (marriage, loan) bookend the family/economic halves (verified by wording).
* **`nursing`** (ridāʿ): the two-year obligation.
  * **Rendering:** "Mothers may nurse their infants for two whole years" (2:233) — the only nursing-law in this surah (verified via rg: "nurs-" appears only in the 2:233 line).
