# Water from the rock, lentils for manna — the last two grumbles (2:60–61)
**UID:** 202610140917
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 2:60–61 (Ruku 7 of 40 in al-Baqarah)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[2:60]` – `[2:61]` (2 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The favor-recital ends with two desert scenes: a struck rock gives twelve ordered springs, and a craving for ordinary vegetables trades heaven-food for Egypt — the ruku's verdict stacks three consequences: humiliation, poverty, wrath.
* **Placement:** The two-ayah hinge at the end of the Israel-indictment (2:47–59); shortest ruku of the opening block, placed so 2:62 — the great open-door verse — begins a fresh unit.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[2:60]`** "And recall when Moses prayed for water for his people. We said, 'Strike the rock with your staff.' Thereupon twelve springs gushed out from it, and each tribe recognized its drinking-place. 'Eat and drink from Allah’s provision, and do not corrupt the earth with disobedience.'"
  * **Gloss:** Miracle in administrative detail: twelve springs, one per tribe, each *recognizing its place* — provision with order, then the standing clause against corruption (*fasād*) tagged "with disobedience."
* **`[2:61]`** (clause-chunk a) "And recall when you said, 'O Moses, we cannot endure one kind of food, so call to your Lord to produce for us of what the earth grows: of its herbs, and its cucumbers, and its garlic, and its lentils, and its onions.'"
  * **Gloss:** The complaint with a shopping list — five ground-grown staples named, earth-grown against heaven-sent.
* **`[2:61]`** (clause-chunk b) "He said, 'Would you substitute worse for better? Go down to Egypt, where you will have what you asked for.'"
  * **Gloss:** The trade judged (*worse for better* — the same substitute-verb as 2:59), and the answer is an exile-word: "Go down" — the third *uhbitū* of the surah (2:36, 2:38, 2:61).
* **`[2:61]`** (clause-chunk c) "They were struck with humiliation and poverty, and incurred wrath from Allah. That was because they rejected Allah’s revelations and wrongfully killed the prophets. That was because they disobeyed and transgressed."
  * **Gloss:** Three strikes (humiliation, poverty, wrath), two causal clauses — rejection and prophet-killing, then disobedience-and-transgression; the doubled "That was because" (*dhālika bi-*) is the recital's verdict-form.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`ʿaṣā`** (staff): 2:60 "Strike the rock with your staff."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[20:18]` "This is my staff. I lean on it, and herd my sheep with it"; `[27:10]`, `[28:31]` — "Throw down your staff" in the other tellings — an ordinary shepherd's tool turned sign-instrument (Source: refs verified via corpus).
* **`fasād`** (corruption): 2:60 "do not corrupt the earth with disobedience."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:11]` "Do not make trouble on earth" (the hypocrites' slogan-subject); `[2:27]` "commit evil on earth"; `[2:205]` "strives to spread corruption on earth" — the word threads the surah's crime-list from portraits to power (Source: observable chain, all in s002).
* **`ghaḍab`** (wrath): 2:61 "incurred wrath from Allah."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:90]` "Thus they incurred wrath upon wrath" — same verb of incurrence (*iḥtāllū/alayhim al-ghaḍab*) re-used one ruku later, escalating wrath to a stack (Source: observable; exact Arabic verb identity agent inference — verify).
* **`baddala`** (substitute): 2:61 "Would you substitute worse for better?"
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:59]` "substituted words other than those given to them" — the two substitutions back to back: words, then food (Source: observable).

## 4. Cross-References (The Web)
* **Within surah:** `[2:60]` twelve springs ↔ `[2:61]` the vegetable list — ordered, unmixed heavenly provision is refused for undifferentiated earth-goods; the two verses are each other's irony (Source: agent observation).
* **Within surah:** `[2:61]` "Go down to Egypt" ↔ `[2:36, 2:38]` "Go down" — the Adam-descent verb returns; every "go down" in this surah lands the reader further from the Garden and closer to law (Source: observable; synthesis is agent inference — verify).
* **Within surah:** `[2:61]` "wrongfully killed the prophets" ↔ `[2:87, 2:91]` — "calling some impostors, and killing others" / "Why did you kill Allah's prophets before, if you were believers?" — the charge is repeated with escalation through the Israel-section (Source: observable, refs in s002 verified by full-file read).
