# Surah 2: The Heifer (al-Baqarah)
**UID:** 202610140905
**Type:** Index / Surah Map
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Medinan — the first long surah revealed after the Hijrah; 87th in the classical order of revelation. Source: consensus of classical surah-metadata tradition (as transmitted in the standard revelation-order lists). Disagreement note: scattered transmitted reports attach individual verses — e.g., the closing pair `[2:285–286]` — to later moments; verse-level attributions per report, verify against tafsir.
* **Size:** 286 ayahs, 40 ruku — the longest surah in the Quran; it contains its creedal summit (the Throne verse, `[2:255]`) and, classically, its longest single ayah (the debt record, `[2:282]`).
* **Central theme:** Al-Baqarah builds a believing community (ummah) out of a recited Book: it opens by answering the Fatihah's petition, sorting hearts into guided, sealed, and pretenders (1–20), then retells Israel's covenant history as mirror and warning for the new nation (40–141). Abraham's house and religion are reclaimed as the counter-creed (122–141), and the face of the community turns from Jerusalem to the Ka'bah (142–152). Thereupon the surah legislates the whole life of that nation — food, prayer, fasting, Hajj, fighting, property, marriage, divorce, debt, charity and usury (168–283) — and closes on the creedal handshake of total ownership and total hearing-obeying (284–286).

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Heart-census (ruku 1–4):** the Book self-declares as guidance and sorts humanity threefold — guided, sealed, pretending — before widening to "O people!" and grounding the whole in Adam, installed as vicegerent with a standing guidance-promise (2:38).
* **Israel mirror (ruku 5–14):** invitation → favor-file → covenant breached (Mount, Sabbath, heifer, hard hearts, part-faith-part-denial) → the messenger-arrogance and Gabriel disputes → judgment deferred to the Resurrection. The older community is exhibit and warning for the new one.
* **Abrahamic reclamation and the turn (ruku 15–20):** the House and the Hanif way answer the label-wars (2:136's creed), the qiblah becomes the sorting test and the community its own witness-nation, and the section lands on the tawhid-anchor (2:163–167) audited in creation.
* **Law-as-formation (ruku 21–31):** food → birr → fasting → Hajj, then the "They ask you" cascade through fighting, orphans, marriage, and divorce (2:222–242), each statute bracketed by "Allah is knowing/seeing of what you do" — legislation given as belonging, capped at 2:233's burden-doctrine.
* **Narrative and creed summit (ruku 32–35):** Saul, the river, and Goliath teach the tested few; then 2:254–257 gathers the surah's money-, intercession-, and guidance-law into the Throne verse, followed by three resurrection demonstrations (2:258–260).
* **Two economies and the handshake (ruku 36–40):** charity parables → procurement rules → usury verdict → the debt-record appendix; the surah closes answering its own opening creed — "We hear and we obey" (2:285) and the Fatihah re-voiced as a people's last line (2:286).
