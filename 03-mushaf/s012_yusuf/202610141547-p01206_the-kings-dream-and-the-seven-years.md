# The king's dream: seven fat cows, seven lean, and the storage year (12:43–49)
**UID:** 202610141547
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 12:43–49 (Ruku 6 of 12 in Yusuf)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[12:43]` – `[12:49]` (7 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The court's professional interpreters fail ("Jumbles of dreams") while the forgotten prisoner's interpreter-memory resurfaces at exactly the right hour — and Yusuf reads the king's famine-dream not as mysticism but as logistics: store the grain in its spikes.
* **Placement:** The turn from prison to palace: the endowment of `[12:6]`/`[12:21]` becomes statecraft; the delay of `[12:42]` pays its interest here, one verse after the king's need is stated.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **Story motion (12:43–44):** the royal dream and the expert blank — power has the question, not the answer.
* **`[12:43]`** "The king said, “I see seven fat cows being eaten by seven lean ones, and seven green spikes, and others dried up. O elders, explain to me my vision, if you are able to interpret visions.”"
  * **Gloss:** The crown's dream (two sealed symbols: livestock and grain) and a conditional summons — "if you are able" — that they are not.
* **`[12:44]`** "They said, “Jumbles of dreams, and we know nothing of the interpretation of dreams.”"
  * **Gloss:** "Adghathu ahlam" — this edition's rendering of the stock phrase for unreadable dream-debris (awdhan/ur, cf. "Jumbles of dreams" unique in corpus); the experts' double admission: no title, no skill.
* **Story motion (12:45–46):** the man who was forgotten remembers; the prison's reputation finally testifies.
* **`[12:45]`** "The one who was released said, having remembered after a time, “I will inform you of its interpretation, so send me out.”"
  * **Gloss:** The Satan-inflicted forgetfulness (12:42) un-clips at the hour God set; "having remembered after a time" is the surah's smallest theodicy — the delay was load-bearing.
* **`[12:46]`** "“Joseph, O man of truth, inform us concerning seven fat cows being eaten by seven lean ones, and seven green spikes, and others dried up, so that I may return to the people, so that they may know.”"
  * **Gloss:** The envoy's address — "O man of truth" (siddiq, the same brand as 12:36's "righteous") — proves the cell's teaching had already traveled to the court; Yusuf names himself unasked.
* **Story motion (12:47–49):** the reading as agricultural policy — seven years, a storage protocol, then relief.
* **`[12:47]`** "He said, “You will farm for seven consecutive years. But whatever you harvest, leave it in its spikes, except for the little that you eat.”"
  * **Gloss:** Interpretation immediately becomes instruction: the unthreshed spike is the ancient granary — the dream does not predict a famine; it prescribes how to survive it.
* **`[12:48]`** "Then after that will come seven difficult ones, which will consume what you have stored for them, except for the little that you have preserved."
  * **Gloss:** "the little that you have preserved" mirrors 12:47's exception clause — the reading is all about what the few saved units carry.
* **`[12:49]`** "Then after that will come a year that brings relief to the people, and during which they will press."
  * **Gloss:** After the lean: "a year that brings relief... they will press" — abundance-verb (nasra: pressing fruit/oil/wine) answering 12:36's cupbearer's "pressing wine": prison dream and state dream close on the same hand-motion.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`ru'ya` / "vision" vs `hulm` / "dream":** 12:43–44's two registers.
  * **Rendering:** the king's dream is "my vision" (ru'ya, 12:43, 12:100 "my vision of long ago"); the experts' dismissal "Jumbles of dreams" (12:44) — Yusuf's own dream is consistently "vision" (12:4–5): truthful seeing and dream-debris keep separate words in this translation.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[17:60]` "We did not make the vision We showed you, except as a test" — the same ru'ya-word for the Prophet's vision, classically the Night Journey — *Source: classical tafsir identification — verify.*
* **"interpret visions" / "interpretation of dreams":** 12:43's challenge phrase; 12:44's failure phrase.
  * **Elsewhere:** the ta'wil family traced at ruku 1 §3 (12:6/21/36/101; `[18:78/82]`; `[3:7]`) — here the word's public test: Egypt's experts disclaim the very skill Yusuf was gifted at 12:6.
* **"spikes" (sunbul):** the storage detail — unique corpus use.
  * **Rendering:** "seven green spikes, and others dried up" (12:43), "leave it in its spikes" (12:47) — the dream's second symbol set doubles as the interpretation's method.
* **"press" (nasr):** 12:49's relief-verb.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[12:36]` "pressing wine" — one verb uniting the cupbearer's private dream and the public year of plenty; the surah's symbols interlock.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Meccan; the experts-vs-truthful-servant contrast suits the surah's repeated "but most people do not know" epistemics (`[12:21]`, `[12:40]`).
* **Narrative context:** Genesis 41 gives the same seven-plus-seven structure with different symbol order and an unnamed interpreter ("a young man"); the Quran keeps the scene's grammar but moves the credit to taught revelation — *Source: comparative observation; agent inference — verify.*
