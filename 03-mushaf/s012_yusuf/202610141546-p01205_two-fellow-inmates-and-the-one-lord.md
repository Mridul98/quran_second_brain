# Two fellow inmates, the one Lord, and a forgotten request (12:36–42)
**UID:** 202610141546
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 12:36–42 (Ruku 5 of 12 in Yusuf)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[12:36]` – `[12:42]` (7 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Yusuf turns a cell into a madrasa: two youths bring dreams, he first preaches — "are diverse lords better, or Allah, the One, the Supreme?" — then reads their futures, one to wine served, one to crucifixion; his one request for release is forgotten by Satan's design, and the promise of `[12:6]` runs through "several years."
* **Placement:** The surah's public-ministry phase begins inside the prison; the dream-interpretation gift promised at 12:6 now performs, and the delay at 12:42 is the hinge into the king's own dream (ruku 6).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **Story motion (12:36):** cellmates arrive with dream-fractures; they already smell the gift.
* **`[12:36]`** "Two youth entered the prison with him. One of them said, “I see myself pressing wine.” The other said, “I see myself carrying bread on my head, from which the birds are eating. Tell us their interpretation—we see that you are one of the righteous.”"
  * **Gloss:** Two working-men's dreams (a cupbearer, a baker — *Source: classical identification; roles inferred from the outcomes — verify*); their reason for asking: his reputation among the righteous (muttaqin-class rendering) — the same moral brand as 12:22's reward line.
* **Story motion (12:37–38):** before answering, he changes their god-language — da'wah precedes diagnosis.
* **`[12:37]`** "He said, “No food is served to you, but I have informed you about it before you have received it. That is some of what my Lord has taught me. I have forsaken the tradition of people who do not believe in Allah; and regarding the Hereafter, they are deniers.”"
  * **Gloss:** He claims the predictive gift as taught, not innate, and locates it in a religion he has abandoned others for — the "tradition" word is his exit from Egypt's religion market.
* **`[12:38]`** "“And I have followed the faith of my forefathers, Abraham, and Isaac, and Jacob. It is not for us to associate anything with Allah. This is by virtue of Allah’s grace upon us and upon the people, but most people do not give thanks."
  * **Gloss:** The Abrahamic line named by Yusuf himself (paying `[12:6]`'s genealogy forward); "it is not for us to associate anything with Allah" — tawhid as family grammar, grace as the reason, ungratefulness as the majority report.
* **Story motion (12:39–40):** the sermon proper — the oldest monotheist argument in the surah: names have no authority.
* **`[12:39]`** "“O My fellow inmates, are diverse lords better, or Allah, the One, the Supreme?”"
  * **Gloss:** A rhetorical either/or addressed to two men and to every "diverse lords" system — the surah's cleanest creed line.
* **`[12:40]`** "“You do not worship, besides Him, except names you have named, you and your ancestors, for which Allah has sent down no authority. Judgment belongs to none but Allah. He has commanded that you worship none but Him. This is the right religion, but most people do not know."
  * **Gloss:** Gods as unpaid labels ("names you have named"); sovereignty ("Judgment belongs to none but Allah") and worship are one argument; the refrain "but most people do not know" (cf. 12:21) marks the majority as the epistemic casualty.
* **Story motion (12:41–42):** the verdicts delivered, the escape-plan foiled — God's timetable over Yusuf's.
* **`[12:41]`** "“O my fellow inmates! One of you will serve his master wine; while the other will be crucified, and the birds will eat from his head. Thus the matter you are inquiring about is settled.”"
  * **Gloss:** Dream B's image reads itself (birds and head = exposure); "is settled" — the interpretation closes the case before it happens; "crucified" is this edition's rendering (ṣuliba — cf. the same word at `[5:33]`).
* **`[12:42]`** "And he said to the one he thought would be released, “Mention me to your master.” But Satan caused him to forget mentioning him to his master, so he remained in prison for several years."
  * **Gloss:** The small human ask, swallowed by a designed forgetfulness — "several years" (bid' sinin — a few years) is the surah's longest held breath between promise and payoff; cf. `[18:63]` "the devil who made me forget."

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`ta'wil` ("their interpretation"):** 12:36's request-word; Yusuf answers from 12:6's endowment.
  * **Rendering:** "Tell us their interpretation" (12:36); "the interpretation of dreams" (12:44); "the interpretation of events" (12:6/21/101) — same noun, widening scope.
* **"diverse lords... names you have named":** the creed pair of 12:39–40.
  * **Rendering:** "the One, the Supreme" (al-Wahid al-Qahhar — the two-cell Name pair); "Judgment belongs to none but Allah" — unique wording in the corpus; recovered in the surah's own key at `[12:67]` "The decision rests only with Allah."
  * **Elsewhere:** the "names... sent down no authority" formula is a fixed creed-argument: `[53:23]` "nothing but names, which you have devised, you and your ancestors, for which Allah sent down no authority" and `[7:71]` "over names, which you and your ancestors invented..." — Yusuf preaches the Quran's standard anti-idolatry syllogism inside a cell; "This is the right religion" ↔ "the upright religion" `[6:161]`/`[98:5]` — likely one Arabic term, two English renderings; *Source: agent inference on term identity — verify.*
* **"one of the righteous":** 12:36's introduction of Yusuf by his neighbors.
  * **Elsewhere:** the righteous (muttaqin/abrār idiom) as recognition-mark across the corpus (e.g., `[12:22]` "We thus reward the righteous"); the sabr/taqwa note's "watchfulness" quadrant has here its prison example.
* **"Satan caused him to forget":** 12:42's quiet mechanism.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[18:63]` "It was only the devil who made me forget it"; cf. `[12:5]` "Satan is man's sworn enemy" — the surah's first warning working through 12:42's minute forgetfulness into 12:45's delayed rescue.
