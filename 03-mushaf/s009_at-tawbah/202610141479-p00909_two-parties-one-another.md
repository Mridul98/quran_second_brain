# Two parties of one another: the hypocrites' economy of evil and the believers' promise of Eden (9:67–72)
**UID:** 202610141479
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 9:67–72 (Ruku 9 of 16 in at-Tawbah)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[9:67]` – `[9:72]` (6 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah's great diptych: hypocrite men and women are "of one another" — advocating evil, prohibiting righteousness, withholding their hands, forgotten by Allah as they forgot Him — against believing men and women "friends of one another" with the mirror-program (virtue, prayer, charity, obedience), sealed by the twin promises: the Fire "it is their due" versus gardens, Eden homes, and the greater thing, "approval from Allah."
* **Placement:** Ninth ruku; the theological bottom of the dossier section (6–13): nifaq is now a *class* with its own mutual-aid network, matched class-for-class by faith's counter-network.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[9:67]`** — three clause-chunks:
  * "The hypocrite men and hypocrite women are of one another."
  * "They advocate evil, and prohibit righteousness, and withhold their hands. They forgot Allah, so He forgot them. The hypocrites are the sinners."
  * **Gloss:** Gender-inclusive class-portrait: the mutual party (*baʿḍuhum min baʿḍ*); a three-act negative liturgy (evil-on, good-off, hands-closed) and the forget/forget reciprocity — divine "forgetting" as mirrored abandonment.
* **`[9:68]`** "Allah has promised the hypocrite men and hypocrite women, and the disbelievers, the Fire of Hell, abiding therein forever. It is their due. And Allah has cursed them. They will have a lasting punishment."
  * **Gloss:** The promise formula turned to punishment: hypocrites *joined with* disbelievers as one condemned set; "their due" (*bayṭara*) — the Fire is owed; curse added.
* **`[9:69]`** — two clause-chunks:
  * "Like those before you. They were more powerful than you, and had more wealth and children. They enjoyed their share, and you enjoyed your share, as those before you enjoyed their share."
  * "And you indulged, as they indulged. It is they whose works will fail in this world and in the Hereafter. It is they who are the losers."
  * **Gloss:** The analogy to the vanished generations: enjoyment-of-share is the repeated idiom; "works will fail" (*ḥabiṭat*) — the vain-works verdict of 9:17 now applied to the hypocrite class.
* **`[9:70]`** "Have they not heard the stories of those before them? The people of Noah, and Aad, and Thamood; and the people of Abraham, and the inhabitants of Median, and the Overturned Cities? Their messengers came to them with the clear proofs. Allah never wronged them, but they used to wrong their own selves."
  * **Gloss:** The destruction-roll-call — a sermon's citation list; the theodicy clause closes it: no wrong done, only self-wronging.
* **`[9:71]`** — three clause-chunks:
  * "The believing men and believing women are friends of one another."
  * "They advocate virtue, forbid evil, perform the prayers, practice charity, and obey Allah and His Messenger."
  * "These—Allah will have mercy on them. Allah is Noble and Wise."
  * **Gloss:** The mirror-party's five-act liturgy, answered by mercy instead of curse — every term of 9:67 inverted in order.
* **`[9:72]`** — three clause-chunks:
  * "Allah promises the believers, men and women, gardens beneath which rivers flow, abiding therein forever, and fine homes in the Gardens of Eden."
  * "But approval from Allah is even greater. That is the supreme achievement."
  * **Gloss:** The promise proper: geography (gardens), architecture (Eden homes), and then the outbid — *riḍwān*, approval, greater than the gardens themselves; "supreme achievement" (*al-fawẓ al-ʿaẓīm*).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **"of one another / friends of one another" (baʿḍuhum awliyā'u baʿḍ):** the party-structure verb reused from 9:23's *walā'*.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[5:56]` — "Whoever allies himself with Allah, and His Messenger, and those who believe—surely the Party of Allah is the victorious" — the same alliance-law scored here as class analysis; `[3:110]` — the best community "advocates what is moral, and forbids what is immoral": the believers' program of 9:71 is 3:110's.
* **"withhold their hands" (iqbāḍ al-yad):** 9:67's money-sin.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[5:64]` — "The Jews say, “Allah's hand is tied.” It is their hands that are tied" — the hand idiom's other corpus site; here it is mere stinginess, the infraq pole's negative.
* **"forgot Allah, so He forgot them":** the reciprocity of oblivion.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[59:19]` — "do not be like those who forgot Allah, so He made them forget themselves. These are the sinners" — same construction, same closing label (observable near-twin).
* **"Gardens of Eden" (ʿadn):** 9:72's architecture.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[61:12]` — "beautiful mansions in the Gardens of Eden. That is the supreme success" — the verse-twin (with 9:72's "fine homes"/"supreme achievement" drift); also `[19:61]`, `[35:33]`.
