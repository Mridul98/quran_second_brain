# Mobilize, light or heavy: the cave answers the men who cling to the earth (9:38–42)
**UID:** 202610141476
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 9:38–42 (Ruku 6 of 16 in at-Tawbah)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[9:38]` – `[9:42]` (5 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The Tabuk summons and its sociology: when "Mobilize in the cause of Allah" is announced, the faithful move and the half-dead "cling heavily to the earth"; the ruku answers the reluctance with the Hijrah's cave scene — help refused by the community was never needed, because Allah already helped the Messenger — and closes on the liars' counterfactual oath: "Had we been able, we would have marched out with you."
* **Placement:** Sixth ruku; the hinge into the hypocrite dossier proper (ruku 7–13): from here the surah's courtroom is internal.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[9:38]`** "O you who believe! What is the matter with you, when it is said to you, “Mobilize in the cause of Allah,” you cling heavily to the earth? Do you prefer the present life to the Hereafter? The enjoyment of the present life, compared to the Hereafter, is only a little."
  * **Gloss:** The charge addressed to *believers* — a gravity sin, preferring the "little" enjoyment; "cling heavily to the earth" is the corpus's only such phrase (observable).
* **`[9:39]`** "Unless you mobilize, He will punish you most painfully, and will replace you with another people, and you will not harm Him at all. Allah has power over all things."
  * **Gloss:** The stakes: punishment or replacement — the community is functionary, not necessary.
* **`[9:40]`** — four clause-chunks:
  * "If you do not help him, Allah has already helped him, when those who disbelieved expelled him, and he was the second of two in the cave."
  * "He said to his friend, “Do not worry, Allah is with us.”"
  * "And Allah made His tranquility descend upon him, and supported him with forces you did not see, and made the word of those who disbelieved the lowest, while the Word of Allah is the Highest."
  * "Allah is Mighty and Wise."
  * **Gloss:** The counterfactual proof-case: the one time the Prophet was alone with one friend in a cave, the help was divine (tranquility + unseen forces); "Allah is with us" echoes 9:36's "Allah is with the righteous"; the Word's height answers 9:6's hearable Word.
* **`[9:41]`** "Mobilize, light or heavy, and strive with your possessions and your lives in the cause of Allah. That is better for you, if you only knew."
  * **Gloss:** The command without exemptions of temperament or weight; possessions and lives — the full infraq of self.
* **`[9:42]`** "Had the gain been immediate, and the journey shorter, they would have followed you; but the distance seemed too long for them. Still they swear by Allah: “Had we been able, we would have marched out with you.” They damn their own souls, and Allah knows that they are lying."
  * **Gloss:** The motive-dissection: profit and convenience were the real travel-plan; the sworn counterfactual damns itself — "Allah knows that they are lying" is the dossier's recurring verdict-formula.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **"mobilize" (nafir):** the summons verb.
  * **Rendering:** "Mobilize" `[9:38]`, `[9:41]` (and "mobilized against you" in `[3:173]`'s report).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[24:53]` — "if you commanded them, they would mobilize. Say, “Do not swear”" — the hypocrites' mobilization-counterfactual in one verse, the whole scene of 9:42; `[4:71]`, `[33:18]` — mobilize as military verb (all verified sites of the root's English).
* **"the cave":** 9:40's Hijrah set-piece.
  * **Rendering:** "the second of two in the cave" — the corpus's only "second of two."
  * **Elsewhere:** the word's other cluster is the People of the Cave narrative (Surah 18) and 9:57's hypocrite hideouts "a shelter, or a cave, or a hideout" — the same refuge-word, opposite moral use (observable). Classical reports identify 9:40's cave as Thawr. *Source: classical tafsir — verify.*
* **"tranquility" (sakīna-sibling):** 9:40's sent-down calm.
  * **Rendering:** "tranquility" here; "serenity" at `[9:26]` — one Arabic root, two English words in one surah (translator drift, observable).
* **"swear" + "lying":** the oath-dossier opens.
  * **Elsewhere:** the formula "Allah bears witness that the hypocrites are liars" `[63:1]`; 9:42 begins the surah's swearing chain (9:42, 56, 62, 74, 94–96 — internal, verbatim-checked).

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Medinan; classically the summons is the Tabuk expedition (Year 9) — the surah's internal courtroom scene. *Source: classical tafsir and transmitted campaign reports (Ibn Ishaq) — verify*; the corpus itself never names Tabuk (observable).
* **9:40 reading:** the verse is a consolation-to-rebuke: community failure to help is *irrelevant* to Allah's plan — the agent inference (that it shames reluctance by showing its unnecessary-ness) is grounded in the "If you do not help him" protasis. *Source: agent observation — verify.*
