# The excuse business: exemption asked, discord sown, charity criticized, prayer approached lazily (9:43–59)
**UID:** 202610141477
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 9:43–59 (Ruku 7 of 16 in at-Tawbah)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[9:43]` – `[9:59]` (17 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The dossier's longest deposition: the permission granted too soon (9:43), the typology of excuse-makers (9:44–49), their long-history sedition (9:47–48), their weather-forecasts of calamity (9:50), their rejected spending (9:53–54), their sworn false membership (9:56–57), and their charity-gripping (9:58) — cured only by the contentment formula of 9:59.
* **Placement:** Seventh ruku; the sociological center of the hypocrite section (6–13). Every vice introduced here reappears verbatim later in the surah — this ruku is the file's index card.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[9:43]`** "May Allah pardon you! Why did you give them permission before it became clear to you who are the truthful ones, and who are the liars?"
  * **Gloss:** A rebuke of the Prophet's generosity — permission granted before the truth/lie census was possible.
* **`[9:44]`** "Those who believe in Allah and the Last Day do not ask you for exemption from striving with their possessions and their lives. Allah is fully aware of the righteous."
  * **Gloss:** Definitional: true belief removes the question of exemption; taqwa again as the marker.
* **`[9:45]`** "Only those who do not believe in Allah and the Last Day ask you for exemption. Their hearts are full of doubts, so they waver in their doubts."
  * **Gloss:** The inverse definition; doubt (*shak*) as a heart-condition with wobbling as its gait.
* **`[9:46]`** "Had they wanted to mobilize, they would have made preparations for it; but Allah disliked their participation, so he held them back, and it was said, “Stay behind with those who stay behind.”"
  * **Gloss:** Intent audited by preparation; divine dislike executed as detention — with the stay-behind refrain beginning.
* **`[9:47]`** — two clause-chunks:
  * "Had they mobilized with you, they would have added only to your difficulties, and they would have spread rumors in your midst, trying to sow discord among you. Some of you are avid listeners to them."
  * "Allah is Aware of the wrongdoers."
  * **Gloss:** Their combat value was negative: rumor-lines; and the confession — "some of you are avid listeners": the community's susceptibility named.
* **`[9:48]`** "They tried to cause conflict before, and they hatched plots against you, until the truth prevailed, and the command of Allah became evident—in spite of their dislike."
  * **Gloss:** The pattern has a history (pre-Tabuk sedition); providence ends in manifest command.
* **`[9:49]`** "Among them is he who says, “Excuse me, and do not trouble me.” In fact, they sunk into trouble. In fact, Hell will engulf the disbelievers."
  * **Gloss:** The single self-reported exception ("do not trouble me") walks straight into the trouble it dodged — wordplay of *fitan*.
* **`[9:50]`** "If something good happens to you, it upsets them; and if a calamity befalls you, they say, “We took our precautions in advance,” and they depart, happy."
  * **Gloss:** The reverse-empathy signature — see the near-twin `[3:120]`; "we took our precautions" is hindsight as theology.
* **`[9:51]`** "Say, “Nothing will happen to us except what Allah has ordained for us; He is our Protector.” In Allah let the faithful put their trust."
  * **Gloss:** The counter-doxology: ordination (*qadar*) + protection + trust (*tawakkul*) — the formula of 3:173 again.
* **`[9:52]`** "Say, “Are you expecting for us anything other than one of the two excellences? As for us: we are expecting that Allah will afflict you with a punishment from Himself, or at our hands. So wait, we are waiting with you.”"
  * **Gloss:** Two-endings confidence: victory or martyrdom — both *excellences*; the watchers are watched.
* **`[9:53]`** "Say, “Whether you spend willingly or unwillingly, it will not be accepted from you. You are evil people.”"
  * **Gloss:** Spending without faith has no acceptance channel; the same adverb-pair as 9:29's tax — fiscal parity, opposite verdict.
* **`[9:54]`** "What prevents the acceptance of their contributions is nothing but the fact that they disbelieved in Allah and His Messenger, and that they do not approach the prayer except lazily, and that they do not spend except grudgingly."
  * **Gloss:** The acceptance audit names ritual symptoms: laziness in prayer (4:142's twin) and grudgingness in spending — infraq's intent-condition.
* **`[9:55]`** "Let neither their possessions nor their children impress you. Allah intends to torment them through them in this worldly life, and that their souls depart while they are disbelievers."
  * **Gloss:** Success-as-torment: the same sentence reappears at `[9:85]` verbatim (observable).
* **`[9:56]`** "They swear by Allah that they are of you. But they are not of you. They are divisive people."
  * **Gloss:** Sworn membership denied by the text — 63:1's formula in miniature.
* **`[9:57]`** "Were they to find a shelter, or a cave, or a hideout, they would go to it, rushing."
  * **Gloss:** Kinetic cowardice: three nouns for holes; "hideout" is corpus-unique (observable).
* **`[9:58]`** "And among them are those who criticize you in regard to charities. If they are given some of it, they become pleased; but if they are not given any, they grow resentful."
  * **Gloss:** The distributive complaint — transactional religion: pleased only when paid; echoed at `[9:79]`.
* **`[9:59]`** "If only they were content with what Allah and His Messenger have given them, and said, “Allah is sufficient for us; Allah will give us of His bounty, and so will His Messenger; to Allah we eagerly turn.”"
  * **Gloss:** The cure-portrait of the disease: contentment + the sufficiency dhikr (3:173's words) + tawbah-direction.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **"exemption" (i'dhān):** the excuse-industry's noun.
  * **Rendering:** "ask you for exemption" `[9:44–45]`; "Excuse me" `[9:49]`; chain continues at `[9:86]`, `[9:90]`, `[9:93]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** exemption-language is this surah's monopoly in the corpus (all "exemption" hits are s009 — observable), matching its Tabuk occasion.
* **"lazily" + "grudgingly":** the ritual signature pair of 9:54.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[4:142]` — "when they stand for prayer, they stand lazily, showing off in front of people" — the identical adverb (all corpus hits: 9:54, 4:142); "grudgingly" single-site (9:54). See [Nifaq note](../../02-knowledge/202610141300-k-nifaq_the_inside_trade.md).
* **"spread rumors / sow discord" (muḥrabibūn-root):** 9:47.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[33:60]` — "the rumormongers in the City" — same root-cluster, the Medinan surveillance category.
* **"Allah is sufficient for us":** 9:51's trust verse and 9:59's cure.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[3:173]` — "Allah is enough for us; He is the Excellent Protector" (Uhud report) — the surrogate dhikr the excuse-makers never quite say.
