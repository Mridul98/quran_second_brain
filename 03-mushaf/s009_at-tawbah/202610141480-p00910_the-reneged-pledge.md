# Strive against the disbelievers and the hypocrites: the reneged pledge and the seventy-time refusal (9:73–80)
**UID:** 202610141480
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 9:73–80 (Ruku 10 of 16 in at-Tawbah)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[9:73]` – `[9:80]` (8 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The dossier's verdict round: the Prophet is ordered to strive against *both* disbelievers and hypocrites and be stern; a case history of a failed pledge — sworn bounty, then stinginess — diagnosed as hypocrisy *installed in the heart* as penal consequence; and the intercession door welded shut: forgiveness asked or not asked, seventy times or once, is useless for those who rejected Allah and His Messenger.
* **Placement:** Tenth ruku; the theological hard point of the hypocrite section — nifaq here is not discovered but *sent*: the broken promise's own scar.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[9:73]`** "O Prophet! Strive against the disbelievers and the hypocrites, and be stern with them. Their abode is Hell—what a miserable destination!"
  * **Gloss:** The dual-target command; note the corpus's twin verse `[66:9]` with the identical wording — the Prophet's jihad includes the interior front.
* **`[9:74]`** — four clause-chunks:
  * "They swear by Allah that they said nothing; but they did utter the word of blasphemy, and they renounced faith after their submission."
  * "And they plotted what they could not attain."
  * "They were resentful only because Allah and His Messenger have enriched them out of His grace."
  * "If they repent, it would be best for them; but if they turn away, Allah will afflict them with a painful punishment—in this life and in the Hereafter—and they will have on earth no protector and no savior."
  * **Gloss:** Sworn silence against an actual "word of blasphemy"; the motive exposed: resentment of grace-mediated enrichment; the repentance-exit still open (observable — the surah never closes that door).
* **`[9:75]`** "Among them are those who promised Allah: “If He gives us of His bounty, we will donate and be among the upright.”"
  * **Gloss:** The conditional pledge: bounty-in-exchange-for-charity-and-uprightness — a transaction pre-negotiated with God.
* **`[9:76]`** "But when He has given them of His bounty, they became stingy with it, and turned away in aversion."
  * **Gloss:** The delivery day: stinginess exactly where the pledge said donation — the hand of 9:67 closed at the moment of opening.
* **`[9:77]`** "So He penalized them with hypocrisy in their hearts, until the Day they face Him—because they broke their promise to Allah, and because they used to lie."
  * **Gloss:** The surah's darkest sentence: nifaq as *penalty* handed onto a broken promise; the heart's hypocrisy until the meeting-day; two causes named: promise-breaking and habitual lying.
* **`[9:78]`** "Do they not know that Allah knows their secrets and their conspiracies? And that Allah is the Knower of the unseen?"
  * **Gloss:** Epistemic audit: secret, conspiracy, unseen — three registers, all known.
* **`[9:79]`** "Those who criticize the believers who give charity voluntarily, and ridicule those who find nothing to give except their own efforts—Allah ridicules them. They will have a painful punishment."
  * **Gloss:** The charity-launderers back from 9:58: the wealthy critics sneer at the eager poor who give only what their own labor yields — ridicule answered by divine ridicule.
* **`[9:80]`** "Whether you ask forgiveness for them, or do not ask forgiveness for them—even if you ask forgiveness for them seventy times, Allah will not forgive them. That is because they disbelieved in Allah and His Messenger. Allah does not guide the immoral people."
  * **Gloss:** Intercession refused in hyperbolic arithmetic — "seventy times" (the corpus's other "seventy" sites: `[7:155]` Moses' chosen seventy, `[69:32]` the seventy-cubit chain); the stated ground is disbelief, not the excuse-sins themselves.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **"word of blasphemy" (kalimat al-kufr):** 9:74.
  * **Rendering:** "word of blasphemy" `[9:74]`; "kalimat al-kufr" is the Arabic term (classical lexicon — verify).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[3:177]` — "exchange blasphemy for faith"; `[5:64]` — defiance and blasphemy increased by revelation — the speech-register of the dossier; see [Kalam note](../../02-knowledge/202610132005-k-kalam_allah_revelation_as_speech.md).
* **"promise... broke" (ʿad → ikhlāf):** 9:75–77.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[48:10]` — "Whoever breaks his pledge breaks it to his own loss. And whoever fulfills his covenant with Allah, He will grant him a great reward"; `[3:76]` — "whoever fulfills his commitments and maintains piety—Allah loves the pious." See [Covenant note](../../02-knowledge/202610132002-k-covenant_mithaq.md).
* **"hypocrisy in their hearts" (nifāq as sent verdict):** 9:77 — the only corpus verse where *nifāq* is explicitly a divine penal installation (observable: cf. `[2:10]` sickness *increased* by Allah — same grammar of compounding). Source for reading: [Nifaq note](../../02-knowledge/202610141300-k-nifaq_the_inside_trade.md) §4 — agent inference, verify.
* **"criticize / ridicule" (lazʿ/hamz):** 9:79 ↔ 9:58 — the distribution-complaint pair; "Allah ridicules them" answers the human ridicule (verbal justice pattern of `[2:15]`).
