# Son-claims corrected, hoarded gold branded, and the calendar of the correct religion (9:30–37)
**UID:** 202610141475
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 9:30–37 (Ruku 5 of 16 in at-Tawbah)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[9:30]` – `[9:37]` (8 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The creedal audit of the surah's two "clerical" problems: son-statements that merely emulate older blasphemies, and religious elites who consume wealth, hoard gold and silver, and reschedule the sacred calendar — the same economic and liturgical corruption answered by the tawhid formula (9:31–33) and by the twelve-month decree "of which four are sacred" (9:36–37).
* **Placement:** Fifth ruku; between the jizyah clause (9:29) and the Tabuk summons (9:38): the People of the Book are dealt with creed-first, and the surah's money theology (9:34–35) plants the infraq pole its zakat catalogue (9:60) will answer.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[9:30]`** "The Jews said, “Ezra is the son of Allah,” and the Christians said, “The Messiah is the son of Allah.” These are their statements, out of their mouths. They emulate the statements of those who blasphemed before. May Allah assail them! How deceived they are!"
  * **Gloss:** Two named claims, both classified as *statements out of mouths* — speech-acts, emulating prior blasphemy (cf. `[2:118]` "Their hearts are alike"); the "Ezra" son-claim is corpus-unique here.
* **`[9:31]`** "They have taken their rabbis and their priests as lords instead of Allah, as well as the Messiah son of Mary. Although they were commanded to worship none but The One God. There is no god except He. Glory be to Him; High above what they associate with Him."
  * **Gloss:** The transfer of lordship to religious authorities is the actual shirk diagnosed; the creed formula answers in the same breath.
* **`[9:32]`** "They want to extinguish Allah’s light with their mouths, but Allah refuses except to complete His light, even though the disbelievers dislike it."
  * **Gloss:** Mouth-versus-light: human speech-acts cannot snuff divine illumination — `[61:8]` repeats the verse almost word for word.
* **`[9:33]`** "It is He who sent His Messenger with the guidance and the religion of truth, in order to make it prevail over all religions, even though the idolaters dislike it."
  * **Gloss:** The sending formula (guidance + truth-religion + universal prevail) matches `[61:9]`; "idolaters" in the rendering here where 61:9 says "disbelievers" — drift observable.
* **`[9:34]`** — two clause-chunks:
  * "O you who believe! Many of the rabbis and priests consume people's wealth illicitly, and hinder from Allah’s path."
  * "Those who hoard gold and silver, and do not spend them in Allah’s cause, inform them of a painful punishment."
  * **Gloss:** The clerical-indictment bridge from 9:31: lord-taking becomes wealth-consuming; the hoarders' announcement parallels the disbelievers' of 9:3.
* **`[9:35]`** "On the Day when they will be heated in the Fire of Hell, then their foreheads, and their sides, and their backs will be branded with them: “This is what you hoarded for yourselves; so taste what you used to hoard.”"
  * **Gloss:** The hoard itself becomes the branding iron — wealth kept is testimony against its keeper; "taste" is the recompense verb.
* **`[9:36]`** — three clause-chunks:
  * "The number of months, according to Allah, is twelve months—in the decree of Allah—since the Day He created the heavens and the earth, of which four are sacred. This is the correct religion."
  * "So do not wrong yourselves during them. And fight the polytheists collectively, as they fight you collectively, and know that Allah is with the righteous."
  * **Gloss:** One sentence-chain: the calendar is creation-order, not clan-order; "do not wrong yourselves **during them**" binds the months; the collective-fight clause states reciprocity ("as they fight you collectively"), closing on the taqwa refrain.
* **`[9:37]`** "Postponement is an increase in disbelief— by which those who disbelieve are led astray. They allow it one year, and forbid it another year, in order to conform to the number made sacred by Allah, thus permitting what Allah has forbidden. The evil of their deeds seems good to them. Allah does not guide the disbelieving people."
  * **Gloss:** The *nasiʾ* verdict: rescheduling the sacred is itself disbelief-compounding — religion's calendar is not negotiable sovereignty.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **"rabbis and priests":** the clerical pair.
  * **Rendering:** "rabbis and their priests" `[9:31]`, "rabbis and priests" `[9:34]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[5:63]` — "Why do the rabbis and the priests not prevent them...?" and `[5:82]` — priests and monks as the non-arrogant nearest in affection: the corpus's other two "priest" sites, one indicting, one praising (observable).
* **"hoard" (kanz):** 9:34–35's economic sin.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[3:180]` "They will be encircled by their hoardings on the Day of Resurrection" — the same image without fire-brands; `[7:48]`, `[10:58]`, `[70:18]` — hoarded wealth uniformly futile. See [Infraq vs. riba](../../02-knowledge/202610132004-k-infraq_vs_riba_two_economies.md).
* **"light of Allah":** 9:32's image.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[61:8]` verbatim twin; the guidance↔light pairing of `[5:15–16]` (see kalam note).
* **"Postponement" (nasiʾ):** corpus-unique noun (single hit, `[9:37]`).
  * **Elsewhere:** the pre-Islamic calendar intercalation it names is described by classical sources; the corpus itself supplies only this verdict — *Source: classical tafsir and historical reports on 9:37 — verify.*
