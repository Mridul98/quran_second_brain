# Hunayn: numbers failed, serenity came — and the Sanctuary gets its tax law (9:25–29)
**UID:** 202610141474
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 9:25–29 (Ruku 4 of 16 in at-Tawbah)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[9:25]` – `[9:29]` (5 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah's central object lesson: victory is Allah's, not arithmetic — at Hunayn the Muslims' own "great number impressed" them and availed them nothing, until serenity was sent down; the ruku then reorders the Sanctuary (polytheists barred; provision promised against poverty-fear) and regulates the next tier of relations: fight the People of the Book "until they pay the due tax."
* **Placement:** Fourth ruku; the pivot from the dissolution war (ruku 1–2) to community architecture — the event-memory (Hunayn) that makes the Tabuk reluctance of ruku 6–12 indictable.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[9:25]`** "Allah has given you victory in numerous regions; but on the day of Hunayn, your great number impressed you, but it availed you nothing; and the land, as spacious as it was, narrowed for you; and you turned your backs in retreat."
  * **Gloss:** History as sermon: many victories already given; the one rout is diagnosed as the sin of self-counting; "the land...narrowed" — the spatial claustrophobia that `[9:118]` will reuse for the penitents.
* **`[9:26]`** "Then Allah sent down His serenity upon His Messenger, and upon the believers; and He sent down troops you did not see; and He punished those who disbelieved. Such is the recompense of the disbelievers."
  * **Gloss:** The rescue is entirely divine logistics: *sakīna* (serenity) + invisible troops; the believers' part was to be the recipients.
* **`[9:27]`** "Then, after that, Allah will relent towards whomever He wills. Allah is Forgiving and Merciful."
  * **Gloss:** Even the punished side has a door left open — "relent" (*yatatāwwal*) keeps the surah's tawbah grammar universal.
* **`[9:28]`** "O you who believe! The polytheists are polluted, so let them not approach the Sacred Mosque after this year of theirs. And if you fear poverty, Allah will enrich you from His grace, if He wills. Allah is Aware and Wise."
  * **Gloss:** Sanctuary policy with an economic rider: exclusion from the Mosque may cost trade revenue — "Allah will enrich you from His grace" answers the poverty-fear directly.
* **`[9:29]`** "Fight those who do not believe in Allah, nor in the Last Day, nor forbid what Allah and His Messenger have forbidden, nor abide by the religion of truth—from among those who received the Scripture—until they pay the due tax, willingly or unwillingly."
  * **Gloss:** The People of the Book clause: the corpus's only "due tax" (jizyah); the stop-condition is payment, not conversion — the verb of the sentence ends at a fiscal line, and "from among those who received the Scripture" grammatically restricts the address.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **"serenity" (sakīna):** the sent-down calm.
  * **Rendering:** "His serenity" `[9:26]`; the same root as "tranquility" at `[9:40]` (the cave) — one word, two scenes of the same rescue.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[48:18]` — "sent down serenity upon them" at the pledge under the tree; the corpus's serenity cluster is 9:26, 9:40, 48:18, 48:26 (observable).
* **"impressed you" (taʿjib):** the verb of false security.
  * **Rendering:** 9:25 (numbers), then `[9:55]`, `[9:85]` — "Let neither their possessions nor their children impress you": the same verb turned against the hypocrites' apparent success.
  * **Elsewhere:** the anti-impressment refrain pairs wealth/kids against safety throughout the surah (9:55 ↔ 9:85 verbatim twin).
* **"polluted" (rijs):** 9:28's ritual exclusion ground.
  * **Elsewhere:** the same adjective of the Wine of Paradise — `[37:47]` "Never polluted, and never intoxicating" (observable): the root's range is cultic impurity, and its use for *people* is corpus-unique here.
* **"the due tax" (jizyah):** corpus-unique (single hit, `[9:29]`).
  * **Rendering:** "until they pay the due tax, willingly or unwillingly" — the same adverb-pair ("willingly or unwillingly") used of the hypocrites' rejected spending at `[9:53]`: money given under compulsion moves in opposite directions for the two groups.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Medinan; the Hunayn battle (post-conquest of Mecca, Year 8 by classical reckoning) is the corpus's only named battlefield — *Source: transmitted historical reports/classical tafsir (Ibn Ishaq's sequence) — verify*; the 9:28–29 policies belong to the Year 9 environment of the surah's close.
* **9:29 text-claim discipline:** what the text states is a restricted command ("from among those who received the Scripture") with a fiscal terminus ("until they pay the due tax") — not an open-ended war of coercion; the "willingly or unwillingly" phrase is itself ambiguous in the corpus (verbal rendering). Classical juristic readings supply the dhimmī contract apparatus around it. *Source: classical tafsir and fiqh — verify.*
