# The Pioneers, the penitents, and the mosque of harm: tawbah's infrastructure (9:100–110)
**UID:** 202610141483
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 9:100–110 (Ruku 13 of 16 in at-Tawbah)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[9:100]` – `[9:110]` (11 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** After the census, the constitution: the Pioneers — first Migrants and Supporters and their followers — are the model class with mutual divine pleasure; the confessors (mixed good and bad deeds) enter the repentance pipeline the Prophet is ordered to staff: *receive* contributions, *purify* them with it, *pray for* them — Allah accepts repentance and receives contributions; then the mosque of *ḍirār* (harm, unbelief, division, outpost) is dissected against the mosque founded on piety from day one.
* **Placement:** Thirteenth ruku; the dossier's exit ramp — three classes: the Pioneers (pleased), the confessors (pending), the mosque-builders (indicted); with 9:101's hidden hypocrites bracketing the section's end.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[9:100]`** — three clause-chunks:
  * "The Pioneers—The first of the Migrants and the Supporters, and those who followed them in righteousness."
  * "Allah is pleased with them, and they are pleased with Him."
  * "He has prepared for them Gardens beneath which rivers flow, where they will abide forever. That is the sublime triumph."
  * **Gloss:** The founding generation named as a class (*as-Sābiqūn*): the pleasure-verse (*riḍwān* mutual) — the corpus's happiness formula — plus the gardens and "the sublime triumph."
* **`[9:101]`** "Among the Desert-Arabs around you there are some hypocrites, and among the inhabitants of Medina too. They have become adamant in hypocrisy. You do not know them, but We know them. We will punish them twice; then they will be returned to a severe torment."
  * **Gloss:** The unfindable residual class: hardened in nifaq, known only to God — the dossier's epistemic asymmetry; "punish them twice" keeps the punishment-grammar of 9:126.
* **`[9:102]`** "Others have confessed their sins, having mixed good deeds with bad deeds. Perhaps Allah will redeem them. Allah is Forgiving and Merciful."
  * **Gloss:** The third class: confession + moral mixture; divine verdict left at *perhaps* (*ʿasā*) — hope as policy, tawbah still the surah's engine.
* **`[9:103]`** "Receive contributions from their wealth, to purify them and sanctify them with it; and pray for them. Your prayer is comfort for them. Allah is Hearing and Knowing."
  * **Gloss:** The operational order to the Prophet: receive, purify, sanctify, pray — zakat as cleansing machinery and prayer as *sakīna*-comfort to the penitent.
* **`[9:104]`** "Do they not know that Allah accepts the repentance of His servants, and that He receives the contributions, and that Allah is the Acceptor of Repentance, the Merciful?"
  * **Gloss:** The theology under the machinery: two verbs (accepts repentance, receives contributions) and the doubled name — the divine counterside of 9:53's "it will not be accepted from you."
* **`[9:105]`** "Say, “Work. Allah will see your work, and so will His Messenger, and the believers. Then you will be returned to the Knower of secrets and declarations, and He will inform you of what you used to do.”"
  * **Gloss:** The work-verse: activity under triple observation, ending at the Knower's disclosure — the surah's accountability bracket.
* **`[9:106]`** "Others are held in suspense, awaiting Allah’s decree, as to whether He will punish them, or accept their repentance. Allah is Aware and Wise."
  * **Gloss:** The fourth, unnamed class: suspended verdict — even the community's court defers to the decree.
* **`[9:107]`** — two clause-chunks:
  * "Then there are those who establish a mosque to cause harm, and disbelief, and disunity among the believers, and as an outpost for those who fight Allah and His Messenger."
  * "They will swear: “Our intentions are nothing but good.” But Allah bears witness that they are liars."
  * **Gloss:** The mosque-as-weapon indicted: four charges (harm, unbelief, division, enemy outpost); the sworn good-intention defense met with divine counter-witness — 63:1's formula.
* **`[9:108]`** "Do not stand in it, ever. A mosque founded upon piety from the first day is worthier of your standing in it. In it are men who love to be purified. Allah loves those who purify themselves."
  * **Gloss:** The ruling: permanent boycott of the harm-mosque; the piety-founded mosque (classically Qubāʾ) preferred — purification-lovers as its citizens, with "Allah loves" again on the books.
* **`[9:109]`** "Is he who founds his structure upon piety and acceptance from Allah better, or he who founds his structure on the brink of a cliff that is about to tumble, so it tumbles with him into the Fire of Hell? Allah does not guide the unjust people."
  * **Gloss:** The two foundations as one simile: piety-bedrock versus a cliff-edge — building theology rendered literally as construction; non-guidance formula.
* **`[9:110]`** "The structure which they built will remain questionable in their hearts, until their hearts are stopped. Allah is Knowing and Wise."
  * **Gloss:** The demolished building leaves a psychological residue: doubt lodged until hearts stop — the only corpus use of "hearts are stopped" (observable).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **"The Pioneers" (as-Sābiqūn):** corpus-unique name-class (single hit, `[9:100]`).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[5:119]`, `[58:22]`, `[98:8]` share the pleasure formula ("Allah is pleased with them, and they are pleased with Him") — the Pioneers are its first historical instance; `[57:10]` rewards the ones who "contributed before the conquest" — the priority logic.
* **"pleased with / approval" (riḍwān):** the summit-gift thread.
  * **Rendering:** 9:100; cf. `[9:21]` "acceptance", `[9:72]` "approval from Allah is even greater", `[9:109]` "acceptance from Allah" — one Arabic word, three renderings in this surah (drift, observable).
* **"purify / sanctify" (ṭahūr/tazkiya-verbs):** 9:103, 108.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[87:14]` "Successful is he who purifies himself"; `[74:4]` "purify your clothes" — the purification idiom's Meccan roots now given a fiscal engine in 9:103; see [Infraq note](../../02-knowledge/202610132004-k-infraq_vs_riba_two_economies.md).
* **"Acceptor of Repentance" (al-Tawwāb):** 9:104's divine name-pair with "receives contributions."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:128]`, `[2:160]` — the same name in Abraham's prayer and the repentance-exception clause — the surah's name (at-Tawbah) preached through its own name-list.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Medinan; 9:107–108 classically the mosque of Ḍirār (built by Abu ʿĀmir/the camp of Hypocrisy near Medina, burned on prophetic order) and 9:108's "first day" mosque = Qubāʾ. *Source: classical tafsir and sirah reports (Ibn Ishaq; Bukhari-form reports on Qubāʾ) — verify.*
* **9:102/9:106 reading:** the "perhaps" and the "held in suspense" leave two classes unjudged by the community — the text's own restraint against total excommunication (observable; the identification of the suspense class is classical debate — verify).
