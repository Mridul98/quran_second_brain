# Who attends the mosques: rank, striving, and the loyalty that outranks kin (9:17–24)
**UID:** 202610141473
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 9:17–24 (Ruku 3 of 16 in at-Tawbah)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[9:17]` – `[9:24]` (8 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The community is defined by attendance: who may frequent Allah's places of worship, what counts as a higher rank (belief + emigration + striving with possessions and persons), and what must be dethroned if it competes with Allah and His Messenger — parents, siblings, spouses, wealth, businesses, and beloved homes, in one unsparing list.
* **Placement:** Third ruku. 9:16's inward pivot ("take no supporters apart from Allah") is executed here: the ally question becomes a mosque-membership test (9:17–18), a works-comparison (9:19–22), and a loyalty law (9:23–24).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[9:17]`** "It is not for the polytheists to attend Allah’s places of worship while professing their disbelief. These—their works are in vain, and in the Fire they will abide."
  * **Gloss:** The mosque is defined confessionally: attendance while professing disbelief is an impossibility, not merely a prohibition; "works in vain" opens the surah's key motif.
* **`[9:18]`** "The only people to attend Allah’s places of worship are those who believe in Allah and the Last Day, and pray regularly, and practice regular charity, and fear none but Allah. These are most likely to be guided."
  * **Gloss:** The four-mark census of the true attenders; the guidance verdict is hedged — "most likely" — a mercy of epistemics.
* **`[9:19]`** "Do you consider giving water to pilgrims and maintaining the Sacred Mosque the same as believing in Allah and the Last Day and striving in Allah’s path? They are not equal in Allah’s sight. Allah does not guide the unjust people."
  * **Gloss:** Cultic prestige (the water-service of ʿAbbās, the mosque-maintenance of Shaybah — classical identifications) is demoted below belief-striving; ritual capital is not covenant currency.
* **`[9:20]`** "Those who believe, and emigrate, and strive in Allah’s path with their possessions and their persons, are of a higher rank with Allah. These are the winners."
  * **Gloss:** The triad formula — the surah's definition of the winning rank.
* **`[9:21]`** "Their Lord announces to them good news of mercy from Him, and acceptance, and gardens wherein they will have lasting bliss."
  * **Gloss:** The prize in three names: mercy, *riḍwān* (acceptance — the surah's summit gift, cf. 9:72), gardens.
* **`[9:22]`** "Abiding therein forever. With Allah is a great reward."
  * **Gloss:** Permanence named twice; the reward is with Allah — the note the whole surah keeps paying.
* **`[9:23]`** "O you who believe! Do not ally yourselves with your parents and your siblings if they prefer disbelief to belief. Whoever of you allies himself with them—these are the wrongdoers."
  * **Gloss:** Loyalty's reordering: the *walā'* bond outranks kin when kin "prefer disbelief to belief."
* **`[9:24]`** — two clause-chunks:
  * "Say, “If your parents, and your children, and your siblings, and your spouses, and your relatives, and the wealth you have acquired, and a business you worry about, and homes you love, are more dear to you than Allah, and His Messenger, and the struggle in His cause, then wait until Allah executes His judgment.”"
  * "Allah does not guide the sinful people."
  * **Gloss:** The most concrete de-throning list in the corpus — eight beloved attachments named one by one, ending on homes; then the non-guidance formula.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **"places of worship" (masājid):** the mosques.
  * **Rendering:** "Allah’s places of worship" `[9:17–18]`; the singular "mosque" appears only at `[9:107–108]` (observable).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[72:18]` "The places of worship are for Allah"; `[2:114]` the ruiners of places of worship; `[10:87]` — homes themselves made places of worship: the corpus's widest definition.
* **"emigrate" / "strive" (muhājirūn + mujāhidūn):** the rank formula's verbs.
  * **Rendering:** "believe, and emigrate, and strive...with their possessions and their persons" `[9:20]`; cf. "striving" `[9:16]`, "the struggle in His cause" `[9:24]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[61:11]` — the identical triad ("believe...and strive...with your possessions and yourselves"); `[2:218]` renders the same root "migrated" — translator drift across surahs (observable).
* **"ally" (walā'):** 9:23's verb.
  * **Rendering:** "Do not ally yourselves" / "Whoever of you allies himself."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[5:51]`–`[5:56]` — the same verb's alliance law; `[58:22]` — love for kin who "oppose Allah and His Messenger" ruled out for the believer: this verse's twin with the identical family list.
* **"acceptance" (riḍwān):** 9:21's summit gift.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[9:72]` "But approval from Allah is even greater"; `[3:15]` "purified spouses, and acceptance from Allah" — the same graded blessing.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Medinan; 9:19's water-and-mosque comparison is classically tied to a dispute between ʿAbbās (pilgrim water-service) and the custodians of the Mosque — *Source: classical tafsir (reports collected by Ibn Kathir/al-Tabari on 9:19) — verify.*
* **9:23–24 reading:** the text rules out alliance-with-kin *conditional* on the kin preferring disbelief — it does not command abandonment of unbelieving relatives as such (observable in the "if" clause); the hard reading (disowning) belongs to the *barā'a* ruku. *Source: agent observation — verify.*
