# Be with the sincere: no staying behind, and the stay-behind scholars (9:119–122)
**UID:** 202610141485
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 9:119–122 (Ruku 15 of 16 in at-Tawbah)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[9:119]` – `[9:122]` (4 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Four verses that legislate the community's after-life of the campaign: a general social command — be conscious of Allah and *be with the sincere* (the dossier's remedy is association, not excommunication); a ruling that no Medinan or Desert-Arab may prefer himself to the Messenger, with every hardship auto-credited as a righteous deed; and the surah's most surprising statute: not everyone should march out — a group stays behind to *gain understanding of the religion* and warn their people when the others return.
* **Placement:** Fifteenth ruku; the de-escalation of the mobilization crisis — war is not the whole of religion, and the stay-behind is inverted from the hypocrites' sin (9:49, 86–87, 93) into scholarship's license.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[9:119]`** "O you who believe! Be conscious of Allah, and be with the sincere."
  * **Gloss:** Two clauses, one program: *taqwā* (the surah's refrain) plus band-membership — choose the company of the *ṣādiqūn*, the class 9:75's oath-breakers failed to join.
* **`[9:120]`** — three clause-chunks:
  * "It is not for the inhabitants of Medina and the Desert-Arabs around them to stay behind the Messenger of Allah, nor to prefer themselves to him."
  * "That is because they never suffer any thirst, nor fatigue, nor hunger in the cause of Allah, nor do they take one step that enrages the disbelievers, nor do they gain anything from an enemy, but it is recorded to their credit as a righteous deed."
  * "Allah does not waste the reward of the righteous."
  * **Gloss:** The general ruling against self-preference, grounded in the account-keeping of Allah: five listed hardships (thirst, fatigue, hunger, provoking the enemy, gaining from the enemy) all auto-credited — the bookkeeping opposite of the hoarders' branding (9:35).
* **`[9:121]`** "Nor do they spend any expenditure, small or large, nor do they cross any valley, but it is recorded to their credit. That Allah may reward them in accordance with the best of their deeds."
  * **Gloss:** Spending and marching both enter the ledger at every scale; the reward clause pays at the *best* of deeds, not the worst — grace arithmetic.
* **`[9:122]`** — two clause-chunks:
  * "It is not advisable for the believers to march out altogether. Of every division that marches out, let a group remain behind, to gain understanding of the religion, and to notify their people when they have returned to them, that they may beware."
  * **Gloss:** The deputation statute: total mobilization is *not advisable* — a learning-quorum stays; knowledge is a front-line duty; the warn-back ("that they may beware") makes the returned army a preaching body. The corpus's only "gain understanding of the religion" construction (observable).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **"the sincere" (aṣ-Ṣādiqūn):** 9:119's company-requirement — truthfulness as a *class you join*.
  * **Rendering:** "be with the sincere."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[49:15]` — the doubtless strivers: "These are the sincere"; `[57:19]` — believers and witnesses "are the sincere"; `[59:8]` — the driven-out refugees "are the sincere"; `[33:23]` — "men who are true to what they pledged to Allah" — the pledge-keepers 9:75–77 broke. See [Nifaq note](../../02-knowledge/202610141300-k-nifaq_the_inside_trade.md) — *ṣidq* as its structural opposite.
* **"stay behind" inversion:** the dossier's verb (9:46, 81–87, 93) reused legally in 9:122 ("let a group remain behind") — the same act reclassified by intention (observable wordplay; the classical reading agrees — *Source: classical tafsir on 9:122 — verify*).
* **"recorded to their credit" (kutiba):** 9:120–121's ledger-verb.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[4:81]` — "Allah writes down what they conspire" — the recording-verb's other register: there the conspiracy ledger, here the credit ledger (verified within the writes-down/recorded phrase-family); within surah it answers `[9:35]`'s branded hoard — one stores, the other accrues ([Infraq note](../../02-knowledge/202610132004-k-infraq_vs_riba_two_economies.md)).
* **"Be conscious of Allah" (ittaqū):** the taqwa formula — cf. 9:4/7/36/123 "Allah loves the righteous...is with the righteous." See [Sabr and taqwa](../../02-knowledge/202610132003-k-sabr_and_taqwa.md).
