# No disdain, a clear light, and the last ruling (4:172–176)
**UID:** 202610141125
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 4:172–176 (Ruku 24 of 24 in an-Nisa')
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[4:172]` – `[4:176]` (5 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah's final argument moves in three steps: dignity denied where pride was claimed — the Messiah does not disdain servanthood, nor do the favored angels, and the disdainful will be rounded up to Allah altogether — while the workers receive wages in full plus increase; then the third "O people!" announces what has now "come": a proof and a sent-down clear light, and holding fast is admission into mercy, grace, and a straight path to Himself; and the book closes as its family-law cycle began — "They ask you for a ruling" — with the kalala case answered, the male/female ratio restated, and the clarity-refrain as the surah's last word about itself: "Allah makes things clear for you, lest you err. Allah is Aware of everything."
* **Placement:** The colophon-ruku: 4:172–173 complete the Christology of 4:171 (servant-claim against son-claim), 4:174–175 close the guidance-and-gathering arc of 4:87/4:166, and 4:176 is the ring-return to 4:11–12 — the second "They ask you for a ruling" in the corpus (verified: the phrase occurs only at 4:127 and 4:176) answers the first table's lateral-sibling case from the other side.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[4:172]`** "The Messiah does not disdain to be a servant of Allah, nor do the favored angels."
  * **Gloss:** The counter-thesis to the son-claim: servanthood is not beneath the Messiah — and the angels, higher-ranked claimants in the same market, are his peers in obedience (the favored-angels idiom = muqarrabun; classical — verify English correspondence).
* **`[4:172]`** "Whoever disdains His worship, and is too arrogant—He will round them up to Himself altogether."
  * **Gloss:** The disdain-plus-arrogance pair (istakbara — the Iblis-diad, cf. 2:34's refusal family) with the divine gathering: worship-refusers conscripted whether willing or not (the "round up" — hashr — the same Day-gathering announced at 4:87, verified by reading).
* **`[4:173]`** "But as for those who believe and do good works, He will pay them their wages in full, and will increase His grace for them."
  * **Gloss:** Full wage plus increase (da'if) — the infaq-note's disproportion-grammar ("paid in full, without reckoning," 39:10 — verified) at the surah's edge.
* **`[4:173]`** "But as for those who disdain and are too proud, He will punish them with an agonizing punishment. And they will find for themselves, apart from Allah, no lord and no savior."
  * **Gloss:** The disdain-clause repeated as punishment-entry; "no lord and no savior" — a corpus-unique phrasing (verified) and the negative of 4:75's petition and 4:123's "protector or savior" (within-surah triangulation).
* **`[4:174]`** "O people! A proof has come to you from your Lord, and We sent down to you a clear light."
  * **Gloss:** The third universal address (4:1/4:133/4:170/4:174 — verified cluster); proof (burhan — the warrant-family's fourth robe here; cf. "Our proofs" at `[26:15]`, verified) + "a clear light" — the corpus-unique English pair (verified), al-Ma'idah's twin in different words: `[5:15]` "A light from Allah has come to you, and a clear Book" (verified).
* **`[4:175]`** "As for those who believe in Allah, and hold fast to Him, He will admit them into mercy and grace from Him, and will guide them to Himself in a straight path."
  * **Gloss:** Hold fast (the dossier's amnesty-verb of 4:146, verified — the surah's last word to the wavering is its first word of exit) → mercy, grace, straight path to Himself: the counterfactual wage of 4:67–68 ("We would have given... guided... on a straight path") made actual for a different class than the hypocrites imagined (observable ring — the would-have becomes the will).
* **`[4:176]`** "They ask you for a ruling. Say, “Allah gives you a ruling concerning the person who has neither parents nor children.”"
  * **Gloss:** The kalala case opens — "neither parents nor children" is the same descriptive phrase used at 4:12 (verified — the only two sites): the surah never transliterates the term, it legislates the class by definition.
* **`[4:176]`** "If a man dies, and leaves no children, and he had a sister, she receives one-half of what he leaves. And he inherits from her if she leaves no children."
  * **Gloss:** Full sister: half to her, and reciprocal inheritance from her in the same conditions (the text states the symmetry explicitly).
* **`[4:176]`** "But if there are two sisters, they receive two-thirds of what he leaves."
  * **Gloss:** Two sisters: the two-thirds — mirroring 4:11's daughters-share (an earlier extraction read "twothirds" — line-wrap hyphen artifact, corrected 2026-10-14).
* **`[4:176]`** "If the siblings are men and women, the male receives the share of two females.”"
  * **Gloss:** The mixed-sibling ratio restates 4:11's rule for the collaterals (the closing quotation-mark after "females" appears as printed in the corpus line — an unmatched quote in the source text; anomaly reported, reproduced verbatim here).
* **`[4:176]`** "Allah makes things clear for you, lest you err. Allah is Aware of everything."
  * **Gloss:** The clarity-refrain (bayyana — 4:26's divine intent, now its accomplished act; k-kalam's own vocabulary) and the final knowledge-name — the surah's last sentence about itself is about making things clear, answering the opening "Allah is Watchful over you" (4:1): clarity and watchfulness bracket the whole book (observable ring).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`istakbara` / "disdain... too arrogant"** (4:172–173): the pride-diad.
  * **Elsewhere:** the same English pair-functions run through the corpus's arrogance-vocabulary (`[31:18]` "arrogant showoff" verified; 17:37-family not quoted — verify); within-surah it closes the 4:36/4:37 arrogance-lineage with a Christological referent (observable).
* **`hasaba/zu'ma` / "round them up"** (4:172): forced gathering.
  * **Elsewhere:** 4:87's "He will gather you to the Day of Resurrection" (verified by reading) — the surah's two gatherings: the court one and this conscription one (agent pairing).
* **`wakil + najat` / "no lord and no savior"** (4:173): the twin vacancy.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[4:123]` "protector or savior" (verified by reading) and `[4:75]`'s petition for Protector/Victor (verified) — the last verse of the argument re-states the prayer's two terms as the disdainer's absence (observable closure).
* **`burhan + nur` / "proof... clear light"** (4:174): revelation-as-evidence-as-visibility.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[26:15]` "with Our proofs" (verified), `[5:15]` "a light... and a clear Book" (verified) — the clear-light pair occurs only here in the corpus (verified hapax phrase).
* **`bayyana` / "makes things clear"** (4:176):
  * **Elsewhere:** `[4:26]` "Allah intends to make things clear to you" (verified) — intent stated mid-surah, achievement claimed in its final clause; also the k-kalam note's family (an-Nisa's revelation-speech self-description).
