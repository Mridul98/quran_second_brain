# Lazily, to be seen: the lowest level — and the exception that repents (4:142–152)
**UID:** 202610141122
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 4:142–152 (Ruku 21 of 24 in an-Nisa')
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[4:142]` – `[4:152]` (11 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The hypocrites' final portrait is liturgical: they try to deceive Allah while He is deceiving them, they stand for prayer lazily, to be seen, remembering Allah only a little — wavering between, with no way for the one sent astray; the believers are warned off befriending disbelievers (it hands Allah "a clear case against you"), the hypocrites assigned the lowest level of the Fire — and then the dossier's own amnesty clause: except those who repent, reform, hold fast to Allah, and dedicate their religion to Allah alone; these are with the believers; after which the surah softens the public-sphere rules it just tightened (bad speech disliked except for the wronged; good deeds shown or hidden or offense pardoned, all watched) and ends on the distinction-deniers — "We believe in some, and reject some" — named truly unbelieving, while those who make no distinction between any of the messengers receive their rewards.
* **Placement:** The closing session of the hypocrite-dossier (ruku 9–21): prayer-sickness (4:142) answers Baqarah's heart-sickness (2:10, verified), and the amnesty of 4:146 answers the oscillators of 4:137 (ruku 20) — the surah's dossier is sealed not with exclusion but with the repentance-valve first installed at 4:17.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[4:142]`** "The hypocrites try to deceive Allah, but He is deceiving them."
  * **Gloss:** The exact Baqarah-formula ("They seek to deceive Allah and those who believe, but they deceive none but themselves," 2:9, verified) now with named subjects — the label and the deception-clause meet here.
* **`[4:142]`** "And when they stand for prayer, they stand lazily, showing off in front of people, and remembering Allah only a little."
  * **Gloss:** Three tells in one rite: lethargy (the same "lazily" at `[9:54]`, verified), display (riya' — the "showoff" of 4:36/31:18 family, verified), and dwarfed remembrance ("only a little" — the dhikr-measure the sabr-note counts in its worship-quadrant).
* **`[4:143]`** "Wavering in between, neither with these, nor with those."
  * **Gloss:** Positionless between-position — the road metaphor's negative: not on either path (cf. 4:115's chosen direction; within-surah).
* **`[4:143]`** "Whomever Allah sends astray, you will never find for him a way."
  * **Gloss:** The refrain twin of 4:88 with the verb shifted ("sends" vs "leads" — observable, noted in ruku 12).
* **`[4:144]`** "O you who believe! Do not befriend disbelievers rather than believers. Do you want to give Allah a clear case against you?"
  * **Gloss:** The loyalty-law restated (4:89 band) with its courtroom consequence: alliance-shopping hands the prosecution its sultan — warrant-phrase verified unique to this verse (see ruku 20 note).
* **`[4:145]`** "The hypocrites will be in the lowest level of the Fire, and you will find no helper for them."
  * **Gloss:** Dorak al-jaheem — the bottom rung; the corpus's other "lowest" is mankind's descent at `[95:5]` (verified) — here it becomes the one place prayer-laziness purchases; "helper" (naseer) closes the wali/nasir pair of 4:75/4:123 empty.
* **`[4:146]`** "Except those who repent, and reform, and hold fast to Allah, and dedicate their religion to Allah alone."
  * **Gloss:** Four-condition amnesty — repent (tawba, 4:17 arc), reform (islah, 4:35/4:114 arc — verified family), hold fast (i'tisam — the identical verb-clause of `[3:101]` "Whoever cleaves to Allah has been guided to a straight path," verified), and purify the religion's owner (ikhlas — "dedicate" family at `[7:29]`, `[10:105]`, verified).
* **`[4:146]`** "These are with the believers; and Allah will give the believers a great reward."
  * **Gloss:** "With" — the company-word of 4:69 (within-surah echo): the repentant hypocrite is re-admitted to the blessed caravan, not merely absolved.
* **`[4:147]`** "What would Allah accomplish by your punishment, if you have given thanks, and have believed?"
  * **Gloss:** The counterfactual of divine disinterest in punishing the grateful (cf. 4:39's "what would they have lost" — the surah's paired questions).
* **`[4:147]`** "Allah is Appreciative and Cognizant."
  * **Gloss:** Shakur/Kafur pair-name — gratitude counted; knowledge sealed.
* **`[4:148]`** "Allah does not like the public uttering of bad language, unless someone was wronged."
  * **Gloss:** The speech-law with its grievance exception (the mazlüm's complaint is licensed — Source: classical readings of the jabr-family clause, Ibn Kathir on 4:148 — verify wording); the "bad language" phrase is a corpus hapax (verified).
* **`[4:149]`** "If you let a good deed be shown, or conceal it, or pardon an offense—Allah is Pardoning and Capable."
  * **Gloss:** Three equally-lawful charities (public, private, pardon) — the openness/privacy axis twin of `[2:271]` (verified); the dossier's last word on the hypocrites' exposure-policy: Allah is the one who conceals.
* **`[4:150]`** "Those who disbelieve in Allah and His messengers, and want to separate between Allah and His messengers, and say, “We believe in some, and reject some,” and wish to take a path in between."
  * **Gloss:** The select-creed party: the distinction-desire is the mirror-of-hypocrisy at confessional scale — "a path in between" resumes the wavering clause (observable internal rhyme).
* **`[4:151]`** "These are the unbelievers, truly. We have prepared for the unbelievers a shameful punishment."
  * **Gloss:** The verdict-naming: "the unbelievers, truly" — this phrasing (haqq as adverb of certainty) is unique in the corpus (verified) — the select-creed party is stripped of all confessional cover.
* **`[4:152]`** "As for those who believe in Allah and His messengers, and make no distinction between any of them—He will give them their rewards."
  * **Gloss:** The positive rite of faith: the no-distinction creed — verbatim family of `[2:136]`/`[3:84]`/`[2:285]` (all verified) — the surah ends the dossier on the very clause Baqarah's credo recited.
* **`[4:152]`** "Allah is Forgiver and Merciful."
  * **Gloss:** The refrain the ruku has been working toward: even the distinction-deniers' opposite is met with names of amnesty.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **"lazily" + "showing off" (4:142)**: prayer as performance for an audience of none.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[9:54]` — "they do not approach prayer except lazily" (verified twin); "showoff" at `[4:36]` and `[31:18]` (verified) — the riya' family's two legal sites in this surah (household decalogue; dossier finale).
* **`darak` / "the lowest level"** (4:145): the Fire's floor.
  * **Elsewhere:** the word-idiom is unique in this translation; the opposite register "lowest of the low" for human decline at `[95:5]` (verified as the other "lowest" site) — ascent-and-descent measured on the same ladder (agent framing, verify).
* **`i'tisam` / "hold fast to Allah"** (4:146):
  * **Elsewhere:** `[3:101]` — "Whoever cleaves to Allah has been guided to a straight path" (verified) — and within-surah `[4:175]` — "whoever believes in Allah and holds fast to Him" (verified by reading): the same covenantal grip closes the dossier and closes the surah.
* **"make no distinction between any of them" (4:152)**:
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:136]`, `[3:84]`, `[2:285]` (verified — the credo-list formula) — this surah is the only place the formula appears in negative form ("separate between... believe in some, reject some," 4:150): the dossier and the confessional fallacy share one structure — partial acceptance as rejection (observable pairing + agent synthesis).
