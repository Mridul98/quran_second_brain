# Stand firmly: witnesses of justice, even against yourselves (4:135–141)
**UID:** 202610141121
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 4:135–141 (Ruku 20 of 24 in an-Nisa')
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[4:135]` – `[4:141]` (7 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah's justice-creed: stand firmly for equity as witnesses to Allah — against yourselves, your parents, your relatives; rich or poor, Allah is closer to both — so do not follow desires, for deviation is watched; then the belief-list (Allah, His Messenger, the Book down and the Books before) with its five-part rejection-clause; then the hypocrites' portrait in motion: the oscillators who will not be forgiven nor guided, the glory-seekers allied with disbelievers, the sitters in the mockery-audience (with the like-them warning), and the victory-waiters who claim kinship with whichever side turns — answered: Allah will judge between you, and will give the disbelievers no means of overcoming the believers.
* **Placement:** 4:135 is the qiwam-word of ruku 6 (4:34) turned judicial — the same root now standing, not guarding — and the unit is the hinge between the second women-cycle and the hypocrites' final sorting (ruku 21). Its seating-rule (4:140) is the community's liturgy of dissent: leave the audience, do not become the audience.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[4:135]`** "O you who believe! Stand firmly for justice, as witnesses to Allah, even if against yourselves, or your parents, or your relatives."
  * **Gloss:** Qawwamin — "stand firmly" (the q-w-m root of 4:34's "protectors and maintainers," now re-office: household custodianship and courtroom uprightness are one verb-family — lexical claim per classical lexicons, agent — verify); the self and the kin are named first as the testimony's hardest targets.
* **`[4:135]`** "Whether one is rich or poor, Allah takes care of both."
  * **Gloss:** Class-blindness in the witness-box, grounded in divine nearer-ness (aqra — "takes care of," the comparative of proximity; unique rendering here — verified pattern).
* **`[4:135]`** "So do not follow your desires, lest you swerve. If you deviate, or turn away— then Allah is Aware of what you do."
  * **Gloss:** The desire-ban (cf. 4:27 "those who follow their desires," within-surah) plus the Aware-refrain that fences the surah's last law-clauses (observable).
* **`[4:136]`** "O you who believe! Believe in Allah and His messenger, and the Book He sent down to His messenger, and the Book He sent down before."
  * **Gloss:** The believe-the-Book-past command — the same list-formula as `[2:285]` "believed in Allah, and His angels, and His scriptures, and His messengers" (verified) — revelation-speech's object extended across communities (k-kalam link).
* **`[4:136]`** "Whoever rejects Allah, His angels, His Books, His messengers, and the Last Day, has strayed far in error."
  * **Gloss:** Five-part rejection-clause; "strayed far in error" — the identical verdict-phrase of 4:116 (verified doublet — the surah welds shirk and messenger-rejection as one deviation).
* **`[4:137]`** "Those who believe, then disbelieve, then believe, then disbelieve, then increase in disbelief, Allah will not forgive them, nor will He guide them to a way."
  * **Gloss:** The oscillators: repetition hardens, and the two closures (no forgiveness, no way) echo 4:116's single one — the door that never closes for the repentant (4:110) closes here by self-exclusion (observable contrast).
* **`[4:138]`** "Inform the hypocrites that they will have a painful punishment."
  * **Gloss:** The label's fourth corpus-hit in the surah (verified count in R9's note) and the dossier's announcement-form (cf. 4:138 as the keystone before the seating- and waiting-laws).
* **`[4:139]`** "Those who ally themselves with the disbelievers instead of the believers. Do they seek glory in them? All glory belongs to Allah."
  * **Gloss:** Alliance as a bid for might (i'tezāz/waliya — root notes per classical lexing, agent — verify); the counter-claim "All glory belongs to Allah" is a corpus-unique wording (verified), and 4:141's "no means of overcoming" is its twin clause: the might they buy is the might history is denied (observable pairing).
* **`[4:140]`** "He has revealed to you in the Book that when you hear Allah’s revelations being rejected, or ridiculed, do not sit with them until they engage in some other subject."
  * **Gloss:** The audience-law: revelation is itself an occasion of fellowship — withdrawal is the testimony of 4:135 applied to the speech-event; the near-twin is `[6:68]` "turn away from them, until they engage in another topic... do not sit after the recollection with the wicked" (verified).
* **`[4:140]`** "Otherwise, you would be like them. Allah will gather the hypocrites and the disbelievers, into Hell, altogether."
  * **Gloss:** The like-them warning — passive attendance as complicity — and the two classes merged at the gathering (cf. 4:87's resurrection-gathering, within-surah).
* **`[4:141]`** "Those who lie in wait for you: if you attain victory from Allah, they say, “Were we not with you?” But if the disbelievers get a turn, they say, “Did we not side with you, and defend you from the believers?”"
  * **Gloss:** The victory-waiters' double claim: afterglow-borrowing when you win, protection-racketing when they do — "lie in wait" is the same phrase as `[9:5]` (verified).
* **`[4:141]`** "Allah will judge between you on the Day of Resurrection; and Allah will give the disbelievers no means of overcoming the believers."
  * **Gloss:** The verdict-transfer to the Day, plus the no-overcoming clause: history's ledger is not the final one (Source: classical comfort-reading of this clause for the humiliated believers, Ibn Kathir on 4:141 — verify wording).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`qawwam / qist` / "stand firmly for justice" (4:135)**: the witness-verb of equity.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[5:8]` — "Be upright to Allah, witnessing with justice... Adhere to justice" (verified — the covenant-band's twin command); `[38:26]`, `[21:112]` (verified) — judicial qist; `[57:25]` — "that humanity may uphold justice" (verified) — books+iron for qiwam: the surah's verb is as-Saff's purpose.
* **`witnesses to Allah` (shuhada' l-llah)**: testimony as His, not the parties'.
  * **Elsewhere:** within-surah the witness-cluster is dense: 4:6 (Reckoner-witnessed handover), 4:33/4:41/4:79/4:87/4:135/4:166 — the "Allah suffices as witness" refrain (all verified by reading the lines) — 4:135 is the command-mode of the same office 4:41/4:166 describe.
* **"rejected, or ridiculed" / the mockery cluster (4:140)**: speech-crimes against the revelations.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[9:64]`, `[5:57]`, `[2:67]`, `[15:95]`, `[31:6]` carry the mock-family (verified "mock" scan); `[6:68]` supplies the identical seating-rule (verified) — 4:140 is the legal restatement of 6:68's discipline, with the "you would be like them" clause as its enforcement.
* **`sultan` / "a clear case" (4:144)**: the warrant-grammar in three robes — this surah also renders the same warrant-word "clear authorization" (4:91) and "clear authority" (4:153) (verified occurrences) — authorization to act, authority to prove, a case to answer (observable drift; word-identity per classical lexicons — agent — verify). Glory (4:139 "All glory belongs to Allah") is the possession the sultan-case is built on: alliance-shopping buys what is not for sale (agent observation; the exact phrase "glory belongs to Allah" — corpus singleton, verified).
