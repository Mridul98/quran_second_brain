# The inspiration-chain, the direct speech, and the Word to Mary (4:163–171)
**UID:** 202610141124
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 4:163–171 (Ruku 23 of 24 in an-Nisa')
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[4:163]` – `[4:171]` (9 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah's own credential is filed as inspiration-genealogy: "We have inspired you, as We had inspired Noah and the prophets after him" — a roll-call (Abraham, Ishmael, Isaac, Jacob, the Patriarchs, Jesus, Job, Jonah, Aaron, Solomon; David got the Psalms) — some messengers told, some not told, and Moses given direct speech; the messengers' function: good news and warning, so the excuse-plea is closed; then the witnesses clause — Allah bears witness, the angels bear witness, "though Allah is a sufficient witness" — and two oaths to the two audiences: "O people! The Messenger has come with the truth" and "O People of the Scripture! Do not exaggerate... do not say 'Three'": Jesus is Allah's Word conveyed to Mary, a Spirit from Him — one God, no son.
* **Placement:** The kalam-climax of the revelation-speech arc (4:26 "intends to make things clear" → 4:82 ponder it → 4:105 revealed with truth → here: the mode-list and the direct-speech apex) and the doctrinal partner of ruku 22's Jesus-sequence — the titulary denied to his killers (4:157) is asserted in full here (4:171).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[4:163]`** "We have inspired you, as We had inspired Noah and the prophets after him."
  * **Gloss:** Wahy as chain-of-command: the Prophet's inspiration is placed in a lineage-list, the same verb-form as `[16:123]` "Then We inspired you: Follow the religion of Abraham" (verified).
* **`[4:163]`** "And We inspired Abraham, and Ishmael, and Isaac, and Jacob, and the Patriarchs, and Jesus, and Job, and Jonah, and Aaron, and Solomon. And We gave David the Psalms."
  * **Gloss:** Ten named + the Psalms as a gift apart — the roll-call matches Baqarah's credo-list family (`[2:136]` "what was revealed to Abraham, and Ishmael, and Isaac, and Jacob, and the Patriarchs" — verified) and 3:84's twin; "Psalms" elsewhere at `[17:55]` and `[21:105]` (verified).
* **`[4:164]`** "Some messengers We have already told you about, while some messengers We have not told you about."
  * **Gloss:** The book's self-limitation — the kalam-note's signature evidence (it cites this exact verse): revelation discloses its own omissions.
* **`[4:164]`** "And Allah spoke to Moses directly."
  * **Gloss:** Kalim — the direct-speech apex; the phrase's only other site: `[2:253]` "To some of them Allah spoke directly" (verified) — 4:164 names whom 2:253 generalizes (observable pair; 7:143's appointment-scene — "his Lord spoke to him" — is the narrative twin).
* **`[4:165]`** "Messengers delivering good news, and bringing warnings; so that people may have no excuse before Allah after the coming of the messengers."
  * **Gloss:** The messenger-function binomial (bashir/nadhir — standard formula, e.g. `[3:39]` "gives you good news" verified; the pair-idiom spans the corpus) and the legal-theology clause: sending forecloses excuse ("no excuse before Allah" — unique phrasing, verified).
* **`[4:165]`** "Allah is Powerful and Wise."
  * **Gloss:** Power-knowledge close, the 4:56 family.
* **`[4:166]`** "But Allah bears witness to what He revealed to you. He revealed it with His knowledge. And the angels bear witness. Though Allah is a sufficient witness."
  * **Gloss:** The self-authentication: divine testimony to the revelation's origin, angels seconded, then the kafayya-refrain's crowning use — "Allah is a sufficient witness" (the witness-cluster's summit, cf. 4:79 "Witness enough"); 3:18's structure "Allah bears witness... as do the angels... the Mighty, the Wise" is the exact twin (verified).
* **`[4:167]`** "Those who disbelieve and repel from Allah’s path have gone far astray."
  * **Gloss:** The "far astray" verdict-phrase again (4:116/4:136 family — within-surah triple, observable).
* **`[4:168]`** "Those who disbelieve and transgress; Allah is not about to forgive them, nor will He guide them to any path."
  * **Gloss:** The unforgiven pair: disbelief-plus-transgression closes both doors — forgiveness and guidance (cf. 4:137's identical double-closure on the oscillators, within-surah).
* **`[4:169]`** "Except to the path of Hell, where they will dwell forever. And that is easy for Allah."
  * **Gloss:** The grim punchline — the only "path" left is the one out of guidance; "that is easy for Allah" repeats 4:30's line in form (verified by reading: 4:30 "and that would be easy for Allah") — the surah's ease-clause attached twice to its own punishments.
* **`[4:170]`** "O people! The Messenger has come to you with the truth from your Lord, so believe— that is best for you."
  * **Gloss:** One of the surah's "O people!" frames (4:1/4:133/4:174 — cluster verified by reading): the universal summons; belief stated as best-interest, not coercion.
* **`[4:170]`** "But if you disbelieve, to Allah belongs everything in the heavens and the earth. Allah is Omniscient and Wise."
  * **Gloss:** The ownership-refrain (4:126/128-family/131/132, observable cluster) answering disbelief's futility; knowledge close again.
* **`[4:171]`** "O People of the Scripture! Do not exaggerate in your religion, and do not say about Allah except the truth."
  * **Gloss:** The ghuluw-ban — the twin command at `[5:77]` "do not exaggerate in your religion beyond the truth" (verified) — the surah-pair of the correction.
* **`[4:171]`** "The Messiah, Jesus, the son of Mary, is the Messenger of Allah, and His Word that He conveyed to Mary, and a Spirit from Him."
  * **Gloss:** Four affirmations and one negation's edge: Word (`[3:45]` "a Word from Him. His name is the Messiah" — verified; 3:39's John "confirming a Word from Allah" — verified) and Spirit (`[2:87]` "supported him with the Holy Spirit" — verified site of the Spirit-idiom).
* **`[4:171]`** "So believe in Allah and His messengers, and do not say, “Three.” Refrain—it is better for you."
  * **Gloss:** The plural-denial — the direct answer to `[5:73]` "Allah is the third of three" (verified): the two surahs prosecute the same formula in mirrored phrasing.
* **`[4:171]`** "Allah is only one God. Glory be to Him—that He should have a son."
  * **Gloss:** The tawhid-clause + tanzih — k-tawhid's "unbegotten and unpartnered" register (112-family).
* **`[4:171]`** "To Him belongs everything in the heavens and the earth, and Allah is a sufficient Protector."
  * **Gloss:** Ownership + the final kafayya — "sufficient Protector," the same English formula as 4:45 "sufficient as a Protector" (verified by reading) — the surah's "Allah suffices/is sufficient" constellation (Reckoner 4:6, Protector/Supporter 4:45, Witness 4:79/166, Knower 4:70, Guardian 4:81, Manager 4:132, here Protector again) wraps the whole book in the trust-formula; which office-noun sits under each English robe is classical lexical detail — agent — verify.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`wahy` / "inspired"**: the channel-verb (the kalam-note's mode-1).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[16:123]`, `[42:7]`, `[42:52]` (all verified matches of "inspired you") — and 42:51 (kalam note's own three-modes verse) classifies wahy as permitted speech-mode; 4:163 files this Prophet inside that first category, with the direct-speech of 4:164 as the second (mode-2, "from behind a veil" — Moses case classically) — Source: classical tafsir alignment of 4:164 with 42:51's veil-mode — verify.
* **`kalama` / "spoke... directly"**: the unique attribute-clause of Moses.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:253]` (verified — same adverbial construction), `[7:143]` (narrative scene — verified wording "his Lord spoke to him").
* **`zubur` / "the Psalms"**: the scripture-as-book given to David.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[17:55]` "We gave David the Psalms" (verified exact twin), `[21:105]` (verified) — the corpus's only book-names besides Torah/Gospel/Furqan-family.
* **`ghuluw` / "exaggerate"**: religious overshoot.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[5:77]` (verified) — the pair 4:171/5:77 is the Quran's two direct anti-exaggeration commands, both to People of the Scripture, both adjacent to Trinity material (5:72–78 context — verified site list).
