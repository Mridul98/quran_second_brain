# The household, the tenfold social worship, and the atom's weight (4:34–42)
**UID:** 202610141107
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 4:34–42 (Ruku 6 of 24 in an-Nisa')
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[4:34]` – `[4:42]` (9 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The law turns to the household's failure modes — the husband's responsibility clause and the three-step response to feared disloyalty, then the two-family arbitration when the breach spreads — and suddenly widens into the surah's social decalogue: worship Allah, associate nothing with Him, then nine beneficiaries of goodness, parents through traveler, with arrogance as its anti-model; and everything is then priced: stinginess, show-spend, an atom's weight of injustice, doubled good deeds, and the Day when a witness is brought from every community.
* **Placement:** End of the family-constitution block (ruku 1–6); 4:36 is the ethical center of the surah — the 2:83/2:177 covenant-list restated at the level of the household — and 4:41 pre-enacts the courtroom frame that the hypocrite dossier (ruku 9+) will fill.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[4:34]`** "Men are the protectors and maintainers of women, as Allah has given some of them an advantage over others, and because they spend out of their wealth."
  * **Gloss:** Qiwam assigned with two stated causes — a given advantage and expenditure from wealth; the verse's own "because" structure grounds the duty in the spending obligation of 4:4/4:25 (Source: agent reading of the causal clauses; classical comment ties it to maintenance duty — Ibn Kathir/al-Qurtubi on 4:34 — verify).
* **`[4:34]`** "The good women are obedient, guarding what Allah would have them guard."
  * **Gloss:** The counterparty's virtue phrased as guardianship of what Allah placed in her keeping — not of herself.
* **`[4:34]`** "As for those from whom you fear disloyalty, admonish them, and abandon them in their beds, then strike them. But if they obey you, seek no way against them. Allah is Sublime, Great."
  * **Gloss:** The three graduated steps against nushuz are stated in sequence, and the verse closes by barring the husband any further way against a compliant wife; the limits on the third step (light, non-injurious, last resort; "do not strike Allah's handmaidens") are classical fiqh and prophetic-report material, not the ayah's wording — Source: classical tafsir/fiqh reference (widely cited; verify specific narrations).
* **`[4:35]`** "If you fear a breach between the two, appoint an arbiter from his family and an arbiter from her family. If they wish to reconcile, Allah will bring them together."
  * **Gloss:** When the pair cannot self-correct, two family-hakams are ordered; reconciliation is divinely backed — the surah's dispute-design: private, gendered parity, familial.
* **`[4:35]`** "Allah is Knowledgeable, Expert."
  * **Gloss:** Knowledge-refrain again (khabir — "Expert"): the arbiter's audit above the arbiters.
* **`[4:36]`** "Worship Allah, and ascribe no partners to Him, and be good to the parents, and the relatives, and the orphans, and the poor, and the neighbor next door, and the distant neighbor, and the close associate, and the traveler, and your servants."
  * **Gloss:** One theological term then nine social ones — the community's decalogue; the list's center of gravity is proximity: kin, neighbor, associate, wayfarer, dependent.
* **`[4:36]`** "Allah does not love the arrogant showoff."
  * **Gloss:** The anti-model in two traits at once: arrogance (khubr) and display — the hinge into the wealth-verses.
* **`[4:37]`** "Those who are stingy, and exhort people to stinginess, and conceal what Allah has given them from His bounty. We have prepared for the disbelievers a disgraceful punishment."
  * **Gloss:** Stinginess as contagious pedagogy ("exhort people to stinginess") — 9:34's hoarders' twin; concealment of bounty closes the list's opening verb (ascribe/conceal).
* **`[4:38]`** "And those who spend their money to be seen by people, and believe neither in Allah nor in the Last Day. Whoever has Satan as a companion—what an evil companion."
  * **Gloss:** Show-spend is unbelief's economics; the companion (qarin) idiom foreshadows Satan's "due share of Your servants" at 4:118-120.
* **`[4:39]`** "What would they have lost, had they believed in Allah and the Last Day, and gave out of what Allah has provided for them? Allah knows them very well."
  * **Gloss:** The counterfactual question form — the whole portfolio was always "what Allah has provided."
* **`[4:40]`** "Allah does not commit an atom's weight of injustice; and if there is a good deed, He doubles it, and gives from His Presence a sublime compensation."
  * **Gloss:** The scale: zero loss on the negative side, doubling on the positive — the surah's audit-grammar in one verse.
* **`[4:41]`** "Then how will it be, when We bring a witness from every community, and We bring you as a witness against these?"
  * **Gloss:** The courtroom opens: per-community witnesses, and this community as witness over the others — the prophetic witness-function (cf. 2:143's "witnesses over mankind" family).
* **`[4:42]`** "On that Day, those who disbelieved and disobeyed the Messenger will wish that the earth were leveled over them. They will conceal nothing from Allah."
  * **Gloss:** The burial-wish of the disobedient; concealment fails — the theme that the next ruku's hypocrites will re-enact at 4:46–50 and 4:81.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`qawwam` / "protectors and maintainers"** (4:34) and **"stand firmly"** (4:135): one q-w-m family, two offices — household custodianship and judicial uprightness; cf. `[5:8]` "Be upright to Allah, witnessing with justice" (verified — same imperative family).
  * **Rendering drift:** the noun "protectors" elsewhere in the corpus usually renders awliya (allies) — `[42:9]`, `[17:97]` (verified via rg "protectors"); 4:34 alone renders qawwam with the doubled phrase "protectors and maintainers" (observable).
* **`hakam` / "arbiter"** (4:35): the only English "arbiter" in the corpus; the same root's verb appears at `[4:65]` "call you to arbitrate in their disputes" (verified) — the household's family-judge and the community's prophet-judge share one word.
* **`dharr` / "an atom's weight"** (4:40): the metrology of reckoning.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[99:7–8]` (the atom-weight verdict-pair), `[10:61]`, `[34:3]` (verified) — where used, the atom-weight clause always anchors either justice or knowledge; 4:40 binds both.
* **`qarin` / "companion"** (4:38): "Whoever has Satan as a companion—what an evil companion."
  * **Elsewhere:** the same idiom of an evil assigned companion at `[43:38]` "We assign for him a devil, so he becomes an evil companion to him" (verified); and the Day-friendship reversal at `[43:67]`, `[25:28]` "I never took so-andso for a friend" (verified) — companionship is the surah's word for chosen allegiance, and 4:119 will price it as "a profound loss."

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Medinan (classical consensus) — the household-management and social-welfare verses function as the family-constitution's enforcement chapter.
* **Asbab / narrative context:** none specific transmitted for 4:34–36; classical commentaries read 4:34 against pre-Islamic marital arbitrariness and tie the arbitration of 4:35 to family councils (Source: classical tafsir general framing — no verified single asbab; agent note).
* **Legal content, neutrally stated:** the text orders the husband's qiwam framed by two stated causes, the graduated response to feared disloyalty with a hard stop against further coercion, and the two-family arbitration; it commands nine social goods under the one theological term; it guarantees atom-weight justice and doubling reward. All procedural detail (enforcement, thresholds, court machinery) is classical fiqh extension — Source: classical tafsir/fiqh reference.
