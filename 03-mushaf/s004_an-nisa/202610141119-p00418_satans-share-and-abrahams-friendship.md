# The one unforgiven sin, Satan's claimed share, and Abraham the friend (4:116–126)
**UID:** 202610141119
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 4:116–126 (Ruku 18 of 24 in an-Nisa')
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[4:116]` – `[4:126]` (11 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The shirk-verdict returns (near-verbatim doublet of 4:48) and the surah pulls back the curtain on the adversary's program: the "females" they invoke are the rebellious devil in disguise, who has announced his due share — mislead, entice, slit cattle-ears, alter Allah's creation — a portfolio of promises that are pure delusion; and against that claim the counter-claim is filed in four clauses: the believers enter Gardens by a promise whose truth-question ("who is more truthful in speech than Allah?") is unappealable; salvation runs not by anyone's wishes but by works — male or female, alike — and in the religion of Abraham the Monotheist, whom Allah took for a friend; for to Allah belongs everything, and He encompasses everything.
* **Placement:** The creedal keystone of the second half: the doublet architecture (4:48/4:116) brackets the surah's central dossier, and the wish-verses (4:123–124) answer the entitlements of 4:53–4:55 (a share of the kingdom) and 4:117 (claimed intercession) with the surah's flattest equality clause: whoever works righteousness enters, male or female, wronged "a whit" — the 4:49 idiom resumed.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[4:116]`** "Allah will not forgive that partners be associated with Him; but will forgive anything less than that, to whomever He wills."
  * **Gloss:** The doublet of 4:48 — same rule, reworded ("strayed into far error" replaces "devised a monstrous sin"): association is the one sin structurally unforgiven because it corrupts the category of forgiveness itself (agent gloss of the pairing; classical treatment at both sites — verify).
* **`[4:116]`** "Anyone who ascribes partners to Allah has strayed into far error."
  * **Gloss:** The error-verb (dalala kubra) echoes 4:44's "buy error"; deviation is spatial — astray, far.
* **`[4:117]`** "They invoke in His stead only females. In fact, they invoke none but a rebellious devil."
  * **Gloss:** The feminine names invoked are reclassified: goddess-worship is devil-worship; "rebellious" (marid — the same epithet at `[51:53]` and `[37:30]`, verified) fixes Satan's legal status: expelled, inscribed rebel.
* **`[4:118]`** "Allah has cursed him. And he said, “I will take to myself my due share of Your servants.”"
  * **Gloss:** The curse on the devil (4:46/47/52's refrain turned upward; cf. `[5:13/5:60/5:78]` curse-sites, verified) — and the adversary's speech-act: a "due share" (hazz) claimed as entitlement, a counter-predestination sneered at Allah's distribution.
* **`[4:119]`** "“And I will mislead them, and I will entice them, and I will prompt them to slit the ears of cattle, and I will prompt them to alter the creation of Allah.”"
  * **Gloss:** The four-part program: mislead (cf. 4:113's "mislead you"), inflate expectations (tameannī — the wish-disease of 4:123), ritual mutilation of livestock (the bahira/saiba family named at `[5:103]` "superstitions... Bahirah, Saibah, Wasilah" — verified), and the umbrella: tabdil khalq Allah — "alter the creation of Allah," the corpus's only such claim-here (verified hapax).
* **`[4:119]`** "Whoever takes Satan as a lord, instead of Allah, has surely suffered a profound loss."
  * **Gloss:** Lordship (waliya) is the loss-switch — cf. 4:38's evil companion and 4:76's "allies of the Devil" (within-surah, verified); "profound loss" (khusran mubin) is the phrase's only corpus site (verified).
* **`[4:120]`** "He promises them, and he raises their expectations, but Satan promises them nothing but delusions."
  * **Gloss:** The economics of nifaq exposed: promise + tamanni vs. Allah's binding promise (4:122) — "delusions" (ghurur) elsewhere at `[3:185]` and `[17:64]` (verified — same verb-pair "he promises" at 17:64!).
* **`[4:121]`** "These—their place is Hell, and they will find no escape from it."
  * **Gloss:** The program's terminus; "no escape" (mhays — cf. 4:47's obliteration clause family) closes the devil's claim on space.
* **`[4:122]`** "But as for those who believe and do righteous deeds, We will admit them into gardens beneath which rivers flow, where they will abide forever."
  * **Gloss:** The counter-portrait in the surah's stock Gardens-formula (4:13, 4:57 — verified by reading: the third occurrence).
* **`[4:122]`** "The promise of Allah is true— and who is more truthful in speech than Allah?"
  * **Gloss:** The kalam-question — speech-truth as divine attribute; 4:87's "who speaks more truly" in its promise-mode (within-surah pair).
* **`[4:123]`** "It is not in accordance with your wishes, nor in accordance with the wishes of the People of the Scripture."
  * **Gloss:** The wish-correction: salvation by tameannī — the very inflation Satan sells (4:119/120) — is denied to both parties symmetrically (classical reports read this against Jewish "days numbered" and Christian-election claims — Source: classical tafsir — verify).
* **`[4:123]`** "Whoever works evil will pay for it, and will not find for himself, besides Allah, any protector or savior."
  * **Gloss:** The works-ledger opens; "protector or savior" (waliyy/nasir) — the exact pair of the helpless' prayer at 4:75 (verified by reading): what the oppressed petition Allah for, the evildoer will not find elsewhere.
* **`[4:124]`** "But whoever works righteousness, whether male or female, and is a believer— those will enter Paradise, and will not be wronged a whit."
  * **Gloss:** The surah's equality clause — sex-indifferent works plus faith-condition, fenced by "a whit" (4:49's idiom resumed — verified pair); cf. `[3:195]` "whether male or female... work of any worker... not wasted" (verified).
* **`[4:125]`** "And who is better in religion than he who submits himself wholly to Allah, and is a doer of good, and follows the faith of Abraham the Monotheist?"
  * **Gloss:** Islam (self-submission), ihsan (good-work), Abrahamic faith (hanifiyya) — the three-term definition; the surrender-verb's other sites `[2:112]`, `[31:22]` (verified), and Abraham's own "I have submitted" at `[2:131]` (verified).
* **`[4:125]`** "Allah has chosen Abraham for a friend."
  * **Gloss:** The khalil-verse — God's election of intimacy; the corpus's ironic twin is `[17:73]` "they would have taken you for a friend" and `[25:28]` "I wish I never took so-and-so for a friend" (both verified): friend-language for allegiance, here claimed only by God.
* **`[4:126]`** "To Allah belongs what is in the heavens and what is on earth, and Allah encompasses everything."
  * **Gloss:** The ownership-and-encirclement close — the surah's sovereignty refrain (4:131/4:132/4:171/4:173-family — observable cluster) in its smallest statement.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`khalq Allah` / "the creation of Allah" + "alter"** (4:119): the tabdil claim, stated once in the corpus (verified hapax) — its scope (anatomy? ritual marks? covenant signs?) is a classical debate — Source: tafsir ranges on 4:119 (ear-slitting read with the bahira customs; broader readings later) — verify before teaching it.
* **`hazz` / "due share"** (4:118): the set-portion — the same word renders the inheritance "share" family elsewhere (4:7's "a legal share" is fard, while "share" as English is used for naseeb/hazz elsewhere — observable drift; agent root-assignment — verify).
* **`tameannī` / "raises their expectations" / "wishes"** (4:119–123): the wish-noun runs from Satan's program (4:119 "entice"), to his method (4:120 "delusions"), to the salvation-denial (4:123 "not in accordance with your wishes") — one disease, three exposures (observable within-surah chain).
* **`hanif` / "the Monotheist"** (4:125):
  * **Elsewhere:** `[16:123]` — "Follow the religion of Abraham, the Monotheist" (verified), `[3:67]` (verified in the s003 build's quoted line) — the epithet is always Abrahamic, never sectarian; cf. 3:67–68's s003 treatment.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Medinan; classical readers pair 4:116's doublet with 4:48 as the dossier's two verdicts (one at the People-of-the-Book band's start, one at the hypocrite band's close) — observable placement reading, agent synthesis.

The wish-verse 4:123 is classically tied to reports about both communities' presumptive election claims (Ibn Kathir on 4:123 citing 2:111/2:124 exchanges) — verify wording.
* **Legal content, neutrally stated:** none — this is a creed ruku; its practical clause is 4:124's works-condition, which the schools read as faith-joined necessity, not merit-accounting — Source: classical theology (the murji'a/karama debates orbit these very verses) — verify attributions.
