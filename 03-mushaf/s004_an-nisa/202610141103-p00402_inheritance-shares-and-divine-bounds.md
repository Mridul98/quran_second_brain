# The fraction-table: inheritance as divine bounds (4:11–14)
**UID:** 202610141103
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 4:11–14 (Ruku 2 of 24 in an-Nisa')
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[4:11]` – `[4:14]` (4 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah's most concrete legislation: Allah "instructs" the fixed shares — children (male = two female; daughters two-thirds or one-half; parents a sixth or a third depending on survivors), spouses (half/quarter for him, quarter/eighth for her), lateral siblings a sixth each sharing a third — each tranche twice fenced by "After fulfilling any bequest and paying off debts," then sealed as *hudud*: "These are the bounds set by Allah," with the twin endings of the great attainment or the shameful punishment.
* **Placement:** Directly after 4:7's universal share-principle (ruku 1); the arithmetic the ring-structure needs to close at 4:176's final kalala ruling (ruku 24) — the same lateral-sibling case, answered book-to-bookend (observable).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[4:11]`** "Allah instructs you regarding your children: The male receives the equivalent of the share of two females."
  * **Gloss:** The opening ratio, given as instruction from Allah, not custom: shares track a stated logic (classical reading: load-bearing financial duty — Source: classical tafsir, Ibn Kathir/al-Qurtubi on 4:11 — agent summary, verify).
* **`[4:11]`** "If they are daughters, more than two, they get two-thirds of what he leaves. If there is only one, she gets one-half."
  * **Gloss:** Daughters: two-thirds when a plurality, one-half when single — the fractions stated as law's own numbers.
* **`[4:11]`** "As for the parents, each gets one-sixth of what he leaves, if he had children. If he had no children, and his parents inherit from him, his mother gets one-third. If he has siblings, his mother gets one-sixth."
  * **Gloss:** Parental shares flex by survivors: a sixth each with issue; the mother a third without issue, reduced to a sixth when siblings exist.
* **`[4:11]`** "After fulfilling any bequest and paying off debts. Your parents and your children—you do not know which are closer to you in welfare. This is Allah's Law. Allah is Knowing and Judicious."
  * **Gloss:** The twice-repeated precondition (will and debt first), an epistemic humility line, and the closing refrain — "Allah's Law" (the *hudud* word rendered as Law here), "Knowing and Judicious."
* **`[4:12]`** "You get one-half of what your wives leave behind, if they had no children. If they had children, you get one-fourth of what they leave."
  * **Gloss:** Husband's share: half, or a quarter with issue.
* **`[4:12]`** "After fulfilling any bequest and paying off debts. They get one-fourth of what you leave behind, if you have no children. If you have children, they get one-eighth of what you leave."
  * **Gloss:** Wives' share: a quarter, or an eighth with issue; the bequest-debt fence recurs mid-verse.
* **`[4:12]`** "After fulfilling any bequest and paying off debts. If a man or woman leaves neither parents nor children, but has a brother or sister, each of them gets one-sixth. If there are more siblings, they share one-third."
  * **Gloss:** The *kalala* case — no ascendant, no descendant — for the first time (the word is rendered by description, "neither parents nor children"); lateral siblings a sixth each, sharing a third in plurality. (Earlier corpus extraction read "onethird" — a line-wrap hyphen artifact, corrected 2026-10-14.)
* **`[4:12]`** "After fulfilling any bequest and paying off debts, without any prejudice. This is a will from Allah. Allah is Knowing and Clement."
  * **Gloss:** Fourth recurrence of the fence plus "without any prejudice"; the table called "a will from Allah" (wasiyya — divine testament); refrain: "Knowing and Clement."
* **`[4:13]`** "These are the bounds set by Allah. Whoever obeys Allah and His Messenger, He will admit him into Gardens beneath which rivers flow, to abide therein forever. That is the great attainment."
  * **Gloss:** The arithmetic becomes covenant-boundary: obedience to *these* limits earns the Garden; "the great attainment" (fawz al-a'zam, 5:119's phrase for the nearest companions).
* **`[4:14]`** "But whoever disobeys Allah and His Messenger, and oversteps His bounds, He will admit him into a Fire, wherein he abides forever, and he will have a shameful punishment."
  * **Gloss:** The mirror clause: the same hudud, transgressed, re-bound the hearer in the same vocabulary.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`hudud` / "bounds set by Allah"**: the limit-terms of the law; here applied to the inheritance table.
  * **Rendering:** "the bounds set by Allah" (4:13), "oversteps His bounds" (4:14); cognate law-phrase "These are Allah's limits" ("limits") at `[2:229]` divorce, `[2:230]`, `[2:187]` fasting, `[65:1]` waiting periods, and inside this surah at `[4:24]` ("outside these limits").
  * **Elsewhere:** the h-d-d family always couples transgression with eschatology (2:229–230) — exactly the 4:13–14 form.
* **`wasiyya` / "bequest"**: the testamentary charge that must be settled before shares move.
  * **Rendering:** "After fulfilling any bequest and paying off debts" — a refrain, four times in two ayahs.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[5:106]` — the bequest witness-clause (verified rg); see also `[2:180]`'s testament duty handled in [al-Baqarah ruku 22](../s002_al-baqarah/202610140932-p00222_birr_retaliation_and_testaments.md) (this edition's rendering there uses "testament"; the classical abrogation debate restricting bequests to non-heirs is *fiqh*, not the wording here — Source: classical tafsir/fiqh reference, e.g. reports on "no bequest to an heir" — verify).
* **The fraction set** — "one-half", "two-thirds", "one-third", "one-sixth", "one-fourth", "one-eighth".
  * **Rendering:** the only Quranic passage using this fraction-family as estate arithmetic; the same numerals appear elsewhere for the *night's* portions, `[73:20]` "two-thirds of the night, or half of it, or one-third of it" (verified; an earlier extraction artifact here was corrected 2026-10-14).
* **`fawz al-a'zam` / "the great attainment"**: 4:13's reward label for obeying the bounds.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[5:119]` — "That is the great attainment" for Allah's-pleasing companions (verified) — the phrase otherwise unused.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Medinan — the inheritance table is among the surah's defining Medinan legislation (classical consensus).
* **Asbab / narrative context:** classical reports link these shares to the estate of Sa'd ibn al-Rabi's daughters (the widow and two daughters came to the Prophet; the pre-Islamic practice gave women and minors nothing), narrated by Ibn Kathir and earlier compilations on 4:11 — Source: classical asbab reports, agent condensation; verify wording/chains.
* **Legal content, neutrally stated:** the text orders fixed fractions per survivor-set, with debts and bequests paid first, and obedience to the table framed as obedience to Allah and His Messenger. Everything beyond (graduated residuaries `asaba`, `awl` proportion reduction, exclusion rules for certain heirs) is jurisprudence extrapolated from these verses — Source: classical tafsir/fiqh reference (the schools' systems; not the ayah wording).
