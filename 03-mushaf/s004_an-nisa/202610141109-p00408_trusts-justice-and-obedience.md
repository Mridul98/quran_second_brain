# Trusts, justice, and the architecture of obedience (4:51–59)
**UID:** 202610141109
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 4:51–59 (Ruku 8 of 24 in an-Nisa')
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[4:51]` – `[4:59]` (9 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Envy is read as a theological position: those given a share of the Book "believe in superstition and evil powers" and rank the disbelievers above the believers — and the surah answers with the anatomy of grace (the family of Abraham got Book, wisdom, and a great kingdom; whoever Allah curses has no savior, and the two opposite fates — skinned fire and shaded gardens — are laid out in back-to-back verses) before the unit lands on the community's own charter: render trusts, judge with justice between people, obey Allah, His Messenger, and those in authority among you — and route every dispute back to the same two names.
* **Placement:** Concludes the Scriptural-distortion movement begun at 4:44; 4:58–59 is the hinge verse-pair of the surah's public-law half — "judge with justice" (4:58) will be repriced at 4:135 (ruku 20) as witnessing *against yourselves*.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[4:51]`** "Have you not considered those who were given a share of the Book? They believe in superstition and evil powers, and say of those who disbelieve, “These are better guided on the way than the believers.”"
  * **Gloss:** The second "have you not considered" of the surah's set (verified): faith in jibt-and-taghut-objects, expressed as a ranking claim about guidance — a direct counter-claim to 4:44's "wish you would lose the way."
* **`[4:52]`** "Those are they whom Allah has cursed. Whomever Allah curses, you will find no savior for him."
  * **Gloss:** The curse-refrain's second strike in this band (4:46 → 4:52 → 4:118); "no savior" — the waliyy-word is the same one 4:45 just called "sufficient."
* **`[4:53]`** "Or do they own a share of the kingdom? Then they would not give people a speck."
  * **Gloss:** Reductio: if they held dominion, they'd withhold even a "speck" (naqir — the date-stone's dimple) — greed diagnosed as a claim to co-sovereignty.
* **`[4:54]`** "Or do they envy the people for what Allah has given them of His grace? We have given the family of Abraham the Book and wisdom, and We have given them a great kingdom."
  * **Gloss:** Envy (hasad) named as the motor; the reply is genealogy-of-grace: Book + wisdom + kingdom already given to Abraham's family — the same triad the community is receiving.
* **`[4:55]`** "Among them are those who believed in it, and among them are those who held back from it. Hell is a sufficient Inferno."
  * **Gloss:** The family-of-grace splits by response, not blood — and the kafayya-formula reappears ("sufficient").
* **`[4:56]`** "Those who reject Our revelations—We will scorch them in a Fire. Every time their skins are cooked, We will replace them with other skins, so they will experience the suffering."
  * **Gloss:** Revelation-rejection priced in renewed flesh; "Our revelations" — again the speech-verbs of the kalam-grammar (rejecting *sayings*, not facts).
* **`[4:56]`** "Allah is Most Powerful, Most Wise."
  * **Gloss:** The power-knowledge refrain.
* **`[4:57]`** "As for those who believe and do good deeds, We will admit them into Gardens beneath which rivers flow, abiding therein forever. They will have purified spouses therein, and We will admit them into a shady shade."
  * **Gloss:** The twin clause: same pair-form as 4:13; "purified spouses" (azwaj mutahhara) answers 4:49's false self-purification with real, given purity — observable ring in three verses.
* **`[4:58]`** "Allah instructs you to give back things entrusted to you to their owners. And when you judge between people, judge with justice. Allah’s instructions to you are excellent. Allah is All-Hearing, All-Seeing."
  * **Gloss:** The public-law charter in one ayah: trust-restitution and judicial qist, bracketed by "Allah's instructions are excellent" and the hearing-seeing names — the same h-d-d-law cadence as the inheritance band.
* **`[4:59]`** "O you who believe! Obey Allah and obey the Messenger and those in authority among you. And if you dispute over anything, refer it to Allah and the Messenger, if you believe in Allah and the Last Day. That is best, and a most excellent determination."
  * **Gloss:** The obedience chain — Allah, Messenger, ulu-l-amr — with the dispute-circuit back to the first two; "if you believe" makes the return the test of the profession (the same structure 4:65 will make an oath).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`jibt` + `taghut` / "superstition and evil powers"** (4:51): the paired nonsense-powers.
  * **Rendering:** the taghut-family is rendered "evil powers" here and "Satanic sources" at `[4:60]` (both verified — the same surah uses two English registers, with 4:60's own clause glossing the referent through Satan's intent); classical lexing: jibt = sorcery/false objects, taghut = the tyrant/deceiver beyond due limits — Source: classical tafsir (Ibn Kathir on 4:51 collecting the Companion glosses) — verify.
* **`naqir` / "a speck"** (4:53): the date-pit's dimple — the measure of withheld nothing.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[35:13]` — the invoked-none "do not own even a speck" (verified) — same metrology for false partners; cf. this surah's "a whit" pair 4:49/4:124 (verified) — the small-things cluster guards both justice and tawhid.
* **`amanat` / "things entrusted"** (4:58).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[3:75]` — "if you entrust him with a heap of gold, he will give it back to you" (verified) — the People-of-the-Book honesty portrait, here turned into the community's general trust-law; the term's other famous site (33:72's offered weights) uses different vocabulary in this translation.
* **`ulu-l-amr` / "those in authority"** (4:59): exactly two occurrences in the corpus, both in this surah — `[4:59]` and `[4:83]` (verified) — the second one ("refer it to the Messenger and to those in authority among them") is the internal gloss on the first; the identity of the referents (rulers? judges? commanders?) is classical dispute — Source: range of positions in classical tafsir, agent condensation — verify.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Medinan (classical consensus); the address "O you who believe!" at 4:59 marks the community's own constitutional register.
* **Asbab / narrative context:** 4:58 is classically tied to the restoring of the Ka'bah key to Uthman ibn Talha on the day of the conquest (transmitted in the Sirah/tafsir corpora — Ibn Kathir on 4:58) — Source: classical asbab — verify; the "entrust" wording generalizes the case (agent observation). 4:51–54 classically read against rabbinic-envy reports concerning prophethood in the Arab line (Ibn Kathir family) — agent condensation — verify.
* **Legal content, neutrally stated:** the text orders trust-restitution to owners, impartial justice between all people (not only believers), and a dispute-routing chain; the scope of ulu-l-amr obedience (and its limits when commanding sin) is *fiqh*/tafsir extrapolation — Source: classical positions, e.g. "no obedience to creation in disobedience of the Creator" hadith used to fence 4:59 — verify attribution before quoting.
