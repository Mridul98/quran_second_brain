# Surah 4: WOMEN (an-Nisa')
**UID:** 202610141101
**Type:** Index / Surah Map

## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Medinan — classical consensus treats the whole surah as Medinan (a rare minority report attaches `[4:1]`'s address to the Makkan period; not adopted here). Source: consensus of classical tafsir (Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi surah intros) and classical chronological lists, which place it 92nd by revelation order (al-Suyuti, *al-Itqan*, commonly cited ordering — Source: classical consensus lists; verify against canonical order tables).
* **Size:** 176 ayahs, 24 ruku — the fourth surah, the second-longest of the Medinan block after al-Baqarah.
* **Central theme:** **The architecture of the community** — guardianship of the vulnerable (orphans, women, the helpless), inheritance as divinely fixed arithmetic, marriage and conjugal ethics, property ethics, justice as witnessed testimony, and the outer limit of the hypocrites. The surah's famous heading "The Women" opens with the one-soul creation (`[4:1]` — one *nafs*, its mate, many men and women propagated) and closes its family-law cycles with knowledge-refrains: the surah brackets its legal units with "Allah is Knowing and Judicious" (`[4:11]`), "Knowing and Clement" (`[4:12]`), "All-Knowing, Most Wise" (`[4:24]`), "Allah is Watchful over you" (`[4:1]`), and finishes the whole with "Allah makes things clear for you, lest you err. Allah is Aware of everything" (`[4:176]`) — the arithmetic of shares is fenced by an All-Aware witness (observable corpus fact: these refrain-lines close the share/ruling paragraphs).
* **Address rhythm (observable):** three "O people!" addresses frame the argument (`[4:1]` at the opening, `[4:170]` and `[4:174]` in the closing movement), while "O you who believe!" drives the legal and martial bands (`[4:19]`, `[4:29]`, `[4:43]`, `[4:59]`, `[4:71]`, `[4:94]`, `[4:101]`, `[4:135]`, `[4:136]`, `[4:144]`).

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Family-wealth constitution (ruku 1–5, 4:1–33):** one origin (one soul), then the vulnerable protected by law: orphan property, marriage ceilings, dowry as gift, the fraction-table of inheritance, the repentance valve, the prohibition lists, and the meta-line that states Allah's intent — to make clear, redeem, lighten (4:26–28) — since humans are weak and covetous (4:28, 4:32).
* **Household and society as worship (ruku 6–8, 4:34–59):** the household's two arbiters, then the tenfold social decalogue (4:36), the atom's-weight of justice (4:40), then the hinge to communal discipline: prayer purity (4:43), the distorted Scripture (4:46), trusts and just judgment (4:58), obedience architecture (4:59).
* **The hypocrite dossier (ruku 9–13, 4:60–96):** arbitration refused and sought (4:60–65), mobilization and the laggard (4:71–76), death and doctrine (4:77–87), faction and treaty (4:88–91), and blood-law with its investigation duty (4:92–96).
* **Movement's fiqh (ruku 14–15, 4:97–104):** emigration when oppression blocks practice; prayer shortened and in ranks of fear — the community's rites survive war and flight.
* **Justice, counsel, and creed (ruku 16–18, 4:105–126):** no advocacy for the traitorous; the value of private counsels; the one unforgivable sin, Satan's declared program, and the equal-wage creed — male or female — anchored in Abraham's hanifiyya.
* **Second women-cycle and the hypocrites' end (ruku 19–21, 4:127–152):** the ruling about widowed women answered back to ruku 1–3 (4:127 rings 4:3's fairness command, observable), reconciliation, the impossibility of perfect equity, then the justice-witness verse (4:135) and the hypocrites' final sorting (4:138–152).
* **People of the Book and closure (ruku 22–24, 4:153–176):** the covenant narrative — Mount, pledge, breach, the claimed killing of the Messiah denied and raised — then the revelation-speech climax (4:163–166, Allah spoke to Moses directly), the trim-and-true word about Jesus (4:171), and the final kalala ruling with the clarity refrain (4:176).
