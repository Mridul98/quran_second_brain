# "We only intended goodwill": the hypocrites' arbitration test (4:60–70)
**UID:** 202610141110
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 4:60–70 (Ruku 9 of 24 in an-Nisa')
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[4:60]` – `[4:70]` (11 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The hypocrites are profiled through their dispute-behavior: they claim belief in what was revealed *and* run to satanic sources for judgment, shun the Prophet when summoned to the revealed, then swear by Allah after calamity "we only intended goodwill and reconciliation"; Allah knows their hearts, so the command is to turn, admonish, and speak penetrating words — and the ruku answers with the theology of messengership: no messenger was sent except to be obeyed by Allah's leave; repentance-and-Messenger-prayer finds Allah Relenting; they will not believe until they call you to arbitrate, feel no resentment, and submit completely; and the reward-chain is stated as company — the prophets, the sincere, the martyrs, the upright: "Excellent are those as companions."
* **Placement:** Opens the hypocrite dossier proper (the label "hypocrites" first appears in this surah at 4:61 — in this edition's ordering the noun occurs nowhere in al-Baqarah's pretense-portrait 2:8–20 (verified: rg in s002 empty; 3:167 is the only pre-4 occurrence) — the dossier names what Baqarah described). 4:66's counterfactual ("Leave your homes") pre-legislates the emigration duty of ruku 14.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[4:60]`** "Have you not observed those who claim that they believe in what was revealed to you, and in what was revealed before you, yet they seek Satanic sources for legislation, in spite of being commanded to reject them? Satan means to mislead them far away."
  * **Gloss:** The fifth considered-one (verified: "Have you not observed" is unique in-corpus here); the crime is jurisdiction-shopping — preferring satanic arbitration while commanded to reject it; "Satanic sources" is this translation's taghut-register (drift from 4:51's "evil powers", observable).
* **`[4:61]`** "And when it is said to them, “Come to what Allah has revealed, and to the Messenger,” you see the hypocrites shunning you completely."
  * **Gloss:** The label arrives: the shunning (sudd) is the diagnostic — profession in 4:60, avoidance at the summons.
* **`[4:62]`** "How about when a disaster strikes them because what their hands have put forward, and then they come to you swearing by Allah: “We only intended goodwill and reconciliation”?"
  * **Gloss:** Calamity from their own earnings drives them to oath-spinning — "goodwill (irada) and reconciliation (tasliq? rendered 'reconciliation')" — the same reconciliation-word as 4:35's family-arbiter goal.
* **`[4:63]`** "They are those whom Allah knows what is in their hearts. So ignore them, and admonish them, and say to them concerning themselves penetrating words."
  * **Gloss:** Heart-knowledge is Allah's, not the community's burden; the prescription is triple: turn aside, admonish, and speak *nafaaz* — "penetrating words" that reach the wound.
* **`[4:64]`** "We did not send any messenger except to be obeyed by Allah’s leave."
  * **Gloss:** Messengership defined as commissioned authority — obedience to the Messenger is not independent of Allah's permission (pairs with 4:80 "Whoever obeys the Messenger is obeying Allah").
* **`[4:64]`** "Had they, when they wronged themselves, come to you, and prayed for Allah's forgiveness, and the Messenger had prayed for their forgiveness, they would have found Allah Relenting and Merciful."
  * **Gloss:** The repentance-valve reopened (cf. 4:17): the self-wronger's route is presence + dual prayer; "Relenting" = the tawwab-refrain's second robe (verified 2:37/4:16 pair).
* **`[4:65]`** "But no, by your Lord, they will not believe until they call you to arbitrate in their disputes, and then find within themselves no resentment regarding your decisions, and submit themselves completely."
  * **Gloss:** The surah's only divine oath formula ("by your Lord" — the rare oath-cluster shared with [15:92], [19:68], verified) anchors the belief-test: arbitration accepted *without inner resentment* — interiority as the criterion.
* **`[4:66]`** "Had We decreed for them: “Kill yourselves,” or “Leave your homes,” they would not have done it, except for a few of them."
  * **Gloss:** The two unbearable orders measure the hearts: the Mosaic repentance-kill (2:54's "kill your egos" family, conceptually; wording differs in this translation — verified contrast) and emigration; a few would obey.
* **`[4:66]`** "But had they done what they were instructed to do, it would have been better for them, and a firmer confirmation."
  * **Gloss:** Obedience reframed as confirmation (taqrir) — stability as reward.
* **`[4:67]`** "And We would have given them from Our presence a rich compensation."
  * **Gloss:** The would-be blessing one: compensation (ajran karima) "from Our presence" — revelation-voice promise-form.
* **`[4:68]`** "And We would have guided them on a straight path."
  * **Gloss:** The would-be blessing two: the straight path — the Fatihah petition's own phrase (see 1:6, 3:101, verified) — the reward-chain the guidance-note tracks.
* **`[4:69]`** "Whoever obeys Allah and the Messenger— these are with those whom Allah has blessed—among the prophets, and the sincere, and the martyrs, and the upright. Excellent are those as companions."
  * **Gloss:** Obedience's eschatology is company (q: rafiq — "companions" — the anti-4:38 companion of Satan): four ranks — prophets, sincere (siddiq), martyrs (shahid), upright (salih-family rendered "the upright" here while the same root runs "righteous deeds"/"good deeds" elsewhere in this surah, observable drift: 4:57, 4:69, 4:122 by reading).
* **`[4:70]`** "That is the grace from Allah. Allah suffices as Knower."
  * **Gloss:** Rank is fadl, not lineage; the kafayya refrain closes ("suffices as Knower" — cf. 4:6 "suffices as a Reckoner").

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`munafiq` / "hypocrites"** (4:61; dossier through 4:145):
  * **Rendering:** the noun occurs exactly six times in this surah — 4:61, 4:88, 4:138, 4:140, 4:142, 4:145 (verified count in corpus) — and nowhere in al-Baqarah's pretense-portrait 2:8–20 in this translation (verified) — the corpus's "hypocrite" vocabulary is built on the *named* Medinan dossiers (3:167; 4:61+; 9:64–101; 48:6; 57:13; 63:1) rather than on Baqarah's metaphors.
* **`hakama` / "arbitrate"** (4:65): same root as 4:35's "arbiter" (verified — both hits are the corpus's only "arbitr-" occurrences): the household's two arbiters and the community's one arbiter-prophet are one institution at two scales (observable + agent synthesis).
* **`irada` / "goodwill" + "reconciliation"** (4:62): the oath's two words; "reconcile" (islah-family) elsewhere: `[4:35]`, `[49:9–10]`, `[3:103]`, `[4:114]` (verified) — hypocrisy co-opts reconciliation's own vocabulary, which 4:114 will re-legitimate.
* **`fawz`-class company words** (4:69): "the sincere" (siddiq): `[57:19]` "these are the sincere and the witnesses with their Lord", `[9:119]` "be with the sincere" (verified) — the same honor-rank list as 57:19's eschatological roll-call.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Medinan; the dossier presumes a mixed Madinan polity where profession and jurisdiction split (observable in the verses' own terms).
* **Asbab / narrative context:** 4:60 is classically tied to reports of two disputants, one insisting on the Prophet's judgment, the other on a soothsayer/jewish tribunal (transmitted in al-Bukhari's chapter on taghut via Abu Hurayra/Ibn Abbas families; echoed by Ibn Kathir on 4:60) — Source: classical asbab, agent condensation — verify chains and wording.
* **Legal content, neutrally stated:** the text orders: avoid and admonish the oath-spinners; accept arbitration as a belief-condition (with the resentment test stated as Allah-knowledge, i.e. interiority is divine territory, not court territory). The political theory of ulu-l-amr obedience built on 4:59/4:65 is *fiqh* — Source: classical tafsir/fiqh reference.
