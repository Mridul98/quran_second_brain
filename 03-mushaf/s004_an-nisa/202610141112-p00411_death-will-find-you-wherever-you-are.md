# Death finds you wherever you are — and the Quran has no cracks (4:77–87)
**UID:** 202610141112
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 4:77–87 (Ruku 11 of 24 in an-Nisa')
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[4:77]` – `[4:87]` (11 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The asked-for postponement exposes the laggards' error — they fear people as Allah ought to be feared, when enjoyment is brief — and the surah answers death-anxiety with its sharpest line: wherever you are, death overtakes you, even in fortified towers; every claim about fortune ("this is from Allah" / "this is from you") is corrected (all is from Allah; bad is from your own selves); obedience-speech is audited by what is conspired at night; then the book's own coherence is posed as proof (ponder the Quran — no discrepancy), news-discipline is legislated (refer security-or-alarm reports to the Messenger and those in authority), the intercession and greeting micro-laws follow, and the ruku crowns itself: "Allah — there is no god except He. He will gather you to the Day of Resurrection... And who speaks more truly than Allah?"
* **Placement:** The doctrine-band inside the mobilization arc: ruku 10 commanded fighting, ruku 11 dismantles the excuses (fear of death, fatalism, rumor), then ruku 12 returns to faction. 4:82 is the surah's kalam-argument — its consistency-claim paired with creation's (67:3, 50:6, verified).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[4:77]`** "Have you not considered those who were told, “Restrain your hands, and perform your prayers, and spend in regular charity”? But when fighting was ordained for them, a faction of them feared the people as Allah is ought to be feared, or even more."
  * **Gloss:** The fourth "have-you-not-considered" (verified): men once told to keep their hands restrained now flinch at the sword — reverence for people misallocated as if it were reverence for Allah.
* **`[4:77]`** "And they said, “Our Lord, why did You ordain fighting for us? If only You would postpone it for us for a short while.” Say, “The enjoyments of this life are brief, but the Hereafter is better for the righteous, and you will not be wronged one bit.”"
  * **Gloss:** The delay-request; the answer prices this life's enjoyment as brief; "one bit" — the small-wrong idiom (same phrase at [17:71], verified).
* **`[4:78]`** "Wherever you may be, death will catch up with you, even if you were in fortified towers."
  * **Gloss:** The ruku's spine-verse: the laggard's calculus (absence = safety) dies here; "fortified towers" — cf. [59:14]'s "fortified strongholds" (verified).
* **`[4:78]`** "When a good fortune comes their way, they say, “This is from Allah.” But when a misfortune befalls them, they say, “This is from you.” Say, “All is from Allah.” So what is the matter with these people, that they hardly understand a thing?"
  * **Gloss:** Fortune-folk theology: attribution switched with the news-item; the correction "All is from Allah" is stated without exception.
* **`[4:79]`** "Whatever good happens to you is from Allah, and whatever bad happens to you is from your own self."
  * **Gloss:** The same ledger now split honestly: good credited to Allah, bad to the self — agency recovered without dethroning providence (the pairing of 4:78's "all" and 4:79's split is the surah's own resolution — observable juxtaposition; classical kalām debate over the two is long — Source: classical tafsir, e.g. Ibn Kathir citing 7:178/42:30 on 4:79 — verify).
* **`[4:79]`** "We sent you to humanity as a messenger, and Allah is Witness enough."
  * **Gloss:** Universal sending; the kafayya-refrain's fourth occurrence in the surah ("Witness enough").
* **`[4:80]`** "Whoever obeys the Messenger is obeying Allah. And whoever turns away—We did not send you as a watcher over them."
  * **Gloss:** The obedience-identity equation (built from 4:59/4:64); the prophet is not a raqib — "watcher" is the same audit-word used of Allah at 4:1/13:33 and of recording angels at [50:18] (verified) — guardianship belongs to God alone.
* **`[4:81]`** "They profess obedience, but when they leave your presence, some of them conspire something contrary to what you said. But Allah writes down what they conspire."
  * **Gloss:** Night-counsel as defection; the counter-surveillance is a writing (Allah "writes down" — kitaba of plots, cf. 10:21-family; the verb anchors 52:42/43:80's night-plot motif, verified occurrences of "conspire").
* **`[4:82]`** "Do they not ponder the Quran? Had it been from any other than Allah, they would have found in it much discrepancy."
  * **Gloss:** The coherence argument: the book's non-contradiction as falsification-test (cf. [47:24] "locks upon their hearts", [38:29] "that they may ponder its Verses" — both verified).
* **`[4:83]`** "When some news of security or alarm comes their way, they broadcast it. But had they referred it to the Messenger, and to those in authority among them, those who can draw conclusions from it would have comprehended it."
  * **Gloss:** News-discipline: rumor-broadcast vs. extraction ("draw conclusions" — istinbat — the word occurs only here in the corpus, verified); the ulu-l-amr of 4:59 reappears inside an information law (observable).
* **`[4:83]`** "Were it not for Allah’s blessing and mercy upon you, you would have followed the Devil, except for a few."
  * **Gloss:** The grace-bracket — the same counterfactual form the surah reuses at 4:113 ("Were it not for Allah's grace towards you...") — blessing-and-mercy named as the only reason rumor doesn't become following (observable within-surah pairing).
* **`[4:84]`** "So fight in the cause of Allah; you are responsible only for yourself. And rouse the believers."
  * **Gloss:** The command re-issued at the personal line: your duty is your own; agitation of others is your share.
* **`[4:84]`** "Perhaps Allah will restrain the might of those who disbelieve. Allah is Stronger in Might, and More Punishing."
  * **Gloss:** "restrain" — the hand-restraint idiom back for the enemy (same verb as 4:77's command and [5:11]/[48:20]'s divine restraint — verified); the comparatives seal it.
* **`[4:85]`** "Whoever intercedes for a good cause has a share in it, and whoever intercedes for an evil cause shares in its burdens."
  * **Gloss:** Mediation taxed: the intercessor (shafa'a-family, here "intercedes for a cause") earns a portion of the outcome's moral ledger either way.
* **`[4:86]`** "When you are greeted with a greeting, respond with a better greeting, or return it."
  * **Gloss:** The surah's etiquette micro-law: tahiyya repaid upward or at par; the same greeting-vocabulary governs Nur's home-entry law ([24:27], [24:61], verified).
* **`[4:86]`** "Allah keeps count of everything."
  * **Gloss:** Even salams are audited — the Reckoner-refrain (4:6) in five words.
* **`[4:87]`** "Allah—there is no god except He. He will gather you to the Day of Resurrection, in which there is no doubt. And who speaks more truly than Allah?"
  * **Gloss:** The creed-shot: name, shahada-clause, gathering, and the speech-truth question — the kalam-note's own ground ("who speaks more truly" is unique in the corpus, verified).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`huroon` / "fortified towers"** (4:78):
  * **Elsewhere:** `[59:14]` — "They will not fight you all together except from fortified strongholds, or from behind walls" (verified) — both occurrences measure false security; 4:78 against death, 59:14 against the community's enemy.
* **`tafakkur` / "ponder the Quran"** (4:82):
  * **Elsewhere:** `[47:24]` — "Will they not ponder the Quran? Or are there locks upon their hearts?" (verified) and `[38:29]` — "that they may ponder its Verses" (verified) — the pondering-verb's object is always this Book; 4:82 supplies its criterion (absence of ikhtilaf/discrepancy).
* **`ikhtilaf` / "discrepancy"** (4:82):
  * **Elsewhere:** `[67:3]` — "You see no discrepancy in the creation... Can you see any cracks?" and `[50:6]` — "it has no cracks" (both verified) — the Quran's coherence claimed exactly like the sky's; note: 67:3/50:6 use the creation-fissure idiom while 4:82 uses much-inconsistency — same English word in this translation, different Arabic measure-terms (Source: classical lexical reading, agent — verify).
* **`kayd`-adjacent plot-verbs / "conspire" + "draw conclusions"** (4:81, 4:83):
  * **Elsewhere:** "conspire/conspiring" at `[7:123]`, `[58:8]`, `[52:42]`, `[43:80]` (verified); "draw conclusions" (istinbat) is a corpus-hapax here (verified) — revelation-surveillance has one word for extracting meaning and many for hiding it (observable).
