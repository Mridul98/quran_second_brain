# The night-and-day invitation: ask forgiveness and the sky opens (71:1–20)
**UID:** 202610142232
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 71:1–20 (Ruku 1 of 2 in Nuh)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[71:1]` – `[71:20]` (20 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Noah's sermon compressed: a night-and-day call that meets fingers-in-ears arrogance, escalates through open/public/private tactics, then offers the repentance-prosperity circuit — istighfar unlocks torrents, wealth, children, gardens, rivers — and grounds it all in three arguments from design: creative stages, layered heavens, and the earth-spread with its wide roads.
* **Placement:** Opening ruku of the shortest juz's sermon-surah; ruku 2 reports the verdict. The offer of 71:11–12 is the surah's promise, and its inversion is ruku 2's plot (the drowned sky).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[71:1]`** "We sent Noah to his people: “Warn your people before there comes upon them a painful punishment.”"
  * **Gloss:** divine commission, warner-only mandate — the same verb opens Muhammad's charge ("Arise and warn," 74:2).
* **`[71:2]`** "He said, “O my people, I am to you a clear warner.”"
  * **Gloss:** the warner self-describes with the surah's title-claim: muneedhar mubeen.
* **`[71:3]`–`[71:4]`** "Worship Allah and reverence Him, and obey me. And He will forgive you of your sins, and reprieve you until a stated term. Allah’s term cannot be deferred once it has arrived, if you only knew."
  * **Gloss:** three imperatives + the carrot-and-limit: forgiveness, respite, and the non-deferrable term (the eschatological brake).
* **`[71:5]`–`[71:6]`** "He said, “My Lord, I have called my people night and day. But my call added only to their flight.”"
  * **Gloss:** the complaint-report: total duration, inverse yield — da'wa is measured by persistence, not results.
* **`[71:7]`** "Whenever I called them to Your forgiveness, they thrust their fingers into their ears, and wrapped themselves in their garments, and insisted, and became more and more arrogant."
  * **Gloss:** four refusals in one verse — plug, cover, dig-in, swell; gesture twin of `[2:19]` "They press their fingers into their ears from the thunderbolts" (verified).
* **`[71:8]`–`[71:9]`** "Then I called them openly. Then I appealed to them publicly, and I spoke to them privately."
  * **Gloss:** strategy escalation: public/private — the exact grammar of spending at `[2:274]` "by night and day, privately and publicly" (verified): total-donation language applied to da'wa.
* **`[71:10]`** "I said, ‘Ask your Lord for forgiveness; He is Forgiving."
  * **Gloss:** the hinge: istighfar addressed to the One named Ghaffar — "He is Forgiving"; Hud's identical campaign at `[11:3]`, `[11:52]` (verified).
* **`[71:11]`** "He will let loose the sky upon you in torrents."
  * **Gloss:** weather reassigned to Allah alone — the rain-as-forgiveness circuit; the sky's other usage is the flood (71:25 via 54:11).
* **`[71:12]`** "And provide you with wealth and children, and allot for you gardens, and allot for you rivers."
  * **Gloss:** the full prosperity inventory — forgiveness cashed in capital goods; a shukr-program list (cf. 14:7's increase decree, verified).
* **`[71:13]`** "What is the matter with you, that you do not appreciate Allah’s Greatness?"
  * **Gloss:** the rebuke names the disease: no reckoning of divine majesty ('izam; Itani's "Greatness" recurs at 40:56 for a different word — drift noted, verified).
* **`[71:14]`** "Although He created you in stages."
  * **Gloss:** developmental creation as argument; the phrase "in stages" is shared with 25:32 and 17:106 — where it describes revelation's descent, not human embryology (verified lexical coincidence; roots differ, verify).
* **`[71:15]`** "Do you not realize that Allah created seven heavens in layers?"
  * **Gloss:** exact twin of `[67:3]` "created seven heavens in layers" (verified); cf. 78:12 "seven strong ones."
* **`[71:16]`** "And He set the moon in their midst for light, and He made the sun a lamp."
  * **Gloss:** siraj = the sun; munira = the moon ("a lamp, and an illuminating moon," 25:61, verified; "blazing lamp," 78:13, verified) — here the lamps are gifts, not weapons; as weapons, 41:12/67:5.
* **`[71:17]`–`[71:18]`** "And Allah germinated you from the earth like plants. Then He will return you into it, and will bring you out again."
  * **Gloss:** agriculture-grammar for anthropology (anbata): sown, harvested, re-sown — resurrection argued from botany.
* **`[71:19]`–`[71:20]`** "And Allah made the earth a spread for you. That you may travel its diverse roads."
  * **Gloss:** bisat — the earth as unrolled ground-cloth; near-twin 78:6 "Did We not make the earth a cradle?" (verified; different Arabic, same semantic move) — the invitation comes with a landscape.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`ghaffar`** (Forgiving): the chain's trigger-name (71:10); same close as 73:20 "Allah is Forgiving and Merciful" (verified).
* **`'izam`** (Greatness): 71:13; the jinn's doxology answers with jadd rendered "Grandeur" (72:3, verified unique) — two vocabularies of majesty, human rebuke and jinn confession. *Source: standard lexical correspondence ('izam/jadd) — verify against print lexicon.*
* **`ahdaran` (stages):** 71:14; English "in stages" recurs at 25:32, 17:106 for revelation's gradual descent (verified phrase-identity; roots differ — *Source: agent observation — verify*).
* **`siraj` (lamp):** 71:16; elsewhere 25:61, 78:13 (sun); plural "lamps/lanterns" at 41:12, 67:5 where they become missiles (verified) — the same hardware read as ornament (71) and as security (72 via 67).
