## 1. Orientation
* **Revelation:** Meccan — an agreed case in the type-lists. *Source: classical type-lists — verify.* Revelation order 36 in the transmitted enumeration lists this brain cites for juz-27 openings. *Source: classical revelation-order lists — verify against a printed table.* The corpus carries no nuzul statement; the unnumbered Basmala heads the surah.
* **Size:** 28 ayahs, 2 ruku; the first surah of the final juz — a single sermon in two movements (offer, then verdict). The only surah named for Noah.
* **Title etymology:** Nuh = Noah, the preacher whose sermon IS the surah — he speaks in first person through most of it (71:5 "I have called my people night and day"); the corpus adds his tenure only at 29:14 (950 years, verified).
* **Reading hint:** the surah runs on one grammar of "increase" — the offer asks Allah to increase the wrongdoers in nothing (71:24/28), while forgiveness was to increase them in torrents, wealth, children, gardens, rivers (71:11–12); the same verb marks the circuit open and closed (observable).
* **Central theme:** The repentance-prosperity circuit and its refusal. Noah calls night and day (71:5) with one offer: ask forgiveness, and the sky opens in torrents, wealth, children, gardens, rivers arrive (71:10–12) — provision as the cash-out of tawba, argued from creative stages, layered heavens, and the moon-light/sun-lamp (71:13–20). When the offer is refused with fingers in ears (71:7), the same sky becomes the Deluge and the inventory becomes the tag of perdition (71:21–25); the surah ends on the widening forgiveness-du'a and the double curse "increase not the wrongdoers except…" (71:24/28).

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Commission (71:1–4):** warn before the painful punishment; worship/revere/obey; forgiveness + reprieve + the non-deferrable term.
* **Campaign (71:5–9):** night and day, open, public, private — met by plug-and-cover arrogance (71:7, twin of 2:19) and inverse yield ("added only to their flight").
* **The offer (71:10–12):** istighfar → torrents → wealth/children/gardens/rivers — Hud's identical circuit at 11:3/11:52 (verified).
* **The arguments (71:13–20):** majesty unreckoned, creation in stages, seven heavens in layers (67:3 twin), moon-light and sun-lamp, germination and return, earth-spread and wide roads.
* **The reversal (71:21–25):** wealth-and-children as perdition-tag; the five named idols (71:23); drowned, then hurled into Fire — the promised sky executed as the flood (54:11–12).
* **The petitions (71:26–28):** no survivor of misguidance, then total amnesty for the believing households — the surah's last word is forgiveness asked, and perdition refused only for the wrongdoers.
