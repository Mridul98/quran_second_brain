# The final petition: five idols, the double drowning, and the two "increase not the wrongdoers" (71:21–28)
**UID:** 202610142233
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 71:21–28 (Ruku 2 of 2 in Nuh)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[71:21]` – `[71:28]` (8 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The sermon's verdict: Noah reports the defiance and its wealth-worship, the people name their five gods, the scheme is answered by drowning-then-Fire, and the surah closes on two petitions — no survivor of misguidance, and universal forgiveness for the believing households — bracketed by the same curse: "do not increase the wrongdoers except in…" (71:24, 71:28).
* **Placement:** Closing ruku; it cashes ruku 1's warnings: the "painful punishment" of 71:1 arrives as 71:25's double penalty, and the refused wealth-and-children offer (71:12) reappears as the tag of perdition (71:21).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[71:21]`** "Noah said, “My Lord, they have defied me, and followed him whose wealth and children increase him only in perdition.”"
  * **Gloss:** the inventory inverted: wealth+children (71:12's promised goods) now mark the misled follower — kufr's balance sheet (cf. Qarun, 28:78, cited in k-shukr).
* **`[71:22]`** "And they schemed outrageous schemes."
  * **Gloss:** the plot-noun doubled for intensity (makr) — the public/private campaign (71:8–9) met organized counter-scheme.
* **`[71:23]`** "And they said, “Do not give up your gods; do not give up Wadd, nor Souwa, nor Yaghoos, and Yaooq, and Nassr."
  * **Gloss:** shirk with a roll-call: five proper names, the corpus's only site for them (verified) — rivals are never anonymous.
* **`[71:24]`** "They have misled many, so do not increase the wrongdoers except in confusion."
  * **Gloss:** the leaders' misguidance-count ("they have misled many") — the statistical ground of the petition to come; "confusion" renders the first curse-word.
* **`[71:25]`** "Because of their wrongs, they were drowned, and were hurled into a Fire. They did not find apart from Allah any helpers."
  * **Gloss:** double punishment — water then Fire; the refused sky-torrents (71:11) executed as the Deluge (54:11's "floodgates", verified); and the helpers-clause = tawhid's verdict: no wali beside Allah.
* **`[71:26]`** "Noah said, “My Lord, do not leave of the unbelievers a single dweller on earth."
  * **Gloss:** the hard petition: no survivor-dweller — the text's wording (the task's "destroy the disbelievers utterly / leave no god of their idols" is NOT the corpus — flagged in the index).
* **`[71:27]`** "If You leave them, they will mislead your servants, and will breed only wicked unbelievers."
  * **Gloss:** the petition's stated reason: misguidance is reproductive — the 71:24 ring ("they have misled many") projected forward; the prayer is quarantine of da'wa, not vengeance.
* **`[71:28]`** "My Lord! Forgive me and my parents, and anyone who enters my home in faith, and all the believing men and believing women; and do not increase the wrongdoers except in perdition.”"
  * **Gloss:** the amnesty widens: self, parents, household-in-faith, all believing men and women — the universal du'a closes the surah, and the second "increase not the wrongdoers" brackets 71:24–28.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`perdition` vs `confusion`:** 71:21 and 71:28 end "except in perdition"; 71:24 "except in confusion" — a deliberate English pair (observable rendering; Arabic roots differ, mahq vs tihan-family — *Source: standard lexicon correspondence — verify*).
  * **Elsewhere:** 72:6 renders a jinn-clause with the same English word "confusion" (verified) — lexical convergence across surahs, roots unverified.
* **`ansar` (helpers):** 71:25 "did not find apart from Allah any helpers" (standard lexicon correspondence — verify) — k-tawhid's exclusion of hidden benefactors in verdict-form.
* **`misled many`:** 71:24 → 71:27 "they will mislead your servants" — the surah's own cause-effect ring (verified internal).
