# What your hands earn, ships like banners, and the people of consultation (42:30–43)
**UID:** 202610142015
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 42:30–43 (Ruku 4 of 5 in ash-Shura)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[42:30]` – `[42:43]` (14 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah's longest ruku builds the community that matches its name: misfortune is earned and yet mostly pardoned (30, 34), the sea shows who commands winds and ships (32–33), this life's provision is temporary while what Allah has endures (36) — and the people defined against it are the seven-trait cohort who avoid major sins, forgive their anger, respond to their Lord, pray, conduct affairs by *shura*, give of divine provision, and defend themselves when wronged (37–39) — with retaliation held strictly even, and pardon held strictly higher (40–43).
* **Placement:** The ethical center of the surah: ruku 2's "uphold the religion and be not divided" gets its portrait here; the governance word *shura* arrives inside a virtue-list, not a statute — the community's constitution is character.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[42:30]`** "Whatever misfortune befalls you, it is because of what your hands have earned; and yet He pardons much."
  * **Gloss:** The earned-consequence rule, half of it mercy — the second time "He pardons much" appears in four verses' reach (cf. 42:34).
* **`[42:31]`** "You are not the ones to interfere on earth; and besides Allah, you have no ally, and no helper."
  * **Gloss:** No escape-route and no backup patron: "interfere" (acrobatics of evasion, corpus-hapax phrase) and the ally/helper pair close the protector-question again (42:6, 9).
* **`[42:32]`** "And of His signs are the ships sailing the sea like flags."
  * **Gloss:** Ships at full sail read on the horizon like banners — creation as sign-literature.
* **`[42:33]`** "If He willed, He could have stilled the winds, leaving them motionless on its surface. Surely in that are signs for every disciplined, grateful person."
  * **Gloss:** Wind-power is loaned, not owned; the recipient is the ṣabbār–shakūr type — "every disciplined, grateful person," the third of the note-tracked couplet renderings (k-shukr cites this exact line).
* **`[42:34]`** "Or He could wreck them, because of what they have earned. And yet He pardons much."
  * **Gloss:** The earned-rule returns inside the sea-sign: wreck-or-pardon is a live option, and pardon is named as the operating mercy.
* **`[42:35]`** "Those who dispute Our signs know that there is no asylum for them."
  * **Gloss:** The sign-disputers have already read their own verdict — asylum (a "way of going back"-space, cf. 42:44) does not exist.
* **`[42:36]`** "Whatever thing you are given is only the provision of this life. But what Allah possesses is better and more lasting for those who believe and rely on their Lord."
  * **Gloss:** The durability test; its passing grade is tawakkul — "rely on their Lord" (mutawakkilūn, the corpus's only such phrase, rg-verified).
* **`[42:37]`** "And those who avoid major sins and indecencies; and if they become angry, they forgive."
  * **Gloss:** Anger is presumed — the praise is for what is done with it (twin spirit of `[3:134]` "who… restrain anger, and forgive people").
* **`[42:38]`** "And those who respond to their Lord, and pray regularly, and conduct their affairs by mutual consultation, and give of what We have provided them."
  * **Gloss:** The name-verse: response → prayer → *shura* → infaq. Governance by consultation and charity by circulation stand in the same breath — neither is legislated here as procedure or rate; both are marked as temperament.
* **`[42:39]`** "And those who, when wronged, defend themselves."
  * **Gloss:** Not passive: self-defense is counted a trait before the rules of it are given (40–42).
* **`[42:40]`** "The repayment of a bad action is one equivalent to it. But whoever pardons and makes reconciliation, his reward lies with Allah. He does not love the unjust."
  * **Gloss:** The equivalence law — retaliation capped at parity, with the higher road (pardon-and-reconcile) priced by Allah Himself.
* **`[42:41]`** "As for those who retaliate after being wronged, there is no blame on them."
  * **Gloss:** Retaliation legalized (no blame), immediately bounded by 42:40's equivalence and 42:42's definition of the real crime.
* **`[42:42]`** "Blame lies on those who wrong people, and commit aggression in the land without right. These will have a painful punishment."
  * **Gloss:** Blame relocates from the defender to the initiator — aggression-without-right is the indictable act.
* **`[42:43]`** "But whoever endures patiently and forgives—that is a sign of real resolve."
  * **Gloss:** The ruku's top step: sabr + afw as the mark of true firmness of purpose (ʿazm al-umūr).

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **shūrā (consultation):** `[42:38]` "conduct their affairs by mutual consultation."
  * **Rendering:** "mutual consultation" — the noun's surah-name-giving site; Itani uses the same phrase for parental weaning decisions at 2:233.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:233]` "by mutual consent and consultation" (domestic law); `[3:159]` "consult them in the conduct of affairs" (prophetic duty) followed by "put your trust in Allah" — consult, decide, entrust, the exact sequence 42:36–38 mirrors (trust last, always); `[18:22]` "do not consult any of them" — the verb's third site (rg-verified).
* **anfaqa (give of provision):** `[42:38]` "and give of what We have provided them."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:3]` "give from what We have provided for them" — near-verbatim; `[13:22]` "spend from Our provisions to them, secretly and openly" — the same trait-list form; k-infraq tracks the circuit as the community's signature verb.
* **tawakkul:** `[42:36]` "those who believe and rely on their Lord."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[3:159]` "Allah loves the trusting"; internal 42:10 "in Whom I trust" — trust binds the ruku-pair 2→4.
* **ṣabr + ʿafw (patient endurance + pardon):** `[42:43]` "whoever endures patiently and forgives."
  * **Rendering:** "endures patiently" is the corpus's only such verb-form (rg-verified); "forgives" (ʿafw) pairs internally with 42:37's anger-and-forgiveness trait and the double "He pardons much" (30, 34).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[3:134]` "restrain anger, and forgive people" — the twin virtue-list entry (rg-verified).

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Meccan (classical lists) — the fact that consultation, charity and retaliation-limits appear here as *traits* rather than procedures is the classical argument for this ruku's Meccan register; the procedural law of shura's application and qisas's details is transmitted to Medinan practice. *Source: agent inference from genre — verify; no legal ruling is claimed from this verse alone.*
* **Asbab / narrative context:** none specific in the corpus for 42:38's cohort. *Source: agent inference — verify.*
