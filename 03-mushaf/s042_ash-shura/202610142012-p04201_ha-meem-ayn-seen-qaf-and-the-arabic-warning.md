# Ha, Meem, Ayn, Seen, Qaf: one inspiration, an Arabic warning to the Central City (42:1–9)
**UID:** 202610142012
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 42:1–9 (Ruku 1 of 5 in ash-Shura)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[42:1]` – `[42:9]` (9 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah's opening ruku authenticates the channel before the content: disconnected letters, then the claim that the same Almighty-Wise God "inspires you, and those before you" (3), a heaven nearly burst by its own weight while its angels beg forgiveness for earth (5), and a commissioned Arabic Quran warning the Central City of the Day of Assembly (7) — ending on the two sovereign moves: community-by-will withheld (8), protection and resurrection granted (9).
* **Placement:** The surah's foundation stone; the only letter-opening in the corpus split across two numbered ayahs (five letter-names total — the single-ayah record stays with 19:1), and the Ha-Meem family's augmented signature; its inspiration-chain `[42:3]`/`[42:7]` is picked up and closed by `[42:51–52]` in ruku 5.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[42:1]`** "Ha, Meem."
  * **Gloss:** The family signature shared with 40:1, 41:1, 43:1, 45:1, 46:1 (rg-verified); untranslated letter-names as numbered speech.
* **`[42:2]`** "Ayn, Seen, Qaf."
  * **Gloss:** The corpus's only two-ayah letter-opening; these three letter-names occur nowhere else, not even at 50:1 ("Qaf. By the Glorious Quran.") or 19:1 ("Kaf, Ha, Ya, Ayn, Saad." — five letters in ONE ayah) — all rg-verified.
* **`[42:3]`** "Thus He inspires you, and those before you—Allah the Almighty, the Wise."
  * **Gloss:** "Thus" (ka-dhālika) points back at the letters and forward to 42:7: revelation arrives to Muhammad as it arrived to the earlier messengers; the channel is one, the senders' names are two (Almighty, Wise).
* **`[42:4]`** "To him belongs everything in the heavens and everything on earth. He is the Sublime, the Magnificent."
  * **Gloss:** Total ownership grounds total authority; "the Sublime, the Magnificent" is this pairing's only corpus site (rg-verified).
* **`[42:5]`** "The heavens above them almost burst apart, while the angels glorify the praises of their Lord, and ask forgiveness for those on earth. Allah is indeed the Forgiver, the Merciful."
  * **Gloss:** The cosmic scene: near-rupture above, intercession below — the angels' forgiveness-prayer answered structurally by 42:5's own verdict line, and dramaturgically at `[40:7]` (Throne-bearers "ask for forgiveness for those who believe").
* **`[42:6]`** "As for those who take masters other than Him: Allah is in charge of them, and you are not responsible for them."
  * **Gloss:** The associationists are delegated to God, discharged from the preacher — the delivery-only clause that recurs at 42:48.
* **`[42:7]`** "Thus We inspired you with an Arabic Quran, that you may warn the Central City and whoever is around it, and to warn of the Day of Assembly, of which there is no doubt; a group in the Garden, and a group in the Furnace."
  * **Gloss:** The commission: language (Arabic), geography (the Central City and its ring), date (the Day of Assembly), and the final census in two groups.
* **`[42:8]`** "Had Allah willed, He could have made them one community, but He admits into His mercy whomever He wills. As for the wrongdoers, they will have no protector and no savior."
  * **Gloss:** Unity-by-fiat refused; mercy-by-will replaces it — the twin of `[11:118]` ("Had your Lord willed, He could have made humanity one community, but they continue to differ") and near-twin of `[5:48]`'s "a single nation."
* **`[42:9]`** "Or have they adopted protectors besides him? But Allah is the Protector, and He gives life to the dead, and He has power over all things."
  * **Gloss:** The ruku's closing argument: if protection is the wanted commodity, only the life-giver can supply it.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **Muqatta'at (the letters):** transliterated letter-names, numbered as ayahs, never glossed.
  * **Rendering:** "Ha, Meem." / "Ayn, Seen, Qaf." — five letter-names across two numbered ayahs; the sole two-ayah opening (rg-verified across all 15 letter-openings).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[19:1]` five letters in one ayah (the single-ayah record); `[50:1]` "Qaf. By the Glorious Quran." — a lone letter fused to its oath in one ayah; the Ha-Meem band `[40:1]`, `[41:1]`, `[43:1]`, `[45:1]`, `[46:1]`.
* **yuha / awhā (inspires):** `[42:3]` "Thus He inspires you, and those before you"; `[42:7]` "Thus We inspired you with an Arabic Quran."
  * **Rendering:** "inspires" — the kalam verb, not "reveals" (the task's paraphrase drift).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[28:7]` "We inspired the mother of Moses" and `[20:77]` "We inspired Moses" — the same verb for non-scriptural guidance, so 42:3's chain claim is about the wahy channel, not a new genre; closed by `[42:52]` "We thus inspired you spiritually."
* **umm al-qurā → "the Central City":** Mecca, the axis of the warning's geography.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[6:92]` "the Mother of Cities and all around it" — the one other corpus site of the phrase, rendered differently (in-book variant); `[42:7]` and `[6:92]` share the exact "warn… and whoever/all around it" frame (both rg-verified).
* **yawm al-jam' → "the Day of Assembly":** the gathering of all humanity, "of which there is no doubt."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[64:9]` "the Day of Gathering" — same root, different rendering (rg-verified); the assembly's mechanics appear at `[42:29]` (the scattered creatures "gathered at will," ruku 3).
