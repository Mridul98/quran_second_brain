# Nothing is like Him, and one religion from Noah to Jesus (42:10–19)
**UID:** 202610142013
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 42:10–19 (Ruku 2 of 5 in ash-Shura)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[42:10]` – `[42:19]` (10 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The ruku hands every disputed matter to Allah's judgment (10), anchors that court in the Incomparable Hearer-Seer (11) and the one religion prescribed through Noah, Abraham, Moses, Jesus and Muhammad (13), diagnoses division as a post-knowledge crime born of resentment (14), and gives the preacher a charter — believe in every sent Book, judge equitably, refuse quarrel (15) — before the Hour countdown (17–18) and the gentle verdict: "Allah is kind towards His worshipers" (19).
* **Placement:** The surah's creed-core: ruku 1 authenticated the channel; this ruku states what the channel delivers — one religion, one Judge, no likeness — and the community ethic of 36–43 will be its fruit.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[42:10]`** "Whatever matter you differ about, its judgment rests with Allah. “Such is Allah, my Lord, in Whom I trust, and unto Him I repent.”"
  * **Gloss:** Disagreement is not adjudicated by partisans but referred to Allah; the quoted confession (note the book's capital "in Whom", class-2 typography) is the believer's answer posture — trust and repentance.
* **`[42:11]`** "Originator of the heavens and the earth. He made for you mates from among yourselves, and pairs of animals, by means of which He multiplies you. There is nothing like Him. He is the Hearing, the Seeing."
  * **Gloss:** Creation's generative order ends in the corpus's unique incomparability clause: nothing resembles Him — yet He hears and sees; affirmation of names without likeness.
* **`[42:12]`** "To Him belongs absolute control of the heavens and the earth. He spreads the bounties to whomever He wills, or reduces it. He is aware of all things."
  * **Gloss:** Sovereignty over provision — the expansion/restriction pair that reappears at 42:27; wealth is distributed by will, not by desert or clan.
* **`[42:13]`** "He prescribed for you the same religion He enjoined upon Noah, and what We inspired to you, and what We enjoined upon Abraham, and Moses, and Jesus: “You shall uphold the religion, and be not divided therein.” As for the idolaters, what you call them to is outrageous to them. Allah chooses to Himself whom He wills, and He guides to Himself whoever repents."
  * **Gloss:** The unity-of-religion verse: one *din* across five names, with the charge to uphold and not divide; election and guidance (to Himself, toward repentant souls) frame the prescription.
* **`[42:14]`** "They became divided only after knowledge came to them, out of resentment among themselves. Were it not for a predetermined decision from your Lord, judgment would have been pronounced between them. Indeed, those who were made to inherit the Book after them are in grave doubt about it."
  * **Gloss:** The autopsy of division: post-revelational, resentment-driven (the twin of `[2:213]`'s "out of mutual envy"); the deferred judgment is mercy, not weakness.
* **`[42:15]`** "To this go on inviting, and be upright as you were commanded, and do not follow their inclinations, and say, “I believe in whatever Book Allah has sent down, and I was commanded to judge between you equitably. Allah is our Lord and your Lord. We have our deeds, and you have your deeds. Let there be no quarrel between us and you. Allah will bring us together, and to Him is the ultimate return.”"
  * **Gloss:** The preacher's charter: invite, stay upright, reject whims, confess all sent Books, judge equitably, renounce quarrel — because the gathering (cf. 42:7's Day of Assembly) and the return settle everything.
* **`[42:16]`** "As for those who dispute about Allah after having answered His call, their argument is invalid with their Lord; and upon them falls wrath; and a grievous torment awaits them."
  * **Gloss:** Argument after answering the call is voided — dispute-after-acceptance is the surah's recurring crime (see 42:35, 42:18).
* **`[42:17]`** "It is Allah who revealed the Book with the truth, and the Balance. And what will make you realize that perhaps the Hour is near?"
  * **Gloss:** Book-with-truth plus Balance (the near-twin of `[57:25]` "the Book and the Balance, that humanity may uphold justice") — and the Hour's lateness flipped into urgency.
* **`[42:18]`** "Those who do not believe in it seek to hasten it; but those who believe are apprehensive of it, and they know it to be the truth. Absolutely, those who question the Hour are in distant error."
  * **Gloss:** Two temperaments toward one Date: impatience as unbelief's tell, apprehension as faith's.
* **`[42:19]`** "Allah is kind towards His worshipers. He provides for whomever He wills. He is the Powerful, the Honorable."
  * **Gloss:** The *latif* verse — gentleness conjoined with power ("kind towards" is the corpus's only such line, rg-verified); the correction of the task's mis-assignment: the "grateful—only for his own soul" line is `[31:12]`, not 42:19.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **mathal (likeness):** `[42:11]` "There is nothing like Him."
  * **Rendering:** plain negative — the only "like Him" clause in the corpus (rg-verified).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[112:4]` "And there is nothing comparable to Him." — the same doctrine's liturgical twin; the verse's own second half supplies the counterweight: "He is the Hearing, the Seeing," worded identically only at `[40:20]`.
* **sharāʾa (prescribe):** `[42:13]` "He prescribed for you the same religion He enjoined upon Noah…"
  * **Rendering:** "prescribed" (with "the same" supplied for the one *din*); the covenant register is absent — 0 covenant/pledge/promise hits in s042 (rg-verified): this is law-enactment grammar, not contract grammar.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[45:18]` "Then We set you upon a pathway of faith" and `[5:48]` "For each of you We have assigned a law and a method" — the same root; 5:48 pairs prescription with the "single nation" conditional this ruku echoes at 42:8's margin.
* **tawakkul (trust):** `[42:10]` "in Whom I trust"; `[42:36]` "rely on their Lord" (ruku 4).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[3:159]` "when you make a decision, put your trust in Allah; Allah loves the trusting" — trust as the sequel to consultation, the exact order 42:36–38 preserves.
* **baghy (resentment/envy):** `[42:14]` "out of resentment among themselves."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:213]` "none differed over it except those who were given it… out of mutual envy between them" — the one other post-knowledge-division autopsy, rendered "mutual envy"; cf. the sects verdicts `[6:159]`, `[30:32]` (both rg-verified).
