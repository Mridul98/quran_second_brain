# The veil, the spirit, and the return of all matters: divine speech legislated, all matters returned (42:44–53)
**UID:** 202610142016
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 42:44–53 (Ruku 5 of 5 in ash-Shura)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[42:44]` – `[42:53]` (10 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The closing ruku settles three accounts at once: the misguided get no protector and no retreat (44–47), the messenger is discharged as delivery-only while man's temperament under mercy and misfortune is laid bare (48), and Allah's sole dominion over the gift of children (49–50) grounds the surah's final doctrine — God speaks only by inspiration, veil, or sent messenger (51) — before the spirit-command lands as light and straight path (52–53) and "all matters revert" to Allah.
* **Placement:** The surah's capstone: it legislates what ruku 1 only claimed (`[42:3]` → `[42:51–52]`, the wahy ring closes), and its final ownership clause repeats the ruku-1 opening almost word-for-word — `[42:4]` "To him belongs everything in the heavens and everything on earth" ↔ `[42:53]` "to whom belongs everything in the heavens and everything on earth" — a deliberate inclusio (rg-verified twin phrasing).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[42:44]`** "Whoever Allah leaves astray has no protector apart from Him. And you will see the transgressors, when they see the torment, saying, “Is there a way of going back?”"
  * **Gloss:** The protector-question (6, 8, 9, 31) is decided by negation; the damned's first word at the sight of torment is the plea for retreat — which 42:35 and 42:46 have already ruled out.
* **`[42:45]`** "And you will see them exposed to it, cowering from disgrace, looking with concealed eyes. Those who believed will say, “The losers are those who lost themselves and their families on the Day of Resurrection.” Indeed, the evildoers are in a lasting torment."
  * **Gloss:** Three looks in one verse: exposed, cowering, eyes held down ("concealed eyes," the corpus's only such phrase, rg-verified); the believers speak the loss-arithmetic — the self and the family were the trade goods.
* **`[42:46]`** "They will have no allies to support them against Allah. Whomever Allah leaves astray has no way out."
  * **Gloss:** 44's verdict restated with the second commodity removed: no ally, no exit — the surah's "no asylum" (35) becomes "no way out" (from the same semantic field as 40:11's "Is there any way out?").
* **`[42:47]`** "Respond to your Lord before there comes from Allah a Day that cannot be turned back. You will have no refuge on that Day, and no possibility of denial."
  * **Gloss:** The imperative present-tense: respond NOW (the 42:38 trait flipped into a command before the deadline); three negatives — no turning back, no refuge, no denial ("cannot be turned back" is a corpus-hapax phrase, rg-verified).
* **`[42:48]`** "But if they turn away—We did not send you as a guardian over them. Your only duty is communication. Whenever We let man taste mercy from Us, he rejoices in it; but when misfortune befalls them, as a consequence of what their hands have perpetrated, man turns blasphemous."
  * **Gloss:** The messenger's discharge (twin of `[42:6]` "you are not responsible for them") followed by anthropology: joy at mercy, blasphemy at earned misfortune — the earned-deed ledger (30, 34) returns one last time. ("befalls them… man" number-shift and the no-space em-dash are book typography — quoted sic, class-2.)
* **`[42:49]`** "To Allah belongs the dominion of the heavens and the earth. He creates whatever He wills. He grants daughters to whomever He wills, and He grants sons to whomever He wills."
  * **Gloss:** Dominion stated as creative will; the children-gift opens with daughters first — against the practice of `[16:57]` ("they attribute to Allah daughters… and for themselves what they desire"): what they despised as a gift, He names as His giving.
* **`[42:50]`** "Or He combines them together, males and females; and He renders whomever He wills sterile. He is Knowledgeable and Capable."
  * **Gloss:** The four-way distribution — daughters, sons, both, none — puts the pagan gender-arbitration ("Are you to have the males, and He the females?" `[53:21–22]`) under divine prerogative; "sterile" is the corpus's only such verb-site and "Knowledgeable and Capable" this wording's only pairing (both rg-verified).
* **`[42:51]`** "It is not for any human that Allah should speak to him, except by inspiration, or from behind a veil, or by sending a messenger to reveal by His permission whatever He wills. He is All-High, All-Wise."
  * **Gloss:** The surah's theological summit: divine address has exactly three modes, and none is face-to-face; "from behind a veil" is the corpus's only theophany-veil phrase and "All-High" this form's only site (rg-verified) — transcendence as the grammar of speech.
* **`[42:52]`** "We thus inspired you spiritually, by Our command. You did not know what the Scripture is, nor what faith is, but We made it a light, with which We guide whomever We will of Our servants. You surely guide to a straight path."
  * **Gloss:** The third ka-dhālika inspiration-ring (3 → 7 → 52): the mode that was claimed now names its vehicle — the spirit by command; the pre-revelation ignorance ("You did not know what the Scripture is, nor what faith is" — a hapax confession, rg-verified) becomes a lamp; guidance-by-will sits inside the light verse itself, then the Prophet's own guiding is affirmed.
* **`[42:53]`** "The path of Allah, to whom belongs everything in the heavens and everything on earth. Indeed, to Allah all matters revert."
  * **Gloss:** The straight path is appositionally identified as Allah's road — the Fatihah's petition (`[1:6]` "Guide us to the straight path") answered with a name and an ownership clause; the surah ends where it began (4) and sends every matter back: "to Allah all matters revert," the exact clause of `[8:44]`.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **kallama / khitab (divine speech):** `[42:51]` "It is not for any human that Allah should speak to him."
  * **Rendering:** "speak to him" — with the three-mode limitation (inspiration / veil / messenger); the veil clause is unique, but the direct-speech verb is elsewhere used of Moses only: `[4:164]` "And Allah spoke to Moses directly"; `[2:253]` "To some of them Allah spoke directly"; `[7:143]` "his Lord spoke to him" (rg-verified).
  * **Elsewhere:** 42:51's veil-mode is the systematization of 7:143/4:164 — the book's one theology-of-revelation-modes verse; cf. `[20:11–12]` where Allah "puts his hand under his wing" for Moses, the near-corpus scene of veiled speech.
* **rūḥ + amr (spirit, by command):** `[42:52]` "We thus inspired you spiritually, by Our command."
  * **Rendering:** "inspired you spiritually" — a compound for waḥy + rūḥ + amr in one clause; the noun "Spirit" itself appears in the Ha-Meem circuit at `[40:15]` "He conveys the Spirit, by His command, upon whomever He wills of His servants" and `[16:2]` "He sends down the angels with the Spirit by His command"; the mystery is policed at `[17:85]` "The Spirit belongs to the domain of my Lord; and you were given only little knowledge."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[97:4]` "In it descend the angels and the Spirit, by the leave of their Lord, with every command" — the same command-descent grammar on the Night scale.
* **nūr (light):** `[42:52]` "We made it a light, with which We guide whomever We will of Our servants."
  * **Rendering:** "a light" — the Book-function shift: from scripture ("You did not know what the Scripture is") to lamp; guidance-by-election folded into the light itself.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[6:91]` Moses' Scripture is "a light and guidance for humanity"; `[57:28]` "a light by which you walk"; `[6:122]` "made for him a light by which he walks among the people"; the negative twin `[24:40]` "He to whom Allah has not granted a light has no light" (all rg-verified).
* **rujūʿ (reverting):** `[42:53]` "Indeed, to Allah all matters revert."
  * **Rendering:** "all matters revert" — the exact closing clause occurs only once elsewhere in the corpus: `[8:44]` "To Allah all matters revert" (rg-verified); the variant form `[3:109]` "to Allah all events are referred" and `[5:18]`/`[24:42]` "to Him is the return"/"to Allah is the ultimate return" show the root's eschatological range.
  * **Elsewhere:** internally, `[42:15]` "to Him is the ultimate return" — the preacher's charter line already pointed here; the surah's last word is the matter's destination.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Meccan (classical lists — verify in print); 42:49–50's gender-gift rebuttal and 51's speech-modes sit in the Meccan polemic against the daughters-attribution cult and address the Prophet's authentication question. *Source: classical lists + corpus internal claims (cf. 16:57, 53:21–22).*
* **Asbab / narrative context:** classical readings map 42:51's three modes concretely (inspiration = prophetic wahy/dream; veil = Moses; messenger = Gabriel), and read the 42:52 "spirit" variously as revelation itself, the Quran, or the Holy Spirit — the corpus decides none of it. *Source: classical exegetical disagreement — verify in print; agent records the spread, prefers none.*
