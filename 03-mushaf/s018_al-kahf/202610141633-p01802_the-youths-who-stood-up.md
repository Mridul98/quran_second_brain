# The youths who stood up — faith increased in guidance (18:13–17)
**UID:** 202610141633
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 18:13–17 (Ruku 2 of 12 in al-Kahf)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[18:13]` – `[18:17]` (5 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The narrator retells the cave "in truth" against the guessers: the youths believed and were *increased* in guidance (13), their hearts were strengthened for a public creedal stand (14–15), their flight is re-read as a mercy-route toward ease (16), and the sun's careful detour around their cave closes the guidance axiom — the guided are truly guided, the misguided have no directing friend (17).
* **Placement:** Ruku 2; the faith-test's sermon moment — where the surah says plainly what its first narrative means.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[18:13]`** "We relate to you their story in truth. They were youths who believed in their Lord, and We increased them in guidance."
  * **Gloss:** A divine narrator's truth-claim ("in truth" — against the head-count guessers of 18:22); the causal chain: belief → increase of guidance. Guidance here is a quantity that grows (cf. 47:17, 19:76).
* **`[18:14]`** "And We strengthened their hearts, when they stood up and said, “Our Lord is the Lord of the heavens and the earth; we will not call on any god besides Him, for then we would have spoken an outrage.”"
  * **Gloss:** Heart-strengthening precedes the stand; the creed-line is cosmic lordship plus exclusivity — and a named horror ("an outrage") at the thought of other gods. The trial of faith is speech before power.
* **`[18:15]`** "“These people, our people, have taken to themselves gods other than Him. Why do they not bring a clear proof concerning them? Who, then, does greater wrong than he who invents lies and attributes them to Allah?”"
  * **Gloss:** The proof-demand as epistemic ethic — partners must produce "a clear proof" (cf. 57:25, 37:156); inventing lies about Allah is named as the greatest wrong, tying back to 18:5's "They say nothing but a lie."
* **`[18:16]`** "“Now that you have withdrawn from them, and from what they worship besides Allah, take shelter in the cave. And your Lord will unfold His mercy for you, and will set your affair towards ease.”"
  * **Gloss:** 18:10's prayer answered verbatim in shape: mercy unfolded, affair set "towards ease" (yusri — the same verb-family as 92:7 "We will ease his way towards ease" and Moses' old prayer 20:26 "ease my task"). Withdrawal is not defeat; it is the route mercy takes.
* **`[18:17]`** "You would have seen the sun, when it rose, veering away from their cave towards the right, and when it sets, moving away from them to the left, as they lay in the midst of the cave. That was one of Allah’s wonders. He whom Allah guides is truly guided; but he whom He misguides, for him you will find no directing friend."
  * **Gloss:** Cosmos choreographed around sleepers — sun and shadow keep a protective curve; "one of Allah's wonders" answers 18:9; the guidance axiom drops mid-story (near-verbatim twin at 7:178 "Whomever Allah guides is the guided one").

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`increased them in guidance`** `[18:13]`:
  * **Rendering:** belief yields more guidance, as a gift ("We increased them").
  * **Elsewhere:** `[47:17]` "As for those who are guided, He increases them in guidance"; `[19:76]` "Allah increases in guidance those who accept guidance" — and 19:76's second clause ("the things that endure—the righteous deeds") is the twin of this surah's 18:46.
* **`ease` (yusri)** `[18:16]`:
  * **Elsewhere:** `[18:88]` (Zul-Qarnain's promised "speech of Our command with ease"), `[92:7]`/`[92:10]` (ease vs. difficulty as two fates), `[20:26]` (Moses' prayer "ease my task") — ease is this surah's counter-weight to constrained hearts.
* **`truly guided`** `[18:17]`:
  * **Elsewhere:** `[7:178]` "Whomever Allah guides is the guided one. And whomever He sends astray—these are the losers"; `[6:149]` "Had He willed, He would have guided you all" — guidance as address and grant together, never coercion.

## 4. Cross-References (The Web)
* **Within surah:** `[18:10]`'s prayer → `[18:16]`'s answer (mercy/ease); `[18:9]`'s "wondrous signs" → `[18:17]` "one of Allah's wonders"; `[18:15]`'s lie-charge echoes `[18:5]`.
* **Parallel:** `[2:256]` "There shall be no compulsion in religion" — the youths flee rather than fight; 18:20's fear of being "forced back into their religion" names coercion from the other side.
* **Parallel:** `[47:17]`, `[19:76]` — the increase-of-guidance formula, shared with the guidance note's core dossier.
* **Within corpus:** `[6:149]` — "the conclusive argument belongs to Allah": the proof-demand of 18:15 restated as God's prerogative.
