# Qaf: the marvel at a warner, the crack-free sky, and the roster of the denied (50:1–15)
**UID:** 202610142115
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 50:1–15 (Ruku 1 of 3 in Qaf)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[50:1]` – `[50:15]` (15 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** A bare letter swears by the Glorious Quran, then lets the disbelievers state their own objection — a warner from among themselves is "something strange"; death-and-dust is "a farfetched return" — and answers it not with argument-about-them but with evidence-around-them: a crack-free sky, a spread earth in pairs, water that revives a dead town; the ruku ends on the surah's keystone question, "Were We fatigued by the first creation?"
* **Placement:** The surah's courtroom opening: indictment (2–5), exhibits (6–11), precedent register (12–14), verdict-question (15) — and 15's fatigue-motif is answered at the surah's far end in 50:38 (bracket).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[50:1]`** "Qaf. By the Glorious Quran."
  * **Gloss:** Letter + oath in one ayah: the transliterated letter is Itani's practice for the muqatta'at (see the fleeting note that quotes this exact verse); the oath-object is "the Glorious Quran" — the phrase occurs only here and `[85:21]` (rg). The book swears by itself; k-kalam territory.
* **`[50:2–3]`** "They marveled that a warner has come to them from among them. The disbelievers say, “This is something strange. When we have died and become dust? This is a farfetched return.”"
  * **Gloss:** Two marvels in the enemies' own words: insider-messenger ("from among them" — the scandal of proximity) and resurrection-after-dust. Verbatim sibling `[38:4]` "And they marveled that a warner has come to them from among them"; the dust-line's family: `[37:16]`, `[23:35–36]`, `[27:67]`, `[13:5]` (all rg-verified); "farfetched" also 23:36's doubled "Farfetched, farfetched is what you are promised."
* **`[50:4]`** "We know what the earth consumes of them, and with Us is a comprehensive book."
  * **Gloss:** The divine counter-ledger to their arithmetic of decay: earth-decomposition is no loss to an omniscient; "consumes" (akala) and "comprehensive book" (kitab hafiz) are each corpus-unique renderings (rg — task's "diminishes/preserving Record" is another edition). The Record family: `[82:10–12]` "Honest recorders... They know everything you do"; the same book also records what the earth eats up.
* **`[50:5]`** "But they denied the truth when it has come to them, so they are in a confused state."
  * **Gloss:** The epistemic consequence: denial first, then confusion (marij) — the surah's verdict on bad theology's effect on good thinking; note the grammar — "when it has come," the truth arrived, so the confusion is not information-failure but posture-failure.
* **`[50:6–7]`** "Have they not observed the sky above them, how We constructed it, and decorated it, and it has no cracks? And the earth, how We spread it out, and set on it mountains, and grew in it all kinds of delightful pairs?"
  * **Gloss:** The visual audit (task claim ✓): a sky without furrows — the same vocabulary as `[67:3]` "Look again. Can you see any cracks?" (rg); the spread earth — "spread it out" (dahada) recurs at `[51:48]` and is Itani's same word as 50:7's; mountains as fasteners (cf. `[16:15]` "cast mountains on the earth, lest it shifts with you"); "delightful pairs" (azwaj — every kind in mating pairs: the pair-motif of `[51:49]`, `[78:8]`, `[39:6]` — rg-verified cluster).
* **`[50:8]`** "A lesson and a reminder for every penitent worshiper."
  * **Gloss:** Purpose-clause of the exhibits: insight (basira) + tadhkira for every awwab (task's "servant who turns back" — same root; Itani "penitent worshiper"; elsewhere the word describes Abraham `[11:75]`, David `[38:30]`, and the promised wives `[66:5]` — rg). Nature's evidence has a receiver-type.
* **`[50:9–10]`** "And We brought down from the sky blessed water, and produced with it gardens and grain to harvest. And the soaring palm trees, with clustered dates."
  * **Gloss:** The evidence narrows to agriculture: blessed (mubarak) water → gardens, harvestable grain, tall palms with packed fruit-sheaths — the resurrection-proof runs through botany (cf. the water-motif across the corpus; the specific pairing here is the surah's own).
* **`[50:11]`** "As sustenance for the servants. And We revive thereby a dead town. Likewise is the resurrection."
  * **Gloss:** THE analogy verse: a town-dead-is revived by rain — "Likewise is the resurrection" (kadhalik al-nushur). Task's names ("Ma'raf", "Aluk"): ✗ the text names nothing; "a dead town" is corpus-unique (rg), and neither name appears in corpus or brain — classical geographies localize the town variously; *Source: agent inference — verify* before naming it anywhere.
* **`[50:12–14]`** "Before them the people of Noah denied the truth, and so did the dwellers of Russ, and Thamood. And Aad, and Pharaoh, and the brethren of Lot. And the Dwellers of the Woods, and the people of Tubba. They all rejected the messengers, so My threat came true."
  * **Gloss:** The precedent register, eight entries: "Russ" appears only here in the corpus (Itani transliterates; classical identifications of ashhab al-Rass vary — labeled, verify); "Dwellers of the Woods" is the same rendering as `[38:13]` and near-`[26:176]` "People of the Woods" / `[15:78]` "people of the Woods" (rg — the aykah-family); "people of Tubba" occurs also at `[44:37]` ("Are they better, or the people of Tubba...? We annihilated them"); the ruku's closing law: rejection of messengers → threat-comes-true.
* **`[50:15]`** "Were We fatigued by the first creation? But they are in doubt of a new creation."
  * **Gloss:** The bracket-question (task's locate ✓): the first creation did not tire Us — why doubt the second? Direct twin `[46:33]` "Allah, who created the heavens and the earth, and was never tired by creating them, is Able to revive the dead?" (rg-verified); the doubt-of-new-creation phrase also anchors `[13:5]` ("will we be in a new creation?"). 50:15 ↔ 50:38: the surah argues resurrection by creator-capability at both ends.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **kitab hafiz (comprehensive book):** 50:4 — the Record that loses no dust.
  * **Rendering:** "comprehensive book" (unique); the capital-R "Record" family elsewhere uses "Honest recorders" `[82:11]`, "watcher" `[50:18]`, "scroll" `[52:2–3]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[82:10–12]` — the witnesses above; link: [k-kalam note](../../02-knowledge/202610132005-k-kalam_allah_revelation_as_speech.md) for revelation-as-booked-speech.
* **awwab (penitent worshiper):** 50:8.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[11:75]` (Abraham — "gentle, kind, penitent"), `[38:30]` (David "an excellent servant. He was penitent"), `[66:5]` (the list of wives); returns inside this surah at 50:32 ("careful penitent") and 50:33 ("repentant heart") — the ruku-1 receiver is the ruku-3 entrant.
* **nushur (resurrection):** 50:11 "Likewise is the resurrection" — the word for the coming-forth of the dead; connects 50:4's earth-that-consumes to 50:44's earth-that-cracks-open (within-surah arc, rg).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[22:5]` runs the identical proof in one breath — "you see the earth still; but when We send down water on it, it vibrates, and swells, and grows all kinds of lovely pairs," spoken to people "in doubt about the Resurrection" (rg-pulled) — the nearest systematic twin of 50:6–11.
* **basira/tadhkira (lesson/reminder):** 50:8 — "A lesson and a reminder" — the pair names the exhibit-system's function.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[50:37]` "a reminder for whoever possesses a heart"; the guidance-note thread (tadhkira as the Quran's self-description, `[53:56]`-adjacent family) — see k-guidance.
