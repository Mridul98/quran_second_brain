* **Central theme:** The certainty of return against wonder. A lone letter and an oath by the Glorious Quran (1) open an argument with people who find three things impossible: a human warner from their own midst (2), resurrection after dust (3), and a second creation (15). The surah answers with evidence from outside (sky without cracks, earth spread, pairs, blessed water reviving a dead town — 6–11), evidence from inside (nearer than the jugular, two recording companions — 16–18), then runs the eschatological reel (Trumpet, driver and witness, Hell's "Are there any more?", Paradise brought near — 19–35). Its two structural brackets are the twin fatigue-questions — "Were We fatigued by the first creation?" (15) and "We created... in six days, and no fatigue touched Us" (38) — and it closes on the Prophet's release: endurance, praise at the praise-hours, and "you are not a dictator over them" (39–45).

## 3. Structural Arc
* **Oath and indictment (1–5):** the letter, the Glorious Quran, the three marvels (warner-from-themselves, dust-return, denial) → "with Us is a comprehensive book" (4) → "they are in a confused state" (5).
* **The outside evidence (6–11):** sky without cracks, spread earth, mountains, delightful pairs — "a lesson and a reminder for every penitent worshiper"; blessed water → gardens, palms, "sustenance for the servants" → "We revive thereby a dead town. Likewise is the resurrection."
* **The inside evidence (16–18):** creation of the human, knowledge of the soul's whispers, proximity past the jugular, the two seated receivers, the watcher for every word.
* **The reel (19–35):** the daze of death; the Trumpet; driver and witness; the screen lifted; the escort's testimony; the stubborn-disbeliever dossier; Hell interviewed ("Are you full? / Are there any more?"); Paradise brought near for every careful penitent; the Day of Eternity "and We have even more."
* **Consolation bracket (36–45):** destroyed stronger generations who explored the lands and found no escape; a reminder for whoever possesses a heart; the six-days/no-fatigue keystone; then the Prophet's release — endure (≈ `[20:130]`, `[38:17]`), praise before sunrise/sunset, in the night and at the end of devotions; the caller, the Shout, the Day of Emergence, the easy gathering; and the final commission: no dictatorship, only Quran-remembrance for those who fear the warning.
