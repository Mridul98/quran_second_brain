## 3. Structural Arc
* **Presence-etiquette (1–5):** no opinions ahead of Allah and His Messenger; no voices over the Prophet's; the callers from behind the chambers; the tested hearts and their forgiveness (3); patience at the door judged better (5).
* **News-ethics (6–8):** the single explicit epistemology verse of the corpus; then the psychology of the guided — faith loved, adorned in hearts; disbelief, mischief, rebellion made hateful (7); "A Grace and Favor" (8).
* **Fraternal surgery (9–10):** two fighting groups — reconcile, fight the aggressor till it complies, reconcile with justice; "Allah loves the equitable" (`[5:42]`, `[60:8]` — exact phrase, rg); brotherhood as ontology, reconciliation as duty, mercy as the aimed-at result.
* **Tongue-audit (11–12):** five prohibitions in one breath, ending in the cannibalism image — the body-politic read through the stomach.
* **Cosmic frame (13–18):** "O people!" — male/female, races and tribes designed *lita'arafu* (mutual knowing, not ranking); the only nobility index is taqwa; the A'rab claim adjudicated (14); the believer definition (15); the "teach Allah your religion" retort (16); the favor-reversal (17); Allah sees all you do (18) — the surah closes where 6 began, on God's knowledge as the ground of ethics.
