# No mocking, no dead flesh: the tongue-audit and the equality verse (49:11–18)
**UID:** 202610142113
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 49:11–18 (Ruku 2 of 2 in al-Hujurat)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[49:11]` – `[49:18]` (8 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Two more "O you who believe!" commands sweep the social body clean of ridicule, labelling, suspicion, spying and backbiting — the last capped by the corpus's most savage image (eating your dead brother's flesh) — before the address widens to "O people!": hierarchy is abolished by creation itself (13), and the Desert-Arabs' claim to belief is refused until faith enters the heart (14–18).
* **Placement:** Ruku 2 completes the surah's ethics: ruku 1 policed voice and news; this ruku polices the tongue's whole range, then grounds every prohibition in the creational charter (13) and the mouth–heart audit (14).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[49:11]`** "O you who believe! No people shall ridicule other people, for they may be better than they. Nor shall any women ridicule other women, for they may be better than they. Nor shall you slander one another, nor shall you insult one another with names. Evil is the return to wickedness after having attained faith. Whoever does not repent—these are the wrongdoers."
  * **Gloss:** Four bans in one breath (task claim ✓): mockery — with the epistemic humility clause "for they may be better than they" (the judging party cannot see the scales); women addressed symmetrically (the corpus's rare gender-parallel command — cf. `[48:5–6]`'s paired lists); name-insulting — "names" as invented labels; then the apostasy-of-manners warning: wickedness after faith (phrase corpus-unique, rg) = a return, not a first sin.
* **`[49:12]`** "O you who believe! Avoid most suspicion—some suspicion is sinful. And do not spy on one another, nor backbite one another. Would any of you like to eat the flesh of his dead brother? You would detest it. So remain mindful of Allah. Allah is Most Relenting, Most Merciful."
  * **Gloss:** Three more bans: suspicion (zann — "avoid most", not all: the casuistry of allowance stated in-text), spying (tajassus), backbiting (ghayba); then the reductio — backbiting is cannibalism of a brother who cannot press charges ("You would detest it" — the body knows the answer the tongue forgets); ends, as ruku 1 did, in taqwa + mercy.
* **`[49:13]`** "O people! We created you from a male and a female, and made you races and tribes, that you may know one another. The best among you in the sight of Allah is the most righteous. Allah is All-Knowing, Well-Experienced."
  * **Gloss:** THE equality verse (task claim ✓ — exact rendering verified): the address switches from "O you who believe" to "O people" — the only such pivot in the surah (rg); gender-first anthropology (male and female — cf. `[4:1]`-type creation language), then races-and-tribes as design ("that you may know one another" — li-ta'arafu, mutual recognition, the purpose-clause that makes diversity *intended*); the only nobility index is taqwa ("the most righteous" — task's "most God-wary" = other edition); "in the sight of Allah" also `[40:35]`; the closing names — All-Knowing, Well-Experienced (khabir: the one who has lived experience of you) — audit the ranking-instinct.
* **`[49:14]`** "The Desert-Arabs say, “We have believed.” Say, “You have not believed; but say, ‘We have submitted,’ for faith has not yet entered into your hearts. But if you obey Allah and His Messenger, He will not diminish any of your deeds. Allah is Forgiving and Merciful.”"
  * **Gloss:** The theological adjudication of a claim (task claim ✓): the a'rab's "We have believed" is refused *as wording*, not punished as lie — they are taught the honest sentence, "We have submitted" (islam ≠ iman: the mouth–heart gap measured exactly — cf. the note under 49:17); "faith has not yet entered" reverses 49:7's "adorned it in your hearts" (inward architecture, both directions); obedience keeps the ledger undiminished ("He will not diminish any of your deeds" — reward-losslessness as mercy-clause); the complete "Desert-Arabs" list: 48:11, 48:16, 9:90, 9:97–99, 9:101, 9:120, 49:14 (rg).
* **`[49:15]`** "The believers are those who believe in Allah and His Messenger, and then have not doubted, and strive for Allah’s cause with their wealth and their persons. These are the sincere."
  * **Gloss:** The definition 14 refused to hand out: belief + no doubt (shakk — the heart's criterion) + jihad bil-mal wa-l-anfus ("their wealth and their persons" — near-twin `[9:81]` "strive with their wealth and their lives... in Allah's way"; `[61:11]` renders the same triad "possessions and yourselves" — observable drift); verdict: "These are the sincere" (siddiqun — roster: `[2:177]` "These are the sincere; these are the pious", `[57:19]`, `[59:8]` — rg).
* **`[49:16]`** "Say, “Are you going to teach Allah about your religion, when Allah knows everything in the heavens and the earth, and Allah is aware of all things?”"
  * **Gloss:** The retort to claim-authorization: informing God of your own category — omniscience as the end of self-certification; the internal echo of 14's "faith has not yet entered" (God, not the heart's owner, is the witness).
* **`[49:17]`** "They regarded it a favor to you that they have submitted. Say, “Do not consider your submission a favor to me; it is Allah who has done you a favor by guiding you to the faith, if you are sincere.”"
  * **Gloss:** The favor-reversal: coming-to-Islam billed as a gift to the Prophet — canceled: the guidance *to* faith is the only favor, and it flows the other way; "if you are sincere" re-runs the 15 test; the whole transaction (claim → favor → rebate) is k-nifaq's market-grammar in miniature.
* **`[49:18]`** "Allah knows the secrets of the heavens and the earth, and Allah is seeing of everything you do."
  * **Gloss:** The closing vault: ghuyub (secrets/unseen) known, 'amma ta'malun (what you do) seen — knowledge of interiority plus surveillance of exteriority; near-twin phrasing `[2:33]` "I know the secrets of the heavens and the earth... what you reveal and what you conceal" (rg). The surah's ethics stand on that double watch.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **ghayba (backbite):** 49:12 — with the flesh-image.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[104:1]` "Woe to every slanderer backbiter" — the word's only other corpus home, in the shortest woe-surah (rg); the two occurrences define the term mutually: one legislates it, the other damns it.
* **zann (suspicion):** 49:12 "Avoid most suspicion—some suspicion is sinful" — the only "suspicion" in the corpus (rg-unique).
  * **Elsewhere (concept):** `[48:12]`/`[48:6]` the evil-thought verbs against the laggers; `[3:154]` "thinking of Allah thoughts that were untrue" — same suspicion-epidemic, same remedy clause as here ("So remain mindful").
* **taqwa criterion:** 49:13 "the most righteous" (akrama... atqaqakum — nobility redefined); Itani renders the comparative here as "righteous", but "piety" at 49:3 and "conscious of Allah" at 49:10 — three renderings of the root-family inside one surah (observable drift).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:177]` "These are the sincere; these are the pious" — the righteousness-verse pairs sincerity and piety exactly as 49:15 does; see the k-sabr_and_taqwa note's ledger for the root-family's spread.
* **aslama / amana (submitted / believed):** 49:14 — the surah's definitional scalpel.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[3:52]` "bear witness that we submit" (the disciples' honest wording — earlier draft cited 3:64, a misref); `[2:131]` "Submit!"—"I have submitted to the Lord of the Worlds"; `[27:44]` the queen: "I have submitted with Solomon, to Allah, Lord of the Worlds" — the verb's honest submissions vs 49:14's premature noun; the islam-before-iman sequence is exactly what 49:14 licenses (all rg-verified).

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Medinan (unanimous); the ruku sits at the surah's hinge from community hygiene to universal anthropology.
* **Asbab / narrative context:** 49:15–17 classically tied to the a'rab delegation(s) whose coming was counted by the Prophet as favor (transmitted reports name varying tribes — Abu Hurayra-type narrations in the tafsir literature); 49:13's particularization to any single individual (e.g., the "noble = pious" correction of a lineage-pride scene) is transmitted, not textual. *Source: classical asbab reports — verify in print; the corpus names no tribe or person.*
