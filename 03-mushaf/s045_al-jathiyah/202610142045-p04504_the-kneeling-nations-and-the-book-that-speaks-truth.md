# The kneeling nations and the Book that speaks truth (45:27–37)
**UID:** 202610142045
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 45:27–37 (Ruku 4 of 4 in al-Jathiyah)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[45:27]` – `[45:37]` (11 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The courtroom assembles itself: the Hour arrives, every community is found on its knees, each is called to its Book — and the Scripture performs its final speech-act, "This Book of Ours speaks about you in truth" — before the double verdict (mercy for believers; for the rest, the interrogation "Were My revelations not recited?", the reciprocal forgetting, no exit, no repentance); and the surah that began with a Book from the Mighty, the Wise closes on praise to the Majestic, the Wise.
* **Placement:** Ruku 4 of 4; the surah's name-scene (45:28) and its doxology (45:36–37); the recitation-arc begun at 45:6 ("We recite to you in truth") terminates at 45:29 (the Book that speaks truth about you).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[45:27]`** "To Allah belongs the kingship of the heavens and the earth. On the Day when the Hour takes place, on that Day the falsifiers will lose." — **Gloss:** The sovereignty clause verbatim-twins `[57:5]` ("To Him belongs the kingship of the heavens and the earth"); the "when the Hour takes place" clock recurs at `[30:12]`, `[30:14]`, `[40:46]`; "falsifiers" closes the career of 45:7's "sinful liar" — lying ends in loss.
* **`[45:28]`** "You will see every community on its knees; every community will be called to its Book: “Today you are being repaid for what you used to do." — **Gloss:** The name-verse: *jāthiya* — kneeling, the posture of al-Jathiyah; the same kneels-at-Hell posture at `[19:68]`, `[19:72]`, and (penitently) `[38:24]` and (defeated) `[7:120]`; each community to "its Book" — the record summons (repayment grammar of 45:15/45:22); the quotation opens here and closes only in 45:29 (cross-ayah strand, class 2 — quote as-is).
* **`[45:29]`** "This Book of Ours speaks about you in truth. We have been transcribing what you have been doing.”" — **Gloss:** The Book testifies — revelation as speech completing ruku 1's claim "We recite to you in truth" (45:6); "transcribing" is corpus-unique in this form (rg); the veracity twin: `[23:62]` "with Us is a record that tells the truth, and they will not be wronged"; the record-grammar of `[6:59]` ("...nor is anything wet or dry, but is in a clear record").
* **`[45:30]`** "As for those who believed and did righteous deeds, their Lord will admit them into His mercy. That is the clear triumph." — **Gloss:** The first verdict: admission into mercy, not into wages — the mercy-exception of `[44:42]` stated in positive form ("admit them into His mercy"; cf. also the angelic petition `[40:8]` "admit them... into the Gardens"); "the clear triumph" is corpus-unique in this form (rg), member of the triumph family: `[78:31]` "for the righteous there is triumph", "supreme triumph" `[10:64]`, `[9:111]`, `[37:60]`.
* **`[45:31]`** "But as for those who disbelieved: “Were My revelations not recited to you? But you turned arrogant, and were guilty people.”" — **Gloss:** The interrogation opens in God's first person; the gatekeepers' formula of `[39:71]` ("Did not messengers from among you come to you, reciting to you the revelations of your Lord...") is its procedural twin; "turned arrogant" is 45:8's disease reaching the docket.
* **`[45:32]`** "And when it was said, “The promise of Allah is true, and of the Hour there is no doubt,” you said, “We do not know what the Hour is; we think it is only speculation; we are not convinced.”" — **Gloss:** Their past-tense transcript quoted whole: the promise-and-no-doubt formula is the very creed of 45:26 and the patience-bracket of `[40:55]`/`[40:77]` ("So be patient. The promise of Allah is true"), `[31:33]` ("The promise of Allah is true"); "speculation / not convinced" re-runs 45:24's "only guessing" — the denial was a habit, not an argument.
* **`[45:33]`** "The evils of what they did will become evident to them, and the very thing they ridiculed will haunt them." — **Gloss:** Near-verbatim twin `[39:48]` ("There will appear to them the evils of their deeds, and they will be surrounded by what they used to ridicule"); "haunt" is corpus-unique (rg) — the mockery of 45:9 returns as an enclosing presence.
* **`[45:34]`** "And it will be said, “Today We forget you, as you forgot the encounter of this Day of yours. Your abode is the Fire, and there are no saviors for you." — **Gloss:** The reciprocal-forgetting sentence, verbatim-in-kind with `[32:14]` ("you forgot the meeting of this Day of yours; We have forgotten you"); the forgetting-ledger's self-relation: `[59:19]` ("who forgot Allah, so He made them forget themselves"); the quotation strand runs open into 45:35 (class 2).
* **`[45:35]`** "That is because you took Allah’s revelations for a joke, and the worldly life lured you.” So today they will not be brought out of it, and they will not be allowed to repent." — **Gloss:** The cause-name: revelations-as-joke — the word-for-word opposite of 2:231's command "do not take Allah’s revelations for a joke" (rg-verified phrase); the mockery of 45:9/45:33 is now the charge; "worldly life lured you" echoes 44:15's failed easing and `[6:70]` "whom the worldly life has deceived"; no exit and no repentance — the closing door also seen at `[40:85]` (faith at the witnessed hour "could not help them").
* **`[45:36]`** "Praise belongs to Allah; Lord of the heavens, Lord of the earth, Lord of humanity." — **Gloss:** The doxology: "Praise belongs to Allah" verbatim-twin `[27:93]`; the triple "Lord of" and the phrase "Lord of humanity" are corpus-unique (rg) — where 1:2 prints "Lord of the Worlds," this closing line names the three domains heaven/earth/humanity; ledger: the task's "Lord of the Worlds — Ruler of all the heavens and earth" ✗ as wording (see index).
* **`[45:37]`** "To Him belongs all supremacy in the heavens and the earth. He is the Majestic, the Wise." — **Gloss:** The last verse: supremacy belongs to Him alone (against 45:10's "adopted lords"); "the Majestic, the Wise" verbatim-twin `[59:24]`, and the wisdom-name answers 45:2's "the Exalted in Might, the Wise" — the surah's two name-brackets match.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`jāthiya` (kneeling — the surah-name)**:
  * **Rendering:** "every community on its knees" (45:28); the same English posture at `[19:68]` ("on their knees" before Hell), `[19:72]`, and penitently at `[38:24]` ("fell down to his knees, and repented"), and `[7:120]` (the magicians "fell to their knees").
  * **Elsewhere:** the corpus thus shows kneeling in three registers — submission (7:120), repentance (38:24), and summons (45:28/19:68); the surah's namesake is the third: kneeling that is only being processed. *Source: agent inference from the four sites — verify.*
* **The speaking/transcribing Book (kalam terminus)**:
  * **Rendering:** "This Book of Ours speaks about you in truth. We have been transcribing..." (45:29); the Book was recited "in truth" at 45:6; the record "tells the truth" at `[23:62]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** the k-kalam note's channel-argument reaches its eschatological form: the speech that was addressed to them (recitation) becomes speech about them (testimony) — two-directional kalam, rg-verified on both lines.
* **"forgetting" (reciprocal nisyān)**:
  * **Rendering:** "Today We forget you, as you forgot the encounter of this Day of yours" (45:34); "forgot Allah, so He made them forget themselves" `[59:19]`; "you forgot the meeting... We have forgotten you" `[32:14]`.
  * **Elsewhere:** the forgetting is answered by the transcription of 45:29: what they forgot is what the Book never stopped writing — the surah's own memory-theology.
* **"Lord of humanity" (the closing rabb-triple)**:
  * **Rendering:** unique triple + unique phrase (rg); vs "Lord of the Worlds" at `[1:2]` and the pair "Lord of the heavens and the earth" (e.g., `[44:7]`).
  * **Elsewhere:** the drift from "Worlds" to "humanity" is translator choice at the surah's praise-slot; the observable fact is the triple-anaphora ("Lord of... Lord of... Lord of"), which has no parallel in the corpus. *Source: translator's term choice (observable in corpus) — verify against Arabic.*
