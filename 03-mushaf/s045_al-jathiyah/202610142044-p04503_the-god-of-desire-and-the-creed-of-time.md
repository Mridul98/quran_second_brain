# The god called Desire and the creed called Time (45:22–26)
**UID:** 202610142044
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 45:22–26 (Ruku 3 of 4 in al-Jathiyah)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[45:22]` – `[45:26]` (5 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Creation "with justice" (22) sets the bench; then two rival gods are unmasked in the same breath — the man who installs his own desire as a deity, sealed hearing-heart-vision like Pharaoh's psychology (23), and the collective creed that makes Time the only killer (24) — whose only argument when answered is to demand the ancestors back (25), leaving the surah's counter-litany to state the actual sequence: life, death, gathering, no doubt (26).
* **Placement:** Ruku 3 of 4; the shortest ruku in the surah, its center of gravity: ruku 1's signs prove the Sender, ruku 2's favors prove the Benefactor, and here the two false substitutes for both are indicted before ruku 4's courtroom closes the argument.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[45:22]`** "Allah created the heavens and the earth with justice, so that every soul will be repaid for what it has earned. And they will not be wronged." — **Gloss:** The cosmological premise: creation is juridical, not casual — the twin thesis of `[44:38–39]` ("not to play... for a specific purpose"); the repayment clause twins `[2:281]` ("each soul will be rewarded fully for what it has earned, and they will not be wronged") and `[39:70]` ("every soul will be fully compensated"); "with justice" pre-loads 45:21's rejected equality.
* **`[45:23]`** "Have you considered him who has taken his desire for his god? Allah has knowingly led him astray, and has sealed his hearing and his heart, and has placed a veil over his vision. Who will guide him after Allah? Will you not reflect?" — **Gloss:** The desire-god portrait, the surah's most quoted line; the sealing recipe is verbatim-twin of `[2:7]` ("set a seal on their hearts and on their hearing, and over their vision is a veil") — now applied to one man's chosen god; "knowingly led him astray" carries the same divine-with-knowledge idiom as 44:32's "chose them knowingly" (human misguidance matched to divine knowing — *Source: agent inference from the paired English — verify*); "Who will guide him after Allah?" twins `[13:33]`'s point ("Whomever Allah misguides has no guide"); the question-form "Have you considered/seen" twin: `[25:43]` "Have you seen him who chose his desire as his god? Would you be an agent for him?" — the canonical pair.
* **`[45:24]`** "And they say, “There is nothing but this our present life; we die and we live, and nothing destroys us except time.” But they have no knowledge of that; they are only guessing." — **Gloss:** The materialist confession, quoted complete: one life, alternating death and birth, Time (dahr) as the only agent — "except time" is the corpus's only such line (rg-verified); the epistemic verdict "no knowledge... only guessing" answers both 45:23's desire-man and 44:35's one-deniers; ledger: the task's appended "they are only like cattle — nay, further astray" is not in 45:23 or 25:43 in this edition — the cattle-line sits at `[25:44]` ("They are just like cattle, but even more errant in their way") and `[7:179]` ("These are like cattle. In fact, they are further astray").
* **`[45:25]`** "When Our clarifying Verses are recited to them, their only argument is to say, “Bring back our ancestors, if you are truthful.”" — **Gloss:** The falsifiability demand; verbatim twin of `[44:36]` (the s044 ledger's ancestor-cry) — the two surahs quote the identical rebuttal-request; "clarifying Verses... recited" continues the ruku-1 recitation-arc (45:6).
* **`[45:26]`** "Say, “Allah gives you life, then He makes you die; then He gathers you for the Day of Resurrection, about which there is no doubt. But most people do not know.”" — **Gloss:** The counter-litany against both gods: the sequence belongs to Allah, not desire or dahr; near-twins `[22:66]` ("He who gives you life, then makes you die, then revives you" — with its verdict "The human being is unappreciative," the shukr ledger) and `[30:40]` (life→death→bring-back with the idol-rebuttal "Can any of your idols do any of that?"); "about which there is no doubt" answers 45:24's "only guessing"; "most people do not know" is the refrain shared with `[44:39]`.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`hawa` (desire/inclination) as rival-deity**:
  * **Rendering:** "taken his desire for his god" (45:23); the same appetite as "the inclinations of those who do not know" (45:18) and `[25:43]` "chose his desire as his god"; `[38:26]` legislates against it: "do not follow desire, lest it diverts you from Allah's path."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[6:70]` "those who take their religion for play and pastime" — desire-worship's sibling portrait; the tawhid note's "partners you cannot see" is most literal here: the partner is installed inside one's own appetite.
* **`dahr` (time) as agent-creed**:
  * **Rendering:** "nothing destroys us except time" (45:24) — a corpus-unique English line (rg); the creed is quoted, never named, in this edition.
  * **Elsewhere:** the dahr-label from pre-Islamic usage is extra-corpus: *Source: classical historiography (the ahl-al-dahr description in tafsir tradition) — verify in print; not derivable from the corpus.*
* **The seal recipe (hearing / heart / vision)**:
  * **Rendering:** "sealed his hearing and his heart... a veil over his vision" (45:23); `[2:7]` verbatim-twin; the ladder runs 45:8 ("as though he did not hear") → 45:23 (actually sealed) — the ruku-1 symptom becomes the ruku-3 diagnosis.
  * **Elsewhere:** rg across the corpus finds the three-sense seal (heart + hearing + sight/vision) at four sites: `[2:7]`, `[6:46]`, `[16:108]`, and here; and `[47:16]` welds the two halves of 45:23 together in one line — "whose hearts Allah has sealed, and they follow their own desires" — the strongest corpus statement that desire-worship and sealing are one event. *Source: observable corpus pairing — verify.*
* **"the Day of Resurrection" (ba'th) vs "the Hour"**:
  * **Rendering:** 45:26 "the Day of Resurrection, about which there is no doubt"; 45:17 "the Day of Resurrection"; 45:27–32 "the Hour" — two clocks for the same deadline within one surah.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[44:40]` "The Day of Sorting Out is the appointed time" supplies the sibling surah's third name; all three are deadline-grammar, rg-verified.
