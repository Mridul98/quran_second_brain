# The Book, the signs, and the sinful liar (45:1–11)
**UID:** 202610142042
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 45:1–11 (Ruku 1 of 4 in al-Jathiyah)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[45:1]` – `[45:11]` (11 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The letters Ha, Meem hand over a Book whose sender is the Mighty, the Wise — and the whole cosmos is empanelled as evidence in three tiers (heavens and earth for believers, your own creation for the firm in faith, night, day, rain, and winds for people who reason); against this courtroom stands one figure, the sinful liar who hears the recitation "as though he did not hear it," and the ruku's two-word verdict: "This is guidance."
* **Placement:** Ruku 1 of 4; the evidentiary opening of al-Jathiyah, answered at the end (ruku 4) by the Book that "speaks about you in truth" (45:29).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[45:1]`** "Ha, Meem." — **Gloss:** Transliterated letters, ayah 1; ledger: the longer letter-string the task suspected is not here — 45:2 is already the Book-claim, while the extra letters sit at `[42:2]` ("Ayn, Seen, Qaf.") and `[19:1]` ("Kaf, Ha, Ya, Ayn, Saad."). Head of the Ha-Meem family 40–46 (all seven rg-verified).
* **`[45:2]`** "The revelation of the Book is from Allah, the Exalted in Might, the Wise." — **Gloss:** The Book-claim: a sender with might-and-wisdom names; near-verbatim twin `[39:1]` ("The revelation of the Book is from Allah, the Mighty and Wise"), kin claims `[32:2]`, `[46:2]`, `[40:2]` — the Ha-Meem cluster's signature opening.
* **`[45:3]`** "In the heavens and the earth are proofs for the believers." — **Gloss:** Witness exhibit one: the cosmos; note the edition's tier-word here is "proofs" (not "signs") — the first rung of the audience ladder.
* **`[45:4]`** "And in your own creation, and in the creatures He scattered, are signs for people of firm faith." — **Gloss:** Exhibit two turns the camera on the witness's own body; "the creatures He scattered" twins `[42:29]` ("the creatures He has spread throughout them") and `[31:10]`; "people of firm faith" is rung two — the same certainty-word that fronts `[44:7]` "if you know for sure."
* **`[45:5]`** "And in the alternation of night and day, and in the sustenance Allah sends down from the sky, with which He revives the earth after its death, and in the circulation of the winds, are marvels for people who reason." — **Gloss:** Exhibit three is the famous dossier shared with `[2:164]` — night/day, sent-down water reviving dead earth, winds, scattered creatures (the ships exhibit of 2:164 arrives one ruku later, at 45:12–13) — with "the earth after its death" as a resurrection-analogy; rung three: "people who reason."
* **`[45:6]`** "These are Allah’s Verses which We recite to you in truth. In which message, after Allah and His revelations, will they believe?" — **Gloss:** The recitation-claim lands the evidence: these Verses are spoken "in truth," and the rhetorical question leaves no other message available — the surah's own kalam-argument; cf. `[45:25]` "When Our clarifying Verses are recited to them" (ruku 3).
* **`[45:7]`** "Woe to every sinful liar." — **Gloss:** The indictment opens on a type, not a name; "Woe" (wayl) is the prophetic threat-formula, cf. `[39:22]` "Woe to those whose hearts are hardened against the mention of Allah."
* **`[45:8]`** "Who hears Allah's revelations being recited to him, yet he persists arrogantly, as though he did not hear them. Announce to him a painful punishment." — **Gloss:** The portrait verbatim-twins `[31:7]` ("he turns away in pride, as though he did not hear them"); the straight apostrophe in "Allah's" is this line's own typography (rg-verified: unique in s045) — quote as-is.
* **`[45:9]`** "And when he learns something of Our revelations, he takes them in mockery." — **Gloss:** Second stage of the disease: from deaf arrogance to open mockery; the close will mirror it verbatim-in-kind: `[45:35]` "you took Allah’s revelations for a joke"; cf. `[31:6]` "to make a mockery of it"; line continues "For such there is a shameful punishment."
* **`[45:10]`** "Beyond them lies Hell. What they have earned will not benefit them at all, nor will those they adopted as lords instead of Allah. They will have a terrible punishment." — **Gloss:** The earnings-versus-allies ledger: neither acquisition nor patronage avails — the association-clause ("those they adopted as lords instead of Allah") is tawhid's negative case; cf. the same collapse at "no friend avails" `[44:41]` and 45:23's desire-lord.
* **`[45:11]`** "This is guidance. Those who blaspheme their Lord’s revelations will have a punishment of agonizing pain." — **Gloss:** Two words close the ruku — the Book itself named "guidance" (hudā), linking the k-guidance note; rejecting these specific revelations costs the "agonizing pain" bracket.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`ayat` (signs/Verses)**:
  * **Rendering:** within one eleven-ayah ruku: "proofs" (45:3), "signs" (45:4), "marvels" (45:5), "Verses" capitalized (45:6), lowercase "revelations" (45:6b–11) — a five-word drift over one root-family, the edition's clearest example of term-choice variance (translation variance per the artifacts note, not an artifact).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:164]` closes its dossier with "are signs for people who understand" — same inventory, single term; the drift is this ruku's, the Arabic's stability is confirmed only by the twin lines. *Source: observable term choice in corpus — verify against Arabic for a firm claim.*
* **The audience ladder** (believers / firm faith / reasoners):
  * **Rendering:** "for the believers... for people of firm faith... for people who reason" (45:3–5), extended ruku 2 by "signs for a people who think" (`[45:13]`).
  * **Elsewhere:** certainty-language of 44:7 ("if you know for sure") and 45:20 ("people who believe with certainty") bookends the pair-surahs — the ladder climbs to yaqin-class English throughout.
* **`wayl` ("Woe")**:
  * **Rendering:** "Woe to every sinful liar" (45:7) — the only "Woe" in s045.
  * **Elsewhere:** the threat-formula saturates the Meccan band: `[39:22]`, `[75:34–35]` ("Woe to you; and woe" doubled), `[28:80]` — the corpus treats "Woe" as un-translatable liturgical speech.
* **"as though he did not hear" (the feigned-deafness formula)**:
  * **Rendering:** 45:8 verbatim; `[31:7]` verbatim ("as though he did not hear them, as though there is deafness in his ears").
  * **Elsewhere:** the twin surah Luqman's mockers share the disease; the pair 45:8/31:7 is the strongest evidence the "sinful liar" portrait is a type-scene, not a biography. *Source: agent inference from the verbatim pair — verify.*
