## 3. Structural Arc
* **Letters, Book, witnesses (1–11):** Ha, Meem → the Mighty-Wise sender → heavens/earth, your own creation, scattered creatures, night-and-day, sent-down sustenance, revived earth, winds — an audience ladder ("believers / people of firm faith / people who reason") → the recited Verses in truth → the sinful liar who hears as though deaf → "This is guidance."
* **Favors and the community (12–21):** the sea, ships, bounty, thanks; whatever is in heavens and earth "at your service — all is from Him"; the command to forgive those who do not hope for Allah's Days; self-earning and return; Israel's Book-wisdom-prophecy and their dispute "only after knowledge came"; the pathway of faith set for the Messenger; illumination, guidance, mercy; the equality-assumption rejected.
* **Two false gods unmasked (22–26):** creation "with justice" frames every soul's repayment; the desire-god — sealed hearing, heart, vision (2:7's recipe on a single man); the time-creed — "nothing destroys us except time... only guessing"; the ancestor-demand (verbatim from 44:36); the counter-litany: "Allah gives you life, then He makes you die; then He gathers you."
* **The courtroom closes (27–37):** kingship and the Hour's loss → every community kneeling, called to its Book → the transcribing Scripture → the double verdict (mercy / "were My revelations not recited?") → "Today We forget you" → "Praise belongs to Allah... He is the Majestic, the Wise."
* **Letters-to-praise ring:** the surah runs from "the revelation of the Book is from Allah, the Exalted in Might, the Wise" (2) to "He is the Majestic, the Wise" (37) — a name-bracket; within it the Book "which We recite to you in truth" (6) becomes "This Book of Ours speaks about you in truth" (29): revelation addressed becomes revelation as verdict.
