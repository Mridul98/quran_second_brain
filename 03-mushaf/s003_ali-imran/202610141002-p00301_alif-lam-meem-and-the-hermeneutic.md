# Alif, Lam, Meem — the Living God and the two kinds of verses (3:1–9)
**UID:** 202610141002
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 3:1–9 (Ruku 1 of 20 in Ali ‘Imran)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[3:1]` – `[3:9]` (9 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The surah re-opens with the same untranslated letters as al-Baqarah, answers them with the purest creed line in the book (3:2), then — before any narrative — installs its own reading rules: some verses are definitive, some unspecific, and hunting the unspecific for "descent" is the symptom of a deviating heart (3:7); the right response is the prayer of the guided for staying guided (3:8–9).
* **Placement:** Opening ruku; it sets the hermeneutic frame (3:7) for every scriptural dispute the surah will later referee (3:19, 3:64–80, 3:105).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[3:1]`** "Alif, Lam, Meem."
  * **Gloss:** The letter-oath again — same opener as `[2:1]`; meaning withheld (see Fleeting note).
* **`[3:2]`** "Allah, there is no god but He, the Living, the Eternal."
  * **Gloss:** The letters resolve into the shahada clause plus two names: self-living, self-subsisting.
* **`[3:3]`** "He sent down to you the Book with the Truth, confirming what came before it; and He sent down the Torah and the Gospel."
  * **Gloss:** Revelation is one sequence: this Book authenticates the earlier two — the surah's scripture-claim in one line.
* **`[3:4]`** "Aforetime, as guidance for mankind; and He sent down the Criterion. Those who have rejected Allah’s signs will have a severe punishment. Allah is Mighty, Able to take revenge."
  * **Gloss:** Purpose clause (guidance) + the Furqan that splits true from false; then the warning edge.
* **`[3:5]`** "Nothing is hidden from Allah, on earth or in the heaven."
  * **Gloss:** Total knowledge — grounds both the Criterion's reliability and the coming account.
* **`[3:6]`** "It is He who forms you in the wombs as He wills. There is no god except He, the Almighty, the Wise."
  * **Gloss:** Sovereignty over the unseen beginning of each life; creed line repeated (inclusio with 3:2).
* **`[3:7]`** "It is He who revealed to you the Book. Some of its verses are definitive; they are the foundation of the Book, and others are unspecific."
  * **Gloss:** The Book self-classifies: muhkam = mother/foundation; mutashabih = the rest.
* **`[3:7]`** "As for those in whose hearts is deviation, they follow the unspecific part, seeking descent, and seeking to derive an interpretation."
  * **Gloss:** The pathology: going after the unspecific to produce *ta'wil* ("descent") the text does not authorize.
* **`[3:7]`** "But none knows its interpretation except Allah and those firmly rooted in knowledge say, “We believe in it; all is from our Lord.” But none recollects except those with understanding."
  * **Gloss:** The cure: the people of knowledge hand the ambiguity to Allah and believe all of it.
* **`[3:8]`** "“Our Lord, do not cause our hearts to swerve after You have guided us, and bestow on us mercy from Your presence; You are the Giver.”"
  * **Gloss:** The taught prayer — guidance is addressed, not owned; it can swerve.
* **`[3:9]`** "“Our Lord, You will gather the people for a Day in which there is no doubt.” Allah will never break His promise."
  * **Gloss:** The Day of gathering as the horizon of the prayer; the narrator's assurance caps it.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`muhkam / mutashabih`**: rendered "definitive" / "unspecific" (elsewhere in the corpus these words appear only here — observable fact).
  * **Rendering:** 3:7; the *umm al-kitab* phrase rendered "the foundation of the Book."
  * **Elsewhere:** 42:14 renders the same split-of-people motif ("They became divided only after knowledge came to them") — the sociological echo of this epistemological verse.
* **`hayy / qayyum`**: "the Living, the Eternal" (3:2).
  * **Rendering:** drift to note: 2:255 renders the pair "the Living, the Everlasting"; 20:111 "the Living, the Eternal."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:255]` (Throne verse), `[20:111]` — same names at the book's most famous and most humble scenes.
* **`ta'wil`**: rendered "descent" (lit. return-place) and "interpretation" in the same verse (3:7).
  * **Rendering:** Itani splits one root across two English words — a term-choice visible in the corpus.
  * **Elsewhere:** the "firmly rooted in knowledge" (rasikhun) also at `[4:162]` — the only other corpus site of the phrase.
* **`al-Furqan`**: "the Criterion" (3:4).
  * **Rendering:** consistent "Criterion" across the corpus.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:53]`, `[2:185]`, `[21:48]`, `[25:1]` — always a *sent-down* discerning book.

## 4. Cross-References (The Web)
* **Parallel:** `[2:1]` — same letters, same untranslated strategy; 3:2 then supplies what 2:1–2 lacks (the creed clause).
* **Within surah:** `[3:19]` and `[3:105]` — "differing after knowledge" is the exact behavior 3:7 pathologizes; the whole Najran movement plays it out.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Medinan (classical consensus); the muhkam/mutashabih verse is used across tafsir traditions to referee living disputes over the Book's ambiguous passages.
* **Asbab / narrative context:** the punctuation of 3:7 ("except Allah" — stop or continue?) is a genuine classical disagreement: those "firmly rooted" either know the ta'wil or only believe. (Source: classical dispute recorded by al-Tabari and Ibn Kathir on 3:7; agent presentation — verify against the Arabic waqf traditions.)
