# Owner of Sovereignty: history's judge and the friends problem (3:21–30)
**UID:** 202610141004
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 3:21–30 (Ruku 3 of 20 in Ali ‘Imran)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[3:21]` – `[3:30]` (10 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** Against those who kill prophets and cite their own scripture selectively (3:21–24), the ruku answers with the most comprehensive sovereignty prayer in the Quran — "You grant sovereignty to whom You will" (3:26–27) — and draws the community's boundary line: believers do not take disbelievers for intimate friends (3:28), because nothing, hidden or disclosed, is unknown to the God who holds all power (3:29–30).
* **Placement:** Third ruku; after 3:19's "differed out of envy," this unit shows envy's violence (killing the just, 3:21) and then dissolves the fear behind it in God's hand of all goodness.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[3:21]`** "As for those who defy Allah’s revelations, and kill the prophets unjustly, and kill those who advocate justice among the people— promise them a painful retribution."
  * **Gloss:** The triple crime: defying revelation, killing prophets, killing justice-advocates.
* **`[3:22]`** "They are those whose deeds will come to nothing, in this world and in the Hereafter; and they will have no saviors."
  * **Gloss:** Deed-invalidation — the recurring bankruptcy grammar (cf. 3:91's ransom clause).
* **`[3:23]`** "Have you not considered those who were given a share of the Scripture, as they were called to the Scripture of Allah to arbitrate between them; then some of them turned back, and declined?"
  * **Gloss:** The "share" (nahs) motif: a portion of scripture accepted, the whole refused when it arbitrates against interest.
* **`[3:24]`** "That is because they said, “The Fire will not touch us except for a limited number of days.” They have been misled in their religion by the lies they fabricated."
  * **Gloss:** The escrow theology: a guaranteed short sentence — named here as self-misleading fabrication.
* **`[3:25]`** "How about when We gather them for a Day in which there is no doubt, and each soul will be paid in full for what it has earned, and they will not be wronged?"
  * **Gloss:** The gathered Day (3:9's prayer) answers the limited-days claim: full payment, no shortchanging.
* **`[3:26]`** "Say, “O Allah, Owner of Sovereignty. You grant sovereignty to whom You will, and You strip sovereignty from whom you will. You honor whom you will, and You humiliate whom you will. In Your hand is all goodness. You are Capable of all things.”"
  * **Gloss:** The sayyid al-mulk prayer — sovereignty as delegated and revoked at will; goodness held in one hand.
* **`[3:27]`** "“You merge the night into the day, and You merge the day into the night; and you bring the living out of the dead, and You bring the dead out of the living; and You provide for whom you will without measure.”"
  * **Gloss:** Sovereignty's credentials in nature: time's folding, life from death, unmeasured provision.
* **`[3:28]`** "Believers are not to take disbelievers for friends instead of believers. Whoever does that has nothing to do with Allah, unless it is to protect your own selves against them. Allah warns you to beware of Him. To Allah is the destiny."
  * **Gloss:** The intimate-friendship (wilaya) boundary with its self-preservation caveat; "to Allah is the destiny" closes the loophole's door.
* **`[3:29]`** "Say, “Whether you conceal what is in your hearts, or disclose it, Allah knows it.” He knows everything in the heavens and the earth. Allah is Powerful over everything."
  * **Gloss:** Inner life is transparent to God — the enforcement mechanism of 3:28.
* **`[3:30]`** "On the Day when every soul finds all the good it has done presented. And as for the evil it has done, it will wish there were a great distance between them. Allah cautions you of Himself. Allah is Kind towards the servants."
  * **Gloss:** Full presentation of the ledger — and the paradox line: the warning itself is kindness.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`awliya` / "friends"**: rendered "take disbelievers for friends" (3:28).
  * **Rendering:** intimacy/betrayal register; same root family as "Guardian" (mawla) at 3:68.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[5:51]` "Do not take the Jews and the Christians as allies" — the canonical twin verse; `[3:118]` "Do not befriend outsiders" — this surah's own reiteration.
* **`malik al-mulk` / "Owner of Sovereignty"**: 3:26 — unique in the corpus as a divine title (observable fact).
  * **Elsewhere:** the sovereignty root recurs in this surah at 3:189 ("To Allah belongs the sovereignty of the heavens and the earth") — the ruku's bookend.
* **`rizq` provision "without measure"**: "You provide for whom you will without measure" (3:27).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:212]` "Allah provides to whomever He wills without measure" (verified); `[3:37]` Mary's provision "without reckoning" (verified) — the same divine hand, two synonyms: unaccountable distribution as a signature of sovereignty.
* **`nasib` / "a share of the Scripture"**: 3:23 ("those who were given a share of the Scripture").
  * **Elsewhere:** `[4:44]` and `[4:51]` — "Have you not considered those who were given a share of the Book?" (verified) — same idiom, same opening question-form; partial possession of scripture as self-deception is a Baqarah-Nisa-AliImran triangle (also `[4:53]` "a share of the kingdom" — same word on the same people).

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Medinan; 3:28's friendship boundary belongs to the same wilaya legislation cluster as 5:51 and 4:144 (classical treatment).
* **Asbab / narrative context:** the "limited number of days" of 3:24 is cited by classical tafsir as a transmitted claim of scriptural communities about their fire-sentence (Ibn Kathir on 3:24, citing 2:80's parallel); 3:23's refusal of arbitration is read against the same background.
