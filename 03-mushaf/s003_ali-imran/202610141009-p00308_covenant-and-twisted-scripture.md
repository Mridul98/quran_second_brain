# Day-end defections and twisted tongues (3:72–80)
**UID:** 202610141009
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 3:72–80 (Ruku 8 of 20 in Ali ‘Imran)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[3:72]` – `[3:80]` (9 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The ruku exposes a strategy — believe at the day's start, reject at its end, so the believers may return (3:72) — and the tribal logic behind it, "trust none except those who follow your religion" (3:73), then answers with grace-grammar: all guidance and grace are Allah's, given to whom He wills (3:73–74). Against the suspicion of bad faith, it sets the honest counter-portrait: some People of the Book return a mound of gold, some not a coin (3:75) — and the criterion becomes covenant-keeping plus piety (3:76–77). It ends on Scripture integrity: twisting with tongues (3:78), and the impossibility that any prophet given book and wisdom would say "be my worshipers" (3:79–80).
* **Placement:** Second half of the Najran movement; 3:79–80 close the question the common word (3:64) opened — what kind of lordship prophethood actually claims.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[3:72]`** "Some of the People of the Book say, “Believe in what was revealed to the believers at the beginning of the day, and reject it at its end, so that they may return.”"
  * **Gloss:** Conversion-as-infiltration plot: tactical belief timed to produce apostasy in the enemy.
* **`[3:73]`** "And trust none except those who follow your religion.” Say, “Guidance is Allah’s guidance."
  * **Gloss:** The exclusivist slogan answered: guidance is not a sect possession but Allah's to give.
* **`[3:73]`** "If someone is given the like of what you were given, or they argue with you before your Lord, say, “All grace is in Allah’s hand; He gives it to whomever He wills.” Allah is Bounteous and Knowing."
  * **Gloss:** The parity threat — receiving the same revelation — deflected to grace's ownership (echoes 3:26's sovereignty prayer).
* **`[3:74]`** "He specifies His mercy for whomever He wills. Allah is Possessor of Sublime Grace."
  * **Gloss:** Election restated as mercy's prerogative, closing the door on the trust-no-outside-us ethic.
* **`[3:75]`** "Among the People of the Book is he, who, if you entrust him with a heap of gold, he will give it back to you. And among them is he, who, if you entrust him with a single coin, he will not give it back to you, unless you keep after him."
  * **Gloss:** Integrity is individual, not communal — the same community contains both extremes.
* **`[3:75]`** "That is because they say, “We are under no obligation towards the gentiles.” They tell lies about Allah, and they know it."
  * **Gloss:** The exemption-doctrine named and indicted: a lie about Allah told knowingly.
* **`[3:76]`** "Indeed, whoever fulfills his commitments and maintains piety—Allah loves the pious."
  * **Gloss:** The criterion verse: awfa (fulfilling pledges) + taqwa = loved by Allah — the covenant note's own counterweight line.
* **`[3:77]`** "Those who exchange the covenant of Allah, and their vows, for a small price, will have no share in the Hereafter, and Allah will not speak to them, nor will He look at them on the Day of Resurrection, nor will He purify them. They will have a painful punishment."
  * **Gloss:** The triple withholding — speech, gaze, purification — on those who sell covenant and oath for pocket change.
* **`[3:78]`** "And among them are those who twist the Scripture with their tongues, that you may think it from the Scripture, when it is not from the Scripture. And they say, “It is from Allah,” when it is not from Allah. They tell lies and attribute them to Allah, knowingly."
  * **Gloss:** Recitation-fraud: fabricated readings laundered through the Scripture's name.
* **`[3:79]`** "No person to whom Allah has given the Scripture, and wisdom, and prophethood would ever say to the people, “Be my worshipers rather than Allah’s.” Rather, “Be people of the Lord, according to the Scripture you teach, and the teachings you learn.”"
  * **Gloss:** Prophethood disqualifies self-worship by definition; its output is rabbani people — belongers to the Lord.
* **`[3:80]`** "Nor would he command you to take the angels and the prophets as lords. Would he command you to infidelity after you have submitted?"
  * **Gloss:** The common word's third clause (3:64) enforced: no angel- or prophet-lordship; kufr after islam is the absurdity being refuted.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`thaman qalil` / "small price"**: 3:77.
  * **Rendering:** consistent "small price" for the covenant-sale idiom.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:41]`, `[2:174]`, `[16:95]` use the identical "small price" rendering; `[2:79]` renders the same word "a little price" — one drift to watch when tracing the idiom (observable).
* **`taranni` / "twist the Scripture with their tongues"**: 3:78.
  * **Rendering:** the vocal distortion — recitation as forgery's delivery method.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[4:46]` "twisting with their tongues and slandering the religion" — same vocal-distortion word (verified); `[2:79]` the hand-written version: "write the Scripture with their own hands, and then say, 'This is from Allah'" — 3:78 is the ear to 2:79's hand.
* **`rabbaniyyun` / "people of the Lord"**: 3:79 — unique in the corpus (observable; the relational noun appears nowhere else in this edition's renderings).
* **`ahd/commitments`**: 3:76 — rendered "fulfills his commitments."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:40]` "fulfill your pledge to Me"; `[6:152]` "fulfill your covenant with Allah" — this exact clause (3:76) is quoted inside the covenant knowledge note as its counterweight (verified in the note itself).

## 4. Cross-References (The Web)
* **Parallel:** `[2:174]` — the no-speech/no-purification pair is imported almost verbatim from Baqarah (3:77 adds "nor will He look at them") — the surah re-cites its own precedent.
* **Parallel:** `[9:31]` — taking priests/rabbis/Messiah as lords, which 3:80 forbids in theory, is charged in practice.
* **Parallel:** `[2:79]` — hand-written scripture laundered as "from Allah": the documentary twin of 3:78's oral fraud.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Medinan; part of the People-of-the-Book argument (same register as 2:75–79's "do you hope they will believe?" cluster).
* **Asbab / narrative context:** 3:72's day-begin/day-end tactic is classically read as a reported *plan* of some Jewish leaders (Ibn Kathir on 3:72, transmitting from Ibn Abbas); Najran setting attaches to 3:80's angel/prophet-lordship denial (Source: classical tafsir reports — verify).
