# "Allah is enough for us": the wounded who answered the call again (3:172–180)
**UID:** 202610141019
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 3:172–180 (Ruku 18 of 20 in Ali ‘Imran)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[3:172]` – `[3:180]` (9 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The coda to Uhud turns to the pursuit: those who responded to Allah and the Messenger after the wound — for the virtuous among them, a great reward (3:172); when the mobilized enemy was announced to them, faith only increased, and they said the surah's keel-laying sentence: "Allah is enough for us; He is the Excellent Protector" (3:173), returning with grace untouched (3:174); fear is re-routed — that was only Satan frightening his allies, so fear Me (3:175); and the consolation completes: do not grieve the head-runners into disbelief — their respite is a downward escalator (3:176–178); Allah will not leave believers un-sorted (3:179), and the hoarders of bounty will find it coiled around them on the Resurrection Day (3:180).
* **Placement:** The narrative movement's last ruku before the thematic close: response-after-wound proves the test of 3:141–142 was passed by some, and 3:180 welds martyrdom's detachment (3:157) back into the surah's wealth-theology.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[3:172]`** "Those who responded to Allah and the Messenger, despite the persecution they had suffered. For the virtuous and the pious among them is a great reward."
  * **Gloss:** Re-enlistment while still wounded; the reward is reserved for the virtuous-and-pious gradation within the responders.
* **`[3:173]`** "Those to whom the people have said, “The people have mobilized against you, so fear them.” But this only increased them in faith, and they said, “Allah is enough for us; He is the Excellent Protector.”"
  * **Gloss:** The threat-intelligence becomes fuel; the hasb clause is this community's Bedouin-declaration creed.
* **`[3:174]`** "So they came back with grace from Allah, and bounty, and no harm having touched them. They pursued what pleases Allah. Allah possesses immense grace."
  * **Gloss:** The pursuit ends without battle — returnee grammar: grace, bounty, zero harm, approval.
* **`[3:175]`** "That is only Satan frightening his partisans; so do not fear them, but fear Me, if you are believers."
  * **Gloss:** The enemy's morale weapon named and deflated; fear is a single-direction commodity (cf. Fatihah's object of worship).
* **`[3:176]`** "And do not be saddened by those who rush into disbelief. They will not harm Allah in the least. Allah desires to give them no share in the Hereafter. A terrible torment awaits them."
  * **Gloss:** The Prophet's grief managed: defectors cannot wound God; their loss is their own portion-less-ness.
* **`[3:177]`** "Those who exchange blasphemy for faith will not harm Allah in the least. A painful torment awaits them."
  * **Gloss:** The same exchange-verb (ibādala) that sells the covenant "for a small price" at 3:77 and 3:187 (both verified) here trades faith itself — the sale grammar of the surah's market theology.
* **`[3:178]`** "Those who disbelieve should not assume that We respite them for their own good. In fact, We only respite them so that they may increase in sinfulness. A humiliating torment awaits them."
  * **Gloss:** The istidraj doctrine: un-punished time is not favor but the slide's slope.
* **`[3:179]`** "Allah will not leave the believers as you are, without distinguishing the wicked from the sincere."
  * **Gloss:** The sorting already done on the clash-day (3:166) declared as standing policy.
* **`[3:179]`** "Nor will Allah inform you of the future, but Allah elects from among His messengers whom He wills. So believe in Allah and His messengers. If you believe and practice piety, you will have a splendid reward."
  * **Gloss:** Unseen is not for sale to anyone — except as revelation selects its messengers; belief + taqwa → splendid reward.
* **`[3:180]`** "Those who withhold what Allah has given them of his bounty should not assume that is good for them. In fact, it is bad for them. They will be encircled by their hoardings on the Day of Resurrection."
  * **Gloss:** Withholding re-specified: not theft but refusal of obligation — and the hoard becomes a collar.
* **`[3:180]`** "To Allah belongs the inheritance of the heavens and the earth, and Allah is well acquainted with what you do."
  * **Gloss:** The estate clause: God inherits everything anyway; the withholding delays only the transfer date.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`hasbuna` / "Allah is enough for us"**: 3:173.
  * **Rendering:** "He is the Excellent Protector" (ni'ma al-wakil).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[3:150]` "Allah is your Master, and He is the Best of Helpers" (same superlative pattern, two ruku back); `[8:62]` "Allah is sufficient for you" (verified); `[46:8]` "He is sufficient witness" (verified) — the root's sufficiency across contract, battle, and testimony.
* **`istidraj` / "We respite them"**: 3:178 — respite (umli) as escalating exposure.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[68:45]` "And I will give them respite. My plan is firm" (verified); `[7:14–15]` (verified) — Iblis's own granted respite: the term's double register (delay for the damned, patience for the repentant) is resolved here as delay-as-damning.
* **`yusaari'u` / "rush into disbelief"**: 3:176 — the haste verb (sara'a) — apostasy framed as speed, not depth; the root's sacred use is 2:148 "race towards goodness" (verified as rendering of mustaari'un — the same verb raced toward God is rushed away from Him).
* **`kanz` / "hoardings"**: 3:180.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[9:34–35]` "those who hoard gold and silver... heated in the Fire... branded" (verified) — the collar-image's twin scene; `[7:48]` "Your hoardings did not avail you" (verified) — the Elevations' verdict quoting this very category.
* **`birr + taqwa` — "the virtuous and the pious"**: 3:172 welds the two once more.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[39:34]` "Such is the reward for the virtuous" (verified); the two words recur welded at `[3:193]` "make us die in the company of the virtuous."

## 4. Cross-References (The Web)
* **Parallel:** `[9:34–35]` — hoarding brands and encircles: 3:180's collar and 9:35's branding are the same prophecy in two vocabularies (both verified).
* **Parallel:** `[8:12]` — "I will cast terror into the hearts of those who disbelieve" (verified): God casting fear outward vs. Satan casting fear at believers (3:151, 3:175) — the surah audits the supplier of every emotion.
* **Within surah:** `[3:157]` ↔ `[3:180]` — "better than what they hoard" answered by "encircled by their hoardings": the martyr's trade and the miser's trade; `[3:141]` ↔ `[3:179]` — the sorting purpose of the test now universal law; `[3:44]` ↔ `[3:179]` — "accounts from the Unseen which We reveal" is the exception proving 3:179's rule (Unseen withheld, except by election); `[3:164]` ↔ `[3:179]` — the elected messenger clause restated.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Medinan; the ruku's responders are classically the Uhud survivors who, hearing Abu Sufyan's turn-back rumor, marched to Hamra al-Asad (below the city) and found the Quraysh had gone home — no combat, hence "no harm having touched them."
* **Asbab / narrative context:** the Hamra al-Asad report is transmitted in Ibn Ishaq's maghazi and cited by Ibn Kathir on 3:172–174; the hasb sentence (3:173) is praised in transmitted supplication literature as the believers'Uhud-day saying (Source: classical reports — verify chain-level details; the corpus itself contains no asbab data).
