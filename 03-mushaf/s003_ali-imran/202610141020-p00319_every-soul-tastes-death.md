# Every soul tastes death: slander heard, pledges buried, tests endured (3:181–189)
**UID:** 202610141020
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 3:181–189 (Ruku 19 of 20 in Ali ‘Imran)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[3:181]` – `[3:189]` (9 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The thematic close begins by writing things down: Allah heard the slur "Allah is poor, and we are rich," and it will be recorded alongside their wrongful killing of prophets (3:181–182); the demand for a fire-consuming offering as proof-conditioned faith (3:183) is answered by the plain fact that the asked-for messengers came with proofs and were assassinated anyway (3:183b), and the pattern of denial repeats across every prophet (3:184). Then the universal axiom — every soul tastes death (3:185) — drops the price onto the present: the world is "enjoyment of delusion," and you will be tested in possessions and persons, and hear much abuse (3:186). Finally: God's pledge to the Scripture-people to proclaim and not conceal — disregarded behind backs for a small price (3:187), the self-praisers cannot evade punishment (3:188), and all sovereignty, again, is Allah's (3:189).
* **Placement:** Ruku 19 gathers the surah's three complaints — slander of God, conditional faith, broken proclamation — under one death-and-reckoning sentence before the people-of-understanding finale.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[3:181]`** "Allah has heard the statement of those who said, “Allah is poor, and we are rich.” We will write down what they said, and their wrongful killing of the prophets; and We will say, “Taste the torment of the burning.”"
  * **Gloss:** Divine hearing + divine scribal record: slur and blood-price written together on one page.
* **`[3:182]`** "“This is on account of what your hands have forwarded, and because Allah is not unjust towards the creatures.”"
  * **Gloss:** The double grounding of punishment: human pre-forwarding, divine non-injustice.
* **`[3:183]`** "Those who said, “Allah has made a covenant with us, that we shall not believe in any messenger unless he brings us an offering to be consumed by fire.”"
  * **Gloss:** A covenant invented as a filter: revelation must pass a sacrificial-fire test to be credible.
* **`[3:183]`** "Say, “Messengers have come to you before me with proofs, and with what you asked for; so why did you assassinate them, if you are truthful?”"
  * **Gloss:** The trap closes: the sign was given and the giver killed — the condition was never the issue.
* **`[3:184]`** "If they accuse you of lying, messengers before you were accused of lying. They came with the proofs, and the Psalms, and the Illuminating Scripture."
  * **Gloss:** Consolation by precedent — and the canon list: proofs, Zabur, a scripture that illuminates.
* **`[3:185]`** "Every soul will have a taste of death, and you will receive your recompense on the Day of Resurrection."
  * **Gloss:** The axiom verse — death's taste universalized, payment deferred to the Resurrection.
* **`[3:185]`** "Whoever is swayed from the Fire, and admitted to Paradise, has won. The life of this world is merely enjoyment of delusion."
  * **Gloss:** The win condition is subtraction (swerved from Fire); the world renamed: meta' al-gharur.
* **`[3:186]`** "You will be tested through your possessions and your persons; and you will hear from those who received the Scripture before you, and from the idol worshipers, much abuse."
  * **Gloss:** The twin test (wealth, self) plus the third wound — auditory: slander from both rival camps.
* **`[3:186]`** "But if you persevere and lead a righteous life—that indeed is a mark of great determination."
  * **Gloss:** sabr + taqwa once more (3:120, 3:179) — and the compound is called 'azm, resolve itself.
* **`[3:187]`** "Allah received a pledge from those who were given the Scripture: “You shall proclaim it to the people, and not conceal it.” But they disregarded it behind their backs, and exchanged it for a small price."
  * **Gloss:** The proclamation-pledge of 3:81's family applied: testimony carries a duty to publish, priced here at pocket change.
* **`[3:187]`** "What a miserable exchange they made."
  * **Gloss:** The exclamation (bi'sa) — the surah's market vocabulary turns on the traders.
* **`[3:188]`** "Do not think that those who rejoice in what they have done, and love to be praised for what they have not done—do not think they can evade the punishment. They will have a painful punishment."
  * **Gloss:** Two crimes in one portrait: rejoicing in deeds done, and craving praise for deeds not done — the flattery-economy indicted.
* **`[3:189]`** "To Allah belongs the sovereignty of the heavens and the earth. Allah has power over all things."
  * **Gloss:** The sovereignty formula returns (3:26's prayer, 3:128–129's battlefield answer) as this ruku's gavel.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`dhawq al-mawt` / "taste of death"**: 3:185.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[21:35]` "Every soul will taste death. We burden you with adversity and prosperity—a test" (verified — nearly verbatim with 3:186's twin test); `[29:57]` "Every soul will taste death. Then to Us you will be returned" (verified) — the corpus's death-axiom triple.
* **`qurban` / "offering to be consumed by fire"**: 3:183.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[5:27]` the accepted offering of Adam's sons (verified) — the fire-test the claimants demand is precisely what God decides, not proof-mechanics for humans.
* **`gharur` / "enjoyment of delusion"**: 3:185.
  * **Rendering:** "enjoyment of delusion" here; the near-twin `[57:20]` renders the same phrase-family "enjoyment of vanity" (verified) — two English faces of meta'-al-gharur/ghurur — trace `rg "enjoyment of"`.
* **`adha` / "much abuse"**: 3:186 — the hurt-root (ʾ-a-dh, rendered "abuse," "hurt," "to hurt" across the corpus) appears here for promised verbal harm, and at `[33:69]` "do not be like those who abused Moses" (verified) — verbal injury to prophets named as a recurring crime; cf. the root's ordinary-law uses at `[2:231]` (verified, divorce "to hurt them") and `[48:25]` (verified, unwitting harm to hidden believers).

* **Asbab / narrative context:** 3:181's "Allah is poor" slur is paired in reports with the same saying quoted at `[5:64]`-time of revelation (Jews' "Allah's hand is chained") — Source: transmitted asbab (Ibn Kathir on 3:181/5:64) — verify whether one event or two.
