# Neither wealth nor children avail: religion with Allah is surrender (3:10–20)
**UID:** 202610141003
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 3:10–20 (Ruku 2 of 20 in Ali ‘Imran)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[3:10]` – `[3:20]` (11 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The ruku strips the two securities this world offers — wealth and children — of any rescue value (3:10, 3:11), lists them as merely "adorned desires" (3:14), then trades them for the better resort (3:15–17) and a five-fold portrait of the righteous — before landing the surah's claim: religion with Allah is islam, and the People of the Scripture differed only out of envy (3:19), so the argument is delivered as a surrender-or-convey ultimatum (3:20).
* **Placement:** Second ruku; the creedal-and-eschatological foundation under the 3:7 hermeneutic — division is now given a motive (envy) and a cure (submission).

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[3:10]`** "As for those who disbelieve, neither their wealth nor their children will avail them anything against Allah. These will be fuel for the Fire."
  * **Gloss:** The two securities become kindling; the same clause returns verbatim at 3:116 — a frame for the community ruku.
* **`[3:11]`** "Like the behavior of Pharaoh’s people and those before them. They rejected Our signs, so Allah seized them for their sins. Allah is Strict in retribution."
  * **Gloss:** History as precedent — the stock Quranic pattern (reject → seize).
* **`[3:12]`** "Say to those who disbelieve, “You will be defeated, and rounded up into Hell—an awful resting-place.”"
  * **Gloss:** A say-command; "defeated" reads ahead to the two-parties sign of 3:13.
* **`[3:13]`** "There was a sign for you in the two parties that met. One party fighting in the way of Allah, and the other was disbelieving. They saw them with their own eyes twice their number. But Allah supports with His help whomever He wills. In that is a lesson for those with insight."
  * **Gloss:** Badr (classical identification): outnumbered 2:1, yet the minority won — divine support outranks arithmetic.
* **`[3:14]`** "Adorned for the people is the love of desires, such as women, and children, and piles upon piles of gold and silver, and branded horses, and livestock, and fields."
  * **Gloss:** The inventory of desire, in order of attachment — the world's balance sheet.
* **`[3:14]`** "These are the conveniences of the worldly life, but with Allah lies the finest resort."
  * **Gloss:** The pivot clause: all of it is commodity; the best destination is with God.
* **`[3:15]`** "Say, “Shall I inform you of something better than that? For those who are righteous, with their Lord are Gardens beneath which rivers flow, where they will remain forever, and purified spouses, and acceptance from Allah.” Allah is Observant of the servants."
  * **Gloss:** The counter-inventory — the same senses answered with permanence plus ridwan (acceptance).
* **`[3:16]`** "Those who say, “Our Lord, we have believed, so forgive us our sins, and save us from the suffering of the Fire.”"
  * **Gloss:** The righteous are first identified by a prayer, not a pedigree.
* **`[3:17]`** "The patient, and the truthful, and the reverent, and the charitable, and the seekers of forgiveness at dawn."
  * **Gloss:** Five traits — sabr opens, dawn-forgiveness closes; a definition of the muttaqin in motion.
* **`[3:18]`** "Allah bears witness that there is no god but He, as do the angels, and those endowed with knowledge—upholding justice. There is no god but He, the Mighty, the Wise."
  * **Gloss:** The shahada with its witnesses: God, angels, people of knowledge — the epistemic chain of 3:7 restored.
* **`[3:19]`** "Religion with Allah is Islam. Those to whom the Scripture was given differed only after knowledge came to them, out of envy among themselves. Whoever rejects the signs of Allah—Allah is quick to take account."
  * **Gloss:** The surah's thesis sentence: one religion, fractured by resentment after certainty.
* **`[3:20]`** "If they argue with you, say, “I have surrendered myself to Allah, and those who follow me.” And say to those who were given the Scripture, and to the unlearned, “Have you surrendered?” If they have surrendered, then they are guided; but if they turn away, then your duty is to convey. Allah is Seeing of the servants."
  * **Gloss:** The ultimatum reduced to one question; the messenger's limit is conveyance.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`islam`** here "surrender/submit" as religion-name:
  * **Rendering:** "Islam" at 3:19; the verb family rendered "surrendered" 3:20, "submitted" from 3:53 onward — Itani uses both words for the same root across the surah.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:136]` "to Him we surrender" — the identical creed formula; `[4:125]` submits-wholly + follows Abraham's faith.
* **`ridwan`** "acceptance from Allah" (3:15):
  * **Rendering:** "acceptance"; the word-cluster recurs at `[9:21]` and `[57:20]` paired with forgiveness — this-world's opposite.
* **`zinah` / adorned desires** (3:14):
  * **Rendering:** "Adorned for the people is the love of desires" — passive, God-not-mentioned: the adorning is left anonymous.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:212]` "Beautified is the life of this world for those who disbelieve" and `[8:48]` Satan "made their deeds appear good" — the same deceptive ornament motif.
* **`sabr`** "the patient" (3:17, first of five traits):
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:155]` the steadfast given good news; `[103:3]` mutual encouragement to patience as saving; `[3:200]` this surah's own closing command.

## 4. Cross-References (The Web)

* **Parallel:** `[42:14]` — "They became divided only after knowledge came to them, out of resentment" — same diagnosis as 3:19, wording nearly identical (observable corpus fact).

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Medinan; the Badr sign of 3:13 anchors the unit after 2 AH, and the "defeated" of 3:12 consoles a community soon to face Uhud (3:121+).
* **Asbab / narrative context:** none transmitted for the unit as a whole. (Source: agent inference for the Badr identification of 3:13 — universally made by classical tafsir per Ibn Kathir — verify; rendering-drift observations are translator choices, observable in corpus.)
