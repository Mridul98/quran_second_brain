# Uhud morning: three thousand angels and an outcome not your concern (3:121–129)
**UID:** 202610141014
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 3:121–129 (Ruku 13 of 20 in Ali ‘Imran)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[3:121]` – `[3:129]` (9 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The recollection opens on the battlefield morning — "remember when you left your home in the morning, to assign battle-positions" (3:121) — with two groups almost faltering before the fighting starts (3:122); the memory of Badr, won "when you were weak" (3:123), backs a promise of angelic reinforcement (3:124–125), which the narrator immediately deflates: it was only hope, victory comes from Allah alone (3:126); and when it comes to the enemy's fate, the Prophet is told the hardest line of the surah — "It is no concern of yours whether He redeems them or punishes them" (3:128), because sovereignty over forgiveness and punishment is Allah's property (3:129).
* **Placement:** The opening scene of the Uhud theodicy (121–179), the surah's longest movement — defeat explained from inside the day, not after the mourners arrive.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[3:121]`** "Remember when you left your home in the morning, to assign battle-positions for the believers. Allah is Hearing and Knowing."
  * **Gloss:** The memory-command starts at deployment: tactical positions, human decisions, God hearing and knowing underneath.
* **`[3:122]`** "When two groups among you almost faltered, but Allah was their Protector. So in Allah let the believers put their trust."
  * **Gloss:** Two tribes' contingents nearly broke (classically Aws and Khazraj, per reports); the trust-refrain answers faltering.
* **`[3:123]`** "Allah had given you victory at Badr, when you were weak. So fear Allah, that you may be thankful."
  * **Gloss:** The precedent verse — the only Badr naming in the corpus (verified via `rg "Badr"`) — weakness as the condition of the win.
* **`[3:124]`** "When you said to the believers, “Is it not enough for you that your Lord has reinforced you with three thousand angels, sent down?”"
  * **Gloss:** The Prophet's encouragement quoted mid-morning; the angel count is specific and spectacular.
* **`[3:125]`** "It is; but if you persevere and remain cautious, and they attack you suddenly, your Lord will reinforce you with five thousand angels, well trained."
  * **Gloss:** Reinforcement indexed to sabr + taqwa — the surah's armor formula (3:120) in the combat register.
* **`[3:126]`** "Allah made it but a message of hope for you, and to reassure your hearts thereby. Victory comes only from Allah the Almighty, the Wise."
  * **Gloss:** The deflation: angels were morale, not mechanics; the same reading closes 8:10's Badr account (verified).
* **`[3:127]`** "He thus cuts off a section of those who disbelieved, or subdues them, so they retreat disappointed."
  * **Gloss:** War's real options — annihilation or humiliation — both end in disappointment, not in your hands.
* **`[3:128]`** "It is no concern of yours whether He redeems them or punishes them. They are wrongdoers."
  * **Gloss:** The covenant of intercession dissolved for the Prophet personally: redemption (tawba-root again) is God's transaction.
* **`[3:129]`** "To Allah belongs everything in the heavens and the earth. He forgives whom He wills, and He punishes whom He wills. Allah is Most Forgiving, Most Merciful."
  * **Gloss:** The sovereignty clause (cf. 3:26) re-issued with forgiveness first even in the power verse.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`sabra/tuq` pair**: 3:125 "persevere and remain cautious" = the sabr+taqwa formula.
  * **Elsewhere:** 3:120 (just before the battlefield), 3:186, 3:200 — the formula's four appearances are all in this surah's endurance arc plus 2:183's fasting-cluster (trace `rg "persevere"`); verified twin: `[2:155]` steadfast given good news.
* **`mala'ika` / "angels, sent down"**: 3:124–125.
  * **Rendering:** "well trained" (murdaab) for the five-thousand rank — the corpus has angelic battle-reinforcement in exactly two places: 3:124–125 and `[8:9]` (verified).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[8:9]` "one thousand angels in succession" (verified) at Badr proper — the corpus's angelic reinforcement is a Badr motif recalled at Uhud.
* **`falter`**: Itani's English uses "falter" for two different moments of the same day — 3:122 (two groups almost faltering before the line was held) and 3:152 (the actual faltering at the spoils); the homonym is a translation artifact worth remembering when tracing the narrative (observable in the corpus renderings).
* **`istithna' clause` — "It is no concern of yours"**: 3:128; classically read against the Prophet's own intercession hopes for his people (Source: Ibn Kathir on 3:128 collects reports including the companion's "who will redeem the people of the household?" — verify the wording).

## 4. Cross-References (The Web)

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Medinan; this is the surah's most historically-anchored ruku — Uhud, Shawwal 3 AH, the Quraysh's revenge battle after Badr.
* **Asbab / narrative context:** the "two groups" of 3:122 are classically identified as Banu Haritha's contingents from Aws and Khazraj, turned back by a rumor, protected by God (Source: reports in Ibn Ishaq's maghazi as transmitted in Ibn Kathir on 3:122 — verify); the angel-figures of 3:124–125 carry the famous Badr/Uhud numeric disagreement among tafsir (313 vs 1000 vs 3000) (Source: dispute recorded by Ibn Kathir/al-Qurtubi on 3:124 — agent summary, verify).
