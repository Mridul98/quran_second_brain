# Sorrow upon sorrow, then security: the anatomy of the rout (3:149–155)
**UID:** 202610141017
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 3:149–155 (Ruku 16 of 20 in Ali ‘Imran)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[3:149]` – `[3:155]` (7 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The autopsy of the collapse proceeds in divine first-person: obey the disbelievers and they turn you on your heels — but Allah is your Master and the Best of Helpers (3:149–150); terror will be thrown into the enemies' hearts for their un-sanctioned partners (3:151); then the day itself is dissected — Allah fulfilled His promise, you were winning by His leave, until you faltered, disputed the command, disobeyed, some wanting this world; He turned you away to test you, and pardoned (3:152); the flight, the Messenger calling from the rear, sorrow upon sorrow as a mercy-that-numbs (3:153); then security descending — slumber on some, and on others the wrong thoughts about Allah of ignorance, "is anything up to us?" (3:154); and the turnbacks of the clash-day are charged to Satan, for their own earning — and Allah has forgiven them (3:155).
* **Placement:** The center of the Uhud narrative; where the theodicy becomes pastoral — the community is described, faulted, and pardoned in the same breath.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[3:149]`** "O you who believe! If you obey those who disbelieve, they will turn you back on your heels, and you end up losers."
  * **Gloss:** The heel-turning idiom (3:144) restated as obedience-warned politics.
* **`[3:150]`** "Allah is your Master, and He is the Best of Helpers."
  * **Gloss:** The counter-loyalty clause — mawla + the corpus's superlative-helper formula.
* **`[3:151]`** "We will throw terror into the hearts of those who disbelieve, because they attribute to Allah partners for which He revealed no sanction. Their lodging is the Fire. Miserable is the lodging of the evildoers."
  * **Gloss:** The enemy's confidence breaks at the root: partners without divine authorization (tawhid's political edge).
* **`[3:152]`** "Allah has fulfilled His promise to you, and you defeated them by His leave; until when you faltered, and disputed the command, and disobeyed after He had shown you what you like."
  * **Gloss:** The precise turning point — victory was real, the falter was at the order (the archers' post, classically).
* **`[3:152]`** "Some of you want this world, and some of you want the next. Then He turned you away from them, to test you; but He pardoned you. Allah is Gracious towards the believers."
  * **Gloss:** The world-split inside the camp — and the double ending: test AND pardon, grace as the last word.
* **`[3:153]`** "Remember when you fled, not caring for anyone, even though the Messenger was calling you from your rear."
  * **Gloss:** The rout's portrait: each man forgot the calling voice behind him.
* **`[3:153]`** "Then He repaid you with sorrow upon sorrow, so that you would not grieve over what you missed, or for what afflicted you. Allah is Informed of what you do."
  * **Gloss:** The paradox medicine: stacked griefs cancel grief — grief inoculated with grief.
* **`[3:154]`** "Then after the setback, He sent down security upon you. Slumber overcame some of you, while others cared only for themselves, thinking of Allah thoughts that were untrue—thoughts of ignorance—saying, “Is anything up to us?”"
  * **Gloss:** Security split the camp: tranquil sleep on the faithful, self-brooding and fatalism on the others.
* **`[3:154]`** "Say, “Everything is up to Allah.” They conceal within themselves what they do not reveal to you. And they say, “If it was up to us, none of us would have been killed here.” Say, “Even if you Had stayed in your homes, those destined to be killed would have marched into their death beds.”"
  * **Gloss:** The counter-fatalism: not "everything is up to us" but death's appointments are real regardless of location.
* **`[3:154]`** "Allah thus tests what is in your minds, and purifies what is in your hearts. Allah knows what the hearts contain."
  * **Gloss:** The day's two operations: examination of intention, purification of heart.
* **`[3:155]`** "Those of you who turned back on the day when the two armies clashed—it was Satan who caused them to backslide, on account of some of what they have earned. But Allah has forgiven them. Allah is Forgiving and Prudent."
  * **Gloss:** The turnbacks get an etiology (Satan + their own earning) and a verdict: forgiven.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`hasb` / "Best of Helpers", "Allah is enough"**: 3:150.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[3:173]` "Allah is enough for us; He is the Excellent Protector" (same family, two ruku on — the verse-cluster's refrain); `[8:62]` "Allah is sufficient for you" (verified) — the sufficiency idiom is the surah's anti-heel formula.
* **`ru'b/terror`**: "We will throw terror into the hearts" — 3:151.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[8:12]` "I will cast terror into the hearts of those who disbelieve" (verified — Badr's angelic speech); `[59:2]`, `[33:26]` (verified) — the same weapon at Banu Nadir; the motif's consistent owner: God throws it, not you fear it.
* **`na's` / "slumber"**: 3:154 — security's physical form, unique in the corpus as a battle-phenomenon; note the collision with `[2:255]` "Neither slumber overtakes Him, nor sleep" (verified) — the only other corpus occurrence of the word, describing exactly what the believers' God does not suffer.
* **`yawm al-laqā'` / "the day the two armies clashed"**: 3:155.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[3:166]` — the same phrase re-opens the next scene (within-surah); `[8:41]` "the Day of Distinction, the day when the two armies met" (verified — Badr's epithet; the idiom's home).
* **`zann al-zannah` / "thoughts of ignorance"**: 3:154 — rendered "thinking of Allah thoughts that were untrue—thoughts of ignorance"; the cognate appears of the hypocrites of Hudaybiyyah `[48:12]` (trace `rg "evil thoughts"` — verified earlier for 48:6's "harbor evil thoughts").

## 4. Cross-References (The Web)
* **Parallel:** `[8:48]` — Satan's Badr moment, "turned on his heels... I see what you do not see" (verified) — the rear-view of 3:155's Satanic backsliding: the same figure quitting at the clash.
* **Within surah:** `[3:140–142]` ↔ `[3:152]` — the test of hearts announced pre-battle is executed on the hill; `[3:153]` ↔ `[3:166–167]` — the flight and the hypocrites' absence are the same event from two angles; `[3:8–9]` ↔ `[3:154]` — the prayer "do not cause our hearts to swerve" answered clinically: the swerving happened, security followed.
* **Parallel:** `[9:83]` — "You were content to sit back the first time, so sit back with those who stay behind" (verified) — the backsliders' later case-law, born from this day.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Medinan, Uhud (Shawwal 3 AH); 3:152's "disputed the command" classically refers to the archers leaving their post over the spoils (Source: Ibn Ishaq's maghazi narrative as cited in Ibn Kathir on 3:152 — verify detail; the corpus itself says "disobeyed after He had shown you what you like").
* **Asbab / narrative context:** 3:154's slumber is transmitted as a sign of true security vs. the anxious sleeplessness of the doubters (Source: classical reports — verify).
