# The annunciation of the Messiah: Mary chosen, the disciples allied (3:42–54)
**UID:** 202610141006
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 3:42–54 (Ruku 5 of 20 in Ali ‘Imran)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[3:42]` – `[3:54]` (13 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The angels address Mary directly (3:42–43), the Quran's only annunciation of a fatherless son is delivered (3:45–47), and the Messiah's curriculum is laid out — Scripture, wisdom, Torah, Gospel (3:48) — so that his whole ministry is bracketed by one phrase, "by Allah's leave" (3:49–50); when disbelief appears, the answer is not debate but alliance: the disciples' "we are Allah's allies" (3:52–53), and the plot-counterplot of 3:54 closes the unit before the taken-up scene.
* **Placement:** Midpoint of the Imran narrative (3:33–63); the Christological core of a Medinan surah in the middle of its scriptural-dispute argument.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[3:42]`** "The angels said, “O Mary, Allah has chosen you, and has purified you. He has chosen you over all the women of the world."
  * **Gloss:** Chosen twice in one line, with the second election as universal — the angels' vocative "O Mary" is unique revelation-speech to a woman in the book (trace `rg "O Mary"`: the only other vocatives are 3:37 from Zechariah and 19:27 from her people).
* **`[3:43]`** "“O Mary, be devoted to your Lord, and bow down, and kneel with those who kneel.”"
  * **Gloss:** Election issues in prayer posture, not privilege: kneel *with* the congregation.
* **`[3:44]`** "These are accounts from the Unseen, which We reveal to you. You were not with them when they cast their lots as to which of them would take charge of Mary; nor were you with them as they quarreled."
  * **Gloss:** The narrator's authenticity claim: this unseen detail (the lot for guardianship, the quarrel) proves revelation, not report.
* **`[3:45]`** "The Angels said, “O Mary, Allah gives you good news of a Word from Him. His name is the Messiah, Jesus, son of Mary, well-esteemed in this world and the next, and one of the nearest."
  * **Gloss:** The annunciation proper — a "Word from Him" named, titled Messiah, ranked among the brought-near.
* **`[3:46]`** "He will speak to the people from the crib, and in adulthood, and will be one of the righteous.”"
  * **Gloss:** Two speech moments bookend his life: the miracle is a sentence, not a sermon.
* **`[3:47]`** "She said, “My Lord, how can I have a child, when no man has touched me?” He said, “It will be so. Allah creates whatever He wills. To have anything done, He only says to it, ‘Be,’ and it is.”"
  * **Gloss:** Mary's question answered with the creatio-adjacent formula kun fayakun — the same answer as Zechariah's, but with "creates."
* **`[3:48]`** "And He will teach him the Scripture and wisdom, and the Torah and the Gospel."
  * **Gloss:** Four possessions taught by God directly — the Gospel (Injil) is *his* book, named forward from 3:3.
* **`[3:49]`** "A messenger to the Children of Israel: “I have come to you with a sign from your Lord. I make for you out of clay the figure of a bird; then I breathe into it, and it becomes a bird by Allah’s leave. And I heal the blind and the leprous, and I revive the dead, by Allah’s leave."
  * **Gloss:** The miracle inventory — each power immediately qualified; he molds, God makes it live.
* **`[3:49]`** "And I inform you concerning what you eat, and what you store in your homes. In that is a sign for you, if you are believers.”"
  * **Gloss:** Domestic foreknowledge added to the list — and the condition clause "if you are believers."
* **`[3:50]`** "“And verifying what lies before me of the Torah, and to make lawful for you some of what was forbidden to you. I have come to you with a sign from your Lord; so fear Allah, and obey me.”"
  * **Gloss:** His program: confirm the Torah, relax some of its restrictions — then the same imperative chain the whole surah uses: fear, obey.
* **`[3:51]`** "“Allah is my Lord and your Lord, so worship Him. That is a straight path.”"
  * **Gloss:** The Messiah's own sermon-text: one Lord, one straight path — tawhid from the surah's Christian center.
* **`[3:52]`** "When Jesus sensed disbelief on their part, he said, “Who are my allies towards Allah?” The disciples said, “We are Allah’s allies; we have believed in Allah, and bear witness that we submit.”"
  * **Gloss:** The hawariyyun answer the alliance call — the community-of-response template the surah will apply to itself at 3:104+.
* **`[3:53]`** "“Our Lord, we have believed in what You have revealed, and we have followed the Messenger, so count us among the witnesses.”"
  * **Gloss:** The disciples' prayer: believe-reveal-follow-witness — the surah's entire vocabulary in four verbs.
* **`[3:54]`** "They planned, and Allah planned; but Allah is the Best of planners."
  * **Gloss:** The counter-plot formula — the same construction as 12:102's "they plotted" motif, here answering the kill plot.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`al-Masih` / "the Messiah"**: 3:45 — named title only in this surah and the Christ-polemic surahs.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[4:157]`, `[4:171]`, `[5:72]`, `[5:75]`, `[9:30–31]` — in all corpus sites the title stays attached to "Jesus son of Mary"; never deified.
* **`kalima` / "a Word from Him"**: 3:45 (see 3:39).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[4:171]` — "His Word that He conveyed to Mary, and a Spirit from Him" — the fullest corpus statement of the title.
* **`hawariyyun` / "disciples"**: 3:52.
  * **Elsewhere:** `[5:111–112]` — same word, same "we have submitted" answer; `[61:14]` — the identical "Who are my supporters towards Allah?" question, the twin of 3:52 (observable).
* **`bi-idhnillah` / "by Allah's leave"**: twice in 3:49, again in 3:50's chain.
  * **Elsewhere:** the miracle-limitation formula corpus-wide (e.g. `[5:110]` repeats each of 3:49's signs with "by My leave") — prophecy's power is borrowed.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Medinan; the Jesus narrative cluster (33–63) sits inside the surah's People-of-the-Book dispute, giving Christians their own scripture's corrected account (classical framing).
