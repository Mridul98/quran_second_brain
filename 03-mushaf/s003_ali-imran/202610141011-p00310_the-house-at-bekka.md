# The first House at Bekka: charity, Abraham's creed, and the messenger among you (3:92–101)
**UID:** 202610141011
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 3:92–101 (Ruku 10 of 20 in Ali ‘Imran)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[3:92]` – `[3:101]` (10 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The charter ruku opens with the spending test — you never reach righteousness until you give of what you cherish (3:92) — wins a Jewish dietary argument back to Torah (3:93–95), and then plants the House: Bekka, blessed, guidance for all people; the Station of Abraham; entry = security; pilgrimage an obligation on whoever can make the way (3:96–97). It closes by addressing both audiences in turn: People of Scripture, why conceal and obstruct when Allah witnesses what you do (3:98–99), and believers, do not let a party of them turn you back — how can you disbelieve when Allah's revelations are recited to you and His Messenger is among you? (3:100–101).
* **Placement:** Opens the community-constitution movement (92–120): economics → sanctuary → identity; its final line (3:101) is the rope-ruku's ramp.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[3:92]`** "You will not attain virtuous conduct until you give of what you cherish. Whatever you give away, Allah is aware of it."
  * **Gloss:** The birr threshold — wealth's emotional center of gravity is the test; the infaq note's own evidence line, quoted verbatim there.
* **`[3:93]`** "All food was permissible to the Children of Israel, except what Israel forbade for itself before the Torah was revealed. Say, “Bring the Torah, and read it, if you are truthful.”"
  * **Gloss:** A dietary polemic resolved by text archaeology: Jacob ("Israel") self-restricted, pre-Torah — so the ban is not divine.
* **`[3:94]`** "Whoever forges lies about Allah after that—these are the unjust."
  * **Gloss:** The knowing-lie charge continues from 3:75/78 — now with the Torah-call as its proof.
* **`[3:95]`** "Say, “Allah has spoken the truth, so follow the religion of Abraham the Monotheist; he was not a Pagan.”"
  * **Gloss:** Truth's address has an itinerary: follow Abraham's hanifiyya — 3:67 restated as command.
* **`[3:96]`** "The first house established for mankind is the one at Bekka; blessed, and guidance for all people."
  * **Gloss:** The sanctuary claim: oldest House, for all humanity — universalism located at a coordinate.
* **`[3:97]`** "In it are evident signs; the Station of Abraham. Whoever enters it attains security. Pilgrimage to the House is a duty to Allah for all who can make the journey."
  * **Gloss:** Three rites compressed: the Station, the security inviolable, the hajj obligation on the able.
* **`[3:97]`** "But as for those who refuse—Allah is Independent of the worlds."
  * **Gloss:** The obligation enforced not by need but by sheer self-sufficiency.
* **`[3:98]`** "Say, “O People of the Scripture, why do you reject the Revelations of Allah, when Allah witnesses what you do?”"
  * **Gloss:** Witness flipped: the observed object becomes the observer.
* **`[3:99]`** "Say, “O People of the Scripture, why do you hinder from Allah’s path those who believe, seeking to distort it, even though you are witnesses? Allah is not unaware of what you do.”"
  * **Gloss:** Obstruction-with-knowledge — they hold the testimony and block the road.
* **`[3:100]`** "O you who believe! If you obey a party of those who were given the Scripture, they will turn you, after your belief, into disbelievers."
  * **Gloss:** The address turns inward: the defection threat is conversational obedience.
* **`[3:101]`** "And how could you disbelieve, when Allah’s revelations are being recited to you, and among you is His Messenger? Whoever cleaves to Allah has been guided to a straight path."
  * **Gloss:** The impossibility formula: recitation + resident Messenger = no plausible unbelief; i'tisam (cleaving) pre-announces the rope.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`birr` / "virtuous conduct"**: 3:92.
  * **Rendering:** "virtuous conduct" here vs "Righteousness" as the opening noun of `[2:177]` (verified) — same Arabic word, two English faces; searching birr in this edition requires both strings.
  * **Elsewhere:** the cherish-motif's twin is 2:177's "Who gives money, though dear" (verified) — the two verses define the same threshold from the act and the attachment sides.
* **`Bakka` / "Bekka"**: 3:96 — the place-name occurs only here in the corpus (observable); the House itself is elsewhere "the Sacred Mosque" (`[2:158]`, `[8:34]`, `[9:28]` verified) and "Kaabah" once at `[5:95]` (verified).
* **`maqam Ibrahim` / "the Station of Abraham"**: 3:97.
  * **Rendering drift:** "Station" (3:97) vs "shrine" at `[2:125]` "Use the shrine of Abraham as a place of prayer" (verified) — one phrase, two renderings; both cite via `rg "Abraham as"`.
* **`istita'a` / "can make the journey"**: 3:97 — the istita'a clause, the hajj's ability-condition; cf. `[2:158]`'s verified "Whoever makes the Pilgrimage to the House, or performs the Umrah" — the obligation's prior vocabulary is already in Baqarah's rites.
