# Hold fast to the rope of Allah, altogether (3:102–109)
**UID:** 202610141012
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 3:102–109 (Ruku 11 of 20 in Ali ‘Imran)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[3:102]` – `[3:109]` (8 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** The community's cohesion charter: revere Allah with the reverence that is His due and die only as surrendered ones (3:102); grab the rope of Allah together and refuse division, remembering the enemy-to-brother conversion and the fire-pit's edge they were pulled from (3:103); let one mid-sized community exist whose job is calling to good and forbidding evil — those are the winners (3:104); do not become like those who split and disputed after the clear proofs (3:105), for the Day sorts faces into white and black (3:106–107); and these recited revelations are truth with no divine appetite for injustice, all events returning to Allah (3:108–109).
* **Placement:** The hinge of the surah's community section: guidance-conditional (3:101) → cohesion-command → the division warning that 3:121+ (Uhud's factions) and 3:167 (the exempted) will dramatize.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[3:102]`** "O you who believe! Revere Allah with due reverence, and do not die except as Muslims."
  * **Gloss:** The hardest request: taqwa at full weight, and persistence in islam unto death — the rope held to the end.
* **`[3:103]`** "And hold fast to the rope of Allah, altogether, and do not become divided. And remember Allah’s blessings upon you; how you were enemies, and He reconciled your hearts, and by His grace you became brethren."
  * **Gloss:** The cable command: union is commanded because reunion was first given — the Aws/Khazraj reconciliation read into "you were enemies."
* **`[3:103]`** "And you were on the brink of a pit of fire, and He saved you from it. Allah thus clarifies His revelations for you, so that you may be guided."
  * **Gloss:** The fire-rim image completes the blessing-list; clarification of revelations = the rope in hand.
* **`[3:104]`** "And let there be among you a community calling to virtue, and advocating righteousness, and deterring from evil. These are the successful."
  * **Gloss:** From "altogether" to "among you": a dedicated subgroup for moral advocacy — hisbah as communal office.
* **`[3:105]`** "And do not be like those who separated and disputed after the clear proofs came to them; for them is a great punishment."
  * **Gloss:** The mirror-warning: the People of the Book as the negative exemplar, their sequence (proof, then split) exactly inverted.
* **`[3:106]`** "On the Day when some faces will be whitened, and some faces will be blackened. As for those whose faces are blackened: “Did you disbelieve after your belief?” Then taste the punishment for having disbelieved."
  * **Gloss:** The eschatological face-color test — the charge for blackened faces is post-belief exit, i.e. the division of 3:105 made eternal.
* **`[3:107]`** "But as for those whose faces are whitened: they are in Allah’s mercy, remaining in it forever."
  * **Gloss:** The counterpart: not in a garden here but *in mercy itself* — the surah's warmest clause.
* **`[3:108]`** "These are the revelations of Allah. We recite them to you in truth. Allah desires no injustice for mankind."
  * **Gloss:** Recitation frame again (cf. 3:58): the text's truthfulness underwrites its fairness.
* **`[3:109]`** "To Allah belongs everything in the heavens and everything on earth, and to Allah all events are referred."
  * **Gloss:** Total ownership plus the referral of all affairs — the rope has an other end, held there.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`habl` / "rope"**: 3:103 (and 3:112's "rope from Allah and a rope from the people").
  * **Rendering:** "rope of Allah" (classically glossed as the Quran, the covenant, or the jama'a — Source: tripartite reading in Ibn Kathir/Tabari on 3:103; agent summary — verify).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[3:112]` — the same image for protected minority status; `[38:10]` "let them ascend the ropes" (verified) — the idiom's sarcastic opposite.
* **`haqqa tuqatihi` / "due reverence"**: 3:102 — a superlative construction ("His true due"; trace with `rg "due reverence"` — the phrase stands alone in this edition's renderings).
* **`amr bil-ma'ruf / nahy an al-munkar`**: rendered "calling to virtue, advocating righteousness, deterring from evil" (3:104); the pair in noun form at 3:110 "advocate what is moral, forbid what is immoral."
  * **Elsewhere:** `[9:71]` believers "advocate virtue, forbid evil" vs `[9:67]` hypocrites "advocate evil, prohibit righteousness" (verified) — the office defines the community's health; `[31:17]` Luqman's "advocate righteousness, forbid evil, and be patient" (verified) welds the duty to sabr, as this surah's end does (3:200).
* **`yakhtalif` / "divided" vs "separated"**: 3:105 renders two verbs (taffariqu, takhtalafa) as "separated and disputed" — the pathology of 3:7's hearts and 3:19's envy now in communal dress.
