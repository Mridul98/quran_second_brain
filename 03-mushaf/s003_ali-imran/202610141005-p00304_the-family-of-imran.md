# The chosen families: a vow, a girl, and an old man's sign (3:31–41)
**UID:** 202610141005
**Type:** Passage / Ruku Note
**Passage:** 3:31–41 (Ruku 4 of 20 in Ali ‘Imran)
**Last Studied:** 2026-10-14

## 1. Unit & Thesis
* **Range:** `[3:31]` – `[3:41]` (11 ayahs)
* **Thesis:** After the love-and-obey clause (3:31), the ruku names the surah's cast: Allah chose the families of Adam, Noah, Abraham and Imran (3:33–34), and then the camera closes on one household — a woman dedicating her unborn child, receiving a daughter instead (3:35–36), the girl Mary raised under Zechariah's care and fed from nowhere (3:37), and the old man who asks for offspring and gets John as good news plus three mute days as his sign (3:38–41).
* **Placement:** Fourth ruku; the turn from the surah's argument to its narrative heart — the "family of Imran" begins here and runs to 3:63.

## 2. Verse Arc (corpus-anchored)
* **`[3:31]`** "Say, “If you love Allah, then follow me, and Allah will love you, and will forgive you your sins.” Allah is Forgiving and Merciful."
  * **Gloss:** The famous love-test: love of God is cashed out as following the Messenger, and returns as God's love.
* **`[3:32]`** "Say, “Obey Allah and the Messenger.” But if they turn away—Allah does not love the faithless."
  * **Gloss:** Obedience stated flatly; "the faithless" (kuffar) for those who turn — strong register.
* **`[3:33]`** "Allah chose Adam, and Noah, and the family of Abraham, and the family of Imran, over all mankind."
  * **Gloss:** The election list — four houses, one chain.
* **`[3:34]`** "Offspring one of the other. Allah is Hearer and Knower."
  * **Gloss:** One progeny interbred by choice; "Hearer" answers the prayers about to be heard.
* **`[3:35]`** "The wife of Imran said, “My Lord, I have vowed to You what is in my womb, dedicated, so accept from me; You are the Hearer and Knower.”"
  * **Gloss:** A vow of the unborn child to God's service — motherhood turned into offering.
* **`[3:36]`** "And when she delivered her, she said, “My Lord, I have delivered a female,” and Allah was well aware of what she has delivered, “and the male is not like the female, and I have named her Mary, and have commended her and her descendants to Your protection, from Satan the outcast.”"
  * **Gloss:** The disappointment and the redirection: a girl accepted as the one; protective prayer over her line.
* **`[3:37]`** "Her Lord accepted her with a gracious reception, and brought her a beautiful upbringing, and entrusted her to the care of Zechariah. Whenever Zechariah entered upon her in the sanctuary, he found her with provision."
  * **Gloss:** Divine *kifala* (guardianship) executed through Zechariah; the unasked-for meal in the holy space.
* **`[3:37]`** "He said, “O Mary, where did you get this from?” She said, “It is from Allah; Allah provides to whom He wills without reckoning.”"
  * **Gloss:** Mary's one line is a whole creed: provision beyond measure or accounting.
* **`[3:38]`** "Thereupon Zechariah prayed to his Lord; he said, “My Lord, bestow on me good offspring from Your presence; You are the Hearer of Prayers.”"
  * **Gloss:** The witnessed miracle becomes the occasion of his own petition.
* **`[3:39]`** "Then the angels called out to him, as he stood praying in the sanctuary: “Allah gives you good news of John; confirming a Word from Allah, and honorable, and moral, and a prophet; one of the upright.”"
  * **Gloss:** The answer mid-prayer; John described as confirmer of "a Word from Allah" — the Christ-title before the Christ-story.
* **`[3:40]`** "He said, “My Lord, how will I have a son, when old age has overtaken me, and my wife is barren?” He said, “Even so, Allah does whatever He wills.”"
  * **Gloss:** Biology protests; the reply is the divine-will formula, verbatim from 3:6's womb-formation.
* **`[3:41]`** "He said, “My Lord, give me a sign.” He said, “Your sign is that you shall not speak to the people for three days, except by gestures. And remember your Lord much, and praise in the evening and the morning.”"
  * **Gloss:** The mute sign — speech withheld so remembrance can be doubled.

## 3. Key Terms & Roots
* **`kifala` / "care"**: rendered "entrusted her to the care of Zechariah" (3:37).
  * **Elsewhere:** the same guardianship verb returns at 3:44 ("which of them would take charge of Mary") — the word's whole corpus life is inside this family narrative (observable via `rg "take charge"`).
* **`rizq bil-hisab / ghayr hisab` / "without reckoning"**: Mary's creed line (3:37).
  * **Rendering:** "without reckoning" here; "without measure" for the identical provision formula at 2:212 and 3:27 — translator synonym drift (observable).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[2:212]`, `[3:27]`; the same formula applied to the steadfast's wages at `[39:10]` — provision and reward both beyond accounting.
* **`kalem al-ibad` / "Hearer of Prayers"**: Zechariah's address (3:38) vs "Hearer and Knower" (3:34–35).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[14:39]` "My Lord is the Hearer of Prayers" — identical phrase in Abraham's thanksgiving for Ishmael; the two chosen families rhyme.
* **`kalima` / "a Word from Allah"**: John "confirming a Word from Allah" (3:39); Jesus himself is "a Word from Him" (3:45).
  * **Elsewhere:** `[4:171]` "His Word that He conveyed to Mary" — the Christological title across surahs.

## 6. Context Notes
* **Revelation context:** Medinan surah narrating pre-Mosaic-to-Mosaic-period material; the Imran/Mary genealogy frame (3:33–36) is treated by classical tafsir as answering "of what family?" questions from the Najran delegation (Source: Ibn Kathir's introduction to 3:33–45 — transmitted as commentary, agent summary, verify).
* **Asbab / narrative context:** none specific transmitted for 3:31–32; they function as the surah's obedience hinge. In 3:36, who speaks the clause "and the male is not like the female" — Allah's reply or the mother — is a genuine classical disagreement, unresolved by the translation's punctuation (Source: dispute recorded by al-Tabari and Ibn Kathir on 3:36 — verify).
