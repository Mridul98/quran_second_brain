# Isa: the Jesus the Quran corrects
**UID:** 202610141301
**Type:** Permanent / Domain Knowledge
**Source:** observable corpus facts in the cited `[s:a]` lines (The Clear Quran, Itani: *bi-idhnillah* rendered "by Allah's leave"/"by My leave"); the three-axis synthesis — nature, death/cross, final interrogation — is agent inference — verify; every narrative detail beyond the wording of 4:157–158 (who was substituted, how the raising occurred) is classical tafsir addition, labeled `Source: classical` where noted; never teach a scenario as Quran.

## Concept
The Quran's Jesus dossier (Isa ibn Maryam, ~65 mentions; corpus anchor cluster: 3:33–63, 4:156–165, 5:72–77 and 109–118, 19:16–35, 43:57–65, 61:6–14) is not a biography but a systematic correction, organized on three axes:

1. **Nature — granted, never owned.** Jesus is honored without measure — "a Word from Him," "the Messiah," "well-esteemed in this world and the next, and one of the nearest" (3:45) — yet every attribute is one of a creature: a servant who speaks first from the crib as *'abd* (19:30), "only a messenger" whose mother "was a woman of truth," both of them people who "used to eat food" (5:75), "just a servant whom We blessed" (43:59), and — the analogy that dissolves the exception — of the same make as Adam, dust then "Be" (3:59). The tell is the permission formula: his own miracle inventory is bracketed by *bi-idhnillah* twice in 3:49 and repeated four times in the divine first-person replay of 5:110 ("by My leave... by My leave... by My leave... by My leave") — power exercised by another's leave is not one's own. "It is not for Allah to have a child" (19:35) closes the dogmatic question; the positive title remains "the Word of truth about which they doubt" (19:34).
2. **Death/cross — denied with precision, silent with intent.** The text (4:157–158) asserts exactly: their claim-speech ("We have killed the Messiah..."), a double negation ("they did not kill him, nor did they crucify him"), an epistemic diagnosis ("but it appeared to them as if they did... They have no knowledge of it, except the following of assumptions"), a re-assertion ("Certainly, they did not kill him"), and a counter-act ("Rather, Allah raised him up to Himself"), with 3:55 supplying the sequencing frame ("I am terminating your life, and raising you to Me"). What the text does NOT assert: any substitute victim, any mechanism of the appearance, any identity for the "them." The substitution narratives are transmitted exegetical lore (`Source: classical tafsir scenarios, Tabari/Ibn Kathir on 4:157 — verify; not Quran`). Note the dossier's placement: the crucifixion denial is in Nisa' (4:157–158), not in the Ali 'Imran annunciation cluster.
3. **Final interrogation — the correction spoken BY the prophet.** At the surah's last court-session Allah addresses Jesus, not the Christians: "did you say to the people, `Take me and my mother as gods rather than Allah?'" (5:116). Jesus disclaims the very idea as a claim he had "no right" to make, and reduces his entire message to the command he received — "worship Allah, my Lord and your Lord" (5:117), verbatim his ministry-creed of 5:72, 3:51 and 43:64. The shirk correction is thus voiced by its apparent object: the deified himself deifies no one; authority over the community passes at the taking ("when You took me to Yourself, you became the Watcher over them," 5:117), and the dossier ends not with polemic but with a prophet's surrender of the verdict: "If You punish them, they are Your servants; but if You forgive them, You are the Mighty and Wise" (5:118).

The three axes are one argument: what is *granted* (nature) cannot be *taken* by others (cross), and cannot be *claimed* by anyone (interrogation).

## Evidence in the Text
* `[3:45]` "Allah gives you good news of a Word from Him. His name is the Messiah, Jesus, son of Mary, well-esteemed in this world and the next, and one of the nearest." — maximum honor, zero divinity; the annunciation's titulary.
* `[3:49]` "And I heal the blind and the leprous, and I revive the dead, by Allah’s leave." — Jesus' first-person miracle list, every sign qualified by permission (formula twice in this ayah — verified).
* `[3:51]` "Allah is my Lord and your Lord, so worship Him. That is a straight path." — his creed-text, the correction in his own preaching voice.
* `[3:55]` "O Jesus, I am terminating your life, and raising you to Me, and clearing you of those who disbelieve." — death and elevation as divine first-person acts, answering the kill-plot of 3:54.
* `[3:59]` "The likeness of Jesus in Allah’s sight is that of Adam: He created him from dust, then said to him, ‘Be,’ and he was." — the analogy-verse: the fatherless argument proves creaturehood, not sonship.
* `[4:157]` "In fact, they did not kill him, nor did they crucify him, but it appeared to them as if they did... Certainly, they did not kill him." — the double denial, the assumption-diagnosis, the re-assertion; no mechanism named (observable).
* `[4:158]` "Rather, Allah raised him up to Himself. Allah is Mighty and Wise." — the only counter-act the text supplies; Allah actor and destination.
* `[5:72]` "They disbelieve those who say, ‘Allah is the Messiah the son of Mary.’ But the Messiah himself said, ‘O Children of Israel, worship Allah, my Lord and your Lord." — the deification verdict answered by the deified's quotation.
* `[5:75]` "The Messiah son of Mary was only a messenger... and his mother was a woman of truth. They both used to eat food." — creaturehood evidence from bodily necessity.
* `[5:110]` "you healed the blind and the leprous, by My leave; and you revived the dead, by My leave" — the Day-replay: God's own first-person retelling repeats the permission formula four times (verified).
* `[5:116]` "did you say to the people, `Take me and my mother as gods rather than Allah?' He will say, ‘Glory be to You! It is not for me to say what I have no right to." — the interrogation: the disclaimer spoken by Jesus himself.
* `[19:30]` "I am the servant of Allah. He has given me the Scripture, and made me a prophet." — the crib speech opens with *'ubudiyyah*; his first public word is tawhid from his own mouth.
* `[19:35]` "It is not for Allah to have a child—glory be to Him." — the dogmatic exclusion, mirroring 5:116's "It is not for me."
* `[43:59]` "He was just a servant whom We blessed, and We made him an example for the Children of Israel." — Zukhruf's demotion clause: *abd*, blessed, exemplary — predicate chain complete.

(2:136 "We make no distinction between any of them" and 2:253 "We gave Jesus son of Mary the clear miracles, and We strengthened him with the Holy Spirit" — verified — place him inside the undifferentiated messenger-list: elevation yes, exception no.)

* `[23:50]` "And We made Mary’s son and his mother a sign, and We sheltered them on high ground with a settled place, and flowing water." — the dossier in one verse: the two are a SIGN (ayya), sheltered, provided — no divinity anywhere in the geography.

## Practical Reading
Watch for the dossier's distribution: the annunciation cluster is Ali 'Imran (3:33–63), but the **cross denial is in Nisa' (4:157–158)** and the **interrogation closes Ma'idah (5:116–118)** — reading only surah 3 misses the correction's sharpest edges. Search handles: `rg "son of Mary" corpus/` for the titulary sites; `rg "by Allah’s leave|by My leave" corpus/` for the permission formula (bi-idhnillah — the Quran's own grammar for borrowed power; cf. 3:145 on death itself "except by Allah’s leave" — verified). Where this dossier surfaces in later builds: **7:157** — the unlettered prophet "whom they find mentioned in the Torah and the Gospel in their possession" (verified — the Jesus-material's forward referent); **61:6** — Jesus announcing "a messenger who will come after me, whose name is Ahmad" (verified; the name "Ahmad" occurs exactly once corpus-wide, at 61:6 — check `rg "Ahmad" corpus/` before quoting rarity claims). When a 4:157 discussion starts naming a substitute or a look-alike, label it `Source: classical` — the verse names nothing. Read 3:49 ↔ 5:110 as one miracle inventory in two registers (petition vs. verdict), and 5:72 ↔ 5:117 as the same creed quoted from ministry and from defense.
